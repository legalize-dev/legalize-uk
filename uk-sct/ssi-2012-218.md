# The Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2012

Made: 4th July 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 6th July 2012

Coming into force: 1st November 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section134(7) of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement No.6) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1stNovember 2012.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1stNovember 2012 is the day appointed for section 125 (reorganisation of restricted funds) of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 to come into force for all purposes[^f00002].

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Government — 4th July 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings section 125 of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (“the Act”) fully into force on 1stNovember 2012. Section 125 amends the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 to insert Chapter 5A which makes provisions about the reorganisation of restricted funds. Section125 was commenced by the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement No.2) Order 2010 ([S.S.I. 2010/321](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2010/321)) on 1st October 2010 for the purpose only of making subordinate legislation.

Sections103, 109, 132, 133 and 134 of the Act came into force on Royal Assent (28thApril 2010).
