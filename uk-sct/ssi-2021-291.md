# The Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provision) Amendment Regulations 2021

Made: 27th August 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 31st August 2021

Coming into force: 29th September 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 63(2) and (3) of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provision) Amendment Regulations 2021 and come into force on 29 September 2021.

#### Amendment of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2021

###### 2

- (1) The Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2021[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 3 (appointed day – pre-application consultation) for “1 October 2021” substitute “1 April 2022”.

- (3) In regulation 4 (transitional provision)—

- (a) in paragraph (1) for “1 October 2021” substitute “1 April 2022”, and

- (b) in paragraph (2) for “1 October 2021” substitute “1 April 2022”.

## Signed

TOM ARTHUR — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 27th August 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2021. The changes made by regulation 2(2) amend those Regulations to bring section 18(3) of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (“the Act”) into force on 1 April 2022 rather than 1 October 2021. Regulation 2(3) makes consequential changes to the transitional provision contained in regulation 4 of those Regulations.
