# The Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 2004

Made: 12th February 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(2) of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 2004.

#### Commencement of section 2 of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002

###### 2

Section 2 of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 shall come into force on 31st March 2004.

## Signed

TAVISH SCOTT — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 12th February 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 2 of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002. The Act received Royal Assent on 7th November 2002. Section 6 came into force on Royal Assent.
