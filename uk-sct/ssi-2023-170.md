# The Feed Additives (Form of Provisional Authorisations) (Cobalt(II) Compounds) (Scotland) Regulations 2023

Made: 30th May 2023

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st June 2023

Coming into force: 30th June 2023

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Feed Additives (Form of Provisional Authorisations) (Cobalt(II) Compounds) (Scotland) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 30 June 2023.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations—

- “*Regulation [(EC) 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831)*” means Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council on additives for use in animal nutrition,

- “*Regulation [(EC) 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767)*” means Regulation [(EC) No. 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the placing on the market and use of feed, amending European Parliament and Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) and repealing Council Directive [79/373/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0373), Commission Directive [80/511/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0511), Council Directives [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471), [83/228/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0228), [93/74/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0074), [93/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0113) and [96/25/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0025) and Commission [Decision 2004/217/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0217)[^f00003].

- (2) Any expression used both in these Regulations and in Regulation [(EC) 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) or Regulation [(EC) 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767) has the same meaning as in Regulation [(EC) 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) or Regulation [(EC) 767/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0767), respectively.

#### Form of provisional authorisations

###### 3

- (1) The prescribed form of provisional authorisation of cobalt(II) acetate tetrahydrate (identification number 3b301) is set out in schedule 1.

- (2) The prescribed form of provisional authorisation of cobalt(II) carbonate (identification number 3b302) is set out in schedule 2.

- (3) The prescribed form of provisional authorisation of cobalt(II) carbonate hydroxide (2:3) monohydrate (identification number 3b303) is set out in schedule 3.

- (4) The prescribed form of provisional authorisation of cobalt(II) sulphate heptahydrate (identification number 3b305) is set out in schedule 4.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make provision as regards the provisional authorisation of feed additives under Regulation [(EC) 1831/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1831) of the European Parliament and of the Council for use in animal nutrition.

Regulation 3 and schedules 1 to 4 prescribe the form of provisional authorisation of 4 feed additives.

Schedule 1 contains the prescribed form of provisional authorisation for cobalt(II) acetate tetrahydrate. Schedule 2 contains the prescribed form of provisional authorisation for cobalt(II) carbonate. Schedule 3 contains the prescribed form of provisional authorisation for cobalt(II) carbonate hydroxide (2:3) monohydrate. Schedule 4 contains the prescribed form of provisional authorisation for cobalt(II) sulphate heptahydrate.

A business and regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no significant impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
