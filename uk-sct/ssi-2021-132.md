# The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2021

Made: 10th March 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 12th March 2021

Coming into force: 1st April 2021

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 10th day of March 2021Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2021 and comes into force on 1st April 2021.

#### Appointment of Chief Inspector

###### 2

Robert Dickson Scott, QFSM is appointed as the Chief Inspector of the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2018[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints Robert Dickson Scott, QFSM as the Chief Inspector of the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

Article 3 revokes the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2018.
