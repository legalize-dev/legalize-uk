# The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2018

Made: 23rd May 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 24th May 2018

Coming into force: 7th June 2018

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 23rd day of May 2018Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2018 and comes into force on 7th June 2018.

#### Appointment of Chief Inspector

###### 2

Simon Gerald Routh-Jones, QFSM is appointed as the Chief Inspector of the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2016[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints Simon Gerald Routh-Jones, QFSM as the Chief Inspector of the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

Article 3 revokes the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2016.
