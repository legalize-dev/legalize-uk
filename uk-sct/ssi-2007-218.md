# The Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2007

Made: 6th March 2007

Coming into force: 1st April 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 40(1) and 43(3) and (4) of the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2007.

- (2) In this Order–

- “the Act” means the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order, in so far as those provisions are not already in force, is 1st April 2007, but only for the purpose specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

## SCHEDULE — Provisions of the Act coming into force on 1st April 2007

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provisions of the Act | Subject matter | Purpose |
| Section 42(2) | Repeals | Only for the purpose of bringing into force the provisions in Schedule 3 of the Act specified in this schedule. |
| Schedule 3 | Repeals | Only for the purpose of bringing into force the repeal specified in the Table below. |

| Enactment | Extent of repeal |
| --- | --- |
| Health and Medicines Act [1988 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/49) | Section 8 |

## Signed

LEWIS MACDONALD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 6th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st April 2007 as the day for the coming into force of the repeal of section 8 of the Health and Medicines Act [1988 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/49) specified in schedule 3 to the [Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/13) (“the Act”).
