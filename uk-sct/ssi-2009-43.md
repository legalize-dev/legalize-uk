# The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2009

Made: 11th February 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 12th February 2009

Coming into force: 19th March 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2009 and comes into force on 19th March 2009.

#### General fund contribution limits

###### 2

The amount of contribution out of its general fund which a local authority may estimate that it will carry to the credit of its housing revenue account for the year 2009–2010 is limited to nil.

## Signed

NICOLA STURGEON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 11th February 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that local authorities must not include in their estimates for the year 2009–2010 any contribution from their general fund to their housing revenue account.
