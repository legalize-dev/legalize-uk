# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2006

Made: 10th January 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 11th January 2006

Coming into force: 2nd February 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 39 of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Rules:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2006 and shall come into force on 2nd February 2006.

#### Amendment of the Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994

###### 2

In rule 120(1)(a) of the Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994[^f00002], for “3” substitute “7”.

## Signed

CATHY JAMIESON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 10th January 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Rules)

These Rules amend the Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 1994 to extend the maximum period for which a prisoner may be granted “short leave”, as defined in rule 120(1)(a), from 3 nights to 7 nights.
