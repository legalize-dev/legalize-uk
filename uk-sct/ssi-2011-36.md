# The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Dissolution) Order 2011

Made: 20th January 2011

Coming into force: 1st April 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections2(1)(b) and 105(6) and (7) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Dissolution) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1stApril 2011.

#### Dissolution of NHS Quality Improvement Scotland and consequent revocations

###### 2

- (1) NHS Quality Improvement Scotland[^f00002] is dissolved.

- (2) The enactments in the Schedule to this Order are revoked.

## SCHEDULE — REVOCATIONS

###### 1

The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland Order 2002[^f00003].

###### 2

The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Transfer of Officers) Regulations 2002[^f00004].

###### 3

The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Amendment) Order 2005[^f00005].

###### 4

The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Establishment of the Scottish Health Council) Regulations 2005[^f00006].

## Signed

SHONA ROBISON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 20th January 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order dissolves the Special Health Board known as NHS Quality Improvement Scotland and revokes the enactments relating to its constitution.
