# The Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2010

Made: 2nd November 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th November 2010

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

In accordance with paragraph7 of schedule2 to that Act they have consulted with such persons or groups of persons as they consider appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Amendment (No.2) Regulations 2010 and come into force on the same day as section44 (the scheme) of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00002].

#### Amendment to the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Regulations 2001

###### 2

In regulation 4(1) (disqualification for appointment) of the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Regulations 2001[^f00003]—

- (a) omit “and” from the end of sub-paragraph (f);

- (b) after “suspended” in sub-paragraph (g) insert—

> > ; and
> (h) any person barred from regulated work with children or adults within the meaning of section92(1) or (2) of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007

## Signed

ADAM INGRAM — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 2nd November 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations come into force on the same day as section44 (which establishes the new vetting and disclosure scheme) of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (“the 2007 Act”); that day will be appointed by the Scottish Ministers by an order under that Act.

Regulation2 amends regulation 4(1) of the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Regulations 2001 to provide that any person barred from regulated work with children or adults within the meaning of section92(1) and (2) of the 2007 Act is disqualified from appointment as convener or member of the Scottish Social Services Council.
