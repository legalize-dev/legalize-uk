# Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Miscellaneous) (No. 2) 2022

Made: 12th October 2022

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 13th October 2022

Coming into force: 11th November 2022

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section104(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

#### Citation and commencement, etc.

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Miscellaneous) (No. 2) 2022.

- (2) It comes into force on 11th November 2022.

- (3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2022

###### 2

- (1) The Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2022[^f00003] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

- (2) In paragraph 1(2), for “28th November 2022” substitute “31st May 2023”.

## Signed

CJM SUTHERLAND — Lord President — I.P.D. — 12th October 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This instrument amends the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2022 to insert a later date for the coming into force of that instrument.
