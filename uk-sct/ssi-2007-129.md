# The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007

Made: 27th February 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 146(2) and 150(2) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

19th March 2007 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of section 142 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005.

###### 3

1st May 2007 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of the provisions of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS OF THE LICENSING (SCOTLAND) ACT 2005 COMING INTO FORCE ON 1ST MAY 2007

- Sections 2 to 6.

- Section 7(2).

- Section 8.

- Sections 10 to 14.

- Sections 16 and 17.

- Section 19(1).

- Section 62.

- Section 66.

- Section 71.

- Sections 90 and 91.

- Section 129.

- Section 134.

- Section 136.

- Section 139.

- Section 141.

- Section 143.

- Section 144, so far as relating to paragraphs 9 and 10 of schedule 6.

- Section 149, so far as relating to the following repeals in schedule 7– sections 1, 2(1) and (3), 3, 6 and 7 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00002]; paragraph 4 of Schedule 8 to the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00003]; section 46 of, and paragraph 106(2), (3) and (5) of Schedule 13 to, the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00004]; and section 2 of the Licensing (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 1996[^f00005].

## Signed

GEORGE LYON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 27th February 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 142 (guidance) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 into force on 19th March 2007 and also commences on 1st May 2007 the various provisions of that Act specified in the Schedule to the Order.
