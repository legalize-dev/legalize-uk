# The Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (Scotland) (Commencement No. 6) Order 2000

Made: 14th April 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 89(1) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999[^f00001], make the following order:

#### Citation and intepretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (Scotland) (Commencement No. 6) Order 2000.

- (2) In this Order, “Act” means the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of–

- (a) section 84(1) of the Act so far as relating to paragraphs 8(3) and (4) of Schedule 12 to the Act; and

- (b) paragraphs 8(3) and (4) of Schedule 12 to the Act,

is 15th April 2000.

## Signed

HENRY McLEISH — A member of the Scottish Executive — 14th April 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 on 15th April 2000–

paragraphs 8(3) and (4) of Schedule 12, and section 84(1) so far as it relates to those paragraphs.
