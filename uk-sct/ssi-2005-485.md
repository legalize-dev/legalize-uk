# The River Awe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Revocation Regulations 2005

Made: 3rd October 2005

Coming into force: 4th October 2005

The Scottish Ministers, on application to them by the River Awe District Salmon Fishery Board, having consulted and directed that notice be given in accordance with paragraphs 10 and 11 of schedule 1 to the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001], as applied by section 33(7) of that Act, and there being no objections or representations duly made with respect to the proposals in the application, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33 of that Act and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the River Awe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Revocation Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 4th October 2005.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The River Awe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Regulations 1991 are hereby revoked[^f00002].

## Signed

JOANNA YOUNG — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 3rd October 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the River Awe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Regulations 1991.
