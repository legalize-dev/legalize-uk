# The Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008

Made: 26th June 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 27th June 2008

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008 and come into force on 1st July 2008 with the exception of regulation 2(3) which comes into force on 30th June 2008.

#### Amendments to the Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Regulations 2008

###### 2

- (1) The Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Regulations 2008[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) For regulation 19(3)(a) (other transitional defences and savings) substitute–

- (a) in the case of–

- (i) a lid containing a gasket that does not comply with the restrictions and specifications for Ref. Nos. 30340, 30401, 56800, 76815, 76866, 88640 and 93760 contained in the Annex to Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 372/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0372) laying down transitional migration limits for plasticisers in gaskets in lids intended to come into contact with foods[^f00005], that the derogation contained in Article 1 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 597/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0597) amending Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 372/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0372)[^f00006] is applicable; or

- (ii) a lid containing a gasket that does not comply with the restriction and specifications for Ref. No. 36640 contained in Annex III to the Directive or a plastic material or article which does not comply with the restrictions and specifications for phthlates under Ref. Nos. 74530, 74640, 74880, 75100 and 75105 contained in Annex III, that the act constituting the alleged offence was committed in relation to a lid or a plastic material or article which was manufactured or imported into the European Community before 1st July 2008; or

- (3) Omit regulation 26(c)(revocations).

- (4) For “stimulant” substitute “simulant” in each place it appears in regulations 2(1) (interpretation) and 9(3) (required standards relating to overall migration limits), Schedule 2 (Provisions Applicable when Testing Compliance with the Migration Limits), paragraphs 6(1) and 9, and Schedule 3 (Overall and Specific Migration Testing Using Food Simulants), Part 3, paragraphs 3 and 4, Part 4, paragraph 4, Part 6, paragraph 1 and Part 7, paragraphs 1 and 3.

#### Amendment to the Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Lid Gaskets) (Scotland) Regulations 2007

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Lid Gaskets) (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00007] at the end of the definition of “the Commission Regulation” insert “as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 597/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0597)[^f00008]”.

#### Amendment to the Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Regulations 2007

###### 4

In regulation 11(5) (migration limits for regenerated cellulose film coated with plastics) of the Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00009], for “regulation 11” substitute “regulation 13”.

## Signed

S ROBISON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 26th June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for enforcement of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 597/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0597) amending Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 372/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0372) laying down transitional migration limits for plasticisers in gaskets in lids intended to come into contact with foods (O.J. No. L 164, 25.06.2008, p.12) (“the Commission Regulation”).

The Commission Regulation extends the application of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 372/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0372) until 30th April 2009.

These Regulations provide for the enforcement of the Commission Regulation by amending regulation 19(3)(a) of the Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Regulations 2008 (“the principal Regulations”) (regulation 2(2)), omitting regulation 26(c) of those Regulations and by amending the definition of “the Commission Regulation” in the Plastic Materials and Articles in Contact with Foods (Lid Gaskets) (Scotland) Regulations 2007.

These Regulations also correct errors in the principal Regulations and make a minor amendment to the Materials and Articles in Contact with Food (Scotland) Regulations 2007 consequential to an error in the principal Regulations.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
