## SCHEDULE 1 — DEFINITIONS OF COMMUNITY LEGISLATION

- “Directive 2004/41” means Directive [2004/41/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0041) of the European Parliament and of the Council repealing certain directives concerning food hygiene and health conditions for the production and placing on the market of certain products of animal origin intended for human consumption and amending Council Directives [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0662) and [92/118/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0118) and Council Decision [95/408/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0408)[^f00010];

- “Regulation 178/2002” means Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00011];

- “Regulation 852/2004” means Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0852) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the hygiene of foodstuffs[^f00012], as read with Regulation 2073/2005;

- “Regulation 853/2004” means Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin[^f00013], as amended by Regulation 2074/2005, Regulation 2076/2005, Regulation 1662/2006 and Regulation 1791/2006 and as read with Directive 2004/41, Regulation 1688/2005, Regulation 2074/2005 and Regulation 2076/2005;

- “Regulation 854/2004” means Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific rules for the organisation of official controls on products of animal origin intended for human consumption[^f00014], as amended by Regulation 882/2004, Regulation 2074/2005, Regulation 2076/2005, Regulation 1663/2006 and Regulation 1791/2006 and as read with Directive 2004/41, Regulation 2074/2005, Regulation 2075/2005 and Regulation 2076/2005;

- “Regulation 882/2004” means Regulation [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls performed to ensure the verification of compliance with feed and food law, animal health and animal welfare rules[^f00015], as amended by Regulation 776/2006 and Regulation 1791/2006 and as read with Regulation 2074/2005 and Regulation 2076/2005;

- “Regulation 1688/2005” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1688/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1688) implementing Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards special guarantees concerning salmonella for consignments to Finland and Sweden of certain meat and eggs[^f00016];

- “Regulation 2073/2005” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2073/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2073) on microbiological criteria for foodstuffs[^f00017];

- “Regulation 2074/2005” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2074) laying down implementing measures for certain products under Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council and for the organisation of official controls under Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council, derogating from Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0852) of the European Parliament and of the Council and amending Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854)[^f00018], as amended by Regulation 1664/2006;

- “Regulation 2075/2005” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2075/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2075) laying down specific rules on official controls for *Trichinella* in meat[^f00019], as amended by Regulation 1665/2006;

- “Regulation 2076/2005” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2076/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2076) laying down transitional arrangements for the implementation of Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853), [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) and [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council and amending Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854)[^f00020], as amended by Regulation 1666/2006;

- “Regulation 776/2006” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 776/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0776) amending Annex VII to Regulation 882/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards Community reference laboratories[^f00021];

- “Regulation 1662/2006” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1662/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1662) amending Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin[^f00022];

- “Regulation 1663/2006” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1663/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1663) amending Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific rules for the organisation of official controls on products of animal origin intended for human consumption[^f00023];

- “Regulation 1664/2006” means Commission Regulation 1664/2006 amending Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2074) as regards implementing measures for certain products of animal origin intended for human consumption and repealing certain implementing measures[^f00024];

- “Regulation 1665/2006” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1665/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1665) amending Regulation [(EC) No. 2075/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2075) laying down specific rules on official controls for *Trichinella* in meat[^f00025];

- “Regulation 1666/2006” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1666/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1666) amending Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2076/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2076) laying down transitional arrangements for the implementation of Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853), [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) and [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00026]; and

- “Regulation 1791/2006” means Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1791/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1791) adapting certain Regulations and Decisions in the fields of free movement of goods, freedom of movement of persons, company law, competition policy, agriculture (including veterinary and phytosanitary legislation), transport policy, taxation, statistics, energy, environment, co operation in the fields of justice and home affairs, customs union, external relations, common foreign and security policy and institutions, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania[^f00027].
