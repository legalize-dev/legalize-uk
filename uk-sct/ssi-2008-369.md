# The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2008

Made: 11th November 2008

Coming into force: 5th December 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2008 and comes into force on 5th December 2008.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 1995[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the following paragraph of this article.

- (2) In article 2(2) for “Council Directive [92/102/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0102) on the identification and registration of animals[^f00003]”, substitute “Council Directive [2008/71/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0071) on the identification and registration of pigs[^f00004]”.

## Signed

RICHARD LOCHHEAD — A member of the Scottish Executive — 11th November 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, amends the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 1995 by replacing the reference to Council Directive [92/102/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0102) on the identification and registration of animals with a reference to Council Directive [2008/71/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0071) of 15th July 2008 on the identification and registration of pigs.

2008/71/EC consolidates the provisions of Council Directive [92/102/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0102) so far as they relate to domesticated pigs.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this Order as it has no impact on the costs of business or the voluntary sector.
