# The Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011

Made: 3rd March 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section33(2) and (3) of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No.1) Order 2011.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph(2), the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 specified in column1 of the Schedule (the subject matter of which is described in column2 of the Schedule) is 30thJune 2011.

- (2) Where a purpose is specified in column3 of the Schedule in relation to any provision, that provision comes into force on 30th June 2011 for that purpose only.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 30thJune 2011 sections1, 5, 7 to 9, 11 to 13, 27, 29(1) and 30 of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”) for all purposes and sections6, 15, 20, 23, 24 and 26 for the limited purposes of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make, or to make provision by means of, subordinate legislation.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 23rdFebruary 2011. Sections31 to 33 of the Act came into force the following day.
