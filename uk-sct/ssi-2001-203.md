# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Amendment (No. 3) Order 2001

Made: 1st June 2001

Coming into force at 5.00 p.m. on: 1st June 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 17(1) of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Order 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Amendment (No. 3) Order 2001 and shall come into force at 5.00 p.m. on 1st June 2001.

#### Amendment of infected area

###### 2

For Schedule 1 of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Order 2001[^f00002], there is substituted the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to this Order.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Amendment Order 2001[^f00003] and the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2001[^f00004] are revoked.

## SCHEDULE — SCHEDULE 1 AS SUBSTITUTED BY THIS ORDER

## Signed

DAVID R DICKSON — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 1st June 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reduces the infected area declared by the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Order 2001 and subject to the restrictions therein.

A map depicting the infected area may be inspected between 9.00 a.m. and 5.00 p.m., Monday to Friday at the Scottish Executive Rural Affairs Department, Pentland House, 47 Robb’s Loan, Edinburgh EH14 1TW.
