# The Discontinuance of Aberdeen and Peterhead Prisons (Scotland) Order 2014

Made: 15th January 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 17th January 2014

Coming into force: 26th February 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(1) of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Discontinuance of Aberdeen and Peterhead Prisons (Scotland) Order 2014 and comes into force on 26th February 2014.

#### Discontinuance of Prisons

###### 2

Aberdeen Prison and Peterhead Prison are discontinued.

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Government — 15th January 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the discontinuance of Aberdeen Prison and Peterhead Prison on the day on which the Order comes into force.
