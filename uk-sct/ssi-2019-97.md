# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 Amendment) (Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering) (No.2) 2019

Made: 19th March 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 20th March 2019

Coming into force: 29th March 2019

The Court of Session makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by sections 66 and 67 of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008[^f00001], section 103(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

#### Citation and commencement, etc.

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 Amendment) (Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering) (No.2) 2019.

- (2) It comes into force on 29th March 2019.

- (3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 Amendment) (Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering) 2019

###### 2

- (1) The Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 Amendment) (Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering) 2019[^f00003] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

- (2) Before paragraph 3(1) (revocation) insert—

> > (A1) Chapter 101 of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994 (terrorist asset-freezing)[^f00004] is revoked.

## Signed

CJM SUTHERLAND — Lord President — I.P.D. — 19th March 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Sederunt amends Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 Amendment) (Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering) 2019 to insert provision to revoke Chapter 101 of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994 which should have been included in that Act of Sederunt.
