# The Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council (Listed Tribunals) (Scotland) Order 2007

Made: 19th September 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 21st September 2007

Coming into force: 1st November 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 49(3) of, and paragraphs 25(2), (3)(a)(ii) and (4) and 26 of Schedule 7 to, the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council (Listed Tribunals) (Scotland) Order 2007 and comes into force on 1st November 2007.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Listed tribunals

###### 2

The tribunals specified in the Schedule to this Order are listed tribunals for the purposes of Schedule 7 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (“the 2007 Act”). The 2007 Act establishes the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council (“the AJTC”), which has both a Scottish and a Welsh Committee, to replace the Council on Tribunals and its Scottish Committee. It provides for the AJTC to keep under review, consider and report on matters relating to “listed tribunals”.

The Act itself provides for only two “listed tribunals”: the First tier Tribunal and the Upper Tribunal. This Order adds further tribunals, in relation to which the Scottish Ministers are the responsible authority, to the “listed tribunals”. Although the remit of the Scottish Committee of the AJTC is not restricted to matters relating to those “listed tribunals” that are listed by Scottish Ministers, that Committee will keep under review, consider and report on matters relating to the tribunals that are listed in this Order.
