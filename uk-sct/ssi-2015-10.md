# The Plant Health (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015

Made: 15th January 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th January 2015

Coming into force: 26th February 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2, 3 and 4(1) of the Plant Health Act 1967 and section 20 of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972 and all other powers enabling them to do so.

This Order makes provision for preventing the introduction and spread of pests in Scotland and is called for by EU obligations in (1) Commission Implementing Decision 2012/270/EU as regards emergency measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of *Epitrix cucumeris*(Harris), *Epitrix similaris*(Gentner), *Epitrix subcrinita*(Lec.) and *Epitrix tuberis*(Gentner) ; (2) Commission Implementing Decision 2014/679/EU amending Implementing Decision 2012/270/EU as regards its period of application and as regards the movement to packing facilities of potato tubers originating in areas demarcated in order to prevent the spread within the Union of *Epitrix cucumeris*(Harris), *Epitrix similaris*(Gentner), *Epitrix subcrinita*(Lec.) and *Epitrix tuberis*(Gentner) ; (3) Commission Implementing Decision 2012/697/EU as regards measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of the genus *Pomacea* (Perry) ; (4) Commission Implementing Directive 2014/78/EU amending Annexes I, II, III, IV and V to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community ; (5) Commission Implementing Directive 2014/83/EU amending Annexes I, II, III, IV and V to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community ; (6) Commission Implementing Decision 2014/422/EU setting out measures in respect of certain citrus fruits originating in South Africa to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of *Phyllosticta citricarpa* (McAlpine) Van der Aa ; (7) Commission Implementing Decision 2014/497/EU as regards measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Union of *Xylella fastidiosa* (Well and Raju) ; and (8) Commission Implementing Decision 2014/690/EU repealing Decision [2006/464/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0464) on provisional emergency measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of *Dryocosmus kuriphilus* Yasumatsu .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015 and comes into force on 26th February 2015.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Scotland) Order 2005

###### 2

The Plant Health (Scotland) Order 2005 is amended in accordance with articles 3 to 12.

#### Amendment of article 2

###### 3

In article 2(1) (general interpretation)—

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)” substitute—

> > “*Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)*” means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community

; and

- (b) for the definition of “protected zone” substitute—

> > “*protected zone*” means a member State or an area in a member State which is recognised as a protected zone exposed to particular plant health risks for the purposes of Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029), as listed in Annex 1 to Commission Regulation [(EC) No 690/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0690) recognising protected zones exposed to particular plant health risks in the Community ;
