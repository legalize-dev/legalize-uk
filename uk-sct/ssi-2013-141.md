# The Regulation of Care (Social Service Workers) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013

Made: 9th May 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 13th May 2013

Coming into force: 1st August 2013

In accordance with section 44(4) of that Act they have consulted the Scottish Social Services Council and such other persons and groups as they consider appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Regulation of Care (Social Service Workers) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st August 2013.

#### Amendment to the Regulation of Care (Social Service Workers) (Scotland) Order 2005

###### 2

In article 1(2) of the Regulation of Care (Social Service Workers) (Scotland) Order 2005 (citation, commencement and interpretation)[^f00002] in the definition of “residential child care worker” omit “, not being a social worker,”.

## Signed

AILEEN CAMPBELL — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 9th May 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the definition of residential child care worker in the Regulation of Care (Social Service Workers) (Scotland) Order 2005 by removing the reference to a social worker, thereby providing that a social worker may also be included in the parts of the register maintained by the Scottish Social Services Council which are relevant to a residential child care workers.
