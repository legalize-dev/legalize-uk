# The Scottish Landfill Tax (Standard Rate and Lower Rate) Order 2025

Made: 18th February 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th February 2025

Coming into force: 1st April 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(2) and (5) of the Landfill Tax (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Scottish Landfill Tax (Standard Rate and Lower Rate) Order 2025 and comes into force on 1 April 2025.

- (2) This Order has effect in relation to taxable disposals made, or treated as made, on or after 1 April 2025.

#### Rates of Scottish landfill tax

###### 2

For the purposes of section 13 of the Landfill Tax (Scotland) Act 2014—

- (a) the standard rate is £126.15,

- (b) the lower rate is £4.05.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Scottish Landfill Tax (Standard Rate and Lower Rate) Order 2024[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

SHONA ROBISON — A member of the Scottish Government — 18th February 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies the standard rate and lower rate for Scottish landfill tax for disposals on or after 1 April 2025. The standard rate is £126.15 and the lower rate is £4.05. Tax is to be calculated by reference to the weight and types of material disposed of, as provided for in section 13 of the Landfill Tax (Scotland) Act 2014.
