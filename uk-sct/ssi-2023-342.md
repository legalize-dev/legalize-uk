# The Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 7) Amendment Regulations 2023

Made: 14th November 2023

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 16th November 2023

Coming into force: 30th November 2023

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 130(2) of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 7) Amendment Regulations 2023 and come into force on 30 November 2023.

#### Amendment of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2023

###### 2

- (1) The Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2023[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the table in the schedule, in the entry for section 69 (interpretation of Part 6), in column 3 (appointed day), for “11 December 2022” substitute “11 December 2023”.

## Signed

FIONA HYSLOP — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 14th November 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2023 (“*the 2023 Regulations*”) in order to correct a clerical error.

Regulation 2(2) amends the date appointed by the 2023 Regulations for the coming into force of section 69 of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019.
