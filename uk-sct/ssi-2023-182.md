# The Management of Offenders (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2023

Made: 14th June 2023

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 16th June 2023

Coming into force: 3rd July 2023

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 63(2) of the Management of Offenders (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Management of Offenders (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 3 July 2023.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

3 July 2023 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 58 (parole board recommendations: publication of test) of the Management of Offenders (Scotland) Act 2019.

## Signed

ANGELA CONSTANCE — A member of the Scottish Government — 14th June 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force section 58 (parole board recommendations: publication of test) of the Management of Offenders (Scotland) Act 2019 (“*the 2019 Act*”) on 3 July 2023.

Section 58 inserts a new subsection (3B) into section 1 of the Prisoners and Criminal Proceedings (Scotland) Act [1993 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/9) (“*the 1993 Act*”), which requires the Parole Board for Scotland to publish, in such manner as it considers appropriate, the test that it will apply in making a recommendation to release a long-term prisoner under subsection (3) of section 1 of the 1993 Act. The Bill for the 2019 Act received Royal Assent on 30 July 2019. Sections 62, 63 and 64 came into force on the following day.
