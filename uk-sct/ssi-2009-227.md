> (6) Council Directive [90/539/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0539).
> (7) Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1774/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1774) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules concerning animal by-products not intended for human consumption[^f00016].

> (9) Council Directive [91/68/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0068).
> (10) Council Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065).
> (11) Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1739/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1739).
> (12) Council Directive [92/118/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0118) laying down animal health and public health requirements governing trade in and imports into the Community of products not subject to the said requirements laid down in specific Community rules referred to in Annex A(I) to Directive [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0662) and, as regards pathogens, to Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425)[^f00018].
> (13) Council Directive [77/504/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0504) on pure bred breeding animals of the bovine species[^f00019].
> (14) Council Directive [88/661/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0661) on the zootechnical standards applicable to breeding animals of the porcine species[^f00020].
> (15) Council Directive [89/361/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0361) concerning pure bred breeding sheep and goats[^f00021].
