# The Home Detention Curfew Licence (Prescribed Standard Conditions) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2008

Made: 18th March 2008

Coming into force: 20th March 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by article 11(4)(b) of the Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Statutory Instruments) Order 1999[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Home Detention Curfew Licence (Prescribed Standard Conditions) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2008 and comes into force on 20th March 2008.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Home Detention Curfew Licence (Prescribed Standard Conditions) (Scotland) Order 2008[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 18th March 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Home Detention Curfew Licence (Prescribed Standard Conditions) (Scotland) Order 2008 which prescribes standard conditions to be included in any licence issued in respect of long-term or short-term prisoners released on licence under section 3AA(1) of the Prisoners and Criminal Proceedings (Scotland) Act 1993.

The Scottish Parliament approved a motion to annul that Order on 12th March 2008.
