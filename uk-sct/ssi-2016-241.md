# Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 2016

Made: 30th August 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st September 2016

Coming into force: 27th September 2016

The Court of Session makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section 57(2) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 2016 and comes into force on 27th September 2016.

- (2) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Registration Appeal Court

###### 2

- (1) The court for hearing appeals under section 57(1)(b) of the Representation of the People Act 1983 shall consist of the Right Honourable Lord Brodie, the Right Honourable Lord Drummond Young and the Honourable Lord Matthews.

- (2) The Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 2013[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

CJM SUTHERLAND — Lord President — I.P.D. — 30th August 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Sederunt appoints three judges to hear registration appeals under section 57 of the Representation of the People Act 1983. It revokes the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 2013 ([S.S.I. 2013/236](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2013/236)), which previously made such provision.
