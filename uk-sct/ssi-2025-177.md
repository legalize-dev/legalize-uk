# The Regional Strategic Bodies and Regional Colleges (Glasgow and Lanarkshire) Order 2025

Made: 10th June 2025

Coming into force: 30th July 2025

In accordance with section 60(2B) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00003] and section 34(4)(bc) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00004], a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament[^f00005].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Regional Strategic Bodies and Regional Colleges (Glasgow and Lanarkshire) Order 2025 and comes into force on 30 July 2025.

#### Abolition of regional strategic bodies

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 2A of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (regional strategic bodies)[^f00006] is modified as follows.

- (2) In Part 1 (regional boards), the entry for “Regional Board for Glasgow Colleges” is repealed.

- (3) In Part 2 (other regional strategic bodies), the entry for “New College Lanarkshire” is repealed.

###### 3

The Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council[^f00007] may take such action as is necessary for the purpose of concluding any outstanding matters associated with the abolition of the Regional Board for Glasgow Colleges.

#### Designation of regional colleges

###### 4

- (1) The following colleges of further education, whose boards of management are established in pursuance of Part 1 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992, are designated as regional colleges—

- (a) the board of management of City of Glasgow College[^f00008],

- (b) the board of management of Glasgow Clyde College[^f00009],

- (c) the board of management of Glasgow Kelvin College[^f00010],

- (d) the board of management of South Lanarkshire College[^f00011].

- (2) The designation of a college of further education as a regional college by paragraph (1) is to remain in effect notwithstanding any change to the name of the college.

#### Amendment of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992

###### 5

Paragraphs 3(5), 5(6) and 5D of schedule 2 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00012] are repealed.

#### Amendment of the Lanarkshire Colleges Order 2014

###### 6

Omit articles 4 and 5 of the Lanarkshire Colleges Order 2014[^f00013].

#### Amendment of the Colleges of Further Education and Regional Strategic Bodies (Membership of Boards) (Scotland) Order 2023

###### 7

Omit article 4 of the Colleges of Further Education and Regional Strategic Bodies (Membership of Boards) (Scotland) Order 2023[^f00014].

#### Revocation

###### 8

The Assigned Colleges (Scotland) Order 2014[^f00015] is revoked.

## Signed

GRAEME DEY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 10th June 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order abolishes two regional strategic bodies and designates four regional colleges under the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (“*the 2005 Act*”).

Article 2 modifies schedule 2A of the 2005 Act (regional strategic bodies), removing the entries for the Regional Board for Glasgow Colleges and New College Lanarkshire for the purpose of abolishing those regional strategic bodies.

Article 3 gives the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council the power to take any action necessary to conclude any outstanding matters associated with the abolition of the Glasgow Colleges Regional Board.

Article 4 designates as regional colleges the four colleges which had been assigned to the regional strategic bodies abolished by this Order.

Articles 5, 6 and 7 make consequential amendments to schedule 2 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992, the Lanarkshire Colleges Order 2014 and the Colleges of Further Education and Regional Strategic Bodies (Membership of Boards) (Scotland) Order 2023.

Article 8 revokes the Assigned Colleges (Scotland) Order 2014.
