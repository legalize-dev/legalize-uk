# The Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010

Made: 24th February 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section22(2) of the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The provisions of the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010, in so far as they are not already in force, come into force on 5th April 2010.

## Signed

MICHAEL RUSSELL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 24th February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 5th April 2010 those provisions of the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010 which are not already in force.
