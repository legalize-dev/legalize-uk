# The Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2009

Made: 12th May 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59(2) of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2009.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 31 of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 comes into force on 14th May 2009.

## Signed

STEWART STEVENSON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 12th May 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 31 of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (“the Act”) into force on 14th May 2009.
