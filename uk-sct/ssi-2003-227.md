# The Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Partial Revocation Order 2003

Made: 31st March 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 89(2) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Partial Revocation Order 2003.

###### 2

Article 2(2) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2003[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

MALCOLM CHISHOLM — A member of the Scottish Executive — 31st March 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes article 2(2) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2003. Article 2(2) would otherwise have brought into force on 1st April 2003 Part 4 of, and schedule 1 to, the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000, together with certain provisions from schedules 4 to 6 to that Act.
