# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2006

Made: 9th August 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th August 2006

Coming into force: 11th August 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 , and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 11th August 2006.

#### Amendment to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002

###### 2

- (1) The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In Part VIII of Schedule 2 (import conditions for products)–

- (a) for paragraph 4 of cross heading A (General) substitute–

> > (4) Commission Decision [2005/760/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0760) concerning certain protection measures in relation to highly pathogenic avian influenza in certain third countries for the import of captive birds (O.J. No. L 285, 28.10.05, p.60), as last amended by Commission Decision [2006/522/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0522) (O.J. No. L 205, 27.07.06, p.28).

- (b) for paragraph 5 of cross heading A (General) substitute–

> > (5) Commission Decision [2006/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0007) concerning certain protection measures in relation to the import of feathers from certain third countries (O.J. No. L 5, 10.1.06, p.17) as last amended by Commission Decision [2006/521/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0521) (O.J. No L205, 27.07.06, p.26).

- (c) for paragraph 1 of cross heading E (Animal By Products) substitute–

> > (1) Regulation [(EC) No. 1774/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1774) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down health rules concerning animal by-products not intended for human consumption (O.J. No. L 273, 10.10.02, p.1) as last amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 208/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0208) (O.J. No. L 36, 8.2.06, p.25), and as read with Commission Decision [2005/760/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0760) (O.J. No. L 285, 28.10.05, p.60), as last amended by Commission Decision [2006/522/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0522) (O.J. No. L 205, 27.07.06, p.28).

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 9th August 2006
