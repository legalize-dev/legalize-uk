# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made: 7th March 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 13th March 2002

Coming into force at 5.00 p.m. on: 7th March 2002

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force at 5.00 p.m on 7th March 2002.

#### Amendment of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00002] are amended in accordance with the following provisions of this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 12–

- (a) at the end of paragraph (2), there is inserted “and are satisfied that the operator of the assembly centre has agreed to comply with any requirements as may be specified by the inspector as to the operation of that centre which an inspector believes are necessary to ensure the centre is capable of being operated in accordance with the second indent of paragraph 3(2) of Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425)”; and

- (b) in paragraph (3) for “cattle or pigs”, there is substituted “cattle, pigs, sheep or goats”.

- (3) In Schedule 3, Part I, paragraph 9, after “Commission Decision [97/315/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0315) (O.J. No. L 137, 28.5.97, p.20)”, there is inserted–

> > ;
> Commission Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327) (O.J. No. L 115, 25.4.01, p.12)

## Signed

DAVID R DICKSON — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 7th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Regulations 2000.

They implement in Scotland article 4 of Commission Decision [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) (O.J. No. L 50, 21.2.02, p.98) concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom, repealing Decision [2001/740/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0740) and amending for the eighth time Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327) and the application by Commission Decision [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) of Commission Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327) to the United Kingdom.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations.
