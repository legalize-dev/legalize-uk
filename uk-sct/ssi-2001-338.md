# The Argyll and Clyde Acute Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made: 28th September 2001

Coming into force: 30th September 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12A(1) and 105(6) and (7) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(c) of Schedule 7A to, the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Argyll and Clyde Acute Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 30th September 2001.

- (2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Argyll and Clyde Acute Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 2

For article 4 of the principal Order there is substituted–

> > (4) The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 2 trustees and 5 executive directors.

## Signed

SUSAN C DEACON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 28th September 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Argyll and Clyde Acute Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998 (“the 1998 Order”). Article 2 amends, by substitution, article 4 of the 1998 Order to reduce the number of trustees from 5 to 2. The number of executive directors remains unchanged.
