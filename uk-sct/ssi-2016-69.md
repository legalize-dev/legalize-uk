## SCHEDULE 1 — FEES FOR MATTERS RELATING TO THE SEED MARKETING REGULATIONS

| Matter | *Fee* *£* |
| --- | --- |
| A. Initial fees | A. Initial fees |
| (i) Initial fee per hectare or part thereof, except where sub-paragraph (ii) applies, for applications in respect of crops intended to produce:— |  |
| Pre-basic Seed or Basic Seed | 29.60 |
| Certified Seed, Certified Seed of the First Generation, Certified Seed of the Second Generation or Certified Seed of the Third Generation | 4.25 |
| (ii) Reduced initial fee per hectare or part thereof for applications referred to in sub-paragraph (i)(a) or (b) which are withdrawn before arrangements have been made for the purposes of an official examination. | 2.75 |
| (iii) Late application fee per crop payable in addition to initial fee under sub-paragraph (i)(a) or (b) where application is not made by the date for enabling arrangements to be made for an official examination of a crop grown to produce seed of a species to which any instrument comprising the Seed Marketing Regulations apply. | 29.00 |
| B. Crop inspection fees | B. Crop inspection fees |
| (i) Fee for the official examination of crops other than a hybrid of swede rape intended to produce Pre-basic Seed, Basic Seed, Certified Seed, Certified Seed of the First Generation, Certified Seed of the Second Generation or Certified Seed of the Third Generation, per hectare or part thereof examined. | 18.80 |
| (ii) Fee for the official field inspection of a hybrid of swede rape, per hectare or part thereof examined. | 56.40 |
| (iii) Fee for the further official examination of crops intended to produce Pre-basic Seed, Basic Seed, Certified Seed, Certified Seed of the First Generation, Certified Seed of the Second Generation or Certified Seed of the Third Generation, per hectare or part thereof examined, except:— | 18.80 |
| further official examination of crops for wild oats, per hectare or part thereof examined | 8.65 |
| further official examination of crops in respect of isolation distance, per hectare or part thereof examined | 3.40 |
| further official examination of crops which have been lodged, per hectare or part thereof examined | 7.65 |
| (iv) Fee for the authentication of seed lot sown (where this has not been carried out at crop inspection), per crop. | 7.65 |
| (v) Fee for the official examination of crops where there has been an official post control the results of which have been unsatisfactory, per hectare or part thereof examined. | 18.80 |
| (vi) Fee payable in addition to fee under sub-paragraph (i) to (v) above where arrangements have been made for an official examination to be carried out by a licensed crop inspector and the applicant subsequently requests that the examination be carried out instead by the Scottish Ministers. | 29.00 |
| C. Seed lot fees | C. Seed lot fees |
| Fee for issuing an official certificate or a breeder’s confirmation (other than in the circumstances described under paragraph D below) in respect of a seed lot certifying the seed to be:— |  |
| of a multiplication category | 85.00 |
| of a final generation category | 33.00 |
| D. Seed lot re-entry fee | D. Seed lot re-entry fee |
| Fee for issuing an official certificate or a breeder’s confirmation in respect of a seed lot where, following an application for certification of the seed to be within one particular category, that application has been withdrawn and a new application has been submitted for certification of the seed within another category, as follows:— |  |
| Pre-basic Seed, Basic Seed, Certified Seed or Certified Seed of the First Generation | 35.00 |
| Certified Seed of the Second Generation or Certified Seed of the Third Generation | 17.45 |
| E. Sampling fees | E. Sampling fees |
| Fee per seed lot for the sampling or re-sampling of a seed lot for the purposes of an official examination, and for sampling or re-sampling imported seed intended for further multiplication. | 50.12 |
| F. Official label fees | F. Official label fees |
| Fee per seed lot for the issue and printing of a:— |  |
| tear resistant label for Pre-basic Seed, seeds for export or seeds following the issue of a breeder’s confirmation | 40.50 |
| self adhesive label for Pre-basic Seed, seeds for export or seeds following the issue of a breeder’s confirmation | 28.00 |
| G. OECD Certificate | G. OECD Certificate |
| Fee per certificate for issuing an Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) Certificate. | 25.00 |
| H. Higher Voluntary Standards (HVS) | H. Higher Voluntary Standards (HVS) |
| Fee per seed lot for verification that a seed lot meets the applicable Higher Voluntary Standards (HVS). | 7.00 |
