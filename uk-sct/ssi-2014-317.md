# The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) Regulations 2014

Made: 19th November 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 20th November 2014

Coming into force: 19th December 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(6) of the Clean Air Act 1993[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 19th December 2014.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993

###### 2

Anthracite, semi-anthracite, electricity, gas, low volatile steam coals and the fuels described in the Schedule are declared to be authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) Regulations 2010[^f00002] are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, declare the fuels which are authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993. The authorised fuels are described in regulation 2 and the Schedule.

In effect these Regulations re-enact the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) Regulations 2010 (“the 2010 Regulations”), with the addition of twenty one further fuels (Big K Restaurant Grade Charcoal, Brazier briquettes, Briteheat Plus briquettes, Coalite Ovals briquettes, Direct Charcoal Blue Bag Premium Grade Restaurant Charcoal, Direct Charcoal Blue Bag Grade Restaurant Charcoal, Ecoal 50 briquettes, EDF Fuel briquettes, Excel+ briquettes, G N Grosvenor (Fuel Express) Restaurant Charcoal, Hot Drops, HouseFuel Smokes Ovals (two types), Newburn briquettes, Newburn briquettes (also marketed as Housefuel Burnrite), Newflame Plus briquettes, Newheat briquettes, Pagan Fuel’s Restaurant Charcoal, ZIP 100% Natural Firelogs (two sizes) and ZIP 100% Natural Stove Logs at paragraphs 5, 9, 12, 16, 19, 20, 25, 27, 28, 31, 35, 36, 37, 44, 45, 47, 48, 50, 69, 70 and 71 of the Schedule respectively), and an amendment to the description of Stoveheat Premium briquettes at paragraph 55 of the Schedule. Regulation 3 of these Regulations revokes the 2010 Regulations.

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
