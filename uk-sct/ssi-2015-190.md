# The Forth Road Bridge Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

Made: 6th May 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th May 2015

Coming into force: 1st June 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section7(2) of the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st June 2015.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1st June 2015 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013, so far as not then already in force.

## Signed

DEREK MACKAY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 6th May 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Sections 6 to 8 of the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013 came into force on 29th June 2013 (the day after that Act received Royal Assent). This Order appoints 1st June 2015 as the day on which the remaining provisions of that Act will come into force.
