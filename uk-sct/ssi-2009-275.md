# The Local Government in Scotland Act 2003 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2009

Made: 14th July 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section62(2) of the Local Government in Scotland Act2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as [the Local Government in Scotland Act2003 (Commencement No.4) Order2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/4).

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section40 of the Local Government in Scotland Act 2003 (power of local authorities to invest money) shall come into force on 8thSeptember 2009.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Executive — 14th July 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article2 of this Order appoints a day for the coming into force of section40 of the Local Government in Scotland Act2003 (“theAct”).

Section40 provides that the Scottish Ministers may make Regulations, specifying the investments which local authorities may make and providing for guidance to regulate the investment activity.

The provisions of the Act remaining to be commenced after the making of this Order are sections8 (partially), 9 and11.
