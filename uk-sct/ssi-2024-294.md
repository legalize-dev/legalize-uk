# The Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2024

Made: 29th October 2024

Coming into force: 30th October 2024

In accordance with paragraph 1(6) of schedule 7 of that Act[^f00002], a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2024 and come into force on 30 October 2024.

#### The Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2014

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2014[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In Regulation 4(8) (fees)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “30th October 2024” substitute “1st July 2025”.

- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) after “relevant port” insert “before 1st July 2025”.

## Signed

JIM FAIRLIE — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 29th October 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2014 (the “*2014 Regulations*”), which makes provision relating to fees for documentary, physical and identity checks on certain plants imported into Scotland.

Regulation 4 of the 2014 Regulations makes provision relating to fees for documentary, physical and identity checks on certain plants imported into Scotland.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 4(8) of the 2014 Regulations to change the dates on which fees will be payable for documentary, physical and identity checks on certain consignments originating in an EU Member State, Liechtenstein or Switzerland.
