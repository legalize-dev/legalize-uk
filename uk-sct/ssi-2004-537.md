# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2004

Made: 7th December 2004

Coming into force: 8th December 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 7 and 23(f) and (g) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 8th December 2004.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Amendments to the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978

###### 2

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:–

- (a) in article 6(2) for “31st August 2003” substitute “28th February 2005”[^f00003];

- (b) for Schedule 1 (approved disinfectants), substitute the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to this Order; and

- (c) for Schedule 2 (approved disinfectants subject to transitional provisions), substitute the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, amends the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978 by substituting Schedules 1 and 2 to that Order (article 2). Schedule 1 provides an updated list of disinfectants approved by the Scottish Ministers. Schedule 2 lists disinfectants which can continue to be used as approved disinfectants until 28th February 2005.

This Order was notified in draft to the European Commission in accordance with Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) (O.J. No. L 204, 21/07/98, p.37) as amended by Directive [98/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0048) (O.J. No. L 217, 05/08/98, p.18).

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared in respect of this Order.
