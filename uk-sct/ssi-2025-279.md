# The Cromarty Firth Port Authority Harbour Revision Order 2025

Made: 3rd October 2025

Coming into force: 4th October 2025

In accordance with paragraph 19(6) of that Schedule[^f00005], the Ministers have decided to make this Order with modifications which do not appear to the Ministers substantially to affect the character of the Order.

## PART 1 — Preliminary

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Cromarty Firth Port Authority Harbour Revision Order 2025 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) The Cromarty Firth Port Authority Orders 1973 to 2003[^f00006] and this Order may be cited together as the Cromarty Firth Port Authority Orders 1973 to 2025.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “*the 1973 Order*” means the Cromarty Firth Port Authority Order 1973[^f00007] confirmed by the Cromarty Firth Port Authority Order Confirmation Act 1973[^f00008],

- “*the 2003 Order*” means the Cromarty Firth Port Authority (Constitution) Revision Order 2003[^f00009],

- “*the Authority*” means the Cromarty Firth Port Authority, Port Office, Shore Road, Invergordon, IV18 0HD.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order defines the harbour limits and confers updated powers on the Cromarty Firth Port Authority, including powers to lease, the making of byelaws and issuing of general and special directions. It also amends or repeals obsolete or otherwise unnecessary statutory provision affecting the port.
