# Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2025

Made: 6th May 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th May 2025

Coming into force: 10th June 2025

The Court of Session makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879[^f00001] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

#### Citation and commencement, etc.

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2025.

- (2) It comes into force on 10th June 2025.

- (3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Lands Valuation Appeal Court

###### 2

The judges named for the purposes of appeal under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879 are—

- (a) The Right Honourable Lord Malcolm;

- (b) The Right Honourable Lord Doherty;

- (c) The Right Honourable Lord Tyre; and

- (d) The Honourable Lord Ericht.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2023[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

PAUL CULLEN — Lord President — I.P.D. — 6th May 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Sederunt appoints the judges who may hear appeals under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879. It revokes the Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2023 which previously made such provision.
