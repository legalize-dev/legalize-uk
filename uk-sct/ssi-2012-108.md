# The Public Contracts and Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2012

Made: 28th March 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th March 2012

Coming into force: 1st May 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of their powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Contracts and Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2012 and come into force on the 1stMay 2012.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- (a) “Public Contracts Regulations” means the Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2012[^f00002]; and

- (b) “Utilities Contracts Regulations” means the Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2012[^f00003].

- (3) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Public Contracts Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Public Contracts Regulations are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 1 (GPA Annex 1 Contracting Authorities)—

- (a) for “Scotland, General Register Office” substitute “Scotland, National Records of Scotland”;

- (b) for “Crofters Commission” substitute “Crofting Commission”;

- (c) after the entry for “Scottish Law Commission” insert “Common Services Agency”;

- (d) for “Special Health Boards constituted under section 2 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00004]” substitute “Health Boards and Special Health Boards constituted under section 2 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978”;

- (e) before the entry for “the Office of the Accountant of Court” insert “Health Improvement Scotland”; and

- (f) omit “Scottish Record Office”.

#### Amendment of the Utilities Contracts Regulations

###### 3

- (1) The Utilities Contracts Regulations are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 5 (Consequential Amendments and Revocations)—

- (a) in Part A, in the entry regarding [S.S.I. 2008/376](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2008/376), in column 3, for “the Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2006”, substitute “the Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2006”; and

- (b) in Part B, in the entry regarding [S.I. 2011/1848](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1848), in column 3, after “the Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations” where it first appears, insert “2006”.

## Signed

ALEX NEIL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 28th March 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations correct errors regarding certain health and other bodies in Schedule 1 to the Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2012 and Schedule 5 to the Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2012.
