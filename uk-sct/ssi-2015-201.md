# Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules 1996 Amendment) (No. 3) (Miscellaneous) 2015

Made: 20th May 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 22nd May 2015

Coming into force: 8th June 2015

The High Court of Justiciary makes this Act of Adjournal under the powers conferred by section305 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001], and all other powers enabling it to do so.

#### Citation, commencement etc.

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Adjournal may be cited as the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules 1996 Amendment) (No.3) (Miscellaneous) 2015.

- (2) It comes into force on 8th June 2015.

- (3) A certified copy of this Act of Adjournal is to be inserted in the Books of Adjournal.

#### Amendment of the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules) 1996

###### 2

- (1) The Criminal Procedure Rules 1996[^f00002] are amended in accordance with this paragraph.

- (2) In rule 61.9(1) (where competent authority not known)[^f00003], for “61.3(4)” substitute “61.3(3)”.

- (3) For Form 16.1–C (form of reply to complaint)[^f00004] substitute Form 16.1–C set out in the Schedule to this Act of Adjournal.

- (4) In Form 61.6–B (form of information regarding a non-harassment order under section 254D of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00005], for paragraph (b) substitute—

> > (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 12 months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both such imprisonment and such fine.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

BRIAN GILL — Lord Justice General — I.P.D. — 20th May 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Adjournal)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Adjournal amends the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996 by modifying Form 16.1-C to reflect the abolition of driving licence counterparts. It also corrects drafting errors in rule 61.9 and in Form 61.6-B.
