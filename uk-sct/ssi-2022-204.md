# The Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2022

Made: 8th June 2022

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th June 2022

Coming into force: 24th June 2022

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 130(2) of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2022 and come into force on 24 June 2022.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 is 24 June 2022—

- (a) section 34 (provision of bus services etc. by local transport authorities),

- (b) paragraphs 3(1) and (6)(a)(v)(A) and 4(1) and (2)(a) of the schedule (minor and consequential amendments and repeals).

## Signed

JENNY GILRUTH — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 8th June 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring section 34 and paragraphs 3(1) and (6)(a)(v)(A) and 4(1) and (2)(a) of the schedule of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2019 (“*the 2019 Act*”) into force on 24 June 2022.

Section 34 of the 2019 Act amends the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001 to confer a power on local transport authorities to provide services for the carriage of passengers by road using vehicles that require a PSV operator’s licence to do so. Local transport authorities are able to exercise that power provided they are satisfied that providing such services will contribute to the implementation of their relevant general policies. Paragraph 3(1) and (6)(a)(v)(A) of the schedule of the 2019 Act amends the definition of “relevant general policies” in section 48 of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001 for this purpose. Paragraph 4(1) and (2)(a) of the schedule of the 2019 Act amends section 10 of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2005 to permit the Scottish Ministers to transfer the function of providing services for the carriage of passengers by road using vehicles that require a PSV operator’s licence to do so to Regional Transport Partnerships.

The Bill for the 2019 Act received Royal Assent on 15 November 2019. Sections 128 to 131 came into force the following day.
