# The Scottish Languages Act 2025 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026

Made: 17th November 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th November 2026

Coming into force: 30th November 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 48(2) of the Scottish Languages Act 2025[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Scottish Languages Act 2025 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 30 November 2026.

- (2) In these Regulations, “*the Act*” means the Scottish Languages Act 2025.

#### Appointed day: section 6 of the Act

###### 2

30 November 2026 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 6 of the Act.

## Signed

MAIRI MCALLAN — A member of the Scottish Government — 17th November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations bring section 6 of the Scottish Languages Act 2025 (“*the Act*”) into force on 30 November 2026.

Section 6 of the Act confers a power on the Scottish Ministers to specify in regulations standards and requirements relating to promoting, facilitating and supporting the use of the Gaelic language, applying to relevant public authorities in the exercise of their functions.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 31 July 2025. Section 48(1) of the Act brought into force Part 3 of the Act on the day after Royal Assent.
