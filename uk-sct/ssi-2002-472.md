# The Discontinuance of Legalised Police Cells (Ayr) Rules 2002

Made: 22nd October 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 25th October 2002

Coming into force: 30th November 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14 and 39 of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and on the application of the Strathclyde Joint Police Board, hereby make the following Rules:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Discontinuance of Legalised Police Cells (Ayr) Rules 2002 and shall come into force on 30th November 2002.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Rule made by the Secretary of State under the Prisons (Scotland) Acts, 1860 to 1926[^f00002], dated 31st March 1927[^f00003] appointing police cells in the Burgh of Ayr[^f00004] to be a legal prison for the detention of prisoners for a period not exceeding 14 days before, during or after trial is hereby revoked.

## Signed

JAMES WALLACE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 22nd October 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules provide for the discontinuance, as at 30th November 2002, of the police cells in the possession of the Strathclyde Joint Police Board at Ayr as a place in which prisoners may be detained before, during or after trial for any period not exceeding 14 days.
