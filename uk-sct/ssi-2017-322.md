# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Medium Combustion Plant Directive) (Scotland) Order 2017

Made: 4th October 2017

Coming into force: 1st November 2017

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Medium Combustion Plant Directive) (Scotland) Order 2017 and comes into force on 1st November 2017.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Designation

###### 2

Directive 2015/2193/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the limitation of emissions of certain pollutant into the air from medium combustion plants is designated for the purposes of paragraph 20 of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999.

## Signed

ROSEANNA CUNNINGHAM — A member of the Scottish Government — 4th October 2017
