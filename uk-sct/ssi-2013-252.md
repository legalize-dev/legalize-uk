# The Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2013

Made: 3rd September 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 5th September 2013

Coming into force: 21st September 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38(1) of the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2013 and comes into force on 21st September 2013.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

21st September 2013 is the appointed day on which section 23 (fixing of charges for water services) of the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003 comes into force.

## Signed

PAUL WHEELHOUSE — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 3rd September 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 23 of the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003 (“the Act”) on 21st September 2013. Section 23 of the Act empowers the Scottish Ministers to make regulations containing provision for or in connection with the fixing of charges for the provision of water services for the purposes of the protection of the water environment.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 5th March 2003. Part 3 (sections 35 to 38) of the Act also came into force on that day.
