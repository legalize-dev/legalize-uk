# The Budget (Scotland) Act 2012 Amendment Order 2013

Made: 26th March 2013

Coming into force: 31st March 2013

In accordance with section 7(2) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Budget (Scotland) Act 2012 Amendment Order 2013 and comes into force on 31st March 2013.

- (2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Budget (Scotland) Act 2012.

#### Amendment of amounts specified in section 4 (overall cash authorisations)

###### 2

In section 4 of the Act, for the amount specified—

- (a) in subsection (2) (Scottish Administration), substitute “£31,178,566,000”;

- (b) in subsection (3)(a) (Forestry Commissioners), substitute “£66,750,000”; and

- (c) in subsection (3)(b) (Food Standards Agency), substitute “£9,830,000”.

- (c) in relation to purpose 3, substitute “11,857,138,000”;

- (d) in relation to purpose 4, substitute “2,934,607,000”;

- (e) in relation to purpose 5, substitute “1,850,249,000”;

#### Amendment of schedule 2 (direct-funded bodies)

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Government — 26th March 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Budget (Scotland) Act 2012 (“the Act”).

Article 2 amends the overall cash authorisations specified in section4(2) and (3) of the Act in relation to the Scottish Administration, the Forestry Commissioners and Food Standards Agency.

Article 3 amends the amount of resources (other than accruing resources) that may be used for certain purposes in schedule1 to the Act. It also amends the total amount of those resources.

Article 4 amends the amount of resources (other than accruing resources) that may be used by the Forestry Commissioners, the Food Standards Agency and the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body for certain purposes in schedule 2 to the Act.
