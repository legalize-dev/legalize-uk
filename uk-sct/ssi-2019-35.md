# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2019

Made: 6th February 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th February 2019

Coming into force: 1st April 2019

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7B(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2019 and comes into force on 1 April 2019.

#### Non-domestic rate for 2019-2020

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 49 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2019-2020.

## Signed

KATE FORBES — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 6th February 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes a rate of 49 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2019-2020. A rate of 48 pence in the pound was prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland for the financial year 2018-2019 ([S.S.I. 2018/46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2018/46)).
