# The Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2015

Made: 1st September 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 3rd September 2015

Coming into force: 8th October 2015

In accordance with section 75A(11) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers consulted such bodies as appear to the Scottish Ministers to be appropriate and to be representative of participating schools.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2015 and come into force on 8th October 2015.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Regulations 2001 (“the principal Regulations”) and amending instruments. The principal Regulations relate to the scheme for assisted places at independent schools which was required to be established under section 75A of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 (“the 1980 Act”). Section 5(1) of the Education (Schools) Act 1997 amended section 75A of the 1980 Act with the effect that the assisted places scheme could only be operated in relation to pupils admitted to a place before the 1997-1998 school term until those pupils completed their primary education or, at the Secretary of State’s discretion, until the completion of their secondary education. As such, the scheme is no longer in operation in relation to any particular pupil.
