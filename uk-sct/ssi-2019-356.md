# The Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2019

Made: 29th October 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th October 2019

Coming into force: 10th December 2019

The Scottish Ministers make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 10 December 2019.

#### Amendment of the Landfill Allowances Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2005

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Landfill Allowances Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2005[^f00002] for the definition of “Landfill Directive” substitute—

> > - “Landfill Directive” means Council [Directive 99/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0031) on the landfill of waste[^f00003] as last amended by Directive (EU) 2018/850 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00004],

#### Amendment of the Waste Batteries (Scotland) Regulations 2009

###### 3

- (1) The Waste Batteries (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00005] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation) omit the definition of “the 1994 Regulations”.

- (3) Regulation 4 (amendment of the 1994 Regulations) is revoked.

## Signed

ROSEANNA CUNNINGHAM — A member of the Scottish Government — 29th October 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to legislation relating to waste.

Regulation 2 amends the Landfill Allowances Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2005 ([S.S.I. 2005/157](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2005/157)), to update the reference to Council [Directive 99/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0031) on the landfill of waste.

Regulation 3 amends the Waste Batteries (Scotland) Regulations 2009 ([S.S.I. 2009/247](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/247)), to omit references to the Waste Management Licensing Regulations 1994, which are no longer necessary.

A business and regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact upon business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.
