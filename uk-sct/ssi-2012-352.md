# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2012

Made: 18th December 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 20th December 2012

Coming into force: 1st April 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st April 2013.

#### Non-domestic rate for 2013-2014

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 46.2 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2013-2014.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Government — 18th December 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes a rate of 46.2 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2013-2014. A rate of 45 pence in the pound was the figure prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland for the financial year 2012-2013 ([S.S.I. 2012/27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2012/27)).
