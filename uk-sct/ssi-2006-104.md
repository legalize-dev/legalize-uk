# The Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement and Savings) Amendment Order 2006

Made: 6th March 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 141(2) and 145(2) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement and Savings) Amendment Order 2006.

#### Amendment of 2004 Order

###### 2

Article 2 of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement and Savings) Order 2004[^f00002] is amended by substituting “30th April 2006” for “31st March 2006”.

## Signed

MALCOLM CHISHOLM — A member of the Scottish Executive — 6th March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement and Savings) Order 2004.

Article 2 changes the date upon which the remaining provisions of Parts 7 and 8 of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 shall come into force. The date is changed from 31st March 2006 to 30th April 2006.
