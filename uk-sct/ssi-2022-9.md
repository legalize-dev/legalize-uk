## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [1991 c. 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22). Section 112A was inserted by section 19 of the Transport (Scotland) Act [2005 (asp 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/12) (“*the 2005 Act*”). Section 163(1) includes a definition of “prescribed” relevant to the exercise of the statutory powers under which these Regulations are made. The functions of the Secretary of State under section 163(1) were transferred to the Scottish Ministers, so far as within devolved competence, by virtue of section 53 of the Scotland Act [1998 (c. 46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/46).

[^f00002]: Section 163A was inserted by section 39 of the 2005 Act.

[^f00003]: The Scottish Road Works Register is kept in terms of section 112A(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991 (“*the 1991 Act*”). Section 112A(1) of that Act defines “the SRWR”.

[^f00004]: The Scottish Road Works Commissioner was created by section 16(1) of the 2005 Act and has functions under section 112A(1) of the 1991 Act. Section 112A(1) of that Act defines “the Commissioner”.

[^f00005]: Section 145(1) of the 1991 Act defines “roads authority” as having the same meaning as in section 151 of the Roads (Scotland) Act [1984 (c. 54)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/54).

[^f00006]: [S.S.I. 2008/16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2008/16). Regulation 3 was amended by [S.S.I. 2014/58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2014/58).

[^f00007]: [S.S.I. 2021/48](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2021/48).
