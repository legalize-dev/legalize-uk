# The Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2005

Made: 8th March 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th March 2005

Coming into force: 9th March 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Inshore Fishing (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf and having consulted such bodies as they consider appropriate, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2005 and shall come into force on 9th March 2005.

#### Amendment of the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) Order 1995

###### 2

In article 2 (interpretation) of the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) Order 1995[^f00002] for the definition of “the specified area” substitute–

> > - “the specified area” means that part of Scottish inshore waters lying generally to the east of a straight line drawn from a point at 54°40.6' north latitude and 4°23.45' west longitude (Burrow Head) in a direction of 110°30' true.

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 8th March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) Order 1995 by substituting a new definition for “the specified area” (article 2).
