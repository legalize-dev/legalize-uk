# The Budget (Scotland) Act 2004 Amendment Order 2005

Made: 15th March 2005

Coming into force: 16th March 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(1) of the Budget (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order, a draft of which has, in accordance with section 7(2) of that Act, been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Scottish Parliament:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Budget (Scotland) Act 2004 Amendment Order 2005 and shall come into force on the day following that on which it is made.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Budget (Scotland) Act 2004 (“the 2004 Act”), which makes provision, for the financial year 2004/05, for the use of resources by the Scottish administration and certain bodies whose expenditure is payable out of the Scottish Consolidated Fund, for authorising the use of resources, and for limits on the borrowing of certain public bodies. The 2004 Act was previously amended by the Budget (Scotland) Act 2004 Amendment Order 2004 ([S.S.I. 2004/290](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2004/290)) and the Budget (Scotland) Act 2004 Amendment (No. 2) Order 2004 ([S.S.I. 2004/565](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2004/565)).

In particular, this Order further amends the 2004 Act so as to–

- (a) alter the overall cash authorisations in respect of the Scottish administration and the Forestry Commissioners referred to in section 3 of the 2004 Act (Article 2(2));

- (b) restate certain of the purposes for which resources may be used in accordance with section 1 of and Schedule 1 to the 2004 Act and to insert a new twelfth purpose (Article 2(3)(a)(i) to (iv));

- (c) restate the amount of resources other than accruing resources that may be used for the eleven purposes set out in Schedule 1 to the 2004 Act and to state an amount in respect of the new twelfth purpose (Article 2(3)(b)(i) to (xii));

- (d) restate the amounts of accruing resources in Schedule 1 to the 2004 Act in respect of the first, third, fifth and seventh type of accruing resources (Article 2(3)(c)(i) to (iv));

- (e) add a type of accruing resource and corresponding purpose in Part II of Schedule 2 which may be used by the Scottish Executive Development Department (Article 2(4)(b)) and add a new type of accruing resource as Part 12 of that Schedule which may be used by the Scottish Teachers' and NHS Pension Schemes (Article 2(4)(l));

- (f) alter the overall amounts of accruing resources in Parts 1 to 11 of Schedule 2 to the Act and state an amount in respect of new Part 12 which may be used by the bodies referred to therein without individual limit (Article 2(4));

- (g) in Schedule 3 to the 2004 Act to add in as directly funded bodies the Commissioner for Public Appointments in Scotland and the Commissioner for Children and Young Persons in Scotland and alter the amount of resources other than accruing resources which the first and third categories of directly funded bodies referred to therein may use for the purposes specified (Article 2(5));

- (h) alter in Schedule 4 the amount of accruing resources in respect of the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body which may be used without individual limit (Article 2(6));

- (i) alter in Schedule 5 the amount of borrowing in respect of Scottish Water (Article 2(7)).
