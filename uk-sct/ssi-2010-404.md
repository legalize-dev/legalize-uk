# The Fees in the Registers of Scotland Amendment Order 2010

Made: 18th November 2010

Coming into force: 10th January 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section25 of the Land Registers (Scotland) Act 1868[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fees in the Registers of Scotland Amendment Order 2010 and comes into force on 10thJanuary 2011.

#### Amendment of the Fees in the Register of Scotland Order 1995

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Fees in the Register of Scotland Order 1995[^f00002] (table of fees in the Registers of Scotland) substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — REPLACEMENT SCHEDULE TO THE FEES IN THE REGISTERS OF SCOTLAND ORDER 1995

## Signed

JIM MATHER — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 18th November 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)
