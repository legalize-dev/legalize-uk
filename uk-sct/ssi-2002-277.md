# The New Water and Sewerage Authorities Dissolution (Scotland) Order 2002

Made: 7th June 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 7th June 2002

Coming into force: 29th June 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 24(1) of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the New Water and Sewerage Authorities Dissolution (Scotland) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 29th June 2002.

#### Dissolution of authorities

###### 2

29th June 2002 is hereby specified as the date on which the new water and sewerage authorities are dissolved.

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 7th June 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves the new water and sewerage authorities established under the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994. The functions of these authorities have previously been transferred to Scottish Water.
