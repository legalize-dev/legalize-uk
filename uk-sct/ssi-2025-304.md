# The Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2025

Made: 29th October 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 31st October 2025

Coming into force: 10th November 2025

The Scottish Ministers have consulted the Secretary of State in accordance with section 219(4) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2025 and come into force on 10 November 2025.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

31 March 2026 is the day appointed for the coming into force of Part 2 of schedule 8 and section 179 (so far as it relates to that Part) of the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023.

## Signed

ANGELA CONSTANCE — A member of the Scottish Government — 29th October 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force on 31 March 2026 Part 2 of schedule 8 and section 179 (so far as it relates to that part) of the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023 (“*the Act*”).

Schedule 8 of the Act (as introduced by section 179) amends Part 3 of the Proceeds of Crime Act [2002 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29) (“*POCA*”) (confiscation: Scotland) to extend the confiscation regime under POCA to cryptoassets.
