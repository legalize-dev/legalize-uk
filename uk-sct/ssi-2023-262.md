# The Hunting with Dogs (Scotland) Act 2023 (Commencement) Regulations 2023

Made: 14th September 2023

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th September 2023

Coming into force: 3rd October 2023

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 31(2) of the Hunting with Dogs (Scotland) Act 2023[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Hunting with Dogs (Scotland) Act 2023 (Commencement) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 3 October 2023.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

3 October 2023 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the Hunting with Dogs (Scotland) Act 2023, so far as not already in force[^f00002].

## Signed

MAIRI GOUGEON — A member of the Scottish Government — 14th September 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations appoint 3 October 2023 as the day on which all of the provisions contained in the Hunting with Dogs (Scotland) Act 2023 (“*the Act*”) will come into force, other than sections 26, 31 and 32 of the Act (which came into force on 8 March 2023 by virtue of provision made in section 31(1) of the Act).
