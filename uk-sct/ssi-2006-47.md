# The Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2006

Made: 6th February 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43(3) and (4) of the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2006.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 4 of the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005, in so far as not already in force, is 7th February 2006.

## Signed

ANDREW P KERR — A member of the Scottish Executive — 6th February 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 7th February 2006 as the day for the coming into force of section 4 of the [Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/13) (“the Act”), in so far as it is not already in force.
