## SCHEDULE — Enabling Powers

These Regulations are made under the following provisions of the Police Act 1997 as read with section126(3)[^f00029] of that Act—

- (a) sections113B(9) and125(5); and

- (b) having regard to the meaning of “prescribed” in section 125(1) and section126(1), sections 112(1)(b), 112(2)(a), 112(3), 113A(1)(b), 113A(3)(a), 113A(6), 113A(6)(c), 113B(1)(b), 113B(2)(b), 113B(3)(a), 113B(5A), 113B(9), 113B(11)(c), 113CA(1), 113CA(2)(b), 113CB(1), 113CB(2)(b), 114(1)(b), 116(1)(b), 118(2)(a), 118(2A)(e) and 119(7).
