# The Scottish Homes (Dissolution) Order 2005

Made: 30th November 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th November 2005

Coming into force: 31st December 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 87(1) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Homes (Dissolution) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 31st December 2005.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

“Scottish Homes” means the body corporate established by section 1 of the Housing (Scotland) 1989 Act[^f00002].

#### Dissolution of Scottish Homes

###### 3

The date specified for the dissolution of Scottish Homes is 31st December 2005.

## Signed

JOHANN M LAMONT — A member of the Scottish Executive — 30th November 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the date for the dissolution of Scottish Homes (which was established as a body corporate by section 1 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988).

All functions of Scottish Homes were transferred to the Scottish Ministers by section 84 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001. All property and liabilities of Scottish Homes were transferred to the Scottish Ministers by–

- (a) the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property etc.) Order 2001 ([S.S.I. 2001/396](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/396));

- (b) the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property and Liabilities) Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/532](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/532)); and

- (c) the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property and Liabilities) Order 2005 ([S.S.I. 2005/439](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2005/439)).
