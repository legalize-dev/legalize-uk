# The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008

Made: 17th June 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 20th June 2008

Coming into force: 15th September 2008

In accordance with section 61(2) of that Act[^f00002], they have consulted with such representative organisations as they think fit.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008 and come into force on 15th September 2008.

#### Amendment of the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001

###### 2

In regulation 13(1)(b) and (2)(b) of the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00003] for “£54” substitute “£57”.

## Signed

STEWART STEVENSON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 17th June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fees for the registration of, or the variation of the registration of, a local bus service, other than a community bus service. The fee is increased from £54 to £57 (an increase of 5.6%). The fees were last increased on 1st April 2007.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from Bus and Taxi Policy, the Scottish Government, Bus, Freight and Roads Division, Area 2-F (North), Victoria Quay, Edinburgh EH6 6QQ.
