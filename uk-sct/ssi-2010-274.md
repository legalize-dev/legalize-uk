# The Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment (No. 2) Rules 2010

Made: 1st July 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 2nd July 2010

Coming into force: 15th August 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections17(4) of, and paragraph 11 of Schedule 1 to, the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment (No. 2) Rules 2010 and come into force on 15th August 2010.

#### Amendment of the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment Rules 2010

###### 2

In rule 1(1) (citation, commencement and interpretation) of the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment Rules 2010[^f00002] for “16th August” substitute “14th November”.

## Signed

ADAM INGRAM — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st July 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules change the date on which the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment Rules 2010 come into force from 16th August 2010 to 14thNovember 2010.
