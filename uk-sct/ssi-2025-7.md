# The Little Loch Broom Scallops Several Fishery (Variation) Order 2025

Made: 15th January 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 17th January 2025

Coming into force: 9th March 2025

With regard to paragraph 3 of that schedule, no objections were received and no representations were made in writing to the Scottish Ministers in respect of the draft of this Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Little Loch Broom Scallops Several Fishery (Variation) Order 2025 and comes into force on 9 March 2025.

#### Variation of the Little Loch Broom Scallops Several Fishery Order 2015

###### 2

- (1) The Little Loch Broom Scallops Several Fishery Order 2015[^f00003] is varied in accordance with the following paragraphs of this article.

- (2) In article 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “the applicant”, for “guarantee and having its registered office at No. 2 Isle-Ewe, Aultbea, Achnasheen IV22 2JW” substitute “shares and incorporated under the Companies Act 2006[^f00004], whose registered company number is SC461741, and having its registered office at The Boom, Mellon Charles, Aultbea, Achnasheen, IV22 2JL”.

- (3) In article 3 (right of several fishery) for “9th March 2025” substitute “9 March 2035”.

## Signed

MAIRI GOUGEON — A member of the Scottish Government — 15th January 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order varies the Little Loch Broom Scallops Several Fishery Order 2015 by—

- (a) amending the description of “the applicant”, Scot-Hatch Limited (article 2(2)(a)); and

- (b) extending the period for which the right of several fishery in Little Loch Broom, near Ullapool, is granted to Scot-Hatch Limited (“*the applicant*”) for a period of 10 years (article 2(3)).
