# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006

Made: 28th June 2006

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 178(6)(b) and (9) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001], and having consulted with the Secretary of State in accordance with section 178(6) of that Act, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006.

#### Appointed days and extent

###### 2

The days appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 are–

- (a) 30th June 2006, for–

- (i) paragraphs 1 to 6 and 8 to 14 of Schedule 15 (Private Security Industry Act 2001[^f00002]: Scottish extent) so far as they extend to Scotland; and

- (ii) section 171(1), so far as relating to those paragraphs in their extent to Scotland; and

- (b) 6th July 2006, for–

- (i) paragraphs 1 to 7 and 10 to 14 of Schedule 15 (Private Security Industry Act 2001: Scottish extent) so far as they extend to England and Wales;

- (ii) paragraph 8 so far as it extends to England and Wales, for the purpose of making regulations;

- (iii) section 171(1), so far as relating to those paragraphs in their extent to England and Wales; and

- (iv) section 171(2) (Private Security Industry Act 2001: Scottish extent).

## Signed

CATHY JAMIESON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 28th June 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (“the 2005 Act”) which extend the Private Security Industry Act 2001 to Scotland (“the 2001 Act”) and make various amendments to that Act in consequence. This Order commences the amendments in Schedule 15 to the 2005 Act for Scotland on 30th June 2006 and for England and Wales on 6th July 2006.

The 2001 Act is already in force in England and Wales. The amendments made by virtue of this Order shall come into force on the appointed day for England and Wales. However as the 2001 Act is not yet in force in Scotland the amendments made to the 2001 Act by virtue of this Order shall then require to be commenced for Scotland. This will be achieved by a commencement order made under the 2001 Act ([SSI 2006/ 382 (C.32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2006/382)).
