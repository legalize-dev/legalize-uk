# The M8 Motorway (Junction 10 Westerhouse Slip Roads) (Speed Limit) Regulations 2011

Made: 8th February 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th February 2011

Coming into force: 13th March 2011

In accordance with section 134(2)[^f00002] of that Act they have consulted with such representative organisations as they think fit.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the M8 Motorway (Junction 10 Westerhouse Slip Roads) (Speed Limit) Regulations 2011 and come into force on 13th March 2011.

- (2) In these Regulations “M8 Motorway” means the M8/A8 Edinburgh-Greenock Trunk Road.

#### Restriction of Speed

###### 2

No person is to drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 30mph on those lengths of road specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — 30 MPH SPEED LIMIT

###### 1

That length of the off-slip road connecting with the main eastbound carriageway of the M8 Motorway from the junction with the roundabout at Glasgow Fort (Junction 10) in a westerly direction for a distance of 105 metres or thereby.

###### 2

That length of the on-slip road connecting with the main eastbound carriageway of the M8 Motorway from the junction with the roundabout at Glasgow Fort (Junction 10) in an easterly direction for a distance of 80 metres or thereby.

## Signed

KEITH BROWN — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 8th February 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations introduce a speed limit of 30mph on the slip roads at Junction 10 of the M8 Motorway which were constructed as a consequence of the ‘Glasgow Fort’ retail development.
