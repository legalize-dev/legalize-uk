# The Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2005

Made: 22nd March 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 71(2) of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2005.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003, insofar as it is not already in force, is 1st April 2005.

## Signed

LEWIS MACDONALD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 22nd March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st April 2005 as the date for coming into force of sections 1 to 70 and schedules 1 to 4 of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003.

Section 71 of that Act came into force on Royal Assent on 1st May 2003.
