# The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2004

Made: 4th March 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th March 2004

Coming into force: 1st April 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 134 and 163(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 1st April 2004.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003

###### 2

In regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003[^f00002] for “£20.00” substitute “£21.00”.

## Signed

NICOL STEPHEN — A member of the Scottish Executive — 4th March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for an increase to £21.00 (previously £20.00) in the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by road works authorities in Scotland under regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/415](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/415)).
