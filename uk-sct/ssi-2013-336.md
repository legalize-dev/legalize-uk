# The Food (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2013

Made: 20th November 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 22nd November 2013

Coming into force: 1st January 2014

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002].

#### Citation, extent and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Food (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2013, extend to Scotland only and come into force on 1st January 2014.

#### Amendments to the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation (2)(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) omit ““Regulation 1162/2009,””; and

- (b) for “and Regulation 28/2012” substitute “, “Regulation 28/2012”, “Regulation 702/2013” and “Regulation 1079/2013””.

- (3) In Schedule 1 (definitions of EU legislation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “Regulation 1162/2009”;

- (b) in the definition of “Regulation 853/2004” for “Regulation 1162/2009” substitute “Regulation 1079/2013”;

- (c) in the definition of “Regulation 854/2004” for “Regulation 1162/2009” substitute “Regulation 1079/2013”;

- (d) in the definition of “Regulation 882/2004” for “Regulation 1162/2009” substitute “Regulation 702/2013”;

- (e) at the end, insert—

> > - “Regulation 702/2013” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 702/2013 on transitional measures for the application of Regulation [(EC) No 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the accreditation of official laboratories carrying out official testing for Trichinella[^f00004];
> - “Regulation 1079/2013” means Commission Regulation (EU) No 1079/2013 laying down transitional measures for the application of Regulations [(EC) No 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) and [(EC) No 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00005].

#### Amendments to the Official Feed and Food Controls (Scotland) Regulations 2009

###### 3

- (1) The Official Feed and Food Controls (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00006] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) for “Regulation 1162/2009” substitute “Regulation 702/2013”.

- (3) In Schedule 1 (definitions of EU Legislation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “Regulation 1162/2009”;

- (b) in the definition of “Regulation 853/2004” for “Regulation 1162/2009” substitute “Regulation 1079/2013”;

- (c) in the definition of “Regulation 882/2004” for “Regulation 1162/2009” substitute “Regulation 702/2013”;

- (d) at the end, insert—

> > - “Regulation 702/2013” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 702/2013 on transitional measures for the application of Regulation [(EC) No 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the accreditation of official laboratories carrying out official testing for *Trichinella*.

#### Amendment to the Food Safety Act 1990

###### 4

In section 9(5)(a) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00007] (inspection and seizure of suspected food), for “4(a)” substitute “4(b)”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations implement Commission Regulation (EU) No 1079/2013 laying down transitional measures for the application of Regulations [(EC) No 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) and [(EC) No 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council (“the Commission Regulation”). They also implement Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 702/2013 on transitional measures for the application of Regulation [(EC) No 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the accreditation of official laboratories carrying out official testing for *Trichinella* (“the Commission Implementing Regulation”). In addition they correct a minor error in the Food Safety Act 1990 (“the 1990 Act”).

These Regulations implement the Commission Regulation by amending the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006 to extend transitional measures until 31 December 2016, by omitting the definition of and references to [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1162/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1162) (“Regulation 1162/2009”), which is being repealed, and inserting references to the Commission Regulation and the Commission Implementing Regulation (regulation 2).

The Regulations implement the Commission Implementing Regulation by amending the Official Feed and Food Controls (Scotland) Regulations 2009 to extend transitional measures until 31 December 2016, by omitting the definition of and references to Regulation 1162/2009, and inserting references to the Commission Implementing Regulation (regulation 3).

These Regulations make a minor amendment to section 9(5)(a) of the 1990 Act to correct an erroneous reference to a paragraph number (regulation 4).

A business and regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact is foreseen on business or on the public or voluntary sectors.
