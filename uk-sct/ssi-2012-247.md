# The Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2012

Made: 5th September 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 7th September 2012

Coming into force: 1st January 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section16(1) of the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No.2) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1stJanuary 2013.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1stJanuary 2013 is the appointed day for Parts 1 and 2 of the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 to come into force, in so far as they are not then in force.

## Signed

FIONA HYSLOP — A member of the Scottish Government — 5th September 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st January 2013 all the provisions of Parts 1 and 2 of the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”) which are not already in force.

The Act received Royal Assent on 20th April 2011 and Part 3 of the Act came into force on that day.
