# The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2016

Made: 10th February 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 12th February 2016

Coming into force: 26th February 2016

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of February 2016Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service (Appointment of Chief Inspector) Order 2016 and comes into force on 26th February 2016.

#### Appointment of Chief Inspector

###### 2

Martyn Anthony Emberson, QFSM is appointed as the Chief Inspector of the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Appointment of Chief Inspector) (Scotland) Order 2011[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints Martyn Anthony Emberson, QFSM as the Chief Inspector of the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service.

Article 3 revokes the Fire and Rescue Authorities (Appointment of Chief Inspector) (Scotland) Order 2011.
