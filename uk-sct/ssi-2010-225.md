# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made: 9th June 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 11th June 2010

Coming into force: 5th July 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972 and all other powers enabling them to do so.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of that Act and it appears to the Scottish Ministers that it is expedient for the references to the legislation set out in Schedule 1 to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2007 to be construed as references to those instruments as amended from time to time.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010 and come into force on 5th July 2010.

#### Amendment to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2007

###### 2

- (1) The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2007 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 24(2) (consignments and products illegally brought in) for “paragraph (4)” in the first place that term occurs substitute “ paragraph (5) ”.

- (3) For Schedule 1 (import conditions) substitute the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.
