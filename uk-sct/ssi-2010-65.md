# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007 Amendment Order 2010

Made: 23rd February 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 26th February 2010

Coming into force: 1st April 2010

In accordance with section34(5) of that Act they have consulted with such persons as they considered appropriate.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007 Amendment Order 2010 and comes into force on 1stApril 2010.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007

###### 2

Schedule1 to the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE1 TO THE FIREFIGHTERS’ PENSION SCHEME (SCOTLAND) ORDER2007

After Part 13 (payment of awards) insert—

> > (1) For the purposes of meeting the liabilities of an authority incurred under this Scheme the Scottish Ministers may pay sums to the authority.
> (2)
>   (1) An authority shall provide to the Scottish Ministers such information as the Scottish Ministers may, by written notice, require in connection with their functions under rule 1 of this Part.
>   (2) An authority shall respond to a request under paragraph (1) within such period as the Scottish Ministers specify in the notice, or such longer period as they may in any particular case allow.
> (3) An authority shall have regard to guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers for the purposes of this Part.

## Signed

FERGUS EWING — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 23rd February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007, and has effect from 1stApril 2010.

This Order provides power for the Scottish Ministers to make payments to Fire and Rescue Authorities for the purpose of meeting those authorities’ liabilities under the 2007 Scheme.
