# The National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

Made: 7th September 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 37(1) of the National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000 is 8th September 2000.

## Signed

SARAH BOYACK — A member of the Scottish Executive — 7th September 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 8th September 2000 as the date on which the National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000 will come into force.
