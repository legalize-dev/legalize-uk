# The Antisocial Behaviour (Amount of Fixed Penalty) (Scotland) Order 2005

Made: 1st March 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 3rd March 2005

Coming into force: 1st April 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 130(1) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Antisocial Behaviour (Amount of Fixed Penalty) (Scotland) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

#### Amount of fixed penalty

###### 2

The penalty payable in respect of a fixed penalty offence within the meaning of section 128(1) of the Antisocial Behaviour (Scotland) Act 2004 is £40.

## Signed

HUGH HENRY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the amount of the fixed penalty which is payable if a fixed penalty notice has been given for the commission of a fixed penalty offence in an area which has been prescribed by the Scottish Ministers by regulations under section 129(2) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 is £40.
