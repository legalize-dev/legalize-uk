# The Fundable Bodies (Scotland) Order 2008

Made: 12th December 2008

Coming into force

In accordance with section 34(4) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Fundable Bodies (Scotland) Order 2008.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 1st January 2009 with the exception of article 2(c) and (d) which comes into force on 25th January 2009.

#### Modification of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005

###### 2

In schedule 2 to the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00002] (fundable bodies)–

- (a) for “Central College of Commerce” substitute “Central College Glasgow”;

- (b) for “Glasgow College of Nautical Science” substitute “Glasgow College of Nautical Studies”;

- (c) after the entry for Edinburgh College of Art insert–

> > - Edinburgh Napier University

; and

- (d) omit the entry for Napier University.

## Signed

FIONA HYSLOP — A member of the Scottish Executive — 12th December 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the Act”) established the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council and made provision relating to the funding of bodies which provide further and higher education. Those bodies are described in the Act as “fundable bodies” and are listed in schedule 2 to the Act.

Article 2(a) and (b) updates the list of fundable bodies with effect from 1st January 2009 by amending schedule 2 to the Act to reflect the change of name of Central College of Commerce to Central College Glasgow and to correct an error in the name of Glasgow College of Nautical Studies.

Article 2(c) and (d) updates the list of fundable bodies with effect from 25th January 2009 by amending schedule 2 to the Act to reflect the change of name of Napier University to Edinburgh Napier University on this date.
