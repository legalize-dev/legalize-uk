# The Assigned Colleges (University of the Highlands and Islands) Order 2018

Made: 15th November 2018

Coming into force: 5th December 2018

In accordance with section 34(4)(bd)[^f00002] of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Assigned Colleges (University of the Highlands and Islands) Order 2018 and comes into force on 5 December 2018.

#### Assignation

###### 2

- (1) Argyll College UHI Ltd.[^f00003] is assigned to the University of Highlands and Islands[^f00004].

- (2) Argyll College UHI Ltd. is to remain assigned to the University of Highlands and Islands notwithstanding any change of name of Argyll College UHI Ltd.

## Signed

RICHARD LOCHHEAD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 15th November 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order assigns Argyll College UHI Ltd. to the University of Highlands and Islands which is a regional strategic body under section 7B(1) and Part 2 of schedule 2A of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005. Article 2(2) provides that Argyll College UHI Ltd. remains assigned notwithstanding any change of name of the college of further education.
