# The Avian Influenza (Preventive Measures) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006

Made: 20th June 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 14th July 2006

Coming into force: 22nd June 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Avian Influenza (Preventive Measures) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 22nd June 2006.

#### Amendment of the Avian Influenza (Preventive Measures) Regulations 2005

###### 2

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Avian Influenza (Preventive Measures) Regulations 2005[^f00002] for the definition of “the Commission Decision” substitute–

> > - “the Commission Decision” means Commission Decision [2005/734/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0734) laying down biosecurity measures to reduce the risk of transmission of highly pathogenic avian influenza caused by Influenza virus A subtype H5N1 from birds living in the wild to poultry and other captive birds and providing for an early detection system in areas at particular risk[^f00003] as amended, the last amending instrument being Commission Decision [2006/405/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0405)[^f00004];

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 20th June 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Avian Influenza (Preventive Measures) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 (“the principal Regulations”). The principal Regulations give effect to Commission Decision [2005/734/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0734) laying down biosecurity measures to reduce the risk of transmission of highly pathogenic avian influenza caused by Influenza virus A subtype H5N1 from birds living in the wild to poultry and other captive birds and providing for an early detection system in areas at particular risk (O.J. No. L 274, 20.10.05, p.105) (“Commission Decision [2005/734/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0734)”) as amended by Commission Decision [2005/745/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0745) (O.J. No. L 279, 22.10.05, p.79) and Commission Decision [2005/855/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0855) (O.J. No. L 316, 2.12.05, p.21).

Commission Decision [2005/734/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0734) has been subsequently amended by Commission Decision [2006/405/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0405) to extend the period of application of the measures provided for in Commission Decision [2005/734/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0734) (as amended) to 31st December 2006.

These Regulations by replacing the definition of the Commission Decision in the principal Regulations give effect to this amendment.
