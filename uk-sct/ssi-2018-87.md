# The Scottish Landfill Tax (Standard Rate and Lower Rate) Order 2018

Made: 27th February 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th February 2018

Coming into force: 1st April 2018

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(2) and (5) of the Landfill Tax (Scotland) Act 2014 and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Scottish Landfill Tax (Standard Rate and Lower Rate) Order 2018 and comes into force on 1st April 2018.

- (2) This Order has effect in relation to taxable disposals made, or treated as made, on or after 1st April 2018.

#### Rates of Scottish landfill tax

###### 2

For the purposes of section 13 of the Landfill Tax (Scotland) Act 2014—

- (a) the standard rate is £88.95; and

- (b) the lower rate is £2.80.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Scottish Landfill Tax (Standard Rate and Lower Rate) Order 2017 is revoked.

## Signed
