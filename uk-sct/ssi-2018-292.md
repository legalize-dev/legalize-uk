# The Private Landlord Registration (Information and Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2018

Made: 25th September 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 27th September 2018

Coming into force: 1st December 2018

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 83(3) and 141(2)(b) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Private Landlord Registration (Information and Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2018 and come into force on 1 December 2018.

#### Amendment of the Private Landlord Registration (Information and Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2005

###### 2

In schedule 2 of the Private Landlord Registration (Information and Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 (fees for registration) omit paragraph 9 (internet applications – further discount).

#### Saving provision

###### 3

Regulation 2 does not have effect in relation to any application for registration under section 83 (application for registration) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 received by a local authority before 1December 2018.

## Signed

KEVIN STEWART — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 25th September 2018
