# The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Scotland) Regulations 2014

Made: 11th February 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 13th February 2014

Coming into force: 7th April 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948[^f00001], as applied by section 87(3) and (4) of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00002], and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Scotland) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 7th April 2014.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Sum needed for personal requirements

###### 2

For the purposes of section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948, as applied by section 87(3) and (4) of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968 (the amount which a local authority shall assume that a person will need for their personal requirements per week), the amount prescribed is £24.55.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Scotland) Regulations 2013[^f00003] are revoked.

## Signed

ALEX NEIL — A member of the Scottish Government — 11th February 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948 as applied by section 87(3) and (4) of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968 (“the 1968 Act”) requires a local authority to assume in assessing a person’s liability to pay for accommodation provided under the 1968 Act or section 25 of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 that they will need for their personal requirements such sum per week as may be prescribed by regulations.

These Regulations prescribe the sum which will be £24.55 per week.

These Regulations revoke the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Scotland) Regulations 2013 which prescribed £23.90 as the sum persons were assumed to need for personal requirements per week for the year commencing 8th April 2013.

No Business and Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of these Regulations.
