# The Good Food Nation (Scotland) Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Regulations 2023

Made: 29th November 2023

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th December 2023

Coming into force: 14th December 2023

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 28(2) of the Good Food Nation (Scotland) Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Good Food Nation (Scotland) Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Regulations 2023 and come into force on 14 December 2023.

#### Amendment of the Good Food Nation (Scotland) Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2023

###### 2

- (1) The Good Food Nation (Scotland) Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2023[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 3 (appointed days), for “31 December 2023” substitute “30 June 2024”.

## Signed

MAIRI GOUGEON — A member of the Scottish Government — 29th November 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Good Food Nation (Scotland) Act 2022 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2023. Regulation 2 substitutes the date on which sections 3 (requirement to lay proposed plan before the parliament), 7 (reporting) and 8 (review and revision of plan) of the Good Food Nation (Scotland) Act 2022 come into force. Sections 3, 7 and 8 will come into force on 30 June 2024, instead of on 31 December 2023.
