# The Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2008

Made: 14th April 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 148(4) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2008.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The provisions of Part 6 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, in so far as they apply in relation to Scotland, come into force on 21st April 2008.

## Signed

LINDA FABIANI — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 14th April 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings Part 6 (Protection of Cultural Objects on Loan) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 into force on 21st April 2008 in relation to Scotland. Part 6 provides for the protection from seizure or forfeiture of cultural objects on loan from abroad for temporary public exhibition in the United Kingdom.
