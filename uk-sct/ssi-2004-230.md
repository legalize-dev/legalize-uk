# The Community Right to Buy (Register of Community Interests in Land Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 2004

Made: 18th May 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 21st May 2004

Coming into force: 15th June 2004

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(7) of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Right to Buy (Register of Community Interests in Land Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 15th June 2004.

#### Charges for Copies and Extracts

###### 2

The charges which may be made by the Keeper of the Register of Community Interests in Land for supplying copies or extracts of entries in that Register are specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — EXTRACTING AND COPYING FEES

###### 1

For each page of an extract, plain copy or duplicate

###### 2

For a plan (A4 size) (black and white)

###### 3

For the authentication of each extract

###### 4

For the handling of second and subsequent extracts and for copies

## Signed

ALLAN WILSON — Authorised to sign on behalf of the Scottish Ministers — 18th May 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify the charges which may be made by the Keeper of the Register of Community Interests in Land for supplying copies or extracts from the Register.
