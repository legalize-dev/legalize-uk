# The Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2024

Made: 9th January 2024

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 11th January 2024

Coming into force: 31st January 2024

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(2) of the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 6) Regulations 2024 and come into force on 31 January 2024.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

31 January 2024 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 17 (Advertising and brandsharing), section 18 (Free distribution and nominal pricing) and section 19 (Sponsorship) of the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016.

## Signed

JENNI MINTO — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 9th January 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force section 17 (Advertising and brandsharing), section 18 (Free distribution and nominal pricing) and section 19 (Sponsorship) of the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care (Scotland) Act 2016 (“*the Act*”), being the remaining provisions of the Act to be commenced.

Regulation 2 provides that the day appointed for these sections to come into force is 31 January 2024.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 6th April 2016. Sections 34, 35, 36 and 37 of the Act came into force the following day.
