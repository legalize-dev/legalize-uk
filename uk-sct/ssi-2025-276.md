# The Scottish Water, Prevention of Water Pollution (Milngavie Waterworks) Byelaws Extension Order 2025

Made: 26th September 2025

Coming into force: 28th September 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Water, Prevention of Water Pollution (Milngavie Waterworks) Byelaws Extension Order 2025 and comes into force on 28 September 2025.

#### Extension

###### 2

The period during which the Scottish Water, Prevention of Water Pollution (Milngavie Waterworks) Byelaws 2015[^f00002] are to remain in force is extended to 29 September 2026.

## Signed

JO BLEWETT — A member of staff of the Scottish Ministers — 26th September 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order extends the period during which the Scottish Water, Prevention of Water Pollution (Milngavie Waterworks) Byelaws 2015 are to remain in force to 29 September 2026. A copy of the Byelaws may be obtained from Scottish Water, The Bridge, Buchanan Gate Business Park, Cumbernauld Road, Stepps, G33 6FB, and online at www.scottishwater.co.uk.
