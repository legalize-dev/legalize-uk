# The Town and Country Planning (Fees for Appeals) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026

Made: 3rd February 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 5th February 2026

Coming into force: 1st April 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 252 and 275 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (Fees for Appeals) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026 and come into force on 1 April 2026.

#### Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Fees for Appeals) (Scotland) Regulations 2025

###### 2

Subject to regulation 5, the Town and Country Planning (Fees for Appeals) (Scotland) Regulations 2025[^f00002] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

#### Amendment of regulation 3 - payment of fees

###### 3

In regulation 3 (payment of fees), in paragraphs (3) and (4) for “£143” substitute “£149”.

#### Amendment of the schedule - calculation of fees

###### 4

- (i) in paragraph 7(b) (planning permission in principle granted before 1 April 2022), for “categories 1, 4, 17, or 21” substitute “categories 1, 4, 7, 13 or 17”,

- (ii) in paragraph 8(b) (planning permission in principle granted before 12 December 2024), for “categories 1, 4, 17, or 21” substitute “categories 1, 4, 7, 13 or 17”,

- (iii) in paragraph 9(b) (planning permission in principle granted before 9 June 2025), for “categories 1, 4, 17, or 21” substitute “categories 1, 4, 7, 13 or 17”,

- (iv) after paragraph 9 insert—

> > (9A) Where the appeal is made in respect of an application for the approval, consent or agreement required by a condition imposed on a grant of permission in principle and—

#### Saving provision

###### 5

- (1) The amendments made by regulations 2 to 4 apply only to appeals under—

- (a) section 47(1) of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997 (appeal against planning decision),

- (b) section 47(1) of that Act as applied by regulation 21 of the Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (Scotland) Regulations 1984[^f00003],

- (c) section 154(1)(a) of that Act (appeal against refusal of certificate of lawful use or development),

where notice of appeal is given to the Scottish Ministers on or after 1 April 2026.

- (2) The Town and Country Planning (Fees for Appeals) (Scotland) Regulations 2025 continue to apply in respect of an appeal where notice of appeal is given to the Scottish Ministers before 1 April 2026 as they did immediately before that date.

#### Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 2015 and saving provision

###### 6

- (1) Subject to paragraph (3), the Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 2015[^f00004] (“*the 2015 Regulations*”) are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 55A (fees for appeals)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£572” substitute “£594”,

- (b) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£286” substitute “£297”.

- (3) The amendments made by this regulation apply only to appeals under section 19(1) of the Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Act 1997[^f00005] where notice of appeal is given to the Scottish Ministers on or after 1 April 2026 and regulation 55A of the 2015 Regulations continues to apply in respect of any appeal where notice of appeal is given to the Scottish Ministers before 1 April 2026 as it did immediately before that date.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Town and Country Planning (Fees for Appeals) (Scotland) Regulations 2025 (“*the Fees for Appeals Regulations*”) and the Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 2015.

Regulation 3 amends regulation 3 of the Fees for Appeals Regulations to increase the fees for an appeal in respect of the consent for the display for advertisements under regulation 15 of the Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (Scotland) Regulations 1984 and for an application to develop land without complying with previous conditions.

Regulation 4(a)(i)-(iii) changes the respective categories referred to in provisions about maximum fees for appeal in respect of planning permissions in principle granted in different time periods.

Regulation 4(a)(iv) inserts a new paragraph 9A into Part 1 of the schedule of the Fees for Appeals Regulations to set a maximum fee for certain fees for appeal in respect of applications for planning permission granted on or after 9 June 2025 and before 1 April 2026.

Regulation 4(b) amends Part 2 of the schedule to the Fees for Appeals Regulations to increase the fees for appeal in respect of the applications for planning permission for the provision of facilities for sport or recreation and applications for approval, consent or agreement required by a condition imposed on a grant of planning permission in principle.

Regulation 4(c) also makes changes to fees for appeal in relation to planning permission. It substitutes new tables for those in Part 3 of the schedule of the Fees for Appeals Regulations.

Regulation 5 provides that the changes made by these Regulations apply only to appeal made after 1 April 2026.

Regulation 6 amends the Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 2015 to increase fees for appeal in relation to applications made under those Regulations.
