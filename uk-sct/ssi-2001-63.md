# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory Amendment Order 2001

Made: 4th March 2001

Coming into force at 7.00 p.m. on: 4th March 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 17(1) of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Order 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 4th March 2001 at 7.00 p.m.

#### Amendment of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Declaratory Orders

###### 2

- (1) In Schedule 2 to each of the Orders specified in paragraph (2) below, there shall be inserted after paragraph 2(b), as follows:–

> > (ba) no person shall transport animals through the infected area, except if the animals are transiting through that area by motorway, trunk road or railway;

- (2) The Orders referred to in paragraph (1) above are–

- (a) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory Order 2001[^f00002]; and

- (b) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 2) Order 2001[^f00003].

## Signed

D J CRAWLEY — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 4th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the two Scottish Declaratory Orders declaring infected areas under Part III of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Order 1983 (S.I. [1983/1950](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1983/1950)) in order to permit the transit of animals through those infected areas by motorway, trunk road or railway. These amendments are in consequence of articles 4 and 5 of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Declaratory (Controlled Area) (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2001 ([S.S.I. 2001/60](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/60)).
