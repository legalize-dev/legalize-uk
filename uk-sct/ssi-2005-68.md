# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (Nos. 5 and 9) (Scotland) Orders 2004 Partial Revocation Order 2005

Made: 11th February 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 14th February 2005

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and (2) and 24(3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (Nos. 5 and 9) (Scotland) Orders 2004 Partial Revocation Order 2005 and shall come into force at 1630 hours on 11th February 2005.

#### Revocations

###### 2

- (1) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 5) (Scotland) Order 2004[^f00002] is hereby revoked in so far as it designates the area described as Area 2 in the Schedule to that Order.

- (2) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 9) (Scotland) Order 2004[^f00003] is hereby revoked in so far as it designates the area described as Area 3 in the Schedule to that Order.

## Signed

J T BROWN — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 11th February 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is a partial revocation of the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 5) (Scotland) Order 2004 and a partial revocation of the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 9) (Scotland) Order 2004 (“the principal Orders”) which prohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops within the areas designated in the Schedules to each of the principal Orders (article 4 of each of the principal Orders);

- (b) movement of such scallops out of the designated areas (article 5 of each of the principal Orders); and

- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of, supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such scallops from the designated areas (article 6 of each of the principal Orders).

The effect of this Order is to remove the prohibitions in respect of the areas of sea designated as Area 2 in the Schedule to the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 5) (Scotland) Order 2004 and Area 3 in the Schedule to the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 9) (Scotland) Order 2004.
