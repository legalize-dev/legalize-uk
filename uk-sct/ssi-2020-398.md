# The Official Feed and Food Controls (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2020

Made: 23rd November 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 25th November 2020

Coming into force: 30th December 2020

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Official Feed and Food Controls (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 30 December 2020.

#### Amendment of the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) in paragraph (1) in the definition that begins “Regulation 178/2002”—

- (i) before ““Regulation 178/2002”” insert ““Directive 2004/41[^f00004]”,”,

- (ii) after ““Regulation 853/2004”,” insert ““Regulation 1688/2005[^f00005]”,”, and

- (iii) after ““Regulation 579/2014”,” insert ““Regulation 2015/1375[^f00006]”,”, and

, and

- (c) in paragraphs (1) and (2) after “border control post” in the first place it occurs in each paragraph insert “, in relation to relevant products,”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for the execution and enforcement of the delegated and implementing regulations under Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls and other official activities performed to ensure the application of food and feed law, rules on animal health and welfare, plant health and plant protection products, amending Regulations [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999), [(EC) No. 396/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0396), [(EC) No. 1069/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1069), [(EC) No. 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1107), (EU) No. 1151/2012, (EU) No. 652/2014, (EU) 2016/429 and (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament and of the Council, Council Regulations [(EC) No. 1/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0001) and [(EC) No. 1099/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1099) and Council Directives [98/58/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0058), [1999/74/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0074), [2007/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0043), [2008/119/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0119) and [2008/120/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0120), and repealing Regulations [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) and [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council, Council Directives [89/608/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0608), [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0662), [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425), [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496), [96/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0023), [96/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0093) and [97/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0078) and Council [Decision 92/438/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1992/0438) of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls and other official activities.

These Regulations amend the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006, the Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 2007, the Official Feed and Food Controls (Scotland) Regulations 2009 and the Plastic Kitchenware (Conditions on Imports from China) (Scotland) Regulations 2011.

A Business and Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
