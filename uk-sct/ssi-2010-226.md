# The National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made: 9th June 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th June 2010

Coming into force: 2nd July 2010

In accordance with paragraph24(1) of Schedule7 to the Tribunals Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00003], they have consulted the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council and its Scottish Committee.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010 and come into force on 2nd July 2010.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00004].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The principal Regulations are amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

- (2) In regulation2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) insert after the definition of “complaint”—

> > - “dental body corporate” means a body corporate entitled by virtue of section43 of the Dentists Act 1984[^f00005] to carry on the business of dentistry;

- (b) insert after the definition of “doctor’s panel”—

> > - “him or her” includes “it” when referring to a practitioner who is a dental body corporate;
> - “his or her” includes “its” when referring to a practitioner who is a dental body corporate;

- (c) for the definition of “General Dental Services Regulations” substitute ““General Dental Services Regulations” means, in respect of the period to 1st July 2010, the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996[^f00006] and, in respect of the period from 2nd July 2010, the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010[^f00007];”;

- (d) in the definition of “practitioner”—

- (i) for “Schedules 1 and 2” substitute “Schedule 1”;

- (ii) after “dentist”, insert “a dental body corporate,”;

- (e) in the definition of “terms of service” after “dentists” insert “or dental bodies corporate”;

- (f) in the definition of “treatment” after “dentist” insert “or dental body corporate”.

- (3) In regulation2(2)(a) after “Regulations” insert “in respect of the period to 1st July 2010 and paragraph37 of Schedule1 to the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010 in respect of the period from 2nd July 2010”.

- (4) In regulation4(6)(a)(ii) (provisions relating to the start of disciplinary proceedings) after “dentist” insert “ora dental body corporate”.

- (5) In regulation5(3)(b) (referral to discipline committee) after “dentist” insert “ora dental body corporate”.

- (6) In regulation6(7)(c) (time limits)—

- (a) after “dentist” where it first occurs insert “ordental body corporate”;

- (b) omit “by the dentist” in both places, where it appears.

- (7) In regulation10(7)(b) (procedure on appeal to Scottish Ministers) after “dentist” insert “ora dental body corporate”.

- (8) In regulation11 (recovery of amounts from practitioners following appeal) after “dentist” in each place where it appears insert “or dental body corporate”.

- (9) In regulation16 (attendance by member of Council on Tribunals) and the heading to that regulation for “Council on Tribunals” substitute “Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council”.

- (10) In Schedule1, (constitution of discipline committees) paragraph2(4)(b) after “dentist” insert “or dental body corporate”.

- (11) In Schedule2 (procedure for investigation by discipline committees)—

- (a) omit paragraph2(1)(a) (disciplinary matters in relation to non listed deputy or assistant to a dentist);

- (b) in paragraph2(1) omit “deputy, assistant or”;

- (c) in paragraph 5 (attendance at hearing)—

- (i) in sub-paragraph (2)(a) after “practitioner” insert “or, where the practitioner is a dental body corporate, a person who exercises some control over that dental body corporate”;

- (ii) after sub-paragraph (5) insert—

> > (6) For the purposes of paragraph 5(2)(a) a person who exercises some control over a body corporate is, in the case of a—
>   (a) company, a director,
>   (b) limited liability partnership, a member,
>   (c) partnership, a partner.

- (d) in paragraph9 (interpretation)—

- (i) for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

> > - “lay member” has the same meaning given to it in paragraph 8 of Schedule 1

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (b)(ii) after “dentist” where it second occurs, insert “or a dental body corporate”.

- (12) In Schedule4 (advisory committees)—

- (a) after paragraph 2 insert—

> > (2A) A failure to ensure a proper degree of skill and attention in the treatment of a patient is specified for the purposes of regulation 11(2) in relation to a dental body corporate.

- (b) in paragraph5 after “dentists” insert “or dental bodies corporate”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 (“theprincipal Regulations”) which make provision relating to the NHSdiscipline committees.

These Regulations are consequential upon the coming into force of certain amendments made to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (“the1978 Act”) by the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the2005 Act”) as regards dental bodies corporate. They also contain an amendment to the principal Regulations correcting a cross reference to “practitioner” which erroneously included a member of the joint discipline committee for the purposes of that definition in Schedule 2.

Regulation2(2)(a) inserts a new definition of “dental body corporate”. The definition of “dental body corporate” is by reference to section43 of the Dentists Act 1984 as amended by the Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2011)).

Regulation2(2)(b) inserts new definitions for the phrases “him or her” and “his or her” which modify those phrases to include “it” and “its” respectively when they refer to a dental body corporate.

Regulation 2(2)(c) amends the definition of “General Dental Services Regulations” to take account of the revocation of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/177](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/177)) and their replacement by the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010 ([S.S.I. 2010/208](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2010/208)), (the “GDS regulations 2010”).

Regulation2(2)(c) amends the definition of “practitioner” by inserting “dental body corporate”.

Regulation 2(2)(d) limits the exception of the application of the definition of “practitioner” to Schedule 1 and consequently applies that definition to Schedule 2. This, together with the amendment in Regulation 2(11)(d)(i) corrects a cross reference to practitioner in Schedule 2 which erroneously included a member of the joint discipline committee for the purposes of that Schedule.

Regulation2(2)(e) amends the definition of “terms of service” by inserting “or dental body corporate”.

Regulation 2(2)(f) amends the definition of “treatment” by inserting “or dental body corporate”.

Regulation2(3) amends regulation2(2)(a) of the principal Regulations to take account of the revocation of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996 and their replacement by the GDS Regulations 2010 on 2nd July 2010.

Regulations2(4), (5), (6), (7), (8), (10) and (11)(d)(ii) amend regulations4(6)(a), 5(3)(b), 6(7)(c), 10(7)(b) and 11, Schedule1, paragraph2(4)(b) and Schedule 2, paragraph 9(b)(ii) respectively of the principal Regulations by inserting references to “dental body corporate”.

Regulation2(9) amends regulation16 of the principal Regulations by substituting reference to the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council for reference to the Council on Tribunals following the replacement of the latter by the former in terms of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.

Regulation 2(11)(a) and (b) amends paragraph2 of Schedule2 of the principal Regulations in consequence of the GDS Regulations 2010.

Regulation 2(11)(c) amends paragraph 5(2)(a) of Schedule 2 to provide for circumstances where a dental body corporate may be required to attend at discipline committee hearing.
