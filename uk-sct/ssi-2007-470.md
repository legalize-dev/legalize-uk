# The Contaminants in Food (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007

Made: 22nd October 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 23rd October 2007

Coming into force: 14th November 2007

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Contaminants in Food (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007 and come into force on 14th November 2007.

#### Amendment of the Contaminants in Food (Scotland) Regulations 2007

###### 2

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Contaminants in Food (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00004], for the definition of “the Commission Regulation” substitute–

> > - “the Commission Regulation” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1881/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1881) setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs[^f00005], as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1126/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1126) amending Regulation [(EC) No. 1881/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1881) setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs as regards Fusarium toxins in maize and maize products[^f00006].

## Signed

SHONA ROBISON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 22nd October 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Contaminants in Food (Scotland) Regulations 2007 ([S.S.I. 2007/29](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2007/29)) (“the 2007 Regulations”). They make provision for the execution and enforcement of Commission Regulation (EC) No. [1126/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/1126/2007) amending Regulation (EC) No. [1881/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/1881/2006) setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs as regards *Fusarium* toxins in maize and maize products.

The Regulations amend the definition of “the Commission Regulation” in the 2007 Regulations so as to include the amendments effected by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1126/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1126) (*regulation 2*).

A full regulatory assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
