## SCHEDULE 1 — CAPITAL ACTIVITIES

###### 1

Erection of–

- (a) A stock fence;

- (b) Gate and posts;

- (c) A single stile; or

- (d) A double stile.

###### 2

Conversion of a stock fence to a deer fence.

###### 3

Erection of a scare or temporary fence or rabbit proof netting.

###### 4

Erection of a deer fence marked to reduce bird collision.

###### 5

Erection of a post and rail fence as a tree guard or water gate.

###### 6

Building or restoration of traditional drystone or flagstone dykes or walls.

###### 7

Installation of self supporting guard for hedge plants.

###### 8

Installation of a water trough.

###### 9

Planting, replanting, coppicing or laying of a hedge.
