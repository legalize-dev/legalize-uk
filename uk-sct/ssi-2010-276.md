# The Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies and Sources of Information) (Scotland) Amendment of Commencement Dates Order 2010

Made: 1st July 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 2nd July 2010

Coming into force: 15th August 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections23(2)(c) and 26(2)(i) of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies and Sources of Information) (Scotland) Amendment of Commencement Dates Order 2010 and comes into force on 15th August 2010.

#### Amendment of the Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010

###### 2

In article 1 (citation and commencement) of the Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010[^f00002] for “16th August” substitute “14th November”.

#### Amendment of the Additional Support for Learning (Sources of Information) (Scotland) Order 2010

###### 3

In article 1 (citation and commencement) of the Additional Support for Learning (Sources of Information) (Scotland) Order 2010[^f00003] for “16th August” substitute “14th November”.

## Signed

ADAM INGRAM — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st July 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order changes the date on which the Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010 and the Additional Support for Learning (Sources of Information) (Scotland) Order 2010 come into force from 16th August 2010 to 14th November 2010.
