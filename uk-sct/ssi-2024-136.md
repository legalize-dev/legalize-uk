# The Tied Pubs (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2024

Made: 1st May 2024

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 3rd May 2024

Coming into force: 24th June 2024

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 25(2) of the Tied Pubs (Scotland) Act 2021[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Tied Pubs (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2024 and come into force on 24 June 2024.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Tied Pubs (Scotland) Act 2021, so far as not already in force, is 24 June 2024.

## Signed

RICHARD LOCHHEAD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st May 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force the Tied Pubs (Scotland) Act 2021 (“*the Act*”) so far as not already in force, on 24 June 2024.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 5 May 2021. Section 3 (duty to act consistently with regulatory principles), section 4 (Scottish Ministers’ duty to seek to make code and appoint adjudicator), section 5 (review of the code and adjudicator’s performance), Part 3 (interpretation) and Part 4 (final provisions) came into force on the following day.

The Tied Pubs (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2024 commenced sections 2 (Scottish Pubs Code Adjudicator), 13 (investigation policy) and schedule 2 (Scottish Pubs Code Adjudicator) of the Act on 30 April 2024.
