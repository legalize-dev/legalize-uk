# The National Bus Travel Concession Scheme for Older and Disabled Persons (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015

Made: 17th March 2015

Coming into force: 1st April 2015

In accordance with section 52(3) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Bus Travel Concession Scheme for Older and Disabled Persons (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st April 2015.

#### Amendments to the National Bus Travel Concession Scheme for Older and Disabled Persons (Scotland) Order 2006

###### 2

- (1) The National Bus Travel Concession Scheme for Older and Disabled Persons (Scotland) Order 2006[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) At the end of article 12(2B)(d) (capped level of funding) omit “and”.

- (3) At the end of article 12(2B)(e) insert—

> > ;
> (f) for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2015 £202,000,000; and
> (g) for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2016 £212,000,000

- (4) At the end of article 12(2D)(a) (reimbursement rate) omit “and”.

- (5) At the end of article 12(2D)(b) insert—

> > ;
> (c) 57.1% for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2015; and
> (d) 56.9% for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2016

## Signed

DEREK MACKAY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 17th March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends article 12 of the National Bus Travel Concession Scheme for Older and Disabled Persons (Scotland) Order 2006 to set the capped level of funding and the reimbursement rate for the financial years 2015/16 and 2016/17 (article 2).
