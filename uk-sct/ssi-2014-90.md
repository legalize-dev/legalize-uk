# The Social Care (Self-directed Support) (Scotland) Act 2013 (Consequential and Saving Provisions) Order 2014

Made: 27th March 2014

Coming into force: 1st April 2014

In accordance with section 26(3) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Social Care (Self-directed Support) (Scotland) Act 2013 (Consequential and Saving Provisions) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st April 2014.

#### Consequential modifications

###### 2

- (1) The enactments mentioned in the first column of the table in Part 1 of the Schedule are repealed to the extent specified in the second column of that table.

- (2) The enactments mentioned in the first column of the table in Part 2 of the Schedule are revoked to the extent specified in the second column of that table.

- (3) The provisions of the enactments mentioned in the first and second columns of the table in Part 3 of the Schedule are amended to the extent specified in the third column of that table.

#### Saving provision

###### 3

The modifications in article 2 of this Order do not have effect in respect of any payment made under section 12B of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00002] after this Order comes into force.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes modifications in consequence of the Social Care (Self-directed Support) (Scotland) Act 2013 (“the 2013 Act”).

Section 25 of the 2013 Act repeals section 12B of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968 (“the 1968 Act”) which make provision in relation to direct payments in respect of community care services. New provision for direct payments is made in the 2013 Act.

Article 2 and the Schedule repeal, revoke and amend enactments contained in primary and secondary legislation in consequence on the repeal of section 12B of the 1968 Act and the new provision for direct payments in the 2013 Act.

Article 3 contains a saving provision so that the enactments specified in the Schedule continue to apply without modification in relation to any payments which are made under section 12B of the 1968 Act after 1st April 2014.
