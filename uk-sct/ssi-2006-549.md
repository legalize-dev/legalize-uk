# The Regulation of Scallop Dredges (Scotland) Revocation Order 2006

Made: 16th November 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 17th November 2006

Coming into force: 9th December 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3 and 15(3) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Regulation of Scallop Dredges (Scotland) Revocation Order 2006 and shall come into force on 9th December 2006.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Regulation of Scallop Dredges (Scotland) Order 2005[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 16th November 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Regulation of Scallop Dredges (Scotland) Order 2005 ([S.S.I. 2005/371](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2005/371)).
