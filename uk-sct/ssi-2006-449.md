# The Plant Protection Products (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2006

Made: 30th August 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th September 2006

Coming into force: 26th September 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 26th September 2006.

#### Amendment of the Plant Protection Products (Scotland) Regulations 2005

###### 2

The Plant Protection Products (Scotland) Regulations 2005 are amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

#### Amendment of regulation 2 (interpretation)

###### 3

In regulation 2 (interpretation) in paragraph (1) for the definition of “the Directive” substitute–

> > “*the Directive*” means Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market , as amended–
> (a) by the instruments listed in Part I of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
> (b) from 1st October 2006, by the instruments listed in Part II of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
> (c) from 1st November 2006, by the instrument listed in Part III of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
> (d) from 1st January 2007, by the instrument listed in Part IV of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
> (e) from 1st February 2007, by the instruments listed in Part V of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
> (f) from 1st April 2007 by the instruments listed in Part VI of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
> (g) from 1st August 2007 by the instrument listed in Part VII of Schedule 1 to these Regulations; and
> (h) from 1st November 2007 by the instrument listed in Part VIII of Schedule 1 to these Regulations;
