# The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Modification) (No. 2) Revocation Order 2020

Made: 29th April 2020

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

In accordance with section 99(2) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Modification) (No. 2) Revocation Order 2020 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Modification) (No. 2) Order 2019[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Government — 29th April 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Modification) (No. 2) Order 2019.
