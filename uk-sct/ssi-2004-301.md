# The Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2004

Made: 22nd June 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 24th June 2004

Coming into force: 2nd July 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 73(f) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 2nd July 2004.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

In paragraph 6(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2004[^f00002] for “Scottish Ministers” substitute “Secretary of State for the Home Department”.

## Signed

JAMES R WALLACE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 22nd June 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct a technical defect in paragraph 6(a)(i) of Schedule 1 to the Education Maintenance Allowance (Scotland) Regulations 2004.
