# The Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003

Made: 30th October 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 89(2) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 85 of and schedule 3 to the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (so far as not already in force) shall come into force on 4th November 2003.

## Signed

CATHY JAMIESON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 30th October 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 4th November 2003 as the date for the coming into force (so far as not already in force) of section 85 of and schedule 3 to the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000.

The provisions of the Act remaining to be commenced after this Order are certain provisions from schedules 4 to 6.
