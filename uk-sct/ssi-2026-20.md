# The National Health Service (Functions of the Common Services Agency) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Order 2026

Made: 21st January 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 23rd January 2026

Coming into force: 1st April 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1)(b), 10(3), and 105(6) and (7), of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001], and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service (Functions of the Common Services Agency) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Order 2026 and comes into force on 1 April 2026.

- (2) In this Order “*NHS Education for Scotland*” means the Special Health Board constituted under the NHS Education for Scotland Order 2002[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the National Health Service (Functions of the Common Services Agency) (Scotland) Order 2008

###### 2

- (1) The National Health Service (Functions of the Common Services Agency) (Scotland) Order 2008[^f00003] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In article 2 (functions of the Agency)—

- (a) after paragraph (a) insert—

> > (aa) exercise the power of the Scottish Ministers under section 16 of the Act[^f00004] to assist any voluntary organisations whose activities include the provision of a service similar or related to a service provided by the Agency under the Act;
> (ab) exercise the power of the Scottish Ministers under section 16B of the Act[^f00005] to give financial assistance to voluntary organisations limited to assisting such organisations to which that section would apply if the expression “*relevant service*” as defined in section 16B(3) were limited to the provision of services provided by the Agency;

- (b) for paragraph (la) substitute—

> > (la) exercise functions in relation to education and training relating to the health service including, without prejudice to the foregoing generality, functions specified in section 47 of the Act (making available facilities for education, research and training);

#### Dissolution and revocation

###### 3

- (1) NHS Education for Scotland is dissolved.

- (2) The following enactments are revoked—

- (a) the NHS Education for Scotland Order 2002,

- (b) the Scottish Council for Postgraduate Medical and Dental Education and NHS Education for Scotland (Transfer of Staff) Regulations 2002[^f00006],

- (c) the NHS Education for Scotland Amendment Order 2006[^f00007], and

- (d) the NHS Education for Scotland Amendment Order 2021[^f00008].

#### Transfer of property etc

###### 4

- (1) With effect from 1 April 2026, all property, rights, liabilities and obligations to which paragraph (2) applies, are transferred to, and vest in, the Agency.

- (2) Paragraph (1) applies to—

- (a) all property (including corporeal and incorporeal moveable property) which immediately before the transfer date was owned by NHS Education for Scotland, and

- (b) all rights, liabilities and obligations which, immediately before 1 April 2026, subsisted in respect of NHS Education for Scotland.

#### Consequential amendments

###### 5

The schedule has effect.

#### Transitional provisions

###### 6

- (1) Any relevant thing done by or in relation to NHS Education for Scotland before 1 April 2026, so far as is required for continuing its effect on and after that date, has effect as if done by or in relation to the Agency.

- (2) Any relevant thing (which may include legal proceedings) which, immediately before 1 April 2026, is in the process of being done by or in relation to NHS Education for Scotland may continue to be done by or in relation to the Agency on and after that date.

- (3) No right to terminate or vary a contract, arrangement or instrument is to operate or become exercisable, and no provision of any contract, arrangement or instrument is to operate or become exercisable or be contravened, by reason of any transfer under or other operation of this Order.

- (4) The transfers provided for by this Order are to be made—

- (a) irrespective of any requirement for consent that would otherwise apply (whether arising under any enactment, instrument, agreement or otherwise), and

- (b) whether or not they would otherwise be capable of being transferred.

- (5) In this article “*relevant thing*” means anything which, if it were to be done on or after 1 April 2026, would be done by or in relation to the Agency.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order dissolves NHS Education for Scotland and transfers the functions carried out by that body to the Common Services Agency for the Scottish Health Service (“*the Agency*”).

Article 2 amends article 2 of the National Health Service (Functions of the Common Services Agency) (Scotland) Order 2008 to transfer the specified functions of NHS Education for Scotland to the Agency, including in relation to Scottish Ministers’ powers under sections 16 and 16B of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 to assist voluntary organisations.

Article 3 dissolves NHS Education for Scotland and revokes previous legislation made in relation to that body.

Article 4 transfers the property, rights, liabilities and obligations of NHS Education for Scotland to the Agency.

Article 5 and the schedule make consequential amendments to primary and secondary legislation.

Article 6 provides that things done by or in relation to NHS Education for Scotland before 1 April 2026 will have effect as if done by or in relation to the Agency, and contracts cannot be terminated, varied, exercised or contravened by reason of this Order.
