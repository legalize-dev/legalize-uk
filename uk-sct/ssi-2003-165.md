# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003

Made: 10th March 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

#### Amendment to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002

###### 2

- (1) The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)–

- (a) in the definition of “official fish inspector” for “an environmental health officer” substitute “a person”; and

- (b) in the definition of “owner” insert “or part of a consignment” after “product or consignment”.

- (3) In regulation 3A (other exemptions)[^f00003]–

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a)–

- (i) after “traveller (or” omit “that”; and

- (ii) for “him or her” substitute “that traveller”; and

- (b) in paragraph (1)(b)–

- (i) after “traveller (or” omit “that”; and

- (ii) for “him or her” substitute “that traveller”.

- (4) In regulation 4(c) (enforcement authorities) insert “at premises” after “paragraph (a), and”.

- (5) In regulation 33(6) (products transported under supervision) for “premises” substitute “establishment”.

- (6) In Schedule 2 (import conditions for products)–

- (a) for the entry in section C of Part I (protective measures - all products) substitute–

> > Commission Decision [2002/994/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0994) (O.J. No. L 348, 21.12.2002, p.154), as amended by Commission Decision [2003/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0072) (O.J. No. L 26, 31.1.2003, p.84)

- (b) in Part V (fresh poultry-meat) after section D (health certification requirements) insert–

> > (1) Brazil
>   Commission Decision [2002/794/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0794) (O.J. No. L 276, 12.10.2002, p.66).
> (2) Thailand
>   Commission Decision [2002/251/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0251) (O.J. No. L 84, 28.3.2002, p.77).

; and

- (c) in section H of Part IX (protective measures - fishery products) omit paragraphs 2 (China and Vietnam), 5 (Pakistan) and 7 (Vietnam).

#### Revocations

###### 3

The Food and Animal Feedingstuffs (Products of Animal Origin from China) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00004] and the Food and Animal Feedingstuffs (Products of Animal Origin from China) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002[^f00005] are revoked.

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 10th March 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, amend the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 (S.S.I. [2002/445](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/445)) (“the principal Regulations”) as follows:

1 They amend the principal Regulations to clarify a number of minor drafting points (regulation 2(2) to (5)).

2 They implement Commission Decision [2002/994/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0994), as amended by Commission Decision [2003/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0072) (which repealed Commission Decision [2002/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0069) concerning certain protective measures with regard to products of animal origin imported from China) (regulation 2(6)(a)).

3 They implement Commission Decisions [2002/251/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0251) and [2002/794/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0794) concerning certain protective measures with regard to poultrymeat and poultrymeat products from Thailand and Brazil respectively (regulation 2(6)(b)).

4 They implement Commission Decisions [2002/770/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0770) and [2002/771/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0771) revoking previous decisions concerning protective measures with regard to fishery and aquaculture products imported from Vietnam and Pakistan respectively (regulation 2(6)(c)).

5 They revoke, consequentially, the Food and Animal Feedingstuffs (Products of Animal Origin from China) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 and the Food and Animal Feedingstuffs (Products of Animal Origin from China) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002 (regulation 3). A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations.
