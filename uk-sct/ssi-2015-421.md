# The International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015

Made: 9th December 2015

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th day of December 2015Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015.

- (2) This Order comes into force on the later of—

- (a) the day after the day on which it is made; or

- (b) the day on which the United Kingdom becomes a member of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Order 2009

###### 2

- (1) The International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Order 2009[^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) After Schedule 15, insert Schedule 16 (Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank) set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Order 2009 to confer immunities and privileges, insofar as they are within devolved competence, upon the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank and its officers, employees, experts and persons engaged on missions for the Bank. The Order gives effect to the Articles of Agreement of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank done in Beijing on 29th June 2015 (Cm. 9126). It should be read together with the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (Immunities and Privileges) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1884](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1884)) which confers additional immunities and privileges.
