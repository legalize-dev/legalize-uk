# The Vulnerable Witnesses (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2015

Made: 3rd June 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 5th June 2015

Coming into force: 1st July 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 25 of the Vulnerable Witnesses (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Vulnerable Witnesses (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st July 2015.

#### Day appointed

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 10 of the Vulnerable Witnesses (Scotland) Act 2004 (application of vulnerable witnesses provisions to proceedings in the district court) is 1st July 2015.

## Signed

MICHAEL MATHESON — A member of the Scottish Government — 3rd June 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 10 of the Vulnerable Witnesses (Scotland) Act 2004 (“the Act”) on 1st July 2015 (article 2).

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 14th April 2004.
