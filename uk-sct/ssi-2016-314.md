# The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Part 4 and Part 5 Complaints) Revocation Order 2016

Made: 5th October 2016

Coming into force: 6th October 2016

In accordance with section 99(2) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Part 4 and Part 5 Complaints) Revocation Order 2016 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Part 4 and Part 5 Complaints) Order 2016[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Government — 5th October 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Part 4 and Part 5 Complaints) Order 2016.
