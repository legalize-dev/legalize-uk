| **Notes:** The method of analysis used to measure the concentration of the constituents in Schedule 6 shall be able to measure at least concentrations equal to the parametric value with the specified accuracy, precision and detection limits. Regardless of the sensitivity of the method of analysis, the result must be expressed to the same number of decimal places as the maximum limit set out in Schedule 6 for the particular constituent being analysed. Accuracy is the systematic error and represents the difference between the average value of a large number of repeated measurements and the exact value. Precision represents the random error and is expressed in general as the standard deviation (within a batch and between batches) of a sample of results from the average.
