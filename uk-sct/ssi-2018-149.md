# The Wild Animals in Travelling Circuses (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018

Made: 9th May 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 11th May 2018

Coming into force: 28th May 2018

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9(2) of the Wild Animals in Travelling Circuses (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Wild Animals in Travelling Circuses (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 28th May 2018.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 to 8 and schedules 1 and 2 of the Wild Animals in Travelling Circuses (Scotland) Act 2018 is 28th May 2018.

## Signed

R CUNNINGHAM — A member of the Scottish Government — 9th May 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force sections 1 to 8 and schedules 1 and 2 of the Wild Animals in Travelling Circuses (Scotland) Act 2018 (“the Act”).

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 24th January 2018. Sections 9 and 10 came into force the following day.
