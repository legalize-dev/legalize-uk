# The Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2024

Made: 3rd October 2024

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 7th October 2024

Coming into force: 1st November 2024

In accordance with section 54(1) of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00002], the Scottish Ministers have approved the making of these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2024 and come into force on 1 November 2024.

#### Replacement of change of name registration forms

###### 2

- (1) The Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1997[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) For the form in schedule 18, substitute the form in schedule 1 of these Regulations.

- (3) For the form in schedule 19, substitute the form in schedule 2 of these Regulations.

- (4) For the form in schedule 20, substitute the form in schedule 3 to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1997.

They update the following forms prescribed in the schedules of those Regulations—

- the form in schedule 18 (Form 21- application for recording of forename(s) or change of forename(s) of child under 12 months of age),

- the form in schedule 19 (Form 23- application for recording of change of forename(s) or surname(s) of child under 16 years of age), and

- the form in schedule 20 (Form 24- application for recording of change of forename(s) or surname(s) of person over 16 years of age).

The Regulations apply from 1 November 2024.
