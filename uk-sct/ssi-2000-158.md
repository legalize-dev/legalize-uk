# The Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2000

Made: 25th May 2000

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 31st May 2000

Coming into force: 21st June 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 21st June 2000.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

SUSAN C DEACON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 25th May 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations, which apply to Scotland, revoke the Animal Feedingstuffs from Belgium (Control) (Scotland) Regulations 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/16)), and implement in Scotland, in relation to products for animal feeding, Commission Decision [2000/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0301) repealing the protective measure with regard to contamination by dioxins of certain products or porcine and poultry origin intended for human or animal consumption (O.J. No. L97, 19.04.2000, p.16).
