# The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2004

Made: 13th February 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 17th February 2004

Coming into force: 15th March 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 15th March 2004.

#### General fund contribution limits

###### 2

The amount of contribution out of their general fund which a local authority may estimate that they will carry to the credit of their housing revenue account for the year 2004–2005 shall be limited to nil.

## Signed

MARGARET CURRAN — A member of the Scottish Executive — 13th February 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that local authorities may not include in their estimates for the year 2004–2005 any contribution from their general fund to their housing revenue account.
