# The Purity Criteria for Colours, Sweeteners and Miscellaneous Food Additives (Scotland) Regulations 2009

Made: 29th April 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th April 2009

Coming into force: 25th May 2009

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Purity Criteria for Colours, Sweeteners and Miscellaneous Food Additives (Scotland) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 25th May 2009.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Colours in Food Regulations 1995[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)–

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” substitute the following–

> > - “Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128)” means Commission Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128) laying down specific purity criteria concerning colours for use in foodstuffs[^f00005];

- (b) in the definition of “permitted colour”, for the expression “the annex to Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” substitute the expression “Annex I to Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128)”.

#### Amendment of the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995

###### 3

- (1) The Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995[^f00006] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)–

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” substitute the following–

> > - “Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060)” means Commission Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060) laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs;

- (b) in the definition of “permitted sweetener”, for the expression “the annex to Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” substitute the expression “Annex I to Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060)”.

#### Amendment of the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995

###### 4

- (1) The Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995[^f00007] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)–

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [96/77/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0077)” substitute the following–

> > - “Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084)” means Commission Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084) laying down specific purity criteria on food additives other than colours and sweeteners[^f00008] as that Directive stood before the amendment made by Commission Directive [2009/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0010) amending Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084) laying down specific purity criteria on food additives other than colours and sweetners[^f00009];

- (b) in the definition of “purity criteria”, in paragraph (a) for the expression “the Annex to Directive [96/77/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0077)” substitute the expression “Annex I to Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084)”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for the implementation of three new Commission Directives (“the new Directives”) which consolidate existing European Community rules governing the purity criteria for colours, sweeteners and other miscellaneous additives used in food.

These Regulations further amend in relation to Scotland the Colours in Food Regulations 1995, the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995 and the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995 (“the domestic provisions”).

Implementation of the Directives which the new Directives replace was by reference to them in domestic provisions, and these Regulations therefore update those references.

The new Directives are–

- (a) Commission Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128) laying down specific purity criteria for colours for use in foodstuffs;

- (b) Commission Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060) laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs; and

- (c) Commission Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084) laying down specific purity criteria on food additives other than colours and sweeteners.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
