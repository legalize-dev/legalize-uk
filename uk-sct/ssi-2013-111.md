# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 2) (Fees of Shorthand Writers) 2013

Made: 27th March 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th March 2013

Coming into force: 21st May 2013

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.2) (Fees of Shorthand Writers) 2013 and comes into force on 21st May 2013.

- (2) A certified copy of this Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Increase in fees of shorthand writers

###### 2

- (1) The table of fees in rule42.16(3) of the Rules of the Court of Session[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the following subparagraph.

- (2) In ChapterIV (transcripts of evidence etc.)[^f00003]—

- (a) in paragraph1 (attendance of shorthand writer), for the amounts of £40.75 and £162.80 substitute the amounts of £41.75 and £166.80 respectively; and

- (b) in paragraph2 (notes of evidence: extension by shorthand writer or transcriber), for the amounts of £6.65, £8.15 and £0.55 substitute the amounts of £6.80, £8.35 and £0.57 respectively.

#### Saving

###### 3

Paragraph2 of this Act of Sederunt shall not affect fees chargeable for work done, or outlays incurred, before 21st May 2013.

## Signed

BRIAN GILL — Lord President — I.P.D. — 27th March 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Sederunt increases the fees payable to shorthand writers in the Court of Session by 2.45%. The last increase was effected by the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment) (Fees of Shorthand Writers) 2012 ([S.S.I. 2012/100](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2012/100)).

This Act of Sederunt does not apply to fees chargeable for work done before it comes into force.
