# The Police (Injury Benefit) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2007

Made: 30th January 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th January 2007

Coming into force: 31st January 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Police Pensions Act 1976[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consultation with the Police Negotiating Board[^f00002] for the United Kingdom, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police (Injury Benefit) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2007 and come into force on 31st January 2007.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Police (Injury Benefit) (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00003] are revoked.

## Signed

TOM MCCABE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 30th January 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, provide for the revocation of the Police (Injury Benefit) (Scotland) Regulations 2006.
