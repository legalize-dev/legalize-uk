# The Plant Protection Products Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2001

Made: 20th April 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 23rd April 2001

Coming into force: 14th May 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and, in respect of regulation 5 of the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 14th May 2001.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Plant Protection Products Regulations 1995

###### 2

Regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Plant Protection Products Regulations 1995 shall be amended–

- (a) by substituting for the definition of “ the Directive ”–

> > “the Directive” means Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market , as amended by–
> (a) Commission Directive [93/71/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0071) ;
> (b) Commission Directive [94/37/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0037) ;
> (c) Commission Directive [94/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0079) ;
> (d) Commission Directive [95/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0035) ;
> (e) Commission Directive [95/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0036) ;
> (f) Commission Directive [96/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0012) ;
> (g) Commission Directive [96/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0046) ;
> (h) Commission Directive [96/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0068) ;
> (i) Council Directive [97/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0057) ;
> (j) Commission Directive [97/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0073) ;
> (k) Commission Directive [98/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0047) ;
> (l) Commission Directive [1999/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0001) ;
> (m) Commission Directive [1999/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0073) ;

> (p) Commission Directive [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0049) (as from 1st July 2001);
> (q) Commission Directive [2000/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0050) ;
> (r) Commission Directive [2000/66/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0066) (as from 1st August 2001);
> (s) Commission Directive [2000/67/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0067) (as from 1st August 2001); and
> (t) Commission Directive [2000/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0068) (as from 1st August 2001);

and

- (b) by substituting for the definition of “ the Ministers ”–

> > “the Ministers” means the Scottish Ministers;
> “the UK Ministers” means the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State acting jointly;

###### 3

After regulation 25 (service of documents) of the Plant Protection Products Regulations 1995 there shall be inserted–

> > (25A)
>   (1) The Ministers may arrange with the agreement of the UK Ministers, for any of their functions under these Regulations to be exercised on their behalf by a Minister of the Crown.
>   (2) An arrangement under this regulation in respect of any function may include provisions for any fees payable under any enactment to any part of the Scottish Administration in respect of that function to be collected by a Minister of Crown or Government Department on behalf of that part of the Scottish Administration.
>   (3) An arrangement under this regulation shall be in writing and be signed by, or on behalf of, the Scottish Ministers and the UK Ministers and such an arrangement may be subject to such conditions (including conditions as to the costs and charge for costs) as may be agreed.

#### Revocation

###### 4

The Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1999 are hereby revoked.

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 20th April 2001
