# The Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Order 2012

Made: 13th February 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 15th February 2012

Coming into force: 23rd February 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section16(1) of the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No.1) Amendment Order 2012 and comes into force on 23rdFebruary 2012.

#### Amendment of Commencement No.1 Order

###### 2

- (1) The Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No.1) Order 2012[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2, after “(interpretation) of” insert “, and the schedule (authorities to which Part1 applies) to,”.

## Signed

FIONA HYSLOP — A member of the Scottish Executive — 13th February 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No.1) Order 2012 so that the schedule to the Act also comes into force on 24thFebruary 2012.
