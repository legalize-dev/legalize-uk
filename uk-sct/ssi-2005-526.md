# The Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2005

Made: 25th October 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 27th October 2005

Coming into force: 25th November 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2005 and shall come into force on 25th November 2005.

#### Revocations

###### 2

The following Orders are hereby revoked–

- (i) the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003[^f00002];

- (ii) the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003[^f00003]; and

- (iii) the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2004[^f00004].

## Signed

HUGH HENRY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 25th October 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/441](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/441)), the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/519](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/519)) and the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2004 ([S.S.I. 2004/287](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2004/287)). These orders prescribed offences for the purposes of section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003.
