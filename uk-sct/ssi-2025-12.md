# The Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exclusions and Exceptions) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2025

Made: 21st January 2025

Coming into force: 7th February 2025

In accordance with section 10(2)[^f00002] of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exclusions and Exceptions) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2025 and comes into force on 7 February 2025.

#### Amendment of the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exclusions and Exceptions) (Scotland) Order 2013

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1 (proceedings) of the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exclusions and Exceptions) (Scotland) Order 2013[^f00003] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) After paragraph 30 insert—

> > (31) Proceedings under Chapter 3 (sexual harm prevention orders) or 4 (sexual risk orders) of Part 2 of the Abusive Behaviour and Sexual Harm (Scotland) Act 2016[^f00004].

## Signed

ANGELA CONSTANCE — A member of the Scottish Government — 21st January 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends schedule 1 of the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exclusions and Exceptions) (Scotland) Order 2013, which by virtue of article 3(1)(a) of that Order contains a list of proceedings in relation to which the application of section 4(1) of the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 is excluded. The effect is that evidence of previous convictions which have become “spent” under that Act is nonetheless admissible in those proceedings. This Order adds to the list in schedule 1 proceedings relating to sexual harm prevention orders and sexual risk orders under the Abusive Behaviour and Sexual Harm (Scotland) Act 2016.
