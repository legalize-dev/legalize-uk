# The Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (Scotland) Regulations 2005

Made: 17th March 2005

Coming into force: 11th April 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 11(2) and 36(1) and (2)(b) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations, a draft of which has, in accordance with section 37(2) of that Act, been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 11th April 2005.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the Act” means the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986.

#### Application

###### 2

These Regulations shall apply only in relation to any case where an application for advice and assistance is made on or after 11th April 2005.

#### Amendment of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986

###### 3

For the weekly sum of £197 specified in section 8(a) of the Act[^f00002] substitute the weekly sum of £203.

###### 4

For the capital sum of £1,370 specified in section 8 of the Act[^f00003] substitute the capital sum of £1,412.

###### 5

For the weekly sum of £83 specified in section 11(2)(a) of the Act[^f00004] substitute the weekly sum of £86.

#### Liability to pay fees or outlays under section 11(2) of the Act

###### 6

The maximum amount of fees or outlays which a client is liable to pay under section 11(2) of the Act[^f00005], where that client’s disposable income falls within a range specified in the first column of the following table, is the amount specified in relation to that range in the second column:–

| 1 Exceeding £86 but not exceeding £93 a week | £7 |

| 4 Exceeding £107 but not exceeding £114 a week | £28 |

| 6 Exceeding £121 but not exceeding £128 a week | £42 |
| 7 Exceeding £128 but not exceeding £135 a week | £49 |
| 8 Exceeding £135 but not exceeding £142 a week | £56 |

| 15 Exceeding £184 but not exceeding £191 a week | £105 |

#### Revocation

###### 7

The Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (Scotland) Regulations 2004[^f00006] are hereby revoked except in relation to any case where an application for advice and assistance is made before 11th April 2005.

## Signed

HUGH HENRY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 17th March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, in relation to any case where an application for advice and assistance is made on or after 11th April 2005–

- (a) increase the disposable income limit for eligibility for advice and assistance under the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986 from £197 a week to £203 a week (regulation 3);

- (b) increase the disposable capital limit for advice and assistance from £1,370 to £1,412 (regulation 4);

- (c) increase the weekly disposable income above which a person is required to pay a contribution from £83 to £86 (regulation 5); and

- (d) prescribe the scale of contributions to be paid where the weekly disposable income exceeds £86 but does not exceed £203 (regulation 6).

The Regulations also revoke the Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (Scotland) Regulations 2004 except in relation to any case where an application for advice and assistance is made before 11th April 2005 (regulation 7).
