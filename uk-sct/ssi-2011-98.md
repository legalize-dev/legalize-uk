# The Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 (Fixed Penalty) Amendment Regulations 2011

Made: 10th February 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 14th February 2011

Coming into force: 1st April 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph2 of Schedule8B to the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section163A of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00002] they have consulted with such— persons considered by them to be representative of the interests of undertakers; road works authorities; and other persons, as they think appropriate.

persons considered by them to be representative of the interests of undertakers;

road works authorities; and

other persons,

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 (Fixed Penalty) Amendment Regulations 2011 and come into force on 1stApril 2011.

#### Amendment of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 (Fixed Penalty) Regulations 2008

###### 2

In regulation5 (time limit for giving fixed penalty notice) of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 (Fixed Penalty) Regulations 2008[^f00003] for “60” substitute “91”.

## Signed

KEITH BROWN — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 10th February 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for an increase in the time period for giving a fixed penalty notice from 60days to 91days under regulation5 of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 (Fixed Penalty) Regulations 2008.
