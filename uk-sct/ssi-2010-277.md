# The Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2010

Made: 1st July 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section26(3) of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2009[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act2009 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2010.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraphs(2) and (3), the provisions of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act2009, so far as not already in force, come into force on 14thNovember 2010.

- (2) Paragraph(1) does not apply to section23.

- (3) Section18(2) comes into force on that date immediately after section19(2) comes into force.

## Signed

ADAM INGRAM — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st July 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 14thNovember 2010 the provisions of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2009 (“the Act”) which are yet to be commenced, except for section23 (collection of data on additional support needs).

Sections 18(2) and 19(2) of the Act both amend section 19(3) of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act [2004 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2004/4). The amendment made by section 19(2) of the Act must legally apply before the amendment made by section 18(2), article 2(3) accordingly provides that the former comes into force immediately before the latter.
