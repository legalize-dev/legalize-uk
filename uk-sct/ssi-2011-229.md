# The Fundable Bodies (Royal Conservatoire of Scotland) Order 2011

Made: 17th March 2011

Coming into force: 7th November 2011

In accordance with section34(4) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fundable Bodies (Royal Conservatoire of Scotland) Order 2011 and comes into force on 7th November 2011.

#### Modification of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005

###### 2

In schedule2 to the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (fundable bodies)[^f00002], for “Royal Scottish Academy of Music and Drama” substitute “Royal Conservatoire of Scotland”.

## Signed

MICHAEL W RUSSELL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 17th March 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the Act”) established the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council and made provision relating to the funding of bodies which provide further and higher education. These bodies are described in the Act as “fundable bodies” and are listed in schedule2 to the Act.

This Order updates the list of fundable bodies by amending schedule2 to the Act to reflect the change of name of Royal Scottish Academy of Music and Drama to Royal Conservatoire of Scotland.
