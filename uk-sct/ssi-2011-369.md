# The Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (Consequential Modifications) (No. 2) Order 2011

Made: 25th October 2011

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

In accordance with section 133(2)[^f00002] of that Act a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (Consequential Modifications) (No.2) Order 2011 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it was made.

#### Modifications to the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005

###### 2

Section 78(5) of the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00003] (meaning of “relevant premises”) is amended as follows:—

- (a) in paragraph (c) for “subsection (4)” where it first appears, to the end, substitute “paragraph3 of schedule 12 to the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 except where the service is provided as mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)(c)(ii) of that paragraph;”;

- (b) in paragraph (d) for “subsection (5) of that section” substitute “section 10F(1) (a) - (d) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00004]”;

- (c) in paragraph (e) for “subsection (9) of that section” substitute “paragraph 6 of schedule12 to the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010”.

## Signed

NICOLA STURGEON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 25th October 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes consequential modifications to the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 in connection with the commencement of Parts 5 and 6 of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010.
