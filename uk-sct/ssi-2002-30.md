# The Children’s Hearings (Legal Representation) (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2002

Made: 25th January 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th January 2002

Coming into force: 22nd February 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42(1) and (2)(a), (b), (d) and (i) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Rules:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Children’s Hearings (Legal Representation) (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2002 and shall come into force on 22nd February 2002.

#### Amendment of the Children’s Hearings (Legal Representation) (Scotland) Rules 2001

###### 2

In Rule 5(1)(a) of the Children’s Hearings (Legal Representation) (Scotland) Rules 2001[^f00002] for “the Social Work (Panels of Persons to Safeguard the Interests of Children) (Scotland) Regulations 2001” substitute “the Panels of Persons to Safeguard the Interests of Children (Scotland) Regulations 2001”.

## Signed

CATHY JAMIESON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 25th January 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend The Children’s Hearings (Legal Representation) (Scotland) Rules 2001. Rule 2 amends an incorrect reference to the Panels of Persons to Safeguard the Interests of Children (Scotland) Regulations 2001.
