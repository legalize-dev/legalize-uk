# Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2021

Made: 30th August 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st September 2021

Coming into force: 29th September 2021

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section104(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

#### Citation and commencement, etc.

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2021.

- (2) It comes into force on 29th September 2021.

- (3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2020[^f00003] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

- (2) In paragraph 1(3) (cessation), for “30th September 2021” substitute “31st March 2022”.

## Signed

CJM SUTHERLAND — Lord President — I.P.D. — 30th August 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2020. That Act of Sederunt made temporary modifications to require all simple procedure claims to be submitted using Civil Online or the internet interface to the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service’s case management system. The modifications are due to expire on 30thSeptember 2021. This Act of Sederunt amends that expiry date to substitute a new date of expiry of 31st March 2022 so as to extend the life of the provisions for a period of 6 months.
