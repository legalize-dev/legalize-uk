# The Circular Economy (Scotland) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026

Made: 17th November 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th November 2026

Coming into force: 4th December 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 27(2) of the Circular Economy (Scotland) Act 2024[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Circular Economy (Scotland) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 4 December 2026.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “*the 1990 Act*” means the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00002],

- “*the Act*” means the Circular Economy (Scotland) Act 2024.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2), 4 December 2026 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act—

- (a) section 15 (household waste requirements),

- (b) section 16 (code of practice on household waste recycling),

- (c) section 17 (targets for local authorities relating to household waste recycling).

- (2) Section 15 of the Act comes into force on 4 December 2026 only for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to consult on guidance to be issued under section 46ZE(1)[^f00003] (guidance) of the 1990 Act in accordance with section 46ZE(2) of the 1990 Act.

## Signed

GILLIAN MARTIN — A member of the Scottish Government — 17th November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations bring provisions of the Circular Economy (Scotland) Act 2024 (“*the Act*”) into force.

Regulation 2(1) brings sections 15, 16 and 17 of the Act into force on 4 December 2026. Regulation 2(2) provides that section 15 of the Act is only brought into force for the purpose of enabling Scottish Ministers to consult on guidance.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 8 August 2024. Sections 26, 27 and 28 came into force on the following day.
