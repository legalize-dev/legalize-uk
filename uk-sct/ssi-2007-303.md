# The Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007

Made: 4th June 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 5th June 2007

Coming into force: 12th July 2007

They have carried out consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007 and come into force on 12th July 2007.

#### Amendment of the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Scotland) Regulations 1999

###### 2

- (1) The Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Scotland) Regulations 1999[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation), for the definition of the Commission Regulation substitute–

> > - “the Commission Regulation” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 445/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0445) laying down certain detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2991/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/2991) laying down standards for spreadable fats and of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1898/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/1898) on the protection of designations used in the marketing of milk and milk products[^f00005];

## Signed

SHONA ROBISON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 4th June 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision for the enforcement and execution of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 445/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0445), which revokes and replaces in its entirety Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 577/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0577) (and its amendments), laying down certain detailed rules for the application of the Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2991/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/2991) and of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1898/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/1898) on the protection of designations used in the marketing of milk and milk products, as amended. Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 445/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0445) does not introduce any changes to existing legal requirements.

These Regulations update the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Scotland) Regulations 1999, replacing the reference to Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 577/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0577), and its amendments, with one to its replacement Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 445/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0445).

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
