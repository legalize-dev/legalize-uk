# The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Appointment of Chief Inspector) (Scotland) Order 2011

Made: 9th February 2011

Coming into force: 10th February 2011

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th day of February 2011Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Authorities (Appointment of Chief Inspector) (Scotland) Order 2011 and comes into force on 10thFebruary 2011.

#### Appointment of Chief Inspector

###### 2

Steven Cameron Torrie, QFSM is appointed as Chief Inspector of Fire and Rescue Authorities for the purposes of section43(1)(a) of the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Appointment of Chief Inspector) (Scotland) Order 2009[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE
