# The Serious Crime Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2009

Made: 3rd June 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 94(3) and (4)(a), (b), (d) and (e) of the Serious Crime Act 2007[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Serious Crime Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2009.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Serious Crime Act 2007 is 18th June 2009–

- (a) section 75(4) and (5) (use of production orders for detained cash investigations);

- (b) section 76(4) to (6) (use of search warrants etc. for detained cash investigations);

- (c) section 77 (further provision about detained cash investigations) in so far as it relates to the provisions in sub-paragraph (d); and

- (d) in Schedule 10 (detained cash investigations: further provision)–

- (i) paragraph 1 in so far as it relates to the following paragraphs of that Schedule;

- (ii) paragraphs 14 to 23; and

- (iii) paragraph 25 in so far as it extends to Scotland.

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 3rd June 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences provisions of the Serious Crime Act 2007 on 18th June 2009. Those provisions bring into force certain amendments to the Proceeds of Crime Act [2002 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29) which extend the use of production orders and search warrants to detained cash investigations.
