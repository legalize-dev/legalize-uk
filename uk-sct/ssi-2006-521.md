# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006

Made: 26th October 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st November 2006

Coming into force: 15th December 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 112(1)(a)[^f00001], 113A(1)(a)[^f00002], 113B(1)(a)[^f00003], 114(1)(a)[^f00004] and 116(1)(a)[^f00005] of the Police Act 1997[^f00006], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 15th December 2006.

#### Amendment of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Scotland) Regulations 2006

###### 2

For the form set out in Schedule 2 to the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00007], substitute the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

CATHY JAMIESON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 26th October 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 by substituting a new application form for that previously prescribed in respect of applications for criminal conviction certificates, criminal record certificates and enhanced criminal record certificates under Part 5 of the Police Act 1997.
