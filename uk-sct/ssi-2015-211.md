# The Water Environment (River Basin Management Planning etc.) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2015

Made: 26th May 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th May 2015

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2) and (3)

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, and it appears to the Scottish Ministers that it is expedient for the references in regulation 3(c) to Regulation [(EC) No 1107/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1107) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00003] and to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00004] to be references to each instrument as amended from time to time.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Water Environment (River Basin Management Planning etc.) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2015.

- (2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on 14th September 2015.

- (3) Regulation 7 comes into force on 15th September 2015.

- (4) In these Regulations, “the 2013 Regulations” means the Water Environment (River Basin Management Planning: Further Provision) (Scotland) Regulations 2013[^f00005].

#### Amendment of Parts 1 and 2 (river basin management planning etc.) of the 2013 Regulations

###### 2

In the 2013 Regulations—

- (a) in regulation 2(1) (interpretation), for the definition of “the Water Framework Directive” substitute—

> > - “the Water Framework Directive” means [Directive 2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy[^f00006] as last amended by Commission Directive 2014/101/EU[^f00007].

- (b) in regulation 3(1) (setting of environmental objectives), after “5 to 10” insert “and 17”; and

- (c) in regulation 12(1)(b)(iii) (content of monitoring programme), for “Directive” substitute “Water Framework Directive”.

#### Amendment of Part 3 (measures in relation to surface water) of the 2013 Regulations

###### 3

In the 2013 Regulations—

- (a) for regulation 16 (interpretation of Part 3) together with its cross heading, substitute—

> > (16)
>   (1) In this Part—
>     - “biota taxon” means a particular aquatic taxon within the taxonomic rank ‘sub-phylum’, ‘class’ or their equivalent;
>     - “[Directive 2009/90/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0090)” means Commission [Directive 2009/90/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0090) laying down, pursuant to [Directive 2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060) of the European Parliament and of the Council, technical specifications for chemical analysis and monitoring of water status[^f00008];
>     - “EQS” means environmental quality standards or, where the context so requires, environmental quality standard;
>     - “existing obligations” means the obligations (on environmental quality standards in the field of water policy) under [Directive 2008/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0105) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00009] (“the original Directive”) before it was amended by Directive 2013/39/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00010] including, in particular, the achievement of good surface water chemical status in relation to the substances and the associated EQS listed in the original Directive;
>     - “limit of detection” means the output signal or concentration value above which it can be affirmed, with a stated level of confidence that a sample is different from a blank sample containing no determinand of interest;
>     - “limit of quantification” means a stated multiple of the limit of detection at a concentration of the determinand that can reasonably be determined with an acceptable level of accuracy and precision;
>     - “matrix” means a compartment of the aquatic environment, namely water, sediment or biota;
>     - “Part A” means Part A of Annex I to the Priority Substances Directive (as read with the definitions and notes contained in that Part);
>     - “Part B” means Part B of Annex I to the Priority Substances Directive;
>     - “the Priority Substances Directive” means [Directive 2008/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0105) of the European Parliament and of the Council on environmental quality standards in the field of water policy[^f00011] as amended by Directive 2013/39/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00012]; and
>     - “watch list” means the watch list of substances established under Article 8b(1) and (2) of the Priority Substances Directive.
>   (2) For the purposes of this Part, the limit of quantification may be calculated using an appropriate standard or sample, and may be obtained from the lowest calibration point on the calibration curve, excluding the blank.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Water Environment (River Basin Management Planning: Further Provision) (Scotland) Regulations 2013 (“the 2013 Regulations”) and the Water Environment (Controlled Activities) (Scotland) Regulations 2011 to further protect the water environment.

Regulation 2 amends Parts 1 and 2 of the 2013 Regulations to take account of amendments made to [Directive 2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060) (“the Water Framework Directive”) as last amended by Commission Directive 2014/101/EU.

Regulation 3 amends Part 3 of the 2013 Regulations to take account of amendments made to [Directive 2008/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0105) (“the Priority Substances Directive”) by Directive 2013/39/EU.

Regulation 4 amends Part 4 of the 2013 Regulations to take account of amendments made to [Directive 2006/118/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0118) (“the Groundwater Directive”) by Commission Directive 2014/80/EU.

Regulation 5 amends the Water Environment (Controlled Activities) (Scotland) Regulations 2011 to take account of the amendments made to the Water Framework Directive, the Priority Substances Directive and the Groundwater Directive including by, in particular, Directive 2013/39/EU, Commission Directive 2014/80/EU and Commission Directive 2014/101/EU.

Regulation 6 ensures that existing environmental quality standards for priority substances continue to apply until those standards in the 2013 Regulations are revised by virtue of these Regulations.

Regulation 7 revokes the Water Environment (Groundwater and Priority Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 2009.

The powers in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 are exercised so far as may be necessary (to supplement the other powers cited) for the purposes of implementing, or enabling the implementation of, obligations created or arising by or under the Water Framework Directive (as last amended), the Priority Substances Directive (as amended) and the Groundwater Directive (as amended), and for the purposes of dealing with matters arising out of or related to any such obligations. In particular, the powers are cited to enable provision to be made in regulation 5 to amend the 2013 Regulations and in regulation 7 to revoke the Water Environment (Groundwater and Priority Substances) (Scotland) Regulations 2009.
