# The Fundable Bodies (The Scottish Agricultural College) (Scotland) Order 2008

Made: 4th June 2008

Coming into force: 1st August 2008

In accordance with section 34(4) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fundable Bodies (The Scottish Agricultural College) (Scotland) Order 2008 and comes into force on 1st August 2008.

#### Modification of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005

###### 2

In schedule 2 to the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (fundable bodies) after the entry for The Robert Gordon University insert “The Scottish Agricultural College”.

## Signed

FIONA HYSLOP — A member of the Scottish Executive — 4th June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the Act”) established the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council and made provision relating to the funding of bodies which provide further and higher education. Those bodies are described in the Act as “fundable bodies” and are listed in schedule 2 to the Act.

This Order updates the list of fundable bodies by amending schedule 2 to the Act to add The Scottish Agricultural College to the list of fundable bodies.
