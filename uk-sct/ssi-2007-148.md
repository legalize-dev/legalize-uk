# The Education (Graduate Endowment, Student Fees and Support) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2007

Made: 6th March 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 6th March 2007

Coming into force: 1st August 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 49(3), 73(f), 73B and 74(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001]; sections 1 and 2 of the Education (Fees and Awards) Act 1983[^f00002]; section 1 of the Education (Graduate Endowment and Student Support) Scotland Act 2001[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Graduate Endowment, Student Fees and Support) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st August 2007.

- (2) These Regulations shall extend to Scotland only.

#### Revocation

###### 2

- (1) The Regulations specified in Schedule 1 are revoked in their entirety.

- (2) The Regulations specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 are revoked to the extent specified in column 3 thereof.

#### Transitional provisions

###### 3

- (1) Anything done, anything begun or any right or liability acquired under or by virtue of any provision of the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1992[^f00004], the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Regulations 1995[^f00005], the Education (Fees and Awards) (Scotland) Regulations 1997[^f00006], the Students' Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1999[^f00007], the Education (Student Loans) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00008], the Graduate Endowment (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00009] and the Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2004[^f00010], shall be deemed as the case may be to have been done, begun or acquired, and anything begun may be continued under or by virtue of the corresponding provision of, respectively, the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00011]; the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00012]; the Education (Fees and Awards) (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00013]; the Students' Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00014]; the Education (Student Loans) (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00015]; the Graduate Endowment (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00016] and the Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00017] (collectively “the Consolidated Regulations”).

- (2) The revocation by these Regulations of a saving made on the previous revocation or amendment of a provision does not affect the operation of the saving insofar as it remains capable of having effect.

- (3) The revocation by these Regulations of a transitional provision relating to the coming into force of a provision re-enacted in the Consolidated Regulations does not affect the operation of that transitional provision, so far as it remains capable of having effect, in relation to the provision as re-enacted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke instruments as a result of the consolidation of the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1992, the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Regulations 1995, the Education (Fees and Awards) (Scotland) Regulations 1997, the Students' Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1999, the Education (Student Loans) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, the Graduate Endowment (Scotland) Regulations 2001 and the Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2004. The effect of the revoked regulations is preserved in relation to any right or liability acquired by virtue of the revoked regulations.
