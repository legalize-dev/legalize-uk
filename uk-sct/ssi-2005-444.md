# The Mental Health (Form of Documents) (Scotland) Regulations 2005

Made: 12th September 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 13th September 2005

Coming into force: 5th October 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 325 of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mental Health (Form of Documents) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 5th October 2005.

- (1) In these Regulations–

- (a) “the 2003 Act” means the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003; and

- (b) any reference to a numbered section is a reference to the section bearing that number in the 2003 Act.

#### Form of documents

###### 2

Any document required or authorised to be prepared by virtue of a section of the 2003 Act listed in column 1 of Schedule 1 shall contain the particulars set out in the corresponding form listed in column 2 of that Schedule and set out in Schedule 2 and shall be used in the circumstances specified in column 3 of Schedule 1.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Regulations provide for the form of certain documents required or authorised under the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003, the information to be set out in the forms and the circumstances in which those forms should be used.
