# The Tuberculosis (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2018

Made: 22nd June 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 22nd June 2018

Coming into force: 30th June 2018

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 8(1), 28, 32(2) and (3), 34(7) and 83(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Tuberculosis (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2018 and comes into force on 30th June 2018.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Tuberculosis (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Order 2018[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

FERGUS EWING — A member of the Scottish Government — 22nd June 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Tuberculosis (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Order 2018.
