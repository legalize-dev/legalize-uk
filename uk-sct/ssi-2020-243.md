# The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2020

Made: 17th August 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th August 2020

Coming into force: 7th September 2020

The Scottish Ministers make following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 206(2) of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2020 and comes into force on 7 September 2020.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

In relation to section 122 of the Act (children’s advocacy services)—

- (a) 7 September 2020 is the appointed day for the coming into force of sub-sections (4) – (7),

- (b) 21 November 2020 is the appointed day for the coming into force of sub-sections (1) – (3).

## Signed

MAREE TODD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 17th August 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 122 of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”) on 7 September 2020 (sub-sections (4) – (7)) and 21 November 2020 (sub-sections (1) to (3)). Section 122 makes provision in relation to children’s advocacy services.
