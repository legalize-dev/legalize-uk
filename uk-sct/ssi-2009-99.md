# The European Communities (European Order for Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2009

Made: 10th March 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 11th March 2009

Coming into force: 6th April 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations, for the purpose of restricting to the sheriff courts only the effect of Regulation [(EC) No. 1896/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1896) of 12th December 2006 creating a European order for payment procedure, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (European Order for Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 6th April 2009.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations “*Order for Payment Regulation*” means Regulation [(EC) No. 1896/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1896) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12th December 2006 creating a European order for payment procedure.

- (2) Unless provided otherwise, a word or expression used in these Regulations and in the Order for Payment Regulation has the same meaning in these Regulations as it has in the Order for Payment Regulation.

#### Jurisdiction

###### 3

An application for a European order for payment in accordance with Article 7(1) of the Order for Payment Regulation shall be made to and determined exclusively by the sheriff court.

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 10th March 2009
