# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Highland Council) Designation Amendment Order 2025

Made: 13th May 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 15th May 2025

Coming into force: 24th June 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(3) of schedule 3 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraphs 1(1)(d) and 2(1)(c) of that schedule the Scottish Ministers have received an application for this Order from Highland Council[^f00002].

In accordance with paragraphs 1(3) and 2(3) of that schedule the Scottish Ministers have consulted the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Highland Council) Designation Amendment Order 2025 and comes into force on 24 June 2025.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Highland Council) Designation Order 2016 (“*the 2016 Order*”).

Article 3 of the 2016 Order designated the part of the Highland local government area specified in schedule 1 of the Order as a permitted parking area and as a special parking area in accordance with the provisions of schedule 3 of the Road Traffic Act 1991. While the Order is in force, certain specified parking offences are decriminalised in the Highland Council area.

Paragraph 1 of schedule 1 of the 2016 Order specified the whole of the Highland local government area, but excluded certain specified lengths of trunk road. The lengths of excluded trunk road are subject to the exceptions specified in remaining paragraphs of schedule 1 of the 2016 Order, with the effect of bringing those specified lengths within the designated area.

Article 2 of this Order substitutes the list of lengths of trunk road which are currently specified in paragraphs 2 to 7 of schedule 1 of the 2016 Order. The substituted list extends certain lengths of trunk road which were previously specified in the 2016 Order and adds a number of new lengths to that list. The effect of this is to extend the Highland local government area which is designated by article 3 of the 2016 Order as a permitted parking area and a special parking area.
