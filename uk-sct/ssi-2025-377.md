# The Social Security (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2025

Made: 26th November 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st December 2025

Coming into force: 15th December 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(2) and (3) of the Social Security (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2025[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2025 and come into force on 15 December 2025.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

15 December 2025 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 18 (information for audit of social security system) of the Social Security (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2025 only for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to consult in accordance with section 87B(6) (obtaining information for audit – consultation requirement) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00002].

## Signed

SHIRLEY-ANNE SOMERVILLE — A member of the Scottish Government — 26th November 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations bring section 18 of the Social Security (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2025 (“*the Act*”) into force on 15 December 2025 only for the purpose of public consultation in accordance with section 87B(6) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (“*the 2018 Act*”).

Section 18 of the Act inserts new sections 87B, 87C, 87D and 87E into the 2018 Act to give the Scottish Ministers powers to require individuals to provide information in order to audit the social security system as a whole.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 23 January 2025. Sections 24, 25, 26, 27 and 28 of the Act came into force on the day after Royal Assent in accordance with section 27(1) of the Act.
