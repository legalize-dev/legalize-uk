# The Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006 (Consequential Amendment) Order 2008

Made: 23rd June 2008

Coming into force: 24th June 2008

In accordance with section 59(3) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006 (Consequential Amendment) Order 2008 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### The Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006: consequential amendment

###### 2

In section 4(4)(b) of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004[^f00002] (trafficking people for exploitation), for “the Human Organ Transplants Act [1989 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/31)” substitute “Part 1 of the [Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2006/4)”.

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 23rd June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes an amendment to the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004 (“the 2004 Act”), which is considered to be necessary in consequence of the Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”). The 2006 Act repealed the Human Organ Transplants Act 1989 (“the 1989 Act”) in so far as it has effect in Scotland to make replacement provision for Scotland with regard to transplantation. As a consequence, the reference in section 4(4)(b) of the 2004 Act to offences committed under the 1989 Act is replaced with a reference to the 2006 Act.
