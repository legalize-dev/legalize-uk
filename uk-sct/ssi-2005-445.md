# The Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) Order 2005

Made: 12th September 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 13th September 2005

Coming into force: 5th October 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 330 of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 5th October 2005.

#### Modifications of enactments

###### 2

The modifications to the Subordinate Legislation listed in the Schedule to this Order shall have effect.

## SCHEDULE — MODIFICATION OF SUBORDINATE LEGISLATION
