# The Gaelic Language (Scotland) Act 2005 Commencement Order 2006

Made: 25th January 2006

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(2) of the Gaelic Language (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gaelic Language (Scotland) Act 2005 Commencement Order 2006.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Gaelic Language (Scotland) Act 2005 is 13th February 2006.

## Signed

PETER J PEACOCK — A member of the Scottish Executive — 25th January 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 13th February 2006 as the day on which the Gaelic Language (Scotland) Act 2005 will come into force.
