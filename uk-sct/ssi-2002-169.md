# The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Amendment (No. 2) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made: 28th March 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th April 2002

Coming into force: 29th March 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Amendment (No. 2) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 29th March 2002.

#### Amendment of the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2002

###### 2

Regulation 1(2) of the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2002[^f00002] is omitted.

## Signed

MARY MULLIGAN — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 28th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2002 (“the principal Regulations”).

They continue in force the provisions of the principal Regulations, which amend the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994, the Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995 and the Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations 1995, in order to continue to implement Commission Decision [2001/304/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0304) on marking and use of certain animal products in relation to Decision [2001/172/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0172) concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom, as amended by Commission Decision [2001/345/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0345) and Commission Decision [2002/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0049).
