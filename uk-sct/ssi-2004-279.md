# The Pig Carcase (Grading) Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2004

Made: 9th June 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th June 2004

Coming into force: 5th July 2004

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pig Carcase (Grading) Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 5th July 2004.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Pig Carcase (Grading) Regulations 1994

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) of the Pig Carcase (Grading) Regulations 1994 substitute for the definition of “the Commission Decision” the following–

> > “*the Commission Decision*” means Commission Decision [2004/370/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0370) authorising methods for grading pig carcases in the United Kingdom pursuant to Regulation [(EEC) 3220/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1984/3220) determining the Community scale for grading pig carcases ;

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Pig Carcase (Grading) Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2003 are hereby revoked.

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 9th June 2004
