# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 2) (Scotland) Partial Revocation (No. 5) Order 2000

Made: 29th November 2000

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th December 2000

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section1(1) and (2) and section 24(3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf,hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions)(Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 2) (Scotland) PartialRevocation (No. 5) Order 2000 and shall come into force at 1730 hours on29th November 2000.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Food Protection(Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 2)(Scotland) Order 2000[^f00002].

#### Partial Revocation of the principal Order

###### 3

The principal Order is revoked insofar as it designates–

- (a) any part of Areas A, B, C, D and E described in the Schedule to thisOrder being areas within Area 1 described in the Schedule to the principalOrder; and

- (b) any part of Area 2 described in the Schedule to the principal Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the fifth partial revocation of the Food Protection(Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 2)(Scotland) Order 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/291](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/291)) (“the principal Order”) whichprohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops within the areas designated in theSchedule to that Order (article 4 of the principal Order);

- (b) movement of such scallops out of these areas (article 5 of theprincipal Order); and

- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of,supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such scallops fromthe designated areas (article 6 of the principal Order).

The effect of this Order is to remove the prohibitions in respect ofthe areas of sea designated in the Schedule to this Order, which form partof the area of sea designated as Area 1 in the Schedule to the principalOrder, and to remove the prohibitions in respect of the area of seadesignated as Area 2 in the Schedule to the principal Order.
