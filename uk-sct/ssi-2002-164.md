# The Plant Health (Great Britain) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2002

Made: 27th March 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th March 2002

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Great Britain) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993

###### 2

The Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993[^f00002] is amended in accordance with articles 3 to 8 below.

> > - “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)” means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)[^f00003] of 8th May 2000 on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community as amended by Commission Directive [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033)[^f00004] and by Commission Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028)[^f00005];

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993 (“the principal Order”), in relation to Scotland, so as to implement certain elements of Commission Directives [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (O.J. No. L 77 20.3.02, p.23) and [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) (O.J. No. L 77, 20.3.02, p.26) which are not implemented in other legislation. Commission Directive [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) amends the protected zones established under Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (OJ No. L169, 10.7.00, p.1) (“the principal Plant Health Directive”) and scheduled in the Annex to Commission Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) (O.J. No. L 127, 9.5.01, p.38). Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) provides for consequential amendments to certain Annexes of the principal Plant Health Directive.

The amendments in this Order implementing Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) are at articles 4 to 7 inclusive. Directive [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) is implemented by article 8, which amends Schedule 8 to the principal Order. The principal changes to the protected zones are removal of Great Britain from the protected zone in respect of *Beet necrotic yellow vein virus* (known as rhizomania) (articles 4, (7)(a) and (c) and (8)), and re-definition of the Italian and Austrian protected zones in respect of the bacterium *Erwinia amylovora* (Burr.) Winsl. et al (known as fireblight) (articles 5, 6, 7(b) and 8). A small amendment is made to the description of *Citrus tristeza virus* in Schedule 8 (article 8(b)(ii)).

An additional amendment is made to the definition of Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) so as to incorporate amendments made to it by Commission Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (article 3).

Finally, the Order revokes a number of provisions in the Plant Health (Great Britain) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2001, which amended the principal Order and which amendments have been superceded (article 9).
