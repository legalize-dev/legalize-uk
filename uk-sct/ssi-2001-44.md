# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2001

Made: 19th February 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 21st February 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

#### Non-domestic rate for 2001-02

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 47.0 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2001-02.

## Signed

ANGUS MACKAY — A member of the Scottish Executive — 19th February 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a rate of 47.0 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2001-02.
