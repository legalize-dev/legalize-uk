# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2011

Made: 27th January 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th January 2011

Coming into force: 1st March 2011

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], section6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2011 and shall come into force on 1st March 2011.

- (2) A certified copy of this Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of fees

###### 2

- (1) Subject to subparagraph(2) below, for the Table of Fees in Schedule1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No. 2) 2002[^f00003] substitute the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt.

- (2) Subparagraph(1) shall apply to work carried out by a sheriff officer on or after 1stMarch 2011but shall not affect the fees payable as respects any work so carried out before that date.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Sederunt amends Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No.2) 2002, by substituting a new Table of Fees.

The fee levels in the new Table of Fees represent an increase of 3.6% on the existing fees. This increased takes effect from 1st March 2011.
