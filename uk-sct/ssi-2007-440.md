# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Court Ordinary Cause, Summary Application, Summary Cause and Small Claim Rules) Amendment (Council Regulation (EC) No. 1348 of 2000 Extension to Denmark) 2007

Made: 25th September 2007

Coming into force: 9th October 2007

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred by section 32 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971[^f00001], and, these rules making provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, and it appearing to the Lords of Council and Session that it is expedient for the reference to Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1348/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1348) of 29th May 2000 on service in the Member States of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil and commercial matters to be construed as a reference to that instrument as amended from time to time, under and by virtue of the powers conferred by paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], and under and by virtue of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, having approved draft rules submitted to them by the Sheriff Court Rules Council in accordance with section 34 of the said Act of 1971, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Court Ordinary Cause, Summary Application, Summary Cause and Small Claims Rules) Amendment (Council Regulation (EC) No. 1348 of 2000 Extension to Denmark) 2007 and shall come into force on 9th October 2007.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of the Sheriff Court Ordinary Cause Rules

###### 2

- (1) The Ordinary Cause Rules in Schedule 1 to the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraph.

- (2) For rule 5.5(8) (service on persons furth of Scotland)[^f00004] there shall be substituted the following:–

> > (8) In this rule “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1348/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1348) of 29 May 2000 on service in the Member States of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters as amended from time to time and as applied by the Agreement of 19 October 2005 between the European Community and the Kingdom of Denmark on the service of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters[^f00005];

#### Amendment of the Summary Application Rules

###### 3

- (1) The Act of Sederunt (Summary Applications, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules) 1999[^f00006] shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraph.

- (2) For rule 2.12(8) (service on persons furth of Scotland)[^f00007] there shall be substituted the following:–

> > (8) In this rule “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1348/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1348) of 29 May 2000 on service in the Member States of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters as amended from time to time and as applied by the Agreement of 19 October 2005 between the European Community and the Kingdom of Denmark on the service of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters;

#### Amendment of the Summary Cause Rules

###### 4

- (1) The Summary Cause Rules in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Summary Cause Rules) 2002[^f00008] shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraph.

- (2) For rule 5.7(14) (service on persons outwith Scotland)[^f00009] there shall be substituted the following:–

> > (14) In this rule “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1348/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1348) of 29 May 2000 on service in the Member States of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters as amended from time to time and as applied by the Agreement of 19 October 2005 between the European Community and the Kingdom of Denmark on the service of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters;

#### Amendment of the Small Claim Rules

###### 5

- (1) The Small Claim Rules in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Small Claim Rules) 2002[^f00010] shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraph.

- (2) For rule 6.5(15) (service on persons outwith Scotland)[^f00011] there shall be substituted the following:–

> > (15) In this rule “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1348/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1348) of 29 May 2000 on service in the Member States of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters as amended from time to time and as applied by the Agreement of 19 October 2005 between the European Community and the Kingdom of Denmark on the service of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters;

## Signed

A C HAMILTON — Lord President I.P.D. — 25th September 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt makes various amendments to the rules applicable in the sheriff court relating to the service of documents on persons outwith Scotland. Those amendments are consequential upon the extension of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1348/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1348) of 29th May 2000 on service in the Member States of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters to the Kingdom of Denmark by an Agreement between the European Community and the Kingdom of Denmark dated 19th October 2005 and coming into force on 1st July 2007.

In particular, amendments are made to each of the Ordinary Cause Rules, the Summary Application, Statutory Applications and Appeals etc. Rules, the Summary Cause Rules and the Small Claim Rules.
