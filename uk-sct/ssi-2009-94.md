# The Representation of the People (Absent Voting at Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2009

Made: 9th March 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th March 2009

Coming into force: 1st April 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 62(1)(a) of the Local Electoral Administration and Registration Services (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People (Absent Voting at Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2009 and comes into force on 1st April 2009.

#### Disclosure of personal identifiers record information

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 18A of the Representation of the People (Absent Voting at Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00002] (the personal identifiers record) is amended as follows.

- (2) After paragraph (1) insert–

> > (1A) The returning officer may disclose information held in the personal identifiers record to any candidate or agent attending proceedings on receipt of postal ballot papers in accordance with regulation 24B of the Representation of the People (Postal Voting for Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00003].

- (3) Omit paragraph (2).

## Signed

BRUCE CRAWFORD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 9th March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends regulation 18A of the Representation of the People (Absent Voting at Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Regulations 2007 to provide that information from the personal identifiers record may be disclosed to candidates and agents at proceedings involving receipt of postal ballot papers.

The Order has been made to address concerns about the ability of the returning officer to disclose this information.
