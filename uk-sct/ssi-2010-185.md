# The Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2010

Made: 10th May 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections40(1) and 43(3) and (4) of the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No.6) Order 2010.

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005; and

- “the 1978 Act” means the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00002].

#### Appointed days

###### 3

The days appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act are:—

- (a) 20thMay 2010 for the provisions of the Act specified in column1 of Schedule1 to this Order but, those provisions shall only come into force on that date for the purpose specified in column3 of that Schedule; and

- (b) 2ndJuly 2010 for the provisions of the Act specified;

- (i) in column1 of Schedule 2 to this Order, in so far as those provisions are not already in force, but, where a particular purpose is specified in column3 of that Schedule in relation to that provision, that provision shall only come into force for the purpose specified in that column; and

- (ii) in the Table of Repeals in Schedule 2.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force further provisions of the [Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/13) (“the Act”).

Article3(a) appoints 20thMay 2010 as the day for the coming into force of sections15 (arrangements for the provision of general dental services) and 17 (lists of persons undertaking to provide or approved to assist in the provision of general dental services) of the Act, but only for the purpose of making regulations under the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (“the 1978 Act”).

Article3(b) appoints 2nd July 2010 as the day for the coming into force of the remainder of sections15 and 17, and for bringing into force section26(3)(b) and schedule2, paragraphs2(3), (16) and 19(b), and 3(c) (minor and consequential amendments) to, the Act.

Article3(b) also appoints 2nd July 2010 as the day for the coming into force of section26(2)(c)(ii) and (e)(ii), 26(3)(c), sections42(1) and (2) (bringing into force provisions in schedule2) and schedule2, paragraph15 of the Act, but only for the purposes specified in column3 of Schedule2. The purposes specified in column3 of Schedule2 relate to the amendments made by section17 of the Act to section25(2) of the Act relating to the lists to be prepared and maintained by Health Boards of persons undertaking to provide or approved to assist in the provision of general dental services, or in the case of section42 to the commencement of the commenced paragraphs of schedule2 and 3 that are introduced by that section.

Article3(b) also brings into force on 2nd July 2010 schedule 3 of the Act but only for the purpose of the repeals specified in the table to Schedule 2.
