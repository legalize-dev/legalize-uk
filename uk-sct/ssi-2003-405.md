# The Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2003

Made: 2nd September 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 3rd September 2003

Coming into force: 30th September 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 30th September 2003.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Regulations 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Regulations 2003[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In regulation 2 in the definition of “Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042)” after “collagen” insert “as amended by Commission Decision [2003/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0503)[^f00003]”.

- (3) In regulation 5(1)(b)(iv) after “years” insert “from the date on which the record was made”.

- (4) For regulation 5(1)(d) substitute–

> > (d) records of the sources of all incoming raw materials and of the destination of all outgoing products in relation to the establishment are made and then kept for a period of at least two years from the date on which the record was made;

#### Amendment of the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996

###### 3

In paragraphs 9 and 12 of Schedule 3 to the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996[^f00004] after “Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) (O.J. No. L 13, 18.1.03, p.24)” insert “as amended by Commission Decision [2003/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0503) (O.J. No. L 170, 9.7.03, p.30)”.

## Signed

TOM McCABE — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 2nd September 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, amend the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Regulations 2003 (“the principal Regulations”). The principal Regulations gave effect to Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) (O.J. No. L 13, 18.1.03, p.24). That Decision was subsequently amended by Commission Decision [2003/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0503) (O.J. No. L 170, 9.7.03, p.30). That subsequent Decision delayed the date of application of Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) from 30th June 2003 to 30th September 2003.

The date the principal Regulations come into force was amended in anticipation of Commission Decision [2003/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0503) by the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/328](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/328)).

These regulations amend the definition of Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) in the principal Regulations to include a reference to Commission Decision [2003/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0503) (regulation 2(2)).

These regulations also clarify the period for which records must be kept under the principal Regulations (regulation 2(3) and (4)).

The provisions of Directive [92/118/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0118) (O.J. No. L 62, 15.3.93, p.49) relating to intra-community trade are implemented by the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/3124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3124)). These regulations amend the 1996 Regulations to give effect to the change made to Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) by Commission Decision [2003/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0503) (regulation 3).

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
