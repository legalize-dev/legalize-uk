# The Legal Aid and Advice and Assistance (Solicitors’ Travel Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2011

Made: 26th January 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th January 2011

Coming into force: 28th February 2011

The Scottish Ministers make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section33(2) and (3) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid and Advice and Assistance (Solicitors’ Travel Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2011 and come into force on 28th February 2011.

#### Application

###### 2

These Regulations apply only in respect of travel commenced on or after 28th February 2011.

#### Amendment of the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1989

###### 3

In the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1989[^f00002]—

- (a) in Schedule 3[^f00003], after paragraph 5 insert—

| 5A. | The fee for each quarter hour (or part thereof) spent travelling— |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | (a) by a solicitor | £5.45 |
|  | (b) by a solicitor’s clerk | £2.70 |

- (b) in Schedule 5[^f00004], after paragraph 5 insert—

| 5A. | The fee for each quarter hour (or part thereof) spent travelling— |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | (a) by a solicitor | £7.27 |
|  | (b) by a solicitor’s clerk | £3.59 |

- (c) in Schedule 6[^f00005], for “0.8 units” substitute “£7.27” in—

- (i) paragraph 6 of the notes on the operation of Chapter I; and

- (ii) paragraph 21 of the notes on the operation of Chapter II.

#### Amendment of the Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1989

###### 4

In Schedule 1 to the Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1989[^f00006], in Part 1 of the Table of Fees, after paragraph 5 insert—

| 5A. | The fee for each quarter of an hour spent travelling— |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | (a) by a solicitor | £5.28 | £6.34 |
|  | (b) by a solicitor’s clerk | £2.63 | £3.17 |

#### Amendment of the Legal Aid in Contempt of Court Proceedings (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1992

###### 5

In Schedule 1 to the Legal Aid in Contempt of Court Proceedings (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1992[^f00007], after paragraph 4 insert—

> > (4A) The fee for each quarter of an hour (or part thereof) spent travelling—
>   (a) by a solicitor is £5.28;
>   (b) by a solicitor’s clerk is £2.63.

|  | (b) by a solicitor’s clerk | £2.63 | £3.18 |

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 26th January 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the fees payable to solicitors, and their clerks, for travelling in connection with the provision of advice and assistance (including assistance by way of representation) and legal aid.
