# The Scottish Register of Tartans Fees Order 2009

Made: 13th January 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 14th January 2009

Coming into force: 5th February 2009

In accordance with section 14(3) of that Act the Scottish Ministers have consulted the Keeper of the Scottish Register of Tartans[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Register of Tartans Fees Order 2009 and comes into force on 5th February 2009.

#### Fees payable

###### 2

The fee payable to the Keeper of the Scottish Register of Tartans under a provision of the Scottish Register of Tartans Act 2008 specified in column 1 of the Schedule in respect of the matter specified in relation to it in column 2 of the Schedule shall be the fee specified in relation to it in column 3 of the Schedule.
