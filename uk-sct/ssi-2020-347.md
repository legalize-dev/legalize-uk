# The Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2020

Made: 1st November 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: at 9.00 a.m. on 2nd November 2020

Coming into force: at 6.00 a.m. on 2nd November 2020

In accordance with paragraph 6(2) and (3) of schedule 19 of the Act, the Scottish Ministers are of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by, a resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2020 and come into force at 6.00 a.m. on 2 November 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Regulations 2020

###### 2

In paragraph 1(2)(m) of schedule 3 of the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Regulations 2020[^f00002] (requirement to close certain premises in a Level 2 area to members of the public), omit “or amusement arcade”.

## Signed

BEN MACPHERSON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st November 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations (which come into force at 6 a.m. on 2 November 2020) amend the restrictions and requirements in the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Regulations 2020.

They amend the Level 2 restrictions provided for in those Regulations, to remove amusement arcades in a Level 2 area from the requirement to close to the public.
