# The Scottish Tribunals (Listed Tribunals) Regulations 2019

Made: 29th October 2019

Coming into force: 7th November 2019

In accordance with section 79(2)(b) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Scottish Tribunals (Listed Tribunals) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 7 November 2019.

#### Amendment of the Tribunals (Scotland) Act 2014

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1 of the Tribunals (Scotland) Act 2014 is amended as follows.

- (2) In Part 1, after paragraph 9 insert—

> > (9A) A Bus Lane Adjudicator

- (3) In Part 2—

- (a) in paragraph 13, after sub-paragraph (9), insert—

> > (9A) The entry in paragraph 9A relates to the functions exercisable by a bus lane adjudicator by virtue of Parts 4 and 5 of the Bus Lane Contraventions (Charges, Adjudication and Enforcement) (Scotland) Regulations 2011[^f00002].

- (b) in paragraph 13(9), omit “and 73(3)” and insert “, 73(3) and paragraph 5(2) of schedule 6”.

## Signed

ASH DENHAM — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 29th October 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations add a bus lane adjudicator to the tribunals listed in schedule 1 of the Tribunals (Scotland) Act 2014 whose functions may be transferred to the Scottish Tribunals under that Act. They also specify the functions exercisable by a bus lane adjudicator.

In addition, these Regulations amend the functions of a parking adjudicator.
