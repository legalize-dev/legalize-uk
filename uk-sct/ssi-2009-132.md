# The Local Government Finance Act 1992 (Scotland) Amendment Order 2009

Made: 11th March 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 11th March 2009

Coming into force: 26th March 2009

In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule they have consulted with such associations of local authorities as appear to them to be appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Finance Act 1992 (Scotland) Amendment Order 2009 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Amendment of the Local Government Finance Act 1992 (Scotland) Order 2009

###### 2

For Schedule 1 (revenue support grant and distribution of non-domestic rate income) to the Local Government Finance Act 1992 (Scotland) Order 2009[^f00002], substitute the Schedule contained in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Local Government Finance Act 1992 (Scotland) Order 2009 by replacing Schedule 1 to that Order with the Schedule contained in this Order. Only the figures contained in column 2 of the Schedule are affected. Column 2 lists the amount of revenue support grant which is payable to each local authority in Scotland in respect of the financial year 2009–2010.
