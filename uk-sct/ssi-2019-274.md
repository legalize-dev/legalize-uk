# The Town and Country Planning and Electricity Works (EU Exit) (Scotland) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Amendment Regulations 2019

Made: 2nd September 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th September 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(1) and (3) of schedule 2 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Town and Country Planning and Electricity Works (EU Exit) (Scotland) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Amendment Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

#### Amendment of the Town and Country Planning and Electricity Works (EU Exit) (Scotland) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2019

###### 2

- (1) The Town and Country Planning and Electricity Works (EU Exit) (Scotland) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2019[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 1 (citation and commencement), for “7 days after exit day” substitute “on the day after exit day”.

- (3) In regulation 5 (amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017)—

- (a) in paragraph (10)(c), in the new paragraph (6A)(e) for “after “Directive”” substitute “at the end”,

- (b) in paragraph (14)(a)(i) after “United Kingdom” insert “level”.

- (4) In regulation 6(11)(a)(i) (amendment of the Electricity Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017) after “United Kingdom” insert “level”.

## Signed

KEVIN STEWART — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 2nd September 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Town and Country Planning and Electricity Works (EU Exit) (Scotland) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2019 (“the 2019 Regulations”), and are made in exercise of the powers in paragraph 1(1) and (3) of schedule 2 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16).

Regulation 2(2) amends the coming into force date of the 2019 Regulations.

Regulation 2(3)(a) amends regulation 5(10)(c) of the 2019 Regulations, which amends regulation 60 (revocations and transitional provisions) of the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017 (“the 2017 Regulations”), to clarify where the amending text is to be inserted into the 2017 Regulations.

Regulations 2(3)(b) and 2(4) amend regulations 5(14)(a)(i) and 6(11)(a)(i), respectively, of the 2019 Regulations, to correct a missing word.

No Business and Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations, as no impact upon business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.
