# The Local Electoral Administration (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement) Order 2011

Made: 21st June 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 23rd June 2011

Coming into force: 29th June 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section21(1) of the Local Electoral Administration (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Electoral Administration (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement) Order 2011 and comes into force on 29th June 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

29th June 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Local Electoral Administration (Scotland) Act 2011—

- (a) Part 1 (Electoral Management Board for Scotland); and

- (b) Part 2 (Electoral Commission: Local Government Elections).

## Signed

AILEEN CAMPBELL — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 21st June 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Sections20, 21 and 22 of the Local Electoral Administration (Scotland) Act 2011 came into force on 21stApril 2011, being the day after the day on which the Bill received Royal Assent. This Order brings into force on 29th June 2011 all other provisions of the Act.
