# The Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Scotland) (No. 2) Amendment Regulations 2007

Made: 25th June 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 26th June 2007

Coming into force: 27th June 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

These Regulations–

- (a) may be cited as the Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Scotland) (No. 2) Amendment Regulations 2007;

- (b) come into force on 27th June 2007; and

- (c) extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendments to the Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2005[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)–

- (a) for the definitions of Community instruments appearing immediately after the definition of “cutting up” substitute–

> > - “Decision 2007/411”, “Directive 2004/41”, “Regulation 999/2001”, “Regulation 178/2002”, “Regulation 852/2004”, “Regulation 853/2004”, “Regulation 854/2004”, “Regulation 882/2004”, “Regulation 1688/2005”, “Regulation 2073/2005”, “Regulation 2074/2005”, Regulation 2075/2005”, “Regulation 2076/2005”, “Regulation 575/2006”, “Regulation 1664/2006”, “Regulation 1665/2006”, Regulation 1666/2006”, “Regulation 1791/2006” and “Regulation 1923/2006” have the meanings respectively given to them in the Schedule;

; and

- (b) in the combined definition of “material” and “product” for “Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598)” substitute “Decision 2007/411”.

- (3) For the Schedule (definitions of Community legislation) substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2005 by–

- (a) updating the definitions of certain Community instruments that are referred to in those Regulations *(regulation 2(2)(a), (3) and the Schedule)*; and

- (b) substituting a reference to a Commission Decision in those Regulations *(regulation 2(2(b))*.

The Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2005 gave effect in relation to Scotland to Article 1(1) of Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) prohibiting the placing on the market of products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1st August 1996 for any purpose and exempting such animals from certain control and eradication measures laid down in Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) (O.J. No. L 204, 5.8.2005, p.22). Article 1(1) of Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) provided that certain products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1st August 1996 could not be placed on the market.

Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) has now been repealed and replaced by Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0411) prohibiting the placing on the market of products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1st August 1996 for any purpose and exempting such animals from certain control and eradication measures laid down in Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) and repealing Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598).

Article 1(1) of Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0411) is in identical terms to Article 1(1) of Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) and the Bovine Products (Placing on the Market) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2005 as amended by these Regulations give effect in relation to Scotland to Article 1(1) of Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0411).

No full regulatory assessment has been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
