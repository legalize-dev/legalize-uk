# The Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2009

Made: 7th December 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section11(4) of the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No.2) Order 2009.

#### The appointed day for paragraph1 of the schedule

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of paragraph1 of the schedule to the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act2009 is 8th January 2010.

## Signed

NICOLA STURGEON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 7th December 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 8th January 2010 as the day on which paragraph1 of the schedule to the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009 (“the Act”) comes into force.

Paragraph 1 of the schedule to the Act amends paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 and adds such members of committees and sub-committees of Health Boards as may be prescribed to the categories of persons who the Scottish Ministers may pay remuneration to.
