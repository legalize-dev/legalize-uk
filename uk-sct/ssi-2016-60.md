# The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2016

Made: 28th January 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st February 2016

Coming into force: 31st August 2016

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 102(3) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2016 and comes into force on 31st August 2016.

#### Day appointed

###### 2

31st August 2016 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014—

- (a) so far as not already in force—

- (i) sections 19 to 45;

- (ii) section 68; and

- (iii) schedules 2 and 3[^f00002]; and

- (b) section 95.

## Signed

AILEEN CAMPBELL — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 28th January 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force provisions of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (“the 2014 Act”).

The Order commences all of the sections in Parts 4 and 5 of the 2014 Act (sections 19 to 45), so far as not already in force, with effect from 31st August 2016. Some of these sections were partially commenced by an earlier commencement order for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make orders under the sections.

The Order commences section 68 of the 2014 Act, so far as not already in force, with effect from 31st August 2016. Section 68 was partially commenced by an earlier commencement order for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make orders under that section.

The Order fully commences schedules 2 and 3 to the 2014 Act.

The Order also commences section 95 of the 2014 Act, for all purposes, with effect from 31st August 2016.
