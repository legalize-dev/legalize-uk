Made: 23rd February 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 24th February 2016

Coming into force: 18th April 2016

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Concession Contracts (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2016 and come into force on 18th April 2016.

#### Amendment of the Concession Contracts (Scotland) Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Concession Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016[^f00013] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “contracting body”, for “contracting body” substitute “contracting entity”;

- (b) after paragraph (3) insert—

> > (4) In these Regulations, references to the following Directives, Regulation and Commission Decisions are references to the Directives, Regulation and Commission Decisions as amended from time to time—
>   (a) [Directive 1999/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0093) of the European Parliament and of the Council on a Community framework for electronic signatures[^f00014];
>   (b) Regulation [(EC) 2195/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/2195) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the Common Procurement Vocabulary (CPV)[^f00015];
>   (c) [Directive 2002/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0021) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the common regulatory framework for electronic communication networks and services[^f00016];
>   (d) [Directive 2004/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0039) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning markets for financial instruments amending Council Directives [85/611/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0611) and [93/6/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0006) and [Directive 2000/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0012) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council [Directive 93/22/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0022)[^f00017];
>   (e) Commission [Decision 2009/767/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2009/0767) setting out measures facilitating the use of procedures by electronic means through the points of single contact under [Directive 2006/123/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0123) of the European Parliament and of the Council on services in the internal market (notified under document C(2009)7806)[^f00018];
>   (f) [Directive 2010/13/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0013) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the coordination of certain provisions laid down by law, regulation or administrative action in member States concerning the provision of audio-visual media services[^f00019];
>   (g) [Directive 2013/34/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2013/0034) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the annual financial statements, consolidated financial statement and related reports of certain types of undertaking[^f00020], amending [Directive 2006/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0043) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Directives [78/660/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0660) and [83/349/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0349);
>   (h) Commission [Decision 2011/130/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2011/0130) establishing minimum requirements for the cross-border processing of documents signed electronically by competent authorities under [Directive 2006/123/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0123) of the European Parliament and of the Council on services in the internal market (notified under document C(2011) 1081[^f00021];
>   (i) [Directive 2014/23/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0023) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the award of concession contracts[^f00022];
>   (j) [Directive 2014/24/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0024) of the European Parliament and of the Council on public procurement and repealing [Directive 2004/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0018)[^f00023]; and
>   (k) [Directive 2014/25/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0025) of the European Parliament and of the Council on procurement entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors and repealing [Directive 2004/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0017)[^f00024].

- (3) In regulation 32 (rules applicable to communication), in paragraph (18) delete “as amended from time to time,”.

- (4) In regulation 51 (duty owed to economic operators) in paragraph (5) for “GPS” substitute “GPA”.

- (5) In regulation 63 (transitional provision and saving where procurement was commenced before 18th April 2016)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “47(1)(a) to (3)” substitute “47(1)(a), (2) and (3)”;

- (b) in paragraph (2), for “service” substitute “services”.

- (6) In regulation 64 (transitory provision prior to full commencement of regulation 32(1) to (7)), in paragraph (2) for “2106” substitute “2016”.
