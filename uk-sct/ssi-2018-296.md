# The Renfrewshire Council (White Cart Water Navigation) Harbour Revision Order 2018

Made: 3rd October 2018

Coming into force: 4th October 2018

In accordance with paragraph 19(2) of that schedule[^f00007], Ministers have decided to make the Order applied for with modifications which do not appear to them substantially to affect the character of the Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Renfrewshire Council (White Cart Water Navigation) Harbour Revision Order 2018 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the applicant” means Renfrewshire Council;

- “the deposited plan” means the drawing numbered 117084-SWECO-SBR-C-DR-S-41002, entitled “The Renfrewshire Council (White Cart Water Navigation) Harbour Revision Order 2018”, signed with reference to this Order, of which one is deposited with the Scottish Ministers at the Scottish Government, Victoria Quay, Edinburgh, EH6 6QQ and the other deposited at the offices of the Chief Executive, Renfrewshire Council, Renfrewshire House, Cotton Street, Paisley, PA1 1WB; and

- “the works” means the bridge over the navigable waters of the White Cart Water commencing at a point on the south bank of the White Cart Water at Wright Street in Renfrew heading in a generally north-westerly direction to a point on the north bank of the White Cart Water connecting with a new road to be constructed to provide a link to Arran Avenue in Renfrew.

#### Rights of navigation to be interfered with

###### 3

- (1) After the date on which this Order comes into force the applicant and any person authorised by the applicant may extinguish or interfere with the enjoyment of any right of navigation to the extent only that such extinguishment or interference is necessary for the purposes of constructing, using and maintaining the works shown indicatively on the deposited plan.

- (2) After the date on which construction of the works is completed any right of navigation on the affected area shall be enjoyed only by persons and vessels capable of passing safely beneath, and without interference with, the works shown indicatively on the deposited plan.

#### Revocation

###### 4

On the day on which this Order comes into force the Renfrewshire Council (Cart Navigation) Harbour Revision Order 2007[^f00008] shall be revoked.

## Signed

F PACITTI — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 3rd October 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order allows for the interference with, or extinguishment of, public rights of navigation on part of the White Cart Water within the local government area of Renfrewshire for the purposes of constructing, using and maintaining a bridge to be constructed over that part of the river.

This Order revokes The Renfrewshire Council (White Cart Water Navigation) Harbour Revision Order 2007.
