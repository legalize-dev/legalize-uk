# The Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Authorities) Amendment Order 2015

Made: 16th September 2015

Coming into force: 17th September 2015

In accordance with section 2(8) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Authorities) Amendment Order 2015 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Amendments to the schedule to the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011

###### 2

In the schedule to the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (authorities to which Part 1 of that Act applies)—

- (a) under the heading “Others”, in the appropriate place according to alphabetical order, add the following entries—

> > Criminal Courts Rules Council

> > Panel appointed under regulation 90C of the Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Regulations 2012[^f00003]

> > Scottish Civil Justice Council

> > Scottish Fire and Rescue Service

> > Scottish Sentencing Council

; and

> > TS Prestwick Holdco Ltd (registered number SC462050)

- (b) remove the following entries—

> > Children’s Panel

> > Learning and Teaching Scotland

; and

> > Scottish Crime and Drug Enforcement Agency

- (c) For the entry—

- (i) “Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Constabulary and Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Constabulary (appointed under section 33 of the Police (Scotland) Act [1967 (c.77)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/77))” substitute “Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Constabulary and Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Constabulary (appointed under section 71 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act [2012 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2012/8))”;

- (ii) “Scottish Court Service” substitute “Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service”; and

- (iii) “Chief constables of police forces in Scotland” substitute “Chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland”.

## Signed

FIONA HYSLOP — A member of the Scottish Government — 16th September 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the schedule to the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”) which lists the bodies, office-holders and other persons that are subject to Part 1 of the Act. Part 1 of the Act makes provision for records management plans.
