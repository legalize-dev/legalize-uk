# The Cost of Living (Tenant Protection) (Scotland) Act 2022 (Amendment of Expiry Date) Regulations 2023

Made: 26th September 2023

Coming into force: 27th September 2023

In accordance with section 7(6) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers have laid before the Scottish Parliament a statement of reasons as to why this instrument should be made.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Cost of Living (Tenant Protection) (Scotland) Act 2022 (Amendment of Expiry Date) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendment of expiry date of Part 1 of the Cost of Living (Tenant Protection) (Scotland) Act 2022

###### 2

- (1) The Cost of Living (Tenant Protection) (Scotland) Act 2022 is amended as follows.

- (2) In section 7(1) (expiry of Part 1) for “30 September 2023”[^f00002] substitute “31 March 2024”.

## Signed

PATRICK HARVIE — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 26th September 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Cost of Living (Tenant Protection) (Scotland) Act 2022 (“*the 2022 Act*”) so as to change the expiry date of Part 1 of that Act. Part 1 was originally scheduled to expire on 31 March 2023. Regulation 2(2) of the Cost of Living (Tenant Protection) (Scotland) Act 2022 (Amendment of Expiry Dates and Rent Cap Modification) Regulations 2023 ([S.S.I. 2023/82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2023/82)) extended the expiry date to 30 September 2023. Regulation 2 of these Regulations amends section 7(1) of the 2022 Act so that Part 1 will instead expire on 31 March 2024.
