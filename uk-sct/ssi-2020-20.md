# The Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 10) Regulations 2020

Made: 5th February 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 7th February 2020

Coming into force: 26th April 2020

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 130(2) of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 10) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 26 April 2020.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 specified in column 1 of the table in the schedule (the subject-matter of which is specified in column 2 of that table) is 26 April 2020.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force certain provisions of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 (“the 2016 Act”).

Regulation 2 appoints 26 April 2020 for the coming into force of the provisions specified in column 1 of the schedule of these Regulations.

The Bill for the 2016 Act received Royal Assent on 22 April 2016. Sections 125 to 127 and 131 came into force the following day.
