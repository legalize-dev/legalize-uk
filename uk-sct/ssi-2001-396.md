# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property etc.) Order 2001

Made: 29th October 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th October 2001

Coming into force: 20th November 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 85(1) and 109(2) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property etc.) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 20th November 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “Scottish Homes” means the body corporate established by section 1 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988[^f00002].

#### Transfer of property and liabilities

###### 3

All rights and interests of Scottish Homes in, and all liabilities of Scottish Homes in relation to, the property specified in the Schedule to this Order and the contents thereof, shall hereby transfer to and vest in the Scottish Ministers.

#### Consequential and supplementary provision

###### 4

- (1) Nothing in this Order shall have the effect of completing the title of the Scottish Ministers to any land transferred by this Order, or to any other right or interest transferred by this Order title to which requires to be completed by registration for the purpose of making it a real right.

- (2) Any property, in respect of which but for this Order–

- (a) delivery or possession; or

- (b) intimation of its assignation,

would be required in order to complete title to it, shall transfer to and vest in the Scottish Ministers by virtue of this Order as if at the date on which the transfer of the property by this Order takes effect the Scottish Ministers had taken delivery or possession of the property or had made intimation of its assignation to them, as the case may be.

#### Transitional provision and savings

###### 5

Without prejudice to article 3 above–

- (a) the transfer by this Order of any property, right, interest or liability of Scottish Homes shall not affect the validity of anything done (or having effect as if done) by or in relation to Scottish Homes before the date on which the transfer takes effect;

- (b) anything (including legal proceedings) which, at the time when the transfer takes effect, is in the process of being done by or in relation to Scottish Homes may, so far as it relates to any property, right, interest or liability transferred by this Order, be continued by or in relation to the Scottish Ministers;

- (c) anything done (or having effect as if done) by or in relation to Scottish Homes in connection with any property, right, interest or liability transferred to the Scottish Ministers by this Order shall, if in force at the time when the transfer takes effect, have effect as if done by or in relation to the Scottish Ministers in so far as that is required for continuing its effect after that time.
