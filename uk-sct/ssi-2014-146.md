# The Assigned Colleges (University of the Highlands and Islands) Order 2014

Made: 28th May 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 30th May 2014

Coming into force: 1st August 2014

In accordance with section 7C(6) of that Act, they have consulted with the University of the Highlands and Islands, the governing bodies of the colleges to which this Order relates, the representatives of any trade union which is recognised by the colleges or which otherwise appears to the Scottish Ministers to be representative of the colleges’ staff, the colleges’ students’ associations, the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council, the local authorities for the areas in which the colleges are situated, and other persons appearing to them as likely to be affected by the Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Assigned Colleges (University of the Highlands and Islands) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st August 2014.

#### Assigned Colleges

###### 2

- (1) The following colleges of further education[^f00002] are assigned to the University of the Highlands and Islands[^f00003]—

- The Board of Management of Inverness College[^f00004];

- The Board of Management of Lews Castle College[^f00005];

- The Board of Management of Moray College[^f00006];

- The Board of Management of The North Highland College[^f00007];

- The governing body of Orkney College[^f00008];

- The Board of Management of Perth College[^f00009];

- The governing body of Shetland College[^f00010];

- West Highland College UHI[^f00011].

- (2) A college of further education assigned under paragraph (1) remains assigned to the University of the Highlands and Islands notwithstanding any change of name of the college of further education.

## Signed

MICHAEL RUSSELL — A member of the Scottish Government — 28th May 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order assigns each of the eight colleges of further education listed in article 2(1) to the University of the Highlands and Islands which is a regional strategic body under section 7B(1) of, and Part 2 of schedule 2A to, the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005. Article 2(2) provides that each college of further education remains assigned notwithstanding any change of name of the college of further education.
