# The Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2009

Made: 24th February 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 25th February 2009

Coming into force: 1st April 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 1st April 2009.

- (2) This Order applies to offences in respect of which the procurator fiscal receives the relevant police report on or after 1st April 2009.

- (3) In paragraph (2) “relevant police report” means the report made for the purposes of deciding whether to bring proceedings in respect of an offence.

#### Revocation

###### 2

This Order revokes the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2009[^f00002].

#### Prescribed Offences

###### 3

An offence specified in the Schedule to this Order is prescribed for the purposes of section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00003].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the offences for which a victim will be afforded an opportunity to make a victim statement for the purposes of section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003.

The effect of the Order is that a victim statement may be made where proceedings are to be taken or are likely to be taken in respect of one or more of the prescribed offences. The order only applies to offences reported to the procurator fiscal on or after the 1st April 2009.

The revocation is to correct an error in the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2009.
