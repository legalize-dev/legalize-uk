# The Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2013

Made: 6th February 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 7th February 2013

Coming into force: 31st March 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 153 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2013 and come into force on 31st March 2013.

#### Amendment of the Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2012

###### 2

In regulation 6(2) of the Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2012[^f00002] for “paragraph (2)” substitute “paragraph (1)”.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Government — 6th February 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2012 to correct an error in regulation 6 of that instrument, which revokes the Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) Regulations 2012 ([S.S.I.2012/28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2012/28).
