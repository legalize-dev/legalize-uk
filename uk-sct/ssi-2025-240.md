# The Offshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Order 2025

Made: 28th August 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st September 2025

Coming into force: 16th October 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 5(1) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Offshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Order 2025 and comes into force on 16 October 2025.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “*beam trawl*” means a trawl which has its mouth extended by a beam, bar or other rigid device and is designed and rigged to operate on or near the seabed,

- “*bottom trawl*” means a trawl designed and rigged to operate on or near the seabed, other than a beam trawl,

- “*co-ordinate*” means a co-ordinate of latitude and longitude according to the World Geodetic System 1984[^f00002],

- “*creel, pot, or trap*” means a basket, small cage, pot, receptacle or container with one or more openings or entrances which may be baited and placed either on the seabed or suspended above it for the purpose of catching sea fish,

- “*demersal mobile gear*” means— a demersal seine net, a demersal trawl, a dredge,

- “*demersal seine net*” means an encircling net, attached to ropes, which is actively moved in the water by one or more fishing vessels,

- “*demersal static gear*” means any gear or catch operation that does not require an active movement, that makes or has the potential to make contact with the sea bed, and includes— a creel, pot, or trap, a floated demersal longline, a static net,

- “*demersal trawl*” means a beam trawl or a bottom trawl,

- “*dredge*” means— a mechanical dredge, a suction dredge which raises material and sea fish from the seabed by the use of a solids pump, air lift or water jets which dig into the seabed,

- “*entangler net*” means a static net consisting of a wall of netting rigged so that the netting is hung onto ropes to create a greater amount of slack netting than a gillnet,

- “*gillnet*” means a static net made up of a single piece of net and held vertically in the water by floats and weights,

- “*static net*” means any set net and includes— an entangler net, a gillnet, a trammel net,

- “*trammel net*” means a static net made up of several layers of netting with two outer layers of a large mesh size and a sheet of small mesh sandwiched between them,

- “*trawl*” means a net closed at the back by a bag or codend which is actively moved in the water by one or more fishing vessels.

#### Requirement to stow fishing gear

###### 22

- (1) While a fishing vessel is within a protected area the master of the vessel must ensure that any fishing gear carried on board the vessel is properly stowed in such a way that it may not be readily used.

- (2) In this article—

- (a) “*protected area*” means an area described in any of the schedules,

- (b) “*fishing gear*” means, in relation to a protected area, the fishing gear of the description specified in the article relating to that area.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prohibits specified methods of fishing within specified areas.

Article 3 and schedule 1 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in the Braemar Pockmarks Special Area of Conservation.

Article 4 and schedule 2 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in two areas in the Central Fladen Marine Protected Area.

Article 5 and schedule 3 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in the Darwin Mounds Special Area of Conservation.

Article 6 and schedule 4 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in two areas of the Scottish zone in East of Gannet and Montrose Fields Marine Protected Area.

Article 7 and schedule 5 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in six areas of East Rockall Bank Special Area of Conservation.

Article 8 and schedule 6 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in two areas of the Scottish zone in the Faroe-Shetland Sponge Belt Marine Protected Area.

Article 9 and schedule 7 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in three areas of the Scottish zone in the Firth of Forth Banks Complex Marine Protected Area.

Article 10 and schedule 8 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in three areas of the Scottish zone in the Geike Slide and Hebridean Slope Marine Protected Area.

Article 11 and schedule 9 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in two areas of the Scottish zone in the North-east Faro-Shetland Channel Marine Protected Area.

Article 12 and schedule 10 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in an area of the Scottish zone in the North West Rockall Bank Special Area of Conservation.

Article 13 and schedule 11 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in two areas of the Scottish zone in the Norwegian Boundary Sediment Plain Marine Protected Area.

Article 14 and schedule 12 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in an area of the Scottish zone in the Pobie Bank Reef Special Area of Conservation.

Article 15 and schedule 13 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in the Scanner Pockmarks Special Area of Conservation.

Article 16 and schedule 14 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in five areas of the Scottish zone in Solan Bank Reef Special Area of Conservation. An exception to the prohibition applies in relation to the Solan Bank Reef Fifth Protected Area to allow sea fishing with a demersal trawl or a demersal seine net within a specified period.

Article 17 and schedule 15 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in three areas of the Scottish zone in the Stanton Banks Special Area of Conservation.

Article 18 and schedule 16 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in two areas of the Scottish zone in The Barra Fan and Hebrides Terrace Seamount Marine Protected Area.

Article 19 and schedule 17 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in the West of Scotland Marine Protected Area.

Article 20(1) and schedule 18 prohibit fishing for sea fish with a beam trawl or dredge throughout the whole of the West Shetland Shelf MPA. Additionally, article 20(2) and schedule 18 prohibit fishing for sea fish with a bottom trawl or demersal seine net within two smaller areas in that MPA.

Article 21 and schedule 19 prohibit fishing for sea fish with specified fishing gear in two areas of the Scottish zone within the Wyville Thomson Ridge Special Area of Conservation.

Article 22 regulates the storage of fishing gear on fishing vessels within each of the protected areas.

It is an offence under section 5(1) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 for any person to contravene a prohibition imposed by this Order. The maximum penalties are specified by section 11 of that Act. On summary conviction, the maximum penalty is a fine not exceeding £50,000. On conviction on indictment, the maximum penalty is an unlimited fine.
