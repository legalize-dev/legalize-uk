# The Budget (Scotland) Act 2010 Amendment Order 2010

Made: 30th June 2010

Coming into force: 1st July 2010

In accordance with section7(2) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Budget (Scotland) Act 2010 Amendment Order 2010 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) In this Order, “the 2010 Act” means the Budget (Scotland) Act 2010.

#### Amendment of section 3 of the 2010 Act

###### 2

- (1) Section3 of the 2010 Act (overall cash authorisations) is amended in accordance with paragraph(2).

- (2) In paragraph(a) (in relation to the Scottish Administration), for “£30,999,875,000” substitute “£31,022,875,000”.

#### Amendment of schedule 1 to the 2010 Act

###### 3

- (1) Schedule1 to the 2010 Act (the Scottish Administration) is amended in accordance with paragraph(2).

- (2) In column 2 (amount of resources other than accruing resources)—

- (a) in relation to the first purpose, for “£267,194,000” substitute “£266,908,000”;

- (b) in relation to the second purpose, for “£3,198,354,000” substitute “£2,472,968,000”;

- (c) in relation to the third purpose, for “£12,293,344,000” substitute “£12,179,042,000”;

- (d) in relation to the fourth purpose, for “£2,875,489,000” substitute “£2,806,071,000”;

- (e) in relation to the fifth purpose, for “£1,948,118,000” substitute “£1,931,429,000”;

- (f) in relation to the sixth purpose, for “£516,803,000” substitute “£511,056,000”;

- (g) in relation to the seventh purpose, for “£264,371,000” substitute “£263,371,000”;

- (h) in relation to the eighth purpose, for “£119,590,000” substitute “£118,905,000”;

- (i) in relation to the tenth purpose, for “£22,406,000” substitute “£22,045,000”;

- (j) in relation to the eleventh purpose, for “£10,496,000” substitute “£10,126,000”;

- (k) in relation to the fourteenth purpose, for “£98,235,000” substitute “£85,942,000”; and

- (l) in relation to the total amounts of resources, for “£34,658,752,000” substitute “£33,712,215,000”.

#### Amendment of schedule 2 to the 2010 Act

###### 4

- (1) Schedule2 to the 2010 Act (direct-funded bodies) is amended in accordance with paragraph(2).

- (2) In column2 (amount of resources other than accruing resources)—

- (a) in relation to the first purpose, for “£96,400,000” substitute “£80,000,000”;

- (b) in relation to the third purpose, for “£102,050,000” substitute “£91,304,000”; and

- (c) in relation to the fourth purpose, for “£7,384,000” substitute “£7,349,000”.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Executive — 30th June 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Budget (Scotland) Act 2010 (“the 2010 Act”).

This Order amends the 2010 Act so as to—

- (a) alter the overall cash authorisations in relation to the Scottish Administration in section3 of the 2010 Act (article2);

- (b) alter the amount of resources other than accruing resources that may be used for certain of the purposes set out in schedule1 to the 2010 Act and alter the total of amounts of resources for all of the purposes (article3(2));

- (c) alter the amount of resources other than accruing resources of direct-funded bodies in schedule2 to the 2010 Act in relation to the first, third and fourth purposes (article4(2)).
