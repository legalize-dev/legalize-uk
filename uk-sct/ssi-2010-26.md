# The Food Enzymes (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made: 3rd February 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th February 2010

Coming into force: 12th February 2010

There has been consultation as required by Article9 of Regulation (EC) No.178/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Food Enzymes (Scotland) Amendment Regulations2010 and come into force on 12th February 2010.

#### Amendment of the Food Enzymes (Scotland) Regulations2009

###### 2

- (1) The Food Enzymes (Scotland) Regulations2009[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) For regulation 8 substitute—

> > (8) In the Schedule to the Caseins and Caseinates (Scotland) Regulations 1986[^f00005], in column 2 of Part II (technological adjuvants and bacterial cultures) after “rennet” and “other milk-coagulating enzymes” in each case add “meeting the requirements of Regulation [(EC) No. 1332/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1332)[^f00006] of the European Parliament and of the Council on food enzymes”.

## Signed

SHONA ROBISON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 3rd February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations correct an error in the Food Enzymes (Scotland) Regulations2009. They amend the Caseins and Caseinates (Scotland) Regulations 1986 and delete from the 2009 Regulations the provision which purported to amend the Caseins and Caseinates Regulations 1985 ([S.I. 1985/2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/2026)). Those 1985 Regulations do not in fact have any application in Scotland.

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
