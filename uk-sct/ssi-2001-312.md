# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Revocation Order 2001

Made: 10th September 2001

Coming into force at 6.00 p.m. on: 10th September 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 17(1) of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease Order 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Revocation Order 2001 and shall come into force at 6.00 p.m. on 10th September 2001.

#### Revocations

###### 2

The Orders referred to in the Schedule hereto are revoked to the extent specified in that Schedule.

(This note is not part of the Order)
