# The Non Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2006

Made: 8th March 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 9th March 2006

Coming into force: 1st April 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(3)(c) of Schedule 2 to the Local Government and Rating Act 1997[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

#### Amendment of the Non Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Order 2005

###### 2

The Non Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Order 2005[^f00002] is amended by substituting for Schedule 1 (designated rural areas) the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Non Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Order 2005 (“the 2005 Order”) by substituting a new Schedule which adds or removes certain localities from column 2 of Schedule 1 to the 2005 Order. This is because the population of those localities has either fallen below or risen above 3,000. The effect of this is that these localities are now to be considered as falling within or outwith the local authority areas designated as rural areas for the purpose of paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Local Government and Rating Act 1997 by article 2 of the 2005 Order.
