# The Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Revocation Order 2019

Made: 27th November 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 2nd December 2019

Coming into force: 27th January 2020

In accordance with section 14(4) of the 2001 Act, the Scottish Ministers have laid before the Scottish Parliament a statement summarising all the observations to which they had to have regard under section 14(3) of the 2001 Act in relation to the Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Order 2019[^f00002], and have specified that they consider it appropriate to revoke that Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Revocation Order 2019 and comes into force on 27 January 2020.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Order 2019 is revoked.

## Signed

MICHAEL RUSSELL — A member of the Scottish Government — 27th November 2019
