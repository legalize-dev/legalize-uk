# The Historic Environment Scotland Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2015

Made: 13th May 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 15th May 2015

Coming into force: 1st October 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 31(2) of the Historic Environment Scotland Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Historic Environment Scotland Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st October 2015.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions, to the extent not already in force, of the Historic Environment Scotland Act 2014 specified in column 1 of the Schedule (the subject matter of which is described in column 2 of the Schedule) is 1st October 2015.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st October 2015 for all purposes sections 1 to 8, 10, 11, 15 to 18, 20 to 24 and 28 and schedules 1 to 4 and 6 of the Historic Environment Scotland Act 2014 (“the Act”). The Act is now fully in force.
