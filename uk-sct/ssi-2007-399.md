# The Water Industry Commissioner for Scotland (Dissolution) Order 2007

Made: 3rd September 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 4th September 2007

Coming into force: 1st October 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 2 of the Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Water Industry Commissioner for Scotland (Dissolution) Order 2007 and comes into force on 1st October 2007.

#### Appointed date

###### 2

The appointed date for the dissolution of the office of Water Industry Commissioner for Scotland[^f00002] is 1st October 2007.

## Signed

STEWART STEVENSON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 3rd September 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st October 2007 as the date for the dissolution of the office of the Water Industry Commissioner for Scotland (“the Commissioner”). The functions of the Commissioner are now carried out by the Water Industry Commission for Scotland, a body corporate established under section 1 of the [Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/3).

## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [2005 asp 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/3).

[^f00002]: The Water Industry Commissioner for Scotland was established under section 67A(1) of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act [1994 (c. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/39) (as inserted by section 12 of the Water Industry Act [1999 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/9)) and continued in existence by virtue of section 1(1) of the [Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2002/3).
