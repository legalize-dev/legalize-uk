# The Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2023

Made: 14th March 2023

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 16th March 2023

Coming into force: 31st March 2023

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 102(2) of the Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 31 March 2023.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 92 (heat networks supply targets) of the Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021 is 31 March 2023.

## Signed

PATRICK HARVIE — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 14th March 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring section 92 of the Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021 (“*the Act*”) into force on 31 March 2023.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 30 March 2021. Sections 97 to 103 came into force on the following day.
