# The Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2022

Made: 1st February 2022

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 3rd February 2022

Coming into force: 1st April 2022

In accordance with section 6A(1D) of the Valuation and Rating (Scotland) Act 1956[^f00002] they have consulted such associations of local authorities and such other persons as they think appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2022 and comes into force on 1 April 2022.

#### Amendment of the Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Order 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Order 2005[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 4(3) (gas)[^f00004] for “Scotland Gas Networks Limited” substitute “Scotland Gas Networks plc”.

- (3) In the table forming part of article 7A(2) (fixed line telecommunications)[^f00005]—

- (a) omit the entry in the first column (Company Name) for “Centurylink Communications UK Limited” and the corresponding registration number in the second column (Registration Number) “02495998”,

- (b) omit the entry in the first column for “SSE Telecommunications Limited” and the corresponding registration number in the second column “SC213457”,

- (c) after the entry for “JISC Services Limited” insert in the first column “Lumen Technologies UK Limited” with the respective corresponding registration number in the second column “02495998”, then insert in the first column “NEOS Networks Limited” with the respective corresponding registration number in the second column “SC213457”,

- (d) in the entry for “NEOS Networks Limited” with the corresponding registration number “03477297”, in the first column, for “NEOS Networks Limited” substitute “NNXYZ Limited”.

## Signed

TOM ARTHUR — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st February 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Order 2005 (“*the principal Order*”).

Article 2(2) updates a reference to a company name in article 4 of the principal Order, relating to gas.

Article 2(3) updates references in a table in article 7A of the principal Order to companies regarded as “fixed line operators”.

The effect of being a company referred to in article 4 or article 7A is that lands and heritages occupied by the company are entered as a single entry in a single valuation roll, for the area specified in the Order, rather than being entered in different valuation rolls held by the valuation authorities in whose areas they are actually located.
