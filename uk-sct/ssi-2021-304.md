# The UK Withdrawal from the European Union (Continuity) (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2021

Made: 6th September 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th September 2021

Coming into force: 1st October 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 51(2) of the UK Withdrawal from the European Union (Continuity) (Scotland) Act 2021[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the UK Withdrawal from the European Union (Continuity) (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 1 October 2021.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1 October 2021 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions in the UK Withdrawal from the European Union (Continuity) (Scotland) Act 2021—

- (a) sections 19 to 46 (environmental governance),

- (b) schedule 1 (Environmental Standards Scotland),

- (c) schedule 2 (Environmental Standards Scotland: strategy).

## Signed

MICHAEL MATHESON — A member of the Scottish Government — 6th September 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are the second set of commencement regulations made by the Scottish Ministers under section 51 of the UK Withdrawal from the European Union (Continuity) (Scotland) Act 2021 (“the Act”) and bring into force certain provisions of the Act on 1 October 2021.

Regulation 2 brings Chapter 2 of Part 2 and schedules 1 and 2 of the Act into force.

Chapter 2 of Part 2 and schedules 1 and 2 of the Act provide for a new system of environmental governance for Scotland and establishes a new Scottish public body, Environmental Standards Scotland, and gives it a range of functions and powers.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 29 January 2021. In accordance with section 51(1) of the Act, sections 48 to 53 came into force the following day.
