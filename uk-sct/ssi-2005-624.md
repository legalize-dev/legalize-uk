# The Less Favoured Area Support Scheme (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2005

Made: 1st December 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 5th December 2005

Coming into force: 12th January 2006

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Less Favoured Area Support Scheme (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 12th January 2006.

- (2) These Regulations apply to holdings in respect of which the Scottish Ministers are the competent authority, in accordance with the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment and Support Schemes (Integrated Administration and Control System) Regulations 2005 .

#### Amendment of the Less Favoured Area Support Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2005

###### 2

The Less Favoured Area Support Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2005 are amended in accordance with the following regulations.

#### Amendment to Schedule 1

###### 3

In Schedule 1 (livestock units and stocking density), for the table in Part II (calculation of stocking density) substitute–

| Total LFASS Livestock Units (A + B + C + D + E + F + G + H + I) | = | _J_LU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible Land |  | _K_Ha |
| Stocking Density (to two decimal places) |  | J divided by K |

#### Amendment to Schedule 2

###### 4

In Part I (environmental legislation) of Schedule 2 (usual good farming practices), after paragraph 12 insert–

> > (13) The Ancient Monuments (Class Consents) (Scotland) Order 1996 .
> (14) The Forestry Act 1967 .
> (15) The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000 .

#### Amendment to Schedule 7

###### 5

In Schedule 7 (rates of payment for less favoured area support), after Part I (more disadvantaged land) insert:

| *FRAGILITY CATEGORY* | *RATE* |
| --- | --- |
| Very Fragile (Islands) | £41.50 |
| Fragile (Mainland) | £39.50 |
| Standard | £33.50 |

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 1st December 2005
