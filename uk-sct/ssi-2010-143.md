# The Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010

Made: 8th April 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 12th April 2010

Coming into force: 16th August 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section23(2)(c) of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010 and shall come into force on 16thAugust 2010.

#### Amendment of paragraph 2 of the Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies) (Scotland) Order 2005

###### 2

For paragraph2(c) of the Additional Support for Learning (Appropriate Agencies) (Scotland) Order 2005[^f00002] (appropriate agencies) substitute—

> > (c) The Skills Development Scotland Co. Limited

## Signed

ADAM INGRAM — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 8th April 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies that The Skills Development (Scotland) Co. Limited is an “appropriate agency” for the purpose of section23 of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004 and removes that specification from Scottish Enterprise and Highlands and Islands Enterprise, which no longer carry out relevant functions.
