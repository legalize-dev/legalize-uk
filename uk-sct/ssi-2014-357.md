# The Conservation of Salmon (Annual Close Time and Catch and Release) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2014

Made: 10th December 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 12th December 2014

Coming into force: 9th January 2015

In accordance with paragraphs 10, 11, 12 and 14(1) of schedule 1 to that Act they have consulted such persons as they considered appropriate, directed that notice be given of the general effect of these Regulations and considered representations and objections made.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Conservation of Salmon (Annual Close Time and Catch and Release) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2014 and come into force on 9th January 2015.

#### Amendment to the Conservation of Salmon (Annual Close Time and Catch and Release) (Scotland) Regulations 2014

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Conservation of Salmon (Annual Close Time and Catch and Release) (Scotland) Regulations 2014[^f00002], in the entry for the Urr Salmon Fishery District (numbered 50), in column 3, for “[31st October] [29th November]” substitute “30th November”.

## Signed

RICHARD LOCHHEAD — A member of the Scottish Government — 10th December 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Conservation of Salmon (Annual Close Time and Catch and Release) (Scotland) Regulations 2014 to correct a typographical error in entry number 50 (Urr Salmon Fishery District) of the Schedule to those Regulations.
