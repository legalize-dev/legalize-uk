# The Non-Domestic Rates (Relief for New and Improved Properties) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2019

Made: 22nd March 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 22nd March 2019

Coming into force: 31st March 2019

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 153 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rates (Relief for New and Improved Properties) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2019 and come into force on 31 March 2019.

#### Amendment of the Non-Domestic Rates (Relief for New and Improved Properties) (Scotland) Regulations 2019

###### 2

- (1) The Non-Domestic Rates (Relief for New and Improved Properties) (Scotland) Regulations 2019[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) For the formula in regulation 10(5)(b) substitute the formula—

> > $BL×1.527×ARV 366×RV$

## Signed

KATE FORBES — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 2019-03-22

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Non-Domestic Rates (Relief for New and Improved Properties) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

These Regulations replace the formula at regulation 10(5)(b) in those Regulations, so that when they come into force on 1 April 2019, the formula is as it should be.
