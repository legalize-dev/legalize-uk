# The Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

Made: 17th January 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(3) and (4) of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 is 9th February 2005:–

- (a) Part 1 (access rights) so far as not already in force; and

- (b) section 99 (amendments, repeals and savings) and schedule 2, so far as not already in force.

## Signed

LEWIS MACDONALD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 17th January 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 9th February 2005 as the date for the coming into force of the remaining provisions of Part 1 of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 (“the Act”), and the consequential amendments in schedule 2 to the Act which relate to that Part. This will bring fully into force the provisions of the Act on access rights.
