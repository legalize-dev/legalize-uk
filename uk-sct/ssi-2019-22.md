# Act of Sederunt (Computer Evidence in the Sheriff Court) (Revocation) 2019

Made: 30th January 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st February 2019

Coming into force: 5th March 2019

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section104(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

#### Citation and commencement, etc.

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Computer Evidence in the Sheriff Court) (Revocation) 2019.

- (2) It comes into force on 5th March 2019.

- (3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Revocation of Acts of Sederunt

###### 2

The Act of Sederunt (Computer Evidence in the Sheriff Court) 1969[^f00003] and the Act of Sederunt (Computer Evidence in the Sheriff Court Amendment) 1970)[^f00004], so far as still in force, are revoked.

## Signed

CJM Sutherland — Lord President — I.P.D. — 30th

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Act of Sederunt revokes, so far as still in force, the Act of Sederunt (Computer Evidence in the Sheriff Court) 1969 and the Act of Sederunt (Computer Evidence in the Sheriff Court Amendment) 1970.
