# The Harbour Authority Designation (Scotland) Order 2014

Made: 9th June 2014

Coming into force: 16th June 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 40A(4)(c) of the Harbours Act 1964[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Harbour Authority Designation (Scotland) Order 2014 and comes into force on 16th June 2014.

#### Designation of harbour authority

###### 2

- (1) The Company, as harbour authority for the harbour area specified in section 14 of the Order set out in the Schedule to the Clyde Port Authority Confirmation Act 1965[^f00002], is a designated harbour authority in terms of section 40A of the Harbours Act 1964.

- (2) In paragraph (1), “the Company” means Clydeport Operations Limited (formerly Clydeport Limited) to which functions as harbour authority were transferred by the Clyde Port Authority Scheme 1991 Confirmation Order 1992[^f00003].

## Signed

JOHN NICHOLLS — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 9th June 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates Clydeport Operations Limited as a harbour authority which can give harbour directions as provided for in sections 40A to 40D of the Harbours Act 1964.
