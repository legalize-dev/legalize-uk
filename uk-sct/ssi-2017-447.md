# The International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2017

Made: 13th December 2017

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of December 2017

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 5 of the International Organisations Act 1968 and all other powers enabling Her Majesty to do so.

In accordance with section 10(1) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament .

Accordingly, Her Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2017 and comes into force on the date on which the Protocol on Privileges and Immunities of the Unified Patent Court done in Brussels on 29th June 2016 enters into force in the United Kingdom .

#### Amendment of the International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Order 2009

###### 2

- (1) The International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Order 2009 is amended as follows.

- (2) After schedule 16, insert schedule 17 (Unified Patent Court) set out in the schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
