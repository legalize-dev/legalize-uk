# The Annual Close Time (Permitted Periods of Fishing) (River Dee (Aberdeenshire) Salmon Fishery District) Order 2012

Made: 26th June 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th June 2012

Coming into force: 1st October 2012

In accordance with paragraphs10, 11 and 14(1) of Schedule1 to that Act, they have consulted such persons as they considered appropriate, directed that notice be given of the general effect of this Order and considered objections made.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Annual Close Time (Permitted Periods of Fishing) (River Dee (Aberdeenshire) Salmon Fishery District) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1stOctober 2012.

#### Periods permitted to fish for and take salmon by rod and line within the annual close time

###### 2

- (1) Paragraph (2) applies to the part of the River Dee (Aberdeenshire) salmon fishery district comprising the River Dee[^f00002].

- (2) The periods prescribed within the annual close time for salmon[^f00003] when it is permitted to fish for and take salmon by rod and line are—

- (a) in 2012, 1st to 15thOctober 2012; and

- (b) in each subsequent year, 1stFebruary to 10thFebruary and 27thAugust to 15thOctober,

all dates inclusive.

## Signed

WILLIE COWAN — A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers — 26th June 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The annual close time of the River Dee (Aberdeenshire) salmon fishery district for the purposes of section37(1) and (2) of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003 (“the 2003 Act”) is 27th August to 10thFebruary, by virtue of the District of the River Dee (Aberdeenshire) (Annual Close Time) Order 1950 ([S.I.1950/299](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1950/299)).

Section 37 of the 2003 Act prohibits all modes of fishing for and taking salmon in a salmon fishery district during the annual close time. It is an offence under section14 of the 2003 Act to fish for or take salmon during the annual close time. An exception to this is where provision has been made for periods within the annual close time during which fishing for and taking salmon by rod and line is permitted.

It is permitted to fish for or take salmon by rod and line in the River Carron and the River Cowie within the district by virtue of the Annual Close Time (River Dee (Aberdeenshire) Salmon Fishery District) Order 1989 ([S.I.1989/1499](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1499)).

This Order prescribes the periods when rod and line fishing for salmon is permitted during the annual close time in the part of the salmon fishery district comprising the River Dee. The periods are 1stFebruary to 10thFebruary and 27thAugust to 15thOctober in each year, and 1st to 15thOctober in 2012, all dates inclusive (article 2(2)). A map illustrating the extent of the River Dee within the district is appended to this note for information purposes only.

A Business Regulatory Impact Assessment (“BRIA”) has been prepared in relation to this Order and placed in the Scottish Parliament Information Centre. Copies of the BRIA are available from Marine Scotland, Victoria Quay, Leith, Edinburgh, EH6 6QQ and on line at www.legislation.gov.uk.
