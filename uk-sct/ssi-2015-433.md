# The Food (Scotland) Act 2015 (Consequential Provisions) (No. 2) Order 2015

Made: 16th December 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 18th December 2015

Coming into force: 5th February 2016

The Scottish Ministers, make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 61(a) of the Food (Scotland) Act 2015[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Food (Scotland) Act 2015 (Consequential Provisions) (No. 2) Order 2015.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 5th February 2016.

#### Modification of the General Food Regulations 2004

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the General Food Regulations 2004[^f00002], in the definition of “the Agency”, substitute “Food Standards Scotland” for “the Food Standards Agency”.

## Signed

MAUREEN WATT — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 16th December 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes provision consequential on the Food (Scotland) Act 2015 that Food Standards Scotland is the competent authority for the purposes of certain provisions of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) (OJ No. L 31, 1.2.2002, p.1) relating to Scotland.
