# The Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2007

Made: 1st March 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 75(2) of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2007.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

19th March 2007 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990:–

- (a) section 25;

- (b) section 26;

- (c) section 27;

- (d) section 28;

- (e) section 29; and

- (f) Schedule 2.

## Signed

JOHANN M LAMONT — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 19th March 2007 sections 25 29 of and Schedule 2 to the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (“the Act”). Sections 25 29 and Schedule 2 provide for professional bodies to apply for rights of audience and rights to conduct litigation.
