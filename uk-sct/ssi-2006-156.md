# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006

Made: 16th March 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 16th March 2006

Coming into force: 17th March 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 17th March 2006.

#### Amendments to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002

###### 2

- (1) The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In Part VIII of Schedule 2 (import conditions for products)–

- (a) for paragraph 4 of cross heading A (General) substitute[^f00003]–

> > (4) Commission Decision [2005/760/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0760) concerning certain protection measures in relation to highly pathogenic avian influenza in certain third countries for the import of captive birds (O.J. No. L 285, 28.10.05, p.60), as last amended by Commission Decision [2006/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0079) (O.J. No. L 36, 8.2.06, p.48).

- (b) after paragraph 4 of cross heading A (General) insert–

> > (5) Commission Decision [2006/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0007) concerning certain protection measures in relation to the import of feathers from certain third countries (O.J. No. L 5, 10.1.06, p.17) as amended by Commission Decision [2006/183/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0183) (O.J. No. L 65, 7.03.06, p.49)

; and

- (c) for paragraph 1 of cross heading E (Animal By Products) substitute–

> > (1) Regulation [(EC) No. 1774/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1774) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down health rules concerning animal by-products not intended for human consumption (O.J. No. L 273, 10.10.02, p.1) as last amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 208/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0208) (O.J. No. L 36, 8.2.06, p.25), and as read with Commission Decision [2005/760/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0760) (O.J. No. L 285, 28.10.05, p.60), as last amended by Commission Decision [2006/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0079) (O.J. No. L 36, 8.2.06, p.48).

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 16th March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 ([S.S.I. 2002/445](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/445)) (“the principal Regulations”).

These Regulations implement Council Directive [97/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0078), which lays down the principles governing the organisation of veterinary checks on products entering the Community from third countries (O.J. No. L 24, 30.1.98, p.9). They also give effect to the restriction on the importation of feathers from certain third countries contained in Commission Decision [2006/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0007) (O.J. No. L 5, 10.1.06, p.17) as amended by Commission Decision [2006/183/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0183) (O.J. No. L 65, 7.03.06, p.49).

Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations lists the import conditions with which products must comply, by reference to the relevant Community legislation. These Regulations amend Part VIII of Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations to refer to Commission Decision [2006/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0007), as amended (regulation 2(2)(b)).

Amendments are also made to Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations to refer to Commission Decision [2006/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0079) which further amends Commission Decision [2005/760/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0760) (concerning certain protection measures in relation to highly pathogenic avian influenza in certain third countries for the import of captive birds) (O.J. No. L 285, 28.10.05, p.60) (regulation 2(2)(a)) and to update the reference to Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1774/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1774) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down health rules concerning animal by products not intended for human consumption (O.J. No. L 273, 10.10.02, p.1) (regulation 2(2)(c)).

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations.
