# The Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2005

Made: 16th March 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(2)(b) and 35(1) of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2005.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

3rd May 2005 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 17 and Schedule 1 (Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland) of the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004.

## Signed

PETER J PEACOCK — A member of the Scottish Executive — 16th March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 3rd May 2005 section 17 of and Schedule 1 to the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004. Section 17 creates the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (“the Tribunals”) and introduces Schedule 1. Schedule 1 makes provision about the constitution, appointment to and procedures of the Tribunals, and other matters.
