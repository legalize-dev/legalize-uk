# The Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2010

Made: 15th April 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 16th April 2010

Coming into force: 17th April 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2010 and come into force on 17th April 2010.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Regulations2008[^f00002] are revoked.

## Signed

SHONA ROBISON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 15th April 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Regulations2008, which implemented in Scotland Commission Decision [2008/352/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0352). That Decision was repealed with effect from 15th April 2010 by Commission Regulation (EU) 258/2010 (O.J. No. L80, 26.3.2010, p.28).
