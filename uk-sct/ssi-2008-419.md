# The Freshwater Fish Conservation (Prohibition on Fishing for Eels) (Scotland) Regulations 2008

Made: 15th December 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 18th December 2008

Coming into force: 26th January 2009

In accordance with paragraph 11 of schedule 1 to that Act they have directed that notice be given of the general effect of these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Freshwater Fish Conservation (Prohibition on Fishing for Eels) (Scotland) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 26th January 2009.

#### Prohibition on fishing for eels

###### 2

No person shall fish for or take eels of the species *Anguilla Anguilla*, except under the authority of a licence granted by the Scottish Ministers.

## Signed

RICHARD LOCHHEAD — A member of the Scottish Executive — 15th December 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, made under section 51A of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003, prohibit the fishing for eels of the species *Anguilla Anguilla* except under the authority of a licence granted by the Scottish Ministers.

By virtue of section 51A(8) of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003 any person who acts in contravention of these Regulations shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.

These Regulations are made in implementation of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1100/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1100) establishing measures for the recovery of the stock of European eel (Anguilla Anguilla).
