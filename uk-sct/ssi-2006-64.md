# The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2006

Made: 14th February 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 17th February 2006

Coming into force: 15th March 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 15th March 2006.

#### General fund contribution limits

###### 2

The amount of contribution out of their general fund which a local authority may estimate that they will carry to the credit of their housing revenue account for the year 2006–2007 shall be limited to nil.

## Signed

MALCOLM CHISHOLM — A member of the Scottish Executive — 14th February 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that local authorities may not include in their estimates for the year 2006–2007 any contribution from their general fund to their housing revenue account.
