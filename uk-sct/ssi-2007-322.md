# The Discontinuance of Low Moss Prison (Scotland) Order 2007

Made: 7th June 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th June 2007

Coming into force: 2nd July 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(1) of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Discontinuance of Low Moss Prison (Scotland) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 2nd July 2007.

#### Discontinuance of Low Moss Prison

###### 2

Low Moss prison is hereby discontinued.

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 7th June 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the discontinuance of Low Moss prison on 2nd July 2007.
