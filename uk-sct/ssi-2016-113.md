# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2016

Made: 22nd February 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 24th February 2016

Coming into force: 1st April 2016

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2016 and comes into force on 1st April 2016.

#### Non-domestic rate for 2016-2017

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 48.4 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2016-2017.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Government — 22nd February 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes a rate of 48.4 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2016-2017. A rate of 48 pence in the pound was the figure prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland for the financial year 2015-2016 ([S.S.I. 2015/47](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2015/47)).
