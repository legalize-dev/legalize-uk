# The Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 13) Regulations 2021

Made: at 12.10 p.m. on 19th January 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: at 3.30 p.m. on 19th January 2021

Coming into force: 20th January 2021

In accordance with paragraph 6(2) and (3) of schedule 19 of the Act, the Scottish Ministers are of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by, a resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 13) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 20 January 2021.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Regulations 2020

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus) (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Regulations 2020[^f00002] are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

#### Changes to levels of areas

###### 3

In the table in schedule 6 (areas)—

- (a) in column 2 (area) of entry 34 (Na h-Eileanan Siar), after “Siar” insert “(except the area set out in row 35)”,

- (b) after entry 34 insert—

| 35 | The Isle of Barra and the Isle of Vatersay | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

MICHAEL RUSSELL — A member of the Scottish Government — At 12.10 p.m. on 19th January 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Restrictions and Requirements) (Local Levels) (Scotland) Regulations 2020 (“the Local Levels Regulations”). These Regulations come into force on 20 January 2021.

Regulation 3 amends the table at schedule 6 of the Local Levels Regulations, to apply level 4 restrictions and requirements to the Isle of Barra and the Isle of Vatersay.
