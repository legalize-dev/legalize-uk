# The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Modification of Primary Legislation) Order 2013

Made: 18th June 2013

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

In accordance with sections 197(2) and 204(3) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Modification of Primary Legislation) Order 2013 and comes into force on the same day as section 7 of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (holding of children’s hearing).

#### Modification of enactments

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1, which makes modifications of certain enactments, has effect.

- (2) The enactments specified in Schedule 2 are repealed to the extent specified.
