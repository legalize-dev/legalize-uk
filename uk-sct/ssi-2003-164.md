# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003

Made: 10th March 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 10th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 8.

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)–

- (a) after the definition of “third country” there shall be inserted–

> > “*wine-sector product*” for the purposes of these Regulations, includes aromatized wine;

; and

- (b) in the definition of “the Wine Standards Board”, for “of The Vintners' Company” there shall be substituted “ , a company limited by guarantee registered in England under company registration number 1127386 ”.

###### 4

After regulation 6 there shall be inserted–

> > (6A) Declarations submitted each year for the purposes of articles 2 and 4 of Commission Regulation [(EC) 1282/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1282) (detailed rules on the gathering of information to identify and monitor the wine market) shall take into account the quantities held at 1st December in that year.
> (6B) A notification of enrichment as referred to in Annex V(G)(5) to Council Regulation 1493/1999 shall be submitted to the Wine Standards Board not less than 48 hours prior to the commencement of the enrichment operation.

###### 5

For regulation 8(1) (prohibition of movement) there shall be substituted–

> > (1) Where an authorised officer inspects any wine-sector product, the officer may prohibit its being moved if he has reason to believe–
>   (a) an offence has been, is being or is likely to be committed in respect of it by contravention of, or failure to comply with, any relevant Community provision referred to in column 1 or 2 of Part I, II, IV or VIII of Schedule 9;
>   (b) there is, or is likely to be, a risk to public health in relation to that product; or
>   (c) there has been, or is likely to be, any fraudulent treatment of that product.

###### 6

In regulation 11(2) (review of prohibitions etc. on movement of wine-sector products by certain enforcement authorities), the word “each” shall be omitted.

###### 7

For Schedule 6 (community provisions) there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 8

In Schedule 9 (relevant community provisions)–

- (a) in item 1 of Part I, for the entry in column 2 there shall be substituted– “ Commission Regulation 884/2001 : All articles except 1, 2, 9, 17, 18, 20, 21, 22 and 23 ”;

- (b) in item 1 of Part III, for the entry in column 2 shall be substituted “ Commission Regulation 1282/2001: Articles 2, 3, 4, 6 and 11 ”; and

- (c) for item 1 of Part IV, shall be substituted the following entry:–

| 1Council Regulation 1493/1999: Title V | Commission Regulation 883/2001 : All articles except 6, 7, 13, 16, 19, 29, 34, 35 and 36 | Requirements relating to trade with third countries in products in the wine sector |
