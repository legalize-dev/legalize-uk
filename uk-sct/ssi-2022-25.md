# The Health Protection (Coronavirus) (International Travel and Operator Liability) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2022

Made: at 11.50 a.m. on 26th January 2022

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: at 2.45 p.m. on 26th January 2022

Coming into force: at 4.00 a.m. on 29th January 2022

In accordance with section 122(6) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers consider that these Regulations need to be made urgently, without a draft having been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (International Travel and Operator Liability) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2022 and come into force at 4.00 a.m. on 29 January 2022.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (International Travel and Operator Liability) (Scotland) Regulations 2021

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus) (International Travel and Operator Liability) (Scotland) Regulations 2021[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In Part 1 of schedule 6 (specified competitions), after paragraph 32 insert—

> > (32A) DNA (Dynamic New Athletics).

#### Saving

###### 3

The amendment made by these Regulations does not apply in relation to any person who arrived in Scotland before 4.00 a.m. on 29 January 2022, and the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (International Travel and Operator Liability) (Scotland) Regulations 2021 continue to apply to such persons as if the amendment made by these Regulations had not been made.

## Signed

MICHAEL MATHESON — A member of the Scottish Government — 2022-01-26

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus) (International Travel and Operator Liability) (Scotland) Regulations 2021 by adding to the list of specified competitions in Part 1 of schedule 6 of those Regulations an entry for DNA (Dynamic New Athletics), an indoor athletics event which is to take place at Emirates Arena in Glasgow on 5 February 2022.

Schedule 6 contains a list of specified competitions, participants in which benefit from exemptions under the Regulations.
