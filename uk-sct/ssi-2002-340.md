# Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2002

Made: 9th July 2002

Coming into force: 1st August 2002

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2002 and shall come into force on 1st August 2002.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Lands Valuation Appeal Court

###### 2

- (1) The judges named for the purposes of appeal under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879 shall be as follows:–

- The Right Honourable Lord Gill;

- The Honourable Lord Nimmo Smith;

- The Honourable Lord Philip;

- The Honourable Lord Kingarth;

- The Honourable Lord Clarke.

- (2) The Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 1997[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

W DOUGLAS CULLEN — Lord President, I.P.D. — 9th July 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt appoints the judges who may hear appeals under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879. The Act of Sederunt revokes the Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 1997.
