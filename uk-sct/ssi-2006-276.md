# The Robert Gordon University (Establishment) (Scotland) Order 2006

Made: 23rd May 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 23rd May 2006

Coming into force: 13th June 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 46 and 60 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Robert Gordon University (Establishment) (Scotland) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 13th June 2006.

#### Establishment of institution

###### 2

There shall be established on 13th June 2006 an institution for the provision of higher education to be known as The Robert Gordon University, which is incorporated as a body corporate by this Order.

## Signed

NICOL STEPHEN — A member of the Scottish Executive — 23rd May 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is one of several instruments, being the Designation of Institutions of Higher Education (Scotland) Order 2006, the Fundable Bodies (Scotland) Order 2006, the Robert Gordon University (Scotland) Order of Council 2006, the Robert Gordon University (Closure) (Scotland) Order 2006 and the Academic Awards and Distinctions (The Robert Gordon University) (Scotland) Order of Council 2006, which give effect to The Robert Gordon University’s desire that the university itself be incorporated rather than its governing body. This change necessitates the closing of the university as it is currently constituted and establishing a new institution of higher education.

This Order establishes a new institution of higher education to be called The Robert Gordon University and incorporates it as a body corporate. The University is established on 13th June 2006.
