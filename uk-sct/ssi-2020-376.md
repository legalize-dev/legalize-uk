# The Police Act 1997 and the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (Fees) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: 11th November 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 13th November 2020

Coming into force: 26th December 2020

In accordance with section 70(3) of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, the Scottish Ministers have had regard to the circumstances in which the fees are payable, and the desirability of maintaining an appropriate balance among the quality of the performance of their vetting, barring and disclosure functions, the cost of that performance and the fees paid to them in respect of that performance.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 and the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (Fees) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 26 December 2020.

#### Amendment of Expiry Period of the Police Act 1997 and the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (Fees) (Coronavirus) Regulations 2020

###### 2

In Paragraph (1) of Regulation 5 (expiry) of the Police Act 1997 and the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (Fees) (Coronavirus) Regulations 2020[^f00003] for “6 months” substitute “9 months”.

## Signed

MAREE TODD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 11th November 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police Act 1997 and the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (Fees) (Coronavirus) Regulations 2020 (“the 2020 Regulations”). Regulation 5 of the 2020 Regulations provides that they will cease to have effect after 6 months from the date on which they came into force. They came into force on 26 June 2020. These Regulations amend the 2020 Regulations so that they will cease to have effect after 9 months from the date on which they came into force.
