# The Budget (Scotland) Act 2010 Amendment Order 2011

Made: 15th March 2011

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

In accordance with section7(2) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Budget (Scotland) Act 2010 Amendment Order 2011 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) In this Order, “the 2010 Act” means the Budget (Scotland) Act 2010.

#### Amendment of section3 of the 2010 Act

###### 2

- (1) Section3 of the 2010 Act (overall cash authorisations) is amended in accordance with paragraphs(2) to (5).

- (2) In paragraph(a) (in relation to the Scottish Administration), for “£31,800,947,000” substitute “£31,218,013,000”.

- (3) In paragraph(b) (in relation to the Forestry Commissioners), for “£79,946,000” substitute “£77,569,000”.

- (4) In paragraph(c) (in relation to the Foods Standards Agency), for “£11,015,000” substitute “£10,225,000”.

- (5) In paragraph(d) (in relation to the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body), for “£78,721,000” substitute “£78,774,000”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Budget (Scotland) Act 2010 (“the 2010 Act”).

This Order amends the 2010 Act so as to—

- (a) alter the overall cash authorisations in relation to the Scottish Administration, the Forestry Commissioners, the Food Standards Agency and the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body in section3 of the 2010 Act (article2);

- (b) alter the first, fourth, fifth and sixth purposes for which resources may be used in accordance with section1 of, and schedule1 to, the 2010 Act (article 3(2));

- (c) alter the amount of resources other than accruing resources that may be used for all of the purposes set out in schedule1 to the 2010 Act and the total of amounts of resources for all of the purposes (article3(3));

- (d) alter the amount of accruing resources in respect of certain of the purposes set out in schedule1 to the 2010 Act and the total of amounts of resources for all of the purposes (article 3(4));

- (e) alter the amount of resources other than accruing resources of direct-funded bodies in schedule2 to the 2010 Act in relation to the first and second purposes (article4(2)); and

- (f) alter the amount of borrowing by certain statutory bodies in schedule3 to the 2010 Act in relation to the fourth enactment (article5(2)).
