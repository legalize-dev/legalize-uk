# The Dissolution of Local Health Councils (Scotland) Order 2005

Made: 1st March 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 3rd March 2005

Coming into force: 1st April 2005

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 8 of the National Health Service Reform (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Dissolution of Local Health Councils (Scotland) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

#### Dissolution of Local Health Councils

###### 2

The date specified for the purposes of section 8(1) of the National Health Service Reform (Scotland) Act 2004 (the date on which local health councils established by virtue of section 7 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00002] are dissolved) is 1st April 2005.

## Signed

ANDREW P KERR — A member of the Scottish Executive — 1st March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies 1st April as the date on which all local health councils established by virtue of section 7 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act [1978 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/29) are dissolved.
