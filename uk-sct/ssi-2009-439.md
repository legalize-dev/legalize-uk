# The Public Contracts and Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Amendment (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 10th December 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 11th December 2009

Coming into force: 19th December 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Contracts and Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Amendment (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 19thDecember 2009.

#### Amendment of the Public Contracts and Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2009

###### 2

- (1) The Public Contracts and Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2009[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (amendment of the Public Contracts Regulations)—

- (a) in paragraph (4)(b)(i), for “second” substitute “first”; and

- (b) in paragraph (5)(b)(i), for “second” substitute “first”.

## Signed

JOHN SWINNEY — A member of the Scottish Executive — 10th December 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations correct errors in regulation 2(4)(b)(i) and (5)(b)(i) of the Public Contracts and Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2009.
