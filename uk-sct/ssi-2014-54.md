# The High Hedges (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2014

Made: 26th February 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 28th February 2014

Coming into force: 1st April 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 38(2) of the High Hedges (Scotland) Act 2013[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the High Hedges (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st April 2014.

#### Day appointed

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the High Hedges (Scotland) Act 2013 (so far as not already in force) is 1st April 2014.

## Signed

DEREK MACKAY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 26th February 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force those provisions of the High Hedges (Scotland) Act 2013 (“the Act”) which are yet to be commenced. The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 2nd May 2013 and, in terms of section 38(1) of the Act, sections 34, 36, 38 and 39 of the Act accordingly came into force on 3rd May 2013. The remaining provisions of the Act are brought into force by this Order on 1st April 2014 and the Act will be fully in force on that date.
