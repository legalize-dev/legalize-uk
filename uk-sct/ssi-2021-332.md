# The Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2021

Made: 22nd September 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 24th September 2021

Coming into force: 11th October 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(2) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 11 October 2021.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 7(2)(g) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 is 11 October 2021.

## Signed

MICHAEL MATHESON — A member of the Scottish Government — 22nd September 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations commence section 7(2)(g) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019. Section 7(2)(g) places a requirement upon Scottish Ministers to consult the Scottish Fuel Poverty Advisory Panel in preparing the Fuel Poverty Strategy.
