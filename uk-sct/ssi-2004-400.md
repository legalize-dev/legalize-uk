# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2004

Made: 15th September 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 16th September 2004

Coming into force: 7th October 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38(4) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2004 and shall come into force on 7th October 2004.

#### Additions to the list of prescribed conservation bodies

###### 2

- (1) The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In Part II of the Schedule insert, in proper alphabetical order, the bodies listed in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — CONSERVATION BODIES PRESCRIBED UNDER SECTION 38(4) OF THE TITLE CONDITIONS (SCOTLAND) ACT 2003

- Alba Conservation Trust

- Glasgow Conservation Trust West

- Tayside Building Preservation Trust

- The Scottish Wildlife Trust

- United Kingdom Historic Building Preservation Trust

## Signed

HUGH HENRY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 15th September 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/453](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/453)) by adding to the list of bodies prescribed as conservation bodies. Conservation bodies are bodies in favour of whom it is competent to create a conservation burden under section 38(1) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 and to preserve rights to enforce real burdens by registration of a notice under section 27 or 27A of the Abolition of Feudal Tenure etc. (Scotland) Act 2000.
