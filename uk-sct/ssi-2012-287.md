# The Population (Statistics) Act 1938 Modifications (Scotland) Order 2012

Made: 24th October 2012

Coming into force: 1st January 2013

In accordance with section 44(5) of the Family Law (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00003] and section259(8)(b)[^f00004] of the Civil Partnership Act 2004, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Population (Statistics) Act 1938 Modifications (Scotland) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st January 2013.

#### Modification of the Population (Statistics) Act 1938

###### 2

In the Schedule (particulars which may be required) to the Population (Statistics) Act 1938[^f00005], for paragraph1(c) substitute—

> > (c) the number of previous children born to the mother and how many of them were—
>   (i) born alive; and
>   (ii) still-born;
> (d) where the parents are married to, or in a civil partnership with, each other—
>   (i) the date of their marriage or formation of civil partnership; and
>   (ii) whether the mother had been married or in a civil partnership before that date.

## Signed

FIONA HYSLOP — A member of the Scottish Government — 24th October 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order modifies the Population (Statistics) Act [1938 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1938/12) in consequence of the abolition of the status of illegitimacy. It also makes modifications in consequence of the Civil Partnership Act [2004 (c.33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/33).
