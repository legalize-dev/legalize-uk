# The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2009

Made: 25th February 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 26th February 2009

Coming into force: 1st April 2009

In accordance with section 163A of that Act[^f00002] they have consulted with such– persons considered by them to be representative of the interests of undertakers; road works authorities; and other persons, as they think appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2009 and come into force on 1st April 2009.

#### Amendment of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003

###### 2

In regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003[^f00003] for “£25.00” substitute “£30.00”.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008[^f00004] are revoked.

## Signed

STEWART STEVENSON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 25th February 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for an increase to £30.00 (previously £25.00) in the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by road works authorities in Scotland under regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003 and revoke a previous instrument which increased the fees in 2008.
