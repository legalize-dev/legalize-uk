# The Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003

Made: 25th June 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 26th June 2003

Coming into force: 29th June 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003 and come into force on 29th June 2003.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Regulations 2003

###### 2

In regulation 1(1) of the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) Regulations 2003[^f00002] for “30th June 2003” substitute “30th September 2003”.

## Signed

TOM McCABE — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 25th June 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, amend the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Regulations 2003 (“the principal Regulations”) by substituting the coming into force date of 30th June 2003 with a new date of 30th September 2003.

These Regulations are made in anticipation of a new Commission Decision which will be made by 1st July 2003. That Decision will amend the date of application of Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) (O.J. L 13, 18.1.93, p.24), which was implemented by the principal Regulations. The new date of application will be 30th September 2003.
