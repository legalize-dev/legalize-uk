###### 19

In the case of any compound feeding stuff for animals other than pet animals–

- (a) all the feed materials shall be declared in the statutory statement–

- (i) by their specific names; and

- (ii) with an indication, in descending order, of the percentage by weight of each feed material contained in the compound feeding stuff, subject to a limit of variation of ± 15% in relation to each declared percentage; and

- (b) the statutory statement shall contain the following declaration–

> > - The exact percentage by weight of feed materials used in this feeding stuff may be obtained from … (name or trade name, address or registered office, telephone number and email address of the supplier of the compound feeding stuff).

## “PART IX — EUROPEAN COMMUNITY REGULATIONS BY WHICH ADDITIVES ARE CONTROLLED

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2316/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/2316) concerning authorisation of new additives and amending the conditions for authorisation of a number of additives already authorised in feedingstuffs[^f00005].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1594/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1594) amending the conditions for the authorisation of an additive in feedingstuffs[^f00006].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2439/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/2439) on the conditions for authorisation of additives belonging to the group “binders, anti-caking agents and coagulants” in feedingstuffs[^f00007].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1353/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1353) concerning the permanent authorisation of an additive and the provisional authorisation of new additives, new additive uses and new preparations in feedingstuffs[^f00008].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2437/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2437) concerning the permanent authorisation of an additive and the provisional authorisation of new additives in feedingstuffs[^f00009].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2013/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2013) concerning the provisional authorisation of a new additive use and the permanent authorisation of an additive in feedingstuffs[^f00010].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2200/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2200) concerning provisional authorisation of additives in feedingstuffs[^f00011].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 256/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0256) concerning the provisional authorisation of new additives, the prolongation of provisional authorisation of an additive and the permanent authorisation of an additive in feedingstuffs[^f00012].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1252/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1252) concerning the provisional authorisation of a new additive in feedingstuffs)[^f00013].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1876/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1876) concerning the provisional authorisation of a new use of an additive in feedingstuffs[^f00014].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2188/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/2188) concerning the provisional authorisation of new uses of additives in feedingstuffs[^f00015].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 261/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0261) concerning the provisional authorisation of new uses of additives in feedingstuffs[^f00016].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 316/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0316) concerning the permanent authorisation of an additive in feedingstuffs and the provisional authorisation of an additive already authorised in feedingstuffs[^f00017].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 666/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0666) provisionally authorising the use of certain micro-organisms in feedingstuffs[^f00018].

- Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 668/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0668) concerning the permanent authorisation of an additive in feedingstuffs[^f00019].
