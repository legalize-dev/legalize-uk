# The Colours in Food Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2007

Made: 1st March 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 2nd March 2007

Coming into force: 6th April 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1)(a) and 17(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, having had regard in accordance with section 48(4A)[^f00002] of that Act to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency and after consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Colours in Food Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 6th April 2007.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Colours in Food Regulations 1995[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)”, for “and Directive [2004/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0047)[^f00005]”, substitute–

> > - , Directive [2004/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0047)[^f00006] and Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033)[^f00007]

## Signed

LEWIS MACDONALD — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 1st March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, implement Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) amending Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) as regards sunset yellow FCF (E 110) and titanium dioxide (E 171).

They do so by updating the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” in regulation 2(1) of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995, to reflect the amendment made to that Directive by Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) (regulation 2).

Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) amends Commission Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) by substituting revised purity criteria for the colours sunset yellow FCF (E 110) and titanium dioxide (E 171).

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared to accompany these Regulations, as they will not impose any costs on business.
