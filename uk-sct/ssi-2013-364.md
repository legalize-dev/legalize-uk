# The Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Order 2013

Made: 19th December 2013

Coming into force: 31st December 2013

In accordance with section 4(4) of that Act[^f00002], a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Order 2013 and comes into force on 31st December 2013.

#### Revocation of the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009

###### 2

The Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009[^f00003] is revoked.

#### Incidental, consequential and saving provision

###### 3

- (1) Notwithstanding article 2, section 1(1) to (5) and (7) of the Act continues in force for the specified areas.

- (2) Section 6(4) of the Act has effect only in respect of the specified areas.

- (3) In this article—

- “the Act” means the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009;

- “specified areas” means the areas for which— Fife Health Board; and Dumfries and Galloway Health Board,

are constituted[^f00004].

## Signed

ALEX NEIL — A member of the Scottish Government — 19th December 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009, which commenced sections 1 to 3 of the Health Boards (Membership and Elections) (Scotland) Act 2009 in respect of Fife and Dumfries and Galloway Health Board areas for the purpose of piloting Health Board elections in those areas. This Order brings the pilot scheme to an end in terms of section 6 of the 2009 Act. The Order also makes incidental, consequential and saving provision.
