# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2017

Made: 17th January 2017

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th January 2017

Coming into force: 1st April 2017

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7B(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2017 and comes into force on 1st April 2017.

#### Non-domestic rate for 2017-2018

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 46.6 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2017-2018.

## Signed

DEREK MACKAY — A member of the Scottish Government — 17th January 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes a rate of 46.6 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2017-2018. A rate of 48.4 pence in the pound was prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland for the financial year 2016-2017 ([S.S.I. 2016/133](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2016/133)).
