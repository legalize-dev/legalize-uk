# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2002

Made: 21st November 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 25th November 2002

Coming into force: 1st January 2003

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2002 and shall come into force on 1st January 2003.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of fees

###### 2

- (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2) below, the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt shall be substituted for the Table in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1994[^f00003] and shall apply to work carried out by a sheriff officer on or after 1 January 2003.

- (2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not affect the fees payable as respects any work so carried out before 1 January 2003.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the order)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1994 (S.I. [1994/392](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/392)) by increasing the fees payable to sheriff officers by about 2.6%.

The last increase was in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2001 ([S.S.I. 2001/439](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/439)).
