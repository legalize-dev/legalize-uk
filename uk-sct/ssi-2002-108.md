# The Scottish Commission for the Regulation of Care (Staff Transfer Scheme) Order 2002

Made: 6th March 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 8th March 2002

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30 of the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001], and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consulting in accordance with the requirements prescribed under section 30(3) of that Act[^f00002], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Commission for the Regulation of Care (Staff Transfer Scheme) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

#### The scheme

###### 2

The persons listed in the Schedule (being persons who were immediately before the transfer of employees under this Order employed under a contract of employment with a local authority or Health Board on work which would have continued but for the provisions of Part 1 of the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001) shall on 1st April 2002 transfer to and become employees of the Commission.
