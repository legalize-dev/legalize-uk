# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Amendment (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2010

Made: 16th September 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 21st September 2010

Coming into force: 31st October 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section26(5) of the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00001], sections 12 and 16 of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00002] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

#### Citation, commencement, effect and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Amendment (Scotland) (No.2) Order 2010.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 31stOctober 2010, and, subject to paragraph (3), has effect from 1st April 2010[^f00003].

- (3) Paragraph 7 of the Schedule has effect from 31stOctober 2010.

- (4) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Amendment of the Firefighters Pension Scheme Order 1992

###### 2

Schedule 2 to the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Order 1992[^f00004] is amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme which is set out in Schedule2 to the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Order 1992 and, with the exception of paragraph 7 of the Schedule to this Order, has effect from 1st April 2010.

This Order introduces provisions supporting the introduction of a new funding system for firefighter pensions.

A new Part LA is inserted into the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme by paragraph 8 of the Schedule to this Order. Part LA makes provision for—

- the establishment and maintenance of a Firefighters’ Pension Account and the payments that are to flow into and out of that Account (rules LA1 to LA3);

- the treatment of awards made under the Compensation Scheme for firefighters in terms of the accounts from which such awards are to be made (rule LA4);

- transfers between a fire and rescue authority’s Firefighters’ Pension Account and Firefighters’ Operating Account and the effect of those transfers (rules LA5 and LA6); and

- a requirement for fire and rescue authorities to provide information etc. in relation to the Firefighters’ Pension Account to the Scottish Ministers (rule LA7).

Paragraphs 5 and 6 of the Schedule to this Order make provision in relation to the transfer of pension history information and sums where a firefighter moves from one fire and rescue authority to another.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this Order, as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
