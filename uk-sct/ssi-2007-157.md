# The Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2007

Made: 6th March 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 6th March 2007

Coming into force: 1st August 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 259(1), (2) and (4) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st August 2007.

###### 2

This Order shall extend to Scotland only.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Order specified in column 1 of the Schedule is revoked to the extent specified in column 3 thereof.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes certain articles of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) Order 2005 as a result of the consolidation of the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/580](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/580)), the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/1739](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1739)), the Education (Fees and Awards) (Scotland) Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/93)), the Students' Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/1131](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1131)), the Education (Student Loans) (Scotland) Regulations 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/200](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/200)), the Graduate Endowment (Scotland) Regulations 2001 ([S.S.I. 2001/280](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/280)) and the Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2004 ([S.S.I. 2004/273](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2004/273)).
