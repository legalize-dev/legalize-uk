# The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made: 24th February 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 25th February 2010

Coming into force: 1st April 2010

as they think appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 2

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations2010 and come into force on 1st April 2010.

#### Amendment of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003

###### 3

In regulation3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations2003[^f00003] for “£30.00” substitute “£31.00”.

#### Revocation

###### 4

The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2009[^f00004] are revoked.

## Signed

STEWART STEVENSON — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 24th February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for an increase to £31.00 (previously £30.00) in the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by road works authorities in Scotland under regulation3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations2003. These Regulations also revoke the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2009 which increased the fees in2009.
