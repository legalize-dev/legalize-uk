# The Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3, Consequential Provisions and Revocation) Order 2009

Made: 17th November 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections122(2) and 128(2) of the Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008 (Commencement No.3, Consequential Provisions and Revocation) Order 2009.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008.

#### Commencement of provisions of the Act and consequential repeal and revocation

###### 2

- (1) The provision of the Act specified in column(1) of schedule1 (the subject matter of which is mentioned in column(2)) comes into force on 1stDecember 2009.

- (2) The amendment of the enactment specified in schedule2 comes into force on 1stDecember 2009.

- (3) The provisions of the Act specified in column(1) of schedule3 (the subject matter of each provision being mentioned in column(2)) come into force on 1stJanuary 2010.

- (4) The consequential repeal and revocation specified in schedule4 come into force on 1stJanuary 2010.

#### Revocation

###### 3

Article3(d) of the Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008 (Commencement No.2, Savings and Consequential Provisions) Order 2009[^f00002] is revoked on 1stDecember 2009.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force further provisions of the Public Health (etc.) Scotland Act 2008 (“the Act”.)

Article2(1) and schedule1 bring Part8 of the Act (regulation of provision of sunbeds) into force on 1stDecember 2009 in so far as it is not already in force.

Article2(2) and schedule2 make a consequential amendment to the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980. In consequence, a savings provision included in the Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008 (Commencement No.2, Savings and Consequential Provisions) Order 2009 is revoked by article3 at the same time.

Article2(3) and schedule3 bring Part2 (notifiable diseases, notifiable organisms and health risk states) and Schedule1 (list of notifiable diseases and notifiable organisms) of the Act into force on 1stJanuary 2010, together with various repeals and revocations.

Article2(4) and schedule4 bring afurther repeal and revocation into force on 1stJanuary 2010. The repeal of section275(7)(a) of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997 is consequential on the repeal of section32 of the Public Health (Scotland) Act 1897. [The Public Health (Notification of Infectious Diseases) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1989/2250](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/2250) are revoked in consequence of the commencement of Part2 of the Act.
