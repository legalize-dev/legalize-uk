# The Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Order 2001

Made: 25th October 2001

Coming into force: 31st October 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(2), (3) and (4) of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 31st October 2001.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In article 4 (transitional and savings provisions) of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2000[^f00002] after paragraph (3) there shall be inserted–

> > (4) Notwithstanding the coming into force on 1st November 2001 of sections 46 and 50 of the Act[^f00003] for the purposes specified in relation to those sections in column 3 of Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order, sections 10 and 11 of and Schedule 2 to the 1965 Act[^f00004] shall continue in force as originally enacted in respect of any investigation, consideration or proceedings instituted in terms of those provisions prior to 1st November 2001.

## Signed

JACK McCONNELL — A member of the Scottish Executive — 25th October 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2000 (“the Commencement Order”). Article 2 inserts into the Commencement Order further transitional and savings provisions. It provides for the current procedures under sections 10 and 11 of and Schedule 2 to the Teaching Council (Scotland) Act 1965 to continue to apply to cases where consideration by the Investigating and Disciplinary Committees has commenced prior to 1st November 2001.
