# The Marine (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement No. 3 and Consequential Provisions) Order 2013

Made: 18th September 2013

Coming into force: 1st November 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 165(1) and 168(1) of the Marine (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Marine (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement No. 3 and Consequential Provisions) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st November 2013.

- (2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Marine (Scotland) Act 2010.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1st November 2013 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of section 167 of the Act so far as relating to paragraph 4 of schedule 4 to the Act.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The Orders specified in column 1 of the Table in the Schedule are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force those provisions of the Marine (Scotland) Act 2010 (“the Act”) which have yet to come into force and with the coming into force of this Order the Act is fully commenced.

Article 2 of the Order brings into force on 1st November 2013 the provisions of section 167 of the Act so far as relating to paragraph 4 of schedule 4 to the Act. Paragraph 4 of schedule 4 to the Act repeals section 1 of the Protection of Wrecks Act [1973 (c.33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/33) so far as it extends to Scotland.

Article 3 of the Order revokes the Orders made under section 1 of the Protection of Wrecks Act 1973 in respect of sites situated in Scotland.
