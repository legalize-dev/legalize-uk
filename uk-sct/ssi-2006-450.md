# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2006

Made: 30th August 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 1st September 2006

Coming into force: 2nd September 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 2nd September 2006.

#### Amendment to The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00002] are amended in accordance with the following paragraph of this regulation.

- (2) After the entry for Commission Decision [99/558/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0558) in paragraph 5 of Part II of Schedule 5 (Registered horses for racing etc.) insert–

> > - Commission Decision [2006/542/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0542) (O.J. No. L214, 4.8.2006, p.59),

## Signed

ROSS FINNIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 30th August 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Scotland) Regulations 2000. They implement Commission Decision [2006/542/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0542) on animal health conditions and veterinary certification for the re-entry of registered horses for racing, competition and cultural events after temporary export (O.J. No. L214, 4.8.2006, p.59) (regulation 2(2)).
