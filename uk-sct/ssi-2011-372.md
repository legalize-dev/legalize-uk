# The Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011

Made: 27th October 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 31st October 2011

Coming into force: 1st December 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section33(2) and (3) of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No.2) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1stDecember 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions (to the extent not already in force) of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 specified in column1 of the Schedule (the subject matter of which is described in column2 of the Schedule) is 1stDecember 2011.
