# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2006

Made: 9th March 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 9th March 2006

Coming into force: 31st March 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38(7) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 31st March 2006.

#### Addition to the list of prescribed conservation bodies

###### 2

Part II of the Schedule to the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended by deleting the entry “Glasgow Conservation Trust West”.

## Signed

HUGH HENRY — Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers — 9th March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/453](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/453)) by deleting Glasgow Conservation Trust West from the list of prescribed conservation bodies. Conservation bodies are bodies in favour of whom it is competent to create a conservation burden under section 38(1) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003.
