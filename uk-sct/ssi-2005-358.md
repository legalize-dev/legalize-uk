# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 2005

Made: 20th June 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 178(3), (4)(d) and (6)(a) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001] and having consulted with the Secretary of State in accordance with section 178(6)(a) of that Act, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 2005.

#### Appointed day and extent

###### 2

1st July is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005–

- (a) section 96 (mutual assistance in freezing property or evidence) so far as it has effect for the purpose of conferring functions on the Scottish Ministers;

- (b) section 156 (payments by Scottish Ministers to police authorities etc. in relation to the prevention, detection and enforcement of certain traffic offences)[^f00002];

- (c) section 164 (criminal records checks: verification of identity) to the extent that it extends to Scotland; and

- (d) section 166(2) (further amendments to Police Act 1997 as it applies to Scotland) to the extent that it extends to Scotland.

## Signed

CATHY JAMIESON — A member of the Scottish Executive — 20th June 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 96, 156, 164 and 166(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 on 1st July 2005 to the extent specified.
