# The Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants etc.) Act 2004 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2004

Made: 11th November 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 48(3) of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004[^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2004.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Commencement

Sections 4 and 5 of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004 shall come into force on 1st December 2004.

## Signed

HUGH HENRY — Authorised to sign on behalf of the Scottish Ministers — 11th November 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, brings into force on 1st December 2004 sections 4 and 5 of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004.

Section 4 creates a new offence of trafficking people for exploitation. Section 5 makes supplementary provision in respect of section 4 and adds the new offence to Schedule 4 (Lifestyle Offences – Scotland) to the Proceeds of Crime Act [2002 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29).
