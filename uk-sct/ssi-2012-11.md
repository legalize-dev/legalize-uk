## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [1991 c.22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22). Section 112A was inserted by section 19 of the Transport (Scotland) Act [2005 (asp 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2005/12) (“the 2005 Act”). The functions of the Secretary of State in section 163(1), so far as they were exercisable within devolved competence, were transferred to the Scottish Ministers, by virtue of section 53 of the Scotland Act [1998 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/46).

[^f00002]: Section 163A was inserted by section 39 of the 2005 Act.

[^f00003]: The Scottish Road Works Register is kept in terms of section 112A(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991 (“the Act”).

[^f00004]: The Scottish Road Works Commissioner is created by section 16(1) of the 2005 Act and defined in section 112A(1) of the Act.

[^f00005]: Section 145(1) of the Act defines “roads authority” as having the same meaning as in section 151 of the Roads (Scotland) Act [1984 (c.54)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/54).

[^f00006]: [S.S.I.2008/16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2008/16), amended by [S.S.I.2009/26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/26).
