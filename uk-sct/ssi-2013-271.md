# The Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013 (Commencement No. 2) Amendment Order 2013

Made: 17th September 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 19th September 2013

Coming into force: 10th October 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 25(2) of the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013 (Commencement No. 2) Amendment Order 2013 and comes into force on 10th October 2013.

#### Amendment of Commencement No.2 Order

###### 2

In the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2013[^f00002], in article 2 omit “20 (contributions for criminal legal aid),”.

## Signed

KENNY MACASKILL — A member of the Scottish Government — 17th September 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2013 so that section 20 of the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013, which concerns contributions for criminal legal aid, does not come into force on 11th October 2013.
