# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Amendment)(No.2) (Wales) Regulations 2003

Made: 31st July 2003

Coming into force: 1st August 2003

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by sections 15(1), 35(1), 36(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977 [^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Amendment) (No.2) (Wales) Regulations 2003 and will come into force on 1st August 2003.

- (2) These Regulations apply to Wales only.

#### Amendment of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1992

###### 2

The National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1992 (“the principal Regulations”)[^f00002] are amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

#### Amendment of regulation 2 of the principal Regulations

###### 3

In regulation 2 of the principal Regulations (interpretation)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)insert the following definition in the appropriate place in the alphabetical order—

> > - “local health board” has the meaning assigned to it by section 16BA of the Act[^f00003];

- (b) in paragraph (1A) for “health authority” substitute “ local health board”;

- (c) after paragraph (1A) insert the following paragraph —

> > (1B) In the application of these Regulations in relation to Wales “Health Authority” shall, in each place where these words occur, have effect as if there were substituted the words “local health board”.

#### Amendment of regulation 19 of the principal Regulations

###### 4

In regulation 19 of the principal Regulations (Statement of Dental Remuneration) at the end of the Table set out below paragraph (1), insert —

- (a) in column (1) the Roman numerals “XI” and “XII”, and

- (b) in column (2) “Paternity Payments” and “Adoptive Leave Payments”.

#### Amendment of Schedule 4 to the principal Regulations

###### 5

In Part II of Schedule 4 to the principal Regulations (prior approval treatment), in each of paragraphs 1 and 2, for “£375” substitute “£390”.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

Rhodri Morgan — First Minister — 31st July 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1992 ([S.I.1992/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/661)) (“the principal Regulations”) which regulate the terms upon which general dental services are provided under the National Health Service Act 1977.

Regulation 2 of the principal Regulations is amended to add a definition of “local health board” and contains provisions so that references to “FHSA” and “Health Authority” are treated as references to a local health board.

These Regulations amend regulation 19(1) of the principal Regulations so that two new determinations, XI and XII, are added to the table listing the determinations. The subject matter of determination XI is “Paternity Payments” and of determination XII is “Adoptive Leave Payments”.

They also amend Schedule 4 to the principal Regulations to increase (from £375 to £390) the amount specified therein as the maximum cost, or likely cost, of care and treatment which a dentist may undertake without seeking the prior approval of the Dental Practice Board.
