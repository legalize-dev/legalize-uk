# The Persons Providing Education at Further Education Institutions in Wales (Conditions) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 10 October 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 13 October 2009

Coming into force: 4 November 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by section 136(c), section 210(7) and section 214(1) and (2) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001], and now vested in them make the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Persons Providing Education at Further Education Institutions in Wales (Conditions) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and they come into force on 4 November 2009.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendments to the Persons Providing Education at Further Education Institutions in Wales (Conditions) Regulations 2007

###### 2

- (1) The Persons Providing Education at Further Education Institutions in Wales (Conditions) Regulations 2007[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the English text of regulation 3(1), after “the Police Act 1997”, omit the words “of that Act”.

- (3) In the Welsh text of regulation 3(1), for “o'r Ddeddf honno” substitute “o Ddeddf yr Heddlu 1997”.

## Signed

Jane Hutt — Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 10 October 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Education (Miscellaneous Amendments relating to Safeguarding Children) (Wales) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2544 (W.206)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2009/2544) (“the 2009 Regulations”) make amendments to various sets of regulations made under the Education Act [1996 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56), the School Standards and Framework Act [1998 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/38), and the Education Act [2002 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32) to reflect changes that will arise as a result of the commencement (on 12 October 2009) of the barring provisions in the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act [2006 (c. 47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/47) (“the SVGA”) and the commencement of new provisions (inserted by the SVGA) in the Police Act [1997 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/50).

These Regulations amend the 2009 Regulations so as to correct two mistakes in them. Regulation 8(2)(a)(ii) of the English text of the 2009 Regulations duplicates a reference to the Police Act 1997 by referring to the “Police Act 1997” and then by referring to “that Act”. Accordingly, regulation 2(1) of these Regulations deletes the duplicated reference. Regulation 8(2)(a)(ii) of the Welsh text of the 2009 Regulations omits a reference to the Deddf yr Heddlu 1997 and instead refers to “that Act”. Accordingly in regulation 2(2) of these Regulations the words “o'r Ddeddf honno” are substituted for “o Ddeddf yr Heddlu 1997”.
