# The Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 (Firefighters' Pension Scheme) (Wales) Order 2004

Made: 9 November 2004

Coming into force: 11 November 2004

The National Assembly for Wales makes the following order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 36, 60 and 62 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001]:

#### Application, name and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 (Firefighters' Pension Scheme) (Wales) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 11 November 2004.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “the Scheme” means the scheme established under section 26 of the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00002] and set out in the Firemen’s Pension Scheme Order 1992[^f00003].

#### Preservation of existing pension scheme

###### 3

- (1) Notwithstanding the repeal of section 26 of the Fire Services Act 1947[^f00004], the Scheme shall continue in force.

- (2) The enactments listed in the Schedule to this Order shall continue to have effect for the purposes of the Scheme as if they had not been amended or repealed, as appropriate, by the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004.

#### Change of scheme name

###### 4

- (1) The new name of the Scheme shall be the “Firefighters' Pension (Wales) Scheme”.

- (2) Any reference to the Scheme in any enactment, instrument or other document (including the Scheme itself) shall be construed as a reference to the Scheme by its new name.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the Firemen’s Pension Scheme (“the Scheme”), established under section 26 of the Fire Services Act 1947, to continue in force despite the repeal by the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 of that section. For the purposes of the Scheme, this Order continues to give effect to certain enactments amended or repealed by the 2004 Act.

This Order also provides for the name of the Scheme to be changed to the “Firefighters' Pension (Wales) Scheme”. Any reference to the Scheme in any enactment, instrument or other document (including the Scheme itself) will be construed as a reference to the Scheme by its new name.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of businesses, charities or voluntary bodies.
