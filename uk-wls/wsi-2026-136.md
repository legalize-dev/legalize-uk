# The Education (School Attendance Order) (Wales) Regulations 2026

Made: 17 November 2026

Coming into force: 14 December 2026

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 437(3) and 569(4) and (5) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Regulations.

#### Title, coming into force and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Education (School Attendance Order) (Wales) Regulations 2026 and they come into force on 14 December 2026.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Prescribed form of a school attendance order

###### 2

- (1) The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed as the form of a school attendance order to be served by a local authority in Wales for the purpose of section 437(3) of the 1996 Act.

- (2) In this regulation—

- “*the 1996 Act*” (“*Deddf 1996*”) means the Education Act 1996;

- “*local authority in Wales*” (“*awdurdod lleol yng Nghymru*”) has the meaning given by section 579(1)[^f00003] of the 1996 Act;

- “*school attendance order*” (“*gorchymyn mynychu’r ysgol*”) has the meaning given by section 437(3) of the 1996 Act.

#### Consequential amendments to the Education (School Attendance Order) Regulations 1995

###### 3

- (1) The Education (School Attendance Order) Regulations 1995[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the first regulation bearing the number 1[^f00005] (citation and commencement)—

- (a) in the heading, for “Citation and commencement” substitute “Citation, commencement and application”;

- (b) the existing provision becomes paragraph (1);

- (c) after that paragraph insert—

> > (2) These Regulations apply in relation to England.

- (3) In the regulation bearing the number 2[^f00006], after “authority” insert “in England”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

Under section 437(3) of the Education Act 1996 a local authority is required to serve a school attendance order, which is to be in the prescribed form, on the parent of a child of compulsory school age who fails to satisfy the authority that the child is receiving suitable education, and the authority is of the opinion that it is expedient that the child should attend school. These Regulations prescribe the form of a school attendance order to be served by the local authority in Wales for the purposes of section 437(3).

Regulation 3 makes consequential amendments to the Education (School Attendance Order) Regulations 1995 that continue to apply in relation to England.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations.
