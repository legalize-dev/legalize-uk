# The North East Wales National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2008

Made: 30 June 2008

Coming into force: 1 July 2008

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by section 204(1) and paragraph 28(1) of Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001] and after completion of consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 2 to that Act[^f00002] hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and Commencement

###### 1

The Title of this Order is the North East Wales National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2008 and it comes into force on 1 July 2008.

#### Dissolution of NHS Trust

###### 2

The North East Wales National Health Service Trust established by the North East Wales National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998[^f00003] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Edwina Hart — Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 30 June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the North East Wales National Health Service Trust on 1 July 2008.
