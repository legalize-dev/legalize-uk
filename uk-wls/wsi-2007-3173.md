# The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2007

Made: 7 November 2007

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 8 November 2007

Coming into force: 30 November 2007

There has been open and transparent consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00006] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2007, they come into force on 30 November 2007 and apply in relation to Wales.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00007] are revoked.

#### Consequential amendments to the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (Wales) Regulations 2004

###### 3

- (1) The English text of regulation 8 (inspection, seizure and detention of suspected animal feed) of the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (Wales) Regulations 2004[^f00008] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In paragraph (5) for the expression “Subject to paragraphs (5A), (5B) and (6)” substitute the expression “Subject to paragraph (6)”.

- (3) Paragraphs (5A) and (5B) are omitted.

#### Consequential amendment of the Official Feed and Food Controls (Wales) Regulations 2006

###### 4

In Schedule 2 (definition of relevant feed law) of the Official Feed and Food Controls (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00009], paragraph (f) is omitted.

## Signed

G. Thomas — Under authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 7 November 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations revoke the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2005 ([S.I.2005/1323 (W.97)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2005/1323), and make consequential amendments to the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (Wales) Regulations 2004 (S.I. [2004/3221 (W.277)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2004/3221)) and the Official Feed and Food Controls (Wales) Regulations 2006 (S.I. [2006/590 (W.66)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/590)).

2 The Regulations implement Commission Decision [2007/157/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0157) repealing Decision [2005/317/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0317) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism Bt 10 in maize products (OJ No. L68, 8.3.2007, p.8).

3 A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations because no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
