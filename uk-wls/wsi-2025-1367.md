# The Building Act 1984 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2025

Made: 12 December 2025

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 120(1) and 134(1)(b) of the Building Act 1984[^f00001].

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Building Act 1984 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2025.

#### Provision coming into force on 1 July 2026

###### 2

Section 33 of the Building Act 1984[^f00002] (tests for conformity with building regulations) comes into force on 1 July 2026.

## Signed

Rebecca Evans — Cabinet Secretary for Economy, Energy and Planning, one of the Welsh Ministers — 12 December 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the first commencement order made under the Building Act [1984 (c. 55)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/55) (“*the Act*”) in relation to Wales only. However, two commencement orders prior to the devolution of building safety to Wales have brought provisions of the Act into force in relation to England and Wales. This Order brings into force section 33 of the Act in relation to Wales. Section 33 makes provision in relation to testing and sampling for the purpose of ascertaining whether any provision of building regulations have been or may be contravened.
