# The Relocation Grants (Forms of Application) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2000

Made: 20th June 2000

Coming into force in accordance with Regulation 1 (1)

The National Assembly for Wales makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers given to the Secretary of State by sections 132(4) and 146(1) and (2) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00001] which are now vested in the National Assembly so far as exercisable in Wales[^f00002]:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Relocation Grants (Forms of Application) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force 14 days after the day on which they are made.

- (2) These Regulations apply to Wales only.

#### Amendments

###### 2

- (1) The form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) Regulations 1997[^f00003] is amended as specified in the English text of the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) The form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Welsh Form of Application) Regulations 1999[^f00004] is amended as specified in the Welsh text of the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 3

These Regulations shall not have effect in relation to applications for a grant made before 3rd April 2000.

## Explanatory note

(This note does not form part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) Regulations 1997 (“the 1997 Regulations”) and the Form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Welsh Form of Application) Regulations 1999 (“the 1999 Regulations”).

The Relocation Grants Regulations 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2764](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2764)), apply the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2890](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2890)) as they have effect from time to time, with the modifications prescribed. Amendments to the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 by the Housing Renewal Grants (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2000 ([S.I. 2000/973](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/973)(W.43) in so far as they apply in Wales, have accordingly necessitated amendments to the 1997 Regulations and the 1999 Regulations.
