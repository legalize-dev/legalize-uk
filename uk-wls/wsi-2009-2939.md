# The Contaminants in Food (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 2 November 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 5 November 2009

Coming into force: 27 November 2009

The Welsh Ministers, make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1)(a) and 17(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001].

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Contaminants in Food (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and they come into force on 27 November 2009.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment of the Contaminants in Food (Wales) Regulations 2009

###### 2

- (1) The Contaminants in Food (Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the Welsh text of regulation 2(1) for “Rhif XXX/2008” substitute “Rhif 124/2009”.

## Signed

Gwenda Thomas — Deputy Minister for Social Services, under authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 2 November 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Contaminants in Food (Wales) Regulations 2009 (“the Principal Regulations”) revoke and re-enact with changes the Contaminants in Food (Wales) Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/840 (W.73)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2007/840), as amended).

The Principal Regulations make provision for the execution and enforcement of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1881/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1881) setting maximum permitted levels (“MPLs”) for contaminants in foodstuffs (OJ No. L364, 20.12.2006, p.5).

They also provide for the execution and enforcement of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 124/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0124) (OJ No. L40, 11.2.2009, p.7) concerning MPLs for certain feed additives that may in specified circumstances occur in food.

These Regulations amend regulation 2(1) of the Principal Regulations to correct a typographical error in the Welsh text.
