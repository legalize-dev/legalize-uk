# The Children Act 2004 (Commencement No. 10) (Wales) Order 2025

Made: 7 March 2025

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by section 67(3) of the Children Act 2004[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order.

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Children Act 2004 (Commencement No. 10) (Wales) Order 2025.

#### Provisions coming into force on 10 March 2025

###### 2

Section 29 of the Children Act 2004[^f00003] (information databases: Wales) comes into force on 10 March 2025.

## Signed

Lynne Neagle — Cabinet Secretary for Education, one of the Welsh Ministers — 7 March 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, made by the Welsh Ministers, brings into force on 10 March 2025 section 29 of the Children Act 2004 (information databases: Wales). Section 29 provides for the establishment and operation of databases for the purpose of arrangements under section 25 or 28 of the Children Act 2004 or under section 175 of the Education Act 2002.
