# The Waste (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Wales) Regulations 2011

Made: 28 March 2011

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 28 March 2011

Coming into force: 29 March 2011

The Welsh Ministers are designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the prevention, reduction and management of waste.

The Welsh Ministers make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Title, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Waste (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Wales) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations—

- (a) come into force on 29 March 2011; and

- (b) apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment of the Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005

###### 2

The Schedule, which provides for amendment of the Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005 , has effect.

#### Amendment of the Landfill Allowances Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2004

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) of the Landfill Allowances Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2004 , in the definition of “*waste facility*” (“**cyfleuster gwastraff**”), for “Article 1(e) and (f) of Council Directive [75/442/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0442) on waste”, substitute “ Article 3(19) and (15) of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste ”.

#### Notes

Attribution of the hazardous properties “toxic” (and “very toxic”), “harmful”, “corrosive”, “irritant”, “carcinogenic”, “toxic to reproduction”, “mutagenic” and “ecotoxic” is made on the basis of the criteria laid down by Annex VI, to Council Directive [67/548/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1967/0548) of 27 June 1967 on the approximation of laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous substances.
