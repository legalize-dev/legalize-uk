# The Feeding Stuffs and the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2001

Made: 16th October 2001

Coming into force: 3rd November 2001

In exercise of the powers conferred on Ministers of the Crown by sections 66(1), 68(1) and (1A), 69(1), 74A and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970,which are now exercisable in Wales by the National Assembly for Wales, and after consultation as required by section 84(1) of that Act with the persons or organisations appearing to represent the interests concerned, and being designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2) (in so far as these Regulations cannot be made under the powers in the Agriculture Act 1970 specified above), the National Assembly for Wales makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs and the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2001, shall apply to Wales only and shall come into force on 3rd November 2001.

## Explanatory note

*(This note does not form part of the Regulations)*

1These Regulations, which apply to Wales only and come into force on 3rd November 2001, amend the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001 and the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) Regulations 1999 insofar as they apply to Wales.

2The Regulations implement —

aCommission Decision [2000/285/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0285) amending Decision [91/516/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1991/0516) which established a list of ingredients whose use is prohibited in feedingstuffs (OJ No. L94, 14.4.2000, p. 43), and

bDirective [2000/16/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0016) of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Directive [79/373/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0373) on the marketing of compound feedingstuffs (OJ No. L105, 3.5.2000, p. 36), and

cCouncil Directive [96/25/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0025) on the circulation of feed materials (OJ No. 125, 23.5.1996, p.35).

3The Regulations —

amodify measures on the marketing of compound feed stuffs and the circulation of feed materials (regulation 6);

bmodify measures on the control of compound feeding stuffs to prohibit the use of material from waste treatment processes (regulation 10);

cadd the three new (directly applicable) Commission Regulations specified below to the list, in Part IX of Schedule 3 to the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001 (regulation 14). The new Commission Regulations are —

dmake minor typographical and other miscellaneous amendments to existing legislation.

4A regulatory appraisal for these Regulations has been prepared pursuant to section 65 of the Government of Wales Act 1998 and placed in the library of the National Assembly for Wales. Copies may be obtained from the Food Standards Agency, 1st Floor, Southgate House, Cardiff CF10 1EW.
