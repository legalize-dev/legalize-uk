# The Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made: 10 January 2006

Coming into force: 11 January 2006

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 537A(1), (2) and (4) and 569(4) and (5) of the Education Act 1996[^f00001] and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales[^f00002], the National Assembly for Wales hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and they come into force on 11 January 2006.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (Wales) Regulations 2003[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2, in the appropriate place in alphabetical order insert the following new definitions—

> > - “school” (“*ysgol*”) means a school maintained by a local education authority, but does not include a pupil referral unit;
> - “school year” (“*blwyddyn ysgol*”) means the period beginning with the first school term to begin after July and ending with the beginning of the first such term to begin after the following July;

- (3) In regulation 2, for the definition of “sixth form pupil” substitute—

> > - “sixth form pupil” (“*disgybl chweched dosbarth*”) means a pupil at a school who is in receipt of education suitable to the requirements of pupils above compulsory school age;

- (4) In Part 1 of the Schedule, in paragraph 5, omit “and, if so, with whom”.

- (5) In Part 2 of the Schedule, in paragraph 1 for “sixth form pupil at the school” substitute “person who is, or was at any previous time during the school year in which the request for the information was made, a pupil in the school’s sixth form”.

- (6) In Part 2 of the Schedule, in paragraph 1(c) after “beginning” insert “and end”.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004].

D. Elis-Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 10 January 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make minor amendments to the Education (Information About Individual Pupils) (Wales) Regulations 2003 (“the 2003 Regulations”) by—

- (a) adding a new definitions of “school”, substituting a new definition of “sixth form pupil”, and adding a new definition of “school year”;

- (b) slightly altering the wording of paragraph 5 of Part 1 of the Schedule to the 2003 Regulations so that the governing body of a school will no longer be required to specify with whom a pupil speaks Welsh at home;

- (c) altering the wording of paragraph 1 of Part 2 of that Schedule so that information which the governing body may be required to provide about sixth form pupils can include information about pupils who have left the school during the course of the school year; and

- (d) slightly altering the wording of paragraph 21(c) of that Part so that the information which may be required about pupils' basic skills needs for literacy and numeracy will now include pupils' needs at the end, as well as at the beginning, of their learning activities programme.
