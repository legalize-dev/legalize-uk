# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2021

Made: 16 September 2021

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 41(3) and (4)(a) of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2016[^f00001].

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2021.

- (2) In this Order, “the 2016 Act” means the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2016.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1 January 2022

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2016 Act come into force on 1 January 2022 so far as they are not already in force—

- (a) section 11;

- (b) section 28.

#### Provision coming into force on 1 February 2022

###### 3

Section 18 of the 2016 Act comes into force on 1 February 2022.

## Signed

Dawn Bowden — Deputy Minister for Arts and Sport and Chief Whip under authority of the Minister for Economy, one of the Welsh Ministers — 16 September 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the second commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2016 (“the 2016 Act”).

Article 2 of this Order brings the following provisions of the 2016 Act into force on 1 January 2022, so far as these provisions are not already in force—

- (a) section 11 (heritage partnership agreements);

- (b) section 28 (heritage partnership agreements).

Article 3 of this Order brings section 18 (register of historic parks and gardens) of the 2016 Act into force on 1 February 2022.
