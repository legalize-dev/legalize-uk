# The Seeds and Vegetable Plant Material (Nomenclature Changes) (Wales) Regulations 2014

Made: 5 March 2014

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 7 March 2014

Coming into force: 31 March 2014

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and it appears to the Welsh Ministers that it is expedient for the reference in these Regulations to Annex II to Council Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072)[^f00005] on the marketing of vegetable propagating and planting material, other than seed to be construed as a reference to that Annex as amended from time to time.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Seeds and Vegetable Plant Material (Nomenclature Changes) (Wales) Regulations 2014.

- (2) They apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 31 March 2014.

#### Amendment of the Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012

###### 2

- (1) The Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012[^f00006] are amended as follows.

- (2) In column 1 (Plants to which the Regulations apply) of Schedule 1 (Seed to which these Regulations apply) for *“Lycopersiconesculentum* Mill”, substitute—

> > *Solanum lycopersicum* L.

#### Amendment of the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations 1995

###### 3

- (1) The Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations 1995[^f00007] are amended in relation to Wales as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (Interpretation)—

- (a) Omit the definition of “Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033)”;

- (b) After the definition of “Directive [93/61/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0061)”, insert—

> > - “Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072)” means Council Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072) on the marketing of vegetable propagating and planting material, other than seed;

- (3) In regulation 3(1) (Plant material to which these Regulations apply), for sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) substitute—

> > (a) plant material of the genera and species set out in Annex II as amended from time to time, to Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072) and their hybrids; and
> (b) rootstocks and other parts of plants of other genera or species and their hybrids if plant material of a genus or species set out in Annex II, as amended from time to time, to Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072) or of a hybrid of such a genus or species is or is to be grafted on to them.

- (4) In regulation 5(d) (Quality requirements for plant material), for sub-paragraph (ii) substitute—

> > (ii) it belongs to a variety officially accepted in a member State in accordance with Article 9 of Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072); and

## Signed

Alun Davies — one of the Welsh Ministers — Minister for Natural Resources and Food, — 5 March 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012 ([S.I 2012/245 (W.39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2012/245)) and the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/2652](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2652)) (“the Vegetable Plant Regulations”) in relation to Wales.

These Regulations implement Commission Implementing Directive 2013/45/EU (OJ No L 213, 8.8.2013 p.20) which amends Directive [2002/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0055) (OJ No L 193, 20.7.2002, p.33), Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072) (OJ No L 205, 1.8.2008, p.28) and Directive [2009/145/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0145) (OJ No L 312, 27.11.2009, p.44) as regards the botanical name of tomato to reflect revisions to the International Code of Botanical Nomenclature.

Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033) (OJ No L 157, 10.6.1992, p.1) has been repealed by Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072) on the marketing of vegetable propagating and planting material, other than seed. The Vegetable Plant Regulations are amended to reflect this repeal and now apply to plant material of the genera and species listed in Annex II as amended from time to time to Directive [2008/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0072).

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations.
