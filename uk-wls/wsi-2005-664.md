# The Local Government (Best Value Performance Indicators) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 2005

Made: 8 March 2005

Coming into force: 1 April 2005

The National Assembly for Wales, having consulted those persons which it is required, by section 4(3) of the Local Government Act 1999[^f00001], to consult, makes the following order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 4(1)(a) and (2) and section 29(1) of that Act.

#### Name, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The name of this order is the Local Government (Best Value Performance Indicators) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 2005 and it comes into force on the 1 April 2005.

- (2) This order applies only in relation to Wales.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Local Government (Best Value Performance Indicators) (Wales) Order 2002[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00003].

D. Elis-Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 8 March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order revokes the Local Government (Best Value Performance Indicators) (Wales) Order 2002 which specifies, for Wales, performance indicators by reference to which the performance of county councils, county borough councils and national park authorities (as best value authorities), in exercising their functions, were to be measured from the 1 April 2002. This order will come into force on the 1 April 2005.

The Local Government (Best Value Performance Indicators) (Wales) Order 2005 specifies, for Wales, performance indicators by reference to which the performance of county councils and county borough councils, in exercising their functions, will be measured from 1 April 2005.
