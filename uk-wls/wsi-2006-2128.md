# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made: 29 July 2006

Coming into force: 1 August 2006

The National Assembly for Wales is designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

- (3) These Regulations come into force on 1 August 2006.

#### Amendment to the Animal and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00003] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 1(3)—

- (a) in the definition of “Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425)” for “paragraph 4(a)” substitute “paragraph 5(a)”; and

- (b) in the definition of “Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496)” for “paragraph 4(b)”, substitute “paragraph 5(b)”.

- (3) In regulation 16(4), for “1st August 2006”, substitute “1 January 2007”.

- (4) In regulation 19(9), after “an inspector” add “in the case of sub-paragraphs (a) and (b), and a veterinary inspector in the case of sub-paragraph (c)”.

- (5) In regulation 21(3), for “the veterinarian inspector”, substitute “the veterinary inspector”.

- (6) In regulation 22(6), delete “22 or”.

- (7) In regulation 28(2) for “paragraph 28”, substitute “paragraph (1)”.

- (8) In regulation 35(1), after “Part IV” and before “of”, insert “ of Schedule 8 to ”.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

Rhodri Morgan — The First Minister of the National Assembly — 29 July 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2006 (S.I. [2006/1536 (W. 153)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/1536)) (“the principal Regulations”).

Regulation 2(3) amends the principal Regulations to give effect to the extension of the European Union ban under Commission Decision [2005/760/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0760) (OJ L. No. 285, 28.10.2005, p.52, as amended by Commission Decision [2006/522/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0522), OJ No. L 205, 27.7.2006, p.28) on the import of captive birds from third countries until 1 January 2007.

Regulation 2 of these Regulations also makes minor adjustments and corrections to other provisions in the principal Regulations.
