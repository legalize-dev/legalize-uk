# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made: 29 July 2006

Coming into force: 1 August 2006

The National Assembly for Wales is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

- (3) These Regulations come into force on 1 August 2006.

#### Amendment to the Animal and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2006 are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 1(3)—

- (a) in the definition of “Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1990/425)” for “paragraph 4(a)” substitute “ paragraph 5(a) ”; and

- (b) in the definition of “Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/496)” for “paragraph 4(b)”, substitute “ paragraph 5(b) ”.

- (3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

- (4) In regulation 19(9), after “an inspector” add “ in the case of sub-paragraphs (a) and (b), and a veterinary inspector in the case of sub-paragraph (c) ”.

- (5) In regulation 21(3), for “the veterinarian inspector”, substitute “ the veterinary inspector ”.

- (6) In regulation 22(6), delete “22 or”.

- (7) In regulation 28(2) for “paragraph 28”, substitute “ paragraph (1) ”.

- (8) In regulation 35(1), after “Part IV” and before “of”, insert “ of Schedule 8 to ”.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998

Rhodri Morgan — The First Minister of the National Assembly — 2006-07-29
