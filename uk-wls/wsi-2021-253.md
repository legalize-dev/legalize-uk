# The Business Tenancies (Extension of Protection from Forfeiture etc.) (Wales) (Coronavirus) Regulations 2021

Made: 5 March 2021

Laid before Senedd Cymru: 9 March 2021

Coming into force: 31 March 2021

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 82(12) of the Coronavirus Act 2020 .

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

- (1) Enw’r Rheoliadau hyn yw Rheoliadau Tenantiaethau Busnes (Estyn Gwarchodaeth rhag Fforffediad etc.) (Cymru) (Coronafeirws) 2021.

- (2) Daw’r Rheoliadau hyn i rym ar 31 Mawrth 2021.

#### Extension of relevant period providing protection from forfeiture etc.

###### 2

At ddibenion adran 82 (tenantiaethau busnes yng Nghymru a Lloegr: gwarchodaeth rhag fforffediad etc.) o Ddeddf y Coronafeirws 2020, mae’r “relevant period”, fel y’i diffinnir yn is-adran (12) o’r adran honno yn gorffen, o ran Cymru, â 30 Mehefin 2021.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Business Tenancies (Extension of Protection from Forfeiture etc.) (Wales) (Coronavirus) (No. 3) Regulations 2020 are revoked.

## Signed

Ken Skates — Minister for Economy, Transport and North Wales, one of the Welsh Ministers — 2021-03-05
