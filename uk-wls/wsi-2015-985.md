# The Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2015

Made: 25 March 2015

Coming into force: 16:00 on 25th March 2015

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the power conferred upon them by section 156(2) of the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2015.

- (2) In this Order “Measure” means the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 108 of the Measure (enforcement policy document) comes into force at 16:00 on 25 March 2015.

## Signed

Carwyn Jones — First Minister of Wales — 25 March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 156(2) of the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011 (“the Measure”). Article 2 of this Order brings section 108 of the Measure into force which requires the Welsh Language Commissioner to have an enforcement policy document.
