# The Honey (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 28 February 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 29 February 2008

Coming into force: 24 March 2008

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002], as last amended by Regulation [(EC) No. 1642/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1642) of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations are called the Honey (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 24 March 2008.

#### Amendment of the Honey (Wales) Regulations 2003

###### 2

The Honey (Wales) Regulation 2003[^f00004] are amended as provided in regulation 3.

###### 3

In Schedule 2 for the words “not more than 0.8 mS/cm” insert the following—

> > - not less than 0.8 mS/cm

## Signed

Gwenda Thomas — Under authority for the Minister of Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers. — 28 February 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations make minor amendments to the Honey (Wales) Regulations 2003 (S.I. [2003/3044 (W.288)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2003/3044). They implement the Corrigendum to EC Directive [2001/110](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/110) which corrected an error to Annex II of that Directive

2 A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
