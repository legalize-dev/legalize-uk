# The Business Tenancies (Extension of Protection from Forfeiture etc.) (Wales) (Coronavirus) Regulations 2020

Made: 17 June 2020

Laid before Senedd Cymru: 19 June 2020

Coming into force: 30 June 2020

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 82(12) of the Coronavirus Act 2020 .

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

- (1) Enw’r Rheoliadau hyn yw Rheoliadau Tenantiaethau Busnes (Estyn Gwarchodaeth rhag Fforffediad etc.) (Cymru) (Coronafeirws) 2020.

- (2) Dawʼr Rheoliadau hyn i rym ar 30 Mehefin 2020.

#### Extension of relevant period providing protection from forfeiture etc.

###### 2

At ddibenion adran 82 (tenantiaethau busnes yng Nghymru a Lloegr: gwarchodaeth rhag fforffediad etc.) o Ddeddf y Coronafeirws 2020, mae’r “relevant period” fel y’i diffinnir yn is-adran (12) o’r adran honno yn dod i ben, o ran Cymru, ar 30 Medi 2020.

## Signed

Ken Skates — Minister for Economy, Transport and North Wales, one of the Welsh Ministers — 2020-06-17
