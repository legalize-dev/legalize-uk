# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 2012

Made: 28 November 2012

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 11 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and sections 1(1), (2) and (10) and 24(1) and (3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00002], no longer being of the opinion that there exists or may exist circumstances which are likely to create a hazard to human health through human consumption of food as specified in the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) Order 1991[^f00003], make the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) (Revocation) Order 2012. It applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Orders specified in the Schedule are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

1 This Order revokes, in relation to Wales, the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/5)), the Export of Sheep (Prohibition) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/58)) and related orders. The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) Order 1991 contains emergency prohibitions restricting various activities in order to prevent human consumption of food which is derived from sheep in certain designated areas, and which has been, or may have been rendered unsuitable for that purpose in consequence of the escape of radioactive substances from a nuclear reactor situated at Chernobyl in the Ukraine. The Export of Sheep (Prohibition) Order 1991 contains related restrictions on exporting to the EU, sheep which have been moved from areas subject to these emergency prohibitions. The effect of this Order is to remove the emergency prohibitions from the remaining areas which are subject to restriction and lift the restrictions on exporting to the EU, sheep which have been moved from these areas.

2 A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as it has no impact on the costs of business.
