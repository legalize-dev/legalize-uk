# The Purity Criteria for Colours, Sweeteners and Miscellaneous Food Additives (Wales) Regulations 2009

Made: 27 April 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 29 April 2009

Coming into force: 25 May 2009

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1)(a) and 17(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990 :

In accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act, the Welsh Ministers have had regard to the relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency.

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Purity Criteria for Colours, Sweeteners and Miscellaneous Food Additives (Wales) Regulations 2009 and they come into force on 25 May 2009.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Colours in Food Regulations 1995 are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” and substitute the following definition—

> > “*Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128)*” means Commission Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128) laying down specific purity criteria concerning colours for use in foodstuffs ;

- (b) in the definition of “permitted colour”, for the expression “the annex to Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” substitute the expression “ Annex 1 to Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128) ”.

#### Amendment of the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995

###### 3

- (1) The Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995 are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation) —

- (a) omit the definition “Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” and substitute the following definition—

> > “*Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060)*” means Commission Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060) laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs ;

- (b) in the definition of “permitted sweetener”, for the expression “the annex to Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” substitute the expression “ Annex 1 to Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060) ”.

#### Amendment of the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995

###### 4

- (1) The Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995 are amended in accordance with paragraph (2);

- (2) In regulation 2 (Interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition “Directive [96/77/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0077)” and substitute the following definition—

> > “*Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084)*” means Commission Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084) laying down specific purity criteria on food additives other than colours and sweeteners as that Directive was before the amendments made by Commission Directive [2009/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0010) ;

- (b) in the definition of “purity criteria”, in sub-paragraph (a) for the expression “the Annex to Directive [96/77/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0077)” substitute the expression “ Annex I to Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084) ”.

## Signed

G. Thomas — Deputy Minister for Social Services under authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 27 April 2009
