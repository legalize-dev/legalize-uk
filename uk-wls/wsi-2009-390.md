# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 26 February 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 27 February 2009

Coming into force: 20 March 2009

The Welsh Ministers are designated for the purposes of making Regulations under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community.

The Welsh Ministers make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009, they apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 20 March 2009.

#### Amendment to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2006 are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 1(2) delete the definition of “bluetongue susceptible animal”.

- (3) For regulation 1(5) substitute—

> > (5) Unless specifically defined in these Regulations, any expression used in these Regulations has the meaning it bears in—
>   (a) Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425) concerning veterinary and zootechnical checks applicable in intra-Community trade in certain live animals and products with a view to the completion of the single market (OJ No. L224, 18.8.90, p.29 as last amended by Directive [2002/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0033) of the European Parliament and of the Council, OJ No. L315, 19.11.2002, p.14); and
>   (b) Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) laying down the principles governing the organisation of veterinary checks on animals entering the Community from third countries (OJ No. L268, 24.9.1991, p.56 as last amended by Council Directive [2006/104/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0104), OJ No. L363, 20.12.2006, p.352 ).

- (4) In regulation 35 delete “and subject to paragraph (1),”.

- (5) Schedule 1 is deleted.

- (6) For Schedules 2, 3, and 7 to those Regulations, substitute the replacements set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Amendment to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales)(Amendment) Regulations 2007

###### 3

Regulation 2(2) of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 is revoked.
