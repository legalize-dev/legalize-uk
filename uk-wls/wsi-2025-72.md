# The Environment (Air Quality and Soundscapes) (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2025

Made: 23 January 2025

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power contained in section 30(3) of the Environment (Air Quality and Soundscapes) (Wales) Act 2024[^f00001].

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Environment (Air Quality and Soundscapes) (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2025.

- (2) In this Order, “*the Act*” means the Environment (Air Quality and Soundscapes) (Wales) Act 2024.

#### Provision coming into force on 31 January 2025

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 11 (promoting active travel as a way of reducing or limiting air pollution) of the Act is 31 January 2025.

## Signed

Ken Skates — Cabinet Secretary for Transport and North Wales, one of the Welsh Ministers — 23 January 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the first commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Environment (Air Quality and Soundscapes) (Wales) Act 2024 (“*the Act*”).

This Order brings section 11 (promoting active travel as a way of reducing or limiting air pollution) of the Act into force on 31 January 2025.
