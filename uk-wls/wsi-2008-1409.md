# The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study) (Wales) Order 2008

Made: 30 May 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 2 June 2008

Coming into force: 1 August 2008 except as provided for in articles 4(1), 5(1), 6(1), 7(1), 8(1), 9(1), 10(1), 11(1), 12(1), 13(1), 14(1), 15(1) and (2)

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by section 108(3)(a) and (b), (5) and section 210 of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] and having made such arrangements for consultation as they consider appropriate in accordance with section 117 of the Education Act 2002 make the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study) (Wales) Order 2008 and, subject to articles 4(1), 5(1), 6(1), 7(1), 8(1), 9(1), 10(1), 11(1), 12(1), 13(1), 14(1) and 15(1) and (2) below, it comes into force on 1 August 2008.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In this Order—

- “the art and design document” (“*y ddogfen celf a dylunio*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Art and design in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00003];

- “the design and technology document” (“*y ddogfen dylunio a thechnoleg*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Design and technology in the National Curriculum for Wales”([^f00004]

- “the English document” (“*y ddogfen Saesneg*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “English in the National Curriculum for Wales”([^f00005]

- “the geography document” (“*y ddogfen ddaearyddiaeth*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Geography in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00006];

- “the history document” (“*y ddogfen hanes*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “History in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00007];

- “the information and communication technology document” (“*y ddogfen technoleg gwybodaeth a chyfathrebu*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Information and communication technology in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00008];

- “the mathematics document” (“*y ddogfen fathemateg*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Mathematics in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00009];

- “the modern foreign languages document” (“*y ddogfen ieithoedd tramor modern*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Modern foreign languages in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00010];

- “the music document” (“*y ddogfen gerddoriaeth*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Music in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00011];

- “the physical education document” (“*y ddogfen addysg gorfforol*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Physical education in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00012];

- “the science document” (“*y ddogfen wyddoniaeth*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Science in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00013];

- “the Welsh document” (“*y ddogfen Gymraeg*”) means the document published by the Welsh Ministers in January 2008 entitled “Welsh in the National Curriculum for Wales”[^f00014]; and

references to the first, second, third and fourth key stages are to be interpreted in accordance with section 103 of the Education Act 2002.

#### Revocation and transitional provisions

###### 3

- (1) The Orders listed in the Schedule are revoked on 1 August 2008.

- (2) Despite paragraph (1) the provisions of the Orders listed in the Schedule continue to apply to pupils in the second, third and fourth key stages until such time as the provisions of this Order apply to them.

#### Art and design

###### 4

- (1) The provisions of this Order insofar as they relate to the art and design document come into force on—

- (a) 1 August 2008 in respect of pupils in the first three years of the second key stage and the first two years of the third key stage; and

- (b) 1 August 2009 in respect of pupils in the fourth year of the second key stage and the third year of the third key stage.

- (2) The provisions relating to the attainment target and programmes of study set out in the art and design document have effect for the purpose of specifying the attainment target and programmes of study in relation to art and design.

- (3) The examples printed in italics in the art and design document (which serve to illustrate the programmes of study described therein) do not form part of the provision made by this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The National Curriculum for Wales will be revised from 1 August 2008. This Order gives legal effect to the programmes of study, which set out what pupils should be taught and attainment targets for them. Details of these are set out in twelve documents called:

- “Art and design in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Design and technology in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “English in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Geography in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “History in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Information and communication technology in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Mathematics in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Modern foreign languages in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Music in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Physical education in the National Curriculum for Wales”;

- “Science in the National Curriculum for Wales”; and

- “Welsh in the National Curriculum for Wales”.

This Order also sets out arrangements for phasing in the revised National Curriculum.
