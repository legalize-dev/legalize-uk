# The Non-Domestic Rating (Electric Vehicle Charging Point Relief) (Wales) Regulations 2026

Made: 25 November 2026

Coming into force: 1 April 2027

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [paragraph 8B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/schedule/4ZA/paragraph/8B)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/schedule/4ZA/paragraph/8B) of [Schedule 4ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/schedule/4ZA) and [paragraph 2B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/schedule/4ZB/paragraph/2B)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/schedule/4ZB/paragraph/2B) of [Schedule 4ZB](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/schedule/4ZB) to the [Local Government Finance Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/contents)[^f00001].

In accordance with the Senedd approval procedure applied by section 143A(5)(h) and (i) of that Act[^f00002], a draft of this Welsh statutory instrument was laid before, and approved by resolution of, Senedd Cymru.

#### Title, coming into force, expiry and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Non-Domestic Rating (Electric Vehicle Charging Point Relief) (Wales) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 1 April 2027 and have effect for the period beginning with that date and ending with 31 March 2036.

- (3) In these Regulations—

- “*the Act*” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the [Local Government Finance Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/contents);

- “*electric vehicle charging point*” (“*man gwefru​ cerbydau trydan*”) means a facility for charging a road vehicle that can be propelled by electrical power (whether or not it can also be propelled by another kind of power);

- “*hereditament*” (“*hereditament*”) has the meaning given by [section 64](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/section/64) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41/contents)[^f00003].

#### Conditions for full relief - occupied hereditaments

###### 2

- (1) The conditions to be satisfied for the purposes of paragraph 8B(2) of Schedule 4ZA to the Act are that the hereditament—

- (a) consists wholly or mainly of an electric vehicle charging point and associated parking bay, and

- (b) is accessible and open to the public for public use.

- (2) A hereditament continues to satisfy these conditions where the electric vehicle charging point temporarily ceases to operate and is unavailable for public use.

#### Conditions for full relief - unoccupied hereditaments

###### 3

The conditions to be satisfied for the purposes of paragraph 2B(2) of Schedule 4ZB to the Act are that, when next occupied, the hereditament will—

- (a) consist wholly or mainly of an electric vehicle charging point and associated parking bay, and

- (b) be accessible and open to the public for public use.

## Signed

Elin Jones — Cabinet Minister for Finance, one of the Welsh Ministers — 25 November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations, which apply in relation to Wales only, set out the conditions to be satisfied for an electric vehicle charging point and associated parking bay to be eligible for full relief from non-domestic rating liability.

Regulation 2 sets out the conditions to be met under paragraph 8B(2) of Schedule 4ZA to the Local Government Finance Act 1988 where the hereditament is occupied. Regulation 3 sets out the conditions to be met under paragraph 2B(2) of Schedule 4ZB to that Act where the hereditament is unoccupied.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations. A copy can be obtained from the Non-Domestic Rates Policy and Reform Division, Welsh Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ.
