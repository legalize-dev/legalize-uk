# The Beef and Pig Carcase Classification (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made: 26 March 2012

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 28 March 2012

Coming into force: 18 April 2012

The Welsh Ministers being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the EU, make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Beef and Pig Carcase Classification (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

- (3) These Regulations come into force on 18 April 2012.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations, “the 2011 Regulations” (“*Rheoliadau 2011*”) means the Beef and Pig Carcase Classification (Wales) Regulations 2011[^f00003].

#### Amendment of regulation 2(1) of the 2011 Regulations

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) of the 2011 Regulations, in the Welsh text, after the definition of “darpariaeth eidion Ewropeaidd” (“*European beef provision*”), insert—

> > - ystyr “darpariaeth moch Ewropeaidd” (“*European pig provision*”) yw darpariaeth a bennir yng ngholofn 2 o Atodlen 2, y disgrifir ei chynnwys yng ngholofn 3 o'r Atodlen honno;

## Signed

Alun Davies — Deputy Minister for Agriculture, Food, Fisheries and European Programmes, under authority of the Minister for Business, Enterprise, Technology and Science, one of the Welsh Ministers — 26 March 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Beef and Pig Carcase Classification (Wales) Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1826 (W.198)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2011/1826)) (“the 2011 Regulations”).

Specifically these Regulations amend section 2(1) in the Welsh text of the 2011 Regulations to insert a definition of “darpariaeth moch Ewropeaidd” (“European pig provision”).
