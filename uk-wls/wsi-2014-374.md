# The Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2014

Made: 20 February 2014

Coming into force: 21 February 2014

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 13(2) and 45(5) of the Housing Act 1988[^f00001] and which are now vested[^f00002] in the Welsh Ministers so far as exercisable in relation to Wales, make the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2014.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 21 February 2014.

- (3) These Regulations apply in relation to premises situated in Wales.

#### Amendment of Prescribed Forms

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) Regulations 1997 (Forms prescribed for the purposes of Part I of the Housing Act 1988)[^f00003]—

- (a) in paragraph 2 of the Guidance Notes to Form 4D (landlord’s notice proposing a new rent under an assured periodic tenancy of premises situated in Wales)[^f00004] omit from “You should also notify” to “claiming benefit” and substitute—

> > You are required to also notify the Housing Benefit office in your local authority if you are claiming a Benefit or the Department for Work and Pensions if you are claiming Universal Credit. The Gov.UK website provides further advice: https://www.gov.uk

; and

- (b) in paragraph 2 of the Guidance Notes to Form 4E (landlord’s or licensor’s notice proposing a new rent or licence fee under an assured agricultural occupancy of premises situated in Wales)[^f00005] omit from “You should also notify” to “claiming benefit” and substitute—

> > You are required to also notify the Housing Benefit office in your local authority if you are claiming a Benefit or the Department for Work and Pensions if you are claiming Universal Credit. The Gov.uk website provides further advice: https://www.gov.uk

## Signed

Carl Sargeant — Minister for Housing and regeneration, one of the Welsh Ministers — 20 February 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to certain forms which are prescribed in the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) Regulations 1997[^f00006], as amended[^f00007].

These Regulations make amendments in consequence of the Welfare Reform Act [2012 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/5) and insert references in those forms to universal credit.
