# The Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010 (Commencement No.2, Savings and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) and (Consequential Amendments) Order 2011

Made: 1 March 2011

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 74(2)(c) and 75(3) of the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010 (Commencement No.2, Savings and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) and (Consequential Amendments) Order 2011.

- (2) In this Order “the Commencement No. 2 Order” (“*Gorchymyn Cychwyn Rhif 2*”) means the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010 (Commencement No.2, Savings and Transitional Provisions) Order 2010[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the Commencement No.2 Order

###### 2

- (1) With effect from 4 March 2011, the Commencement No.2 Order is amended as follows.

- (2) After article 4, insert the following—

> > (5) Schedule 4, which contains amendments to miscellaneous subordinate legislation in consequence of the commencement of the provisions specified in Schedule 1, has effect and comes into force on 1 April 2011.

- (3) After Schedule 3, insert Schedule 4 which is set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010 (Commencement No.2, Savings and Transitional Provisions) Order 2010 (“the Commencement No.2 Order”) to make provision for amendments to secondary legislation consequential upon the repeal of Part XA of and Schedule 9A to the Children Act 1989.

Article 2 inserts article 5 (consequential amendments to miscellaneous subordinate legislation) and Schedule 4 into the Commencement No. 2 Order.
