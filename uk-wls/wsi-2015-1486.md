# The Building (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2015

Made: 7 July 2015

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 9 July 2015

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(3)

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 1 and 34 of and paragraphs 1, 2, 4, 4A, 7, 8 and 10 of Schedule 1 to the Building Act 1984[^f00001], now exercisable by them[^f00002], having consulted, in accordance with section 14(7) of that Act, the Building Regulations Advisory Committee for Wales and such other bodies as appear to them to be representative of the interests concerned, make the following Regulations:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Building (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales but do not apply in relation to excepted energy buildings in Wales.

- (3) These Regulations come into force on the following dates—

- (a) this regulation, regulations 2, 3, 4(a) and the Schedule on 31 July 2015; and

- (b) regulation 4(b) on 31 December 2015.

- (4) In this regulation “excepted energy building” (“*adeilad ynni a eithrir*”) has the meaning given by the Schedule to the Welsh Ministers (Transfer of Functions) (No. 2) Order 2009[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the Building Regulations 2010

###### 2

The Building Regulations 2010[^f00004] are amended as follows.

###### 3

For the Table in Schedule 3 (self-certification schemes and exemptions from requirement to give building notice or deposit full plans) substitute the Table as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 4

In regulation 43(4) (pressure testing)—

- (a) after “ the British Institute of Non-Destructive Testing” insert “, the Independent Airtightness Testing Scheme Limited[^f00005]”; and

- (b) omit “the British Institute of Non-Destructive Testing,”.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Carl Sargeant — Minister for Natural Resources, one of the Welsh Ministers — 7 July 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Building Regulations 2010 (“the 2010 Regulations”) in relation to Wales.

Regulation 3 substitutes the Table in Schedule 3 to the 2010 Regulations for the revised and updated Table in the Schedule to these Regulations.

The Table in Schedule 3 (self-certification schemes and exemptions from requirement to give building notice or deposit full plans) to the 2010 Regulations lists all the bodies which have been authorised to operate competent person self-certification schemes for various types of work. Installers who are registered with these schemes are authorised to certify their own work as compliant with the 2010 Regulations without having it checked by a building control body.

Regulation 4 amends regulation 43(4) (pressure testing) of the 2010 Regulations in relation to the bodies who may certify compliance with that regulation.

A regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations. Copies are available from the Welsh Government at Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ and on the Welsh Government’s website at www.wales.gov.uk.
