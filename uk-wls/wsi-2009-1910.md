# The Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009

Made: 15 July 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 20 July 2009

Coming into force: 10 August 2009

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, the Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2009; they apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 10 August 2009.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In regulation 3 (Restricted animal, restricted poultry and restricted meat: definitions) of the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00003], in paragraph 8 of the English version, for “protected” substitute “protection”.

## Signed

Elin Jones — Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 15 July 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 3 of the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008 No. 1275](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1275)), by substituting “protection” for “protected” in paragraph 8 of the English version.
