There are other amendments not relevant to these Regulations. The powers under sections 5 and 40 are now vested in the Welsh Ministers so far as they are exercisable in relation to Wales. They were transferred to the National Assembly for Wales by article 2 of, and Schedule 1 to, the National Assembly for Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/672](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/672)) and subsequently transferred to the Welsh Ministers by section 162 of, and paragraph 30 of Schedule 11 to, the Government of Wales Act [2006 (c.32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/32) by virtue of being “relevant Assembly functions” as defined in paragraph 30(2).
