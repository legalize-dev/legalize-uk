# The Education Maintenance Allowances (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2009

Made: 30 March 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 1 April 2009

Coming into force: 24 April 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the National Assembly for Wales by section 181 of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] and now exercisable by them[^f00002], make the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Education Maintenance Allowances (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 24 April 2009 and apply in relation to Wales.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Education Maintenance Allowances (Wales) Regulations 2007[^f00003] are revoked.

## Signed

Jane Hutt — Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 30 March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Education Maintenance Allowances (Wales) Regulations 2007.
