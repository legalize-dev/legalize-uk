# The Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 13) (Wales) Order 2010

Made: 7 March 2010

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the power conferred upon the National Assembly for Wales by section 216(3), (4) and (5) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 13) (Wales) Order 2010.

###### 2

In this Order, the “2002 Act” (“*Deddf 2002*”) means the Education Act 2002.

#### Appointed day

###### 3

The following provisions of the 2002 Act come into force on 12 April 2010:

- (a) section 19(8) (governing bodies of maintained schools);

- (b) section 20(4) (instruments of government of governing bodies of maintained schools);

- (c) section 24 (federation of schools); and

- (d) section 25 (federations: supplementary provisions).

## Signed

Leighton Andrews — Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 7 March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12 April 2010 provisions of the Education Act 2002 specified in article 3 of the Order. These are the provisions that relate to the federation of schools.
