# The Tuberculosis (Testing and Powers of Entry) (Wales) Order 2008

Made: 21 October 2008

Coming into force: 22 October 2008

A draft of this Order was laid before the National Assembly for Wales under section 62D of the Animal Health Act 1981 and has been approved by a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales.

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Tuberculosis (Testing and Powers of Entry) (Wales) Order 2008; it applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on the day after it is made.

#### Specification of “disease”

###### 2

Tuberculosis is specified as a disease (“*clefyd*”) for the purpose of section 62D (tests and samples: power of entry) of the Animal Health Act 1981.

## Signed

Elin Jones — Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 21 October 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies tuberculosis as a disease for the purposes of section 62D of the Animal Health Act 1981.
