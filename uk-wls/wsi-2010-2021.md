# The Local Land Charges (Amendment) (Wales) Rules 2010

Made: 8 August 2010

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 10 August 2010

Coming into force: 31 August 2010

The Welsh Ministers make the following Rules in exercise of the power conferred on the Lord Chancellor by section 14 of the Local Land Charges Act 1975[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002]:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Rules is The Local Land Charges (Amendment) (Wales) Rules 2010 and they come into force on 31 August 2010.

- (2) These Rules apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment to the Local Land Charges Rules 1977

###### 2

In Schedule 3 (Fees) to the Local Land Charges Rules 1977[^f00003], item 5 (personal search in the whole or in part of the register) is omitted.

## Signed

Jane E. Hutt — Minister for Business and Budget, one of the Welsh Ministers — 8 August 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Local Land Charges Rules 1977 (S.I. [1977/985](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1977/985)) by removing from Schedule 3 (Fees) the whole of the entry for item 5 (personal search in the whole or in part of the register in respect of one parcel of land or, where the search extends to more than one parcel, those parcels). The fee is incompatible with the Environmental Information Regulations 2004[^f00004] which implement Council Directive [2003/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0004)[^f00005] on public access to environmental information.
