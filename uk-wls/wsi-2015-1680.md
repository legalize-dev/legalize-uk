# The Violence against Women, Domestic Abuse and Sexual Violence (Wales) Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2015

Made: 8 September 2015

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 25(3) of the Violence against Women, Domestic Abuse and Sexual Violence (Wales) Act 2015[^f00001].

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Violence against Women, Domestic Abuse and Sexual Violence (Wales) Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2015.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

5 October 2015 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Violence against Women, Domestic Abuse and Sexual Violence (Wales) Act 2015—

- (a) section 2 (violence against women and girls);

- (b) section 3 (duty to prepare, publish and review a national strategy);

- (c) section 4 (duty to implement the national strategy);

- (d) section 9 (information about educational provision to promote the purpose of this Act);

- (e) section 11 (national indicators);

- (f) section 12 (annual progress reports by the Welsh Ministers);

- (g) section 22 (annual plan and annual reports); and

- (h) section 23 (publication of reports).

## Signed

Leighton Andrews — Title of Minister, one of the Welsh Ministers — 8 September 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the first commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Violence against Women, Domestic Abuse and Sexual Violence (Wales) Act 2015 (“the Act”).

Article 2 of the Order provides that 5 October 2015 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act set out in that article and referred to below:

- section 2 (violence against women and girls)

- section 3 (duty to prepare, publish and review a national strategy)

- section 4 (duty to implement the national strategy)

- section 9 (information about educational provision to promote the purpose of this Act)

- section 11 (national indicators)

- section 12 (annual progress reports by the Welsh Ministers)

- section 22 (annual plan and annual reports)

- section 23 (publication of reports)

Sections 1 (the purpose of this Act), 24 (interpretation), 25 (commencement) and 26 (short title) came into force on Royal Assent on 29 April 2015 by virtue of section 25(1) of the Act.

Sections 10 (guidance to further and higher education institutions), 14 (meaning of ”relevant authority”), 15 (power to issue statutory guidance), 16(consultation and National Assembly for Wales procedures), 17 (duty to follow statutory guidance), 18 (policy statements: requirements and ancillary powers), 19 (directions), 20 (National Adviser) and 21 (functions of the Adviser) came into force on 29 June 2015 by virtue of section 25(2) of the Act.
