# The Education and Inspections Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) (Wales) Order 2009

Made: 20 April 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the National Assembly for Wales by sections 181 and 188(3) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Education and Inspections Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009.

###### 2

In this Order “the 2006 Act” (“*Deddf 2006*”) means the Education and Inspections Act 2006.

#### Appointed day

###### 3

The following provisions of the 2006 Act come into force on 1 September 2009:

- (a) section 4 (duty to identify children not receiving education);

- (b) section 164 (information about children receiving funded education outside school);

- (c) section 184 (repeals) to the extent it relates to the provision set out in paragraph (d);

- (d) in Part 6 of Schedule 18, the repeal in the Education Act 1996 in section 437(8) of the definition of “suitable education”.

## Signed

Jane E. Hutt — Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 20 April 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings sections 4 and 164 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006 into force on 1 September 2009.

Section 4 inserts a new section 436A into the Education Act 1996 which requires local education authorities to make arrangements to establish the identities of children in their area who are not receiving suitable education.

Section 164 inserts a new section 537B into the Education Act 1996. This allows the Welsh Ministers to make regulations requiring information to be provided about children receiving education outside school which is funded by a local education authority and makes provision about sharing such information.
