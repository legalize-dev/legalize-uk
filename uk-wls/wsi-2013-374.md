# The Coleg Cambria (Incorporation) Order 2013

Made: 20 February 2013

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 22 February 2013

Coming into force: 26 March 2013

Accordingly, the Welsh Ministers make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Coleg Cambria (Incorporation) Order 2013 and it comes into force on 26 March 2013.

#### New Corporation

###### 2

On 26 March 2013 a body corporate called Coleg Cambria is to be established, for the purpose of establishing and conducting from the operative date an educational institution of the same name.

#### Operative date

###### 3

The operative date appointed in relation to Coleg Cambria is 1 August 2013, and the corporation is to conduct the educational institution of Coleg Cambria from that date.

## Signed

Leighton Andrews — Minister for Education and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 20 February 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called “Coleg Cambria” under section 16(1)(a) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (“the Act”) for the purpose of conducting the educational institution of the same name. The Order also provides for the appointment of 1 August 2013 as the operative date for the purposes of Part 1 of the Act, being the date from which Coleg Cambria is to conduct the educational institution of the same name.

A draft and summary of this Order have been published in accordance with section 16A of the Act and the Further Education Corporations (Publication of Draft Orders) (Wales) Regulations 2007.
