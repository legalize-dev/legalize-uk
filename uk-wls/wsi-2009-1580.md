# The Swine Vesicular Disease (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 25 June 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 26 June 2009

Coming into force: 17 July 2009

The Welsh Ministers, who are designated [^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is The Swine Vesicular Disease (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009; they apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 17 July 2009.

#### Amendment of The Swine Vesicular Disease (Wales) Regulations 2009

###### 2

The Swine Vesicular Disease (Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00003] (“the Principal Regulations”) are amended in accordance with Regulation 3.

#### Amendments to the English version of the Principal Regulations

###### 3

In Regulation 41(a) of the English version of the Principal Regulations insert the word “intentionally” after the word “to”.

## Signed

Elin Jones — Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 25 June 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Swine Vesicular Disease (Wales) Regulations 2009 (S.I. [2009/1372 (W.135)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2009/1372) (“the Principal Regulations”) which implement in Wales the provisions of Council Directive [92/119/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0119) introducing general Community measures for the control of certain animal diseases and specific measures relating to swine vesicular disease (OJ No. L 62, 15.3.1993, p. 69) in so far as that Directive controls swine vesicular disease and Council Directive [2007/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0010) (OJ No. L 63, 1.3.2007, p. 24).

Regulation 3 amends the Principal Regulations by inserting the word “intentionally” into the English version of Regulation 41(a).

A regulatory impact assessment on the effect of this instrument on the costs of business has not been prepared.
