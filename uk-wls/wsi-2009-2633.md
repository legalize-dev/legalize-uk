# The Welsh College of Horticulture (Dissolution) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 29 September 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 1 October 2009

Coming into force: 23 October 2009

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] on the Secretary of State and now vested in them[^f00002].

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Welsh College of Horticulture (Dissolution) (Amendment) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 23 October 2009.

#### Amendment of the Welsh College of Horticulture (Dissolution) Order 2009

###### 2

In the Welsh text of the Welsh College of Horticulture (Dissolution) Order 2009[^f00003] at paragraph 2 of Part 1 of the Schedule omit “ymyl du a chroeslinellau” and substitute “ymyl du a llinellau lletraws”.

## Signed

John Griffiths — Deputy Minister for Skills under authority of the Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 29 September 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects an error in the Welsh text of the Welsh College of Horticulture (Dissolution) Order 2009.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
