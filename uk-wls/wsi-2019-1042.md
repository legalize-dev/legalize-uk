# The Whelk Fishing (Wales) Order 2019

Made: 24 June 2019

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 25 June 2019

Coming into force: 4 July 2019

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1), 1(3) 1(4), 1(6), 5(1) and 5(2) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 , now vested in them .

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) Enw’r Gorchymyn hwn yw Gorchymyn Pysgota am Gregyn Moch (Cymru) 2019 a daw i rym ar 4 Gorffennaf 2019.

- (2) Yn ddarostyngedig i baragraff (3), mae’r Gorchymyn hwn yn gymwys o ran Cymru.

- (3) Mae erthyglau 3 a 5 yn gymwys o ran parth Cymru.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, unless the context requires otherwise—

- “*the Act*” (“**y Ddeddf**”) means the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 ;

- “*British fishing boat*” (“**cwch pysgota Prydeining**”) means a fishing boat which is either registered in the United Kingdom under Part II of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 or is owned wholly by persons qualified to own British ships for the purposes of that part of the Act;

- “*Specified minimum size*” (“**maint lleiaf penodedig**”) means—55 millimetres before 4 July 2020, and65 millimetres on and after 4 July 2020;

- “*Wales*” (“**Cymru**”) has the meaning given in section 158 of the Government of Wales Act 2006 ;

- “*Welsh zone*” (“**parth Cymru**”) has the meaning given in section 158 of the Government of Wales Act 2006 ; and

- “*Whelk*” (“**cregyn moch**”) means shellfish of the species *Buccinum undatum*.

#### Fishing prohibition for specified whelk

###### 3

Mae cychod pysgota Prydeinig wedi eu gwahardd rhag pysgota am gregyn moch sy’n llai na’r maint lleiaf penodedig.

#### Prohibition on landing of specified whelk

###### 4

At ddibenion adran 1(1) o’r Ddeddf (sy’n gwahardd glanio unrhyw bysgodyn môr o unrhyw ddisgrifiad nad yw’n bodloni’r gofynion o ran maint a ragnodir mewn perthynas â physgod môr o’r disgrifiad hwnnw) rhagnodir mai’r maint lleiaf ar gyfer cregyn moch yw’r maint lleiaf penodedig.

#### Prohibition on carriage of specified whelk

###### 5

At ddibenion adran 1(3) o’r Ddeddf (sy’n gwahardd cludo ar gychod pysgota penodedig unrhyw bysgodyn môr o unrhyw ddisgrifiad nad yw’n bodloni’r gofynion o ran maint a ragnodir mewn perthynas â physgod môr o’r disgrifiad hwnnw), mae cychod pysgota Prydeinig wedi eu gwahardd rhag cludo unrhyw gregyn moch sy’n llai na’r maint lleiaf penodedig.

#### Measurement of whelk

###### 6

For the purposes of articles 3, 4 and 5, the size of a whelk is to be measured in accordance with paragraph 7 of Annex XIII to Council Regulation [(EC) No 850/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1998/850) for the conservation and management of fishery resources through technical measures for the protection of juvenile marine organisms .

#### Revocation and Amendments

###### 7

- (1) Byelaw 11 (Whelk – Minimum Size) of the former South Wales Sea Fisheries Committee is revoked.

- (2) In Byelaw 19 (Specified Fish Sizes) of the former North Western and North Wales Sea Fisheries Committee ,in the Table specifying minimum shellfish size, delete the row relating to Whelk.

- (3) In the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1, Consequential, Transitional and Savings Provisions) (England and Wales) Order 2010 , in the Table in Schedule 3, delete the row relating to Byelaw 11.

## Signed

Lesley Griffiths — Minister for Environment, Energy and Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 2019-06-24
