# The References to Health Authorities Order 2007

Made: 20 March 2007

Coming into force: 1 April 2007

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the power conferred on it by section 75 of the Health Act 2006[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the References to Health Authorities Order 2007 and the Order comes into force on 1 April 2007.

- (2) In this Order “subordinate legislation” has the same meaning as in the Interpretation Act 1978[^f00002].

#### Extent

###### 2

A modification by this Order of an enactment has the same extent as the enactment modified.

#### Modifications of Acts of Parliament

###### 3

The enactments specified in the Schedule are amended as there specified.

#### Modifications of references to Health Authorities in subordinate legislation

###### 4

All references in subordinate legislation to a Health Authority or Health Authorities are to be treated as references to a Local Health Board or Local Health Boards as appropriate, unless the context otherwise requires.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments relating to the establishment of Local Health Boards in Wales from 1 April 2003 and the abolition of Health Authorities on the same date. The Order makes amendments of enactments appropriate to reflect the fact that functions of former Health Authorities were transferred to the Assembly under the Health Authorities (Transfer of Functions, Staff, Property, Rights and Liabilities and Abolition (Wales) Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/813](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/813)) and such functions were then made exercisable by Local Health Boards under the Local Health Boards (Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2003 (S.I. [2003/150](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/150)). The Order makes further amendments to enactments in consequence of, or in connection with, the abolition of Welsh Health Authorities.

The Order also provides that in relation to subordinate legislation all references to a Health Authority or Health Authorities are to be treated as references to a Local Health Board or Local Health Boards unless the context otherwise requires.
