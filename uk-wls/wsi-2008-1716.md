# The Childcare Act 2006 (Provision of Information) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 28 June 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 1 July 2008

Coming into force: 31 July 2008

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by section 27 of the Childcare Act 2006[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] make the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Childcare Act 2006 (Provision of Information) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and they come into force on 31 July 2008.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendments to the Childcare Act 2006 (Provision of Information) (Wales) Regulations 2008.

###### 2

- (1) The Childcare Act 2006 (Provision of Information) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00003] are amended as follows:—.

- (2) In regulation 4(1)(f) in the Welsh text only for “Gynulliad Cenedlaethol Cymru” substitute “Weinidogion Cymru”.

- (3) In regulation 4(1)(h) in the English text only for “National Assembly for Wales” substitute “Welsh Ministers”.

- (4) In regulation 6, in the Welsh text only, for “awdurdodau lleol” substitute “awdurdod lleol”.

- (5) In regulation 7, in the Welsh text only, between “gwybodaeth” and “yn” insert “ar unrhyw wasanaethau neu gyfleusterau eraill neu unrhyw gyhoeddiadau”.

- (6) In regulation 9,in the Welsh text only for “adnoddau” substitute “ffynonellau”.

## Signed

Jane E. Hutt — Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 28 June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Childcare Act 2006 (Provision of Information) (Wales) Regulations 2008 to correct discrepancies in the text of the Regulations.
