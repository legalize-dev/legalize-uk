# The Newport (Communities) and the Torfaen (Communities) (Consequential and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2026

Made: 24 November 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2) and (3)

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 41(1), (2) and (3) of the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013[^f00001].

#### Title and coming into force

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Newport (Communities) and the Torfaen (Communities) (Consequential and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2026.

- (2) For any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) of the 1976 Regulations[^f00002], these Regulations come into force on 22 December 2026.

- (3) For all other purposes, these Regulations come into force at 23:59 hours on the day before the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2027.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations—

- “*the 1976 Regulations*” (“*Rheoliadau 1976*”) means the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976;

- “*new community councils*” (“*cynghorau cymuned newydd*”) has the same meaning as in the Newport Order and the Torfaen Order and they are specified in paragraph (2) of this regulation;

- “*the Newport Order*” (“*Gorchymyn Casnewydd*”) means the Newport (Communities) Order 2026[^f00003];

- “*the Torfaen Order*” (“*Gorchymyn Torfaen*”) means the Torfaen (Communities) Order 2026[^f00004];

words and expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as they have in the Local Government Act 1972 as saved, except so far as a contrary intention appears.

- (2) For the purposes of these Regulations, the new community councils are—

- (a) Graig, established by article 11 of the Newport Order,

- (b) Croesyceiliog and Llanyrafon, established by article 13 of the Torfaen Order,

- (c) Cwmbran, established by article 28 of the Torfaen Order,

- (d) Ponthir, established by article 36 of the Torfaen Order, and

- (e) Pontypool, established by article 52 of the Torfaen Order.

#### Initial expenses etc. of new community councils

###### 3

- (1) In this regulation—

- “*the 1992 Act*” (“*Deddf 1992*”) means the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00005];

- “*the prospective billing authority*” (“*yr awdurdod bilio arfaethedig*”) means Newport City Council or Torfaen County Borough Council as relevant;

- “*the relevant financial year*” (“*y flwyddyn ariannol berthnasol*”) means the financial year beginning 1 April 2027.

- (2) For the relevant financial year, section 32 of the 1992 Act (calculation of budget requirement by authorities in Wales) applies to the new community councils and the prospective billing authority but as if—

- (a) in making a calculation under section 32(2)(a) of the 1992 Act—

- (i) the amount notified under paragraph (6) of this regulation were an item mentioned in section 35(1) of the 1992 Act (special items for purposes of section 34) relating to the new community, and

- (ii) the area of the prospective billing authority included the new community;

- (b) subsection (6) were omitted.

- (3) For the relevant financial year, section 41 of the 1992 Act (issue of precepts by local precepting authorities) applies to the new community councils and the prospective billing authority but as if the prospective billing authority, in making calculations under section 32 of the 1992 Act (originally or by way of substitution) in its estimate under section 32(2)(a), were required to take into account an amount equal to that notified to it under paragraph (6) of this regulation.

- (4) For the relevant financial year, sections 52X(1) (calculations to be net of precepts) and 52Y(2) (information for purposes of Chapter 4A) of the 1992 Act apply to the new community councils and the prospective billing authority but as if the aggregate amount of precepts anticipated by the prospective billing authority under regulations made under section 41 of the 1992 Act included the amounts notified to the new community councils under paragraph (6) of this regulation.

- (5) For the relevant financial year, section 50 of the 1992 Act (calculation of budget requirement by authorities in Wales) applies to the new community councils and the prospective billing authority as if each of the new community councils were required to secure that the amount calculated as its budget requirement for that year does not exceed the amount notified to that new community council under paragraph (6) of this regulation.

- (6) The appropriate amount to be notified to each of the new community councils under this paragraph must be notified by the Welsh Ministers in writing to the prospective billing authority and to the relevant new community council.

- (7) For the relevant financial year, the Local Authorities (Precepts) (Wales) Regulations 1995[^f00006] apply to the new community councils and the prospective billing authority but as if—

- (a) regulation 5 (information on schedules of instalments) were omitted;

- (b) in paragraph 8 of the Schedule (rules for determination of schedules of instalments)—

- (i) in sub-paragraphs (1), (2) and (3), the words after the first reference to “that financial year” were omitted;

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (1)(a), the reference to “April” were a reference to “May”.

#### New community councils

###### 4

- (1) The proper officer of Newport City Council must convene the first meeting of the new community council established by article 11 of the Newport Order.

- (2) The proper officer of Torfaen County Borough Council must convene the first meeting of each of the new community councils established by articles 13, 28, 36 and 52 of the Torfaen Order.

#### New community councils - residuary successors

###### 5

For the purposes of any provision of the 1976 Regulations which refers to the residuary successor of a dissolved authority—

- (a) for the dissolved community council of Graig, the new community council of Graig is specified as the residuary successor;

- (b) for the dissolved community council of Croesyceiliog and Llanyrafon, the new community council of Croesyceiliog and Llanyrafon is specified as the residuary successor;

- (c) for the dissolved community council of Cwmbran, the new community council of Cwmbran is specified as the residuary successor;

- (d) for the dissolved community council of Ponthir, the new community council of Ponthir is specified as the residuary successor;

- (e) for the dissolved community council of Pontypool, the new community council of Pontypool is specified as the residuary successor.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

The Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru made the Newport (Communities) Order 2026 (“*the Newport Order*”) and the Torfaen (Communities) Order 2026 (“*the Torfaen Order*”) which established new community councils amongst other things.

These Regulations are made by the Welsh Ministers as a consequence of the Newport Order and the Torfaen Order in order to facilitate the administrative arrangements of the newly established community councils.

Regulation 1(2) applies to the coming into force date for preparatory administrative changes that need to take effect before the local government elections in 2027, to include financial arrangements and the convening of the first meeting of the newly established community councils. Regulation 1(3) applies to the residuary successor of the dissolved community councils, which comes into force at 23:59 hours the day before the local government elections.

Regulation 3 makes provision for the financial arrangements relating to the initial expenses of the newly established community councils. It modifies provisions of the Local Government Finance Act 1992 for the purpose of determining appropriate initial budgets and to set precept limits for the first year of operation.

Regulation 4 makes provision for the convening of the first meeting of the newly established community councils.

Regulation 5 makes provision for the residuary successor for each of the community councils being dissolved.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations.
