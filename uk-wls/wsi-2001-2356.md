# The Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2001

Made: 26th June 2001

Coming into force: 1st July 2001

The National Assembly for Wales, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2001, shall apply to Wales and shall come into force on 1st July 2001.

#### Amendment of the Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998

###### 2

- (1) The Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998[^f00003] shall be amended in their application to Wales in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3) below.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation) for the definition of “the Decision” there shall be substituted—

> > - “the Decision” means Commission Decision [96/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0301) authorising member States temporarily to take additional measures against the dissemination of *Pseudomonas solanacearum* (Smith) Smith as regards Egypt[^f00004] as amended by the instruments listed in the Schedule to these Regulations;

- (2) After regulation 6 there shall be inserted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## **THE SCHEDULE** — **New Schedule to the Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998**

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00005]

Dafydd Elis Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 26th June 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply to Wales only and come into force on 1st July 2001, implement Commission Decision [2000/568/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0568) amending Decision [96/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0301) authorising member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination of *Pseudomonas solanacearum* (Smith) Smith as regards Egypt (OJ No. L238, 22.9.2000, p.59).

The Regulations amend the definition of “the Decision” in the Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998 (regulation 2(2)). Commission Decision [2000/568/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0568) renews the framework within which potatoes may be imported from Egypt into the territory of the European Community.

No regulatory appraisal has been prepared in respect of these Regulations.
