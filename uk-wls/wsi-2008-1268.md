# The Food Labelling (Declaration of Allergens) (Wales) Regulations 2008

Made: 6 May 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 8 May 2008

Coming into force: 31 May 2008

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Food Labelling (Declaration of Allergens) (Wales) Regulations 2008 and they come into force on 31 May 2008.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment of the Food Labelling Regulations 1996

###### 2

- (1) The Food Labelling Regulations 1996[^f00003] are amended in so far as they apply in relation to Wales in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (7).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of Directive 2000/13 for the words “Commission Directive [2005/26/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0026)” to the end, substitute “Commission Directive [2007/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0068) amending Annex IIIa to Directive [2000/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0013) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards certain food ingredients[^f00004];”.

- (3) In regulation 13(8)(c)—

- (a) omit the words “paragraphs 1 to 11 of”; and

- (b) omit the passage beginning with the words “other than” and ending with the words “that allergenic ingredient”.

- (4) In regulation 34B(1)—

- (a) for the words “paragraphs (2) and (3)” substitute “paragraph (2)”; and

- (b) omit the words “paragraphs 1 to 11 of”.

- (5) In regulation 34B(2)(b)—

- (a) omit the words “subject to paragraph (3) of this regulation,”; and

- (b) omit the words “paragraphs 1 to 11 of”.

- (6) In regulation 50 (transitional provision), after paragraph (14) insert the following—

> > (15) In any proceedings for an offence under regulation 44(1)(a) it is a defence to prove that—
>   (a) the food concerned was sold before 31st May 2009 or marked or labelled before that date; and
>   (b) the matters constituting the alleged offence would not have constituted an offence under these Regulations if the amendments made by regulation 2 of the Food Labelling (Declaration of Allergens) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00005] had not been in operation when the food was sold.

- (7) For the contents of Schedule AA1 (allergenic ingredients) substitute the content of the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The following Regulations or provisions are revoked—

- (a) Regulation 34B(3) of, and Schedule 2A to, the Food Labelling Regulations 1996;

- (b) The Schedule to the Food Labelling (Amendment) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2004[^f00006];

- (c) The Food Labelling (Amendment) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2005[^f00007];

- (d) The Food Labelling (Amendment) (Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) Regulations 2005[^f00008];

- (e) The Food Labelling (Declaration of Allergens) (Wales) Regulations 2007[^f00009].

## SCHEDULE — Content to be inserted as Schedule A1 to the Food Labelling Regulations 1996

###### 1

Cereals containing gluten (i.e. wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt, kamut or their hybridised strains),

- except: wheat-based glucose syrups including dextrose[^f00010]; wheat-based maltodextrins[^f00010] ; glucose syrups based on barley; cereals used for making distillates or ethyl alcohol of agricultural origin for spirit drinks and other alcoholic beverages.

###### 2

Crustaceans.

###### 3

Eggs.

###### 4

Fish,

- except: fish gelatine used as a carrier for vitamin or carotenoid preparations; fish gelatine or isinglass used as a fining agent in beer and wine.

###### 5

Peanuts.

###### 6

Soybeans,

- except: fully refined soybean oil and fat(1); natural mixed tocopherols (E306), natural D-alpha tocopherol, natural D-alpha tocopherol acetate, natural D-alpha tocopherol succinate from soybean sources; vegetable oils derived from phytosterols and phytosteral esters from soybean sources; plant stanol ester produced from vegetable oil sterols from soybean sources.

###### 7

Milk (including lactose),

- except: whey used for making distillates or ethyl alcohol of agricultural origin for spirit drinks and other alcoholic beverages; lactitol.

###### 8

Nuts, i.e. almonds (*Amygdalus communis* L.), hazelnuts (*Corylus avellana*), walnuts (*Juglans regia*), cashews (*Anacardium occidentale*), pecan nuts (*Carya illinoiesis* (*Wangenh.) K.Koch*), Brazil nuts (*Bertholletia excelsa*), pistachio nuts (*Pistacia vera*), macadamia nuts and Queensland nuts (*Macadamia terniflora*),

- except: nuts used for making distillates or ethyl alcohol of agricultural origin for spirit drinks and other alcoholic beverages.

###### 9

Celery.

###### 10

Mustard.

###### 11

Sesame seeds.

###### 12

Sulphur dioxide and sulphites at concentrations of more than 10 mg/kg or 10 mg/litre expressed as SO₂ .

###### 13

Lupin.

###### 14

Molluscs.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Food Labelling Regulations 1996 (“the principal Regulations”) in so far as they apply in relation to Wales. The principal Regulations extend to the whole of Great Britain.

These Regulations implement in Wales Commission Directive No. [2007/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0068) (OJ No. L310, 28.11.2007, p.11) amending Annex IIIa to Directive [2000/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0013) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards certain food ingredients. The ingredients in question are those likely to cause an allergic reaction in some consumers.

The Regulations amend the principal Regulations in respect of the labelling requirements for foods containing allergenic ingredients, and provide a transitional period for foods that are marked, labelled or sold before 31 May 2009.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from the Food Standards Agency, 11th Floor, Southgate House, Wood Street, Cardiff, CF10 1EW.
