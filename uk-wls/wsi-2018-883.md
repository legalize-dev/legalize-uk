# The Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2018

Made: 18 July 2018

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 115(4) of the Deregulation Act 2015[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2018.

#### Provisions coming into force on 21 September 2018

###### 2

The appointed day for Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the Deregulation Act 2015 to come into force is 21 September 2018.

## Signed

Eluned Morgan — Minister for Welsh Language and Lifelong Learning, under the authority of the Cabinet Secretary for Education, one of the Welsh Ministers — 18 July 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the Deregulation Act 2015 (“the Act”).

Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the Act amends sections 18, 19 and 20 of the Apprenticeship, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009. The effect of these amendments is to clarify the operation of the existing provisions in respect of the Welsh Ministers ability to issue apprenticeship frameworks.
