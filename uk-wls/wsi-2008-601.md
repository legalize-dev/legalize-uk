## SCHEDULE 1 — DEFINITIONS OF COMMUNITY LEGISLATION

- “Directive 2004/41” (“*Cyfarwyddeb 2004/41*”) means Directive [2004/41/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0041) of the European Parliament and of the Council repealing certain directives concerning food hygiene and health conditions for the production and placing on the market of certain products of animal origin intended for human consumption and amending Council Directives [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0662) and [92/118/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0118) and Council Decision [95/408/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0408)[^f00010];

- “Regulation 178/2002” (“*Rheoliad 178/2002*”) means Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00011];

- “Regulation 852/2004” (“*Rheoliad 852/2004*”) means Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0852) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the hygiene of foodstuffs[^f00012] as read with Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2073/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2073);

- “Regulation 853/2004” (“*Rheoliad 853/2004*”) means Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin[^f00013] as read with Directive 2004/41, Regulation 1688/2005, Regulation 2074/2005 and Regulation 2076/2005;

- “Regulation 854/2004” (“*Rheoliad 854/2004*”) means Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific rules for the organisation of official controls on products of animal origin intended for human consumption[^f00014] as read with Directive 2004/41, Regulation 2074/2005, Regulation 2075/2005 and Regulation 2076/2005;

- “Regulation 882/2004” (“*Rheoliad 882/2004*”) means Regulation [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls performed to ensure the verification of compliance with feed and food law, animal health and animal welfare rules[^f00015] as read with Regulation 2074/2005 and Regulation 2076/2005;

- “Regulation 1688/2005” (“*Rheoliad 1688/2005*”) means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1688/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1688) implementing Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards special guarantees concerning salmonella for consignments to Finland and Sweden of certain meat and eggs[^f00016];

- “Regulation 2073/2005” (“*Rheoliad 2073/2005*”) means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2073/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2073) on microbiological criteria for foodstuffs[^f00017];

- “Regulation 2074/2005” (“*Rheoliad 2074/2005*”) means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2074/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2074) laying down implementing measures for certain products under Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council and for the organisation of official controls under Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council, derogating from Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0852) of the European Parliament and of the Council and amending Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854)[^f00018];

- “Regulation 2075/2005” (“*Rheoliad 2075/2005*”) means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2075/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2075) laying down specific rules on official controls for Trichinella in meat[^f00019]; and

- “Regulation 2076/2005” (“*Rheoliad 2076/2005*”) means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2076/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2076) laying down transitional arrangements for the implementation of Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853), [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) and [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council and amending Regulations [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) and [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854)[^f00020].
