# The Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025

Made: 10 December 2025

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 147(2) of the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024[^f00001].

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025.

#### Provisions coming into force on 16 March 2026

###### 2

The following provisions of the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 come into force on 16 March 2026—

- (a) section 40(2);

- (b) section 90;

- (c) section 91 to the extent not already in force;

- (d) section 92(1)(c) and (3) to the extent not already in force;

- (e) section 93;

- (f) section 96(5), (6) and (8) to the extent not already in force;

- (g) section 124(3)(a)(ii);

- (h) Schedule 2.

## Signed

Rebecca Evans — Cabinet Secretary for Economy, Energy and Planning, one of the Welsh Ministers — 10 December 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the second commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (“*the Act*”).

Article 2 of this Order brings the following sections of the Act into force on 16 March 2026—

- (a) section 40(2) (appointing a person or a panel of persons to examine an application to revoke or change an infrastructure consent order);

- (b) section 90 (power to change or revoke infrastructure consent orders);

- (c) section 91 (procedure: changing and revoking infrastructure consent orders);

- (d) section 92(1)(c) and (3) (changing and revoking infrastructure consent orders: formalities) to the extent not already in force;

- (e) section 93 (changing or revoking an infrastructure consent order: compensation);

- (f) section 96(5), (6) and (8) (legal challenges) to the extent not already in force;

- (g) section 124(3)(a)(ii) (fees for performance of infrastructure consent functions and services);

- (h) Schedule 2 (compensation for changing or revoking infrastructure consent orders).
