# The Government of Wales Act 2006 (Budget Motions and Designated Bodies) Order 2018

Made: 12 November 2018

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 14 November 2018

Coming into force: 21 January 2019

In accordance with section 126A(4) and (6) of that Act the Welsh Ministers have consulted, where they think it appropriate, the Treasury and the Treasury has consented to the making of this Order.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Government of Wales Act 2006 (Budget Motions and Designated Bodies) Order 2018.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 21 January 2019.

#### Revocations

###### 2

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Government of Wales Act 2006 (Budget Motions and Designated Bodies) Order 2016[^f00002];

- (b) the Government of Wales Act 2006 (Budget Motions and Designated Bodies) Order 2017[^f00003].

#### Designations

###### 3

A body which is listed in the Schedule to this Order is a designated body for the purposes of section 126A of the Government of Wales Act 2006 in relation to the Welsh Ministers[^f00004].

## SCHEDULE — Designated Bodies

- Career Choices Dewis Gyrfa Ltd

- DCFW Limited

- Hybu Cig Cymru-Meat Promotion Wales

- Innovation Point Limited

- Life Sciences Hub Wales Limited

- Local Health Boards (as established under section 11 of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00005])

- National Academy for Educational Leadership

- Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust[^f00006]

- Sector Development Wales Partnership Limited

- Transport for Wales

- Velindre University National Health Service Trust[^f00007]

- Welsh Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust[^f00008]

- Welsh Revenue Authority[^f00009]

- WGC Holdco Limited

## Signed

Mark Drakeford — Cabinet Secretary for Finance, one of the Welsh Ministers — 12 November 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the bodies listed in the Schedule for the purpose of including within a Budget motion the resources expected to be used by those bodies.

The Order replaces the previous designations made by the Government of Wales Act 2006 (Budget Motions and Designated Bodies) Order 2016 ([S.I. 2016/1096 (W. 260)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2016/1096)) and the Government of Wales Act 2006 (Budget Motions and Designated Bodies) Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/946 (W. 235)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2017/946)) and therefore revokes those instruments.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.
