# The Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025 (Commencement and Saving Provisions) Order 2026

Made: 19 November 2026

Coming into force: 1 April 2027

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(3) and (4) of the Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025[^f00001] make the following Order.

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025 (Commencement and Saving Provisions) Order 2026.

- (2) In this Order—

- “*the 1969 Act*” (“*Deddf 1969*”) means the [Mines and Quarries (Tips) Act 1969](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents)[^f00002];

- “*the Act*” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the [Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/4/contents);

- “*exploratory tests*”, “*local authority*” and “*remedial operations*” have the meanings given in [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/part/2) of [the 1969 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents).

#### Saving provisions

###### 3

- (1) Despite the commencement of [section 75](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/4/section/75) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/4/contents), sections 11-26(1), 27, 28, 30-33 and, 36 of, and Schedules 2, 3 and 4 to, the 1969 Act continue to apply as those provisions had effect immediately before 1 April 2027 in relation to—

- (a) any notice served by a local authority in Wales under Part 2 of, or paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to, the 1969 Act before 1 April 2027, or any action directly connected with such a notice;

- (b) any investigations, exploratory tests or inspections carried out by a local authority in Wales under [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/13) of [the 1969 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents) that began before 1 April 2027;

- (c) any remedial operations carried out by a local authority in Wales under [section 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/17)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/17) of [the 1969 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents) that began before 1 April 2027.

- (2) Despite the commencement of [section 75](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/4/section/75) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/4/contents), [section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/12) of [the 1969 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents) continues to apply as it had effect immediately before 1 April 2027, if—

- (a) any notice has been served by a local authority in Wales under Part 2 of, or paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to, the 1969 Act before 1 April 2027, and any action directly connected with such a notice is being carried out;

- (b) any investigations, exploratory tests or inspections are being carried out by a local authority in Wales under [section 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/13) of [the 1969 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents) and began before 1 April 2027;

- (c) any remedial operations are being carried out by a local authority in Wales under [section 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/17)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/17) of [the 1969 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents) and began before 1 April 2027.

- (3) Despite the commencement of [section 75](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/4/section/75) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/4/contents), [section 26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/26)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/section/26) of [the 1969 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/10/contents) continues to apply as it had effect immediately before 1 April 2027, in relation to any works completed in the course of remedial operations for the purpose of ensuring the stability of a disused tip under [article 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2026/137/article/3/made)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2026/137/article/3/1/made), for a period of 12 years from the date the works were completed.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order is the only commencement order made under the Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025 (“*the Act*”).

Section 92(1) of the Act brought Part 5 into force on 12 September 2025, which was the day after the Act received Royal Assent. Section 92(2) of the Act brings sections 1, 2 and 5 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Act into force on 1 April 2027. Article 2 brings the remaining provisions of the Act into force on 1 April 2027.

Article 3 sets out saving provisions. Article 3(1) makes saving provisions in relation to notices served or tests or operations that began before 1 April 2027. Article 3(2) makes saving provisions in relation to information notices, and article 3(3) makes saving provision in relation to liability for wilful damage or interference with any works completed in the course of remedial operations for the purpose of ensuring the stability of a disused tip under article 3(1).
