# The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (Wales) Order 2015

Made: 16 April 2015

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 20 April 2015

Coming into force: 11 May 2015

In accordance with the requirements of section 26(4) of that Act, the Welsh Ministers— have given any local authority affected and any other person affected an opportunity to submit objections or representations with respect to the subject matter of this Order; and have consulted with the Joint Nature Conservation Committee, being the advisory body[^f00003] the Welsh Ministers considered was best able to advise them as to whether this Order should be made.

have given any local authority affected and any other person affected an opportunity to submit objections or representations with respect to the subject matter of this Order; and

have consulted with the Joint Nature Conservation Committee, being the advisory body[^f00003] the Welsh Ministers considered was best able to advise them as to whether this Order should be made.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Variation of Schedule 9) (Wales) Order 2015.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 11 May 2015.

#### Variation of Schedule 9

###### 2

In Part 1B of Schedule 9 to the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (animals no longer normally present)[^f00004], in the entry relating to “Beaver, Eurasian” omit “(but not in relation to Wales)”.

## Signed

Carl Sargeant — Minister for Natural Resources, one of the Welsh Ministers — 16 April 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, which applies in relation to Wales, varies Part 1B of Schedule 9 to the Wildlife and Countryside Act [1981 (c. 69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/69) (“the Act”), which lists animals no longer normally present in Great Britain which may not be released or allowed to escape into the wild.

Article 2 of this Order adds the Eurasian Beaver to Part 1B of Schedule 9 to the Act in relation to Wales (the Eurasian Beaver is currently included only in relation to England).

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order. A copy can be obtained from the Welsh Government (Land, Nature and Forestry Division), Rhodfa Padarn, Llanbadarn Fawr, Aberystwyth, Ceredigion, SY23 3UR.
