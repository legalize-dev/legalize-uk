# The Curd Cheese (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2007

Made: 5 March 2007

Coming into force: 6 March 2007

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations are called the Curd Cheese (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 6 March 2007.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Curd Cheese (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00003]) are revoked.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

D. Elis-Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 05 March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations revoke the Curd Cheese (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (Wales) Regulations 2006 (S.I. [2006/2792 (W.233)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/2792)).

2 Those Regulations implemented in relation to Wales Commission Decision [2006/694/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0694) prohibiting the placing on the market of curd cheese manufactured in a dairy establishment in the United Kingdom (OJ No. L283, 14.10.2006, p.59). Commission Decision [2006/694/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0694) which prohibited the placing on the market of curd cheese manufactured in a dairy establishment in the United Kingdom has been repealed by Commission Decision C (2007) 639 of 2 March 2007.

3 No regulatory appraisal has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
