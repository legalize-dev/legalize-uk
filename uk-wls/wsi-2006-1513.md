## THE SCHEDULE

#### Compensation

| Animal or product | Compensation (£) | Compensation (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | A | B |
| (a) Where the National Assembly has granted a derogation under point 9 of Annex VII to the Community TSE Regulation the compensation for a female sheep is £30 if it is killed after the first year of the derogation period. | (a) Where the National Assembly has granted a derogation under point 9 of Annex VII to the Community TSE Regulation the compensation for a female sheep is £30 if it is killed after the first year of the derogation period. | (a) Where the National Assembly has granted a derogation under point 9 of Annex VII to the Community TSE Regulation the compensation for a female sheep is £30 if it is killed after the first year of the derogation period. |
| (b) Where the National Assembly has granted a derogation in accordance with that point in respect of any ram in a flock, the compensation for any lamb in that flock killed after the first year of the period of derogation is £25. | (b) Where the National Assembly has granted a derogation in accordance with that point in respect of any ram in a flock, the compensation for any lamb in that flock killed after the first year of the period of derogation is £25. | (b) Where the National Assembly has granted a derogation in accordance with that point in respect of any ram in a flock, the compensation for any lamb in that flock killed after the first year of the period of derogation is £25. |
| Male sheep or goat | 90 | 90 |
| Female sheep[^f01000] or goat | 90 | 65 |
| Lamb (under 12 months old)[^f01001] or kid (under 12 months old) | 50 | 40 |
| Embryos | 150 | 150 |
| Ova | 5 | 5 |

#### Note on the rates

The rate in Column A is payable in all cases until 14 June 2006.

#### On or after 14 June 2006

- (a) the rate in Column A is payable if–

- (i) the owner notifies an animal under paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 of the Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Wales) Regulations 2006;

- (ii) the animal for which compensation is being paid was in the flock or herd before 14 June 2006;

and

- (b) the rate in Column B is payable in all other cases.
