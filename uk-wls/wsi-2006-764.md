# The Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made: 14 March 2006

Coming into force: 20 March 2006

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 76(4) and 140(4) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001] and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales[^f00002], and after consultation with such representatives of local government as appears to it to be appropriate, in accordance with section 76(5) of the said Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and they come into force on 20 March 2006.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

In relation to any financial year beginning on or after 1 April 2006, regulation 3 of the Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) (Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00003] is amended—

- (a) by the omission of paragraphs (e) and (f); and

- (b) by the addition, at the end, of—

> > (k) Welsh Local Government Association.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004].

D. Elis-Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 14 March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 84E of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 provides for revenue support grant to be payable to specified bodies. A specified body is any body which provides services for local authorities and is specified in regulations.

These Regulations amend the Revenue Support Grant (Specified Bodies) (Wales) Regulations 2000 by removing the Public Private Partnerships Programme Limited and Syniad from the list of specified bodies and adding the Welsh Local Government Association. The amendments have effect for the financial years beginning on or after 1 April 2006.
