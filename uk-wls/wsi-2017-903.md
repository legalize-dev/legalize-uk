# The Trade Union (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) Order 2017

Made: 11 September 2017

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 3 of the Trade Union (Wales) Act 2017[^f00001].

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Trade Union (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) Order 2017.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Trade Union (Wales) Act 2017.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Sections 1 and 2 of the Act come into force on 13 September 2017.

## Signed

Mark Drakeford — Cabinet Secretary for Finance and Local Government, one of the Welsh Ministers — 11 September 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences sections 1 and 2 of the Trade Union (Wales) Act 2017. These provisions come into force on 13 September 2017.
