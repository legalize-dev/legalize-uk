# The Assembly Learning Grants (European Institutions) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 1 August 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 4 August 2009

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 22, 42(6) and 43(1) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00001] and now exercisable by them[^f00002] make the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement, application and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Assembly Learning Grants (European Institutions) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

- (2) Subject to paragraph (3) these Regulations come into force on 1 September 2009.

- (3) Regulations 8 and 9 come into force on 31 August 2009.

###### 2

In these Regulations—

- “the Amendment Regulations” (“*y Rheoliadau Diwygio*”) means the Assembly Learning Grants (European Institutions) (Wales) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2008[^f00003]; and

- “the Principal Regulations” (“*y Prif Reoliadau*”) means the Assembly Learning Grants (European Institutions) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00004].

#### Amendment of the Principal Regulations

###### 3

The Principal Regulations are amended in accordance with Regulations 4 to 7.

###### 4

In regulation 18(3), for “27,115 euro” substitute “28,200 euro”.

###### 5

In regulation 19(3), for “11,090 euro” substitute “13,450 euro”.

###### 6

In regulation 20(3)—

- (a) for “**A** is £2,402” substitute “**A** is £2,463”; and

- (b) for “**B** is £49”, substitute “**B** is £50”.

###### 7

In regulation 20(5), for “**B** is £99”, substitute “**B** is £102”.

#### Amendment of the Amendment Regulations

###### 8

The Amendment Regulations are amended in accordance with regulation 9.

###### 9

In the Welsh text of regulation 7(2), for “17,640 ewro”, substitute “18,099 ewro”.

## Signed

Jane E. Hutt — Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 1 August 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Assembly Learning Grants (European Institutions) (Wales) Regulations 2008 (“the Principal Regulations”).

The Principal Regulations provide for support for eligible students taking designated higher education courses at the Bologna Center, the College of Europe or the European University Institute.

The Principal Regulations provide that a grant for fees is available for one eligible student at the Bologna Center. A grant for fees, grants for living and other costs, a disabled students' allowance and a grant for dependants are available for up to two eligible students at the College of Europe. Grants for living and other costs, a disabled students' allowance and a grant for dependants are available for one eligible student at the European University Institute.

The Principal Regulations were amended by the Assembly Learning Grants (European Institutions) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 in order that they apply in respect of an academic year beginning on or after 1 September 2009. These Regulations amend the amounts of certain support payable to students for an academic year beginning on or after 1 September 2009.

Regulation 4 amends the maximum amount of grant for fees payable to a Bologna Center student.

Regulation 5 amends the maximum amount of grant for fees payable to a College of Europe student.

Regulations 6 and 7 amend the figures used in calculating grant for living costs and grant for travel payable to a College of Europe student.

Regulations 8 and 9 correct a typographical error in the Welsh text of the Assembly Learning Grants (European Institutions) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008.
