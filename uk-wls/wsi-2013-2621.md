# The Building (Amendment No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2013

Made: 14 October 2013

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 16 October 2013

Coming into force: 14 November 2013

The Welsh Ministers have consulted the Building Regulations Advisory Committee for Wales and such other bodies as appear to the Welsh Ministers to be representative of the interests concerned in accordance with section 14(7) of that Act.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of these regulations is the Building (Amendment No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2013.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales but do not apply in relation to excepted energy buildings in Wales.

- (3) These Regulations come into force on 14 November 2013.

- (4) In this regulation “excepted energy building” (“*adeilad ynni a eithrir”*) has the same meaning as in Article 1(3) and the Schedule to the Welsh Ministers (Transfer of Functions) (No.2) Order 2009[^f00003].

#### Amendment to the Building Regulations 2010

###### 2

In the Building Regulations 2010[^f00004] for Schedule 3 (Self-certification Schemes and Exemptions from Requirement to Give Building Notice or Deposit Full Plans)[^f00005] substitute Schedule 3 as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Carl Sargeant — Minister for Housing and Regeneration, one of the Welsh Ministers — 14 October 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Building Regulations 2010 (“the 2010 Regulations”).

Regulation 2 substitutes a new Schedule 3 to the 2010 Regulations in relation to Wales.

The new Schedule 3 adds bodies to the list of bodies in paragraphs 13, 17, 21, 22, 23 and 24 which are able to register persons authorised under the self-certification scheme provided for by regulations 12 and 20 of the 2010 Regulations. The remaining paragraphs are unchanged from the Schedule as it applied in relation to Wales other than excepted energy buildings in Wales.

A regulatory impact assessment has been carried out in respect of the proposed amendments to the 2010 Regulations. The assessment was carried out by the UK Government on an England and Wales basis and is published at: http://www.legislation. gov.uk/uksi/2013/1105/impacts
