# The Public Transport Users' Committee for Wales (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 2 November 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 3 November 2009

Coming into force: 25 November 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by sections 8, 9 and 10 of the Transport (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Public Transport Users' Committee for Wales (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2009. It comes into force on 25 November 2009 and applies in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment to the Public Transport Users' Committee for Wales (Establishment) Order 2009

###### 2

- (1) The Public Transport Users' Committee for Wales (Establishment) Order 2009[^f00003] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In the Welsh text, in article 7(1)(ch), there is substituted the word “gweithdrefn” for the word “gweithdrefnau.”

- (3) In the Welsh text, in article 8(1) there is substituted the word “weithdrefn” for the word “weithdrefnau.”

## Signed

Ieuan Wyn Jones — Minister for the Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers — 2 November 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order amends the Public Transport Users' Committee for Wales (Establishment) Order 2009.

Specifically this Order amends articles 7(1)(ch) and 8(1) in respect of the Welsh text only, replacing the word “gweithdrefnau” (meaning “procedures”) with the word “gweithdrefn” (meaning “procedure”).
