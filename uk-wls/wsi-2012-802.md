# The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2012

Made: 10 March 2012

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the power conferred by section 241(3) of the Planning Act 2008[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2012.

#### Provisions coming into force on 30 April 2012 in relation to Wales

###### 2

The following provisions of the Planning Act 2008 (so far as they are not yet in force) shall come into force in relation to Wales on 30 April 2012—

- (a) section 188;

- (b) section 197 and Schedule 11; and

- (c) section 238 and Schedule 13, so far as they give effect to the repeals specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — Repeals (Wales)

| Reference | Extent of repeal |
| --- | --- |
| Town and Country Planning Act [1990 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8) | Section 61A(1) In Schedule 4A, paragraph 2(4) and (5) |

## Signed

John Griffiths — Minister for Environment and Sustainable Development, one of the Welsh Ministers — 10 March 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, on 30 April 2012, the following provisions of the Planning Act 2008, in relation to Wales, so far as they are not already in force —

section 188 (local development orders: removal of requirement to implement policies);

section 197 and Schedule 11 (appeals: miscellaneous amendments); and

section 238 and Schedule 13 so far as they give effect to the repeals specified in the Schedule to this Order, which relate to local development orders.
