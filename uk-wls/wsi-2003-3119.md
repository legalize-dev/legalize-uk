# The Feeding Stuffs, the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) and the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) (Amendment) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2003

Made: 2nd December 2003

Coming into force: 3rd December 2003

The National Assembly for Wales acting in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 66(1), 74A and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001] (as read with regulation 14 of the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00002] and articles 2 and 6 of the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Dissolution) Order 2002[^f00003]) and now exercisable by it in relation to Wales[^f00004], after consultation as required by section 84(1) of that Act and by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00005], and being designated[^f00006] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00007] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by that last mentioned section (in so far as these Regulations cannot be made under the powers of the Agriculture Act 1970 specified above), makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs, the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) and the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) (Amendment) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2003.

- (2) These Regulations shall apply to Wales only and shall come into force on 3rd December 2003.

#### Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001

###### 2

- (1) The Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001[^f00008] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) For Part II of the Table set out in Schedule 3 (permitted additives and provisions relating to their use) there shall be substituted the Part set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (3) In Part IX of Schedule 3 —

- (a) the following text shall be inserted immediately after the reference to Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 688/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0688) —

> > - Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 871/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0871) permanently authorising a new additive manganomanganic oxide in feedingstuffs¹⁷.
> - Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 877/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0877) provisionally authorising the use of the acidity regulator “Benzoic acid” in feedingstuffs¹⁸.

; and

- (b) the following text is inserted at the end —

> > (17) OJ No. L125, 21.5.2003, p.3
> (18) OJ No. L126, 22.5.2005, p.24.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations, which apply to Wales only, further amend the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001 (S.I. [2001/343 (W. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2001/343), as already amended).

2 These Regulations implement Commission Directive [2003/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0007) amending the conditions for authorisation of canthaxanthin in feedingstuffs in accordance with Council Directive [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524) (OJ No. L22, 25.1.2003, p. 28).

3 These Regulations modify the controls in the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001 regulating the presence of additives in feedingstuffs by specifying new maximum levels in feed for the colourant canthaxanthin (*regulation 2(2) and the Schedule*).

4 They also provide for the enforcement of Commission Regulation (EC) No.871/2003 permanently authorising a new additive manganomanganic oxide in feedingstuffs (OJ No. L125, 21.5.2003, p.3) and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 877/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0877) provisionally authorising the use of the acidity regulator “Benzoic acid” in feedingstuffs (OJ No. L126, 22.5.2003, p.24) (*regulation 2(3)*).

5 These Regulations also make consequential amendments to regulations 7(1) and 25(1) of the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2001 (*regulation 3*) and consequential amendments to the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999 and the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) Regulations 1999 (*regulations 4 to 11*).

6 A regulatory appraisal has been prepared in relation to these Regulations and has been placed in the library of the National Assembly for Wales, together with a transposition note setting out how the main elements of Directive [2003/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0007) are transposed into domestic law by these Regulations. Copies may be obtained from the Animal Feed Unit of the Food Standards Agency, 11th Floor, Southgate House, Cardiff, CF10 1EN.
