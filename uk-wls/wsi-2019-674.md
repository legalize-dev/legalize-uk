# The Plant Health (Amendment) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 19th March 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2)

The Welsh Ministers make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by—in relation to Part 1, the powers mentioned in paragraphs (b) and (c);in relation to Part 2, section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 ;in relation to Parts 3 to 5, paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 .

The Welsh Ministers make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by—

in relation to Part 1, the powers mentioned in paragraphs (b) and (c);

in relation to Part 2, section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 ;

in relation to Parts 3 to 5, paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 .

The Welsh Ministers are designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union.

A draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by the National Assembly for Wales in accordance with paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972 and paragraph 1(8) of Schedule 7 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.
