# The Agriculture Act 2020 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Regulations 2020

Made: 29 December 2020

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the power conferred by section 57(3)(b) of the Agriculture Act 2020[^f00001], make the following Regulations:

#### Title

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Agriculture Act 2020 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Regulations 2020.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1 January 2021

###### 2

The following provisions of the Agriculture Act 2020 come into force on 1 January 2021—

- (a) paragraphs 7 and 8 of Part 2 of Schedule 5 (provision relating to Wales), and

- (b) section 46 (Wales), so far as relating to those paragraphs.

## Signed

Lesley Griffiths — Minister for Environment, Energy and Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 29 December 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made by the Welsh Ministers and bring into force provisions of the Agriculture Act [2020 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/21) (“the Act”).

Regulation 2(a) brings into force, on 1 January 2021, paragraphs 7 and 8 of Part 2 of Schedule 5 to the Act.

Regulation 2(b) brings into force, on 1 January 2021, section 46 of the Act so far as relating to those paragraphs.

Paragraph 7 of Schedule 5 provides for the Welsh Ministers to make and publish a declaration that there are “exceptional market conditions” (as defined in that paragraph) which justify the making available of powers conferred by paragraph 8 of Schedule 5.

Paragraph 8 of Schedule 5 provides that the Welsh Ministers may, while a declaration made under paragraph 7 has effect, give, or agree to give, financial assistance to agricultural producers in Wales, subject to such conditions as the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate.
