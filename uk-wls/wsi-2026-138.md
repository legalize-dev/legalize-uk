# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 23 November 2026

Coming into force: 16 December 2026

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 83(5)(b), 84(b), 87 and 196(2) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014[^f00001], make the following Regulations.

#### Title and coming into force

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 16 December 2026.

#### Amendments to the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015

###### 2

- (1) The [Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2015/1818/contents)[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 8 (contact with a child in care), for “d” substitute “e”.

- (3) In regulation 14(2) (notification of placement), in sub-paragraph (c), after “person” in the first place it occurs, insert “mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 34(1) of the 1989 Act”.

- (4) In Schedule 2 (care and support plans), in paragraph 3(1), for “sibling for whom the responsible authority or another authority is providing accommodation, and the children have not been placed together” substitute “brother or sister (including any person who is in that relationship by virtue of a marriage or civil partnership or an enduring family relationship)”.

## Signed

Delyth Jewell — Deputy Minister for Social Care, Mental Health and Women’s Health, under the authority of the Cabinet Minister for Health and Care, one of the Welsh Ministers — 23 November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the [Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2015/1818/contents) (“*the 2015 Regulations*”).

The 2015 Regulations make provision about care planning for looked after children, and other associated matters.

[Regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2026/138/regulation/2/made) of these Regulations amends the 2015 Regulations.

Regulation 2(2) amends regulation 8 of the 2015 Regulations to require local authorities to have regard to the child’s care and support plan when considering whether contact between the child and any brother or sister (as provided for in section 34(1)(e) of the Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41) (“*the 1989 Act*”)) is consistent with safeguarding and promoting the well-being of the child.

Regulation 2(3) amends regulation 14(2)(c) of the 2015 Regulations to include a reference to persons mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 34(1) of the 1989 Act.

Regulation 2(4) amends Schedule 2 to the 2015 Regulations by making an amendment to paragraph 3(1) of that Schedule to require the arrangements made to promote contact between the child and their brother or sister to be included in the child’s care and support plan.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations.
