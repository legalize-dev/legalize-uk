# The Coleg Llysfasi College Further Education Corporation (Dissolution) Order 2010

Made: 6 July 2010

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 8 July 2010

Coming into force: 1 August 2010

Accordingly, the Welsh Ministers make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Coleg Llysfasi College Further Education Corporation (Dissolution) Order 2010 and it comes into force on 1 August 2010.

#### Dissolution and Transfer

###### 2

On 1 August 2010 Coleg Llysfasi College Further Education Corporation is dissolved and its property, rights and liabilities are transferred to Deeside College Further Education Corporation (Corfforaeth Addysg Bellach Coleg Glannau Dyfrdwy), being a corporate body wholly or mainly engaged in the provision of educational facilities or services.

#### Transfer of Staff

###### 3

Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 applies to any person employed by Coleg Llysfasi College Further Education Corporation immediately before 1 August 2010 as if the references in that section to—

- (a) a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;

- (b) the operative date were to 1 August 2010;

- (c) the transferor were to Coleg Llysfasi College Further Education Corporation; and

- (d) the corporation were to Deeside College Further Education Corporation (Corfforaeth Addysg Bellach Coleg Glannau Dyfrdwy).

## Signed

Leighton Andrews — Minister for Children, Education and Lifelong Learning, one of the Welsh Ministers — 6 July 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves the further education corporation established to conduct the educational institution called Coleg Llysfasi College with effect from 1 August 2010. It provides for the transfer of its property, rights and liabilities to Deeside College Further Education Corporation (Corfforaeth Addysg Bellach Coleg Glannau Dyfrdwy) and secures the rights of its employees by applying, with modification, section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act [1992 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/13).
