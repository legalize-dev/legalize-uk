# The Nutrition and Health Claims (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 19 July 2010

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 21 July 2010

Coming into force: 1 October 2010

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety [^f00003], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is The Nutrition and Health Claims (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and they come into force on 1 October 2010.

#### Amendment to the Nutrition and Health Claims (Wales) Regulations 2007

###### 2

The Nutrition and Health Claims (Wales) Regulations 2007[^f00004] are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) for the definition of “the Regulation” substitute the following definition—

> > - “the Regulation” (“*y Rheoliad*”) means Regulation [(EC) No. 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1924) of the European Parliament and of the Council on nutrition and health claims made on foods including any amendments to the Annex to that Regulation as may be made from time to time[^f00005].

## Signed

Gwerda Thomas — Deputy Minister for Social Services, under authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 19 July 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the definition of “the Regulation”, meaning Regulation [(EC) No. 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1924) of the European Parliament and of the Council on nutrition and health claims made on foods (OJ No. L12, 18.1.2007, p.3), contained in regulation 2 of the Nutrition and Health Claims (Wales) Regulations 2007 (S.I. [2007/2611](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2611)). The effect of the amendment is that a reference to the Regulation in S.I. [2007/2611](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2611) now includes any future amendments to its Annex, which consists of the list of authorised nutrition claims and conditions relating to their use (regulation 3).
