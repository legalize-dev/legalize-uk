# The Wales Centre for Health (Transfer of Functions, Property, Rights and Liabilities and Abolition) (Wales) Order 2009

Made: 28 September 2009

Coming into force: 1 October 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 3(4), (5) and (6) and 8 of the Health (Wales) Act 2003[^f00001], make the following Order—

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Wales Centre for Health (Transfer of Functions, Property, Rights and Liabilities and Abolition) (Wales) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 1 October 2009.

- (2) This Order applies to Wales.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 2003 Act” (“*Deddf 2003*”) means the Health (Wales) Act 2003;

- “transfer date” (“*dyddiad trosglwyddo*”) means 1 October 2009;

- “Wales Centre for Health” (“*Canolfan Iechyd Cymru*”) means the body known as Wales Centre for Health established by section 2 of the Health (Wales) Act 2003.

#### Transfer of the functions of the Wales Centre for Health to the Welsh Ministers

###### 3

With effect from the transfer date, all the functions of the Wales Centre for Health, as set out in section 3 of the 2003 Act, are transferred to the Welsh Ministers.

#### Transfer of property

###### 4

With effect from the transfer date, all property of the Wales Centre for Health will transfer to the Welsh Ministers, including the property listed in Schedule 1.

#### Enforceability of rights and transfer of liabilities

###### 5

- (1) Any right that was immediately before the transfer date enforceable by or against the Wales Centre for Health is transferred to the Welsh Ministers.

- (2) Any liability arising from the exercise of its functions that was, immediately before the transfer date, enforceable against the Wales Centre for Health is transferred to the Welsh Ministers.

#### Abolition of the Wales Centre for Health

###### 6

The Wales Centre for Health is abolished with effect from 1 October 2009.

#### Consequential revocation of the Wales Centre for Health (Constitution, Membership and Procedures) Regulations 2004

###### 7

The Wales Centre for Health (Constitution, Membership and Procedures) Regulations 2004[^f00002] are revoked with effect from the transfer date.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order abolishes on 1 October 2009 the body known as the Wales Centre for Health established under section 2 of the Health (Wales) Act 2003.

This Order revokes the Order specified in article 7 and makes provision for the transfer of functions (article 3); property (article 4) and rights and liabilities (article 5) to the Welsh Ministers.
