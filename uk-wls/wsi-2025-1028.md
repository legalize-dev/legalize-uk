# The Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2025

Made: 16 September 2025

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 126(2) of the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2025.

#### Day appointed for the coming into force of provisions on health impact assessments

###### 2

All of the provisions of Part 6 of the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (health impact assessments) come into force on 19 September 2025.

## Signed

Jeremy Miles — Cabinet Secretary for Health and Social Care, one of the Welsh Ministers — 16 September 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 126(2) of the Public Health (Wales) [Act 2017 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2017/2) (“*the Act*”) enables the Welsh Ministers to bring into force specified provisions of the Act. This Order is made by the Welsh Ministers in exercise of that power.

Article 2 brings into force the whole of Part 6 of the Act, which relates to health impact assessments, on 19 September 2025.
