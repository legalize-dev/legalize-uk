# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (City and County of Swansea) Designation Order 2008

Made: 15 July 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 17 July 2008

Coming into force: 1 September 2008

Accordingly, the Welsh Ministers make this Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (City and County of Swansea) Designation Order 2008 and shall come into force on 1 September 2008.

#### Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area

###### 2

The Welsh Ministers designate the area described in the Schedule as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and

- (b) a special enforcement area.

## SCHEDULE — Area designated as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area

This Order applies to the whole of the city and county of Swansea except the length of the M4 Motorway and its slip roads within the city and county and the A483 Fabian Way from its junction with the A4067 to the boundary with the county borough of Neath Port Talbot.

## Signed

Ieuan Wyn Jones — Minister for the Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers — 15 July 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the area described in the Schedule as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004.
