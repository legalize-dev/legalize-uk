# The Welshpool Church in Wales Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2020

Made: 24 November 2020

Laid before Senedd Cymru: 25 November 2020

Coming into force: 1 January 2021

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred upon the National Assembly for Wales by section 2(1) of the Act[^f00002], and now vested in them, on the application of the governing body, make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Welshpool Church in Wales Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2020 and it comes into force on 1 January 2021.

#### Exemption from the School Session Regulations

###### 2

Regulation 4(2), (3) and (4) of the Changing of School Session Times (Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00003] does not apply to the governing body of Welshpool Church in Wales Primary School[^f00004] of Gungrog Road, Welshpool, Powys, SY21 7EJ.

#### Time period

###### 3

This Order will have effect until 31 August 2021.

## Signed

Kirsty Williams — Minister for Education, one of the Welsh Ministers — 24 November 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that regulation 4(2), (3) and (4) of the Changing of School Session Times (Wales) Regulations 2009 does not apply to the governing body of Welshpool Church in Wales Primary School. This will allow the governing body to make alterations to when the school sessions start and end, including when the school day starts and ends, from 1 January 2021, rather than at the beginning of a school term or school year. Article 3 specifies that the Order has effect until 31 August 2021.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.
