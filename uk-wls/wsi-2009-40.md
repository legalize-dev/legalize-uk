# The Local Government (Assistants for Political Groups) (Remuneration) (Wales) Order 2009

Made: 14 January 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 19 January 2009

Coming into force: 1 April 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 9(4), 9(4A) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Local Government (Assistants for Political Groups) (Remuneration) (Wales) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 1 April 2009.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Local Government and Housing Act 1989;

- “the National Joint Council” (“*y Cyd-gyngor Cenedlaethol*”) means the National Joint Council for Local Government Services; and

- “the National Joint Council’s Scheme” (“*Cynllun y Cyd-gynor Cenedlaethol*”) means the National Joint Council’s Scheme of Conditions of Service.

###### 3

- (1) The point specified for the purposes of section 9(4)(a) of the Act is spinal column point 44 of the salary scales for local government officers incorporated in the National Joint Council’s Scheme.

- (2) The amount which corresponds to the point specified in paragraph (1) is such amount as is published by the National Joint Council from time to time with respect to full-time posts under a local authority in England and Wales.

#### Revocation

###### 4

In so far as it applies to relevant authorities in Wales, the Local Government (Assistants for Political Groups) (Remuneration) Order 1995[^f00003] is revoked.

## Signed

Brian Gibbons — Minister for Social Justice and Local Government, one of the Welsh Ministers — 14 January 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

One of the requirements relating to the terms on which a person is appointed to, or holds, an appointment in pursuance of section 9 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (assistants for political groups) is that the annual rate of remuneration for the post is less than £13,500 or such higher amount as may be specified by Order made by the Welsh Ministers.

This Order, which applies to local authorities in Wales provides for the annual rate of remuneration to be the maximum amount that is from time to time fixed by the National Joint Council for Local Government Services as that applicable to spinal column point 44 on their salary scales for local government officers.

As respects local authorities in Wales, the Local Government (Assistants for Political Groups) (Remuneration) Order 1995 is revoked.
