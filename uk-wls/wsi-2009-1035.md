# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2009

Made: 22 April 2009

Coming into force: 23 April 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by section 21 of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001] and with the consent of the Treasury in accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to that Act, hereby make the following Order.

#### Title, Commencement and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 23 April 2009.

- (2) In this Order “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

#### Originating Capital of NHS Trusts

###### 2

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule is the amount specified in corresponding column 2 of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

| NHS Trust | Originating Capital |
| --- | --- |
| Abertawe Bro Morgannwg University National Health Service Trust[^f00002] | £485,420,000 |
| Cwm Taf National Health Service Trust[^f00003] | £277,694,000 |
| Hywel Dda National Health Service Trust[^f00004] | £165,253,000 |
| North Wales National Health Service Trust[^f00005] | £306,609,000 |

## Signed

Edwina Hart — Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 22 April 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines that amount of originating capital, provided for in paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006, of certain National Health Service Trusts established under that Act.
