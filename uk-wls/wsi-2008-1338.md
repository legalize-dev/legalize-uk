# The Abortion (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2008

Made: 21 May 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 22 May 2008

Coming into force: 17 June 2008

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2 of the Abortion Act 1967[^f00001] hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement, application and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Abortion (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2008 and they come into force on 17 June 2008.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

- (3) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” (“*y prif Reoliadau*”) means the Abortion Regulations 1991[^f00002].

#### Amendment of regulation 2 of the principal Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the principal Regulations, insert the following definitions in the appropriate place in the alphabetical order—

> > - “the Statistics Board” means the Statistics Board established under section 1 of the Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007[^f00003];
> - “the Chief Medical Officer for Wales” means the Chief Medical Officer to the Welsh Assembly Government;

#### Amendment of Regulation 4 of the principal Regulations

###### 3

In regulation 4(2)(b) (which relates to notice of termination and information relating to the termination) of the principal Regulations, for “National Assembly for Wales” substitute “Welsh Assembly Government”.

#### Amendment of regulation 5 of the principal Regulations

###### 4

In regulation 5 (restriction on disclosure of information) of the principal Regulations —

- (a) in paragraph (a)(i), for “the National Assembly for Wales” substitute “the Welsh Assembly Government”;

- (b) for paragraph (a)(ii) substitute—

> > (ii) to the chairman of the Statistics Board or a member of the Statistics Board’s staff who has been duly authorised by the chairman.

## Signed

Edwina Hart — Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 21 May 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulations 2 and 4 of the Abortion Regulations 1991[^f00004] (“the principal Regulations”) in order to reflect the changes upon the title of the Chief Medical Officer for Wales consequential to the establishment of the Welsh Assembly Government in accordance with the Government of Wales Act 2006[^f00005].

They also amend regulations 2 and 5 of the principal Regulations to reflect the changes made by the Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007[^f00006] which establishes an independent statistics board which is responsible for the statistical functions previously carried out by the Registrar General through its administrative arm, the Office of National Statistics.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
