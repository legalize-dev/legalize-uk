# The Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Amendment) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2002

Made: 23rd December 2002

Coming into force: 24th December 2002

The National Assembly for Wales, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Amendment) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2002; they apply in relation to Wales, and come into force on 24th December 2002.

#### Amendment of the Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998

###### 2

- (1) The Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998[^f00003] shall be amended in their application to Wales in accordance with paragraph (2) below.

- (2) In the Schedule, in the column headed “*Instrument*”, after “Commission Decision [2001/664/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0664)”, there is added “Commission Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903)”[^f00004], and in the column headed “*Reference*”, after “OJ No. L233, 31.8.01, p. 49”, there is added “OJ No. L312, 15.11.02, p. 28”.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00005]

Rhodri Morgan — First Minister of the National Assembly for Wales — 23rd December 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement in Wales Commission Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903) (OJ No. L312, 15.11.02, p. 28) amending Commission Decision [96/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0301) (“the principal Decision”) (OJ No. L115, 9.5.1996, p.47) authorising member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination of *Pseudomonas solanacearum* (Smith) Smith as regards Egypt. They do so by adding Commission Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903) to the list of instruments amending the principal Decision contained in the Schedule to the Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998 ([SI 1998/201](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/201)) (regulation 2). Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903) renews the framework within which potatoes may be imported from Egypt into the territory of the European Community for the 2002/2003 season.

*Pseudomonas solanacearum* (Smith) Smith is now referred to as *Ralstonia solanacearum* Smith (Yabuuchi) et al. (see Council Directive [98/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0057) (OJ No. L235, 21.9.1998)).

No Regulatory Appraisal has been carried out in respect of the extension of these controls to the 2002/2003 season.
