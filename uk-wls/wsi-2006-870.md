# The Children Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) (Wales) Order 2006

Made: 21 March 2006

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 67(3) and (4), of the Children Act 2004 [^f00001] and with the consent of the Secretary of State [^f00002] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Name, interpretation and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order is called the Children Act 2004 (Commencement No. 7) (Wales) Order 2006.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Children Act 2004.

- (3) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

- (1) 1 September 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 25 of the Act (Co-operation to improve well-being: Wales).

- (2) 1 October 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 31 of the Act (Establishment of LSCBs[^f00003] in Wales).

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

D. Elis-Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 21 March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made by the National Assembly for Wales, under the Children Act 2004 [^f00001] (“the Act”) and applies to Wales.

The Order brings into force on 1 September 2006, section 25 of the Act. Section 31 of the Act will come into force on 1 October 2006. The consent of the Secretary of State has been obtained in respect of the commencement of both sections of the Act.
