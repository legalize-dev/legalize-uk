# The Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2009

Made: 23 March 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 325(3) and (4) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title, application and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2009.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

- (3) In this Order “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 and “the commencement date” (“*y dyddiad cychwyn*”) means 30 March 2009.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 315 (armed forces: local connection test) comes into force on the commencement date in relation to applications—

- (i) for an allocation of housing accommodation, to which section 166 of the Housing Act 1996[^f00002] applies; or

- (ii) to which Part VII (homelessness), as applied by section 183(1) (application for assistance) of that Act, applies,

made on or after the commencement date.

## Signed

Jocelyn Davies — Under authority of the Minister for Environment, Sustainability and Housing, one of the Welsh Ministers — 23 March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the first Commencement Order made under the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 in relation to Wales.

Article 2 brings into force section 315 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 on 30 March 2009.
