# The Food (Jelly Mini-Cups) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2011

Made: 2 March 2011

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 7 March 2011

Coming into force: 31 March 2011

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Food (Jelly Mini-Cups) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2011 and they come into force on 31 March 2011.

#### Revocation

###### 2

- (1) The Food (Jelly Mini-Cups) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00004] are revoked.

- (2) The Food (Jelly Mini-Cups) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2010[^f00005] are revoked.

## Signed

Gwenda Thomas — Deputy Minister for Social Services, under authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 2 March 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations revoke the Food (Jelly Mini-Cups) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/3379) (W.301)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2009/3379)) (“the principal Regulations”) together with the Food (Jelly Mini-Cups) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/1376 (W.121)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2010/1376)).

2 The principal Regulations implemented in relation to Wales Commission Decision [2004/374/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0374) suspending the placing on the market and import of jelly mini-cups containing the food additives E400, E401, E402, E403, E404, E405, E406, E407, E407a, E410, E412, E413, E414, E415, E417 and/or E418 (OJ No. L118, 23.4.2004, p.70) (“Commission Decision [2004/374/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0374)”). Commission Decision [2004/374/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0374) has been repealed by Commission Directive 2010/69/EU amending the Annexes to European Parliament and Council Directive [95/2/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0002) on food additives other than colours and sweeteners (OJ No. L279, 23.10.2010, p.22).
