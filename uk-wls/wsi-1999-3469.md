# The Relocation Grants (Form of Application) and (Welsh Form of Application) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 1999

Made: 22nd December 1999

Coming into force: 23rd December 1999

The National Assembly for Wales makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers given to the Secretary of State by sections 132(3) and (4) and 146(1) and (2) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00001] as extended by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00002], which are now vested in it[^f00003]:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) and (Welsh Form of Application) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 23rd December 1999.

- (2) These Regulations apply to Wales only.

#### Amendments

###### 2

- (1) The form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) Regulations 1997[^f00004] is amended as set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

- (2) the form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Welsh Form of Application) Regulations 1999[^f00005] is amended as set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend:

- (a) the Form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/2847](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2847) as amended) to be used for an application for relocation grant payable under sections 131 to 140 of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996; and

- (b) the equivalent Welsh Form set out in the Schedule to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Welsh Form of Application) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/2315](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2315)).

The Relocation Grants Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/2764](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2764)), apply the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/2890](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2890)) as they have effect from time to time, with the modifications prescribed. Amendments to the Housing Renewal Grants Regulations 1996 by the Housing Renewal Grants (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/3468](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/3468)) have necessitated amendments to the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) Regulations 1997 and the Relocation Grants (Form of Application) (Welsh Form of Application) Regulations 1999, and these Regulations make the necessary amendments.
