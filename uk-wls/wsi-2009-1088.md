# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009

Made: 27 April 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 28 April 2009

Coming into force: 28 May 2009

The Welsh Ministers are designated[^f00001] for the purposes of making Regulations under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, and make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009, they apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 28 May 2009.

#### Amendment to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Wales) Regulations 2007

###### 2

The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Wales) Regulations 2007[^f00003] are amended by replacing paragraphs (7) and (8) of regulation 4 with the following paragraph—

> > (7) Part 3 (with the exception of regulation 25) and Parts 4 to 9 do not apply in relation to products of animal origin specified in Article 2 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No 206/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0206) on the introduction into the Community of personal consignments of products of animal origin[^f00004].

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006[^f00005] are revoked.

## Signed

Elin Jones — Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 27 April 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Wales) Regulations 2007 to take account of the changes in personal imports permitted from third countries brought about by Commission Regulation [(EC) No 206/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0206) on the introduction into the Community of personal consignments of products of animal origin.

These Regulations also revoke the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (Wales) (Amendment) No. 2) Regulations 2006.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.
