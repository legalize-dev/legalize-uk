## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [2004 c. 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/21). The functions under section 21 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 are now vested in the Welsh Ministers so far as they are exercisable in relation to Wales. They were previously vested in the National Assembly for Wales by section 62 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004. By virtue of paragraph 30 of Schedule 11 to the Government of Wales Act [2006 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/32), they were transferred to the Welsh Ministers.
