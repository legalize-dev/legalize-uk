# The Commissioner for Older People (Wales) Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2006

Made: 10 October 2006

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 23 of the Commissioner for Older People (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Name, interpretation and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order is called the Commissioner for Older People (Wales) Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2006.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Commissioner for Older People (Wales) Act 2006.

- (3) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

14 October 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of sections 1 to 22 of the Act.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00002]

D. Elis-Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 10 October 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made by the National Assembly for Wales, under the Commissioner for Older People (Wales) Act 2006[^f00003] (“*the Act*”) and applies to Wales.

The Order brings into force on 14 October 2006 sections 1 to 22 of the Act.

The remaining provisions of the Act came into force on Royal Assent.
