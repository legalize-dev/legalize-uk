# The TSE (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 24 May 2005

Coming into force: 31 May 2005

The National Assembly for Wales, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures in the veterinary field for the protection of public health, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section, after consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the TSE (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2005, apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 31 May 2005.

#### Amendment of the TSE (Wales) Regulations 2002

###### 2

- (1) The TSE (Wales) Regulations 2002[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 3 (interpretation) for the definition of “the Community Transitional Measures” substitute the following definition —

> > - “the Community Transitional Measures” means — Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1248/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1248) of 22 June 2001 amending Annexes III, X and XI to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards epidemio-surveillance and testing of transmissible spongiform encephalopathies[^f00005]; Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1326/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1326) of 29 June 2001 laying down transitional measures to permit the changeover to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies, and amending Annexes VII and XI to that Regulation[^f00006]; Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 270/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0270) of 14 February 2002 amending Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards specified risk material and epidemio-surveillance for transmissible spongiform encephalopathies and amending Regulation [(EC) No. 1326/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1326) as regards animal feeding and the placing on the market of ovine and caprine animals and products thereof[^f00007]; Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1494/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1494) of 21 August 2002 amending Annexes III, VII and XI to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and the Council as regards monitoring of bovine spongiform encephalopathy, eradication of transmissible spongiform encephalopathy, removal of specified risk materials and rules for importation of live animals and products of animal origin[^f00008]; Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1139/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1139) of 27 June 2003 amending Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards monitoring programmes and specified risk material[^f00009]; and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1492/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1492) of 23 August 2004 amending Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards eradication measures for transmissible spongiform encephalopathies in bovine, ovine and caprine animals, the trade and importation of semen and embryos of bovine and caprine animals and specified risk material[^f00010];

- (3) In paragraph (2)(b) of regulation 33 (removal of specified risk material from carcases in slaughterhouses) insert the phrase “, ileum” before the word “and” where it appears first.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00011].

D. Elis-Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly — 24 May 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations amend the TSE (Wales) Regulations 2002 (S.I. [2002/1416 (W.142)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2002/1416) as already amended).

2 These Regulations amend S.I. [2002/1416 (W.142)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2002/1416) by — a bringing up to date the definition of “the Community Transitional Measures” in regulation 3(1) of that instrument so as to cover the amendment of Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies (OJ No. L147, 31.5.2001, p.1) by — i Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1494/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1494) (OJ No. L225, 22.8.2002, p.3), ii Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1139/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1139) (OJ No. L160, 28.6.2003, p.22), and iii Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1492/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1492) (OJ No. L274, 24.8.2004, p.3) (regulation 2(2)), and b adding the ileum to the list of tissues that have to be removed from older sheep and goats that have been slaughtered in a slaughterhouse or brought to a slaughterhouse following slaughter elsewhere (regulation 2(3)).

3 A regulatory appraisal of the effect that these Regulations will have on the costs of business has been prepared pursuant to section 65 of the Government of Wales Act 1998 and placed in the Library of the National Assembly for Wales. Copies may be obtained from the Food Standards Agency, 1st Floor, Southgate House, Cardiff CF10 1EW.
