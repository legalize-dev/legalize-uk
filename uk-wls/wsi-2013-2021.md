# The Free School Lunches and Milk (Universal Credit) (Wales) Order 2013

Made: 15 August 2013

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 16 August 2013

Coming into force: 6 September 2013

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 512ZB(4)(a)(ai), 512ZB(4)(b)(ai) and 568 of the Education Act 1996[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

#### Title, commencement, application and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Free School Lunches and Milk (Universal Credit) (Wales) Order 2013 and it comes into force on 6 September 2013.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

- (3) In this Order —

- “the 1996 Act” (“Deddf 1996”) means the Education Act 1996; and

- “universal credit” (“credyd cynhwysol”) means universal credit under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012.[^f00003]

#### Prescribed circumstances: receipt of universal credit

###### 2

- (1) The circumstances that are prescribed for the purposes of section 512ZB(4)(a)(ai) of the 1996 Act are that C’s parent is in receipt of universal credit on or after 6 September 2013.[^f00004]

- (2) The circumstances that are prescribed for the purposes of section 512ZB(4)(b)(ai) of the 1996 Act are that C is in receipt of universal credit on or after 6 September 2013.

## Signed

Huw Lewis — Minister for Education and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers — 15 August 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes, for the purposes of section 512ZB of the Education Act 1996, that where a person or their parent is in receipt of universal credit on or after 6 September 2013, they are within section 512ZB(4).

Persons within section 512ZB(4) are eligible for free school lunches and milk when a request has been made by them (or on their behalf) that these be provided free of charge.
