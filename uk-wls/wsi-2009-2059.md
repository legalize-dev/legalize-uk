# The Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 23 July 2009

Coming into force: 1 October 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of powers conferred on them by sections 18(1) and 204(1) of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006(1[^f00001]), after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 18(3) of that Act, make the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 1 October 2009.

- (2) In this Order, “the principal Order” (“*y prif Or hymyn*”) means the Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00002].

#### Amendment of article 3 of the principal Order

###### 2

Article 3(2)(d) of the principal Order is omitted.

## Signed

Edwina Hart — Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers. — 23 July 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 in order to amend the functions of Velindre National Health Service Trust.

The functions are amended to take into account the exercise by the ublic Health Wales National Health Service Trust of public health functions formerly exercisable by Velindre National Health Service Trust.
