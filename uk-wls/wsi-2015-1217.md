# The Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2015

Made: 20 April 2015

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the power conferred upon them by section 156(2) of the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2015.

- (2) In this Order “Measure” means the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Part 7 of the Measure (Welsh Language Tribunal) comes into force on 30 April 2015 to the extent it is not already in force.

## Signed

Carwyn Jones — First Minister of Wales — 20 April 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 156(2) of the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011 (“the Measure”). Article 2 of this Order brings Part 7 of the Measure into force to the extent that it is not already in force on 30 April 2015. Part 7 of the Measure makes provision in relation to the Welsh Language Tribunal.
