# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 16) Regulations 2021

Made: at 4.50 p.m. on 1 December 2021

Laid before Senedd Cymru: 2 December 2021

Coming into force: at 8.00 p.m. on 1 December 2021

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 45B and 45P(2) of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00001], make the following Regulations.

#### Title and coming into force

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 16) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into force at 8.00 p.m. on 1 December 2021.

#### Amendment to the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Wales) Regulations 2020

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Wales) Regulations 2020[^f00002] are amended as follows.

#### Amendment to regulation 6HB(3)

###### 3

In regulation 6HB(3) (consequences of receiving inconclusive test result: regulation 2A travellers), in the Welsh text, for “14 o ddiwrnodau” substitute “10 niwrnod”.

## Signed

Eluned Morgan — Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — At 4.50 p.m. on 1 December 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Wales) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/574 (W. 132)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2020/574)) (“*the International Travel Regulations*”).

These Regulations correct an error in the Welsh text of the International Travel Regulations.

There has been no regulatory impact assessment in relation to these Regulations due to the need to put them in place urgently to deal with a serious and imminent threat to public health.
