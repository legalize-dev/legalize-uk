# The Children and Young Persons Act 2008 (Commencement No.3) (Wales) Order 2010

Made: 11 March 2010

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 40 and 44(3) and (5) of the Children and Young Persons Act 2008[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title, interpretation and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Children and Young Persons Act 2008 (Commencement No.3) (Wales) Order 2010.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Children and Young Persons Act 2008.

- (3) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

31 March 2010 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act —

- (a) section 8(3) (provision of accommodation and maintenance for children who are looked after by a local authority), Schedule 2 (transitory modifications of Schedule 2 to the Children Act 1989)[^f00002] and Schedule 4 (repeals) in so far as it relates to the repeal relating to section 12 of the Adoption and Children Act 2002[^f00003]; and

- (b) section 34.

## Signed

Gwenda Thomas — Deputy Minister for Social Services under the authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 11 March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 31 March 2010 sections 8 and 34 of, and Schedules 2 and 4 to, the Children and Young Persons Act 2008, to the extent specified in the Order.
