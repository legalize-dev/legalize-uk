# The Plant Health (Amendment) (Wales) Order 2002

Made: 11th July 2002

Coming into force: 6 August 2002

The National Assembly for Wales, in relation to Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food by sections 2, 3(1) to (4) and 4(1) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001] as read with section 20 of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972[^f00002] and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales[^f00003] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Amendment) (Wales) Order 2002, it applies to Wales, and comes into force on 6th August 2002.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993 (“the principal Order”) so as to implement certain elements of Commission Directives [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) (OJ No. L77, 20.3.02, p.26) and [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (OJ No. L77, 20.3.02, p.23) in Wales which are not implemented in other legislation. Directive [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) amends the protected zones established under Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (OJ No. L169, 10.7.2000, p.1) (“the principal Plant Health Directive”) and scheduled in the Annex to Commission Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) (OJ No. L127, 9.5.2001, p. 38). Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) provides for consequential amendments to certain Annexes of the principal Plant Health Directive.

The amendments in this Order implementing Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) are at article 2(3) to 2(6) inclusive. Directive [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) is implemented by article 2(7), which amends Schedule 8 to the principal Order. The principal changes to the protected zones are removal of Great Britain from the protected zone in respect of *Beet necrotic yellow vein virus* (known as rhizomania) (article 2(3), (6)(a) and (c) and (7)(b)(i)), and re-definition of the Italian and Austrian protected zones in respect of the bacterium Erwinia amylovora (Burr.) Winsl. et al (known as fireblight) (article 2(4), (5), (6)(b) and (7)(a)). An amendment is made to the description of Citrus tristeza virus in Schedule 8 (article 2(7)(b)(ii)).

An additional amendment is made to the definition of Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) in article 2(1) of the principal Order so as to incorporate amendments made to it by Commission Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (article 2(2)).

A regulatory appraisal in respect of the changes to the protected zone in respect of *Beet necrotic yellow vein virus* is available from Plant Health Branch, National Assembly for Wales, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ.
