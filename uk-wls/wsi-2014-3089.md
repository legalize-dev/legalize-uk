# The Food Hygiene Rating (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No.2) Order 2014

Made: 18 November 2014

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers contained in section 27(2) of the Food Hygiene Rating (Wales) Act 2013[^f00001].

#### Title

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Food Hygiene Rating (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No.2) Order 2014.

#### Provision coming into force on 28 November 2014

###### 2

The appointed day for the coming into force of section 2(5)(b) of the Food Hygiene Rating (Wales) Act 2013 is 28 November 2014.

## Signed

Mark Drakeford — Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 18 November 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the second commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Food Hygiene Rating (Wales) Act 2013 (“the Act”).

Article 2 brings section 2(5)(b) of the Act fully into force on 28 November 2014 so as to extend the definition of a “food business establishment” to include an establishment that supplies food to another business.
