# The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Performance Indicators) (Wales) Order 2011

Made: 21 February 2011

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 1 March 2011

Coming into force: 1 April 2011

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 8(1) of the Local Government (Wales) Measure 2009[^f00001], and having consulted those persons they are required, by section 8(3) of that Measure, to consult, hereby make the following Order.

#### Title, commencement, and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Performance Indicators) (Wales) Order 2011.

- (2) This Order applies to fire and rescue authorities in Wales and comes into force on 1 April 2011.

#### Performance indicators

###### 2

The performance indicators by reference to which the performance of each fire and rescue authority in Wales will be measured in exercising their functions are hereby specified in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE — Risk Reduction and Community Safety

| Reference | Indicator |
| --- | --- |
| FRS/RRC/S/001 | i Total number of fires attended per 10,000 population; ii Total number of primary fires attended per 10,000 population; iii Total number of fires attended which were started accidentally in dwellings per 10,000 dwellings; iv Total number of fires attended which were started deliberately per 10,000 population. |
| FRS/RRC/S/002 | The number of: Deaths caused by fires per 100,000 population; Deaths caused by fires which were started accidentally per 100,000 population; Deaths caused by fires which were started deliberately per 100,000 population; Injuries (excluding precautionary checks) arising from fires per 100,000 population; Injuries (excluding precautionary checks) arising from fires which were started accidentally per 100,000 population; Injuries (excluding precautionary checks) arising from fires which were started deliberately per 100,000 population. |
| FRS/RRC/S/003 | The total number of fires in non-domestic premises per 1,000 non-domestic premises. |

## Signed

Carl Sargeant — Minister for Social Justice and Local Government, one of the Welsh Ministers — 21 February 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies for Wales performance indicators by reference to which the performance of fire and rescue authorities, in exercising their functions, will be measured from 1 April 2011.

Article 2 of this Order specifies that the performance indicators are set out in the Schedule to this Order.

A regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in connection with this Order and a copy can be obtained from Fire and Rescue Branch, Community Safety Division, Welsh Assembly Government, Rhydycar, Merthyr Tydfil, CF48 1UZ (telephone 01685 729221).
