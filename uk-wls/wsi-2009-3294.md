# The Local Transport Act 2008 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 2009

Made: 14 December 2009

Coming into force: 31 January 2010

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 134(6) of the Local Transport Act 2008[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title, application and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Local Transport Act 2008 (Commencement No.2) (Wales) Order 2009 and it applies in relation to Wales.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Local Transport Act 2008.

#### Commencement of local transport provisions in relation to Wales

###### 2

31 January 2010 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act—

- (a) Section 13 (in so far as it is not already in force).

- (b) Sections 14—18.

- (c) Section 68(2).

- (d) Section 131 so far as it relates to the following—

- Part 1 of Schedule 7,

- In Part 2 of Schedule 7, the repeals relating to section 116(2) and Schedule 10 of the Transport Act 2000[^f00002],

- In Part 2 of Schedule 7, the repeal relating to the Enterprise Act 2002[^f00003],

- In Part 3 of Schedule 7, the repeals relating to Sections 74, 75 and 79 of the Transport Act 1985[^f00004],

- In Part 3 of Schedule 7, the repeals relating to the Transport Act 2000,

- In Part 5 of Schedule 7, the repeals relating to the Transport Act 2000,

and accordingly those repeals.

## Signed

Ieuan Wyn Jones — Minister for the Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers — 14 December 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 31 January 2010 the following provisions of the Local Transport Act 2008 (“the Act”)[^f00005].

Part 3 Sections 13 to 18 (with the exception of Section 13(1) and 13(2) which came into force on 1 April 2009) which amend powers in the Transport Act 2000[^f00006] for local transport authorities to make quality partnership schemes.

Part 4 Section 68(2) which extends the powers of the Welsh Ministers to subsidise the provision of certain passenger transport services.

Part 8 Section 131 in so far as it relates to repeals in Schedule 7 and accordingly those repeals.
