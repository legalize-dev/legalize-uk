# The Food (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2004

Made: 13 July 2004

Coming into force: 31 July 2004

The National Assembly for Wales being designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by that section, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Food (Emergency Control) (Wales) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 2) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 31 July 2004.

#### Amendment to the Food (Peanuts from China) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2002

###### 2

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Peanuts from China) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2002 for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there is substituted the following definition —

> > “*the Commission Decision*” means Commission Decision [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) imposing special conditions on the import of peanuts and certain products derived from peanuts originating in or consigned from China as amended by Commission Decision [2002/233/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0233) , Commission Decision [2002/678/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0678) , Commission Decision [2003/550/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0550) and Commission Decision [2004/429/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0429) amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community ;

#### Amendment to the Food (Figs, Hazelnuts and Pistachios from Turkey) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2002

###### 3

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Figs, Hazelnuts and Pistachios from Turkey) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2002 for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there is substituted the following definition —

> > “*the Commission Decision*” means Commission Decision [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) imposing special conditions on the import of figs, hazelnuts and pistachios and certain products derived thereof originating in or consigned from Turkey as amended by Commission Decision [2002/233/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0233), Commission Decision [2002/679/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0679) , Commission Decision [2003/552/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0552) and Commission Decision [2004/429/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0429) amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community ;

#### Amendment to the Food (Brazil Nuts) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2003

###### 4

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Brazil Nuts) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2003 for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there is substituted the following definition —

> > “*the Commission Decision*” (“**Penderfyniad y Comisiwn**”) means Commission Decision [2003/493/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0493) imposing special conditions on the import of Brazil nuts in shell originating in or consigned from Brazil as amended by Commission Decision [2004/428/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0428) amending Decision [2003/493/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0493) as regards the points of entry through which Brazil nuts in shell originating in or consigned from Brazil may only be imported into the Community ;

#### Amendment to the Food (Pistachios from Iran) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2003

###### 5

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Pistachios from Iran) (Emergency Control) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2003 for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there is substituted the following definition —

> > “*the Commission Decision*” (“**Penderfyniad y Comisiwn**”) means Commission Decision [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830) repealing Commission Decision [97/613/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0613) and imposing special conditions on the import of pistachios and certain products derived from pistachios originating in or consigned from Iran as amended by Commission Decision [1998/400/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1998/0400) , Commission Decision [2000/238/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0238) , Commission Decision [2003/551/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0551) and Commission Decision [2004/429/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0429) amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community ;

#### Amendment to the Food (Peanuts from Egypt) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2003

###### 6

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Peanuts from Egypt) (Emergency Control) (Wales) Regulations 2003 for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there is substituted the following definition —

> > “*the Commission Decision*” (“**Penderfyniad y Comisiwn**”) means Commission Decision [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049) repealing Decision [1999/356/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0356) and imposing special conditions on the import of peanuts and certain products derived from peanuts originating in or consigned from Egypt as amended by Commission Decision [2003/580/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0580) and Commission Decision [2004/429/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0429) amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community ;
