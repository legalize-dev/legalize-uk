# The Feeding Stuffs (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 7 July 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 9 July 2008

Coming into force: 30 July 2008

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
