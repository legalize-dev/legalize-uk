# The Feeding Stuffs (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 7 July 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 9 July 2008

Coming into force: 30 July 2008

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 66(1), 68(1), 74A and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001].

There has been consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00002] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Title and Commencement

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and they come into force on 30 July 2008.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In the title of the Welsh text of Schedule 7, insert “ac a fwriedir” after “a ganiateir”.

- (3) In Chapter A of Schedule 7 (permitted feeding stuffs intended for particular nutritional purposes and provisions relating to their use), for the entries relating to reduction of the risk of milk fever, substitute the entries set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations further amend the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2006 (S.I. [2006/116 (W.14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/116)) as already amended by S.I. [2006/617 (W.69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/617), S.I. [2006/2928 (W.263)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/2928), S.I. [2006/3256 (W.296)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/3256) and S.I. [2007/3171 (W.277)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2007/3171) (“the Feeding Stuffs Regulations”).

2 These Regulations provide for the implementation of Commission Directive [2008/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0004) amending Directive [94/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0039) as regards feeding stuffs intended for the reduction of the risk of milk fever (OJ No. L6, 10.1.2008, p.4).

3 The Regulations amend the table in Schedule 7 (permitted feeding stuffs intended for particular nutritional purposes and provisions relating to their use) to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations, by replacing the existing entries relating to the reduction of the risk of milk fever with the revised entries contained in Commission Directive [2008/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0004). This Directive permits, subject to specified labelling requirements, two additional types of dietetic feed to be marketed for the reduction of milk fever in dairy cows.

4 These Regulations also correct an error in the Welsh language text of the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (regulation 2(2)).

5 A full regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations.
