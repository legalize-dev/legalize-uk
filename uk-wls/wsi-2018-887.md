# The Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2018

Made: 19 July 2018

Coming into force: 2 August 2018

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 18(1), 203 and 204(1) of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 to, the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001], after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 18(3) of that Act, make the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2018 and it comes into force on 2 August 2018.

- (2) In this Order, “the principal Order” (“y prif Orchymyn*”)* means the Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00002].

#### Amendment of article 1 of the principal Order

###### 2

In article 1(2) of the principal Order, in the definition of “the trust”, for “Velindre National Health Service Trust” substitute “Velindre University National Health Service Trust”.

#### Amendment of article 2 of the principal Order

###### 3

For article 2 of the principal Order, substitute—

> > (2) There is hereby established an NHS trust which shall be called Velindre University National Health Service Trust or Ymddiriedolaeth Gwasanaeth Iechyd Gwladol Prifysgol Felindre.

#### Significant teaching commitment

###### 4

After article 4 of the principal Order, insert—

> > (4A)
>   (1) The trust is to be regarded as having a significant teaching commitment by virtue of paragraph 5(3)(b) of Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00003].
>   (2) One of the non-executive directors must be appointed from Cardiff University.

#### Saving

###### 5

The change of name effected by regulation 3 does not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or

- (b) invalidate any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to Velindre National Health Service Trust, and all instruments or other documents which refer to that name must be construed as referring to the trust.

## Signed

Vaughan Gething — Cabinet Secretary for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 19 July 2018

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Velindre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 (“the principal Order”) in order to change the name of Velindre National Health Service Trust to Velindre University National Health Service Trust.

As Velindre National Health Service Trust will now have a significant teaching commitment, the Order specifies this and further amends the principal Order to prescribe that one of the non-executive directors must be appointed from Cardiff University.
