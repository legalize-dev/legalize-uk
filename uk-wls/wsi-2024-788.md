# The Agriculture Act 2020 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Regulations 2024

Made: 9 July 2024

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 57(3)(a) of the Agriculture Act 2020[^f00001], make the following Regulations.

#### Title and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the Agriculture Act 2020 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1 September 2024

###### 2

The following provisions of the Agriculture Act 2020 (“*the 2020 Act*”) come into force on 1 September 2024—

- (a) paragraphs 10 to 16 of Schedule 3 (succession on death or retirement: condition relating to occupation of a commercial unit),

- (b) paragraph 18 of Schedule 3 (succession on death or retirement: condition relating to suitability), and

- (c) section 36, so far as it relates to the paragraphs referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this regulation.

#### Transitional Provision

###### 3

- (1) Paragraph (2) applies to any application to the Tribunal made under Part 4 of the 1986 Act for a direction entitling the applicant to a tenancy of an agricultural holding on the death or retirement of a tenant where the date of death or the date of the giving of the retirement notice, as applicable, was before 1 September 2024.

- (2) In relation to any application to which this paragraph applies, Part 4 of the 1986 Act continues to apply on and after 1 September 2024 as it had effect immediately before that date, as if the amendments made by paragraphs 10 to 16 and 18 of Schedule 3 to the 2020 Act had not come into force.

- (3) In this regulation—

- “*the 1986 Act*” (“**Deddf 1986**”) means the Agricultural Holdings Act 1986[^f00002];

- “*application*” (“**cais**”) includes any proceedings arising out of any such application or any direction given in any such proceedings.

- (4) Expressions used in this regulation and in Part 4 of the 1986 Act have the same meaning as in that Part.

Huw Irranca-Davies — Cabinet Secretary for Climate Change and Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 9 July 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force in Wales, on 1 September 2024, paragraphs 10 to 16 and paragraph 18 of Schedule 3 to, and, in part, section 36 of the Agriculture Act [2020 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/21) (“*the Act*”). These are the third commencement regulations under the Act which apply in relation to Wales.

Regulation 2(a) brings into force paragraphs 10 to 16 of Schedule 3 to the Act which provide for the amendment of the Agricultural Holdings Act [1986 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/5) (“*the 1986 Act*”) in relation to succession on the death or retirement of a tenant and the condition that must be met by applicants relating to the occupation of a commercial unit.

Regulation 2(b) brings into force paragraph 18 of Schedule 3 to the Act which provides for the amendment of section 53 of the 1986 Act in relation to succession on the death or retirement of a tenant and the condition that must be met by applicants relating to suitability with regard to applications for the tenancy of a holding by a nominated successor.

Regulation 2(c) brings into force section 36 of the Act so far as it relates to the above provisions.

Regulation 3 makes transitional provision.
