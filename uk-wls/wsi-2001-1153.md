# The National Health Service Trusts (Cardiff and Vale National Health Service Trust) (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2001

Made: 19th March 2001

Coming into force: 31st March 2001

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State for Wales by section 9(1) and (8) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling the Secretary of State in that behalf, and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales[^f00002], with the consent of the Treasury[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Cardiff and Vale National Health Service Trust) (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 31st March 2001.

- (2) This Order shall apply to Wales only.

#### Originating Capital of National Health Service Trusts

###### 2

The originating capital of the Cardiff and Vale National Health Service Trust shall be One Hundred and nintey four million, one hundred and forty two thousand pounds (£194,142,000 ).

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

D. Elis Thomas — The Presiding Officer of the Assembly — 13th March 2001

We consent

Greg Pope — Jim Dowd — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 19th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, provided for in section 9 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, of the Cardiff and Vale National Health Service Trust established under that Act.
