# The Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No.4) (Wales) Order 2007

Made: 5 November 2007

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 181 of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002[^f00001] on the National Assembly for Wales and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

#### Title and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No.4) (Wales) Order 2007.

- (2) This order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Provision coming into force on 30 November 2007

###### 2

Section 153 of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 comes into force on 30 November 2007 to the extent it is not already in force.

## Signed

Jocelyn Davies — Under authority of the Minister for the Environment Sustainability and Housing, one of the Welsh Ministers — 5 November 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 153 of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 (“the Act”) in relation to Wales.

Section 153 of the Act inserts section 21B into the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, which requires that a demand for service charges must be accompanied by a summary of the rights and obligations of the tenant.
