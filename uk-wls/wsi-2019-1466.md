## Footnotes

[^f00001]: 2019 anaw 2; *see* section 28 for the definition of “regulations”. Schedule 2 is introduced by section 9 of the 2019 Act.

[^f00002]: By virtue of regulation 3 of the Renting Homes (Fees etc.) (Wales) Act 2019 (Transitional Provision for Assured Shorthold Tenancies) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1151) (W. 201)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2019/1151), the references in the Renting Homes (Fees etc.) (Wales) Act 2019 to a contract-holder are to be read as references to a tenant under an assured shorthold tenancy under Part 1 of the Housing Act 1988.

[^f00003]: *See* paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 to the Act for the definition of “holding deposit”.

[^f00004]: Under paragraph 4(c) of Schedule 1 to the Act, a holding deposit must not exceed an amount equivalent to one week’s rent under the contract.
