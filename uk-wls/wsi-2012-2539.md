# The Local Government (Performance Indicators) (Wales) Order 2012

Made: 2 October 2012

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 8 October 2012

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(3)

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 8(1)(a), 8(2) and 50(2) of the Local Government (Wales) Measure 2009[^f00001], and having consulted those persons which they are required, by section 8(3) of that Measure, to consult, make the following Order.

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Local Government (Performance Indicators) (Wales) Order 2012.

- (2) This Order applies only to Welsh improvement authorities which are county and county borough councils (“local authorities”).

- (3) This Order comes into force on 1 November 2012 and applies in relation to the financial year beginning on 1 April 2013 and subsequent financial years.

- (4) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Performance indicators

###### 2

The performance indicators in each Schedule to this Order are specified for the purpose of section 8(1) of the Local Government Wales Measure 2009(1) in relation to the functions detailed in the respective Schedule.

#### Revocation and Saving

###### 3

The Local Government (Performance Indicators and Standards) (Wales) Order 2010[^f00002] is revoked but continues to have effect in so far as it applies to Performance Indicators and Standards for the financial year beginning on 1 April 2012.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies, for Wales, performance indicators by reference to which the performance of county councils and county borough councils, in exercising functions, will be measured from 1 April 2013 and in relation to subsequent financial years. The Order revokes the Local Government (Performance Indicators and Standards) (Wales) Order 2010 (S.I. [2010/482 (W.51)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2010/482)).

Article 2 identifies, by reference to the Schedules, which performance indicators will be used to measure the performance of which functions.

Schedules 1 to 6 detail the prescribed performance indicators for the different functions as follows:

- Schedule 1 Social Services Indicators

- Schedule 2 Housing Indicators

- Schedule 3 Education Indicators

- Schedule 4 Waste Management Indicators

- Schedule 5 Transport Indicators

- Schedule 6 Culture and Sport Indicators
