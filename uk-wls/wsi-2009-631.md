# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2009

Made: 11 March 2009

Coming into force: 6 April 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 170(3) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title and Application

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2009.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

6 April 2009 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 —

- (a) section 147 (abolition of maintenance liability of relatives);

- (b) Schedule 13 (transitional provisions relating to section 147); and

- (c) Part 5 of Schedule 15 (repeals and revocations) and section 166 (repeals) insofar as it relates to that Part.

## Signed

Gwenda Thomas — Under authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 11 March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6 April 2009 section 147 of the Health and Social Care Act [2008 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/14) (and associated provisions). Section 147 removes the liability of a spouse or parent to contribute to the cost of accommodation provided by a local authority for the person concerned under Part 3 of the National Assistance Act 1948.
