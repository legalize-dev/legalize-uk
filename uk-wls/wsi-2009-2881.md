# The Feed (Specified Undesirable Substances) (Wales) Regulations 2009

Made: 27 October 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 29 October 2009

Coming into force: 23 November 2009

There has been consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of section 84(1) of the Agriculture Act 1970 or as appropriate of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00002] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Title and Commencement

###### 1

The title of these Regulations is the Feed (Specified Undesirable Substances) (Wales) Regulations 2009 and they come into force on 23 November 2009.

#### Amendment to the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2006

###### 2

After Chapter D of Schedule 5 (prescribed limits for undesirable substances) to the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00003], as Chapter E add the entries set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations make further amendments to the Feeding Stuffs (Wales) Regulations 2006 (S.I. [2006/116 (W.14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/116) as already amended by S.I. [2006/617 (W.69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/617), S.I. [2006/2928 (W.263)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/2928), S.I. [2006/3256 (W.296)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/3256), S.I. [2007/3171 (W.277)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2007/3171), S.I. [2008/1806 (W.174)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2008/1806) and S.I. [2009/106 (W.20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2009/106)) (“the Feeding Stuffs Regulations”).

2 These Regulations provide for the implementation of Commission Directive [2009/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0008) amending Annex I to Directive [2002/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards maximum levels of unavoidable carry-over of coccidiostats or histomonostats in non-target feed (OJ No. L40, 11.2.2009, p.19).

3 The Regulations amend Schedule 5 to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations by the addition of a Chapter E setting specified limits in relation to the carry-over into non-target feeding stuffs of certain zootechnical feed additives, (*regulation 2 and the Schedule*).
