# The School Organisation (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) Regulations 2011

Made: 28 January 2011

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 1 February 2011

Coming into force: 1 March 2011

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 28 (3), 28(8), 29(3), 29(7) , 31(3), 31(8), 34, 35, 138(7) and (8), and 144 of, and paragraph 7 of Schedule 6, paragraphs 5 and 12 of Schedule 7 and paragraph 2 of Schedule 8 to, the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], and in the exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by sections 113A(9) and 152(5) and (6) of, and paragraphs 20(2), 22, 28(2), 30, 39(2), 40 and 41 of Schedule 7 and paragraph 1 of Schedule 7A to, the Learning and Skills Act 2000[^f00002] and now vested in them, make the following Regulations.

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) The title of these Regulations is the School Organisation (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 1 March 2011.

- (3) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

#### Amendment of the Education (Schools Organisation Proposals) (Wales) Regulations 1999

###### 2

The Education (School Organisation Proposals) (Wales) Regulations 1999[^f00003] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 5—

- (i) before sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (2) insert—

> > (za) on a school day and not fewer than fifteen school days before the end of a school term;

- (ii) before sub-paragraph (d) of paragraph (3) insert—

> > (zd) on a school day and not fewer than fifteen school days before the end of a school term;

- (iii) before sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (4) insert—

> > (za) on a school day and not fewer than fifteen school days before the end of a school term;

- (iv) before sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (5) insert—

> > (za) on a school day and not fewer than fifteen school days before the end of a school term;

- (b) in regulation 7—

- (i) in paragraph (2) for the words after “(as the case may be)”, substitute “within the period of one month after the date of publication of the proposals.”; and

- (ii) in paragraph (3) for the words after “Schedule 6 to the Act within”, substitute “the period of two weeks after the end of the objection period.”;

- (c) in Schedule 5 for the modification to paragraph 7 of Schedule 6 to the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 substitute—

> > - That paragraph shall have effect as if— in sub-paragraph (1) for “section 28, 29 or 31” there were substituted “paragraph 10(4)”; in sub-paragraph (2)(a) for “such period as may be prescribed” there were substituted “one month after the date on which the proposals were published”; in sub-paragraph (2)(b) for “such period as may be prescribed” there were substituted “two weeks after the end of the objection period”; in sub-paragraph (3) for “such period as may be prescribed” there were substituted “one month after the date on which the proposals were published”

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the—

- (a) Education (School Organisation Proposals) (Wales) Regulations 1999,

- (b) Education (Maintained Special Schools) (Wales) Regulations 1999,

- (c) Change of Category of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2001, and

- (d) School Organisation Proposals by the National Assembly for Wales Regulations 2004,

so that any notice required to be published is published on a school day and not fewer than fifteen school days before the end of a school term.

Once the notice has been published, the time for objections to the proposal is amended so that objections must be received within one month.

The Education (School Organisation Proposals) (Wales) Regulations 1999 and the Education (Maintained Special Schools) (Wales) Regulations 1999 are further amended so that if the publication of the proposals is by a local authority, the local authority must send to the Welsh Ministers copies of the objections with their observations thereon within the period of two weeks after the end of the objection period.
