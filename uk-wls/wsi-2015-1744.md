# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015

Made: 25 September 2015

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of powers conferred by section 199(2) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Title and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) The title of this Order is the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.

#### Provisions coming into force on 21 October 2015

###### 2

The appointed day for the following provisions of the Act coming into force is 21 October 2015—

- (a) section 132 (The National Independent Safeguarding Board); and

- (b) section 133 (Regulations about the National Board).

## Signed

Mark Drakeford — Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers — 25 September 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the second Commencement Order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (“the Act”).

Article 2 of this Order provides that 21 October 2015 is the appointed day for the coming into force of sections 132 and 133 of the Act.

Section 132 establishes the National Independent Safeguarding Board (“The National Board”) and sets out duties and functions of that Board.

Section 133 gives the power to make regulations which allow for the making of further provision about the National Board.
