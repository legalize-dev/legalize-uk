# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) (Partial Revocation) Order 1994

Made: 11th January 1994

Laid before Parliament: 17th January 1994

Coming into force: 19th January 1994

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) and (3) of the said Act[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) (Partial Revocation) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 19th January 1994.

#### Partial revocation

###### 2

- (1) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) Order 1991 is hereby revoked–

- (a) with respect to those parts of the designated area numbered 1 of Schedule 1 to that Order which are set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order; and

- (b) with respect to the designated area numbered 14 of Schedule 1 to that Order.

- (2) In consequence of that revocation that Order shall be amended as follows–

- (a) for paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 there shall be substituted the paragraphs set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order;

- (b) in article 6(1)(a)(i) and 6(1)(b)(i), immediately after the word “1,”, there shall be inserted the word “1A,”; and

- (c) the word “14”, in article 6(1)(a)(v) and 6(1)(b)(v) shall be omitted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further partially revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) Order 1991 (“the principal Order”) which contains emergency prohibitions restricting various activities in order to prevent human consumption of food which has been or which may have been rendered unsuitable for that purpose in consequence of the escape of radioactive substances from a nuclear reactor situated at Chernobyl in the Ukraine.

This partial revocation reduces a designated area in the County of Powys subject to the restrictions set out in the principal Order and also provides that a designated area in the County of Gwynedd is no longer subject to the said restrictions.

Maps showing the areas which are designated by the principal Order and the areas derestricted by this Order are available for inspection during normal office hours at the offices of the Welsh Office Agriculture Department at Penrallt, Caernarfon, Gwynedd LL55 1EP; Government Buildings, Spa Road East, Llandrindod Wells, Powys LD1 5HA and Cathays Park, Cardiff, South Glamorgan CF1 3NQ.
