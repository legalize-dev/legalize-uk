## SCHEDULE

| *Column* 1 | *Column* 2 |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or departments* | *Matters in relation to which the Ministers or departments are designated* |
| The Secretary of State | Authorisation of the carrying on of insurance business and regulation of such business and its conduct. |
| The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State | Materials and articles in contact with food or drink or intended for such contact. |
| Any Northern Ireland department or the head of any such department | Both the preceding items in this column. |
