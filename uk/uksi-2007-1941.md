# The Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2007

Made: 9th July 2007

Laid before Parliament: 10th July 2007

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 10th July 2007

Coming into force: 1st August 2007

The Secretary of State and the Welsh ministers are designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 , as read with paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to that Act , in relation to the common agricultural policy.

The Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in that section and it appears to the Secretary of State and to the Welsh Ministers that it is expedient for the reference to Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414), concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market , to be construed as a reference to that instrument as amended from time to time.

Acting jointly, the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers (the Welsh Ministers acting in relation to Wales only), in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section, make the following Regulations.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 1st August 2007.

#### Amendments

###### 2

In the Plant Protection Products Regulations 2005 —

- (a) In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” substitute—

> > “*the Directive*” means Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market ;

; and

- (b) omit Schedule 1 (instruments amending Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414)).

## Signed

Jane Davidson — Minister for Sustainability and Rural Development, one of the Welsh Ministers — 26th June 2007

Phil Woolas — Minister of State — 9th July 2007
