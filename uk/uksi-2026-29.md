# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Lamda Limited) (Amendment) Order 2026

Made: 9th January 2026

Coming into force: 31st January 2026

The Office for Students has complied with the procedure on giving notifications in accordance with section 48 of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Lamda Limited) (Amendment) Order 2026 and comes into force on 31st January 2026.

#### Variation of the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Lamda Limited) Order 2021

###### 2

- (1) The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Lamda Limited) Order 2021[^f00002] is varied in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) For article 2, substitute—

> > (2) Lamda Limited (registered company number 00364456) is authorised to grant taught awards of the kind mentioned in section 42(2)(a) of the said Act for an indefinite period.

## Signed

Jean Arnold — Deputy Director of Quality — Office for Students — 9th January 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order varies the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Lamda Limited) Order 2021 (as amended by [S.I. 2024/1063](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1063)), to remove the time restriction, so that Lamda Limited’s (UKPRN: 10003758) authorisation remains for an indefinite period.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
