# The Suckler Cow Premium (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made: 9th January 1995

Laid before Parliament: 9th January 1995

Coming into force: 30th January 1995

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Suckler Cow Premium (Amendment) Regulations 1995, shall extend to Great Britain and shall come into force on 30th January 1995.

#### Amendments to the Suckler Cow Premium Regulations 1993

###### 2

- (1) The Suckler Cow Premium Regulations 1993[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraph of this regulation.

- (2) In paragraph (2) of regulation 4 (retention of records), delete sub-paragraph (b). Insert instead—

- (b) retain for such period any records which he has kept under—

- (i) article 3(1) of the Movement of Animals (Records) Order 1960[^f00004] and article 9(1) of the Bovine Animals (Identification, Marking and Breeding Records) Order 1990[^f00005], and

- (ii) article 5(1) of the Bovine Animals (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 1995[^f00006];

provided that the suckler cows for which an application for Community premium was made are clearly distinguished from the other suckler cows recorded in such records.

## Signed

Michael Jack — Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 9th January 1995

Hector Monro — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 6th January 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations make a change to the Suckler Cow Premium Regulations 1993 (S.I. [1993/1441](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1441) as already amended by S.I. [1994/1528](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1528), together referred to in this note as the “Existing Regulations”).

2 Like the Existing Regulations, these Regulations extend to Great Britain only.

3 The amendment is due to the making of the Bovine Animals (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 1995 (S.I. [1995/12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/12), referred to in this note as the “BARIM Order”) which implements in the UK Council Directive [92/102/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0102) (OJ No. L355, 5.12.92, p.32).

4 The BARIM Order replaces the Movement of Animals (Records) Order 1960 (S.I. [1960/105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1960/105), to which amendments relevant to the Existing Regulations were made by S.I. [1961/1493](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1961/1493) and S.I. [1989/879](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/879)) and the Bovine Animals (Identification, Marking and Breeding Records) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/1867](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1867), amended by S.I. [1993/503](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/503)) (together referred to in this note as the “Existing Orders”).

5 Regulation 4(2)(b) of the Existing Regulations requires, in certain circumstances, the retention of records made under the Existing Orders. It is amended to take account of their replacement by the BARIM Order.
