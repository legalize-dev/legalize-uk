# The Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas (Amendment) Order 2000

Made: 9th March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27A(2) of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas (Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

#### Magistrates' courts committee areas

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas Order 1999[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes a new list for the list of magistrates' courts committee areas in the Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas Order 1999. The list in that Order was the list of magistrates' courts committee areas in existence immediately before 27th September 1999. The new list substituted by this Order takes account of changes that were effected by Orders made before 27th September 1999, but that come into force on 1st April 2000.
