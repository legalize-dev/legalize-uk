# The Phoenix National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994

Made: 13th January 1994

Laid before Parliament: 20th January 1994

Coming into force: 10th February 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Bristol and District Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Phoenix National Health Service Trust[^f00002] and the Southmead Health Services National Health Service Trust[^f00003], having consulted those Trusts in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Phoenix National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 10th February 1994.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Phoenix Trust” means the Phoenix National Health Service Trust;

- “the Southmead Trust” means the Southmead Health Services National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property— prepared by and agreed upon by the Phoenix Trust and the Southmead Trust on 27th July 1993; and signed on behalf of both of those Trusts; which items represent part of the property transferred to the Southmead Trust by the Southmead Health Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1993[^f00004].

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 10th February 1994 the trust property shall be transferred from the Southmead Trust to the Phoenix Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Health — 13th January 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Phoenix National Health Service Trust on 10th February 1994 trust property formerly held by the Southmead Health Services National Health Service Trust and which had previously been transferred to the latter Trust by the Bristol and District Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
