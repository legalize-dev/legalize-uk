## SCHEDULE

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or departments* | *Matters in relation to which the Ministers or departments are designated* |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to sound pressure level requirements for the marketing, placing into service and use of equipment for cutting grass. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to safety as regards simple pressure vessels. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to relations between commercial agents and their principals. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to recognition of higher-education diplomas or formal qualifications required for the pursuit of professions or other occupations. |
| The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and any one or more Secretaries of State | Measures relating to quick-frozen food intended for human consumption. |
| The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and any one or more Secretaries of State | Measures relating to the description of and other requirements relating to spirit drinks. |
| Any Northern Ireland department | The preceding items in this column. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the approval of telecommunications terminal equipment. |

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council
