# The Antarctic Act 1994 (Commencement) Order 1996

Made: 3rd October 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 35 of the Antarctic Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Act 1994 (Commencement) Order 1996.

#### Commencement of Section 7 of the Antarctic Act 1994

###### 2

Section 7 of the Antarctic Act 1994 shall come into force on 1st November 1996.

## Signed

Malcolm Rifkind — Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs — 3rd October 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st November 1996 section 7 of the Antarctic Act 1994. The section makes provision for the protection of Antarctic fauna and flora.
