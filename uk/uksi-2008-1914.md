# The European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2008

Made: 15th July 2008

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(1)

Accordingly, he makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 6(2)(b) and (3)(b) of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 2002[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2008 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) In this Order a reference to an electoral region or constituency is a reference to that region or constituency as it is for the time being.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2004[^f00003] is revoked.

#### Returning Officers in England

###### 3

The designated returning officer at a European Parliamentary election for the electoral region in England (including the combined region[^f00004]) specified in the left-hand column of the Schedule to this Order shall be the person who is the acting returning officer[^f00005] at a parliamentary election for the constituency specified in the right-hand column of the Schedule.

#### Returning Officer for Wales

###### 4

The designated returning officer at a European Parliamentary election for the electoral region of Wales shall be the person who is the acting returning officer at a parliamentary election for the Preseli Pembrokeshire constituency.

#### Returning Officer for Scotland

###### 5

The designated returning officer at a European Parliamentary election for the electoral region of Scotland shall be the person who is the returning officer[^f00006] at a parliamentary election for the Edinburgh South constituency.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes and replaces the European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1056](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1056)), which appointed returning officers for the electoral regions in England, Scotland and Wales for the European Parliamentary elections in 2004. Article 3 of, and the Schedule to, this Order designate returning officers at European Parliamentary elections for electoral regions in England. Articles 4 and 5 designate the returning officer for such elections for the electoral regions of Wales and Scotland respectively.

[^f00005]: “Acting returning officer” is defined in section 28(1) of the Representation of the People Act [1983 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2). Section 28(1)(b) provides that in the case of any other constituency the acting returning officer is such electoral registration officer as may be designated in an order made by the Secretary of State.
