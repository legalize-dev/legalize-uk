# The Animal Health, Alien Species in Aquaculture and Invasive Non-native Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 4th April 2019

Laid before Parliament: 5th April 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft of the instrument being laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Health, Alien Species in Aquaculture and Invasive Non-native Species (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force immediately before exit day.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular section 8(2)(a) and (g)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

They make amendments to the Invasive Non-native Species (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/223](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/223)), the Aquatic Animal Health and Alien Species in Aquaculture (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/451](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/451)), the Aquatic Animal Health and Alien Species in Aquaculture (Amendment) (England and Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/452](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/452)) and the Import of and Trade in Animals and Animal Products (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/795](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/795)) to correct various errors.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
