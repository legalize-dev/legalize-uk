# The Registration of Consultant Lobbyists (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 2nd July 2015

Laid before Parliament: 9th July 2015

Coming into force: 28th July 2015

The Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 22(2) of the Transparency of Lobbying, Non-Party Campaigning and Trade Union Administration Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Consultant Lobbyists (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations extend to the United Kingdom.

- (3) These Regulations come into force on 28th July 2015.

#### Amendment of the Registration of Consultant Lobbyists Regulations 2015

###### 2

In Regulation 5(1)(b) of the Registration of Consultant Lobbyists Regulations 2015[^f00002] for “paragraph (1)” substitute “section 5(1)”.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster

John Penrose — Minister for Constitutional Reform — Cabinet Office — 2nd July 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend regulation 5(1)(b) of the Registration of Consultant Lobbyists Regulations 2015 to correct an incorrect cross-reference.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations as no impact on the public, voluntary or private sectors is foreseen. A regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely cost and benefits of complying with the Registration of Consultant Lobbyists Regulations 2015. A copy can be obtained from the Constitutional Settlement Division of the Cabinet Office, 1 Horseguards Road, London SW1A 2HQ.
