# The Willington C Gas Pipeline (Correction) Order 2015

Made: 18th August 2015

Coming into force: 19th August 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers in paragraph 1(4) and (8) of that Schedule, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Willington C Gas Pipeline (Correction) Order 2015 and comes into force on 19th August 2015.

#### Corrections

###### 2

The Willington C Gas Pipeline Order 2014 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made; and

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Willington C Gas Pipeline Order 2014, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request from RWE Generation UK plc under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
