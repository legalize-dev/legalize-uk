# The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1988

Made: 19th July 1988

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 3(3) of the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1988.

###### 2

The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 shall come into force on 19th September 1988.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 19th July 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1986 into force on 19th September 1988.
