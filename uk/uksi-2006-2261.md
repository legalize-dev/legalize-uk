# The A550 Trunk Road (Improvement between Deeside Park and Ledsham) (Detrunking) Order 1994 (Revocation) Order 2006

Made: 9th August 2006

Coming into force: 1st September 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10, 12 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A550 Trunk Road (Improvement between Deeside Park and Ledsham) (Detrunking) Order 1994 (Revocation) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st September 2006.

###### 2

The A550 Trunk Road (Improvement between Deeside Park and Ledsham) (Detrunking) Order 1994 is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Drysdale — A Divisional Director Highways Agency — 9th August 2006
