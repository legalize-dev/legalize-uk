# The Bus Lane Contraventions (Approved Local Authorities) (England) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 2007

Made: 23rd May 2007

Laid before Parliament: 25th May 2007

Coming into force: 25th June 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 144(3)(b) and (14) of the Transport Act 2000[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Bus Lane Contraventions (Approved Local Authorities) (England) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 25th June 2007.

#### Specification of approved local authorities

###### 2

North Tyneside Metropolitan Borough Council is specified as an approved local authority for the purposes of section 144 of the Transport Act 2000 (civil penalties for bus lane contraventions)[^f00002].

#### Amendment to the Bus Lane Contraventions (Approved Local Authorities) (England) Order 2005

###### 3

- (1) The Schedule to the Bus Lane Contraventions (Approved Local Authorities) (England) Order 2005[^f00003] shall be amended as set out in paragraph (2).

- (2) After the row in the table relating to Norfolk County Council, insert the following row—

| North Tyneside Metropolitan Borough Council | [S.I. 2007/1511](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1511) |
| --- | --- |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

G. Merron — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 23rd May 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Bus Lane Contraventions (Penalty Charges, Adjudication and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2757](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2757)), made under section 144 of the Transport Act 2000, provide for the enforcement of bus lane restrictions through the imposition, by approved local authorities, of penalty charges in respect of contraventions of such restrictions. This Order specifies the Metropolitan Borough of North Tyneside (“North Tyneside”) as an approved local authority for these purposes.

The Order also makes consequential amendments to the Bus Lane Contraventions (Approved Local Authorities) (England) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2755](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2755)) to add North Tyneside to the list of approved local authorities set out in the Schedule to that Order.

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been undertaken, since the Order is concerned with the enforcement of existing traffic restrictions and prohibitions and does not therefore constitute an additional burden on business. The costs incurred by local authorities undertaking enforcement are expected to be paid from the penalty charge income.
