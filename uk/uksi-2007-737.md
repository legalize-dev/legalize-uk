# The Norfolk (Coroners’ Districts) Order 2007

Made: 7th March 2007

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 2007

Coming into force: 6th April 2007

The Secretary of State has made such modifications of the draft as he thinks fit and makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(2) of the Coroners Act 1988.

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Norfolk (Coroners’ Districts) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 6th April 2007.

- (2) This Order shall not have effect in relation to any inquest begun before the day on which it comes into force or to any post-mortem examination which, before that day, a coroner has directed or requested a medical practitioner to make.

###### 2

The existing Norwich and Central Norfolk Coroner’s District and the King’s Lynn Coroner’s District shall be amalgamated to form a single Coroner’s District called the Greater Norfolk Coroner’s District.

###### 3

Accordingly, the County of Norfolk shall be divided into two coroners’ districts and each such coroner’s district shall consist of the area specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order opposite to the name of the coroner’s district in column 1 of that Schedule.

###### 4

The Norfolk (Coroners’ Districts) Order 2001[^f00003] is revoked.

## SCHEDULE

| *Column 1* *Name of Coroner’s District* | *Column 2* *Area comprised in Coroner’s District* |
| --- | --- |
| Greater Norfolk | The Districts of Breckland, Broadland, North Norfolk, South Norfolk and the City of Norwich. The Borough of King’s Lynn and West Norfolk. |
| Great Yarmouth | The Borough of Great Yarmouth. |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Harriet Harman, — Minister of State, — Department for Constitutional Affairs — 7th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The County of Norfolk was divided into three coroners’ districts by the Norfolk (Coroners’ Districts) Order 2001.

This Order replaces the two Coroners’ Districts of Norwich and Central Norfolk and King’s Lynn by the District of Norwich and Central Norfolk (as substituted by the Schedule to this Order).
