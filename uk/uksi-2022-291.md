# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

Made: 14th March 2022

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 112(1)(b), 113A(1)(b), 113B(1)(b), and 125 of the Police Act 1997[^f00001] as extended to Jersey by the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Jersey) Order 2010[^f00002], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 and come into force at the end of the period of one week beginning with the day on which they are registered in the Royal Court of Jersey.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Jersey.

#### Amendments to the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010

###### 2

- (1) The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3 (fees for criminal conviction certificates, criminal record certificates and enhanced criminal record certificates)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “£23”, substitute “£18”;

- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “£23”, substitute “£18”;

- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for “£40”, substitute “£38”.

## Signed

Rachel Maclean — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Home Office — 14th March 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the prescribed fees in the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/1087](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1087)). The fees for criminal conviction certificates (also known as basic certificates) are reduced from £23 to £18.

The fees for criminal record certificates (also known as standard certificates) are reduced from £23 to £18, and the fees for enhanced criminal record certificates are reduced from £40 to £38. This will mirror the fee reductions applied to applications in England and Wales.

The amended fees apply to applications for criminal conviction certificates, criminal record certificates and enhanced criminal records certificates in Jersey.
