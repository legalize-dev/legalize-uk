# The Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type Approval) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019

Made: 26th March 2019

Laid before Parliament: 27th March 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of that Act in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type-approval scheme[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type Approval) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019 and come into force on the day after these Regulations are laid.

#### Amendment of the Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type Approval) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

###### 2

In the Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type Approval) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00003], in regulation 1(b)(i), for “22 days after the day on which these Regulations are made”, substitute “on 29th March 2019”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Jesse Norman — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 26th March 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Road Vehicles and Non-Road Mobile Machinery (Type Approval) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/648](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/648)) (“the principal Regulations”) to vary the date for commencement of Part 3 of that instrument (which provides for amendments under the European Communities Act [1972 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68) to be made to definitions in the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 ([S.I. 1981/154 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1981/154)). This is necessary due to the late making of the principal Regulations such that Part 3 would not properly commence before exit day.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been prepared and is available alongside this instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
