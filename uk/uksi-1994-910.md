# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1994

Made: 23rd March 1994

Laid before Parliament: 25th March 1994

Coming into force: 15th April 1994

The review body appointed under section 1(1) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001] having made a report following the reference to them of certain matters, the Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act and after consulting such associations of local education authorities, such bodies representing the interests of governors of voluntary and grant-maintained schools and such bodies representing school teachers as appeared to him to be concerned, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 15th April 1994.

#### Application

###### 2

This Order applies to all school teachers (as defined in the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991) in England and Wales.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 1993 by uprating the amounts of scales and allowances.

The retrospective effect of these changes is authorised by section 2(4)(f) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991.
