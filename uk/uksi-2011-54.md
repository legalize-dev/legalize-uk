# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) Order 2011

Made: 11th January 2011

Laid before Parliament: 17th January 2011

Coming into force: 7th February 2011

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) Order 2011.

#### Commencement of variation

###### 2

The variation of the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 made by the Secretary of State on 11th January 2011, whereby paragraph 1(a) of Article 46 was varied by substituting “6.25p” for “6.29p”, shall come into force on 7th February 2011.

## Signed

Ed Vaizey — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 11th January 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 7th February 2011 a variation to the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 made by the Secretary of State on 11th January 2011.

The Public Lending Right Scheme provides for payments to be made to authors based on the number of times their books are lent out by public libraries. The effect of the variation is to decrease the rate per loan as set out in article 46(1)(a) of the Scheme from 6.29p to 6.25p. The rate per loan is the sum attributable to each qualifying loan for the purpose of calculating the amount payable in respect of loans of a particular book by public libraries.
