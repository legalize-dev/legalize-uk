# The Millennium Commission (Reduction in Membership) Order 2003

Made: 20th November 2003

Laid before Parliament: 26th November 2003

Coming into force: 1st January 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to the National Lottery etc. Act 1993[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Millennium Commission (Reduction in Membership) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st January 2004.

#### Reduction in membership

###### 2

Paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 6 to the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 is amended so as to substitute “eight” for “nine”.

## Signed

Estelle Morris — Minister of State, — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 20th November 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reduces the total number of members to be appointed to the Millennium Commission (established under section 40 of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993) from nine to eight.
