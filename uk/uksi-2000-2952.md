# The Banking Consolidation Directive (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2000

Made: 1st November 2000

Laid before Parliament: 1st November 2000

Coming into force: 22nd November 2000

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Banking Consolidation Directive (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2000, and shall come into force on 22nd November 2000.

- (2) In section 220(1)[^f00004], in the definition of “authorised credit institution”, for “Council Directive [77/780/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0780)” there is substituted “Directive [2000/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0012) of the European Parliament and of the Council”[^f00005].

> > - “credit institution” means a credit institution as defined in article 1 of Directive [2000/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0012) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 March 2000 relating to the taking up and pursuit of the business of credit institutions, that is to say an undertaking whose business is to receive deposits or other repayable funds from the public and to grant credits for its own account;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make consequential amendments to give effect to Directive [2000/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0012) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 March 2000 relating to the taking up and pursuit of the business of credit institutions. That Directive codifies and repeals Council Directives [73/183/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0183), [77/780/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0780), [89/299/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0299), [89/646/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0646), [89/647/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0647), [92/30/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0030) and [92/121/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0121). These Regulations make the necessary changes to United Kingdom enactments which refer to the repealed Directives.
