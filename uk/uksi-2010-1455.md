# The Home Information Pack (Suspension) Order 2010

Made: 19th May 2010

Laid before Parliament: 20th May 2010

Coming into force: 21st May 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 162 of the Housing Act 2004[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Home Information Pack (Suspension) Order 2010 and comes into force on 21st May 2010.

#### Suspension of duties under sections 155 to 159 of the Housing Act 2004

###### 2

The operation of the duties imposed by sections 155 to 159 of the Housing Act 2004 is suspended.

## Signed

Eric Pickles — Secretary of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 19th May 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order suspends the operation of all of the duties imposed by sections 155 to 159 of the Housing Act 2004 relating to the content and provision of home information packs.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available and is annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside the instrument on the OPSI website (www.opsi.gov.uk) and copies have also been placed in the Library of each House of Parliament.
