# The Plant Protection Products (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made: 17th April 2004

Laid before Parliament: 21st April 2004

Coming into force: 14th May 2004

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by that section, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and come into force on 14th May 2004.

#### Amendment of the Plant Protection Products (Fees) Regulations 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Protection Products (Fees) Regulations 2003[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) For paragraph (2) of regulation 1 (title, commencement, extent and interpretation) there shall be substituted:

> > (2) In these Regulations—
>   - “the 1991 Directive” means Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market[^f00004] amended as set out in the definition of “the Directive” in the 2003 Regulations;
>   - “the 2003 Regulations” means the Plant Protection Products Regulations 2003[^f00005] or the Plant Protection Products (Scotland) Regulations 2003[^f00006] as appropriate;
>   - “data” means scientific evidence submitted in support of an application under the 2003 Regulations;
>   - “relevant authority” means the authority in Great Britain to which an application is submitted;
>   - “the Schedule” means the Schedule to these Regulations,
>   - and any reference in the Schedule to an identified item or an identified note refers to the item or note so identified in the Schedule.

- (3) In regulation 2 (fees), for:

- (a) “the 1995 Regulations”, wherever the expression appears, there shall be substituted “the 2003 Regulations”;

- (b) “Note F” in paragraph (2)(c) there shall be substituted “Note C”.

- (4) After regulation 3 (transitional provisions) the following regulation shall be added:

> > (3A)
>   (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amendments made by the Plant Protection Products (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 shall not apply in respect of any application made before the amendments come into force.
>   (2) The amount of any fee relating to any activity carried out after these Regulations are amended by the Plant Protection Products (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004, in connection with any application made before the amendments come into force, is based on the fee specified in these Regulations as amended by those Regulations.

- (5) For the Schedule there shall be substituted the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations which are made under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 amend the Plant Protection Products (Fees) Regulations 2003 (S.I. [2003/660](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/660)), which set fees for the processing and examination of applications for approval for plant protection products and related works.

The Regulations alter the fees that are payable as detailed in the table of comparison with the Plant Protection Products (Fees) Regulations 2003 set out below.

The structure of Part A of the table in the Schedule to the Regulations has been amended to reflect changes to, and to clarify, the elements of the processing and examination required.

The Regulations also update the definitions for the “1991 Directive” and “data” and substitute a new definition, “the 2003 Regulations”, for the “1995 Regulations” to reflect the revocation of the Plant Protection Products Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/887](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/887) as amended) and their re-enactment with modifications for England and Wales in the Plant Protection Products Regulations 2003 (S.I. [2003/3241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3241)) and for Scotland in the Plant Protection Products (Scotland) Regulations 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/579](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/579)).

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared and placed in the library of each House of Parliament. Copies can be obtained from Pesticides Safety Directorate, Room 314, Mallard House, Kings Pool, 3 Peasholme Green, York YO1 2PX or from the website www.pesticides.gov.uk.
