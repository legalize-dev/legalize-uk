# The Customs Duties (Portugal) Order 1988

Made: 20th July 1988

Laid before the House of Commons: 20th July 1988

Coming into force: 10th August 1988

The Treasury, by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5(2) and (3) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, on the recommendation of the Secretary of State, hereby make the following Order:–

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Customs Duties (Portugal) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 10th August 1988.

- (2) In this Order–

- “relevant goods” means goods which, at the time of their importation into the United Kingdom, are entitled to benefit from the progressive reduction of customs duties in trade between Portugal and the other member States of the European Communities as constituted on 31st December 1985 by virtue of the provisions of Title III of Part Four of the Act annexed to the Treaty concerning the accession of the Kingdom of Spain and the Portuguese Republic to the European Economic Community and the European Atomic Energy Community, signed at Madrid and Lisbon on 12th June 1985, and to the Decision of the Council of the European Communities of 11th June 1985 on the accession of the Kingdom of Spain and the Portuguese Republic to the European Coal and Steel Community[^f00002];

- references to a heading or subheading are references to a heading or subheading of the Combined Nomenclature of the European Economic Community[^f00003];

- references in Schedule 1 or 3 to this Order to a rate of duty, unless otherwise stated, are references to a rate calculated as a percentage of the value of the goods;

- references to the weight of any goods, unless otherwise stated, are references to their net weight, and “gross” in relation to the weight of any goods means inclusive of the weight of any earth or any other growing medium in which the goods are imported and of any container in or on which they are made up;

- where symbols or abbreviations are used for units of measurement they are those authorised by the Weights and Measures (Quantity Marking and Abbreviations of Units) Regulations 1987[^f00004] except that the symbol “hl” is used for the unit “hectolitre”; and

- “ECU” means European Currency Unit as defined in Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 3180/78](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1978/3180)[^f00005] and any amount expressed as a number of ECUs shall be converted to sterling at such rate as may be required by the customs rules of the European Communities[^f00006].

- (3) This Order does not impose or increase any customs duty or restrict any relief from customs duty otherwise than in pursuance of a Community obligation.

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2) and articles 3, 4, 5 and 6(1) below, from 10th August 1988 up to and including 31st December 1995, where in relation to any relevant goods which fall within a heading or subheading specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order a rate of customs duty is shown in column 3 as from 10th August 1988 or as from 1st January in any subsequent year, then, on the importation into the United Kingdom of any of those goods during the relevant period, there shall be charged a duty of customs at the rate so shown for that period.

- (2) If the entry “O” appears in the said column 3 in relation to any relevant goods for any period, no customs duty shall be charged on the importation into the United Kingdom of any of those goods during that period or any subsequent year.

###### 3

From 10th August 1988 up to and including 31st December 1995 no customs duty shall be charged on any relevant goods which fall within subheading specified in column 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order and are of a description specified in column 2.

###### 4

Subject to articles 5 and 6(1) below, from 10th August 1988 up to and including 31st December 1995, where in relation to any relevant goods which fall within a subheading specified in column 1 of Schedule 3 to this Order and are of a description specified in column 2 a rate of customs duty is shown in column 3 as from 10th August 1988 or as from 1st January in any subsequent year, then, on the importation into the United Kingdom of any of those goods during the relevant period, there shall be charged a duty of customs at the rate so shown for that period instead of any higher rate shown in column 3 of Schedule 1 in relation to the goods in respect of that period.

###### 5

In the case of any goods to which article 2 or 4 applies, where in accordance with any directly applicable Community provision the rate of duty chargeable is lower than the rate chargeable under those Articles or is nil, customs duty shall be chargeable on the importation of the goods into the United Kingdom at the lower rate.

###### 6

- (1) Where a period of time or a description of goods is specified in column 2 of Schedule 1 or 3 to this Order in relation to any heading or subheading, the duty provided for in relation to that heading or subheading by article 2 or 4 above shall apply only during that period or to goods of that description as the case may be.

- (2) Any description of goods in column 2 of Schedule 1, 2 or 3 to this Order shall be taken to comprise all goods which would be classified under an entry in the same terms constituting a subheading.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order implements obligations of the United Kingdom concerning Portugal under the Treaty of Accession of Spain and Portugal to the European Communities from 10th August 1988 until 31st December 1995. It provides for the reductions in the customs duties charged on the importation of goods into the United Kingdom from Portugal which are to be made under the Act annexed to the Treaty from 10th August 1988 and from the beginning of each subsequent calendar year during the remainder of the transitional period (see European Communities No. 27 and 28 (1985) Cmnd 9634 and 9635). Article 6 of the Customs Duties (Spain and Portugal) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/2183](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2183)) would be effective to implement the reduction in customs duties determined by Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 839/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/0839) (OJ No. L87, 31.3.1988, p.1) from 1st July 1988 to the end of 1988 but as from 10th August 1988 this Order replaces as far as Portugal is concerned the Customs Duties (Spain and Portugal) Order, (which is revoked by the Customs Duties (Spain and Portugal) (Revocation) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/1259](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1259))) in order to make known the actual rates of duty, which are shown in the Schedules to this Order.

Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 839/88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1988/0839) totally suspends from 1st July 1988 certain customs duties applicable to imports from Portugal.

Certain duties are charged by reference to a number of ECUs. The present rate of conversion for 1988 is 1 ECU equals £0.693890 (see Commission Notice of 1.10.1987, (OJ No. C262, 1.10.1987, p.1)).

Goods in Schedule 2 are subject to a total exemption from customs duty and goods in Schedule 3 are subject to a partial exemption from customs duty instead of any higher rate which would otherwise apply under Schedule 1.
