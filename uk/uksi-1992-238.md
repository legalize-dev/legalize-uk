# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) Order1992

Made: 5th February 1992

Coming into force: 28th February 1992

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1)(a), (b) and (c) and (2), 23(f) and (g) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 28th February 1992.

#### Amendment

###### 2

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) for the provisions of Schedule 1 to that Order (which lists disinfectants approved by the Minister) there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to this Order;

- (b) for the provisions of Schedule 2 to that Order (which lists disinfectants subject to transitional provisions) there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to this Order; and

- (c) in article 6(2) of that Order (which specifies the date until which disinfectants subject to transitional provisions remain approved) for the words “31st December 1991” there shall be substituted the words “30th June 1992”.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1991[^f00003], and the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1991[^f00004] are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978 by substituting new Schedules for Schedules 1 and 2 to that Order (Article 2(a) and (b)). Schedule 1, which lists approved disinfectants, includes newly approved disinfectants, and Schedule 2 lists disinfectants which are now omitted from Schedule 1 but which may nevertheless continue to be used as approved disinfectants until 30 June 1992 (Article 2(c)).
