# The Finance Act 2009, Schedule 50 (Record-keeping) (Appointed Day) Order 2010

Made: 16th March 2010

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 98(2) of the Finance Act 2009[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2009, Schedule 50 (Record-keeping) (Appointed Day) Order 2010.

###### 2

1st April 2010 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the amendments made by Schedule 50 to the Finance Act 2009 (Record-keeping).

## Signed

Tony Cunningham — Frank Roy — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 1st April 2010 as the day on which the amendments made by Schedule 50 to the Finance Act 2009 come into force. That Schedule amends existing legislation about the obligations to keep records for the purposes of environmental taxes (aggregates levy, climate change levy and landfill tax), insurance premium tax and stamp duty land tax.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
