# The General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 1994

Made: 11th March 1994

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 11th day of March 1994 By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken the said rules into consideration, are hereby pleased to approve the same, and hereby revoke with effect from 1st April 1994 the General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 1993.

This Order may be cited as the General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 1994.

## GENERAL OPTICAL COUNCIL — *The Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) (No. 2) Rules 1993*

In pursuance of their powers under section 10 of the Opticians Act 1989, the General Optical Council hereby make the following Rules:—

###### 1

These rules may be cited as the Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) (No.2) Rules 1993.

###### 2

In the Registration and Enrolment Rules 1976 (which in their original form are scheduled to the General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment Rules) Order of Council 1977[^f00003]) the Appendix shall be amended by replacing the figures £45, £26 and £16, on each occasion where they appear, by the figures £50, £29 and £18 respectively.

###### 3

The Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules 1993[^f00004] (which will be spent on the coming into force of these Rules) are hereby revoked.

###### 4

These Rules shall come into force on 1st April 1994 and shall apply to applications for registration, enrolment, retention, restoration and transfer to take effect on or after 1st April 1994 whether made before, on or after that date.

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Rules approved by this Order increase with effect from 1st April 1994 the fees payable to the General Optical Council by ophthalmic and dispensing opticians and bodies corporate carrying on business as opticians for registration, enrolment or retention, restoration to and transfer within the register of opticians. The fees were last fixed in April 1993 and the order of increase is approximately 11 per cent.

This Order also revokes a previous Order of Council which becomes spent on the revocation of Rules which are scheduled to it.
