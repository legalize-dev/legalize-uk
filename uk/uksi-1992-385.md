# The Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges, etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 25th February 1992

Laid before Parliament: 4th March 1992

Coming into force: 1st April 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by sections 4(5) and (6), 5(1)(a) and 11(1) of the Refuse Disposal (Amenity) Act 1978[^f00001] and sections 101(4) and (5), 102(2)(a) and 142(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002] and, in respect of matters prescribed for the purposes of provisions of that Act of 1984, after consultation with representative organisations as required by section 134(2) of that Act, I hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges, etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

- (2) Regulation 2(1) of the Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges, etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1991[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

###### 2

Regulation 3(1)(a), (2)(a) and (3) of the Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges, etc.) Regulations 1989[^f00004] shall be amended by the substitution of “ £95” for “ £85” in each place where it occurs.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Home Office — 25th February 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges, etc.) Regulations 1989 (as amended by the Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges, etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 1991) so as to increase, with effect from 1st April 1992, the sum or charge payable under the 1989 Regulations in respect of the removal by the police or a local authority of a vehicle from £85 to £95.
