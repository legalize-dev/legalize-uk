# The Gate Burton Energy Park (Correction) Order 2024

Made: 27th November 2024

Coming into force: 28th November 2024

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gate Burton Energy Park (Correction) Order 2024and comes into force on 28th November 2024.

#### Corrections

###### 2

The Gate Burton Energy Park Order 2024 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to the Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.
