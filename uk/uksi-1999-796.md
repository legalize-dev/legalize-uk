# The Northumbria Ambulance Service and the Durham County Ambulance Service National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made: 15th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northumbria Ambulance Service and the Durham County Ambulance Service National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts

###### 2

- (1) The Northumbria Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust established by the Northumbria Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00005] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

- (2) The Durham County Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust established by the Durham County Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00006] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 15th March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 1999:

- the Northumbria Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust, and

- the Durham County Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust.

It should be read in conjunction with the North East Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00007], which establishes a new ambulance NHS trust.
