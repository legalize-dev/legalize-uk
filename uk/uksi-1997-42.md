# The Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) Order 1997

Made: 13th January 1997

Laid before Parliament: 20th January 1997

Coming into force: 10th February 1997

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 20(1A) of Schedule 1 to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 10th February 1997.

- (2) This Order shall not extend to Scotland.

###### 2

Paragraph 20(1) (grant or renewal of permit or licence) of Schedule 1 (bookmaker’s permits, betting agency permits and betting office licences) to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963[^f00002] shall be amended by the substitution, for paragraph (a) to (e) of the following paragraphs:

> > (a) in the case of the grant of a bookmaker’s permit, £160;
> (b) in the case of the grant of a betting agency permit, £160;
> (c) in the case of the grant of a betting office licence, £125;
> (d) in the case of the renewal of a bookmaker’s permit or betting agency permit, £20;
> (e) in the case of the renewal of a betting office licence, £25;

###### 3

The Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) (No. 2) Order 1991[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Timothy Kirkhope — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 13th January 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases certain fees payable in England and Wales to the appropriate authority under paragraph 20(1) of Schedule 1 to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963. The fees for the grant of a bookmaker’s permit (paragraph 20(1)(a)) and for the grant of a betting agency permit (paragraph 20(1)(b)) are increased from £133 to £160, and the fee for the grant of a betting office licence is increased from £106 to £125 (paragraph 20(1)(c)). The fee for renewal of a bookmaker’s permit or betting agency permit is increased from £16 to £20 (paragraph 20(1)(d)) and the fee for the renewal of a betting office licence is increased from £20 to £25 (paragraph 20(1)(e)).
