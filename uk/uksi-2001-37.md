# The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2001

Made: 12th February 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament: 14th February 2001

Coming into force: 15th March 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 15th March 2001.

#### General fund contribution limits

###### 2

The amount of contribution out of their general fund which a local authority may estimate that they will carry to the credit of their housing revenue account for the year 2001 2002 shall be limited to nil.

## Signed

J M BAILLIE — A member of the Scottish Executive — 12th February 2001
