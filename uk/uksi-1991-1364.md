# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1991

Made: 11th June 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 5) Order 1991.

###### 2

The provisions of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 specified in the Schedule hereto shall come into force on 1st July 1991.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 11th June 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st July 1991 a number of provisions in the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990. These provisions, together with a brief indication of their subject matter, are listed in the Schedule to the Order.
