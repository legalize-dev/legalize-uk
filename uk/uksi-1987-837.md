# The Sumburgh Airport Shops Order 1987

Made: 6th May 1987

Coming into force: 7th May 1987

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section1(2) of the Shops (Airports) Act 1962[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Sumburgh Airport Shops Order 1987 and shall come into force on 7th May 1987.

###### 2

Sumburgh Airport is hereby designated for the purposes of section 1 of the Shops (Airports) Act 1962[^f00003], as being an airport at which there appears to the Secretary of State to be a substantial amount of international passenger traffic.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

R E Clarke — An Under Secretary in the — Department of Transport — 6th May 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 1 of the Shops (Airports) Act 1962, as amended by section 70 of the Airports Act 1986 exempts traders at airports designated for the purposes of the Act as being airports at which there appears to the Secretary of State to be a substantial amount of international passenger traffic from the provisions of Parts I and IV of the Shops Act [1950 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1950/28), which relate to hours of closing and Sunday trading. By this Order the Secretary of State designates Sumburgh Airport as such an airport.
