# The Armed Forces Act 1981 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1982

Made: 27th March 1982

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 29(1) of the Armed Forces Act 1981, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 1981 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1982.

###### 2

The provisions of the Armed Forces Act 1981, so far as they are not already in force, shall come into force on 1 May 1982.

## Signed

Peter Blaker — Minister of State for the Armed Forces — Ministry of Defence — 27th March 1982

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1 May 1982 those provisions of the Armed Forces Act 1981 which are not already in operation.
