# The Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2002

Made: 28th March 2002

Laid before Parliament: 4th April 2002

Coming into force: 26th April 2002

The Forestry Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 2 and 3(1) to (4) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001] as read with section 20 of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972[^f00002], hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 26th April 2002.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1993

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1993[^f00003] (“the Order”) shall be amended in accordance with this article.

- (2) In article 2(1) of the Order—

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)”[^f00004] there shall be substituted the following text:

> > - ‘Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)’ means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) of 8th May 2000 on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community as amended by Commission Directive [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033)[^f00005] of 8th May 2001 amending certain annexes to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029), and by Commission Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028)[^f00006] of 19th March 2002 amending certain annexes to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029);

- (3) In Schedule 5 (Restrictions on consignments of relevant material to member States), Part B, items 8 and 22 shall be deleted.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Forestry Commissioners is hereunto affixed on 28th March 2002.

Frank Strang — Secretary to the Forestry Commissioners

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1993 so as to implement certain elements of Commission Directives [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) (OJ No. L 77, 20.3.02, p.26) and [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (OJ No. L 77, 20.3.02, p.23) in Great Britain which are not implemented in other legislation. Directive [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) amends the protected zones established under Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (OJ No. L 169, 10.7.00, p.1) (“the principal Plant Health Directive”) and scheduled in the Annex to Commission Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) (OJ No. L 127, 9.5.01, p.38). Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) provides for consequential amendments to certain Annexes of the principal Plant Health Directive.

The amendment to the protected zones implemented by this Order concerns the removal of the protected zone in Corsica in respect of the pest *Matsucoccus feytaudi* Duc. (article 2(3)).

An additional amendment is made to the definition of Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) so as to incorporate amendments made to it by Commission Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (article 2(2)).

No Regulatory Appraisal has been prepared in respect of this Order.
