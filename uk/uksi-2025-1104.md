# The Proscribed Organisations (Name Change) (Revocation) Order 2025

Made: 17th October 2025

Laid before Parliament: 20th October 2025

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(8) of the [Terrorism Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11)[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Proscribed Organisations (Name Change) (Revocation) Order 2025 and comes into force on the twenty-first day after the day on which it is laid before Parliament.

- (2) This Order extends to the United Kingdom.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The [Proscribed Organisations (Name Change) Order 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/615)[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Hanson — Minister of State — Home Office — 17th October 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

Part 2 of the Terrorism Act [2000 (c.11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11) makes provision about proscribed organisations (including setting out offences in relation to such organisations in sections 11 to 13). An organisation is proscribed if it is listed in Schedule 2 to that Act or operates under the same name as an organisation so listed (section 3(1)).

The Proscribed Organisations (Name Change) Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/615](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/615)) specified “Hay’at Tahrir Al-Sham” as another name for the organisation listed in Schedule 2 as Al-Qa’ida.

Article 2 of this Order revokes the Proscribed Organisations (Name Change) Order 2017 meaning that Hay’at Tahrir Al-Sham ceases to be treated as another name for Al-Qa’ida.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
