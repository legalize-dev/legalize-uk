# The British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986

Made: 30th October 1986

In pursuance of section 3(2) of the British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986.

###### 2

The British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 shall come into force in relation to the British Council on 10th November 1986.

## Signed

Geoffrey Howe — Her Majesty's Principal Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs — 30th October 1986

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the British Council and Commonwealth Institute Superannuation Act 1986 into force on 10th November 1986 in relation to the British Council only.
