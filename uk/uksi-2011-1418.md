# The Bribery Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2011

Made: 7th June 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(1) of the Bribery Act 2010[^f00001]. Before doing so, in accordance with section 19(4) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted the Scottish Ministers[^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Bribery Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2011.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The Bribery Act 2010 comes into force on 1st July 2011 to the extent that it is not already in force.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — Secretary of State for Justice — Ministry of Justice — 7th June 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st July 2011 those provisions of the Bribery Act [2010 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/23) which were not brought into force on Royal Assent by section 19(2) of that Act.

## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [2010 c. 23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/23).

[^f00002]: Section 19(4) of the Bribery Act 2010 provides that the Secretary of State must consult the Scottish Ministers before making an order under that section in connection with any provision of that Act which would be within the legislative competence of the Scottish Parliament if it were contained in an Act of that Parliament.
