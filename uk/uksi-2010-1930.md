# The Occupational Pension Schemes (Levies) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 28th July 2010

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1.

In accordance with section 317(1) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted with such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Levies) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendment of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Levies) Regulations 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Occupational Pension Schemes (Levies) Regulations 2005[^f00002] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 7 (schemes eligible for only part of the financial year), after paragraph (2) insert—

> > (3) Where a scheme to which this regulation applies becomes an eligible scheme during a financial year by virtue of—
>   (a) regulation 2(1A) of the Pension Protection Fund (Entry Rules) Regulations 2005[^f00003] (schemes which are not eligible schemes); or
>   (b) regulation 2A of the Pension Protection Fund (Partially Guaranteed Schemes) (Modification) Regulations 2005[^f00004] (schemes to which paragraph (1) of regulation 2 does not apply),
> paragraph (2) does not apply to the scheme (and so the full amount of the administration levy is payable).

- (3) In regulation 16 (partially guaranteed schemes)—

- (a) at the beginning of paragraph (1) insert “Subject to paragraph (1A),”; and

- (b) after paragraph (1) insert—

> > (1A) This regulation does not apply where regulation 2A of the Pension Protection Fund (Partially Guaranteed Schemes) (Modification) Regulations 2005 (schemes to which paragraph (1) of regulation 2 does not apply) applies.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Steve Webb — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 28th July 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations implement, in respect of the administration levy, the Decision of the European Commission [55/2007/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/2007/0055) of 11th February 2009 (O.J. L242, 15.9.09. p.21) that the exemption from the payment of a levy to the Pension Protection Fund corresponding to the pension liabilities covered by a Crown guarantee constitutes State aid that is incompatible with the common market within the meaning of Article 87(1) of the EC Treaty, now Article 107 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 7 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Levies) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/842](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/842)) (The Levies Regulations) as it applies to an occupational pension scheme with a Crown guarantee which becomes an eligible scheme during a financial year. Regulation 2 also amends regulation 16 of the Levies Regulations as it applies to a scheme with a partial Crown guarantee to which regulations 2A of the Pension Protection Fund (Partially Guaranteed Schemes) (Modification) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/277](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/277)) applies.

A full impact assessment has not been published for this instrument as it has only a negligible impact on the private, public and voluntary sectors.
