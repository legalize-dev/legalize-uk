# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2001

Made: 20th November 2001

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 138(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2001.

- (2) In this Order, “the 2001 Act” means the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001.

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2001 Act shall come into force on 1st December 2001:

- (a) sections 17 to 28 (closure of licensed and unlicensed premises);

- (b) sections 30 to 32 (sale of alcohol to persons under eighteen and drunkenness or disorder on licensed premises);

- (c) section 107(1)(a) and (b), (2) and (3) (payment of allowances to police authority members);

- (d) section 133;

- (e) section 137 (repeals) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 7 referred to in paragraphs (f) and (g) below;

- (f) Part 1 of Schedule 7; and

- (g) In Schedule 7, Part 4, the entries relating to Schedule 2 and 2A to the Police Act 1996[^f00002] in so far as not already in force.

###### 3

The following provisions of the 2001 Act shall come into force on 1st January 2002:

- (a) sections 122 to 125 (police ranks);

- (b) section 137 (repeals) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 7 referred to in paragraph (c) below; and

- (c) in Schedule 7, Part 4, the entries relating to sections 12 and 13 of the Police Act 1996.

## Signed

John Denham — Minister of State — 20th November 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 specified in article 2 on 1st December 2001 and the provisions specified in article 3 on 1st January 2002.
