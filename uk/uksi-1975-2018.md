# The Farriers (Registration) Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975

Made: 2nd December 1975

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 19(2) of the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1975.

###### 2

The Farriers (Registration) Act 1975, except section 16, shall come into force on 1st January 1976.

## Signed

Roy Jenkins — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 2nd December 1975

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st January 1976 the provisions of the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975 other than section 16 (offences by unregistered persons).
