# The Flood and Water Management Act 2010 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2024

Made: 12th March 2024

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 49(3)(a) of the Flood and Water Management Act 2010[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Flood and Water Management Act 2010 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2024.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

The following provisions of the Flood and Water Management Act 2010, so far as not already commenced, come into force on 15th March 2024—

- (a) section 34 (special administration) so far as it relates to paragraph 6 of Schedule 5, and

- (b) paragraph 6 of Schedule 5 (special administration).

## Signed

Robbie Moore — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 12th March 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order brings into force provisions of the Flood and Water Management Act [2010 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/29).

Article 2 specifies provisions of the Act that come into force, so far as not already commenced, on the 15th March 2024. The specified provisions relate to the special administration regime for water companies and include amendments to the Water Industry Act [1991 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56).
