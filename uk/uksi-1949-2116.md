# The Federated Superannuation System for Universities (Temporary Service) (Amendment) Regulations 1949

Made: 16th November 1949

Laid before Parliament: 17th November 1949

Coming into Operation: 18th November 1949

The Lords Commissioners of His Majesty's Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Them by subsection (3) of section five of the Superannuation Act, 1946, and of all other powers enabling Them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1

The Federated Superannuation System for Universities (Temporary Service) Regulations, 1949 [^f00001], shall be amended (i) by substituting for sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (3) of Regulation 4 the following sub-paragraph:—

> > (a) of any payment made to the officer by way of war bonus during the period beginning on the 1st day of February, 1940, and ending on the 30th day of June, 1944, or

###### 2

- (1) These Regulations shall be cited as the Federated Superannuation System for Universities (Temporary Service) (Amendment) Regulations, 1949.

- (2) The Interpretation Act, 1889, shall apply to the interpretation of these Regulations as it applies to the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 3

These Regulations shall come into force on the 18th day of November 1949.

## Signed

Joseph Henderson — Richard Adams — Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty's Treasury — Dated this 16th day of November, 1949

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The F.S.S.U. (Temporary Service) Regulations provide for superannuation contributions in respect of temporary Government service before appointment to a permanent post superannuable under F.S.S.U. These Regulations amend the main Regulations in two particulars:—

1. Regulation 4 (3) (a) of the main Regulations would have excluded from the salary upon which the contribution is to be based not only 1939 war bonus paid before 1st July, 1944, but also 1914–1918 war bonus paid during the period following 1919. This was not intended, and Regulation 1 (1) restricts the exclusion to 1939 war bonus.

2. Regulation 1 (ii) is a drafting amendment.
