# The Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas (Amendment) Order 2001

Made: 5th March 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27A(2) of the Justices of the Peace Act 1997[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas (Amendment) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

#### Magistrates' courts committee areas

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas Order 1999[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes a new list of magistrates' courts committee areas in the Magistrates' Courts Committee Areas Order 1999. The new list substituted by this Order takes account of changes to magistrates' courts committee areas effected by Orders that establish on 1st April 2001 the following magistrates' courts committees:

- Greater Manchester (the magistrates' courts committees for Manchester, North and West Greater Manchester, Oldham, Stockport, Tameside and Trafford are abolished);

- South Yorkshire (the magistrates' courts committees for Barnsley, Doncaster, Rotherham and Sheffield are abolished);

- Sussex (the magistrates' courts committees for East Sussex and West Sussex are abolished); and

- West Mercia (the magistrates' courts committees for Hereford and Worcester and Shropshire are abolished).
