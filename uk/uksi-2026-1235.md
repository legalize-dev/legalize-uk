# The Voter Identification (Amendment of List of Specified Documents) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2026

Made: 24th November 2026

Coming into force: 25th November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by rules 28(3) and 37(1F) of [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/schedule/1) to the [Representation of the People Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/contents) (“*the 1983 Act*”)[^f00001].

In accordance with [section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41/section/7)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41/section/7) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41/section/2)[(e)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41/section/2) of the [Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41/contents)[^f00002] the Electoral Commission has been consulted prior to the making of these Regulations.

In accordance with [section 201](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/section/201)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/section/201) of [the 1983 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/contents)[^f00003] a draft of these Regulations has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Voter Identification (Amendment of List of Specified Documents) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

- (3) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland.

#### Amendment of rule 37(1E) of the Parliamentary Elections Rules

###### 2

- (1) Rule 37(1E) of [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/schedule/1) to the [Representation of the People Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/contents) (Parliamentary Elections Rules: voting procedure and voter identification requirements: Northern Ireland)[^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) After sub-paragraph (b) insert—

> > (ba) a passport or passport card issued by a country listed in [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/61/schedule/3) to the [British Nationality Act 1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/61/contents) (countries whose citizens are Commonwealth citizens)[^f00005];

- (3) After sub-paragraph (n)[^f00006] insert—

> > (o) an identity card bearing the Proof of Age Standards Scheme hologram (a PASS card);
> (p) a badge of a form prescribed under [section 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/44/section/21) of the [Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/44/contents)[^f00007] or [section 14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/53/section/14) of the [Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons (Northern Ireland) Act 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/53/contents) (Blue Badge Scheme)[^f00008];
> (q) a Ministry of Defence Form 100 (HM Armed Forces Veteran Card).

#### Amendment of Forms used in parliamentary elections

###### 3

[Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1741/schedule/3) (Forms) to the [Representation of the People (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1741/contents)[^f00009] is amended as follows—

- (a) for Form A (elector’s official poll card) substitute the form in [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1235/schedule/1/made);

- (b) for Form B (proxy’s official poll card) substitute the form in [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1235/schedule/2/made).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations make a change to the list of the types of photographic identification that can be used by a person wishing to vote in person at a polling station at UK Parliamentary elections in Northern Ireland. The changes to the UK Parliamentary election rules also apply to elections to the Northern Ireland Assembly by virtue of [article 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2599/article/3) of, and [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2599/schedule/1) to, the [Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) Order 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2599/contents) ([S.I. 2001/2599](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2599)).

Regulation 2 adds the PASS card, the Blue Badge, the Veteran card and Commonwealth passports to the list of acceptable identification in rule 37(1E) of Schedule 1 to the Representation of the People Act [1983 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2).

Regulation 3 and the Schedules replace the Poll Cards for the UK Parliamentary Elections (the same forms are used for Northern Ireland Assembly elections) to reflect the changes to acceptable voter identification.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
