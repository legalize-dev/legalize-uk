# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2002

Made: 4th March 2002

Laid before Parliament: 4th March 2002

Coming into force: 6th March 2002

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by that section, and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2002 and shall extend to England.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into force on 6th March 2002.

#### Amendment of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00003] shall be amended in relation to England in accordance with the following provisions of this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 12—

- (a) in paragraph (2) after the words “Council Directive [64/432/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1964/0432)” there shall be added the words—

> > and is satisfied that the operator of the assembly centre has agreed to comply with the requirements for the operation of the centre which an inspector has specified in an operational agreement as being the requirements the inspector believes are necessary to ensure the centre is capable of being operated in accordance with the second indent of Article 3(2) of Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425)

; and

- (b) in paragraph (3), after the words “cattle or pigs” there shall be inserted the words “or sheep or goats”.

- (3) In Schedule 3, Part I, paragraph 9, after the words “Commission Decision [97/315/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0315) (OJ No. L137, 28.5.97, p. 20);” there shall be inserted the following—

> > Commission Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327) (OJ No. L115, 25.4.2001, p. 12), as amended by Decisions [2001/349/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0349) (OJ No. L123, 4.5.2001, p. 26), [2001/378/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0378) (OJ No. L132, 15.5.2001, p. 31), [2001/394/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0394) (OJ No. L138, 22.5.2001, p. 36), [2001/416/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0416) (OJ No, L149, 2.6.2001, p. 40), [2001/488/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0488) (OJ No. L176, 29.6.2001, p. 75), [2001/709/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0709) (OJ No. L261, 29.9.2001, p. 69), [2001/904/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0904) (OJ No. L335, 19.12.2001, p. 21) and [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) (OJ No. L50, 21.2.2002, p. 98);

- (4) In Schedule 3, Part I after paragraph 15 there shall be added the following paragraph—

> > (16) Commission Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327) (OJ No. L115, 25.4.2001, p. 12), as amended by Decisions [2001/349/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0349) (OJ No. L123, 4.5.2001, p. 26), [2001/378/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0378) (OJ No. L132, 15.5.2001, p. 31), [2001/394/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0394) (OJ No. L138, 22.5.2001, p. 36), [2001/416/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0416) (OJ No. L149, 2.6.2001, p. 40), [2001/488/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0488) (OJ No. L176, 29.6.2001, p. 75), [2001/709/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0709) (OJ No. L 261, 29.9.2001, p. 69), [2001/904/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0904) (OJ No. L 335, 19.12.2001, p. 21) and [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) (OJ No. L50, 21.2.2002, p. 98).
>   Relevant provisions: article 2a

## Signed

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 6th March 2002, amend the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000 (S.I. [2000/1673](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/1673), as amended) to make provision in relation to England to give effect to—

- article 4 of Commission Decision [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) (OJ No. L50, 21.2.2002, p. 98), concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom, repealing Decision [2001/740/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0740) (OJ No. L277, 20.10.2001, p. 30) and amending for the eighth time Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327); and Commission Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327) concerning restrictions to the movement of animals of susceptible species with regard to foot-and-mouth disease (OJ No. L115, 25.4.2001, p. 12), as amended.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations.
