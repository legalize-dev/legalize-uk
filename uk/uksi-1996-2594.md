# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1996

Made: 15th October 1996

Laid before Parliament: 25th October 1996

Coming into force: 18th November 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of October 1996 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Education (Schools) Act 1992[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 18th November 1996.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 19th November 1996.

## SCHEDULE

- Dorian Bradley

- Janet Brennan

- Christine Mary Brown

- Peter Daw

- Anthony Peter Matthews

- Michael Raleigh

- Maurice John Smith

- Barbara Wintersgill

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council
