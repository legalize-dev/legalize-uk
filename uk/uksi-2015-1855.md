# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Appeals under Part 2) (Amendment) Order 2015

Made: 29th October 2015

Laid before Parliament: 5th November 2015

Coming into force: 30th November 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 89(3), 90(2) and 459(2) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Appeals under Part 2) (Amendment) Order 2015.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 30th November 2015.

#### Amendment of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Appeals under Part 2) Order 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Appeals under Part 2) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 11 (leave to appeal to Supreme Court), before “33” insert “13B(4),[^f00003]”.

- (3) In article 12 (application for leave to appeal), in paragraph (1), before “33” insert “13B(4),”.

- (4) In article 14 (presence at hearing), before “33” insert “13B(4),”.

## Signed

Mike Penning — Minister of State — 29th October 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Appeals under Part 2) Order 2003 ([S.I. 2003/82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/82)) to insert in Part 3 of the Order references to an appeal to the Supreme Court under section 13B(4) of the Proceeds of Crime Act [2002 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29). Section 13B was inserted into the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 by section 7 of the Serious Crime Act [2015 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9) which came into force on 1st June 2015.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact is foreseen on the private, voluntary or public sectors.
