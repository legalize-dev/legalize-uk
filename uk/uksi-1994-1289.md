# The County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1994

Made: 11th May 1994

Coming into force: 6th June 1994

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Forms) (Amendment) Rules 1994.

- (2) In these Rules, a form referred to by number means the form so numbered in the Schedule to the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982[^f00001] and “the Schedule” means that Schedule.

###### 2

There shall be inserted in the Schedule, after forms N.1(SPC) and N.11R, the forms contained in the schedule to these Rules.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

The undersigned members of the Rule Committee, appointed by the Lord Chancellor under section 75 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00002], having made these Rules, certify them and submit them to the Lord Chancellor.

Frank J. White — J. H. Wroath — Neil Butter — Helen Paling — Margaret Wilby — W. A. Vincent — Peter Birts — Henrietta Manners — E. C. Gee — R. J. Winstanley

I allow these Rules, which shall come into force on 6th June 1994.

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 11th May 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules prescribe the form of summons and acknowledgment of service to be used in proceedings in the Central London County Court Business List, which is established by the County Court (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1994 (S.I. [1994/1288](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1288)).
