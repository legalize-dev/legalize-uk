# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2018

Made: 10th October 2018

Coming into force: 11th October 2018

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of October 2018Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2018 and comes into force on 11th October 2018.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
