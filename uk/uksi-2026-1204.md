# The Value Added Tax (Distance Selling) (Amendments) Regulations 2026

Made: 16th November 2026

Laid before the House of Commons: 18th November 2026

Coming into force: 1st January 2027

The Treasury make regulations 1 to 4 in exercise of the powers conferred by section 40A(7) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001].

The Commissioners for His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs make regulations 1 and 5 in exercise of the powers conferred by section 40A(8) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and effect

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Distance Selling) (Amendments) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 1st January 2027.

###### 2

- (1) [Regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1204/regulation/4/made) has effect in relation to reporting periods beginning on or after 1st January 2027.

- (2) In [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1204/regulation/2/1/made), “reporting period” has the meaning given by [paragraph 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/schedule/9ZE/paragraph/11) of [Schedule 9ZE](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/schedule/9ZE) to the [Value Added Tax Act 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/contents)[^f00003].

#### Amendments to the Value Added Tax Act 1994

###### 3

- (1) [Schedule 9ZD](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/schedule/9ZD) to the [Value Added Tax Act 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/contents) (distance selling of goods from Northern Ireland: special accounting scheme) is amended as follows.

- (2) In [paragraph 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/schedule/9ZD/paragraph/2) (“scheme supply”)—

- (a) after “the VAT Directive”, insert “, or that is deemed to be an “intra-Community distance sale of goods” under Article 369aa of the VAT Directive[^f00004],”;

- (b) for “that Article” substitute “those Articles”.

- (3) Before [Part 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23/schedule/9ZD/part/6) (appeals), insert—

> > (36A)
>   (1) [Part 21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2518/part/21) (repayments to traders outside the United Kingdom) of the [Value Added Tax Regulations 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2518/contents) ([S.I. 1995/2518](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2518)) has effect in relation to—
>     (a) a person registered under the OSS scheme, and
>     (b) a participant in a non-UK scheme,
>   as it applies to a trader (within the meaning of [those Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2518/contents)) subject to the following modifications.
>   (2) Regulation 186 (repayments of VAT)[^f00005] has effect as if after “imported by him into the United Kingdom”, there were inserted “by virtue of their entry into Northern Ireland”.
>   (3) [That Part](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2518/part/21) has effect as if regulation 188 (persons to whom Part 21 applies) were omitted.

###### 4

In Schedule 9ZE to the Value Added Tax Act 1994, in paragraph 41(3) (special accounting scheme for distance selling of goods imported to Northern Ireland: refund of UK VAT), for the words from “regulations” to the end, substitute “regulation 188 (persons to whom Part 21 applies) were omitted”.

###### 5

In Schedule 9ZF to the Value Added Tax Act 1994, in the modification made by paragraph 17(3) (modifications etc in connection with Schedules 9ZD and 9ZE: modifications to Part 20A of the Value Added Tax Regulations 1995)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), after “Act”, insert “or a qualifying supply of goods for the purposes of Schedule 9ZE of the Act”;

- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “that Schedule” substitute “either of those Schedules”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend Schedules 9ZD, 9ZE and Part 3 of Schedule 9ZF to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 and come into force on 1st January 2027. They implement certain amendments made by Council Directive (EU) 2025/516 to Council [Directive 2006/112/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0112) (“*the VAT Directive*”), pursuant to the United Kingdom’s obligations under the Windsor Framework.

Schedule 9ZD makes provision for a special accounting scheme for intra-Community distance sales of goods from Northern Ireland, the one stop shop scheme (“OSS scheme”). Regulation 3(2) expands the scope of supplies eligible for the scheme to include supplies that, from 1st January 2027 to 30th June 2028, are treated as intra‑Community distance sales of goods for the purposes of the OSS scheme under the VAT Directive.

Regulation 3(3) inserts a new Part 5A into Schedule 9ZD, which applies Part 21 of the Value Added Tax Regulations 1995 (“*the VAT Regulations*”) (repayments of VAT to traders outside the United Kingdom) with modifications, to persons registered under the OSS scheme and participants in a non-UK scheme. As a result, those persons may claim repayment of VAT under Part 21 of the VAT Regulations.

Schedule 9ZE provides a special accounting scheme for distance sales of goods imported into Northern Ireland, the import one stop shop scheme (“IOSS scheme”). Regulation 4 removes a modification to the application of Part 21 of the VAT Regulations in relation to refunds of VAT to IOSS scheme participants for reporting periods beginning on or after 1st January 2027. As a result, the Commissioners may now require the appointment of a VAT representative as a condition of repayment of VAT to IOSS scheme participants.

Paragraph 17 of Schedule 9ZF applies Part 20A of the VAT Regulations (repayments to EU traders incurring VAT on goods in Northern Ireland) to participants in the OSS and IOSS schemes. Regulation 5 modifies the application of those provisions in respect of the IOSS scheme.

A Tax Information and Impact Note covering the OSS and IOSS schemes was published on 18 May 2021 alongside the Finance Act 2021 and is available on the website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins. It remains an accurate summary of the impacts that apply to this instrument.
