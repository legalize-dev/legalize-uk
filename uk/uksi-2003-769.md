# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2003

Made: 20th March 2003

Laid before Parliament: 20th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

Now, therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act[^f00003] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

#### Application

###### 2

This Order applies to school teachers (as defined in section 5(1) and (2) of the Act[^f00004]) in England and Wales.

#### School Teachers' Pay and Conditions

###### 3

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2002[^f00005] shall be amended with effect from 1st April 2003 as follows:

- (a) in paragraph 1.2 the following definitions shall be inserted in the appropriate places in alphabetical order—

> > - “the Inner London Area” means the area comprising the areas of the London boroughs of Barking and Dagenham, Brent, Camden, City of London, Ealing, Greenwich, Hackney, Hammersmith and Fulham, Haringey, Islington, Kensington and Chelsea, Lambeth, Lewisham, Merton, Newham, Southwark, Tower Hamlets, Wandsworth and Westminster;

> > - “the Outer London Area” means Greater London, excluding the Inner London Area;

> > - “the Fringe Area” means:
> - in Berkshire: the Districts of Bracknell Forest, Slough and Windsor and Maidenhead;
> - in Buckinghamshire: the Districts of South Buckinghamshire and Chiltern;
> - in Essex: the Districts of Basildon, Brentwood, Epping Forest, Harlow and Thurrock;
> - in Hertfordshire: the Districts of Broxbourne, Dacorum, East Hertfordshire, Hertsmere, St Albans, Three Rivers, Watford and Welwyn Hatfield;
> - in Kent: the Districts of Dartford and Sevenoaks;
> - in Surrey: the whole County; and
> - in West Sussex: the District of Crawley;

> > - “the London Area” comprises the Inner London Area, the Outer London Area and the Fringe Area;

- (b) in paragraph 1.2 for the definition of “Induction Regulations” there shall be substituted the following definition:“ “Induction Regulations” means the Education (Induction Arrangements for School Teachers) (Consolidation)(England) Regulations 2001[^f00006] or the Education (Induction Arrangements for School Teachers)(Wales) Regulations 2003[^f00007];”

> > (1.7) Where a table containing a pay scale, pay spine or salary range contains two columns of annual salaries, the column headed “England and Wales” applies to teachers serving in England and Wales except those serving in the Inner London Area and the column headed “Inner London” applies to teachers serving in the Inner London Area.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2002 (the Document) by uprating the amounts of salary and certain allowances and by removing the Inner London allowance and replacing it with a new set of Inner London pay scales. The following are increased and a new scale is added for the Inner London area—

- (a) the leadership group pay spine set out in paragraph 4.5 of the Document;

- (b) the head teacher groups' pay ranges set out in paragraph 6.3 of the Document;

- (c) the pay scale for classroom teachers set out in paragraph 15.3.2 of the Document;

- (d) the pay scale for post-threshold teachers set out in paragraph 17.2 of the Document;

- (e) the pay spine for advanced skills teachers set out in paragraph 28.1 of the Document; and

- (f) the pay scale for unqualified teachers set out in paragraph 36.1 of the Document.

The following allowances are increased—

- (a) the management allowances set out in paragraph 23.1 of the Document;

- (b) the recruitment and retention allowances set out in paragraph 24.1 of the Document;

- (c) the special educational needs allowance set out in paragraph 25.1 of the Document; and

- (d) the Outer London and Fringe Area allowances set out in paragraph 41.1 of the Document.

The increases take effect from 1st April 2003.

The Order makes various safeguarding provisions in respect of the new Inner London pay scales and includes in the definition of “Induction Regulations” induction regulations which apply to Wales.
