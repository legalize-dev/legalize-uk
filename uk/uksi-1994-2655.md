# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Racal Network Services Limited) Order 1994

Made: 10th October 1994

Laid before Parliament: 17th October 1994

Coming into force: 15th November 1994

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Racal Network Services Limited) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 15th November 1994.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Ian Taylor — Parliamentary Under–Secretary of State for Trade and Technology — Department of Trade and Industry — 10th October 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Racal Network Services Limited a licence on 2nd September 1994 under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in that licence in the United Kingdom. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 17th October 1994. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Racal Network Services Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 15th November 1994.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, 50 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7JJ.
