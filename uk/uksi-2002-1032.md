# The District of Craven (Ribble Banks Parish Council) (Electoral Changes) Order 2002

Made: 26th March 2002

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17[^f00003] and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the District of Craven (Ribble Banks Parish Council) (Electoral Changes) Order 2002.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

#### Changes to elections arrangements for the Parish of Ribble Banks

###### 2

- (1) The term of office of any parish councillor elected on the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2003 for the Ribble Banks Parish Council shall be three years.

- (2) Elections for all parish councillors for the Ribble Banks Parish Council shall be held simultaneously on the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2006 and in every fourth year thereafter.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

Alan Whitehead — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions — 26th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes changes to the arrangements for the elections for parish councillors for the Ribble Banks Parish Council that are consequential upon electoral changes made in relation to the District of Craven by the District of Craven (Electoral Changes) Order 2000. These changes are designed to bring the dates of elections of parish councillors into line with the dates of election for councillors for the district ward of Settle and Ribble Banks, into which now fall the parishes represented by councillors elected to the Ribble Banks Parish Council. Those parishes are the parishes of Halton Rest, Rathmell and Wigglesworth.
