# The Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority (Combination Scheme) Order 2026

Made: 16th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 19th November 2026

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3)(b) and (11), 3(2), (3)(a) to (g), and (6) to (9), and 60(2) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001] (“*the 2004 Act*”).

It appears to the Secretary of State, in accordance with section 2(2) and (2A) of the 2004 Act, that in the interests of economy, efficiency and effectiveness there should be a single fire and rescue authority for the combined area of the East Surrey Council and the West Surrey Council.

In accordance with section 2(5) of the 2004 Act, before making this Order the Secretary of State consulted—

(a) Surrey County Council, East Surrey Council and West Surrey Council[^f00002],

(b) any local authority all or part of whose area forms part of the combined area[^f00003], and

(c) any other persons she considered appropriate.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority (Combination Scheme) Order 2026 and shall come into force in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) This article, article 2 and the Schedule come into force on 15th December 2026.

- (3) Article 3 (modification) comes into force on 1st April 2027.

- (4) This Order extends to England and Wales.

#### Combination Scheme

###### 2

The scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order has effect.

#### Modification

###### 3

- (1) The Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 2013[^f00004] are modified as follows.

- (2) Regulation 64 (special circumstances where revised actuarial valuations and certificates must be obtained) of the Local Government Pension Scheme Regulations 2013 is to be read as if after paragraph (8) there were inserted—

> > (8A) Paragraph (8B) applies where the exiting employer is the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority and the liabilities of the fund in respect of benefits due to the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority’s current and former employees (or those of any predecessor authority) have been or are to be transferred to the Authority by virtue of the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority (Combination Scheme) Order 2026.
> (8B) Where this paragraph applies, no exit payment is due under paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) does not apply.

## Schedule — The Scheme

## Part 1 — Commencement and interpretation

#### Commencement

###### 1

This Scheme comes into force—

- (a) for the purposes of constituting the Surrey Combined Fire and Rescue Authority as the fire and rescue authority for the combined area constituted by the Scheme solely for the performance by that authority of any functions necessary for bringing the Scheme into full operation on 1st April 2027, on 15th December 2026;

- (b) for all other purposes, on 1st April 2027.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Scheme—

- “*the 2011 Act*” means the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011[^f00005];

- “*the Authority*” means the fire and rescue authority constituted for the combined area by paragraph 4;

- “*the combined area*” means the fire and rescue authority area comprising the areas referred to in paragraph 3;

- “*constituent authorities*” means East Surrey Council and West Surrey Council;

- “*relevant police and crime commissioner*” means a police and crime commissioner— whose area is the same as, or contains all of, the area of the Authority, or all or part of whose area falls within the area of the Authority;

- “*Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority*” means Surrey County Council as a fire and rescue authority[^f00006].

## Part 2 — General

#### The combined area

###### 3

The areas of the East Surrey Council and the West Surrey Council are combined to become the combined area.

#### The combined fire and rescue authority

###### 4

- (1) There is constituted as the fire and rescue authority for the combined area a body corporate to be known as the Surrey Combined Fire and Rescue Authority.

- (2) The Authority is constituted in accordance with the provisions of Part 3 of this Scheme.

#### Financial provision

###### 5

The Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority must meet the costs of the Authority incurred for the purposes of bringing the Scheme into full operation on 1st April 2027.

## Part 3 — Composition and proceedings of the Authority

#### Duty to appoint members and number of members

###### 6

- (1) Each constituent authority must appoint members of the Authority in accordance with sub-paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) On 1st April 2027 there must be 10 members appointed to the Authority—

- (a) 5 from East Surrey Council; and

- (b) 5 from West Surrey Council.

- (3) Thereafter, each constituent authority must appoint as members of the Authority such number of members of the constituent authority as may be determined by the Authority.

- (4) Subject to paragraph (5), in determining the number of members to be appointed by each constituent authority, the Authority must, so far as is practicable, ensure that the number of members is proportionate to the number of local government electors in the areas of each of the constituent authorities.

- (5) The number of members must not exceed 25.

- (6) Each member of the Authority is appointed in accordance with this Part.

#### Appointment of a relevant police and crime commissioner as a member of the Authority

###### 7

- (1) The Authority may appoint a relevant police and crime commissioner to be a member of the Authority.

- (2) But the Authority may only make such an appointment in response to a request from the relevant police and crime commissioner.

- (3) If the Authority receives a request from a relevant police and crime commissioner, it must—

- (a) consider the request;

- (b) agree to or refuse the request;

- (c) give reasons for its decision to agree to or refuse the request; and

- (d) publish those reasons in such manner as it thinks appropriate.

#### Arrangement for another person to attend meetings on behalf of the relevant police and crime commissioner

###### 8

- (1) Sub-paragraph (2) applies where a relevant police and crime commissioner—

- (a) is appointed as a member of the Authority; and

- (b) arranges, under section 18 of the 2011 Act, for another person to attend a meeting of the Authority on the relevant police and crime commissioner’s behalf.

- (2) Where this sub-paragraph applies, the person attending the meeting on behalf of the relevant police and crime commissioner may speak at that meeting but—

- (a) may not vote; and

- (b) is not to be treated as a member of the Authority for any purpose.

#### Office of Authority members

###### 9

A member of the Authority appointed under paragraph 6 comes into office on the date of their appointment and, subject to paragraphs 11 to 13, holds office for such period or periods as determined by the constituent authority which appoints them.

#### Term of office of relevant police and crime commissioner as Authority member

###### 10

- (1) A relevant police and crime commissioner appointed as a member of the Authority under paragraph 7—

- (a) comes into office on the date of that appointment; and

- (b) continues to be a member of the Authority until the relevant police and crime commissioner’s term of office comes to an end in accordance with section 50(7)(b) of the 2011 Act.

- (2) But if a vacancy arises in the office of the relevant police and crime commissioner[^f00007] before the end of that term, the relevant police and crime commissioner ceases to be a member of the Authority on the date on which the vacancy in the office is regarded under section 59(1) of the 2011 Act as occurring.

#### Resignation from the Authority membership

###### 11

A member of the Authority may resign their membership of the Authority by giving notice in writing to the officer of the Authority whose function it is to receive such notice.

#### Cessation of membership of the Authority

###### 12

- (1) A member of the Authority who ceases to be a member of the constituent authority which appointed that person ceases to be a member of the Authority.

- (2) A person is disqualified from being a member of the Authority if they hold any paid office or employment to which sub-paragraph (3) applies.

- (3) This sub-paragraph applies to any paid office or employment (other than the office of chair or vice-chair of the Authority), appointments to which are or may be made or confirmed by—

- (a) the Authority;

- (b) any committee or sub-committee of the Authority; or

- (c) a joint committee or board on which the Authority are represented.

- (4) For the avoidance of doubt any agreed allowance received for the purpose of being a member of the Authority will not cause a member to be disqualified.

#### Appointment of a replacement member

###### 13

If a member of the Authority appointed by a constituent authority resigns, becomes disqualified or otherwise ceases to be a member of the Authority within the last six months of the member’s intended term of office, the constituent authority which appointed them is not required to appoint a replacement member for the remainder of the intended term unless—

- (a) on the occurrence of the vacancy (or in the case of a number of simultaneous vacancies, the occurrence of the vacancies) the total number of unfilled vacancies in the membership of the Authority exceeds one third of the number of members of the Authority referred to in paragraph 6; or

- (b) without such an appointment a meeting of the Authority would not be quorate as a result of the quorum requirements imposed by paragraph 16.

#### Election of chair or vice-chair

###### 14

- (1) The Authority must elect a chair, and may elect a vice-chair, from among its members.

- (2) Subject to paragraphs 11 to 13, the chair and (if elected) the vice-chair hold office for such period or periods, not exceeding one year, as the Authority determines.

- (3) Sub-paragraph (2) does not prevent a person who holds or has held office as chair or vice-chair from being elected or re-elected to either of those offices.

- (4) On a casual vacancy occurring in the office of chair or, if a vice-chair has been elected, the vice-chair, the Authority must elect from among its members a person to replace the chair, and may elect a person to replace the vice-chair.

- (5) The election to replace the chair under sub-paragraph (4) must take place not later than the next ordinary meeting of the Authority.

#### First meeting of the Authority

###### 15

The first meeting of the Authority must be—

- (a) held as soon as is practicable; and

- (b) convened by the clerk to the Authority.

#### Quorum requirement of meetings

###### 16

At a meeting of the Authority the quorum is one half of the total number of members of the Authority, or such greater number of members as the Authority may determine, including at least one member of each of the constituent authorities.

#### Application of general provisions of the Local Government Act 1972

###### 17

Sections 85(1), (2), (2A), (3) and (3A), 99 (in so far as it relates to Part 6 of Schedule 12), 101 to 103, 106, 120, 121 and 123 of, and Part 6 of Schedule 12 to, the Local Government Act 1972[^f00008] apply to the Authority and its members as if references in those provisions to a principal council or a local authority were references to the Authority.

## Part 4 — Officers and employees of the Authority

#### Continuity of employment and transfer of employment, rights, powers, duties and liabilities

###### 18

- (1) The contract of employment of any person employed by the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority immediately before 1st April 2027 shall have effect, on or after 1st April 2027, as if originally made between the person so employed and the Authority.

- (2) All of the rights, powers, duties and liabilities of the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority under or in connection with any such contract are transferred to the Authority.

- (3) Any act or omission before 1st April 2027, in relation to the transfer of that contract, is deemed to have been an act or omission in relation to the Authority.

#### Secondments

###### 19

In the case of a person who, immediately before 1st April 2027 is seconded to the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority, the secondment is to have effect, on or after 1st April 2027, as a secondment to the Authority.

#### Relevant powers of local authorities under Local Government Act 1972

###### 20

Sections 114, 115, 116, 117(1) and (2) and 119(1) and (2) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00009] apply to the officers and employees of the Authority as if references in those provisions to a local authority, other than references to a parish council, were references to the Authority.

## Part 5 — Property etc held solely for fire and rescue authority purposes

#### Property etc held solely for fire and rescue authority purposes

###### 21

There shall, on 1st April 2027, be transferred to the Authority from the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority all property, rights and liabilities held or incurred by it solely in connection with the provision of fire and rescue services.

#### Continuity

###### 22

- (1) The abolition of the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority, the transfer of its functions and its property, rights and liabilities do not affect the validity of anything done before the abolition or transfer.

- (2) Paragraphs (3) to (5) apply where any functions, property, rights or liabilities are transferred by or under this Part from Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority to the Authority.

- (3) There may be continued by or in relation to the Authority anything, including legal proceedings, which—

- (a) relates to any of the functions, property or rights transferred; and

- (b) is in the process of being done by or in relation to the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority immediately before 1st April 2027.

- (4) There has effect as if made or done by or in relation to the Authority—

- (a) anything which was made or done by or in relation to the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority for the purposes of, or otherwise in connection with, any of the functions, property, rights or liabilities transferred; and

- (b) which is in effect immediately before 1st April 2027.

- (5) The Authority is to be substituted for the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority in any instruments, contracts or legal proceedings which—

- (a) relate to any of the functions, property, rights or liabilities transferred; and

- (b) are made or commenced before 1st April 2027.

- (6) In this Scheme, a reference to the transfer of a function includes a reference to the abolition of a function and the conferral of a corresponding function on another person.

#### Transfers: supplementary provision

###### 23

- (1) All property, rights and liabilities transferred by this Part are to be transferred, notwithstanding that they may be or include—

- (a) property, rights and liabilities that would not otherwise be capable of being transferred; or

- (b) rights and liabilities under enactments.

- (2) No right of reverter, right of pre-emption, right of forfeiture, right of re-entry, right to compensation, option or similar right affecting any land or other property is to operate or become exercisable as a result of any transfer of land or other property by virtue of this Part, whether or not any consent required to the transfer has been obtained.

- (3) No right to terminate or vary a contract or instrument is to operate or become exercisable, and no provision of a contract or relevant document is to operate or become exercisable or be contravened, by reason of the transfer made by this Part.

- (4) In paragraph (3), “*relevant document*” means—

- (a) any enactment, other than an enactment contained in sections 2 and 3 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004;

- (b) any subordinate legislation made otherwise than under those provisions; or

- (c) any deed or other instrument.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order makes a scheme which combines the areas of the East Surrey and West Surrey Councils (“*the Councils*”) into a combined area and creates a fire and rescue authority for that area. Under the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, the Councils become the fire and rescue authorities for their respective areas when Surrey County Council is abolished under the Surrey (Structural Changes) Order 2026 ([S.I. 2026/564](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/564)) on 1st April 2027. For the purposes of this Order, they are treated as though they are already the fire and rescue authority for their respective areas. The Scheme comes into force on 15th December 2026 for transitional purposes, prior to it coming fully into operation on 1st April 2027. The fire and rescue authority for the combined area is to be known as the Surrey Combined Fire and Rescue Authority (“*the Authority*”). The Scheme provides for the appointment and terms of office of its members, and for meetings of the Authority. The Scheme also provides for the transfer of staff, property, rights and liabilities from the Surrey Fire and Rescue Authority to the Authority. A full regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
