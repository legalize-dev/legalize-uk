## Footnotes

[^f00002]: [2002 c. 29](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29). Section 377A was inserted by paragraph 115 of Schedule 8 to the Serious Crime Act [2007 (c. 27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/27) and amended by paragraph 24 of Schedule 1 to the Criminal Finances Act [2017 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/22) and [S.I. 2014/834](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/834).

[^f00003]: The revised code of practice brought into operation by this Order replaces the code of practice brought into operation in relation to England and Wales and Northern Ireland on 31st January 2018 by [S.I. 2018/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/93).

[^f00004]: Section 7(2)(e) of the Criminal Finances Act 2017 repealed section 357(9) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 referred to in sections 377A(1)(b) and 377A(2)(b) of that Act.

[^f00005]: Section 459(6)(aa) was inserted by paragraph 119(4) of Schedule 8 to the Serious Crime Act 2007.
