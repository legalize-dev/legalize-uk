# The A23 Trunk Road (Croydon) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1997

Made: 19th November 1997

Coming into force: 1st December 1997

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A23 Trunk Road (Croydon) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1997, and shall come into force on 1st December 1997.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A23 Trunk Road (Croydon) Red Route Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In the title preceding article 10 the word “Suspension” is substituted with the word “Revocation”.

###### 4

For Article 10(1) substitute—

> > (10)
>   (1) The Croydon (Waiting and Loading Restriction) Order 1977, Order No. 23; the Croydon (Waiting and Loading Restriction) Order 1991, Order No. 20; the Croydon (Waiting and Loading Restriction) Order 1991, Order No. 27; the Croydon (Waiting and Loading Restriction) Order 1991, Order No. 34; and the Croydon (Waiting and Loading Restriction) Order 1993, Order No. 23; as amended, are hereby revoked, in so far as their provisions relate to any part of the trunk road red route.

###### 5

Article 10(2) is deleted.

###### 6

In the table in Schedule 2B for item 8 substitute—

> > Brighton Road, Purley – north west side, between a point 2 metres south west of the party wall between Nos. 102 and 104 Brighton Road and a point level with the party wall of Nos. 116 and 118 Brighton Road.

###### 7

In the table in Schedule 2D item 1 is deleted.

###### 8

In the table in Schedule 3B for item 2 substitute—

> > between a point level with the party wall of Nos. 116 and 118 Brighton Road and a point 15 metres south west of that point.

###### 9

In the table in Schedule 4 item 43 is deleted.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 19th November 1997
