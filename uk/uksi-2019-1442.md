# The Eggs and Poultrymeat (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

Made: at 12.10 p.m. on 31st October 2019

Laid before Parliament: at 5.00 p.m. on 31st October 2019

Coming into force: 23rd November 2019

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003] during the preparation, evaluation and revision of food law in relation to these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Eggs and Poultrymeat (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 23rd November 2019.

#### Amendment of the Eggs and Chicks (England) Regulations 2009

###### 2

- (1) The Eggs and Chicks (England) Regulations 2009[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3(6)(a), after “any provision of” insert “the Single CMO Regulation[^f00005] or”.

- (3) In Schedule 1, in Part 1, in the table, in row 1 (Article 74)—

- (a) for the entry in column 1, for “Article 74 or Regulation (EU) 2013” substitute “Article 116 of the Single CMO Regulation”;

- (b) in column 2, omit “Article 116 of, and”.

- (4) In Schedule 2, in Part 1—

- (a) for the heading substitute—

- (b) in the table—

- (i) in the heading to column 1, at the end insert “, or where stipulated, the Single CMO Regulation”;

- (ii) in the heading to column 2, omit “of Regulation (EU) 2013”;

- (iii) in row 1 (Article 74), in column 1 for “Article 74” substitute “Article 116 of the Single CMO Regulation”.

- (5) In Schedule 3, in the table, in row 3 (Point 2, second sub-paragraph, paragraph (a), of Part D of Annex 2), in column 2, for “Article 74 and” substitute “Article 116 of the Single CMO Regulation,”.

#### Amendment of the Poultrymeat (England) Regulations 2011

###### 3

In Schedule 1 to the Poultrymeat (England) Regulations 2011[^f00006], in Part 1, in the table, in row 1 (Article 74)—

- (a) in column 1, for “Article 74 of Regulation (EU) 2013” substitute “Article 116 of the Single CMO Regulation”;

- (b) for the entry in column 2 substitute—

> > Part B(I)(2) and (3) and (III)(1) of Annex 14 to the Single CMO Regulation and the Commission Regulation

## Signed

George Eustice — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — At 12.10 p.m. on 31st October 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Eggs and Chicks (England) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2163](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2163)) and the Poultrymeat (England) Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/452](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/452)) to adjust references in the enforcement provisions within those Regulations to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ No L 347, 20.12.2013, p. 671) and Council Regulation [(EC) No 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234) (OJ No L 299, 16.11.2007, p. 1).

An Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
