# The Education Act 2011 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2012

Made: 28th August 2012

In accordance with section 82(5) of that Act the Secretary of State has consulted the Welsh Ministers.

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education Act 2011 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2012.

- (2) In this Order “EA 2011” means the Education Act 2011.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st September 2012

###### 2

The following provisions of EA 2011 come into force on 1st September 2012—

in Schedule 11, paragraph 2 so far as it relates to a new school that is a pupil referral unit, and section 37 and paragraph 1 to the extent that they relate to that paragraph.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st October 2012

###### 3

The following provisions of EA 2011 come into force on 1st October 2012—

section 13 and Schedule 4.

#### Provision coming into force on 1st August 2013

###### 4

The following provision of EA 2011 comes into force on 1st August 2013—

section 73 to the extent that it is not already in force.

#### Provision coming into force on 1st September 2013

###### 5

The following provision of EA 2011 comes into force on 1st September 2013—

section 1 to the extent that it is not already in force.

## Signed

Nick Gibb — Minister of State — Department for Education — 28th August 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the provisions of the Education Act 2011 that are identified in articles 2, 3, 4 and 5 into force on 1st September and 1st October 2012 and 1st August and 1st September 2013 respectively.

Article 2 commences paragraph 2 of Schedule 11, which imposes a requirement on local authorities to seek proposals for the establishment of new Academies, so far as that paragraph relates to a new school that is a pupil referral unit.

Article 3 brings into force section 13 and Schedule 4 which make provision for the imposition in England and Wales of restrictions on the reporting of alleged offences by teachers.

Article 4 commences fully section 73 which is concerned with the functions of the Chief Executive of Skills Funding.

Article 5 commences fully section 1 which makes provision regarding free of charge early years provision.
