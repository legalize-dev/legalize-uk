# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2010

Made: 24th May 2010

Laid before Parliament: 26th May 2010

Coming into force: 18th June 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001]. In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act he has consulted with such persons as appear to him to be requisite.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 18th June 2010.

#### Designation

###### 2

The sports ground specified in the Schedule to this Order (being a sports ground which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 10,000 spectators) is designated as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the Sandy Park Stadium, occupied by Exeter Rugby Club Limited, as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.
