# The Insolvency (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 4th March 2009

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 2009

Coming into force: 6th April 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 411(7) and 412(4) of, and paragraphs 27 of Schedule 8 and 30 of Schedule 9 to, the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00001] and Rule 12.1(g) of the Insolvency Rules 1986[^f00002], makes the following Regulations—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Insolvency (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 6th April 2009.

#### Amendment of the Insolvency Regulations 1994

###### 2

For Table 2 and Table 3 in Schedule 2 to the Insolvency Regulations 1994[^f00003], substitute—

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Regulation 35 of the Insolvency Regulations 1994 provides that the official receiver is entitled to remuneration under that regulation while acting as interim receiver, provisional liquidator, liquidator or trustee. The hourly rates of remuneration payable by virtue of regulation 35 are set out in Tables 2 and 3 in Schedule 2 to the 1994 Regulations.

The hourly rates have been increased and are set out in the new Tables 2 and 3 which are substituted by these Regulations for the existing Tables in the 1994 Regulations.
