# The British Coal Corporation (Change of Quorum) Regulations 1997

Made: 25th June 1997

Laid before Parliament: 26th June 1997

Coming into force: 1st July 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(7) of the Coal Industry Nationalisation Act 1946[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The British Coal Corporation (Change of Quorum) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 1st July 1997.

#### Amendment to the Coal Industry Nationalisation (National Coal Board) Regulations 1946

###### 2

In regulation 4 (Quorum) of the Coal Industry Nationalisation (National Coal Board)[^f00003] Regulations 1946[^f00004]:—

- for the word “three” there shall be substituted “two”.

## Signed

John Battle, — Minister of State for Science, Energy and Industry, — Department of Trade and Industry — 25th June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 4 (Quorum) of the Coal Industry Nationalisation (National Coal Board) Regulations 1946 (as amended by The British Coal Corporation (Change of Quorum) Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/1506](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1506))) by substituting a quorum of two for the quorum of three required at a meeting of the British Coal Corporation.
