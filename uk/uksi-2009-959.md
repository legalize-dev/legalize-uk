# The Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2009

Made: 6th April 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 245(5) of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2009.

- (2) This Order extends to England only.

#### Provisions coming into force on 30th April 2009

###### 2

The following provisions of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007 shall come into force on 30th April 2009 in relation to England—

- (a) section 126 (reference of local crime and disorder matters to crime and disorder committees etc);

- (b) the entry in Part 6 of Schedule 18 (repeals) relating to the Police and Justice Act 2006; and

- (c) section 241 (repeals) so far as relating to that entry.

## Signed

Vernon Coaker — Minister of State — 6th April 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences further provisions of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act [2007 (c.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/28) (“the 2007 Act”) in relation to England.

Section 126 makes amendments to section 19 of the Police and Justice Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”), including in particular the substitution of a new section 19(3) to (8B). The new provisions state that a local authority must ensure that its crime and disorder committee (as created under section 19 of the 2006 Act) has the power to make a report or recommendations on relevant matters to the local authority and to ensure that any member of the authority who is not a member of the committee has a power to refer relevant matters to the committee. The new provisions also set out certain procedures that must be followed where a person refers a relevant matter to the local authority.

This Order also commences an associated repeal.
