# The Personal Injuries (Civilians) Amendment Scheme 2000

Made: 3rd February 2000

Laid before Parliament: 15th February 2000

Coming into force: 10th April 2000

The Secretary of State for Social Security, with the approval of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 2 of the Personal Injuries (Emergency Provisions) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Scheme:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Amendment Scheme 2000 and shall come into force on 10th April 2000.

- (2) In this Scheme, the expression “the principal Scheme” means the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983[^f00003].

#### Amendment of article 18 of the principal Scheme

###### 2

In article 18 of the principal Scheme (unemployability allowances) in paragraph (2) for the amount “£3,016” there shall be substituted the amount “£3,042”.

#### Substitution of Schedules 3 and 4 to the principal Scheme

###### 3

For Schedules 3 and 4 to the principal Scheme (rates of pensions and allowances payable in respect of disablement and death) there shall respectively be substituted the Schedules set out in the Schedule to this Scheme and numbered 3 and 4.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

This Scheme further amends the Personal Injuries (Civilians) Scheme 1983 (S.I. [1983/686](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1983/686)) (“the principal Scheme”) which makes provision for the payment of pensions and allowances to or in respect of civilians who were killed or injured during the 1939–45 World War by increasing the amounts of allowances, pensions and awards payable under that Scheme and also increasing the amounts of income to be disregarded for the purposes of certain parts of the Scheme.

This Scheme does not impose any costs on business.
