# The Chevening Estate Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987

Made: 17th July 1987

The Lord Privy Seal in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 5(2) of the Chevening Estate Act 1987[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Chevening Estate Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987.

###### 2

The Chevening Estate Act 1987 shall come into force on 1st September 1987.

## Signed

Lord Privy Seal — 17th July 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st September 1987 the Chevening Estate Act 1987.
