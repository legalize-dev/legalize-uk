# The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1991

Made: 4th March 1991

Laid before the House of Commons: 6th March 1991

Coming into force: 27th March 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 1(1) and (2) of Schedule 4 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to him to be appropriate in accordance with paragraph 2(2) of the said Schedule, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1991 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the House of Commons.

#### Revenue support grants for 1990-91

###### 2

- (1) The local authorities to which revenue support grant is payable in respect of the financial year 1990-91 are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) The amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order in respect of the financial year 1990-91 shall be the amount redtermined in relation to that local authority set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1990 (which determined the amount of revenue support grant payable to each local authority in respect of the financial year 1990-91)[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order redetermines the amount of the revenue support grant payable to each local authority specified in the Schedule to the Order in respect of the financial year 1990-91.

As a consequence, it revokes the Revenue Support Grant (Scotland) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/131](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/131)) which previously determined such amounts.
