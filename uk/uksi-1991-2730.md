# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No.7) Order 1991

Made: 3rd December 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990([^f00001]), hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No.7) Order 1991.

###### 2

The provisions of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 specified in the Schedule hereto shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

## SCHEDULE

| Provision of the Act | Subject matter of provision |
| --- | --- |
| Section 79 and schedule 12 | Widowers' pensions |
| Section 80 | Widows' and widowers' pensions: supplemental |
| Section 102 | Arbitration: want of prosecution |
| Section 116(3) | Provision with respect to the Children Act 1989([^f00002]) |
| Paragraphs 26-30 and 33-35 of schedule 18 and section 125(3) so far as it relates to them | Consequential amendments |
| Paragraph 9 of schedule 19 and sections 125(6) so far as it relates to that paragraph. | Transitional[^f00003]) |
| Section 125(7) and schedule 20 so far as they provide for the repeal of or words in, sections 18(3), 20(6), 22(5), 24 and 25 of, and paragraph 15(3) of schedule 1 to, the Judicial Pensions Act 1981[^f00002] | Repeals |

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 3rd December 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 1992 provisions in the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 relating to payment of widows' and widowers' pensions under the Judicial Pensions Act 1981, the want of prosecution in arbitration cases and the power to make rules of court for Northern Ireland with respect to the Children Act 1989.
