# The Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Guidelines) Order 1999

Made: 25th July 1999

Coming into force: 31st July 1999

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by paragraph 8 of Schedule 2 to the 1998 Act, hereby orders as follows:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Guidelines) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 31st July 1999.

#### Revised Guidelines

###### 2

The revised guidelines laid in draft before Parliament on 30th June 1999 as to the exercise by the Commission of its functions under section 8 of the 1998 Act shall come into operation on 31st July 1999.

###### 3

The Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Guidelines) Order 1998[^f00002] (which brought into operation the draft guidelines laid before Parliament on 23rd February 1998) is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Marjorie Mowlam — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 25th July 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 31st July 1999 as the date on which the revised guidelines relating to the exercise by the Parades Commission for Northern Ireland (“the Commission”) of its functions under section 8 of the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (“the 1998 Act”) (power to impose conditions on public processions) are to come into operation.

The revised guidelines, which are issued by the Commission under section 5 of the 1998 Act, replace the guidelines brought into operation on 3rd March 1998 by the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act (Guidelines) Order 1998 (which is revoked by this Order).

The revised guidelines are published by the Commission and copies may be obtained from the Commission at 12th Floor, Windsor House, Bedford Street, Belfast BT2 7EL.
