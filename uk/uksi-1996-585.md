# The Local Government (Publication of Information about Unused and Underused Land) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 1996

Made: 5th March 1996

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 1996

Coming into force: 3rd April 1996

The Secretary of State for the Environment, after consultation with such associations of authorities to whom section 2 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001] applies as to appear to him to be concerned, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3 of that Act[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Publication of Information about Unused and Underused Land) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 3rd April 1996.

#### Revocation of 1992 Regulations

###### 2

The Local Government (Publication of Information about Unused and Underused Land) (England) Regulations 1992[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

David Curry — Minister of State, — Department of Environment — 5th March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Local Government (Publication of Information about Unused and Underused Land) (England) Regulations 1992, which required local authorities to publish the information specified in the Code of Recommended Practice for the Publication of Information about Unused and Underused Land owned by the Secretary of State for the Environment under section 2 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980.
