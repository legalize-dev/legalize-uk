# The Trade in Animals and Related Products (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 4th April 2019

Laid before Parliament: 5th April 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21(b) of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 .

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft of the instrument being laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — Introduction

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Trade in Animals and Related Products (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland only.

## PART 2 — Amendment to primary legislation

#### Amendment to the Destructive Imported Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 1933

###### 2

In section 9 of the Destructive Imported Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 1933 , omit subsection (2).

## PART 3 — Amendment to subordinate legislation

#### Amendment to the Sales, Markets and Lairs Order (Northern Ireland) 1975

###### 3

- (3) In Articles 16(1) and 17, for “intra-Community trade” substitute “ trade with the EU ”.
