# The Territorial Sea Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987

Made: 21st July 1987

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of July 1987 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 4(2) of the Territorial Sea Act 1987[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Territorial Sea Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987.

###### 2

The Territorial Sea Act 1987 shall come into force on 1st October 1987.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Territorial Sea Act 1987 into force on 1st October 1987.
