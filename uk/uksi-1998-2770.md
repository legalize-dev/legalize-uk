# The Limit in Relation to Provision of Digital Programme Services Order 1998

Made: 12th November 1998

Laid before Parliament: 13th November 1998

Coming into force: 14th November 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 7(9)(c) and (10) of Part III of Schedule 2 to the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Limit in Relation to Provision of Digital Programme Services Order 1998 and shall come into force on 14th November 1998.

#### Amendment to paragraph 7 of Part III of Schedule 2 to the Broadcasting Act 1990

###### 2

Paragraph 7 of Part III of Schedule 2 to the Broadcasting Act 1990 shall be amended as follows:–

- (a) in sub-paragraph (7)(b) the number “24” shall be substituted by the number “40” and the number “4” shall be substituted by the number “10”;

- (b) in sub-paragraph (7)(c) the number “24” shall be substituted by the number “40”.

## Signed

Chris Smith — Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport — 12th November 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the limit in relation to provision of digital programme services detailed in paragraph 7 of Part III of Schedule 2 to the Broadcasting Act 1990 by substituting different numbers for certain of the numbers for the time being specified in sub-paragraph (7).
