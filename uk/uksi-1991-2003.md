# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991

Made: 5th September 1991

Laid before Parliament: 16th September 1991

Coming into force: 7th October 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1) and (5) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act hereby makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 7th October 1991.

#### Preliminary

###### 2

The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986[^f00002] shall be further amended in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

#### Regulation 30 (view to the front)

###### 3

In regulation 30(2) after “81/643” there shall be inserted “, 88/366, 90/630”.

#### Amendments to regulation 46 (seat belt anchorage points)

###### 4

- (1) Regulation 46 shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (4)(b), after “82/318” there shall be inserted “or 90/629” and after the words “Regulation 14” there shall be inserted the words “or 14.01 or 14.02”.

- (3) In paragraph (4B)(a), after “82/318” there shall be added “or 90/629”.

- (4) In paragraph (6), the word “and” immediately following sub-paragraph (a) shall be ommitted, and for sub-paragraph (b) there shall be substituted the following paragraphs—

> > (b) a seat is a “non protected seat” if it is not a front seat and the screen zones within the protected area have a combined surface area of less than 800cm²; and
> (c) “screen zone” and “protected area” in relation to a seat, shall be construed in accordance with paragraph 4.3.3 of Annex 1 to a Community Directive 81/575[^f00003].

#### Amendments to Regulation 47 (seat belts)

###### 5

- (1) Regulation 47 shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (3), after “77/541” there shall be inserted “or 82/319 or 90/628” and for the words “that Directive applies” there shall be substituted the words “those Directives apply”.

- (3) In paragraph (8), in the definition of “designated approval mark” for the words “and 23A” there shall be substituted the words “, 23A and 23B”.

- (1) Table 1 of Schedule 2 shall be amended as follows.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986.

First they amend regulation 30 (view to the front of a motor vehicle) by enabling a vehicle to meet the requirements of the regulation by complying with Council Directive [77/649/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0649) as amended by Commission Directive [81/643/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0643), Commission Directive [88/366/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0366) and Commission Directive [90/630/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0630). A vehicle can, as before, meet the requirements of regulation 30 by complying with Council Directive [77/649/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0649) or with that Directive as amended by Commission Directive [81/643/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0643).

Secondly, they amend regulation 46 (seat belt anchorage points) by enabling a vehicle to meet the requirements of the regulation by complying with Council Directive [76/115/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0115) as amended by Council Directive [81/575/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0575) Commission Directive [82/318/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0318) and Commission Directive [90/629/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0629). A vehicle can, as before, meet the requirements of the regulation by complying with Council Directive [76/115/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0115) or with that directive as amended by Council Directive [82/318/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0318). The Regulations also make provisions for vehicles that comply with the provisions of ECE Regulation 14 as amended.

Thirdly, they amend regulation 47 (seat belts) so as to enable a vehicle to meet the requirements of the regulation by complying with Council Directive [77/541/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0541) as amended by Commission Directive [82/319/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0319) or with that Directive as further amended by Commission Directive [90/628/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0628). A vehicle can. as before, meet the requirements of regulation 47 by complying with Council Directive [77/541/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0541).

Copies of the ECE Regulations (and amendments) and of the EEC Directives (and amendments) referred to in these Regulations and in this note can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office. The details of the ECE Regulations and the EEc Directives are set out in the Table below.
