# The General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 2011

Made: 24th January 2011

Coming into force: 31st January 2011

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall the 24th day of January 2011By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

By virtue of sections 50C(2)[^f00002] and (3) of that Act, such rules shall not come into force until approved by Order of the Privy Council.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) type=start slip=2011-08-01 time=1313590554322(Amendment) Rules Order of Council 2011type=end slip=2011-08-01 time=1313590554322 and comes into force on 31st January 2011.

#### Privy Council approval

###### 2

Their Lordships, having taken the Rules contained in the Schedule into consideration, are pleased to and do approve them.

## SCHEDULE — The General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) (Amendment) Rules 2010

The General Dental Council make the following Rules in exercise of their powers conferred by sections 2(6) and 50C(5) of, and paragraph 8(1)(c) of Schedule 1 to, the Dentists Act 1984[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) (Amendment) Rules 2010 and shall come into force on 31st January 2011.

#### Amendment of rule 4 of the General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) Rules 2009

###### 2

In the General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) Rules 2009[^f00004], in rule 4(1) (the Committees)—

- (a) in subparagraph (b) (Interim Orders Committee), for “100” substitute “175”;

- (b) in subparagraph (c) (Professional Conduct Committee), for “100” substitute “175”;

- (c) in subparagraph (d) (Health Committee), for “50” substitute “175”;

- (d) in subparagraph (e) (Professional Performance Committee), for “100” substitute “175”; and

- (e) in subparagraph (f) (Registration Appeals Committee), for “100” substitute “175”.

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order approves the General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) (Amendment) Rules 2010, which are scheduled to the Order.

Rule 1 sets out citation and commencement.

Rule 2 sets out amendments to rule 4(1) of the General Dental Council (Constitution of Committees) Rules 2009, which were scheduled to [SI 2009/1813](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1813). The amendments increase the maximum number of persons who may be appointed under rule 4(1) to the Interim Orders Committee, the Professional Conduct Committee, the Professional Performance Committee and the Registration Appeals Committee from 100 to 175 persons. The maximum number of persons who may be appointed to the Health Committee is increased from 50 to 175 persons.
