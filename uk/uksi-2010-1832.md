# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2010

Made: 21st July 2010

Coming into force: 22nd July 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of July 2010Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2010 and comes into force on 22nd July 2010.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE

### Persons appointed on 22nd July 2010

- Debora Barazetti-Scott

- Matthew Eric Brazier

- Sheena Doyle

- Wendy Margaret Ghaffar

- Simon John Rushall

- John Joseph Tarpey

- Melanie Jane Knowles

- Lee Anthony Northern

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council
