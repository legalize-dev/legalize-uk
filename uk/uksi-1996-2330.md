# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made: 9th September 1996

Laid before Parliament: 10th September 1996

Coming into force: 1st October 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 54(1) and 61 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 1st October 1996.

#### Preliminary

###### 2

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984[^f00002] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

#### Amendments to regulation 3 (interpretation)

###### 3

In regulation 2(1), after the definition of “ECE Regulation” there shall be inserted the following definitions—

> > - “emissions or noise item” means an item in Part I of Schedule 1 in respect of which the subject matter specified in column (2) of that Part is “exhaust emissions” or “noise and silencers;”.
> - “Framework Directive vehicle” means a vehicle to which these Regulations apply and which is a “vehicle” within the meaning of Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156)[^f00003] as amended by Council Directive [87/403/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0403)[^f00004], Council Directive [92/53/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0053)[^f00005] and Commission Directive 93/81[^f00006];

#### Amendments to regulation 4

###### 4

- (1) Regulation 4 shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (1A), there shall be inserted at the beginning the words “Subject to paragraph (3B),” and the words after sub-paragraph (b) shall be omitted.

- (3) In paragraph (3A), for the words “items” onwards there shall be substituted the words “any emissions or noise item”.

- (4) After paragraph (3A) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> > (3B) No emissions or noise item shall apply to a Framework Directive vehicle on or after the date specified in column (6) of the item.

- (5) At the end of paragraph (4) there shall be added the words “; and for the purposes of this paragraph, items 2A and 2B shall be regarded as items having the same subject matter”.

- (6) For paragraph (11) there shall be substituted—

> > (11) Schedules 1B and 1C (which treat certain emissions or noise items as if in certain circumstances specified dates were substituted for the entries in columns (5) and (6)) shall have effect.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984.

Regulation 3 of these Regulations inserts definitions of “emissions or noise item” and “Framework Directive vehicle” into regulation 2 of the 1984 Regulations.

Regulation 4 amends the provisions relating to the applicability of type approval requirements, as contained in regulation 4 of the 1984 Regulations, in relation to emissions and noise.

Regulation 5 amends the type approval requirements set out in Schedule 1 to the 1984 Regulations in relation to exhaust emissions, noise and silencers and electromagnetic compatibility of vehicles.

Regulation 6 makes certain changes to Schedule 1B (modification of applicable dates of type approval requirements) to the 1984 Regulations.

Regulation 7 inserts into the 1984 Regulations a new Schedule 1C which modifies the effect of those Regulations in relation to “end of series vehicles” and “late entry into service vehicles” as defined in that Schedule.
