# The A428 Black Cat to Caxton Gibbet (Correction) Order 2023

Made: 16th January 2023

Coming into force: 17th January 2023

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A428 Black Cat to Caxton Gibbet (Correction) Order 2023 and comes into force on 17th January 2023.

#### Corrections to the 2022 Order

###### 2

The 2022 Order is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the A428 Black Cat to Caxton Gibbet Development Consent Order 2022 a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following a written request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
