# The Security Service Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made: 10th November 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 7(2) of the Security Service Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Security Service Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989.

###### 2

The Security Service Act 1989 shall come into force on 18th December 1989.

## Signed

David Waddington — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 10th November 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 18th December 1989 all the provisions of the Security Service Act 1989.
