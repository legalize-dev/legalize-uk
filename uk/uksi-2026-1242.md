# The English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provisions and Saving) Regulations 2026

Made: 25th November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 108(6) and (9) of, and paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 34 to, the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provisions and Saving) Regulations 2026.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “*the 2014 Act*” means the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014[^f00002];

- “*the 2026 Act*” means the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026;

- “*appointing person*” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Local Audit (Appointing Person) Regulations 2015[^f00003];

- “*Category 1 authority*” has the meaning given in regulation 2(1) of the Accounts and Audit Regulations 2015[^f00004];

- “*financial year*” has the meaning given in section 3(4) of the 2014 Act;

- “*health service body*” has the meaning given in section 3(9)[^f00005] of the 2014 Act;

- “*opted in authority*” has the meaning given in regulation 2 of the Local Audit (Appointing Person) Regulations 2015.

#### Provisions coming into force on 27th November 2026

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2026 Act come into force on 27th November 2026—

- (a) section 87 (licensing of gambling premises: impact assessments);

- (b) section 90 (local audit providers: registration and public provision);

- (c) section 92 (audit providers to nominate lead partner);

- (d) section 95 (review of audit and reporting arrangements at Secretary of State’s request);

- (e) section 99 (minor and consequential amendments), so far as relating to the provisions specified in paragraph (h);

- (f) Schedule 34 (the local audit office), so far as it is not already in force;

- (g) Schedule 35 (local audit: registration bodies, registered providers and qualifications);

- (h) the following provisions of Schedule 36 (local audit: minor and consequential amendments)—

- (i) paragraph 1, so far as relating to the provisions mentioned in sub-paragraphs (ii) to (xiii);

- (ii) paragraph 13 in relation to a Category 1 authority or a health service body;

- (iii) paragraph 19;

- (iv) paragraph 21 in relation to a Category 1 authority or a health service body, except so far as it inserts section 32H into the 2014 Act;

- (v) paragraph 22(a), (c), (d), (e) and (h);

- (vi) paragraph 23;

- (vii) paragraph 24(1), (2) and (4);

- (viii) paragraph 24(3) in relation to a Category 1 authority or a health service body;

- (ix) paragraph 26(8);

- (x) paragraph 27 in relation to a Category 1 authority or a health service body;

- (xi) paragraph 29(3)(a) and (4);

- (xii) paragraph 31 except so far as the substitution made by that paragraph relates to paragraph 1(3)(d) of Schedule 11 to the 2014 Act (disclosure of information);

- (xiii) paragraphs 32 to 35.

#### Transitional provision: initial business plan of the Local Audit Office

###### 3

The duty of the Local Audit Office under paragraph 14(1)(a) and (2) of Schedule 1A to the 2014 Act (business planning)[^f00006]—

- (a) is disapplied in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2026;

- (b) is modified in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2027 to require the Local Audit Office to publish a business plan in respect of that year as soon as reasonably practicable.

#### Transitional provision: second audits

###### 4

- (1) This regulation applies in relation to a financial year beginning on or before 1st April 2026.

- (2) Section 32D of the 2014 Act[^f00007] (second audits where first auditor ineligible) has effect in relation to a Category 1 authority which is an opted in authority as if—

- (a) in subsection (1) after “appointed” there were inserted “by an appointing person”;

- (b) in subsections (2) to (4)—

- (i) for “Local Audit Office”, in each place it occurs, there were substituted “Secretary of State”;

- (ii) for “relevant authority”, in each place it occurs, there were substituted “appointing person”;

- (c) in subsection (5), for “authority” there were substituted “appointing person”;

- (d) in subsection (8)—

- (i) for “the relevant authority” there were substituted “the appointing person who appointed the auditor”;

- (ii) after “this section” there were inserted “and must ensure that the authority is either not required to pay a fee for the first audit or is refunded any fee paid.”.

- (3) Section 32D has effect in relation to a Category 1 authority which is not an opted in authority as if for the “Local Audit Office” in subsections (2) and (4), in each place it occurs, there were substituted “Secretary of State”.

#### Transitional provision: appointment of partnerships

###### 5

- (1) This regulation applies in relation to a financial year beginning on or before 1st April 2026.

- (2) Section 32E of the 2014 Act[^f00008] (effect of appointing a partnership) has effect as if—

- (a) in subsection (1), the reference to a partnership appointed under section 6G or 7 includes a reference to a partnership appointed under the 2014 Act before 1st April 2027; and

- (b) in subsection (6) for “the Local Audit Office” there were substituted—

- (i) “the appointing person” where the authority is a Category 1 authority which is an opted in authority, or

- (ii) “the relevant authority” where the authority is a Category 1 authority which is not an opted in authority.

#### Transitional provision: health service body audits

###### 6

- (1) In this regulation “*relevant arrangements*” means arrangements for—

- (a) the Financial Reporting Council Limited[^f00009] to carry out inspections of local auditors under the 2014 Act in relation to the audits of health service bodies that, immediately before 27th November 2026, were major local audits within the meaning of regulation 12 of the Local Audit (Professional Qualifications and Major Local Audit) Regulations 2014[^f00010] for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2025; and

- (b) the funding of those inspections.

- (2) Any relevant arrangements in existence immediately before 27th November 2026 are to continue in effect and are to be treated as arrangements made—

- (a) under paragraph 9(1)(b) of Schedule 1C[^f00011] to the 2014 Act (eligibility and regulation of registered providers), and

- (b) for the purposes of Schedule 1C to the 2014 Act, so that the Financial Reporting Council Limited is to be treated as a person given functions by those arrangements for the purposes of paragraph 1(3)(c) of Schedule 11 to the 2014 Act.

#### Saving provision: professional qualifications and delegation of functions

###### 7

Despite the repeal of Schedule 5[^f00012] to the 2014 Act in relation to a Category 1 authority or health service body—

- (a) the transfer, by the Local Audit (Delegation of Functions) and Statutory Audit (Delegation of Functions) Order 2014[^f00013] to the Financial Reporting Council Limited of functions of the Secretary of State under the Local Audit (Professional Qualifications and Major Local Audit) Regulations 2014 continues to have effect; and

- (b) Schedule 5 to the 2014 Act continues to have effect so far as necessary to enable the functions referred to in paragraph (a) to continue to be exercised by the Financial Reporting Council Limited.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations are the sixth commencement regulations made under the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act [2026 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23) (“*the 2026 Act*”).

Regulation 2 brings into force section 87 of the 2026 Act on 27th November 2026. This section amends the Gambling Act [2005 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/19) to make provision for gambling impact assessments.

Regulation 2 also brings into force several provisions relating to the establishment of the Local Audit Office on 27th November 2026 including provisions relating to the registration, regulation, provision and oversight of local audit providers by the Local Audit Office, the nomination of lead audit partners and the review of audit and reporting arrangements at the request of the Secretary of State.

Regulation 3 makes transitional provision in respect of the establishment of the Local Audit Office to provide that the Office does not need to publish a business plan in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2026 and provides that the Office must publish a business plan in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2027 as soon as reasonably practicable.

Regulation 4 makes transitional provision in relation to the financial year beginning on or before 1st April 2026 in relation to circumstances in which a second audit of a relevant authority’s accounts is required due to auditor ineligibility.

Regulation 5 makes transitional provision in relation to the financial year beginning on or before 1st April 2026 in relation to the appointment of a partnership as an auditor of a relevant authority.

Regulation 6 makes transitional provision in relation to the inspection of local auditors by the Financial Reporting Council Limited, relating to certain health service bodies for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2025.

Regulation 7 makes saving provision in relation to the recognition of professional qualifications for local audit.

An Impact Assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. An Impact Assessment was produced for the 2026 Act and is available from https://bills.parliament.uk/publications/63710/documents/7405, and copies can be obtained from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DF.
