# The Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made: 29th June 1993

Laid before Parliament: 2nd July 1993

Coming into force: 1st August 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st August 1993.

#### Amendment of 1993 Regulations

###### 2

For regulation 11(6) of the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1993[^f00002] there is substituted—

> > (6) In paragraph (5) “disability-related benefits” means—
>   (a) the benefits or allowances payable pursuant to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00003] known as invalidity benefit[^f00004], severe disablement allowance[^f00005], disability living allowance[^f00006] and industrial injuries benefit[^f00007];
>   (b) disability premium and severe disability premium payable pursuant to the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987[^f00008]; and
>   (c) disability working allowance payable under sections 123(1)(c) and 129 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992.

## Signed

John Patten — Secretary of State for Education — 29th June 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations substitute a revised paragraph (6) in regulation 11 of the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1993 to correct a defect in the drafting of that paragraph. The revised paragraph substitutes for references to the Social Security Act 1975 references to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, a consolidating Act which replaces the 1975 Act.
