# The Glan Hafren National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1996

Made: 29th January 1996

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Glan Hafren National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Dissolution of NHS trust

###### 2

The Glan Hafren National Health Service Trust established by the Glan Hafren National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

## Signed

William Hague — Secretary of State for Wales — 29th January 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 1996 of the Glan Hafren National Health Service Trust established on 16th November 1992.
