# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made: 11th February 1998

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of February 1998 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 2 and 13 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1998.

###### 2

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 1997[^f00002] is revoked.

###### 3

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986[^f00003] is amended by deleting Schedules 1 and 2 and substituting therefor Schedules 1 and 2 which are set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986. It adds the Czech Republic and Turkmenistan to the list of Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, done at The Hague on 25th October 1980 (Cm. 33).
