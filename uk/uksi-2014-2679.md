# The South West Water Authority (Dissolution) Order 2014

Made: 30th September 2014

Coming into force: 1st November 2014

In accordance with section 4(5) of the Act, the Secretary of State has consulted the South West Water Authority, the Environment Agency[^f00002] and South West Water Limited and is satisfied that nothing further remains to be done by the South West Water Authority under any scheme under Schedule 2 to the Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the South West Water Authority (Dissolution) Order 2014; and

- (b) comes into force on 1st November 2014.

#### Dissolution of the South West Water Authority

###### 2

The South West Water Authority is dissolved.

## Signed

John Bourne — Deputy Director in the Water Availability and Quality Programme — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 30th September 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the dissolution of the South West Water Authority.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as no cost to the business or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
