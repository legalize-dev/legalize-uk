# The Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2014

Made: 26th September 2014

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 139(6) of the Children and Families Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children and Families Act 2014 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2014.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st October 2014

###### 2

Sections 91 to 95 of the Children and Families Act 2014 come into force on 1st October 2014 for the purposes of making orders or regulations only.

## Signed

Edward Timpson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Education — 26th September 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force provisions of the Children and Families Act [2014 (c.6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/6) (“the 2014 Act”) on 1st October 2014. Certain provisions came into force on the passing of the 2014 Act. Provisions commenced by previous Commencement Orders are noted below.

Article 2 commences certain sections in Part 5 (tobacco, nicotine products and smoking) of the 2014 Act for the purposes of making orders or regulations only, on 1st October 2014.
