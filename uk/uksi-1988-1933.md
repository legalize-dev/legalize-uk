# The Scottish Land Court (Fees) Amendment Rules 1988

Made: 31st October 1988

Coming into force: 5th December 1988

The Scottish Land Court, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 29 of the Crofters Holdings (Scotland) Act 1886[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and with the approval of the Treasury, hereby make the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Scottish Land Court (Fees) Amendment Rules 1988 and shall come into force on 5th December 1988.

###### 2

For the Table of Fees contained in Appendix 1 to the Scottish Land Court Rules 1979[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

###### 3

The Scottish Land Court (Fees) Amendment Rules 1987[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Table of Fees in the Scottish Land Court Rules 1979 for two purposes—

- (1) to increase (with some exceptions) the fees payable in respect of applications to the Scottish Land Court; and

- (2) to impose, at paragraphs (1)(e), (2)(i), (3)(c) and (d) for the first time, fees in respect of Hearings and ddditional fees where an application is dismissed or withdrawn.

The Rules also revoke the Scottish Land Court (Fees) Amendment Rules 1987.
