# The Cattle Identification (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 12th May 1999

Laid before Parliament: 13th May 1999

Coming into force: 4th June 1999

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the said section 2(2) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, make the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Cattle Identification (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 4th June 1999.

#### Amendment to the Cattle Identification Regulations 1998

###### 2

The Cattle Identification Regulations 1998 are amended by substituting the following for regulation 29(2)–

> > (2) For the purposes of Article 7.3 of the Council Regulation (supply of information concerning cattle) the competent authority is the local authority or–
>   (a) in England, the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food;
>   (b) in Scotland, the Secretary of State for Scotland;
>   (c) in Wales–
>     (i) until 1st July 1999, the Secretary of State for Wales;
>     (ii) on or after 1st July 1999, the National Assembly for Wales.
> (2A) The competent authority referred to the second time in Article 7.4 of the Council Regulation (keeping a register of cattle) is the local authority or–
>   (a) in England, the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food;
>   (b) in Scotland, the Secretary of State for Scotland;
>   (c) in Wales–
>     (i) until 1st July 1999, the Secretary of State for Wales;
>     (ii) on or after 1st July 1999, the National Assembly for Wales.

## Signed

Jeff Rooker — Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 10th May 1999

Sewel — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 12th May 1999
