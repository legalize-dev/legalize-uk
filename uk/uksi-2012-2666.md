# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2012

Made: 23rd October 2012

Coming into force: 19th November 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 113A(6) and 125 of the Police Act 1997[^f00001] as extended and modified by the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Guernsey) Order 2009[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 19th November 2012.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Guernsey.

#### Amendment of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) Regulations 2009

###### 2

In regulation 9 of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) Regulations 2009 (central records: prescribed details)[^f00003] for “National Policing Improvement Agency” substitute “Secretary of State”.

## Signed

Taylor of Holbeach — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 23rd October 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which come into force on 19th November 2012, amend the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) Regulations 2009 (“the 2009 Regulations”).

Regulation 2 amends regulation 9 of the 2009 Regulations in order to replace the reference to the National Policing Improvement Agency (“NPIA”) with a reference to the Secretary of State. The NPIA is being phased out and the Home Office has now taken over the function of running the Police National Computer (“PNC”). It is the names database of convictions, cautions, warnings and reprimands which is held on the PNC which is prescribed as “central records” for the purposes of section 113A(6) of the Police Act 1997.
