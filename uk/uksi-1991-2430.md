# The Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

Made: 22nd October 1991

The Secretary of State, in the exercise of his powers under Section 15(6) of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991.

###### 2

The provisions of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991 shall come into force on 23rd October 1991.

## Signed

Timothy Sainsbury — Minister for Trade, — Department of Trade and Industry — 22nd October 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 23rd October 1991 the whole of the Export and Investment Guarantees Act 1991.
