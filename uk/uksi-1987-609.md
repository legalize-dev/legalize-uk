# The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1987

Made: 30th March 1987

Laid before Parliament: 9th April 1987

Coming into force: 4th May 1987

In pursuance of sections 14(2) and 51 of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and after consultation with the Gaming Board for Great Britain, I hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 4th May 1987.

- (2) These Regulations do not extend to Scotland.

###### 2

Regulation 5(1) of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) Regulations 1984[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the words “£4”, in both places where they occur, the words “£4.60”.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 30th March 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the maximum charges which may be made for admission to gaming on bingo club premises in England and Wales from £4 to £4.60.
