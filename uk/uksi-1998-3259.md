# The National Crime Squad Service Authority (Levying) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made: 22nd December 1998

Laid before Parliament: 8th January 1999

Coming into force: 1st February 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 62 of the Police Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Crime Squad Service Authority (Levying) (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st February 1999.

###### 2

- (1) The National Crime Squad Service Authority (Levying) Order 1997[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

- (2) Paragraph (4) of article 13 (payment of the levy) shall be omitted; and accordingly, in paragraphs (2) and (3) of that article, the words “type=start time=1187965075452Subjecttype=end time=1187965075452 to paragraph (4) below” shall be omitted.

- (3) For the Schedule there shall be substituted the following Schedule:

> > **SCHEDULE**
>   (1) Each police authority shall contribute to the total amount of the levies issued under section 62 of the 1997 Act a sum which is such proportion of that total amount as is equal to the proportion which the sum of:
>     (a) the amount of the grant made to that authority for the relevant year under section 46 of the 1996 Act; and
>     (b) the amount of that authority’s standard spending assessment for the police service block in the relevant year,
>   bears to the sum of–
>     (i) the aggregate amount of grants made for the relevant year under section 46 of the 1996 Act; and
>     (ii) the aggregate of the standard spending assessments for the police service block in the relevant year.
>   (2) In this Schedule–
>     (a) any reference to the amount of grant made under section 46 of the 1996 Act or to the aggregate amount of grants so made shall be construed as a reference to the amount of grant or, as the case may be, aggregate amount of grants made in pursuance of a formula or other rule applied under subsection (4) of that section which applies to all police authorities; and accordingly any special payment made to one or more police authorities in pursuance of any other formula or rule, or of any consideration taken into account in relation to that police authority under subsection (3) of that section, shall for the purposes of this Schedule be disregarded;
>     (b) any reference to the standard spending assessment for the police service block of a police authority is a reference to the police element of its standard spending assessment as used in calculating the amount of revenue support grant payable to that authority in accordance with the basis of distribution in the local government finance report relating to the relevant year made by the Secretary of State under section 78A of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00003]; and any reference to the aggregate of the standard spending assessments for the police service block is a reference to the total amount of the police element of the standard spending assessments calculated for the receiving authorities (within the meaning of Part V of that Act).

## Signed

Paul Boateng — Minister of State — 22nd December 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the National Crime Squad Service Authority (Levying) Order 1997 by substituting, for the Schedule to that Order, the Schedule set out in article 2(2) of the Order. The Schedule contains the formula for apportionment between police authorities of the total amount of the levies which the National Crime Squad Service Authority proposes to issue for the financial year. The Order also provides for the omission of article 13(4) of that Order, which is spent.
