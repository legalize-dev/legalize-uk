# The Plant Varieties Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1998

Made: 8th April 1998

The Ministers, in exercise of their powers under section 54(3) of the Plant Varieties Act 1997[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Varieties Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1998.

#### Provisions coming into force on 8th May 1998

###### 2

The Plant Varieties Act 1997, other than sections 49, 53 and 54[^f00002], shall come into force on 8th May 1998.

## Signed

Donoughue — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 8th April 1998

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Win Griffiths — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 7th April 1998

Dubs — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Northern Ireland Office — 8th April 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 8th May 1998 those parts of the Plant Varieties Act 1997 which did not come into force on the passing of that Act.
