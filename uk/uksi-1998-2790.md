# The Bethlem and Maudsley National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1998

Made: 12th November 1998

Laid before Parliament: 16th November 1998

Coming into force: 8th December 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of facilities previously managed by the former Camberwell Health Authority and now managed by the Bethlem and Maudsley National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the King’s College Hospital Special Trustees[^f00003] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Bethlem and Maudsley National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 8th December 1998.

- (2) In this Order–

- “the Special Trustees” means the King’s College Hospital Special Trustees;

- “the Trust” means the Bethlem and Maudsley National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the item specified in part B of the Certificate of Authentication of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Special Trustees and the Trust on 15th August 1996; which certificate has been signed on behalf of both the Special Trustees and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 8th December 1998 the trust property shall be transferred from the Special Trustees to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Alan Milburn — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 12th November 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Bethlem and Maudsley National Health Service Trust on 8th December 1998 trust property held by the King’s College Hospital Special Trustees. A copy of the Certificate of Authentication of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, Financial and Audit Support Branch, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
