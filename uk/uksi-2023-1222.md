# The Nationality and Borders Act 2022 (Commencement No. 8) Regulations 2023

Made: 16th November 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 87(1) of the Nationality and Borders Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Nationality and Borders Act 2022 (Commencement No. 8) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Provision coming into force on 20th November 2023

###### 2

Section 46(8) of the Nationality and Borders Act 2022 (removals: notice requirements) comes into force on 20th November 2023.

## Signed

Robert Jenrick — Minister of State — Home Office — 16th November 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force on 20th November 2023 section 46(8) of the Nationality and Borders Act [2022 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/36). Section 46(8) relates to the power of the First-tier Tribunal to grant immigration bail without the consent of the Secretary of State.
