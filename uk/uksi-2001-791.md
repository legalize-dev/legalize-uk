# The Special Trustees for the Former United Birmingham Hospitals (Transfer of Trust Property) Revocation Order 2001

Made: 7th March 2001

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 2001

Coming into force: 31st March 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(6) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Special Trustees for the Former United Birmingham Hospitals (Transfer of Trust Property) Revocation Order 2001 and shall come into force on 31st March 2001.

#### Revocation of the Special Trustees for the Former United Birmingham Hospitals (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001

###### 2

The Special Trustees for the Former United Birmingham Hospitals (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 7th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Special Trustees for the Former United Birmingham Hospitals (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2001 (“the first Order”). That Order is to be replaced by the Special Trustees for the Former United Birmingham Hospitals (Transfer of Trust Property) No 2 Order 2001[^f00003] (“the Second Order”). The first Order cited incorrect names for the trustees to whom trust property was to be transferred. The second Order will effect the transfer of trust property intended to be effected by the first Order.
