# The Energy Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004

Made: 26th July 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 198(2) of the Energy Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Energy Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The provisions of the Energy Act 2004 specified in the Schedule shall come into force on 27th July 2004.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS COMING INTO FORCE ON 27th JULY 2004

| Provision | Subject-matter of provision |
| --- | --- |
| Section 1 | The Nuclear Decommissioning Authority |
| Section 2 | Constitution of NDA |
| Section 10 | Powers for carrying out functions |
| Section 72 | Transfer of authorisations |
| Section 73 | Applications for variation of authorisations |
| Section 74 | Periodic reviews of authorisations |
| Section 75 | Consequential amendments of the 1993 Act |
| Schedule 1 | The Nuclear Decommissioning Authority |
| Schedule 15 | Amendments of 1993 Act |

## Signed

Stephen Timms, — Minister of State for Energy, E-Commerce and Postal Services, — Department of Trade and Industry — 26th July 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 27th July 2004, provisions of the Energy Act 2004 specified in the Schedule to the Order.

The remaining provisions will be brought into force by subsequent orders.
