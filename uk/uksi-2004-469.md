# The Cheshire and Wirral Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Wirral and West Cheshire Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Amendment Order 2004

Made: 11th February 2004

Coming into force: 3rd March 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Cheshire and Wirral Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Wirral and West Cheshire Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Amendment Order 2004 and shall come into force on 3rd March 2004.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Establishment Order” means the Cheshire and Wirral Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Wirral and West Cheshire Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002[^f00003]; and

- “the trust” means the National Health Service Trust established by the Establishment Order.

#### Increase in the number of non-executive directors of the trust

###### 2

In article 4 of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust) “5 non-executive directors” shall be replaced by “6 non-executive directors”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Melanie Johnson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 11th February 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Cheshire and Wirral Partnership National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Wirral and West Cheshire Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002, which established the Cheshire and Wirral Partnership National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order increases the number of non-executive directors of the trust from 5 to 6.
