# The Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005

Made: 27th February 2005

Laid before Parliament: 2nd March 2005

Coming into force: 3rd March 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 144 of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980[^f00001], and after consultation with the rule committee appointed under that section, makes the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 2005 and shall come into force on 3rd March 2005.

###### 2

For Forms C1, C2 and C7 in Schedule 1 to the Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) Rules 1991[^f00002] substitute the forms set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Falconer of Thoroton, *C.* — Dated 27th February 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

The Forms that are substituted by these Rules are substituted so as to correct a defect in the forms that were substituted by the Family Proceedings Courts (Children Act 1989) (Amendment) Rules 2004 ([S.I. 2004/3376](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3376)).
