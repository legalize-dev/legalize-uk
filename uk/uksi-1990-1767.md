# The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 5) Order 1990

Made: 23rd August 1990

Coming into force: 31st August 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 5) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st August 1990.

###### 2

The Export of Goods (Control) Order 1989[^f00003] shall be further amended as follows—

- (a) in article 4(f) the words “or in Namibia” shall be deleted;

- (b) in Group 3E in Part II of Schedule 1 after entry PL7010 there shall be inserted the following new entry—

“PL7016 Aircraft, helicopters and steerable parachutes having a maximum all up weight of not more than 390kg W”.

## Signed

M Coolican — An Assistant Secretary — Department of Trade and Industry — 23rd August 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1989 by

- (a) extending the exclusion from control in article 4 thereof for personal firearms to exports to Namibia;

- (b) introducing export control for microlights, that is aircraft, helicopters and steerable parachutes having a maximum all up weight of not more than 390kg.
