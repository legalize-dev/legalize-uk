## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982 so as—

(a) to amend the form of request for entry of judgment in a default action (Rule 2);

(b) to provide new certificates of service in a number of forms (Rules 3 to 8);

(c) to substitute new forms of admission, defence and counterclaim and of reply to an application for attachment of earnings order (Rule 9);

(d) to substitute a new form of request for an administration order (Rule 10).
