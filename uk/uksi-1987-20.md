# The European Assembly Elections (Day of By-election) (Midlands West Constituency) Order 1987

Made: 14th January 1987

Laid before Parliament: 22nd January 1987

Coming into Force: 23rd January 1987

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the European Assembly Elections Act 1978[^f00002], I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the European Assembly Elections (Day of By-election) (Midlands West Constituency) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 23rd January 1987.

###### 2

The by-election to fill the vacancy in the European Assembly constituency of Midlands West shall be held on 5th March 1987.

## Signed

Home Office

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 14th January 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 5th March 1987 as the day on which the by-election to fill the vacancy in the European Assembly constituency of Midlands West shall be held.
