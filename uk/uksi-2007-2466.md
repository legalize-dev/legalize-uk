# The Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 2007

Made: 20th August 2007

Laid before Parliament: 28th August 2007

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 28th August 2007

Coming into force: 18th September 2007

Acting jointly, the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers (the Welsh Ministers acting in relation to Wales only), in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section, make the following Regulations.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 18th September 2007.

#### Amendments

###### 2

In the Plant Protection Products Regulations 2005[^f00005], in paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” substitute—

> > “the Directive” means Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market, as amended from time to time;

## Signed

Elin Jones — Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers — 20th August 2007

Phil Woolas — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 20th August 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Plant Protection Products Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/1435](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1435)) which implement in England and Wales Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market (OJ No. L230, 19.8.91, p.1).

A new definition for that Directive is substituted to the effect that references to that Directive are references to it as amended from time to time.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
