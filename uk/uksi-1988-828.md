# The South West Water Authority (River Lew Discharge) Order 1988

Made: 28th April 1988

Coming into force: 29th April 1988

The Secretary of State for the Environment, on the application of the South West Water Authority, and in exercise of powers conferred on him by sections 1 and 2(3)[^f00001]of the Water Resources Act 1971[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the South West Water Authority (River Lew Discharge) Order 1988 and shall come into force on the 29th April 1988.

###### 2

The South West Water Authority may for the purpose of augmenting the flow of the River Lew discharge water from the Roadford Reservoir into the River Lew at a discharge point between National Grid References SX 505968 and SX 508973 at a rate not exceeding 81,500 cubic metres per day.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Dinah Nichols — An Under Secretary in the — Department of the Environment — 28th April 1988
