# The Superannuation (Civil Service Transfer Rules) (Revocation) Rules 1972

Made: 9th November 1972

Laid before Parliament: 17th November 1972

Coming into Operation: 11th December 1972

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2 of the Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1948, as amended by paragraph 25 of Schedule 6 to the Superannuation Act 1972, and now vested in him[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Rules:—

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Superannuation (Civil Service Transfer Rules) (Revocation) Rules 1972, and shall come into operation on 11th December 1972.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these Rules as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament, and as if these Rules and the Rules hereby revoked were Acts of Parliament.

###### 3

- (1) The Rules specified in the Schedule to these Rules are hereby revoked.

- (2) The revocation of any Rules by paragraph (1) above shall not affect the operation of those Rules in relation to any person who became, or ceased to be, employed as a civil servant before 1st June 1972.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules revoke the Rules set out in the Schedule, which contain provisions relating to the pensions of persons transferring between the Civil Service and certain other employments. Those provisions have now been incorporated in the Principal Civil Service Pension Scheme made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972.
