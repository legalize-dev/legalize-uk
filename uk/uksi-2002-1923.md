# The Contaminants in Food (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made: 22nd July 2002

Laid before Parliament: 22nd July 2002

Coming into force: 24th July 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16(1)(a) and (f), 17(2), 26(1)(a) and (3) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], having had regard, in accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act, to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency, and after consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], and in accordance with section 48(4) and (4B) of that Act, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Contaminants in Food (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 24th July 2002.

#### Amendment of the Contaminants in Food (England) Regulations 2002

###### 2

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 of the Contaminants in Food (England) Regulations 2002[^f00004] (interpretation)—

- (a) for the definition of “the Commission Regulation” there shall be substituted the following definition—

> > - “the Commission Regulation” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 466/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0466) setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs[^f00005] as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 221/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0221)[^f00006], Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 257/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0257)[^f00007], Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 472/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0472)[^f00008] as corrected by a corrigendum published on 23rd March 2002[^f00009] and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 563/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0563)[^f00010] as corrected by a corrigendum published on 14th June 2002[^f00011];

; and

- (b) for the definition of “port health authority” there shall be substituted the following definition—

> > - “port health authority” means— in relation to the London port health district (within the meaning given to that phrase for the purposes of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00012] by section 7(1) of that Act), the Common Council of the City of London; and in relation to any port health district constituted by order under section 2(3) of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984, a port health authority for that district constituted by order under section 2(4) of that Act.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Contaminants in Food (England) Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/890](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/890)) make provision for the execution and enforcement in England of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 466/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0466) setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs (OJ No. L77, 16.3.2001, p.1, as amended). There is therefore a definition of that Commission Regulation in regulation 2(1) of [S.I. 2002/890](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/890), which was intended to take account of the amendments to it. That defines “the Commission Regulation” as Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 466/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0466) as amended by several further Commission Regulations.

The amendments to Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 466/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0466) include those effected by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 563/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0563) (OJ No. L86, 3.4.2002, p.5).

An error in Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 563/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0563) necessitated the issue of a corrigendum to it, which was published on 14th June 2002 (OJ No. L155, 14.6.2002, p.63). These Regulations give effect to that corrigendum by amending the definition of “the Commission Regulation” in regulation 2(1) of the Contaminants in Food (England) Regulations 2002.

These Regulations also correct an error in the definition of “port health authority” in that regulation.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
