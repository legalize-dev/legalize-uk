# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 4) Order 2001

Made: 11th December 2001

Coming into force: 12th December 2001

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of December 2001 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 4) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 12th December 2001.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 12th December 2001.

## SCHEDULE

| Stephen Abbott Ann Berger Lynn Churchman Keith Dennis Ruth James Helen Morgan Meena Wood | Jan Bennett Michael Blaylock Gillian Close John Evans Catharine Jones Gillian Reay |
