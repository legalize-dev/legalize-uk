# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) Order 1990

Made: 8th June 1990

Coming into force: 1st July 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st July 1990.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — designated owners

- Evansgrove Limited, whose registered office is at 100 Wood Street, London EC2P 2AJ.

- Texaco North Sea UK Company, whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at 1 Knightsbridge Green, London SW1X 7QJ.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates Texaco North Sea UK Company and Evansgrove Limited, as owners of the pipe-lines described in the Schedule, which lie above and between a number of offshore oilfields situated east of the Highland Region of Scotland.
