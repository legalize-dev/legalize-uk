# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (England) (Partial Revocation) Order 1996

made: 16th January 1996

Laid before Parliament: 16th January 1996

Coming into force: 17th January 1996

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and section 24(1) and (3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (England) (Partial Revocation) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 17th January 1996.

#### Partial Revocation

###### 2

- (1) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (England) Order 1991[^f00002] is hereby revoked so far as it designates any part of the area set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) In consequence of that revocation the designated area for the purposes of that Order is that area set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further partially revokes the [Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (England) Order 1991 (S.I. 1991 No. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/6) which contains emergency prohibitions restricting various activities in order to prevent the human consumption of food which has been or may have been rendered unsuitable for that purpose in consequence of the escape of radioactive substances from a nuclear reactor situated at Chernobyl in the Ukraine.

This partial revocation reduces the area subject to the restriction set out in the principal Order. The last partial revocation was contained in the [Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (England) (Partial Revocation) Order 1995 (S.I. 1995 No. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/39).
