# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2004

Made: 6th May 2004

Coming into force: 7th May 2004

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 6th day of May 2004 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No.2) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 7th May 2004.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 7th May 2004.

## SCHEDULE — Persons Appointed

- Anita Wade

- Lisa Williams

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council
