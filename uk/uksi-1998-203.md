# The Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made: 31st January 1998

Coming into force: 1st April 1998

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred upon him by section 143 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

###### 2

The licensing scheme set out in the Schedule to the Principal Order shall be amended as follows:

- (a) in paragraph 4, by substituting for the words “INDEPENDENT TELEVISION ASSOCIATION” the words “INDEPENDENT TELEVISION ASSOCIATION LIMITED”; and

- (b) in paragraph 10 there shall be substituted for all the words which appear immediately before the words “NOTE 1”, the following:

> > (a) For licences taking effect on or after 1st April 1998 but on or before 31st March 1999, the annual tariff, when calculated on a full-time or full-time equivalents per capita basis by category of student in an Educational Establishment, shall be:
>   (i) Students in Primary Educational Establishments (including Educational Establishments known as Preparatory Schools)—21p per capita.
>   (ii) Students in Secondary Educational Establishments—42p per capita.
>   (iii) Students in Educational Establishments of Further Education (including Educational Establishments formerly known as Sixth Form Colleges)—78p per capita.
>   (iv) Students in Educational Establishments of Higher Education (including Higher Education Colleges and Universities)—£1.28 per capita.
>   (v) Students in Educational Establishments other than Primary, Secondary, Further and Higher Education designated from time to time by the Secretary of State—a fee related to the above by type.
> (b) For licences taking effect on or after 1st April 1999, the annual tariff, when calculated on a full-time or full-time equivalents per capita basis by category of student in an Educational Establishment, shall be:
>   (i) Students in Primary Educational Establishments (including Educational Establishments known as Preparatory Schools)—21p per capita.
>   (ii) Students in Secondary Educational Establishments—42p per capita.
>   (iii) Students in Educational Establishments of Further Education (including Educational Establishments formerly known as Sixth Form Colleges)—80p per capita.
>   (iv) Students in Educational Establishments of Higher Education (including Higher Education Colleges and Universities)—£1.30 per capita.
>   (v) Students in Educational Establishments other than Primary, Secondary, Further and Higher Education designated from time to time by the Secretary of State—a fee related to the above by type.

## Signed

Ian McCartney — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 31st January 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The licensing scheme operated by the Educational Recording Agency Limited was certified for the purposes of section 35 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, by the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) Order 1990 (as amended by amendment Orders in 1992, 1993, 1994 and 1996) made under section 143 of that Act.

A variation of the licensing scheme is not effective unless a corresponding amendment of the Order certifying the scheme is made.

This Order, made under section 143 of the Act, further amends the licensing scheme as scheduled to the 1990 Order to provide for—

- (a) certain increases in the annual tariffs for 1998 and 1999; and

- (b) a change of name from Independent Television Association to Independent Television Association Limited.
