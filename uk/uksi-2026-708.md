# The Access to the Countryside (Coastal Margin) (Aldeburgh to Hopton-on-Sea) (No. 2) Order 2026

Made: 29th June 2026

Coming into force: 30th June 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3A(10) of the Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Access to the Countryside (Coastal Margin) (Aldeburgh to Hopton-on-Sea) (No. 2) Order 2026 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) In this Order—

- “*the act*” means the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949[^f00002];

- “*the relevant approval*” means the approval, by the Secretary of State[^f00003] under section 52(1) of the Act[^f00004] of the proposal contained in the— England Coast Path Aldeburgh to Hopton-on-Sea AHS4, Southwold to Pakefield report on 18th September 2025; England Coast Path Aldeburgh to Hopton-on-Sea AHS5, Pakefield to Lowestoft report on 23rd June 2022.

- “*the relevant report*” means the report submitted by Natural England to the Secretary of State under sections 51 and 55A of the Act[^f00005] on 29th January 2020[^f00006] entitled— England Coast Path Aldeburgh to Hopton-on-Sea AHS4, Southwold to Pakefield; England Coast Path Aldeburgh to Hopton-on-Sea AHS5, Pakefield to Lowestoft.

#### End of access preparation period

###### 2

In relation to land which is coastal margin, as a result of the relevant approvals of the proposals contained in the relevant reports, the day appointed as the day on which the access preparation period[^f00007] ends, is 24th June 2026.

## Signed

Hayman of Ullock — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 29th June 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order appoints the day on which the access preparation period ends in relation to land which is coastal margin as a result of the approval by the Secretary of State of proposals contained in reports relating to coastal access along the long-distance route between Aldeburgh and Hopton-on-Sea. Copies of the notices of approval are available at www.gov.uk and may also be obtained by writing to the Department for Environment, Food, and Rural Affairs, Seacole Building, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1 4DF.

An impact assessment has not been prepared in respect of this order because the assumptions underlying the Order were reflected in the impact assessment which was prepared for the Marine and Coastal Access Act [2009 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/23). It is available at www.gov.uk, or may be obtained by writing to the Commons and Access Implementation Team at the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs at the address given above.
