# The Llandeilo–Carmarthen Trunk Road (A40) (Pont-ar-Gothi By-Pass) (Revocation) Order 1998

Made: 25th August 1998

Coming into force: 16th September 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

The Llandeilo–Carmarthen Trunk Road (Pont-ar-Gothi By-Pass) Order 1976[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

###### 2

This Order shall come into force on 16th September 1998 and may be cited as the Llandeilo–Carmarthen Trunk Road (A40) (Pont-ar-Gothi By-Pass) (Revocation) Order 1998.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

D. M. Timlin — Head of Roads Administration Division, Welsh Office — 25th August 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes an Order made under section 7 of the Highways Act 1959 which provided for a by-pass of Pont-ar-Gothi in the County of Dyfed.
