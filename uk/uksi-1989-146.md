# The Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made: 6th February 1989

Coming into force: 28th February 1989

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 13(2) and 45(1) of the Housing Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 28th February 1989.

###### 2

In regulation 3(5) of the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) Regulations 1988[^f00002] substitute “or” for “which is not”.

## Signed

Nicholas Ridley — Secretary of State for the Environment — 31st January 1989

Peter Walker — Secretary of State for Wales — 6th February 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in the Assured Tenancies and Agricultural Occupancies (Forms) Regulations 1988. The effect of the correction is to prescribe form no. 5 in the Schedule to those Regulations for a notice under section 13(2) of the Housing Act 1988 proposing a new rent for an assured agricultural occupancy (as well as for an assured tenancy).
