# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1991

Made: 15th April 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No.4) Order 1991.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 shall come into force on 1st May 1991—

- (a) section 111; and

- (b) paragraph 53 of Schedule 18 and section 125(3) so far as it relates to that paragraph.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 15th April 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st May 1991 a provision in the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 relating to costs against legal representatives in criminal proceedings.
