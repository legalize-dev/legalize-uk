# The Carlisle Hospitals, the North Lakeland Healthcare and the West Cumbria Health Care National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001

Made: 1st March 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Carlisle Hospitals, the North Lakeland Healthcare and the West Cumbria Health Care National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts

###### 2

- (1) The Carlisle Hospitals National Health Service Trust established by the Carlisle Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

- (2) The North Lakeland Healthcare National Health Service Trust established by the North Lakeland Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00006] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

- (3) The West Cumbria Health Care National Health Service Trust established by the West Cumbria Health Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00007] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 1st March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 2001,

- the Carlisle Hospitals National Health Service Trust;

- the North Lakeland Healthcare National Health Service Trust; and

- the West Cumbria Health Care National Health Service Trust.

It should be read in conjunction with:

- the North Cumbria Acute Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00008]; and

- the North Cumbria Priority Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00009],

which establish new NHS trusts.
