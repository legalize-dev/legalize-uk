# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Amendment) Order 2012

Made: 30th May 2012

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

A draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Amendment) Order 2012.

- (2) This Order shall come into force one month after the day on which it is made.

#### Amendment of section 1(1) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001

###### 2

In the Table in section 1(1) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00002], before the entry relating to section 55 of the British Transport Commission Act 1949[^f00003] insert—

| Section 2(1) of the Parks Regulation (Amendment) Act 1926[^f00004] so far as it creates an offence against the Parks Regulation Act 1872[^f00005] relating to any of the following provisions of the Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces Regulations 1997 ([S.I. 1997/1639](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1639))— | Failing to comply with, or contravening, those Regulations by— |
| --- | --- |
|  |  |
| (a) regulation 3(3) | dropping or leaving litter or refuse except in a receptacle provided for the purpose |
|  |  |
| (b) regulation 3(4) | using a pedal cycle, a roller blade etc except on a Park road or in a designated area |
|  |  |
| (c) regulation 3(6) | failing to remove immediately any faeces deposited by an animal of which that person is in charge |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Crispin Blunt — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 30th May 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Table in section 1(1) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act [2001 (c.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16) to add further offences in respect of which a penalty notice may be given. The offences are littering, dog fouling or cycling in the Royal Parks.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the private or voluntary sectors.
