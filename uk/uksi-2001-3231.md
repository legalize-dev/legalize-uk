# The Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Restoration of Devolved Government) (No.2) Order 2001

Made: 22nd September 2001

Laid before Parliament: 26th September 2001

Coming into force: 23rd September 2001

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(2) of the Northern Ireland Act 2000, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Restoration of Devolved Government) (No.2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 23rd September 2001.

###### 2

Section 1 of the Northern Ireland Act 2000 shall cease to have effect on 23rd September 2001.

###### 3

The Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Suspension of Devolved Government) (No.2) Order 2001[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

John Reid — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 22nd September 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that section 1 of the Northern Ireland Act 2000 is to cease to have effect on 23rd September 2001. Section 1, which came into force again on 22nd September 2001 by virtue of the Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Suspension of Devolved Government) (No.2) Order 2001 (S.I. [2001/3230](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3230)), provides for the suspension of devolved government in Northern Ireland. The effect of the Order is to restore devolved government in Northern Ireland on 23rd September 2001.
