# The Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1991

Made: 28th May 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 164(3) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Commencement No. 8) Order 1991.

#### Provisions coming into force on 31st May 1991

###### 2

The following provisions of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 shall come into force on 31st May 1991—

- sections 29 to 32;

- section 50;

- section 51;

- section 60 in so far as it relates to anything deposited at a place for the deposit of waste, or in a receptacle for waste, provided by a waste disposal contractor under arrangements made with a waste disposal authority;

- section 68;

- section 71;

- section 72;

- section 75;

- section 77;

- section 147;

- section 162(1) in so far as it relates to paragraph 31(1) to (3), (4)(a) and (c) and (5)(b) and (c) of Schedule 15;

- section 162(2) in so far as it relates to the repeal in Part II of Schedule 16 of section 2 of the Control of Pollution Act 1974[^f00002] and the repeals made in that Part in relation to sections 7(2) and 9(1) of the Control of Pollution (Amendment) Act 1989[^f00003].

## Signed

Michael Heseltine — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 28th May 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force on 31st May 1991 provisions of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 relating to—

- (a) the separation of waste regulation from waste disposal functions where the same authority has both functions;

- (b) waste disposal plans of waste regulation authorities;

- (c) functions of waste disposal authorities, including transitional arrangements;

- (d) supervision and enforcement;

- (e) registers relating to deposits in the sea etc;

- (f) certain amendments of the Control of Pollution (Amendment) Act 1989 and related repeals.
