# The Water Appointment (Monopolies and Mergers Commission) Regulations 1989

Made: 7th July 1989

Laid before Parliament: 7th July 1989

Coming into force: 1st September 1989

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 14(7) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Water Appointment (Monopolies and Mergers Commission) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

###### 2

These Regulations have effect in relation to any reference made to the Monopolies and Mergers Commission for the determination of any question or other matter in pursuance of provisions contained in an instrument of appointment of a water or sewerage undertaker made under Chapter I of Part II of the Water Act 1989.

###### 3

The provisions of the Water Act 1989 listed in column 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations shall apply to a reference described in regulation 2 above as they apply to references under section 16 of that Act (modification references to Monopolies Commission), subject to the modifications (if any) set out in column 2 of the Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations regulate the procedure of the Monopolies and Mergers Commission on references made to them by the Director General of Water Services under the instrument of appointment of a water undertaker or a sewerage undertaker made pursuant to the Water Act 1989.

The Regulations provide for such matters as the time limit for the Commission to report on references; the service of copies and publication of references; and the power of the Commission to require the attendance of witnesses and the production of documents.
