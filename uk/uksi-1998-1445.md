# The Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Qualifying Authorities) Order 1998

Made: 10th June 1998

Laid before Parliament: 17th June 1998

Coming into force: 8th July 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(4) of Schedule 6 to the Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Qualifying Authorities) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 8th July 1998.

###### 2

The Medway Towns District Council, being a relevant local authority which is not a qualifying authority and in respect of which a change of circumstances since the relevant date has significantly affected the way in which it carries out its functions, shall be a qualifying authority for the purposes of Schedule 6 to the Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Glenda Jackson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 10th June 1998

## Explanatory note

(This Note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the Medway Towns District Council to be a qualifying authority for the purposes of Schedule 6 to the Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996.
