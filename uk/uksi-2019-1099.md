# The Persistent Organic Pollutants (Various Amendments) Regulations 2019

Made: 12.45 p.m. on 9th July 2019

Laid before Parliament: 4.30 p.m. on 9th July 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 2

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the ECA 1972 and it appears to the Secretary of State that it is expedient for certain references to Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (recast)[^f00003] to be construed as references to that Regulation as amended from time to time.

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Persistent Organic Pollutants (Various Amendments) Regulations 2019.

#### Commencement

###### 2

- (1) Regulations 1, 2 and 5 come into force on 15th July 2019.

- (2) Regulations 3 and 4 come into force on 12th August 2019.

#### Amendment to the Environment Act 1995

###### 3

In section 41(1)(e) of the Environment Act 1995[^f00004], for the words from “Regulation” to “[Directive 79/117/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0117)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (recast)”.

#### Amendment to the Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005

###### 4

For Schedule 8 to the Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00005], substitute the new Schedule 8 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Amendments to the Persistent Organic Pollutants Regulations 2007

###### 5

In the Persistent Organic Pollutants Regulations 2007[^f00006]—

- (a) for regulation 2 substitute—

> > (2) In these Regulations, “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021” means Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (recast), as amended from time to time.

- (b) in regulation 3(1), for “Regulation [(EC) No. 850/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0850)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021”;

- (c) in regulation 4(1)—

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), for “Pollution Prevention and Control Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003” substitute “Pollution Prevention and Control (Industrial Emissions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00007]”;

- (d) in regulation 5, for the words from “Regulation” to “substances)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 (control of manufacturing, placing on the market and use, and the listing of substances)”;

- (e) in regulation 6(1), for the words from “Regulation” to “waste)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 (stockpiles)”;

- (f) in regulation 7(1), for the words from “Regulation” to “waste)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 (waste management)”;

- (g) in regulation 8(1), for “Regulation [(EC) No. 850/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0850)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021”;

- (h) in regulation 9(2)—

- (i) in the words before sub-paragraph (a), for “Regulation [(EC) No. 850/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0850)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021”;

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (a), for “Regulation [(EC) No. 850/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0850)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2019/1021”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations supplement Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (recast) (OJ No. L 169, 25.6.2019, p. 45).

Regulation (EU) 2019/1021 replaces and repeals Regulation [(EC) No 850/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0850) of the European Parliament and of the Council on persistent organic pollutants (OJ No. L 158, 30.4.2004, p. 7). Regulations 3 to 5 make various amendments to the following enactments to reflect this—

- (a) the Environment Act [1995 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25),

- (b) the Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/894](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/894)), and

- (c) the Persistent Organic Pollutants Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/3106](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3106)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
