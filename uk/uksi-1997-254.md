# The Superannuation (Admission to Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972) (No. 2) Order 1997

Made: 6th February 1997

Laid before Parliament: 6th February 1997

Coming into force: 27th February 1997

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(5) and (8)(a) of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Admission to Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972) (No. 2) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 27th February 1997.

###### 2

Employment by the Local Government Boundary Commission for Wales is hereby added to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 with effect from 1st April 1996.

## Signed

Roger Freeman — Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster for the Minister for the Civil Service — 6th February 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds employment by the Local Government Boundary Commission for Wales to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 so that section 1 of that Act (under which the Principal Civil Service Pension Scheme, the Civil Service Additional Voluntary Contribution Scheme and the Civil Service Compensation Scheme have been made) may apply to it.

Provision for the establishment of the Local Government Boundary Commission for Wales was made by section 53 of the Local Government Act [1972 (c. 70)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70) and Schedule 8 thereto.

Under the power conferred by section 1(8)(a) of the Superannuation Act 1972 the Order takes effect from 1st April 1996.
