# The Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made: 10th March 2001

Laid before Parliament: 12th March 2001

Coming into force: 14th March 2001

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 47 and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 14th March 2001.

###### 2

Regulation 28 of the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2001[^f00002] shall be amended by the substitution of the table set out below for the table set out in paragraph (1) of that regulation.

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| Description of school | Amount to be added |
| Primary school with fewer than 100.5 pupils | £7,000 |
| Primary school with 100.5 pupils or more but with fewer than 200.5 pupils | £13,500 |
| Primary school with 200.5 pupils or more but with fewer than 400.5 pupils | £24,000 |
| Primary school with 400.5 pupils or more but with fewer than 600.5 pupils | £30,000 |
| Primary school with 600.5 pupils or more | £48,000 |
| Secondary school with fewer than 600.5 pupils | £58,000 |
| Secondary school with 600.5 pupils or more but with fewer than 1,200.5 pupils | £70,000 |
| Secondary school with 1,200.5 pupils or more | £82,000 |
| Special school with fewer than 100.5 pupils | £20,000 |
| Special school with 100.5 pupils or more | £28,000 |

## Signed

Estelle Morris — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 10th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Financing of Maintained Schools (England) Regulations 2001 by substituting a new table in regulation 28(1). This table sets out the specific amount, determined by the kind and size of the school, to be added to the amount determined under Part III of those Regulations in determining the budget share of a maintained school for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2001.
