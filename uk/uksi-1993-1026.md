# The Cranfield Airport (Designation) (Detention and Sale of Aircraft) Order 1993

Made: 5th April 1993

Laid before Parliament: 19th April 1993

Coming into force: 10th May 1993

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 88(10) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cranfield Airport (Designation) (Detention and Sale of Aircraft) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 10th May 1993.

###### 2

Cranfield Airport is hereby designated for the purposes of section 88 of the Civil Aviation Act 1982.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Caithness — Minister of State, — Department of Transport — 5th April 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 88 of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 empowers the person owning or managing an aerodrome to which the section applies to detain and sell aircraft where default is made in the payment of airport charges. Section 88(10), as amended by the Airports Act 1986, provides that the section applies to (amongst others) any aerodrome designated for the purposes of the section by an order made by the Secretary of State. This Order designates Cranfield Airport as an aerodrome to which section 88 applies.
