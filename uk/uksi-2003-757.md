# The Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2003

Made: 12th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 157(5) of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2003, and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

#### Extension of byelaws

###### 2

The Anglian Water Services Ltd Water Parks Byelaws 1993, made on the 20th April 1993, shall continue to have effect until 20th April 2004.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the Secretary of State

John Roberts — A Senior Civil Servant, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 12th March 2003
