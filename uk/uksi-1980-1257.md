# The Health Services Act 1980 (Commencement) Order 1980

Made: 19th August 1980

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 26(2) of the Health Services Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf I hereby make the following Order:-

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health Services Act 1980 (Commencement) Order 1980.

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 14 of the Health Services Act 1980 shall be 8th September 1980.

## Signed

W.S.I. Whitelaw — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — 19th August 1980

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 14 of the Health Services Act 1980 which amends section 14 of the Health Services Act 1976 (c.83) by imposing a requirement to give notice of notifiable changes (as defined in that section) in hospital premises and makes certain consequential amendments.
