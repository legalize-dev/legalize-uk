# The Dual-Use Items (Export Control) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made: 14th January 2002

Laid before Parliament: 15th January 2002

Coming into force: 5th February 2002

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the control of the export of goods and measures relating to trade in dual use items, including the transmission of software and technology in intangible form, in the exercise of the powers conferred on her by that section, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as The Dual-Use Items (Export Control) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 5th February 2002.

- (2) In this Regulation the “principal Regulations” are the Dual-Use Items (Export Control) Regulations 2000 .

###### 2

Regulation 2(1) of the principal Regulations providing for the definition of “the Regulation” is amended by deleting “as amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2889/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2889) of 22 December 2000 and Council Regulation [(EC) No. 458/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0458) of 6 March 2001 ” and substituting the following:

> > as amended by the Council Regulations listed in Schedule 1A

###### 3

Insert after Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations a new Schedule 1A as follows:

> > **SCHEDULE 1A**
>   Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2889/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2889) of 22 December 2000 .
>   Council Regulation [(EC) No. 458/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0458) of 6 March 2001 .
>   Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2432/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2432) of 20 November 2001 .

###### 4

Schedule 2 of the principal Regulations is amended by deleting entries IC950 and IE950.

## Signed

Nigel Griffiths — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Competition and Consumer Affairs, — Department of Trade and Industry — 14th January 2002

## Explanatory note

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

aExtending the definition of “the Regulation” to include Council Regulation [(EC) 2432/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2432) of 20 November 2001 (O.J. No. L 338, of 20/12/01 p.1);

bCreating a new Schedule 1A to list amendments to Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1334/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1334); and

cRemoving the general prohibition on exportation to any destination except Member States of Materials and Technology covered by 1C950 and 1E950 respectively. These items are now included in Annex 1 of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1334/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1334).
