# The Funding Agency for Schools Dissolution Order 1999

Made: 6th October 1999

Laid before Parliament: 8th October 1999

Coming into force: 1st November 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 132(1) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Funding Agency for Schools Dissolution Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st November 1999.

###### 2

The Secretary of State hereby specifies 1st November 1999 as the date on which the Funding Agency for Schools shall be dissolved.

## Signed

Jacqui Smith — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 6th October 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies 1st November 1999 as the date on which the Funding Agency for Schools is to be dissolved under section 132 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998.
