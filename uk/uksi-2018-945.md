# The Children and Social Work Act 2017 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2018

Made: 21st August 2018

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 70(2) of the Children and Social Work Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Children and Social Work Act 2017 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2018.

###### 2

“The Act” means the Children and Social Work Act 2017.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st September 2018

###### 3

To the extent that they are not already in force, the following provisions of the Act come into force on 1st September 2018—

- (a) section 36 (Social Work England); and

- (b) Schedule 3 (Social Work England).

## Signed

Nadhim Zahawi — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Education — 21st August 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are the fifth commencement regulations made under the [Children and Social Work Act 2017 (c.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/16) (“the Act”). Section 10, Schedule 1 and Part 3 of the Act came into force when the Act was passed.

Regulation 3 fully commences section 36 (3) and brings into force the remaining provisions of Schedule 3 to the Act which relate to Social Work England (“the regulator”). The provisions relate to: the power to delegate functions; the membership of committees; and the requirement to maintain records and accounts and to produce annual accounts to the Secretary of State and the Comptroller and Auditor General.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
