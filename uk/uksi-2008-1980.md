# The Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 23rd July 2008

Laid before Parliament: 28th July 2008

Coming into force: 8th September 2008

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 .

The Secretary of State for Transport has been designated for the purposes of section 2(2) in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme and in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of agricultural or forestry tractors and of their components, and in particular any type approval scheme for such tractors .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 8th September 2008.

#### Amendment of the Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) Regulations 2005

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 2 of the Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) Regulations 2005 is amended as follows.

- (2) After the definition of “certificate of conformity” insert—

> > “*codified*” in relation to a directive means codified in accordance with the procedure provided for in the interinstitutional agreement of 20 December 1994 on accelerated working method for the official codification of legislative texts made between the Commission of the European Communities, the European Parliament and the Council of the European Union ;

- (3) For the definition of “separate directive” substitute—

> > “*separate directive*” means—
> 1. a directive listed in Annex II, Chapter B of the Tractor Type Approval Directive; or
> 2. where such a directive has been codified, the codified directive,
> as amended by such Community instruments as are in force on the day the Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 are made;.

- (4) In the definition of “the Tractor Type Approval Directive” for “as read with Council Directive [2004/66/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0066) and as amended by Commission Directives [2005/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0013) and [2005/67/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0067)” substitute “as amended by such Community instruments as are in force on the day the Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 are made”.
