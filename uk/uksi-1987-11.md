# The Housing Revenue Account Rate Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1987

Made: 7th January 1987

Laid before Parliament: 20th January 1987

Coming into force: 11th February 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 23A of the Housing (Financial Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account Rate Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 11th February 1987.

#### Rate fund contribution limit

###### 2

The amount of contribution out of its general fund which each of the local authorities listed in Column 1 of the Schedule hereto may estimate that it will carry to the credit of its housing revenue account for the year 1987-88 shall not exceed the amount set out in relation to that authority in Column 2 thereof.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)
