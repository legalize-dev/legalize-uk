# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) (No. 2) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: At 10.00 a.m. on 18th August 2020

Laid before Parliament: At 2.00 p.m. on 18th August 2020

Coming into force: 19th August 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) (No. 2) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 19th August 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) (No. 2) Regulations 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 4(2)(b), omit “or outdoor”.

- (3) In the Schedule—

- (a) omit paragraphs 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10;

- (b) for paragraph 12, substitute—

> > (12) Indoor swimming pools, including indoor facilities at water parks.

## Signed

Matt Hancock — Secretary of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — at 10.00 a.m. on 18th August 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/824](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/824)) to permit the following to reopen from 19th August 2020 in the area of Leicester City Council: outdoor swimming pools and outdoor facilities at water parks, nail bars and salons, tanning booths and salons, spas and beauty salons, massage parlours, tattoo parlours, and body and skin piercing services.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it is of limited duration.
