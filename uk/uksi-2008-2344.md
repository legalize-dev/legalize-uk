# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Buckinghamshire) (District of Wycombe) Designation Order 2008

Made: 1st September 2008

Laid before Parliament: 4th September 2008

Coming into force: 1st October 2008

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Transport[^f00002] makes this Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Buckinghamshire) (District of Wycombe) Designation Order 2008 and shall come into force on 1st October 2008.

#### Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area

###### 2

The Secretary of State designates the area described in the Schedule as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and

- (b) a special enforcement area.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Buckinghamshire) (High Wycombe Town Centre) Order 1997[^f00003] and the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Buckinghamshire) (High Wycombe Town Centre) (Amendment) Order 1999[^f00004] are revoked.

## SCHEDULE — Area designated as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area

This Order applies to the area of the District of Wycombe except—

- (a) the section of the M40, including the on and off slip roads, that lies within the area;

- (b) the section of the A404 south of the Handy Cross roundabout, including the on and off slip roads, that lies within the area; and

- (c) the Handy Cross roundabout.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Rosie Winterton — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 1st September 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the area described in the Schedule as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004. The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Buckinghamshire) (High Wycombe Town Centre) Order 1997 and the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Buckinghamshire) (High Wycombe Town Centre) (Amendment) Order 1999 are revoked.

No Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no significant effect on the cost to business and the voluntary sector.
