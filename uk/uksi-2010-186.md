# The Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provision) (Amendment) Order 2010

Made: 29th January 2010

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by section 182(5) of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^f00001] makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provision) (Amendment) Order 2010.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provision) (Amendment) Order 2010[^f00002] in paragraph 27(a) omit the reference to the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00003].

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 29th January 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional Provision) Order 2010 to omit from the provisions specified as coming into force by virtue of that Order the reference in Part 2 of Schedule 23 to the 2009 Act to the repeal of section 7 of the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) [1966 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/apni/1966/20).
