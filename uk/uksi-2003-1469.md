# The Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2003

Made: 4th June 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 170(4) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2003.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The provisions of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on the date specified in column 2 of that Schedule, but where a particular purpose is specified in relation to any such provision in column 3 of that Schedule, the provision shall come into force on that date only for that purpose.

## SCHEDULE

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Section 26 (charges: immigration control) | 5th June 2003 | For the purpose of enabling the Secretary of State to exercise the power to make subordinate legislation |
| Section 26 (charges: immigration control) (so far as not already in force) | 30th June 2003 |  |
| Section 169(1) and (3) (minor and consequential amendments and repeals) | 30th June 2003 | For the purposes of the provisions of Schedules 14 and 16 commenced by this Order |
| Paragraph 83 of Schedule 14 (consequential amendments) | 30th June 2003 | For the purposes of the commencement by this Order of paragraph 86 of Schedule 14 |
| Paragraph 86 of Schedule 14 (consequential amendments) | 30th June 2003 |  |
| In Schedule 16 (repeals), the entry relating to section 9 of the Immigration Act 1988[^f00002] | 30th June 2003 |  |

## Signed

Beverley Hughes — Minister of State — 4th June 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 5th June 2003 section 26 (charges: immigration control) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 for the purpose of enabling the Secretary of State to exercise the power to make subordinate legislation and brings that section fully into force on 30th June 2003. The Order also commences the repeal of section 9 (charges) of the Immigration Act 1988 from 30th June 2003.
