# The Scottish Parliament (Disqualification) Order 2003

Made: 27th February 2003

Coming into force: 28th February 2003

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of February 2003 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 15 of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Parliament (Disqualification) Order 2003 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Specified Office-Holders

###### 2

An office-holder of a description specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order is disqualified from being a member of the Scottish Parliament.

###### 3

An office-holder of a description specified in column 1 of Part II of the Schedule to this Order is disqualified from being a member of the Scottish Parliament for any constituency specified in relation to that office-holder in column 2 of that Part of that Schedule and for any region specified in relation to that office-holder in column 3 of that Part of that Schedule.

#### Revocation of the Scottish Parliament (Disqualification) Order 1999

###### 4

The Scottish Parliament (Disqualification) Order 1999[^f00002] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the office holders who are disqualified from being a member of the Scottish Parliament. Part I of the Schedule specifies the office holders who are disqualified from being a member of the Parliament and Part II of the Schedule specifies the office holders who are disqualified from being a member of the Parliament but only for particular constituencies or regions.
