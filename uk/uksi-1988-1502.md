# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1988

Made: 25th August 1988

Laid before Parliament: 6th September 1988

Coming into force: 3rd October 1988

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907 [^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926 [^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1988 and shall come into force on 3rd October 1988.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of fees

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1978 as amended by the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1986 by increasing the fees payable to Sheriff Officers by about 12%.
