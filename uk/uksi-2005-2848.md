# The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2005

Made: 12th October 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 60 and 61 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004[^f00001] makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2005.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The following provisions of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 shall come into force on 18th October 2005—

- (a) sections 32 to 34 (the victims' code);

- (b) section 47 and Schedule 7 (investigations by Parliamentary Commissioner); and

- (c) section 54(1), (2)(a) and (b), (3)(a) and (b) and (4) to (8) (disclosure of information).

## Signed

Fiona Mactaggart — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 12th October 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 set out in article 2 on 18th October 2005.
