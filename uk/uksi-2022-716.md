# The Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2022

Made: 28th June 2022

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 95(1) of the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2022.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

Section 11(2), (3) and (5) of the Criminal Justice and Courts Act 2015 (initial release and release after recall: life sentences) comes into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

## Signed

Dominic Raab — Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 28th June 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the eighth commencement order made under the Criminal Justice and Courts Act [2015 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/2).

Article 2 brings into force section 11(2), (3) and (5) of that Act. Subsections (2) and (5) amend section 32 of the Crime (Sentences) Act [1997 (c.43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/43) (recall of life prisoners while on licence) to set out the current test applied by the Parole Board for release after recall of indeterminate prisoners, whether recalled before or after the provision comes into force. Subsection (3) amends section 128 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act [2012 (c.10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/10) (power to change test for release on licence of certain prisoners) to provide that the Secretary of State may by order amend that test for indeterminate sentence prisoners serving a sentence for public protection (IPP’s). This would be in line with the remit of that section.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
