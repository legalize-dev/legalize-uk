# The Finance (No. 2) Act 2005, Sections 48(1) to (4), (Appointed Day) Order 2009

Made: 30th July 2009

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 48(5) of the Finance (No.2) Act 2005[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance (No. 2) Act 2005, type=start slip=2010-12-01 time=1291633894434Sections 48(1) to (4)type=end slip=2010-12-01 time=1291633894434, (Appointed Day) Order 2009.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of subsections (1) to (4) of section 48 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2005 is 30th July 2009.

## Signed

Alistair Darling — Dave Watts — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 30th July 2009
