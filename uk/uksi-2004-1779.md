# The Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000 (Amendment No.2) Regulations 2004

Made: 12th July 2004

Laid before Parliament: 12th July 2004

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 2

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000 (Amendment No.2) Regulations 2004.

#### Commencement

###### 2

- (1) Except as provided for in paragraph (2), these Regulations come into force on 13th July 2004.

- (2) Regulation 4 comes into force on 21st July 2004.

#### Revocation

###### 3

Regulation 3(3) of the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000 (Amendment) Regulations 2004[^f00003] is revoked.

#### Amendment of the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000

###### 4

After article 1(3) of the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000[^f00004], insert—

> > (3A) In paragraph (3)—
>   (a) “person within the United Kingdom” includes—
>     (i) any person who is carrying on any business in the United Kingdom; and
>     (ii) any person who is, whether on board an aircraft or otherwise, in the airspace above any part of the United Kingdom; and
>   (b) “ship” has the same meaning as in section 313 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00005].

## Signed

Joan Ryan — Nick Ainger — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 12th July 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, made in exercise of the power in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, revoke regulation 3(3) of the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000 (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and insert into the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000 a provision with the same effect as that which was to have been inserted by regulation 3(3).

Regulation 3(3) is revoked and replaced because the provision it inserted into the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2000 was incorrectly numbered.
