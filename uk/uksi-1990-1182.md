# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable and Satellite Television Holdings Ltd) (West Glamorgan) Order 1990

Made: 4th June 1990

Laid before Parliament: 7th June 1990

Coming into force: 5th July 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable and Satellite Television Holdings Ltd) (West Glamorgan) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 5th July 1990.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Eric Forth — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 4th June 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Cable and Satellite Television Holdings Ltd on 18th May 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the City of Swansea and part of its outlying District, the District of Neath and most parts of the Districts of Lliw Valley and Port Talbot. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 7th June 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Cable and Satellite Television Holdings Ltd will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its West Glamorgan public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Atlantic House, Holborn Viaduct, London EC1N 2HQ.
