# The Injuries in War (Shore Employments) Compensation (Amendment) Scheme 1992

Made: 9th March 1993

The Defence Council, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Injuries in War Compensation Act 1914 (Session 2)[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], hereby make the following Scheme:—

###### 1

The Injuries in War (Shore Employments) Compensation Scheme 1914[^f00003] shall be amended as follows:

###### 2

This Scheme shall be deemed to have come into effect on 6th April 1992, but no payment shall be made thereunder in respect of any period before that date.

###### 3

This Scheme may be cited as the Injuries in War (Shore Employments) Compensation (Amendment) Scheme 1992, and the Injuries in War (Shore Employments) Compensation Schemes 1914 to 1991 and this Scheme may be cited together as the injuries in War (Shore Employments) Compensation Schemes 1914 to 1992.

## Signed

Archie Hamilton — Christopher France — Members of the Defence Council

We consent,

Timothy Wood — Timothy Kirkhope — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 9th March 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Injuries in War (Shore Employments) Compensation Schemes 1914 to 1991 provide for the payment of weekly allowance to small numbers of ex-members of the Women’s Auxiliary Forces who suffered disablement from their services overseas during the 1914-18 war. This amending Scheme provides that the maximum weekly allowance payable shall be increased from £84.90 to £89.00 and that other allowances shall be increased proportionately. The increase is deemed to have come into effect on 6th April 1992.
