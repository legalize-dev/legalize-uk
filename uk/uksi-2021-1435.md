# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Entry to Venues and Events) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: 14th December 2021

Laid: at 11.15 a.m. on 15th December 2021

Coming into force: at 6.00 a.m. on 15th December 2021

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Entry to Venues and Events) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into force at 6.00 a.m. on 15th December 2021.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, and apply in relation to England.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Entry to Venues and Events) (England) Regulations 2021

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Entry to Venues and Events) (England) Regulations 2021 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 4(8)(b)—

- (a) in paragraph (i), for “(5) and (6)” substitute “(4) and (5)”;

- (b) in paragraph (ii), for “(5), (6) and (7)” substitute “(4), (5) and (6)”.

- (3) In regulation 6(4), for “29th December” substitute “31st December”.

- (4) In regulation 18, in the opening words, after “14(1)” insert “, 17(3)(b)”.

## Signed

Maria Caulfield — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Health and Social Care — 14th December 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations correct errors in the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Entry to Venues and Events) (England) Regulations 2021.

No impact assessment has been provided for these Regulations.
