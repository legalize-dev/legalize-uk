# The Renters’ Rights Act 2025 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026

Made: 24th November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 145(1) and (7) of the Renters’ Rights Act 2025[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Renters’ Rights Act 2025 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “*the 2025 Act*” means the Renters’ Rights Act 2025;

- “*residential landlord*” and “residential tenancy” have the meanings given in section 63(1) of the 2025 Act.

- (3) A reference to a named region is to the region of that name specified in the first column of the table in Schedule 1, and has the extent specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that table.

#### Provisions coming into force by region

###### 2

- (1) Section 75(1)(a) and (b) of the 2025 Act (the database) comes into force—

- (a) for the purposes of entries in respect of persons who are residential landlords in relation to dwellings which are located in a region specified in the first column of the table in Schedule 2, on the date specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that table;

- (b) for the purposes of entries in respect of dwellings which are located in a region specified in the first column of the table in Schedule 2, on the date specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that table.

- (2) Section 82(3) of the 2025 Act (restriction on letting dwellings) comes into force for the purposes of persons who are residential landlords in relation to dwellings which are located in a region specified in the first column of the table in Schedule 3, on the date specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that table.

- (3) Section 85(1)(b) of the 2025 Act (other duties) comes into force for the purposes of local housing authorities[^f00002] located in a region specified in the first column of the table in Schedule 3, on the date specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that table.

- (4) Section 86(2) of the 2025 Act (access to the database) comes into force for the purposes of relevant authorities located in a region specified in the first column of the table in Schedule 3, on the date specified in the corresponding entry in the second column of that table.

- (5) In this regulation—

- “*dwelling*” means a dwelling that is let under a residential tenancy;

- “*relevant authority*” means an authority listed in paragraphs (a) to (e) of section 86(2) of the 2025 Act.

#### Provisions coming into force on 15th December 2026

###### 3

The following provisions of the 2025 Act come into force on 15th December 2026—

- (a) section 63 (meaning of “*residential landlord*”), in so far as not already in force;

- (b) section 75(2) and (3) (the database);

- (c) section 76 (the database operator), in so far as not already in force;

- (d) section 77 (making entries in the database), in so far as not already in force;

- (e) section 81 (fees for landlord and dwelling entries), in so far as not already in force;

- (f) section 82(5) (validity of residential tenancy);

- (g) section 84 (allocation of unique identifiers);

- (h) section 85(1)(a) and (d), (2) to (4) (other duties of database operator);

- (i) section 86(3) (access to the database);

- (j) section 87 (disclosure by database operator etc), in so far as not already in force;

- (k) section 88 (use of information from the database);

- (l) section 89 (removal of entries from database);

- (m) section 91 (financial penalties), in so far as not already in force;

- (n) section 92 (offences);

- (o) section 93 (power to direct database operator and local housing authorities);

- (p) section 96 (interpretation);

- (q) the following provisions of section 98 (rent repayment order for offences under the Housing Act 1988 and sections 67 and 92 of the 2025 Act)—

- (i) subsection 2(d), in so far as it inserts rows 9 and 10;

- (ii) subsection (5)(a)(i), in so far as not already in force;

- (iii) subsection (5)(a)(ii), in so far as it inserts a reference to row 10;

- (iv) subsection (6)(a)(i), in so far as not already in force;

- (v) subsection (6)(a)(ii), in so far as it inserts a reference to row 10.

#### Provisions coming into force on 15th September 2027

###### 4

Section 75(1)(a) and (b) of the 2025 Act (the database) comes into force for the purposes of entries in respect of persons who intend to become residential landlords, and entries in respect of dwellings intended to be let under residential tenancies, on 15th September 2027.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations are the fourth commencement regulations made under the Renters’ Rights Act [2025 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/26) (“*the 2025 Act*”) and bring into force provisions governing the Private Rented Sector Database contained in Chapters 3 and 4 of Part 2 of that Act.

Regulation 2 brings into force the duty in section 75(1)(a) and (b) of the 2025 Act on the database operator to establish and operate a database containing entries in respect of persons who are residential landlords and entries in respect of dwellings which are let under a residential tenancy. This requirement is brought into force on different days for dwellings located in different regions in England, and for landlords of those dwellings, as specified in the table in Schedule 2.

Regulation 2 also brings into force the following provisions of the 2025 Act on different days in relation to residential landlords of dwellings located in different regions in England, and public authorities located in different regions in England, as specified in the table in Schedule 3—

- Section 82(3) which is the duty on a person who is a residential landlord in relation to a dwelling to ensure that there is both an active landlord and active dwelling entry in the database, and to update those entries in accordance with regulations made under section 78;

- Section 85(1)(b) which is the duty on the database operator to ensure that local housing authorities are able to edit the database for the purposes of carrying out their functions under Chapter 3 of Part 2;

- Section 86(2) which is the duty on the database operator to give access to information in the database to specified public authorities.

Regulation 3 brings into force on 15th December 2026 other provisions of Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 of Part 2 of the 2025 Act which relate to the operation of the database by the database operator, the making and updating of landlord and dwelling entries and the payment of fees, active and inactive entries, the removal of entries, the use of information from the database, and financial penalties and offences.

Regulation 4 brings into force on 15th September 2027 the duty on the database operator in section 75(1)(a) and (b) of the 2025 Act to establish and operate a database containing entries in respect of persons who intend to become residential landlords, and the dwellings they intend to let under a residential tenancy.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as a full assessment was produced in relation to the 2025 Act. Copies can be obtained at https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/59-01/0127/amend/Renters_Rights_Bill-IA.pdf or from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government at 2 Marsham Street, London, SW1P 4DF.
