# The International Copper Study Group (Legal Capacities) Order 1999

Made: 21st July 1999

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of July 1999 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1 of the Act[^f00002] or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the International Copper Study Group (Legal Capacities) Order 1999.

- (2) It shall come into force on the date on which the Terms of Reference of the International Copper Study Group[^f00003] enter into force in respect of the United Kingdom. That date shall be notified in the London, Edinburgh and Belfast Gazettes.

###### 2

The International Copper Study Group is an organisation of which the United Kingdom and other sovereign powers are members.

###### 3

The International Copper Study Group shall have the legal capacities of a body corporate.

## Signed

A.K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order confers the legal capacities of a body corporate on the International Copper Study Group. This legal capacity is conferred in accordance with paragraph 14(a) of the Terms of Reference of the International Copper Study Group (Terms of Reference) (Cm.3993). The Order will enable Her Majesty’s Government to give effect to paragraph 14(a) of the Terms of Reference, and will come into force on the date on which the Terms of Reference enter into force in respect of the United Kingdom.
