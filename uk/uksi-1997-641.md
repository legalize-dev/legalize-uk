# The New Town (Irvine) Dissolution Order 1997

Made: 4th March 1997

Coming into force: 31st March 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 36G(1) of the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with Irvine Development Corporation, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the New Town (Irvine) Dissolution Order 1997 and shall come into force on 31st March 1997.

- (2) In this Order, “Irvine Development Corporation” means the Irvine Development Corporation established by the New Town (Irvine) (Development Corporation) Order 1967[^f00002].

###### 2

The Secretary of State hereby appoints 31st March 1997 as the date for the dissolution of Irvine Development Corporation.

## Signed

George Kynoch — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 4th March 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 31st March 1997 as the date for the dissolution of the Irvine Development Corporation.
