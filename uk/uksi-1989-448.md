# M606 The Bradford (Oakenshaw–Staygate) Motorway Scheme 1967 (Revocation) Scheme 1988 Confirmation Instrument 1989

Made: 6th March 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 16 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

M606 The Bradford (Oakenshaw–Staygate) Motorway Scheme 1967 (Revocation) Scheme 1988 is hereby confirmed.

###### 2

The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme are deposited at the Department of Transport and at the offices of the City of Bradford Metropolitan Council, Room 102,1st Floor, City Hall, Bradford.

###### 3

The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 4

This Instrument may be cited as M606 The Bradford (Oakenshaw–Staygate) Motorway Scheme 1967 (Revocation) Scheme 1988 Confirmation Instrument 1989.

## THE SCHEDULE — M606 THE BRADFORD (OAKENSHAW–STAYGATE) MOTORWAY SCHEME 1967 (REVOCATION) SCHEME 1988

The City of Bradford Metropolitan Council in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other enabling powers and as highway authority for that part of the special road known as the M606 Motorway (Bradford Ring Road to M62 Chain Bar Interchange Section) and connecting roads which lie within their area hereby make this Scheme:—

###### 1

the Bradford (Oakenshaw–Staygate) Motorways Scheme 1967 is hereby revoked in respect of that part of the special road known as the M606 (Bradford Ring Road to the M62 Chain Bar Interchange Section) and connecting roads which lie in their area.

###### 2

this Scheme may be cited as M606 The Bradford (Oakenshaw–Staygate) Motorway Scheme 1967 (Revocation) Scheme 1988.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

J. F. Ballard — Regional Director Yorkshire and Humberside — Department of Transport — 6th March 1989
