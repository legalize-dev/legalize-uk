# The Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2013

Made: 19th July 2013

Laid before the House of Commons: 22nd July 2013

Coming into force: 1st October 2013

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 12(4) of the Finance Act 1997[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 1st October 2013.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations “quarter” means the first three months of an accounting period.

#### Application and revocation

###### 3

- (1) These Regulations apply in the case of payments on account of gaming duty for any quarter that ends on or after 31st October 2013.

- (2) The Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2011[^f00002] are revoked.

#### The amount of payments on account

###### 4

For the purpose of calculating payments on account of gaming duty in the cases to which these Regulations apply, substitute the following table for the Table in regulation 5 of the Gaming Duty Regulations 1997[^f00003].

| *Part of gross gaming yield* | *Rate* |
| --- | --- |
| The first £1,121,250 | 15 per cent |
| The next £773,000 | 20 per cent |
| The next £1,353,750 | 30 per cent |
| The next £2,857,250 | 40 per cent |
| The remainder | 50 per cent |

## Signed

Edward Troup — Ruth Owen — Two of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs — 19th July 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend regulation 5 of the Gaming Duty Regulations 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2196](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2196)) which deals with the amount of payments on account. They substitute a new Table reflecting changes to the bands of gross gaming yield for gaming duty made by section 183 of the Finance Act [2013 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/29)[^f00004], and will apply in the case of payments on account of gaming duty for any quarter that ends on or after 31st October 2013.

The parts of the gross gaming yield in the Table are half the value of the new bands of gross gaming yield shown in section 183 of the Finance Act 2013. This is because the period covered is the first three months of a six month accounting period.

A Tax Information and Impact Note has not been prepared for this instrument as it contains no substantive changes to tax policy.
