# The Licensed Victuallers' National Homes (Charter Amendment) Order 2000

Made: 17th May 2000

Coming into force: 1st June 2000

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of May 2000 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers contained in section 15(2) of the said Act, and all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased, by and with the advice of her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, that the said Supplemental Charter be amended in the manner set forth in the Schedule to this Order.

This Order may be cited as the Licensed Victuallers' National Homes (Charter Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st June 2000.

## SCHEDULE — AMENDMENT TO THE SUPPLEMENTAL CHARTER OF THE LICENSED VICTUALLERS' NATIONAL HOMES

Rule 3 of the Supplemental Charter shall be deleted and replaced by the following rule:

> > (3)
>   (a) The Society is established for charitable purposes only.
>   (b) The principal objects of the Society are to provide, construct, improve, manage and maintain Homes and any associated amenities for and to contribute towards the maintenance of distressed or aged persons in need and their partners to mitigate the ills consequent on old age affecting such persons with priority being given to persons who have been licensed victuallers and thereafter to persons who have worked in breweries, distilleries and other industries providing goods and services to the licensed trade and thereafter to distressed or aged persons in need and others as aforesaid.

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Charter of the Licensed Victuallers' National Homes (“the charity”) so as to give effect to a Scheme made by the Charity Commissioners for England and Wales extending the permitted application of the funds of the charity. The funds will now additionally benefit distressed or aged persons who have worked in breweries and distilleries and other distressed or aged persons in need, as well as (as before) distressed or aged former licensed victuallers to whom priority will be given.
