# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2007

Made: 24th October 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 178(8) and (9) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2007.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 163(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005, insofar as it relates to sections 113B(10)(a), (b), (c) and (d) of the Police Act 1997[^f00002], shall come into force on 12th November 2007 in England and Wales only.

## Signed

Meg Hillier — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 24th October 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings section 163(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 into force on 12th November 2007, to the extent indicated in article 2. The effect is to bring into force, in England and Wales, provisions inserted by that section into the Police Act 1997 which make the Royal Navy Regulating Branch, the Royal Marines Police, the Royal Military Police and the Royal Air Force Police police forces for the purpose of section 113B of the Police Act 1997. Police forces under that section may be prescribed or determined as relevant police forces for the purpose of obtaining information to be disclosed in an enhanced criminal record certificate under Part V of the 1997 Act.
