# The A1 Motorway (Walshford to Dishforth Section: Parts of A1 Northbound Carriageway) Scheme 1992

Made: 15th July 1992

Coming into force: 14th August 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by sections 16 and 17 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:—

###### 1

The Secretary of State is authorised to provide along the route described in the Schedule to this Scheme a special road for the use of traffic of Classes I and II of the classes of traffic set out in Schedule 4 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 2

The lengths of the special road are indicated by heavy black lines on the deposited plan.

###### 3

The special road shall become a trunk road on the date when this Scheme comes into force.

###### 4

In this Scheme:—

- (1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;

- (2)

- (i) “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA16/YHCPD 5 marked “the A1 Motorway (Walshford to Dishforth Section: Parts of A1 Northbound Carriageway) Scheme 1992”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY.

- (ii) “the special road” means the special road mentioned in article 1 of this Scheme.

###### 5

This Scheme shall come into force on 14th August 1992 and may be cited as the A1 Motorway (Walshford to Dishforth Section: Parts of A1 Northbound Carriageway) Scheme 1992.

## THE SCHEDULE

Lengths of the A1 trunk road northbound carriageway from a point on the A1 trunk road450 metres south of its junction with Whixley Lane (marked “A” on the map numbered 1 in the deposited plan) then going in a generally northerly direction to a point 1,850 metres north of its junction with Guy Lane (marked “B” on the map numbered 2 in the deposited plan) all in the Borough of Harrogate in the County of North Yorkshire.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

J. P. Henry — Regional Director Yorkshire and Humberside Region — Department of Transport — 15th July 1992
