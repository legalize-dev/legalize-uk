# The Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 19th May 2009

Laid before Parliament: 22nd May 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 22nd May 2009

Coming into force: 1st July 2009

The Secretary of State in relation to England and, with the agreement of the Welsh Ministers, as respects those parts of Wales within the catchment areas of the rivers Dee, Wye and Severn, and the Welsh Ministers as respects the remainder of Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 82 and 219(2) of the Water Resources Act 1991[^f00001], and the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by section 102 of that Act make the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 1 July 2009.

#### Amendment of the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) Regulations 1997

###### 2

In the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) Regulations 1997[^f00002], for “Directive [79/923/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0923)”, wherever occurring, substitute “Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113)[^f00003]”.

## Signed

Jane Davidson — Minister for Environment, Sustainability and Housing One of the Welsh Ministers — 19th May 2009

Huw Irrance-Davies — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th May 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the [Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) Regulations 1997 (S.I. 1997/1332)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1332) to reflect the codification of Council Directive [79/923/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0923) and its amendments into a single text now contained in Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113) of the European Parliament and of the Council.

No impact assessment has been produced for this instrument as no impact on business or the voluntary sector is foreseen.
