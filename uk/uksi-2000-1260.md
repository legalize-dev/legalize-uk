# The GLA Side Roads (London Borough of Bromley) Designation Order 2000

Made: 8th May 2000

Laid before Parliament: 15th May 2000

Coming into force: 19th June 2000

Now therefore the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by section 124A of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the GLA Side Roads (London Borough of Bromley) Designation Order 2000 and shall come into force on 19th June 2000.

#### Designation of GLA side roads

###### 2

- (1) The roads in the London borough of Bromley specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as GLA side roads[^f00002].

- (2) The description of a road includes the intersection of that road and any other road that crosses it at the same level.

- (3) In the Schedule “m” means metres.

- (4) In accordance with section 124A(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, 3rd July 2000 is hereby specified as the date on which the designated roads are to become GLA side roads.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 272 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 inserts a new section 124A into the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 whereby the Secretary of State may designate roads or proposed roads which are to be GLA side roads, for which Transport for London will be the traffic authority. This Order designates the roads in the London Borough of Bromley specified in the Schedule as GLA side roads with effect from 3rd July 2000.
