# The School Admissions (Admission Arrangements and Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

Made: 14th October 2025

Laid before Parliament: 16th October 2025

Coming into force: 6th November 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 88C, 88F and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the School Admissions (Admission Arrangements and Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 6th November 2025.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of Schedule 4 to the School Admissions (Admission Arrangements and Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (England) Regulations 2012

###### 2

In the table in Part 1 of Schedule 4 to the School Admissions (Admission Arrangements and Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (England) Regulations 2012[^f00002] (Rabbinic authorities and other representative bodies), before the entry relating to “Office of Rabbi of The Gateshead Jewish Community” insert—

| “Office of the Rabbis of Od Yosef Hai Synagogue, Hendon | Nancy Reuben Primary School | Barnet | NW4 1DJ”. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Georgia Gould — Minister of State — Department for Education — 14th October 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the School Admissions (Admission Arrangements and Co-ordination of Admission Arrangements) (England) Regulations 2012 ([S.I. 2012/8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/8)).

Regulation 2 amends Schedule 4 to those Regulations, to add a new entry for a school - Nancy Reuben Primary School - and a rabbinic authority for that school - Office of the Rabbis of Od Yosef Hai Synagogue, Hendon - to the table in Part 1.

An Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside these Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
