# The Interception of Communications Act 1985 (Commencement) Order 1986

Made: 26th February 1986

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 12(2) of the Interception of Communications Act 1985, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Interception of Communications Act 1985 (Commencement) Order 1986.

###### 2

The Interception of Communications Act 1985 shall come into force on 10th April 1986.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 26th February 1986

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 10 April 1986 the provisions of the Interception of Communications Act 1985.
