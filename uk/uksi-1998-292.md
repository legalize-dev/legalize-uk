# The National Heritage Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1998

Made: 16th February 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 4(2) of the National Heritage Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Heritage Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1998.

###### 2

The National Heritage Act 1997 shall come into force on 4 March 1998.

## Signed

Chris Smith — Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport — 16th February 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 4 March 1998 as the day for the coming into force of the National Heritage Act 1997.
