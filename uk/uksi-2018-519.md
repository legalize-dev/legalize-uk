# The Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2018

Made: 24th April 2018

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 24th day of April 2018Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty, on the advice of Her Privy Council and in exercise of the power conferred by section 382(2) of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2018.

###### 2

The Armed Forces Act 2006 shall, instead of expiring at the end of 11th May 2018, expire at the end of 11th May 2019.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 382 of the Armed Forces Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”) provides that the 2006 Act will expire at the end of one year beginning with the day on which the Armed Forces Act 2016 (“the 2016 Act”) was passed, unless continued in force by Order in Council in accordance with that section. The 2016 Act was passed on 12th May 2016. The Armed Forces Act (Continuation) Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/569](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/569)) provided for the continuation of the 2006 Act for a period of 12 months. This Order provides for the continuation of the 2006 Act for a further period of 12 months. It provides so that the 2006 Act expires at the end of 11th May 2019.
