# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 1998

Made: 13th July 1998

Laid before Parliament: 14th July 1998

Coming into force: 20th July 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 1 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, and having regard to the matters specified in section 2(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations–

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 20th July 1998.

###### 2

The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998[^f00002] are hereby amended by substituting the following paragraph for regulation 2(b)–

> > (b) in regulation 5(3), the following paragraph shall be inserted into the list of classes in relation to the definition of “relevant licence”–
>   (aa) On-Site Private Business Radio;

## Signed

Barbara Roche — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Small Firms, Trade and Industry, — Department of Trade and Industry — 13th July 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations substitute a new regulation 2(b) in the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998 ([S.I. 1998/1567](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1567)) for the purpose of applying the appropriate sub-division lettering to the new licence class of On-Site Private Business Radio in regulation 5(3) of the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/1331](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1331), as amended by S.I.s [1996/1464](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1464), [1997/1006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1006), [1997/1885](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1885), [1998/460](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/460) and [1998/1567](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1567)).
