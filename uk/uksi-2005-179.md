# The Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2005

Made: 1st February 2005

Coming into force: 11th February 2005

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraphs 3(1)(b) and (c) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2005 and shall come into force on 11th February 2005.

- (2) In this Order, “the Establishment Order” means the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00003].

#### Amendment of nature and functions of the trust

###### 2

- (1) In article 3(1) of the Establishment Order (nature and functions of the trust) “5(1)(a)” shall be replaced by “5(1)”.

- (2) Article 3(2) of the Establishment Order (nature and functions of the trust) shall be replaced by the following—

> > (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, for the purposes of the health service.

#### Increase in the number of non-executive directors of the trust

###### 3

In article 4(1) of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust) “5 non-executive directors” shall be replaced by “6 non-executive directors”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 1st February 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994, which established the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order removes the references to the establishments from which the trust carries out its functions. Article 3 of the Order increases the number of non-executive directors of the trust required from 5 to 6.
