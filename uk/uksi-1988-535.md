# The National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

Made: 16th March 1988

Laid before Parliament: 21st March 1988

Coming into force: 11th April 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 72 and 108(1) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 11th April 1988.

###### 2

In regulation 3 of the National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Regulations 1976([^f00002]) for the reference to

## Signed

Michael B Forsyth — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 16th March 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 3 of the National Health Service (Supply of Goods at Clinics etc.) (Scotland) Regulations 1976 by substituting for the reference to the Welfare Food Order 1975 a reference to the Welfare Food Regulations 1988.
