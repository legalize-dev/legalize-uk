# The School Curriculum Development Committee and Secondary Examinations Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1988

Made: 12th December 1988

Laid before Parliament: 13th December 1988

Coming into force: 3rd January 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 15(3) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the School Curriculum Development Committee and Secondary Examinations Council (Designation of Staff) Order 1988, and shall come into force on 3rd January 1989.

#### Designation of staff to be transferred from existing bodies

###### 2

- (1) Any employee of the School Curriculum Development Committee who is named in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order is designated as respects the Council specified in relation to that employee in column 2 of that Schedule.

- (2) Any employees of the Secondary Examinations Council who is named in column 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order is designated as respects the Council specified in relation to that employee in column 2 of that Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order concerns certain staff of the School Curriculum Development Committee and the Secondary Examinations Council. Those bodies are to be wound up in consequence of the establishment (under section 14 of the Education Reform Act 1988) of the Curriculum Council for Wales, the National Curriculum Council, and the School Examinations and Assessment Council. Under section 15 of that Act, the Secretary of State may designate (either individually or by class or description) employees of the existing bodies for automatic transfer to the new Councils. Where an individual is so designated there is no termination of, or change in the terms of, his contract of employment. This Order lists those staff so designated.
