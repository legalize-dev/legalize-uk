# The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1985

Made: 9th August 1985

Laid before Parliament: 28th August 1985

Coming into Operation: 19th September 1985

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 56(4) of the Housing Act 1980, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following order:—

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1985 and shall come into operation on 19th September 1985.

###### 2

The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 6) Order 1982 is hereby amended by the omission from the list set out in the Schedule thereto of the following body:

> > (1) Asda Property Holdings Limited.

###### 3

The Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 7) Order 1983 is hereby amended by the omission from the list set out in the Schedule thereto of the following body:

> > (5) Fairview Estates p.l.c.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

George Young — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of the Environment — 7th August 1985

Nicholas Edwards — Secretary of State for Wales — 9th August 1985

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 56 of the Housing Act 1980 deals with assured tenancies. Such tenancies are subject to Part II of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 (c. 56), as modified by Schedule 5 to the 1980 Act, if they would otherwise have been protected tenancies or housing association tenancies under the Rent Act 1977 (c. 42). They can only be granted by bodies approved under section 56 of the 1980 Act in respect of dwelling-houses erected after the passing of that Act.

This order amends the Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 6) Order 1982 and the Assured Tenancies (Approved Bodies) (No. 7) Order 1983 by omitting two bodies from the lists of approved bodies contained in the Schedules to these orders.
