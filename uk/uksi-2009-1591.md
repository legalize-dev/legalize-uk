# The Mobile Roaming (European Communities) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 29th June 2009

Laid before Parliament: 30th June 2009

Coming into force: 1st July 2009

The Secretary of State has been designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to electronic communications[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Mobile Roaming (European Communities) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 1st July 2009.

#### Amendments

###### 2

The Mobile Roaming (European Communities) Regulations 2007[^f00003] are amended as set out in the Schedule.

#### Savings

###### 3

The amendment made to regulation 5(4) of the Mobile Roaming (European Communities) Regulations 2007 shall not apply in relation to any breach of an obligation under—

- (a) the Regulation [(EC) No. 717/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0717) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27th June 2007 on roaming on public mobile telephone networks within the Community and amending Directive [2002/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0021)[^f00004]; or

- (b) regulation 13 of the Mobile Roaming (European Communities) Regulations 2007

which occurs before the coming into force of these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

###### 1

In regulation 1(2) (Citation, commencement and interpretation), for the definition of “the EU Mobile Roaming Regulation” substitute—

> > - “the EU Mobile Roaming Regulation” means the Regulation [(EC) No. 717/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0717) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27th June 2007 on roaming on public mobile telephone networks within the Community and amending Directive [2002/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0021) as amended by Regulation [(EC) No. 544/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0544) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18th June 2009 amending Regulation [(EC) No. 717/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0717) and Directive [2002/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0021)[^f00005];

###### 2

In regulation 5(4) (Amount of penalty under regulation 4) for the definition of “relevant business” substitute—

> > - “relevant business” means so much of any business carried on by the notified provider as consists in the provision of terrestrial public mobile communications services;

## Signed

Carter — Minister for Communications, Technology and Broadcasting — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 29th June 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Mobile Roaming (European Communities) Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1933](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1933)) (“the principal Regulations”).

The principal Regulations implement Articles 8 and 9 of the Regulation [(EC) No 717/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0717) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27th June 2007 on roaming on public mobile telephone networks within the Community and amending Directive [2002/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0021) (“the EU Mobile Roaming Regulation”) (OJ No. L171, 29.6.2007, p32).

The EU Mobile Roaming Regulation was amended by the Regulation [(EC) No. 544/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0544) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18th June 2009 amending Regulation [(EC) No. 717/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0717) and Directive [2002/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0021) (OJ No. L167, 29.06.2009, p12).

Paragraph 1 of the schedule to these Regulations amends the definition of the EU Mobile Roaming Regulation in the principal Regulations so that it now refers to the EU Mobile Roaming Regulation as amended.

Paragraph 2 of the schedule to these Regulations amends the definition of “relevant business” for the purpose of calculating the amount of penalty under regulation 4 of the principal Regulations.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
