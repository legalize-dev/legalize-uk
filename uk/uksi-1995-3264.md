# The Local Government Changes (Rent Act Registration Areas) Order 1995

Made: 14th December 1995

Laid before Parliament: 21st December 1995

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 17 and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001] and of all powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Changes (Rent Act Registration Areas) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Amendment of registration areas

###### 2

For the purposes of Part IV of the Rent Act 1977 (registration of rents under regulated tenancies)[^f00002] there shall be—

- (a) the Avon registration area comprising the counties[^f00003] of—

- (i) Bath and North East Somerset;

- (ii) the City of Bristol;

- (iii) North West Somerset; and

- (iv) South Gloucestershire;

- (b) the Cleveland registration area comprising the counties[^f00004] of—

- (i) Hartlepool;

- (ii) Middlesbrough;

- (iii) Redcar and Cleveland; and

- (iv) Stockton-on-Tees;

- (c) the Humberside registration area comprising the counties[^f00005] of—

- (i) the City of Kingston upon Hull;

- (ii) the East Riding of Yorkshire;

- (iii) North East Lincolnshire; and

- (iv) North Lincolnshire; and

- (d) the North Yorkshire registration area comprising the counties[^f00006] of North Yorkshire and York.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

David Curry — Minister of State, — Department of the Environment — 14th December 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Registration areas are the organisational units for the rent officer service and rent assessment committees. Section 62 of the Rent Act 1977 provides that registration areas for the purposes of Part IV (Registration of Rent Under Regulated Tenancies) of that Act outside London are counties. Article 2 of this Order redefines the registration areas named in that article, as a consequence of the abolition of the counties in Avon, Cleveland, Humberside and the reorganisation of North Yorkshire under the provisions of the Local Government Act 1992, and includes the county of York within the registration area of North Yorkshire.
