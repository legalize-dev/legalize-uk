# The Air Navigation (Single European Sky) (Penalties) (Amendment) Order 2018

Made: 12th December 2018

Laid before Parliament: 19th December 2018

Coming into force: 10th January 2019

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of December 2018

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and section 60(1) and (2)(b) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 , is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Single European Sky) (Penalties) (Amendment) Order 2018 and comes into force on 10th January 2019.

#### Amendments to the Air Navigation (Single European Sky) (Penalties) Order 2009

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Single European Sky) (Penalties) Order 2009 is amended as follows.

#### Interpretation

###### 3

In article 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “the common charging scheme regulation”, for “Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1794/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1794)” substitute “ Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 391/2013 ” ; and

- (b) in the definition of “notified”, for “article 155 of the Air Navigation Order 2005” substitute “ paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Air Navigation Order 2016 ” .

#### Compliance and monitoring

###### 4

In articles 8(1) (compliance and monitoring) and 9(1)(e) (offences in relation to documents, records and explanations), for “17” substitute “ 20 ”.

#### Penalties

###### 5

In article 11(5) (penalties) for “5.1, 6.1, 6.2, 7, 8.1 or 11” substitute “ 6.1, 7.1, 7.2, 8, 9.1 or 12 ”.

#### Review

###### 6

In article 14(2) (review) for “Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1794/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1794) of December 2006” substitute “ Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 391/2013 of 3 May 2013 ”.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
