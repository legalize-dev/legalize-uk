# The Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) (Temporary Continuation of 2022 Order and Adjustments) Order 2026

Made: 25th February 2026

Laid before the House of Commons: 26th February 2026

Coming into force: 23rd March 2026

The Treasury make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(2) and 2(2) and (3) of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) Act 1979[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and effect

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) (Temporary Continuation of 2022 Order and Adjustments) Order 2026 and comes into force on 23rd March 2026.

- (2) [Article 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/3/enacted) has effect in relation to products charged with duty under the Oil Act in the period beginning with 23rd March 2026 and ending with 31st August 2026.

- (3) [Articles 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/4/enacted) to [7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/7/enacted) have effect in relation to products charged with duty under the Oil Act in the period beginning with 1st September 2026 and ending with 30th November 2026.

- (4) [Articles 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/8/enacted) to [11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/11/enacted) have effect in relation to products charged with duty under the Oil Act in the period beginning with 1st December 2026 and ending with 28th February 2027.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- (a) “*the Oil Act*” means the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979[^f00002];

- (b) expressions used in this Order and in section 27(1) (interpretation) of the Oil Act[^f00003] have the same meaning as in the Oil Act;

- (c) “*aqua methanol additive or extender duty*” means the duty charged by section 6AG(1) of the Oil Act (excise duty on aqua methanol) where the chargeable use is within subsection (2)(b) of that section[^f00004];

- (d) “*fuel oil*” has the same meaning as in section 11(2) of the Oil Act (rebate on heavy oil)[^f00005];

- (e) “*fuel substitutes duty*” means the duty charged by section 6A(1) of the Oil Act (fuel substitutes)[^f00006];

- (f) “*hydrocarbon oil duty*” means the duty charged by section 6(1) of the Oil Act (excise duty on hydrocarbon oil)[^f00007];

- (g) “*natural road fuel gas*” has the same meaning as in section 5(2) of the Oil Act (road fuel gas)[^f00008].

#### Continuation of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) Order 2022

###### 3

The Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) Order 2022[^f00009] continues in force until the end of 31st August 2026, instead of expiring in accordance with section 2(2) of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) Act 1979, article 2 of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) Order 2022 (Continuation) Order 2023[^f00010], article 2 of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) Order 2022 (Continuation) Order 2024[^f00011], or article 2 of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) Order 2022 (Continuation) Order 2025[^f00012].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order, which comes into force on 23rd March 2026, makes temporary adjustments to the liabilities to excise duty (and, where applicable, the rights to rebate in respect of such duty) in respect of liquid fuels that are chargeable by virtue of the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act [1979 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/5) (“*the Oil Act*”). The adjustments made by this Order are all in the form of a deduction from the amount payable (or an addition to the amount of rebate allowable) of a specified percentage not exceeding 10 per cent.

[Article 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/3/enacted) provides for the continuation of the Excise (Surcharges or Rebates) (Hydrocarbon Oils etc.) Order 2022 ([S.I. 2022/365](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/365)) (“*the 2022 Order*”) until the end of 31st August 2026. The 2022 Order provides for adjustments of liabilities to excise duty and rights of rebate in respect of excise duty in accordance with sections 1(2) and 2(3) of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) Act 1979. Section 2(2) of that Act (which applies in respect of orders under section 1 - see section 2(1)) - provides that an order shall cease to be in force at the expiration of a period of one year from the date on which it takes effect, unless continued in force by a further order. The 2022 Order was continued in force by [S.I. 2023/329](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/329), [S.I. 2024/300](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/300) and [S.I. 2025/228](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/228).

[Articles 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/4/enacted), [6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/6/enacted) and [7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/7/enacted) adjust liabilities to duty, and [article 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/5/enacted) provides adjustments to rebates, in relation to products charged with duty in the period beginning with 1st September 2026 and ending with 30th November 2026.

[Articles 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/8/enacted), [10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/10/enacted) and [11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/11/enacted) adjust liabilities to duty, and [article 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/164/article/9/enacted) provides adjustments to rebates, in relation to products charged with duty in the period beginning with 1st December 2026 and ending with 28th February 2027.

For ease of reference and comprehension of effect, the figures in columns *(B)* and *(D)*of the Tables in this Order indicate the amounts of excise duty payable both before and after the adjustment is made.

A Tax Information and Impact Note covering this instrument will be published on the website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.

[^f00024]: Section 1(4) of the Excise Duties (Surcharges or Rebates) Act 1979 provides: “The adjustment under this section of a right to any drawback, rebate or allowance in respect of a duty or goods charged with a duty shall be made where the right arises while the order is in force with respect to the duty (whenever the duty became due); but in calculating the amount to be adjusted any adjustment under this section of the liability to the duty shall be disregarded”. In the Oil Act, the rebates are set by reference to the underlying rate of duty that applies to the product, which are also set in the Oil Act. The adjusted rebate (as provided by [articles 5](#article-5) and [9](#article-9) of this instrument) is also calculated by reference to the underlying rate of duty set by the Oil Act that applies to the product, and not by reference to that rate as adjusted by [articles 4](#article-4) and [8](#article-8) of this instrument. The percentage addition to the right to rebate is calculated accordingly.
