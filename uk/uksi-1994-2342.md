# The Nottinghamshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994

Made: 7th September 1994

Laid before Parliament: 14th September 1994

Coming into force: 5th October 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Nottingham Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Nottinghamshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Nottinghamshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 5th October 1994.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Nottingham Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the Nottinghamshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 9th March 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 5th October 1994 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 7th September 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Nottinghamshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust on 5th October 1994 trust property formerly held by the Nottingham Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the National Health Service Executive, National Health Service Trust Unit, Room 1/W/29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
