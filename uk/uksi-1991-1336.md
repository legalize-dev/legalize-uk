# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Telford Telecommunications Limited) Order 1991

Made: 7th June 1991

Laid before Parliament: 11th June 1991

Coming into force: 9th July 1991

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Telford Telecommunications Limited) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 9th July 1991.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 7th June 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Telford Telecommunications Limited on 12th April 1991 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in most of the District of the Wrekin including the towns of Telford and Newport and an adjoining part of the District of Bridgnorth. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 11th June 1991. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunica- tion systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Telford Telecommuni- cations Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 9th July 1991.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
