# The Education (Further Education Corporations) (No. 3) Order 1992

Made: 1st December 1992

Laid before Parliament: 2nd December 1992

Coming into force: 29th December 1992

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16(2), 17 and 89(4) of that Act, the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Further Education Corporations) (No. 3) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 29th December 1992 (“the Commencement Date”).

###### 2

On the Commencement Date there shall be established for the purpose of conducting from the operative date each of the educational institutions specified in Parts I and II of the Schedule to this Order a body corporate called by the same name as the institution it is to conduct.

###### 3

On 1st March 1993 there shall be established a body corporate called “The Ridge College, Stockport” for the purpose of conducting from the operative date the existing educational institution of that name.

###### 4

The “operative date”, in relation to the further education corporations established by this Order and the institutions which they are to conduct, shall be—

- (a) in the case of the educational institutions specified in Parts I and II of the Schedule to this Order, 1st April 1993; or

- (b) in the case of The Ridge College, Stockport, 1st September 1993.

## THE SCHEDULE

## PART I

- Herefordshire College of Art and Design

- Kensington and Chelsea College

- Truro College

## PART II

- Havering Sixth Form College

- Newham Sixth Form College

- Oldham Sixth Form College

## PART III

- The Ridge College, Stockport

## Signed

John Patten — Secretary of State for Education — 1st December 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes further education corporations under section 16(2) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting certain specified educational institutions. In the case of The Ridge College, Stockport it appoints 1st September 1993 as the “operative date” for the purposes of Part I of that Act; and in the case of the other institutions for which further education corporations are established it appoints 1st April 1993.
