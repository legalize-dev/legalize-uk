# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (Amendment) 1992

Made: 5th March 1992

Laid before Parliament: 16th March 1992

Coming into force: 6th April 1992

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (Amendment) 1992 and shall come into force on 6th April 1992.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of fees

###### 2

- (1) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990[^f00003], shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraphs.

- (2) In Items 3(c)(ii) (poindings) and 4(a)(ii) (poindings of motor vehicles, heavy plant or machinery), for the figures “75.00” wherever they occur substitute “75.05”.

- (3) In Item 6(c) (Sales, officer attending warrant sale conducted by auctioneer)—

- (a) in both summary cause and ordinary cause Band 2, for the figures “20.80” substitute “35.20”;

- (b) in both summary cause and ordinary cause Band 3, for the figures “20.80” substitute “44.50”.

- (4) In Item 6(d)(i) (officer and witness attending warrant sale and being unable to execute same for whatever reason) in ordinary cause Bands 1, 2 and 3, for the figures “26.35” substitute “39.50”.

- (5) In Items 7(a) (ejections, arranging ejection) and 8(a) (arranging possession), in ord inary cause Bands 1, 2 and 3, for the figures “39.10” substitute “39.50”.

- (6) The fees as amended by this paragraph shall apply to work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

## Signed

J.A.D. Hope — Lord President, I.P.D. — 5th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends certain fees in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 1990 ([S.I. 1990/381](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/381)).
