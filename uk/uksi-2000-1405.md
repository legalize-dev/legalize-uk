# The A41 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (No. 2) Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 2000

Made: 22nd May 2000

Coming into force: 30th May 2000

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A41 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (No. 2) Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 2000, and shall come into force on 30th May 2000.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A41 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (No. 2) Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In schedule 2B item 1 is substituted as follows:—

| 1. | The A41 Hendon Way (northbound carriageway), from a point 24.7 metres east of the eastern kerb-line of Lyndale eastward for a distance of 43 metres. | At any time | None | None |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

###### 4

In schedule 2C a new item is added as follows:—

| 1. | The A41 Hendon Way (northbound carriageway), from a point 67.7 metres east of the eastern kerb-line of Lyndale eastward for a distance of 12 metres. | At any time |
| --- | --- | --- |

###### 5

In schedule 4 item 3 is substituted as follows:—

| 3. | The A41 Hendon Way (northbound carriageway), from a point 17.3 metres west of the easternmost wall of Wendover Court, Hendon Way to a point 12 metres east thereof. |
| --- | --- |

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 22nd May 2000
