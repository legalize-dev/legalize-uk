# The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997

Made: 4th June 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 15(1) of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997.

###### 2

Sections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 to 12 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 16th June 1997.

## Signed

Alun Michael — Minister of State — 4th June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 16th June 1997 sections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 to 12 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997. Sections 13 to 16 of that Act came into force on 21st March 1997.
