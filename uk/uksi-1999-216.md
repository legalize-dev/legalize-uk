# The Civil Courts (Amendment) Order 1999

Made: 2nd February 1999

Laid before Parliament: 2nd February 1999

Coming into force: 1st March 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(1) of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Courts (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st March 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “the 1983 Order” means the Civil Courts Order 1983[^f00002], and a Schedule referred to by number alone means the Schedule so numbered in that Order.

#### Amendments to the 1983 Order

###### 3

In Schedule 1, “Corby” shall be deleted from its position in the second column opposite the entry for “Northampton” in the first column.

###### 4

In Schedule 3, the entry relating to Corby[^f00003] shall be deleted.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, C. — Dated 2nd February 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Courts Order 1983 so as to close Corby County Court on 1st March 1999.
