# The Meat Preparations (Amendment and Transitory Modification) (England) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: at 1.00 p.m. on 22nd March 2021

Laid before Parliament: at 4.30 p.m. on 22nd March 2021

Coming into force: 30th March 2021

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 11A(1) of Schedule 2 to the Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulation 2011[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Meat Preparations (Amendment and Transitory Modification) (England) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2021;

- (b) come into force on 30th March 2021; and

- (c) apply in relation to England only.

#### Amendment to the Meat Preparations (Amendment and Transitory Modification) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Meat Preparations (Amendment and Transitory Modification) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3(1), for “31st March 2021” substitute “30th September 2021”.

## Signed

Gardiner of Kimble — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — At 1.00 p.m. 22nd March 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations apply in relation to England only and are made in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 11A(1) of Schedule 2 to the Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1197](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1197)).

Regulation 2 amends the date in regulation 3(1) of the Meat Preparations (Amendment and Transitory Modification) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1666](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1666)) in order to extend the effect of those Regulations, which, in relation to imports originating from or produced in, establishments situated in EEA member States, the Faroe Islands, Greenland or Switzerland, temporarily suspend the requirement for meat preparations imported into England to be deep frozen. That requirement is imposed by Article 3(3) of Commission [Decision 2000/572/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0572) laying down the animal and public health and veterinary certification conditions for imports of meat preparations into the Community from third countries (EUDN 2000/572). The extension of the suspension is until midnight on 30th September 2021.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact, or no significant impact, on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
