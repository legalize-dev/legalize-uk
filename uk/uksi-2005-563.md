# The Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2005

Made: 6th March 2005

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 2005

Coming into force: 30th March 2005

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 97(5) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 30th March 2005.

###### 2

In article 2 of the Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) Order 2004[^f00002]for the words “31st March 2005” there shall be substituted the words “31st March 2006”.

## Signed

On the authority of the Lord Chancellor

Christopher Leslie — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Department of Constitutional Affairs — 6th March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) Order 2004 (S.I. [2004/2198](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2198)), by extending the period of the pilot schemes provided for in that Order from 31st March 2005 until 31st March 2006.
