# The Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution: Substances Other than Oil) (Intervention) Order 1997

Made: 29th July 1997

Laid before Parliament: 6th August 1997

Coming into force: 1st September 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 138A(1)(a) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Prevention of Pollution: Substances Other than Oil) (Intervention) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st September 1997.

###### 2

The substances prescribed for the purposes of section 138A of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 (Application of sections 137 and 138 to substances other than oil) are those listed in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes substances other than oil, the threat of pollution from which (following a shipping casualty) triggers the Secretary of State’s powers of intervention under section 137 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 in accordance with section 138A of that Act.

The Order gives effect to the Protocol relating to Intervention on the High Seas in Cases of Marine Pollution by Substances other than Oil 1973 (Cmnd. 6038) (“the 1973 Protocol”).

The substances are listed in the Schedule. They are the substances enumerated in the Annex to the 1973 Protocol, as amended in 1997.
