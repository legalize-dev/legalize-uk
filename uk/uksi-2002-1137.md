# The Education (Bursaries for School Teacher Training) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2002

Made: 19th April 2002

Laid before Parliament: 19th April 2002

Coming into force: 13th May 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 50 and 63(3) of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Bursaries for School Teacher Training) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 13th May 2002.

- (2) These Regulations shall apply in relation to England.

###### 2

The Education (Bursaries for School Teacher Training) (England) Regulations 2002[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

###### 3

- (1) In regulation 2(1) delete the definition of “fast track teacher”.

- (2) In regulation 4(1) insert a comma after “head teacher” and delete the words “or fast track teacher, who has satisfactorily completed his induction period before the relevant date and”.

- (3) Delete regulation 4(3).

## Signed

Stephen Timms — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 19th April 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Bursaries for School Teacher Training) (England) Regulations 2002 in two ways. The exclusion of fast track teachers from eligibility for bursaries and the requirement that to be eligible a teacher must have successfully completed an induction period are removed from those Regulations.
