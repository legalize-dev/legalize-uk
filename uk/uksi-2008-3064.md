# The Christmas Bonus (Relevant Week) Order 2008

Made: 26th November 2008

Laid before Parliament: 26th November 2008

Coming into force: 27th November 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 150(4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Christmas Bonus (Relevant Week) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 27th November 2008.

#### Relevant week for the year 2008

###### 2

For the purposes of subsection (4) of section 150 (interpretation of Part 10) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, the relevant week in relation to the year 2008 is the week beginning Monday, 22nd December.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

Rosie Winterton — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 26th November 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides that the relevant week for the purposes of Part 10 (Christmas bonus for pensioners) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) is the week beginning Monday, 22nd December for the year 2008.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
