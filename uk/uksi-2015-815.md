# The Environmental Protection (Anglers’ Lead Weights) (England) Regulations 2015

Made: 19th March 2015

Laid before Parliament: 23rd March 2015

Coming into force: 1st July 2015

The Secretary of State is designated in relation to the environment[^f00003] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004]. The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and section 140(1)(c), (2), (3)(d) and (9) of the 1990 Act.

#### Citation, extent, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Protection (Anglers’ Lead Weights) (England) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales but apply in England only.

- (3) They come into force on 1st July 2015.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations—

- “lead” includes any alloy or compound of lead;

- “lead weight” means split shot or any other thing suitable for weighing fishing lines and which is neither incorporated and fully enclosed in the core of a fishing line nor incorporated in the construction of the swim-feeder, a self-cocking float or a fishing fly; and

- “supply” does not include supply for or by way of export.

#### Prohibition of supply of lead in the form of weights

###### 3

- (1) Subject to regulation 4, the supply of lead in the form of a lead weight for the purpose of weighting fishing lines is prohibited.

- (2) Unless the contrary is shown, a person supplying split shot is presumed to supply it for the purpose of weighting fishing lines.

- (3) A person who contravenes paragraph (1) commits an offence.

#### Exceptions

###### 4

Regulation 3(1) does not prohibit the supply of a lead weight of 0.06 grams or less, or a lead weight of more than 28.35 grams.

#### Penalty

###### 5

- (1) Paragraph (2) applies if section 85(2) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^f00005] is in force on the day on which these Regulations are made.

- (2) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 3 is liable on summary conviction to a fine.

- (3) Paragraph (4) applies if section 85(2) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 is not in force on the day on which these Regulations are made.

- (4) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 3 is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

#### Revocations

###### 6

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (1) the Control of Pollution (Anglers’ Lead Weights) Regulations 1986 [^f00006]; and

- (2) the Control of Pollution (Anglers’ Lead Weights) (Amendment) Regulations 1993[^f00007].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations consolidate provisions previously contained in the Control of Pollution (Anglers’ Lead Weights) Regulations 1986 ([S.I. 1986/1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1992)) and the Control of Pollution (Anglers’ Lead Weights) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/49](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/49)), both of which are revoked by regulation 6. The Regulations apply in England only.

Subject to certain exceptions, regulation 3 prohibits the supply of lead in the form of lead weights (as defined by regulation 2) for the purpose of weighting fishing lines, and creates a rebuttable presumption that a person supplying split shot does so for the purpose of weighting fishing lines. The offence is punishable on summary conviction with a fine (regulation 5).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen. These Regulations were notified in draft under [Directive 98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) (as amended).
