# The Blackburn with Darwen (Maintained Nursery School Governance) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made: 4th March 2007

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 2007

Coming into force: 30th March 2007

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 2(1) and 3(1)[^f00003] of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the authority, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Blackburn with Darwen (Maintained Nursery School Governance) (Amendment) Order 2007 and comes into force on 30th March 2007.

#### Amendment

###### 2

Article 3 of the Blackburn with Darwen (Maintained Nursery School Governance) Order 2004 is amended as follows:

- (a) for Article 3(b) substitute:

> > (b) sections 7(b) and 13(2) of the Education Act 2005 as if after the words “in the case of a maintained school” the words “other than a maintained nursery school” were added.

- (b) after Article 3(d) insert a new sub-paragraph:

> > (dd) On and after the date section 88 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006 comes into force, section 88(5) of that Act (*responsibility of governing body for discipline*) as if paragraph (c) of that subsection were omitted.

- (c) omit Article 3(l) and (m).

###### 3

In Article 5 of the Blackburn with Darwen (Maintained Nursery School Governance) Order 2004, for “30th March 2007” substitute “30th March 2009”.

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 2007-02-04

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under sections 2 and 3 of the Education Act 2002, to amend the Blackburn with Darwen (Maintained Nursery School Governance) Order 2004. Article 2 extends the period for which the Council of Borough of Blackburn with Darwen is exempt from the requirement in Part 3 of that Act to constitute a governing body for seven of its nine maintained nursery schools, for a further two years.
