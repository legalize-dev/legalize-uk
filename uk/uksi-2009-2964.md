# The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Exemption) (Aviation Security) Regulations 2006 (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 4th November 2009

Laid before Parliament: 9th November 2009

Coming into force: 1st December 2009

In accordance with section 24(4)[^f00002] of that Act he has consulted the Security Industry Authority and the Scottish Ministers.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Exemption) (Aviation Security) Regulations 2006 (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 1st December 2009.

#### Amendments to the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Exemption) (Aviation Security) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Exemption) (Aviation Security) Regulations 2006[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the heading to regulation 1 for “Citation, commencement and extent” substitute “Citation and commencement”.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 4th November 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Exemption) (Aviation Security) Regulations 2006 so as to extend those Regulations to Northern Ireland.
