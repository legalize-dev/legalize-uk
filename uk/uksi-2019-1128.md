# The Plant Health (Amendment) (England) Order 2019

Made: at 4.57 p.m. on 15th July 2019

Laid before Parliament: at 6.00 p.m. on 15th July 2019

Coming into force: at 7.00 p.m. on 15th July 2019

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2 and 3(1) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002], and the Forestry Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by those sections, make this Order.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Amendment) (England) Order 2019.

- (2) It comes into force at 7.00 p.m. on 15th July 2019.

- (3) It extends to England and Wales and applies in relation to England only.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Forestry) Order 2005

###### 2

In the table in Part C of Schedule 4 to the Plant Health (Forestry) Order 2005[^f00003], in item 7E, in the entry in the third column, omit paragraph (c) (but not the “or” following it).

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (England) Order 2015

###### 3

In the table in Part C of Schedule 4 to the Plant Health (England) Order 2015[^f00004], in item 7, in the entry in the third column, omit paragraph (c) (but not the “or” following it).

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (England) (Amendment) Order 2019

###### 4

In article 7(c) of the Plant Health (England) (Amendment) Order 2019[^f00005], omit paragraph (iv).

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Forestry) (Amendment) (England) Order 2019

###### 5

In article 8(c) of the Plant Health (Forestry) (Amendment) (England) Order 2019[^f00006], omit paragraph (ii).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Plant Health (Forestry) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2517](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2517)) and the Plant Health (England) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/610](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/610)) in order to prevent:

- (a) the introduction of *Thaumetopoea processionea* L. (the Oak Processionary Moth) into the area in England which is recognised as a protected zone for this harmful plant pest (“the protected area”); and

- (b) the spread of this harmful plant pest within the protected area.

Articles 2 and 3 amend the technical requirements that must be satisfied when bringing in certain plants of *Quercus* L., intended for planting, into the protected area or when moving those plants within the protected area.

Articles 4 and 5 make consequential amendments to the Plant Health (England) (Amendment) Order 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1070](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1070)) and the Plant Health (Forestry) (Amendment) (England) Order 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1075](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1075)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
