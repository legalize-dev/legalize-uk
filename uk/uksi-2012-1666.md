# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No.2) Order 2012

Made: 27th June 2012

Laid before Parliament: 28th June 2012

Coming into force: 20th July 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001]. In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act he has consulted with such persons and bodies as appear to him to be requisite.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No.2) Order 2012 and comes into force on 20th July 2012.

#### Designation

###### 2

The sports grounds specified in the Schedule to this Order, each of which being a sports ground—

- (a) at which association football matches are played;

- (b) which is occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited; and

- (c) which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators[^f00002],

are designated as sports grounds requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

## SCHEDULE

| Sports Ground | Occupier of Sports Ground |
| --- | --- |
| Highbury Stadium Park Avenue Fleetwood Lancashire FY7 6TX | Fleetwood Wanderers Limited (trading as Fleetwood Town Football Club) |
| New York Stadium Don Street Rotherham S60 1AH | Rotherham United Football Club (RUFC) Limited |

## Signed

Hugh Robertson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 27th June 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates Highbury Stadium, occupied by Fleetwood Wanderers Limited (trading as Fleetwood Town Football Club), and New York Stadium, occupied by Rotherham United Football Club (RUFC) Limited, as sports grounds requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument.
