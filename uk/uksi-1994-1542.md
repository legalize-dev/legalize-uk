# The Combined Probation Areas (Gloucestershire) Order 1994

Made: 8th June 1994

Coming into force: 1st July 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 2 of the Probation Service Act[^f00001]), and after the consultation required by that section, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Gloucestershire) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

###### 2

That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Gloucestershire Probation Area[^f00003] shall be amended by–

- (a) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Cheltenham, North Cotswold and Tewkesbury;

- (b) inserting in alphabetical order in column 1 “North Gloucestershire”, and corresponding to this in column 3 “8”.

###### 3

The eight members of the Gloucestershire Probation Committee appointed before 1st July 1994 by the justices for the petty sessions areas of Cheltenham, North Cotswold and Tewkesbury shall continue in office as if they had been appointed by the justices for the new petty sessions area of North Gloucestershire[^f00004].

## Signed

Michael Howard — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 8th June 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the amalgamation of the petty sessions areas of Cheltenham, North Cotswold and Tewkesbury into the new petty sessions area of North Gloucestershire effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Gloucestershire) Order 1994 (S.I. [1994/719](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/719)) which comes into force on 1st July 1994.
