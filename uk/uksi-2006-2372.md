# The Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2006

Made: 24th August 2006

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(1) of the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2006.

- (2) This Order extends to England, Wales and Northern Ireland only.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

26th September 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 in so far as that Act is not already in force.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Caroline Flint — Minister of State for Public Health — Department of Health — 24th August 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 (“the Act”) in so far as it is not already in force. Section 2 of the Act was not previously in force in relation to website advertising. The Tobacco Advertising and Promotion Act 2002 etc. (Amendment) Regulations 2006[^f00002] also insert new provisions into the Act.

This Order does not apply to Scotland.
