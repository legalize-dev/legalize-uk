# The Special Hospitals Service Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 1990

Made: 19th September 1990

Laid before Parliament: 26th September 1990

Coming into force: 17th October 1990

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by section 11 of and paragraph 9(7)(b) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Special Hospitals Service Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Amendment Order 1990 and shall come into force on 17th October 1990.

#### Amendment of Order

###### 2

- (1) The Special Hospitals Service Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1989[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this article.

- (2) After article 4 there shall be added the following article—

> > (5) The Authority is specified for the purposes of paragraph 9(7)(b) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 1977.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Virginia Bottomley — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 19thSeptember 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Special Hospitals Service Authority (Establishment and Constitution) Order 1989 by specifying the Authority for the purposes of paragraph 9(7)(b) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 1977. The amendment enables the Secretary of State to pay remuneration to members of the Authority.
