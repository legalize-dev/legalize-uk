# The Poultry Breeding Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1993

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury in accordance with the said section 84(1), hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Poultry Breeding Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 30th August 1993.

#### Fees

###### 2

There shall be paid to the Minister in respect of each transaction described in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order the fee specified opposite in column 2:

- (a) in the case of the transactions in paragraph 3, by the applicant when applying for authorisation, and

- (b) in any other case, by the person in whose name the holding or premises are registered under the appropriate Order, such fee being payable within 14 days of demand being made by the Minister.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1992[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE — FEES

###### 1

Supervision by an officer of the Minister of taking samples under Schedule 2 of the Poultry Breeding Flocks and Hatcheries Order 1993[^f00003].

###### 2

- (a) Examination by an officer of the Minister of samples of composite faeces taken under paragraph 3 of Part I of Schedule 2 to that Order;

###### 3

- (a) Inspection prior to the authorisation of a laboratory for the purposes of the Poultry Breeding Flocks and Hatcheries Order 1993 or the Processed Animal Protein Order 1989[^f00004];

###### 4

Inspection of a laboratory with a view to deciding whether the annual authorisation should be renewed or revoked.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order replaces the Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1992. It provides for fees to recover the full cost of the transactions specified in the Order in respect of breeding flocks and hatcheries and for the purpose of authorising laboratories under the Poultry Breeding Flocks and Hatcheries Order 1993 and the Processed Animal Protein Order 1989. The number of fees payable has been reduced.
