# The Swindon (Electoral Changes) Order 2015

Made: 3rd February 2015

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Local Government Boundary Commission for England has decided to give effect to the recommendations and, in exercise of the power conferred by section 92(3) of the Act, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Swindon (Electoral Changes) Order 2015.

- (2) This Order comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on the day after it is made;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2015.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 2014 Order” means the Swindon Borough (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2014[^f00003];

- “ordinary day of election of councillors” has the meaning given by section 37 of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00004];

- “district ward” means a district ward established by article 3 of the Swindon (Electoral Changes) Order 2012[^f00005].

#### Related alterations of district ward boundaries

###### 3

- (1) The area that ceases to be part of the parish of Blunsdon St Andrew and becomes part of the parish of Stratton St Margaret in consequence of article 10(1)(i) of the 2014 Order ceases to be part of Blunsdon and Highworth district ward and becomes part of Penhill and Upper Stratton district ward.

- (2) The formerly unparished area that becomes part of the parish of Haydon Wick in consequence of article 10(3)(ii) of the 2014 Order ceases to be part of Penhill and Upper Stratton district ward and becomes part of Haydon Wick district ward.

## Signed

Sealed with the seal of the Local Government Boundary Commission for England on the 3rd day of February 2015

Max Caller — Chairman — Local Government Boundary Commission for England — 3rd February 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to recommendations made by Swindon Borough Council to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England for related alterations of district ward boundaries. These alterations are related to parish boundary changes made by the Swindon Borough (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2014.

The new district ward boundaries apply in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2015.

Article 3 alters the district wards of (i) Blunsdon and Highworth, (ii) Penhill and Upper Stratton and (iii) Haydon Wick.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
