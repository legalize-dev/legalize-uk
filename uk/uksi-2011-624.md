# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2011

Made: 28th February 2011

Coming into force: 31st March 2011

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 31st March 2011.

#### Originating capital of NHS trusts

###### 2

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Regulations determines the amount of the originating capital, in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of National Health Service trusts established under that Act between 1st April 2010 and 1st November 2010.
