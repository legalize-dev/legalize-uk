# The Asylum Support (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations 2009

Made: 11th March 2009

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 2009

Coming into force: 5th April 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 166(3) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(a) of Schedule 8 to, the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Asylum Support (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 5th April 2009.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Asylum Support (Amendment) Regulations 2009[^f00002] are revoked.

## Signed

Phil Woolas — Minister of State — 11th March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Regulation 2 revokes the Asylum Support (Amendment) Regulations 2009. This revocation takes effect from the 5th April 2009.
