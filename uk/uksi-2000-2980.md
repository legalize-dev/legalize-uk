# The Submarine Pipelines (Designated Owners) (Revocation) Order 2000

Made: 2nd November 2000

Coming into force: 30th November 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(1) of the Petroleum Act 1998[^f00001] and after giving the persons designated in accordance with that section by the Orders mentioned in article 2 below an opportunity of being heard with respect to the revocations provided for by that article, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipelines (Designated Owners) (Revocation) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 30th November 2000.

###### 2

The Orders[^f00002] mentioned in the Schedule to this Order shall be revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Orders mentioned in the Schedule to the Order to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule. The provisions revoked are Orders made by the Secretary of State under section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975 designating certain companies as the owners of particular submarine pipe-lines.

The revoked provisions had become out of date in that many of the companies designated by those provisions as the owners of particular pipelines had ceased to own them.

The provisions of the 1975 Act, together with certain other legislation relating to offshore petroleum production, were consolidated by the Petroleum Act 1998.
