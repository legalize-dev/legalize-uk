# The Adoption (Amendment) Rules 1991

Made: 5th August 1991

Laid before Parliament: 2nd September 1991

Coming into force: 14th October 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 66(1) and 67(1) of the Adoption Act 1976[^f00001], hereby makes the following Rules:–

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the Adoption (Amendment) Rules 1991 and shall come into force on 14th October 1991.

- (2) In these Rules “the 1984 rules” means the Adoption Rules 1984[^f00002], and, unless the context otherwise requires, a provision or form referred to by number alone means the provision or form so numbered in those Rules.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Adoption Rules 1984 which regulate adoption matters in the High Court and the county courts.

The amendments have four purposes. First, they update references, including references to Acts which are superseded by the Adoption Act 1976.

Secondly, they make amendments consequential upon the Children Act 1989. This second category consists mainly of changes of terminology in the law relating to children affected by the 1989 Act.

Thirdly, they permit the proper officer to refer an application for an adoption order to the district judge, rather than to the judge, for directions on preliminary matters.

Fourthly, they replace the term “illegitimate child” wherever it previously appeared in these rules.
