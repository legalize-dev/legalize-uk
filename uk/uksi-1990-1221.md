# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 5) Order 1990

Made: 8th June 1990

Coming into force: 1st July 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 5) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st July 1990.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — designated owners

- Amerada Hess Ltd., whose registered office is at 2 Stephen Street, Tottenham Court Road, London W1P 1PL.

- Enterprise Oil plc., whose registered office is at 5 Strand, London WC2N 5HU.

- Mobil North Sea Limited, whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at Mobil Court, 3 Clements Inn, London WC2A 2EB.

- North Sea, Inc., whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at Rivermill House, 152 Grosvenor Road, London SW1V 3JL.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the companies listed in Part I of the Schedule as owners of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule. The pipe-lines lie between installations situated above the Ness and Beryl Oilfields, east of the Orkney Islands.
