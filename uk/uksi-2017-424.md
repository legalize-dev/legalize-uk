# The National Health Service Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) (Revocation) and the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) (Amendment) Order 2017

Made: 15th March 2017

Coming into force: 1st April 2017

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 51 and 273(1) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) (Revocation) and the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) (Amendment) Order 2017 and comes into force on 1st April 2017.

#### Revocations

###### 2

The instruments specified in the Schedule (which relate to the appointment of trustees for National Health Service trusts by the Secretary of State) are revoked.

#### Substitution of Schedule 1 to the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2007

###### 3

For Schedule 1 to the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2007[^f00002] substitute—

> > **SCHEDULE1**
>   University Hospitals Bristol NHS Foundation Trust

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of, and the Schedule to, this Order revoke six statutory instruments relating to the appointment of trustees for National Health Service trusts.

Article 3 of this Order substitutes Schedule 1 to the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1766](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1766)) to provide that the only NHS foundation trust for which the Secretary of State must appoint trustees is the University Hospitals Bristol NHS Foundation Trust.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on private or voluntary sectors.
