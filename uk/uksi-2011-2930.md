# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 7) Order 2011

Made: 8th December 2011

Laid before Parliament: 13th December 2011

Coming into force: 14th December 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 7) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 14th December 2011.

###### 2

In this Order, “the Schedule” means the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002].

###### 3

In Part 1 of the Schedule, after “Resolution 1896 (2009) of 30 November 2009 (Democratic Republic of the Congo).” insert—

> > Resolution 1952 (2010) of 29 November 2010 (Democratic Republic of the Congo).
> Resolution 2021 (2011) of 29 November 2011 (Democratic Republic of the Congo).

###### 4

- (1) Part 2 of the Schedule is amended as follows.

- (2) After “Council Decision 2011/299/CFSP of 23 May 2011 (Iran)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2011/783/CFSP of 1 December 2011 (Iran)[^f00003]”.

- (3) For “Council Decision 2011/273/CFSP of 9 May 2011 (Syria) as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2011/302/CFSP of 23 May 2011 (Syria), Council Implementing Decision 2011/367/CFSP of 23 June 2011 (Syria), Council Implementing Decision 2011/488/CFSP of 1 August 2011 (Syria) and Council Implementing Decision 2011/515/CFSP of 23 August 2011 (Syria) and as amended by Council Decision 2011/522/CFSP of 2 September 2011 (Syria).” substitute “Council Decision 2011/782/CFSP of 1 December 2011 (Syria)[^f00004].”.

## Signed

Damian Green — Minister of State — 8th December 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000.

It amends Part 1 of the Schedule to include the United Nations Resolutions set out in article 3 of this Order, which relate to the Democratic Republic of the Congo. It also amends Part 2 of the Schedule to list Council Decision 2011/783/CFSP of 1 December 2011 (Iran) and Council Decision 2011/782/CFSP of 1 December 2011 (Syria). Council Decision 2011/782/CFSP replaces Council Decision 2011/273/CFSP and related measures on Syria.

Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the Schedule (as amended) or falling within a description specified in such an instrument will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the 2000 Order).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
