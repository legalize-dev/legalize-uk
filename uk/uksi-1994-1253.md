# The Finance Act 1994, Section 47, (Appointed Day) (No. 2) Order 1994

Made: 9th May 1994

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in the exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 47 of the Finance Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1994, section 47, (Appointed Day) (No. 2) Order 1994.

###### 2

Section 47 of the Finance Act 1994 shall have effect in relation to amounts mentioned in section 21(1)(a) of the Finance Act 1988[^f00002] which become due on or after 10th May 1994.

###### 3

The Finance Act 1994, section 47, (Appointed Day) Order 1994[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

E. Woods — Commissioner of Customs and Excise — 9th May 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the amendments made to section 21 of the Finance Act 1988 (set-off of value added tax credits) by section 47 of the Finance Act 1994 shall have effect in relation to amounts which become due on or after 10th May 1994.
