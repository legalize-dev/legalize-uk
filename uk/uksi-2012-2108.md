# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made: 14th August 2012

Coming into force: 10th September 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 113B(9) and 125 of the Police Act 1997[^f00001] as extended and modified by the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Jersey) Order 2010[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 10th September 2012.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Jersey.

#### Amendment of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010

###### 2

Regulation 9 of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010[^f00003] is omitted.

## Signed

James Brokenshire — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 14th August 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke regulation 9 of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Jersey) Regulations 2010 which prescribed which police forces were “relevant” police forces for the purposes of providing information to be disclosed on an enhanced criminal record certificate under section 113B of the Police Act [1997 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/50). Under changes made by section 82(3) of the Protection of Freedoms Act [2012 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/9), police forces no longer need to be prescribed, rather the Secretary of State will determine the relevant chief officer force in relation to a certificate.
