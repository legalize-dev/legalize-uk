# The Registration of Births and Deaths (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: 8th June 2020

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Registrar General, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5 and 39 of the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953[^f00001], with the approval of the Secretary of State, makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births and Deaths (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendment of the Registration of Births and Deaths Regulations 1987

###### 2

In the Registration of Births and Deaths Regulations 1987[^f00002], in regulation 9(1) (entry of particulars on registration)[^f00003], omit “in the presence of the informant”.

## Signed

Given under my hand on

Myrtle Lloyd — Registrar General — 5th June 2020

I approve,

Kevin Foster — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Home Office — 8th June 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Registration of Births and Deaths Regulations 1987 ([S.I. 1987/2088](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2088)) (‘the 1987 Regulations’). They amend regulation 9(1) of the 1987 Regulations to allow for the entry of information in a birth registration entry without the registrar needing to be in the presence of the informant.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
