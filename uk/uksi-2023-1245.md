# The Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 9) and Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 8) (Revocation) Order 2023

Made: at 12.00 p.m. on 21st November 2023

Laid before Parliament: at 5.00 p.m. on 21st November 2023

Coming into force

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 75(3) of the Immigration Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 9) and Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 8) (Revocation) Order 2023.

- (2) Except as provided by paragraph (3), this Order comes into force on 28th November 2023.

- (3) Article 2 comes into force on 14th December 2023.

- (4) This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Provisions coming into force on 14th December 2023

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Immigration Act 2014 is 14th December 2023 so far as not already in force—

- (a) section 23(6) (power to amend the amount for the time being specified in section 23(2)); and

- (b) section 25(5) (power to amend the amount for the time being specified in section 25(4)).

#### Revocation of the Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2023

###### 3

The Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2023[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Robert Jenrick — Minister of State — Home Office — At 12.00 p.m. on 21st November 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the ninth commencement order made under the Immigration Act [2014 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/22).

Article 2 brings into force on 14th December 2024 sections 23(6) and 25(5) of the Immigration Act 2014, so far as not already in force. Those same provisions were previously brought into force in respect of premises located in the areas of five local authorities in England by [S.I. 2014/2771](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2771) and in respect of premises located in the rest of England by [S.I. 2016/11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/11).

Article 3 revokes the Immigration Act 2014 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2023 ([S.I. 2023/1215](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1215)), in consequence of a defect in that instrument.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
