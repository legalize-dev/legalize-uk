# The Combined Probation Areas (Hertfordshire) Order 1993

Made: 26th August 1993

Coming into force: 1st September 1993

In exercise of powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Hertfordshire) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st September 1993.

###### 2

That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Hertfordshire Probation Area[^f00003] shall be amended by omitting the entry in respect of the petty sessions area of South Mimms.

## Signed

Michael Howard — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 26th August 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the abolition of the petty sessions area of South Mimms effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Hertfordshire) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/2147](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2147)) which comes into force on 1st September 1993.
