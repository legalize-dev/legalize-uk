# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989 (Designated Ports) Order 1999

Made: 19th October 1999

Coming into force: 22nd November 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 16(3) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989 (Designated Ports) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 22nd November 1999.

###### 2

There shall be inserted in Part I of Schedule 6 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, at the appropriate places in alphabetical order–

- (a) under the heading “Seaports”, the words “Campbeltown”, “Southampton”, “Torquay” and “Troon”; and

- (b) under the heading “Airports”, the words “Sheffield City”.

## Signed

Jack Straw — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 19th October 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds Campbeltown, Southampton, Torquay and Troon Seaports, and Sheffield City Airport, to the list of ports designated, for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 5 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, in Part I of Schedule 6 to that Act.
