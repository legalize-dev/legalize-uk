# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (England) Order 2022

Made: 8th February 2022

Laid before Parliament: 14th February 2022

Coming into force: 7th March 2022

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that the conditions in section 1(1) of that Act are satisfied[^f00002] and has, in accordance with section 18(4) of that Act, consulted with such persons and bodies of persons as appear requisite to the Secretary of State.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (England) Order 2022 and comes into force on 7th March 2022.

#### Amendment to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015

###### 2

- (1) The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 1 (sports grounds designated under article 2(1)), after the entry for The Recreation Ground, occupied by Bath Rugby Club, insert—

| Alexander Stadium, Walsall Road, Perry Barr, B42 2LR | Birmingham City Council |
| --- | --- |

## Signed

Nigel Huddleston — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport — 8th February 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Under section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act [1975 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/52) (“*the Act*”), where the Secretary of State is of the opinion that a sports ground has accommodation for more than the specified number of spectators, the Secretary of State may by order designate that sports ground as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Act.

This Order amends the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/661)) (“*the 2015 Order*”) so as to designate Alexander Stadium under article 2(1) of the 2015 Order as a sports ground for which a safety certificate is required under the Act.

Alexander Stadium is occupied by Birmingham City Council and is a sports ground in England which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, has accommodation for more than 10,000 spectators.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
