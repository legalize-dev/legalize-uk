# The Derby College (Incorporation) Order 2001

Made: 27th November 2001

Laid before Parliament: 30th November 2001

Coming into force: 1st January 2002

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Derby College (Incorporation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st January 2002 (“the commencement date”).

###### 2

There shall be established as from the commencement date a body corporate called “Derby College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name.

###### 3

The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 1st January 2002, and the corporation shall conduct Derby College from that date.

## Signed

Margaret Hodge — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 27th November 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called Derby College under section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 1st January 2001 as the operative date for the purposes of Part I of that Act.
