# The Railways Pensions Guarantee (Prescribed Persons) Order 2007

Made: 4th June 2007

Laid before Parliament: 4th June 2007

Coming into force: 1st August 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order exercising the powers conferred by paragraph 11(5) of Schedule 11 to the Railways Act 1993[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Railways Pensions Guarantee (Prescribed Persons) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st August 2007.

#### Prescribed persons

###### 2

For the purpose of determining whether the 1994 Pensioners’ Section of the Railways Pension Scheme [^f00002] satisfies the conditions in paragraph 11(3) of Schedule 11 to the Railways Act 1993 there shall be disregarded the membership of that section of any persons admitted to membership of that section with effect from 30th December 2000 who were formerly members of the BR Section of the Railways Pension Scheme.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Tom Harris — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 4th June 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes classes of persons whose membership of the 1994 Pensioners Section of the Railways Pension Scheme is to be disregarded for the purpose of determining whether that section satisfies the conditions in paragraph 11(3) of Schedule 11 to the Railways Act 1993. The prescribed persons are those admitted to membership of that section with effect from 30th December 2000 who were formerly members of the BR section of the Railways Pension Scheme.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business, charities, voluntary bodies or the private sector.
