# The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 2001

Made: 18th July 2001

Laid before Parliament: 30th July 2001

Coming into force: 30th November 2001

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 18th day of July 2001 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 1(1) and 1(4) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in England) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 30th November 2001.

###### 2

Michael John Tomlinson is hereby re-appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in England for the period from 1st December 2001 until 30th April 2002.

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)
