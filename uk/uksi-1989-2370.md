# The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1989

Made: 13th December 1989

Laid before Parliament: 29th December 1989

Coming into force: 1st April 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 of and Schedule 3 to the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

#### Fees

###### 2

The fees payable under the enactments specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order in respect of the certificates described in column 2 of that Schedule shall (in place of the amounts previously prescribed which are specified in column 3 thereof[^f00002] be respectively the amounts specified in column 4 thereof.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1982[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order sets out, with effect from 1st April 1990, the fee payable for a certificate of birth, death or marriage issued for special purposes under certain statutory provisions. The fee is increased as at that date from £1.50 to £3.00. As a consequence, the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1982 is revoked.
