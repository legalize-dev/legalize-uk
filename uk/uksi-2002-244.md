# The Mackworth College (Dissolution) Order 2002

Made: 8th February 2002

Laid before Parliament: 8th February 2002

Coming into force: 1st March 2002

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 27 of the Act, the Secretary of State, after consulting the corporation and with the consent of the further education corporation known as Derby College, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Mackworth College (Dissolution) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st March 2002.

###### 2

On 1st March 2002 the Corporation shall be dissolved and all of its property, rights and liabilities shall be transferred to Derby College being a body corporate established for purposes which include the provision of educational facilities or services.

###### 3

Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Act shall apply to any person employed by the corporation immediately before 1st March 2002 as if the references in that section—

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;

- (b) to the operative date were to 1st March 2002;

- (c) to the transferor were to the corporation; and

- (d) to the corporation were to Derby College

## Signed

Margaret Hodge — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 8th February 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves with effect from 1st March 2002 the further education corporation established to conduct the Mackworth College. It provides for the transfer of its property, rights and liabilities to Derby College and secures the rights of its employees by applying section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
