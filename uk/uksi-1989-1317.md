# The Atomic Energy Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made: 22nd July 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 7(2) of the Atomic Energy Act 1989[^f00001], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Atomic Energy Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989.

###### 2

The Atomic Energy Act 1989 shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

## Signed

Michael Spicer — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Energy — 22nd July 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Atomic Energy Act 1989 into force on 1st September 1989.
