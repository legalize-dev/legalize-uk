# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2006

Made: 31st January 2006

Laid before Parliament: 2nd February 2006

Coming into force: 1st March 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and 18(2) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001]. In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act she has consulted with such persons as appear to her requisite.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1 March 2006.

#### Amendment to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1986

###### 2

In Schedule 1 to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 1986[^f00002], the entry in respect of Ellis Sports Ground shall be omitted.

## Signed

Tessa Jowell — Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 31st January 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order de-designates Ellis Sports Ground, in Workington, Cumbria with the effect that it will no longer require a safety certificate under section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.
