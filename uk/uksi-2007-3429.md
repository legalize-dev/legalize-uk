# The Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 1st December 2007

Laid before Parliament: 7th December 2007

Coming into force: 1st January 2008

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in that section and it appears to the Secretary of State that it is expedient for the reference to Commission Regulation [(EC) No 2707/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2707) laying down rules for applying Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255) as regards Community aid for supplying milk and certain milk products to pupils in educational establishments to be construed as a reference to that instrument as amended from time to time.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972 .

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007; and

- (b) come into force on 1st January 2008.

#### Amendment

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 2 of the Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) (England) Regulations 2001 is amended as follows.

- (2) For the definition of “the Commission Regulation”, substitute—

> > “*the Commission Regulation*” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No 2707/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2707) laying down rules for applying Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255) as regards Community aid for supplying milk and certain milk products to pupils in educational establishments, as amended from time to time;

- (3) For the definition of “the Council Regulation”, substitute—

> > “*the Council Regulation*” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255) on the common organisation of the market in milk and milk products;

## Signed

Jeff Rooker — Minister of State — 1st December 2007
