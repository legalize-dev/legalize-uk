# The Education (Designated Institutions) (England) Order 2016

Made: 11th February 2016

Laid before Parliament: 17th February 2016

Coming into force: 9th March 2016

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) (England) Order 2016 and comes into force on 9th March 2016.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to England.

#### Designation of institution

###### 2

The Anglo-European College of Chiropractic, being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within section 129(2) of the Education Reform Act 1988, is designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

## Signed

Jo Johnson — Minister of State for Universities and Science — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 11th February 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the Anglo-European College of Chiropractic as eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

An impact assessment has not been produced for the Order because it has no impact on businesses, civil society organisations or the public sector. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk .
