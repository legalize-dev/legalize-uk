# The Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) (Revocation) Order 1998

Made: 16th December 1998

Coming into force: 7th January 1999

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 16th day of 16th December 1998 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 6 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) (Revocation) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 7th January 1999.

###### 2

The Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) Order 1954[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Wireless Telegraphy (Colonial Ships and Aircraft) Order 1954 (S.I. [1954/488](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1954/488)).
