# The Lotteries (Registration Authority Fees) (Scotland) Order 1991

Made: 31st October 1991

Laid before Parliament: 11th November 1991

Coming into force: 2nd December 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 18(1) and 24(2) of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

–

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Lotteries (Registration Authority Fees) (Scotland) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

- (2) This Order shall extend to Scotland only. 2.The fee payable under paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 to the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00002] (which makes provision for the registration of a society for the purposes of section 5 of that Act and prescribes a fee payable upon such registration to a registration authority) shall, instead of £25, be £35. 3.The fee payable under paragraph 9 of Schedule 1 to the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00003] (which provides for an annual fee payable by a society registered under Part I of that Schedule to a registration authority) shall, instead of £12.50, be £17.50. 4.The Lotteries (Registration Authority Fees) (Scotland) Order 1985[^f00004] is hereby -revoked.

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 31st October 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, increases from £25 to £35 the fee payable to a registration authority on the registration of a society under Part I of Schedule 1 to the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976. It also increases the annual fee payable by a registered society from £12.50 to £17.50.
