# The Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988

Made: 10th December 1988

Laid before Parliament: 29th December 1988

Coming into force: 30th January 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 20 and 29(1) of the Building (Scotland) Act 1959 [^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 30th January 1989.

###### 2

For Schedule 2 to the Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Regulations 1981 [^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — SCHEDULE SUBSTITUTED FOR SCHEDULE 2

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 10th December 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations replace and simplify the scale of building control fees in the Building (Procedure) (Scotland) Regulations 1981. The average increase is 20%. Flat rate and minimum fees are increased to £12.
