# The Local Review Committee (Scotland) Revocation Rules 1995

Made: 9th May 1995

Laid before Parliament: 11th May 1995

Coming: 1st June 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 18(5) of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Revocation Rules 1995 and shall come into force on 1st June 1995.

###### 2

Rule 8 of the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Rules 1967[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

###### 3

The Local Review Committee (Scotland) Rules 1967 and the Rules specified in the Schedule to these Rules are revoked with effect from 1st September 1995.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules revoke the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Rules 1967. Rule 8 of those Rules is revoked on 1st June. The remainder of those Rules (including the various Rules amending those Rules) are revoked with effect from 1st September 1995.
