# The Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made: 13th July 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 6(2) of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992.

###### 2

Sections 1 to 5 of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 shall come into force on 17th July 1992.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — Her Majesty’s Principal Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs — 13th July 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 17th July 1992 Sections 1 to 5 of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991.
