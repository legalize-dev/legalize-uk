# The Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004 (Commencement No.4) Order 2005

Made: 21st February 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 48(3) of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004 [^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004 (Commencement No.4) Order 2005.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 10(3), (4) and (5) of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004 shall come into force on 31st March 2005.

## Signed

Des Browne — Minister of State — 21st February 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 10(3),(4) and (5) of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004.
