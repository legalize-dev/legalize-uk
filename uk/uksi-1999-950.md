# The Returning Officers (Parliamentary Constituencies) (England) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made: 23rd March 1999

Coming into force: 5th April 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 28(1)(b) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Returning Officers (Parliamentary Constituencies) (England) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 5th April 1999.

###### 2

Schedule 3 to the Returning Officers (Parliamentary Constituencies) (England) Order 1995[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the district named in the right-hand column of that Schedule opposite to the name of any county constituency which–

- (a) is listed in the left-hand column of that Schedule, and

- (b) is also listed in the left-hand column of the Table below,

the district named in the right-hand column of that Table opposite to the name of the county constituency in question.

| Name of county constituency | District of which registration officer is acting returning officer |
| --- | --- |
| Chatham and Aylesford | Medway |
| Hereford | Herefordshire |
| Leominster | Herefordshire |
| Medway | Medway |
| Newbury | West Berkshire |
| North Swindon | Swindon |
| South Swindon | Swindon |
| The Wrekin | Telford and Wrekin |

## Signed

George Howarth — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 23rd March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 3 to the Returning Officers (Parliamentary Constituencies) (England) Order 1995 to take account of various structural change orders under Part II of the Local Government Act [1992 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/19) and changes of names made by councils.
