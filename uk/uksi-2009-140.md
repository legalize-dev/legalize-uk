# The Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2009

Made: 29th January 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 153(7) and (8) of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2009.

#### Appointed Date

###### 2

The date appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008 is 1st February 2009 -

- (a) section 48 (1)(a) (the giving of youth conditional cautions);

- (b) section 123 (Review of anti-social behaviour orders etc.);

- (c) section 124 (Individual support orders);

- (d) section 148(2) (Consequential etc. amendments and transitional and saving provisions) in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in paragraph (f);

- (e) in Schedule 9 (Alternatives to prosecution for offenders under 18) –

- (i) paragraph 1;

- (ii) paragraph 3 but only to the extent that it inserts sections 66G and 66H of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00002];

- (iii) paragraph 4; and

- (f) paragraphs 33 and 34 of Schedule 27 (Transitory, transitional and saving provisions).

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

David Hanson — Minister of State — Ministry of Justice — 29th January 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act [2008 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/4) set out in article 2 on 1st February 2009.
