# The Plant Health (Export Certification) (England) (Amendment) Order 2015

Made: 23rd February 2015

Laid before Parliament: 26th February 2015

Coming into force: 6th April 2015

The Secretary of State[^f00001], with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(1) and 4A of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00002].

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Export Certification) (England) (Amendment) Order 2015 and comes into force on 6th April 2015.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Export Certification) (England) Order 2004

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health (Export Certification) (England) Order 2004[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) For Schedule 3[^f00004] substitute—

> > **SCHEDULE3**

| Services in respect of applications for certificates | Fee (£) | *Fee* *(small exporter)(£)* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (1) Services for consignments other than grain: | (1) Services for consignments other than grain: | (1) Services for consignments other than grain: |
| (a) inspection and, where necessary, laboratory examination | 65.24 for each quarter hour or part thereof with a minimum fee of 130.48 | 32.62 for each quarter hour or part thereof with a minimum fee of 65.24 |
| (b) laboratory examination only | 36.35 | 18.18 |
| (c) issue of a certificate where no inspection or laboratory examination is required | 13.87 | 6.94 |
| (2) Services for consignments of grain: | (2) Services for consignments of grain: | (2) Services for consignments of grain: |
| monitoring of inspections carried out by a person authorised under article 3(3) and, where necessary, laboratory examination carried out by an authorised officer | 59.53 | 29.74 |

- (3) For Schedule 4[^f00005] substitute—

> > **SCHEDULE4**

| Service | Fee (£) | Fee (small exporter) (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-export service | 49.68 for each quarter hour or part thereof with a minimum fee of 99.36 | 24.84 for each quarter hour or part thereof with a minimum fee of 49.68 |

## Signed

de Mauley — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 16th February 2015

We consent

Alun Cairns — Gavin Barwell — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 23rd February 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Plant Health (Export Certification) (England) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1404](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1404)) to provide for a 10.7% increase to the fees specified in that Order. The fee levels set out in this Order form the final part of a move towards full cost recovery of fees over three years. Further details are provided in the Explanatory Memorandum.

An impact assessment of the effects of this Order on the costs of business, in relation to the types of fee covered, is published with its Explanatory Memorandum on www.legislation.gov.uk and is available at www.gov.uk or from the Plant Health Policy and Bee Policy Unit, Defra, 10GA07, Sand Hutton, York YO14 1LZ.
