# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2000

Made: 19th December 2000

Laid before Parliament: 20th December 2000

Coming into force: 21st December 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 21st December 2000.

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — DESIGNATED INSTRUMENTS

## PART 1 — RESOLUTIONS OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL OF THE UNITED NATIONS

- Resolution 1127 (1997) of 28th August 1997 (Angola).

- Resolution 1171 (1998) of 5th June 1998 (Sierra Leone).

## PART 2 — INSTRUMENTS MADE BY THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION

- Common Position 97/759/CFSP of 30th October 1997 (Angola)[^f00003].

- Common Position 96/635/CFSP of 28th October 1996 (Burma)[^f00004].

- Common Position 2000/346/CFSP of 26th April 2000 (Burma)[^f00005].

- Common Position 98/240/CFSP of 19th March 1998 (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia)[^f00006].

- Common Position 98/725/CFSP of 14th December 1998 (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia)[^f00007].

- Common Position 99/318/CFSP of 10th May 1999 (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia)[^f00008].

- Common Position 2000/56/CFSP of 24th January 2000 (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia)[^f00009].

- Common Position 98/409/CFSP of 29th June 1998 (Sierra Leone)[^f00011].

## Signed

Barbara Roche — Minister of State — 19th December 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 by adding Common Position 2000/696/CFSP of 10th November 2000 to the list of instruments made by the Council of the European Union which are designated for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 (as inserted by section 8 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999). Any person named by or under a designated instrument, or of a description specified in it, will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000).
