# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2008

Made: 12th March 2008

Coming into force: 13th March 2008

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of March 2008Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2008 and comes into force on 13th March 2008.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE

### Persons appointed on 13th March 2008

- David Thomas Ansell

- Melanie Mary Hunt

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council
