# The Energy Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004

Made: 30th September 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 198(2) of the Energy Act 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Energy Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2004.

- (2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Energy Act 2004.

#### Commencement

###### 2

- (1) The provisions of the Act specified in Schedule 1 shall come into force on 5th October 2004.

- (2) The provisions of the Act specified in Schedule 2 shall come into force on 1st December 2004.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Energy Act 2004 as follows:

- (a) the provisions in Schedule 1 come into force on 5th October 2004, and

- (b) the provisions in Schedule 2 come into force on 1st December 2004.

The remaining provisions will be brought into force by subsequent orders.
