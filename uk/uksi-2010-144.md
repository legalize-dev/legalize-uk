# The Export Control (Iran) (Amendment) Order 2010

Made: 27th January 2010

Laid before Parliament: 28th January 2010

Coming into force: 29th January 2010

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to measures relating to the interruption or reduction, in part or completely, of economic relations with one or more countries which are not Member States.

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and by sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7 of the Export Control Act 2002 , makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export Control (Iran) (Amendment) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 29th January 2010.

###### 2

In article 1(2) of the Export Control (Iran) Order 2007 —

- (a) in the definition of “CML items”, for “(2007/197/CFSP)” substitute “ (OJ No C 65, 19.3.2009, p1) ”;

- (b) for the definition of “the Regulation” substitute—

> > “*the Regulation*” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 423/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0423) as last amended by Council Regulation (EU) No 1228/2009

## Signed

Ian Lucas — Minister for Business and Regulatory Reform — 27th January 2010
