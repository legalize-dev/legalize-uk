# The A65 Trunk Road (Gargrave Bypass) Order 1990, as varied by the A65 Trunk Road (Gargrave Bypass) Order 1990 Amendment and New Trunk Road Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2006

Made: 18th December 2006

Coming into force: 12th January 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A65 Trunk Road (Gargrave Bypass) Order 1990, as varied by the A65 Trunk Road (Gargrave Bypass) Order 1990 Amendment and New Trunk Road Order 1993 (Revocation) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 12th January 2007.

###### 2

The A65 Trunk Road (Gargrave Bypass) Order 1990, as varied by the A65 Trunk Road (Gargrave Bypass) Order 1990 Amendment and New Trunk Road Order 1993, is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Bagley — A Divisional Director Highways Agency — 18th December 2006
