# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 15th December 2008

Laid before Parliament: 19th December 2008

Coming into force: 15th January 2009

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2008;

- (b) apply in England; and

- (c) come into force on 15th January 2009.

#### Amendment to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) Regulations 2006[^f00003] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (1) In regulation 1(2) delete the definition of “bluetongue susceptible animal”.

- (2) For regulation 1(5) substitute—

> > (5) Unless specifically defined in these Regulations, any expression used in these Regulations has the meaning it bears in—
>   (a) Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425) concerning veterinary and zootechnical checks applicable in intra-Community trade in certain live animals and products with a view to the completion of the single market (OJ No L224, 18.8.90, p. 29 as last amended by Directive [2002/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0033) of the European Parliament and of the Council, OJ No. L 315, 19.11.2002, p. 14); and
>   (b) Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) laying down the principles governing the organisation of veterinary checks on animals entering the Community from third countries (OJ L 268, 24.9.1991, p. 56 as last amended by Council Directive [2006/104/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0104), OJ No. L363, 20.12.2006, p. 352 ).

- (3) After regulation 1(6) add—

> > (7) Any reference in these Regulations to an instrument of the European Community is a reference to that instrument as amended from time to time.

- (4) In regulation 35 delete “and subject to paragraph (1),”.

- (5) Schedule 1 is deleted.

- (6) For Schedules 2, 3, and 7 to those Regulations, substitute the replacements set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Amendment to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England)(Amendment) Regulations 2007

###### 3

Regulation 2(2) of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007[^f00004] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations replace Schedules 2, 3 and 7 to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) Regulations 2006 ([S. I. 2006/1471](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1471)). The new Schedules incorporate a number of new and amended pieces of Community legislation that have come into force since those Regulations came into force.

The Regulations provide that references to the Community instruments to in those Regulations are now references as amended from time to time.

They make some small corrections to the 2006 Regulations.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.
