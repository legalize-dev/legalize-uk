# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2012

Made: 13th August 2012

Laid before Parliament: 15th August 2012

Coming into force: 17th August 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No.2)Order 2012 and shall come into force on 17th August 2012.

###### 2

- (1) Part 2 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) After “Council Implementing Decision 2012/334/CFSP of 25 June 2012 (Afghanistan)” insert “and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2012/393/CFSP of 16 July 2012 (Afghanistan)[^f00003] and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2012/454/CFSP of 1 August 2012 (Afghanistan)[^f00004]”.

- (3) After “Council Decision 2012/205/CFSP of 23 April 2012 (Iran)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2012/457/CFSP of 2 August 2012 (Iran)[^f00005]”.

- (4) After “Council Decision 2011/635/CFSP of 26 September 2011 (Somalia)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2012/388/CFSP of 16 July 2012 (Somalia)[^f00006]”.

- (5) After “Council Implementing Decision 2012/335/CFSP of 25 June 2012 (Syria)” insert “and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2012/424/CFSP of 23 July 2012 (Syria)[^f00007]”.

## Signed

Damian Green — Minister of State — 13th August 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000.

It amends Part 2 of the Schedule to list Council Implementing Decision 2012/393/CFSP of 16 July 2012 (Afghanistan), Council Implementing Decision 2012/454/CFSP of 1 August 2012 (Afghanistan), Council Decision 2012/457/CFSP of 2 August 2012 (Iran), Council Decision 2012/388/CFSP of 16 July 2012 (Somalia) and Council Implementing Decision 2012/424/CFSP of 23 July 2012 (Syria).

Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the Schedule (as amended) or falling within a description specified in such an instrument will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the 2000 Order).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
