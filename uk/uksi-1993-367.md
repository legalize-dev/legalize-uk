# The Probation (Amendment) Rules 1993

Made: 23rd February 1993

Laid before Parliament: 3rd March 1993

Coming into force: 1st April 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by paragraph 18(1)(a) of Schedule 3 to the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], I hereby make the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Probation (Amendment) Rules 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

###### 2

For rule 9 of the Probation Rules 1984[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following rule—

> > (9)
>   (1) A probation committee shall appoint annually from among its members a chairman and may so appoint a deputy chairman.
>   (2) A probation liaison committee shall appoint annually from among its members, other than co-opted members, a chairman and may so appoint a deputy chairman.
>   (3) A committee referred to in paragraph 1 or 2 of this rule shall fill a vacancy in the office of chairman whenever it occurs.

###### 3

For rule 11 of the Probation Rules 1984[^f00003] there shall be substituted the following rule—

> > (11) A probation committee shall appoint such person as it thinks fit as its treasurer.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 23rd February 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Probation Rules 1984 by substituting a new rule 9 (rule 2 of these Rules) and rule 11 (rule 3 of these Rules). The change in rule 9 broadens the eligibility of members of a probation committee to be the chairman or deputy chairman of the committee to include co-opted members. Under new rule 11 a probation committee is to appoint such person as it thinks fit to be its treasurer.
