# The Foreign Fields (Specification) (Amendment) Order 1993

Made: 18th June 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 9(5) and 12(2) of the Oil Taxation Act 1983[^f00001]and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Foreign Fields (Specification) (Amendment) Order 1993.

###### 2

The Foreign Fields (Specification) Order 1991[^f00002](hereinafter referred to as “the 1991 Order”) shall have effect subject to the amendment set out in article 3 below.

###### 3

The Schedule set out in this Order shall be substituted for the Schedule to the 1991 Order.
