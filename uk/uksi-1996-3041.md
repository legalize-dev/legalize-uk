# The Criminal Appeal Act 1995 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1996

Made: 4th December 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 32 of the Criminal Appeal Act 1995[^f00001] the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Appeal Act 1995 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1996.

###### 2

For the purposes of making recommendations and appointments under section 8 of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Criminal Appeal Act 1995, that section and paragraphs 1 and 2 of that Schedule shall come into force on 12th December 1996.

## Signed

Michael Howard — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 4th December 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12th December 1996 section 8 of and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 1 to the Criminal Appeal Act 1995 for the purposes of making recommendations for the appointment of members of the Criminal Cases Review Commission and making such appointments.
