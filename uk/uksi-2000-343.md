# The Greater London Authority (Allocation of Grants for Precept Calculations) Regulations 2000

Made: 14th February 2000

Laid before Parliament: 15th February 2000

Coming into force: 16th February 2000

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 88(3)(b) and 89(5)(b) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Greater London Authority (Allocation of Grants for Precept Calculations) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 16th February 2000.

- (2) These Regulations have effect in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2000.

#### Amounts of P1 and P2

###### 2

- (1) The amounts in column 2 of the Table are specified in respect of the items (other than police grant) listed in column 1, as the amounts the aggregate of which constitutes P1 for the purposes of section 88(2) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00002].

- (2) The amounts in column 3 of the Table are specified in respect of the items listed in column 1, as the amounts the aggregate of which constitutes P2 for the purposes of section 89(4) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Sections 88 and 89 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 set out how the Greater London Authority is to calculate the amounts of its council tax for the City of London and the remainder of Greater London. Separate calculations are necessary for these two parts of Greater London because the Metropolitan Police Authority is not responsible for the provision of police services in the City of London.

The Greater London Authority will not come into existence until May 2000 and is not expected to assume its full powers and functions until 3rd July 2000. As part of the transitional arrangements the Secretary of State will issue the precepts for the amounts of council tax for the Greater London Authority.

These Regulations, which apply for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2000, set out how the Secretary of State must allocate income from redistributed non-domestic rates and the specified grants when carrying out the calculations of the amounts of council tax for the two parts of the Greater London Authority area.
