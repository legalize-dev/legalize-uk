# The Superannuation Act 2010 (Repeal of Limits on Compensation) Order 2010

Made: 16th December 2010

Laid before the House of Commons: 20th December 2010

Coming into force: 21st December 2010

The Minister for the Civil Service makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(5)(a) and (8)(b) of the Superannuation Act 2010[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation Act 2010 (Repeal of Limits on Compensation) Order 2010 and comes into force on 21st December 2010.

#### Repeal

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2) below, section 3 of the Superannuation Act 2010 is repealed.

- (2) Section 3 of the Superannuation Act 2010 shall continue to have effect in relation to any provision of a scheme made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00002] before the time at which section 3 of the 2010 Act came into force except in so far as the provision has effect by virtue of a scheme made after that time.

## Signed

Francis Maude — for the Minister for the Civil Service — 16th December 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 3 of the Superannuation Act [2010 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/37) limits the amount of compensation that can be provided, under a scheme made under section 1 of the Superannuation Act [1972 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/11), to people who suffer loss of office or employment. This Order repeals section 3 of the 2010 Act in so far as it affects schemes made after the commencement of section 3.
