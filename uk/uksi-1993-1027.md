# The Weymouth and Portland Harbour Revision Order 1993

Made: 5th April 1993

Coming into force: 8th April 1993

Now therefore the Secretary of State (being the appropriate Minister under section 14(7) of the Act)[^f00002], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14 of the Act and now vested in him[^f00003], and of all other power s enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Weymouth and Portland Harbour Revision Order 1993 and shall come into force on 8th April 1993.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the Act of 1887” means the Weymouth and Melcombe Regis Corporation Act 1887[^f00004];

- “the added limits” means the area described in the Schedule to this Order;

- “the Authority” means the Weymouth and Portland Borough Council.

#### Extension of Limits

###### 3

- (1) The limits within which the Authority have jurisdiction in accordance with the Act of 1887 are hereby extended to include the added limits.

- (2) All enactments conferring rights, powers, privileges or immunities or imposing duties, obligations or liabilities upon the Authority in relation to the harbour mentioned in the Act of 1887 or upon the harbour master for that harbour and all byelaws and regulations made by the Authority which immediately before the coming into force of this Order relate to or have effect within the said harbour shall relate to or have effect within the added limits.

- (3) Section 8 of the Act of 1887 shall have effect as if after the words “the parish of Radipole” there were inserted the words “and shall include the added limits as defined in the Weymouth and Portland Harbour Revision Order 1993”.

## SCHEDULE — DESCRIPTION OF ADDED LIMITS

An area enclosed by a line drawn from a point on the shoreline at position 50deg 37.51'N 02deg 26.475'W to the north-east corner of the dockyard port of Portland (position 50deg 36.42'N 02deg 23.88'W) and thence in a 343.5 degrees (true) direction for 1.675 nautical miles to the shoreline (position 50deg 38.01'N 02deg 24.60'W).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

Caithness — Minister of State, — Department of Transport — 5th April 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the limits within which the Weymouth and Portland Borough Council has jurisdiction by including a portion of Weymouth Bay from a point approximately 1.7 nautical miles east of the Nothe Fort to the borough boundary adjacent to Redcliffe Point, Weymouth as part of the harbour.

The applicant for the Order is the Borough Council of Weymouth and Portland.
