# The Offender Management Act 2007 (Establishment of Probation Trusts) (Amendment) Order 2012

Made: 3rd May 2012

Laid before Parliament: 4th May 2012

Coming into force: 30th May 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 13(6) of Schedule 1 to, the Offender Management Act 2007[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Offender Management Act 2007 (Establishment of Probation Trusts) (Amendment) Order 2012 and shall come into force on 30th May 2012.

#### Amendment to the Offender Management Act 2007 (Establishment of Probation Trusts) Order 2010

###### 2

- (1) The Offender Management Act 2007 (Establishment of Probation Trusts) Order 2010[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) After article 3 insert—

> > (3A) Wales Probation Trust is designated as a Welsh probation trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Crispin Blunt — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 3rd May 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Offender Management Act 2007 (Establishment of Probation Trusts) Order 2010 by inserting a new article 3A. New article 3A designates Wales Probation Trust as a “Welsh probation trust”. The amendment will enable the Auditor General for Wales to examine the accounts of the Wales Probation Trust in accordance with paragraph 13 of Schedule 1 to the Offender Management Act 2007.
