# The Hounslow (Various Roads) Traffic Order 1969 (Variation) Order 1995

Made: 5th December 1995

Coming into force: 9th December 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) and Part IV of Schedule 9 to the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and after consultation with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Hounslow (Various Roads) Traffic Order 1969 (Variation) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 9th December 1995.

###### 2

- (1) The Hounslow (Various Roads) Traffic Order 1969[^f00003] shall be varied as follows:

- (2) Before article 10 there shall be inserted the following article—

> > (9A) Subject to the provisions of this Order no person shall cause any vehicle the width of which exceeds 6 feet 6 inches to proceed on the flyover or the slip road.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

R. T. Thorndike — Director Highways Agency London Network Management Division — 5th December 1995
