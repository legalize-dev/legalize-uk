## SCHEDULE

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or departments* | *Matters in relation to which the Ministers or departments are designated* |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to construction products. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to apparatus which is liable to cause electromagnetic disturbance and to apparatus the performance of which is liable to be affected by such disturbance. |
| Any one or more Secretaries of State and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries & Food | Measures relating to the prevention, reduction and elimination of pollution of water. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to prospectuses on offers of transferable securities to the public. |
| Any Northern Ireland department | The preceding items in this column. |

## Signed
