# The Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made: 28th July 1995

In the exercise of the powers conferred upon my by section 3(2) of the Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995.

###### 2

The Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995 shall come into force on 5th September 1995.

## Signed

Home Office

Mishael Howard — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 28th July 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 5th September 1995 the Prisoners (Return to Custody) Act 1995.
