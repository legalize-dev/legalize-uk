# The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made: 18th June 2008

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 211(2) of the Legal Services Act 2007[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2008.

#### Amendment of the Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) Order 2008

###### 2

- (1) The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitory Provisions) Order 2008[^f00002] is amended as follows.
