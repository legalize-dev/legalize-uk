# The Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988

Made: 26th January 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 9 of the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1988.

###### 2

The provisions of the Diplomatic and Consular Premises Act 1987 which are not already in force shall come into force on 3rd February 1988.

## Signed

Geoffrey Howe — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 26th January 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 3rd February 1988 those provisions of the Act which are not already in force.
