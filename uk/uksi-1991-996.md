# The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1991

Made: 16th April 1991

Coming into force: 16th May 1991

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 16th day of April 1991 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred upon Her by section 1(1) of the Consular Fees Act 1980[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order shall come into force on 16th May 1991 and may be cited as the Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1991.

###### 2

Part II of the table of consular fees in the Schedule annexed to the Consular Fees Order 1989[^f00002] is amended as follows–

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Consular Fees Order 1989 as amended by the Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1990 so as to replace the two entry visa with a new short term multiple entry visa valid for six months. The fee remains the same at £30.
