# The Sea Fisheries (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2024

Made: 15th October 2024

Laid before Parliament: 16th October 2024

Coming into force: 16th December 2024

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 15(2) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1241) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conservation of fisheries resources and the protection of marine ecosystems through technical measures[^f00001], which now vest in the Secretary of State acting in the capacity of a fisheries administration[^f00002], and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(1)(b) and (c) and section 36(4)(a) and (j) of the Fisheries Act 2020[^f00003] (“*the 2020 Act*”), makes the following Regulations.

In accordance with Article 15(2) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1241), the Secretary of State has obtained scientific evidence to support the measures made under Article 15(2) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1241) contained in these Regulations.

In accordance with Article 15(2C) of [Regulation (EU) 2019/1241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1241), the Secretary of State has consulted such bodies or persons as appear to be representative of the interests likely to be substantially affected by these Regulations and such other bodies or persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

In accordance with section 40(1), (2) and (3) of the 2020 Act[^f00004], the Secretary of State has, where necessary, obtained the consent of the Welsh Ministers, Scottish Ministers and the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland to the making of these Regulations.

In accordance with section 41(1) of the 2020 Act, the Secretary of State has consulted the Welsh Ministers, the Scottish Ministers, the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland and such other persons likely to be affected by the measures made under section 36(1)(b) and (c) and section 36(4)(a) of the 2020 Act contained in these Regulations, as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Fisheries (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2024 and come into force on 16th December 2024.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conservation of fisheries resources and the protection of marine ecosystems through technical measures

###### 2

- (1) [Regulation (EU) 2019/1241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1241) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conservation of fisheries resources and the protection of marine ecosystems through technical measures is amended as follows.

- (a) for the full stop at the end of point (55), substitute a semicolon;

The gear is made up of a net which is similar in design to a bottom trawl. This definition includes the use of Danish seines and Scottish seines.

> > (10) Restrictions on the engine power of vessels using flyseining gear within 12 nautical miles of the coast of the United Kingdom in ICES divisions 7d and 7e

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to assimilated law in the field of sea fisheries.

Regulation 2 makes minor amendments to [Regulation (EU) 2019/1241](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2019/1241) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conservation of fisheries resources and the protection of marine ecosystems through technical measures (EUR 2019/1241). It introduces a minimum conservation reference size for lemon sole, turbot and brill in ICES divisions 7d and 7e and increases it for crawfish in English waters.

Regulation 2 also introduces a minimum mesh size requirement for flyseining gear in the English waters of ICES divisions 7d and 7e. The engine power of vessels using flyseining gear within 12 nautical miles of the English coast in those ICES divisions is additionally limited to 221 kW.

Regulation 3 amends Council [Regulation (EU) 2020/123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0123), by removing seabass catch limits for United Kingdom registered fishing vessels from Council [Regulation (EU) 2020/123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0123). It is intended that catch limits will be managed through vessel licence conditions.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary sector or community bodies is foreseen.
