# The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment Scheme (Set-aside) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 1st March 2007

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 2007

Coming into force: 31st March 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations under the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003]:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment Scheme (Set-aside) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 31st March 2007.

#### Amendment

###### 2

- (1) The Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment Scheme (Set-aside) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2—

- (a) for the definition of “Commission Regulation 795/2004” in paragraph (1), substitute—

> > - “*Commission Regulation 795/2004*” means [Commission Regulation (EC) No 795/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0795) laying down detailed rules for the implementation of the single payment scheme provided for in Council Regulation [(EC) No 1782/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1782) establishing common rules for direct support schemes under the common agricultural policy and establishing certain support schemes for farmers, as amended from time to time;

[^f00005];

- (b) for the definition of “Commission Regulation 1973/2004” in paragraph (1), substitute—

> > - “*Commission Regulation 1973/2004*” means [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1973/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1973) laying down detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1782/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1782) as regards the support schemes provided for in Titles IV and IVa of that Regulation and the use of land set aside for the production of raw materials, as amended from time to time;

[^f00006];

- (c) for the definition of “the Council Regulation” in paragraph (1), substitute—

> > - “*the Council Regulation*” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 1782/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1782) establishing common rules for direct support schemes under the common agricultural policy and establishing certain support schemes for farmers and amending Regulations [(EEC) No 2019/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1993/2019), [(EC) No 1452/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1452), [(EC) No 1453/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1453), [(EC) No 1454/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1454), [(EC) 1868/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/1868), [(EC) No 1251/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1251), [(EC) No 1254/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1254), [(EC) No 1673/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1673), [(EEC) No 2358/71](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1971/2358) and [(EC) No 2529/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2529), as amended from time to time;

[^f00007]; and

- (d) omit paragraph (2).

- (3) In regulation 4, omit paragraph (4).

- (4) In paragraph 13(3) of Schedule 1—

- (a) for the definition of “Compendium of UK Organic Standards”, substitute—

> > - “*Compendium of UK Organic Standards*” means the Compendium of UK Organic Standards, September 2006 Edition published by the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs;

- (b) for the definition of “[Council Regulation (EEC) No 2092/1991](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2092)”, substitute—

> > - “[Council Regulation (EEC) No 2092/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2092)” means [Council Regulation (EEC) No 2092/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2092) on organic production of agricultural products and indications referring thereto on agricultural products and foodstuffs, as amended from time to time;

[^f00008];

- (c) in the definition of “Organic land”, for “[Council Regulation (EEC) No 2092/1991](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2092)” both times it appears, substitute “[Council Regulation (EEC) No 2092/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2092)”.

## Signed

Jeff Rooker — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 1st March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Common Agricultural Policy Single Payment Scheme (Set-aside) (England) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/3385](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3385)) to incorporate amendments to Community legislation, provide for the incorporation of future amendments to that Community legislation in national law and to update the reference to the Compendium of Compendium of UK Organic Standards so as to refer to the most recent edition.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
