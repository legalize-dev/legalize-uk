# The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (England) Regulations 2014

Made: 5th March 2014

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 2014

Coming into force: 6th April 2014

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(6) of the Clean Air Act 1993[^f00001].

#### Citation, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (England) Regulations 2014;

- (b) apply in England; and

- (c) come into force on 6th April 2014.

#### Authorised fuels for the purposes of Part 3 of the Clean Air Act 1993

###### 2

For the purposes of Part 3 of the Clean Air Act 1993, the following are authorised fuels—

- (a) anthracite;

- (b) semi-anthracite;

- (c) electricity;

- (d) gas;

- (e) low volatile steam coals; and

- (f) the fuels described in the Schedule.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2013[^f00002] are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke and replace with amendments the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (England) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/2111](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2111)).

These Regulations list authorised fuels for the purposes of Part 3 of the Clean Air Act 1993. Four additional fuels are authorised and have been inserted into the Schedule in paragraphs 18, 19, 35 and 36.

A full impact assessment has not been produced as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
