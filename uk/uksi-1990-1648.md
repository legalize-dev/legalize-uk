# The Cambridge, East Surrey, East Worcestershire and Yorkshire (Pipelaying and Other Works)(Codes of Practice) Order 1990

Made: 27th July 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 162(7) of the Water Act 1989, and after consulting all such persons as he considers it appropriate to consult, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cambridge, East Surrey, East Worcestershire and Yorkshire (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1990.

#### Codes of Practice

###### 2

The Codes of Practice prepared by the Companies and certified as such by the Secretary of State are hereby approved.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

Dinah Nichols — An Under Secretary in the — Department of the Environment — 27thJuly 1990
