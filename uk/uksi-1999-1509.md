# The Social Security Amendment (Non-Cash Vouchers) Regulations 1999

Made: 27th May 1999

Laid before Parliament: 4th June 1999

Coming into force: 1st July 1999

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of powers conferred upon him by sections 123(1), 136(3), 137(1) and 175(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001] and sections 12(1), 35(1) and 36(1), (2) and (4) of the Jobseekers Act 1995[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation, in respect of those provisions in these Regulations relating to housing benefit and council tax benefit, with organisations appearing to him to be representative of the authorities concerned[^f00003] and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00004], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security Amendment (Non-Cash Vouchers) Regulations 1999 and, subject to the following paragraphs of this regulation, they shall come into force on 1st July 1999.

- (2) Where a claimant for either income support or a jobseeker’s allowance–

- (a) has an award of either of those benefits which is current on 30th June 1999;

- (b) that award is reviewed on or after 1st July 1999; and

- (c) the decision on that review takes effect on or after 1st July 1999,

regulation 2(5) or, as the case may be, 2(6) of these Regulations shall have effect in relation to that claimant from the date from which the decision on the first review of that award after 30th June 1999 takes effect.

- (3) In relation to any claimant for either council tax benefit or housing benefit, regulation 2(1) or, as the case may be, 2(4) of these Regulations shall have effect, where that claimant has an award of either of those benefits which is current on 30th June 1999, from the first day of the first benefit period to commence for that claimant on or after 1st July 1999 and for the purposes of this paragraph, “benefit period” shall have the same meaning as it has in regulation 57 of the Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992[^f00005] or, as the case may be, in regulation 66 of the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00006].

- (4) In relation to any claimant for either family credit or disability working allowance, regulation 2(2) or, as the case may be, 2(3) of these Regulations shall have effect, where that claimant has an award of either of those benefits which expires after 1st July 1999, on the day following the expiration of that award.

#### Earnings of employed earners

###### 2

- (1) In regulation 19 of the Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992[^f00007] (which relates to earnings of employed earners)–

- (a) at the end of paragraph (1), there shall be added the following sub-paragraph–

> > (k) the amount of any payment by way of a non-cash voucher which has been taken into account in the computation of a person’s earnings in accordance with regulation 18(22) to (25) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979[^f00008].

- (b) at the beginning of paragraph (2)(a), there shall be inserted the words “subject to paragraph (3),”;

- (c) after paragraph (2), there shall be added the following paragraph–

> > (3) Paragraph (2)(a) shall not apply in respect of any non-cash voucher referred to in paragraph (1)(k).

- (2) In regulation 21 of the Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991[^f00009] (which relates to earnings of employed earners)–

- (a) at the end of paragraph (1), there shall be added the following sub-paragraph–

> > (j) the amount of any payment by way of a non-cash voucher which has been taken into account in the computation of a person’s earnings in accordance with regulation 18(22) to (25) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979.

- (b) in paragraph (2)(a), for the words “paragraph (3)” there shall be substituted the words “paragraphs (3) and (4)”;

- (c) after paragraph (3), there shall be added the following paragraph–

> > (4) Paragraph (2)(a) shall not apply in respect of any non-cash voucher referred to in paragraph (1)(j).

- (3) In regulation 19 of the Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00010] (which relates to earnings of employed earners)–

- (a) at the end of paragraph (1), there shall be added the following sub-paragraph–

> > (i) the amount of any payment by way of a non-cash voucher which has been taken into account in the computation of a person’s earnings in accordance with regulation 18(22) to (25) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979.

- (b) in paragraph (2)(a), for the words “paragraph (3)” there shall be substituted the words “paragraphs (3) and (4)”;

- (c) after paragraph (3), there shall be added the following paragraph–

> > (4) Paragraph (2)(a) shall not apply in respect of any non-cash voucher referred to in paragraph (1)(i).

- (4) In regulation 28 of the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations 1987[^f00011] (which relates to earnings of employed earners)–

- (a) at the end of paragraph (1), there shall be added the following sub-paragraph–

> > (k) the amount of any payment by way of a non-cash voucher which has been taken into account in the computation of a person’s earnings in accordance with regulation 18(22) to (25) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979.

- (b) at the beginning of paragraph (2)(a), there shall be inserted the words “subject to paragraph (3),”;

- (c) after paragraph (2), there shall be added the following paragraph–

> > (3) Paragraph (2)(a) shall not apply in respect of any non-cash voucher referred to in paragraph (1)(k).

- (5) In regulation 35 of the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987[^f00012] (which relates to earnings of employed earners)–

- (a) at the end of paragraph (1), there shall be added the following sub-paragraph–

> > (j) the amount of any payment by way of a non-cash voucher which has been taken into account in the computation of a person’s earnings in accordance with regulation 18(22) to (25) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979.

- (b) at the beginning of paragraph (2)(a), there shall be inserted the words “subject to paragraph (2A),”;

- (c) after paragraph (2), there shall be added the following paragraph–

> > (2A) Paragraph (2)(a) shall not apply in respect of any non-cash voucher referred to in paragraph (1)(j).

- (6) In regulation 98 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^f00013] (which relates to earnings of employed earners)–

- (a) at the end of paragraph (1), there shall be added the following sub-paragraph–

> > (h) the amount of any payment by way of a non-cash voucher which has been taken into account in the computation of a person’s earnings in accordance with regulation 18(22) to (25) of the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979.

- (b) at the beginning of paragraph (2)(a), there shall be inserted the words “subject to paragraph (2A),”;

- (c) after paragraph (2), there shall be added the following paragraph–

> > (2A) Paragraph (2)(a) shall not apply in respect of any non-cash voucher referred to in paragraph (1)(h).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Council Tax Benefit (General) Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/1814](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1814)), the Disability Working Allowance (General) Regulations 1991 (S.I. [1991/2887](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/2887)), the Family Credit (General) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1973](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1973)), the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1971)), the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1967](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1967)) (collectively referred to below as “the income-related benefits”) and the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/207](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/207)).

In particular, regulation 2 of these Regulations provides that, for the purposes of the income-related benefits and jobseeker’s allowance, non-cash vouchers which are not disregarded as payments under the Social Security (Contributions) Regulations 1979 (S.I. [1979/591](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1979/591) as amended) shall be treated as earnings. The Regulations referred to above have also been amended by regulation 2 so as to ensure that such vouchers are not treated as payments in kind.

These Regulations do not impose a charge on business.
