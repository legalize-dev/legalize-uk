# The Firearms Act 1982 (Commencement) Order 1983

Made: 27th September 1983

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 4(3) of the Firearms Act 1982, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Firearms Act 1982 (Commencement) Order 1983.

###### 2

The Firearms Act 1982 shall come into force on 1st November 1983.

## Signed

Leon Brittan — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 27th September 1983

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Firearms Act 1982 into force on 1st November 1983.
