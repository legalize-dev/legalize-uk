# The Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Victims' Code of Practice) Order 2006

Made: 6th March 2006

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 2006

Coming into force: 3rd April 2006

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004 (Victims' Code of Practice) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 3rd April 2006.

###### 2

The code of practice entitled “The Code of Practice for Victims of Crime” laid before Parliament on 19th October 2005 shall come into operation on 3rd April 2006.

## Signed

Fiona Mactaggart — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 6th March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation on 3rd April 2006 the Code of Practice for Victims of Crime, issued pursuant to section 32 of the Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004, making provision as to the services to be provided to a victim of criminal conduct by persons which have functions relating to victims of criminal conduct or any aspect of the criminal justice system.
