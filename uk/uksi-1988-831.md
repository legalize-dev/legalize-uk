# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (City Centre Cable) Order 1988

Made: 5th May 1988

Laid before Parliament: 9th May 1988

Coming into force: 6th June 1988

Whereas the Secretary of State has granted to City Centre Cable on 3rd May 1988 a licence (“the Licence”) under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984[^f00001] (“the Act”), to which section 8 of the Act applies, for the running of the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to the Licence (“the Applicable Cabled Systems”):Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (City Centre Cable) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 6th June 1988.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

John Butcher — Parliamentary under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 5th May 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to City Centre Cable on 3rd May 1988 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 9th May 1988. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, City Centre Cable will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Atlantic House, Holborn Viaduct, London EC1N 2HQ.
