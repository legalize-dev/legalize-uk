# The Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

Made: 18th December 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 19(1) of the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 shall come into force on 6th April 2001.

## Signed

Kim Howells, — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Consumers and Corporate Affairs, — Department of Trade and Industry — 18th December 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 into force on 6th April 2001.
