# The Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2005

Made: 22nd June 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 108(3) of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2005.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st July 2005

###### 2

Section 32 of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 (sale of aerosol paint to children) shall come into force on 1st July 2005.

## Signed

Hazel Blears — Minister of State — 22nd June 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 32 of the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005 on 1st July 2005.
