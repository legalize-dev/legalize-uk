# The Non-metropolitan and Welsh Counties (Local Statutory Provisions) Order 1986

Made: 2nd December 1986

Laid before Parliament: 10th December 1986

Coming into Operation: 31st December 1986

The Secretary of State for the Environment in relation to England, and the Secretary of State for Wales in relation to Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 262(9)(b) of the Local Government Act 1972 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following order:—

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Non-metropolitan and Welsh Counties (Local Statutory Provisions) Order 1986 and shall come into operation on 31st December 1986.

###### 2

- (1) The date on which all local statutory provisions applying to the whole or part of the local government areas specified below, so far as those provisions so apply, are to cease to have effect is hereby postponed to 31st December 1987.

- (2) The specified areas are—

- (a) in England, the counties of Cleveland and Essex and the cities of Exeter, Plymouth and York; and

- (b) in Wales, the counties of Dyfed, Mid-Glamorgan and West Glamorgan.

## Signed

Nicholas Ridley — Secretary of State for the Environment — 2nd December 1986

Nicholas Edwards — Secretary of State for Wales — 2nd December 1986

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 262(9) of the Local Government Act 1972 provides that all local statutory provisions to which that sub-section applies cease to have effect on specified dates, but allows the date to be postponed, in relation to any local government area, by statutory order.

The specified date in relation to non-metropolitan counties in England and all counties in Wales was postponed to 31st December 1986 by the Non-metropolitan and Welsh Counties (Local Statutory Provisions) Order 1983 (S.I. 1983/619).

This order further postpones the specified date in relation to the local government areas mentioned in article 2(2). Relevant provisions in those areas will continue in force until 31st December 1987 unless otherwise repealed.
