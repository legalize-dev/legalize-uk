# The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 1997

Made: 10th June 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 15(2) of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 1997.

###### 2

Sections 3(1) and (2) and 6 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 16th June 1997.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, C. — Dated 10th June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 16th June 1997 sections 3(1) and (2) and 6 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997. Sections 13 to 16 of the Act came into force at Royal Assent on 21st March 1997.
