# The Home Guard (Amendment) Order 1989

Made: 2nd August 1989

Laid before Parliament: 10th August 1989

Coming into force: 1st September 1989

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of August 1989 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by the said section 12(1) and (3) and of all other powers enabling Her in that behalf, is pleased by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Home Guard (Amendment) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

#### Public funds to include reference to community charge

###### 2

- (1) The Warrant of 21st December 1964[^f00007], concerning pensions and other grants in respect of disablement or death due to service in the Home Guard, shall be amended by the inclusion in article 1(6) after the words “general or local rate” of the words “moneys provided under the Abolition of Domestic Rates etc. (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00008]”.

- (2) The Order of 22nd December 1964[^f00009] concerning pensions and other grants in respect of disablement or death due to service in the Home Guard after 27th April 1952 shall be amended by the inclusion in article 1(6) after the words “general or local rate” of the words “moneys provided under the Abolition of Domestic Rates etc. (Scotland) Act 1987”[^f00010]

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to previous instruments relating to the payment of pensions and other grants in respect of disablement or death due to service in the Home Guard. The amendments are consequential upon the abolition in Scotland of domestic rates and their replacement by the community charge.
