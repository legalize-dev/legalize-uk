# The Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Revocation) Order 1998

Made: 21st August 1998

Laid before Parliament: 4th September 1998

Coming into force: 7th September 1998

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with the sea fishing industry in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 5(1), 15(3), 20(1) and 22(2)(a) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Revocation) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 7th September 1998.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1998[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Jeff Rooker — Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 19th August 1998

Sewel — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 21st August 1998

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Peter Hain — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 17th August 1998

Tony Beeton — Assistant Secretary, Northern Ireland Office — 18th August 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1998 which prohibits fishing for cod in sea areas ICES Statistical Divisions VIIb, c, d, e, f, g, h, j and k by certain British fishing boats (article 2).
