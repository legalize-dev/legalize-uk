# The Taxes (Interest Rate) (Amendment No. 4) Regulations 1992

Made: 15th October 1992

Laid before the House of Commons: 16th October 1992

Coming into force: 6th November 1992

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 178 of the Finance Act 1989[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Taxes (Interest Rate) (Amendment No. 4) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 6th November 1992.

#### Amendments to the Taxes (Interest Rate) Regulations 1989

###### 2

In regulation 5 of the Taxes (Interest Rate) Regulations 1989[^f00002]

- (a) for “6th June 1992” there shall be substituted “6th November 1992”;

- (b) for “10.50 per cent. per annum” there shall be substituted “9.75 per cent. per annum”.

## Signed

Nicholas Baker — Irvine Patnick — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 15th October 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend with effect from 6th November 1992 regulation 5 of the Taxes (Interest Rate) Regulations 1989 (S.I. [1989/1297](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1297)), as amended by the Taxes (Interest Rate) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 (S.I. [1991/889](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/889)), which specifies the official rate of interest for the purposes of section 160 of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (taxation of beneficial loans made to employees). The official rate of interest also determines “the appropriate percentage” for the purposes of the additional charge to tax in respect of certain living accommodation provided for employees under section 146 of the 1988 Act.

The amendment made by these Regulations specifies a new official rate of interest (9.75 per cent. per annum) in substitution for the rate of 10.50 per cent. per annum specified by S.I. [1992/1338](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1338). Previous rates were specified by S.I. [1989/1297](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1297) and S.I. [1991/889](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/889), [1120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1120), [1377](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1377), [1695](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1695), [2070](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/2070), and S.I. [1992/265](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/265).
