# The Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 24th June 2010

Laid before the House of Commons: 24th June 2010

Coming into force: 1st October 2010

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 12(4) of the Finance Act 1997[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 1st October 2010.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations “quarter” means the first three months of an accounting period.

#### Application and revocation

###### 3

- (1) These Regulations apply in the case of payments on account of gaming duty for any quarter that ends on or after 31st October 2010.

- (2) The Gaming Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are revoked.

#### The amount of payments on account

###### 4

For the purpose of calculating payments on account of gaming duty in the cases to which these Regulations apply, substitute the following Table for the Table in regulation 5 of the Gaming Duty Regulations 1997[^f00003]:

| Part of gross gaming yield | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| The first £987,500 | 15 per cent |
| The next £680,750 | 20 per cent |
| The next £1,192,500 | 30 per cent |
| The next £2,516,750 | 40 per cent |
| The remainder | 50 per cent |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend regulation 5 of the Gaming Duty Regulations 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2196](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2196)) which deals with the amount of payments on account. They substitute a new Table reflecting changes to the bands of gross gaming yield for gaming duty made by section 20 of the Finance Act [2010 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/13)[^f00004], and will apply in the case of payments on account of gaming duty for any quarter that ends on or after 31st October 2010.

The duty rates in the table are those for the first three months of each six-monthly accounting period, hence the bands represent half of the new bands of gross gaming yield shown in section 20 of the Finance Act 2010. The interim quarterly payments under these Regulations are intended to be roughly half of the expected duty liability for the six-monthly period.

A full and final Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as a negligible impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
