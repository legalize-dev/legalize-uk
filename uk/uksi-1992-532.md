# The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provision) Order 1992

Made: 5th March 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5(4)(b) and 6(5) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provision) Order 1992.

###### 2

In this Order “the 1991 Act” means the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991.

###### 3

Subject to article 4 of this Order, sections 2(1) to (6) and (8), 3 and 6(3) of and Schedule 2 to the 1991 Act shall come into force on 6th March 1992.

###### 4

Until the date on which the first pay and conditions order made under section 2(1) of the 1991 Act comes into force, nothing in article 3 above shall affect the continued operation of the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1991[^f00002].

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — Secretary of State for Education and Science — 5th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (Commencement No.1) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/1874](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1874)) brought into force sections 1, 4, 5 and 6(1), (2), (4) and (5) of and Schedule 1 to the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991.

This Order brings into force the remaining provisions of the 1991 Act, except section 2(7), on 6th March 1991. Article 4, however, preserves the operation of the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 1991 until an Order under section 2(1) of the 1991 Act comes into force.
