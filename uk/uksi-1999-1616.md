# The Public Record Office (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 9th June 1999

Coming into force: 1st August 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(5) of the Public Records Act 1958[^f00001] and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations–

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Record Office (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st August 1999.

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1999[^f00002], paragraph 6.1(ix) (Additional finishes) shall be omitted.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, C. — Dated 4th June 1999

We concur,

Jim Dowd — Jane Kennedy — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 9th June 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations delete from the Public Record Office (Fees) Regulations 1999 fee 6.1(ix), which related to the supply of copies of records with additional finishes.
