# The Education (QCA Levy) (Revocation) Regulations 2008

Made: 25th March 2008

Laid before Parliament: 8th April 2008

Coming into force: 30th September 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 36 and 54(3) of the Education Act 1997[^f00001].

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (QCA Levy) (Revocation) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 30th September 2008.

###### 2

The Education (QCA Levy) Regulations 2002[^f00002] and the Education (QCA Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 [^f00003] are revoked.

## Signed

David Lammy — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Innovation, Universities and Skills — 25th March 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Education (QCA Levy) Regulations 2002 and the regulations which amended them with effect from 30th September 2008.
