# The Birmingham Heartlands and Solihull (Teaching) National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1997

Made: 24th February 1997

Laid before Parliament: 3rd March 1997

Coming into force: 25th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the former Solihull Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Birmingham Heartlands and Solihull (Teaching) National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted Solihull Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Birmingham Heartlands and Solihull (Teaching) National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 25th March 1997.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means Solihull Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the Birmingham Heartlands and Solihull (Teaching) National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 15th January 1997, which Schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 25th March 1997 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Horam — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 24th February 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Birmingham Heartlands and Solihull (Teaching) National Health Service Trust on 25th March 1997 trust property formerly held by Solihull Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
