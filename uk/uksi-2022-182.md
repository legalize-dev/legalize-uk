## SCHEDULE

| Column 1 | Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant statutory provision | Relevant statutory provision | Subject of provision | Old limit | New limit |
| 1 | Section 145E(3) of the 1992 Act | Amount of award for unlawful inducement relating to trade union membership or activities or for unlawful inducement relating to collective bargaining. | £4,341 | £4,554 |
| 2 | Section 156(1) of the 1992 Act[^f00022] | Minimum amount of basic award of compensation where dismissal is unfair by virtue of section 152(1) or 153 of the 1992 Act. | £6,634 | £6,959 |
| 3 | Section 176(6A) of the 1992 Act[^f00023] | Minimum amount of compensation where individual excluded or expelled from union in contravention of section 174 of the 1992 Act and not admitted or re-admitted by date of tribunal application. | £10,132 | £10,628 |
| 4 | Section 31(1) of the 1996 Act | Limit on amount of guarantee payment payable to an employee in respect of any day. | £30 | £31 |
| 5 | Section 120(1) of the 1996 Act[^f00024] | Minimum amount of basic award of compensation where dismissal is unfair by virtue of section 100(1)(a) or (b), 101A(d), 102(1) or 103 of the 1996 Act. | £6,634 | £6,959 |
| 6 | Section 124(1ZA)(a) of the 1996 Act[^f00025] | Limit on amount of compensatory award for unfair dismissal. | £89,493 | £93,878 |
| 7 | Section 186(1)(a) and (b) of the 1996 Act | Limit on weekly amount payable to an employee, pro-rated where referable to a shorter period, in respect of a debt to which Part 12 of the 1996 Act applies and which is referable to a period of time. | £544 | £571 |
| 8 | Section 227(1) of the 1996 Act[^f00026] | Maximum amount of “a week’s pay” for the purpose of calculating a redundancy payment or for various awards including the basic or additional award of compensation for unfair dismissal. | £544 | £571 |
