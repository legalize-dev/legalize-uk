# The Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) Order 2022

Made: 14th June 2022

Laid before Parliament: 16th June 2022

Coming into force: 1st September 2022

The Minister for the Cabinet Office has consulted the Welsh Ministers in accordance with section 4(7) of that Act[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Freedom of Information (Additional Public Authorities) Order 2022.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 1st September 2022.

- (3) In this Order, “*the Act*” means the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

#### Additional public authorities

###### 2

In Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Act (local government) after paragraph 35E insert—

> > (35F) A corporate joint committee established by regulations under Part 5 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021.

###### 3

In Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Act (other public bodies and offices: general) insert, at the appropriate place in each case, the entries listed in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — Entries inserted in Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Act

- The Biometrics and Forensics Ethics Group.

- The Education Workforce Council.

- The Independent Welsh Pay Review Body.

- Social Care Wales.

## Signed

True — Minister of State — Cabinet Office — 14th June 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The obligations under the Freedom of Information Act [2000 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36) (“*the Act*”) apply to public authorities. Section 3(1) provides that a “*public authority*” includes a body or holder of any office which is listed in Schedule 1 to the Act. The Minister for the Cabinet Office may add bodies and office holders to Schedule 1 by an order under section 4(1), provided that the conditions in section 4(2) and (3) are satisfied. By section 4(6), an order under section 4(1) may relate to a specified person or office or to persons or offices falling within a specified description.

Article 2 and 3 of this Order adds the bodies and office holders listed in the Schedule to this Order to Part 2 and Part 6 of Schedule 1 to the Act.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
