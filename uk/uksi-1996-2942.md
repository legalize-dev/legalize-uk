# The A41 Trunk Road (Watford Way/Hendon Way, Barnet) Temporary Prohibition of Traffic Order 1996

Made: 22nd November 1996

Coming into force: 23rd November 1996

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 14 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A41 Trunk Road (Watford Way/Hendon Way, Barnet) Temporary Prohibition of Traffic Order 1996 and shall come into force on the 23rd November 1996.

###### 2

This Order is made because the Traffic Director for London is satisfied that traffic on the roads, or lengths of road, mentioned in the schedule to this Order should be prohibited because works are being or are proposed to be executed on or near those roads or lengths of road.

###### 3

During the period starting at 00.01 hours on 23rd November 1996 for a period of six months or when those works have been completed, whichever is the sooner, the prohibitions specified in article 4 below shall have effect.

###### 4

Subject to the provisions of article 5 below, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle to enter or proceed in the lengths of road specified in the schedule to this Order.

###### 5

- (1) The prohibitions specified in article 4 above shall apply only during such times and to such extent as shall from time to time be indicated by traffic signs specified by regulations or authorised under section 64 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984.

- (2) The prohibitions specified in article 4 above shall not apply in respect of—

- (i) any vehicle being used for the purpose of those works, or for fire brigade, ambulance or police purpose, or

- (ii) anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden.

## SCHEDULE

#### Lengths of roads subject to prohibitions:

The roads listed below, from the kerb line (extended) of the A41 Watford Way/Hendon Way to the highway backline (extended) of the A41 Watford Way/Hendon Way:

- Prothero Gardens NW4

- Cheyne Walk NW4

- Neeld Crescent NW4

- Rundell Crescent NW4

- Graham Road NW4

- Elliot Road NW4

- Park Road NW4

- Wayside NW11

- Ridge Hill NW11

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 22nd November 1996
