# The King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services National Health Service Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made: 22nd March 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services National Health Service Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the the King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00004];

- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

#### Change of name of NHS trust and transitional provisions

###### 2

- (1) The trust shall be called the Sherwood Forest Hospitals National Health Service Trust instead of the King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services National Health Service Trust, and accordingly in article 1 of the Order in the definition of “the trust”, and in article 2 of the Order, for the words “King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services” there are substituted the words “Sherwood Forest Hospitals”.

- (2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person, or

- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name;

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as if referring to it under its new name.

#### Further amendments of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 3

- (1) For article 3 of the Order (nature and functions of the trust) there shall be substituted the following article—

> > (3)
>   (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>   (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, for the purposes of the health service, at or from:
>     (a) King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services, Mansfield Road, Sutton-in-Ashfield, Nottinghamshire NG17 4JL;
>     (b) Newark General Hospital, London Road, Newark on Trent, Nottinghamshire;
>     (c) Ashfield Community Hospital, Portland Street, Kirkby in Ashfield, NG17 7AE;
>     (d) Mansfield Community Hospital, Stockwell Gate, Mansfield, NG18 5QJ;
>   and at or from any associated hospitals, establishments and facilities.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Yvette Cooper — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Public Health, — Department of Health — 22nd March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 (“the Establishment Order”) which established the King’s Mill Centre for Health Care Services National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order changes the name of the trust to the Sherwood Forest Hospitals National Health Service Trust. Article 3 substitutes a new article 3 of the Establishment Order (functions of the trust).

This Order should be read in conjunction with the Nottingham Healthcare and the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2001. The Sherwood Forest Hospitals National Health Service Trust is to carry out certain functions previously carried out by the Central Nottinghamshire Healthcare National Health Service Trust.
