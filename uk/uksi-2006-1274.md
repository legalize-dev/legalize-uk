# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2006

Made: 9th May 2006

Laid before Parliament: 11th May 2006

Coming into force: 1st June 2006

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 122(1), 123, 124 and 210(7) of that Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st June 2006.

#### Amendments to the Document

###### 2

- (1) The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2005[^f00003] is amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

- (2) After paragraph 55.4 insert the following—

> > (55A) For the purposes of this paragraph, the term “salary sacrifice arrangement” means any arrangement under which the teacher gives up the right to receive part of his gross salary in return for the employer’s agreement to provide him with a benefit-in-kind under any of the following schemes—
>   (a) a child care voucher or other child care benefit scheme;
>   (b) a cycle or cyclist’s safety equipment scheme; or
>   (c) a mobile telephone scheme; and
> that benefit in-kind is exempt from income tax [^f00004].
> (55A.2) Where the employer operates a salary sacrifice arrangement, the teacher may participate in any such arrangement and his gross salary may be reduced accordingly for the duration of his participation in it.
> (55A.3) Participation in any salary sacrifice arrangement shall have no effect upon the determination of any safeguarded sum to which the teacher may be entitled under any provision of the Document.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2005 (“the Document”) in order to allow teachers to participate voluntarily in certain salary sacrifice arrangements and in order to ensure that where a teacher is entitled to a safeguarded sum under the Document participation in a salary sacrifice arrangement does not affect the determination of that safeguarded sum with effect from 1st June 2006.

This Order also amends the Document to make provision for the assimilation of Advanced Skills Teachers onto a new pay spine with effect from 1st September 2006. These changes result from a recommendation made by the School Teachers' Review Body in Part 2 of their 13th Report in 2004.
