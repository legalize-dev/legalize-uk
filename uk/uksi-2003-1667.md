# The Education Act 2002 (Commencement No.6 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2003

Made: 30th June 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 216(2), (4) and (5) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2003.

- (2) In this Order —

- “the 1998 Act” means the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 [^f00002],

- “the 2002 Act” means the Education Act 2002.

#### Transitional and saving provisions

###### 6

The Schedule to this Order shall have effect for the purposes of making transitional and saving provisions in relation to the provisions to which it refers.

## SCHEDULE — TRANSITIONAL AND SAVING PROVISIONS

#### Communication in schools

###### 1

Despite the coming into force of section 38(2)(b) of the 2002 Act, the academic years in respect of which comparative statistics must be included in a report shall not include any year beginning before 1st August 2002 (so that the report in respect of 1st August 2003 to 31st July 2004 will include comparative statistics in respect of the preceding year only).

#### Health hazards in FE institutions

###### 2

Despite the repeal of section 218(1)(e) of the Education Reform Act 1988, regulations 4, 5 and 7 of, and Schedule 1 to the Education (Schools and Further and Higher Education) Regulations 1989 [^f00003] shall remain in force.

#### Teachers' qualifications and health standards

###### 3

Despite the repeal of section 218(5) of the Education Reform Act 1988, regulations 5, 6 and 7 (and regulation 3 to the extent that it relates to those regulations) of the Education (Teachers' Qualifications and Health Standards) (England) Regulations 1999 [^f00004] shall remain in force.

#### Saving of existing instruments of government

###### 4

Despite the coming into force of section 19(1) of the 2002 Act and the repeal of section 36(1) of and Schedule 9 to the 1998 Act, all instruments of government of maintained schools shall remain in force until replaced by a new instrument of government made in accordance with the School Governance (Constitution) Regulations 2003 [^f00005].

#### Maintained nursery schools and delegated budgets

###### 5

The application of Chapter 4 of Part 2 of the 1998 Act to maintained nursery schools made by the amendment of section 45 of that Act by paragraph 99 of Schedule 21 to the 2002 Act, shall have effect in the period ending immediately before 1st April 2004 only for the purpose of the financing of maintained nursery schools in any financial year beginning on and after that date.

#### Independent schools

###### 6

Despite the coming into force of section 165 of the 2002 Act, that section (except for subsections (1), (2), (12) and (13)) shall not apply until 1st September 2005 to any school which was on 31st August 2003 provisionally registered pursuant to section 465(3) of the Education Act 1996.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force certain provisions of the Education Act 2002 on 1st August, September, and October 2003. The provisions are listed in articles 2, 3, 4, and 5.

Article 2 relates to provisions concerning the power to make the Teachers' Pay and Conditions document.

Article 3 relates to qualified teacher status, the requirement to be qualified and registered, health and fitness of teachers, GTCE powers and records and hazardous material in FE institutions.

Article 4 relates to the new framework for school governance in Part 3 of the 2002 Act (except for sections 31 and 38), and power to make regulations on allowances in respect of education or training.

Article 5 relates to communication with schools and head teacher qualifications.

The provisions listed in article 2 are non-devolved (teachers' pay and conditions). They are brought into force for all purposes in accordance with the Secretary of State’s power in section 216(2). The provisions listed in articles 3, 4 and 5 are brought into force for England only, in accordance with the Secretary of State’s power in section 216(4).

Article 6 and the Schedule relate to transitional provisions in relation to the existing regulation on health hazards in FE institution and teachers' qualifications and health standards, the saving of existing instruments of government, financing of maintained nursery schools for the remainder of this financial year, and provisionally registered independent schools.
