# The Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Designation of Participating Countries) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) (No. 2) Order 2011

Made: 3rd February 2011

Coming into force

A draft of this Order has been approved by each House of Parliament in accordance with section 50(5)(a) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 .

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(2)(b) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (‘the 2003 Act’), makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Designation of Participating Countries) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) (No. 2) Order 2011 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

###### 2

This Order extends to England and Wales and Northern Ireland.

###### 3

Japan is designated as a participating country under section 51(2)(b) of the 2003 Act for the purposes of sections 32, 35, 43, 44 and 45 of the 2003 Act.

## Signed

Baroness Neville-Jones — Minister of State — 3rd February 2011
