# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 11) Order 1989

Made: 28th April 1989

Coming into force: 1st June 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 11) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st June 1989.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — designated owners

- Det Norske Oljeselskap A/S, Dokveien 1, 1345 Vika, 0113 Oslo 1, Norway.

- British Gas United Kingdom Inc., whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at 59 Bryanston Street, Marble Arch, London W1A 2AZ.

- Texaco Britain Ltd., whose registered office is at 1 Knightsbridge Green, London SW1X 7QJ.

- Unocal UK Ltd., whose registered office is at 32 Cadbury Road, Sunbury-on-Thames, Middlesex TW16 7LU.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the companies listed in Part I of the Schedule as owners of the pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule. The pipe-line lies between the Heather and Ninian oilfields situated north east of the Shetland Islands.
