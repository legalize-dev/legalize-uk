# The Education Act 2002 (Commencement No.4 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2003

Made: 28th January 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 216(2),(4) and (5) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) Order 2003.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 1996 Act” means the Education Act 1996[^f00002].

- “the 2002 Act” means the Education Act 2002.

#### Transitional and saving provisions

###### 6

A child receiving nursery education and for whom school lunches are being provided immediately before 6th April 2003 shall, until he ceases to receive nursery education, be treated as if there were no prescribed requirements applying to him under section 512(3)(a) of the 1996 Act (as substituted by section 201 of the 2002 Act).

###### 7

Despite the amendment of section 484 of the 1996 Act made by subsection (2) of section 18 of, and paragraph 49 of Schedule 21 to, the 2002 Act

- (a) the Education Standards Fund (England) Regulations 2002 [^f00003], and

- (b) the Education (Partnership Grant) Regulations 1998 [^f00004](to the extent that they are made under section 484 of the 1996 Act)

shall remain in force in relation to the period ending on 1st April 2004.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Education Act 2002 on 1th March, 1th April and 6th April 2003.

Article 2 commences certain subsections of sections 19, 20, 21, 33, 35 and 36, and sections 23 and 26 (powers to make regulations in respect of the government of maintained schools), and amendments in Schedules 21 consequential on certain Welsh provisions, on 1th March.

Article 3 commences section 209 (paid chairmen for local leaning and skills councils) on 1th April 2003.

Article 4 commences the repeal of specific grant making powers listed in section 18, certain subsections of sections 19, 20, 24, 74 and sections 25 and 34 (provisions in respect of federated schools and new schools), sections 44 (in part) and 45 (accounts and financial statements of maintained schools), section 72, Schedules 9 and part of 10 (reorganisation of sixth forms), section 153 (powers of LEAs in respect of funded nursery education), and sections 207 and 208 (recoupment) together with associated consequential amendments and repeals to those provisions in Schedules 21 and 22, on 1th April. Section 39(1) in respect of the inclusion of maintained nursery schools in the definition of maintained schools is still not commenced, and all related provisions relating to maintained nursery school governing bodies remain uncommenced.

Article 5 commences sections 200 (remission of charges) and 201 (LEA functions concerning school lunches, milk etc) – but not in respect of its application to funded nursery education – on 6th April 2003 to co-incide with the tax year.

The provision listed in article 3 only applies to England. It is brought into force for all purposes in accordance with the Secretary of State’s power in section 216(2). The provisions listed in articles 2, 4 and 5 are brought into force for England only, in accordance with the Secretary of State’s power in section 216(4).

The Order also makes transitional and saving provisions in respect of the entitlement to school lunches, and repeal of certain specific grant making regulations.
