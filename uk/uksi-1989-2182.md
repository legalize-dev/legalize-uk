# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (East London Telecommunications Limited) Order 1989

Made: 23rd November 1989

Laid before Parliament: 27th November 1989

Coming into force: 27th December 1989

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (East London Telecommunications Limited) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 27th December 1989.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Eric Forth — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Trade and Industry — 23rd November 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to East London Telecommunications Limited on 16th November 1989 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunications systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the London Boroughs of Redbridge and Barking and Dagenham and the whole of the London Borough of Bexley except Thamesmead Town. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 27th November 1989. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, East London Telecommunications Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Atlantic House, Holborn Viaduct, London EC1N 2HQ.
