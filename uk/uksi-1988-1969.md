# The Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 1988

Made: 31st October1988

Laid before Parliament: 18th November 1988

Coming into force: 11th January 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by sections 6(7), 8(5), 40(7) and 41 of the Data Protection Act 1984[^f00001] and after consultation with the Registrar and with the approval of the Treasury in accordance with the said section 40(7), I hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 11th January 1989.

###### 2

The Data Protection (Fees) (No. 2) Regulations 1987[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

###### 3

The fee payable under section 6(7) of the Data Protection Act 1984 on an application for registration and under section 8(5) of that Act on an application for the renewal of registration shall be £56.

## Signed

Home Office

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 14th October 1988

We approve these Regulations.

David Lightbown — David Maclean — Two of the Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 31st October 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase from £40 to £56 the fee which is to accompany an application to the Data Protection Registrar for registration or for renewal of registration under the Data Protection Act 1984.
