# The Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987 (Commencement No.3) (Scotland) Order 1988

Made: 21st March 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 50(2) of the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987([^f00001]) (“the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987 (Commencement No.3) (Scotland) Order 1988, and shall extend to Scotland only.

###### 2

The provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st June 1988.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS OF THE ACT COMING INTO FORCE ON 1ST JUNE 1988

| *Provisions of the Act* | *Subject matter of provisions* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 44 | Licensing of indoor sports premises in Scotland |
| Section 48 | Luminous tube signs: Scotland |

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 21st March 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, brings into force sections 44 and 48 of the Fire Safety and Safety of Places of Sport Act 1987 on 1st June 1988.
