# The Enterprise Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2004

Made: 17th July 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 279 of the Enterprise Act 2002[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Enterprise Act 2002 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2004.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of paragraph 4 of Schedule 5 to the Enterprise Act 2002 for all remaining purposes is the day after the day on which this Order is made.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe, — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Competition and Consumers, — Department of Trade and Industry — Date 17th July 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force paragraph 4 of Schedule 5 to the Enterprise Act 2002 for all remaining purposes. It therefore brings into force section 49(1)(b) of the Competition Act 1998 (as substituted by paragraph 4 of Schedule 5 to the Enterprise Act 2002). This provision provides a right of appeal against an award of damages by the Competition Appeal Tribunal (including a right of appeal against any amount awarded).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
