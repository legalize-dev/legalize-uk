# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 12) Order 1988

Made: 1st June 1988

Coming into force: 1st July 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 12) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st July 1988.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of each pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the companies listed in Part I of the Schedule as owners of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule. The pipe-lines are used in connection with production from the Balmoral oilfield, situated east of the Highland Region of Scotland.
