# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2004

Made: 15th February 2004

Laid before Parliament: 17th February 2004

Coming into force: 10th March 2004

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by that section, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2004; they apply in relation to England only and come into force on 10th March 2004.

#### Amendments to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2003

###### 2

The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2003[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with these Regulations.

#### Substitution of regulation 62

###### 3

For regulation 62 there shall be substituted the following—

> > (62) Any person who—
>   (a) contravenes a provision of these Regulations, other than—
>     (i) the provisions contained in regulations 8(2) and 19(3) referring to payment of costs; and
>     (ii) the provisions contained in regulations 23(7), 28, 43(5), 45(2) and 55; or
>   (b) fails to comply with a notice served upon him under these Regulations,
> shall be guilty of an offence.

#### Amendment to regulation 65

###### 4

For paragraph (1) of regulation 65 there shall be substituted the following—

> > (1) Any notice served under these Regulations by the Secretary of State, the Agency, an official veterinary surgeon, an official fish inspector or an authorised officer may be served on a person by—
>   (a) delivering it to that person;
>   (b) leaving it at his proper address; or
>   (c) posting it to his proper address.

## Signed

Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Environment Food and Rural Affairs — 15th February 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2003 ([S.I. 2003/3177](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3177)) by providing in regulation 62 that contravention of certain provisions does not constitute an offence and by correcting an error in regulation 65.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations.
