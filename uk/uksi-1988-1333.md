# The Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988

Made: 28th July 1988

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 20(3) of the Licensing Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensing Act 1988 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1988.

###### 2

The provisions of the Licensing Act 1988 specified in the Schedules to this Order shall come into force on the dates mentioned in the headings of those Schedules.

| *Provisions of the Act* | *Subject matter of provisions* |
| --- | --- |

| Section 3. | Restriction orders. |
| Section 5. | Discretion with respect to grant, and limitation, of special hours certificates. |
| Section 9. | Provisional grant of new licence or removal. |
| Section 11. | Extension of duration of justices' licences and canteen licences. |
| Section 13. | Power of clerk to licensing justices to grant unopposed renewals of justices' licences and canteen licences. |

| Section 19(1) to the extent necessary to bring into force paragraphs 1, 7, 8(a), 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15(a) and 19 of Schedule 3. | Minor amendments of the principal Act[^f00002]. |

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 28th July 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 22nd August 1988 and 1st March 1989 respectively the provisions of the Licensing Act 1988 set out in Schedules 1 and 2 to the Order. All other provisions of the Act not set out in those Schedules will come into force on 1st August 1988 (S.I. [1988/1187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1187)).
