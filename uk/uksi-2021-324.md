# The Agricultural Holdings (Transitional Provision) (England) Regulations 2021

Made: 16th March 2021

Coming into force: 17th March 2021

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 54(1) of the Agriculture Act 2020 .

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Agricultural Holdings (Transitional Provision) (England) Regulations 2021 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

- (2) These Regulations apply in England only.

#### Transitional provision

###### 2

- (1) From the day on which these Regulations come into force until the relevant date, section 39(8) of the 1986 Act as it had effect immediately before the Agriculture Act 2020 was passed applies for the purposes of section 39 of the 1986 Act (other than subsection (8A)).

- (2) In this regulation—

- “*the 1986 Act*” means the Agricultural Holdings Act 1986 ;

- “*relevant date*” means the day on which the first regulations made under section 39(8) of the 1986 Act which apply in England come into force.

## Signed

Victoria Prentis — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 16th March 2021
