# The Land Authority for Wales (Abolition) Order 1999

Made: 8th February 1999

Coming into force: 28th February 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 139(1) and 154(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Land Authority for Wales (Abolition) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 28th February 1999.

- (2) In this Order–

- “the Authority” means the Land Authority for Wales.

#### Abolition of the Authority

###### 2

The Authority is hereby abolished.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Peter Hain — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 8th February 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order abolishes the Land Authority for Wales which was established under the Community Land Act 1975[^f00002].
