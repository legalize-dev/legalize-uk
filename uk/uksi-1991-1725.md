# The Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1991

Made: 24th July 1991

Coming into force: 14th August 1991

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of July 1991 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 6(1) of, and paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 1 to, the Films Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to provide, and it is hereby provided, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Films Co-Production Agreements (Amendment) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 14th August 1991.

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Films Co-Production Agreements Order 1985 (“the principal Order”) provides that films made in accordance with the agreements between the Government of the United Kingdom and the Governments of the countries listed in the Schedule, as amended by S.I. [1985/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/2001), [1990/1513](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1513), are to be treated as British films for the purposes of Schedule 1 to the Films Act 1985 (Certification for the purposes of section 72 of the Finance Act [1982 (c. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/39) in case of British films). The present Order extends the list of agreements scheduled to the principal Order to include a further amendment made by a Protocol dated 5th July 1991 (Cm. 1592) to the agreement with Canada of 12th September 1975 (Cmnd. 6380) as amended on 9th July 1985 (Cmnd. 9887).
