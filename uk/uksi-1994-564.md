# The Birmingham City Council, (Birmingham and Fazeley Canal Bridge) Scheme 1990 Confirmation Instrument 1994

Made: 28th February 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:–

###### 1

The Birmingham City Council, (Birmingham and Fazeley Canal Bridge) Scheme 1990 is hereby confirmed with modifications.

###### 2

The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plans (as modified) are deposited at the offices of the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY and at the offices of Birmingham City Council, Ingleby House, 11–14 Cannon Street, Birmingham.

###### 3

The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 4

This Instrument may be cited as the Birmingham City Council, (Birmingham and Fazeley Canal Bridge) Scheme 1990 Confirmation Instrument 1994.

## THE SCHEDULE — THE BIRMINGHAM CITY COUNCIL, (BIRMINGHAM AND FAZELEY CANAL BRIDGE) SCHEME 1990

Birmingham City Council of The Council House, Colmore Row, Birmingham B1 1BB (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980 and all other enabling powers:–

1 The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of the Birmingham and Fazeley Canal the bridge specified in the Schedule to the Scheme as part of the highway in the City of Birmingham known as A34 Lancaster Street which they are proposing to improve (by widening).

2 This Scheme may be cited as the Birmingham City Council (Birmingham and Fazeley Canal Bridge) Scheme 1990.

## THE SCHEDULE TO THE SCHEME — PLANS AND SPECIFICATIONS OF THE BIRMINGHAM AND FAZELEY CANAL BRIDGE

A new bridge spanning 9.1m – 15.3m the navigable waters of the Birmingham and Fazeley Canal adjoining the south western side of the present bridge carrying Lancaster Street, with a headway of 2.6m as more particularly delineated on the following plans–

1 Specification Plan No. CRD/7110/16.

2 General arrangement of West Bridge Plan No. SD/B/10004/2 given under the common seal of the Council 1st March 1990.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

B. J. Billington — 28th February 1994
