# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation (Amendment) Order 2001

Made: 20th September 2001

Laid before Parliament: 24th September 2001

Coming into force: 15th October 2001

Now, therefore, the Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 18(1) and (4)(d)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation (Amendment) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 15th October 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation Order 2000[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 3

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Order 2000 (“the principal Order” which designated areas in Breckland, Clun, the North Peak, the Suffolk River Valleys and the Test Valley as environmentally sensitive areas.

The Order amends, with effect from 15th October 2001, some of the rates of payments to be made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 (*article 3*).

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of this Order.
