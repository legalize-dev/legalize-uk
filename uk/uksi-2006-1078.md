# The Monkseaton Community High School (Governing Body Procedures) Order 2006

Made: 5th April 2006

Laid before Parliament: 18th April 2006

Coming into force: 12th May 2006

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Monkseaton Community High School (Governing Body Procedures) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 12th May 2006.

#### Relaxation and modification

###### 2

Regulation 12(2) of the School Governance (Procedures) (England) Regulations 2003[^f00003] shall, in respect of the meetings of the governing body of the Monkseaton Community High School, apply to the governing body as if the words “present and” were omitted.

#### Time period

###### 3

This Order shall have effect until 11th May 2009.

## Signed

Jacqui Smith — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 5th April 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The School Governance (Procedures) (England) Regulations 2003, as amended (“the Regulations”), provide that every question to be decided at a meeting of the governing body must be determined by a majority of the votes of the governors present and voting on the question (regulation 12(2) of the Regulations). This Order enables the governing body of the Monkseaton Community High School to hold meetings otherwise than in accordance with regulation 12(2) by relaxing the requirement for all governors to be physically present at such meetings.

The Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 relaxes the requirement for all governors to be physically present in order for the governing body to make their decisions by modifying regulation 12(2) to that effect. Article 3 provides that the Order has effect until 11th May 2009.
