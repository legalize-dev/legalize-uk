# The A41 Trunk Road in Cheshire (County of Shropshire Border to the A41/A51 Junction) (Detrunking) Order 2001

Made: 21st November 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A41 Trunk Road in Cheshire (County of Shropshire Border to the A41/A51 Junction) (Detrunking) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

###### 2

In this Order—

| i “the plan” | means the plan numbered HA10/PS/256, consisting of sheets 1 and 2 and marked “The A41 Trunk Road in Cheshire (County of Shropshire Border to the A41/A51) (Detrunking) Order 2001”, signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions, and deposited at the DTLR Records Management Branch, Ashdown House, Sedlescombe Road North, St Leonards-on-Sea, East Sussex TN37 7GA. |
| --- | --- |
| ii “principal road” | as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments and instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State; and |
| iii “the trunk road” | means the A41 Trunk Road. |

###### 3

The lengths of the trunk road described in the Schedule to this Order shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road from the date on which the order comes into force.

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF THE TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The lengths of the trunk road ceasing to be trunk road are situated in the City of Chester in the County of Cheshire between the Shropshire County Council border (marked “A” on the plan) and to a point approximately 165 metres north of the junction of Bradley Green Road and the A41 Trunk Road, (marked “B” on the plan) and a point approximately 445 metres north west of the junction of Old Coach Road and the A41 Trunk Road (marked “C” on the plan) and the A41/A51 junction (marked “D” on the plan) is shown by broad black dashes on the plan, and is approximately 27km in length.
