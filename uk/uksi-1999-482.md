# The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 25th February 1999

Laid before Parliament: 3rd March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the said section 2(2) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations–

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) (Amendment) Regulations 1999, and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Amendment of the Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1996

###### 2

The Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1996[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 9 of these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 1st April 1999, amend for the third time the Common Agricultural Policy (Wine) Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/696](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/696)) (“the principal Regulations”) which provide for the enforcement of EC Regulations concerned with the production and marketing of wine and related products.

These Regulations–

- (a) revise and bring up to date Schedules 1 and 2 to the principal Regulations so as to include references to relevant EC Regulations which have been adopted since the coming into force of the second amending Regulations (S.I. [1998/453](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/453)) (regulations 3 to 8);

- (b) add the varieties “Phoenix” and “Regent” to the list of vine varieties for producing table wine, contained in Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations (regulation 9).

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of these Regulations.
