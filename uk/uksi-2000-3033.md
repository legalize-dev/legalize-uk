# The Environment Act 1995 (Commencement No. 18) (England and Wales) Order 2000

Made: 9th November 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 125(3) of the Environment Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environment Act 1995 (Commencement No. 18) (England and Wales) Order 2000.

#### Provision coming into force on 1st December 2000

###### 2

Section 116 of the Environment Act 1995 shall come into force on 1st December 2000 in so far as it relates to paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 21[^f00002].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Michael Meacher — Minister of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 9th November 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 brings into force on 1st December 2000 paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 21 to the Environment Act 1995, which substitutes a new section 221 of the Water Industry Act 1991 (Crown application).

## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [1995 c. 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25). Though some functions of the Secretary of State under the 1995 Act in relation to Wales were transferred by article 2 of and Schedule 1 to the National Assembly of Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/672](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/672)), the functions of the Secretary of State under section 125 of the 1995 Act are retained in relation to Wales, *see* article 2(e) of S.I. [1999/672](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/672).

[^f00002]: This paragraph substitutes section 221 of the Water Industry Act [1991 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56). Section 221 of that Act extends to England and Wales only (*see* section 222(3) of that Act), and accordingly so does paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 21 to the Environment Act 1995 (*see* section 125(10) of that Act).
