# The Pensions Increase (Compensation to Staff of Teachers' Training Establishments) Regulations 1975

Made: 9th September 1975

Laid before Parliament: 18th September 1975

Coming into Operation: 10th October 1975

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(2) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Compensation to Staff of Teachers' Training Establishments) Regulations 1975, and shall come into operation on 1975.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these Regulations as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 3

These Regulations shall apply to a pension payable under Part IV or Part V of the Colleges of Education (Compensation) Regulations 1975[^f00001], other than a pension which, before the date on which these Regulations come into operation, is an official pension for the purposes of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971.

###### 4

The Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 shall have effect in relation to a pension to which these Regulations apply as if it were specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to that Act.

## Signed

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Civil Service on 9th September 1975.

C.R. Morris — Minister of State — Civil Service Department

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for the payment of increases under the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 on the following kinds of compensation payable under the Colleges of Education (Compensation) Regulations 1975:—

(*a*) long-term compensation for loss of employment or loss or diminution of emoluments;

(*b*) retirement compensation for loss of employment or loss or diminution of emoluments;

(*c*) compensation to the widow, child or other dependant or to the personal representatives of a claimant who was a pensionable officer.

The Regulations apply to a compensation payment which does not already qualify for increases under the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971, namely, a payment in respect of service as a member of the non-teaching staff of a teachers' training establishment which is not maintained by a local education authority.
