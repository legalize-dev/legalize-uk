# The Milk (Cessation of Production) (Revocation) Scheme 2007

Made: 18th February 2007

Laid before Parliament: 26th February 2007

Coming into force: 6th April 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Milk (Cessation of Production) Act 1985[^f00001], makes the following Scheme:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the Milk (Cessation of Production) (Revocation) Scheme 2007 and comes into force on 6th April 2007.

#### Revocations

###### 2

- (1) The following Schemes are revoked in relation to England only—

- (a) the Milk (Partial Cessation of Production) (England and Wales) Scheme 1986[^f00002]; and

- (b) the Milk (Cessation of Production) (England and Wales) Scheme 1987[^f00003].

- (2) The following Schemes are revoked—

- (a) the Milk (Partial Cessation of Production) (Scotland) Scheme 1986[^f00004]; and

- (b) the Milk (Cessation of Production) (Scotland) Scheme 1987[^f00005].

## Signed

Jeff Rooker — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 18th February 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme revokes four milk (cessation of production) schemes which are no longer in operation.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
