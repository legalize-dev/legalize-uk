# The Bristol Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1995

Made: 6th November 1995

Laid before Parliament: 15th November 1995

Coming into force: 1st January 1996

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 134 and 135 of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 26 to, the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Bristol Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

#### Urban development area

###### 2

- (1) Articles 2 and 3 of the Bristol Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1988[^f00002] and the whole of the Bristol Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 1988[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

- (2) The Bristol Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1988 shall be revoked with effect from 1st April 1996.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

David Curry — Minister of State, — Department of the Environment — 6th November 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Bristol Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1988 which designated the Bristol urban development area and established the Bristol Development Corporation. Articles 2 and 3 of that Order are revoked from 1st January 1996 and the remainder from 1st April 1996. The Bristol Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 1988 is also revoked from 1st January 1996.
