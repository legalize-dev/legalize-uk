# The Companies Act 1985 (Operating and Financial Review) (Repeal) Regulations 2005

Made: 14th December 2005

Laid before Parliament: 15th December 2005

Coming into force: 12th January 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 257 of the Companies Act 1985[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 1985 (Operating and Financial Review) (Repeal) Regulations 2005.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 12th January 2006.

- (3) In these Regulations “the 1985 Act” means the Companies Act 1985.

#### Repeal of requirement to prepare operating and financial review

###### 2

- (1) Section 234AA of the 1985 Act (duty of directors of quoted company to prepare operating and financial review)[^f00002] is repealed.

- (2) In consequence of that repeal—

- (a) the 1985 Act is amended in accordance with Schedule 1 to these Regulations, and

- (b) the enactments specified in Schedule 2 to these Regulations are amended in accordance with that Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations remove the requirement in section 234AA of the Companies Act 1985 for directors of quoted companies (as defined in section 262(1) of that Act) to prepare operating and financial reviews for financial years beginning on or after 1st April 2005 (regulation 2(1)). Regulation 2(2)(a) and Schedule 1 amend the Companies Act 1985 consequentially on that repeal. Regulation 2(2)(b) and Schedule 2 make consequential amendments to the Companies (Revision of Defective Accounts and Report) Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/2570](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2570), as last amended by S.I. [2005/2282](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2282)), the Companies (Summary Financial Statement) Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/2092](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2092), as last amended by S.I. [2005/2281](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2281)) and the Limited Liability Partnerships Regulations 2001 (S.I. [2001/1090](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1090), as last amended by S.I. [2005/1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1989)).

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from the Department of Trade and Industry, Corporate Law and Governance Directorate, 1 Victoria Street, London, SW1H 0ET (it is also available electronically at www.dti.gov.uk/cld). A copy has been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament.
