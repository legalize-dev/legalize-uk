Section 425(1) of the Act provides that the definition applies for the purposes of the Act. The existing definition of “EEA State” excludes Bulgaria and Romania which became Community member States on 1st January 2007. It is amended in order correctly to transpose the single market directives (listed in paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to the Act)."
