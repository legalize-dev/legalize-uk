# The Cosmetic Products (Safety) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006

Made: 15th August 2006

Laid before Parliament: 17th August 2006

Coming into force: 1st September 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 11 of the Consumer Protection Act 1987 .

In accordance with section 11(5) of that Act he has consulted such organisations as appear to him to be representative of interests substantially affected by the following Regulations, such other persons as he considered appropriate and the Health and Safety Commission.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Cosmetic Products (Safety) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 1st September 2006.

- (2) In these Regulations “*the Principal Regulations*” means the Cosmetic Products (Safety) Regulations 2004 .

#### Amendment to the Principal Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Principal Regulations are amended as follows.

- (2) After regulation 5(15)(c) insert—

> > (d) under entry numbers 1212 to 1233 (inserted by Directive [2006/65/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0065)) shall be placed on the market or supplied after 31st November 2006;
> provided that no account shall be taken of any substance which is present only as a trace which could not reasonably have been removed during or after manufacture.

- (3) In Schedule 2 (List of Directives amending Directive [76/867/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0867)) insert at the end—

> > (46) Commission Directive [2006/65/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0065) (O.J. No. L198, 20.07.2006, p.11).

- (4) In Part 2 of Schedule 4 delete the entries numbered 17, 23, 40 and 42.

- (5) In Part 2 of Schedule 4—

- (a) in the entries numbered 1, 2, 8, 13, 15, 30, 34, 41, 43, 45, 46, 51, 52, 53, 54, 57, 59 and 60 for the date “31.8.2006” in the seventh column wherever it occurs substitute “ 31.12.2007 ”;

- (b) in the entries numbered 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 31, 32, 33, 35, 36, 37, 38, 39, 44, 47, 48, 49, 50, 55, 56 and 58 for the date “31.12.2006” in the seventh column wherever it occurs substitute “ 31.12.2007 ”.
