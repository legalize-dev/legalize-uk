# The Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1988

Made: 8th March 1988

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1988

Coming into force: 1st April 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 104(1) of the Friendly Societies Act 1974[^f00001] and by article 2 of the Friendly Societies (Fees) Order 1971[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st April 1988.

###### 2

The Friendly Societies Regulations 1975[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for Schedule 2 thereto the following Schedule:—

The Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1987[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations supersede the Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations 1987. They generally increase by about 6% the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted and for the inspection of documents under the Friendly Societies Act 1974. However, fees for registration of a society using model rules remain unchanged at £150, and certain other fees remain unchanged.
