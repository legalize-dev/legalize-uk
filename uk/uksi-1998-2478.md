# The Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1998

Made: 5th October 1998

Coming into force: 19th October 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 19th October 1998.

#### Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 2

In article 3(2) of the Leeds Community and Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00005] (functions of the trust) there shall be added the following sub-paragraph–

> > (e) to provide and manage under section 5(1)(b) of the Act hospitals and other establishments and facilities in the area of Leeds Health Authority associated with the reprovision of hospital accommodation and services from High Royd’s Hospital, Menston, Ilkley, LS29 6AQ and Roundhay Wing, St. James’s University Hospital, Beckett Street, Leeds LS9 7TF (including the management of teaching and research facilities) and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services and community health services.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Alan Milburn — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 5th October 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, namely to provide and manage hospitals or other establishments or facilities in the area of Leeds Health Authority.
