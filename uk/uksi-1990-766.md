# The Juvenile Courts (London) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made: 28th March 1990

Coming into force: 1st April 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by paragraphs 14 and 20 of Schedule 2 to the Children and Young Persons Act 1933[^f00001], as amended by section 17(1) of the Children and Young Persons Act 1963[^f00002] read with section 12(1) of the Administration of Justice Act 1964[^f00003], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Juvenile Courts (London) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

###### 2

In this Order, “the 1975 Order” means the Juvenile Courts (London) Order 1975[^f00004].

###### 3

For Schedule 1 to the 1975 Order there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

David Waddington — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 28th March 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Juvenile Courts (London) Order 1975 as amended by the Juvenile Courts (London) (Amendment) Order 1984, which assigns divisions to juvenile courts for the Inner London boroughs and the City of London and specifies the places where such courts may sit. The effect of the Order is to provide a new address for juvenile court sittings which formerly took place at The Court House, 58B Bow Road, E3 4DJ and which served the Thames division and the City of London. The new courthouse is situated at 66 Bow Road, E3 4DH.
