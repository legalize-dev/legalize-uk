# The Sex Offenders Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made: 2nd August 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 10(2) of the Sex Offenders Act 1997[^f00001] the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Sex Offenders Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997.

###### 2

The Sex Offenders Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st September 1997.

## Signed

Home Office

Alun Michael — Minister of State — 2nd August 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Sex Offenders Act 1997 on 1st September 1997.
