# The Local Government Act 1985 (Police and Fire and Civil Defence Authorities) Precepts Limitation Order 1988

Made: 9th February 1988

Coming into force: 14th February 1988

Now, therefore, I, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 1 and 4(4) of the Rates Act 1984, as applied by section 68(4) of the Local Government Act 1985, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1985 (Police and Fire and Civil Defence Authorites) Precepts Limitation Order 1988 and shall come into force on 14th February 1988.

###### 2

There is hereby prescribed, under section 1 of the Rates Act 1984, as applied by section 68(4) of the Local Government Act 1985, as the maximum amount in the pound for the precept issued for the financial year beginning on 1st April 1988 by an authority listed in the Schedule to this Order, the amount there set against the name of the authority.

## SCHEDULE

| Greater Manchester Police Authority | 15.38p |
| --- | --- |
| Northumbria Police Authority | 14.40p |
| West Midlands Police Authority | 14.11p |
| West Yorkshire Police Authority | 15.72p |
| Merseyside Fire and Civil Defence Authority | 8.39p |
| South Yorkshire Fire and Civil Defence Authority | 11.21p |
| West Yorkshire Fire and Civil Defence Authority | 13.41p |

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of StateZ — 9th February 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes maximum precepts, in respect of the financial year beginning on 1st April 1988, for such of the police and fire and civil defence authorities established under Part IV of the Local Government Act 1985 as are listed in the Schedule to the Order. In each case the maximum is shown against the name of the authority (in respect of the current financial year, precepts were set by section 7 of and Schedule 2 to the Local Government Finance Act [1987 (c. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/6), obviating the need for an Order in 1987).
