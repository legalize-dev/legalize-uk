#### Amendment of the Education (Independent School Standards) Regulations 2014

###### 9

- (1) The Education (Independent School Standards) Regulations 2014 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “the National Minimum Standards for Accommodation of Students under Eighteen by Further Education Colleges”; and

- (b) in the definitions of “the National Minimum Standards for Boarding Schools” and “the National Minimum Standards for Residential Special Schools”, for “November 2012” in both places where it occurs, substitute “ April 2015 ”.

- (3) In paragraph 8(b) of Part 3 of the Schedule, omit “or the National Minimum Standards for Accommodation of Students under Eighteen by Further Education Colleges”.
