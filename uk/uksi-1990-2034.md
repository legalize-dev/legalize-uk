# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions)(Copper or Other Metal Poisoning) (England) (Revocation) Order 1990

Made: 16th October 1990

Laid before Parliament: 17th October 1990

Coming into force: 17th October 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Copper or Other Metal Poisoning) (England) (Revocation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 17 October 1990.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Copper or Other Metal Poisoning) (England) Order 1990[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 16 October 1990.

Trumpington — Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Copper or Other Metal Poisoning) (England) Order 1990 which contained emergency prohibitions in order to prevent human consumption of food which may have been rendered unsuitable for that purpose in consequence of animals consuming copper or some other metal.
