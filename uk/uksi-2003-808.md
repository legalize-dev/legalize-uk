# The Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No.4) Order 2003

Made: 17th March 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 108(2) of the Police Reform Act 2002[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2003.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Police Reform Act 2002 shall come into force on 1st April 2003—

- (a) section 9 and Schedule 2 (Independent Police Complaints Commission) to the extent not already in force;

- (b) section 39 (police powers for contracted-out staff) in so far as not already in force;

- (c) section 42 (supplementary provisions relating to designations and accreditations);

- (d) sections 51 to 53 (persons in police detention);

- (e) sections 54, 55, 57 and 58 (blood samples) to the extent not already in force;

- (f) section 63 and sections 65 and 66 (anti-social behaviour) to the extent not already in force;

- (g) section 86(1), (2)(b) and (5)(police members of NCIS);

- (h) section 87(1), (2)(b) and (5)(police members of NCS);

- (i) section 96 (president of ACPO);

- (j) section 97(2) in so far as it relates to section 5(1)(c) and (d) of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00002], (6) and (12)(crime and disorder reduction partnerships);

- (k) section 107(2) (repeals) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 8 in paragraph (l) below; and

- (l) in Schedule 8 (repeals), the entries relating to:

- (i) the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994;

- (ii) Schedules 2 and 2A to the Police Act 1996;

- (iii) sections 9 and 55 of the Police Act 1997; and

- (iv) section 80(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001.

## Signed

John Denham — Minister of State — 17th March 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Police Reform Act 2002 set out in article 2 on 1st April 2003.
