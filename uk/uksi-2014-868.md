# The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

Made: 31st March 2014

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

In accordance with section 53(3) of that Act, a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

- (2) Regulation 2(a) applies only in relation to a person—

- (a) first diagnosed with mesothelioma on or after the day on which these Regulations come into force; or

- (b) where the date of the first diagnosis with mesothelioma is unknown, if a claim is made by that person on or after the day on which these Regulations come into force.

- (3) Regulation 2(b) applies only in relation to the dependant of a person who dies on or after the day on which these Regulations come into force.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Schedule to the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations 2008 by substituting revised tables of the amount of lump sum payments payable to people with mesothelioma or to their dependants.

A full impact assessment has not been published for this instrument as it has no impact on the private sector or civil society organisations.
