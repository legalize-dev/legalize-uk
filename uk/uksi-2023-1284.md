# The African Development Bank (Sixteenth Replenishment of the African Development Fund) Order 2023

Made: 28th November 2023

Coming into force: 29th November 2023

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the African Development Bank (Sixteenth Replenishment of the African Development Fund) Order 2023 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “*the Bank*” means the African Development Bank established by an agreement dated 4th August 1963, as ratified by the Government on 29th April 1983[^f00002];

- “*the Fund*” means the African Development Fund of the Bank established by an agreement dated 29th November 1972, as ratified by the Government on 30th June 1973[^f00003];

- “*the Government*” means the Government of the United Kingdom;

- “*the Resolution*” means Resolution No. F/BG/2023/01 entitled ‘The Sixteenth General Replenishment of the Resources of the African Development Fund’ adopted by the Board of Governors of the Fund on 2nd April 2023.

#### Relevant Payments

###### 3

The Secretary of State may, on behalf of the Government—

- (a) make payment of a further contribution to the Fund not exceeding £450 million in accordance with the arrangements made between the Government and the Fund in accordance with the Resolution, and

- (b) make payment of sums required to redeem any non-interest-bearing and non-negotiable notes or other obligations which may be issued or created by the Secretary of State and accepted by the Fund pursuant to the arrangements mentioned in paragraph (a),

in accordance with section 11 of the Act.

## Signed

Andrew Mitchell — Minister of State — Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office — 23rd November 2023

We approve,

Scott Mann — Stuart Anderson — Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty’s Treasury — 28th November 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order approves the making of payments on behalf of the Government of the United Kingdom to the African Development Fund of sums not exceeding £450 million as a further contribution to the resources of the African Development Fund. The payment approved by this Order will be made pursuant to Resolution No F/BG/2023/01 adopted by the Board of Governors of the Fund on 2nd April 2023. The Order also provides for the redemption of non-interest-bearing and non-negotiable notes issued by the Secretary of State pursuant to those arrangements.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.

The provisions of the Resolution may be obtained by application to the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office, King Charles Street, London SW1A 2AH.
