# The Geneva Conventions (Amendment) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1998

Made: 24th June 1998

Coming into force: 20th July 1998

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of June 1998 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 7(2) of the Geneva Conventions (Amendment) Act 1995[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Geneva Conventions (Amendment) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1998.

###### 2

The Geneva Conventions (Amendment) Act 1995 shall come into force on 20th July 1998.

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Geneva Conventions (Amendment) Act 1995 into force on 20th July 1998.
