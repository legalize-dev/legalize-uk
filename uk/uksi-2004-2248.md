# The Primary Care Trusts (Establishment) (Amendment No.3) Order 2004

Made: 31st August 2004

Coming into force: 1st October 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Primary Care Trusts (Establishment) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st October 2004.

- (2) This Order applies to England.

#### Amendment of Primary Care Trust Establishment Orders

###### 2

The Schedule (area of trust) to each of the Primary Care Trust Establishment Orders listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall be amended as specified in the corresponding entry in column 2.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the areas of the Primary Care Trusts listed in the Schedule to reflect changes in the boundaries of those Primary Care Trusts.
