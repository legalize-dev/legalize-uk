# The Infrastructure Act 2015 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2015

Made: 21st July 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 57(7)(c) of the Infrastructure Act 2015[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Infrastructure Act 2015 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2015.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the Act” means the Infrastructure Act 2015.

#### Provisions coming into force on 30th July 2015

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the day appointed for the coming into force of section 50 (onshore hydraulic fracturing: safeguards) of the Act is 30th July 2015.

- (2) Section 50 comes into force only for the purposes of the insertion of the following provisions in the Petroleum Act 1998[^f00002]—

- (a) section 4B(4);

- (b) section 4B(5);

- (c) section 4B(6);

- (d) section 4B(7).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Andrea Leadsom — Minister of State — Department of Energy and Climate Change — 21st July 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force section 50 of the Infrastructure Act [2015 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/7) (“the Act”) for the purpose only of the duty to make provision by regulations to define “protected groundwater source areas” and “other protected areas” in section 4A of the Petroleum Act [1998 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/17). In consequence, the Secretary of State is required to lay a draft of such regulations before Parliament by 31st July 2015.

An impact assessment of the effect that section 50 of the Act will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector has been produced, and a copy is available at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukia/2015/254/pdfs/ukia_20150254_en.pdf. No separate impact assessment has been produced for this instrument.
