# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2020

Made: 17th April 2020

Laid before Parliament: 21st April 2020

Coming into force: 13th May 2020

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that the conditions in section 1(1) of that Act are satisfied[^f00002] and has, in accordance with section 18(4) of that Act, consulted with such persons and bodies of persons as appear requisite to the Secretary of State.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2020 and comes into force on 13th May 2020.

#### Amendment to the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015

###### 2

- (1) The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 2 (sports grounds designated under article 2(2)), after the entry for Griffin Park, occupied by Brentford Football Club, insert—

| Brentford Community Stadium, Lionel Road South, Brentford TW8 0RU | Brentford Football Club and London Irish Rugby Football Club |
| --- | --- |

## Signed

Nigel Huddleston — Minister for Sport, Tourism and Heritage — Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport — 17th April 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/661](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/661)) so as to designate Brentford Community Stadium, Brentford, as a sports ground for which a safety certificate is required under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act [1975 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/52).

Brentford Community Stadium is occupied by Brentford Football Club (a member of the Football League Limited) and London Irish Rugby Football Club, and is a sports ground in England and Wales at which association football matches are played and which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or significant, impact on private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

[^f00002]: By section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975, the Secretary of State may by order designate as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under that Act any sports ground which, in the Secretary of State’s opinion, has accommodation for more than 10,000 spectators.
