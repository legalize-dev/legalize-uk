# The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2018

Made: 29th January 2018

Coming into force: 1st April 2018

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 29th day of January 2018

By the Lords of Her Majesty's Most Honourable Privy Council

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2018 and comes into force on 1st April 2018.

#### Approval

###### 2

The Regulations set out in the Schedule are approved.

## SCHEDULE — The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

The Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, in exercise of its powers under section 11 of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966, makes the following Regulations.

For Part 4 (Fees payable) of the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) Regulations 2014, substitute—

| Type of registrant | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| New graduates registered by 31 December following graduation | 165 |
| Other persons registered between 1 April and 30 September | 330 |
| Other persons registered between 1 October and 31 March | 165 |

| Type of fee | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| Normal registration fee | 330 |
| Registration fee for practice restricted to period of six months or less | 165 |
| Retention fee | 330 |

| Circumstances | Column A – Fee payable if payment received on or before 30 April (£) | Column B – Fee payable if payment received after 30 April (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard registrant | 330 | 365 |
| Overseas practising registrant | 165 | 200 |
| Non-practising registrant | 54 | 89 |

| Fee paid (£) | New circumstance | *Extra sum payable if change takes place before 1 October* *(£)* | *Extra sum payable if change takes place on or after 1 October* *(£)* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 54 or 89 | Practising, but not in the British Islands | 111 | 55 |
| 54 or 89 | Practising in the British Islands | 276 | 138 |
| 165 or 200 | Practising in the British Islands | 165 | 82 |

| Fee paid (£) | New circumstance | Sum to be credited (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 330 or 365 | Non-practising registrant | 138 |
| 330 or 365 | Overseas practising registrant | 82 |
| 165 or 200 | Non-practising registrant | 55 |

| Type of restoration | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| Restoration under section 11(3) of the Act following removal for non-receipt of retention fee | 330 |
| Restoration in other circumstances | 82 |

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
