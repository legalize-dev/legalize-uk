# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement) (No. 7) Order 2006

Made: 12th July 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 178(8) and (9) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001], makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement) (No. 7) Order 2006.

###### 2

Section 144 of and Schedule 10 to the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (parental compensation orders)[^f00002] shall come into force on 20th July 2006 in the areas specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

###### 1

The counties of Hampshire, Hertfordshire, Nottinghamshire and Worcestershire.

###### 2

The cities of Leicester and York.

###### 3

The metropolitan boroughs of Gateshead and South Tyneside.

###### 4

The London boroughs of Southwark and Wandsworth.

## Signed

Tony McNulty — Minister of State — 12th July 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 144 of and Schedule 10 to the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (“the Act”) on 20th July 2006 in the ten local authority areas listed in the Schedule. Section 144 of and Schedule 10 to the Act insert sections 13A to 13E into the Crime and Disorder Act [1998 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37) and make further minor consequential amendments which make provision about parental compensation orders.
