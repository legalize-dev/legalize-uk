# The Immigration Appeals (Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 1991

Made: 8th July 1991

Laid before Parliament: 16th July 1991

Coming into force: 31st October 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22 of, and paragraph 25 of Schedule 2 to, the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Rules:–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Immigration Appeals (Procedure) (Amendment) Rules 1991 and shall come into force on 31st October 1991.

#### Amendment of Immigration Appeals (Procedure) Rules 1984

###### 2

In rule 27 of the Immigration Appeals (Procedure) Rules 1984, for “10 miles” there shall be substituted “16 kilometres”.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C — 8th July 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Immigration Appeals (Procedure) Rules 1984 to substitute a measurement in kilometres for a measurement in miles in rule 27, in pursuance of the European Community Council Directive of 27th November 1989 amending Directive [80/181/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0181) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to units of measurement ([89/617/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0617))
