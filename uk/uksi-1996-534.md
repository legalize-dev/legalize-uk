# The National Park Authorities (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 5th March 1996

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 1996

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 63(1) and (2) and 75(3), (4) and (7) of the Environment Act 1995[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Park Authorities (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order:—

- “the 1995 Act” means the Environment Act 1995;

- “the principal Order” means the National Park Authorities (Wales) Order 1995[^f00002].

#### Application of other enactments and instruments with modifications

###### 3

- (1) The principal Order shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraph.

- (2) In Schedule 5 to the principal Order (application of other enactments and instruments with modifications) after paragraph 2 there shall be inserted the paragraphs set out in Part 1 of the Schedule hereto (applications of other enactments with modifications) and after paragraph 10 there shall be added the paragraphs set out in Part 2 of the Schedule hereto (application of other instruments with modifications).

## THE SCHEDULE

## PART 1 — APPLICATION OF OTHER ENACTMENTS WITH MODIFICATIONS

###### 1

After paragraph 2 of Schedule 5 to the principal Order insert the following paragraphs—

> > (2A)
>   (1) Subject to the following provisions of this paragraph, Part VIII of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00003] (financial administration) shall apply as if a National Park authority were a relevant authority as referred to in that Part.
>   (2) In relation to a National Park authority, Part VIII of that Act shall have effect as if references to the commencement day were construed as references to the 1st April 1996.
>   (3) In relation to the person having responsibility for the administration of the financial affairs of a National Park authority under section 151 of the 1972 Act, section 113(2)(b) of the Act of 1988 (qualifications of responsible officer) shall have effect as if a National Park authority were an authority mentioned in section 111(2)(a) to (k) of that Act of 1988.
> (2B) Schedule 11 to the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00004] shall apply as if in paragraph 1(3) (persons to be notified of a compulsory works order application) at the end there were added the following sub-paragraph—
>   (h) a National Park authority whose area is or includes the whole or any part of a relevant locality.
> (2C) The Water Resources Act 1991[^f00005] shall apply as if—
>   (a) In section 72(2)(a) (watercourses excluded from Chapter II Part II of the Act) at the end there were added the following sub-paragraph—
>     (v) a National Park authority.
>   (b) In section 158(1) (works agreements for water resources purposes) the words “or National Park authority” were inserted after “joint planning board”;
>   (c) In section 197(5) (provision of information about water flow etc.) the words “or National Park authority” were inserted after “joint planning board”;
>   (d) In Schedule 5 (procedure relating to statements on minimum acceptable flow) in paragraph 2(3)(a) the words “or National Park authority” were inserted after “joint planning board”; and
>   (e) Schedule 6 (orders providing for exemption from restrictions on abstraction) in paragraph 1(4)(b) the words “or National Park authority” were inserted after “joint planning board”.
> (2D) The Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00006] shall apply as if a National Park authority were a local authority for the purposes of section 11(4)(a) (inquiries and hearings).

## PART 2 — APPLICATION OF OTHER INSTRUMENTS WITH MODIFICATIONS

###### 2

After paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to the principal Order add the following paragraphs—

> > (11) The Commons (Schemes) Regulations 1982[^f00007] shall apply as if—
>   (a) a National Park authority were a council for the purposes of those Regulations;
>   (b) In paragraph 1 of Form I (Form of Scheme) of the Schedule the words “or National Park authority” were inserted after the words “district council”; and
>   (c) In Form II (Form of Notice) of the Schedule the words “or National Park authority” were inserted after the words “district council”.
> (12) The Operations in Areas of Archaeological Importance (Forms of Notice etc.) Regulations 1984[^f00008] shall apply as if—
>   (a) in regulation 4 (prescribed steps to be taken by local councils) the words “or National Park authority” were inserted after the “council”; and
>   (b) in Part I of Schedule I (form of notice of carrying out operations) in Service of This Notice—
>     (i) after the words “London borough council or” there were inserted the words “where the site of the operations is within a National Park, the notice is to be served instead on the National Park authority or”;
>     (ii) after the word “situated” there were inserted the words “*except* that where the site is partially within a National Park the notice is to be served on the National Park authority to the extent that the site is within its area and”; and
>     (iii) after the words “such council” there were inserted “or authority”.
> (13) The Removal and Disposal of Vehicles Regulations 1986[^f00009] shall apply as if in paragraph (cc) of the definition of “local authority” in regulation 12(1)(e) after the word “Wales,” there were inserted “a National Park authority or”.
> (14) The Set-Aside Regulations 1988[^f00010] shall apply as if in regulation 2(1) the definition of “National Park Authority” included a National Park authority established by this Order.
> (15) The Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Regulations 1990[^f00011] shall apply as if—
>   (a) in regulation 9(1) (claims for compensation and listed building purchase notices) after the words “London borough” there were inserted “or a National Park authority”; and
>   (b) in paragraph (2) of the form set out in Part II of Schedule I (notification of refusal of listed building consent or conservation area consent) after the words “council of the district” there were inserted the words “or National Park authority”.
> (16) The Litter Control Areas Order 1991[^f00012] shall apply as if in paragraph (1)(j) of article 2 (land which may be designated as a litter control area) there were added at the end—
>   (xiii) a National Park authority
> (17) The Town and Country Planning General Regulations 1992[^f00013] shall apply as if in regulation 12(1) after “London borough” there were inserted the words “or National Park authority”.
> (18) The Transport and Works (Model Clauses for Railways and Tramways) Order 1992[^f00014] shall have effect as if paragraph 12(7)(a) of Schedule 1 (model clauses for railways) and paragraph 17(7)(a) of Schedule 2 (model clauses for tramways) included a reference to a sewer or drain which belongs to a National Park authority.
> (19) The Local Authorities (Recovery of Costs for Public Path Orders) Regulations 1993[^f00015] shall apply as if a National Park authority were an authority for the purposes of the Regulations.
> (20) The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations 1994[^f00016] shall apply as if the definition of “local authority” in regulation 6(3)(b) (competent authorities) included a National Park authority.
> (21) The Town and Country Planning (General Development Procedure) Order 1995[^f00017] shall apply as if in Part 2 of Schedule I to that Order (notification to be sent to applicant when planning permission is refused or granted subject to conditions) in the final paragraph after the words “City of London” there were inserted “or National Park authority”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Gwilym Jones — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 5th March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the National Park Authorities (Wales) Order 1995. Article 18 of and Schedule 5 to the principal Order apply to the National Park authorities in Wales certain enactments and instruments, some with modifications. This Order applies to those authorities the further enactments and instruments some with modifications, which are specified in the Schedule to this Order.
