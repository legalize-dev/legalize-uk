# The Visiting Forces (Designation) Order 2010

Made: 15th December 2010

Coming into force: 16th December 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of December 2010Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty, on the advice of Her Privy Council and in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1(2) and 15(2) of the 1952 Act, makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Visiting Forces (Designation) Order 2010 and shall come in to force on 16th December 2010.

###### 2

Algeria is hereby designated for the purposes of sections 2 to 19 of, and the Schedule to, the 1952 Act.

###### 3

This Order shall not extend to any of the territories specified in section 15(3) of the 1952 Act.

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The United Kingdom and Algeria concluded a defence co-operation accord in October 2009. In order to effect the provision made in that accord, it is necessary to designate Algeria as a country to which the Visiting Forces Act 1952 applies.
