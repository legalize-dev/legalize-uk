# The European Parliamentary Elections (Day of By-election) (Merseyside West Constituency) Order 1996

Made: 22nd October 1996

Laid before Parliament: 30th October 1996

Coming into force: 1st November 1996

Now, therefore, in pursuance of paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1978[^f00002] the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Day of By-election) (Merseyside West Constituency) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st November 1996.

###### 2

The by-election to fill the vacancy in the European Parliamentary constituency of Merseyside West shall be held on 12th December 1996.

## Signed

Home Office

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 22nd October 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 12th December 1996 as the day on which the by-election to fill the vacancy in the European Parliamentary constituency of Merseyside West shall be held.
