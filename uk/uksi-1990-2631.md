# The Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Variation) Order 1990

Made: 21st December 1990

Laid before Parliament: 11th January 1991

Coming into force: 1st February 1991

In pursuance of section 7(4) and (5) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00001], on the recommendation of the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Variation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st February 1991.

###### 2

The Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order 1986[^f00002] shall be varied by the addition, at the end of paragraph 1(a) of Part I of the Schedule, of “*N*/Hydroxy-tenamphetamine” and “4-Methyl-aminorex”.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 21st December 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order 1986 by adding to Part I of the Schedule (which specifies the controlled drugs which are designated as drugs to which section 7(4) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 applies) *N*/Hydroxy-tenamphetamine and 4-Methyl-aminorex. These drugs become subject to control under that Act by virtue of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Modification) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/2589](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2589)).
