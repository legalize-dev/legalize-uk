## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order extends to Jersey, with modifications, Part 1 and Part 4 of the Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015 (“the Act”) together with provisions of Schedules 1 to 5 to the Act dependent on those Parts.

Part 1 of the Act contains powers to place temporary restrictions on travel. The powers relate to the seizure of travel documents and the imposition of temporary exclusion orders.

Part 4 of the Act enables authority-to-carry schemes to be introduced and amends legislation in relation to the provision of passenger, crew and service information.
