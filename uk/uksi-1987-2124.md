# The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987

Made: 8th December 1987

Laid: 11th December 1987

Coming into force: 1st January 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16 and 34 of the Vehicles (Excise) Act (Northern Ireland) 1972[^f00001], now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987 and shall come into force on 1st January 1988.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1973[^f00003] shall be further amended in accordance with these Regulations.

###### 3

In regulation 29(1)(a), the words from “and all recovery vehicles” to “business” shall be omitted.

###### 4

In regulation 36 —

- (a) in paragraph (3) the words “or a recovery vehicle” to “business” shall be omitted, and

- (b) sub-paragraph (l) of paragraph 4 shall be omitted.

###### 5

In regulation 39(1), sub-paragraph (b) shall be omitted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Peter Bottomley — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 8th December 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations further amend the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1973 in consequence of the Finance Act 1987.

2 Section 2 of the Finance Act 1987 amended the Vehicles (Excise) Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 for the purpose of establishing recovery vehicles as a class of vehicles chargeable with a specific duty of excise.

3 Previously, under section 16 of the 1972 Act a motor trader could take out a trade licence for all recovery vehicles kept by him for the purpose of dealing with disabled vehicles in the course of that business. As a result of the amendments made by the 1987 Act trade licences can no longer be taken out for a recovery vehicle kept for that purpose.

4 Part V of the Principal Regulations deals with trade licences. The amendments remove from Part V the provisions relating to recovery vehicles.
