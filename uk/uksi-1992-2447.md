# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1992

Made: 14th October 1992

Laid before Parliament: 14th October 1992

Coming into force: 6th November 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 49 and 51(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 6th November 1992.

###### 2

The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988[^f00003] shall be further amended in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations (“the 1988 Regulations”) as follows.

Regulation 3 makes the following amendments to the fees prescribed by the 1988 Regulations:

- (a) The fee payable on an application for a first examination or periodical test is increased from £32.40 to £34.70 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £17.60 to £17.90 in the case of a trailer.

- (b) The fee for a re-test or further re-test within 14 days after the first examination or earlier re-test at the same station or at another station and by arrangement is increased from £16.40 to £17.60 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £9.50 to £9.70 in the case of a trailer. The fee for re-tests in other circumstances is increased from £32.40 to £34.70 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £17.60 to £17.90 in the case of a trailer.

- (c) The fee payable on a request for an amendment to a plating certificate following a notifiable alteration or for a re-examination where a particular in the certificate is or may be no longer applicable is increased from £13.60 to £13.90.

- (d) The fee payable on an application for a replacement for a lost or defaced plate, certificate or Ministry test date disc is increased from £9.30 to £9.50.
