# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2014

Made: 18th September 2014

Laid before Parliament: 19th September 2014

Coming into force: 10th October 2014

In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted with such persons as appear to the Secretary of State to be requisite.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2014 and comes into force on 10th October 2014.

#### Designation

###### 2

The sports ground specified in the Schedule to this Order, being a sports ground—

- (a) at which association football matches are played;

- (b) which is occupied by a club which is a member of the Football Association Premier League Limited; and

- (c) which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators[^f00002],

is designated as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

## SCHEDULE

| Sports Ground | Occupier of Sports Ground |
| --- | --- |
| The City Football Academy Arena Etihad Campus 400 Ashton New Road Manchester M11 4TQ | Manchester City Football Club Limited |

## Signed

Helen Grant — Minister for Sport and Tourism — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 18th September 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the City Football Academy Arena, occupied by Manchester City Football Club Limited, as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument.
