# The Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Fees) Order 1991

Made: 30th September 1991

Laid before Parliament: 7th October 1991

Coming into force: 2nd December 1991

In pursuance of sections 18(1)(f) and 24(2) of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Fees) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

- (2) This Order shall not extend to Scotland.

###### 2

The fee payable under paragraph 18 of Schedule 3 to the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00002] (which relates to permits for the commercial provision of amusements with prizes) shall, instead of £25, be £32.

###### 3

The Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Fees) Order 1988[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 30th September 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases from £25 to £32 the fee payable in England and Wales to a local authority or other appropriate authority on the grant or renewal of a permit for amusements with prizes provided under section 16 of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976.
