# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (United Cable Television (London South) Plc) (Merton & Sutton) Order 1990

Made: 27th February 1990

Laid before Parliament: 28th February 1990

Coming into force: 28th March 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (United Cable Television (London South) Plc) (Merton & Sutton) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 28th March 1990.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Eric Forth — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 27th February 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to United Cable Television (London South) Plc on 24 January 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the London Boroughs of Merton and Sutton. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 28th February 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, United Cable Television (London South) Plc will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its Merton and Sutton public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Atlantic House, Holborn Viaduct, London EC1N 2HQ.
