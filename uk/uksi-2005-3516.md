# The A3 Trunk Road (Thursley Junction) (One Way Traffic) Order 2005

Made: 15th December 2005

Coming into force: 16th January 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and 2(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A3 Trunk Road (Thursley Junction) (One Way Traffic) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 16th January 2006.

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the trunk road” means the A3 Trunk Road at Thursley near Hindhead in the Borough of Waverley in the County of Surrey.

- “the slip road” means the slip road which forms a length of Dyehouse Road between the A3 Thursley junction bridge and the northbound carriageway of the trunk road.

###### 3

No person shall cause or permit any vehicle to enter or proceed in any direction other than north easterly on the slip road to the trunk road at the Thursley junction.

###### 4

The provision of article 3 above shall not apply to—

- (a) any vehicle being used for police, ambulance or fire brigade purposes; or

- (b) anything done at the direction of, or with the permission of, a police officer in uniform or a traffic warden.
