# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (No. 2) Regulations 1987

Made: 9th March 1987

Laid before Parliament: 16th March 1987

Coming into force: 6th April 1987

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4 and 20 of the Legal Aid Act 1974[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (No. 2) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 6th April 1987.

###### 2

For the weekly sum of £54 specified in section 4(2) of the Legal Aid Act 1974[^f00002] there shall be substituted the weekly sum of £56.

###### 3

For the sums specified in Schedule 3 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (No. 2) 1980[^f00003], there shall be substituted the following sums:—

- Exceeding £56 but not exceeding £64 a week £5

- Exceeding £64 but not exceeding £68 a week £11

- Exceeding £68 but not exceeding £72 a week £15

- Exceeding £72 but not exceeding £76 a week £20

- Exceeding £76 but not exceeding £80 a week £24

- Exceeding £80 but not exceeding £84 a week £28

- Exceeding £84 but not exceeding £88 a week £33

- Exceeding £88 but not exceeding £92 a week £37

- Exceeding £92 but not exceeding £96 a week £41

- Exceeding £96 but not exceeding £100 a week £46

- Exceeding £100 but not exceeding £106 a week £51

- Exceeding £106 but not exceeding £112 a week £58

- Exceeding £112 but not exceeding £118 a week £64

## Signed

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, C. — Dated 9th March 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase from £54 to £56 a week the disposable income above which a person receiving legal advice and assistance under Part I of the Legal Aid Act 1974 is required to pay a contribution and amend the scale of contributions.
