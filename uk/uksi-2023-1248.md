# The Non-Domestic Rating Act 2023 (Commencement No. 1 and Saving Provision) (England) Regulations 2023

Made: 21st November 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(6) and (7) of the Non-Domestic Rating Act 2023[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating Act 2023 (Commencement No. 1 and Saving Provision) (England) Regulations 2023.

- (2) In these Regulations “*the Act*” means the Non-Domestic Rating Act 2023.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The following provisions of the Act come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made—

- (a) section 15(2) (multipliers) (and section 15(1) so far as relating to that subsection);

- (b) Part 5 of the Schedule (provisions consequential on section 15).

#### Saving provision

###### 3

The amendments to Schedule 7 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00002] made by section 15(2) of, and Part 5 of the Schedule to, the Act do not have effect in relation to the non-domestic rating multiplier, small business non-domestic rating multiplier or special authority’s multipliers for the financial year[^f00003] beginning on 1st April 2023.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Levelling Up, Housing and Communities

Simon Hoare — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Levelling Up, Housing and Communities — 21st November 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These are first commencement regulations made under the Non-Domestic Rating Act [2023 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/53) (“*the Act*”) in England.

Regulation 2 brings section 15(2) of, and Part 5 of the Schedule to, the Act into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made. Section 15(2) inserts Part A1 into Schedule 7 to the Local Government Finance Act [1988 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41), for England. It re-enacts provisions currently in Part 1 of Schedule 7, makes changes to the way the non-domestic rating multipliers are to be indexed each year and makes some changes to the administrative process. Part 5 of the Schedule makes amendments consequential on the amendments made by section 15(2).

Regulation 3 makes saving provision for the chargeable financial year beginning on 1st April 2023.
