# The Proceeds of Crime Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made: 7th October 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 16(3) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 1995,[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995 and extends to England and Wales only.

###### 2

The provisions of the Proceeds of Crime Act 1995 (save for section 14 (enforcement in UK of overseas forfeiture and restraint orders) and section 16 (short title, interpretation, commencement and extent) which came into force on the passing of that Act) shall come into force on 1st November 1995.

## Signed

Home Office

David Maclean — Minister of State — 7th October 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 1st November 1995, the provisions of the Proceeds of Crime Act which were not brought into force on the passing of that Act.
