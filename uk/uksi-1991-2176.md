# The Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) (No. 2) Order 1991

Made: 30th September 1991

Laid before Parliament: 7th October 1991

Coming into force: 2nd December 1991

In pursuance of paragraph 20(1A) of Schedule 1 to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may ce cited as the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) (No. 2) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

- (2) This Order shall not extend to Scotland.

###### 2

Paragraph 20(1) (grant or renewal of permit or licence) of Schedule 1 (bookmaker’s permits, betting agency permits and betting office licences) to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 shall be amended by the substitution, for paragraphs (a) to (e), of the following paragraphs:

> > (a) in the case of the grant of a bookmaker’s permit, £133;
> (b) in the case of the grant of a betting agency permit, £133[^f00002];
> (c) in the case of the grant of a betting office licence, £106[^f00003];
> (d) in the case of the renewal of a bookmaker’s permit or betting agency permit, £16[^f00004]
> (e) in the case of the renewal of a betting office licence, £20[^f00005];

###### 3

The Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) Order 1987[^f00006] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 30th September 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases certain fees payable in England and Wales to the appropriate authority under paragraph 20(1) of Schedule 1 to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963. The fees for the grant of a bookmaker’s permit (paragraph 20(1)(a)) and for the grant of a betting agency permit (paragraph 20(1)(b)) are increased from £100 to £133, and the fee for the grant of a betting office licence is increased from £80 to £106 (paragraph 20(1)(c)). The fee for renewal of a bookmaker’s permit or betting agency permit is increased from £12 to £16 (paragraph 20(1)(d)) and the fee for renewal of a betting office licence is increased from £15 to £20 (paragraph 20(1)(e)).
