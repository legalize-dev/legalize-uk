# The Contracting Out (Functions in relation to the Management of Crown Lands) Order 1998

Made: 9th February 1998

Coming into force: 10th February 1998

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 69 of that Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Contracting Out (Functions in relation to the Management of Crown Lands) Order 1998 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Contracting out of functions

###### 2

The functions now vested in the Secretary of State under section 21 of the Crown Lands Act 1851[^f00002] may be exercised by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as may be authorised in that behalf by the Secretary of State.

## Signed

Chris Smith — Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport — 9th February 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note does not form part of the Order)

This Order enables the Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport to authorise another person (or that person’s employees) to perform those management functions, in relation to certain properties owned by the Sovereign in right of the Crown, which are vested in him by section 21 of the Crown Lands Act 1851.
