# The Land Charges Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972

Made: 21st December 1972

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 19(2) of the Land Charges Act 1972, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Land Charges Act 1972 (Commencement) Order 1972.

###### 2

The Land Charges Act 1972 shall come into operation on 29th January 1973.

## Signed

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, C — Dated 21st December 1972

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Land Charges Act 1972 into operation on 29th January 1973.
