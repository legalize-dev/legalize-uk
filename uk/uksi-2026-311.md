# The Immigration, Nationality and Passports (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 17th March 2026

Laid before Parliament: 18th March 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2) to (4)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations with the consent of the Treasury[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 68(1), (2), (7) and (10) and 74(8)(a), (b) and (d) of the Immigration Act 2014[^f00002] and sections 86(1) and (3), 87(2) and 93(6)(a) of the Immigration Act 2016[^f00003].

## PART 1 — Introductory

#### Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration, Nationality and Passports (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) Except as specified in paragraphs (3) and (4), these Regulations come into force at 9.00 a.m. on 8th April 2026.

- (3) Regulations 9 and 10 come into force on 9th April 2026.

- (4) Part 3 comes into force on 8th April 2026.

- (5) Subject to paragraphs (6) and (7), these Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

- (6) The following provisions also extend to the Isle of Man for the same purpose as the provisions of the 2018 Regulations which they amend—

- (a) this regulation;

- (b) regulation 2 in so far as it relates to regulation 9;

- (c) regulation 9.

- (7) The following provisions also extend to the Bailiwick of Guernsey and the Bailiwick of Jersey for the same purpose as the provisions of the 2018 Regulations which they amend—

- (a) this regulation;

- (b) regulation 2 in so far as it relates to regulation 10;

- (c) regulation 10.

- (8) In these Regulations, “*the 2018 Regulations*” means the Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Regulations 2018[^f00004].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/330](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/330)) (“*the 2018 Regulations*”) which set fees for the exercise of various functions in connection with immigration and nationality; and the Passport (Fees) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/660](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/660)) which set fees for the exercise of various functions in connection with passports.

Regulations 2 to 10 and Schedules 1 to 7 amend regulation 2 of, and Schedules 1 to 4 and 8 to 10 to, the 2018 Regulations, replacing existing fee amounts with increased fee amounts (in addition to which there are two fee decreases in Schedule 8). The following amendments are also made to the 2018 Regulations—

- regulations 4, 5 and 6 amend the exemptions to fees relating to entry clearance under Appendix Innovator Founder in Schedules 1, 2 and 3;

- regulation 9 substitutes fees for entry clearance to the Isle of Man as a Tier 4 Migrant with a new description of fees for Appendix Student and Appendix Child Student;

- regulations 9 and 10 introduce fees for an electronic travel authorisation to enter the Isle of Man, Jersey and Guernsey respectively.

Regulations 11 to 13 and Schedule 8 set out amendments which increase various fees in the Passport (Fees) Regulations 2022 and amend references to multiple passport applications to refer to a single passport application.

A full impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs to business has been produced. An Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
