# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2002

Made: 10th October 2002

Laid before Parliament: 10th October 2002

Coming into force: 11th October 2002

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 11th October 2002.

#### Amendment to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2002

###### 2

Schedule 2 to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2002[^f00003] (import conditions) is amended in accordance with this regulation—

- (a) for the entry in section C of Part I (protective measures–all products) there is substituted the following entry—

> > Commission Decision [2002/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0069) (OJ No. L30, 31.1.2002, p.50), as amended by Commission Decision [2002/441/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0441) (OJ No. L151, 11.6.2002, p.16), Commission Decision [2002/573/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0573) (OJ No. L181, 11.7.2002, p.21) and Commission Decision [2002/768/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0768) (OJ No. L260, 28.9.2002, p.31).

; and

- (b) in section H of Part IX (protective measures–fishery products)—

- (i) for paragraph 2 there is substituted—

> > (2) Commission Decision [2001/699/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0699) (OJ No. L251, 20.9.2001, p.11) as amended by Commission Decision [2002/770/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0770) (OJ No. L265, 3.10.2002, p.16).

, and

- (ii) paragraphs 5 and 7 are omitted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 10th October 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations amend section C of Part I and section H of Part IX of Schedule 2 to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2002 (S.I. [2002/1227](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1227), as amended) in order to give effect in England to the following Commission Decisions— Commission Decision [2002/768/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0768) amending Decision [2002/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0069) concerning certain protective measures with regard to the products of animal origin imported from China (OJ No. L260, 28.9.2002, p.31); Commission Decision [2002/770/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0770) amending Commission Decision [2001/699/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0699) and repealing Commission Decision [2002/250/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0250) to revoke the protective measures with regard to the fishery and aquaculture products imported from Vietnam (OJ No. L265, 3.10.2002, p.16); Commission Decision [2002/771/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0771) repealing Decision [2002/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0062) concerning protective measures with regard to the fishery and aquaculture products imported from Pakistan (OJ No. L265, 3.10.2002, p.18).

2 The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2002 implement for England Council Directive [97/78/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0078) laying down the principles governing the organisation of veterinary checks on products entering the Community from third countries (OJ No. L24, 30.1.98, p.9).

3 The products to which S.I. [2002/1227](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1227) applies are defined in regulation 2(1) of and Schedule 3 to that instrument.

4 Schedule 2 to S.I. [2002/1227](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1227) lays down the conditions, by reference to the Community legislation listed in it, for the import of those products into England from third countries.

5 No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
