# The Superannuation (Museums and Galleries Commission) Order 1986

Made: 3rd December 1986

Laid before Parliament: 8th December 1986

Coming into Operation: 1st January 1987

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(5) and section 1(8)(b) of the Superannuation Act 1972 (hereinafter referred to as “*the 1972 Act*”) and now vested in them[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Museums and Galleries Commission) Order 1986 and shall come into operation on 1st January 1987 (hereinafter called “*the commencement date*”).

###### 2

The Standing Commission on Museums and Galleries is hereby removed from Schedule 1 to the 1972 Act.

###### 3

Employment as a member of the staff of the Museums and Galleries Commission is hereby added to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the 1972 Act.

###### 4

For the purposes of any scheme made under section 1 of the 1972 Act, there shall be deemed to have been no break at the commencement date in the employment of a present member of the staff of the Standing Commission on Museums and Galleries who, as from that date, is employed by the Museums and Galleries Commission.

## Signed

Peter Lloyd — Tim Sainsbury — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 3rd December 1986

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Employment with the Standing Commission on Museums and Galleries is an employment by a body listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 and so falls within those employments to which the principal civil service pension scheme (made under section 1 of that Act) may apply. This body will cease to exist, when the Museums and Galleries Commission is established by Royal Charter on 1 January 1987. This Order therefore removes the Standing Commission on Museums and Galleries from Schedule 1 to the 1972 Act, and adds the Museums and Galleries Commission to the said Schedule in each case with effect from 1st January 1987. Paragraph 4 of the Order provides for continuity of reckonable service of the present staff of the Standing Commission on Museums and Galleries under the principal civil service pension scheme.
