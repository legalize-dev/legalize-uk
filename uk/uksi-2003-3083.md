# The National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2003

Made: 26th November 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 42(3) of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2003.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1st December 2003 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 22 of the Act (abolition of Community Health Councils in England), in so far as it is not already in force[^f00002].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Minister of State, — Department of Health — 26th November 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the seventh Commencement Order made by the Secretary of State under the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (“the Act”). One Order has also been made by the National Assembly for Wales.

Article 2 brings into force on 1st December 2003 section 22 of the Act, which provides for the abolition of Community Health Councils established for districts in England and of the Association of Community Health Councils for England and Wales.
