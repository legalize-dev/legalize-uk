# The Partnerships (Unrestricted Size) No. 8 Regulations 1991

Made: 2nd December 1991

Coming into force: 1st January 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by sections 716(2)(d) and 744 of the Companies Act 1985[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Partnerships (Unrestricted Size) No. 8 Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

###### 2

Section 716(1) of the Companies Act 1985 does not prohibit the formation for the purpose of carrying on practice as lawyers of a partnership which is a multi-national partnership within the meaning of section 89(9) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00002].

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 2nd December 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Section 716 of the Companies Act 1985 prohibits the formation of partnerships of more than 20 persons. These regulations exempt from that prohibition partnerships formed for the purpose of carrying on practice as lawyers which are multi-national partnerships within the meaning of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990.
