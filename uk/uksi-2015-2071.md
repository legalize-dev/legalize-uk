# The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility (Correction) Order 2015

Made: 21st December 2015

Coming into force: 22nd December 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers in paragraph 1(4) and (8) of that Schedule, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility (Correction) Order 2015 and comes into force on 22nd December 2015.

#### Corrections

###### 2

The Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out provisions or text to be omitted; and

- (c) column 3 sets out any provisions or text to be substituted in its place.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott — Head of National Infrastructure Consents and Coal Liabilities — Department of Energy and Climate Change — 21st December 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Preesall Underground Gas Storage Facility Order 2015, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request from Halite Energy Group Limited under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
