# The Licensing (Sunday Hours) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made: 20th July 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 5 of the Licensing (Sunday Hours) Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Sunday Hours) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995.

###### 2

The Licensing (Sunday Hours) Act 1995 shall come into force on 6th August 1995.

## Signed

Home Office

Nicholas Baker — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 20th July 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6th August 1995 the whole of the Licensing (Sunday Hours) Act 1995.
