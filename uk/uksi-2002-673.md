# The Travel Concessions (Eligibility) Act 2002 (Commencement) (England) Order 2002

Made: 12th March 2002

The Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2(1)[^f00001] of the Travel Concessions (Eligibility) Act 2002[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Travel Concessions (Eligibility) Act 2002 (Commencement) (England) Order 2002.

#### Provisions coming into force in England

###### 2

Section 1 of the Travel Concessions (Eligibility) Act 2002 shall come into force on 1st April 2003 as respects England.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions

Sally Keeble — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions — 12th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings all the substantive provisions of the Travel Concessions (Eligibility) Act 2002 into force on 1st April 2003 as respects England.
