# The Courts-Martial Appeal (Amendment No.2) Rules 2007

Made: 20th April 2007

Laid before Parliament: 26th April 2007

Coming into force

The Lord Chief Justice of England, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 49 of the Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968[^f00001], and with the approval of the Lord Chancellor, hereby makes the following Rules:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Courts-Martial Appeal (Amendment No.2) Rules 2007 and shall come into force on 28th May 2007

#### Amendments to the Courts-Martial Appeal Rules 1968

###### 2

- (1) The Courts-Martial Appeal Rules 1968[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) For the definition of legal adviser in paragraph (3)(c) of rule 8C there is substituted—

> > (c) is a member of the Bar of Northern Ireland or a solicitor of the Supreme Court of Northern Ireland; or

## Signed

Approved

C J Phillips — Falconer of Thoroton C — Department of Constitutional Affairs — Date 20th April 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the definition of “legal adviser” in the Courts-Martial Appeal Rules 1968 ([S.I. 1968/1071](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1968/1071)) so as to make reference to a member of the Bar of Northern Ireland or a solicitor of the Supreme Court of Northern Ireland.
