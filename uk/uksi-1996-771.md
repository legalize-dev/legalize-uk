# The Adventure Activities (Licensing) (Designation) Order 1996

Made: 18th March 1996

Laid before Parliament: 26th March 1996

Coming into force: 16th April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1) of the Activity Centres (Young Persons' Safety) Act 1995[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Adventure Activities (Licensing) (Designation) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 16th April 1996.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Activity Centres (Young Persons' Safety) Act 1995.

#### Designation

###### 2

Tourism Quality Services Limited[^f00002] (being a body nominated by the Health and Safety Commission under section 1(2) of the Act) is hereby designated for the purposes of section 1(1) of the Act.

## Signed

Henley — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 18th March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 1(1) of the Activity Centres (Young Persons' Safety) Act 1995 provides that the Secretary of State shall designate a person to exercise prescribed functions relating to the licensing of persons providing facilities for adventure activities. A person designated is referred to in the Act as “the licensing authority”. This Order designates Tourism Quality Services Limited.
