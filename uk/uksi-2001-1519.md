# The Armed Forces Act 1996 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2001

Made: 18th April 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 36(2) of the Armed Forces Act 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 1996 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2001.

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2)—

- (a) sections 3 and 4 of the Armed Forces Act 1996, and

- (b) (so far as not already in force) section 35 of and Schedule 7 to that Act,

shall come into force on 1st May 2001.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall not bring into force the repeal by Schedule 7 to the Armed Forces Act 1996 of section 46 of the Mental Health Act 1983[^f00002], section 69 of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00003] and Article 52 of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00004].

## Signed

John F. Speller — Minister of State, Ministry of Defence — 18th April 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st May 2001 sections 3 and 4 of the Armed Forces Act 1996 (which relate to discharge certificates and the mode of making regulations relating to enlistment etc.) It also brings into force on that date the remaining repeals made by Schedule 7, except for those which are consequential on section 8 of and Schedule 2 to the 1996 Act (which relate to findings of unfitness to stand trial and insanity).
