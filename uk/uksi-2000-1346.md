# The Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2000

Made: 17th May 2000

Coming into force: 1st June 2000

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of May, 2000 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 60 (other than subsection (3)(r)), 61, 77, 101, and 102 of and Schedule 13 to the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001], as extended to the Bailiwick of Jersey by the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Jersey) Order 1990[^f00002], and section 35 of the Airports Act 1986[^f00003], as extended to the Bailiwick of Jersey by the Airports Act 1986 (Jersey) Order 2000[^f00004], is pleased by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st June 2000.

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 1972[^f00005]is hereby revoked, but any Regulations made by virtue of that Order shall remain in force as if they had been made by virtue of the corresponding provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In this Order, the expression “Jersey” means the Bailiwick of Jersey and the territorial waters adjacent thereto.

###### 4

The Air Navigation (No.2) Order 1995[^f00006], as amended by the Air Navigation Order 1996[^f00007], the Air Navigation (Second Amendment) Order 1997[^f00008], the Air Navigation (Third Amendment) Order 1998[^f00009], the Air Navigation (Fourth) Amendment) Order 1999[^f00010] and the Air Navigation (Fifth Amendment) Order 1999[^f00011], shall apply in relation to Jersey with the exceptions and modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and replaces the Air Navigation (Jersey) Order 1972 (S.I. [1970/452](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1970/452)). It extends to Jersey, with modifications, most of the provisions of the Air Navigation (No. 2) Order 1995 (S.I. [1995/1970](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1970)), as amended by the Air Navigation (Amendment) Order 1996 (S.I. [1996/1301](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1301)), the Air Navigation (Second Amendment) Order 1997 (S.I. [1997/287](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/287)), the Air Navigation (Third Amendment) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/753](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/753)), the Air Navigation (Fourth Amendment) Order 1999 (S.I. [1997/1123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1123)) and the Air Navigation (Fifth Amendment) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/2059](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2059)).
