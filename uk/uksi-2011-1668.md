# The Prospectus Regulations 2011

Made: 7th July 2011

Laid before Parliament: 8th July 2011

Coming into force: 31st July 2011

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act, make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and amendments

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Prospectus Regulations 2011 and come into force on 31st July 2011.

- (2) In section 86(1)(b) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00003] (exempt offers to the public), for “100 persons” substitute “150 persons”.

- (3) In paragraph 9(1) of Schedule 11A to that Act (transferable securities), for “the total consideration of the offer is less than 2,500,000 euros (or an equivalent amount)” substitute “the total consideration of the offer in the European Union is less than 5,000,000 euros (or an equivalent amount)”.

## Signed

Michael Fabricant — Jeremy Wright — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 7th July 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend two provisions of the Financial Services and Markets Act [2000 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8) to implement in part Directive 2010/73/EU (OJ No L 327, 11.12.2010, p.1) of the European Parliament and of the Council. That Directive amends Directive [2003/71/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0071) on the prospectus to be published when securities are offered to the public or admitted to trading (the Prospectus Directive) and Directive [2004/109/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0109) on the harmonisation of transparency requirements in relation to information about issuers whose securities are admitted to trading on a regulated market (the Transparency Directive).

Regulation 1(2) increases the number of persons to whom an offer may be directed before it ceases to be an exempt offer from 100 to 150 persons. Regulation 1(3) increases from 2.5 to 5 million euros the limit for the total consideration of the offer in the European Union below which it is not unlawful to offer transferable securities to the public without an approved prospectus first having been made available to the public.

An impact assessment of the effect of this instrument on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from the Securities and Markets Team, HM Treasury, 1 Horse Guards Road, London SW1A 2HQ and is published with the Explanatory Memorandum alongside the instrument on legislation.gov.uk .
