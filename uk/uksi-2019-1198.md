# The European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2019

Made: 16th August 2019

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 25(4) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2019.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 1 (repeal of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002]) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 is the day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

## Signed

Steve Barclay — Secretary of State for Exiting the European Union — Department for Exiting the European Union — 16th August 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are the fourth set of commencement regulations made under the European Union (Withdrawal) Act[2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16). Regulation 2 brings section 1 of that Act into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made. Section 1 provides that the European Communities Act [1972 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68) is repealed on exit day.
