# The A47 Wansford to Sutton (Corrections) Order 2023

Made: 16th June 2023

Coming into force: 17th June 2023

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A47 Wansford to Sutton (Corrections) Order 2023 and comes into force on 17th June 2023.

#### Corrections to the 2023 Order

###### 2

- (1) The A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent Order 2023 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the A47 Wansford to Sutton Development Consent Order 2023 ([S.I. 2013/218](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/218)) a development consent order under the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
