# The Action for Education and Employment South East Sheffield Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2003

Made: 3 March 2003

Laid before Parliament: 13 March 2003

Coming into force: 17 April 2003

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 [^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Action for Education and Employment South East Sheffield Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 17th April 2003.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In this Order-

- “Action for Education and Employment South East Sheffield Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Action for Education and Employment South East Sheffield Education Action Zone Order 2000 [^f00002].

#### The extension of the Action for Education and Employment South East Sheffield Education Action Zone

###### 3

The Action for Education and Employment South East Sheffield Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 17th April 2003.

## Signed

David Miliband — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 3

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Action for Education and Employment South East Sheffield Education Action Zone for a further period of two years.
