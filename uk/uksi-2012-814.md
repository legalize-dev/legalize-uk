# The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (England) Regulations 2012

Made: 13th March 2012

Laid before Parliament: 16th March 2012

Coming into force: 6th April 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 20(6) and 63(1) of the Clean Air Act 1993[^f00001].

#### Citation, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (England) Regulations 2012;

- (b) apply in England; and

- (c) come into force on 6th April 2012.

#### Authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993, the following are authorised fuels—

- (a) anthracite;

- (b) semi-anthracite;

- (c) electricity;

- (d) gas;

- (e) low volatile steam coals; and

- (f) the fuels described in Schedule 1.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The regulations listed in Schedule 2 are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations consolidate six sets of regulations which declare fuels to be authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993. Those regulations are [SI 2008/514](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/514), [SI 2008/2342](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/2342), [SI 2009/2191](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2191), [SI 2010/576](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/576), [SI 2011/715](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/715) and [SI 2011/2105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2105). Two additional fuels are authorised (Excel+ briquettes and Newheat briquettes). Briteheat and Stoveheat Premium briquettes are two names for the same product and they are therefore listed together.

A full impact assessment has not been produced as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors foreseen.
