# The Birmingham-Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (King’s Lynn Southern Bypass) Order 1971 Partial Revocation Order 1996

Made: 4th July 1996

Coming into force: 8th August 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Birmingham-Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (King’s Lynn Southern Bypass) Order 1971 Partial Revocation Order 1996 and shall come into force on 8th August 1996.

###### 2

The Birmingham-Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (King’s Lynn Southern Bypass) Order 1971[^f00003] is hereby revoked insofar as it authorises the Secretary of State to construct:

- (a) that length of new trunk road between a point 310 metres west of the centre of the Hardwick Roundabout (as itself authorised by that Order to be constructed) and its easterly junction with the A47 Trunk Road; and

- (b) two routes for slip roads to connect the said Hardwick Roundabout to the length of new trunk road described above and given the reference numbers 11 and 12.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

R. R. Bineham — Director, Highways Agency,Northern Operations Division — 4th July 1996
