# The New Opportunities Fund (Specification of Initiatives) (No.2) Order 2005

Made: 16th November 2005

Coming into force: 17th November 2005

In accordance with section 60(2)[^f00002] of that Act, a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the New Opportunities Fund (Specification of Initiatives) (No.2) Order 2005 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Specification of initiatives

###### 2

- (1) For the purposes of section 43B(1) of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 the following initiatives are specified—

- (a) an initiative to promote community learning;

- (b) an initiative to promote community safety and cohesion;

- (c) an initiative to promote physical and mental well being;

- (d) an initiative to involve local people in small-scale projects in their communities that are concerned or connected with health, education or the environment.

- (2) The reference in this Order to promoting community safety is to making communities places in which it is, or is perceived to be, safer to live and work, in particular by the reduction of actual or perceived levels of crime and other anti-social behaviour.

#### Revocation

###### 3

- (1) The Orders specified in the Schedule are hereby revoked.

- (2) Anything done by the New Opportunities Fund pursuant to the Orders specified in the Schedule which has effect immediately before the day this Order comes into force shall, so far as necessary for continuing its effect on or after that day, have effect as if done pursuant to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies initiatives concerned or connected with health, education or the environment and accordingly allows the New Opportunities Fund to make grants, or make or enter into arrangements, under section 43B of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993 which are designed to give effect to those initiatives.
