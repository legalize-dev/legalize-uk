# The Local Government Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 1973

Made: 2nd March 1973

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 273(2) of the Local Government Act 1972 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

### Citation, application and interpretation

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1972 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 1973.

###### 2

This order shall apply to the area consisting of the counties established by section 20 (new local government areas in Wales).

###### 3

- (1) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of this order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

- (2) In this order, “*the Act*” means the Local Government Act 1972.

- (3) Any reference in this order to a numbered section, Part or Schedule shall be construed as a reference to the section, Part or Schedule bearing that number in the Act.

### Other provisions

###### 8

Sections 53 to 59 shall come into force on 1st December 1973.

###### 9

- (1) Sections 154 to 167, 196(3) and 197(4), and section 251(1) in so far as it extends to paragraph 7(1) of Schedule 29, shall come into force—

- (a) as respects the accounts of county councils and the committees and officers thereof, on 16th April 1973;

- (b) as respects the accounts of district councils and authorities to be covered by resolutions thereof under section 154(2)(d) and the committees and officers of such councils and authorities, on 14th May 1973; and

- (c) as respects the accounts of any authority to which article 7 above applies, at the date specified in that article in respect of that authority.

- (2) Sections 154(2) and 164 shall come into force as respects the accounts of any such joint committee as is described in section 154(2)(e) on the date on which the joint committee is established.

- (3) Section 251(1) in so far as it extends to paragraph 7(2) to (5) of Schedule 29 shall come into force on 1st April 1973.

###### 10

- section 111, 168, 171 and 214 (3) and (4)

- section 217(7) in so far as it extends to paragraph 3 of Schedule 27

- section 219(2)

- section 240 except subsections (3) and (7)

### Interim action

###### 11

For the purposes of the preparation or service of any precept, or the preparation or approval of any rate, to be made in respect of a period commencing on 1st April 1974, any provision of the Act which is, under section 273(1) thereof, not in force on 1st January 1974 shall come into force on that date.

###### 12

For the purposes of taking any action authorised by any enactment (whether or not involving the expenditure of money) by any authority to ensure effective and convenient local government as from 1st April 1974, the provisions of the Act of which section 273(1) applies, so far as they have; on 1st April 1973, not been brought into force, shall be deemed to have come into force on that date.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 273(1) of the Local Government Act 1972 provides that the provisions of the Act specified in section 273(3) shall, except in so far as brought into force earlier by an order under section 273(2), not come into force until 1st April 1974. This Order, made under section 273(2), brings a number of the provisions specified in section 273(3) into force at earlier dates which differ according to the purposes and areas in question. The Order applies to Wales.
