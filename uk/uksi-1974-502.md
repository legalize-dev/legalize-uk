# The Motorways Traffic (Speed Limit) Regulations 1974

Made: 21st March 1974

Laid before Parliament: 21st March 1974

Coming into Operation: 29th March 1974

The Secretary of State for the Environment (so far as relating to motorways in England), the Secretary of State for Scotland (so far as relating to motorways in Scotland) and the Secretary of State for Wales (so far as relating to motorways in Wales), in exercise respectively of their powers under section 13 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1967 and of all other enabling powers and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with the provisions of section 107(2) of that Act, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations shall come into operation on 29th March 1974 and may be cited as the Motorways Traffic (Speed Limit) Regulations 1974.

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations, the expression “*motorway*” —

- (a) so far as relating to motorways in England and Wales, has the meaning assigned to it in the Motorways Traffic Regulations 1959[^f00001]; and

- (b) so far as relating to motorways in Scotland, has the meaning assigned to it in the Motorways Traffic (Scotland) Regulations 1964[^f00002].

- (2) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these Regulations as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament, and as if for the purposes of section 38 of that Act these Regulations were an Act of Parliament and the Regulations revoked by Regulation 6 below were an Act of Parliament thereby repealed.

###### 3

No person shall drive a motor vehicle on a motorway at a speed exceeding 70 miles per hour:

###### 4

No person shall drive a motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 60 miles per hour on any of the lengths of motorway specified in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

###### 5

No person shall drive a motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 50 miles per hour on any of the lengths of motorway specified in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

###### 6

The Motorways Traffic (Speed Limit) Regulations 1973[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations re-impose a maximum speed limit of 70 miles per hour (in lieu of the 50 miles per hour speed limit imposed by the Regulations revoked by these Regulations) on the driving of motor vehicles on motorways, except on the lengths of motorway referred to in Regulations 4 and 5 (on which speed limits of 60 m.p.h. and 50 m.p.h. are respectively re-imposed by these Regulations) and except on other lengths of motorway which are for the time being subject to speed limits lower than 70 m.p.h.
