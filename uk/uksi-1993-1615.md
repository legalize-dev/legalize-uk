# The Health Boards (Membership and Procedure) (No. 2) Amendment Regulations 1993

Made: 24th June 1993

Laid before Parliament: 5th July 1993

Coming into force: 26th July 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(10), 105(7) and 108(1) of, and by paragraph 11 of Schedule 1 to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001], as read with article 5 of, and Part I of the Schedule to, the Health Education Board for Scotland Order 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## GENERAL

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Boards (Membership and Procedure) (No. 2) Amendment Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 26th July 1993.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Health Boards (Membership and Procedure) (No.2) Regulations 1991[^f00003].

#### Amendment to the principal Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 5 of the principal Regulations (Resignation and removal of members) there is inserted after paragraph (4) the following paragraph:–

> > (5) If the Secretary of State considers that it is not in the interests of the health service that a member of a Board should continue to hold that office he may forthwith terminate his appointment.

#### Application of Regulations

###### 3

These Regulations shall not apply except in relation to a member of a Board who is appointed on or after the coming into force of these Regulations.

## Signed

Fraser of Carmyllie — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 24th June 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Health Boards (Membership and Procedure) (No.2) Regulations 1991 ([S.I. 1991/809](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/809)). The amendment provides that the Secretary of State may remove a member of a Health Board from office if he decides that such removal is in the interests of the health service. Regulation 3 restricts the application of the amendment only to those members of Health Boards appointed on or after the date of coming into force of the Regulations.
