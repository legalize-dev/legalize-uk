# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991

Made: 27th November 1991

Laid before Parliament: 9th December 1991

Coming into force: 30th December 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated([^f00001]) for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972([^f00002]) in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 30th December 1991.

###### 2

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980([^f00003]) shall be further amended as follows.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) Regulations 1980.

2 The type approval requirements relating to emission standards are, as an alternative to the existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) as amended by Council Directives [74/290/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0290), [83/351/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1983/0351), [88/76/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0076), [88/436/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0436), [89/458/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0458) and [91/441/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0441) and Commission Directives [77/102/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0102), [78/665/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0665) and [89/491/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0491).

3 The type approval requirements relating to braking devices are, as an alternative to the existing requirements, the requirements of Council Directive [71/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0320) as amended by Commission Directives [74/132/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0132), [75/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0524), [79/489/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0489), [85/647/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0647), [88/194/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0194) and [91/422/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0422).

4 The types approval requirements relating to spray-suppression systems are the requirements of Council Directive [91/226/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0226).
