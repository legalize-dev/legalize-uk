# The Outer Space Act 1986 (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made: 27th February 1993

Laid before Parliament: 4th March 1993

Coming into force: 1st April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4(3)(d) and 11 of the Outer Space Act 1986[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Outer Space Act 1986 (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

###### 2

The Outer Space Act 1986 (Fees) Regulations 1989[^f00002] shall be amended in the definition of “further education” in regulation 2 by inserting after the words “given in section 41 of the Education Act 1944”, the following words:—

> > - as read with section 14 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00003]

## Signed

E Leigh — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Department of Trade and Industry — 27th February 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Further and Higher Education Act 1992 extends the meaning of “further education” to include full-time education provided by a sixth form college. These Regulations amend The Outer Space Act 1986 (Fees) Regulations 1989 by providing a definition of “further education” consistent with this extended meaning, so that exemption from the fee payable on an application for a licence under the Outer Space Act 1986 extends to such a college.
