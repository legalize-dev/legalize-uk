# The School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) (Amendment) Order 2023

Made: 18th July 2023

Laid before Parliament: 19th July 2023

Coming into force: 1st September 2023

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 123(4)(a) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) (Amendment) Order 2023 and comes into force on 1st September 2023.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019

###### 2

- (1) The School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2(2) (incentive payments to school teachers)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (c) omit “and”; and

- (b) after sub-paragraph (d) insert—

> > and
> (e) the International Relocation Payment,

## Signed

Nick Gibb — Minister of State — Department for Education — 18th July 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order amends the School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1133](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1133)).It adds the International Relocation Payment to the list of schemes under which payment is not to be treated as remuneration for the purposes of section 122(1) of the Education [Act 2002 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on business or civil society organisations. The instrument has minimal impact on the public sector.
