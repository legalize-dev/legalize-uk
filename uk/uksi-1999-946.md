# The Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Amendment Regulations 1999

Made: 23rd March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 23rd March 1999

Coming into force: 24th March 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by section 126(4) and 128(1) of, and paragraph 12 of Schedule 5 to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Amendment Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 24th March 1999.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means The Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1996[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

In Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations (Special Health Authorities of which the chairman and members are not disqualified under regulation 10(1)(g))[^f00003], the following entries are inserted at the appropriate point in the alphabetical order: “National Health Service Information Authority”, “National Institute for Clinical Excellence”.

## Signed

Frank Dobson — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State, — Department of Health — 23rd March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Health Authorities (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1996 (“the principal Regulations”), which make provision for the membership and procedure of Health Authorities.

In particular, they amend Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations (Special Health Authorities of which the chairman and members are not disqualified from being the chairman or a member of a Health Authority) to include the National Health Service Information Authority and the National Institute for Clinical Excellence.
