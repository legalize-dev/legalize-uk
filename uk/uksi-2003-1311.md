# The Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993 (Addition of Qualifying Judicial Offices) Order 2003

Made: 13th May 2003

Laid before Parliament: 19th May 2003

Coming into force: 30th June 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1(8) of the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993 (Addition of Qualifying Judicial Offices) Order 2003 and comes into force on 30th June 2003.

###### 2

In Part 2 of Schedule 1 (offices which may be qualifying judicial offices) to the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993, after the entry relating to a member of a panel constituted under article 7(1) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, insert—

- “Adjudicator to Her Majesty’s Land Registry”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Scotland of Asthal — Parliamentary Secretary, — Lord Chancellor’s Department — Dated 13th May 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds to the list of offices eligible for judicial pensions under the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993 that of Adjudicator to Her Majesty’s Land Registry. This office is created by section 107 of the Land Registration Act 2002.
