# The Social Security (Fees Payable by Qualifying Lenders) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

Made: 23rd February 2016

Laid before Parliament: 1st March 2016

Coming into force: 1st April 2016

In accordance with section 173(1)(b) of the 1992 Act, the Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that the proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Fees Payable by Qualifying Lenders) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 1st April 2016.

#### Amendment of paragraph 7 of Schedule 9A to the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987

###### 2

In paragraph 7 of Schedule 9A to the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987 (fees payable by qualifying lenders)[^f00002], for “£0.46” substitute “£0.44”.

#### Amendment of paragraph 9(2) of Schedule 5 to the Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013

###### 3

In paragraph 9(2) of Schedule 5 to the Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013 (fees payable by qualifying lenders)[^f00003], for “£0.46” substitute “£0.44”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Freud — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 23rd February 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend paragraph 7 of Schedule 9A to the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987 ([S.I. 1987/1968](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1968)) and paragraph 9(2) of Schedule 5 to the Universal Credit, Personal Independence Payment, Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance (Claims and Payments) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/380](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/380)) by reducing from £0.46 to £0.44 the fee which qualifying lenders pay for the purpose of defraying administrative expenses incurred by the Secretary of State in making payments in respect of mortgage interest direct to qualifying lenders.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no new impact on business or civil society organisations.
