# The British Nationality (Maldives) Order 2017

Made: 12th April 2017

Laid before Parliament: 19th April 2017

Coming into force: 12th May 2017

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 12th day of April 2017

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred upon Her by section 37(2) of the British Nationality Act 1981 , by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, orders as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the British Nationality (Maldives) Order 2017 and comes into force on 12th May 2017.

#### Amendment of Schedule 3 to the British Nationality Act 1981

###### 2

In Schedule 3 to the British Nationality Act 1981 (countries whose citizens are Commonwealth citizens), omit “Maldives”.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
