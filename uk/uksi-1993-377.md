[1988/1843](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1843)) transferred certain of those functions, including those in relation to registration of births, deaths and marriages to the Secretary of State for Health.

[^f00003]: S.I. [1992/2982](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2982). That Order was not legally effective as respects the matters dealt with in Article 3 of this Order.
