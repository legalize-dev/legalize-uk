# The Mission and Pastoral etc. (Amendment) Measure 2018 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2018

Made: 25th September 2018

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14 of the Mission and Pastoral etc. (Amendment) Measure 2018[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Mission and Pastoral etc. (Amendment) Measure 2018 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2018.

#### Provision coming into force on 1st October 2018

###### 2

Section 3(5)(a) of the Mission and Pastoral etc. (Amendment) Measure 2018 (pastoral schemes and orders: notice, publication and amendment) comes into force on 1st October 2018.

## Signed

+ Justin Cantuar: — Archbishop of Canterbury

+Sentamu Ebor: — Archbishop of York — 25th September 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 1st October 2018 as the day on which section 3(5)(a) of the Mission and Pastoral etc. (Amendment) Measure 2018, which amends section 9(3) of the Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011 (pastoral schemes and orders: notice, publication and amendment), comes into force.
