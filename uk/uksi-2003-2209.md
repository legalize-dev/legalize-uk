# The Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2003

Made: 28th August 2003

Laid before Parliament: 29th August 2003

Coming into force: 19th September 2003

Whereas the Treasury are a government department designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to (i) measures relating to the interruption or reduction, in part or completely, of economic relations with one or more countries which are not Member States, and (ii) measures relating to the movement of capital and to payments, between Member States and between Member States and countries which are not Member States;

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2003 and come into force on 19th September 2003.

#### Amendment to the Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001

###### 2

For the definition of “Council Decision” in Article 2(1) of the Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 there is substituted—

> > “*Council Decision*” means Council Decision [2003/480/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0480) implementing Article 2(3) of Regulation [(EC) No. 2580/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2580) on specific restrictive measures directed against certain persons and entities with a view to combating terrorism and repealing Decision [2002/974/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0974) ;

#### Revocation

###### 3

Regulation 2 of the Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 (Amendment) Regulations 2003 is revoked.

## Signed

Joan Ryan — Jim Murphy — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 28th August 2003
