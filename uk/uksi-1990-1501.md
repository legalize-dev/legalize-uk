# The Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1990

Made: 24th July 1990

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of July 1990 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the Armed Forces Act 1986[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:—

###### 1

The Army Act 1955[^f00002], the Air Force Act 1955[^f00003] and the Naval Discipline Act 1957[^f00004] shall continue in force for a period of twelve months beyond 31st August 1990 that day being the day on which they would otherwise expire.

###### 2

This Order may be cited as the Army, Air Force and Naval Discipline Acts (Continuation) Order 1990.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order enables the provisions of the Army Act 1955, the Air Force Act 1955and the Naval Discipline Act 1957 to continue in force for a period of twelve months beyond 31st August 1990.
