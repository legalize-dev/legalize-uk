# The Social Security Commissioners Procedure (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 7th May 1992

Laid before Parliament: 8th May 1992

Coming into force: 1st June 1992

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by the enactments specified in the Schedule to these Regulations and now vested in him[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with the Lord Advocate and, in accordance with section 10 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1971[^f00002], with the Council on Tribunals, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security Commissioners Procedure (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 1st June 1992.

#### Definition of “appeal tribunal”

###### 2

In regulation 2 of the Social Security Commissioners Procedure Regulations 1987[^f00003], for the definition of “appeal tribunal” there shall be substituted the following—

> > “appeal tribunal” means a social security appeal tribunal constituted in accordance with section 97(2) to (2E) of the Act or a disability appeal tribunal constituted in accordance with Schedule 10A to the Act;

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS CONFERRING POWERS EXERCISED IN MAKING THESE REGULATIONS

- Section 6 of the National Insurance Act 1974[^f00004]

- Sections 101(5A), 101(5B), 114(1), 114(2C), 114(5) and 115(1) of the Social Security Act 1975[^f00005]

- Section 14 of the Social Security Act 1980[^f00006]

- Section 4 of the Forfeiture Act 1982[^f00007]

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — 7th May 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Security Commissioners Procedure Regulations 1987 by extending the definition of “appeal tribunal” to include a disability appeal tribunal constituted in accordance with Schedule 10A to the Social Security Act 1975. Schedule 10A was inserted into the Social Security Act 1975 by paragraph 16 of Schedule 1 to the Disability Living Allowance and Disability Working Allowance Act [1991 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/21).
