# The Criminal Defence Service (Funding) (Police Station Advice and Assistance) Order 2010

Made: 9th March 2010

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 2010

Coming into force: 6th April 2010

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(3) of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001]. He has consulted the General Council of the Bar and the Law Society in accordance with section 25(2) of that Act and has had regard to the matters specified in section 25(3) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement, application and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Defence Service (Funding) (Police Station Advice and Assistance) Order 2010 and comes into force on 6th April 2010.

###### 2

This Order applies to claims by suppliers under the Unified Contract (Crime) 2008 for fees in relation to work done for an individual at a police station where the first attendance in person on that individual at the police station in relation to the investigation in question occurs on or after 6th April 2010.

###### 3

In this Order—

- (a) “police station” has the same meaning as in the Unified Contract (Crime) 2008;

- (b) “the Unified Contract (Crime) 2008” means the contract between the Commission and a supplier for the provision of funded services which includes the contract for signature, the schedules, the standard terms 2008 and the crime specification, published by the Commission in July 2008[^f00002].

#### Remuneration under contract

###### 4

ForAnnex 1 to the crime specification of the Unified Contract (Crime) 2008, the Commission must substitute Annex 1 in the Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the fees payable for advice and assistance given to individuals at police stations, as part of the Criminal Defence Service maintained by the Legal Services Commission. It reduces the fees in those areas (about 60% of the total number of areas) where a reduction is least likely to have an impact on the supply of advice and assistance. The Order requires the Commission to replace the annex of fees in its contract with suppliers with the annex set out in the Schedule to the Order.

An impact assessment relating to the fee changes introduced by this instrument has been published by the Ministry of Justice as part of “Legal Aid: Funding Reforms – Response to Consultation” and is available from the Ministry of Justice, 102 Petty France, London SW1H 9AJ or at www.justice.gov.uk.
