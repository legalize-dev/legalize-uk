# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (References to Financial Investigators) (Amendment) Order 2006

Made: 14th January 2006

Laid before Parliament: 23rd January 2006

Coming into force: 1st March 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 453 and 459(2) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (References to Financial Investigators) (Amendment) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st March 2006.

###### 2

The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (References to Financial Investigators) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

> > - “the Royal Mail” means the company Royal Mail Group Plc (registered number 4138203);

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (References to Financial Investigators) Order 2003 (“the 2003 Order”). By virtue of article 2 of the 2003 Order, a reference to an accredited financial investigator in the provisions of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 which is listed in column 1 of the Schedule to that Order is a reference to such an investigator as falls within the description which is specified in relation to that provision in column 2 of that Schedule.

Articles 4, 5, 8, 10, 12 and 14 of this Order amend the Schedule to the 2003 Order to add references that apply in England and Wales to the Serious Organised Crime Agency (as defined by the amendment made by article 3 to this Order), the Royal Mail (as defined by the amendment made by article 3 to this Order) and the Home Office. Articles 6, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15 of this Order amend the Schedule to the 2003 Order to add references that apply in Northern Ireland to the Serious Organised Crime Agency, the Royal Mail and the Home Office.
