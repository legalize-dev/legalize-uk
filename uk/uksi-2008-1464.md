# The Taxation of Benefits under Government Pilot Schemes (Up-Front Childcare Fund) Order 2008

Made: 9th June 2008

Laid before the House of Commons: 9th June 2008

Coming into force: 1st July 2008

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 151(1)(a) and (7)(a) of the Finance Act 1996[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Taxation of Benefits under Government Pilot Schemes (Up-Front Childcare Fund) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 1st July 2008.

#### Income tax exemption under Part 2 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003

###### 2

The Income Tax Acts shall have effect in relation to any amount of benefit payable by virtue of the Government pilot scheme known as the Up-Front Childcare Fund as if it was exempt from income tax as employment income under Part 2 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003[^f00002].

## Signed

Dave Watts — Frank Roy — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 9th June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is concerned with benefit payable under the Government pilot scheme known as the Up-Front Childcare Fund. The Order provides that any amount of benefit payable by virtue of that pilot scheme is to be treated as exempt from income tax under Part 2 (employment income: charge to tax) of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003.

A full and final Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
