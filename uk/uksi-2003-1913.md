# The London Regional Transport (Dissolution) Order 2003

Made: 15th July 2003

Coming into force: 16th July 2003

Now therefore the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 302 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the London Regional Transport (Dissolution) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 16th July 2003.

#### Dissolution of London Regional Transport

###### 2

London Regional Transport is hereby dissolved.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Tony McNulty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 15th July 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of London Regional Transport on 16th July 2003. On 15th July 2003 London Regional Transport’s remaining property, rights and liabilities were transferred to Transport for London pursuant to a transfer scheme made under section 409(2) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999.
