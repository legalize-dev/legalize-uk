# The Climate Change Agreements, CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme and Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020

Made: 8th July 2020

Laid before Parliament: 9th July 2020

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2)

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1) of, and paragraph12(b) of Schedule 4 to, the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act2020[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Climate Change Agreements, CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme and Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into force immediately before IP completion day.

#### Amendment of the Climate Change Agreements (Amendment of Agreements) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018

###### 2

- (1) The Climate Change Agreements (Amendment of Agreements) (EU Exit) Regulations2018[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”;

- (b) in paragraph (2), in the inserted definition of “Annex 1 activities”, for “exit day” in both places it occurs substitute “IP completion day”.

#### Amendment of the CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations2018

###### 3

- (1) The CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(2)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph(a), in the substituted paragraph 16(3A) of Schedule 1 to the CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme Order 2013[^f00004], for “exit day” in each place it occurs substitute “IP completion day”;

- (b) in sub-paragraph(b), in the substituted paragraph 28(1) of that Schedule, for “exit day” in each place it occurs substitute “IP completion day”;

- (c) in sub-paragraph (c), for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

#### Amendment of the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations2018

###### 4

- (1) The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations2018[^f00005] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3(3)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (b), in the inserted paragraph 1A of Schedule 1 to the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations2014[^f00006], for “exit day” in each place it occurs substitute “IP completion day”;

- (b) for sub-paragraph (c) substitute—

> > (c) after paragraph 3 insert—
>   (3A) Paragraph 3 does not apply in respect of a qualification date on or after IP completion day.

## Signed

Kwasi Kwarteng — Minister of State for Business, Energy and Clean Growth — Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy — 8th July 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by section41(1) of, and paragraph12(b) of Schedule 4 to, the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act[2020 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/1) in order to replace references to “exit day” with references to “IP completion day”.

Regulation 2 amends the Climate Change Agreements (Amendment of Agreements) (EU Exit) Regulations2018 ([S.I. 2018/1205](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1205)).

Regulation 3 amends the CRC Energy Efficiency Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations2018 ([S.I. 2018/1336](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1336)).

Regulation 4 amends the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations2018 ([S.I. 2018/1342](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1342)). The amendment in regulation 4(2)(b) is supplementary to the amendment in regulation 4(2)(a). It retains paragraph3 of Schedule 1 to the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations2014 (S.I.2014/1643) (which was omitted by [S.I. 2018/1342](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1342)) for the purpose of a conversion to euros for a qualification date before IP completion day.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
