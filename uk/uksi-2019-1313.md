# The Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019

Made: at 2.00 p.m. on 7th October 2019

Laid before Parliament: at 4.00 p.m. on 7th October 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of these Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00002] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

## PART 1 — Introductory

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2019 and come into force in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) These Regulations come into force as follows—

- (a) as regards regulations 1, 4 and 8, immediately before exit day;

- (b) as regards all other regulations, on exit day.

- (3) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland only.

## PART 2 — Amendment of the Agriculture (Food) Provisions

#### Amendment of the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

###### 2

In the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00003], in Schedule 2, in Part 2 in the Table—

- (a) omit rows 18, 19 and 20 (relating to Article 8 of [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 589/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0589));

- (b) in row 26 (Article 12(2), fourth sub-paragraph), in the second column, for “Chapter III of Council [Directive 1999/74/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0074)” substitute “Schedule 3 to the Northern Ireland welfare regulations”.

## PART 3 — Amendment of the Animals Provisions

#### Amendment of the Importation of Animal Pathogens Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

###### 3

- (1) The Importation of Animal Pathogens Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Article 2, in the definition of “another Member State”—

- (a) for “another” substitute “a”;

- (b) omit “other than the United Kingdom”.

- (3) In Article 6, for “another” substitute “a”.

## PART 4 — Amendment of the Horticulture Provisions

#### Amendment of the Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

#### Amendment of the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

> > - In regulation 5— in paragraph (3), for “European Union” substitute “United Kingdom”; at the end insert—

## PART 6 — Amendment of the Wildlife Provisions

#### Amendment of the Invasive Alien Species (Enforcement and Permitting) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019

###### 9

- (1) The Invasive Alien Species (Enforcement and Permitting) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019[^f00011] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Parts 1 and 3, in each place where it occurs (except as otherwise indicated in paragraph (3)(b)), for “Union list” substitute “list of species of special concern”.

- (3) In Article 2(2)—

- (a) after the definition of “licence”, insert—

> > - “the list of species of special concern” means the list of species in the Annex to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/1141 adopting a list of invasive alien species of Union concern pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 1143/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council, as amended from time to time;

- (b) omit the definition of “the Union list”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of EU derived law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular, the deficiencies referred to in paragraphs (a), (c), (d) and (g) of section 8(2)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

Part 2 amends the Eggs and Chicks Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 ([S.R. 2010 No. 125](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2010/125)). It omits the Union requirements on the marking of eggs for cross-border delivery between Member States and substitutes a new domestic law reference.

Part 3 makes consequential amendments to the Importation of Animal Pathogens Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No. 433](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/433)) to substitute an amended reference to a Member State.

Part 4 amends the Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/1101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/1101)) to insert a corrected reference to the community marketing rules offences.

Part 5 amends the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No. 415](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/415)), the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/66)), the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 2018 ([S.R. 2018 No. 184](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2018/184)) and the Marketing of Seeds and Plant Propagating Material (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/211](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/211)).

Part 6 amends the Invasive Alien Species (Enforcement and Permitting) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019 ([S.R. 2019 No. 159](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2019/159)). It makes new substitutions in relation to the “Union List”.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
