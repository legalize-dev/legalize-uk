# The Armed Forces (Amendment of Service Courts Rules) Rules 2026

Made: 16th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 19th November 2026

Coming into force: 11th December 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 151, 153, 163 and 288 of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the Armed Forces (Amendment of Service Courts Rules) Rules 2026 and come into force on 11th December 2026.

- (2) These Rules extend to England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and British Overseas Territories except Gibraltar.

#### Amendment of the Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009

###### 2

In rule 23(2) of the Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009[^f00002] (record of proceedings), after sub-paragraph (d) insert—

> > (da) a record of any view announced by the court on passing sentence that the offence involved domestic abuse within the meaning of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021[^f00003] carried out by the offender, and for the purpose of this sub-paragraph, “*sentence*” includes any order made when dealing with that offender;

#### Amendment of the Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009

###### 3

In rule 22(2) of the Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009[^f00004] (record of proceedings), after sub-paragraph (f) insert—

> > (fa) a record of any view announced by the court on passing sentence that the offence involved domestic abuse within the meaning of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 carried out by the offender, and for the purpose of this sub-paragraph, “*sentence*” includes any order made when dealing with that offender;

#### Amendment of the Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009

###### 4

In rule 30(2) of the Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009[^f00005] (record of proceedings), after sub-paragraph (d) insert—

> > (da) a record of any view announced by the court on passing sentence that the offence involved domestic abuse within the meaning of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 carried out by the offender, and for the purpose of this sub-paragraph, “*sentence*” includes any order made when dealing with that offender;

#### Amendment of the Armed Forces (Summary Hearing and Activation of Suspended Sentences of Service Detention) Rules 2009

###### 5

In rule 27(1) of the Armed Forces (Summary Hearing and Activation of Suspended Sentences of Service Detention) Rules 2009[^f00006] (written record), after sub-paragraph (i) insert—

> > (ia) a record of any view of the commanding officer on passing sentence that the charge involved domestic abuse within the meaning of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 carried out by the offender, and for the purpose of this sub-paragraph, “*sentence*” includes any order made when dealing with that offender;

## Signed

Calvin Bailey — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Ministry of Defence — 16th November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

Explanatory Note

Under section 253A(2) of the Armed Forces Act [2006 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52), inserted by section 6 of the Sentencing Act [2026 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/2), the court or commanding officer has a duty to make a statement in open court when passing sentence for a service offence if the court or officer is of the view that the offence involved domestic abuse carried out by the offender.

These Rules amend the Armed Forces (Court Martial) Rules 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2041](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2041)), the Armed Forces (Service Civilian Court) Rules 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1209](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1209)), the Armed Forces (Summary Appeal Court) Rules 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1211](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1211)) and the Armed Forces (Summary Hearing and Activation of Suspended Sentences of Service Detention) Rules 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1216](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1216)) to ensure that any such view is included in the official record of the proceedings or summary hearing.

A full impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
