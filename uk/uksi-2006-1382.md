# The Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2006

Made: 19th May 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 107 of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2006 and extends to England and Wales.

#### Provisions coming into force on 31st May 2006

###### 2

The following provisions of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006 come into force on 31st May 2006—

- section 47;

- section 52 and Schedule 5;

- section 53 and Schedule 6;

- sections 56 and 57;

- sections 78 and 79;

- section 80 and Schedule 7;

- sections 81 to 86.

## Signed

Barry Gardiner — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th May 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force certain provisions of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act [2006 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/16) (“the Act”) on 31st May 2006.

Detailed explanation of the provisions can be found in the Explanatory Notes for the Act, which are available from The Stationery Office, PO Box 29, Norwich NR3 1GN (or online at www.opsi.gov.uk).
