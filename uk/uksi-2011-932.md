# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No.3) Order 2011

Made: 24th March 2011

Laid before Parliament: 25th March 2011

Coming into force: 26th March 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No.3) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 26th March 2011.

###### 2

In Part 1 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002], insert “Resolution 1973 (2011) of 17 March 2011 (Libya).” at the appropriate place.

###### 3

In Part 2 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 insert after “Council Decision 2011/137/CFSP of 28th February 2011 (Libya)”, “as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2011/175/CFSP of 21 March 2011 (Libya)” [^f00003].

## Signed

Damian Green — Minister of State — 24th March 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 (“the 2000 Order”) by adding to Part 1 of the Schedule to the 2000 Order United Nations Security Council Resolution 1973 (2011) of 17 March 2011 and to Part 2 of the Schedule to the 2000 Order European Union Council Implementing Decision 2011/175/CFSP of 21 March 2011, made in response to the situation in Libya.
