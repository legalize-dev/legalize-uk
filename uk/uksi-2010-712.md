# The Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008 (Commencement No.14) Order 2010

Made: 9th March 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 153(7) of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008 (Commencement No.14) Order 2010.

- (2) In this Order “the 2008 Act” means the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008.

#### Appointed date

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2008 Act come into force on 23 March 2010—

- (a) section 74 (hatred on the grounds of sexual orientation) to the extent that it is not already in force;

- (b) section 148(1) (consequential etc. amendments and transitional and saving provision), to the extent that it refers to paragraph 70 (Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00002]) of Schedule 26 (minor and consequential amendments);

- (c) section 149 (repeals and revocations), to the extent that it refers to the entry relating to section 233 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003, in Part 2 (sentencing) of Schedule 28 (repeals and revocations);

- (d) Schedule 16 (hatred on the grounds of sexual orientation) to the extent that it is not already in force;

- (e) in Schedule 26 (minor and consequential amendments), paragraph 70 (Criminal Justice Act 2003); and

- (f) in Part 2 (sentencing) of Schedule 28 (repeals and revocations), the entry relating to section 233 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003.

###### 3

The following provisions of the 2008 Act come into force on 1 April 2010—

- (a) section 148(1) (consequential etc. amendments and transitional and saving provision), to the extent that it refers to paragraph 83 (Offender Management Act 2007[^f00003]) of Schedule 26 (minor and consequential amendments); and

- (b) in Schedule 26 (minor and consequential amendments), paragraph 83 (Offender Management Act 2007).

###### 4

Section 144 (power to require data controllers to pay monetary penalty) of the 2008 Act, to the extent that it is not already in force, comes into force on 6 April 2010.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Claire M Ward — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 9th March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings provisions of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008[^f00004] into force. The provisions set out in article 2 come into force on 23 March 2010. The provisions set out in article 3 come into force on 1 April 2010. The provision set out in article 4 comes into force on 6 April 2010.
