# The Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1994

Made: 17th August 1994

Laid before Parliament: 24th August 1994

Coming into force: 19th September 1994

The Secretary of State for Education in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 231(2) and (3)(d) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 19th September 1994.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) Order 1990[^f00002] .

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 3

Condition B specified in article 2(2) of the principal Order, subject to which the property known as the former Rutland Primary School, Ashfield Street, London E1 was transferred to the London Borough of Tower Hamlets by that Order[^f00003], shall cease to apply in relation to that property, and accordingly in Schedule 11 to the principal Order the letter “B” opposite the entry relating to the property shall be deleted.

## Signed

Eric Forth — Minister of State, — Department for Education — 17th August 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Education (Inner London Education Authority) (Property Transfer) Order 1990, which transferred the premises of the former Rutland School to the London Borough of Tower Hamlets subject to a condition that the premises should be brought into use for Tower Hamlets' purposes as a local education authority by 1st April 1997 and, if not, disposed of with the proceeds of sale being distributed to the other inner London councils. This Order lifts that condition and amends the 1990 Order.
