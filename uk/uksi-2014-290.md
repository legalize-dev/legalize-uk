# The Further Education Loans (Amendment) Regulations 2014

Made: 11th February 2014

Laid before Parliament: 13th February 2014

Coming into force: 7th March 2014

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 22 and 42(6) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Further Education Loans (Amendment) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 7th March 2014.

#### Amendment of Further Education Loans Regulation 2012

###### 2

The Further Education Loans Regulations 2012[^f00002] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

#### Designated Courses

###### 3

In paragraph (1) of regulation 4 of the Further Education Loans Regulations 2012 omit sub-paragraphs (g) and (h).

#### Period of Eligibility

###### 4

In paragraph (3) of regulation 5 of the Further Education Loans Regulations 2012 omit sub-paragraphs (g) and (h).

## Signed

Matthew Hancock — Minister of State for Skills and Enterprise — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 11th February 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Further Education Loans Regulations 2012 to remove the Advanced Apprenticeship Framework and the Higher Apprenticeship Framework from the list of further education courses designated under regulation 4(1) of those Regulations, and make consequential changes.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
