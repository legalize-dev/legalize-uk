# The School Teachers’ Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019

Made: 16th July 2019

Laid before Parliament: 19th July 2019

Coming into force: 1st September 2019

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 123(4)(a) of the Education Act 2002 .

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the School Teachers' Incentive Payments (England) Order 2019 and comes into force on 1st September 2019.

- (2) This Order applies only in relation to school teachers in England.

#### Incentive payments to school teachers

###### 2

- (1) A lump sum payment made by the Secretary of State to a school teacher under one of the schemes mentioned in paragraph (2) is not to be treated as remuneration for the purpose of section 122(1) of the Education Act 2002.

- (2) The schemes are—

- (a) the Mathematics Early-Career Payments Pilot,

- (b) the Mathematics and Physics Teacher Retention Payments Pilot, and

- (c) the Teachers' Student Loan Reimbursement Pilot,

each administered by the Department for Education.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The School Teachers' Incentive Payments (England) Order 2018 is revoked.

## Signed

Nick Gibb — Minister of State — 2019-07-16
