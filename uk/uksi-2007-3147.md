# The South Tees Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2007

Made: 31st October 2007

Laid before Parliament: 6th November 2007

Coming into force: 1st December 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 213, 217(2) and 272(8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], having regard to changes in the functions of the North Yorkshire and York Primary Care Trust[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the South Tees Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1stDecember2007.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the South Tees Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00003];

- “the new Trust” means the North Yorkshire and York Primary Care Trust; and

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the new Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 9th October2007 and on behalf of the new Trust on 17th October2007.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trust to the new Trust.

- (2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference in relation to the trust property to the trustees of the trust property or to the old Trust shall be construed as a reference to the new Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Ben Bradshaw — Minister of Health — Department of Health — 31st October 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order transfers to the North Yorkshire and York Primary Care Trust, trust property held by the South Tees Hospitals National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
