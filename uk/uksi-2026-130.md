# The Education (Student Fees, Awards, Support and Loan Repayments) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 10th February 2026

Laid before Parliament: 12th February 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2), (3) and (4)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 2 of the Education (Fees and Awards) Act 1983[^f00001], sections 22 and 42(6) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00002] and sections 10(4)(b) and 119(5) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017[^f00003].

## Part 1 — Introductory

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Fees, Awards, Support and Loan Repayments) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) Subject to [paragraphs (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/1/3/enacted) and [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/1/4/enacted), these Regulations come into force on 5th March 2026.

- (3) [Regulation 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/7/enacted) (amendments to the payment of grants for living and other costs) comes into force on 9th March 2026.

- (4) [Part 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/part/4/enacted) comes into force on 1st April 2026.

#### Extent and application

###### 2

- (1) Any amendment made by these Regulations has—

- (a) the same extent, and

- (b) except as provided in paragraphs (2) and (3), the same application,

as the provision amended.

- (2) The amendments made by the following provisions apply only in relation to the provision of support to a student in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1st August 2026, whether anything done under these Regulations is done before, on or after that date—

- (a) [regulation 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/5/enacted) (amendments to the childcare grant);

- (b) [regulation 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/8/enacted) (amendments to remove the household income assessment of eligible care leavers in relation to loans for living costs);

- (c) [regulation 14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/14/enacted) (amendments relating to new payment rates for student support) and the Schedule (amendments to the Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011 relating to new payment rates for student support).

- (3) The amendments made by the following provisions apply only in relation to the provision of support to a student in relation to a course which begins on or after 1st August 2026, whether anything done under these Regulations is done before, on or after that date—

- (a) [regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/4/enacted) (amendments to the adult dependants’ grant), except where the student transfers to the course from a designated course that began before that date;

- (b) [regulation 24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/24/enacted) (amendments relating to the amount of loan for postgraduate master’s degrees);

- (c) [regulation 31](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/31/enacted) (amendments relating to the amount and payment of loan for postgraduate doctoral degrees).

- (4) In [paragraph (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/2/2/enacted), an “academic year” is the period of twelve months beginning with 1st January, 1st April, 1st July or 1st September of the calendar year in which the academic year of the course in question begins, according to whether the academic year of the course begins, respectively—

- (a) on or after 1st January and before 1st April,

- (b) on or after 1st April and before 1st July,

- (c) on or after 1st July and before 1st August, or

- (d) on or after 1st August and on or before 31st December.

- (5) In paragraph (3)(a), “*designated course*” has the meaning given in regulation 2(1) of the Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011[^f00004].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend various instruments relating to student finance.

[Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/part/1/enacted) makes general provision.

[Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/part/2/enacted) amends the [Education (Student Support) Regulations 2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) ([S.I. 2011/1986](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986)) (“*the Student Support Regulations*”).

[Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/part/3/enacted) amends the following related Regulations (“*the related Regulations*”):

- the [Education (Fees and Awards) (England) Regulations 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/779/contents) ([S.I. 2007/779](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/779)),

- the [Further Education Loans Regulations 2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1818/contents) ([S.I. 2012/1818](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1818)),

- the [Education (Postgraduate Master’s Degree Loans) Regulations 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/606/contents) ([S.I. 2016/606](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/606)) (“*the Master’s Degree Loans Regulations*”),

- the [Higher Education (Fee Limit Condition) (England) Regulations 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1189/contents) ([S.I. 2017/1189](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1189)), and

- the Education (Postgraduate Doctoral Degree Loans and the Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) (Amendment) (No. 2) etc.) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/599](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/599)) (“*the Doctoral Degree Loans Regulations*”).

[Part 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/part/4/enacted) amends the [Education (Student Loans) (Repayment) Regulations 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/470/contents) ([S.I. 2009/470](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/470)) (“*the Repayment Regulations*”).

[Regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/4/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to provide that adult dependants’ grant is only payable in respect of an adult dependant who is ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom. Where the adult dependant is ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom for only part of the relevant year, the amount of adult dependants’ grant payable is pro-rated accordingly.

[Regulation 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/5/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to provide that, for the purposes of childcare grant, prescribed childcare charges, other than in exceptional circumstances, do not include charges for childcare provided by a nanny.

[Regulation 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/6/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to provide that students who are lone parents qualify for higher rates of loans for living costs.

[Regulation 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/7/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to introduce a 13 week cut off point by when a person seeking payment of prescribed childcare charges must apply for that payment, other than in exceptional circumstances.

[Regulation 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/8/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to remove the household income assessment of eligible care leavers in relation to loans for living costs.

[Regulation 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/9/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to allow overstayers satisfying paragraph SUI 13.1 of the immigration rules to retain eligible student status. [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/part/3/enacted) makes corresponding amendments to the related Regulations where relevant.

[Regulation 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/10/enacted) amends the definition of an Erasmus Year in [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to provide that, for the purposes of grants and loans for living and other costs, students studying on the Welsh Government’s International Learning Exchange Programme are to be treated in the same way as students studying on the Turing Scheme.

[Regulation 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/11/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to update references to the Ukraine Schemes following amendments made to the immigration rules. [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/part/3/enacted) makes corresponding amendments to the related Regulations.

[Regulation 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/12/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to update references to Scottish legislation following the consolidation of various pieces of Scottish subordinate legislation. [Regulation 23](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/23/enacted) makes corresponding amendments to [the Master’s Degree Loans Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/606/contents).

[Regulation 13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/13/enacted) amends [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to remove references to the Scottish healthcare allowance, which no longer applies to English domiciled students.

[Regulation 14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/14/enacted) and [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/schedule/enacted) amend [the Student Support Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1986/contents) to update the payment rates for financial support for students in relation to academic years which begin on or after 1st August 2026.

[Regulation 24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/24/enacted) amends [the Master’s Degree Loans Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/606/contents) to update the maximum amount of the postgraduate master’s degree loan in relation to courses which begin on or after 1st August 2026.

[Regulation 31](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/130/regulation/31/enacted) amends the Doctoral Degree Loans Regulations to update the maximum amount of the doctoral degree loan, and the maximum individual payment of that loan in connection with any academic year, in relation to courses which begin on or after 1st August 2026.

Regulations 33, 34 and 35 amend [the Repayment Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/470/contents) to update references to HMRC, correct a typographical error and bring student loan repayments made through the self-assessment system provided for in [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/470/part/3) of [those Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/470/contents) into scope of the penalties established by [sections 116](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/26/section/116) and [117](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/26/section/117) of, and [Schedules 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/26/schedule/25) and [26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/26/schedule/26) to, the [Finance Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/26/contents) (c. 26).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
