# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made: 17th February 2003

Laid before Parliament: 20th February 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Part I of Schedule 11 to the Children Act 1989[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

#### Amendments to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991

###### 2

In the section headed “South Eastern Circuit” in Schedule 2 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002]—

- (a) omit the entry for the petty sessions area of Beccles;

- (b) in the entry for the petty sessions area of Cambridge in column (ii), for “Peterborough County Court” substitute “Cambridge County Court”;

- (c) after the entry for the petty sessions area of Eastbourne insert—

| East Cambridgeshire | Cambridge County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (d) omit the entry for the petty sessions area of Ely;

- (e) for the entry for the petty sessions area of Felixstowe substitute—

| Fenland | Peterborough County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (f) after the entry for the petty sessions area of Hastings insert—

| Haverhill and Sudbury | Ipswich County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (g) for the entry for the petty sessions area of Huntingdon substitute—

| Huntingdonshire | Peterborough County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (h) omit the entries for the petty sessions areas of Ipswich, Lowestoft and Mildenhall;

- (i) for the entry for the petty sessions area of Newmarket substitute—

| North East Suffolk | Ipswich County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (j) for the entry for the petty sessions area of North Witchford substitute—

| North West Suffolk | Ipswich County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (k) omit the entry for the petty sessions area of Risbridge;

- (l) for the entry for the petty sessions area of St Edmundsbury substitute—

| St Edmundsbury and Stowmarket | Ipswich County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (m) omit the entry for the petty sessions area of Saxmundham;

- (n) after the entry for the petty sessions area of Slough insert—

| South East Suffolk | Ipswich County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (o) omit the entries for the petty sessions areas of Stow, Sudbury and Cosford, Toseland, Wisbech and Woodbridge.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Rosie Winterton — Parliamentary Secretary — Lord Chancellor’s Department — Dated 17th February 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to Schedule 2 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/1677](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1677)), which allocates care centres to petty sessions areas grouped according to circuit. The amendments make Cambridge County Court a care centre with effect from 1st April 2003.
