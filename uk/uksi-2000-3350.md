# The Finance Act 2000, Schedule 6 Part V, (Appointed Day) Order 2000

Made: 21st December 2000

The Treasury, in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 30(3) of the Finance Act 2000[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2000, Schedule 6 Part V, (Appointed Day) Order 2000.

###### 2

The date appointed as the day on which paragraphs 59 and 60 of Part V of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 2000 come into force is 2 January 2001.

###### 3

The date appointed as the day on which paragraphs 53, 54, 55, 56, 57, 58 and 61 of Part V of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 2000 come into force is 29 January 2001.

## Signed

Bob Ainsworth — Greg Pope — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 21st December 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 2 January 2001 the provisions of primary law that empower Customs and Excise to make regulations about the registration process for climate change levy[^f00002] (CCL).

The primary law applying to CCL registration is brought into force by the Order on 29 January 2001[^f00003].
