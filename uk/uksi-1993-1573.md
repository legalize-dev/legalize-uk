# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1993

Made: 23rd June 1993

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 23rd day of June 1993 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 2 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1993.

- (2) The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 5) Order 1992[^f00002] is revoked.

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986. It adds Mauritius and Greece to the list of the Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction signed at The Hague on 25th October 1980 (Cm 33).
