# The Immigration (Variation of Leave) (No.2) Order 1991

Made: 25th April 1991

Coming into force: 26th April 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by sections 3(3)(a) and4(1) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Variation of Leave)(No.2) Order 1991 and shall come into force on the day after the day onwhich it is made.

###### 2

The condition imposed by article 2 of the Immigration (Variation ofLeave) Order 1991[^f00002]is hereby removed.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 25th April 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes the condition imposed by the Immigration(Variation of Leave) Order 1991 ([S.I.1991/77](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/77)) (revoked by [S.I.1991/980](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/980)) which requires all Iraqi nationals who are not also either Britishcitizens or persons exercising an enforceable Community right or rightsunder any provision made under section2(2) of the European Communities Act [1972 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68) and who have a limited leave to enter or remain in the UnitedKingdom, to register with the police as a condition of that leave ifsuch a requirement is not already such a condition.
