# The Contracting Out (Administration of the Teachers' Pensions Scheme) Order 2003

Made: 25th June 2003

Coming into force: 26th June 2003

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 69(2) and (4) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Contracting Out (Administration of the Teachers' Pensions Scheme) Order 2003 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the functions” mean the functions conferred on the Secretary of State by regulations made under all or any of—

- (a) sections 9, 12 and 24 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00003],

- (b) sections 16(2) to (4) and 130(a) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00004], or

- (c) section 172 of the Pensions Act 1995[^f00005].

#### Contracting out of functions

###### 3

The functions, or any of them, may be exercised by, or by the employees of, such person (if any) as may be authorised in that behalf by the Secretary of State.

#### Revocation

###### 4

The Contracting Out (Administration of the Teachers' Superannuation Scheme) Order 1996[^f00006] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

David Miliband — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 25th June 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order enables the Secretary of State, who has functions conferred on him by regulations made under the Superannuation Act 1972, the Pension Schemes Act 1993 and the Pensions Act 1995 regarding the teachers' pension scheme, to authorise another person, or that person’s employees, to carry out on his behalf those functions to such extent as is specified in the authorisation.

This Order revokes the Contracting Out (Administration of the Teachers' Superannuation Scheme) Order 1996. This Order differs from the revoked Order in that it includes the functions conferred by regulations made under section 172 of the Pensions Act 1995.

The current regulations made under sections 9, 12 and 25 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Superannuation Act 1972, sections 16(2) to (4) and 130(a) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 and section 172 of the Pensions Act 1995 include:

- the Teachers (Compensation for Redundancy and Premature Retirement) Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/311](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/311)) as amended by S.I.
