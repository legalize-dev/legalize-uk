# The Local Government (Promotion of Economic Development) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 14th September 1992

Laid before Parliament: 21st September 1992

Coming into force: 12th October 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 34(1) and (2) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Promotion of Economic Development) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 12th October 1992.

#### Addition of scheduled authorities

###### 2

The new Schedule in the Schedule to these Regulations shall be substituted for Schedule 1 (scheduled authorities) to the Local Government (Promotion of Economic Development) Regulations 1990[^f00002].

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Michael Howard — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 14th September 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 7 of the Local Government (Promotion of Economic Development) Regulations 1990 (“the principal regulations”) restricts the types of financial assistance for economic development of their areas which may be provided by local authorities in England other than the district and London boroughs named in Schedule 1 to those Regulations, and the corresponding county councils.

These Regulations replace that Schedule. No authority is removed from the Schedule. There are added 41 councils of districts in the counties of Dorset, East Sussex, Essex, Hampshire, Hertfordshire, Kent, Surrey and West Sussex, the Common Council of the City of London and 14 London borough councils.
