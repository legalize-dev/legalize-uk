# The Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made: 22nd June 1995

Laid before Parliament: 29th June 1995

Coming into force: 21st July 1995

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Coventry Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 21st July 1995.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Coventry Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 9th February 1995, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 21st July 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Health — 22nd June 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Walsgrave Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 21st July 1995 trust property formerly held by the Coventry Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from NHS Executive, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
