# The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 2) Order 1993

Made: 2nd July 1993

Coming into force: 24th July 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 2) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 24th July 1993.

###### 2

The Export of Goods (Control) Order 1992[^f00003] shall be further amended as follows:—

- in Group 3 of Part III of Schedule 1: at the end of entry 1C351 there shall be inserted— except: Isolated live cultures of a. to d. above in the form of vaccines[^f00004] ; at the end of entry 1C352 there shall be inserted— except: Isolated live cultures of a. or b. above in the form of vaccines ; entry 1C990 shall be deleted; after entry 1C991 there shall be inserted the following entry— 1C992 Isolated live cultures in the form of vaccines as follows: Bacillus anthracis Botulinum toxin. ; in entry 7A101 there shall be added at the end “, and specially designed components therefor”; in entry 7A102 there shall be added at the end “, and specially designed components therefor”; in entry 9A105 for “1.1 MNs” there shall be substituted “0.841 MNs”; after entry 9A106 there shall be inserted the following entry— 9A107 Solid propellant rocket engines, usable in **missiles**, other than those specified in entry 9A007, having a total impulse capacity of 0.841 MNs or greater[^f00005] ; in entry 9A119 after “9A105” there shall be inserted “, 9A107”; in entry 9B115 for “, 9A105, 9A106, 9A108, 9A109,” there shall be substituted “, 9A 105 to 9A109,”; in entry 9B116 for “, 9A104 to 9A106, 9A108, 9A109” there shall be substituted “, 9A104 to 9A109”; in entry 9E101 for “, 9A104 to 9A106, 9A108 to 9A111” there shall be substituted “, 9A104 to 9A111”; in entry 9E102: for “, 9A104 to 9A106, 9A108 to 9A111,” there shall be substituted “, 9A104 to 9A111,”; for “, 9B115, 9B116, 9B117,” there shall be substituted “, 9B115 to 9B117,”.

## Signed

M. V. Coolican — An Assistant Secretary, — Department of Trade and Industry — 2nd July 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1992:

- (i) by removing most vaccines from control;

- (ii) by removing fluorine from control;

- (iii) by extending the control on certain accelerometers designed for use in inertial navigation systems or in guidance systems to include specially designed components;

- (iv) by extending the control on certain gyros usable in missiles to include specially designed components;

- (v) by extending the control on certain solid and liquid fuelled rocket engines and rocket stages including specially designed production equipment, production facilities and technology required for their development, production or use.
