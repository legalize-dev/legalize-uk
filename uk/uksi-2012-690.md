# The Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 2012

Made: 4th March 2012

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 2012

Coming into force: 6th April 2012

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 2012, apply in relation to England only and come into force on 6th April 2012.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Authorised Officers (Meat Inspection) Regulations 1987[^f00004] are revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Paul Burstow — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 4th March 2012

(This note is not part of the Regulations)
