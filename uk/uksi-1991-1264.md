# The Employment Codes of Practice (Revocation) Order 1991

Made: 28th May 1991

Coming into force: 1st June 1991

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powersconferred on him by section 12(7) of the Employment Act 1990, herebymakes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Employment Codes of Practice(Revocation) Order 1991 and comes into force on 1st June 1991.

#### Revocation of Codes of Practice

###### 2

The following Codes of Practice are revoked —

- (a) the Code of Practice having effect under paragraph 4 of Schedule 17to the Employment Protection Act 1975[^f00002] (known as the Industrial Relations Code of Practice);

- (b) the Code of Practice issued in 1983 under section 3 of theEmployment Act 1980[^f00003] entitled“Closed Shop Agreements and Arrangements”.

## Signed

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Eric Forth — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Employment — 28th May 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order, which comes into force on 1st June 1991, revokes theIndustrial Relations Code of Practice originally issued by the Secretaryof State in 1972 under section 3 of the [Industrial Relations Act 1971(c.72)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/72). On the repeal of that Act by the Trade Unionand Labour RelationsAct [1974 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/52), the Code was maintained in force by Part I of Schedule1 to the 1974 Act; since the repeal of that Part by the EmploymentProtection Act 1975, the Code has been maintained in force by paragraph4 of Schedule 17 to the 1975 Act.

The Order also revokes the Code of Practice issued by the Secretary of State under section 3 of the Employment Act 1980 with the title“Closed Shop Agreements and Arrangements”. The Code was issued in 1983 and superseded an earlier Code ofPractice issued under the same section and having the same title.
