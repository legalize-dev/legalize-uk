# The Gambling Act 2005 (Operating Licence Conditions) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 10th March 2010

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

In accordance with section 355(4) of that Act a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by resolution of each House.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Gambling Act 2005 (Operating Licence Conditions) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendment

###### 2

- (1) The Gambling Act 2005 (Operating Licence Conditions) Regulations 2007[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3(3) for “50 pence” substitute “£1”.

- (3) In regulation 3(6)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “£50” substitute “£100”;

- (b) in paragraph (b) for “£35” substitute “£70”.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 10th March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Gambling Act 2005 (Operating Licence Conditions) Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/2257](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/2257)).

The 2007 Regulations specify conditions which are to be attached to operating licences issued under Part 5 of the Gambling Act [2005 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/19) in respect of facilities for prize gaming made available in bingo halls (that is, premises in respect of which a bingo premises licence has effect).

The effect of the amendments is to modify those conditions to increase the maximum participation fee and money prize levels.

The maximum participation fee for any one chance to win a prize in a particular game is increased from 50 pence to £1.

In addition, where a prize for which a game is played is money, the maximum amount of the prize is increased from £50 to £100 in cases where no persons under 18 years of age are permitted on the premises at any time when the game is being played; and from £35 to £70 in all other cases.

An Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of these Regulations.
