# The Welsh Health Common Services Authority (Revocation) Regulations 1999

Made: 11th March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 18th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise powers conferred on him by section 126(4) of and paragraphs 10 and 12 of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- These Regulations may be cited as the Welsh Health Common Services Authority (Revocation) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Revocation of Regulations

###### 2

The Welsh Health Common Services Authority Regulations 1990[^f00002]are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jon Owen Jones — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 11th March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations revoke the Welsh Health Common Services Authority Regulations 1990 which provided for the membership and procedure of the Welsh Health Common Services Authority.
