# The Gaming (Bingo) Act (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made: 2nd March 1992

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1992

Coming into force: 1st May 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph 5(1) of the Schedule to the Gaming (Bingo) Act 1985[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Bingo) Act (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st May 1992.

###### 2

In article 2 of the Gaming (Bingo) Act (Fees) Order 1986[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£33,000” there shall be substituted “£145,000”; and

- (b) in paragraph (b), for “£70,000” there shall be substituted “£140,000”.

###### 3

The Gaming (Bingo) Act (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1989[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 2nd March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 2(a) of the Gaming (Bingo) Act (Fees) Order 1986 so as to increase from £33,000 to £145,000 the fee payable to the Gaming Board for Great Britain for the issue of a certificate issued by the Board to an organiser of games of multiple bingo under the Schedule to the Gaming (Bingo) Act 1985. This Order also amends article 2(b) of the 1986 Order so as to increase from £70,000 to £140,000 the fee payable to the Gaming Board for Great Britain for the continuing in force, for a period of three years, of such a certificate.
