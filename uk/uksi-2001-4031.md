# The National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Amendment (No. 3) (England) Regulations 2001

Made: 17th December 2001

Laid before Parliament: 19th December 2001

Coming into force: 10th January 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977 [^f00001] and section 5(7) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Amendment (No. 3) (England) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 10th January 2002.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England only.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 11 of the National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990[^f00003] (disqualification for appointment of chairman and non-executive directors), after paragraph 5[^f00004] there is inserted—

> > (5A) A person shall not be disqualified by paragraph (1)(e) from being the chairman or a non-executive director of an NHS trust by reason of being a chairman or non-executive member of the National Patient Safety Agency[^f00005] or the National Treatment Agency[^f00006].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 17th December 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 11 of the National Health Service Trusts (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 1990, which make provision relating to the membership and procedure of National Health Service trusts, so as to provide that the chairman and members of the National Patient Safety Agency and the National Treatment Agency are not disqualified for appointment as a chairman or a non-executive director of a National Health Service trust.
