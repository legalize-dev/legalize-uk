# The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 28th October 1992

Laid before Parliament: 5th November 1992

Coming into force: 1st December 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 3(1), 4(1) and 19(1)[^f00001] of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations shall come into force on 1st December 1992 and may be cited as the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1992.

###### 2

For the Schedule to the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — (Testing)

###### 1

Fee for the testing of a unit of transport equipment £150

###### 2

Fee for the use of the facilities of a designated station for each day, or part of a day, in which such facilities are used for the purpose of the testing of a unit of transport equipment £365

###### 3

Fee for the certification of a unit of transport equipment which has been tested £50

## PART II — (Type Approval)

###### 4

Fee for the certification of a vehicle which a certifying officer is satisfied conforms with a type vehicle £50

## PART III — (Examination)

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kenneth Carlisle — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 28th October 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations change most of the fees charged under the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985, as amended by the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1987, for testing, type approval and examination of goods vehicles, railway wagons and containers carrying perishable foodstuffs on international journeys.

The fees are, and were previously, as follows:

| *TESTING* | *OLD FEE* | *NEW FEE* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Fee for testing unit of equipment | £250 | £150 |
| 2 Fee for use of designated station for each day, or part of a day, in which used for testing unit of equipment | £260 | £365 |
| 3 Fee for certification of unit of equipment which has been tested | £55 | £50 |

| *TYPE APPROVAL* | *OLD FEE* | *NEW FEE* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Fee for testing unit of equipment as a type vehicle and inspecting arrangements for ensuring conformity of production | £750 | £850 |
| 2 Fee for use of designated station for each day, or part of a day, in which used for testing unit of equipment as a type vehicle | £260 | £365 |
| 3 Fee for certification of a type vehicle | £325 | £245 |
| 4 Fee for certifying that vehicle conforms with a type vehicle | £60 | £50 |

| *EXAMINATION* | *OLD FEE* | *NEW FEE* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Fee for examination of unit of equipment at the Refrigerated Vehicle Test Centre | £55 | £55 |
| 2 Fee for examination of unit of equipment at place other than the Refrigerated Vehicle Test Centre— a for up to four units presented at the same time for examination, for each unit | £160 | £160 |
| b for each additional unit | £40 | £40 |
| 3 Fee for certification of vehicle which has been examined | £55 | £50 |
