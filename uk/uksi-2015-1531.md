# The Antarctic Act 1994 (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 2015

Made: 15th July 2015

Coming into force: 1st August 2015

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of July 2015Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by sections 34(2) and (3) of the Antarctic Act 1994[^f00001], and 18(2) of the Antarctic Act 2013[^f00002] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Act 1994 (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st August 2015.

#### Amendment of the Antarctic Act 1994 (Isle of Man) Order 1995

###### 2

The Antarctic Act 1994 (Isle of Man) Order 1995[^f00003] is amended as follows—

- (a) in article 2, after “the Antarctic Act 1994” insert “as amended by sections 14, 15 and 16 of the Antarctic Act 2013”.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Antarctic Act 1994 (Isle of Man) Order 1995 ([S.I. 1995/1035](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1035)), which extended the Antarctic Act [1994 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/15) to the Isle of Man with certain modifications, to extend the amendments made to that Act by Part 2 of the Antarctic Act [2013 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/15) to the Isle of Man.
