# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 1990

Made: 5th March 1990

Laid before Parliament: 12th March 1990

Coming into force: 2nd April 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 49, 51(1), 53(5) and 63(5) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Goods Vehicle (Plating and Testing Regulations 1988 (the “1988 Regulations”) as follows.

1 Regulation 13 makes the following amendments to the fees prescribed by the 1988 Regulations. a The fee payable on an application for a first examination or periodical test is increased from £26.50 to £28.00 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £14.50 to £15.25 in the case of a trailer. b The fee for a re-test or further re-test within 14 days after the first examination or earlier re-test at the same station or at another station and by arrangement is increased from £13.50 to £14.25 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £7.75 to £8.25 in the case of a trailer. The fee for re-tests in other circumstances is increased from £26.50 to £28.00 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £14.50 to £15.25 in the case of a trailer. c The fee payable on a request for an amendment to a plating certificate following a notifiable alteration or for a re-test where a particular in the certificate is or may be no longer applicable is increased from £11.00 to £11.75. d The fee payable on an application for a replacement for a lost or defaced plate, certificate or ministry test date disc is increased from £7.75 to £8.25. The Regulations do not affect the supplement to the fees payable for examinations or tests on Saturdays.

2 The Regulations substitute the word “constable” for a reference to “police constable”.

3 The opportunity has been taken to update references to the Road Traffic Act 1972 to refer to the Road Traffic Act 1988.
