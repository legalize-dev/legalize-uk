# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2015

Made: 25th February 2015

Laid before Parliament: 27th February 2015

Coming into force: 6th March 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2015 and comes into force on 6th March 2015.

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] substitute the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to this Order.

###### 3

The instrument listed in Schedule 2 to this Order is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 (the “2000 Order”) by substituting a new Schedule into the 2000 Order. Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the new Schedule or falling within a description in such an instrument will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the 2000 Order).

The effect of substituting the Schedule is to add to and delete from the list of designated instruments.

The following UN resolutions are added to Part 1 of the Schedule: Resolution 2160 (2014) of 17 June 2014 (Afghanistan), Resolution 2161 (2014) of 17 June 2014 (Al Qaida), Resolution 2170 (2014) of 15 August 2014 (Al Qaida), Resolution 2178 (2014) of 24 September 2014 (Al Qaida), Resolution 2196 (2015) of 29 January 2015 (Central African Republic), Resolution 2078 (2012) of 28 November 2012 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Resolution 2198 (2015) of 29 January 2015 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Resolution 2087 (2013) of 22 January 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea), Resolution 2188 (2014) of 9 December 2014 (Liberia), Resolution 2174 (2014) of 27 August 2014 (Libya) and Resolution 2140 (2014) of 26 February 2014 (Yemen).

The additions to Part 2 of the Schedule are Council Implementing Decision 2014/701/CFSP of 8 October 2014 (Afghanistan), Council Implementing Decision 2014/439/CFSP of 8 July 2014 (Belarus), Council Decision 2014/750/CFSP of 30 October 2014 (Belarus), Council Implementing Decision 2015/118/CFSP of 26 January 2015 (Côte d’Ivoire), Council Implementing Decision 2014/862/CFSP of 1 December 2014 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Council Decision 2014/728/CFSP of 20 October 2014 (Republic of Guinea), Council Decision 2014/776/CFSP of 7 November 2014 (Iran), Council Decision 2015/236/CFSP of 12 February 2015 (Iran), Council Decision 2014/700/CFSP of 8 October 2014 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea), Council Decision 2014/487/CFSP of 22 July 2014 (Libya), Council Decision 2014/727/CFSP of 20 October 2014 (Libya), Council Decision 2014/751/CFSP of 30 October 2014 (Republic of Moldova), Council Implementing Decision 2014/729/CFSP of 20 October 2014 (Somalia), Council Implementing Decision 2014/488/CFSP of 22 July 2014 (Syria), Council Implementing Decision 2014/678/CFSP of 26 September 2014 (Syria), Council Implementing Decision 2014/730/CFSP of 20 October 2014 (Syria), Council Implementing Decision 2015/117/CFSP of 26 January 2015 (Syria), Council Decision 2014/499/CFSP of 25 July 2014 (Ukraine), Council Decision 2014/508/CFSP of 30 July 2014 (Ukraine), Council Decision 2014/658/CFSP of 8 September 2014 (Ukraine), Council Decision 2014/801/CFSP of 17 November 2014 (Ukraine), Council Decision 2014/855/CFSP of 28 November 2014 (Ukraine), Council Decision 2015/241/CFSP of 9 February 2015 (Ukraine), Council Decision 2014/932/CFSP of 18 December 2014 (Yemen) and Council Decision 2015/277/CFSP of 19 February 2015 (Zimbabwe).

The deletions from Part 1 of the Schedule are Resolution 1893 (2009) of 29 October 2009 (Côte d’Ivoire), Resolution 1946 (2010) of 15 October 2010 (Côte d’Ivoire), Resolution 1980 (2011) of 28 April 2011 (Côte d’Ivoire), Resolution 2045 (2012) of 26 April 2012 (Côte d’Ivoire), Resolution 1896 (2009) of 30 November 2009 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Resolution 1952 (2010) of 29 November 2010 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Resolution 2021 (2011) of 29 November 2011 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Resolution 2136 (2014) of 30 January 2014 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Resolution 1874 (2009) of 12 June 2009 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea), Resolution 1928 (2010) of 7 June 2010 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea), Resolution 1985 (2011) of 10 June 2011 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea), Resolution 1903 (2009) of 17 December 2009 (Liberia), Resolution 1961 (2010) of 17 December 2010 (Liberia) and Resolution 2025 (2011) of 14 December 2011 (Liberia).

The deletions from Part 2 of the Schedule are Common Position 2000/696/CFSP of 10 November 2000 (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia) and Common Position 2001/155/CFSP of 26 February 2001 (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia).

Article 3 of and Schedule 2 to this Order revoke an instrument which previously amended the 2000 Order.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
