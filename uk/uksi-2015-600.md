# The Child Trust Funds (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 9th March 2015

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 2015

Coming into force: 6th April 2015

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12(2) and 28(1) of the Child Trust Funds Act 2004[^f00001], make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Child Trust Funds (Amendment) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 6th April 2015.

#### Amendment of the Child Trust Fund Regulations 2004

###### 2

The Child Trust Funds Regulations 2004[^f00002] are amended as follows.

###### 3

In regulation 9(2) and (3)(annual limit on subscriptions), for “£4,000”, substitute “£4,080”.

## Signed

Mark Lancaster — David Evennett — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 9th March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Child Trust Fund Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1450](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1450)) to increase the annual subscription limit to £4,080.

A Tax Information and Impact Note has not been prepared for this instrument as it contains no substantive change to tax policy.
