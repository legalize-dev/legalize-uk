# The (A1) London—Edinburgh—Thurso Trunk Road (Newcastle Western Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1986 Variation Order 1988

Made: 24th March 1988

Coming into force: 21st April 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

The (A1) London—Edinburgh—Thurso Trunk Road (Newcastle Western Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1986[^f00003] is hereby varied by substituting, for the figure “1240”, in Schedule 1 and in Schedule 3, the figure “1490”.

###### 2

This Order shall come into force on 21st April 1988 and may be cited as the (A1) London-Edinburgh-Thurso Trunk Road (Newcastle Western Bypass and Slip Roads) Order 1986 Variation Order 1988.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

J. A. Owen — Regional Director Northern Region — Department of Transport — 24th March 1988
