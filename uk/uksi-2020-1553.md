# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2020

Made: 16th December 2020

Coming into force: 17th December 2020

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 16th day of December 2020Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(2) of the Education Act 2005[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2020 and comes into force on 17th December 2020.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru.

## SCHEDULE

- Persons appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru.

- Alan George Edwards

- Carl Sherlock

- Charlotte Elizabeth Leese

- Gwawr Meirion Tudno Williams

- Jeffrey John Beecher

- John Tristan Roberts

- Nicola May Thomas

- Robert Richard Thomas

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
