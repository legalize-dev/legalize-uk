# The Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2008

Made: 23rd December 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(5) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2008.

###### 2

Sections 19 to 21 (disclosure of information and the intelligence services) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 together with Schedule 1 (disclosure and the intelligence services: consequential amendments) to that Act shall come into force after the day on which this order is made.

## Signed

Phil Woolas — Minister of State — 23rd December 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings sections 19 to 21 of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 into force on the day after the day on which it is made.
