# The Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1993

Made: 25th February 1993

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1992[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1993.

###### 2

The Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1992 shall come into force on 5th April 1993.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern *,C.* — Dated 25th February 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 5th April 1993 as the day on which the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act [1992 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/56) shall come into force.

The Act makes provision to amend the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) Act [1920 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1920/33) and the Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act [1972 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/18) in order to enable the new forms of procedure which now apply in proceedings in magistrates' courts in England and Wales under the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act [1978 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/22) or the Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41) to be used for reciprocal enforcement of maintenance orders and of claims for the recovery of maintenance.
