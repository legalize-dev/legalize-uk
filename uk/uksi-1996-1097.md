# The Bath-Lincoln Trunk Road A46 (Upper Swainswick to A420 Cold Ashton Roundabout) Orders 1987 Revocation Order 1996

Made: 11th April 1996

Coming into force: 3rd May 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 41 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Bath-Lincoln Trunk Road A46 (Upper Swainswick to A420 Cold Ashton Roundabout) Orders 1987 Revocation Order 1996 and shall come into force on 3rd May 1996.

###### 2

The following Orders are hereby revoked—

- (a) The Bath-Lincoln Trunk Road A46 (Upper Swainswick to A420 Cold Ashton Roundabout) Order 1987[^f00003], and

- (b) The Bath-Lincoln Trunk Road A46 (Upper Swainswick to A420 Cold Ashton Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1987[^f00004].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D. W. Ward — Director Road Programme Directorate Southern Operations — 11th April 1996
