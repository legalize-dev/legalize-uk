# The Recall Petition (Petition Officers’ Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

Made: 17th April 2024

Coming into force: 18th April 2024

The Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury, makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(1) and (3) of, and paragraph 3(1) and (2) of Schedule 1 to, the Recall of MPs Act 2015[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Recall Petition (Petition Officers’ Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

#### Amendment of the Recall Petition (Petition Officers’ Charges) Regulations 2024

###### 2

- (1) The Recall Petition (Petition Officers’ Charges) Regulations 2024[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the table in Schedule 2 (maximum recoverable amount for specified expenses), for the row containing the entry for “renting, heating, lighting and cleaning any building or room”, substitute—

| Renting, heating, lighting and cleaning any building or room | £9,932.50 per designated signing place | £12,415.62 per designated signing place | £11,918.99 per designated signing place |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Recall Petitions (Petition Offices’ Charges) Regulations 2024 ([S.I. 2024/298](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/298)) (“*the 2024 Regulations*”).

The 2024 Regulations prescribe the maximum amounts which a petition officer may recover in respect of a recall petition under the Recall of MPs Act 2015 (“*the Act*”). Schedule 2 to the 2024 Regulations specifies maximum recoverable amounts for petition officers for particular forms of expense incurred.

These Regulations amend the 2024 Regulations by substituting a row in Schedule 2 of the 2024 Regulations to include words initially omitted from that table in error. This additional wording clarifies that the maximum recoverable amount in respect of “Renting, heating, lighting and cleaning any building or room” is per designated signing place.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
