# The Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (Commencement) Order 1981

Made: 19th March 1981

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 155(7) of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (Commencement) Order 1981.

###### 2

The Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 shall come into force on 6th July 1981.

## Signed

W.S.I. Whitelaw — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 19th March 1981

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 into force on 6th July 1981.
