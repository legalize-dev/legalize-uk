# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Northeye) Order 1992

Made: 15th September 1992

Laid before Parliament: 24th September 1992

Coming into force: 15th October 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001],I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Northeye) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 15th October 1992.

###### 2

The prison known as Northeye Prison and situate in the county of East Sussex shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 15th September 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Prison Northeye on 15th October 1992.
