# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage IV) Designation (Amendment) Order 2001

Made: 20th September 2001

Laid before Parliament: 24th September 2001

Coming into force: 15th October 2001

Now, therefore, the Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 18(1) and (4)(d)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage IV) Designation (Amendment) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 15th October 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage IV) Designation Order 2000[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 3

- (1) Article 4(2) of the principal Order shall be amended by the substitution, for sub-paragraphs (a) and (b), of the following sub-paragraphs—

> > (a) item 12 in Part 3 of Schedule 1;
> (b) item 13, 14 or 15 in Part 3 of Schedule 3; or
> (c) item 10, 11 or 12 in Part 3 of Schedule 6.

- (2) Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the principal Order shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in item 8—

- (i) by the substitution for the entry in column 1 of—

> > (8) Management of woodland to enhance its nature conservation and landscape value.

; and

- (ii) in column 2, by deleting the figure “20”;

- (b) in item 9, by the substitution in column 2 of “£0.23” for the figure “£0.25”; and

- (c) after item 11, by inserting in column 1—

- (i)

> > (12) Hedge management.

; and

- (ii) in column 2, “£0.30 per metre”.

- (3) Part 3 of Schedule 3 to the principal Order shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in item 8—

- (i) by the substitution for the entry in column 1 of—

> > (8) Management of woodland to enhance its nature conservation and landscape value.

; and

- (ii) in column 2, by deleting the figure “15”;

- (b) in item 9, by the substitution in column 2 of “£0.23” for the figure “£0.25”; and

- (c) by inserting after item 14—

- (i) in column 1—

> > (15) Hedge management.

; and

- (ii) in column 2, the figure “£0.30 per metre”.

- (4) Part 3 of Schedule 5 to the principal Order shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in item 8—

- (i) by the substitution for the entry in column 1 of—

> > (8) Management of woodland to enhance its nature conservation and landscape value.

; and

- (ii) in column 2, by deleting the figure “20”;

- (b) in item 10(a), by the substitution in column 1, for “the period ending on 31st August 2001”, of the words “any period before 1st April 2001”; and

- (c) in item 10(b), by the substitution in column 1, for “the period beginning after 31st August 2001”, of the words “any period beginning after 31st March 2001.”

- (5) Part 3 of Schedule 6 to the principal Order shall be amended by inserting after item 11—

- (i) in column 1—

> > (12) Hedge management.

; and

- (ii) in column 2, “£0.35 per metre”.

## Signed

Elliot Morley — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th September 2001

We consent,

Tony McNulty — Gordon Brown — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 20th September 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage IV) Order 2000 (“the principal Order”) which designated areas in the Blackdown Hills, the Cotswold Hills, Dartmoor, the Essex Coast, the Shropshire Hills and the Upper Thames Tributaries as environmentally sensitive areas.

The Order amends, with effect from 15th October 2001, some of the rates of payments to be made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 and rectifies some errors contained in the principal Order (*article 3*).

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of this Order.
