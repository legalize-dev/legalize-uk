# The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1993

Made: 18th January 1993

Laid before Parliament: 19th January 1993

Coming into force: 10th February 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 10th February 1993.

#### General fund contribution limits

###### 2

The amount of contribution out of its general fund which a local authority may estimate that it will carry to the credit of its housing revenue account for the year 1993-94 shall be limited to nil.

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, The Scottish Office — 18th January 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that local authorities may not include in their estimates for the year 1993-94 any contribution from their general fund to their housing revenue account.
