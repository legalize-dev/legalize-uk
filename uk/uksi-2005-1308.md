# The Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 9th May 2005

Laid before Parliament: 12th May 2005

Coming into force: 3rd June 2005

The Secretary of State, being the Minister designated[^f00001] for the purpose of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to the transport of dangerous or environmentally hazardous goods by road, rail or water, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by the said section 2(2)[^f00003], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 3rd June 2005.

- (2) These Regulations do not extend to Northern Ireland.

#### Amendment to the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

###### 2

- (1) The Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations 2002[^f00004] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation) after sub-paragraph (o) add—

> > (p) Commission Directive [2004/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0089) adapting for the fifth time to technical progress Council Directive [96/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0049) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by rail[^f00005];
> (q) Commission Directive [2004/110/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0110) adapting for the sixth time to technical progress Council Directive [96/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0049) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by rail[^f00006]; and
> (r) Commission Directive [2004/111/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0111) adapting for the fifth time to technical progress Council Directive [94/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0055) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by road[^f00007].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) Regulations 2002 (“the 2002 Regulations”) extend the reference to dangerous substances in section 1(1)(c) of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (“the 1974 Act”) to include environmentally hazardous substances. The purpose of the 2002 Regulations is to enable regulations to be made under section 15 of the 1974 Act to implement the Directives referred to in regulation 2 of the 2002 Regulations.

These Regulations amend regulation 2 of the 2002 Regulations to add references to Commission Directives [2004/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0089) (O.J. No. L293, 16.9.2004, p.14), [2004/110/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0110) (O.J. No. L365, 10.12.2004, p.24) and [2004/111/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0111) (O.J. No. L365, 10.12.2004, p.25) so that Regulations can be made under section 15 of the 1974 Act to implement these Directives.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business. Copies of the EC Directive referred to in these regulations can be obtained from the stationary office. This Explanatory Note incorporates the Transposition Note that would otherwise be required.
