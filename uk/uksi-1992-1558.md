# The Beaumaris Port Health Authority (Revocation) Order 1992

Made: 23rd June 1992

Coming into force: 10th July 1992

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 2 and 4 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having given notice required by section 4(2) of that Act of his proposal to make this Order and not having received any notice of objection thereto, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Beaumaris Port Health Authority (Revocation) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 10th July 1992.

#### Revocations

###### 2

The Orders specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE

###### 1

Orders relating to the Beaumaris Port Health Authority:—

- (a) the Order of the Local Government Board dated 1st January 1889

- (b) the Order of the Local Government Board dated 13th March 1897

- (c) the Anglesey Review Order dated 31st July 1933

- (d) the Caernarvonshire Review Order 1934

- (e) the Order of the Minister of Health dated 14th March 1938.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

R. Jarman — An Under Secretary Welsh Office — 23rd June 1992
