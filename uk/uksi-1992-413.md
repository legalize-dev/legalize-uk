# The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1992

Made: 26th February 1992

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1992

Coming into force: 1st April 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

###### 2

For the Table of Fees in the Schedule to the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Table of Fees set out in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3

The Sheriff Court Fees Amendment Order 1991[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE — TABLE OF FEES

#### PART I — COMMISSARY PROCEEDINGS

###### 1

Petition for (a) appointment of executor, (b) restriction of caution, (c) special warrant, (d) sealing up repositories or the like, or (e) appointment of Commissary factor

###### 2

Sealing up repositories or the like, per hour

###### 3

- (a) Receiving and examining inventory of estate, except where sub-paragraph (b), (c) or (d) of this paragraph applies—

- (i) where the amount of the estate vested in or belonging beneficially to the deceased, of which confirmation is required, or for which resealing under the Colonial Probates Act 1892[^f00005] is required, does not exceed—

###### 4

Commissary copying and extracting

- (1) Issuing certificate of confirmation

- (a) if ordered when lodging inventory — each certificate

#### PART II — SHERIFF COURT PROCEEDINGS

Ordinary actions and various other proceedings

###### 5

Initial writ in any proceedings not being proceedings for which any other paragraph of this Table prescribes a fee

###### 6

First writ, reponing note or attendance to state a defence, or oppose an interim order in proceedings to which paragraph 5 of this Table applies, each defender or compearer

#### Bankruptcy proceedings

###### 7

Petition for sequestration of estates

###### 8

Act and warrant of trustee

###### 9

Writing minute of meeting, per hour

###### 10

Application (written or oral) for discharge of trustee

###### 11

Petition for discharge of bankrupt or application for approval of composition or deed of arrangement

###### 12

Sheriff clerk acting as commissioner

###### 12A

Miscellaneous applications including appeals under the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 1985[^f00006]

#### Service of heirs

###### 13

Petition for general or special service or completion of title, or note for a Crown or Prince’s charter, writ or precept

###### 14

Any proceedings under section 12 or 18 of the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982[^f00007]

#### Simplified divorce application

Simplified divorce application (inclusive of all procedures other than those specified in paragraphs 20 and 21 of this Table)

###### 20

#### Miscellaneous

#### PART III COMMISSARY AND SHERIFF COURT PROCEEDINGS — MISCELLANEOUS

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends from 1st April 1992 the Sheriff Court Fees Order 1985 by substi tuting a new Table of Fees in the Schedule to that Order. Most of the fees payable to the sheriff clerk in relation to proceedings in the sheriff court are increased and a new structure for the charging of certain commissary fees is introduced (paragraph 4 of the Schedule).
