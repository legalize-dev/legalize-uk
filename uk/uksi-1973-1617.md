# The International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973

Made: 21st September 1973

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(2) of the International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 hereby orders as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 (Commencement) Order 1973.

###### 2

The International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973 shall come into force on 1st October 1973.

## Signed

Limerick — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Trade — Department of Trade and Industry — 21st September 1973

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st October 1973 the International Cocoa Agreement Act 1973.
