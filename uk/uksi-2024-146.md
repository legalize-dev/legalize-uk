# The Road Vehicles (Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

Made: 13th February 2024

Laid before Parliament: 15th February 2024

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State has consulted with representative organisations in accordance with Article 82(3) of [Regulation (EU) 2018/858](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/0858) and Article 15(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 661/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0661).

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

- (2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on 7th March 2024.

- (3) In regulation 2(3)(b), the entries in the substituted table for “Emissions light duty vehicles (real driving emissions)” in item 2A come into force on 26th March 2024, except for the subject entry including note (3) (which accordingly comes into force on 7th March 2024).

- (4) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend [Regulation (EU) 2018/858](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/0858), Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2020/683](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0683) and Commission [Regulation (EU)1003/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1003).

Regulation 2 updates the table of regulatory acts for GB type-approvals and the table of UN Regulations recognised as equivalent to GB type-approval requirements, to take account of regulatory developments and to reinstate entries omitted in error by the European Commission in [Regulation (EU) 2018/858](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/0858). Regulation 2(4) updates the table of regulatory acts for which a manufacturer may be designated as a technical service, to reinstate an entry omitted in error by the European Commission in [Regulation (EU) 2018/858](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/0858).

Regulation 3 corrects a typographical error in Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2020/683](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/0683).

Regulation 4 amends Commission [Regulation (EU)1003/2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2010/1003) on the type-approval and mounting of rear registration plate spaces to ensure that compliance with updated EU technical requirements can be accepted as alternative to the GB requirements, for the purposes of GB type-approval.

The UN Regulations are issued by the United Nations Economic Commission for Europe. Copies of the UN Regulations referred to in these Regulations can be obtained from the UNECE website: http://www.unece.org/trans/main/wp29/wp29regs.html.

The net costs imposed on business, the voluntary sector and the public sector by these Regulations have been assessed as being less than £5m in any year and therefore a full impact assessment has not been prepared.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been prepared and is available alongside this instrument atwww.legislation.gov.uk, or from the Department for Transport, Great Minster House, 33 Horseferry Road, London, SW1P 4DR.
