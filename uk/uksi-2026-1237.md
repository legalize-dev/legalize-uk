# The Water Supply and Sewerage Services (Customer Service Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 24th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 25th November 2026

Coming into force: 23rd December 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 38(2) to (4), 38ZA(2) to (4), 95(2) to (4), 95ZA(2) to (4) and 213(2)(f) of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001] (“*the 1991 Act*”).

In accordance with sections 39(4)(b) and 96(4)(b) of the 1991 Act (including as applied by sections 39ZA and 96ZA of that Act, respectively), the Secretary of State considers that there are exceptional reasons why it is in the public interest that these Regulations should be made.

Before giving a notice under sections 39(5)(a) and 96(5)(a) of the 1991 Act (including as applied by sections 39ZA and 96ZA of that Act, respectively), in accordance with sections 39(7) and 96(7) of that Act the Secretary of State arranged for such research as the Secretary of State considered appropriate with a view to discovering the views of a representative sample of persons likely to be affected by these Regulations (“the relevant research”).

The Secretary of State has, in accordance with sections 39(5), (6) and (8) and 96(5), (6) and (8) of the 1991 Act (including as applied by sections 39ZA and 96ZA of that Act, respectively), given notice of the proposals for these Regulations and considered the results of the relevant research and every representation or objection with respect to the proposals which has been duly made and not withdrawn.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Water Supply and Sewerage Services (Customer Service Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 23rd December 2026.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the Water Supply and Sewerage Services (Customer Service Standards) Regulations 2008

###### 2

The Water Supply and Sewerage Services (Customer Service Standards) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 5.

#### Amendment of regulation 17IA

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 17IA (domestic customer in arrears) is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (1), after “supply of water” insert “or sewerage services (or both)”.

- (3) In paragraph (2), at the beginning insert “Subject to paragraph (2A),”.

- (4) After paragraph (2) insert—

> > (2A) Paragraph (2) does not apply where the customer is subject to insolvency proceedings.

- (5) In paragraph (3)—

- (a) for sub-paragraph (a) substitute—

> > (a) registers a default status on an account held by a credit reference agency in respect of the customer because the customer has failed to pay them;

- (b) in sub-paragraph (b), for “begins legal proceedings for the purposes of” substitute “files a claim form with a court for the purpose of beginning legal proceedings for”.

- (6) In paragraph (4)(d), at the end insert “or the number of the account held by the English service provider in respect of the customer”.

- (7) In paragraph (6), in the words after sub-paragraph (b), after “must”, in the second place that word occurs, insert “, so far as reasonably practicable,”.

- (8) In paragraph (9), after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

> > (c) “*insolvency proceedings*” means proceedings under any of the following—
>   (i) the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00003];
>   (ii) the Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016[^f00004].

#### Amendment of regulation 17IC

###### 4

- (1) Regulation 17IC (reading of meters) is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (1), in the words before sub-paragraph (a)—

- (a) at the beginning insert “Subject to paragraph (1A),”;

- (b) omit “relevant”;

- (c) at the end insert “(whether having direct access to the meter installation or otherwise)”.

- (3) After paragraph (1) insert—

> > (1A) Where the customer sends a meter reading to the English service provider during a 13-month period referred to in paragraph (1), the requirement in paragraph (1) is deemed to be met for that period.

- (4) In paragraph (2) omit “relevant”.

- (5) In paragraph (3) omit “relevant”.

- (6) In paragraph (4)—

- (a) for the definition of relevant date substitute—

> > - “*relevant date*” means— in relation to a meter other than a smart meter— where the meter was installed before 1st October 2025, that date; in any other case, the day on which the meter is installed; in relation to a smart meter— where the meter was installed before 23rd December 2026, that date; in any other case, the day on which the meter is installed;

- (b) omit the definition of “relevant meter”.

#### Amendment of regulation 17IF

###### 5

For regulation 17IF (indexation) substitute—

> > (17IF)
>   (1) Where, on 1st April of any year, the CPI% is at least 10%—
>     (a) the specified amounts are to be increased by the CPI%, and
>     (b) the resulting amounts are to be rounded to the nearest £5, with numbers at the midpoint being rounded up.
>   (2) An increase under paragraph (1) applies only in respect of a breach of a service standard which occurs on or after the date of the increase.
>   (3) In this regulation—
>     - “*consumer prices index*” means the all items consumer prices index published by the Statistics Board;
>     - “*CPI%*” means the percentage increase or decrease in the consumer prices index— calculated by reference to— the consumer prices index for the previous September, and the consumer prices index for the reference month, and rounded to the nearest 0.1%, with numbers at the midpoint being rounded up;
>     - “*reference month*” means— where no increase under paragraph (1) has taken effect, September 2025; otherwise, the September preceding the 1st April on which the most recent increase took effect;
>     - “*specified amounts*” means each of the amounts payable under regulations 17C to 17IE, 17J and 17JA, as increased from time to time under paragraph (1).

#### Transitional provision

###### 6

- (1) Regulation 4(3) does not apply in relation to any meter reading sent to an English service provider before 23rd December 2026.

- (2) In paragraph (1), “*English service provider*” has the meaning given in regulation 17A of the Water Supply and Sewerage Services (Customer Service Standards) Regulations 2008.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Water Supply and Sewerage Services (Customer Service Standards) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/594](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/594)).

Regulation 3 amends regulation 17IA (domestic customer in arrears) in relation to its application to providers of sewerage services, creating an exception to the paragraph (2) obligations, the relevant steps, the content of an outstanding charges notice and the requirement to use a second means of communication to contact a customer.

Regulation 4 amends regulation 17IC (reading of meters) in relation to its application to smart meters and to create an exception to the paragraph (1) requirement where a customer sends a meter reading.

Regulation 5 substitutes regulation 17IF (indexation). Regulation 17IF was inserted by the Water Supply and Sewerage Services (Customer Service Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 2025 ([S.I. 2025/662](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/662)) to provide for indexation of fees but the provision did not achieve the policy intention. This substitution corrects the indexation mechanism.

Regulation 6 makes transitional provision in relation to regulation 4(3).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
