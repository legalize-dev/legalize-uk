# The Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) (Forms) Order 2015

Made: 11th February 2015

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

Accordingly, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(4) and (6) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998, makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) (Forms) Order 2015.

- (2) This Order comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (3) In this Order—

- “the 2001 Order” means the Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) Order 2001[^f00003];

- “the 2008 Regulations” means the Representation of the People (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2008[^f00004]; and

- “the elections rules” means the parliamentary elections rules contained in Schedule 1 to Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00005].

#### Amendments to Schedule 1 to the 2001 Order

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1 to the 2001 Order (application with modifications of statutory provisions relating to elections)[^f00006] is amended as follows.

- (2) In the right-hand column of the Schedule, in the entry in respect of rule 44B of the elections rules[^f00007], in paragraph (3)(a) omit the words “and authenticated”.

- (3) In the right-hand column of the Schedule, alongside the entry in respect of the form of ballot paper in the Appendix of Forms in the elections rules—

- (a) omit the words “the front of the”; and

- (b) after “ballot paper” insert “(the form of front of ballot paper and the form of back of ballot paper)”.

- (4) In the right-hand column of the Schedule, alongside the entry in respect of the form of directions for the guidance of the voters in voting in the Appendix of Forms in the elections rules, for both paragraphs of the existing text, substitute—

> > For the form substitute the form set out in the Annex to this Schedule.

- (5) In the right-hand column of the Schedule, alongside the entry in respect of the form of declaration to be made by the companion of a voter with disabilities in the Appendix of Forms in the elections rules, before the existing text insert—

> > For the form substitute the form set out in the Annex to this Schedule.

- (6) The Annex to Schedule 1 to the 2001 Order is amended as follows.

- (7) In the form of front of ballot paper—

- (a) in the heading to that form, omit “front of”; and

- (b) replace the form with the form in Schedule 1 to this Order.

- (8) Replace the form of directions as to printing the ballot paper with the form in Schedule 2 to this Order.

- (9) After the form of directions as to printing the ballot paper, insert—

- (a) the form of directions for the guidance of the voters in voting in Schedule 3 to this Order; and

- (b) the form of declaration to be made by the companion of a voter with disabilities in Schedule 4 to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Schedules 1 and 2 to the Northern Ireland Assembly (Elections) Order 2001 apply certain provisions of the Representation of the People Act [1983 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2) and the Representation of the People (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1741](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1741)) to elections to the Northern Ireland Assembly. The forms for use at parliamentary elections are amended by the Parliamentary Elections (Forms) (Northern Ireland) Order 2015. Articles 2 and 3 of this Order amend Schedules 1 and 2 to apply those amended provisions to Assembly elections, with appropriate changes.

The forms which have been changed for Assembly elections (whether by modification or substitution of parliamentary forms) are the forms relating to ballot papers, directions as to printing the ballot paper, declarations to be made by the companion of a voter with disabilities, poll cards, postal poll cards, proxy poll cards, proxy postal poll cards, guidance for voters, proxy papers, certificates of employment, declarations of identity, and declarations of identity at combined polls.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
