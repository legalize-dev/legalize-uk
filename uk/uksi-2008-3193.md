# The Wireless Telegraphy (Register) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2008

Made: 15th December 2008

Coming into force: 5th January 2009

Before making the Regulations OFCOM have given notice of their proposal to do so in accordance with section 122(4)(a) of the Act, published notice of their proposal in accordance with section 122(4)(b) of the Act and have considered the representations made to them before the time specified in the notice in accordance with section 122(4)(c) of the Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Register) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 5th January 2009.

#### Amendment of the Wireless Telegraphy (Register) Regulations 2004

###### 2

Part 4 of the Schedule to the Wireless Telegraphy (Register) Regulations 2004[^f00002] is amended by inserting in Column 2 of Part 4 of the Schedule after “422.0 MHz to 424.0 MHz”—

| 542–550 MHz |
| --- |
| 742–750 MHz |
| 758–766 MHz |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Wireless Telegraphy (Register) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/3155](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3155) as amended by [S.I. 2006/340](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/340), [S.I. 2006/1808](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/1808), [S.I. 2007/381](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/381), [S.I. 2007/3389](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/3389), [S.I. 2008/689](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/689) and [S.I. 2008/2104](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/2104)) (the “principal Regulations”).

The principal Regulations require OFCOM to establish and maintain a public register of relevant information relating to wireless telegraphy licences of certain classes which apply to a station or apparatus operating within certain frequency bands specified in the Schedule to the principal Regulations.

Regulation 2 of these Regulations amends the Schedule to the principal Regulations.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that these Regulations will have on the costs to business is available to the public from OFCOM at Riverside House, 2a Southwark Bridge Road, London SE1 9HA (Tel: 020 7981 3000) or on the OFCOM internet website at www.ofcom.org.uk. Copies of the report have also been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament.
