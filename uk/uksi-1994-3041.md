# The Excise Duty (Amendment of the Isle of Man Act 1979) Order 1994

Made: 28th November 1994

Laid before the House of Commons: 30th November 1994

Coming into force: 1st January 1995

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 1(2) of the Isle of Man Act 1979[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Excise Duty (Amendment of the Isle of Man Act 1979) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st January 1995.

###### 2

- (1) Beer duty shall become a common duty and accordingly the Isle of Man Act 1979 shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In section 1(1) (common duties) in paragraph (b) the words “, other than beer,” shall be omitted.

## Signed

Tim Wood — Andrew Mackay — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 28th November 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 1st January 1995, amends section 1(1) of the Isle of Man Act [1979 (c. 58)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/58). It provides for the excise duty on beer imported into or produced in the United Kingdom or the Isle of Man to be included in the common duty arrangements.
