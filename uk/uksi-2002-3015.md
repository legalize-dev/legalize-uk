# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Commencement No. 1 and Savings) Order 2002

Made: 4th December 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 458(1) and 459(2) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00001], and after consultation with the Scottish Ministers, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Commencement No. 1 and Savings) Order 2002.

- (2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

- (1) The provisions of the Act listed in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 30th December 2002, subject to the savings contained in this Order.

- (2) But where a particular purpose is specified in relation to any such provision in column 2 of that Schedule, the provision concerned shall come into force only for that purpose.

#### Savings

###### 3

Sections 42 to 48 of the Drug Trafficking Act 1994[^f00002] shall continue to have effect in relation to cash seized under section 42 of that Act before 30th December 2002.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 30th December 2002, the following provisions of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (“the Act”):

- (a) sections 89 and 90, which enable the making of an order as to appeals under Part 2 of the Act;

- (b) chapters 1, 3 and 5 of Part 5 (except for sections 312 and 313), which create a new scheme for the search for, and seizure, detention and forfeiture of, cash derived from, or intended for use in, unlawful conduct;

- (c) certain minor and consequential amendments in Schedule 11, together with entries in the repeals Schedule, Schedule 12; and

- (d) other provisions which it is necessary to bring into force in order to give full effect to the provisions listed above.

Article 3 makes savings in relation to the existing scheme for seizure of cash relating to drug trafficking.
