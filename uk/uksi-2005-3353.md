# The Nursing and Midwifery Council (Fees) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 2005

Made: 5th December 2005

Laid before Parliament: 7th December 2005

Coming into force: 1st January 2006

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 5th day of December 2005 By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Having considered the Rules, their Lordships approve them.

This Order may be cited as the Nursing and Midwifery Council (Fees) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 2005 and shall come into force on 1st January 2006.

## SCHEDULE — THE NURSING AND MIDWIFERY COUNCIL (FEES) (AMENDMENT) RULES 2005

The Nursing and Midwifery Council makes the following Rules in exercise of its powers conferred under articles 7(1) and (2), 33(7)(a) and 47(2) of the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001[^f00002].

The Nursing and Midwifery Council has consulted in accordance with articles 7(3) and 47(3) of that Order.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Nursing and Midwifery Council (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2005 and shall come into force on 1st January 2006.

###### 2

In these Rules “the principal Rules” means the Nursing and Midwifery Council (Fees) Rules 2004[^f00003].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which is made under the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001, approves Rules made by the Nursing and Midwifery Council which amend the Nursing and Midwifery Council (Fees) Rules 2004.

Rules 3 makes various adjustments to the fees to be paid in connection with applications for inclusion in the register.

Rule 4 provides that in each three year registration period which begins on or after 1st January 2006 (other than one which ended on 31st December 2005) a registrant will be required to pay a fee for retention of registration at the end of the first and second years of each three year registration period. A registrant will still be required to pay a renewal fee at the end of a registration period if she wishes her registration to be renewed.
