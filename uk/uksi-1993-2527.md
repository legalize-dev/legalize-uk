# The Police (Amendment) (No. 4) Regulations 1993

Made: 16th October 1993

Laid before Parliament: 28th October 1993

Coming into force: 22nd November 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 33 of the Police Act 1964[^f00001], after taking into consideration the recommendations made by the Police Negotiating Board for the United Kingdom and furnishing the said Board with a draft of the Regulations in accordance with section 2(1) of the Police Negotiating Board Act 1980[^f00002], I hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation.

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police (Amendment) (No. 4) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 22nd November 1993, but regulation 2 shall have effect as from 1st April 1993 and regulations 3 and 4 shall have effect as from 1st September 1993.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Police Regulations 1987[^f00003].

#### Removal allowance

###### 2

Regulation 53(7) of the principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the figure “£1,257” the figure “£1,290”.

#### Scales of pay

###### 3

Schedule 5 to the principal Regulations is amended by substituting for Tables A and B the tables set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Dog handler’s allowance

###### 4

Schedule 11 to the principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the figure “£915” the figure “£930” and for the figure “£1,251” the figure “£1,269”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulation)

These Regulations further amend the Police Regulations 1987. Regulation 2 increases, with effect from 1st April 1993, the maximum amount of removal allowance payable in respect of incidental expenses. Regulation 3 increases pay, and regulation 4 increases the dog handler’s allowance, both with effect from 1st September 1993. Retrospection is authorised by section 33(4) of the Police Act 1964.
