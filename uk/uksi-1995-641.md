# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 10) Order 1995

Made: 6th March 1995

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3) of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No. 10) Order 1995.

###### 2

Section 82 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 shall come into force forthwith.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.* — Dated 6th March 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force forthwith section 82 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990. That section amends the Judicial Pensions Act [1981 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/20) to allow Regulations to be made entitling a member of a judicial pension scheme constituted by that Act or by the Sheriffs' Pensions (Scotland) Act [1961 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1961/42) to make voluntary contributions towards the cost of the provision of additional benefits under the scheme.
