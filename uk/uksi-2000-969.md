# The Northumbrian Water and Essex and Suffolk Water (Amendment of Local Enactments Etc.) Order 2000

Made: 23rd March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the Water Industry Act 1991, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Northumbrian Water and Essex and Suffolk Water (Amendment of Local Enactments Etc.) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

- (2) In this Order—

- “Northumbrian” means Northumbrian Water Limited; and

- “Essex and Suffolk” means Essex and Suffolk Water plc.

#### Amendment of references in local statutory provisions etc.

###### 2

- (1) Any reference to Essex and Suffolk, or to the undertaking of Essex and Suffolk, in any local statutory provision and in any charges scheme made by Essex and Suffolk under section 76 of the Water Act 1989 or section 143 of the Water Industry Act 1991 shall have effect, subject to paragraph (2), as a reference to Northumbrian or, as the case may be, to the undertaking of Northumbrian.

- (2) Paragraph (1) only applies to any local statutory provision or charges scheme in relation to the area, things or persons in relation to which or whom that provision or scheme has effect before 1st April 2000.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Bob Dinwiddy — A Senior Civil Servant in the — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 23rd March 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends references in local statutory provisions and charges schemes for the purposes of the replacement of Essex and Suffolk Water plc as water undertaker by Northumbrian Water Limited.
