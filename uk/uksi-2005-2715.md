# The Charges for Inspections and Controls (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2005

Made: 28th September 2005

Laid before Parliament: 3rd October 2005

Coming into force: 31st October 2005

Makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Charges for Inspections and Controls (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2005 and come into force on 31st October 2005.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Charges for Inspections and Controls Regulations 1997 (“the principal Regulations”) which give effect to Article 2 of Council Directive [85/73/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0073) on the financing of veterinary inspections and controls covered by Directives [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0662), [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425), [90/675/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0675) AND [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) (OJ No. L32, 30.1.98, p. 14); that Directive was last amended by Council Directive [97/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0079) (OJ No. L 24, 30.1.98, p. 31). Article 2, as read with Article 5(3) requires member States to ensure that fees covering the actual cost of inspection alternatively the fees provided in Annex B which was inserted by article 1 of Council Directive [96/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0043) amending and consolidating Directive [85/73/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0073) in order to ensure financing of veterinary inspections and controls on live animals and certain animal products and amending Directives [90/675/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0675) and [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) (O.J. L162, 1.07.96, p. 1) are collected to cover the cost of inspections and controls.

The inspections and controls to which the fees apply relate to checks for residues of veterinary medicines and other substances in animals and animal products. The changes (by comparison with the principal Regulations), ranging approximately from decreases of 15% to increases of 110%, are itemised in the table below. Other sums are the same as before.
