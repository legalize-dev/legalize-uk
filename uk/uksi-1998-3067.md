# The Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) (Amendment) Order 1998

Made: 7th December 1998

Coming into force: 10th December 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by sections 12(4) and 30(1)(b) of the Criminal Justice Act 1991[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) (Amendment) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 10th December 1998.

###### 2

In article 4 of the Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) Order 1997[^f00002], for the words “Geografix Limited, Hurricane Way, Norwich NR6 6EW” there shall be substituted the words “Premier Monitoring Services Limited, Centennial Court, Easthampstead Road, Bracknell, Berkshire RG12 1YQ”.

## Signed

Gareth Williams — Minister of State — 7th December 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Curfew Order (Responsible Officer) Order 1997 as respects the definition of a person who is to be made responsible, by a curfew order relating to a place of curfew in Cambridgeshire, Norfolk or Suffolk, for monitoring an offender’s whereabouts during the curfew period.
