# The Mid Essex Community and Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996

Made: 2nd December 1996

Coming into force: 12th December 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Mid Essex Community and Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1996.

#### Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 2

In Article 4 of the Mid Essex Community and Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00003] for “4 non-executive” there shall be substituted “5 non-executive” and for “4 executive” there shall be substituted “5 executive”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Horam — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — 2nd December 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by increasing the number of non-executive directors from 4 to 5 and the number of executive directors from 4 to 5.
