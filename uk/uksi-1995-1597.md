# The A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1995

Made: 19th June 1995

Coming into force: 30th June 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A312 Trunk Road (The Parkway, Hounslow) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 30th June 1995.

###### 2

In this Order—

- “The Parkway” means the A312 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Hounslow known as The Parkway;

- “the junction” means the junction at the intersection of The Parkway and High Street, Cranford; and

- “the traffic island” means the island site in the north-westbound carriageway of The Parkway which extends south-eastwards from the junction for a distance of 12 metres.

###### 3

Every person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the right of the traffic island shall cause that vehicle to turn right at the junction.

###### 4

Every person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the left of the traffic island shall cause that vehicle to proceed straight ahead or turn left at the junction.

###### 5

Nothing in this Order shall apply to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

P J Bevis — A Grade 6 Officer Highways Agency London Office — 19th June 1995
