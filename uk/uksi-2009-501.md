# The Licensed Conveyancers (Compensation for Inadequate Professional Services) Order 2009

Made: 5th March 2009

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 2009

Coming into force: 31st March 2009

In accordance with paragraph 16(3) of that Schedule, he has consulted the Council for Licensed Conveyancers before making this Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensed Conveyancers (Compensation for Inadequate Professional Services) Order 2009 and comes into force on 31st March 2009.

#### Amendment of Courts and Legal Services Act 1990

###### 2

In paragraph 16(1) of Schedule 8 to the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990, for the figure “£5,000”, substitute the figure “£15,000”.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Licensed Conveyancers (Compensation for Inadequate Professional Services) Order 2000[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Bridget Prentice — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 5th March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order increases the maximum compensation that the Council for Licensed Conveyancers may direct a licensed conveyancer to pay his client for inadequate professional services from £5,000 to £15,000, by substituting that figure in the Courts and Legal Services Act [1990 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/41). It revokes the Licensed Conveyancers (Compensation for Inadequate Professional Services) Order 2000 which increased the maximum compensation from £1,000 to £5,000.
