# The Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 8th October 2009

Laid before Parliament: 20th October 2009

Coming into force: 9th November 2009

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Department for Agriculture and Rural Development, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(4)(e) and (6) of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] and after consultation with the Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) (Amendment) Order 2009; and

- (b) comes into force on the 9th November 2009.

#### Amendments to the Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) Order 2007

###### 2

- (1) The Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) Order 2007[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) For article 1(1)(b) substitute—

> > (b) applies in the United Kingdom save for Part 2 which applies in England, Scotland and Wales only; and

- (3) In article 3(2)(b)—

- (a) replace the comma immediately after “Secretary of State” with “or”; and

- (b) omit “or the National Assembly for Wales”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order applies Part 2 (artificial insemination of cows) of the Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1315](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1315)) to Wales. It removes the National Assembly for Wales from the list of persons who may approve training courses in the artificial insemination of cows.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as only a minor impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
