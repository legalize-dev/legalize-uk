# The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) (No. 3) Traffic Order 1999

Made: 28th September 1999

Coming into force: 8th October 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) (No. 3) Traffic Order 1999, and shall come into force on 8th October 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- (a) causing includes permitting;

- (b) “vehicle” includes part of a vehicle;

- (c) “bus” has the meaning given by regulation 22(2) of the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 1994[^f00003];

- (d) “Local bus” and “Local Service” have the meanings given by regulation 4 of the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 1994[^f00003];

- (e) “The trunk road” means the A205 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Lewisham.

#### Prohibition

###### 3

Except as provided in article 4 below, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding westwards in that length of the trunk road known as St Mildreds Road to turn right into Hither Green Lane.

#### Exemptions

###### 4

None of the controls specified in article 3 apply to—

- (a) anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden; or

- (b) in respect of a vehicle being used for ambulance, fire brigade or police purposes.

#### Revocation or variation of existing Orders

###### 5

The Lewisham (Prescribed Routes) (No.3) Traffic Order 1972 and the A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route (Prohibited Turns) Experimental Traffic Order 1998[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 28th September 1999
