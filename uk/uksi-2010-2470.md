# The Reserve Forces Act 1996 (Isle of Man) Order 2010

Made: 13th October 2010

Coming into force: 1st November 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of October 2010Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, in exercise of the power conferred by section 132(3) of the Reserve Forces Act 1996[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Reserve Forces Act 1996 (Isle of Man) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 1st November 2010.

###### 2

The Reserve Forces Act 1996 shall extend to the Isle of Man, subject to the exceptions and modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — EXCEPTIONS AND MODIFICATIONS TO PROVISIONS OF THE RESERVE FORCES ACT 1996 AS EXTENDED TO THE ISLE OF MAN
