# The Right to Purchase (Prescribed Persons) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1994

Made: 4th August 1994

Laid before Parliament: 16th August 1994

Coming into force: 6th September 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 61(11)(w) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Right to Purchase (Prescribed Persons) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1994 and shall come into force on 6th September 1994.

#### Amendment of 1993 Order

###### 2

At the end of the list of prescribed persons in article 2 of the Right to Purchase (Prescribed Persons) (Scotland) Order 1993[^f00002], there shall be added the name “Weslo Housing Management Limited”.

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 4th August 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 qualification for the right to purchase and entitlement to discount depend *inter alia* on occupation as a tenant of a house or of a succession of houses provided by the public sector landlords mentioned in section 61(11) of that Act. Paragraph (w) of section 61(11) states that in addition to the persons specifically mentioned in section 61(11) the Secretary of State may prescribe other persons by Order. This Order adds another person to the list of prescribed persons in the Right to Purchase (Prescribed Persons) (Scotland) Order 1993.
