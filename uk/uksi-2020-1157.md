# The Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Worcestershire Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) (Amendment) Order 2020

Made: 22nd October 2020

Coming into force: 1st November 2020

In accordance with section 25(3) of that Act, the Secretary of State has completed the consultation prescribed by regulations[^f00002] made under that section.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Worcestershire Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) (Amendment) Order 2020 and comes into force on 1st November 2020.

- (2) In this Order, “the Establishment Order” means the Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Worcestershire Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011[^f00003].

#### Change of name and savings

###### 2

- (1) In article 2 of the Establishment Order (Establishment and name of the trust) for “the Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust” substitute “the Herefordshire and Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust”.

- (2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) does not —

- (a) affect any rights or obligations of any person; or

- (b) invalidate any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust and all instruments or other documents which refer to that name must be construed as referring to the Herefordshire and Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust.

#### Removal of specification of “operational date”

###### 3

For article 5 of the Establishment Order (Operational date and accounting date of the trust) and the heading to that article, substitute—

> > (5) The accounting date of the trust is 31st March.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care.

Edward Argar — Minister of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — 22nd October 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Worcestershire Mental Health Partnership National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011, which established the Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 changes the name of the trust to the Herefordshire and Worcestershire Health and Care National Health Service Trust.

Article 3 omits the specification of the trust’s “operational date” as it is of no on-going relevance.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
