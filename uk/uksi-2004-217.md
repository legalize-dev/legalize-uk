# The European Parliamentary Elections (Appointed Day of Poll) Order 2004

Made: 29th January 2004

Laid before Parliament: 3rd February 2004

Coming into force: 9th February 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4 of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Appointed Day of Poll) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 9th February 2004.

###### 2

The poll at the next general election of Members of the European Parliament shall be held on 10th June 2004.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Christopher Leslie — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Constitutional Affairs — 29th January 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 10th June 2004 as the day of the poll for the next general election of MEPs.
