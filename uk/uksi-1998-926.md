# The Law Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

Made: 26th March 1998

Coming into force: 1st April 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12A(1) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(b) of Schedule 7A to, the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Law Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

- (2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Law Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00002].

#### Amendment of article 3 of the principal order

###### 2

For article 3(2)(a) of the principal Order there shall be substituted the following:–

> > (a) to own and manage under section 12A(1)(a) of the Act the hospital establishments or facilities known as Law Hospital, Carluke, Strathclyde Hospital Out-Patient Department, Motherwell and Bellshill Maternity Hospital, Bellshill and establishments associated with those hospitals, including management of the facilities provided at and from those establishments, and also including the management of any teaching and research facilities associated with these establishments and the support services related thereto;

## Signed

Sam Galbraith — Minister for Health Scottish Office — 26th March 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order substitutes a new article 3(2)(a) in the Law Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 to permit the trust to own and manage Bellshill Maternity Hospital, Belshill.
