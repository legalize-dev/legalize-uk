# The Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1993

Made: 4th March 1993

Coming into force: 5th March 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 216(1) of the Education ReformAct 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 5th March 1993.

###### 2

The bodies which are specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as bodies which appear to the Secretary of State to be recognised bodies.

###### 3

The Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1988[^f00002] and the Education (Recognised Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1991[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order lists all those bodies which appear to the Secretary of State to be recognised bodies within section 214(2)(a) or (b) of the Education Reform Act 1988. An award granted by such a body is not an award of a kind referred to in section 214(1), which makes it an offence to grant, offer to grant, or issue any invitation relating to certain unrecognised degrees and awards.

This Order updates and replaces the list of bodies contained in the Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1988 as amended by the Education (Recognised Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1991, both of which are revoked. It includes a number of institutions which have been authorised to award degrees under section 76 of the Further and Higher Education Act [1992 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/13), and section 48 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act [1992 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/37).
