# The Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made: 6th July 2004

Laid before Parliament: 6th July 2004

Coming into force: 7th July2004

Whereas the Treasury are a government department designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to measures relating to (i) the interruption or reduction, in part or completely, of economic relations with one or more countries which are not Member States, and (ii) the movement of capital and to payments, between Member States and between Member States and countries which are not Member States;

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 7th July 2004.

###### 2

At the end of the definition of “*the EC Regulation*” in regulation 1(2) of the Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) Regulations 2004 , insert—

> > - as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1149/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1149) of 22 June 2004 amending Council Regulation [(EC) No 872/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0872) concerning further restrictive measures in relation to Liberia .

## Signed

John Heppell — Joan Ryan — Two Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 6th July 2004
