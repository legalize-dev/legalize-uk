# The Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made: 6th July 2004

Laid before Parliament: 6th July 2004

Coming into force: 7th July2004

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 7th July 2004.

###### 2

At the end of the definition of “the EC Regulation” in regulation 1(2) of the Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) Regulations 2004[^f00003], insert—

> > - as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1149/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1149) of 22 June 2004 amending Council Regulation [(EC) No 872/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0872) concerning further restrictive measures in relation to Liberia[^f00004].

## Signed

John Heppell — Joan Ryan — Two Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 6th July 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Council Regulation [(EC) No 872/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0872) of 29 April 2004 (“the Council Regulation”) imposes a freeze on the funds and economic resources of former Liberian President Charles Taylor and certain other persons associated with him as designated by the Committee established by paragraph 21 of UN Security Council Resolution 1521 (2003) (“the UN Sanctions Committee”) and listed in Annex I to that Regulation. On 14 June 2004 the UN Sanctions Committee decided to add a number of additional associates of former Liberian President Charles Taylor to the list of designated targets. Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1149/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1149) of 22 June 2004 accordingly amends the Council Regulation by replacing Annex I with an extended list of persons subject to restrictive measures.

The Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) Regulations 2004 provide that breaches of certain provisions of the Council Regulation, and certain other acts and omissions, are criminal offences. The present Regulations amend those Regulations so that they refer to the Council Regulation as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1149/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1149).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
