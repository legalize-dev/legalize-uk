# The Hill Farming Improvements Order 1949

Made: 21st March 1949

Laid before Parliament: 25th March 1949

Coming into Operation: 1st April 1949

In pursuance of subsection (4) of section 1 of the Hill Farming Act, 1946, the Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries and the Secretary of State for Scotland hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

In paragraph 2 of the First Schedule to the said Act before the word “Alteration” there shall be inserted the word “Erection,”, and the kinds of operations specified in the said Schedule shall include the erection of farm houses accordingly.

###### 2

This Order may be cited as the Hill Farming Improvements Order, 1949, and shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1949.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries is hereunto affixed this twenty-first day of March, nineteen hundred and forty-nine.

T. Williams — Minister

Given under the Seal of the Secretary of State for Scotland this twenty-first day of March, nineteen hundred and forty-nine.

Arthur Woodburn — Secretary of State

We approve

Wm. Hannan — Joseph Henderson — Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty's Treasury

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Hill Farming Act, 1946, provides for the making of grants for the improvement of hill farming land. The improvements for which grants may be made are set out in the First Schedule to the Act. This Order adds to the improvements the erection of new farm houses.
