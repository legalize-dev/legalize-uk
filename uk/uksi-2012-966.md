# The Postal Services Act 2011 (Specified Day) Order 2012

Made: 27th March 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 25 of the Postal Services Act 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Postal Services Act 2011 (Specified Day) Order 2012.

###### 2

The day specified for the provisions listed in article 3 to come into force is 1st April 2012.

###### 3

The provisions are—

- (a) articles 2 to 12 of the Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012[^f00002]; and

- (b) articles 1(3) and 2 to 9 of the Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Assets) Order 2012[^f00003].

## Signed

Norman Lamb — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Consumer and Postal Affairs — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 27th March 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 25 of the Postal Services Act [2011 (c.5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/5) provides that the Secretary of State may specify by Order a day on which other Orders made under Part 2 of that Act will come into force.

Two Orders, the Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Accrued Pension Rights) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/687](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/687)) and the Postal Services Act 2011 (Transfer of Assets) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/688](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/688)) were laid before Parliament on 9th March 2012. This Order provides that the operative provisions of those Orders will come into force on 1st April 2012.
