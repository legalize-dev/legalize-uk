# The A4 Trunk Road (Hillingdon and Hounslow) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1999

Made: 18th August 1999

Coming into force: 27th August 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A4 Trunk Road (Hillingdon and Hounslow) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1999, and shall come into force on 27th August 1999.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A4 Trunk Road (Hillingdon and Hounslow) Red Route Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In schedule 2B, items 3 and 4 are deleted and replaced with the following items:

| 1. The A4 Bath Road, the south side, between a point opposite the party wall of 750 and 752 Bath Road and a part 1.5 metres east of the party wall of 768 and 770 Bath Road. | 10 am-4pm Monday-Saturday inclusive | 20 minutes | 40 minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2. The A4 Bath Road, the south side, between a point opposite the common boundary of 626 and 628 Bath Road and a point 12.1 metres west thereof. |  |  |  |
| 3. The A4 Bath Road, the south side between a point 18.7 metres west of the common boundary of 626 and 628 Bath Road and a point 17.8 metres west thereof. |  |  |  |

###### 4

In schedule 3B, item 1 and 2 are deleted and replaced with the words “No items”.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 18th August 1999
