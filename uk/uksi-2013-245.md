# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2013

Made: 12th February 2013

Coming into force: 13th February 2013

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2013Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2013 and comes into force on 13th February 2013.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — *Persons appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 13th February 2013*

- Shirley Margaret Bailey

- David Gordon Brown

- Nicholas Andrew Hudson

- Jansy Patricia Kelly

- Brenda Mary McLaughlin

- John Martin Mitchell

- Sally Amanda Rowe

- Dominic Peter Stevens

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
