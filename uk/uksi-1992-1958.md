# The Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 (Amendment) Order 1992

Made: 10th August 1992

Laid before Parliament: 10th August 1992

Coming into force: 11th August 1992

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 28(3) of the said Act of 1991, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 11th August 1992.

###### 2

The following organisation, that is to say—

- “The Ulster Defence Association.”,

- is hereby added to Schedule 2 to the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 (which specifies organisations which are proscribed organisations for the purposes of that Act).

## Signed

P. B. B. Mayhew — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 10th August 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the Ulster Defence Association to the list of proscribed organisations contained in Schedule 2 to the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991.
