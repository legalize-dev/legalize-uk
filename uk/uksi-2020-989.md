# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Young Carer Grants, Short-Term Assistance and Winter Heating Assistance) (Consequential Provision and Modifications) Order 2020

Made: 9th September 2020

Laid before Parliament: 16th September 2020

Coming into force: 9th November 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 104 and 113(5) of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00001].

## PART 1 — Introductory

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Young Carer Grants, Short-Term Assistance and Winter Heating Assistance) (Consequential Provision and Modifications) Order 2020.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 9th November 2020.

- (3) Each amendment made by this Order has the same extent as the provision being amended.

## PART 2 — Young carer grants

### Young carer grants: capital and income disregards

#### Amendment of the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987

###### 2

- (1) The Income Support (General) Regulations 1987[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 10 (capital to be disregarded), after paragraph 76 insert—

> > (77) Any assistance given in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996

###### 3

- (1) The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 8 (capital to be disregarded), after paragraph 69 insert—

> > (70) Any assistance given in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

#### Amendment of the State Pension Credit Regulations 2002

###### 4

- (1) The State Pension Credit Regulations 2002[^f00005] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 15(1) (social security benefits that are not prescribed as income for the purposes of section 15(1)(e) of the State Pension Credit Act 2002), after sub-paragraph (rd) insert—

> > (re) any assistance given in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019;

- (3) In Part 1 of Schedule 5 (capital disregarded for the purpose of calculating income), in paragraph 20(2), after paragraph (u) insert—

> > (v) any assistance given in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

#### Amendment of the Housing Benefit Regulations 2006

###### 5

- (1) The Housing Benefit Regulations 2006[^f00006] are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 6 (capital to be disregarded), after paragraph 66 insert—

> > (67) Any assistance given in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

#### Amendment of the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006

###### 6

- (1) The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006[^f00007] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 29 (meaning of “income”), after paragraph (1)(j)(xviie) insert—

> > (xviif) any assistance given in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019;

- (3) In Part 1 of Schedule 6 (capital to be disregarded generally)—

- (a) in paragraph 21(2), after paragraph (t) insert—

> > (u) any assistance given in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

- (b) after paragraph 26I insert—

> > (26J) Any sum paid by means of assistance in accordance with the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends social security legislation in Great Britain as a consequence of sections 24 (duty to give assistance), 28 (carer’s assistance), 30 (winter heating assistance), and 36 (short-term assistance) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act [2018 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2018/9) (“the 2018 Act”), and regulations made under section 28 of the 2018 Act. The regulations in relation to which consequential provision is made in this Order are the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019 ([S.S.I. 2019/324](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2019/324)), made under section 28 of the 2018 Act.

Article 1(2) provides that this Order comes into force on 9th November 2020.

Parts 2, 3, and 4 provide, respectively, for young carer grants, short-term assistance and winter heating assistance to be disregarded as capital or income in Great Britain when determining a claimant’s entitlement to benefits under the Income Support (General) Regulations 1987 ([S.I. 1987/1967](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1967)), the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/207](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/207)), the State Pension Credit Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/1792](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1792)), the Housing Benefit Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/213](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/213)), the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/214](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/214)), the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/794](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/794)) and (in Parts 2 and 4 only) the Universal Credit Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/376](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/376)).
