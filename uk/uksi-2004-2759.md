# The Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2004

Made: 25th October 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 120(1) of the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2004.

- (2) In this Order, “the 2003 Act” means the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

- (1) The following provisions of the 2003 Act shall come into force on 31st October 2004—

- (a) section 116 (Schedules 1 and 4: sequestration, &c. in Scotland), in so far as it did not come into force on 5th July 2004; and

- (b) sub-section (5) of section 34 (compulsory police services agreement).

- (2) Section 110 (seat belts: delivery drivers) shall come into force on 1st March 2005.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David Jamieson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 25th October 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force:

- on 31st October 2004, the provisions of sections 34 and 116 of the Railways and Transport Safety Act [2003 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/20) not already in force; and

- on 1st March 2005, section 110 of that Act..
