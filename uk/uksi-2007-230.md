# The Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No.3) Order 2007

Made: 31st January 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 77(2) of the Electoral Administration Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No.3) Order 2007.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Electoral Administration Act 2006.

###### 2

- (1) The provisions of the Act specified in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 7th February 2007 in so far as they extend to Northern Ireland.

- (2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 17 (minimum age);

- (b) paragraph 103 of Schedule 1 (amendments); and

- (c) in Schedule 2 (repeals) the entries relating to—

- (i) the Parliamentary Elections Act 1695[^f00002];

- (ii) the Election Act 1707[^f00003];

- (iii) the Parliamentary Elections (Ireland) Act 1823[^f00004]; and

- (iv) the Family Law Reform Act 1969[^f00005].

###### 3

Section 52(4) to (6) of the Act shall come into force on 7th February 2007 for the purposes of local government elections within the meaning of section 22(5)(f) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00006].

## Signed

David Hanson — Minister of State — 31st January 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the provisions specified in article 2, in relation to Northern Ireland, and article 3 on 7th February 2007.
