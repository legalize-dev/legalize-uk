# The Northumberland Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made: 14th November 1996

Laid before Parliament: 21st November 1996

Coming into force: 12th December 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the changes in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the former Northumberland Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the National Health Service trusts specified in the Schedule to this Order, having consulted that Health Authority and those trusts in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Northumberland Health Authority (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 12th December 1996.

- (2) In this Order —

- “the Authority” means Northumberland Health Authority;

- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order;

- “the trust property” means, in the case of each body specified in column (1) of the Schedule, the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared, agreed upon and signed on behalf of the Authority and that body on the date specified in relation to that body in column (2) of the Schedule.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 12th December 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to each of the bodies specified in column (1) of the Schedule.

| (1) | (2) |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient Body | Date of Agreement |
| Cheviot and Wansbeck National Health Service Trust | 16th September 1996 |
| Northgate and Prudhoe National Health Service Trust | 30th September 1996 |
| Northumberland Community Health National Health Service Trust | 24th September 1996 |
| Northumberland Mental Health National Health Service Trust | 19th September 1996 |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Health — 14th November 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers on 12th December 1996 to the National Health Service trusts specified in the Schedule to the Order trust property held by the Northumberland Health Authority (article 2).
