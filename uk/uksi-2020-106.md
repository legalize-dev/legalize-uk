# The Utilities Act 2000 (Amendment of Section 105) Order 2020

Made: 29th January 2020

Laid before Parliament: 4th February 2020

Coming into force: 10th March 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 105(7) of the Utilities Act 2000[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Utilities Act 2000 (Amendment of Section 105) Order 2020 and comes into force on 10th March 2020.

#### Amendment of the Utilities Act 2000

###### 2

- (1) In section 105(3)[^f00002] of the Utilities Act 2000 (general restrictions on disclosure of information), after paragraph (b) insert—

> > (ba) it is a protected disclosure for the purposes of the Employment Rights Act 1996[^f00003] (and for the purposes of ascertaining whether it is a protected disclosure, subsection (9) is to be disregarded);

- (2) In section 105(4)(g)[^f00004] of the Utilities Act 2000, for the words from “under” to the end substitute—

> > —
> (i) under or by virtue of the 1986 Act, the 1989 Act, this Act or any Act or instrument specified in subsection (6), or
> (ii) in reliance on section 47B or 103A of the Employment Rights Act 1996;

## Signed

Kwasi Kwarteng — Minister of State for Business, Energy and Clean Growth — Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy — 29th January 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 105 of the Utilities Act 2000 imposes a general restriction on the disclosure of certain information, subject to a number of specified exemptions. This Order amends that provision to allow for a disclosure which is:

1. a protected disclosure for the purposes of the Employment Rights Act 1996; or

2. for the purposes of civil proceedings brought in reliance on section 47B (protected disclosure – protection from detriment) or 103A (protected disclosure – protection from unfair dismissal) of the Employment Rights Act 1996.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
