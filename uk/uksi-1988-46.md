# The Thames Water Authority (Repeal of Local Enactments) Order 1988

Made: 14th January 1988

Laid before Parliament: 27th January 1988

Coming into force: 17th February 1988

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 1(2)(c)[^f00002], 133(1) and 135(1)[^f00003] of the Water Resources Act 1963 and now vested in him [^f00004], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Thames Water Authority (Repeal of Local Enactments) Order 1988 and shall come into force on the 17th February 1988.

- (2) This Order and the Thames Water Authority Acts and Orders 1974 to 1987 may be cited together as the Thames Water Authority Acts and Orders 1974 to 1988.

#### Repeal of local enactments

###### 2

The enactments specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order (which enable the Thames Water Authority (as the successor to the Metropolitan Water Board[^f00005]) to abstract defined quantities of water from the River Thames between Windsor and Teddington Weir) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in column (2) thereof.

## SCHEDULE

| *Enactment* | *Extent of repeal* |
| --- | --- |
| The Thames Conservancy Act 1932[^f00006]. | In section 162, the words “in any one day any quantity of”, the words “not exceeding twelve hundred million gallons” and paragraphs (2) (6) and (7) of the proviso. |
| The Metropolitan Water Board Act 1946[^f00007]. | In section 80, subsection (1). |
| The Metropolitan Water Board (Emergency Abstraction from River Thames) Water Order 1949[^f00008]. | The whole order. |
| The Metropolitan Water Board (Thames Abstraction) Order 1949[^f00009]. | The whole order. |
| The Metropolitan Water Board (Thames Abstraction) Order 1962[^f00010]. | The whole order. |

## Signed

Nicholas Ridley — Secretary of State for the Environment — 14th January 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order repeals local enactments enabling the Thames Water Authority to abstract limited quantities of water from the River Thames.

The Authority’s right to abstract water from the River Thames between Windsor and Teddington Weir is governed by a licence granted under sections 33 and 34 of the Water Resources Act 1963. The Secretary of State has varied that licence so as to provide for abstraction of amounts of water which differ from those permitted by the local enactments; and this Order accordingly repeals those provisions which are inconsistent with the provisions of the current licence.
