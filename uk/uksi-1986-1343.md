# The Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1986

Made: 30th July 1986

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 6(5) of the Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1986.

###### 2

The Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 shall come into force on 1st November 1986.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 30th July 1986

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings the Marriage (Prohibited Degrees of Relationship) Act 1986 into force on 1st November 1986.
