# The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2002

Made: 6th December 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 138(2) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2002.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 shall come into force on 1st January 2003—

- (a) section 78(1), (3), (4), (5), (6), (8) and (9) (taking fingerprints);

- (b) section 80(3), (5) and (6) (samples);

- (c) section 137 (repeals) in so far as it relates to the entry Criminal Justice Act 1948[^f00002] in Schedule 7, Part 2; and

- (d) the entry Criminal Justice Act 1948 in Schedule 7, Part 2.

## Signed

John Denham — Minister of State — 6th December 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 2003 the provisions set out in article 2.
