# The Surrey and Borders Partnership National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2006

Made: 14th March 2006

Coming into force: 31st March 2006

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and now vested in her, with the consent of Treasury[^f00002]:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Surrey and Borders Partnership National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 31st March 2006.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to England.

#### Originating capital of an NHS trust

###### 2

The originating capital of the Surrey and Borders Partnership National Health Service Trust[^f00003] shall be £206,376,000.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Warner — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 14th March 2006

We consent

Gillian Merron — Dave Watts — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 14th March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order determines the amount of originating capital, provided for in section 9 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, of the Surrey and Borders Partnership NHS Trust established under that Act on 1st April 2005.
