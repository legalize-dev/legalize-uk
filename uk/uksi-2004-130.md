# Legal Deposit Libraries Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2004

Made: 14th January 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 16(1) of the Legal Deposit Libraries Act 2003[^f00001], and after consultation with Scottish Ministers and the National Assembly for Wales in accordance with section 16(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Legal Deposit Libraries Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2004.

###### 2

The Legal Deposit Libraries Act 2003 shall come into force on 1st February 2004.

## Signed

Andrew McIntosh — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 14th January 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Legal Deposit Libraries Act [2003 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/28) into force on 1st February 2004.
