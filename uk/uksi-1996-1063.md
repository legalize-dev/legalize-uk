# The Licensing (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 3rd April 1996

Laid before Parliament: 9th April 1996

Coming into force: 1st May 1996

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 29(1) and 198(2) of the Licensing Act 1964[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st May 1996.

###### 2

The Schedule to the Licensing (Fees) Order 1978[^f00002] shall be amended by inserting, after the entry relating to the grant of a special hours certificate, the following entries—

| Grant of provisional special hours certificate—ss. 77A and 78ZA | 10.00 |
| --- | --- |
| Declaring provisional grant of special hours certificate final—ss. 77A(3) and 78ZA(3) | 4.00 |

## Signed

Home Office

Timothy Kirkhope — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 3rd April 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Licensing (Fees) Order 1978 by prescribing the fees chargeable for the grant of a provisional special hours certificate (£10) and for declaring the provisional grant of such a certificate final (£4). Sections 77A and 78ZA of the Licensing Act 1964, which relate to provisional special hours certificates, were inserted by the Deregulation (Special Hours Certificates) Order 1996 (S.I. [1996/977](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/977)).
