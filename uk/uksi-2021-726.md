# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Investigations: Code of Practice) Order 2021

Made: 17th June 2021

Coming into force: 28th June 2021

In accordance with section 459(6)(a) of the Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Investigations: Code of Practice) Order 2021 and comes into force on 28th June 2021.

#### Revised code of practice

###### 2

The revised code of practice entitled “Code of Practice issued under section 377 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 Investigations” laid in draft before Parliament on 22nd March 2021 comes into operation on 28th June 2021.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Investigations: Code of Practice) Order 2018[^f00003] is revoked.

## Signed

Williams of Trafford — Minister of State — Home Office — 17th June 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 28th June 2021 a revised code of practice prepared by the Secretary of State under section 377 of the Proceeds of Crime Act [2002 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29) (“the 2002 Act”). Revisions to the code of practice are necessitated by the commencement in Northern Ireland of amendments made to Chapter 2 of Part 8 of the 2002 Act by the Criminal Finances Act [2017 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/22).

The revised code of practice relates to the exercise of functions under Chapter 2 of Part 8 of the 2002 Act. Those functions relate to the conduct by appropriate officers (defined in section 378 of the 2002 Act) of certain types of investigations concerning the recovery of the proceeds of criminal conduct. Those appropriate officers include:

- (a) the Director General of the National Crime Agency,

- (b) other National Crime Agency officers,

- (c) officers of Revenue and Customs,

- (d) immigration officers,

- (e) Financial Conduct Authority officers, and

- (f) in relation to England and Wales, accredited financial investigators and constables.

The code of practice brought into operation by this Order replaces the code of practice that was brought into operation by the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Investigations: Code of Practice) Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/84)). This Order revokes [S.I. 2018/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/84).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
