# The Finance Act 2007 Section 46 (Commencement) Order 2008

Made: 4th March 2008

The Treasury makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 46(9) of the Finance Act 2007[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2007 section 46 (Commencement) Order 2008.

#### Commencement of section 46 of the Finance Act 2007

###### 2

Section 46 (1) to (3) and (5) to (7) of the Finance Act 2007 comes into force on 6th April 2008.

## Signed

Alan Campbell — Dave Watts — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 4th March 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 6th April 2008 section 46(1) to (3) and (5) to (7) of the Finance Act 2007.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
