# The Courts Boards Areas Order 2004

Made: 21st April 2004

Coming into force: 1st June 2004

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4(2) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001], having regard to the desirability of specifying areas which are the same as the police areas listed in Schedule 1 to the Police Act 1996[^f00002] and the area consisting of the Metropolitan Police District and the City of London police area, hereby makes the following Order a draft of which has, in accordance with section 108(2) and (3) of the Courts Act 2003, been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Court Boards Areas Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st June 2004.

#### Courts Boards areas

###### 2

Each of the areas respectively named in column 1, and specified in column 2 of Table 1 of the Schedule to this Order, is a courts board area in England.

###### 3

Each of the areas respectively named in column 1, and specified in column 2 of Table 2 of the Schedule to this Order, is a courts board area in Wales.
