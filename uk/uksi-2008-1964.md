# The Export Control (Democratic Republic of Congo) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2008

Made: 22nd July 2008

Laid before Parliament: 23rd July 2008

Coming into force: 24th July 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3, 4, 5 and 7 of the Export Control Act 2002[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export Control (Democratic Republic of Congo) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 24th July 2008.

###### 2

The Export Control (Democratic Republic of Congo) Order 2005[^f00002] is amended as follows.

###### 3

In article 1(2), in the definition of “the Regulation” after the words “26 November 2007” insert “and Council Regulation [(EC) No 666/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0666) of 15 July 2008[^f00003]”.

###### 4

In Article 3, for the words “any person, entity or body in, or for use in, the Democratic Republic of Congo” substitute the words “any non-governmental person, entity or body operating in the territory of the Democratic Republic of Congo” where they appear in paragraph (a) and (b).

## Signed

Malcolm Wicks — Minister of State for Energy, — Department for Business, Enterprise and Regulatory Reform — 22nd July 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order implements in part Council Regulation [(EC) 666/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0666) which amends Council Regulation [(EC) 889/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0889)[^f00004].

Council Regulation [(EC) 666/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0666) was adopted pursuant to Council Common Position 2008/369/CFSP[^f00005] integrating the measures contained in UN Security Council Resolution 1807 (2008).

Council Regulation [(EC) 666/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0666) modifies the scope of the restrictive measures on the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) by limiting these restrictions to non-governmental persons or entities operating in the territory of the DRC.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of business.
