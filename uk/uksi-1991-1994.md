# The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1991

Made: 3rd September 1991

Laid before Parliament: 6th September 1991

Coming into force: 27th September 1991

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in the case of land in England, and the Secretary of State, in the case of land in Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 to the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 27th September 1991.

#### Amendment of principal Order

###### 2

The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) Order 1986([^f00002]) is hereby amended by substituting for the Schedule thereto the provisions contained in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies to land in England and Wales, further amends the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) Order 1986, by substituting for the Schedule to that Order a new Schedule, in which the quota per hectare prescribed by article 3 of that Order is revised (article 2). (The figures for the yield per hectare, prescribed by article 4 of the 1986 Order, remain unchanged). Quota per hectare is prescribed for the purposes of calculating the payment which a tenant in England and Wales is entitled, in certain circumstances, to obtain from his landlord in respect of milk quota on the termination of the tenancy of the land, by Schedule 1 to the Agriculture Act 1986.
