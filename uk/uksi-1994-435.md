# The Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made: 17th February 1994

Laid before Parliament: 1st March 1994

Coming into force: 22nd March 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 102(2) and (4), 138(1)[^f00001], 190(1) and 191(1) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00002] as extended by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

The Regulations may be cited as the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 22nd March 1994.

#### Amendments

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1991[^f00004] is amended by inserting the word “i” before the words “aelod o'ch teulu” in Note 51 (Nodyn 51) of Form 1 (Ffurflen 1) and Form 2 (Ffurflen 2).

## Signed

John Redwood — Secretary of State for Wales — 17th February 1994

## Explanatory note

(Nid yw'r nodyn hwn yn rhan o'r Rheoliadau)

Mae'r Rheoliadau hyn yn egluro amwysedd yn yr Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1991.

EXPLANATORY NOTE (This note is not part of the Regulations) These Regulations clarify an ambiguity in the Housing Renovation etc. Grants (Prescribed Forms and Particulars) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 1991.
