# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2001

Made: 22nd November 2001

Laid before Parliament: 28th November 2001

Coming into force: 21st December 2001

Now, therefore, the Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 18(1) and (4)(d)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation (Amendment)(No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 21st December 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation Order 2000[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 3

Part 3 of Schedule 4 to the principal Order shall be amended, in column 2, by the substitution—

- (a) in item 2, of “270” for the figure “290”; and

- (b) in item 7(a) of “290” for the figure “270”.

## Signed

Elliot Morley — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 21st November 2001

We consent,

Tony McNulty — John Heppell — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 22nd November 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note does not form part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Order 2000 (“the principal Order”) which designated areas in Breckland, Clun, the North Peak, the Suffolk River Valleys and the Test Valley as environmentally sensitive areas, by correcting errors in the payment rates specified in the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II)(Amendment) Order [2001/3195](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3195).

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of this Order.
