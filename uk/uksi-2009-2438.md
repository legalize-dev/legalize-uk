# The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009

Made: 7th September 2009

Laid before Parliament: 9th September 2009

Coming into force: 1st October 2009

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of making Regulations under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the environment.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.
