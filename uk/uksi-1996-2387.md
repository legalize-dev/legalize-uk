# The A1400 Trunk Road (Southend Road, Redbridge) (Prohibition of Right Turn and U-Turns) Order 1996

Made: 9th September 1996

Coming into force: 13th September 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) of, and Part IV of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002],, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A1400 Trunk Road (Southend Road, Redbridge) (Prohibition of Right Turn and U-Turns) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 13th September 1996.

###### 2

In this Order —

- “the un-named road”means the access road to a retail development off Southend Road opposite Lechmere Approach;

- “Southend Road”means that length of the A1400 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Redbridge known as Southend Road;

- “Lechmere Approach”means the road of that name in the London Borough of Redbridge; and

- “the junction”means the junction of Southend Road with Lechmere Approach and the un-named road.

###### 3

Save as provided in article 4 below, no person shall, at the junction, cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in —

- (a) Southend Road westbound carriageway to make a right turn into Lechmere Approach;

- (b) Southend Road eastbound carriageway to make a U-turn so as to proceed in the opposite direction; and

- (c) Southend Road westbound carriageway to make a U-turn so as to proceed in the opposite direction.

###### 4

Nothing in this Order shall apply —

- (a) to anything done at the direction or with the permission of a police constable in uniform;

- (b) to any vehicle being used for police, fire brigade or ambulance purposes.

## Signed
