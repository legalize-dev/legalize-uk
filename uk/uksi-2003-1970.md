# Education (School Performance Targets) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2003

Made: 30th July 2003

Laid before Parliament: 31st July 2003

Coming into force: 1st September 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 19 and 54(3) of the Education Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Performance Targets) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st September 2003.

###### 2

The Education (School Performance Targets) (England) Regulations 1998[^f00002] shall be amended as set out in regulations 3 to 5 of these Regulations.

###### 3

- (1) Amend regulation 4 (targets for pupils aged 15) as follows.

- (2) Omit paragraph (3)(b).

- (3) For paragraph (4) substitute:

> > - In paragraph (3)(a), the reference to pupils achieving particular grades in GCSE examinations shall be construed for the purposes of that provision so as to include a reference to pupils achieving corresponding awards in an equivalent number of vocational qualifications.

###### 4

- (1) Amend regulation 5 (prohibition of revision of performance targets) as follows.

- (2) Re-number the existing text as paragraph (1) and insert at the beginning of the sentence:

> > - Subject to paragraph (2),

- (3) After that paragraph insert the following new paragraph—

> > (2) The governing body of a school to which regulation 3 applies may amend the targets it has set pursuant to regulation 3 for the school year 2003-04 when it sets targets pursuant to regulation 3 for the school year 2004-05.

###### 5

- (1) Amend regulation 6 (publication of performance information) as follows.

- (2) After paragraph (2B) insert—

> > (2C) Paragraph (1) shall not require the governing body to publish information relating to any amendment made under regulation 5(2) to the performance targets set pursuant to regulation 3 for the school year 2003-04.

- (3) In paragraph (5) omit sub-paragraph (b)(ii).

- (4) For paragraph (6) substitute—

> > - In paragraph (5)(b)(i), the reference to pupils achieving particular grades in GCSE examinations shall be construed for the purposes of that provision so as to include a reference to pupils achieving corresponding awards in an equivalent number of vocational qualifications.

## Signed

Stephen Twigg — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 30th July 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Education (School Performance Targets) (England) Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/1532](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1532)). They remove the requirement upon schools to specify a target for the percentage of pupils who will attain 5 GCSEs at grade A* to G including English and Mathematics. They also permit schools to amend the target they have set for the school year 2003-4 in relation to performance at Levels 4 and 5 in English and Mathematics by pupils at Key Stage 2 when they set such targets for the school year 2004-5. The regulations further provide that details of revised targets need not be published.
