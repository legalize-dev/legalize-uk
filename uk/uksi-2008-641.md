# The Disability Discrimination (Public Authorities) (Statutory Duties) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 5th March 2008

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 2008

Coming into force: 6th April 2008

The Welsh Ministers have given their consent in accordance with section 49D(7) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Disability Discrimination (Public Authorities) (Statutory Duties) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 6th April 2008.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the 2005 Regulations” means the Disability Discrimination (Public Authorities) (Statutory Duties) Regulations 2005[^f00002].

#### Disability Equality Schemes

###### 2

Regulation 2 of the 2005 Regulations (preparation and publication of a Disability Equality Scheme) is amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (3)(d)(iii), for “Part I or V of Schedule 1” substitute “Part I, V or VI of Schedule 1”;

- (b) in paragraph (6), at the beginning insert “Subject to paragraphs (7) to (9),”;

- (c) after paragraph (6)(d) add—

> > (e) in the case of a public authority listed in Part VI of Schedule 1, 1st December 2008.

- (d) after paragraph (6) add the following—

> > (7) In the case of a public authority listed in Parts I or II of Schedule 1 which was established on or after 4th December 2006 and before 3rd December 2007, the relevant publication date is 1st December 2008.
> (8) In the case of a public authority listed in Parts I or II of Schedule 1 which is established on or after 3rd December 2007, the relevant publication date is the date falling twelve months after its establishment.
> (9) In the case of a public authority listed in Parts III to V of Schedule 1 which is established on or after the relevant publication date which would have applied to it had the public authority been established before that date, the relevant publication date is the date falling twelve months after the establishment of the public authority.

#### Amendments to the list of public authorities in the 2005 Regulations

###### 3

- (1) In Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the 2005 Regulations—

- (a) omit “The British Tourist Authority”, and at the appropriate place insert, “Visit Britain”;

- (b) omit “The Central Police Training and Development Authority (CENTREX)”, and at the appropriate place insert, “The National Policing Improvement Agency”;

- (c) omit “The Particle Physics and Astronomy Research Council” and “The Council for the Central Laboratory of the Research Councils”, and at the appropriate place insert, “The Science and Technology Facilities Council”;

- (d) omit the entry for the Adult Learning Inspectorate.

- (2) In Part V of Schedule 1 to the 2005 Regulations, in the entry in respect of the Children’s Commissioner for England, after “England” insert “and 11 MILLION”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Disability Discrimination (Public Authorities)(Statutory Duties) Regulations 2005 ([S.I.2005/2966](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2966)). Those Regulations impose duties on public authorities listed in Schedule 1 to prepare and publish a Disability Equality Scheme, to revise it at intervals, to implement certain components of the Scheme, and publish an annual report of progress.

The main purpose of the present Regulations is to extend the list of public authorities to which the duties imposed by the 2005 Regulations apply. The additional public authorities named in new Part VI of Schedule 1 to the 2005 Regulations are required to publish a Disability Equality Scheme on or before 1st December 2008.

These Regulations also impose a duty on any public authority listed in Parts I to V of Schedule 1 but which is created on or after the applicable relevant publication date for that Part (or in the case of those authorities listed in Parts I and II, after 3rd December 2007), to publish a Disability Equality Scheme within a year of the date of its creation. A relevant publication date of 1st December 2008 is specified where a public authority listed in Parts I and II of Schedule 1 was created between 4th December 2006 and 3rd December 2007.

A full impact assessment has not been published for this instrument as it has no impact on the private or voluntary sectors.
