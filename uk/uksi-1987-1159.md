# The Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1987

Made: 2nd July 1987

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 66 of the Education (No. 2) Act 1986[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1987.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the Act” means the Education (No. 2) Act 1986.

#### Coming into force of certain provisions of the Act

###### 3

The provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters mentioned in column 2 thereof) shall come into force on 1st September 1987.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS COMING INTO FORCE ON 1ST SEPTEMBER 1987

| *Provisions of the Act* | *Subject matter of provisions* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 67(6), so far as it relates to the repeals set out in the Appendix to this Schedule. | Repeals. |
| Schedule 6, so far as it relates to the repeals set out in the Appendix to this Schedule. | Repeals. |

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 2nd July 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

By virtue of transitional provisions contained in the Education (No. 2) Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/2203](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/2203)) the application of section 31(8) of the London Government Act 1963 (as amended by paragraph 6 of Schedule 5 to the Local Government Act 1966) (concerning recoupment in Greater London) is confined to further education.

Following the making of regulations (the Further Education (Recoupment) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1158](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1158))) applying the recoupment provisions of section 51(1) and (5) of the 1986 (No. 2) Act to prescribed categories of pupils receiving non-advanced further education, the present Order brings into force, on 1st September 1987, provisions repealing the above-mentioned provisions of the London Government Act 1963 and the Local Government Act 1966. When this Order takes effect the whole of the 1986 (No. 2) Act will have been brought into force.
