# The Welfare Reform Act 2007 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2011

Made: 10th February 2011

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 70(2) of the Welfare Reform Act 2007[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform Act 2007 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

11th February 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Welfare Reform Act 2007—

- (a) section 13(1) to (6) (work-related activity);

- (b) section 15 (directions about work-related activity).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Chris Grayling — Minister for Employment, — Department for Work and Pensions — 10th February 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force two provisions in the Welfare Reform Act 2007, namely—

- section 13(1) to (6) (which allow regulations to make provision for work-related activity), and

- section 15 (directions about work-related activity).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the private or voluntary sectors.
