# The Education (Recognised Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1991

Made: 28th February 1991

Coming into force: 1st March 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 216(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Recognised Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st March 1991.

###### 2

To the list of bodies in the Schedule to the Education (Recognised Bodies) Order 1988[^f00002] there shall be added (at the appropriate place in alphabetical order) the body named “Union Theological College”, being a body which appears to the Secretary of State to be a recognised body.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — Secretary of State for Education and Science — 28th February 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds Union Theological College to the list of bodies designated as appearing to the Secretary of State to be recognised bodies within section 214(2)(a) or (b) of the Education Reform Act 1988. An award granted by such a body is not an award of a kind referred to in section 214(1) of that ACt, which makes it an offence to grant, offer to grant, or issue any invitation relating to certain unrecognised degrees and awards.
