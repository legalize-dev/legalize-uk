# The Pension Protection Fund (Pension Compensation) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 24th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 26th November 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 73(6) and 174(2) of the Pensions Act 1995[^f00001], section 315(2), (4) and (5) of, and paragraph 28(3), (6) and (7) of Schedule 7 to, the Pensions Act 2004[^f00002] and section 144(2) and (4) of, and paragraph 17(6A) and (6B) of Schedule 5 to, the Pensions Act 2008[^f00003].

In accordance with section 120(1) of the Pensions Act 1995 and section 317(1) of the Pensions Act 2004, the Secretary of State has consulted such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pension Protection Fund (Pension Compensation) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) This regulation and regulation 3 come into force on 31st December 2026.

- (3) Regulation 2 and regulation 5 come into force on 31st December 2027.

- (4) Regulation 4 comes into force on—

- (a) 31st December 2026, except in relation to a person entitled to compensation on the discharge of a pension compensation credit in accordance with Schedule 5 to the Pensions Act 2008[^f00004] where paragraph (5) applies in relation to the transferor[^f00005];

- (b) 31st December 2027, for all purposes.

- (5) This paragraph applies where, in relation to the transferor—

- (a) the pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension[^f00006] that accrued during the GMP indexation period,

- (b) that accrual was in relation to GMP indexed service in respect of which the compensation is payable, and

- (c) the admissible rules of the pension scheme—

- (i) did not include a requirement for all or any part of so much of the annual rate of a pension in payment under the scheme as is attributable to the transferor’s pre-1997 service to be increased annually,

- (ii) included such a requirement but it applied only in relation to a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the scheme or for the purposes of complying with a GMP equalisation obligation, or

- (iii) included such a requirement but it did not apply in relation to the pre-1997 service in respect of which the compensation is payable.

- (6) In any case where it is unclear whether the pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period, paragraph (5) has effect as if the scheme so provided.

- (7) In any case where it is unclear whether the accrual of a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the pension scheme (including by virtue of paragraph (6)) was in relation to particular GMP indexed service, paragraph (5) has effect as if the accrual was in relation to such service.

- (8) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the assessment date, the admissible rules of the pension scheme included a requirement of the kind mentioned in paragraph (5)(c)(i), paragraph (5) has effect as if the scheme included such a requirement.

- (9) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the assessment date, a requirement of the pension scheme of a kind mentioned in paragraph (5)(c)(i) (including such a requirement included by virtue of paragraph (8)) applied in relation to particular pre-1997 service, paragraph (5) has effect as if the requirement applied in relation to such service.

- (10) In this regulation, “*pension scheme*” means an occupational pension scheme within the meaning given in section 1 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00007] in relation to which the Board of the Pension Protection Fund has assumed responsibility giving rise to an entitlement to compensation.

- (11) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

#### Amendment of the Pension Protection Fund (Compensation) Regulations 2005

###### 2

In regulation 24 of the Pension Protection Fund (Compensation) Regulations 2005 (annual increase in periodic compensation)[^f00008]—

- (a) in the heading for “post-1997 and pre-1997 service” substitute “post-1997 service, pre-1997 service and GMP indexed service”;

- (b) in paragraph (1), for “and “pre-1997 service”” substitute “, “pre-1997 service” and “GMP indexed service””;

- (c) after paragraph (3) insert—

> > (3A) Pensionable service within paragraph 36(4)(b) of that Schedule is “GMP indexed service” where it is treated for the purposes of the scheme as having occurred in the period beginning with 6th April 1988 and ending with 5th April 1997.

- (d) after paragraph (5) insert—

> > (5A) In relation to any relevant pension credit amount, “*GMP indexed service*” means pension credit rights deriving from rights attributable to—
>   (a) actual service of the transferor in any description of employment to which the scheme applies which—
>     (i) qualifies the transferor for a guaranteed minimum pension under the scheme, and
>     (ii) occurred in the period beginning with 6th April 1988 and ending with 5th April 1997;
>   (b) any notional service allowed in respect of the transferor under the admissible rules which—
>     (i) qualifies the transferor for a guaranteed minimum pension under the scheme, and
>     (ii) relates to service treated for the purposes of the scheme as having occurred in the period beginning with 6th April 1988 and ending with 5th April 1997.

- (e) in paragraph (6)—

- (i) in sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) for “6th April 1997, or, on or after that date” substitute “6th April 1997, in the period beginning with 6th April 1988 and ending with 5th April 1997, or on or after 6th April 1997”;

- (ii) in the closing words for “6th April 1997 and on or after that date” substitute “6th April 1997, in the period beginning with 6th April 1988 and ending with 5th April 1997 and on or after 6th April 1997”.

#### Amendment of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Winding up etc.) Regulations 2005

###### 3

In regulation 4(2)(a) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Winding up etc.) Regulations 2005 (corresponding PPF liability: modifications of the pension compensation provisions etc.)[^f00009], after “25,” insert “25A,”.

#### Amendment of the Pension Protection Fund (Pension Compensation Sharing and Attachment on Divorce etc) Regulations 2011

###### 4

For regulation 32 of the Pension Protection Fund (Pension Compensation Sharing and Attachment on Divorce etc) Regulations 2011 (determination of indexed proportion)[^f00010] substitute—

> > (32)
>   (1) For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of “the general underlying rate” in paragraph 17(4) of Schedule 5 to the Act (annual increase in pension compensation payable on discharge of pension compensation credit), where paragraph (2) applies, “the general indexed proportion” is so much of the pension compensation credit[^f00011] or part of it which is derived from rights relating to pensionable service[^f00012] (whether actual or notional) occurring at any time.
>   (2) This paragraph applies where immediately before the assessment date—
>     (a) the admissible rules of the scheme in respect of which the rights relating to pensionable service from which the pension compensation credit was derived were accrued included a requirement for all or any part of so much of the annual rate of a pension in payment under the scheme as is attributable to a person’s pre-1997 service to be increased annually,
>     (b) that requirement did not apply only in relation to a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the scheme or for the purposes of complying with a GMP equalisation obligation, and
>     (c) that requirement applied in relation to pre-1997 service in respect of which the compensation from which the pension compensation credit was derived is payable.
>   (3) For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of “the general underlying rate” in paragraph 17(4) of Schedule 5 to the Act, where paragraph (4) applies, “the general indexed proportion” is the aggregate of—
>     (a) a proportion, being the same proportion as prescribed for the purposes of the definition of “the notional pre-1997 indexed proportion” in regulations under paragraph 17(6B) of Schedule 5 to the Act, of the pension compensation credit or part of it which is derived from rights relating to pensionable service (whether actual or notional) occurring in the period before 6th April 1997, and
>     (b) so much of the pension compensation credit or part of it which is derived from rights relating to pensionable service (whether actual or notional) occurring on and after 6th April 1997.
>   (4) This paragraph applies where immediately before the assessment date—
>     (a) the scheme in respect of which the rights relating to pensionable service from which the pension compensation credit was derived were accrued provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period,
>     (b) that accrual was in relation to GMP indexed service in respect of which the compensation from which the pension compensation credit was derived is payable, and
>     (c) paragraph (2) does not apply.
>   (5) For the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of “the general underlying rate” in paragraph 17(4) of Schedule 5 to the Act, where paragraphs (2) and (4) do not apply, “the general indexed proportion” is so much of the pension compensation credit or part of it which is derived from rights relating to pensionable service (whether actual or notional) occurring on and after 6th April 1997.
>   (6) In any case where it is unclear whether the pension compensation credit or any part of it is derived from rights relating to pensionable service (whether actual or notional) occurring—
>     (a) before 6th April 1997,
>     (b) in the period beginning with 6th April 1988 and ending with 5th April 1997, or
>     (c) on or after 6th April 1997,
>   the Board may determine as best as it is able, having regard to the admissible rules of the scheme and all the circumstances of the case, how much of the service or notional service from which the pension compensation credit was derived should be treated for the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of “the general underlying rate” in paragraph 17(4) of Schedule 5 to the Act as having occurred before 6th April 1997, how much should be treated as having occurred during the period beginning with 6th April 1988 and ending with 5th April 1997 and how much should be treated as having occurred on or after 6th April 1997.

#### Prescribed percentage for the purposes of the definition of “notional pre-1997 underlying rate”

###### 5

- (1) Paragraph (2) sets out the—

- (a) prescribed percentage for the purposes of both definitions of “notional pre-1997 underlying rate” in paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 7 to the Pensions Act 2004[^f00013], and

- (b) prescribed proportion for the purposes of the definition of “notional pre-1997 underlying rate” in paragraph 17(6B) of Schedule 5 to the Pensions Act 2008[^f00014].

- (2) The prescribed percentage and prescribed proportion are—

- (a) 20 per cent on 1st January 2028, and

- (b) 10 per cent on each indexation date[^f00015] thereafter.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

Regulations 2 and 4 of these Regulations make amendments consequential on section 109 of the Pension Schemes Act [2026 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/22), which amends the Pensions Act [2004 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/35) and the Pensions Act [2008 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/30) to enable periodic compensation payments by the Pension Protection Fund (“*PPF*”) based on pre-1997 service to be increased. Regulation 3 of these Regulations also makes a separate minor and technical amendment. Regulation 5 does not amend existing legislation but is a provision which will enable the PPF to calculate annual increases on compensation where the pre-1997 service in respect of which compensation is payable relates to a guaranteed minimum pension only.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 24 of the Pension Protection Fund (Compensation) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/670](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/670)). Regulation 24 clarifies the terms “post-1997 service” and “pre-1997 service” as used in paragraph 28(6) and (7) of Schedule 7 to the Pensions Act 2004. It also establishes how pensionable service and pension credit rights are to be categorised according to whether they relate to service occurring before or on or after 6th April 1997. Lastly, the regulation confers discretionary power upon the Board of the PPF to determine the allocation of service between pre-1997 and post-1997 periods in cases where it is unclear from the scheme rules how pensionable service or pension credit rights should be categorised. Section 109(2)(d) of the Pension Schemes Act 2026 inserts a definition of “GMP indexed service” into paragraph 28(6) of Schedule 7 to the Pensions Act 2004 and regulation 24 is amended to provide corresponding provision in relation to the definition of GMP indexed service.

Regulation 3 amends regulation 4 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Winding up etc.) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/706](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/706)) so that, when valuing the “corresponding PPF liability” under section 73 of the Pensions Act 1995 (preferential liabilities on winding up) (c. 26) for pension schemes winding up outside the PPF, late retirement factors are omitted.

Regulation 4 substitutes regulation 32 of the Pension Protection Fund (Pension Compensation Sharing and Attachment on Divorce etc) Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/731](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/731)). The substituted regulation 32 defines the “general indexed proportion”, which applies for the purposes of paragraph (a) of the definition of “the general underlying rate” in paragraph 17(4) of Schedule 5 to the Pensions Act 2008. Regulation 32 also includes provision to enable the Board of the PPF to determine the period in which pensionable service took place in cases where it is unclear.

Regulation 5 sets out the percentage of pre-1997 service which is to be subject to indexation in accordance with the amendments made to paragraph 28 of Schedule 7 to the Pensions Act 2004 and paragraph 17 of Schedule 5 to the Pensions Act 2008 by the Pension Schemes Act 2026. This applies where a scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension and the admissible rules of the scheme did not include a requirement for the annual rate of a pension, so far as attributable to pre-1997 service, to be increased annually.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
