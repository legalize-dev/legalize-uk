# The Newfield School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006

Made: 25th November 2006

Laid before Parliament: 4th December 2006

Coming into force: 27th December 2006

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Newfield School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006 and comes into force on 27 December 2006.

#### Exemption from the School Session Regulations

###### 2

The Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002] are not to apply to the governing body of Newfield School[^f00003] of Lees Hall Road, Sheffield S8 9JP.

#### Time period

###### 3

This Order has effect until 31st January 2007.

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 25th November 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 do not apply to the governing body of Newfield School. Article 3 specifies that the Order shall have effect until 31st January 2007.
