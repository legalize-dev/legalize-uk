# The Finance Act 1996, section 8, (Appointed Day) Order 1996

Made: 4th October 1996

The Commissioners of Customs and Excise, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 8(2) of the Finance Act 1996[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1996, section 8, (Appointed Day) Order 1996.

###### 2

The day appointed as the day on which section 8 of the Finance Act 1996 comes into force is 1st November 1996.

## Signed

D.J. Howard — Commissioner of Customs and Excise — 4th October 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st November 1996 as the day on which section 8 of the Finance Act [1996 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/8) comes into force. Section 8 of the Finance Act 1996 repeals section 18 and parts of section 19 of the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act [1979 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/5) which provide for relief from excise duty on heavy hydrocarbon oil used as fuel for ships in home waters (excluding pleasure yachts) and oil used on certain fishing boats.
