# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2010

Made: 1st March 2010

Coming into force: 31st March 2010

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 5 to that Act.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 31st March 2010.

#### Originating capital of NHS trusts

###### 2

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

| *Column 1* *NHS Trust* | *Column 2* *Originating Capital* *£* |
| --- | --- |
| South London Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00002] | 290,854,000 |
| Western Sussex Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00003] | 237,383,000 |

Mike O’Brien — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 25th February 2010

We consent,

Dave Watts — Tony Cunningham — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 1st March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, provided for in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of National Health Service trusts established under that Act on 1st April 2009.
