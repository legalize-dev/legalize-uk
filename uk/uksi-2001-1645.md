# The Medicines (Veterinary Drugs) (General Sale List) Order 2001

Made: 30th April 2001

Coming into force: 27th May 2001

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State concerned with health in England, the Minister of Health, Social Services and Public Safety and the Minister of Agriculture and Rural Development, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51 and 129(4) of the Medicines Act 1968[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consulting such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by this Order[^f00003], and after consulting and taking into account the advice of the Veterinary Products Committee[^f00004], make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Medicines (Veterinary Drugs) (General Sale List) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 27th May 2001.

#### General sale list

###### 2

The following descriptions or classes of medicinal products are specified as being products falling within section 51(1) (general sale list) of the Medicines Act 1968:

- (a) veterinary drugs specified in the Schedule to this Order; and

- (b) veterinary drugs—

- (i) which do not fall within paragraph (a) above, and

- (ii) in respect of which there is a marketing authorisation (as defined in regulation 2(4) of the Marketing Authorisations for Veterinary Medicinal Products Regulations 1994[^f00005]) granted or varied after 30th March 2001 in accordance with an application containing a provision to the effect that the method of sale or supply of the veterinary drug in question is to be as for medicinal products on a general sale list.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Medicines (Veterinary Drugs) (General Sale List) Order 1984[^f00006] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

This Order revokes and replaces the Medicines (Veterinary Drugs) (General Sale List) Order 1984 (S.I. [1984/768](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/768)).

The Order specifies medicinal products, being veterinary drugs, which can with reasonable safety be sold or supplied otherwise than by or under the supervision of a pharmacist (the general sale list) (article 2 and Schedule). This differs from the previous Order in so far as the previous Order specified certain classes of veterinary drugs by reference to their composition.
