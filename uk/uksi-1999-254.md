# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 1999

Made: 28th January 1999

Laid before Parliament: 8th February 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Non-domestic rate for 1999-2000

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Secretary of State prescribes a rate of 48.9 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 1999-2000.

## Signed

Henry B McLeish — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 28th January 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a rate of 48.9 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 1999-2000.
