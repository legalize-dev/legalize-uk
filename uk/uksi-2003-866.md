# The Brighton Health Care National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 2003

Made: 24th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3 of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This order may be cited as the Brighton Health Care National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Establishment Order” means the Brighton Health Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003]; and

- “the trust” means the National Health Service Trust established by the Establishment Order.

#### Change of name of the Trust

###### 2

The trust shall be called the Brighton and Sussex University Hospitals National Health Service Trust instead of the Brighton Health Care National Health Service Trust, and accordingly—

- (a) in article 1(2) of the Establishment Order, in the definition of “the trust”; and

- (b) in article 2 of the Establishment Order,

the words “Brighton Health Care” shall be replaced by “Brighton and Sussex University Hospitals”.

###### 3

The change of name effected by article 2 of this Order shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or

- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on, or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name,

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hazel Blears — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 24th March 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Brighton Health Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991, which established the Brighton Health Care National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order changes the name of the trust from the Brighton Health Care National Health Service Trust to the Brighton and Sussex University Hospitals National Health Service Trust, and makes consequential amendments to the Establishment Order.

(The trust which was also called the Brighton and Sussex University Hospitals National Health Service Trust is dissolved by the National Health Service Trusts (Dissolutions) Order 2003, coming into force on the same day.)
