# The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designations etc.) (Amendment) Order 2022

Made: 24th October 2022

Coming into force: 7th November 2022

In accordance with section 7(1) and (2)(ha) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00002], the Secretary of State has consulted the Electoral Commission before making this Order insofar as it is made under section 54(1)(b) of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designations etc.) (Amendment) Order 2022 and comes into force on 7th November 2022.

- (2) Any amendment made by this Order has the same extent as the provision amended.

#### Amendment of the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Local Authorities) Order 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Local Authorities) Order 2020[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In the table in Schedule 1 (designated local authorities in England), in the column headed “name of police area in England”, for “Hampshire” substitute “Hampshire and Isle of Wight”.

#### Amendment of the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2020

###### 3

- (1) The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2020[^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) In the table in Schedule 1 (police area returning officers: England), in the column headed “name of police area in England”, for “Hampshire” substitute “Hampshire and Isle of Wight”.

#### Amendment of the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Local Returning Officers’ and Police Area Returning Officers’ Charges) Order 2021

###### 4

- (1) The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Local Returning Officers’ and Police Area Returning Officers’ Charges) Order 2021[^f00005] is amended as follows.

- (2) In the table in Schedule 3 (police area returning officers: maximum recoverable amounts for police areas), in the column headed “police area”, for “Hampshire” substitute “Hampshire and Isle of Wight”.

## Signed

Jeremy Quin — Minister of State — Home Office — 24th October 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Local Authorities) Order 2020 ([S.I 2020/134](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/134)), the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2020 ([S.I. 2020/136](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/136)), and the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Local Returning Officers’ and Police Area Returning Officers’ Charges) Order 2021 ([S.I. 2021/390](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/390)), consequential on the renaming of the Hampshire police area as the Hampshire and Isle of Wight police area.

The Police Act 1996 (Amendment and Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/790](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/790)) amended the name of that police area in Schedule 1 to the Police Act [1996 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/16) and made other consequential amendments.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
