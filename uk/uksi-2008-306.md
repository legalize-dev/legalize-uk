# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2008

Made: 7th February 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 178(8) and (9) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2008.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 163(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005, insofar as it relates to section 113B(10)(e) of the Police Act 1997[^f00002], shall come into force on 29th February 2008 in England and Wales only.

## Signed

Meg Hillier — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 7th February 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings section 163(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 into force on 29th February 2008, to the extent indicated in article 2. The effect is to bring into force, in England and Wales, a provision inserted by that section into the Police Act 1997 which makes the Ministry of Defence Police a police force for the purposes of section 113B of the Police Act 1997. Police forces under that section may be prescribed or determined as relevant police forces for the purpose of obtaining information to be disclosed in an enhanced criminal record certificate under Part V of the 1997 Act.
