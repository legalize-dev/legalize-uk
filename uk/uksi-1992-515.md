# The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 6th March 1992

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 1992

Coming into force: 30th March 1992

In exercise of the power conferred on the Secretary of State by paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 30th March 1992.

###### 2

The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1988[^f00002] shall be further amended by the substitution for Schedule 2 of the following Schedule–

> > **SCHEDULE 2**
>   - AFSIL Limited
>   - Brighton Polytechnic Housing Association Limited
>   - International Lutheran Student Centre
>   - St Brigid’s House Limited
>   - The London Hospital Special Trustees
>   - The London Mission (West London) Circuit Meeting of the Methodist Church
>   - The London School of Economics Housing Association
>   - International House Association Trust
>   - International Students Club (Church of England) Limited

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — Secretary of State for Education and Science — 6th March 1992

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 6th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1988 by substituting a new Schedule 2. Four further bodies are specified under paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988: International Lutheran Student Centre, St Brigid’s House Limited, International Students Club (Church of England) Limited, and International House Association Trust.

A tenancy granted by such a body to a student at an educational institution specified in the 1988 Regulations is not an assured tenancy for the purposes of the 1988 Act.
