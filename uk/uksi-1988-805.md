# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 4) Order 1988

Made: 25th April 1988

Coming into force: 31st May 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 4) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 31st May 1988.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of each pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — designated owners

- Amerada Hess Ltd., whose registered office is at 2 Stephen Street, Tottenham Court Road, London, W1P 1PL.

- Amoco (U.K.) Exploration Company Inc., whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at 1 Stephen Street, Tottenham Court Road, London, W1P 1PJ.

- Gas Council (Exploration) Ltd., whose registered office is at 59 Bryanston Street, Marble Arch, London, W1H 7AR.

- Texas Eastern North Sea Inc., whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at Berkeley Square House, Berkeley Square, London W1X 5LE.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the companies listed in Part I of the Schedule as owners of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule, which lie betweeen offshore installations above the Indefatigable gasfield, east of the coast of Lincolnshire.
