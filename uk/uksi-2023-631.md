# The Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2023

Made: 7th June 2023

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(4) and (5) of the Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation, interpretation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2023.

- (2) In these Regulations a reference to a section or Schedule by number alone means the section or Schedule so numbered in the Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022.

- (3) These Regulations have the same extent as the provisions commenced.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

Sections 19 to 30, 32 and 33 and Schedule 3 come into force on the 21st day after the day on which these Regulations are made.

## Signed

Bellamy — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 7th June 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are the third commencement regulations made under the Judicial Review and Courts Act 2022 (“*the Act*”). They bring into force Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the Act, which establishes a new Online Procedure Rule Committee and makes provision about Online Procedure Rules, with the exception of section 31 and Schedule 4 (which will be brought into force at a later date).

An impact assessment has not been published for these Regulations as no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
