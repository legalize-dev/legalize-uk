# The North Mersey Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002

Made: 24th March 2002

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the North Mersey Community National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts

###### 2

The North Mersey Community National Health Service Trust is hereby dissolved and accordingly the North Mersey Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] is revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 24th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 2002:

- the North Mersey Community National Health Service Trust.
