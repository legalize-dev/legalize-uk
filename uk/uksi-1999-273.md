# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation Order 1999

Made: 8th February 1999

Coming into force: 12th February 1999

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 6 and 124(1)(d) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation Order 1999, and shall come into force on 12th February 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In Schedule 2–

- (a) in Part I, after paragraph 4, insert the following–

> > (4A) From a point 4 metres south-east of the south-eastern kerbline of Waterlow Road to a point 16 metres south-east of the south-eastern kerbline of Waterlow Road.

- (b) for paragraph 10 substitute the following paragraph–

> > (10) Between a point 58 metres south of the southern kerbline of Theberton Street and a point 40 metres south of the southern kerbline of Theberton Street.

- (c) in paragraph 22, for the words “30 metres” substitute the words “22 metres”.

- (d) for paragraph 16 substitute the following–

> > (16) Between a point level with the common boundary of Nos. 120 and 121 Upper Street and a point level with the common boundary of Nos. 115 and 116 Upper Street.
