# The Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2016

Made: 14th April 2016

Coming into force: 16th May 2016

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12 of the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2016.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Sections 4, 9 and 11 of the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 shall come into force on 16th May 2016.

## Signed

+Justin Cantuar: — Archbishop of Canterbury

+Sentamu Ebor: — Archbishop of York — 14th April 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force sections 4, 9 and 11 of the Safeguarding and Clergy Discipline Measure 2016 with effect from 16th May 2016.
