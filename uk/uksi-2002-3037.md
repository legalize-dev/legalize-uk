# The Port of Larne (Pilotage Functions) Order 2002

Made: 9th December 2002

Laid before Parliament: 10th December 2002

Coming into force: 31st December 2002

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(4) of the 1987 Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Port of Larne (Pilotage Functions) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 31st December 2002.

#### Application of the Pilotage Act 1987

###### 2

Larne Harbour Limited shall be a competent harbour authority for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David Jamieson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 9th December 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that Larne Harbour Limited is to be a competent harbour authority for the port of Larne for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987.

The applicant for the Order is Larne Harbour Limited which is a company registered in Northern Ireland having its registered office at 9 Olderfleet Road, Larne, Northern Ireland, BT40 1AS.
