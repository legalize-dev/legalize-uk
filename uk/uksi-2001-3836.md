# The East Basildon Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

Made: 28th November 2001

Laid before Parliament: 3rd December 2001

Coming into force: 1st January 2002

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending and varying an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with a consent of the governing bodies of every school participating in the zone. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(2), 10(3) and 138(7) of the Schools Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following order.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This order shall be cited as the East Basildon Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st January 2002.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this order—

- “East Basildon Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the East Basildon Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002].

#### Extension of the East Basildon Education Action Zone Order 1998

###### 3

The East Basildon Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st January 2002.

#### Variation of the East Basildon Education Action Zone 1998

###### 4

There shall be inserted after article 9 of the East Basildon Education Action Zone Order 1998 the following article—

> > (9A) A member of the Forum may be removed from office by the Forum in the following circumstances—
>   (a) in the case of a member who was a governor of a zone school at the time he was appointed to the Forum pursuant to either article 4(a) or article 5, if that person ceases to be a governor at that zone school, or a governor of the relevant category as the case may be;
>   (b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to either article 4(a) or 5, if the school whose governing body appointed him, or to which he was connected, is discontinued;
>   (c) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 4(c), if the body in question is dissolved or otherwise ceases to exist; and
>   (d) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to article 5(a), (b), (c) or (d), if that person no longer appears to the Forum to represent either, health service purchasers and providers; the Social Services Department of Essex County Council; the local voluntary sector; Essex Police; large local businesses; small local businesses; the teachers in the zone schools; or the parents of the children in the zone schools, as the case may be.

###### 5

- (1) The East Basildon Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00003] is varied as follows:

- (2) In Schedule 1, the words “RM plc” are deleted and the words “Holmes Pearman” are inserted.

- (3) In Schedule 2, the words “Fairview School” are substituted with, “South Essex PRU”; the words “Cherry Tree Primary School” are substituted for “Swan Mead County Infant School” and “Swan Mead County Junior School”; and the words “Bardfield Primary School and Nursery” are substituted for “Bardfield County Infant School” and “Bardfield County Junior School”.

- (4) In Schedule 2, the words “Willows Primary School” are substituted for “Manor County Infants School” and “Manor County Junior School”.

## Signed

Stephen Timms — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 28th November 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order extends to the East Basildon Education Action Zone for a further period of two years. The order varies the partners in the Zone and the schools which comprise it and also gives the Forum an additional power to remove members in certain circumstances.
