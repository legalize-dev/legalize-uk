# The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1988

Made: 30th March 1988

Laid before Parliament: 30th March 1988

Coming into force: 31st March 1988

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in the case of land in England, and the Secretary of State, in the case of land in Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 to the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Application, title and commencement

###### 1

This Order, which applies to land in England and Wales, may be cited as the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 31st March 1988.

#### Amendment of principal Order

###### 2

The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby amended by substituting for the Schedule thereto the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) Order 1986, which prescribes the quota per hectare and the average yield per hectare to be taken into account in determining the standard quota for the purposes of calculating the payment which a tenant in England and Wales is entitled, in certain circumstances, by Schedule 1 to the Agriculture Act 1986 to obtain from his landlord in respect of milk quota on the termination of the tenancy of the land.

This Order substitutes for the Schedule to that Order a new Schedule, in which the quota per hectare prescribed by article 3 of that Order is revised (article 2).
