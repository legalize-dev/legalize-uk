# The European Communities (Designation) Order 1979

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of December 1979

Present,

The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the European Communities (Designation) Order 1979 and shall come into operation on 24th January 1980.

#### Designation

###### 2

For the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 the Minister specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order is hereby designated in relation to the matters specified in column 2 of that Schedule and in relation to anything supplemental or incidental to the matters so specified.

## SCHEDULE

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Minister | Matters in relation to which the Minister is designated |
| The Secretary of State | Requirements concerning sea-going tankers entering or leaving UK ports. |

## Signed

N. E. Leigh, — Clerk of the Privy Council.

## Explanatory note

(This Note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the Secretary of State to exercise powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, to make regulations in relation to sea-going tankers entering or leaving U.K. ports.
