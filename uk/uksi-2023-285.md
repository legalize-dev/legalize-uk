# The West Suffolk (Electoral Changes) Order 2023

Made: 8th March 2023

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2), (3) and (4)

The Local Government Boundary Commission for England has decided to give effect to the recommendations and makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 92(3) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the West Suffolk (Electoral Changes) Order 2023.

- (2) This article and article 2 come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

- (3) Article 3 comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on 1st April 2023;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England[^f00003] in 2023.

- (4) Article 4 comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on 15th October 2024;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2026.

- (5) This Order extends to England and Wales but applies in relation to England only.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- (a) “*the Council’s Order of 11th October 2022*” means the West Suffolk (Reorganisation of Community Governance) (Dalham, Ousden, Denston, Wickhambrook, Worlington, Red Lodge) Order 2022[^f00004];

- (b) “*the Council’s Order of 27th October 2022*” means the West Suffolk (Reorganisation of Community Governance) (Bury St Edmunds, Haverhill, Rushbrooke with Rougham) Order 2022[^f00005].

#### Alteration of district ward boundaries

###### 3

- (1) Notwithstanding the amended parish boundary between the parish of Rushbrooke with Rougham and the parish of Bury St Edmunds provided for under the Council’s Order of 27th October 2022, that part of the existing Moreton Hall district ward which remains in Rushbrooke with Rougham parish is to be transferred from Moreton Hall district ward to Rougham district ward.

- (2) The part of the parish of Worlington that becomes part of the parish of Red Lodge by virtue of the Council’s Order of 11th October 2022 ceases to be part of the Manor district ward and becomes part of the Iceni district ward.

#### Alteration of a county electoral division boundary

###### 4

The part of the parish of Worlington that becomes part of the parish of Red Lodge by virtue of the Council’s Order of 11th October 2022 ceases to be part of the Mildenhall county electoral division and becomes part of the Red Lodge county electoral division.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to recommendations made by West Suffolk Council to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England for related alterations following a reorganisation of community review by the Council given effect to by the West Suffolk (Reorganisation of Community Governance) (Dalham, Ousden, Denston, Wickhambrook, Worlington, Red Lodge) Order 2022 and the West Suffolk (Reorganisation of Community Governance) (Bury St Edmunds, Haverhill, Rushbrooke with Rougham) Order 2022 (together being “the Council’s Orders”).

The alterations are a consequence of changes to parish boundaries and warding arrangements under the Council’s Orders which were made by West Suffolk Council under powers including section 86 of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act [2007 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/28). A hard copy of those orders are available for inspection at reasonable times at the offices of West Suffolk House, Western Way, Bury St Edmunds IP33 3YU.

The changes described in article 3 (in relation to district wards) apply in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2023.

The changes described in article 4 (in relation to county electoral divisions) apply in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2025.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
