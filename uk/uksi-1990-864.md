# The Court of Protection (Enduring Powers of Attorney) (Amendment) Rules 1990

Made: 3rd April 1990

Laid before Parliament: 10th April 1990

Coming into force: 1st May 1990

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 106 of the Mental Health Act 1983[^f00001] and with the consent of the Treasury so far as is required by subsection (5) of that section hereby makes the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Court of Protection (Enduring Powers of Attorney) (Amendment) Rules 1990 and shall come into force on 1st May 1990.

###### 2

Column 2 of Schedule 2 to the Court of Protection (Enduring Powers of Attorney) Rules 1986[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) for the figure “£30.00” there shall be substituted the figure “£50.00”

- (b) for the figure “£5.00” there shall be substituted the figure “£10.00”

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 2nd April 1990

We concur

Thomas Sackville — Kenneth Carlisle — Two of the Lord Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 3rd April 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules increase the fees payable in respect of applications for registration of an enduring power of attorney and applications to search the register.
