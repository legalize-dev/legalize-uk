# The Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1994

Made: 29th November 1994

Coming into force: 10th December 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Water Byelaws (Loch an Sgoltaire) Extension Order 1994 and shall come into force on 10th December 1994.

###### 2

The period during which the Strathclyde Regional Council, Prevention of Water Pollution (Loch an Sgoltaire) Byelaws 1985 are to remain in force is hereby extended to 11th December 1995.

## Signed

T D Macdonald — Deputy Chief Engineer, The Scottish Office — 29th November 1994
