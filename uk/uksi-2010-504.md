# The Lowestoft Sixth Form College (Incorporation) Order 2010

Made: 28th February 2010

Laid before Parliament: 2nd March 2010

Coming into force: 23rd March 2010

In accordance with section 51(3) of the Act, the Secretary of State has published a draft of the proposed order by the time and in the manner prescribed by regulation 6 of the Education (Publication of Draft Proposals and Orders) (Further Education Corporations) (England) Regulations 2001[^f00002].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Lowestoft Sixth Form College (Incorporation) Order 2010 and comes into force on 23rd March 2010.

###### 2

There is established as from 23rd March 2010 a body corporate called “the Lowestoft Sixth Form College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name.

###### 3

The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order is 23rd March 2010, and the corporation shall conduct the Lowestoft Sixth Form College from that date.

## Signed

Kevin Brennan — Minister for Further Education, Skills Apprenticeships and Consumer Affairs — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 28th February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order establishes a further education corporation called “the Lowestoft Sixth Form College” under section 16(1)(a) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 23rd March 2010 as the operative date for the purposes of Part I of that Act, being the date from which the further education corporation shall operate the college.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no wider impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
