# The Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made: 21st December 1992

Coming into force: 1st January 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

###### 2

In article 2(b) of the Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) Order 1992[^f00002], for ““Brighouse””there shall be substituted ““Bradford””.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — One Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 21st December 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 2(b) of the Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) Order 1992 to substitute “Bradford” for “Brighouse”.
