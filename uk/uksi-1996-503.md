# The Salford Community Health Care National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made: 29th February 1996

Laid before Parliament: 7th March 1996

Coming into force: 28th March 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Salford Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Salford Community Health Care National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted the Salford and Trafford Health Authority[^f00003] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Salford Community Health Care National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 28th March 1996.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Salford and Trafford Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the Salford Community Health Care National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 6th February 1996, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 28th March 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 29th February 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Salford Community Health Care National Health Service Trust on 28th March 1996 trust property formerly held by the Salford and Trafford Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Department of Health, NHS Trust Unit, Room 1W29, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
