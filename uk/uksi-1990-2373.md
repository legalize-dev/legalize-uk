# The Army Terms of Service (Amendment) Regulations 1990

Made: 27th November 1990

Laid before Parliament: 4th December 1990

Coming into force: 1st January 1991

The Defence Council, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 2 of the Armed Forces Act 1966[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Army Terms of Service (Amendment) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st January 1991.

#### Amendment to Regulations

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 5 of the Army Terms of Service Regulations 1986[^f00002] (transfer to the reserve after notice period) shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (5)(c) for the words “on the relevant date” there shall be substituted “on the date he attains the age of 18 years or 3 months after the date of his attestation, whichever is the later”.

- (3) Paragraph (2) above does not apply where a person enlisted before 1st January 1991.

## Signed

On behalf of the Defence Council

Arran — J. L. Chapple — Members of the Defence Council — 27th November 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend one of the provisions determining the “notice period” for the purposes of regulation 5 of the Army Terms of Service Regulations 1986.
