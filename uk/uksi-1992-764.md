# The Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made: 12th March 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 4(2) of the Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992.

###### 2

The Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992 shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty"s Principal Secretaries of State — 12th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1992 the Aggravated Vehicle-Taking Act 1992.
