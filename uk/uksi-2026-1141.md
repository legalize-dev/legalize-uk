# The English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026

Made: 28th October 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 108(6) of the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026.

#### Provision coming into force on 13th November 2026

###### 2

[Section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23/section/12) (mayoral combined authorities and CCAs: precepts) of the [English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23/contents) comes into force on 13th November 2026.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government

Jim McMahon — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government — 28th October 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations are the fourth commencement regulations under the [English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23/contents).

[Regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1141/regulation/2/made) commences [section 12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23/section/12) of the [English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23/contents). [That section](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23/section/12) amends [section 40](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/14/section/40) of the [Local Government Finance Act 1992 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/14/contents), [section 107G](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/section/107G) of the [Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20/contents) and [section 41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/section/41) of the [Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 (c. 55)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/55/contents). The amendments remove the restriction under which precepts could be issued only in respect of mayoral functions. They also preserve the requirement for a separate component in respect of any police and crime commissioner functions exercised by the mayor.

An Impact Assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. An Impact Assessment was produced for the [English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/23/contents) and is available from https://bills.parliament.uk/publications/63710/documents/7405, and copies can be obtained from the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DF.
