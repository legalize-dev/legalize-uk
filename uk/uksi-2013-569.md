# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2013

Made: 11th March 2013

Coming into force: 31st March 2013

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2013 and comes into force on 31st March 2013.

#### Originating capital of NHS trusts

###### 2

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule is the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

| *Column 1* *NHS Trust* | *Column 2* *Originating Capital* *£* |
| --- | --- |
| The Barts Health National Health Service Trust[^f00002] | 145,855,000 |
| The Isle of Wight National Health Service Trust[^f00003] | nil |
| The Torbay and Southern Devon Health and Care National Health Service Trust[^f00004] | nil |

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 7th March 2013

We consent,

David Evennett — Robert Goodwill — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 11th March 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of National Health Service trusts established under that Act on 1st April 2012.
