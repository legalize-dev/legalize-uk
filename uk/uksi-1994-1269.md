# The Salford Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 1994

Made: 10th May 1994

Coming into force: 20th May 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Salford Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 20th May 1994.

- (2) In this Order unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the Order” means the Salford Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00003];

- “the Trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

#### Change of Name of NHS Trust

###### 2

The trust shall be called the Salford Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust instead of the Salford Hospitals National Health Service Trust and accordingly, in the definition of “the trust” in article 1(2) and in article 2 of the Order after the word “Salford” there shall be inserted the word “Royal”.

#### Transitional provisions

###### 3

The change of name under this Order shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or

- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name,

and all documents or instruments which refer to the trust by its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 10th May 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of an NHS Trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 from the Salford Hospitals National Health Service Trust to the Salford Royal Hospitals National Health Service Trust and makes consequential amendments to the establishment Order and transitional provisions.
