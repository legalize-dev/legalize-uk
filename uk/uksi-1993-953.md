# The Banking Act 1987 (Exempt Persons) Order 1993

Made: 30th March 1993

Laid before Parliament: 2nd April 1993

Coming into force: 23rd April 1993

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 4(3) of the Banking Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with the Bank of England, hereby order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Banking Act 1987 (Exempt Persons) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 23rd April 1993.

#### Exemption

###### 2

Schedule 2 to the Banking Act 1987 shall be amended by the addition of the following new entry after the entry numbered 26—

> > (27) The Council of Europe Resettlement Fund.

## Signed

Tim Wood — Gregory Knight — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 30th March 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds The Council of Europe Resettlement Fund to the list in Schedule 2 to the Banking Act 1987 of persons exempted from section 3 of that Act (restriction on acceptance of deposits.
