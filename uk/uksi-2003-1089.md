# The Borough of Warrington (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made: 10th April 2003

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Electoral Commission[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 17[^f00002] and 26[^f00003] of the Local Government Act 1992, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Borough of Warrington (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2003.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

#### Amendment to the 2002 Order

###### 2

- (1) The Borough of Warrington (Electoral Changes) Order 2002[^f00004] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In article 4(2) the words “before 10th May 2004” shall be substituted for the words “before 6th May 2004”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Borough of Warrington (Electoral Changes) Order 2002, so as to provide for borough councillors holding office immediately before 10th May 2004 to retire on that date and for newly-elected councillors to come into office on that date.
