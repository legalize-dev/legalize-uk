# The Airports Slot Allocation (Second Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made: 30th June 1994

Laid before Parliament: 1st July 1994

Coming into force: 1st July 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001]for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002]in relation to matters relating to the allocation of take off and landing slots for aircraft at airports, in exercise of the powers conferred by tht section hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Airports Slot Allocation (Second Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) of the Airports Slot Allocation Regulations 1993[^f00003], in the definition of “the Council Regulation”, for the words “Council Decision [93/453/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0453) concerning the amendment of the Agreement between the European Economic Community, the Kingdom of Norway and the Kingdom of Sweden on civil aviation” there shall be substituted the words “the EEA Agreement[^f00004]as amended by the Decision of the EEA Joint Committee No. 7/94 of 21 March 1994[^f00005]”.

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) of the Airports Slot Allocation Regulations 1993, after the definition of “the Council Regulation” there shall be inserted the following—

> > and
> - “the EEA Agreement” means the Agreement on the European Economic Area signed at Oporto on 2nd May 1992 as adjusted by the Protocol signed at Brussels on 17th March 1993.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Mackay of Ardbrecknish — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — 30th June 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Airports Slot Allocation Regulations 1993 by amending the definition of “the Council Regulation” in regulation 2(1) so as to take into account the amendment of the Annexes to the EEA Agreement done by the Decision of the EEA Joint Committee No. 7/94 (regulation 2).
