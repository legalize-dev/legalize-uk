# The Child Trust Funds (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2020

Made: 10th March 2020

Laid before Parliament: 12th March 2020

Coming into force: 6th April 2020

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(2) of the Child Trust Funds Act 2004[^f00001], make the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Child Trust Funds (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 6th April 2020.

#### Amendment of the Child Trust Funds Regulations 2004

###### 2

The Child Trust Funds Regulations 2004[^f00002] are amended as follows.

###### 3

In regulation 9(2) and (3) (annual limit on subscriptions)[^f00003] for “£4,368” substitute “£9,000”.

## Signed

Iain Stewart — David Rutley — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 10th March 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Child Trust Funds Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1450](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1450)). They provide for the annual limit on subscriptions to be increased from £4,368 to £9,000 (regulation 3).

A Tax Information and Impact Note will be published on the HM Government website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.
