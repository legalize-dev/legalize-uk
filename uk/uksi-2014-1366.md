# The Crown Agents Holding and Realisation Board (Dissolution) Order 2009

Made: 17th July 2009

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

The Secretary of State is satisfied that the requirements of the provisions cited in paragraphs 24(2)(a) and (b) of Schedule 5 to that Act[^f00003] have been complied with.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crown Agents Holding and Realisation Board (Dissolution) Order 2009 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Dissolution

###### 2

The Crown Agents Holding and Realisation Board is dissolved.

## Signed

Douglas Alexander — Secretary of State for International Development — 17th July 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order dissolves the Crown Agents Holding and Realisation Board (the Board).

Paragraph 24(2) of Schedule 5 to the Crown Agents Act 1979 provides that any liabilities that have been incurred by the Board on or after the 31 March 2008 (the prescribed day by virtue of the Crown Agents Holding and Realisation Board (Prescribed Day) Order 2008, [S.I. 2008/921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/921)) shall become liabilities of the Secretary of State.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
