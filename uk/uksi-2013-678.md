# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2013

Made: 20th March 2013

Laid before Parliament: 22nd March 2013

Coming into force: 27th March 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2013 and shall come into force on 27th March 2013.

###### 2

- (1) Part 2 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) After “Council Implementing Decision 2012/454/CFSP of 1 August 2012 (Afghanistan)” insert “and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2012/809/CFSP of 20 December 2012 (Afghanistan)[^f00003] and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2013/73/CFSP of 31 January 2013 (Afghanistan)[^f00004]”.

- (3) After “Council Implementing Decision 2011/848/CFSP of 16 December 2011 (Democratic Republic of the Congo)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2012/811/CFSP of 20 December 2012 (Democratic Republic of the Congo)[^f00005] and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2013/46/CFSP of 22 January 2013 (Democratic Republic of the Congo)[^f00006]”.

- (4) After “Council Decision 2012/635/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Iran)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2012/829/CFSP of 21 December 2012 (Iran)[^f00007] and as amended by Council Decision 2013/124/CFSP of 11 March 2013 (Iran)[^f00008]”.

- (5) After “Council Decision 2011/860/CFSP of 19 December 2011 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2013/88/CFSP of 18 February 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea)[^f00009]”.

- (6) After “Council Decision 2012/739/CFSP of 29 November 2012 (Syria)” insert “as amended by Council Decision 2013/109/CFSP of 28 February 2013 (Syria)[^f00010]”.

- (7) After “Council Implementing Decision 2012/124/CFSP of 27 February 2012 (Zimbabwe)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2013/89/CFSP of 18 February 2013 (Zimbabwe)[^f00011]”.

## Signed

Mark Harper — Minister of State — 20th March 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000.

It amends Part 2 of that Schedule to list: Council Implementing Decision 2012/809/CFSP of 20 December 2012 (Afghanistan) and Council Implementing Decision 2013/73/CFSP of 31 January 2013 (Afghanistan); Council Decision 2012/811/CFSP of 20 December 2012 (Democratic Republic of the Congo) and Council Implementing Decision 2013/46/CFSP of 22 January 2013 (Democratic Republic of the Congo); Council Decision 2012/829/CFSP of 21 December 2012 (Iran) and Council Decision 2013/124/CFSP of 11 March 2013 (Iran); Council Decision 2013/88/CFSP of 18 February 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea); Council Decision 2013/109/CFSP of 28 February 2013 (Syria); Council Decision 2013/89/CFSP of 18 February 2013 (Zimbabwe);

Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the Schedule (as amended) or falling within a description specified in such an instrument will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
