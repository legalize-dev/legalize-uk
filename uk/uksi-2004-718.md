# The Broadcasting and Communications (Isle of Man) (No.2) Order 2004

Made: 10th March 2004

Coming into force: 26th March 2004

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of March 2004 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 402(3)(b) and (c) and 411(6) and (8) of the Communications Act 2003[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Broadcasting and Communications (Isle of Man) (No.2) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 26th March 2004.

###### 2

Article 1 of the Communications (Isle of Man) Order 2003[^f00002] is hereby amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (4), after the words “(“the No.2 Order”)” insert “or the Office of Communications Act 2002 (Commencement No. 3) and Communications Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2004 (“the Amendment Order”)[^f00003]”;

- (b) in paragraph (4)(a), after the words “the No.2 Order” insert “or the Amendment Order”;

- (c) in paragraph (4)(b), after the words “the No.2 Order” insert “or the Amendment Order”; and

- (d) in paragraph (4)(c), after the words “the No.2 Order” insert “or the Amendment Order”.

## Signed

A.K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends further the Communications (Isle of Man) Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/3198](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3198)) (the 2003 Order), which extended, in part, to the Isle of Man the Office of Communications Act 2002 and the Communications Act 2003 (the 2003 Act).

The amendment is necessary because of an amendment to the Office of Communications Act 2002 (Commencement No.3) and Communications Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/3142](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3142)/c. 125) made by the Office of Communications Act 2002 (Commencement No. 3) and Communications Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2004 (S.I. [2004/545](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/545)/c.21).

By reason of the amendment to the Commencement Order, the date upon which section 278 and consequent provisions in sections 351, 353 and Schedule 12 of the 2003 Act (extended to the Isle of Man by the 2003 Order) will come into force has been changed.

The 2003 Order was previously amended by the Broadcasting and Communications (Isle of Man) Order 2004 ([S.I.2004/309](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/309)).
