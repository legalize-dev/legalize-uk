# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made: 14th August 2012

Coming into force: 10th September 2012

In accordance with article 9 of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Isle of Man) Order 2010, the Secretary of State has consulted the Department of Home Affairs of the Isle of Man.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 10th September 2012.

- (2) These Regulations extend to the Isle of Man.

#### Amendment of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Isle of Man) Regulations 2011

###### 2

Regulation 9 of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Isle of Man) Regulations 2011 (enhanced criminal records certificates: relevant police forces) [^f00003] is omitted.

## Signed

James Borkenshire — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 14th August 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke regulation 9 of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Isle of Man) Regulations 2011 which prescribed which police forces were “relevant” police forces for the purposes of providing information to be disclosed on an enhanced criminal record certificate under section 113B of the Police Act [1997 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/50). Under changes made by section 82(3) of the Protection of Freedoms Act [2012 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/9), police forces no longer need to be prescribed, rather the Secretary of State will determine the relevant chief officer in relation to a certificate.
