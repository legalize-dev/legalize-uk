# The General Osteopathic Council (Conditional Registration) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 1998

Made: 28th October 1998

Coming into force: 29th October 1998

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 28th day of October 1998 By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken the said Rules into consideration, are pleased to, and do hereby, approve the same.

This Order may be cited as the General Osteopathic Council (Conditional Registration) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 1998 and shall come into force on 29th October 1998.

## **THE GENERAL OSTEOPATHIC COUNCIL (CONDITIONAL REGISTRATION) (AMENDMENT) RULES 1998**

The General Osteopathic Council, in exercise of their powers under section 6(3)(l) of the Osteopaths Act 1993[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Rules:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the General Osteopathic Council (Conditional Registration) (Amendment) Rules 1998 and shall come into force on the date on which the Order approving them does so.

#### Amendment of General Osteopathic Council (Conditional Registration) Rules 1998

###### 2

In rule 3(3) of the General Osteopathic Council (Conditional Registration) Rules 1998[^f00003], for the words “registered osteopaths” there shall be substituted the words “fully registered osteopaths”.

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Rules approved by this Order correct an error in the General Osteopathic Council (Conditional Registration) Rules 1998 (approved by S.I. [1998/1020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1020)).
