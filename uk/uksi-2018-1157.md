# The Trade Marks (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 2018

Made: 7th November 2018

Coming into force: 14th January 2019

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 7th day of November 2018Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 108(2) of the Trade Marks Act 1994[^f00001] and section 7(2) of the Intellectual Property (Unjustified Threats) Act 2017[^f00002], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Trade Marks (Isle of Man) (Amendment) Order 2018 and shall come into force on 14th January 2019.

#### Amendments to the Schedule to the Trade Marks (Isle of Man) Order 2013

###### 2

The Schedule to the Trade Marks (Isle of Man) Order 2013[^f00003] is amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

#### Modification of the Intellectual Property (Unjustified Threats) Act 2017 as it extends to the Isle of Man

###### 3

- (1) The Intellectual Property (Unjustified Threats) Act 2017 as it extends to the Isle of Man has effect subject to the following modification.

- (2) For section 7(2)(b) (extent) substitute—

> > (b) section 2(1), (2) and (4);

###### 2

After paragraph 1, insert—

> > (1A) In section 1 (trade marks), for subsection (1) substitute—
>   (1) In this Act “trade mark” means any sign which is capable—
>     (a) of being represented in the register in a manner which enables the registrar and other competent authorities and the public to determine the clear and precise subject matter of the protection afforded to the proprietor, and
>     (b) of distinguishing goods or services of one undertaking from those of other undertakings.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Trade Mark (Isle of Man) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/2601](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2601)) to make further modifications to the Trade Marks Act [1994 (c.26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/26) (“the 1994 Act”) in its application to the Isle of Man.

The amendments in this Order to the 1994 Act correspond to amendments made to the Act (as it has effect in the United Kingdom) by the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act [2007 (c.15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/15), the Legal Services Act 2007 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/3348](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/3348)), the European Union Trade Mark Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/299](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/299)) and the Trade Marks Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/825](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/825)). It also amends the 1994 Act to reflect the operation of the Transfer of Functions (Lord Advocate and Secretary of State) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/678](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/678)).

This Order also modifies the Intellectual Property (Unjustified Threats) Act [2017 (c.14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/14) (“the 2017 Act”) as it extends to the Isle of Man. The modification omits the extension to the Isle of Man of section 2(3) of the 2017 Act as that provision amends section 52 of the 1994 Act which was omitted from the 1994 Act as it applies to the Isle of Man by [S.I. 2013/2601](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2601).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors in the United Kingdom is foreseen.
