# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation (Amendment) Order 1997

Made: 5th June 1997

Laid before Parliament: 10th June 1997

Coming into force: 1st July 1997

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st July 1997.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation Order 1993[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 3

The principal Order shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

#### Rates of payment under agreement

###### 4

In article 6 of the principal Order—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—

- (i) in sub-paragraph (a), for the amount “£12” there shall be substituted the amount “£14”;

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), for the amount “£25” there shall be substituted the amount “£27”;

- (iii) in sub-paragraph (c), for the amount “£32” there shall be substituted the amount “£38”;

- (iv) in sub-paragraph (d), for the amount “£30” there shall be substituted the amount “£34”;

- (v) in sub-paragraph (e), for the amount “£40” there shall be substituted the amount “£50”;

- (b) in paragraph (2), in the table, in the right-hand column, for the figure “50” there shall be substituted the figure “60”.

#### Amendment to Schedule

###### 5

In Schedule 5 (conservation plan operations) to the principal Order, after paragraph 7, there shall be inserted the following paragraph:

> > (8) The creation or restoration of ponds.

#### Saving

###### 6

The amendments made to the principal Order by article 4 of this Order shall not apply in respect of any annual payment to a farmer which relates wholly or in part to any period before 1st April 1997.

## Signed

Elliot Morley — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 4th June 1997

We consent

Bob Ainsworth — Jon Owen Jones — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 5th June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends, with effect from 1st July 1997, the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Exmoor) Designation Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/85), amended by S.I. [1994/928](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/928), [1995/195](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/195), [1995/960](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/960), [1996/3104](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3104)), which designated an area on Exmoor as an environmentally sensitive area.

Subject to a saving provision, the Order amends the rates of payment to be made by the minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 (article 4).

An amendment is also made to add an option to the operations that may be included in a conservation plan (article 5).
