# The Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Modification) Order 1989

Made: 2nd August 1989

Coming into force: 18th September 1989

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of August 1989 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 2(2) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Modification) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 18th September 1989.

###### 2

Schedule 2 to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (which, as amended[^f00002], specifies the drugs which are subject to control under that Act) shall be amended by the insertion in paragraph 1 of Part III of that Schedule–

- (a) after the word “Bromazepam”, of the word “Buprenorphine”, and

- (b) after the word “Oxazolam”, of the word “Pemoline”.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds to Part III of Schedule 2 to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (which specifies the Class C drugs which are subject to control under the Act) Buprenorphine and Pemoline.
