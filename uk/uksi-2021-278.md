# The Public Sector Apprenticeship Targets (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: 9th March 2021

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 2021

Coming into force: 1st April 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections A9(1), (5)(c) and (d), and (6), A10(1)(b), (3) and (7) of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Sector Apprenticeship Targets (Amendment) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 1st April 2021.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales but apply in relation to England only.

#### Amendment of the Public Sector Apprenticeship Targets Regulations 2017

###### 2

The Public Sector Apprenticeship Targets Regulations 2017[^f00002] are amended in accordance with the following regulations.

#### Amendment of regulation 3 (prescribed public bodies)

###### 3

In regulation 3(4) for “the day before the first day of one or more reporting periods in the target period” substitute “31st March 2021”.

- (i) after “1st April” insert “2021”;

- (ii) for “in the following calendar year” substitute “2022”;

- (d) in paragraph (c)—

- (i) after “1st April” insert “2021”;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Public Sector Apprenticeship Targets Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/513](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/513)) (“the 2017 Regulations”). The 2017 Regulations set the public sector apprenticeship target for specified public sector bodies at 2.3% of their headcount, as new apprentice starts, for the period between 1st April 2017 and 31st March 2021. These Regulations provide that the same target will apply for a further year, beginning with 1st April 2021 and ending with 31st March 2022, that period beginning immediately after the end of the target period prescribed in the 2017 Regulations.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
