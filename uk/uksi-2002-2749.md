# The Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

Made: 5th November 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 7(2) of the Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002[^f00001] the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002.

###### 2

The Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 shall come into force on 20th November 2002.

## Signed

Melanie Johnson — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Competition, Consumers and Markets, — Department of Trade and Industry — 5th November 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Copyright, etc. and Trade Marks (Offences and Enforcement) Act 2002 into force on 20th November 2002.
