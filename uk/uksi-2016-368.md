# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2016

Made: 15th March 2016

Coming into force: 16th March 2016

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of March 2016Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2016 and comes into force on 16th March 2016.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

- (1) The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — Persons appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 16th March 2016

Christine Dick

Rachel Jane Holden

Matthew David Tiplin

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
