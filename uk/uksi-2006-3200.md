# The Fraud Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2006

Made: 29th November 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15 of the Fraud Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fraud Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2006.

###### 2

To the extent that it is not already in force, the Fraud Act 2006 shall come into force on 15th January 2007.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 29th November 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 15th January 2007 sections 1 to 14 of and Schedules 1 to 3 to the Fraud Act 2006. Sections 15 and 16 came into force on Royal Assent on 8th November 2006.
