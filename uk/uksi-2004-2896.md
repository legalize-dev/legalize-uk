# The Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2004

Made: 3rd November 2004

Coming into force: 15th November 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraphs 3(1)(b) and (c) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2004 and shall come into force on 15th November 2004.

- (2) In this Order, “the Establishment Order” means the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00003].

#### Amendment of nature and functions of the trust

###### 2

Article 3(2) of the Establishment Order (nature and functions of the trust) shall be replaced by the following—

> > (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, in particular ambulance and associated transport services, for the purposes of the health service.

#### Increase in the number of non-executive directors of the trust

###### 3

In article 4 of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust) “4 non-executive directors” shall be replaced by “5 non-executive directors”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 3rd November 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993, which established the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust, the name of which was changed to the Coventry and Warwickshire Ambulance National Health Service Trust by the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 2004.

Article 2 of the Order amends the nature and functions of the trust and removes the references to the establishments from which the trust carries out its functions. Article 3 of the Order increases the number of non-executive directors of the trust from 4 to 5.
