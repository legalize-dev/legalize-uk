# The Companies House Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2019

Made: 8th October 2019

Laid before the House of Commons: 15th October 2019

Coming into force: 1st April 2020

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 6(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001], with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies House Trading Fund (Revocation) Order 2019.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 1st April 2020.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The instruments specified in the Schedule are revoked to the extent shown in that Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Companies House Trading Fund Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/1795](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1795)) and the Companies House Trading Fund (Amendment) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2622](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2622)), as well as provisions of other instruments to the extent to which they amend the 1991 Order, with effect from 1st April 2020. As a result, the trading fund established by the 1991 Order will cease to exist, the operations of Companies House as defined in Schedule 1 to the 1991 Order will cease to be funded by means of a trading fund, and its assets and liabilities will no longer be appropriated to a trading fund. Companies House will remain an Executive Agency of the Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
