# The Immigration (Carriers' Liability Prescribed Sum) Order 1991

Made: 1st July 1991

Laid before Parliament: 2nd July 1991

Coming into force: 1st August 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1 of the Immigration (Carriers' Liability) Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Carriers' Liability Prescribed Sum) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st August 1991.

###### 2

In place of the sum of £1,000 specified as payable under section 1(1) of the Immigration (Carriers' Liability) Act 1987 (liability of carriers for passengers without proper documents) the sum of £2,000 is hereby prescribed.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 1st July 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order raises from £1,000 to £2,000 the sum payable under the Immigration (Carriers' Liability) Act 1987 by owners or agents of ships or aircraft which bring passengers without proper documents to the United Kingdom.
