# The Court Funds (Amendment) Rules 2001

Made: 5th March 2001

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 38(7) of the Administration of Justice Act 1982[^f00001], and with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Court Funds (Amendment) Rules 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

###### 2

In rule 2(2) of the Court Funds Rules 1987[^f00002], in the definition of “Court Funds Office”, for “Public Trust Office” there shall be substituted “Court Service”.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, *C* — Dated 1st March 2001

We concur,

Jim Dowd — Greg Pope — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 5th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the definition of the Court Funds Office as a consequence of the transfer, with effect from 1st April 2001, of the Court Funds Office from the Public Trust Office to the Court Service.
