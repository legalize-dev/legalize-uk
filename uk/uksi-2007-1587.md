# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2007

Made: 27th May 2007

Laid before Parliament: 4th June 2007

Coming into force: 6th July 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975[^f00001], makes the following Order. In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act she has consulted with such persons as appear to her to be requisite.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 6th July 2007.

#### Designation

###### 2

The sports grounds specified in the Schedule (being sports grounds in England at which association football matches are played, which are occupied by a club which is a member of the Football League Limited or the Football Association Premier League Limited, and which, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, have accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators) are designated as sports grounds requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

## SCHEDULE

| Sports Ground | Occupier of Sports Ground |
| --- | --- |
| New Stadium Oteley Road Shrewsbury SY3 9NB | Shrewsbury Town Football Club |
| Victoria Road Stadium Victoria Road Dagenham Essex RM10 7XL | Dagenham and Redbridge Football Club |
| Stadium MK Stadium Way West Milton Keynes MK1 1ST | Milton Keynes Dons Football Club |

## Signed

Tessa Jowell — Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport — 27th May 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the New Stadium in Shrewsbury, occupied by Shrewsbury Town Football Club; Victoria Road Stadium in Dagenham, occupied by Dagenham and Redbridge Football Club; and Stadium MK, occupied by Milton Keynes Dons Football Club, as sports grounds requiring safety certificates under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.
