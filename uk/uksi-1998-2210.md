# The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Channel Islands and Isle of Man) Regulations 1998

Made: 8th September 1998

Laid before Parliament: 9th September 1998

Coming into force: 5th October 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1998[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and having regard to the matters specified in section 2(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Channel Islands and Isle of Man) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 5th October 1998.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations, “the 1998 Regulations” means the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998[^f00002].

#### Extension of the 1998 Regulations to the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man

###### 3

The 1998 Regulations are hereby amended by the insertion of the following regulation after regulation 2—

> > (3) These Regulations other than regulation 2(d) shall extend to the Bailiwick of Jersey, the Bailiwick of Guernsey and the Isle of Man.

## Signed

Barbara Roche, — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Small Firms, Trade and Industry, — Department of Trade and Industry — 8th September 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/1567](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1567)) as amended by the Wireless Telegraphy (Licence Charges) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/1703](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1703)) (together “the 1998 Regulations”) were made under section 1 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act [1998 (c. 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/6); “the 1998 Act”).

Following the extension of the 1998 Act to Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man respectively by the Wireless Telegraphy (Jersey) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/1512](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1512)), the Wireless Telegraphy (Guernsey) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/1511](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1511)) and the Wireless Telegraphy (Isle of Man) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/1510](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1510)), these Regulations effect the extension of the 1998 Regulations other than regulation 2(d) (which concerns fee increases for holders of certain Public Mobile Operator Licences) to the Islands.
