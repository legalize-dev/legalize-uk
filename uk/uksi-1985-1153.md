# The Ports (Finance) Act 1985 (Commencement) Order 1985

Made: 24th July 1985

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 7(2) of the Ports (Finance) Act 1985 (hereinafter called “*the Act*”) hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Ports (Finance) Act 1985 (Commencement) Order 1985.

###### 2

Sections 1, 2, 6 and 7 of the Act shall come into operation on 5th August 1985.

###### 3

Sections 3, 4 and 5 of the Act shall come into operation on 1st January 1986.

## Signed

Nicholas Ridley — Secretary of State for Transport — 24th July 1985

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings sections 1, 2, 6 and 7 of the Ports (Finance) Act 1985 into force on 5th August 1985, and the remaining sections, namely sections 3, 4 and 5 on 1st January 1986.
