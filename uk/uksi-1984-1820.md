# The European Communities (Definition of Treaties) (Change in Status of Greenland) Order 1984

Made: 22nd November 1984

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 22nd day of November 1984

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament in accordance with section 1 of the European Communities Act 1972 and has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1(3) of the European Communities Act 1972 or otherwise, in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the European Communities (Definition of Treaties) (Change in Status of Greenland) Order 1984. It shall come into operation on the date on which the Treaty specified in the Schedule to this Order enters into force for the United Kingdom. This date will be notified in the London, Edinburgh and Belfast Gazettes.

###### 2

The Treaty together with the Protocol attached to it, specified in the Schedule to this Order, is to be regarded as an EU Treaty as defined in section 1(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

## SCHEDULE

Treaty amending, with regard to Greenland, the Treaties establishing the European Communities and the Protocol attached to it, signed at Brussels on 13th March 1984 (Cmnd. 9283).

## Signed

G.I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order declares the Treaty amending, with regard to Greenland, the Treaties establishing the European Communities with the Protocol attached to it, to be a Community Treaty as defined in section 1(2) of the European Communities Act 1972. The Treaty excludes Greenland from the area of application of the Treaties establishing the European Coal and Steel Community and the European Atomic Energy Community (Cmnd. 7461 and 7462). It, together with its Protocol, amends the Treaty establishing the European Economic Community (Cmnd. 7460) by including Greenland within the scope of Articles 131 to 136 and within Annex IV of that Treaty, which relate to the association of overseas countries and territories with the Community. It repeals the existing Protocol No. 4 to the Act of Accession of 1972 (Cmnd. 7463).

The principal effect of declaring the Treaty and its Protocol to be a Community Treaty as so defined is to bring into play, in relation to it, the provisions of section 2 of the European Communities Act 1972 which provide for the implementation of treaties so specified.
