# The Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) (England) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made: 17th March 2008

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99(1), (2) and (4) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) (England) (Amendment) Order 2008.

#### Preliminary

###### 2

The Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) (England) Order 2007[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

#### Additional definition

###### 3

In article 1(2)—

- (a) “and” shall be omitted; and

- (b) for “31st March 2008.” there shall be substituted “31st March 2008; and”; and

- (c) the following definition shall be added at the end—

> > - “the transitional period”, in relation to a provision modified by this Order, means the period beginning with the second appointed day and ending on the day on which the repeal of that provision comes into force.

#### Provisions not commenced on the second appointed day

###### 4

- (1) Article 3(2) shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In subparagraph (h)—

- (a) in paragraph (i) “and (1B)” and “and (2A)” shall be omitted;

- (b) in paragraph (iv) “5,” shall be omitted;

- (c) in paragraph (vi) for “the definition of “special parking area” in section 3(1) and sections 4 to 14” there shall be substituted “sections 4, 5, 7 and 8”; and

- (d) for paragraph (vii) there shall be substituted—

> > (vii) in the London Local Authorities and Transport for London Act 2003[^f00003], section 15.

#### Transitional modifications

###### 5

The following articles shall be added after article 4—

>   (3) In section 6—
>     (a) in subsection (1) for “parking adjudicators under section 73 of the Act of 1991” there is substituted “adjudicators under regulation 17 of the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (England) General Regulations 2007”;

>     (c) for subsection (3) there is substituted—
>       (3) Regulations 12(6) and (7) (reports by adjudicators and joint committee), 19 (power to require attendance and production of documents) and 25 (recovery of amount payable under an adjudication) of the Bus Lane Contraventions (Penalty Charges, Adjudication and Enforcement) (England) Regulations 2005[^f00007] shall apply—

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No. 5 and Transitional Provisions) (England) Order 2007 (“the Principal Order”) by postponing the commencement of particular repeals, which were due to come into force on the second appointed day (31st March 2008) under that Order, and by the insertion of transitional modifications of various provisions, whose repeal has not been commenced and which currently refer to provisions due to be repealed on 31st March 2008 (called “the second appointed day” in the Principal Order).

*Article 3*inserts a definition of “transitional period” into article 1(2) of the Principal Order. *Article 4* amends article 3(2) of the Principal Order which specifies provisions that are to come into force on the second appointed day. *Article 4*defers the commencement of the repeals of sections 8(1B) and 11(2A) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984, of section 5 of the London Local Authorities Act 1995, of the definition of “special parking area” in section 3(1) of the London Local Authorities Act 2000, of sections 6 and 9 to 14 of the London Local Authorities Act 2000 and of section 14 of the London Local Authorities and Transport for London Act 2003. *Article 5*inserts into the Principal Order transitional modifications of various provisions to apply from the second appointed day until the provisions concerned are repealed.
