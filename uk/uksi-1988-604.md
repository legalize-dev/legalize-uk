# The Dutch Elm Disease (Local Authorities) (Amendment) Order 1988

Made: 23rd March 1988

Laid before Parliament: 30th March 1988

Coming into force: 1st May 1988

The Forestry Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 3(1), (2) and 5(1) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001], as read with section 20 of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Dutch Elm Disease (Local Authorities) (Amendment) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st May 1988.

#### Amendment of the Dutch Elm Disease (Local Authorities) Order 1984

###### 2

The Dutch Elm Disease (Local Authorities) Order 1984[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for the Schedule to that Order (Local Authorities with powers under the Order and the areas within which those powers may be exercised) the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Dutch Elm Disease (Local Authorities) Order 1984 by removing from the list of areas within which local authorities may exercise powers under the Order as follows—

- (a) the county of Humberside, the districts of Fylde and West Lancashire in the county of Lancashire, and those parts of the counties of Cheshire, Clwyd, Cumbria, Derbyshire, Gwynedd and North Yorkshire which were included in the 1984 Order;

- (b) the districts of Bolton, Bradford, Bury, Knowsley, Leeds, Liverpool, Manchester, Oldham, Rochdale, Rotherham, Salford, Sefton, Sheffield, St Helens, Stockport, Tameside, Trafford and Wigan;

- (c) Central Region and those parts of the region of Dumfries and Galloway which were included in the 1984 Order;

- (d) parts of the regions of Fife, Grampian, Highland, Lothian, Strathclyde and Tayside.

In the county of Lancashire, the remaining powers exercised by the county council under the 1984 Order will now be exercised by the district councils of Blackburn, Blackpool, Burnley, Chorley, Pendle and Rossendale, and by the district council of Hyndburn which was not included in the 1984 Order.
