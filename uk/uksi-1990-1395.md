# The Definition of Subsidiary (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 1990

Made: 9th July 1990

Laid before Parliament: 10th July 1990

Coming into force: 1st November 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 144(4) of the Companies Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Definition of Subsidiary (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st November 1990.

###### 2

In section 77(3) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00002], in the definition of “subsidiary”, for the words “has the same meaning as in” substitute “has the meaning given by section 736 of”.

## Signed

John Redwood — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 9th July 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations effect a minor amendment to the Electricity Act 1989, consequential on the new definition of “subsidiary” inserted by section 144 of the Companies Act 1989 into the Companies Act [1985 (c. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/6).
