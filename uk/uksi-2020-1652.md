# The Extradition (Provisional Arrest) Act 2020 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2020

Made: 30th December 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 2(4) and (5) of the Extradition (Provisional Arrest) Act 2020[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Extradition (Provisional Arrest) Act 2020 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2020.

#### Commencement

###### 2

- (1) The following provisions of the Extradition (Provisional Arrest) Act 2020 come into force on IP completion day[^f00002]—

- (a) section 1 (power of arrest for extradition purposes);

- (b) the Schedule (power of arrest for extradition purposes) except for paragraph 4.

- (2) So far as it applies to Norway and Iceland, paragraph 4 of the Schedule to that Act comes into force immediately after IP completion day.

## Signed

James Brokenshire — Minister of State — Home Office — 30th December 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations commence the Extradition (Provisional Arrest) Act [2020 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/18) (“the Act”), other than section 2 which commenced upon Royal Assent. Regulation 2 brings section 1 of the Act and its Schedule (power of arrest for extradition purposes) into force on IP completion day, save for paragraph 4 of the Schedule which is brought into force (so far as it applies to Norway and Iceland only) immediately after IP completion day.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
