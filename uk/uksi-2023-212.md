# The South Oxfordshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2023

Made: 24th February 2023

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2), (3) and (4)

The Local Government Boundary Commission for England has decided to give effect to the recommendations and makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 92(3) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the South Oxfordshire (Electoral Changes) Order 2023.

- (2) This article and article 2 come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

- (3) Article 3 comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on the day after the day on which this Order is made;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England[^f00003] in 2023.

- (4) Article 4 comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on 15th October 2024;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2025.

- (5) This Order extends to England and Wales but applies in relation to England only.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “*the Council’s Order*” means the South Oxfordshire District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2023[^f00004].

#### Alteration of a district ward boundary

###### 3

The area of the parish of Bix and Assendon that becomes part of the parish of Henley-on-Thames by virtue of the Council’s Order ceases to be part of the Woodcote and Rotherfield district ward and becomes part of the Henley-on-Thames district ward.

#### Alteration of a county electoral division boundary

###### 4

The area of the parish of Bix and Assendon that becomes part of the parish of Henley-on-Thames by virtue of the Council’s Order ceases to be part of the Sonning Common county electoral division and becomes part of the Henley-on-Thames county electoral division.

## Signed

Sealed with the seal of the Local Government Boundary Commission for England

Jolyon Jackson — Chief Executive — Local Government Boundary Commission for England — 24th February 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to recommendations made by South Oxfordshire District Council to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England for a related alteration to the boundary between two district wards, and to the boundary between two county electoral divisions, in the Council’s area.

The alterations are a consequence of changes to parish boundaries and warding arrangements under the South Oxfordshire District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2023, which was made by the Council under powers including section 86 of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act [2007 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/28). A hard copy of that Order is available for inspection at reasonable times at the offices of South Oxfordshire District Council, 135 Eastern Avenue, Milton, Abingdon OX14 4SB.

The change described in article 3 applies in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2023.

The change described in article 4 applies in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2025.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
