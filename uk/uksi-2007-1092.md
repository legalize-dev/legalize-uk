# The Equality Act 2006 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007

Made: 29th March 2007

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 93 of the Equality Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Equality Act 2006 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

The following provisions of the Equality Act 2006 shall come into force on 30th April 2007—

- (a) section 40 so far as it relates to paragraph 57 of Schedule 3;

- (b) sections 44 to 52(5);

- (c) sections 52(7) to 69;

- (d) sections 72 to 80; and

- (e) paragraph 57 of Schedule 3.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary for Communities and Local Government

Phil Woolas — Minister of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 29th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force Part 2 of the Equality Act 2006 (Discrimination on Grounds of Religion or Belief) (other than sections 52(6), 70 and 71 which are already in force) on 30th April 2007.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
