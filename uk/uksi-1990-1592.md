# The Plant Breeders' Rights (Amendment) Regulations 1990

Made: 30th July 1990

Laid before Parliament: 7th August 1990

Coming into force: 28th August 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 9(1) and (5), 36 and 38(1) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] as extended to Northern Ireland[^f00002] and the said Minister and the Secretary of State, acting jointly, as respects the Isle of Man, in exercise of the said powers as extended to the Isle of Man[^f00003], and in exercise of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Breeders' Rights (Amendment) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 28th August 1990.

#### Amendment of principal regulations

###### 2

Schedule 3 of the Plant Breeders' Rights Regulations 1978[^f00004] (reproductive and other plant material to be delivered to the controller) shall be amended as follows—

- (a) In Part III (fodder plants)—

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Plant Breeders' Rights Regulations 1978.

Schemes have been made to enable plant breeders' rights to be granted in respect of Festulolium, coriander, borage, sunflower, turnip rape, Kalanchoe, Impatiens, Euphor-bia milii and Scaevola aemula. Parts III, IV and XII of Schedule 3 to the 1978 Regulations (which Schedule was inserted into the 1978 Regulations by the Plant Breeders' Rights (Amendment) Regulations 1982 and amended by S.I. [1985/1092](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/1092)) have been amended so as to specify the reproductive and other material which must be delivered to the Controller when an application is made for a grant of plant breeders' rights in respect of varieties of those kinds (regulation 2(a) and (b)(i)).

The amendment made to sub-paragraph 3(i) of Part IV of Schedule 3 to the 1978 Regulations reduces the maximum content by number of seeds of *Rumex* ssp in varieties of swede rape including oilseed rape, white mustard and brown and black mustard from 20 to 5.
