# The Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Commencement) Regulations 2016

Made: 3rd May 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 63(2) of the Psychoactive Substances Act 2016[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 (Commencement) Regulations 2016.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The Psychoactive Substances Act 2016 comes into force on 26th May 2016 in so far as it is not already in force.

## Signed

Karen Bradley — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Home Office — 3rd May 2016
