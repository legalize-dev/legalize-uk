# The Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1991

Made: 31st October 1991

Laid before Parliament: 11th November 1991

Coming into force: 2nd December 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 20(1A) of Schedule 1 to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

–

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) (No.2) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

- (2) This Order shall extend to Scotland only.

###### 2

Paragraph 20(1) (fees for grant or renewal of bookmakers' permits, betting agency permits and betting office licences) of Schedule 1 to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 shall be amended by the substitution, for paragraphs (a) to (e), of the following paragraphs:–

> > (a) in the case of the grant of a bookmaker’s permit, £133;
> (b) in the case of the grant of a betting agency permit, £133[^f00002];
> (c) in the case of the grant of a betting office licence, £106[^f00003];
> (d) in the case of the renewal of a bookmaker’s permit or betting agency permit, £16[^f00004]; and
> (e) in the case of the renewal of a betting office licence, £20[^f00005];

###### 3

The Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1987[^f00006] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 31st October 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, increases certain fees payable to the appropriate authority under paragraph 20(1) of Schedule 1 to the Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963. The fees for the grant of a bookmaker’s permit and for the grant of a betting agency permit are increased from £100 to £133. The fee for the grant of a betting office licence is increased from £80 to £106. The fee for renewal of a bookmaker’s permit or betting agency permit is increased from £12 to £16. The fee for renewal of a betting office licence is increased from £15 to £20.
