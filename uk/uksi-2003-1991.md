# The A1 Trunk Road (Denwick Interchange Slip Roads) Order 2003

Made: 25th July 2003

Coming into force: 28th August 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the A1 Trunk Road (Denwick Interchange Slip Roads) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 28th August 2003.

- (2) In this Order, all measurements of distance are measured along the routes of the relevant highways.

###### 2

The lengths of highways described in the Schedule to this Order shall become trunk roads from the date when this Order comes into force.

###### 3

The centre line of each of the new trunk roads is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan numbered HA 10/MP/001 marked “The A1 Trunk Road (Denwick Interchange Slip Roads) Order 2003”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at the ODPM-DfT Records Management Branch, Floor 13 (IMD), Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA.

## SCHEDULE — LENGTHS OF EXISTING HIGHWAYS BECOMING TRUNK ROADS

The lengths of highways becoming trunk roads are:—

- (1) A1 northbound exit slip road from its junction with the A1 Trunk Road to its junction with the B1340 near Denwick in the County of Northumberland, a distance of 300 metres;

- (2) A1 northbound entry slip road from its junction with the A1 Trunk Road to its junction with the B1340 near Denwick in the County of Northumberland, a distance of 250 metres.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kevin Lasbury — Divisional Director Highways Agency — 25th July 2003
