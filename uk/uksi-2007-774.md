# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made: 8th March 2007

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 2007

Coming into force: 2nd April 2007

The Lord Chancellor after consulting the Lord Chief Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Part I of Schedule 11 of the Children Act 1989[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 2nd April 2007.

###### 2

The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002] is amended in accordance with articles 3 and 4.

###### 3

- (1) In the section headed “North and West Region” in Schedule 2 (care centres)—

- (a) after the entry for the local justice area of Carlisle and District, insert—

| Chester, Ellesmere Port and Neston | Chester County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (b) after the entry for the local justice area of Fylde Coast, insert—

| Halton | Warrington County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (c) after the entry for the local justice area of Liverpool, insert—

| Macclesfield | Warrington County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (d) after the entry for the local justice area of Manchester City, insert—

| Montgomeryshire | Chester County Court |
| --- | --- |

- (e) after the entry for the local justice area of St Helens, insert—

| South Cheshire | Stoke-on-Trent County Court |
| --- | --- |

; and

- (f) after the entry for the local justice area of Trafford, insert—

| Vale Royal | Chester County Court |
| --- | --- |
| Warrington | Warrington County Court |

- (2) In the section headed “Wales and Cheshire Region” in Schedule 2—

- (a) omit “and Cheshire Region” from the heading “Wales and Cheshire Region”;

- (b) omit the entries for the local justice areas of Chester, Ellesmere Port and Neston, Halton, Macclesfield, Montgomeryshire, South Cheshire, Vale Royal and Warrington; and

- (c) after the entry for the local justice area of Vale of Glamorgan, insert—

| Wrexham | Wrexham County Court |
| --- | --- |

###### 4

In Schedule 3 (adoption centres), after “Worcester County Court”, insert “Wrexham County Court”.

## Signed

Falconer of Thoroton, C. — 8th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Schedule 2 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/1677](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1677)), which allocates care centres to local justice areas grouped according to region, and Schedule 3 to that Order, which designates certain county courts as adoption centres. The amendments make Wrexham County Court a care centre and an adoption centre with effect from 2nd April 2007, make Wales a separate Region and move some of the entries to the North West Region from the Wales Region.
