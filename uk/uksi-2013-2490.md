# The Education (Designated Institutions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2013

Made: 27th September 2013

Laid before Parliament: 2nd October 2013

Coming into force: 1st November 2013

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) (England) (No. 2) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st November 2013.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to England.

#### Designation of institution

###### 2

The National Film and Television School, being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within section 129(2) of the Education Reform Act 1988, is designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

## Signed

David Willetts — Minister of State for Universities and Science — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 27th September 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, made under section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988, designates the National Film and Television School as eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

An impact assessment has not been produced for the Order because it has no impact on businesses, civil society organisations or the public sector. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
