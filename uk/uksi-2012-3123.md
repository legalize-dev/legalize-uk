# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) (No. 2) Order 2012

Made: 17th December 2012

Laid before Parliament: 18th December 2012

Coming into force: 9th January 2013

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) (No. 2) Order 2012.

#### Commencement of variation

###### 2

The variation of the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 made by the Secretary of State on 17th December 2012, whereby paragraph (1)(a) of article 46 was varied by substituting “6.20p” for “6.05p”, comes into force on 9th January 2013.

## Signed

Ed Vaizey — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 17th December 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 9th January 2013 a variation to the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 made by the Secretary of State on 17th December 2012.

The Public Lending Right Scheme provides for payments to be made to authors based on the number of times their books are lent out by public libraries. The effect of the variation is to increase the rate per loan as set out in article 46(1)(a) of the Scheme from 6.05p to 6.20p. The rate per loan is the sum attributable to each qualifying loan for the purpose of calculating the amount payable in respect of loans of a particular book by public libraries.
