# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2016

Made: 9th August 2016

Laid before Parliament: 10th August 2016

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the environmental aspects of product design and makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

- (2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on 1st September 2016.

- (3) Regulation 2(3) comes into force on 1st January 2018.

#### Amendments to the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010

###### 2

- (1) The table at paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (declaration of conformity) to the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) After item 24 insert—

| 25 | A professional refrigerated storage cabinet, blast cabinet, condensing unit or process chiller | [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/1095](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/1095) implementing [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for professional refrigerated storage cabinets, blast cabinets, condensing units and process chillers[^f00004] |
| --- | --- | --- |

- (3) After item 25 (as inserted by paragraph (2)) insert—

| 26 | A local space heater | [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2015/1188](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2015/1188) implementing [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for local space heaters[^f00005] |
| --- | --- | --- |

#### Amendment of the Energy Information Regulations 2011

###### 3

- (1) The table at Schedule 1 (EU measures) to the Energy Information Regulations 2011[^f00006] is amended as follows.

- (2) After the entry relating to residential ventilation units insert—

| Professional refrigerated storage cabinets | Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/1094 supplementing [Directive 2010/30/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0030) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to the energy labelling of professional refrigerated storage cabinets[^f00007] | 1st September 2016 except for the second indent of Article 3(2) of the Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/1094 which applies from 1st July 2019 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solid fuel boilers and packages of a solid fuel boiler, supplementary heaters, temperature controls and solar devices | Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/1187 supplementing [Directive 2010/30/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0030) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to energy labelling of solid fuel boilers and packages of a solid fuel boiler, supplementary heaters, temperature controls and solar devices[^f00008] | The dates set out in Articles 3 and 8 of the Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/1187 |
| Local space heaters | Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/1186 supplementing [Directive 2010/30/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2010/0030) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to the energy labelling of local space heaters[^f00009] | The dates set out in Article 8 of the Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/1186 |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2617](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2617)) (“the 2010 Regulations”) and the Energy Information Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1524](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1524)) (“the 2011 Regulations”) and partially implement Commission Regulations (EU) Nos [2015/1095](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1095) and [2015/1188](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1188), and Commission Delegated Regulations (EU) Nos 2015/1094, 2015/1187 and [2015/1186](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1186) as follows—

- (a) regulation 2 adds two new entries to the table in paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (declaration of conformity) to the 2010 Regulations—

- (i) from 1st September 2016, for professional refrigerated storage cabinets, blast cabinets, condensing units and process chillers;

- (ii) from 1st January 2018, for local space heaters; and

- (b) regulation 3 adds three new entries to the table in Schedule 1 (EU measures) to the 2011 Regulations to apply from the dates referred to in regulation 3 for—

- (i) professional refrigerated storage cabinets;

- (ii) solid fuel boilers and packages of a solid fuel boiler, supplementary heaters, temperature controls and solar devices;

- (iii) local space heaters.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
