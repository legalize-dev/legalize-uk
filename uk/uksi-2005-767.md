# The Higher Education Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005

Made: 16th March 2005

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 52(2) of the Higher Education Act 2004[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Higher Education Act 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2005.

- (2) In this Order “the 2004 Act” means the Higher Education Act 2004.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2004 Act shall come into force on 1 April 2005:

- Section 2;

- Section 49 to the extent it relates to the provision of Schedule 6 specified below;

- Paragraph 3 of Schedule 6;

- Section 50 to the extent it relates to the provision of Schedule 7 specified below; and

- In Schedule 7 —

- the repeal in Schedule 1 of the Superannuation Act 1972 of the entry relating to the Arts and Humanities Research Board.

## Signed

Kim Howells — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 16th March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the third Commencement Order made under the Higher Education Act 2004 and brings into force certain provisions of that Act on 1 April 2005.

Article 2 commences provisions concerning the transfer of property, rights and liabilities from the Arts and Humanities Research Board to the Arts and Humanities Research Council.
