# The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2004

Made: 5th July 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 162(1) and (6) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2004.

###### 2

Section 1(3) and (4) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 shall come into force on 6th July 2004.

###### 3

Section 2 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 shall come into force on 28th July 2004.

## Signed

Des Browne — Minister of State — 5th July 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 1(3) and (4) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 on 6th July 2004, and section 2 of that Act on 28th July 2004.
