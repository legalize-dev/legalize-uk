# The Animal Health and Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: at 12.30 a.m. on 5th September 2019

Laid before Parliament: at 4.00 p.m. on 5th September 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft of the instrument being laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — Introduction

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Health and Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019*.*

- (2) This regulation and regulation 4 come into force immediately before Part 4 of the Animal Health, Invasive Alien Species, Plant Breeders’ Rights and Seeds (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00002] comes into force.

- (3) The remaining regulations come into force immediately before exit day.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular in section 8(2)(a), (b), (d) and (g)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

Part 2 amends legislation concerning animal health required as a result of recent amendments made to EU legislation. It amends retained direct EU legislation and secondary legislation.

Part 3 amends the Genetically Modified Organisms (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/90)) to correct some minor inconsistencies of terminology.

An impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
