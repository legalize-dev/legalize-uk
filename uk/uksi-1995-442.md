# The Gaming Licence Duty (Games) Order 1995

Made: 23rd February 1995

Laid before the House of Commons: 24th February 1995

Coming into force: 1st April 1995

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 13(4) of the Betting and Gaming Duties Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gaming Licence Duty (Games) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

###### 2

Super pan 9 and casino stud poker shall be added to the games mentioned in subsection (3) of section 13 of the Betting and Gaming Duties Act 1981 and, accordingly, that section shall apply to them.

## Signed

Derek Conway — Timothy Kirkhope — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 23rd February 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 1st April 1995, adds two new casino games to the games mentioned in section 13(3) of the Betting and Gaming Duties Act [1981(c. 63)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/63).
