# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2009

Made

Coming into force: 10th December 2009

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th day of December 2009Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty in pursuance of section 19(2) of the Education Act 2005 and in accordance with that recommendation is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 10th December 2009.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru with effect from 10th December 2009.

## SCHEDULE

- Jacqueline Anne Gapper

- Richard James Hawkley

- Stephen John Lamb

- Sarah Jane Morgan

- Iwan Gwynfa Roberts

- Suzanne Margaret Halliwell

- Sarah Elaine Lewis

- Catherine Elizabeth Maitland Evans

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council
