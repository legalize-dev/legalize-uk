# The Administration of Justice Act 1985 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1991

Made: 28th November 1991

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 69(2) of the Administration of Justice Act 1985[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Administration of Justice Act 1985 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1991.

###### 2

Sections 9 and 10 of the Administration of Justice Act 1985 shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 28th November 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 9 and 10 of the Administration of Justice Act 1985 (incorporated practices). Section 9 allows the Council of the Law Society to make rules regulating the carrying on of a solicitor’s practice through bodies corporate, brings into force the provisions of Schedule 2 to the Act (“Incorporated Practices: Supplemental Provisions”) and empowers the Lord Chancellor to apply provisions relating to Solicitors to such bodies corporate (with or without modification). Section 10 establishes an offence of pretending to be a body recognised under section 9.
