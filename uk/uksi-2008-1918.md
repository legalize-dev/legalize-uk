# The Road Safety Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2008

Made: 17th July 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61 of the Road Safety Act 2006 [^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Safety Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2008.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

The following provisions of the Road Safety Act 2006 shall come into force on 18th August 2008—

- (a) section 20;

- (b) section 21; and

- (c) section 30, to the extent not already commenced.

## Signed

Maria Eagle — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 17th July 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force from 18th August 2008 the following provisions of the Road Safety Act 2006.

Section 20, which inserts a new section 2B into the Road Traffic Act 1988 to create an offence of causing death by driving without due care and attention, or without reasonable consideration for other persons. Provision is also made as to penalties, alternative verdicts and other consequential matters.

Section 21, which inserts a new section 3ZB into the Road Traffic Act 1988 to create an offence of causing death by driving when unlicensed, disqualified or uninsured. Provision is also made as to penalties and other consequential matters.

Section 30 (which has previously been commenced in part) which inserts a new section 3ZA into the Road Traffic Act 1988 to explain when a person is to be regarded as driving without due care and attention, or without reasonable consideration for other persons.
