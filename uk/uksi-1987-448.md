## SCHEDULE

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or Departments* | *Matters in relation to which the Ministers or departments are designated* |
| The Secretary of State | Measures in respect of the marketing of, and technical requirements relating to, construction plant and protective structures for construction plant. |
| Any Northern Ireland department | The preceding item in this column. |
| Any Northern Ireland department | Measures in respect of the conditions of, and the recognition of qualifications for, access to and pursuit of activities in the field of pharmacy. |
| The Secretary of State | The conferment and protection of exclusive rights in the topographies of semiconductor products. |
| The Secretary of State | The provision of guarantees, grants and loans in respect of the financing of the construction of ships and mobile offshore installations and their equipment. |
