# The Crime and Policing Act 2026 (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 23rd November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 255(1) and (5) of the Crime and Policing Act 2026[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (Commencement No. 5) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

#### Amendment of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2026

###### 2

- (1) The Crime and Policing Act 2026 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2026[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) For regulation 3(2)(a) and (b) (provision coming into force on 25th November 2026), substitute—

> > (a) civil proceedings as referred to in [section 31](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/31)[(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/31) of the [Domestic Abuse Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents)[^f00003],
> (b) domestic abuse protection order proceedings in the family court under [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/part/3) of the [Domestic Abuse Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents), and
> (c) family proceedings as referred to in [section 31](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/31)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/31) of the [Domestic Abuse Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents).

## Signed

Sarah Jones — Minister of State — Home Office — 23rd November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the [Crime and Policing Act 2026 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/contents) ([S.I. 2026/1158](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158)) (“*the No. 5 Regulations*”).

[The No. 5 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/contents) bring into force a range of provisions in the [Crime and Policing Act 2026 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/20/contents) on specified dates.

[Regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/regulation/3) of [the No. 5 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/contents) brings into force provisions on 25th November 2026 about domestic abuse protection orders. [Regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1230/regulation/2/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1230/regulation/2/2/made) of these Regulations is a correction and substitutes [regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/regulation/3)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/regulation/3)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/regulation/3) and [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/regulation/3) of [the No. 5 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1158/contents) to clarify the definitions of ‘civil proceedings’ and ‘family proceedings’ and to include reference to domestic abuse protection order proceedings in the family court (under Part 3 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. Impact assessments were carried out with regard to the broader package of measures in the [Crime and Policing Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/20/contents), to which these amending regulations relate. Copies can be obtained from the Home Office at 2 Marsham Street, London SW1 4DF, and on the GOV.UK website.
