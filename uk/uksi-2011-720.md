# The Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2011

Made: 9th March 2011

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 211(2) of the Legal Services Act 2007[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Legal Services Act 2007 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2011.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

The following provisions of the Legal Services Act 2007, to the extent that they are not already in force, come into force on 1st April 2011—

- (a) sections 175(2)(e) and 186;

- (b) in Schedule 4 (approved regulators), paragraph 19(2)(f); and

- (c) Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule 18 (immigration advice and immigration services).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

J Djanogly — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 9th March 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the tenth commencement order made under the Legal Services Act [2007 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29) (“the 2007 Act”).

Article 2 commences provisions of the 2007 Act connected with the provision of immigration advice and services. In particular, article 2 commences Part 1 of Schedule 18 which provides for persons to be authorised by designated qualifying regulators to provide immigration advice and services and Part 2 which makes amendments to the Immigration and Asylum Act [1999 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/33) in connection with the provision of those services.
