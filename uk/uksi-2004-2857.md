# The Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004

Made: 29th October 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 29(1) of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st December 2004.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 2004 Act” means the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004;

- “the Authority” means the Gangmasters Licensing Authority.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2004 Act are in force—

- (a) section 1 (establishing the Authority);

- (b) section 2 (directions given to the Authority by the Secretary of State);

- (c) section 3 (work to which the 2004 Act applies);

- (d) section 4 (defining what is meant by “acting as a gangmaster”);

- (e) section 5 (defining the territorial scope of application of the 2004 Act);

- (f) section 6(2) (power to make regulations specifying when a licence is not required);

- (g) section 10 (power to make regulations establishing an appeals process);

- (h) section 25 (general provisions regarding regulations, rules and orders);

- (i) section 26 (meaning of “worker”);

- (j) section 28 (application of the 2004 Act to Northern Ireland) in so far as it relates to paragraphs 1 to 8, 11, 19 and 21 of Schedule 2;

- (k) section 30 (short title and extent);

- (l) Schedule 1 (consequential amendments); and

- (m) paragraphs 1 to 8, 11, 19 and 21 of Schedule 2.

## Signed

Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 29th October 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 specified in article 2 of the Order.
