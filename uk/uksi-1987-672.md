# The Cable and Broadcasting Act 1984 (Commencement No.3) Order 1987

Made: 6th April 1987

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 59(4) of the Cable and Broadcasting Act 1984[^f00001]. I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cable and Broadcasting Act 1984 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1987.

###### 2

Sections 45 and 46 of the Cable and Broadcasting Act 1984 shall come into force on 6th April 1987.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 6th April 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6th April 1987 sections 45 and 46 of the Cable and Broadcasting Act 1984. Section 45 amends section 2 of the Broadcasting Act [1981 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/68) so as to substitute, for 31st December 1996, 31st December 2001, as the date up to which the Independent Broadcasting Authority (IBA) has the function of providing television and local sound broadcasting services, and to remove the restriction on the date beyond which the Secretary of State may extend the duration of the IBA’s function by order. Section 46 relieves the IBA of the duty to invite applications before entering into certain contracts.
