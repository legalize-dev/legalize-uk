# The Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct and Appeals Tribunals) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made: 29th October 2018

Laid before Parliament: 31st October 2018

Coming into force: 1st December 2018

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3A(1) to (3), 4(1) to (5) and 4A(1) to (4) and (7) of the Ministry of Defence Police Act 1987 , makes the following Regulations.

## PART 1 — General

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct and Appeals Tribunals) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 1st December 2018.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “*the 1987 Act*” means the Ministry of Defence Police Act 1987;

- “*the 2009 Regulations*” means the Ministry of Defence Police Appeals Tribunals Regulations 2009 ;

- “*the Conduct Regulations*” means the Ministry of Defence Police (Conduct etc.) Regulations 2015 .
