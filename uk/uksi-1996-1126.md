# The Jobseekers Act 1995 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996

Made: 18th April 1996

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 41(2) and (3) of the Jobseekers Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Jobseekers Act 1995 (Commencement No.2) Order 1996.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

22nd April 1996 is the day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) paragraphs 38, 39, 40, 44, 45, 47, 52, 67, 68, 69, 70, 73, 75 and 76 of Schedule 2 to the Jobseekers Act 1995 (which contain amendments to the Administration Act and the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00002]); and

- (b) section 41(4) of that Act in so far as it relates to those paragraphs.

#### Amendment of Commencement Order No.1

###### 3

In article 2(g) of the Jobseekers Act 1995 (Commencement No.1) Order 1995[^f00003], for the words “section 41(5)” there shall be substituted the words “section 41(4)”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Roger Evans — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — 18th April 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This, the second Order commencing provisions of the Jobseekers Act [1995 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/18), provides for the coming into force on 22nd April 1996 of certain provisions in Schedule 2 which introduce into the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5) and the Local Government Finance Act [1992 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/14) references to the Jobseekers Act 1995.
