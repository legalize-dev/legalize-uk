# The M40 (London–Oxford) Motorway (Stokenchurch to Waterstock Crossroads Section) Variation Scheme 1989

Made: 15th March 1989

Coming into force: 5th April 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by section 16 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

The M40 (London–Oxford) Motorway (Stokenchurch to Waterstock Crossroads Section) Scheme 1970[^f00003] is hereby varied by deleting therefrom that part of the route of the special road described in Schedule 1 thereto which extends for a distance of 242 yards eastwards from a point on the London-Oxford-Fishguard Trunk Road (A40) 25 yards east of Waterstock Lane at Great Milton in Oxfordshire.

###### 2

This Scheme shall come into force on the 5th April 1989 and may be cited as the M40 (London–Oxford) Motorway (Stokenchurch to Waterstock Crossroads Section) Variation Scheme 1989.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

D. Gruffydd Jones — Regional Director South East Region — Department of Transport — 15th March 1989
