# The Traffic Management (The Borough Council of Bolton) Permit Scheme Order 2013

Made: 28th March 2013

Coming into force: 29th April 2013

The Secretary of State for Transport having approved the Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme under section 34(2) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(4) and (5) and 39(2) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management (The Borough Council of Bolton) Permit Scheme Order 2013 and comes into force on 29th April 2013.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme ” means the permit scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order in terms commonly known as the “Greater Manchester Permit Scheme” which was prepared and submitted to the Secretary of State by The Borough Council of Bolton and has been approved by the Secretary of State; and

- “specified streets” has the meaning given by regulation 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007[^f00002].

#### Commencement of Permit Scheme

###### 3

The Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme comes into effect on 29th April 2013.

#### Application of Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007

###### 4

Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 shall apply to the specified streets within the Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to the Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme.

The Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme is designed to control the carrying out of works in specified streets in Bolton by imposing an obligation to obtain a permit from The Borough Council of Bolton before undertaking the works.

*Article 3* gives effect to the permit scheme, which will come into effect on 29th April 2013. The permit scheme referred to in this article is set out in the Schedule to the Order.

*Article 4* applies Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 (which makes provision for the disapplication and modification of enactments in relation to specified works in specified streets) to the specified works and specified streets within the Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme.

A copy of the Borough Council of Bolton Permit Scheme is deposited and can be inspected at the offices of The Borough Council of Bolton, Town Hall, Victoria Square, Bolton, BL1 1RU and is available to inspect on the Council’s website http://www.bolton.gov.uk.
