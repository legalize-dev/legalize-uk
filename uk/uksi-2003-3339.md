# The Watford and South of St Albans–Redbourn–Kidney Wood, Luton (Special Road Scheme 1957) (Park Street to Beechtrees Partial Revocation) Scheme 2004

Made: 11th December 2003

Coming into force: 8th January 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by sections 16 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

This scheme may be cited as the Watford and South of St Albans-Redbourn-Kidney Wood, Luton (Special Road Scheme 1957) (Park Street to Beechtrees Partial Revocation) Scheme 2004 and shall come into force on 8 January 2004.

###### 2

The Watford and South of St Albans-Redbourn-Kidney Wood, Luton, Special Road Scheme 1957[^f00003] is hereby partially revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David W. Ward — A Divisional Director Highways Agency — 11th December 2003
