# The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation Order 1999

Made: 25th January 1999

Coming into force: 5th February 1999

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 6 and 124(1)(d) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation Order 1999 and shall come into force on 5th February 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1993[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In Schedule 2–

- (a) for paragraph 7 substitute the following paragraph–

> > (7) Between a point level with the party wall of Nos. 337 and 335 Archway Road and a point 52 metres north-west of the north-western kerbline of Jacksons Lane.

###### 4

In Schedule 4–

- (a) in paragraph 6, for the words “90 metres” substitute the words “110 metres”.

- (b) for paragraph 7 substitute the following paragraph–

> > (7) Between a point 25 metres south-east of the party wall of Nos. 290 and 292 Archway Road and a point level with the party wall of Nos. 296 and 298 Archway Road.

- (c) for paragraph 9 substitute the following paragraph–

> > (9) Between a point level with the common boundary of No. 154 Archway Road and the electricity substation and a point 12.5 metres north-west of the common boundary of Nos. 136 and 138 Archway Road.

- (d) for paragraph 13 substitute the following paragraph–

> > (13) Between a point 52 metres north-west of the north-western kerbline of Jacksons Lane and a point 5 metres south-east of a point level with the party wall of Nos. 255 and 257 Archway Road.

- (e) delete paragraph 14.

- (f) for paragraph 15 substitute the following paragraph–

> > (15) Between a point level with the south-eastern boundary of No. 203 Archway Road and a point level with the common boundary of Nos. 209 and 211 Archway Road.

- (g) delete paragraph 16.

- (h) for paragraph 17 substitute the following paragraph–

> > (17) Between a point opposite the party wall of Nos. 164 and 166 Archway Road and the borough boundary with the London Borough of Islington.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 25th January 1999
