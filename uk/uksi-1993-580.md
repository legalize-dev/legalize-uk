# The Stirling and Clackmannan Districts (Blackgrange and Blairlogie House) Boundaries Amendment Order 1993

Made: 8th March 1993

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 1993

Coming into force: 1st April 1993

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 17(2) of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order to give effect to the proposals of the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland as submitted to him:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Stirling and Clackmannan Districts (Blackgrange and Blairlogie House) Boundaries Amendment Order 1993, and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order:—

- “the 1977 Regulations” means the Local Government Area Changes (Scotland) Regula tions 1977[^f00002]; and

- “boundary map” means the map prepared by The Scottish Office marked “The Stirling and Clackmannan Districts (Blackgrange and Blairlogie House) Boundaries Amend ment Order 1993” and deposited in accordance with regulation 3 of the 1977 Regulations;

and, in the Schedule to this Order, any reference to a district boundary is a reference to such a boundary as it exists immediately before the commencement of this Order.

#### Application of the 1977 Regulations

###### 3

Save insofar as they can have no application in relation to the area changes effected by this Order, the provisions of the 1977 Regulations shall apply in relation thereto for the purposes of, or in consequence of, this Order.

#### Area changes

###### 4

- (1) That part of Stirling District in Central Region at Blairlogie House, being the Area A described in Part I of the Schedule to this Order and shown delineated, hatched and marked area “A” on the boundary map, shall be transferred to Clackmannan District in Central Region.

- (2) Those parts of Stirling District in Central Region at the A907 road, being the Areas B and C described in Part II of the Schedule to this Order and shown delineated, hatched and marked respectively areas “B” and “C” on the boundary map, shall both be transferred to Clackmannan District in Central Region.

#### Consequential changes in electoral arrangements

###### 5

- (1) Area A described in the Schedule to this Order (being the Area A transferred by article 4(1) of this Order)—

- (a) shall cease to form part of electoral division 15 (Airthrey) of Central Region[^f00003] and of ward 18 (Logie) of Stirling District[^f00004]; and

- (b) shall form part of electoral division 6 (Ochil) of Central Region and ward 12 (Menstrie) of Clackmannan District[^f00005].

- (2) Each of Areas B and C described in the Schedule to this Order (being the Areas B and C transferred by article 4(2) of this Order)—

- (a) shall cease to form part of electoral division 15 (Airthrey) of Central Region and ward 18 (Logie) of Stirling District; and

- (b) shall form part of electoral division 3 (Tullibody) of Central Region and ward 5 (Baingle) of Clackmannan District.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers from Stirling to Clackmannan District both in Central Region on 1st April 1993 three areas amounting in total to approximately 2.2 hectares and makes consequential changes to electoral arrangements.

The map annexed to and forming part of this note gives a general guide to the areas affected by this Order.

Copies of the detailed boundary map defined in article 2 of this Order may be inspected at the offices of any of the local authorities referred to in this Order, and may be inspected at or purchased from the Scottish Office Environment Department’s Map Library, New St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh, EH1 3TG.
