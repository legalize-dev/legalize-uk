# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No.2) Order 2006

Made: 19th July 2006

Coming into force: 4th September 2006

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of July 2006 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred on Her by section 1(2) of the Education Act 2005[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No.2) Order 2006 and comes into force on 4th September 2006.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 4th September 2006.
