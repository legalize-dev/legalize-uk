## SCHEDULE — List of measures for which the Civil Aviation Authority is the national supervisory authority

- Regulation [(EC) No 549/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0549) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 10 March 2004 laying down the framework for the creation of the single European sky (the framework Regulation)

- Regulation [(EC) No 550/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0550) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 10 March 2004 on the provision of air navigation services in the single European sky (the service provision Regulation)[^f00005]

- Regulation [(EC) No 551/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0551) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 10 March 2004 on the organisation and use of the airspace in the single European sky (the airspace Regulation)[^f00006]

- Regulation [(EC) No 552/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0552) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 10 March 2004 on the interoperability of the European Air Traffic Management network (the interoperability Regulation)[^f00007]
