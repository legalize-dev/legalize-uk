# The Unitary Development Plans (West Midlands) (Appointed Day) Order 1988

Made: 1st February 1988

Laid before Parliament: 8th February 1988

Coming into force: 29th February 1988

The Secretary of State for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(1) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Unitary Development Plans (West Midlands) (Appointed Day) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 29th February 1988.

###### 2

29th February 1988 is appointed as the day on which Part I of Schedule 1 to the Local Government Act 1985 shall come into force in the areas of the local planning authorities listed in the Schedule hereto.

## SCHEDULE

- Birmingham City Council

- Coventry City Council

- Sandwell Metropolitan Borough Council

- Solihull Metropolitan Borough Council

- Walsall Metropolitan Borough Council

- Wolverhampton Metropolitan Borough Council

## Signed

Nicholas Ridley — Secretary of State for the Environment — 1st February 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Government Act 1985 abolished the Greater London Council and metropolitan county councils. The Act consequently provides for the replacement in their areas of the existing structure and local development plans under town and country planning legislation by“unitary development plans”. This Order brings the provisions of the 1985 Act relating to unitary development plans into force in the county of the West Midlands other than the metropolitan district of Dudley on 29th February 1988.
