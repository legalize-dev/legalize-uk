# The Greater Manchester Passenger Transport Authority (Increase in Number of Members) Order 1995

Made: 13th June 1995

Laid before Parliament: 21st June 1995

Coming into force: 19th July 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, after consultation with the constituent councils which appoint members of the Greater Manchester Passenger Transport Authority and having regard to the number of local government electors in the areas of those councils respectively, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 29(2) and 103(1) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Greater Manchester Passenger Transport Authority (Increase in Number of Members) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 19th July 1995.

#### Increase in number of members of the authority

###### 2

The number of members of the Greater Manchester Passenger Transport Authority specified in the fourth column of Part II of Schedule 10 to the Local Government Act 1985 shall be altered from “2” to “3” in respect of Rochdale, from “3” to “4” in respect of Stockport and from “3” to “4” in respect of Wigan.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Watts — Minister of State, — Department of Transport — 13th June 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order alters the composition of the Greater Manchester Passenger Transport Authority. From 19th July 1995, the number of members appointed by the Metropolitan District Councils of Rochdale, Stockport and Wigan is to be increased from 2 to 3 in the case of Rochdale and from 3 to 4 in the cases of Stockport and Wigan.
