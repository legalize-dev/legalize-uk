# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Avon Valley) Designation (Amendment) Order 1997

Made: 5th June 1997

Laid before Parliament: 10th June 1997

Coming into force: 1st July 1997

Whereas, pursuant to section 18(1) of the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food has by order designated an area in the Avon Valley as an environmentally sensitive area;

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Avon Valley) Designation (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st July 1997.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Avon Valley) Designation Order 1993[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 3

The principal Order shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

#### Rates of payment under agreement

###### 4

In article 6 of the principal Order—

- (a) in paragraph (1) for the amount “£105” there shall be substituted the amount “£130”.

- (b) in paragraph (2), for amount “£260” there shall be substituted the amount “£265”.

#### Amendment to Schedule

###### 5

In Schedule 4 (conservation plan operations) to the principal Order—

- (a) for paragraph 3, there shall be substituted the following two paragraphs:

> > (3) The creation or restoration of ponds.
> (3A) The restoration of ditches.

- (b) after paragraph 7, there shall be inserted the following paragraph:

> > (8) The reintroduction of pollarding management for neglect trees.

#### Saving

###### 6

The amendments made to the principal Order by article 4 of this Order shall not apply in respect of any annual payment to a farmer which relates wholly or in part to any period before 1st April 1997.

## Signed

Elliot Morley — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 4th June 1997

We consent

Bob Ainsworth — Jon Owen Jones — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 5th June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends, with effect from 1st July 1997, the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Avon Valley) Designation Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/84](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/84), amended by S.I. [1994/927](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/927), [1995/197](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/197), [1996/3104](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3104)), which designated an area in the Avon Valley as an environmentally sensitive area.

Subject to a saving provision, the Order amends the rates of payment to be made by the minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 (article 4).

Amendments are also made to add further options to the operations that may be included in a conservation plan (article 5).
