# The A57 Link Roads Development Consent (Correction) Order 2024

Made: 1st February 2024

Coming into force: 2nd February 2024

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A57 Link Roads Development Consent (Correction) Order 2024 and comes into force on 2nd February 2024.

#### Corrections to the A57 Link Roads Development Consent Order 2022

###### 2

- (1) The A57 Link Roads Development Consent Order 2022 is corrected as follows.

- (2) Article 15 (permanent stopping up and restriction of use of highways, streets and private means of access) of the 2022 Order is corrected as follows.

- (3) In paragraph (1), before the first reference to “streets and private means of access” insert “highways,”.

- (4) In paragraphs (2) and (3), before “street and private means of access” in each of the six places the expression occurs, insert “highway,”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Natasha Kopala — Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit — Department for Transport — 1st February 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the A57 Link Roads Development Consent Order 2022, a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following a request under paragraph 1(5) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
