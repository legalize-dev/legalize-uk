# The Road Traffic Offenders (Prescribed Devices) (No. 2) Order 1992

Made: 12th November 1992

Laid before Parliament: 20th November 1992

Coming into force: 1st January 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 20(9) of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Offenders (Prescribed Devices) (No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

###### 2

A device designed or adapted for recording by photographic or other image recording means the position of motor vehicles in relation to light signals is a prescribed device for the purposes of section 20 of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988.

## Signed

Home Office

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 12th November 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes devices which record the position of vehicles in relation to light signals by photograph or similar means for the purposes of section 20 of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988, as substituted by section 23 of the Road Traffic Act 1991.
