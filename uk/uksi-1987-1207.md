# The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1987

Made: 11th July 1987

Coming into force: 11th July 1987

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1987 and shall come into force forthwith.

###### 2

The interim period specified by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 shall continue until 16th July 1988.

## Signed

Tom King — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Northern Ireland Office — 11th July 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends until 16th July 1988 the period specified in section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (as extended by the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1047](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1047))) for the operation of the temporary provisions for the government of Northern Ireland contained in Schedule 1 to that Act.
