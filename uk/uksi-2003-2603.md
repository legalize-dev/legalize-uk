# The Freedom of Information Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2003

Made: 8th October 2003

The Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 87(3) of the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2003[^f00002].

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Freedom of Information Act 2000; and

- “the publication scheme provisions” mean section 19(1) to 19(4) of the Act, insofar as it is not already in force[^f00003].

#### Commencement on 31st October 2003

###### 2

The publication scheme provisions shall come into force on 31st October 2003 for the following public authorities—

- (a) the public authorities listed in Part III of Schedule 1 to the Act;

- (b) the Distinction and Meritorious Service Awards Committee[^f00004];

- (c) Invest Northern Ireland[^f00005]; and

- (d) The Northern Ireland Council for Postgraduate Medical and Dental Education[^f00006].

#### Commencement on 29th February 2004

###### 3

The publication scheme provisions shall come into force on 29th February 2004 for the following public authorities—

- (a) the public authorities listed in Part IV of Schedule 1 to the Act, except for those specified in paragraph 52(b)[^f00007];

- (b) the public authorities listed in Schedule 1 to this Order; and

- (c) any publicly-owned company as defined in section 6 of the Act.

#### Commencement on 30th June 2004

###### 4

- (1) The publication scheme provisions shall come into force on 30th June 2004 for all public authorities in respect of which those provisions have not been commenced elsewhere, with the exception of—

- (a) that public authority listed in paragraph 17 of Schedule 1 to the Act[^f00008]; and

- (b) the consultative Civic Forum referred to in section 56(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00009].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force certain provisions (“the publication scheme provisions”) in section 19 of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (“the Act”) for certain public authorities. An earlier commencement order (S.I. [2002/2812](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2812) (c. 86)) commenced the publication scheme provisions for other public authorities.

Section 19(1) to (4) of the Act places obligations on public authorities in relation to publication schemes. Articles 2, 3 and 4 of this Order bring the publication scheme provisions into force on 30th October 2003, 29th February 2004 and 30th June 2004 respectively, for the public authorities specified in those Articles. All public authorities will be subject to the publication scheme provisions by 30th June 2004, with the exception of that listed in paragraph 17 of Schedule 1 to the Act and the consultative Civic Forum referred to in section 56(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998.
