# The Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made: 14th July2004

Laid before Parliament: 6th August 2004

Coming into force: 30th August 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 30th August 2004.

#### Amendment

###### 2

- (1) The Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) Regulations 2001[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3(1)(a) substitute “£121” for “£107”.

- (3) In regulation 3(1)(b) substitute “£30” for “£27”.

- (4) In regulation 3(3)(a) substitute “£7” for “£6”.

- (5) In regulation 3(3)(b) substitute “£12.50” for “£11”.

- (6) In regulation 3(3)(c) substitute “£46” for “£41”.

- (7) In regulation 3(4) substitute “£16” for “£14”.

- (8) In regulation 3(5) substitute “£7” for “£6”.

## Signed

David Jamieson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 6th July 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase some of the fees that are payable under the Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) Regulations 2001.

The changes are as follows:

|  | OLD FEE | NEW FEE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ECMT licence for a period of one year | £107 | £121 |
| ECMT licence for a period of less than one year (fee per period of 3 months or less) | £27 | £30 |
| Journey permit for a single journey | £6 | £7 |
| Journey permit for 4 journeys to Turkey | £11 | £12.50 |
| Journey permit for 15 journeys to Morocco | £41 | £46 |
| Removals authorisation | £14 | £16 |
| Swiss permit | £6 | £7 |

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from the Licensing, Roadworthiness and Insurance Division of the Department for Transport, Zone 5/20, Southside, 105 Victoria Street, London SW1P 4DR.
