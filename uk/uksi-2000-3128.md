# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2000

Made: 25th November 2000

Laid before Parliament: 27th November 2000

Coming into force: 28th November 2000

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, being designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 28th November 2000.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000

###### 2

In Schedule 3 Part I, paragraph 1, to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000 after the words “Commission Decision [2000/651/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0651) (OJ No. L272, 25.10.00, p. 46)" there shall be added the following—

> > and Commission Decision [2000/705/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0705) (OJ No. L288, 15.11.00, p. 30)

## Signed

Hayman — Minister of State (Lords) Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 2000-11-25
