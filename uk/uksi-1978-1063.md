# The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1978

Made: 25th July 1978

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 9(3) of the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 (Commencement) Order 1978.

###### 2

The Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978 shall come into force on 21st August 1978.

## Signed

Merlyn Rees — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 25th July 1978
