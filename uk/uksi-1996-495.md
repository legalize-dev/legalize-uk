# The Health Authorities (Wales) (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made: 29th February 1996

Laid before Parliament: 5th March 1996

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(4) and (5) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 2 to the Health Authorities Act 1995[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Health Authorities (Wales) (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 1995 Act” means the Health Authorities Act 1995;

- “new authority” means a Health Authority in Wales established under the 1995 Act[^f00003];

- “old authority” means a District Health Authority in Wales which ceases to exist as a result of the 1995 Act.

#### Transfer of trust property from old to new authorities

###### 2

Trust property[^f00004] held by an old authority named in column 1 of the following Table immediately before 1st April 1996 shall transfer on that date to the new authority specified opposite in column 2 except to the extent that any of that trust property comprises property of the type mentioned in paragraph 4(3) of Schedule 2 to the 1995 Act—

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| *Old Authority* | *New Authority* |
| Clwyd District Health Authority | North Wales Health Authority |
| Dyfed District Health Authority | Dyfed Powys Health Authority |
| Gwent District Health Authority | Gwent Health Authority |
| Gwynedd District Health Authority | North Wales Health Authority |
| Mid Glamorgan District Health Authority | Bro Taf Health Authority |
| Powys District Health Authority | Dyfed Powys Health Authority |
| South Glamorgan District Health Authority | Bro Taf Health Authority |
| West Glamorgan District Health Authority | Morgannwg Health Authority |

## Signed

William Hague — Secretary of State for Wales — 29th February 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

On 1st April 1996, as a result of the Health Authorities Act 1995, Welsh District Health Authorities will cease to exist. Health Authorities established under that Act will come into existence at the same time. This Order, subject to a limited exception, transfers trust property held by those old authorities to the new Welsh authorities.
