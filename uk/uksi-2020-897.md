# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: at 1.00 p.m. on 25th August 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 3.00 p.m. on 25th August 2020

Coming into force: 26th August 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 26th August 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) Regulations 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) Regulations 2020 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—

- (i) omit sub-paragraph (j);

- (ii) omit sub-paragraph (n).

## Signed

James Bethell — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — At 1.00 p.m. on 25th August 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) Regulations 2020 to remove the following areas from “the protected area” for the purposes of those Regulations:

- (a) Wigan Metropolitan Borough Council;

- (b) Rossendale Borough Council.

No impact assessment has been carried out for these Regulations.
