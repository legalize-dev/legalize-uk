# The Horizon National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1997

Made: 9th June 1997

Coming into force: 18th June 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Horizon National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment (No. 2) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 18th June 1997.

#### Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 2

For Article 4 of the Horizon National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00004] there shall be substituted:—

> > (4) The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman—
>   (a) until 30th November 1997, 5 non-executive directors and 5 executive directors,
>   (b) on and after 1st December 1997, 4 non-executive directors and 4 executive directors.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Alan Milburn — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 9th June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the constitution of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by temporarily increasing the number of executive and non-executive directors from 4 to 5 until 30th November 1997.

The number of executive and non-executive directors was reduced from 5 to 4 by the Horizon National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/1376](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1376)) with effect from 11th June 1997. As this Order is made to correct a defect in that Order, it is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Order.
