# The Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals (Revocation) Regulations 1996

Made: 6th March 1996

Laid before Parliament: 7th March 1996

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by sections 16(1), 17, 18, 80, 126(4) and 128(1) of, and paragraphs 12 and 12A of Schedule 5 to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals (Revocation) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Revocation of Regulations

###### 2

The Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals Regulations 1990[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 6th March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Authorities for London Post-Graduate Teaching Hospitals Regulations 1990 which provided for the membership and procedure of the special health authorities for London post-graduate teaching hospitals.
