# The Football Spectators (Designation of Enforcing Authority) Order 1990

Made: 26th March 1990

Coming into force: 24th April 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 14(7) of the Football Spectators Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Football Spectators (Designation of Enforcing Authority) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 24th April 1990.

###### 2

The organisation established under section 41 of the Police Act 1964[^f00002] which is known as the Football Spectators Restriction Orders Authority is hereby designated as the enforcing authority for the purposes of Part II of the Football Spectators Act 1989.

## Signed

David Waddington — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 26th March 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the organisation referred to in article 2 as the enforcing authority for the purposes of Part II of the Football Spectators Act 1989. That authority has the functions set out in sections 19 and 20 of that Act.
