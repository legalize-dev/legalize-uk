# The Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.5) Order 2005

Made: 12th October 2005

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(2) of the Armed Forces Act 2001[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2001 (Commencement No.5) Order 2005.

###### 2

Sections 26, 27 and 28 of the Armed Forces Act 2001 shall come into force on 14th October 2005.

## Signed

Don Touhig — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State Ministry of Defence — 12th October 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 26, 27 and 28 of the Armed Forces Act 2001. Section 26 provides for the making of regulations empowering courts to order the payment to a party to proceedings for an offence under the Army Act [1955 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/18), the Air Force Act [1955 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/19) or the Naval Discipline Act [1957 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1957/53) (the services Acts) of costs incurred by that party as a result of the unnecessary or improper act or omission of another party. Section 27 empowers a court in any proceedings for an offence under any of the services Acts to disallow the wasted costs of a party’s legal or other representative or to order that representative to meet the wasted costs incurred by another party. Section 28 contains provisions supplementary to sections 26 and 27.
