# The Colleges of Education (Compensation) (Amendment) Regulations 1981

Made: 22nd July 1981

Laid before Parliament: 7th August 1981

Coming into Operation: 1st September 1981

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 24 of the Superannuation Act 1972, the Secretary of State for Education and Science hereby, with the consent of the Minister for the Civil Service, makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Colleges of Education (Compensation) (Amendment) Regulations 1981 and shall come into operation on 1st September 1981.

###### 2

In the definition of “direction” in Regulation 2(1) of the Colleges of Education (Compensation) Regulations 1975 after the words “Secretary of State” there shall be inserted the words “before 1st September 1981.”

## Signed

Mark Carlisle — Secretary of State for Education and Science — 20th July 1981

Consent of the Minister for the Civil Service given under her Official Seal on 22nd July 1981.

T.A.A. Hart — Authorised by the Minister for the Civil Service

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the definition of the expression “direction” in the Colleges of Education (Compensation) Regulations 1975 as one given under Regulation 3(2) of the Further Education Regulations 1975. The Education (Schools and Further Education) Regulations 1981 (S.I. 1981/1086), which come into operation on 1st September 1981, revoke Regulation 3(2). The amendment makes it clear that the reference to Regulation 3(2) is not to be construed as including a reference to the provisions analogous thereto contained in the Regulations of 1981.
