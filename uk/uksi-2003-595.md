The extension of that instrument to landfills by regulation 6 of [S.I. 2002/1559](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1559) (which apply to England and Wales and which implement Council Directive [99/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0031) on the landfill of waste (O.J. L182, 16.7.99, p. 1)) has the effect that the qualifications and experience prescribed pursuant to section 74(6) apply to persons managing a landfill pursuant to a landfill permit.
