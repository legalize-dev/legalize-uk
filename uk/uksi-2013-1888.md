# The Environment Agency (Inland Waterways) (Amendment) Order 2013

Made: 24th July 2013

Coming into force: 23rd August 2013

Accordingly, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3 and 5 of, and paragraphs 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12, 13 and 16 of Schedule 1 to the Act, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Environment Agency (Inland Waterways) (Amendment) Order 2013 and comes into force on 23rd August 2013.

#### Amendment of the 2010 Order

###### 2

- (1) The Environment Agency (Inland Waterways) Order 2010 [^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 3(2)(d), omit “paragraph (a) of the definition of “the Medway navigation” in”.

## Signed

de Mauley — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 24th July 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order amends the Environment Agency (Inland Waterways) Order 2010 (“the Order”).

Article 2 deletes wording in the order to ensure that the definition of the River Medway in the Order refers to the whole of section 32 of the Southern Water Authority Act 1982 not just section 32(a) of the Southern Water Authority Act 1982. The effect is to allow the Environment Agency to regulate the safety of boats moored in marinas on the River Medway.
