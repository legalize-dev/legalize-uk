# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Britannia Cablesystems Surrey Limited) Order 1991

Made: 27th March 1991

Laid before Parliament: 11th April 1991

Coming into force: 9th May 1991

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Britannia Cablesystems Surrey Limited) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 9th May 1991.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 27th March 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Britannia Cablesystems Surrey Limited on 6th March 1991 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the North and North East Surrey area. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 11th April 1991. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Britannia Cablesystems Surrey Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on9th May 1991.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
