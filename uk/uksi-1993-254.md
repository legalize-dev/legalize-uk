# The Rules of the Air (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made: 11th February 1993

Coming into force: 12th March 1993

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of his powers under article 69(1) of the Air Navigation Order 1989([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Rules of the Air (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 12th March 1993.

###### 2

The Schedule to the Rules of the Air Regulations 1991([^f00002]) shall be amended as follows:

- (1) In Rule 1(1) the definitions of “apron” and “manoeuvring area” shall be deleted.

- (2) At the end of Rule 7 there shall be added:

> > For the purposes of this rule the expression “simulated instrument flight” shall have the same meaning as in rule 6.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Caithness — Minister of State, — Department of Transport — 11th February 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Rules of the Air Regulations 1991. The changes are:

- (1) The definition of “apron” and “manoeuvring areas” are deleted (Regulation 2(1)). These definitions now appear in an unchanged form in the Air Navigation Order 1989 as amended.

- (2) For the avoidance of doubt it is provided that the meaning of “simulated instrument flight” in Rule 7 is the same as that in Rule 6 (Regulation 2(2)).
