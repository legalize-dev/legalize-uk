# The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made: 5th May 1999

Laid before Parliament: 10th May 1999

Coming into force: 1st June 1999

The Chancellor of the Exchequer, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1) and (2) of, and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order–

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

- (2) In this Order–

- (a) the “1998 Order” means the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) Order 1998[^f00003]; and

- (b) a reference to a numbered column is to the column in the Schedule to the 1998 Order bearing that number.

#### Amendment of the 1998 Order

###### 2

In the Schedule to the 1998 Order in the entry made in respect of the Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 124(3)–

- (a) in column 3 at the end there is inserted “(iii) any other custodian of the register”; and

- (b) in relation to that case in column (4) there is inserted the sum of “£6.50”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Chancellor of the Exchequer

Patricia Hewitt — Economic Secretary to the Treasury — 5th May 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) Order 1998 by inserting an additional fee which is to be payable where a certificate of birth, death or marriage is issued by a custodian of the register other than a registrar or superintendent registrar under the Social Security Administration Act 1992 from 1st June 1999.
