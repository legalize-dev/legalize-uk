# The NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Amendment Order 2008

Made: 17th July 2008

Coming into force: 1st August 2008

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 51(1) and (2), 64(5) and (6) and 273(1) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Amendment Order 2008 and comes into force on 1st August 2008.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the NHS Trusts Order” means the National Health Service Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2000[^f00002];

- “the principal Order” means the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2007[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the Schedule to the principal Order

###### 2

In the Schedule to the principal Order, after the entry “University Hospital Birmingham NHS Foundation Trust”, add the entry “University Hospitals Bristol NHS Foundation Trust”.

#### Amendment of the NHS Trusts Order

###### 3

The Schedule to the NHS Trusts Order is amended by omitting the following entry—

> > - The United Bristol Healthcare National Health Service Trust

#### Transitional

###### 4

- (1) This paragraph applies to a person who, immediately before this Order comes into force, held office as a trustee for the University Hospitals Bristol NHS Foundation Trust by virtue of an appointment made under the NHS Trusts Order[^f00004].

- (2) A person to whom paragraph (1) applies is to continue to hold office as a trustee for that NHS foundation trust, and the principal Order is to apply to that person, as if the person had been appointed as a trustee for that NHS foundation trust under the principal Order for the period specified on the person’s appointment.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Ben Bradshaw — Minister of State — Department of Health — 17th July 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order adds the University Hospitals Bristol NHS Foundation Trust to the Schedule to the NHS Foundation Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2007. This means that the Appointments Commission will appoint trustees for that NHS foundation trust and may remove the trustees from office.

This Order also amends the National Health Service Trusts (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2000 (“the NHS Trusts Order”), which provides for the appointment of trustees to National Health Service trusts. In particular, this Order removes from the NHS Trusts Order the entry for the National Health Service trust which has been authorised with effect from June 2008 as the University Hospitals Bristol NHS Foundation Trust. This Order also makes transitional provision for trustees appointed under the NHS Trusts Order.
