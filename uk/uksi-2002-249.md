# The Pitcairn Court of Appeal Order 2000 (Amendment) Order 2002

Made: 12th February 2002

Laid before Parliament: 22nd February 2002

Coming into force: 4th March 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2002 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers in that behalf by the British Settlements Acts 1887[^f00001] and 1945[^f00002] or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pitcairn Court of Appeal Order 2000 (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 4th March 2002.

###### 2

The Pitcairn Court of Appeal Order 2000[^f00003] shall be amended by the omission of the final sentence in the form of Judicial affirmation in the Schedule to that Order.

## Signed

A.K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes inappropriate language from the form of Judicial affirmation in the Pitcairn Court of Appeal Order 2000.
