# The Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Jersey) Order 1997 (Revocation) Order 2015

Made: 15th November 2015

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of November 2015Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 315(2) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Jersey) Order 1997 (Revocation) Order 2015 and comes into force on the day after it is registered by the Royal Court of Jersey.

#### Revocation of the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Jersey) Order 1997

###### 2

The Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Jersey) Order 1997[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Jersey) Order 1997 ([S.I. 1997/2598](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2598)), which extended to Jersey, subject to exceptions, adaptations and modifications, sections 152 to 170, 172 to 181, 277, 279, 281, 284, 308, 313 and 316 of, and Part I of Schedule 5 to, the Merchant Shipping Act 1995. Those sections have been superseded by the Shipping (Oil Pollution) (Liability and Compensation) (Jersey) Law 2015 (L.8/2015).
