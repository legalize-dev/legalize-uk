# The Communications (Television Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: 3rd February 2021

Laid before Parliament: 8th February 2021

Coming into force: 1st April 2021

The Treasury has consented to the making of these Regulations in accordance with section 365(6) of the CA 2003.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Communications (Television Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2021.

- (3) These Regulations have effect in respect of TV licences issued on or after 1st April 2021.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Communications (Television Licensing) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/692](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/692)) (“the 2004 Regulations”) by providing for increases in—

- (a) the amount payable for most categories of TV licence;

- (b) the amount of each instalment for certain categories of TV licence which are payable in instalments.

In summary—

- (a) the amount usually payable for a colour TV licence is increased from £157.50 to £159.00 and that for a black and white TV licence is increased from £53.00 to £53.50 (see regulations 3 and 5);

- (b) the instalments for a premium instalment licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £162.50 to £164.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (2));

- (c) the instalments for a budget instalment licence and for an easy entry licence are increased so that in each case the total amount payable is increased from £157.50 to £159.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (3) to (34));

- (d) the instalments for a simple payment licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £157.50 to £159.00 (see regulation 4(1), (35) and (36)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
