# The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made: 4th March 1991

Laid before Parliament: 5th March 1991

Coming into force: 28th March 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 2(1) and (5), 3 and 20 of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations shall come into force on 28th March 1991 and may be cited as the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1991.

###### 2

For the definition of “ATP” in regulation 2 (Interpretation and transitional provision) of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following definition—

> > - “ATP” means the Agreement on the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs and on the Special Equipment used for such Carriage (ATP) concluded at Geneva on 1st September 1970 as amended pursuant to article 18 of the agreement[^f00003] and as further amended pursuant to that article by amendments to Annex 1, appendices 1 and 2 which came into operation on 6th July 1989[^f00004] and 30th September 1990[^f00005] and amendments to Annex 2 which came into operation on 28th March 1991[^f00006].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

Christopher Chope — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 4th March 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the definition of the Agreement on the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs and the Special Equipment used for such Carriage (ATP) of 1st September 1970 in the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985 to take account of a further amendment to the agreement.
