# The Trading with the Enemy (Revocation) Order 2011

Made: 5th December 2011

Coming into force: 15th December 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 5, 7 and 15(5) of the Trading with the Enemy Act 1939[^f00001] and section 2 and Schedule 2 to the Emergency Laws (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1953[^f00002], now vested in him[^f00003].

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Trading with the Enemy (Revocation) Order 2011 and comes into force on 15th December 2011.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The instruments specified in the Schedule are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes subordinate legislation made, or treated as having been made, under the Trading with the Enemy Act 1939 (“the 1939 Act”) and the Emergency Laws (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1953.

The legislation falls mainly into 3 categories:

1. Instruments providing for enemy assets to be transferred to a custodian appointed by the Board of Trade.

2. Instruments providing for certain named territories to cease to be treated as enemy territory for the purposes of the 1939 Act.

3. Instruments authorising trade with certain territories notwithstanding section 1 of the 1939 Act.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
