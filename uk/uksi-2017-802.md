# The Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2017

Made: 26th July 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 36(6) of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2017.

#### Provisions in the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 coming into force on 27th July 2017

###### 2

Section 20(2) to (7) and (10) of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016 (consequential amendments) comes into force on 27th July 2017.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Damian Hinds — Minister of State — Department for Work and Pensions — 26th July 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force provisions in the Welfare Reform and Work Act [2016 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/7) relating to loans for mortgage interest.

Regulation 2 brings section 20(2) to (7) and (10) (which contains consequential amendments to primary legislation) into force on 27th July 2017.

Those consequential amendments:

1. bring regulations about loans for mortgage interest within the remit of the Social Security Advisory Committee (section 20(2));

2. apply various procedural provisions that apply to social security benefits to decisions about loans for mortgage interest (section 20(3) to (7));

3. apply provisions on information sharing between the Secretary of State and local authorities and other persons to loans for mortgage interest (section 20(10)).
