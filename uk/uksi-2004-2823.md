# The Finance Act 2003, Section 212, (Commencement) Order 2004

Made: 28th October 2004

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 212(5) of the Finance Act 2003[^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2003, Section 212, (Commencement) Order 2004.

###### 2

Section 212(3) of the Finance Act 2003 has effect for the financial year ending with 31st March 2004.

## Signed

Nick Ainger — Joan Ryan — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 28th October 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provision mentioned in article 2 into effect for the financial year ending with 31st March 2004. By virtue of section 212(5) of the Finance Act 2003 ([2003 c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/14) the provision shall continue to have effect for subsequent financial years.
