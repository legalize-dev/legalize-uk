# The Combined Probation Areas (Oxfordshire) Order 1988

Made: 20th December 1988

Coming into force: 1st January 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001] and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Oxfordshire) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st January 1989.

###### 2

That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Oxfordshire Probation Committee shall be amended by omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Moreton and Wallingford and of Wantage and Faringdon and by inserting in alphabetical order in column 1 “Didcot and Wantage” and corresponding to this in column 3 “3”.

###### 3

The three justices appointed to be members of the Oxfordshire Probation Committee by the justices for the petty sessional divisions of Moreton and Wallingford and Wantage and Faringdon before those divisions were combined to form a new division known as the Didcot and Wantage division shall continue to hold office as members of that Committee, as if they had been appointed by the justices for that new division, until 30th November 1989 and shall then cease to hold office but shall be eligible for re-appointment.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 20th December 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the combination of the petty sessional divisions of Moreton and Wallingford and of Wantage and Faringdon in the new petty sessional division of Didcot and Wantage, effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Oxfordshire) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/1665](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1665)) which comes into force on 1st January 1989.
