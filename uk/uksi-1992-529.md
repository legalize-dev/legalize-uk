# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) (Amendment) 1992

Made: 5th March 1992

Coming into force: 26th March 1992

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00001], section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Lord Lyon King of Arms, enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers at-Arms) (Amendment) 1992 and shall come into force on 26th March 1992.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of fees

###### 2

- (1) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at Arms) 1990[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraphs.

- (2) In Item 1(b) (postal service) in Band 2, for the figures “34.75” substitute “14.75”.

- (3) In Item 3(a)(i) (interdict only, each person at a different address), for the figures “23.10” substitute “73.10”.

- (4) In Item 4(c)(v) (poindings), after the word “value” insert the word “over”.

- (5) In Item 6(b) (sequestrations for rent, poinding of the ground) immediately following the word “sequestration”, for the word “of” substitute the word “or”.

- (6) In Item 7(c) (sales, attending warrant sale alone conducted by auctioneer)—

- (a) in Band 2, for the figures “20.80” substitute “35.20”;

- (b) in Band 3, for the figures “20.80” substitute “44.50”.

- (7) The fees as amended by this paragraph shall apply to work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

## Signed

J.A.D. Hope — Lord President, I.P.D. — 5th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends certain fees in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at Arms) 1990 ([S.I. 1990/379](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/379)).
