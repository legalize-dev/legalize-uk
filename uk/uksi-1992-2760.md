# The Mid Essex Hospital Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992

Made: 4th November 1992

Laid before Parliament: 11th November 1992

Coming into force: 2nd December 1992

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by Mid Essex Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Mid Essex Hospital Services National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Mid Essex Hospital Services National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1992 and shall come into forceon 2nd December 1992.

- (2) In this Order:

- “the Authority” means Mid Essex Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the Mid Essex Hospital Services National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 24th August 1992, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust Property

###### 2

On 2nd December 1992 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 4th November 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Mid Essex Hospital Services National Health Service Trust on 2nd December 1992 trust property formerly held by Mid Essex Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Richmond House, 79 Whitehall, London, SW1A 2NS.
