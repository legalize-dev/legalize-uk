# The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) traf|fi|c Order 1996 Variation Order 1997

Made: 22nd September 1997

Coming into force: 3rd October 1997

The Traf|fi|c Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traf|fi|c Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traf|fi|c Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traf|fi|c Order 1996 Variation Order 1997, and shall come into force on the 3rd October 1997.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A1 Trunk Road (Barnet) Red Route (Clearway) Traf|fi|c Order 1996[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

The table in Schedule 1 is deleted and replaced with the following table:

| (1) | (2) |
| --- | --- |
| Item | Length of Road |
| 1. | The A1 Barnet By Pass Road, Barnet Way and Watford Way, north-east side, from the Greater London boundary at Rowley Lane intersection to its junction with the A41 Trunk Road at Fiveways Corner. |
| 2. | The A1 Barnet By Pass, Barnet Way and Watford Way, south-west side, from Fiveways Corner roundabout to a point 10 metres south-east of the south-eastern kerbline of Hall Lane. |
| 3. | The A1 Barnet By Pass, Watford Way, south-west side, from a point 45 metres north-west of the north-western kerbline of Hall Lane to the Greater London Boundary at Rowley Lane intersection. |
| 4. | The roundabout junctions at Stirling Corner, Northway Circus, Mill Hill Circus and Fiveways Corner. |

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 22th September 1997
