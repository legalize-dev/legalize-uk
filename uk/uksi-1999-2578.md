# The Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 10th September 1999

Laid before Parliament: 22nd September 1999

Coming into force: 15th October 1999

The Secretary of State for Education and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 50 and 89(4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 1999, and shall come into force on 15th October 1999.

- (2) In these Regulations “the 1995 Regulations” means the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) Regulations 1995[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the 1995 Regulations

###### 2

The 1995 Regulations shall be amended in the Schedule as follows.

- (2) For paragraph 11(3) there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

> > (3) The percentages of the numbers of students referred to in sub-paragraph (1) whose main occupation after achieving the qualification was—
>   (a) a course of further education;
>   (b) a course of higher education;
>   (c) continued employment in the post held prior to achieving the qualification, with formal training programme;
>   (d) continued employment in the post held prior to achieving the qualification, with no formal training programme;
>   (e) employment in a different post than that held prior to achieving the qualification, with formal training programme;
>   (f) employment in a different post than that held prior to achieving the qualification, with no formal training programme;
>   (g) following a training programme other than the courses referred to in subparagraphs (a) or (b) above, with no employment;
>   (h) an occupation other than the occupations referred to in subparagraphs (a) to (g); or
>   (i) not known to the governing body of the institution publishing this information.

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 10th September 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) Regulations 1995 (“the 1995 Regulations”). The 1995 Regulations, as amended, require governing bodies of institutions within the further education sector to publish specified information about the educational achievements of their students and their occupations after completing their courses, and to provide specified bodies with copies of the information.

These Regulations require the governing bodies to supply more detailed information about the main occupation of students after completing the course, by distinguishing between descriptive categories of employment and training.
