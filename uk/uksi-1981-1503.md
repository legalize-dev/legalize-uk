# The Armed Forces Act 1981 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1981

Made: 17th October 1981

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 29(1) of the Armed Forces Act 1981, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 1981 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1981.

###### 2

Section 6 of the Armed Forces Act 1981 shall come into operation on 1st November 1981.

## Signed

Peter Blaker — Minister of State for the Armed Forces — Ministry of Defence — 17th October 1981

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1st November 1981 the provisions of section 6 of the Armed Forces Act 1981. These relate to the trial of persons ceasing to be subject to service law and the time limits for trials.
