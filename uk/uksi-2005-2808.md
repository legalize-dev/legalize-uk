# The Parental Responsibility Agreement (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 10th October 2005

Laid before Parliament: 12th October 2005

Coming into force: 30th December 2005

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 4(2) and 104 of the Children Act 1989[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Parental Responsibility Agreement (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 30th December 2005.

#### Amendments to the Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991

###### 2

The Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991[^f00002] are amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

###### 3

For regulation 2 substitute—

> > (2) A parental responsibility agreement—
>   (a) under section 4(1)(b) of the Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41) (acquisition of parental responsibility by father), shall be made in form C(PRA1) set out in the Schedule to these Regulations; and
>   (b) under section 4A(1)(a) of that Act (acquisition of parental responsibility by step-parent), shall be made in form C(PRA2) set out in that Schedule.

###### 4

In regulation 3—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “two copies” substitute “sufficient copies for each person with parental responsibility for the child”; and

- (b) in paragraph (2), for “the child’s mother and one to the child’s father” substitute “each person with parental responsibility for the child”.

###### 5

For the Schedule to the Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991 substitute the Schedule in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991 (S.I. [1991/1478](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1478)) to ensure that a step-parent, who has acquired parental responsibility for a child in accordance with section 4A of the Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41), is sent a copy of a parental responsibility agreement made in respect of that child. A new form is also prescribed for an agreement that the step-parent of a child is to have parental responsibility for the child, within the meaning of section 3 of the Children Act 1989.

A new form for a parental responsibility agreement under section 4(1)(b) of the 1989 Act is also substituted. Minor amendments to ensure clarity are made to this form.
