# The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2011

Made: 25th August 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 49(3) and 53(1) of the Police and Justice Act 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

- (1) The following provisions of the Police and Justice Act 2006 come into force on 3rd October 2011 in the relevant local justice areas[^f00002]—

- (a) section 45 (attendance by accused at certain preliminary or sentencing hearings), to the extent that it is not already in force; and

- (b) section 46 (live link bail).

- (2) The relevant local justice areas are: Central Hertfordshire; Chester, Ellesmere Port and Neston; East Hertfordshire; Halton; Macclesfield; North Hertfordshire; South Cheshire; Vale Royal; Warrington; and West Hertfordshire.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Nick Herbert — Minister of State — Ministry of Justice — 25th August 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 3rd October 2011 the provisions of the Police and Justice Act [2006 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/48) (“the 2006 Act”) set out in article 2(1), but only in certain local justice areas (article 2(2)).

Section 45 of the 2006 Act, in so far as it comes into force by virtue of this Order, inserts section 57C of the Crime and Disorder Act [1998 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37) (“the 1998 Act”), which is amended by section 106 of the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25). Section 57C of the 1998 Act concerns the use of live links at preliminary hearings in magistrates’ courts where the accused is at a police station. A live link is a video-link between a court and a police station.

Section 46 of the 2006 Act amends the Police and Criminal Evidence Act [1984 (c. 60)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/60) to allow police to grant bail subject to a duty to attend at a police station for proceedings in relation to a live link direction.
