# The High Speed Rail (London – West Midlands) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017

Made: 23rd February 2017

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 70(2) of the High Speed Rail (London – West Midlands) Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the High Speed Rail (London – West Midlands) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 11 of and Schedule 14 (amendments to this Act consequential on the Housing and Planning Act 2016[^f00002]) to the High Speed Rail (London – West Midlands) Act 2017 come into force on the day after the day these Regulations are made.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Andrew Jones — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 23rd February 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These are the first and only Commencement Regulations under the High Speed Rail (London – West Midlands) Act 2017 (“the Act”). All other provisions came into force on the passing of the Act.

Regulation 2 commences section 11 and Schedule 14 of the Act which provide for amendments to the Act that are consequential on the Housing and Planning Act 2016.
