# The Portsmouth Water (Constitution and Regulation) Order 1990

Made: 1st August 1990

Laid before Parliament: 10th August 1990

Coming into force: 31st August 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 101(2), (6) and (7) of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Portsmouth Water (Constitution and Regulation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 31st August 1990.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 1845 Act” means the Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845[^f00004]; and

- “the 1863 Act” means the Companies Clauses Act 1863[^f00005].

#### Approval of proposal contained in the special resolution

###### 2

The proposal contained in the special resolution is hereby approved.

#### Cessation of local statutory provisions

###### 3

The date specified for the purposes of section 101(2) of the Act as the date on which the local statutory provisions shall cease to have effect and the memorandum and articles shall come into force is 31st August 1990.

#### Consequential provision

###### 4

The provisions of the Portsmouth Water Acts and Orders 1857 to 1986 referred to in Schedule 2 hereto are repealed to the extent indicated in that Schedule with effect from 31st August 1990.

## SCHEDULE 1 — DOCUMENTS HAVING EFFECT AS MENTIONED IN SECTION 101(2)(b) OF THE WATER ACT 1989

The documents comprising the Memorandum of Association of Portsmouth Water Plc and the Articles of Association of Portsmouth Water Plc[^f00006].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 101(2) of the Water Act 1989 provides for the replacement of provisions relating to the constitution and regulation of a statutory water company contained in its local Acts and Orders by provisions contained in a memorandum and articles which have been approved by special resolution of the members of the company. The replacement provisions have effect only if they are approved by the Secretary of State and come into force on such date as he determines.

This Order approves a proposal by The Portsmouth Water Company that a memorandum and articles of association should have effect in substitution for provisions contained in the Portsmouth Water Acts and Orders 1857 to 1986 (article 2). It also provides that the date on which those provisions cease to have effect is 31st August 1990 (article 3) and for the repeal of the superseded provisions and for consequential repeals (article 4).

This Order is associated with the company’s proposal to convert to the status of a registered company under the Companies Act 1985.
