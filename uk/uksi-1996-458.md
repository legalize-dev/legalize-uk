# The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made: 27th February 1996

Laid before Parliament: 29th February 1996

Coming into force: 22nd March 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 22nd March 1996.

###### 2

Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1988[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition at the end of “The House of St. Gregory and St. Macrina Oxford Limited”.

## Signed

Eric Forth — Minister of State, Deparetment for Education and Employment — 27th February 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1988 to specify The House of St. Gregory and St. Macrina Oxford Limited for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988. The effect of specification is that a tenancy granted by the body to a student at an educational institution specified in the 1988 Regulations is not an assured tenancy for the purposes of the 1988 Act.
