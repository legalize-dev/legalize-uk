# The A41 Trunk Road (Watford Way, Barnet) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1988

Made: 18th January 1988

Coming into force: 24th January 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of, and Part IV of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Secretary of State for the Home Department, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A41 Trunk Road (Watford Way, Barnet) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 24th January 1988.

###### 2

In this Order —

- “Watford Way” means that length of the London—Carlisle—Glasgow—Inverness Trunk Road (A41) in the London Borough of Barnet known as Watford Way;

- “central reservation” means that part of Watford Way which separates the two carriageways of Watford Way for the safety and guidance of vehicles using that road;

- “the junction” means the junction of Watford Way, Aerodrome Road, Aerodrome Road Service Road and Greyhound Hill;

- “the northbound right-turn lane” means the traffic lane, being part of the northbound carriageway of Watford Way, which is bounded on its eastern side by the central reservation, has a width of approximately 3.6 metres and extends from the junction southwards for a distance of 105 metres;

- “the southbound right-turn lane” means the traffic lane, being part of the southbound carriageway of Watford Way, which is bounded on its western side by the central reservation, has a width of approximately 3.6 metres and extends from the junction northwards for a distance of 105 metres.

###### 3

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in the northbound right-turn lane shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Greyhound Hill.

###### 4

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in the southbound right-turn lane shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Aerodrome Road.

###### 5

No person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in Watford Way to enter the service road which lies adjacent to the southern side of Aerodrome Road.

###### 6

No person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in the service road which lies adjacent to the southern side of Aerodrome Road to enter Watford Way.

###### 7

No person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in either carriageway of Watford Way, other than in the northbound right-turn lane or the southbound right-turn lane, to turn right at the junction.

###### 8

The A41 Trunk Road (Watford Way, Barnet) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1987[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

###### 9

Nothing in this Order shall apply to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.
