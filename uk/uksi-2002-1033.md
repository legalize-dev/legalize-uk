# The District of East Riding (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made: 26th March 2002

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17[^f00002] and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the District of East Riding (Electoral Changes) (Amendment) Order 2002.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on the day after that on which it is made.

#### Amendment to the 2001 Order

###### 2

- (1) The District of East Riding (Electoral Changes) Order 2001[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In the table in Schedule 1, in column 2 (area of ward)—

- (a) in relation to the entry in column 1 for the ward of Tranby, there shall be inserted “The parish of Anlaby with Anlaby Common”; and

- (b) in relation to the entry in column 1 for the ward of Willerby and Kirk Ella, there shall be inserted “The parishes of Willerby and Kirk Ella”.

## Signed

Alan Whitehead — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Transport, Local Government and the Regions — 26th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

In order to give full effect to recommendations by the Local Government Commission for England for electoral changes in the District of East Riding, this Order corrects an error in the District of East Riding (Electoral Changes) Order 2001 by inserting into Schedule 1 to that Order a description of the parishes forming the area of the wards of—

- Tranby; and

- Willerby and Kirk Ella.
