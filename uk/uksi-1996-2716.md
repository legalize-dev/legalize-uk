# The Electricity Act 1989 (Disclosure of Information) (Licence Holders) Order 1996

Made: 24th October 1996

Laid before Parliament: 25th October 1996

Coming into force: 1st December 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 57(4) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Electricity Act 1989 (Disclosure of Information) (Licence Holders) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st December 1996.

###### 2

Subsection (2) of section 57 of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00002] shall have effect subject to the following modification, that is to say, after paragraph (a) there shall be inserted the following paragraph–

> > (aa) for the purpose of enabling a licence holder to comply with a condition of his licence under or by virtue of which he is required to disclose the information to another licence holder;.

## Signed

Fraser of Carmyllie — Minister for Energy, — 24th October 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order relates to disclosure of information made between the holders of licences granted under section 6 of the Electricity Act 1989. It adds disclosure made between such licence holders under a licence obligation to the list of exceptions from the restrictions on disclosure of information in section 57(1) of the Electricity Act 1989.
