## Footnotes

[^f00001]: The functions of conservancy authority and harbour authority were conferred on the Dee Conservancy Board by sections 35 and 36 of the Dee Conservancy Act 1889 (c. clvi) and subsequently transferred to the River Dee Catchment Board. That Board was, by virtue of the Rivers Dee and Clwyd Catchment Area Order 1946 ([S.I. 1946/1036](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1946/1036)) combined with the River Clwyd Catchment Board to form the Rivers Dee and Clwyd Catchment Board; this was replaced by the Dee and Clwyd River Board by virtue of the Dee and Clwyd River Board Constitution Order 1950 (S.I 1950/1085) and, in turn, by the Dee and Clwyd River Authority by virtue of the Dee and Clwyd River Authority Constitution Order 1964 ([S.I. 1964/1015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1964/1015)). Section 2 of the Water Act [1973 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/37) transferred the functions of the said River Authority to the Welsh National Water Development Authority and section 142 of the Water Act [1989 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/15) transferred that Authority’s functions of a conservancy authority to the National Rivers Authority. Those functions were subsequently transferred to the Environment Agency by virtue of section 2(1)(a)(vi) of the Environment Act [1995 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25). In relation to the functions of local lighthouse authority for the Dee Estuary, section 193(2) of the Merchant Shipping Act [1995 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/21) had the effect of conferring those functions on the National Rivers Authority as the then statutory harbour authority before they were vested in the Environment Agency, as mentioned above. The Natural Resources Body for Wales, whose head office address is located at Cambria House, 29 Newport Road, Cardiff CF24 0TP (www.naturalresources.wales), was established by the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Establishment) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/1903 (W.230)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2012/1903)) and the above-mentioned functions of the Environment Agency were transferred to that Body by virtue of the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Functions) Order 2013 ([S.I. 2013/755 (W.90)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2013/755)). Mostyn Docks Limited is, however, the harbour authority for that part of the river Dee Estuary described in the Mostyn Docks Orders 1988 to 2016 (see the Mostyn Docks Harbour Revision Order 2016 ([S.I.2016/75](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/75))).
