# The Rail Vehicle Accessibility (Heathrow Express Class 360/2) Exemption Order 2005

Made: 21st January 2005

Laid before Parliament: 1st February 2005

Coming into force: 1st March 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 47(1), (3) and (4) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00001], having consulted the Disabled Persons Transport Advisory Committee and such other persons as he considered appropriate, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rail Vehicle Accessibility (Heathrow Express Class 360/2) Exemption Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st March 2005.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- (a) any reference to “the exempted vehicles” is a reference to the vehicles numbered 63421 to 63424, 72421 to 72424, 78431 to 78434, and 78441 to 78444, which form the electric multiple-units numbered 360201 to 360204 of the class known as “Class 360/2” as manufactured by Siemens in Germany for use by Heathrow Express Limited; and

- (b) any reference to a numbered regulation is a reference to the regulation bearing that number in the Rail Vehicle Accessibility Regulations 1998[^f00002].

#### Authorisation and exemption

###### 3

Subject to articles 4 to 6, the exempted vehicles are authorised to be used for carriage even though they do not conform with regulation 7(b).

#### Condition

###### 4

It is a condition of the exemption granted by article 3 that—

- (a) a transverse low level lighting system is installed on the floor between the saloons and vestibules of an exempted vehicle; and

- (b) the system referred to in sub-paragraph (a) is fully operational whenever the exempted vehicle is being used for carriage.

#### Expiry

###### 5

The exemption granted by article 3, in respect of regulation 7(b), shall cease at the end of February 2007.

###### 6

The authorisation granted by article 3 shall cease immediately in respect of any exempted vehicle if the electric multiple-unit of which the exempted vehicle forms a part is used by a person other than Heathrow Express Limited unless the Secretary of State has been given written notice in advance specifying the name and address of that other person.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Charlotte Atkins — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Transport — 21st January 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order authorises the use for carriage of specified rail vehicles forming the fleet of Class 360/2 Heathrow Express electric multiple-units even though they do not conform with one requirement of the Rail Vehicle Accessibility Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/2456](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2456), amended by S.I. [2000/3215](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3215)) because the carpets in the vestibule do not contrast sufficiently with the carpets in the adjacent passenger saloon.

The Order imposes a condition and sets a time limit on the authorisation.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared in respect of this Order.
