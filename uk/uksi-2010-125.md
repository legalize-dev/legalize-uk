# The Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2010

Made: 21st January 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 116(1) of the Policing and Crime Act 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2010.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Policing and Crime Act 2009.

#### Provisions coming into force on 29th January 2010

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act is 29th January 2010:

- (a) section 10 (police officers engaged on service outside their force etc);

- (b) section 11 (police equipment);

- (c) section 12 (police procedures and practices);

- (d) section 13 (police facilities and services);

- (e) section 28 (selling alcohol to children);

- (f) section 29 (confiscating alcohol from young persons);

- (g) section 30 (offence of persistently possessing alcohol in a public place);

- (h) section 31 (directions to individuals who represent a risk of disorder);

- (i) section 32 (mandatory licensing conditions relating to alcohol);

- (j) section 33 (individual members of licensing authorities to be interested parties);

- (k) section 79 (security planning for airports) in relation to England, Wales and Scotland only;

- (l) section 80 (policing at airports) in relation to England, Wales and Scotland only;

- (m) section 83 (monitoring application);

- (n) section 97 (criminal records: applications);

- (o) section 110 (partial exemption for SCDEA from Firearms Act 1968)

- (p) section 112(1) (minor and consequential amendments and repeals and revocations) insofar as it relates to Part 4 and Part 5 of Schedule 7;

- (q) section 112(2) (minor and consequential amendments and repeals and revocations) insofar as it relates to Part 3 and Part 7 of Schedule 8, and the provisions of Part 8 of Schedule 8 specified in paragraph (u);

- (r) Schedule 4;

- (s) Schedule 6 in relation to England, Wales and Scotland only;

- (t) Part 4 and Part 5 of Schedule 7;

- (u) Part 3, Part 7 and Part 8, insofar as it relates to repeals in the Police Act 1997[^f00002] and the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00003], of Schedule 8.

#### Provision coming into force on 29th January 2010 for the purpose of making regulations

###### 3

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 84 (monitoring: additional fees) for the purpose of making regulations is 29th January 2010.

#### Provision coming into force on 15th March 2010

###### 4

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 1 (duty of police authorities in relation to public accountability) is 15th March 2010.

## Signed

David Hanson — Minister of State — 21st January 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the third Commencement Order made under the Policing and Crime Act 2009 (“the Act”).

Article 2 lists the provisions of the Act which will come into force on 29th January 2010 which include the entirety of Part 3 of the Act (alcohol misuse) and the entirety of Part 7 of the Act (aviation security) for England, Wales and Scotland.

Article 3 provides that section 84 of the Act will come into force on 29th January 2010 for the purpose of making regulations.

Article 4 brings section 1 of the Act into force on 15th March 2010.
