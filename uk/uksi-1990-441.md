# The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1990

Made: 28th February 1990

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 1990

Coming into force: 1st April 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 of, as read with paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

###### 2

The fees payable in relation to the matters specified in Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867[^f00002] (and repeated in column 1 of the Schedule to the Order) shall be varied by substituting those specified in column 3 of the Schedule to this Order (the fees formerly payable being shown in column 2).

###### 3

The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1988[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases most of the fees for the matters listed in Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867. The fees were last increased in 1988. These fees are exclusive of stamp duty when stamp duty is exigible.
