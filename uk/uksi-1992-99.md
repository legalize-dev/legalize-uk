#### Revocation of Order

The provisions of this Order shall have effect in place of the provisions of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (No. 2) Order 1992[^f00003] (under which the fees payable were as respectively set out in column 5 of the Schedule to this Order) and that Order is accordingly revoked.
