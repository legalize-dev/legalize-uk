# The Nuclear Installations (Insurance Certificate) (Amendment) Regulations 1969

Made: 16th January 1969

Laid before Parliament: 27th January 1969

Coming into Operation: 1st February 1969

The Minister of Power and the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 21(3) and 26(1) of the Nuclear Installations Act 1965 and of all other powers them enabling hereby jointly make the following regulations:—

###### 1

These regulations shall come into operation on 1st February 1969 and may be cited as the Nuclear Installations (Insurance Certificate) (Amendment) Regulations 1969.

###### 2

The Nuclear Installations (Insurance Certificate) Regulations 1965[^f00001] shall be amended by deleting paragraph (c) of regulation 3.

## Signed

Roy Mason — Minister of Power — Dated 14th January 1969

William Ross — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Dated 16th January 1969

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These regulations, made by the Minister of Power and the Secretary of State for Scotland, acting jointly, amend the Nuclear Installations (Insurance Certificate) Regulations 1965, which prescribe for the purposes of Section 21(3) of the Nuclear Installations Act 1965 the particulars to be contained in the certificate required when nuclear matter is to be carried in such circumstances that liability may be incurred by the operator of a nuclear installation either under the Act or under corresponding foreign law.

They remove the requirement for the certificate to contain particulars of the total number and identifying marks of the packages of nuclear matter.
