The 1994 Regulations make provision for the purpose of implementing, for Great Britain, Council Directive [92/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0043) on the conservation of natural habitats and of wild flora and fauna (O.J. No. L206, 22.07.92, p.7) as last amended by Council Directive [2006/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0105) of 20 November 2006 adapting Directives [79/409/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0409), [92/43/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0043), [97/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0068), [2001/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0080) and [2001/81/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0081) in the field of environment, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania (O.J. No. L363, 20.12.06, p.368). In relation to Wales, the functions of the Secretary of State under the 1994 Regulations were transferred to the National Assembly for Wales (with the exception of those under regulations 71 to 78) by article 2 of, and Schedule 1 to, the National Assembly for Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/672](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/672)). By virtue of paragraph 30 of Schedule 11 to the Government of Wales Act 2006, the relevant functions were transferred from the National Assembly for Wales to the Welsh Ministers.
