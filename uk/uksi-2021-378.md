# The Spring Traps Approval (Variation) (England) (No. 2) Order 2021

Made: 23rd March 2021

Coming into force: 14th April 2021

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(3) and (7) of the Pests Act 1954[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Spring Traps Approval (Variation) (England) (No. 2) Order 2021 and comes into force on 14th April 2021.

#### Variation of the Spring Traps Approval (England) Order 2018

###### 2

The Schedule to the Spring Traps Approval (England) Order 2018[^f00003] is varied as follows—

- (a) in the entry for the DOC 250, in column 2 (conditions), omit “ferrets,”;

- (b) in the entry for the Perdix Spring Trap, in column 1 (type and make of trap), for “PERDIX Wildlife Supplies” substitute “PERDIX Wildlife Solutions Ltd”.

## Signed

Rebecca Pow — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 23rd March 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order varies the Spring Traps Approval (England) Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018/1190](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1190)). It removes ferrets as a permitted target species of the DOC 250 trap and corrects a reference to the name of the manufacturer of the Perdix Spring Trap.

An impact assessment had not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
