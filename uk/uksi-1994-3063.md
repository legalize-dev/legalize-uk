# The Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994

Made: 1st December 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 68(4) and (5) of the Coal Industry Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Coal Industry Act 1994 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1994.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Coal Industry Act 1994.

#### Provision coming into force on 24th December 1994

###### 2

Part III of Schedule 11 to the Act shall, so far as it relates to the repeals set out in the Schedule to this Order, come into force on 24th December 1994.

## SCHEDULE — REPEALS TAKING EFFECT ON 24TH DECEMBER 1994

| Chapter | Short title | Extent of repeal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 & 10 Geo. 6. c.59. | The Coal Industry Nationalisation Act 1946. | Section 45, for the purposes specified in subsection (1) of that section. |
| [1986 c. 63](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/63). | The Housing and Planning Act 1986. | In Schedule 8, paragraph 8. |
| [1987 c. 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/3). | The Coal Industry Act 1987. | In Schedule 1, paragraphs 9, 17 and, so far as unrepealed, paragraph 20. |

## Signed

Charles Wardle — Parliamentary Under Secretary for Industry and Energy, — 1st December 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force certain of the repeals specified in Part III of Schedule 11 to the Coal Industry Act 1994 not previously brought into force.
