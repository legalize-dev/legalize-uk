# The London Borough of Haringey (Trunk Roads) Red Route (Bus Lanes) Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998

Made: 20th March 1998

Coming into force: 3rd April 1998

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The London Borough of Haringey (Trunk Roads) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 1998, and shall come into force on 3rd April 1998.

#### Variations

###### 2

The London Borough of Haringey (Trunk Roads) Red Route (Bus Lanes) Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] ia varied in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In column 2 of item 2 of the table in the Schedule to the Order the text “opposite the common boundary of No. 154 Archway Road and the electricity sub-station.” is substituted with “1.0 metre south of the party wall of Nos. 154 and 156 Archway Road.”.

###### 4

In column 2 of item 3 of the table in the Schedule to the Order the text “opposite the party wall of Nos. 153 and 155 Archway Road” is substituted with “12.0 metres south of the south-east kerbline of Langdon Park Road”.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 20th March 1998
