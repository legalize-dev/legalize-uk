# The Pensions Appeal Tribunals (Late Appeal) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

Made: 20th March 2023

Coming into force: 3rd April 2023

In accordance with section 11A(5) of that Act[^f00003] a draft of this instrument was laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Appeal Tribunals (Late Appeal) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on the fourteenth day after the day on which they are made.

- (3) These Regulations extend to Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Amendment of the Pensions Appeal Tribunals (Late Appeal) Regulations 2001

###### 2

- (1) The Pensions Appeals Tribunal (Late Appeals) Regulations 2001[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) For regulation 3 (provision for late appeals), substitute—

> > (3)
>   (1) Subject to paragraph (2), where an appeal is not brought within the time limit, it will be treated as having been brought in time if the Secretary of State does not object.
>   (2) No appeal may be brought more than twelve months after the expiry of the time limit.
>   (3) Where the Secretary of State objects to an appeal being treated as having been brought in time, the Tribunal may allow that appeal to be brought not later than twelve months after the expiry of the time limit if it is satisfied that it is in the interests of justice to do so.

- (3) Regulation 4 (prescribed circumstances) is revoked.

## Signed

Andrew Murrison — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Defence — 20th March 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Pensions Appeal Tribunals (Late Appeals) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/1032](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1032)) (“*the 2001 Regulations*”).

The 2001 Regulations relate to war pensions and armed forces compensation schemes and the circumstances in which a Pensions Appeal Tribunal for Scotland or Northern Ireland may, under the Pensions Appeal Tribunals Act [1943 (c. 39 6 and 7 Geo. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1943/39), hear a late appeal against a decision by the Secretary of State. A late appeal is one which is brought more than twelve months after the date of the Secretary of State’s decision, but within twenty-four months of it.

Regulation 2 amends the 2001 Regulations by substituting a new regulation 3. New regulation 3 provides:

- (a) that a late appeal will be treated as made in time if the Secretary of State does not object;

- (b) that a Tribunal may allow a late appeal even if the Secretary of State does object, where it is satisfied that it is in the interests of justice to do so; and

- (c) for the revocation of regulation 4 of the 2001 Regulations.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
