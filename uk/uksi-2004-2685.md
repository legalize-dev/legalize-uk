# The Albion Junior School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2004

Made: 18th October 2004

Laid before Parliament: 25th October 2004

Coming into force: 1st December 2004

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Albion Junior School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2004 and comes into force on 1st December 2004.

#### Exemption from School Session Times Regulations

###### 2

The Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall not apply to the governing body of the Albion Junior School of Halfords Lane, West Bromwich, West Midlands, B71 4LG.

#### Time Limit

###### 3

This Order has effect until 7th January 2005.

## Signed

David Miliband — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 18th October 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 do not apply to the governing body of the Albion Junior School. Article 3 specifies that the Order has effect until 7th January 2005.
