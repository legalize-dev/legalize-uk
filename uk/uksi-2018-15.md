# The Plant Health (Fees) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made: 10th January 2018

Laid before Parliament: 11th January 2018

Coming into force: 8th February 2018

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) of the Finance Act 1973 .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Health (Fees) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 8th February 2018.
