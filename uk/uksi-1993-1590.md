# The Swavesey Internal Drainage Board Award Drains Variation Order 1993

Made: 10th May 1993

Coming into force: 23rd June 1993

Now, therefore, the Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 32(5) and (7) of the 1991 Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Swavesey Internal Drainage Board Award Drains Variation Order 1993 and shall, unless subject to special Parliamentary procedure, come into force on the date of its confirmation by the Minister, in accordance with paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 3 to the 1991 Act.

###### 2

The Scheme is hereby confirmed, subject to the following modifications:–

- (a) for the first paragraph of the Scheme shall be substituted the following paragraphs:–

> > (1) This Scheme shall come into force one month after the date of confirmation of the Order confirming this Scheme.
> (2) In this Scheme:–
>   - “the Award Drains” means the drains shown by green lines and broken green lines on the Map;
>   - “the Culverts” means the culverts shown lettered A, B and C on the Map;
>   - “the Defunct Culvert” means the culvert lettered C on the Map;
>   - “the Defunct Drains” means the Award Drains shown by broken green lines on the Map;
>   - “the Map” means the map sealed by the National Rivers Authority in relation to this Scheme;
>   - “the New Drains” means the drains shown by red lines on the Map.

- (b) the second paragraph of the Scheme shall be numbered “3” and for the words in that paragraph “in the Awards shall from the appointed day be varied” there shall be substituted the words “in the Awards specified below shall be varied”;

- (c) the third paragraph of the Scheme shall be numbered “4”;

- (d) for the words “THE SCHEDULE” there shall be substituted the words “THE AWARDS”.

###### 3

The expenses of the Minister in connection with the making and confirmation of this Order shall be borne by the Swavesey Internal Drainage Board.

## SCHEDULE — SCHEME SUBMITTED BY THE NATIONAL RIVERS AUTHORITY

As from a day to be appointed by the Minister (“the appointed day”) the following provision shall have effect:—

The Board’s obligations to cleanse scour repair and maintain the Award Drains and the Culverts as specified in the Awards shall from the appointed day be varied so as to exclude the Defunct Drains and the Defunct Culvert and substitute in their place the New Drains as defined herein.

AND the Board shall from that date be responsible for the repair and maintenance of the New Drains together with the Award Drains shown by green lines and the Culverts lettered A' and B' on the Map in accordance with the provisions of the Awards but shall from that date have no responsibility for the repair and maintenance of the Defunct Drains and the Defunct Culvert.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order confirms with modifications a Scheme (“the Scheme”) submitted by the National Rivers Authority for the variation of land drainage duties of the Swavesey Internal Drainage Board under specified statutory awards.

The Scheme varies the awards by transferring the Board’s maintenance obligations from drains and a culvert which are now defunct to new drains.

A copy of the map referred to in the Scheme is available for inspection during office hours at the offices of the Swavesey Internal Drainage Board, Middle Level Offices, Dartford Road, March, Cambridgeshire PE15 8AF.
