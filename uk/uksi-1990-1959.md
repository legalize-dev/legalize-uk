# The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 1990

Made: 28th September 1990

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 28th day of September 1990 By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken the said rules into consideration, are hereby pleased to approve the same as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Disciplinary Committee) (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules Order of Council 1990.

## SCHEDULE — THE DISCIPLINARY COMMITTEE (PROCEDURE AND EVIDENCE) (AMENDMENT) RULES 1990

The Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, in exercise of its powers under paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 2 to the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966 hereby makes the following Rules–

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Disciplinary Committee (Procedure and Evidence) (Amendment) Rules 1990.

###### 2

Rule 19 of the Disciplinary Committee (Procedure and Evidence) Rules 1967[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition of the following paragraph–

> > (4) For any aspect of the conduct of the proceedings for which no specific provision is made in these Rules, the procedure shall be such as the Chairman shall determine.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Rules approved by this Order amend the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners Disciplinary Committee (Procedure and Evidence) Rules 1967 empowering the Chairman of the Disciplinary Committee to determine the procedure of any aspect of the conduct of the proceedings not already covered by specific provision in the Rules.
