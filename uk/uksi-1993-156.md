# The Shropshire’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993

Made: 29th January 1993

Coming into force: 5th February 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 [^f00002] and of all powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Shropshire’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993 and shall come into force on 5th February 1993.

#### Amendment of NHS TRust Establishment Order

###### 2

In article 2 of the Shropshire’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003] for the words “North West Anglia Health Care” there shall be substitued the words “Shropshire’s Mental Health”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 29th January 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects an error in the Shropshire’s Mental Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992 in article 2 of which the name of the National Health Service trust established by that Order was incorrectly stated.
