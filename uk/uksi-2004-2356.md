# The Employment Code of Practice (Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures) Order 2004

Made: 3rd September 2004

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on her by section 200(5) of the 1992 Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Employment Code of Practice (Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures) Order 2004.

#### Appointed day for the Code of Practice

###### 2

The day appointed by the Secretary of State under section 200(5) of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 for the coming into effect of the Code of Practice on Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures (the draft of which was laid before both Houses of Parliament on 17 June 2004) issued by the Advisory, Conciliation and Arbitration Service pursuant to that section is 1st October 2004.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Employment Relations, Competition and Consumers — 3rd September 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st October 2004 as the day upon which the Code of Practice on Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures which is being issued by the Advisory, Conciliation and Arbitration Service under section 199 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 will come into effect. The Code of Practice takes account of the Employment Act 2002 (Dispute Resolution) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/752](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/752)) which also come into force on 1st October 2004.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no significant impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies.

The Code of Practice replaces the Code of Practice on Disciplinary and Grievance Procedures which came into effect on 4th September 2000.
