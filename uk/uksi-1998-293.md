# The London Docklands Development Corporation (Alteration of Designated Areas) Order 1998

Made: 12th February 1998

Laid before Parliament: 16th February 1998

Coming into force: 9th March 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 31 of the London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having consulted the councils of the London boroughs of Newham and Tower Hamlets (which are the borough councils for the areas in which there is situated land included within the limits of jurisdiction by this Order), and with the consent of the British Waterways Board so far as is required by section 34(5)(a) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order (which does not include any area of land vested in the Port of London Authority[^f00002])—

#### Citation commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the London Docklands Development Corporation (Alteration of Designated Areas) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 9th March 1998.

- (2) In this Order references to “the 1994 Act” are to the London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994.

#### Alteration of land comprised in the designated areas

###### 2

- (1) In this article, “the Maps” means the maps respectively labelled “Map 1”, “Map 2”, “Map 3”, “Map 4”, “Map 5” and “Map 6” entitled “London Docklands Development Corporation Alteration of Designated Areas Maps 1 to 6 January 1998”, one set of which, signed by an Under Secretary in the Government Office for London, has been deposited and is available for inspection at each of the following offices, that is to say, the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, the London Docklands Development Corporation, and the councils of the London boroughs of Newham, Southwark and Tower Hamlets.

- (2) The designated areas are altered by—

- (a) the removal from the designated areas of the areas shown coloured green on the Maps and described in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order, and

- (b) the inclusion within the designated areas of the areas shown coloured pink on the Maps and described in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Order,

and, as regards any matter arising after the date of the coming into force of this Order, any reference in the 1994 Act to the designated areas shall be a reference to the designated areas as altered by this Order.

- (3) If there is any discrepancy between the areas shown on the Maps and the descriptions in the Schedule to this Order, the Maps shall prevail.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order alters the designated areas referred to in the London Docklands Development Corporation Act 1994 (“the 1994 Act”). These are the areas in respect of which the functions of the London Docklands Development Corporation under the 1994 Act are exercisable. The areas removed are each shown coloured black on Map A below, and the areas included are each shown coloured black on Map B below.

A draft Order laid under section 34(8) of the 1994 Act for the transfer to four successor bodies of the functions of the London Docklands Development Corporation under that Act, which will apply in relation to the designated areas as so altered, was laid before Parliament on 21st January 1998.

The maps referred to in article 2 of the Order may be inspected during normal office hours at the following offices—

- (a) the Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3EB until the end of March 1998 and then Ashdown House, 123 Victoria Street, London SW1E 6DE;

- (b) the London Docklands Development Corporation, Thames Quay, 191 Marsh Wall, London E14 9TJ;

- (c) the council of the London borough of Newham, Town Hall, Barking Road, East Ham, London E6 2RP;

- (d) the council of the London borough of Southwark, Council Offices, Chiltern House, Portland Street, London SE17 2ES; and

- (e) the council of the London borough of Tower Hamlets, Town Hall, Mulberry Place, 5 Clove Crescent, London E14 2BG.
