# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2025

Made: 6th May 2025

Coming into force: 7th May 2025

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 6th day of May 2025Present,The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2025 and comes into force on 7th May 2025.

#### Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — The persons appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 7th May 2025

- Jacqueline Anne Line

- Nicola Mary Lownds

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
