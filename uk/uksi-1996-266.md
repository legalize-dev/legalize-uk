## SCHEDULE — DESIGNATIONS

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or departments* | *Matters in relation to which the Ministers or departments are designated* |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the employment of children and young persons. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the transport of dangerous or environmentally hazardous goods by road or rail. |
| . . . | . . . |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to railways and railway transport. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the carriage of passengers and goods by road. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the management of packaging and packaging waste. |
| Any Northern Ireland Department | The preceding items in this column. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the making of exclusion orders under the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00005]. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to advanced television services, including digital conditional access and subscription management services. |
| . . . | . . . |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to supplementary protection certificates for plant protection products. |
| The Forestry Commissioners | Measures applying otherwise than in relation to Wales relating to the common agricultural policy of the European Union in respect of forestry. |
| The Welsh Ministers | Measures applying in relation to Wales and relating to the common agricultural policy of the European Union in respect of forestry. |
