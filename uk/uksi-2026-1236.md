# The Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 24th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 25th November 2026

Coming into force: 16th December 2026

The Secretary of State[^f00001], considering that the condition in section 45(2) of the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018[^f00002] is met, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1)(a) and (c), 1(3), 3(1)(a) to (e), 5, 9(2), 10(2)(a) and (c), 11(3) and (5), 15(2)(a) and (b), 17(2)(c) and (4), and 45(1)(b) of, and paragraphs 2(a)(i), 2(b), 4(a)(i), 5(a)(i), 6(a)(i), 11(a)(i), 13(a), (g), (k), and (m) and 14(a) of Schedule 1 to, that Act.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 16th December 2026.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations are made under the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act [2018 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13) to amend the Libya (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1665](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1665)) (“*the Libya Regulations*”).

These amendments are being made to ensure compliance with the UK’s UN obligations, specifically to ensure changes made by UN Security Council Resolutions 2769 (2025) and 2819 (2026) to the UN’s Libya sanctions regime are implemented. Regulations 4, 48 and Part 1 of Schedule 4 of the Libya Regulations are amended to provide for the grant of licences that could enable the reinvestment of certain frozen cash reserves of the Libyan Investment Authority (“the LIA”), as provided for in paragraph 14 of Resolution 2769, and transferring the role of global custodian of frozen assets of the LIA, as provided for in paragraph 15 of Resolution 2819. Regulation 18 is amended to ensure that such assets remain subject to that regulation when transferred for the purposes of such a reinvestment or change of custodian, as required by Resolutions 2769 and 2819.

In addition, a new Regulation 20A is inserted to ensure that the receipt of funds of the LIA and the Libyan Africa Investment Portfolio, which UN Security Council Resolution 1970 (2011) requires to be treated as frozen, is prohibited within the United Kingdom or by a United Kingdom person.

These Regulations also amend regulations 5, 23 and 25 to 30 of the Libya Regulations to introduce a new power to designate persons for a targeted arms embargo.

These Regulations also amend regulation 6 (designation criteria) of the Libya Regulations to specify additional activities which threaten the peace, stability and security of Libya or undermine its transition to a democratic, peaceful and independent country, for which a person may be designated.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. An impact assessment was, however, produced for the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 and can be found at: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/653271/Sanctions_and_Anti-Money_Laundering_Bill_Impact_Assessment_18102017.pdf.
