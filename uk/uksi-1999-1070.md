# The National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) (Scotland) Order 1999

Made: 26th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 105(6) of and paragraph 25(1) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service as a whole to make this Order[^f00002], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 25(3) of the said Schedule 7A[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) (Scotland) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Dissolution of NHS Trusts and consequent revocations

###### 2

The National Health Service Trusts specified in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby dissolved, and accordingly the Orders specified in Schedule 2 are hereby revoked.
