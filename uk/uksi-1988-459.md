# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (No. 2) Regulations 1988

Made: 8th March 1988

Laid before Parliament: 15th March 1988

Coming into force: 11th April 1988

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4, 20 and 25 of the Legal Aid Act 1974[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (No. 2) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 11th April 1988.

- (2) The Regulations specified in the first column of the Schedule shall be revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

###### 2

For the weekly sum of £56 specified in section 4(2) of the Legal Aid Act 1974[^f00002], there shall be substituted the weekly sum of £58.

###### 3

For the sums specified in Schedule 3 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (No. 2) 1980[^f00003], there shall be substituted the following sums:—

- Exceeding £58 but not exceeding £66 a week £5.

- Exceeding £66 but not exceeding £71 a week £12.

- Exceeding £71 but not exceeding £76 a week £17.

- Exceeding £76 but not exceeding £81 a week £23.

- Exceeding £81 but not exceeding £86 a week £28.

- Exceeding £86 but not exceeding £91 a week £33.

- Exceeding £91 but not exceeding £96 a week £39.

- Exceeding £96 but not exceeding £101 a week £44.

- Exceeding £101 but not exceeding £106 a week £50.

- Exceeding £106 but not exceeding £111 a week £55.

- Exceeding £111 but not exceeding £116 a week £61.

- Exceeding £116 but not exceeding £122 a week £67.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase from £56 to £58 a week the disposable income above which a person receiving legal advice and assistance under Part I of the Legal Aid Act 1974 is required to pay a contribution and amend the scale of contributions.
