# The Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (Amendment) (No. 2) (England and Wales) Rules 2004

Made: 31st March 2004

Laid before Parliament: 7th April 2004

Coming into force: 30th April 2004

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 36(2) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001] and after consulting the Electoral Commission pursuant to section 7(2)(e) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00002], hereby makes the following Rules:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (Amendment) (No. 2) (England and Wales) Rules 2004.

- (2) These Rules come into force on 30th April 2004.

- (3) These Rules extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of parishes and communities rules

###### 2

- (1) The rules in Schedule 3 to the Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) Rules 1986[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In the Appendix of Forms—

- (a) in the Directions as to printing the ballot paper, in paragraph 2(b), after the words “particulars of the candidates”, insert “and the horizontal rules separating the particulars of the candidates”; and

- (b) in the form of candidate’s consent to nomination, in paragraph (d), after the words “resided in that parish or community”, insert “or within 4.8 kilometres of it”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the First Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford — Minister of State, — Office of the Deputy Prime Minister — 31st March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules correct two errors in Schedule 3 to the Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) Rules 1986, which was inserted by the Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Rules 2004. The errors involve the omission of a few words from the form of candidate’s consent to nomination and from the directions as to how ballot papers are to be printed.
