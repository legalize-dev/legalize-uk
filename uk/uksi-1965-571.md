# The Local Government (Compensation) (Amendment) Regulations 1965

Made: 23rd March 1965

Laid before Parliament: 30th March 1965

Coming into Operation: 1st April 1965

The Minister of Housing and Local Government, as the Minister whom the Treasury have determined to be the appropriate Minister in relation to the persons to whom these regulations relate, in exercise of his powers under section 60(2) of the Local Government Act 1958 as amended by section 63 of the Police Act 1964, the said section 60(2) as applied by section 11(2) of the Public Libraries and Museums Act 1964 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following regulations:—

### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Compensation) (Amendment) Regulations 1965, and shall come into operation on 1st April 1965.

### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “*the regulations of 1963*” means the Local Government (Compensation) Regulations 1963[^f00001] ; and

- “*regulation*” means regulation contained in the regulations of 1963.

- (2) Unless the context otherwise requires, any reference in these regulations, or in the regulations of 1963 as amended by these regulations, to the provisions of any enactment shall be construed as a reference to those provisions as amended or re-enacted by any subsequent enactment.

- (3) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply to the interpretation of these regulations as it applies to the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These regulations amend the Local Government (Compensation) Regulations 1963 made under section 60(2) of the Local Government Act 1958, and extend the scope of those regulations under powers conferred by that section as amended by section 63 of the Police Act 1964 and section 11(2) of the Public Libraries and Museums Act 1964.

The principal amendments to the 1963 Regulations are as follows—

1. The regulations are applied to— officers of local valuation panels affected by local government reorganisation (regulations 3(2)(b) and (6), 4(1), 6, 7(2) and 8(2)); civilian employees of police authorities affected by amalgamations of such authorities under Part I of the Police Act 1964 (regulations 3(2)(a) and (4), 5, 7(1) and 8(1)); library officers affected by the Public Libraries and Museums Act 1964 (regulations 3(2)(b) and (5), 5, 7(1), 8(1) and 9).

2. Provision is made for the payment of retirement compensation to persons entitled under the regulations whose pension rights derive in part from statutory schemes and in part from schemes based on policies of insurance (regulation 18).

3. Persons claiming compensation are required to inform the compensating authority if they become entitled to pension benefits (regulation 19).

4. In assessing compensation no account is to be taken of any temporary increase of remuneration granted in connection with the reorganisation or other event which has led to the claim.

A number of other minor amendments are made to bring the regulations of 1963 in line with the London Government (Compensation) Regulations 1964 (S.I. 1964/1953).
