# The Motor Vehicles (Compulsory Insurance) (Information Centre and Compensation Body) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 17th October 2007

Laid before Parliament: 19th October 2007

Coming into force: 1st December 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to compulsory insurance in respect of, and other means of providing for, civil liability in relation to motor vehicles and trailers[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Compulsory Insurance) (Information Centre and Compensation Body) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st December 2007.

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Compulsory Insurance) (Information Centre and Compensation Body) Regulations 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Motor Vehicles (Compulsory Insurance) (Information Centre and Compensation Body) Regulations 2003[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1), omit the definition of “guarantee fund”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Jim Fitzpatrick — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 17th October 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Compulsory Insurance) (Information Centre and Compensation Body) Regulations 2003 by omitting the definition of “guarantee fund”, an expression which is not used elsewhere in the Regulations, from regulation 2(1). A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
