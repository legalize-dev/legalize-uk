# The North Korea (United Nations Sanctions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2017

Made: 15th November 2017

Laid before Parliament: 22nd November 2017

Coming into force: 13th December 2017

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of November 2017Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1 of the United Nations Act 1946[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the North Korea (United Nations Sanctions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2017 and comes into force on 13th December 2017.

- (2) In this Order, “the principal Order” means the North Korea (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2009[^f00002].

- (3) This Order extends to the United Kingdom.

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 2

The principal Order is amended in accordance with articles 3 and 4.

###### 3

In article 2—

- (a) for the definition of “the Council Regulation”, substitute—

> > - “the Council Regulation” means Council Regulation (EU) 2017/1509 of 30 August 2017 concerning restrictive measures against the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 329/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0329)[^f00003], as amended from time to time;

; and

- (b) in the appropriate places, insert—

> > - “lead and lead ore” means items listed in Annex XIb of the Council Regulation;

; and

> > - “seafood” means all seafood including fish, crustaceans, molluscs, and other aquatic invertebrates in all forms, as listed in Annex XIa of the Council Regulation;

###### 4

In paragraph 2 of article 5[^f00004]—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (f), delete “and”; and

- (b) for sub-paragraph (g) substitute—

> > (g) lead and lead ore;
> (h) seafood; and
> (i) any other goods, excluding food and medicine, which the relevant person has been informed by the Secretary of State could contribute to the development of the operational capabilities of the armed forces of North Korea.

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Articles 3 and 4 of this Order amend the North Korea (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1749](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1749)), as amended, giving effect to certain aspects of further United Nations sanctions imposed against North Korea by the United Nations Security Council in resolution 2371, adopted on 5th August 2017.

This Order amends the offences related to carriage of goods from North Korea to broaden the goods to which the offences apply, to now include seafood, lead and lead ore, as defined in Council Regulation (EU) 2017/1509 as amended.
