## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Articles 3 and 4 of this Order amend the North Korea (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1749](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1749)), as amended, giving effect to certain aspects of further United Nations sanctions imposed against North Korea by the United Nations Security Council in resolution 2371, adopted on 5th August 2017.

This Order amends the offences related to carriage of goods from North Korea to broaden the goods to which the offences apply, to now include seafood, lead and lead ore, as defined in Council Regulation (EU) 2017/1509 as amended.
