# The Isle of Wight National Health Service Primary Care Trust (Establishment) and Isle of Wight Healthcare National Health Service Trust and Isle of Wight Primary Care Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006

Made: 20th September 2006

Coming into force: 1st October 2006

The Secretary of State for Health considers it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 16A(5) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00003] and paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00004].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Isle of Wight National Health Service Primary Care Trust (Establishment) and Isle of Wight Healthcare National Health Service Trust and Isle of Wight Primary Care Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st October 2006.

- (2) In this Order—

- “new PCT” means the Isle of Wight National Health Service Primary Care Trust established by article 2;

- “old PCT” means the Isle of Wight Primary Care Trust established by the Isle of Wight Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2002[^f00005]; and

- “old trust” means the Isle of Wight Healthcare National Health Service Trust established by the Isle of Wight Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1996[^f00006].

#### Establishment and operational date

###### 2

- (1) There is established a Primary Care Trust called the Isle of Wight National Health Service Primary Care Trust.

- (2) The operational date of the trust is 1st October 2006.

#### Area of new PCT

###### 3

The new PCT is established for the area specified in the Schedule.

#### Dissolution

###### 4

The old trust and the old PCT are dissolved.

#### Accounts

###### 5

Any duty imposed on the old trust or the old PCT by or under section 98 of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00007], but not performed by 1st October 2006, shall be performed by the new PCT.

#### Winding up of affairs

###### 6

The new PCT shall take such action as may be necessary for the winding up of the affairs of the old trust and the old PCT.

#### Provision for continuity in the exercise of functions

###### 7

- (1) Anything done before 1st October 2006 by or in relation to the old trust or the old PCT shall be treated on and after that date as if done by or in relation to the new PCT.

- (2) The reference in paragraph (1) to anything done by or in relation to the old trust or the old PCT includes without limitation any applications made, any authorisations, directions or notices given, and any complaints made, to, by or in relation to the old trust or the old PCT.

- (3) Any instrument made by the old trust or the old PCT continues in force in relation to the new PCT in accordance with its terms until it is varied or revoked by the new PCT.

- (4) Any form supplied by the old trust or the old PCT, or any form supplied by the Secretary of State in relation to the old trust or the old PCT, continues to be a valid form in relation to the new PCT until it is cancelled or withdrawn by the Secretary of State or the new PCT, as if any reference contained in that form to the old trust or the old PCT were a reference to the new PCT.

- (5) So far as is necessary or appropriate, a reference in an agreement or other instrument to the old trust or the old PCT shall be treated on and after 1st October 2006 as a reference to the new PCT.

#### Revocations

###### 8

The Isle of Wight Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1996 and the Isle of Wight Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2002 are revoked.

## SCHEDULE — Area of Trust

The county of Isle of Wight.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Norman Warner — Minister of State — Department of Health — 20th September 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the establishment on 1st October 2006 of a Primary Care Trust for the Isle of Wight, to be called the Isle of Wight National Health Service Primary Care Trust (article 2).

It also provides for the dissolution on that date of the Isle of Wight Healthcare National Health Service Trust established on 11th November 1996, and the Isle of Wight Primary Care Trust established on 1st April 2001 (article 4). The Order makes provision at articles 5 and 6 for the Isle of Wight National Health Service Primary Care Trust to complete the accounts and wind up the affairs of the two bodies dissolved by this Order. There is provision for continuity in the exercise of the functions of these bodies at article 7.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as it has no impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies.
