# The Children and Young Persons Act 1963 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1968

Made: 30th October 1968

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 65(6) of the Children and Young Persons Act 1963, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

The remaining provisions of the Children and Young Persons Act 1963, which have not previously come into operation and are specified in the Schedule hereto (which relate to the employment of children and young persons) shall come into operation on 3rd March 1969 except that for the purposes of applying for licences under section 37 of the Act, and granting such licences to have effect on and after the said date, these provisions shall come into operation on 2nd December 1968.

###### 2

This Order may be cited as the Children and Young Persons Act 1963 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1968.

## SCHEDULE

| Provisions of the Act | Subject matter of provisions |
| --- | --- |
| Sections 34 to 44 | Employment of children and young persons. |
| In Schedule 3, paragraphs 4 to 10 and 29 to 32 | Minor and consequential amendments of the Children and Young Persons Act 1933 and the Children and Young Persons (Scotland) Act 1937. |
| In Schedule 4, paragraphs 3 and 4 | Transitional provisions. |
| So much of Schedule 5 as is set out in the Appendix hereto | Repeals. |

## Signed

James Callaghan — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 30th October 1968

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the remaining provisions of the Children and Young Persons Act 1963; the provisions brought into force are set out in the Schedule. These provisions will come into force on 3rd March 1969 except that for the purposes of applying for licences under section 37 and granting such licences to have effect on and after 3rd March 1969, these provisions will come into force on 2nd December 1968.
