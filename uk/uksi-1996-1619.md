# The Stansted Airport Aircraft Movement Limit (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 24th June 1996

Coming into force: 1st July 1996

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 32 of the Airports Act 1986, and after consultation with the Civil Aviation Authority, the airport operator and such of the other persons referred to in section 32(6) as he considered appropriate, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Stansted Airport Aircraft Movement Limit (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on the seventh day after the day on which it is made.

###### 2

In article 2 of the Stansted Airport Aircraft Movement Limit Order 1987 for the figure “78,000” there shall be substituted the figure “120,000”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Goschen — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — 24th June 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Stansted Airport Aircraft Movement Limit Order 1987 by increasing the limit on the number of occasions when aircraft may take-off or land at Stansted Airport from 78,000 to 120,000 per annum.
