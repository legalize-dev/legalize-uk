# The Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: at 1.25 p.m. on 2nd November 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 4.30 p.m. on 2nd November 2020

Coming into force: 23rd November 2020

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the environmental aspects of product design.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 23rd November 2020.

#### Amendment of the Energy Information Regulations 2011

###### 2

- (1) The table set out in paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 (EU measures) to the Energy Information Regulations 2011[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) For the entry relating to household combined washer-driers, substitute—

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This instrument amends the Energy Information Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1524](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1524)) in relation to household combined washer-dryers, household washing machines, household dishwashers, household refrigerating appliances and electronic displays. It substitutes new Commission Delegated Regulations into the list of EU measures in Schedule 1 to the Energy Information Regulations 2011 which allows them to be enforced by the market surveillance authorities.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
