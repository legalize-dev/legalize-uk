# The Childcare (Inspections) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 16th June 2009

Laid before Parliament: 24th June 2009

Coming into force: 31st July 2009

The Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 49(2)(a) and 104(2) of the Childcare Act 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Childcare (Inspections) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 31st July 2009.

#### Amendment to the Childcare (Inspections) Regulations 2008

###### 2

For regulation 3(2)(a) of the Childcare (Inspections) Regulations 2008[^f00002], substitute—

> > (a) on or before 31st July 2012, and

## Signed

Dawn Primarolo — Minister of State — Department for Children, Schools and Families — 16th June 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Childcare (Inspections) Regulations 2008 (“the principal Regulations”) to alter the prescribed interval for certain childcare inspections. The effect of the amendment is to require Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills to inspect all early years provision to which regulation 3 of the principal Regulations applies once before 1st August 2012. The amendment will ensure that early years provision which was inspected on or after 1st September 2008 and before 1st August 2009 is not required to be inspected again before 1st August 2012.
