# The Upper Spey and Associated Waters Protection (Renewal) Order 1993 Variation Order 1996

Made: 9th January 1996

Coming into force: 1st February 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(10) of the Freshwater and Salmon Fisheries (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Upper Spey and Associated Waters Protection (Renewal) Order 1993 Variation Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st February 1996.

#### Variation

###### 2

The Upper Spey and Associated Waters Protection (Renewal) Order 1993[^f00002] shall be varied by omitting, in article 4, the words “During the period from 6th January 1994 to 6th October 1996, both dates inclusive,”.

## Signed

D R Dickson — Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office — 9th January 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the prohibition contained in article 4 of the Upper Spey and Associated Waters Protection (Renewal) Order 1993 (“the 1993 Order”) which prohibits fishing for or taking freshwater fish in inland waters in the area prescribed without legal right or without written permission from someone having such a right.

The effect of the variation is to continue the 1993 Order in force for an unlimited period.

The prescribed area referred to consists of those parts of the catchment areas of the Rivers Spey, Spean and Tummel lying within Highland and Tayside Regions. The area is illustrated on a map attached to the Upper Spey and Associated Waters Protection Order 1982 (“the 1982 Order”) and is defined by reference to a detailed large scale map which may be inspected at the Scottish Office Agriculture, Environment and Fisheries Department, Pentland House, Robb’s Loan, Edinburgh on any working day between 10.00am and 4.00pm.

The map annexed to and forming part of this note gives a general guide to the boundaries of the prescribed area.

The 1982 Order was renewed in accordance with the Orders listed in the footnote to article 2.
