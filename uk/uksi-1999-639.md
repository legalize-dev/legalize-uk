# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Kent) (Borough of Maidstone) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1999

Made: 8th March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

NOW, the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(3) of Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Kent) (Borough of Maidstone) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal order” means the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Kent) (Borough of Maidstone) Order 1997[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal order

###### 3

- (1) The principal order shall be amended as follows.

- (2) Article 3 shall be omitted.

- (3) In article 4 for the words “the area to which this order applies in accordance with article 3 above” there shall be substituted “the Borough of Maidstone”.

- (4) Schedules 1 and 2 shall be omitted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Glenda Jackson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 8th March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Kent) (Borough of Maidstone) Order 1997 designated a permitted parking area and a special parking area in part of the Borough of Maidstone. This Order amends the 1997 Order so as to extend the permitted and special parking areas to the whole of the Borough of Maidstone.
