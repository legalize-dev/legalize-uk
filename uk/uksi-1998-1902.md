# The Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1998

Made: 30th July 1998

Coming into force: 1st September 1998

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 15(2) of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1998.

###### 2

Subsections (3) to (9) of section 3 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st September 1998.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, C. — Dated 30th July 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that subsections (3) to (9) of section 3 of the Protection from Harassment Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st September 1998.
