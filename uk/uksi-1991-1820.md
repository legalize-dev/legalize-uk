# The Broadcasting (Prescribed Countries) Order 1991

Made: 5th August 1991

Coming into force: 3rd October 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 43 of the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Broadcasting (Prescribed Countries) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 3rd October 1991.

###### 2

The countries listed in the Schedule to this Order are hereby specified as prescribed countries for the purposes of section 43 of the Broadcasting Act 1990.

## SCHEDULE

#### PRESCRIBED COUNTRIES

- Belgium

- Denmark

- Federal Republic of Germany

- France

- Greece

- Ireland

- Italy

- Luxembourg

- Netherlands

- Portugal

- Spain

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 5th August 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Broadcasting Act 1990 describes one type of television programme service under that Act as a non-domestic satellite service. Part of the definition of this service is that it consists in the transmission of television programmes by satellite for general reception in the United Kingdom or in a prescribed country (or both) where the programmes are transmitted from a place which is either in the United Kingdom or is neither in the United Kingdom nor in any prescribed country.

A prescribed country is any country specified as such in this Order.
