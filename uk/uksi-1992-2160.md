# The United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting (Membership Proposal) Approval Order 1992

Made: 8th September 1992

Laid before Parliament: 9th September 1992

Coming into force: 30th September 1992

Now, therefore, in exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 2(4) of the Nurses, Midwives and Health Visitors Act 1992, I hereby signify my approval of the proposal that after the coming into force of section 1 of that Act the United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting shall consist of sixty members.

This Order may be cited as the United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting (Membership Proposal) Approval Order 1992.

## Signed

Virginia Bottomley — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 8th September 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order approves the proposal of the United Kingdom Central Council for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting that its membership shall consist of sixty members after the coming into force of section 1 of the Nurses, Midwives and Health Visitors Act 1992. By virtue of sub-section (2) of that section, forty of the sixty members would be appointed by the Secretary of State following an election, and twenty would be appointed by the Secretary of State in accordance with sub-sections (4) and (5) of that section (which lay down criteria for meeting the appointments).
