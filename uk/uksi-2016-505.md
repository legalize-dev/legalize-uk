# The Approval of Code of Management Practice (Private Retirement Housing) (England) Order 2016

Made: 18th April 2016

Laid before Parliament: 20th April 2016

Coming into force: 1st June 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 87 and 100 of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993[^f00001], and being satisfied that appropriate arrangements have been made for publishing the code approved by this Order, makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Approval of Code of Management Practice (Private Retirement Housing) (England) Order 2016 and shall come into force on 1st June 2016.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to the management of residential properties in England only.

#### Approval of code of practice

###### 2

With the exception of Appendix 3, “Private Retirement Housing Code of Practice - England” (ISBN 978-0-9526691-7-3), which is to be published by the Association of Retirement Housing Managers[^f00002], is approved.

#### Revocation of order and withdrawal of approval

###### 3

The Approval of Code of Management Practice (Private Retirement Housing) (England) Order 2005[^f00003] is revoked and the approval given by that Order[^f00004] is withdrawn.

#### Saving and transitional provision

###### 4

Articles 2 and 3 do not apply for the purposes of section 87(7)(a) and (b) of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (use of code in proceedings) in relation to an act or omission alleged to have occurred before this Order comes into force.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Brandon Lewis — Minister of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 18th April 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

By this Order the Secretary of State approves, subject to the exception mentioned below, a code of practice relating to the management of residential property by landlords and others who discharge the management function. The approved code is the “Private Retirement Housing Code of Practice – England” (ISBN 978-0-9526691-7-3) by the Association of Retirement Housing Managers (“ARHM”). Appendix 3 of that code has not been approved as it is not relevant for the purposes of section 87 of the 1993 Act.

By this Order the Secretary of State also withdraws approval for “The Code of Practice for Private Retirement Housing” (ISBN 0-9526691-2-9) by the same association which was given by the Approval of Code of Management Practice (Private Retirement Housing) (England) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/3307](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3307)). That Order is revoked.

Failure to comply with any provision of any approved code does not of itself render any person liable to any proceedings, but in any proceedings, the codes of practice shall be admissible as evidence and any provision that appears to be relevant to any question arising in the proceedings is taken into account.

The approval and withdrawal of approval provided for in this Order apply to the management of residential properties in England only. By article 4 of this Order, the “The Code of Practice for Private Retirement Housing” (ISBN 0-9526691-2-9) continues to have effect for the purposes of proceedings relating to acts or omissions that are alleged to have occurred before this Order comes into force.

No impact assessment has been prepared in relation to this Order. An electronic version of the approved code is available free of charge at www.arhm.org. A printed version is also available from ARHM at a cost of £20.
