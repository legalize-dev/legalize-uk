# The Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 (Isle of Man) Order 1991

Made: 19th November 1991

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of November 1991 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 6(4) of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 (Isle of Man) Order 1991 and shall come into force on the day on which sections 1 to 5 of the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 come into force in accordance with an order under section 6(2) of that Act.

###### 2

The Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 shall extend to the Isle of Man with the modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order (being modifications which appear to Her Majesty to be appropriate).

## SCHEDULE — MODIFICATIONS IN THE EXTENSION OF THE ARMS CONTROL AND DISARMAMENT (INSPECTIONS) ACT 1991 TO THE ISLE OF MAN

###### 1

Any reference to a provision of the Arms Controland Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 shall be construed, unless the contrary intention appears, as a reference to it as it has effect in the Isle of Man.

###### 2

Any reference to the United Kingdom, except in the expression “Her Majesty’s Government in the United Kingdom”, shall be deemed to include a reference to the Isle of Man.

###### 3

In section 3, subsection (3) shall be omitted.

###### 4

In section 4, for subsection (2) there shall be substituted the following subsection:

> > (2) In subsection (1)–
>   - “Crown land” means land in which there is a Crown interest; and
>   - “private interest” means an interest which is not a Crown interest;
> and for this purpose–
> - “Crown interest” means an interest– belonging to Her Majesty in right of the Crown (including the Crown in right of the Government of the Isle of Man), or belonging to a government department of the United Kingdom or a Department or Statutory Board of the Government of the Isle of Man, or an interest held in trust for Her Majesty for the purposes of any such government department, Department or Statutory Board.

###### 5

In section 5–

- (a) in subsection (7), at the end of the definition of “enactment”, there shall be added the words “, an Act of Tynwald and an instrument made under an Act of Tynwald”, and

- (b) at the end there shall be added the following subsection:

> > (8) In relation to the Isle of Man, any reference in this section to any privilege or immunity enjoyed by diplomatic agents in accordance with any provision of the 1961 Articles shall be construed as a reference to the privilege or immunity which would be so enjoyed if the Isle of Man were a part of the United Kingdom.

###### 6

In section 6, subsections (2) to (4) shall be omitted.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Arms Control and Disarmament (Inspections) Act 1991 to the Isle of Man with the modifications specified in the Schedule to the Order.
