# The Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) (Didsbury) Order 2003

Made: 22nd October 2003

Coming into force: 12th November 2003

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1 and 5 of, and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 1 to, the Act hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) (Didsbury) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 12th November 2003.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 1991 Act” means the Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) Act 1991[^f00003]; and

- “the plans and sections” means the plans and sections certified by the Secretary of State as the plans and sections for the purposes of this Order.

#### Double lines

###### 3

- (1) In the description of Work No. 5 in section 4 of the 1991 Act, for the words “(single line)” there shall be substituted “(single or double line)”.

- (2) In so far as they relate to Work No. 5 in section 4 of the 1991 Act, the plans and sections shall be deemed to be the deposited plans and sections for the purposes of the 1991 Act.

#### Certification of plans

###### 4

Greater Manchester Passenger Transport Executive shall, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the plans and sections to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of the plans and sections referred to in this Order; and a document so certified shall be admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.

## Signed

Signed by authority of theSecretary of State for Transport

Ellis Harvey — Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit, — Department for Transport — 22nd October 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order modifies the Greater Manchester (Light Rapid Transit System) Act 1991 to enable Greater Manchester Passenger Transport Executive to construct as a single or double line part of the Metrolink extension to Didsbury which was authorised as a single line by that Act.

A copy of the plans and sections is available for inspection free of charge during working hours at GMPTE, 9 Portland Street, Piccadilly Gardens, Manchester M60 1HX.
