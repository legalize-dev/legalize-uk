# The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (East London River Crossing (A13 to A2)) Orders 1988 and 1991, Revocation Order 1997

Made: 7th March 1997

Coming into force: 26th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10, 41 and 106(1) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001]and now vested in him[^f00002]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (East London River Crossing (A13 to A2)) Orders 1988 and 1991, Revocation Order 1997 and shall come into force on 26th March 1997.

###### 2

The following Orders are hereby revoked:

- (a) The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (East London River Crossing (A13 to A2) Trunk Road and Slip Roads) Order 1988[^f00003];

- (b) The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (East London River Crossing (A13 to A2) Supplementary Trunk Road and Slip Roads) Order 1988[^f00004];

- (c) The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (East London River Crossing (A13 to A2) Slip Roads) Order 1991[^f00005]; and

- (e) The A406 London North Circular Trunk Road (East London River Crossing (A13 to A2) No. 2 Bridge) Order 1991[^f00006].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

D. W. Ward — Director Road Programme Directorate, Southern Operations — 7th March 1997
