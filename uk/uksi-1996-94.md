# The Land Registers (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1996

Made: 11th January 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(2) of the Land Registers (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Land Registers (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1996.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The Land Registers (Scotland) Act 1995 shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 11th January 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Land Registers (Scotland) Act 1995 on 1st April 1996.
