# The Mount Vernon and Watford Hospitals and the St. Albans and Hemel Hempstead National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2000

Made: 13th March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Mount Vernon and Watford Hospitals and the St. Albans and Hemel Hempstead National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

#### Dissolution of the NHS trusts

###### 2

The Mount Vernon and Watford Hospitals National Health Service Trust established by the Mount Vernon and Watford Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00005] and the St. Albans and Hemel Hempstead National Health Service Trust established by the St. Albans and Hemel Hempstead National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00006] are hereby dissolved, and accordingly those Orders are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 13th March 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 2000 of the Mount Vernon and Watford Hospitals and the St. Albans and Hemel Hempstead National Health Service Trusts.

This Order should be read in conjunction with the West Hertfordshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00007].
