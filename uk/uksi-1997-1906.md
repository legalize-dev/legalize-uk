# The Knives Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997

Made: 25th July 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 11(3) of the Knives Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Knives Act 1997 (Commencement) (No. 1) Order 1997.

###### 2

Sections 1 to 7, 9 and 10 of the Knives Act 1997 shall come into force on 1st September 1997.

## Signed

Alun Michael — Minister of State — 25th July 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st September 1997 sections 1 to 7, 9 and 10 of the Knives Act 1997. Section 11 came into force on Royal Assent (19th March 1997).
