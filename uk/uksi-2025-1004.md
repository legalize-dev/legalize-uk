# The Non-Contentious Probate (Amendment) Rules 2025

Made: 5th September 2025

Laid before Parliament: 9th September 2025

Coming into force: 3rd November 2025

The President of the Family Division of the High Court (the judicial office holder[^f00001] nominated by the Lord Chief Justice), with the agreement of the Lord Chancellor, makes these Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by section 127 of the [Senior Courts Act 1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/54)[^f00002] and section 2(5) of the Colonial Probates Act 1892[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the Non-Contentious Probate (Amendment) Rules 2025 and come into force on 3rd November 2025.

- (2) These Rules extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the Non-Contentious Probate Rules 1987

###### 2

The [Non-Contentious Probate Rules 1987](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024)[^f00004] are amended in accordance with the following rules.

#### Amendment to rule 2 (interpretation)

###### 3

In [rule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/2/enacted)[^f00005], in the definition of “online portal”[^f00006], for the words from “or 5A” to the end, substitute “, 5A (alternative online procedure for personal applications) or 36 (grants to trust corporations and other corporate bodies)”.

#### Amendment to rule 4 (application for grants through solicitors or probate practitioners)

###### 4

In [rule 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/4/enacted)[^f00007], in paragraph (5), after “Wales” insert “or Scotland”.

#### Amendments to rule 27 (grants where two or more persons entitled in the same degree)

###### 5

In rule 27[^f00008]—

- (a) after paragraph (6), insert—

> > (6A) In resolving a dispute between persons entitled to a grant of administration in the same degree, the district judge or registrar may apply the following order of priority, namely—
>   (a) the person with the support of the majority of those entitled in the same degree;
>   (b) of the persons entitled in the same degree, the person who first lodges an application with the registry;
>   (c) a neutral person agreed by the parties to the dispute;
>   (d) a neutral person appointed by the registry in accordance with directions.

- (b) after paragraph (8), insert—

> > (9) In paragraph (6A), “*a neutral person*” means any person not entitled to a grant of administration in the same degree as the parties in dispute.

#### Amendments to rule 36 (grants to trust corporations and other corporate bodies)

###### 6

In rule 36[^f00009]—

- (a) before paragraph (1) insert—

> > (A1) An application for a grant to a trust corporation must be made using the online portal.
> (A2) An application made under paragraph (A1) must be made by completing and sending the online application form provided through the online portal and electronically paying the appropriate fee.
> (A3) Where original documents are required to be sent in support of an application made under paragraph (A1), these must be sent separately to the registry in accordance with instructions given by the registry.

- (b) in paragraph (2)—

- (i) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “affidavits or”;

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (b), for “lodged” substitute “filed”;

- (c) in paragraph (3), for “lodged” substitute “filed”.

#### Amendments to rule 41 (amendment and revocation of grant)

###### 7

In rule 41[^f00010]—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “paragraph (2)” substitute “paragraphs (2) and (3)”;

- (b) after paragraph (2) insert—

> > (3) Where, by reason of official error or delay, a grant has been issued (save for a grant under rule 52(b)) despite the entry or receipt in the registry of a caveat at the time of issue, a district judge or registrar must make an order revoking that grant.

#### Amendments to rule 44 (caveats)

###### 8

In rule 44[^f00011]—

- (a) omit paragraph (2)(a) and the “or” immediately after that sub-paragraph;

- (b) in paragraph (6), at the end, insert “which must be accompanied by a statement in support of the directions sought”;

- (c) after paragraph (6) insert—

> > (6A) In disposing of any summons for directions under paragraph (6), where two or more persons are entitled to a grant of administration in the same degree, the district judge or registrar may apply the order of priority set out in rule 27(6A).

- (d) in paragraph (7)—

- (i) for “hearing” substitute “disposal”;

- (ii) for “above” substitute “, whether by hearing or otherwise,”.

#### Amendment to rule 49 (address for service)

###### 9

In rule 49, after “Wales”, insert “or Scotland”.

#### Amendment to the First Schedule (forms)

###### 10

In the [First Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024/schedule/FIRST), for Form 4 (warning to caveator)[^f00012], substitute Form 4 in [the Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/schedule/enacted) to these Rules.

#### Amendments to the Third Schedule (exceptions to the requirement to use the online portal for applications for grants made through solicitors or probate practitioners)

###### 11

In the [Third Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024/schedule/THIRD)[^f00013]—

- (a) after “rule 27” insert “(4) to (8)”;

- (b) omit “A grant under rule 36 (Grants to trust corporations and other corporate bodies).”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

Explanatory Note

These Rules make amendments to the [Non-Contentious Probate Rules 1987](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) ([S.I. 1987/2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024)) (“*the 1987 Rules*”).

[Rule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/3/enacted) amends the definition of “online portal” in rule 2 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) to include a reference to applications made under rule 36 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024).

[Rule 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/4/enacted) amends rule 4 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) to enable a solicitor or probate practitioner based in Scotland, who is entitled to carry out probate activities in England and Wales, to provide an address of their place of business in Scotland when making applications for a grant on behalf of a client. [Rule 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/9/enacted) makes a similar amendment to rule 49 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) in relation to the provision of an address for service.

[Rule 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/5/enacted) amends rule 27 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) by introducing an order of priority that a district judge or registrar can choose to apply to a dispute between two or more people that are equally entitled to apply for a grant of administration. [Rule 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/6/enacted) amends rule 36 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) to require all applications for a grant to trust corporations to be made using the online portal. [Rule 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/7/enacted) amends rule 41 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) to require a district judge or registrar to revoke a grant issued in error, where a caveat had been entered at the time of the grant being issued.

[Rule 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/8/enacted) amends rule 44 of [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) to remove the ability to enter a caveat in person at a registry and to require a caveator wishing to issue and serve a summons for directions to also provide a statement in support of the directions they are seeking. It also amends rule 44, to enable a district judge or registrar to apply the new order of priority inserted into rule 27 when disposing of a summons for directions in cases where two or more people are equally entitled to apply for a grant of administration.

[Rule 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/10/enacted) replaces Form 4, in the [First Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024/schedule/FIRST) to [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024), and [rule 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1004/rule/11/enacted) amends the [Third Schedule](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024/schedule/THIRD) to [the 1987 Rules](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/2024) to remove certain exceptions to the requirement that applications made by solicitors and probate practitioners are made through the online portal.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
