# The Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Anguilla) Order 2019

Made: 10th April 2019

Coming into force: 8th May 2019

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 10th day of April 2019

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 108 of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 .

Her Majesty, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, orders as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Anguilla) Order 2019 and comes into force on 8th May 2019.

#### Extension of provisions of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 to Anguilla

###### 2

The provisions of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (“*the Act*”) specified in the first column of the table in the Schedule extend to Anguilla with the modifications specified in the second column of that table.
