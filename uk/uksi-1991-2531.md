# The Mining Industry Act 1926 (Metrication) Regulations 1991

Made: 7th November 1991

Laid before Parliament: 8th November 1991

Coming into force: 30th November 1991

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001]for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002]in relation to units of measurement to be used for economic, health, safety or administrative purposes, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) and (4) of the said Act of 1972, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Mining Industry Act 1926 (Metrication) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 30th November 1991.

#### Mining Industry Act 1926

###### 2

On and after 1st January 1995, in section 23(1) of the Mining Industry Act 1926[^f00003], for the words “one hundred feet” there shall be substituted “30.480 metres”.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — Secretary of State for Education and Science — 7th November 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement, for the purposes of the Mining Act 1926, Council Directive No. [80/181/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0181) (O.J. 1980, L39, 15.2.80, p.40) as amended by Council Directive No. [89/617/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0617) (O.J. 1989, No. L357, 7.12.89, p.28), which establishes the legal units of measurement to be used throughout the Community. These Regulations change, with effect on and after 1st January 1995, the reference in section 23(1) of the Mining Act 1926 from feet to the equivalent distance expressed in metres (regulation 2).
