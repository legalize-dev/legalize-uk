# The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 2023

Made: 17th May 2023

Coming into force: 18th May 2023

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of May 2023Present,The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Him by section 63(3)(b) of the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 2023 and comes into force on 18th May 2023.

- (2) This Order extends to the Isle of Man.

#### Revocation of the Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Isle of Man) Order 2021

###### 2

The Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Isle of Man) Order 2021[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) (Isle of Man) Order 2021 ([S.I. 2021/1257](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/1257)) (“*the 2021 Order*”). That Order extended to the Isle of Man, with modifications, the Republic of Belarus (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/600](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/600)) as amended from time to time.

In consequence of legislation now made by the Isle of Man to the same effect, the 2021 Order is now redundant and is therefore revoked.
