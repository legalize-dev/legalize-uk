# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Herts Cable Limited) (West Hertfordshire) Order 1990

Made: 3rd September 1990

Laid before Parliament: 5th September 1990

Coming into force: 3rd October 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Herts Cable Limited) (West Hertfordshire) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 3rd October 1990.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Douglas Hogg — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 3rd September 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Herts Cable Ltd. a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the West Hertfordshire area. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 5th September 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Herts Cable Limited will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its West Hertfordshire public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
