# The Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002

Made: 4th May 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 127(1) of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002.

###### 2

Part 7 of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 (sections 58 to 75, together with Schedules 5 and 6) shall come into force on 31st May 2002.

## Signed

John Denham — Minister of State — 4th May 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings Part 7 of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 (security of pathogens and toxins) into force on 31st May 2002.
