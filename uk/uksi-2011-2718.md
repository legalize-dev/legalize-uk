## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland, only “designated” Ministers and departments can exercise the powers in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act [1972 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68) to make orders, rules, regulations and schemes. This is one of a series of Orders by which Ministers and departments are designated for the purposes of section 2(2) in relation to different subject areas.

Article 2 designates the Treasury in relation to discrimination.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
