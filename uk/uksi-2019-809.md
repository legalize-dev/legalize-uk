# The Animal Health, Plant Health, Seeds and Seed Potatoes (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 4th April 2019

Laid before Parliament: 5th April 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft of the instrument being laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — Introductory

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Health, Plant Health, Seeds and Seed Potatoes (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force as follows—

- (a) as regards this Part, Part 3 and regulation 8, immediately before exit day;

- (b) as regards Part 2 and regulations 6 and 7, other than regulation 7(15)(a), on exit day;

- (a) as regards regulation 7(15)(a), on the day one year after the day on which exit day falls.

## PART 2 — Animal Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular section 8(2)(b), (c), (d) and (g)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

Chapter 1 of Part 2 amends requirements of Commission Implementing Decision 2014/709 concerning animal health control measures relating to African swine fever.

Chapter 2 of Part 2 makes an amendment to the Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (England) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/731](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/731)) consequential on the United Kingdom’s withdrawal from the European Union.

Part 3 makes amendments to subordinate legislation relating to plant health. Regulation 4 amends the Plant Health (Amendment) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/786](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/786)) to make further amendments to the Plant Health (Forestry) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/2517](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/2517)) (in relation to England) and the Plant Health (England) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/610](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/610)). Regulation 5 amends the Plant Health (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/787](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/787)).

Part 4 makes amendments to secondary legislation regulating the marketing of agricultural seed, seed potatoes and fruit plant and propagating material.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
