# The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) (No. 2) Order 1992

Made: 21st February 1992

Laid before Parliament: 3rd March 1992

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 8(1)(b) and (1A) and 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) (No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force—

- (a) on 1st April 1992 for all purposes other than those specified in paragraph (b) of this article;

- (b) on 1st April 1993 for the purposes of the abolition of Camberwell Health Authority, Lewisham and North Southwark Health Authority and West Lambeth Health Authority and the establishment of South East London Health Authority.

#### Abolition of Authorities

###### 2

The District Health Authorities listed in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby abolished.

#### Establishment and names of new Authorities

###### 3

For each of the districts determined by the National Health Service (Determination of Districts) Order 1981[^f00002] and specified in column (1) of Schedule 2 to this Order there is hereby established a District Health Authority which shall be known by the name specified in relation to that district in column (2) of that Schedule.

#### Amendment of National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990

###### 4

In consequence of articles 2 and 3 of this Order the entries in columns (1) and (2) of Schedule 1 to the National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990[^f00003] shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) by the omission from Part 1 of the entries relating to Durham District and North West Durham District and the insertion after the entries relating to Northumberland District of the entries—

| North Durham | North Durham Health Authority |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order abolishes on 1st April 1992 the District Health Authorities named in Schedule 1 to the Order with the exception of Camberwell Health Authority, Lewisham and North Southwark Health Authority and West Lambeth Health Authority which are abolished on 1st April 1993. The Order establishes the six new District Health Authorities specified in Schedule 2 to the Order. The National Health Service (District Health Authorities) Order 1990 is amended to reflect these changes.

The changes made to District Health Authorities in this Order are reflected in changes to the districts of the abolished Health Authorities made by the National Health Service (Determination of Districts) (No. 2) Order 1992.
