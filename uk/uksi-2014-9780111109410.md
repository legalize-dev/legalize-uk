# The Urban Development Corporations in England (Area and Constitution) Order 2014

Made: 4th February 2014

Laid before Parliament: 10th February 2014

Coming into force: 31st March 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 134 and 135 of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 26 to, the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Urban Development Corporations in England (Area and Constitution) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 31st March 2014.

#### Revocations

###### 2

- (1) The following are revoked—

- (a) the Thurrock Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 2003[^f00002];

- (b) the London Thames Gateway Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 2004[^f00003]; and

- (c) article 2 of the WNDC Order.

- (2) The West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 2011[^f00004] is revoked with effect from 31st July 2014.

- (3) So much of the WNDC Order as has not been revoked is revoked with effect from 31st July 2014.

- (4) In this article “the WNDC Order” means the West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 2004[^f00005].

## Signed

Signed by the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Eric Pickles — Secretary of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 4th February 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Orders which established the urban development areas in Thurrock, the London Thames Gateway and West Northamptonshire and which designated urban development corporations for those areas.

No Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen
