# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Contamination of Feeding Stuff) (England) (No. 2) Amendment Order 1989

Made: 10th November 1989

Coming into force: 11th November 1989

Laid before Parliament: 13th November 1989

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by the said section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) and (3) of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Contamination of Feeding Stuff) (England) (No. 2) Amendment Order 1989 and shall come into force on 11th November 1989.

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 2

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Contamination of Feeding Stuff) (England) (No. 2) Order 1989 shall be amended by substituting for the Schedule to that Order (the designated area) the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — SUBSTITUTING NEW SCHEDULE TO THE PRINCIPAL ORDER

## Signed

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 10th November 1989.

Derek Andrews — Permanent Secretary Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Contamination of Feeding Stuff) (England) (No. 2) Order 1989 (“the principal Order”) which contains emergency prohibitions restricting various activities in order to prevent human consumption of food rendered unsuitable for that purpose in consequence of the escape of lead into imported animal feeding stuff.

The Order substitutes a new Schedule to the principal Order (article 2 and Schedule). Certain farms, holdings and premises are removed from the designated area within which certain activities are prohibited by the principal Order. Further farms, holdings and premises (shown in italics in the new Schedule) are designated. Some corrections are made to the description of areas already designated. Movement of cattle and food from any farm, holding or premises described in the new Schedule is controlled by the principal Order which also imposes certain other restrictions throughout the United Kingdom.
