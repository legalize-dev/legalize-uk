# The Mental Health (Discrimination) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2013

Made: 8th July 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 4(2) of the Mental Health (Discrimination) Act 2013[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Mental Health (Discrimination) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2013.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

Section 2 of the Mental Health (Discrimination) Act 2013 comes into force on 15th July 2013.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

Helen Grant — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 8th July 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order brings section 2 of the Mental Health (Discrimination) Act [2013 (c.8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/8) into force. Section 2 amends the Juries Act [1974 (c.23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/23) in relation to qualification for jury service based on mental health.
