# The Non-Domestic Rates (No.2) (Scotland) Order 1993

Made: 23rd March 1993

Laid before Parliament: 24th March 1993

Coming into force: 1st April 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 7A(1), 35(2) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rates (No.2) (Scotland) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

#### Non-domestic rates for 1993-94

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7A of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Secretary of State prescribes in respect of the financial year 1993-94 for each local authority set out in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order the non-domestic rate, expressed as an amount of pence per pound, set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Order 1993[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the non-domestic rate for the financial year 1993-94 for each local authority in Scotland listed in the Schedule.

The Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Order 1993 (which previously prescribed the non-domestic rate for that year for each authority) is revoked.
