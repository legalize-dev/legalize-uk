# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Edinburgh) (No. 3) Regulations 2026

Made: 20th November 2026

Coming into force: 29th December 2026

The Secretary of State has decided that it is necessary in the public interest to restrict the flying of unmanned aircraft in the area specified in regulation 3(1) by reason of the intending gathering there of a large number of persons.

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 239 of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Edinburgh) (No. 3) Regulations 2026 and they come into force on 29th December 2026.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The times referred to in regulation 3(2) are Co-ordinated Universal Time[^f00002].

#### Restricted airspace

###### 3

- (1) Subject to paragraph (3), during the periods specified in paragraph (2) no unmanned aircraft is to fly below 1,600 feet above mean sea level within the area bounded by a circle having a radius of 1 nautical mile which is centred at 555655N 0031202W.

- (2) The periods referred to in paragraph (1) begin with—

- (a) 1700 hours on 29 December 2026 and end with 0100 hours on 30 December 2026, and

- (b) 1800 hours on 31 December 2026 and end with 0200 hours on 01 January 2027.

- (3) Paragraph (1) does not apply to any unmanned aircraft flying in accordance with a permission issued by the Police Scotland Aviation Safety and Security Unit.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations impose restrictions on the flying of unmanned aircraft in the vicinity of Edinburgh, where the gathering of a large number of persons is due to take place for the Torchlight Parade and Hogmanay celebrations. The Civil Aviation Authority and the Department for Transport agree that it is necessary in the public interest to restrict the flying of unmanned aircraft in that area for reasons of public safety and security.

The times referred to in regulation 3(2) are Co-ordinated Universal Time (UTC) which is the same as Greenwich Mean Time (GMT).

In relation to the permission referred to in regulation 3(3), the Police Scotland Aviation Safety and Security Unit may be contacted as notified.

Full details of these Regulations will be promulgated by Aeronautical Information Circular (AIC) and Notice to Aviation (NOTAM).

The point of contact in the Civil Aviation Authority is Mr Thomas Shainberg, Airspace Regulator, Safety and Airspace Regulation Group, on telephone number 03301 382515.
