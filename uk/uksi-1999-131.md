# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999

Made: 23rd January 1999

Laid before Parliament: 26th January 1999

Coming into force

NOW, the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(3) of Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999.

- (2) Paragraph 4 of Schedule 2 and article 5, so far as it relates to that paragraph, shall come into force on 16th February 1999 and the remainder of this Order shall come into force on 5th April 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order–

- “the 1984 Act” means the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00003];

- “the 1991 Act” means the Road Traffic Act 1991; and

- “the parking area” means the area designated as a permitted parking area and as a special parking area by article 4 of this Order.

#### Application

###### 3

This Order applies to the City of Manchester excluding–

- (a) all trunk roads in the City;

- (b) the lengths of road described in Part I of Schedule 1 to this Order; and

- (c) that part of the City described in Part II of Schedule 1.

#### Designation of permitted parking area and special parking area

###### 4

The Secretary of State hereby designates the area to which this Order applies as–

- (a) a permitted parking area; and

- (b) a special parking area.

#### Modification and application of Part II of the 1991 Act

###### 5

Sections 66, 69 to 74, 78, 79 and 82 of, and Schedule 6 to, the 1991 Act shall apply in relation to the parking area and as so applied shall have effect subject to the modifications specified in Schedule 2 to this Order.

#### Modification of other provisions

###### 6

The 1984 Act shall be modified in relation to the parking area as specified in Schedule 3 to this Order.

## PART II — AREA IN THE VICINITY OF MANCHESTER AIRPORT EXCLUDED FROM THE PARKING AREA

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the City of Manchester, excluding trunk roads, the lengths of road described in Part I of Schedule 1 and the area around Manchester Airport described in Part II of that Schedule, as both a permitted parking area and a special parking area in accordance with Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Act 1991 and applies with modifications various provisions of Part II of that Act to the designated area. In particular section 73 of the Road Traffic Act 1991 is modified so as to provide for the appointment of parking adjudicators by a joint committee of local authorities of which Manchester City Council is a constituent authority. The Order also modifies the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 in relation to the designated area.
