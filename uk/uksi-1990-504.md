# The Sea Fish Licensing (Variation) Order 1990

Made: 7th March 1990

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 1990

Coming into force: 11th March 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with the sea fishing industry in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 4, 15(3), 20(1) and 22(2)(a) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Sea Fish Licensing (Variation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 11th March 1990.

#### Variation of the Sea Fish Licensing Order 1989

###### 2

The Sea Fish Licensing Order 1989[^f00002] shall be varied by substituting for Schedule 2 thereto (areas of seaand descriptions of sea fish in respect of which prohibition of fishing without licence applies) the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — CONTAINING NEW SCHEDULE 2 TO THE SEA FISH LICENSING ORDER 1989

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 7th March 1990.

John Selwyn Gummer — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Sanderson of Bowden — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 7th March 1990

Peter Walker — Secretary of State for Wales — 7th March 1990

Peter Bottomley — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Northern Ireland — 7th March 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Sea Fish Licensing Order 1989 which prohibits fishing, subject to exceptions, by British fishing boats in specified areas of sea for the principal species of sea fish, unless authorised by a licence.

This Order extends the scope of that prohibition to include fishing for all species of sea fish in ICES IIa (Norwegian Sea) and IV (North Sea) (article 2 and Schedule).
