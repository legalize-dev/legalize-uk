# The Education (Student Loans) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998

Made: 11th August 1998

Laid before Parliament: 12th August 1998

Coming into force: 1st September 1998

In exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(1) and (1A) of Schedule 2 to the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 1st September 1998.

###### 2

The Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1998[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

###### 3

In regulation 8(5), for “£4” there shall be substituted “£4.50”.

## Signed

Andrew Smith — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 11th August 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1998 so as to increase the payment to be made by a lender under those Regulations to the governing body of an institution for sending a completed and certified eligibility form, from £4 to £4.50.
