# The Annual Close Time (Lossie Salmon Fishery District) Order 1989

Made: 31st July 1989

Coming into force: 21st August 1989

The Secretary of State, on application to him by the Lossie District Salmon Fishery Board, having carried out all necessary consultations and complied with all necessary procedures as required by paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 to the Salmon Act 1986([^f00001]) as applied by section 6(6) of that Act (there being no objections or representations duly made with respect to the proposals in the application), in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 6(3) of that Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Annual Close Time (Lossie Salmon Fishery District) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 21st August 1989.

#### Annual close time dates and period for salmon fishing by rod and line

###### 2

For the Lossie salmon fishery district—

- (a) the dates of the annual close time are prescribed as 27th August to 24th February; and

- (b) the period within that time when it is permitted to fish for and take salmon by rod and line is prescribed as the period from 27th August to 31st October, both dates inclusive.

## Signed

T. J. Kelly — Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office — 31st July 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

In relation to the Lossie salmon fishery district this Order prescribes—

- (a) 27th August to 24th February as the dates of its annual close time, and

- (b) the period from 27th August to 31st October within that annual close time during which fishing for salmon by rod and line is permitted.
