# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 10 and Saving Provisions) Order 2005

Made: 5th July 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 330(4)(b), 336(3) and (4) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 10 and Saving Provisions) Order 2005.

- (2) In this Order “the 2003 Act” means the Criminal Justice Act 2003.

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (3), the provisions of the 2003 Act specified in paragraph (2) shall come into force in relation to Northern Ireland[^f00002] on 15th July 2005.

- (2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are —

- (a) section 32 (initial duty of disclosure by prosecutor);

- (b) section 33(2) (defence disclosure);

- (c) section 36 (further provisions about defence disclosure);

- (d) section 37 (continuing duty of disclosure by prosecutor);

- (e) section 38 (application by defence for disclosure);

- (f) section 39 (faults in defence disclosure), except in so far as it inserts new section 11(4), (7) and (11) of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996[^f00003];

- (g) section 331 (further minor and consequential amendments) to the extent necessary to bring into force the provisions of Schedule 36 (further minor and consequential amendments) specified in sub-paragraph (i);

- (h) section 332 (repeals) to the extent necessary to bring into force the provisions of Schedule 37 (repeals) specified in sub-paragraph (j);

- (i) Part 3 of Schedule 36 (further minor and consequential amendments: disclosure); and

- (j) Part 3 of Schedule 37 (repeals: disclosure).

- (3) The coming into force of the provisions referred to in paragraph (2) is of no effect in relation to alleged offences into which a criminal investigation within the meaning of section 1(4) of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996 has begun before 15th July 2005.

###### 3

- (1) Section 307(4) (enforcement of regulations implementing Community legislation on endangered species)[^f00004] shall come into force on 21st July 2005.

## Signed

Fiona Mactaggart — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 5th July 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 which are listed in articles 2(2) and 3 on 15th July and 21st July 2005 respectively. Article 2(3) provides a saving provision in relation to the commencement of the provisions referred to in article 2(2) in respect of pre-commencement criminal investigations.
