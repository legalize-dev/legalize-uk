# The Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 23rd June 1999

Laid before Parliament: 25th June 1999

Coming into force: 16th August 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Regulations:–

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 16th August 1999.

###### 2

Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1998[^f00002] shall be amended by the insertion at the appropriate place of “Campus Accommodation Ltd”.

## Signed

George Mudie — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 22nd June 1999

Jon Owen Jones — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 23rd June 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 to the Assured and Protected Tenancies (Lettings to Students) Regulations 1998 to specify Campus Accommodation Ltd for the purpose of paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1988. The effect of specification is that a tenancy granted by the body to a student at an educational institution specified in the 1998 Regulations is not an assured tenancy for the purposes of the 1988 Act.
