# The Electricity (Standards of Performance) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 26th August 2010

Coming into force: 1st October 2010

The Secretary of State has consented to making these Regulations in accordance with sections 39(1), 39A(1) and 39B(3) and (5) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Electricity (Standards of Performance) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 1st October 2010.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the earlier Regulations” means the Electricity (Standards of Performance) Regulations 2010[^f00004].

#### Amendment of the earlier Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The earlier Regulations are amended as follows:

- (a) regulation 13 (estimate of charges for connection) is revoked;

- (b) in regulation 19 (appointments), after paragraph (1) insert—

> > (1A) But this regulation does not apply to a visit arising out of the application of the Electricity (Connection Standards of Performance) Regulations 2010[^f00005].

- (c) in regulation 21(2)(a) (payments), for “11 to 15 inclusive,” substitute “11, 12, 14, 15”;

- (d) in regulation 23(1) (timing of notification), omit “13,”;

- (e) in regulation 24(1) (notice of rights), for “5 to 15 inclusive” substitute “5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14 and 15,”;

- (f) in the table in Part 1 of Schedule 1, omit the rows which refer to paragraphs (2) and (3) of regulation 13.

#### Transitional provision

###### 3

- (1) Nothing in regulation 2 affects the application of any provision of the earlier Regulations to a situation where, for the purposes of regulation 13 of those Regulations, the applicable date occurred before the coming into force of these Regulations.

- (2) In this regulation, the “applicable date” has the meaning given to it in the earlier Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the [Electricity (Standards of Performance) Regulations 2010 (S.I 2010/698)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/698) (the “earlier Regulations”).

Regulation 13 of the earlier Regulations, the ‘Estimate of charges for connection’ standard, is revoked, and associated references are deleted, as that standard is the subject of the Electricity (Connection Standards of Performance) Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2088](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2088)) from 1st October 2010.

Regulation 19 of the earlier Regulations, the ‘Appointments’ standard, is amended so that it does not apply to a visit arising out of the application of the Electricity (Connection Standards of Performance) Regulations 2010.
