# The Road Safety Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008

Made: 14th July 2008

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61 of the Road Safety Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Safety Act 2006 (Commencement No.3) Order 2008.

#### Commencement of section 46

###### 2

Section 46 of the Road Safety Act 2006 (which extends Part 2 of the Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001[^f00002] to Scotland and Northern Ireland) shall come into force on 31st July 2008.

#### Transitional provision

###### 3

Sections 17(1) and 28 of the Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001 shall not apply before 1st November 2008 to any person in Scotland or Northern Ireland.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jim Fitzpatrick — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 14th July 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 31st July 2008 section 46 of the Road Safety Act 2006 (*article 2*).

Section 46 extends Part 2 of the Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001 to Scotland and Northern Ireland. Part 2 of the 2001 Act contains provisions regulating registration plate suppliers

The offences in section 17(1) (carrying on business as a registration plate supplier whilst unregistered) and 28 (selling a plate or other device as a registration plate and supplying a plate or device for unlawful activities) of the Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001 do not apply to persons in Scotland or Northern Ireland until 1st November 2008 (*article 3*).
