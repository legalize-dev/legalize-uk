# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.5) (Family Actions and Miscellaneous) 1996

Made: 9th October 1996

Coming into force: 1st November 1996

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No.5) (Family Actions and Miscellaneous) 1996 and shall come into force on 1st November 1996.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Rules of the Court of Session 1994 in relation to, and makes provision for, proceedings under Part I of the Children (Scotland) Act [1995 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/36). Part I of the Act makes new provision for parental responsibilities and parental rights, guardianship, the administration of a child’s property and court orders with respect to these matters.
