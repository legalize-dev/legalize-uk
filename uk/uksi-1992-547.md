# The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made: 6th March 1992

Coming into force: 9th March 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 9th March 1992.

###### 2

The Export of Goods (Control) Order 1991[^f00003] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) for head (c) in entry PL 5001 in Group 1 in Part II of Schedule 1, there shall be substituted the following—

> > (c) Leg-irons, shackles (excluding any pair of handcuffs the maximum dimension of which when locked does not exceed 240mm) and gangchains, specially designed for restraining human beingsC

; and

- (b) in Schedule 2—

- (i) after “Albania” there shall be inserted—

> > Armenia
> Azerbaijan
> Belarus

- (ii) for “Czech and Slovak Republic” there shall be substituted—

> > Czech and Slovak Federal Republic
> Estonia
> Georgia

- (iii) after “Hungary” there shall be inserted—

> > Kazakhstan
> Kirgizstan
> Latvia
> Lithuania
> Moldova

- (iv) after “Romania” there shall be inserted—

## Signed

M. Coolican — An Assistant Secretary, — Department of Trade and Industry — 6th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1991

- (i) by bringing under control certain handcuffs; and

- (ii) by replacing the reference in Schedule 2 to the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics by references to those countries which previously formed that country.
