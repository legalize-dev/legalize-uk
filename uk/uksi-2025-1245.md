# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (ESCP Europe Business School) (Amendment) Order 2025

Made: 14th November 2025

Coming into force: 4th December 2025

The Office for Students has complied with the procedure on giving notifications in accordance with section 48 of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (ESCP Europe Business School) (Amendment) Order 2025 and shall come into force on 4th December 2025.

#### Variation of the Power to Award Degrees etc. (ESCP Europe Business School) Order 2024

###### 2

- (1) The Power to Award Degrees etc. (ESCP Europe Business School) Order 2024[^f00002] is varied in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) For article 3, substitute––

> > (3) The authorisation in article 2 is for a fixed term beginning on 2nd September 2024 and expiring at the end of the day on 2nd December 2030.

## Signed

Nicholas Holland — Head of Quality and Standards — Office for Students — 14th November 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order varies the Power to Award Degrees etc. (ESCP Europe Business School) Order 2024 to extend ESCP Europe Business School’s authorisation to award degrees. The expiry of ESCP Europe Business School’s (UKPRN: 10002313) authorisation is changed from 2nd December 2027 to 2nd December 2030.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
