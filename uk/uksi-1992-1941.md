# The Timeshare Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made: 6th August 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by subsections (6) and (8) of section 12 and section 13(2) of the Timeshare Act 1992[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Timeshare Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992.

###### 2

The Timeshare Act 1992 shall come into force on 12th October 1992.

## Signed

Denton of Wakefield — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 6th August 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12th October 1992 the Timeshare Act 1992.
