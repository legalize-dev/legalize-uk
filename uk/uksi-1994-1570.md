# The Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1994

Made: 15th June 1994

Laid before Parliament: 24th June 1994

Coming into force: 15th July 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 2 of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) -(Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 15th July 1994.

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1 to the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1992[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In item 2—

- (a) in the third column, there shall be added—

> > - 94/12/EC†

- (b) in the fourth column, there shall be added—

> > - L100, 19.4.94, p.42

- (3) For the Note at the end there shall be substituted—

> > Note: The Directives marked with a * are Commission Directives, while those marked with a † are Directives of the European Parliament and the Council. The other Directives are Council Directives.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Key — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 15th June 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 1 to the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1992 and are made in consequence of Directive [94/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0012) of the European Parliament and the Council.

Directive [94/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0012) further amends Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) which deals with the measures to be taken against air pollution by emissions from motor vehicles.

Copies of the EC Directives which are relevant to these Regulations can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office. The details of the Directives are set out below.
