# The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Cayman Islands) (No. 2) Order 1992

Made: 28th October 1992

Laid before Parliament: 9th November 1992

Coming into force: 1st December 1992

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 28th day of October 1992 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

###### 1

—This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Cayman Islands) (No. 2) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st December 1992.

###### 2

—The Merchant Shipping Law 1992[^f00002] enacted by the Legislature of the Cayman Islands, is hereby confirmed.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, confirms a Law of 1992 enacted by the Legislature of the Cayman Islands which codifies and consolidates the law in force in the Cayman Islands relating to the operation of merchant ships insofar as such operation involves matters of safety and marine pollution, including the making of further provision as to the powers of surveyors and inspectors, the liability of charterers and offences by bodies corporate.

The Law makes consequential amendments and repeals, as set out in the schedules to the Law, to the Merchant Shipping (Submersible Craft) Law 1991 and the Registration of Merchant Ships Law 1991, and to the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, the Merchant Shipping Act 1970 and the Merchant Shipping Act 1988 (in so far as those Acts apply or have been extended to the Cayman Islands). The Law also makes a technical amendment to section 11(6) of the Registration of Merchant Ships Law 1991.
