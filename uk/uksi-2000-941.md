# The Civil Procedure (Modification of Enactments) Order 2000

Made: 1st April 2000

Laid before Parliament: 4th April 2000

Coming into force: 2nd May 2000

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 4(1) of the Civil Procedure Act 1997[^f00001], makes the following Order—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Procedure (Modification of Enactments) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 2nd May 2000.

###### 2

In the proviso to section 3(4) of the Lands Tribunal Act 1949[^f00002], after the words “rules of court,” insert “in England and Wales appeal to the court and otherwise”.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, C. — Dated 1st April 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Lands Tribunal Act 1949 following the making of a new rule for appeals in rule 19 of and Schedule 5 to the [Civil Procedure (Amendment) Rules 2000 (S.I. 2000 No. 221](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/221)). Appeals from the Lands Tribunal in England and Wales to the Court of Appeal will no longer be by way of case stated.
