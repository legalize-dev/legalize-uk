# The A40 Trunk Road (Western Avenue, Hillingdon) (30 MPH Speed Limit) Order 1996

Made: 18th November 1996

Coming into force: 22nd November 1996

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 84(1) and (2) and 124 of, and paragraph 27(1) of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A40 Trunk Road (Western Avenue, Hillingdon) (30 mph Speed Limit) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 22nd November 1996.

###### 2

In this Order —

- “the relevant length”means that length of the westbound access slip road between its junction with the western kerb line of Hercies Road and a point 319 metres to the west of that junction;

- “the westbound access slip road”means that length of trunk road that links Long Lane (A437) to the westbound main carriageway of the trunk road;

- “Long Lane”and “Hercies Road”mean the roads so named in the London Borough of Hillingdon;

- “the trunk road”means that part of the A40 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Hillingdon.

###### 3

No person shall drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 30 miles per hour in the relevant length.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

P J Bevis — Assistant Director Highways Agency London Network Management Division — 18th November 1996
