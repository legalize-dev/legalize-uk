# The Contracting Out (Metropolitan Police and Civil Staffs Pensions) Order 1997

Made: 19th July 1997

Coming into force: 20th July 1997

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 69 of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Contracting Out (Metropolitan Police and Civil Staffs Pensions) Order 1997 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Contracting out of functions

###### 2

- (1) Functions conferred on the Secretary of State, as the police authority for the metropolitan police district, by section 8A of the Police Pensions Act 1976[^f00002] or by any regulations made under section 1 or 8A of that Act may be exercised by, or by the employees of, such person (if any) as may be authorised in that behalf by the Secretary of State.

- (2) Functions conferred on the Secretary of State by section 15(2)(a) of the Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1967[^f00003] (superannuation of metropolitan civil staffs) or by provisions (being the principal civil service pension scheme and enactments and instruments relating to civil service pensions) having effect for the purposes of those functions by virtue of section 15(2)(b) of that Act may be exercised by, or by the employees of, such person (if any) as may be authorised in that behalf by the Secretary of State.

## Signed

Alun Michael — Minister of State — 19th July 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order enables the Secretary of State to authorise another person, or that person’s employees, to exercise his functions in relation to pensions payable out of the Metropolitan Police Fund.
