# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made: 19th August 2002

Laid before Parliament: 19th August 2002

Coming into force: 20th August 2002

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 20th August 2002.

#### Amendment to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2002

###### 2

In Schedule 2 to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2002 (import conditions), for the entry contained in Section C of Part I (protective measures—all products) there shall be substituted the following entry—

> > Commission Decision [2002/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0069) (OJ No. L30, 31.1.2002, p.50) as amended by Commission Decision [2002/441/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0441) (OJ No. L151, 11.6.2002, p.16) and Commission Decision [2002/573/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0573) (OJ No. L181, 11.7.2002, p.21).

#### Revocation of the Food and Animal Feedingstuffs (Products of Animal Origin from China) (Emergency Control) (England) Regulations 2002

###### 3

The Food and Animal Feedingstuffs (Products of Animal Origin from China) (Emergency Control) (England) Regulations 2002 are revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Hazel Blears — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 19th August 2002

## Explanatory note

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

1These Regulations update the reference to Commission Decision [2002/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0069) concerning certain protective measures with regard to the products of animal origin imported from China (OJ No. L30, 31.1.2002, p. 50) in Section C of Part I of Schedule 2 to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2002 (S.I. 2002/1227), by including in that reference references to the two Decisions which amend Decision [2002/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0069), namely Commission Decision [2002/441/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0441) (OJ No, L151, 11.6.2002, p. 16) and Commission Decision [2002/573/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0573) (OJ No. L181, 11.7.2002, p.21).

2These Regulations also revoke the Food and Animal Feedingstuffs (Products of Animal Origin from China) (Emergency Control) (England) Regulations 2002 (S.I. 2002/1614) which previously implemented Commission Decision [2002/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0069) as amended by Commission Decision [2002/441/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0441).

3No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
