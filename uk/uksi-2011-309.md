# The Registrar of Companies (Fees) (Companies, Overseas Companies and Limited Liability Partnerships) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made: 8th February 2011

Laid before Parliament: 14th February 2011

Coming into force: 6th April 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 243(3) and 1063(1) to (3) of the Companies Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Registrar of Companies (Fees) (Companies, Overseas Companies and Limited Liability Partnerships) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and come into force on 6th April 2011.

- (2) In these Regulations “the Main Regulations” means the Registrar of Companies (Fees) (Companies, Overseas Companies and Limited Liability Partnerships) Regulations 2009[^f00002].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Registrar of Companies (Fees) (Companies, Overseas Companies and Limited Liability Partnerships) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2101](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2101)) (“the Main Regulations”) as amended by the Registrar of Companies (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2439](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2439)), which require payment of fees in respect of functions performed by the registrar of companies relating to companies, overseas companies and limited liability partnerships.

The Regulations amend the Main Regulations by:

- (1) increasing fees in respect of—

- (a) same day registration of a company under section 14 of the Companies Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”) where documents are delivered in hard copy form from £50.00 to £100.00 and where such documents are delivered other than for same day registration from £20.00 to £40.00, but that increase to £40.00 does not apply to the registration of a Welsh company delivering documents in Welsh or a community interest company or an unlimited company,

- (b) registration of an annual return delivered in hard copy form by a company or limited liability partnership from £30.00 to £40.00,

- (c) same day registration of a limited liability partnership under section 3 of the Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 (“the 2000 Act”) where documents are delivered in hard copy form from £50.00 to £100.00 and where such documents are delivered other than for same day registration from £20.00 to £40.00, but that increase to £40.00 does not apply to the registration of a Welsh limited liability partnership delivering documents in Welsh,

- (d) same day registration of a UK establishment of an overseas company from £50.00 to £100.00,

- (e) certified copies or certificates from £15.00 to £20.00, and where documents are required for same day delivery or collection the fee is increased from £50.00 to £60.00.

- (2) inserting the following fees—

- (a) £18 for the registration of a company under section 14 of the 2006 Act where documents are delivered by the Web Incorporation Service,

- (b) £30 for same day registration and £8 for other than same day registration of a change of name of a company or limited liability partnership where the documents are delivered electronically,

- (c) £30 for same day registration and £14 for other than same day registration of a limited liability partnership under section 3 of the 2000 Act where the documents are delivered electronically,

- (d) £50 for same day registration and £10 for other than same day registration of a reduction of share capital under section 644 or 649 of the 2006 Act.

The fees outlined in 2(b) to (d) above were previously set administratively by the registrar. A copy of the registrar’s determination setting out all his administrative fees is available on www.companieshouse.gov.uk.

Other fees are reduced including the registration of annual returns delivered electronically by a company or limited liability partnership and registration of an overseas company’s accounting documents.

The fee changes apply with effect from 6th April 2011.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from the Policy and Planning Section, Companies House, Crown Way, Maindy, Cardiff CF14 3UZ or on www.companieshouse.gov.uk and is annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
