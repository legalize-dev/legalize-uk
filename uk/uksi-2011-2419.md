# The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2011

Made: 4th October 2011

Laid before Parliament: 7th October 2011

Coming into force: 1st November 2011

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 213, 217(2) and 272(7) and (8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], having regard to changes in the functions of the Oxford University Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 1st November 2011.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the trustees” means the trustees for the Oxford University Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00003];

- “the old Trust” means the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust[^f00004];

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the trustees and signed on behalf of the old Trust and on behalf of the trustees on 9th September 2011[^f00005].

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trust to the trustees.

- (2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the trustees of the trust property or to the old Trust shall be construed as a reference to the trustees.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Paul Burstow — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 4th October 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order transfers to the trustees for the Oxford University Hospitals National Health Service Trust, trust property held by the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
