# The Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Amendment Regulations 2001

Made: 4th September 2001

Laid before Parliament: 10th September 2001

Coming into force: 8th October 2001

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 138(1)(a) and (4) and 175(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Amendment Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 8th October 2001.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the General Regulations” means the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 1987 .

#### Amendment of the General Regulations

###### 2

- (1) There shall be inserted in paragraph (4) of regulation 7 of the General Regulations (entitlement), after sub-paragraph (a)—

> > (aa) a person in receipt of asylum support under section 95 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 ;

- (2) In paragraph (6) of regulation 7 of the General Regulations, the words from “;or” at the end of sub-paragraph (b) to “both aged under 60, £500” at the end of sub-paragraph (c) shall be omitted.

- (3) Regulation 9 of the General Regulations (effect of capital) shall be omitted.

#### Transitional provision

###### 3

These Regulations shall not have effect with respect to any claim for a Sure Start Maternity Grant or a funeral payment made before 8th October 2001.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Maria Eagle — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Work and Pensions — 4th September 2001
