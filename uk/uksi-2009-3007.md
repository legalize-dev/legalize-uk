# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2009

Made: 17th November 2009

Coming into force: 18th November 2009

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of November 2009Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2009 and comes into force on 18th November 2009.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE

### Persons appointed on 18th November 2009

- Patrick Leeson

- John Goldup

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council
