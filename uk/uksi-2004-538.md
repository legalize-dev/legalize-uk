# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Bedfordshire) (Districts of Mid-Bedfordshire and South Bedfordshire) (Amendment) Order 2004

Made: 1st March 2004

Laid before Parliament: 5th March 2004

Coming into force: 5th April 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(3) of Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Act 1991 hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Bedfordshire) (Districts of Mid-Bedfordshire and South Bedfordshire) (Amendment) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 5th April 2004.

#### Amendment of principal Order

###### 2

- (1) The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Bedfordshire) (Districts of Mid-Bedfordshire and South Bedfordshire) Order 2004[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) For article 3(2)(c), substitute—

> > (c) the A5—
>   (i) from the southern boundary of the district to its junction with Beech Road, Dunstable;
>   (ii) from its junction with French’s Avenue, Dunstable, to its junction with the road to Tebworth, south of Hockliffe;
>   (iii) from the former junction with Church Lane, north of Hockliffe, to the north western boundary of the district.

- (3) In paragraph 7(2) of Schedule 1, for the definition of “parking authority” substitute—

> > - “parking authority”— in relation to— a parking place provided or authorised under section 32(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00003], or designated under section 45 of that Act, or a vehicle that is or was stationary in such a parking place, means the authority (Bedfordshire County Council, Mid-Bedfordshire District Council or South Bedfordshire District Council) by whom the parking place was provided, authorised or designated, and in relation to any other place, or a vehicle that is or was stationary in any other place, means Bedfordshire County Council;

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Tony McNulty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 1st March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (County of Bedfordshire) (Districts of Mid-Bedfordshire and South Bedfordshire) Order 2004 (the “Principal Order”). Article 3(2)(c) of the Principal Order is amended so that certain parts of the A5 in the district of South Bedfordshire are included in the permitted parking area and special parking area.

The definition of “parking authority” inserted into section 82(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 for the purposes of the Principal Order is replaced. The authority that provides, authorises or designates a parking place under section 32(1) (power of local authorities to provide parking places) and section 45(1) (designation of paying parking places on highways) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 is the parking authority in respect of that parking place, or any vehicle left stationary in that parking place. For any other place to which the Principal Order applies, or vehicle left stationary in that place (including the A5 trunk road), the Bedfordshire County Council is the parking authority. A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
