# The Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment No. 2)Order 2002

Made: 6th November 2002

Laid before Parliament: 7th November 2002

Coming into force: 9th November 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1A(1) and (2) of the Immigration (Carrier’s Liability) Act 1987[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Transit Visa) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2002.

###### 2

This Order shall come into force on 9th November 2002.

###### 3

In this Order “the 1993 Order” means “the Immigration (Transit Visa) Order 1993”[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the 1993 Order

###### 4

In the Schedule to the 1993 Order, after “Uganda”, there be inserted “Zimbabwe”.

## Signed

Beverley Hughes — Minister of State — 6th November 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Immigration (Transit Visa) Order 1993 by adding Zimbabwe to the list of countries and territories set out in the Schedule to that Order whose nationals or citizens require a visa to pass through the United Kingdom in transit to another country or territory.
