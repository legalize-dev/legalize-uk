# The Copyright (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1992

Made: 4th June 1992

Coming into force: 1st July 1992

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 4th day of June 1992 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph 36(3) of Schedule 1 to that Act, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Isle of Man) (Revocation) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st July 1992.

###### 2

The Copyright (Isle of Man) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Copyright (Isle of Man) Order 1986, which extended the Copyright Act 1956 to the Isle of Man. It is made consequent on the enactment of legislation in the Isle of Man equivalent to Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.
