# The Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Amendment Regulations 2003

Made: 27th February 2003

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 2003

Coming into force: 7th April 2003

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 138(1)(a) and (4) and 175(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Amendment Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 7th April 2003.

#### Amendment of regulation 7A of the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 1987

###### 2

In regulation 7A(2)(g) and (5)(b) of the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 1987 (amount of funeral payment)[^f00003], in each place where the sum “£600” appears there shall be substituted the sum “£700”.

#### Transitional provision

###### 3

Where, in respect of a death which occurs before 7th April 2003, a claim is made for funeral expenses from the social fund, regulation 2 shall not have effect with respect to that claim.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Malcolm Wicks — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 27th February 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend the Social Fund Maternity and Funeral Expenses (General) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/481](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/481) as amended) in so far as those Regulations relate to claims for payment of funeral expenses.

In particular, regulation 2 amends regulation 7A(2)(g) so as to increase the amount of the cap on the residuary category of funeral expenses which may be met out of the social fund from £600 to £700 in respect of deaths which occur on or after 7th April 2003.

These Regulations do not impose a charge on business.
