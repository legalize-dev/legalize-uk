# The Value Added Tax (Abolition of Zero-Rating for Tax-Free Shops) Order 1999

Made: 11th June 1999

Laid before the House of Commons: 11th June 1999

Coming into force: 1st July 1999

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 30(4) and 96(9) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Abolition of Zero-Rating for Tax-Free Shops) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st July 1999 and shall have effect in relation to supplies made on or after that date.

###### 2

Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 shall be varied by deleting from–

- (a) Part I, the entry relating to tax-free shops, and

- (b) Part II, Group 14 (tax-free shops)[^f00002].

###### 3

The Value Added Tax (Tax Free Shops) Order 1995[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Jim Dowd — David Jamieson — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 11th June 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order deletes, with effect from 1st July 1999, Group 14 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act [1994 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/23), which Group zero-rated the sale of specified quantities of goods sold either in a tax-free shop or on board a ship or aircraft, in either case to a traveller making a journey to another member State.

The zero-rating of such supplies was permitted only until 30th June 1999 by Article 28k of Council Directive [77/388/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0388) (O.J. L.145, 13.6.1977, p. 1), which was inserted by Council Directive [91/680/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0680) (O.J. L.376, 31.12.1991, p. 1) and amended by Council Directive [94/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0004) (O.J. L.60, 3.3.1994, p. 14).
