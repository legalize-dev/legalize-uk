# The Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2006

Made: 9th October 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 31(3) of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2006.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

- (1) The provisions of the Act specified in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 16th October 2006.

- (2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 7 (data collection);

- (b) section 8 (tenure);

- (c) section 9 (annual reports);

- (d) section 30(1) (minor and consequential amendments etc.) in so far as it relates to the provision referred to in sub-paragraph (e); and

- (e) paragraph 9 of Schedule 4 (minor and consequential amendments).

###### 3

- (1) The provisions of the Act specified in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 1st December 2006.

- (2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) sections 2 to 5 (periodical canvass etc.);

- (b) section 30(1) (minor and consequential amendments etc.) in so far as it relates to the provisions referred to in sub-paragraph (c); and

- (c) paragraphs 7(1), (2)(a) and 8 of Schedule 4 ( minor and consequential amendments).

## Signed

Peter Hain — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 9th October 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 16th October 2006 and 1st December 2006 the provisions of the Northern Ireland (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006 listed in articles 2 and 3 respectively.
