# The Further Education Teachers’ Qualifications (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2013

Made: 29th July 2013

Laid before Parliament: 8th August 2013

Coming into force: 1st September 2013

The Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 136(a) and (c) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Further Education Teachers’ Qualifications (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2013 and come into force on 1st September 2013.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Regulations listed in the first column of the Schedule are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Further Education Teacher’s Qualifications (England) Regulations 2007 (“the 2007 Regulations”) together with amending Regulations. The 2007 Regulations apply to teachers employed in further education institutions on or after 1st September 2001. Subject to limited exceptions, the 2007 Regulations—

- (a) prohibit people from teaching in a further education institution unless they hold a Preparing to Teach Award or its equivalent;

- (b) prohibit people from teaching in a further education institution unless they hold a specified qualification, or its equivalent; and

- (c) allow teachers time in which to comply with the relevant requirements.

With the revocation of these Regulations it will no longer be compulsory for teachers to obtain these qualifications before they are employed by a further education institution.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations as they will have no impact on the costs of business, charities or the public sector. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
