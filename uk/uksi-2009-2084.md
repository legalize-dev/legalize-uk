# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made: 27th July 2009

Laid before Parliament: 31st July 2009

Coming into force: 26th August 2009

In accordance with section 195(2) of that Act he has consulted with such representative organisations as he thinks fit.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 26th August 2009.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

#### Saving

###### 10

- (1) The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982 shall continue to apply in relation to a relevant vehicle or a relevant part as if these Regulations had not been made.

- (2) For the purposes of this regulation—

- (a) “*relevant vehicle*” means a vehicle—

- (i) in relation to which a certificate was issued under section 47 of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00012] or under section 55 or 58 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00013] before the date on which these Regulations come into force; and

- (ii) which is first licensed under the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994[^f00014] on or after that date; and

- (b) “*relevant part*” means a vehicle part—

- (i) in relation to which a certificate was issued under section 47 of the Road Traffic Act 1972 or under section 55 or 58 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 before the date on which these Regulations come into force; and

- (ii) which is supplied on or after that date.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval for Goods Vehicles) (Great Britain) Regulations 1982 (“the 1982 Regulations”).

The Regulations—

- (a) amend definitions in consequence of the implementation of Directives [2005/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0055) and [2006/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0040) and the repeal of Council Directive [70/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0156) of 6th February 1970 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the type approval of motor vehicles and their trailers (OJ No. L42, 23.2.70, p.1 (SE 1970 (I), p.96)) (*regulation 3(2)*) and make other consequential amendments (*regulations 3(4)(a), 4, 6(3)(a), (10),* (*11)(b)* and *(12)* and *9*);

- (b) implement the Directives which amend Directives [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157) (OJ No. L42, 23.2.70, p.16 (SE 1970 (I), p.111)), [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) (OJ No. L76, 6.4.70, p.1 (SE 1970 (I), p.171)), [72/306/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0306) (OJ No. L190, 20.8.72, p.1 (SE 1972 (III), p.889)) and [92/24/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0024) (OJ No. L129, 14.5.92, p.154) and which amend and apply Directive [2005/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0055). (*Regulation 3(3)* substitutes new paragraph (2A) into regulation 2 of the 1982 Regulations so that references to EC instruments include those amendments referred to in the following paragraph at sub-paragraphs (f) to (i) for the period from 17th July 2003 to the date of making of these Regulations and only in so far as the provisions affect goods vehicle type approval. *Regulation 3(3)*also inserts new paragraph (2B) which provides that paragraph (2A) shall have no application to item 5 in Schedule 1 to the 1982 Regulations. This will have the effect that the reference to Council Directive [72/245/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0245) in item 5 is to be construed as only being amended by Directive [89/491/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0491));

- (c) clarify the meaning of vehicle classes (*regulation3(4)(b)*);

- (d) prescribe, in terms of the Schedule 1 requirements, the type approval requirements as being those which are applicable (to vehicles or parts thereof) on or after the date the Regulations come into force or any later date specified (*regulation 5(2)*);

- (e) omit redundant provisions (*regulation 5(3)*);

- (f) amend Schedule 1 (type approval requirements) to omit requirements the date of cessation of application of which has passed, implement the Directives referred to in the following paragraph at sub-paragraphs (a) to (e), provide for a lesser standard to apply in respect of radio suppression requirements, omit construction and use requirements pertaining to speed limiters and make consequential changes (*regulation 6(2), (3)(b)* and (*c), (4)* to *(9), (11)(a)* and *(13)* to *(16)* and *7*);

- (g) make consequential amendments to the form of information document for goods vehicles (*regulation 8(2)*); and

- (h) provide for savings in relation to vehicles or parts approved before the date the Regulations come into force (*regulation 10*).

The Directives implemented by these Regulations, and hence in so far they relate to the national type approval of goods vehicles, are—

- (a) Council Directive [70/221/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0221) of 20th March 1970 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to liquid fuel tanks and rear underrun protection of motor vehicles and their trailers (OJ No. L76, 6.4.70, p.23) as amended by Directives [79/490/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0490) (OJ No. L128, 26.5.79 p.22), [81/333/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0333) (OJ No. L131, 18.5.81 p.4), [97/19/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0019) (OJ No. L125, 16.5.97 p.1), [2000/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0008) (OJ No. L106, 3.5.2000, p.7) and [2006/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0020) (OJ No. L48, 18.2.2006, p.16);

- (b) Council Directive [80/1268/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/1268) of 16th December 1980 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to the fuel consumption of motor vehicles (OJ No. L375, 31.12.80, p.36) as amended by Directives [89/491/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0491) (OJ No. L238, 15.8.89, p.43), [93/116/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0116) (OJ No. L329, 30.12.93, p.39), [1999/100/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0100) (OJ No. L334, 28.12.99, p.36) and [2004/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0003) (OJ No. L49, 19.2.2004, p.36);
