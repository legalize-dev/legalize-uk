# The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2018

Made: 6th December 2018

Coming into force: 7th December 2018

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station (Correction) Order 2018.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 7th December 2018.

#### Corrections

###### 2

- (1) The Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2018 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

## SCHEDULE — Corrections

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy

Gareth Leigh — Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning — Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy — 6th December 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order corrects errors in the Eggborough Gas Fired Generating Station Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018/1020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/1020)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act [2008 (c.29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29), following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
