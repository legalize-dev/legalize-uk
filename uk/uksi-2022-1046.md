# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2022

Made: 12th October 2022

Coming into force: 13th October 2022

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of October 2022 Present, The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 3) Order 2022 and comes into force on 13th October 2022.

#### Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
