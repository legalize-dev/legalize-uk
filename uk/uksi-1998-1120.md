# The Bank of England Act 1998 (Commencement) Order 1998

Made: 24th April 1998

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 45 of the Bank of England Act 1998[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Bank of England Act 1998 (Commencement) Order 1998.

###### 2

The Bank of England Act 1998 shall come into force on 1st June 1998.

## Signed

Jim Dowd — Graham Allen — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 24th April 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st June 1998 all the provisions of the Bank of England Act 1998.
