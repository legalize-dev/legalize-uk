# The Northern Ireland (Emergency and Prevention of Terrorism Provisions) (Continuance) Order 1992

Made: 15th June 1992

Coming into force: 16th June 1992

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 69(3)(a) of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991[^f00001] and section 27(11)(a) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00002], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Emergency and Prevention of Terrorism Provisions) (Continuance) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 16th June 1992.

###### 2

In this Order—

- (a) “the temporary provisions of the 1991 Act” means Parts I to VIII of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 except—

- (i) section 7, Part III of Schedule 1 and, so far as they relate to offences which are scheduled offences by virtue of that Part, sections 3, 9 and 10;

- (ii) sections 63 and 64; and

- (iii) paragraph 20 of Schedule 4;

- (b) “the 1989 Act provisions” means the provisions referred to in section 27(11) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, that is to say Parts III and V of that Act so far as they have effect in Northern Ireland and relate to proscribed organisations for the purposes of section 28 of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 or offences or orders under that section.

###### 3

The temporary provisions of the 1991 Act except section 34 and Schedule 3, and the 1989 Act provisions, shall continue in force for a period of twelve months beginning with 16th June 1992.

## Signed

P. B. B. Mayhew — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 15th June 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Apart from this Order, the temporary provisions of the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 would expire with 15th June 1992. They are currently in force by virtue of section 69(1) of the 1991 Act and the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/1181](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1181)). This Order continues in force the temporary provisions of the 1991 Act for a period of twelve months beginning with 16th June 1992, except for section 34 and Schedule 3. Those provisions relate to the detention of terrorists and are not in force because, by virtue of section 69(4) of the 1991 Act, the Secretary of State is deemed to have made an order under section 69(3)(b), suspending their operation.

This Order also continues in force for the same period the provisions referred to in section 27(11) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, which would but for this Order also expire with 15th June 1992.
