# The Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 (Amendment)) Order 2010

Made: 28th June 2010

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 269(4) of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 (Amendment)) Order 2010.

#### Amendment of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional and Transitory Provisions) and (Commencement No. 2 (Amendment)) Order 2010

###### 2

Article 3 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional and Transitory Provisions) and (Commencement No. 2 (Amendment)) Order 2010[^f00002] (provision coming into force on 1st July 2010) is revoked.

## Signed

Nick Gibb — Minister of State — Department for Education — 28th June 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of this Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes article 3 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (Commencement No. 3 and Transitional and Transitory Provisions) and (Commencement No. 2 (Amendment)) Order 2010, which would have brought into force section 155 of the 2009 Act on 1st July 2010.

Section 155 imposes a duty on the Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation to keep under review, in particular circumstances, any system used by the Secretary of State which allocates values to qualifications by reference to the level of attainment indicated by the qualifications.
