## SCHEDULE — DESIGNATION

| (1) | (2) |
| --- | --- |
| Ministers or departments | Matters in relation to which the Ministers or departments are designated |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to lifts and the safety components for use in lifts. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the procedure for, nature of and enforcement of decisions concerning competition between undertakings. |
| Any Northern Ireland Department | Measures in respect of the conditions of, and the recognition of qualifications for, access to and pursuit of activities in the field of pharmacy. |
