# The Pembrokeshire National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1993

Made: 2nd March 1993

Coming into force: 12th March 1993

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of powers conferred by section 9(1) and (4) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Pembrokeshire National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital Debt) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 12th March 1993.

- (2) In this Order “the originating capital debt” means the originating capital debt of Pembrokeshire National Health Service Trust.

#### Originating capital debt of Pembrokeshire NHS trust

###### 2

- (1) The originating capital debt shall be £32,312,738.

- (2) The originating capital debt shall be divided between an initial loan of £16,156,369 and public dividend capital of £16,156,369.

## Signed

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 19th February 1993

We consent,

Tim Wood — Tim Kirkhope — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 2nd March 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the amount of the originating capital debt provided for insection 9 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 of Pembrokeshire NHS Trust established under that Act with an operational date of 1 April 1992. It provides also for the division of the originating capital debt between loan and public dividend capital.
