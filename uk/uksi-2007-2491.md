# The Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007

Made: 20th August 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(3)(b) of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.2) Order 2007.

###### 2

Section 20(1) of, and Schedule 8 to, the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (reconstitution of district policing partnerships) shall come into force on 4th September 2007.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 20 August 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 4th September 2007 section 20(1) of, and Schedule 8 to, the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006, which make provision for the reconstitution of district policing partnerships.
