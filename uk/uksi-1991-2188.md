# The Broadcasting (Local Delivery Services) (Amendment) Order 1991

Made: 30th September 1991

Laid before Parliament: 9th October 1991

Coming into force: 1st November 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 72(1)(b) and (3) of the Broadcasting Act 1990[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Broadcasting (Local Delivery Services) (Amendment) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st November 1991.

###### 2

The Schedule to the Broadcasting (Local Delivery Services) Order 1990[^f00002] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) in paragraph 1 of Part I, for the definition of “DBS service” there shall be substituted the following definition:

> > - “the DBS service” means any DBS services which are provided by the Commission in accordance with Part II of Schedule 11 to the 1990 Act;

, and

- (b) in paragraph 2(f) of Part II, the word “local” shall be omitted.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 30th September 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects two errors made in the Broadcasting (Local Delivery Services) Order 1990 ([S.I. 1990/2389](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2389)). Article 2replaces the definition of “DBS service” so as to refer to the provision of such a service by the Independent Television Commission under the transitional provisions in Schedule 11 to the Broadcasting Act 1990 instead of a definition which relied on repealed provisions in the Cable and Broadcasting Act [1984 (c. 46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/46). Article 2(b) omits the word “local” which is superfluous to the reference in paragraph 2(f) of Part II of the Schedule to the 1990 Order to any national radio service.
