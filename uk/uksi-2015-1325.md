# The Common Agricultural Policy (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 14th May 2015

Laid before Parliament: 18th May 2015

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] in relation to the common agricultural policy[^f00002] and makes these Regulations under the powers conferred by that section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy (Amendment) Regulations 2015 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendment of the Common Agricultural Policy (Control and Enforcement, Cross-Compliance, Scrutiny of Transactions and Appeals) Regulations 2014

###### 2

- (1) The Common Agricultural Policy (Control and Enforcement, Cross-Compliance, Scrutiny of Transactions and Appeals) Regulations 2014[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (2) of regulation 3 (competent authority), for “Where” substitute “Subject to paragraph (3), where”.

- (3) In paragraph (3) of regulation 3, for the opening words, substitute “A farmer who has a holding situated in more than one territory on the last date on which the single application may be submitted may request that the competent authority is”.

- (4) After paragraph (3) of regulation 3 insert—

> > (3A) Where a holding is situated in more than one territory, the final date on which the single application may be submitted, and the final date on which an application for the allocation of payment entitlements may be submitted, are the dates set in the territory of the competent authority for that holding in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (5) For paragraph (1) of regulation 5 (applications) substitute—

> > (1) For the purposes of Article 13(1) of the Horizontal Implementing Regulation[^f00004], Article 1 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/747 derogating from Implementing Regulation (EU) No 809/2014 as regards the final date of submission of the single application, aid applications or payment claims, the final date for notification of amendments to the single application or payment claim and the final date for applications for allocation of payment entitlements or the increase of the value of payment entitlements under the basic payment scheme for the year 2015[^f00005], and Article 12 of the Horizontal Delegated Regulation[^f00006], the final date on which a single application, aid application or payment claim may be submitted to the Secretary of State is—
>   (a) if the relevant claim year is 2015, 15th June,
>   (b) in all other cases, 15th May but if that date is a Saturday, Sunday, Bank Holiday or other public holiday, the next working day.

#### Amendment of the Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes (England) Regulations 2014

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 8 of the Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes (England) Regulations 2014[^f00007] (payment entitlements) is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph 1(a)—

- (a) for “15th May” substitute “15th June”; and

- (b) omit “or” at the end.

- (3) After paragraph 1(a) insert—

> > (aa) from 16th June 2015 to 15th May 2016 (both those dates included), if 2016 is the first calendar year in which the transferee may include those entitlements in the transferee’s application for direct payments;

- (4) For paragraph (3) substitute—

> > (3) For the purposes of Article 22(1) of the Horizontal Implementing Regulation and Article 3 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/747 derogating from Implementing Regulation (EU) No 809/2014 as regards the final date of submission of the single application, aid applications or payment claims, the final date for notification of amendments to the single application or payment claim and the final date for applications for allocation of payment entitlements or the increase of the value of payment entitlements under the basic payment scheme for the year 2015, applications for the allocation of payment entitlements may be submitted—
>   (a) if the relevant calendar year is 2015, on or before 15th June 2015;
>   (b) in all other cases, on or before 15th May of the relevant calendar year.

## Signed

George Eustice — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 14th May 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Common Agricultural Policy (Control and Enforcement, Cross-Compliance, Scrutiny of Transactions and Appeals) Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/3263](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3263)) (“the Control Regulations”) and the Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes (England) Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/3259](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3259)) (“the Basic Payment Regulations”) in reliance on the derogations provided for in Articles 1 and 3 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/747 derogating from Implementing Regulation (EU) No 809/2014 as regards the final date of submission of the single application, aid applications or payment claims, the final date for notification of amendments to the single application or payment claim and the final date for applications for allocation of payment entitlements or the increase of the value of payment entitlements under the basic payment scheme for the year 2015 (OJ No L 119, 12.5.2015, p21).

Regulation 2 amends regulation 3 (competent authority) of the Control Regulations to make further provision to determine the competent authority in the case of holdings that are situated in more than one territory on the last date on which the single application may be submitted. It also amends regulation 5 (applications) to change the final date for submitting a single application, aid application or payment claim in 2015.

Regulation 3 amends regulation 8 (payment entitlements) of the Basic Payment Regulations to modify the period in which a transferor must notify the Secretary of State of a transfer of payment entitlements for transferees who may first include those entitlements in their 2015 and 2016 applications for direct payments, and to change the deadline for submitting applications for allocation of payment entitlements in 2015.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
