# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2014

Made: 27th May 2014

Coming into force: 28th May 2014

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of May 2014Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2014 and comes into force on 28th May 2014.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — Persons appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 28th May 2014

- Paul Cocker

- Steven Gauntley

- Catherine Leahy

- Mary Rayner

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
