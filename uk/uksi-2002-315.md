# The Export of Goods (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia) (Control) (Revocation) Order 2002

Made: 12th February 2002

Coming into force: 7th March 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in her,[^f00002] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia) (Control) (Revocation) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 7th March 2002.

###### 2

The Export of Goods (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia) (Control) Order 1998 is revoked[^f00003].

## Signed

Nigel Griffiths — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Small Business, — Department of Trade and Industry — 12th February 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Export of Goods (Federal Republic of Yugoslavia) (Control) Order 1998 which prohibited the exportation of any goods specified in the Annex to Council Regulation [(EC) No. 926/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/0926) (OJ No. L 130, 1.5.1998, p.1.) to any destination in the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia. Council Regulation [(EC) No 2156/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2156) of 5 November 2001 (OJ No. L 289, of 6.11.2001, p.5) removed the prohibition on the sale or supply to the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia of equipment which might be used for internal repression or terrorism and repealed Council Regulation [(EC) No 926/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/0926).
