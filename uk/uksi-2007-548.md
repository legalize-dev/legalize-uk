# The Enduring Powers of Attorney (Prescribed Form) (Amendment) Amendment Regulations 2007

Made: 23rd February 2007

Laid before Parliament: 26th February 2007

Coming into force: 30th March 2007

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the Enduring Powers of Attorney Act 1985[^f00001]:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Enduring Powers of Attorney (Prescribed Form) (Amendment) Amendment Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 30th March 2007.

###### 2

In regulation 3 of the Enduring Powers of Attorney (Prescribed Form) (Amendment) Regulations 2005[^f00002], for “1st April 2007” substitute “1st October 2007”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Cathy Ashton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Constitutional Affairs — Date 23 February 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Enduring Powers of Attorney (Prescribed Form) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/3116](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3116)), which substituted a new prescribed form for an Enduring Power of Attorney in the Enduring Powers of Attorney (Prescribed Form) Regulations 1990 ([S.I. 1990/1376](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1376)). Regulation 3 of the 2005 Regulations permits the continued use, before 1st April 2007, of the form that was replaced by the 2005 Regulations.

These Regulations amend the date in regulation 3 of the 2005 Regulations so as to enable the form that was replaced by the 2005 Regulations to continue to be used until 1st October 2007.
