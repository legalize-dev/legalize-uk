# The Environment Act 1995 (Commencement No. 18) (Scotland) Order 2000

Made: 12th July 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 125(3) of the Environment Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Environment Act 1995 (Commencement No. 18) (Scotland) Order 2000.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

#### Provision coming into force on 14th July 2000

###### 2

Section 57 of the Environment Act 1995, in so far as not already in force, shall come into force on 14th July 2000.

## Signed

JOHN REID — Secretary of State for Scotland — 12th July 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, brings into force on 14th July 2000 section 57 of the Environment Act [1995 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25) in relation to the insertion of section 78S (exclusion from registers of information affecting national security) of the Environment Protection Act [1990 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43). The other provisions inserted by section 57 of the Environment Act 1995 were brought into force for Scotland by [S.S.I. 2000/180](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/180).
