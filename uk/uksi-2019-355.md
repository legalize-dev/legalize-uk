# The Braintree (Electoral Changes) Order 2019

Made: 25th February 2019

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2) and (3)

The Local Government Boundary Commission for England has decided to give effect to the recommendations and makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 92(3) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Braintree (Electoral Changes) Order 2019.

- (2) This article and article 2 come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

- (3) Article 3 comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on the day after the day on which this Order is made;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England[^f00003] in 2019.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 2018 Order” means the Braintree District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2018[^f00004];

- “the map” means the map marked “Map referred to in the Braintree District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2018”, held by Braintree District Council[^f00005].

#### Alteration of district ward boundaries

###### 3

Each area described in the first column of the table in the Schedule ceases to be part of the district ward listed in the corresponding entry in the second column of that table and becomes part of the district ward listed in the corresponding entry in the third column of that table.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to recommendations made by Braintree District Council to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England for related alterations of district ward boundaries.

These alterations are a consequence of changes to certain parish boundaries introduced by the Braintree District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2018 (accessible at http://www.lgbce.org.uk/resources/database-of-local-government-orders/eastern/essex). That Order was made under section 86 of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007. Printed copies of the Order together with its map are available for inspection at the offices of Braintree District Council, Causeway House, Bocking End, Braintree, Essex, CM7 9HB.

The changes to district ward boundaries under article 3 apply in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2019.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
