# The Magistrates’ Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2018

Made: 30th August 2018

Laid before Parliament: 4th September 2018

Coming into force: 22nd October 2018

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 125A(3) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) (Amendment) Order 2018 and shall come into force on 22nd October 2018.

#### Amendment to the Magistrates’ Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) Order 2000

###### 2

For Article 2 (specified provisions) of the Magistrates’ Courts Warrants (Specifications of Provisions) Order 2000[^f00002], substitute—

> > (2) The following provisions are specified for the purposes of section 125A(3) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980—
>   (a) sections 7(1) and (2) of the Bail Act 1976[^f00003];
>   (b) sections 1(1)(b)[^f00004], 13(1), 76(1)[^f00005], 83(1)(b) and (2)[^f00006], 86(4)[^f00007], 97(2) and (3), 97A(3)[^f00008] and 136(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980;
>   (c) section 40(2A)[^f00009] and (3) of the Child Support Act 1991;
>   (d) paragraphs 4(3) and (5) of Schedule 3 to the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00010];
>   (e) in the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000[^f00011]—
>     (i) sections 1(7)[^f00012], 104(1)(b) and 123(1)(b)[^f00013];
>     (ii) paragraph 3(2)(b)[^f00014] and paragraph 9C(2)(b)[^f00015] of Schedule 1;
>     (iii) paragraph 3(1)(b) of Schedule 3[^f00016];
>     (iv) paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5[^f00017];
>     (v) paragraph 7(2) of Schedule 7[^f00018];
>     (vi) paragraph 6(2) of Schedule 8 (but only where an application is made under paragraph 2(1)[^f00019] of that Schedule);
>   (f) in the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00020]—
>     (i) sections 256AC(1)(b)[^f00021] and 256C(1)(b)[^f00022];
>     (ii) paragraphs 7(2)(b) and (4), 8(2)(b) and (4), 13(6) and 25(1)(b) of Schedule 8;
>     (iii) paragraphs 6(2)(b) and 20(1)(b) of Schedule 12;
>     (iv) paragraph 8(1)(b) of Schedule 19A[^f00023];
>   (g) paragraphs 5(1)(b) and 20(1)(b) of Schedule 2 to the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008[^f00024];
>   (h) regulations 41(1) and 42(5)(b) of the Community Charges (Administration and Enforcement) Regulations 1989[^f00025];
>   (i) regulations 16(3)(a) and 17(5)(b) of the Non-Domestic Rating (Collection and Enforcement) (Local Lists) Regulations 1989[^f00026]; and
>   (j) regulations 47(3)(a) and 48(5)(b) of the Council Tax (Administration and Enforcement) Regulations 1992[^f00027].

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Lord Chancellor

Lucy Frazer — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 30th August 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Sections 125A to 125D of the Magistrates’ Courts Act [1980 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/42), as inserted by sections 92, 93, 94 and 96 of the Access to Justice Act [1999 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/22), make provision for the enforcement of warrants issued under statutory provisions specified by order. This Order amends the Magistrates’ Courts Warrants (Specification of Provisions) Order 2000 which specifies such provisions.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
