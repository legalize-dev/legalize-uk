# The Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) (England and Wales) Regulations 2023

Made: 12th June 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 208(1) and (6) of the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation, interpretation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional and Saving Provisions) (England and Wales) Regulations 2023.

- (2) In these Regulations “*the 2022 Act*” means the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Provisions coming into force on 13th June 2023

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2022 Act come into force on 13th June 2023—

- (a) section 194 (Disregard of certain convictions or cautions);

- (b) section 195 (Pardons for certain convictions or cautions).

#### Transitional and saving provisions

###### 3

The amendments made by section 194 of the 2022 Act to sections 92, 93, 94, 99, 100 and 101 of the Protection of Freedoms Act 2012[^f00002] do not apply to an application to disregard a conviction or caution which has been received by the Secretary of State on the day before section 194 of the 2022 Act came into force.

## Signed

Sarah Dines — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Home Office — 12th June 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force sections 194 and 195 of the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act [2022 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/32).

The first provision commenced in regulation 2 extends the scope of the disregard scheme in Chapter 4, Part 5 of the Protection of Freedoms Act [2012 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/9) to include any offence that was used to criminalise same-sex sexual activity which has since been repealed or abolished by enactment. The second provision commenced in regulation 2 achieves the same effect in relation to posthumous pardons for certain convictions and pardons in sections 164 to 169 of the Policing and Crime Act [2017 (c. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/3).

Regulation 3 provides that the amendments made by section 194 of the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 do not apply to the sections relevant to an application to disregard a conviction or caution under Chapter 4, Part 5 of the Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 where that application was received by the Secretary of State before the date on which section 194 came into force.
