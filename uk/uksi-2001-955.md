# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2001

Made: 14th March 2001

Coming into force: 15th March 2001

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of March 2001 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 15th March 2001.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 15th March 2001.
