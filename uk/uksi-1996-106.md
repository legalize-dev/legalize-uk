# The Tyne Riverside Enterprise Zones (North Tyneside) (Designation) (No. 1) Order 1996

Made: 18th January 1996

Laid before Parliament: 29th January 1996

Coming into force: 19th February 1996

Now therefore the Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 5 of the Schedule and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Tyne Riverside Enterprise Zones (North Tyneside) (Designation) (No. 1) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 19th February 1996.

###### 2

- (1) Any reference in this Order to the Zone area is a reference to the area—

- (a) the boundary of which is represented by the outside edge of a black line edged internally with a stippled band on the map; and

- (b) to which the Tyne Riverside Enterprise Zone No. 1 (Hadrian Business Park South) Scheme relates.

- (2) In this Order:

- “the council” means North Tyneside Metropolitan Borough Council; and

- “the map” means the map prepared in duplicate, marked “Map referred to in the Tyne Riverside Enterprise Zones (North Tyneside) (Designation) (No. 1) Order 1996”, and signed by an Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment, one duplicate of which has been deposited and is available for inspection at the principal offices of the council and the other at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment.

#### Enterprise zone in North Tyneside

###### 3

—The Zone area is hereby designated as an enterprise zone, and the council is hereby designated as the enterprise zone authority for that zone.

#### Period of designation

###### 4

Designation under this Order shall take effect on 19th February 1996, and the designated area shall remain an enterprise zone for a period of ten years from that date.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — 16th January 1996

We consent,

Bowen Wells — Michael Bates — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 18th January 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates as an enterprise zone an area which is located in the Wallsend neighbourhood of the metropolitan borough of North Tyneside to the east of the A19(T) road, and which has been the subject of the Tyne Riverside Enterprise Zone No. 1 (Hadrian Business Park South) Scheme adopted by the North Tyneside Metropolitan Borough Council.

The designation is effective for a period of 10 years.

The location and area of the enterprise zone are shown on the plan forming part of this note. The detailed map referred to in article 2(2) may be inspected at the principal offices of the North Tyneside Metropolitan Borough Council, and at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment.
