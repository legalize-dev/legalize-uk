# The National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2002

Made: 20th June 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 41(3) of the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2002.

- (2) This Order extends to England only.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 41(10) of and paragraph 28 of Schedule 2 to the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997 (minor and consequential amendments) insofar as they relate to the definition of “health service body” is 24th June 2002.

## Signed

Alan Milburn — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State, — Department of Health — 20th June 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 24th June 2002 a provision of the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act 1997 which inserts the definition of “health service body” into section 128 of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00002].
