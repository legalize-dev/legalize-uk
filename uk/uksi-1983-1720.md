# The Water Act 1983 (Dissolution of Water Space Amenity Commission) Order 1983

Made: 21st November 1983

The Secretary of State, being satisfied that the duty of the Water Space Amenity Commission under paragraph 9 of Schedule 2 to the Water Act 1983 has been discharged, in exercise of his powers under paragraph 10(2) of the said Schedule 2 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Water Act 1983 (Dissolution of Water Space Amenity Commission) Order 1983.

###### 2

On 1st December 1983 the Water Space Amenity Commission shall cease to exist.

## Signed

Patrick Jenkin — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — 21st November 1983

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order appoints 1st December 1983 as the day on which the Water Space Amenity Commission will cease to exist.
