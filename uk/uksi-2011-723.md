# The Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2011

Made: 10th March 2011

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 2011

Coming into force: 1st April 2011

In accordance with section 4A(8) of that Act the Treasury have consulted, where they think it appropriate, the Scottish Ministers, the Department of Finance and Personnel for Northern Ireland and the Welsh Ministers.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as The Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000 (Estimates and Accounts) Order 2011.

- (2) It comes into force on 1st April 2011.

#### Designations

###### 2

A body listed in column 2 of a table in the Schedule is a designated body in relation to the department named in column 1 of that table for the financial year that ends on 31st March 2012.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates specified central government bodies to named government departments for the purposes of those departments’ supply estimates and resource accounts. In doing so it gives effect to changes to government financial reporting introduced as part of the “Clear Line of Sight” project: see the debate and resolution which approved the project’s implementation in House of Commons Debates, 5th July 2010, vol. 513, cc85-108.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
