# The Antarctic Act 1994 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made: 26th May 1997

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 35 of the Antarctic Act 1994[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Antarctic Act 1994 (Commencement) Order 1997.

#### Commencement of Section 5 of the Antarctic Act 1994

###### 2

Section 5 of the Antarctic Act 1994 shall come into force on 1st June 1997.

## Signed

Tony Lloyd — For the Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs — 26th May 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st June 1997 Section 5 of the Antarctic Act 1994. The section makes provision for permits for British vessels and aircraft entering Antarctica.
