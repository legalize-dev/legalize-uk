# The Taxation of Chargeable Gains (Gilt-edged Securities) Order 2006

Made: 30th January 2006

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by paragraph 1 of Schedule 9 to the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Taxation of Chargeable Gains (Gilt-edged Securities) Order 2006.

#### Securities specified as gilt-edged securities

###### 2

For the purposes of Schedule 9 to the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (gilt-edged securities) the following securities are specified—

- 4¼% Treasury Gilt 2011;

- 4¾% Treasury Stock 2020;

- 4¼% Treasury Stock 2036;

- 1¼% Index-linked Treasury Gilt 2055;

- 4¼% Treasury Gilt 2055.

## Signed

Dave Watts — Tom Watson — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 30th January 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies five further gilt-edged securities, disposals of which are exempt from tax on chargeable gains in accordance with section 115 of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (“the 1992 Act”).

Other gilt-edged securities, disposals of which are exempt from tax on chargeable gains in accordance with section 115, are specified in Part 2 of Schedule 9 to the 1992 Act and in [S.I. 1993/950](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/950), [1994/2656](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/2656), [1996/1031](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1031), [2001/1122](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1122), [2002/2849](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2849), [2004/438](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/438) and [2005/276](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/276).

A complete list of gilts to which this and previous Orders apply may be found on the HM Revenue & Customs website (www.hmrc.gov.uk) or obtained by writing to HM Revenue & Customs, Ministerial Correspondence Unit, 1st Floor, Ferrers House, PO Box 38, Castle Meadow Road, Nottingham, NG2 1BB.
