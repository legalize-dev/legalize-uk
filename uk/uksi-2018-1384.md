# The Designation of Rural Primary Schools (England) Order 2018

Made: 18th December 2018

Coming into force: 27th December 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(7)(b) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006 .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Designation of Rural Primary Schools (England) Order 2018 and comes into force on 27th December 2018.

#### Designation of rural primary schools

###### 2

The schools in the list called “List of designated rural primary schools 2018”, published on the Gov.uk website on 18th December 2018, are designated as rural primary schools in England.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Designation of Rural Primary Schools (England) Order 2017 is revoked.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the Secretary of State

Lesley Jones — Deputy Director — 18th December 2018
