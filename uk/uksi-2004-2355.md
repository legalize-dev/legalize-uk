# The Town and Country Planning (London Borough of Camden) Special Development (Amendment) Order 2004

Made: 7th September 2004

Laid before Parliament: 15th September 2004

Coming into force: 6th October 2004

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 59 and s.333(7) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, application and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (London Borough of Camden) Special Development (Amendment) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 6th October 2004.

- (2) This Order applies to land within the designated area.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 2004 Order” means the Town and Country Planning (London Borough of Camden) Special Development Order 2004[^f00002] and expressions used in this Order shall have the meaning which they bear in the 2004 Order.

#### Amendment of the 2004 Order

###### 3

In article 4 of the 2004 Order (publicity for applications for planning permission)—

- (a) for “paragraphs (2) to (5)” substitute “paragraphs (2) to (6)”;

- (b) after paragraph (5) of the paragraphs inserted into the 1995 Order insert—

> > (6) Where the notice is, without any fault or intention of the local planning authority, removed, obscured or defaced before the period of 21 days referred to in paragraph (3)(a), (3A)(a), (4)(a) or (5)(a) has elapsed, the local planning authority shall be treated as having complied with the requirements of the relevant paragraph if they have taken reasonable steps for protection of the notice and, if need be, its replacement

## Signed

Signed by authority of the First Secretary of State

Keith Hill — Minister of State, — Office of the Deputy Prime Minister — 7th September 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 4 of the Town and Country Planning (London Borough of Camden) Special Development Order 2004 so as to include a reference to paragraphs (3)(a), (3A)(a), (4)(a) and (5)(a) in article 8(6) of the Town and Country Planning (General Development Procedure) Order 1995 ([S.I.1995/419](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/419)) .

This provision deals with compliance with the 21 day period for the display of site notices in the event notices are removed, obscured or defaced.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of businesses, charities, or voluntary bodies.
