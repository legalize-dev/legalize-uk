# The A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent (Amendment) Order 2026

Made: 15th April 2026

Coming into force: 16th April 2026

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers in paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act 2008, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent (Amendment) Order 2026 and comes into force on 16th April 2026.

#### Amendment of the A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024

###### 2

The A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024 is amended as follows.

#### Certification of new, revised or substituted plans

###### 3

- (1) The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of any new, revised or substituted plans to the Secretary of State for certification that they are true copies of the plans referred to in this Order.

- (2) A plan so certified by the Secretary of State is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the A66 Northern Trans-Pennine Development Consent Order 2024 ([S.I. 2024/360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/360)) (“*the 2024 Order*”), a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008 (“*the Act*”).

This Order follows an application under paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to the Act to make the following non-material changes to the 2024 Order—

- (a) article 4 provides for six amendments to the Work No. descriptions and the insertion of new Work No. 0102-1D-A in Schedule 1 (authorised development);

- (b) articles 5 to 13 provided for amendments to Schedule 2 (permanent stopping up of highways and private means of access and provision of new highways and private means of access). Articles 5 and 6 amend the tables in Parts 1 and 3 that relate to Scheme 03 (Penrith to Temple Sowerby) and articles 7 to 13 amend the tables in Parts 1 and 3 that relate to Scheme 0405 (Temple Sowerby to Appleby) in Schedule 2;

- (c) article 14 provides for five amendments to the measurements of highways set out in Schedule 7 (classification of roads, etc.);

- (d) article 15 provides for four amendments to the measurement of highways set out in Schedule 8 (traffic regulation measures etc.);

- (e) article 3 provides for the certification of new, revised or substituted plans required to show the non-material changes to the 2024 Order; and

- (f) article 16 provides for amendments to Schedule 10 (documents to be certified) required due to the new, revised or substituted plans are set out in the Schedule to this Order. This Order substitutes the table in Schedule 10 for the table in the Schedule to this Order.

A copy of the substituted plans mentioned in this Order may be inspected free of charge during working hours at the offices of National Highways, Three Snowhill, Snow Hill Queensway, Birmingham, B4 4GA.
