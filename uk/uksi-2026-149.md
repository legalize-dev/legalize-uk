# The Utilities Act 2000 (Amendment of Section 105) Order 2026

Made: 19th February 2026

Laid before Parliament: 23rd February 2026

Coming into force: 16th March 2026

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the power conferred by [section 105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/section/105) [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/section/105) of the [Utilities Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/contents)[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Utilities Act 2000 (Amendment of Section 105) Order 2026 and comes into force on 16th March 2026.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

#### Amendment to the Utilities Act 2000

###### 2

In section 105(3) of the Utilities Act 2000 (exceptions to the general restriction on disclosure of information), after paragraph (ac), insert—

> > (aca) it is made for the purpose of facilitating the performance of any functions of—
>   (i) the Secretary of State under section 13 of the Energy Prices Act 2022[^f00002], or
>   (ii) any person under regulations under section 19 of that Act;
> (acb) it is made by a person who is subject to a direction given under section 22 of the Energy Prices Act 2022 for the purpose of complying with the direction;

## Signed

Alan Whitehead — Minister of State — Department for Energy Security and Net Zero — 19th February 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

Article 2 inserts new paragraphs into [section 105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/section/105) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/section/105) of the [Utilities Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/contents) (c. 27) (“*the Act*”).

The effect of this insertion is to create exemptions to the prohibition on information sharing in [section 105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/section/105) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/section/105) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/27/contents). New paragraph (aca) exempts disclosure made for the purpose of facilitating the performance of the Secretary of State’s functions under section 13 of the Energy Prices Act [2022 (c. 44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/44) or the performance of the functions of any person under regulations under section 19 of that Act. New paragraph (acb) exempts disclosure made by a person subject to a direction under section 22 of that Act where the disclosure is for the purpose of complying with the direction.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
