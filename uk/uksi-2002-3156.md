# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2002

Made: 17th December 2002

Coming into force: 18th December 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of December 2002 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) (No. 2) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 18th December 2002.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 18th December 2002.

## SCHEDULE

- Paul Wagstaff

- Mary Hamby

- Sonja Oyen

- Andrew Bennett

- David Jones

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

(This note is not part of the Order)
