# The Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement for a Transmission Licence) (East Anglia One) (England) Order 2022

Made: 16th May 2022

Laid before Parliament: 20th May 2022

Coming into force: 10th June 2022

The Secretary of State has given notice of the proposal to make this Order in accordance with section 5(2) and (3) of that Act, and consulted with the Scottish Ministers[^f00002], and has considered the representations made in relation to the proposal.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Electricity (Individual Exemption from the Requirement for a Transmission Licence) (East Anglia One) (England) Order 2022 and comes into force on 10th June 2022.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

- (3) This Order applies in relation to England only.

#### Exemption from section 4(1)(b) of the Electricity Act 1989

###### 2

- (1) The Secretary of State grants exemption from section 4(1)(b) of the Act (prohibition on unlicensed transmission of electricity for supply) to East Anglia One Limited in respect of transmission[^f00003] taking place over the transmission system[^f00004] connected to the East Anglia One Offshore Wind Farm.

- (2) The exemption in paragraph (1) of this Article applies for the period beginning with 10th June 2022 and ending with the earlier of—

- (a) the day after the day on which the transmission system connected to East Anglia One Offshore Wind Farm is transferred to a successful bidder;

- (b) 13th April 2023.

- (3) In this Article—

- (a) “the Act” means the Electricity Act 1989;

- (b) “East Anglia One Limited” means East Anglia One Limited, a company registered in England and Wales with the company number 07366753;

- (c) “East Anglia One Offshore Wind Farm” means the generating station located approximately 48.3 kilometres from the shore, in the southern North Sea, off the Suffolk coast, its coordinates at its central point being Latitude 52.2282999 and Longitude 2.4935852;

- (d) “successful bidder” means, in relation to the transfer of a transmission system, a person to whom, as a result of a tender exercise, an offshore transmission licence within the meaning of section 6C(5) of the Act[^f00005] (competitive tenders for offshore transmission licences) has been or is to be granted in respect of that transmission system.

## Signed

Greg Hands — Minister of State — Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy — 16th May 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order grants exemption from the requirement to hold an electricity transmission licence.

Section 4(1)(b) of the Electricity Act [1989 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29) prohibits the transmission of electricity for supply without a licence. The Secretary of State, exercising the powers under section 5(1) of the Electricity Act 1989, grants a time limited exemption from the requirement to hold a transmission licence to East Anglia One Limited in respect of the East Anglia One Offshore Wind Farm.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it is a temporary measure. No, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been prepared and is available alongside this instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
