# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1995

Made: 5th August 1995

Laid before Parliament: 8th August 1995

Coming into force: 15th August 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 89(3), (6) and (7)[^f00001], 105(1), (2) and (3)[^f00002], 108(1)[^f00003] and 192(3)[^f00004] of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00005], after consulting with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 15th August 1995.

###### 2

In Schedule 3 (categories of motor vehicles for driving test purposes) to the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1987[^f00006], in the entry relating to category N, for the words in column (2) there shall be substituted the words “Vehicle specified in sub-paragraph (j) or (jj) of regulation 27(1) of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Large Goods and Passenger-Carrying Vehicles) Regulations 1990.”[^f00007].

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the Secretary of State

Steven Norris — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 5th August 1995.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations contain a minor amendment consequential upon the substitution (by S.I. [1995/2075](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2075)) of two new exemptions from the provisions of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Large Goods and Passenger-Carrying Vehicles) Regulations 1990, which are now specified in paragraphs (j) and (jj) of regulation 27(1) of those regulations, for the exemption previously contained in sub-paragraph (j) of regulation 27(1).

Category N is redefined so as to correspond with the classes of vehicle specified in regulation 27(1)(j) and (jj), being vehicles which are not used on roads which are repairable at the public expense or are so used for only short distances.
