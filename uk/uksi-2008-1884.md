# The General Teaching Council for England (Eligibility for Provisional Registration) Regulations 2008

Made: 15th July 2007

Laid before Parliament: 22nd July 2008

Coming into force: 1st September 2008

In accordance with section 42(9) of that Act he has consulted the General Teaching Council for England.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the General Teaching Council for England (Eligibility for Provisional Registration) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 1st September 2008.

- (2) They apply only in relation to England.

#### Eligibility for provisional registration

###### 2

- (1) A person is eligible for provisional registration if the person is not for the time being—

- (a) subject to a direction under section 142(1)(a) of the Education Act 2002[^f00002] (prohibition from teaching, etc);

- (b) subject to a disciplinary order made under Schedule 2 to the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998 (disciplinary powers of Council), by virtue of which the person is not eligible for registration; or

- (c) ineligible for registration as a teacher in Scotland or Northern Ireland, or disqualified from being a teacher in any school, by virtue of—

- (i) section 11 of the Teaching Council (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00003];

- (ii) regulation 9 of the Teachers’ (Eligibility) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004]; or

- (iii) regulations made under article 36 of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00005].

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Children, Schools and Families — 15th July 2008

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe those persons who are eligible for provisional registration with the General Teaching Council for England.
