# The Legal Aid (Information about Financial Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

Made: 1st April 2014

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

In accordance with section 41(6) and (7)(h) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid (Information about Financial Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendment of the Legal Aid (Information about Financial Resources) Regulations 2013

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Legal Aid (Information about Financial Resources) Regulations 2013[^f00004], after paragraph 5 insert—

> > (5A) Maternity allowance payable under section 35B of the 1992 Act[^f00005] or section 35B of the 1992 (Northern Ireland) Act[^f00006].

## Signed

Shailesh Vara — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 1st April 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 22 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act [2012 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/10) (“the Act”) provides that the relevant authority (in practice, the Director of Legal Aid Casework) may make an information request to the Secretary of State, a relevant Northern Ireland Department (defined in section 22(8) of the Act as the Department for Social Development in Northern Ireland or the Department of Finance and Personnel in Northern Ireland) or the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs for the purposes of facilitating a determination about an individual’s financial resources for the purpose of legal aid available under Part 1 of the Act.

A request for information under section 22 can include a request for certain information about prescribed benefits. The prescribed benefits are prescribed in regulations 5, 6 and 7 of, and the Schedule to, the Legal Aid (Information about Financial Resources) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/628](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/628)). Regulation 2 of these Regulations adds to the list of prescribed benefits in the Schedule to the Legal Aid (Information about Financial Resources) Regulations 2013 maternity allowance payable under section 35B of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) or section 35B of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7). An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument has it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations.
