# The Corn Returns (Variation) Regulations 1990

Made: 3rd July 1990

Coming into force: 1st August 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5 and 14 of the Corn Returns Act 1882[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling themin that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Corn Returns (Variation) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st August 1990.

#### Variation of the Corn Returns Regulations 1976

###### 2

The Corn Returns Regulations 1976[^f00003] shall be further varied by substituting for Schedule 2 thereto (which sets out the form of returns required by those Regulations) the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Corn Returns (Amendment) Regulations 1981[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## FORM APS 4 (Rev. 1990)

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on

John Selwyn Gummer — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 3rd July 1990.

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 27th June 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Corn Returns Regulations 1976 by substituting a new form of return for the previous form set out in theSchedule to the Corn Returns (Amendment) Regulations 1981. The principal change is in the footnotes to clarify the appropriate use of each row of the return.
