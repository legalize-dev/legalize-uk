# The Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2004

Made: 23rd August 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 80(1) of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2004.

###### 2

- (1) The provisions of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 listed in paragraph (2) shall come into force on 2nd September 2004.

- (2) The provisions referred to in paragraph (1) are—

- (a) section 46 (exclusion orders);

- (b) section 52 (community sentences: electronic monitoring of requirements) to the extent that it is not already in force, save only in so far as it relates to exclusion requirements;

- (c) section 74 (amendments) in so far as it relates to the provisions referred to in sub-paragraph (d);

- (d) in Schedule 7 (minor and consequential amendments)—

- (i) paragraph 161 to the extent that it is not already in force;

- (ii) paragraph 196 to the extent that it is not already in force, except for—

- (aa) sub-paragraph (c)(iii); and

- (bb) the references to paragraph 8 of Schedule 2 to the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000[^f00002];

- (iii) in paragraph 197–

- (aa) in sub-paragraph (f), the entries referring to an exclusion order; and

- (bb) sub-paragraph (g) to the extent that it is not already in force;

- (iv) in paragraph 199, sub-paragraphs (2), (5) and (21)(a) to (e) to the extent that they are not already in force.

## Signed

Fiona Mactaggart — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 23rd August 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force sections 46 (exclusion orders) in full and 52 (community sentences: electronic monitoring of requirements) in part of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act [2000 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43) on 2nd September 2004, together with related minor and consequential amendments.
