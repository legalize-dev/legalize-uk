# The Health Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007

Made: 1st May 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 83(7) and (8) of the Health Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation, interpretation and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Health Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007.

- (2) In this Order references to sections, Parts and Schedules are references to sections and Parts of, and Schedules to, the Health Act 2006.

- (3) In relation to the commencement of sections 1 to 12 (other than section 5(4)), sections 76 and 77 and Schedules 1 and 2, this Order applies only in relation to England.

- (4) In relation to section 5(4), this Order applies in relation to England, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

- (5) In relation to section 13, this Order applies in relation to England and Wales.

#### Appointed day for provisions relating to smoking

###### 2

1st July 2007 is the day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) Part 1 and Schedules 1 and 2, in so far as they are not already in force;

- (b) sections 76 and 77, in so far as they relate to offences under that Part.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Caroline Flint — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 1st May 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Health Act 2006 which relate to smoke-free premises, places and vehicles (sections 1 to 12 and Schedules 1 and 2) and the age for sale of tobacco, etc. (section 13).
