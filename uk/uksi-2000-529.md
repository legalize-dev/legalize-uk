# The A406 Trunk Road (Gunnersbury Avenue, Hounslow) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 2000

Made: 29th February 2000

Coming into force: 17th March 2000

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A406 Trunk Road (Gunnersbury Avenue, Hounslow) Red Route (Prescribed Route) Traffic Order 2000, and shall come into force on 17th March 2000.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order–

- (a) a reference to an article followed by a number is a reference to the article of this Order so numbered;

- (b) causing includes permitting.

#### Prescribed Routes

###### 3

Subject to the provisions of article 4, no person shall cause any vehicle on entering the A406 Gunnersbury Avenue from the northernmost exit of the unnamed service road fronting Nos. 127 to 139 and The International School of London, Gunnersbury Avenue in the London Borough of Hounslow to proceed in any direction other than left and northbound into A406 Gunnersbury Avenue.

#### Exemptions

###### 4

The provisions of article 3 shall not apply–

- (a) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden; or

- (b) to a vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purposes.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 29th February 2000
