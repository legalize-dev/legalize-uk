# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales (No. 2) Order 1999

Made: 13th April 1999

Laid before Parliament: 23rd April 1999

Coming into force: 1st June 1999

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 13th day of April 1999 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) (No. 2) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales with effect from 1st June 1999.

## SCHEDULE

- Ulan Connick Esquire

- Huw Collins Esquire

- Mrs. Carolyn Currie

- Jeffrey Rees Jones Esquire

- Mrs. Janice Thomas

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council
