# The Pensions Increase (Parliamentary Commissioner) Regulations 1979

Made: 4th June 1979

Laid before Parliament: 12th June 1979

Coming into Operation: 4th July 1979

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(2) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Parliamentary Commissioner) Regulations 1979 and shall come into operation on 4th July 1979.

- (2) In these Regulations “*pension*” means a pension within the meaning of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971.

###### 2

- (1) The Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 shall have effect in relation to a pension payable by virtue of paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 of the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 as if it were a pension specified in Part I of Schedule 2 of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971.

## Signed

Given under the official seal of the Minister for the Civil Service on the 4th day of June 1979.

Paul Channon — Minister of State — Civil Service Department

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations apply the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 to a pension payable to a Parliamentary Commissioner who exercises the right of election, given by Schedule 1 of the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967, for the statutory scheme for pensions and other benefits applicable to the judicial offices listed in Schedule 1 of the Judicial Pensions Act 1959. The Regulations contain no provision for retrospective effect since no former Parliamentary Commissioner elected for that scheme before his retirement.
