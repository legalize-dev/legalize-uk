# The Transport and Works Act 1992 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1998

Made: 5th February 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 70(1) of the Transport and Works Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Transport and Works Act 1992 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1998.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

The provisions of the Transport and Works Act 1992 specified in the Schedule to this Order (which relate to repeals) shall come into force on 26th February 1998.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS COMING INTO FORCE ON 26TH FEBRUARY 1998

- Section 68 insofar as it relates to the entries in Schedule 4 referred to below.

- The entries in Part I of Schedule 4 relating to the following enactments: The Town Police Clauses Act 1889[^f00002] The Notice of Accidents Act 1894[^f00003] The Notice of Accidents Act 1906[^f00004] Section 43 of the Road and Rail Traffic Act 1933[^f00005] The Transport Charges &c (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1954[^f00006] The Public Service Vehicles (Arrest of Offenders) Act 1975[^f00007] The Channel Tunnel Act 1987[^f00008].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Glenda Jackson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 5th February 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 26th February 1998 certain repeals provided for in section 68 of, and Schedule 4 to, the Transport and Works Act 1992.
