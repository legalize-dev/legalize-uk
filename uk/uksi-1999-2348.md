# The A205 Trunk Road (Lambeth) Red Route (Prohibition of Traffic) Traffic Order 1999

Made: 19th August 1999

Coming into force: 6th September 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencment and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A205 Trunk Road (Lambeth) Red Route (Prohibition of Traffic) Traffic Order 1999, and shall come into force on 6th September 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order:—

- (a) a reference to an article followed by a number is a reference to the article of this order so numbered;

- (b) Causing includes permitting;

- (c) “Cavendish Road” means that length of the A205 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Lambeth known as Cavendish Road;

- (d) “Hazelbourne Road” means the road of that name in the London Borough of Lambeth and in the London Borough of Wandsworth.

#### Prohibition

###### 3

- (1) Subject to the provisions of article 4, no person shall cause any vehicle, to:—

- (a) enter Cavendish Road from Hazelbourne Road; or

- (b) proceed from Cavendish Road to Hazelbourne Road.

#### Exemptions

###### 4

The provisions of articles 3 do not apply:—

- (a) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden;

- (b) to a vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purposes; or

- (c) to a pedal cycle as defined in regulation 4 of the Traffic Signs Regulations and General Directions 1994[^f00003].

#### Revocation or variation of existing Orders

###### 5

The A205 Trunk Road (Lambeth) Red Route (Prohibition of Traffic) Experimental Traffic Order 1998[^f00004] is hereby revoked in so far as its provisions relate to any length of road specified in article 2 of this order.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 19th August 1999
