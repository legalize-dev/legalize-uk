## SCHEDULE

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or Department* | *Matters in relation to which the Ministers or Department are designated.* |
| The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food | Measures relating to the conservation of natural habitats and of wild fauna and flora[^f00005] |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to maritime transport. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to the approval of satellite earth station equipment. |
| The Secretary of State and the Treasury | Measures relating to the movement of capital and to payments, between Member States and between Member States and countries which are not Member States. |
| The Secretary of State and the Treasury | Measures relating to the interruption or reduction, in part or completely, of economic relations with one or more countries which are not Member States. |
