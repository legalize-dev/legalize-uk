# The East Riding Health Authority (Change of Name) Order 2000

Made: 8th May 2000

Laid before Parliament: 9th May 2000

Coming into force: 1st June 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 8(4) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited at the East Riding Health Authority (Change of Name) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st June 2000.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Establishment Order” means the Health Authorities (England) Establishment Order 1996[^f00002];

- “the Authority” means the Health Authority established by article 2 of the Establishment Order for the area described in column (2) of the Schedule to that Order as the counties of East Riding of Yorkshire and the city of Kingston-upon-Hull.

#### Change of name of the Health Authority

###### 2

- (1) The name by which the Authority are known is changed to East Riding and Hull Health Authority.

- (2) In column (1) of the Schedule to the Establishment Order, for the entry “East Riding Health Authority” substitute “East Riding and Hull Health Authority”.

#### Transitional provisions

###### 3

The change of name under this Order shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or

- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the Authority by its previous name, and all instruments and other documents which refer to the Authority under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 8th May 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of East Riding Health Authority to East Riding and Hull Health Authority, and makes a consequential amendment to the Health Authorities (England) Establishment Order 1996 and transitional provisions.
