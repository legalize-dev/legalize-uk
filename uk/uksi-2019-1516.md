# The Designation of Rural Primary Schools (England) Order 2019

Made: 19th December 2019

Coming into force: 27th December 2019

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(7)(b) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006 .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Designation of Rural Primary Schools (England) Order 2019 and comes into force on 27th December 2019.

#### Designation of rural primary schools

###### 2

The schools named in the list called “List of designated rural primary schools 2019”, published on the Gov.uk website on 18th December 2019, are designated as rural primary schools in England.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Designation of Rural Primary Schools (England) Order 2018 is revoked.

## Signed

For and on behalf of the Secretary of State

Kim Sibley — Deputy Director — 2019-12-19
