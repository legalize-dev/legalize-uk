# The Sheffield Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

Made: 21st March 2002

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Sheffield Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the Sheffield Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00004];

- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

#### Change of name of NHS trust and transitional provisions

###### 2

- (1) The trust shall be called the Sheffield Children’s National Health Service Trust instead of the Sheffield Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust, and accordingly in article 1 of the Order in the definition of “the trust”, and in article 2 of the Order, for “Sheffield Children’s Hospital” there shall be substituted “Sheffield Children's”.

- (2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or

- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before, on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name,

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

#### Further amendment of NHS trust establishment order

###### 3

- (1) For article 3 of the Order (nature and functions of the trust) there shall be substituted the following article—

> > (3)
>   (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>   (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services and community health services, for the purposes of the health service at or from Sheffield Children’s Hospital, Western Bank, Sheffield, S10 2TH, and at or from any associated hospitals, establishments or facilities.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Yvette Cooper — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 21st March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Sheffield Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991, which established the Sheffield Children’s National Health Service Trust, and changes the name of that trust.

Article 2 of the Order changes the name of the trust from the Sheffield Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust to the Sheffield Children’s National Health Service Trust, and makes consequential amendments to the Establishment Order and transitional provisions. Article 3 of the order substitutes a new article 3 of the Establishment Order (nature and functions of the trust) so that the trust can provide hospital accommodation and services and community health services.
