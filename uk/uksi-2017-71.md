## SCHEDULE — The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

Part 4 (Fees payable) of the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) Regulations 2014 be replaced by the following—

| Type of registrant | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| New graduates registered by 31 December following graduation | 157 |
| Other persons registered between 1 April and 30 September | 314 |
| Other persons registered between 1 October and 31 March | 157 |

| Type of fee | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| Normal registration fee | 314 |
| Registration fee for practice restricted to period of six months or less | 157 |
| Retention fee | 314 |

| Circumstances | Column A – Fee payable if payment received by 30 April (£) | Column B – Fee payable if payment received after 30 April (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard registrant | 314 | 349 |
| Overseas practising registrant | 157 | 192 |
| Non-practising registrant | 51 | 86 |

| Fee paid (£) | New circumstance | *Extra sum payable if change takes place before 1 October**(£)* | *Extra sum payable if change takes place on or after 1 October**(£)* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 51 or 86 | Practising but not in the British Islands | 106 | 53 |
| 51 or 86 | Practising in the British Islands | 263 | 131 |
| 157 or 192 | Practising in the British Islands | 157 | 78 |

| Fee paid (£) | New circumstance | Sum to be credited (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 314 or 349 | Non-practising registrant | 131 |
| 314 or 349 | Overseas practising registrant | 78 |
| 157 or 192 | Non-practising registrant | 53 |

| Type of restoration | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| Restoration under section 11(3) of the Act following removal for non-receipt of retention fee | 314 |
| Restoration in other circumstances | 78 |

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
