# The Childcare (Correction to Miscellaneous Amendments) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 28th March 2019

Laid before Parliament: at 12.15 p.m. on 29th March 2019

Coming into force: at 10.00 p.m. on 29th March 2019

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 84(1) and (3), 84A(1) and (3), 90(2) and 104(2) of the Childcare Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Childcare (Correction to Miscellaneous Amendments) (England) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force at 10.00 p.m. on 29th March 2019.

#### Amendment of the Childcare (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) (England) Regulations 2018

###### 2

In regulation 1 of the Childcare (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) (England) Regulations 2018[^f00002] for “at 11.00 p.m. on 29th March 2019” substitute “on exit day”.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Education

Nadhim Zahawi — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Education — 28th March 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend regulation 1 of the Childcare (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) (England) Regulations 2018. The amendment changes the commencement date of those Regulations from 11.00 p.m. on 29th March 2019 to exit day (as defined in section 20 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
