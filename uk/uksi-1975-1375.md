# The Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

Made: 14th August 1975

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 19(6) of the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975.

###### 2

The Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 shall come into force on 1st September 1975.

## Signed

Roy Jenkins — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — 14th August 1975

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975 on 1st September 1975.
