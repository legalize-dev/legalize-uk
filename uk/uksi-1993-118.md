# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1993

Made: 22nd January 1993

Coming into force: 22nd February 1993

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00001], section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Lord Lyon King of Arms, enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1993 and shall come into force on 22nd February 1993.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of fees

###### 2

- (1) Subject to the provisions of the following sub-paragraphs, for the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1990[^f00003], substitute the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt.

- (2) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1990 as substituted by sub-paragraph (1) above shall apply to work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

- (3) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1990 having effect before the substitution under sub-paragraph (1) above shall continue to have effect in respect of work done before the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at Arms) 1990 (S.I. [1990/379](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/379)) by increasing the fees payable to Messengers-at-Arms by about 4%. The last increase was in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1992 (S.I. [1992/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/87)) as amended by the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) (Amendment) 1992 (S.I. [1992/529](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/529)).
