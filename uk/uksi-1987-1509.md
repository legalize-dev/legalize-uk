# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1987

Made: 24th August 1987

Laid before Parliament: 4th September 1987

Coming into force: 1st October 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 47(1) and 50(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 199(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation, commencement and introduction

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 1st October 1987.

###### 2

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984[^f00003] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

#### Amendment of regulation 3 (application)

###### 3

In paragraph (1)(a)(ii) of regulation 3, for the words “40 kilometres per hour” there shall be substituted the words “50 kilometres per hour”.

#### Amendments relating to noise and silencers

###### 4

In regulation 4 (type approval requirements—application), the following new paragraphs shall be added at the end—

> > (8) Subject to paragraph (9), where, in relation to item 14B, 14C or 14D in Schedule 1, a date is specified in column (6) of that Schedule (being a date of cessation of application of type approval requirements), that date shall not apply to any vehicle for which, or for a model of which, there is a type approval certificate or (as the case may be) a Minister’s approval certificate in force at the time but, in relation to any such vehicle, column (6) shall be read and shall have effect as if there appeared in that column a date one year later than the date specified therein.
> (9) In relation to any vehicle—
>   (a) which has 5 or more forward gears and a maximum power to maximum gross weight ratio of not less than 75 kilowatts per tonne; and
>   (b) for which, or for a model of which, there is a type approval certificate or (as the case may be) a Minister’s approval certificate in force on 30th September 1988,
> notwithstanding the specification in item 14B or 14C in column (6) of Schedule 1 of any date, that column shall be read and have effect as if no date appeared therein.

###### 5

In Schedule 1—

- (a) in items 14B, 14C and 14D, in column (6) “30.9.1988” shall be inserted as the date of cessation of application of those type approval requirements;

- (b) in item 14D, in column (3)(b) immediately before the date “18.5.81” there shall be inserted the reference “L 131”; and

- (c) after item 14D, there shall be inserted as item 14E the item so numbered in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Amendments relating to brakes

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984. Regulations 4 and 5 revise the type approval requirements for noise and silencers in regulation 4 and items 14B, 14C and 14D in Schedule 1 and add a new item 14E to that Schedule so that—

- (i) all vehicles for which, or for a model of which, there is no type approval or Minister’s approval certificate in force on 30th September 1988 must meet the standards contained in Council Directive [84/424/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0424) with the exception of certain vehicles having 5 or more forward gears, and

- (ii) all vehicles for which, or for a model of which, there is a type approval or Minister’s approval certificate in force on 30th September 1988 must meet the above standard on 1st October 1989, save that a derogation is included in item 14E for certain vehicles having 5 or more forward gears.

Regulation 6 provides for the cessation of the application of certain of the prescribed requirements specified in items 13B, 13C and 13D in Schedule 1 relating to brakes, with the result that after 30th September 1989 requirements prescribed will be those set out in either Council Directive [71/320/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0320) as amended by Commission Directives [75/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0524), [79/489/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0489) and [85/647/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0647) or ECE Regulation 13 (05 series of amendments).

Regulation 7 makes amendments to the type approval requirements in Schedule 1 relating to headlamps, lights and reflectors; doors, latches and hinges; brakes; seats, seat belts and their anchorages; anti-theft devices; interior fittings; emissions; external projections; and safety glass. Regulation 8 amends the references to the Construction and Use Regulations, to refer to the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986.

Copies of the EEC Directives referred to in these Regulations may be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office.
