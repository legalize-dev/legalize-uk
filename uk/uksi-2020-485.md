# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: 4th May 2020

Laid before Parliament: 5th May 2020

Coming into force: 26th May 2020

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the environmental aspects of product design.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 26th May 2020.

#### Amendment of the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010

###### 2

In paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 to the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010[^f00003] (declaration of conformity) for the second column of entry 1 in the table substitute—

| [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2019/1782](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1782/2019) laying down ecodesign requirements for external power supplies pursuant to [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing [Commission Regulation (EC) No 278/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0278)[^f00004] |
| --- |

## Signed

Kwasi Kwarteng — Minister of State — Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy — 4th May 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This instrument amends the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2617](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2617)) in relation to external power sources to substitute a new Commission Regulation into the list of implementing measures that must be included in a declaration of conformity, to replace a now repealed Commission Regulation.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
