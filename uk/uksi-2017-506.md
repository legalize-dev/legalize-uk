# The Water Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments etc.) Order 2017

Made: 30th March 2017

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(1)

In accordance with section 104(2) of the Water Act 2003 and section 90(3) of the Water Act 2014, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — General

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Water Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments etc.) Order 2017 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 1991 Act” means the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00003];

- “the 2014 Act” means the Water Act 2014.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Water Industry Act [1991 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56), as amended by the Water Act [2014 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/21) (the “2014 Act”), makes changes to the water supply licensing regime in England and Wales and introduces a new sewerage licensing regime in the areas of sewerage undertakers that are wholly or mainly in England.

Part 2 of this Order makes amendments to primary legislation in consequence of those changes and also in consequence of the Water Act [2003 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/37). Part 3 makes consequential amendments and modifications to secondary legislation. Part 4 makes a transitional provision.

Impact assessments of the effect that the 2014 Act will have on the costs of business, the voluntary sector and the public sector is available from the Water Services Team, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, Nobel House, 17 Smith Square, London SW1P 3JR and are published alongside the 2014 Act at www.legislation.gov.uk. No separate impact assessment has been produced for this instrument.
