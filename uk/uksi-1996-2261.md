# The North Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made: 23rd August 1996

Laid before Parliament: 5th September 1996

Coming into force: 1st October 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by Mid Glamorgan District Health Authority[^f00002] resulting from the establishment of North Glamorgan National Health Service Trust[^f00003], having consulted Bro Taf Health Authority[^f00004] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the North Glamorgan National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st October 1996.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means Bro Taf Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means North Glamorgan National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 21st June 1996, which schedule has been signed on behalf of the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 1st October 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Gwilym Jones — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — 23rd August 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to North Glamorgan National Health Service Trust on 1st October 1996 trust property formerly held by Bro Taf Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Welsh Office, HFM3, New Crown Building, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF1 3NQ.
