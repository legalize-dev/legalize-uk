# The West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 2011

Made: 3rd October 2011

Laid before Parliament: 10th October 2011

Coming into force: 17th November 2011

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 135(1) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 26 to, the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) (Amendment) Order 2011 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Amendment of the West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 2004

###### 2

In article 3(2) of the West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 2004[^f00002] for “eleven” substitute “seven”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Baroness Hanham — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 3rd October 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 2004 (“the 2004 Order”) established three areas in Northamptonshire as an urban development area, and established the West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (“the Corporation”) to regenerate the area.

Article 3 of the 2004 Order provides that the Corporation shall consist of eleven members in addition to the chairman and deputy chairman. Article 2 amends article 3(2) to provide that the Corporation shall consist of seven members in addition to the chairman and deputy chairman.

No Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
