# The Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1980

Made: 3rd October 1980

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 89(3) and (4) of the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1980.

- (2) In this Order “*the Act of 1978*” means the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978.

###### 2

The provisions of the Act of 1978 specified in column 1 of Schedule 1 to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in column 2 thereof) shall come into force on 1st February 1981.

###### 3

The transitional provisions in Schedule 2 to this Order shall have effect.

## SCHEDULE 2 — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

###### 1

In this Schedule “*the relevant date*” means the date on which section 33 of the Children Act 1975 comes into force.

In relation to an application made before the relevant date for an order under section 2, 6 or 7 of the Act of 1978, section 19(2) of that Act shall have effect as if the words “a parent of the child but” were omitted and for the words “that parent” there were substituted the words “that person”.

In relation to an applicant under section 20 of the Act of 1978 for the variation or revocation of an order under section 11(3) of that Act pursuant to an application made before the relevant date, subsection (12)(b) of the said section 20 shall have effect as if the words “a parent of that child but” were omitted and for the words “that parent” there were substituted the words “that person”.

In relation to an order under section 8(2) of the Act of 1978 pursuant to an application made before the relevant date, section 25(2)(c) of that Act shall have effect as if the words “a parent of that child but” were omitted.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st February 1981 the provisions of the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 mentioned in Schedule 1 to this Order.

Schedule 2 to this Order contains transitional provisions adapting certain provisions of the Act of 1978 under section 89(4) in consequence of the partial operation of the Children Act 1975, and in particular in consequence of the fact that Part II of the Act of 1975 (which deals with the custody of children) has not yet been brought into force.

Other Orders bringing into force provisions of the Act of 1978 were made by S.I. 1978/997, 1489 and 1490 (for scotland) and 1979/731.
