# The Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2009

Made: 16th July 2009

Laid before Parliament: 23rd July 2009

Coming into force: 1st November 2009

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 213, 217(2) and 272(7) and (8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], having regard to changes in the functions of the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 1st November 2009.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust;

- “the new Trust” means the Central and North West London NHS Foundation Trust[^f00003];

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the new Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 9th February and 16th February 2009 and on behalf of the new Trust on 13th March 2009.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the old Trust to the new Trust.

- (2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference in relation to the trust property to the trustees of the trust property or to the old Trust shall be construed as a reference to the new Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Mike O’Brien — Minister of Health, — Department of Health — 16th July 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order transfers to the Central and North West London NHS Foundation Trust, trust property held by the Hillingdon Hospital National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the authorisation of the Central and North West London NHS Foundation Trust can be inspected at Monitor, 4 Matthew Parker Street, London, SW1H 9NP, www.monitor-nhsft.gov.uk. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
