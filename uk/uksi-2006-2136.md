# The Drugs Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2006

Made: 1st August 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 24 (3) of the Drugs Act 2005[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Drugs Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2006.

###### 2

Section 20 (anti-social behaviour orders: intervention orders) of the Drugs Act 2005 shall come into force on 1st October 2006.

## Signed

Joan Ryan — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 1st August 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings section 20 of the Drugs Act 2005 into force on 1st October 2006.
