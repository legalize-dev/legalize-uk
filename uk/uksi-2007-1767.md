# The Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made: 20th June 2007

Laid before Parliament: 22nd June 2007

Coming into force: 13th July 2007

The Secretary of State for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(4)(e) of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] and after consultation with the Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) (Amendment) Order 2007; and

- (b) comes into force on 13th July 2007.

#### Amendments to the Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) Order 2007

###### 2

- (1) The Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) Order 2007[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) For article 1(1)(b) substitute—

> > (b) applies in the United Kingdom save for Part 2 which applies in England and Scotland only; and

- (3) In article 3(2)(b) omit “, the Scottish Ministers”.

- (4) In article 8(1)(a)(i) for “and” substitute “or”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order applies Part 2 (artificial insemination of cows) of the Veterinary Surgery (Artificial Insemination) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1315](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1315)) to Scotland. It removes the Scottish Ministers from the list of persons who may approve training courses in the artificial insemination of cows and makes one other minor amendment.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
