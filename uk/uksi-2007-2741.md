# The Haringey Sixth Form Centre (Governing Body) Order 2007

Made: 16th September 2007

Laid before Parliament: 24th September 2007

Coming into force: 22nd October 2007

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act[^f00004], the Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families, on the application of the temporary governing body of the School, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Haringey Sixth Form Centre (Governing Body) Order 2007 and comes into force on 22nd October 2007.

#### Exemption

###### 2

Regulations 12(3)(e) and (f), 13(1), 30(2) and paragraph 1 of Schedule 6 to the School Governance (Constitution) (England) Regulations 2007[^f00005] are not to apply to the governing body of the School.

#### Time Period

###### 3

This Order has effect up to and including 31st August 2010.

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Children, Schools and Families — 16th September 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 exempts the governing body of Haringey Sixth Form Centre from the requirements in the School Governance (Constitution) (England) Regulations 2007 for the governing body to consist of certain numbers of governors falling within specified categories. It also exempts the governing body from the requirement that all governors must be aged 18 or over at the date of appointment or election. Article 3 provides that the Order has effect up to and including 31st August 2010.
