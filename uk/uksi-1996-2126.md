# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Oxford) Order 1996

Made: 14th August 1996

Laid before Parliament: 16th August 1996

Coming into force: 7th September 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Oxford) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 7th September 1996.

###### 2

The prison known as H.M. Prison Oxford and situate in the county of Oxfordshire shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

## Signed

Timothy Kirkhope — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 14th August 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Prison Oxford on 7th September 1996.
