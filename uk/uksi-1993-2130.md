# The East Worcester and Severn Trent Water (Amendment of Local Enactments etc.) Order 1993

Made: 25th August 1993

Coming into force: 1st September 1993

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the East Worcester and Severn Trent Water (Amendment of Local Enactments etc.) Order 1993 and shall come into force on1st September 1993.

#### Amendment of references in local statutory provisions etc.

###### 2

- (1) As from 1st September 1993 any reference to, or to the undertaking of, East Worcester in any local statutory provision and in any charges scheme made by East Worcester under section 76 of the Water Act 1989 or section 143 of the Water Industry Act 1991 shall have effect, subject to paragraph (2) below, as a reference to Severn Trent or, as the case may be, to the undertaking of Severn Trent.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall only apply to any local statutory provision or charges scheme in relation to the area, things or persons in relation to which or to whom that provision or scheme has effect before 1st September 1993.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Neil Summerton — An Under Secretary in the — Department of the Environment — 25th August 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends references in local statutory provisions and charges schemes for the purposes of the replacement of East Worcester Water plc as water undertaker by Severn Trent Water Ltd.
