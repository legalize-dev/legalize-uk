# The Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board (Reconstitution) Order 2026

Made: 26th February 2026

Coming into force: 27th February 2026

Under section 3(1)(a) of the Land Drainage Act 1991 (“*the 1991 Act*”)[^f00001], the Environment Agency has prepared a Scheme making provision for the matters specified in section 3(2)(d) and (i)[^f00002] of the 1991 Act and submitted the Scheme to the Secretary of State for confirmation.

The Secretary of State has published a notice of intent to make this Order confirming the Scheme in accordance with paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3[^f00003] to the 1991 Act and has sent this notice to the relevant local authorities and other bodies specified in paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule.

No objection has been made to the draft Order.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(5) of the 1991 Act[^f00004].

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board (Reconstitution) Order 2026 and comes into force on 27th February 2026.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales, and applies to England only.

#### Confirmation of Scheme

###### 2

- (1) The Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency is confirmed with modifications.

- (2) The Scheme, as modified by the Secretary of State, is set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## Schedule — Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency as modified by the Secretary of State

###### 1

This Scheme comes into force on the day after the day on which the Order confirming this Scheme is made.

###### 2

In this Scheme—

- “*the Act*” means the Land Drainage Act 1991;

- “*the Board*” means the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board constituted by the Scheme confirmed by the Yorkshire Ouse and Hull River Authority (Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District) Order 1968[^f00005] and reconstituted by the Scheme confirmed by the Yorkshire Ouse and Hull River Authority (Reconstitution of the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board) Order 1970[^f00006];

- “*the commencement date*” means the date on which this Scheme comes into force;

- “*elected members*” means those members of the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board who are appointed by the Secretary of State pursuant to this Scheme or elected in accordance with Schedule 1 to the Act and any applicable regulations made under the Act;

- “*the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District*” means the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District constituted by the Scheme confirmed by the Yorkshire Ouse and Hull River Authority (Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District) Order 1968;

- “*property*” includes books of account, other books, deeds, maps, papers and other documents, in whatever medium held, and computer and other electronic records;

- “*rights and obligations*” means all rights, powers, duties (including statutory powers and duties), obligations and liabilities.

###### 3

- (1) On the commencement date the Board is reconstituted to comprise six elected members[^f00007].

- (2) The first elected members of the reconstituted Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board are to be appointed by the Secretary of State and are to hold office until the expiry of one year from the first occurrence of 1st November following the day on which they are appointed.

###### 4

From the commencement date, the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District comprises one electoral division[^f00008] and accordingly paragraph 6 of the Scheme confirmed by the Yorkshire Ouse and Hull River Authority (Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District) Order 1968 which divides the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District into three electoral divisions ceases to have effect.

###### 5

The property, rights and obligations of the Board immediately before its reconstitution in accordance with paragraph 3 become the property, rights and obligations of the reconstituted Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board upon its reconstitution.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

William Harrington — Head of Rural Flood Risk — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 26th February 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order confirms, with modifications, a Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency making provision for the reconstitution of the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage Board and its drainage district so that the number of elected members is reduced from twelve to six and the number of electoral divisions of the Goole and Airmyn Internal Drainage District is reduced from three to one.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
