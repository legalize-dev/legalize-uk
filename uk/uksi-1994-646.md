# The Education (National Curriculum) (Assessment Arrangements for English, Welsh, Mathematics and Science) (Key Stage 1) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1994

Made: 10th March 1994

Coming into force: 1st April 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(2)(c), (5), (6), (7) and (8) and section 232(5) and (6) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement, application and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education (National Curriculum) (Assessment Arrangements for English, Welsh, Mathematics and Science) (Key Stage 1) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

- (2) This Order applies to maintained schools in Wales only.

- (3) In this Order, the principal Order means the Education (National Curriculum) (Assessment Arrangements for English, Welsh, Mathematics and Science) (Key Stage 1) (Wales) Order 1993[^f00002].

#### Amendment of Principal Order

###### 2

In article 3(1) of the principal Order, there shall be substituted for the definition of “the assessment authority”, the following definition:

> > - “the assessment authority” means the Awdurdod Cwricwlwm ac Asesu Cymru or the Curriculum and Assessment Authority for Wales[^f00003] and includes any person or body appointed by that authority to discharge its functions under this Order;

## Signed

John Redwood — Secretary of State for Wales — 10th March 1994

## Explanatory note

(This Note is not part of the Order)

With some exceptions not material for the purposes of the present Order, the functions of the School Curriculum and Assessment Authority are, in relation to Wales, now vested in Awdurdod Cwricwlwm ac Asesu Cymru or the Curriculum and Assessment Authority for Wales. The present Order amends the definition of “the assessment authority” in the Education (National Curriculum) (Assessment Arrangements for English, Welsh, Mathematics and Science) (Key Stage 1) (Wales) Order 1993 to reflect this change.
