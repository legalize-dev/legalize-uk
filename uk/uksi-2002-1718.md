# The Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2002

Made: 4th July 2002

Coming into force: 27th July 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 105(2) and 108(3) and (7) of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 2000 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2002.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to England and to police authorities in Wales[^f00002].

#### Provisions of the Local Government Act 2000 coming into force on 27th July 2002

###### 2

The following provisions of the Local Government Act 2000 shall come into force on 27th July 2002—

- (a) section 90;

- (b) in Schedule 6 the repeal of—

- (i) in section 80(1)(e) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00003], the words “or under the Audit Commission Act 1998”;

- (ii) in section 16(1)(a) of the Audit Commission Act 1998[^f00004], the words “or 18”;

- (iii) section 17(1)(b) of that Act and “and” preceding it;

- (iv) in section 17(2) of that Act, the words “subject to subsection (3)” and paragraphs (a) and (b) and the word “and” following paragraph (b);

- (v) section 17(3), (5)(b), (7) and (8) of that Act; and

- (vi) section 18[^f00005] of that Act.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford — Minister of State, — Office of the Deputy Prime Minister — 4th July 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force further provisions of the Local Government Act 2000 relating to the repeal of surcharge.
