# The Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978

Made: 18th July 1978

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 89(3) of the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1978.

###### 2

The provisions of the Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates' Courts Act 1978 specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in column 2 thereof) shall come into force forthwith.

## Signed

Merlyn Rees — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 18th July 1978
