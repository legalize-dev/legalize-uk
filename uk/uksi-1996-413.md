# The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1996

Made: 20th February 1996

Laid before Parliament: 7th March 1996

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 of, as read with paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Variation of fees

###### 2

Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with article 3 of, and the Schedule to, this Order.

###### 3

In relation to those matters in Schedule B which are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order, the fees payable shall be varied by substituting the fees specified in column 2 of the Schedule to this Order for the fees specified in column 3 of that Schedule (being the fees payable immediately prior to the coming into force of this Order).

###### 4

The Lyon Court and Office Fees (Variation) Order 1995[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the fees for matters listed in Schedule B to the Lyon King of Arms Act 1867.

The matters concerned are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to the Order. Column 2 of the Schedule lists the new fees and column 3 lists the fees which are to be replaced. The fees were last varied in 1995 and the fees specified in this Order represent an increase on average of approximately 3%.

The fees contained in Schedule B to the 1867 Act are exclusive of stamp duty when stamp duty is exigible.
