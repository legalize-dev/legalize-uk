# The Education (Leeds College of Music) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2005

Made: 4th July 2005

Laid before Parliament: 11th July 2005

Coming into force: 1st August 2005

The Secretary of State to whom it appears that the full-time equivalent enrolment number of the institution conducted by Leeds College of Music[^f00001] for courses of higher education exceeds 55 per cent of its total full-time equivalent enrolment number, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122A of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Leeds College of Music) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st August 2005.

###### 2

On the date on which this Order comes into force Leeds College of Music shall cease to be a further education corporation and shall become a higher education corporation.

## Signed

Bill Rammell — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 4th July 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the transfer of Leeds College of Music from the further education sector to the higher education sector. On 1st August 2005 that further education corporation becomes a higher education corporation.
