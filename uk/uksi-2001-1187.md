# The Employment Relations Act 1999 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2001

Made: 26th March 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 45(1) of the Employment Relations Act 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Employment Relations Act 1999 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2001.

###### 2

In this Order, “the Act” means the Employment Relations Act 1999.

#### Commencement

###### 3

The following provisions of the Act shall come into force on 18th April 2001—

- (a) section 41 and Schedule 8; and

- (b) the repeals specified in column 3 of Schedule 9 to the Act, to the extent specified in column 3 of the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 18th April 2001 section 41 of, and Schedule 8 to, the Employment Relations Act 1999. Section 41 introduces Schedule 8, which substitutes a new section 193 of the Employment Rights Act 1996 (national security) and a new section 10 of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996, which contains powers to make provision in the employment tribunal procedure regulations to deal with cases concerning national security. Schedule 8 also introduces a new section 10A (power to make provision in the employment tribunal procedure regulations in respect of confidential information) and a new section 10B (restriction of publicity in cases involving national security) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996.

Schedule 8 also makes corresponding changes to section 30 of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996 in relation to the Employment Appeal Tribunal rules on national security, and repeals paragraph 4(1)(b) of Schedule 3 to the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Minister’s certificate as to national security).
