# The Primary Care Trusts (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002

Made: 26th March 2002

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1) and (2) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Primary Care Trusts (Establishment) Amendment Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

#### Amendment of Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Orders

###### 2

- (1) Each of the Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Orders listed in the Schedule shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (2) of Article 1 (citation, commencement and interpretation)—

- (a) the definition of “Executive Committee” shall be omitted;

- (b) for the definition of “member”, “officer member” and “non-officer member” there shall be substituted—

> > - “member” has the meaning given in regulation 1(2) of the Membership Regulations;

- (3) Article 4 (members of the trust) shall be omitted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes from all those PCT establishment orders listed in the Schedule the article (article 4) which sets out their membership. It also makes consequential amendments to the definitions in article 1. In future, PCTs will be free to set their own membership within the constraints of the national framework as set out in the Primary Care Trust (Membership, Procedure and Administration Arrangements) Regulations 2000 ([S.I. 2000/89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/89)).
