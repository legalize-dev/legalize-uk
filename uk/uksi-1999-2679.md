# The Companies (Welsh Language Forms) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1999

Made: 22nd September 1999

Coming into force: 23rd September 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 88(2) and 744 of the Companies Act 1985[^f00001], as extended by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Welsh Language Forms) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

###### 2

- (1) Form 88(2)CYM in the Schedule to these Regulations, with such variations as circumstances require, is a form prescribed for the purposes of section 88(2) of the Companies Act 1985.

- (2) Form 88(2)CYM in the Schedule to the Companies (Welsh Language Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Tim Lonsdale — for Secretary of State — Department of Trade and Industry — 22nd September 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe an amended Form 88(2)CYM for the purposes of section 88(2) of the Companies Act 1985, with effect from 23rd September 1999; the form is in Welsh as well as in English. It replaces Form 88(2)CYM as prescribed in the Companies (Welsh Language Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 which is revoked. The only amendment to the form is to make clear in what circumstances details must be given of the allotment of shares which are allotted as fully or partly paid up otherwise than in cash.
