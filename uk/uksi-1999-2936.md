# The Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1999

Made: 26th October 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 101(3) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1999.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Northern Ireland Act 1998 shall come into force on 28th October 1999:

- Section 40(10), so far as relating to paragraph 6 of Schedule 5

- Paragraph 6 of Schedule 5.

## Signed

George Howarth — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 26th October 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 28th October 1999 the enabling powers in paragraph 6 of Schedule 5 to the Northern Ireland Act 1998, under which Her Majesty may by Order in Council provide for the Northern Ireland Assembly Commission to be treated as a Crown body.
