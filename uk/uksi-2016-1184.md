# The Air Quality Standards (Amendment) Regulations 2016

Made: 6th December 2016

Laid before Parliament: 8th December 2016

Coming into force: 31st December 2016

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) in relation to the environment[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and amendment

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Quality Standards (Amendment) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 31st December 2016.

- (2) The Air Quality Standards Regulations 2010[^f00003] are amended as follows.

#### Regulation 2

###### 2

- (1) In regulation 2 (definitions) for the definition of “[Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050)”[^f00004] substitute—

> > - “[Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050)” means [Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050) of the European Parliament and of the Council on ambient air quality and cleaner air for Europe;

- (2) In that regulation for the definition of “[Directive 2004/107/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0107)”[^f00005] substitute—

> > - “[Directive 2004/107/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0107)” means [Directive 2004/107/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0107) of the European Parliament and of the Council relating to arsenic, cadmium, mercury, nickel and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons in ambient air;

#### Part 3 of Schedule 1

###### 3

In Part 3 of Schedule 1 (sampling points etc.)—

- (a) for sub-paragraphs (a) and (b) of paragraph 1 substitute—

> > (a) the flow around the inlet sampling probe must be unrestricted (free in an arc of at least 270° or, for sampling points at the building line, 180°) without any obstructions affecting the airflow in the vicinity of the inlet (that is to say some metres away from buildings, balconies, trees and other obstacles and at least 0.5 m from the nearest building in the case of sampling points representing air quality at the building line);
> (b) the inlet sampling point must be between 1.5 m (the breathing zone) and 4 m above the ground unless the station is representative of a large area and higher siting is appropriate. Any derogations must be fully documented;

- (b) after the full-stop at the end of sub-paragraph (e) of paragraph 1 insert “A “major junction” is a junction which interrupts the traffic flow and causes different emissions (due to vehicles stopping and starting) from the rest of the road.”;

- (c) after paragraph 2 insert—

> > (3) Any deviation from the criteria listed in this Part must be documented in accordance with Part 4.

#### New Part 4 of Schedule 1

###### 4

After Part 3 of Schedule 1 insert—

> > (1) The Secretary of State must for all zones and agglomerations fully document the site-selection procedures and record information to support the network design and choice of location for all monitoring sites.
> (2) The documentation must include compass-point photographs of the area surrounding monitoring sites and detailed maps.
> (3) Where supplementary methods are used within a zone or agglomeration, the documentation must include details of these methods and information on how the criteria listed in Article 7(3) of [Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050) are met.
> (4) The documentation must be updated as necessary and reviewed at least every 5 years, to ensure that selection criteria, network design and monitoring site locations remain valid and optimal over time.

## Signed

Thérèse Coffey — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 6th December 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Air Quality Standards Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/1001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1001)). Those Regulations transpose [Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050) on ambient air quality etc. and [Directive 2004/107/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0107) relating to arsenic etc. in ambient air. Both Directives were amended by Commission Directive 2015/1480.

Regulation 2 amends the definition of “[Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050)” so that each reference to the Directive in the Regulations is a reference to the Directive as amended by Commission Directive 2015/1480. A similar change is made to the definition of “[Directive 2004/107/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0107)”.

Regulations 3 and 4 make amendments to Schedule 1 (which concerns sampling points). Regulation 3 makes amendments to Part 3 of Schedule 1. The changes follow amendments made by Commission Directive 2015/1480 to Section C of Annex III to [Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050).

Regulation 4 inserts a new Part 4 in Schedule 1. The new Part 4 concerns documentation and review of site selection. This change follows the replacement by Commission Directive 2015/1480 of Section D of Annex III to [Directive 2008/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0050).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. A transposition note for the Air Quality Standards Regulations 2010 is available with those Regulations onwww.legislation.gov.uk
