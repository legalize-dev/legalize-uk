# The Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1996

Made: 21st February 1996

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 66(3) of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1996.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the 1994 Act” means the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994.

#### Commencement of certain provisions of Part II of the 1994 Act

###### 3

The provisions of Part II of, and Schedules 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 to, the 1994 Act which are specified in Schedule 1 to this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Commencement of certain provisions of Part VII of the 1994 Act

###### 4

The provisions of Part VII of, and Schedules 15, 16, 17 and 18 to, the 1994 Act which are specified in Schedule 2 to this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1996.
