# The Monopolies and Mergers Commission (Increase in Membership) Order 1989

Made: 21st July 1989

Laid before Parliament: 21st July 1989

Coming into force: 1st September 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(3) of the Fair Trading Act 1973[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Monopolies and Mergers Commission (Increase in Membership) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

###### 2

The maximum number of regular members of the Monopolies and Mergers Commission is hereby increased to fifty.

###### 3

The Monopolies and Mergers Commission (Increase in Membership) Order 1982[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Francis Maude — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Corporate Affairs, — Department of Trade and Industry — 21st July 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order supersedes the Monopolies and Mergers Commission (Increase in Membership) Order 1982 (S.I. [1982/815](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/815)). It increases the maximum number of regular members of the Monopolies and Mergers Commission from 32 to 50.
