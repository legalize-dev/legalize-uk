# The Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Armed Forces) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made: 6th July 1990

Laid before Parliament: 19th July 1990

Coming into force: 13th August 1990

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 113(1) of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Armed Forces) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 13th August 1990.

###### 2

The Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Armed Forces) Order 1985[^f00002] shall be amended as follows. In article 2 (Interpretation) in the definition of “service policeman”, for the words “Special Investigation” there shall be substituted the word “Regulating”.

## Signed

Tom King — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 6th July 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (Application to Armed Forces) Order 1985 by substituting a reference to a member of the Royal Naval Regulating Branch for the reference to a member of the Royal Naval Special Investigation Branch in the definition of “service policeman”.
