# The A13 Trunk Road (Ripple Road, Barking and Dagenham) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1991

Made: 26th September 1991

Coming into force: 1st October 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consulting the Secretary of State for the Home Department, pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A13 Trunk Road (Ripple Road, Barking and Dagenham) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1991, and shall come into force on 1st October 1991.

###### 2

In this Order:—

- “central reservation” means that part of Ripple Road which separates its two carriageways for the safety and guidance of vehicles using that road;

- “Ripple Road” means that length of the A13 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham known as Ripple Road;

- “Morrison Road” means the road of that name in the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham;

- “eastern traffic island” means the island site in the westbound carriageway of Ripple Road which extends to the junction from a point 25 metres east of the junction;

- “western traffic island” means the island site in the eastbound carriageway of Ripple Road which extends to the junction from a point 25 metres west of the junction;

- “the junction” means the junction of Ripple Road with Morrison Road and the Sports Centre access road; and

- “the Sports Centre access road” means the new road construction to the north side of Ripple Road, opposite Morrison Road.

###### 3

Paragraph 2 of article 3 of the A13 Trunk Road (Ripple Road, Barking and Dagenham) (Prohibition of Use of Gaps in Central Reservation) Order 1987[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

###### 4

Any person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the right of the eastern traffic island shall cause that vehicle to turn right into the Sports Centre access road.

###### 5

Any person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the right of the western traffic island—

- (a) shall cause the vehicle to turn right into Morrison Road; or

- (b) is permitted to cause the vehicle to make a U-turn at the junction in order to proceed in Ripple Road in the opposite direction.

###### 6

Save as provided in articles 4 and 5 of this Order, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in Ripple Road to make a right turn or to make a U-turn at the junction.

###### 7

Nothing in this Order shall apply:

- (a) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or of a traffic warden;

- (b) to a vehicle being used for police, ambulance, or fire brigade purposes; or

- (c) to a vehicle being used by the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham in pursuance of its statutory powers and duties.
