# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2009

Made: 27th May 2009

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 170(3) and (4) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00001] makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care Act (Commencement No.10) Order 2009.

#### Appointed day for provisions relating to the Appointments Commission

###### 2

4th June 2009 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Health and Social Care Act 2008—

- (a) paragraph 21 of Schedule 10 (amendment to the Health Act 2006[^f00002]) in so far as not already in force; and

- (b) section 127 in so far as it gives effect to that provision.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the Secretary of State for Health.

Ben Bradshaw — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 27th May 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the amendment to section 60 of the Health Act [2006 (c.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/28) which requires the Appointments Commission to exercise so much of any function of the Privy Council relating to the appointment of members of the Office of the Health Professions Adjudicator as the Privy Council specifies in a direction.
