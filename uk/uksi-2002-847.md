# The Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2002

Made: 26th March 2002

Laid before Parliament: 28th March 2002

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1) and (4) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to changes in the area and functions of the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Health Authority and having consulted that Health Authority, the North Middlesex Hospital National Health Service Trust and the Barnet Primary Care Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Health Authority[^f00002];

- “the new Trust” means the North Middlesex Hospital National Health Service Trust[^f00003] or the Barnet Primary Care Trust as the context requires;

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the two schedules of trust property more specifically described in paragraphs (1) and (2) of article 2 of this Order.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On 1st April 2002 the trust property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed upon by the old Trust and the North Middlesex Hospital National Health Service Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 27th November 2001 and on behalf of the North Middlesex Hospital National Health Service Trust on 28th November 2001, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00004], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the North Middlesex Hospital National Health Service Trust.

- (2) On 1st April 2002 the trust property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed upon by the old Trust and the Barnet Primary Care Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 27th November 2001 and on behalf of the Barnet Primary Care Trust on 26th November 2001, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00005], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the Barnet Primary Care Trust.

###### 3

- (1) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the relevant new Trust and any reference to the old Trust in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the relevant new Trust.

- (2) In paragraph (1) of this article references to the relevant new Trust are to be read as references to the new Trust to which the property which is the subject of the trust instrument has been transferred under article 2 of this Order.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 26th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the North Middlesex Hospital National Health Service Trust and the Barnet Primary Care Trust, trust property held by the Barnet, Enfield and Haringey Health Authority and makes related provision. A copy of the schedules specifying the trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
