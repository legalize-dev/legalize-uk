# The Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 8th February 2015

Laid before Parliament: 12th February 2015

Coming into force: 6th April 2015

The Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) They come into force on 6th April 2015.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976

###### 2

In regulation 8(1) of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976[^f00003] (circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as gainfully employed), for “£102”, in both places where it occurs, substitute “£110”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Mark Harper — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 8th February 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976 ([S.I. 1976/409](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/409)) (“the principal Regulations”).

Under section 70 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4), a person is entitled to a carer’s allowance for any day on which that person is caring for a severely disabled person provided that, among other things, the person claiming carer’s allowance is not gainfully employed.

Regulation 8(1) of the principal Regulations prescribes that a person is only to be treated as gainfully employed on any day in a week if their earnings in the immediately preceding week have exceeded a prescribed amount. That amount is currently £102. The amendment made by regulation 2 raises that amount to £110.

An impact assessment has not been published for this instrument as it has no impact on business or civil society organisations.
