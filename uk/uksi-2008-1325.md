# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2008

Made: 20th May 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 178(8) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No.13) Order 2008.

###### 2

Section 170 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 shall come into force on 1st June 2008.

## Signed

Derek Twigg — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Defence — 20th May 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 170 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005. Section 170 makes amendments to section 25 of the Armed Forces Act [2001 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/19), which in turn inserted new sections into the Army Act [1955 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/18), the Air Force Act [1955 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1955/19) and the Naval Discipline Act [1957 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1957/53) (‘the Service Discipline Acts’). The amended sections of the Service Discipline Acts now contain provisions equivalent to that provided for in the civilian courts by section 2 of the Criminal Procedure (Attendance of Witnesses) Act [1965 (c. 69)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1965/69), as amended by section 169 of the 2005 Act, and change the criteria that a judge advocate must be satisfied are met before issuing a warrant for the arrest of a witness in proceedings before a court-martial.
