# The Urenco Enrichment Company Limited (Designation) Order 2003

Made: 5th September 2003

Coming into Operation: 5th September 2003

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of her powers under section 19(2) of the Atomic Energy Authority Act 1971[^f00001] hereby orders as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Urenco Enrichment Company Limited (Designation) Order 2003.

###### 2

Urenco Enrichment Company Limited is hereby designated for the purposes of section 19 of the Atomic Energy Authority Act 1971.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the Secretary of State

Claire Durkin, — Head of Energy Innovation and Business Unit, — Department of Trade and Industry — 5th September 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates Urenco Enrichment Company Limited for the purposes of section 19 of the Atomic Energy Authority Act 1971. That section provides that the security provisions in paragraphs 4–6 of Schedule 1 to the Nuclear Installations Act 1965[^f00002] (as set out in the Schedule to the Atomic Energy Authority Act 1971, and as amended by section 2(1) of the Atomic Energy Authority (Special Constables) Act 1976[^f00003]), shall apply in relation to a company so designated.
