# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 8) Order 2013

Made: 6th November 2013

Coming into force: 7th November 2013

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 6th day of November 2013Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 8) Order 2013 and comes into force on 7th November 2013.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — Persons appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 7th November 2013

- Diana Choulerton

- Elizabeth Wendy Farr

- Sarah Hubbard

- Christine Moira Malone

- Michael Anthony Pennington

- Gaynor Elizabeth Roberts

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
