# The A13 Trunk Road (New Road, Havering) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1988

Made: 13th October 1988

Coming into force: 17th October 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of, and Part IV of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Secretary of State for the Home Department, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A13 Trunk Road (New Road, Havering) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 17th October 1988.

###### 2

In this Order—

- “New Road” means that length of the London—Tilbury Trunk Road (A13) in the London Borough of Havering known as New Road;

- “central reservation” means that part of New Road which separates the two carriageways of New Road for the safety and guidance of vehicles using that road;

- “the junction” means the junction of New Road, Manor Way and Cherry Tree Lane;

- “the westbound right-turn lane” means the traffic lane, being part of the westbound carriageway of New Road, which is bounded on its northern side by the central reservation, has a width of approximately 3.5 metres and extends from the junction eastwards for a distance of 8 metres;

- “the westbound left-turn lane” means the traffic lane, being part of the westbound carriageway of New Road, which lies adjacent to the kerbline of that carriageway, has a width of approximately 4.5 metres and extends from the junction eastwards for a distance of 30 metres;

- “the eastbound left-turn lane” means the traffic lane, being part of the east-bound carriageway of New Road, which lies adjacent to the kerbline of that carriageway, has a width of approximately 3 metres and extends from the junction westwards for a distance of 19 metres.

###### 3

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in the eastbound left-turn lane shall cause that vehicle to turn left into Cherry Tree Lane.

###### 4

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in the westbound right-turn lane shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Cherry Tree Lane.

###### 5

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in the westbound left-turn lane shall cause that vehicle to turn left into Manor Way.

###### 6

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in any traffic lane of the eastbound carriageway of New Road, other than in the eastbound left-turn lane, shall, upon reaching the junction continue eastwards.

###### 7

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in any traffic lane of the westbound carriageway of New Road, other than in the westbound right-turn lane and in the westbound left-turn lane, shall, upon reaching the junction continue westwards.

###### 8

Article 11 of the Trunk Roads (Various Roads, Havering) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1973[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

###### 9

Nothing in this Order shall apply to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.
