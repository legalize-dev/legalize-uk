# The Civil Courts (Amendment No. 3) Order 1996

Made: 10th October 1996

Laid before Parliament: 14th October 1996

Coming into force: 4th November 1996

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 99(1) of the Supreme Court Act 1981[^f00001], sections 2(1) and 26 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00002] and by sections 117(4) and 374 of the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order: —

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Courts (Amendment No. 3) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 4th November 1996.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “the 1983 Order” means the Civil Courts Order 1983[^f00004], and an article or Schedule referred to by number alone means the article or Schedule so numbered in that Order.

#### Amendments to the 1983 Order

###### 3

In paragraph (2) of article 4: —

- (a) the following words shall be inserted at the end: —

- “and the name of the district registry at Torquay shall be the Torquay and Newton Abbot District Registry”; and “,” shall be substituted for the word “and”.

###### 4

In Schedule 1 the word “Goole” shall be omitted from the second column opposite the entry for Kingston upon Hull and reinserted in the second column opposite the entry relating to Doncaster.

###### 5

In Schedule 1 the words “Torquay and Newton Abbot” shall be substituted for the word “Torquay” in the second column opposite the entry relating to Torquay.

###### 6

In Schedule 3 in the entry relating to Doncaster, the words “Kingston upon Hull” shall be substituted for the word “Leeds” in the third column.

###### 7

In Schedule 3 in the entry relating to Goole, the word “Doncaster” shall be substituted for the word “Wakefield” in the fourth column.

###### 8

In Schedule 4 after paragraph 11 there shall be inserted the following: —

> > (11A) The name of the county court at Torquay shall be the Torquay and Newton Abbot County Court..

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — 10th October 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Courts Order 1983 so as to: —

- (a) change the name of the Torquay District Registry to the Torquay and Newton Abbot District Registry *(article 3)*;

- (b) make Goole County Court a district of Doncaster District Registry, instead of Kingston upon Hull District Registry (for the purposes of defining a district of a District Registry in Schedule 1 to the 1983 Order) *(article 4)*;

- (c) change the name of the Torquay County Court to the Torquay and Newton County Court *(articles 5 and 8)*;

- (d) assign Doncaster to Kingston upon Hull County Court instead of Leeds County Court for Admiralty purposes *(article 6)*; and

- (e) attach Goole to Doncaster County Court, instead of Wakefield County Court for the purposes of insolvency and winding up petitions (article 7).
