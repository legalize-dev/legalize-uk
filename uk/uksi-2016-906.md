# The Health and Social Care (Safety and Quality) Act 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016

Made: 9th September 2016

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(4) of the Health and Social Care (Safety and Quality) Act 2015[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Social Care (Safety and Quality) Act 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2016.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the Act” means the Health and Social Care (Safety and Quality) Act 2015.

#### Provisions coming into force on 26th September 2016

###### 2

The following provisions of the Act come into force on 26th September 2016—

- (a) section 5 (objectives in relation to the regulation of health and social care professions);

- (b) the Schedule (objectives of regulators of health and social care professions).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Phillip Dunne — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 9th September 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force provisions of the Health and Social Care (Safety and Quality) Act [2015 (c.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/28) (“the Act”). It is the second such set of regulations to be made under the Act.

Regulation 2(a) brings into force, on 26th September 2016, section 5 of the Act, which relates to the regulation of health and social care professionals. Section 5(1) of the Act amends section 25 of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act [2002 (c.17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/17) (“the 2002 Act”) (Professional Standards Authority for Health and Social Care). The amendment to section 25 of the 2002 Act substitutes for the existing paragraph (2A) a new paragraph (2A) and adds paragraph (2B), which relate to the over-arching objective of the Professional Standards Authority for Health and Social Care (“the Authority”). Section 5(2) of the Act gives effect to the Schedule to the Act, which makes provision about the objectives of certain regulators of health and social care professionals in respect of which the Authority exercises functions, and about when committees or panels of those regulators should have regard to those objectives.

Regulation 2(b) brings into force, on 26th September 2016, the Schedule to the Act. The Schedule to the Act amends specified legislation to make provision for a consistent over-arching objective of public protection for specified regulators of health and social care professionals. The legislation which is amended by the Schedule is: the Dentists Act [1984 (c.24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/24), the Opticians Act [1989 (c.44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/44), the Osteopaths Act [1993 (c.21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/21), the Chiropractors Act [1994 (c.17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/17), the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 ([S.I. 2002/253](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/253)), the Health and Social Work Professions Order 2001 ([S.I. 2002/254](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/254)), and the Pharmacy Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/231](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/231)).
