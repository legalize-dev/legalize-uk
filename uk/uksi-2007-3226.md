# The Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) (No 3) Order 2007

Made: 14th November 2007

Coming into force: 1st February 2008

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 14th day of November 2007Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 19(1) of the Education Act 2005 and in accordance with that recommendation, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) (No 3) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st February 2008.

###### 2

Dr William Skene Maxwell is hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru for a period of five years commencing on 1st February 2008.

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints Dr William Skene Maxwell to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru for a period of five years from 1st February 2008. The present appointment of Susan Lewis under the Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) (No 2) Order 2007 ends on 31st January 2008.
