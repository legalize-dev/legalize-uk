# The Communications (Television Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

Made: 6th February 2025

Laid before Parliament: 10th February 2025

Coming into force: 1st April 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 365(1) and (4)(b) and 402(3) of the Communications Act 2003[^f00001], including as extended by— the Communications (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Order 2003[^f00002]; the Communications (Jersey) Order 2003[^f00003]; the Communications (Isle of Man) Order 2003[^f00004]; the Communications (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Order 2004[^f00005]; the Broadcasting and Communications (Jersey) Order 2004[^f00006].

the Communications (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Order 2003[^f00002];

the Communications (Jersey) Order 2003[^f00003];

the Communications (Isle of Man) Order 2003[^f00004];

the Communications (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Order 2004[^f00005];

the Broadcasting and Communications (Jersey) Order 2004[^f00006].

The Treasury has consented to the making of these Regulations in accordance with section 365(6) of the Communications Act 2003.

#### Citation, commencement, effect and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Communications (Television Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2025.

- (3) These Regulations have effect in respect of TV licences issued on or after 1st April 2025.

- (4) These Regulations extend to—

- (a) England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland,

- (b) the Channel Islands, except regulation 4(37) and (38) and Schedule 2, and

- (c) the Isle of Man.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Communications (Television Licensing) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/692](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/692)) (“*the 2004 Regulations*”) by providing for increases in—

- (a) the amount payable for most categories of TV licence;

- (b) the amount of each instalment for certain categories of TV licence which are payable in instalments.

In summary—

- (a) the amount usually payable for a colour TV licence is increased from £169.50 to £174.50 and that for a black and white TV licence is increased from £57.00 to £58.50 (see regulations 3 and 5);

- (b) the instalments for a premium instalment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £174.50 to £179.50 (see regulation 4(1) and (2));

- (c) the instalments for a budget instalment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £169.50 to £174.50 (see regulation 4(1) and (3) to (9));

- (d) the instalments for an easy entry TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £169.50 to £174.50 (see regulation 4(1) and (10) to (36));

- (e) the instalments for a simple payment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £169.50 to £174.50 (see regulation 4(1), (37) and (38)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
