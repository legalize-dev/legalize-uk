# The Northern Ireland (Executive Formation etc) Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2023

Made: 30th January 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 14(1) of the Northern Ireland (Executive Formation etc) Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Executive Formation etc) Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Provisions coming into force on 7th February 2023

###### 2

The following provisions of the Northern Ireland (Executive Formation etc) Act 2022 come into force on 7th February 2023—

- (a) section 6 (NI ministerial appointment functions);

- (b) section 7 (Minister of the Crown appointment functions);

- (c) section 8 (Joint UK appointment functions etc);

- (d) section 9 (Sections 6 to 8: core definitions).

## Signed

Steve Baker — Minister of State — Northern Ireland Office — 30th January 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force provisions of the Northern Ireland (Executive Formation etc) Act [2022 (c. 48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/48) (“*the 2022 Act*”).

Regulation 2 brings into force sections 6 to 9 of the 2022 Act on 7th February 2023. Sections 6 to 9 contain provisions allowing appointments to be made to specified public bodies during the current period without a Northern Ireland Executive or Ministers.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
