# The Herefordshire Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made: 19th September 2000

Coming into force: 2nd October 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in the exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Herefordshire Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 2nd October 2000.

#### Amendment of Establishment Order

###### 2

In the Herefordshire Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00003]—

- (a) in article 1(2), the definition of “community health services” shall be omitted; and

- (b) article 8 (exercise of functions) shall be omitted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Yvette Cooper — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Public Health, — Department of Health — 19th September 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the establishment order (“the Order”) of the Herefordshire Primary Care Trust (“the trust”), a Primary Care Trust established by Order under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977 (“the Act”) by removing the restriction in article 8 of the Order whereby, in the exercise of functions, the trust shall not provide services directly to patients, other than community health services.
