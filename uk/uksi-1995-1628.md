# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1995

Made: 28th June 1995

Laid before Parliament: 10th July 1995

Coming into force: 1st August 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 28th day of June 1995 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 5(2) of the Education (Schools) Act 1992[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st August 1995.

###### 2

Barry Gregson–Allcott, Esquire, Mrs Elizabeth Ross Kidd and Andrew Robert Large, Esquire, are hereby appointed to be three of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales.

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council
