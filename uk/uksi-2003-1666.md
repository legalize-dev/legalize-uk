# The Employment Act 2002 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003

Made: 2nd July 2003

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 55(2) of the Employment Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Employment Act 2002 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003.

- (2) In this Order, any reference to a numbered section or Schedule is a reference to the section or Schedule bearing that number in the Employment Act 2002.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of —

- (a) section 49; and

- (b) paragraphs 9, 10, 15, and paragraph 8 so far as it relates to those paragraphs, 51 and 55 of Schedule 7, and section 53 so far as it relates to those paragraphs,

shall be 5th July 2003.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Des Browne — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 2nd July 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force further provisions of the Employment Act [2002 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/22).

The provisions set out in Article 2 are brought into force on 5th July 2003 to enable regulations to be made to require the partner of a person who is in receipt of certain benefits at a higher rate referable to that partner to attend a work-focused interview and to make related provision.
