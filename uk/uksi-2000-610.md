# The Special Trustees for the Royal Throat, Nose & Ear Hospital and the Special Trustees for the Royal Free Hospital (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 2000

Made: 9th March 2000

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(6) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, it appearing to him that the functions of the Special Trustees for the Royal Throat, Nose & Ear Hospital and of the Special Trustees for the Royal Free Hospital[^f00002] should be discharged by the trustees for the Royal Free Hampstead National Health Service Trust[^f00003] and having consulted the said Special Trustees and the trustees for the said National Health Service trust, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Special Trustees for the Royal Throat, Nose & Ear Hospital and the Special Trustees for the Royal Free Hospital (Transfers of Trust Property) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the new trustees” means the trustees for the Royal Free Hampstead National Health Service Trust;

- “the Special Trustees” means the Special Trustees for the Royal Throat, Nose & Ear Hospital and the Special Trustees for the Royal Free Hospital;

- “the trust property” means— in relation to the Special Trustees for the Royal Throat, Nose & Ear Hospital, the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared by, agreed upon by and signed on behalf of those Special Trustees and the new trustees on 7th March 2000, in relation to the Special Trustees for the Royal Free Hospital, the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared by, agreed upon by and signed on behalf of those Special Trustees and the new trustees on 7th March 2000.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On 1st April 2000 the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00004], shall be transferred from the Special Trustees to the new trustees.

- (2) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new trustees and any reference to the Special Trustees in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the Royal Free Hampstead National Health Service Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 9th March 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the trustees for the Royal Free Hampstead National Health Service Trust trust property held by the Special Trustees for the Royal Throat, Nose & Ear Hospital and by the Special Trustees for the Royal Free Hospital and makes related provision. Copies of the schedules of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Financial and Audit Support Branch, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
