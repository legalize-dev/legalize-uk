# The East Midlands Enterprise Zones (Mansfield) (Designation) Order 1995

Made: 29th August 1995

Laid before Parliament: 1st September 1995

Coming into force: 22nd September 1995

Now therefore the Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 5 of the Schedule and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the East Midlands Enterprise Zones (Mansfield) (Designation) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 22nd September 1995.

###### 2

In this order:

- “the Council” means the Mansfield District Council;

- “the map” means the map prepared in duplicate, marked “Map referred to in the East Midlands Enterprise Zones (Mansfield) (Designation) Order 1995”, and signed by an Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment, one duplicate of which has been deposited and is available for inspection at the principal offices of the Council and the other at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment; and

- “the Zone areas” means the two areas— the boundaries of which are represented by the outside edge of a black line edged internally with a stippled band on the map; and to which the East Midlands Enterprise Zone No. 5 (Crown Farm) Scheme and the East Midlands Enterprise Zone No. 6 (Crown Farm) Scheme respectively relate.

#### Enterprise zones in Mansfield

###### 3

The Zone areas are hereby designated as enterprise zones, and the Council is hereby designated as the enterprise zone authority for those zones.

#### Period of designation

###### 4

Designation under this Order shall take effect on 22nd September 1995, and the designated areas shall remain enterprise zones for a period of 10 years from that date.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford — An Under Secretary in the — Department of the Environment — 21st August 1995

We consent,

Bowen Wells — Simon Burns — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 29th August 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order designates as enterprise zones two areas which are located in the Crown Farm area of the district of Mansfield, and which have been the subject of two enterprise zone schemes adopted by the Mansfield District Council (the East Midlands Enterprise Zone No. 5 (Crown Farm) Scheme and the East Midlands Enterprise Zone No. 6 (Crown Farm) Scheme).

The designation is effective for a period of 10 years.

The location and areas of the enterprise zones are shown on the plan forming part of this note. The detailed map referred to in article 2 may be inspected at the principal offices of the Mansfield District Council, and at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment.
