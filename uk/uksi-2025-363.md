# The Immigration, Nationality and Passport (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

Made: 18th March 2025

Laid before Parliament: 19th March 2025

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2) to (4)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations with the consent of the Treasury[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 68(1) and (7) and 74(8)(a), (b) and (d) of the Immigration Act 2014[^f00002] and sections 86(1) and (3), 87(2) and 93(6)(a) of the Immigration Act 2016[^f00003]. These Regulations are made further to the provision in the Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Order 2016[^f00004].

## Part 1 — Introductory

#### Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration, Nationality and Passport (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), these Regulations come into force at 9.00 a.m. on 9th April 2025.

- (3) [Regulations 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/5/enacted)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/5/3/enacted), [7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/7/enacted), [8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/8/enacted), [10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/10/enacted)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/10/3/enacted) and [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/10/6/enacted) and [11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/11/enacted)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/11/3/enacted) and [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/11/4/enacted) come into force on 1st May 2025.

- (4) [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/part/3/enacted) and [paragraph 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/schedule/3/paragraph/1/enacted) of [Schedule 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/schedule/3/enacted) come into force on 10th April 2025.

- (5) Subject to [paragraphs (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/1/6/enacted) and [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/1/7/enacted), these Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

- (6) The following provisions of these Regulations also extend to the Isle of Man for the same purpose as the provisions of [the principal Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/330) which they amend—

- (a) [this regulation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/1/enacted);

- (b) [regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/2/enacted) in so far as it relates to [regulation 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/10/enacted);

- (c) [regulation 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/10/enacted).

- (7) The following provisions of these Regulations also extend to the Bailiwick of Guernsey and the Bailiwick of Jersey for the same purpose as the provisions of [the principal Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/330) which they amend—

- (a) [this regulation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/1/enacted);

- (b) [regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/2/enacted) in so far as it relates to [regulation 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/363/regulation/11/enacted);

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Immigration and Nationality (Fees) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/330](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/330)) (“*the principal Regulations*”) (which set fees for the exercise of various functions in connection with immigration and nationality) and the Passport (Fees) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/660](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/660)) (which set fees for the exercise of various functions in connection with passports).

Regulations 2 to 11 and Schedules 1 to 7 amend Schedules 1 to 4 and 6 to 10 to the principal Regulations, replacing existing fee amounts with increased fee amounts.

Regulations 3 and 4 amend certain references the Immigration Rules in Schedules 1 and 2 to the principal Regulations. This is because Part 7 of the Immigration Rules was replaced by Appendix Afghan Relocation and Assistance Policy (ARAP), Appendix Family Reunion (Protection) was renamed as Appendix Family Reunion (Sponsors with Protection) and Appendix Child staying with or joining a Non-Parent Relative (Protection) was renamed as Appendix Child Relative (Sponsors with Protection).

Regulations 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 omit entries which provide for functions, relating to assessment of overseas qualifications, which will no longer be in operation, in Schedules 3, 6, 7, 9 and 10 to the principal Regulations. The functions which will no longer be in operation are those relating to downloadable digital statements and priority services and the recognised appropriate language level of overseas qualification service.

Part 3 and Schedule 8 set out amendments which increase various fees in the Passport (Fees) Regulations 2022.

A full impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs to business has been produced. An Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
