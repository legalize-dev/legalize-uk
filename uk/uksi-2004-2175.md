# The Street Works (Charges for Occupation of the Highway) (England) (Revocation) Order 2004

Made: 23rd August 2004

Coming into force: 24th August 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 74A(2) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Street Works (Charges for Occupation of the Highway) (England) (Revocation) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 24th August 2004.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The following Orders are revoked—

- the Street Works (Charges for Occupation of the Highway) (England) (Middlesbrough Borough Council) Order 2002[^f00002];

- the Street Works (Charges for Occupation of the Highway) (England) (London Borough of Camden) Order 2002[^f00003]; and

- the Street Works (Charges for Occupation of the Highway) (England) (Transport for London) Order 2002[^f00004].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Mike Talbot — Divisional Manager, Integrated and Local Transport Directorate, — Department for Transport — 23rd August 2004
