# The Motor Cycles Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made: 12th February 2001

Laid before Parliament: 21st February 2001

Coming into force: 15th March 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, being a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 for the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Cycles Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 15th March 2001.

#### Amendments to Schedule 1 (Separate Directives)

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the Motor Cycles Etc. (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1999 shall be amended in accordance with regulation 3.

###### 3

At the end of the Table shall be inserted in the first, second, fourth and fifth columns the entries specified below.

| (1) | (2) | (4) | (5) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | 2000/7/EC† | L106,3.5.2000, p. 1 | Speedometers for two or three-wheeled motor vehicles |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Larry Whitty — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 12th February 2001
