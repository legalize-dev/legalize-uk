# The LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget and the Financing of Maintained Schools (No. 2) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2004

Made: 16th March 2004

Laid before Parliament: 16th March 2004

Coming into force: 31st March 2004

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 45A, 47 and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement, and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget and the Financing of Maintained Schools (No.2) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 31st March 2004.

- (2) Regulation 2 amends the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (England) Regulations 2003 [^f00002] (“the Budget regulations”) and Regulation 3 amends the Financing of Maintained Schools (No. 2) (England) Regulations 2003 [^f00003] (“the FMS regulations”).

- (3) These Regulations apply in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2004.

#### Amendment to the Budget regulations

###### 2

- (1) In respect of each of the local education authorities named in the first column in the table in the Schedule, the words set out in the second column against each authority’s name are added after the formula and before the word “where” in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2.

- (2) The words “and excepting that deducted in accordance with paragraph 24 of Schedule 2 to the 2002 Regulations insofar as that expenditure related to maintained nursery schools” are added at the end of the definition of “C” in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2.

- (3) The words “or any maintained nursery school” are omitted from paragraph 15(e) of Schedule 3.

#### Amendment to the FMS regulations

###### 3

- (1) The words “regulation 8(6)(g) or (h)” are substituted for “regulation 8(5)(g) or (h)” in regulation 26.

- (2) The following paragraph is substituted for paragraph 3 of Schedule 2:

> > - Save where paragraph 4 applies, where the relevant number of pupils in a school for the financial year beginning either on 1st April 2003 or on 1st April 2004, is 75 or fewer, that school’s guaranteed funding level shall be $A+(BxG/Hx1.04),$ where— A and B have the same meaning as in paragraph 2, G is the total funding within the initially determined adjusted budget share for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2003 determined on the basis of pupil numbers, and H is the relevant number of pupils for the financial year beginning on 1st April 2003.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the LEA Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (England) Regulations 2003, and the Financing of Maintained Schools (No. 2) (England) Regulations 2003. In respect of a number of authorities they add a cash figure to the calculation required to be made in respect of the limit on the expenditure which may be deducted from the particular authority’s schools budget, and correct two other minor errors in the Budget regulations. They also correct two errors in the FMS Regulations.
