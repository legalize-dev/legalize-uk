# The Fertilisers (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 17th January 2019

Laid before Parliament: 24th January 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to the European (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## PART 1 — Introductory

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Fertilisers (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Northern Ireland only.

## PART 2 — Amendment of the Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992

###### 2

- (1) The Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2A, in the heading and in the regulation, for “EC”, substitute “UK”.

- (3) After regulation 2A, insert—

> > (2B) These Regulations do not apply to fertilisers designated as “EC fertilisers” which comply with the requirements set out in Regulation [(EC) 2003/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2003)[^f00003] as it has effect in EU law, as amended from time to time, where those fertilisers are placed on the market before the end of the period of two years beginning with exit day.

- (4) In Part I of Schedule 2, in sub-paragraph 1(j), for “European Union”, substitute “United Kingdom”.

## PART 3 — Amendment of the EC Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 3

- (1) The EC Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3—

- (a) in the heading, for “EC”, substitute “UK”;

- (b) in paragraph (1), in the words before sub-paragraph (a), for “an “EC”, substitute “a “UK”.

- (3) In regulation 4(a), for “an “EC”, substitute “a “UK”.

- (4) In regulation 5—

- (a) in the heading, for “EC”, substitute “UK”;

- (b) in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3), in the words before sub-paragraph (a), in each place it occurs, for “an EC”, substitute “a UK”.

- (5) In regulations 6(a), 7(a), and 8(a), for “an EC”, substitute “a UK”.

- (6) In regulation 12, for paragraph (2), substitute—

> > (2) An inspector may take with him such other persons he considers necessary.

- (7) In regulation 16(a)(ii), omit “(2) or (5)”.

- (8) In regulation 17(1), for “an EC”, substitute “a UK”.

## Signed

George Eustice — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 17th January 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address deficiencies, in Northern Ireland legislation relating to fertilisers, arising from the withdrawal of the UK from the European Union.

Regulation 2 amends the Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992 ([S.R. 1992 No. 187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1992/187)) to remove references to EEC fertilisers and EC fertilisers, since the EU fertilisers regime is dealt with in other legislation. Regulation 3 amends the EC Fertilisers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 503](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/503)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
