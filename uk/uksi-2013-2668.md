[^f00009]: [S.I. 2010/2130](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2130).

[^f00010]: Section 1 of the Children and Young Persons Act 2008 requires providers of social work services to be bodies corporate.

[^f00011]: Section 2 of the Children and Young Persons Act 2008 provides for the functions to be discharged by, or under the supervision of, registered social workers.
