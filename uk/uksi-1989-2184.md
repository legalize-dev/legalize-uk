# The Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1989

Made: 21st November 1989

Laid before Parliament: 4th December 1989

Coming into force: 1st January 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st January 1990.

###### 2

The Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) Regulations 1983[^f00002] shall be amended by the deletion of the reference to the Agreement dated 9th April 1980 between the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the Government of the Republic of Ireland on the International Carriages of Goods by Road [^f00003] in Schedule 1 to the said Regulations.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Robert Atkins — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 21st November 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations amend the Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) Regulations 1983. The amendment deletes the reference to the Agreement dated 9th April 1980 between the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland on the International Carriage of Goods by Road from Schedule 1 to the Regulations of 1983 because of the abolition of the requirements for permits for goods vehicles from the Republic of Ireland by the Goods Vehicles (Operators' Licences) (Temporary Use in Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 S.I. [1989/2183](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/2183).
