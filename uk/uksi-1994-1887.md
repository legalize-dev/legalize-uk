## SCHEDULE

| *(1)* | *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or Departments* | *Matters in relation to which Ministers or Departments are designated* |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to pregnant workers and/or workers who have recently given birth and/or workers who are breastfeeding. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to informational and educational materials dealing with the feeding of infants and young children, donations of informational or educational equipment or materials, and donations and/or low price sales of supplies of infant formulae. |
| Any Northern Ireland Department | The preceding items in this column. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to promotion of the development and structural adjustment of regions whose development is lagging behind. |
| The Secretary of State | Measures relating to promotion of rural development. |

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council
