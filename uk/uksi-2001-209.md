# The Education (Designated Institutions) Order 2001

Made: 24th January 2001

Laid before Parliament: 5th February 2001

Coming into force: 1st August 2001

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Employment hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st August 2001.

###### 2

The institution which is specified in the Schedule to this Order, being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within subsection (2) of section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988, is hereby designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

## SCHEDULE

The Royal Agricultural College.

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 24th January 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates as from 1st August 2001 The Royal Agricultural College as an institution eligible to receive funds from the Higher Education Funding Council for England.
