# The Local Authorities (Functions and Responsibilities) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 12th October 2026

Laid before Parliament: 13th October 2026

Coming into force: 2nd November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9D of the Local Government Act 2000[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities (Functions and Responsibilities) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 2nd November 2026.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the Local Authorities (Functions and Responsibilities) (England) Regulations 2000

###### 2

The [Local Authorities (Functions and Responsibilities) (England) Regulations 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/2853/contents)[^f00002] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

#### Amendment of Schedule 1 (functions not to be the responsibility of an authority’s executive)

###### 3

- (1) Schedule 1 is amended as follows.

- (2) For paragraph A (functions relating to town and country planning and development control) substitute the new paragraph A in the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (3) In paragraph I (miscellaneous functions), in column (2), in relation to the entry relating to item 47, for “the Trees Regulations 1999” substitute “the Town and Country Planning (Trees) Regulations 1999”.

#### Amendment of Schedule 2 (functions which may be (but need not be) the responsibility of an authority’s executive)

###### 4

At the end of Schedule 2, insert—

> > 1. Any function of a local planning authority relating to the making, or revocation of, a local development order under [sections 61A to 61D](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/61A) of and [Schedule 4A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/schedule/4A) to the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/contents) and [article 38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/595/article/38) of the [Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/595/contents) ([S.I. 2015/595](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/595)).
> 2. Any powers of entry of the local planning authority under [section 324](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/section/324) of the [Town and Country Planning Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/contents).
> 3. Any powers of entry of the local planning authority under [section 88](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/9/section/88) of the [Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/9/contents).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Local Authorities (Functions and Responsibilities) (England) Regulations 2000 (“*the 2000 Regulations*”). They apply in relation to local authorities in England.

Part 1A of the Local Government Act 2000 provides that, for an English local authority that operates executive arrangements, the discharge of its functions is to be the responsibility of an executive of the authority, unless otherwise specified. In the 2000 Regulations, Schedule 1 specifies functions which are not be the responsibility of an authority’s executive, and Schedule 2 specifies functions which may be (but not need be) the responsibility of an authority’s executive.

Regulation 3 amends Schedule 1 of the 2000 Regulations by the substitution of a new paragraph A. The new paragraph A updates and consolidates the existing paragraph A, in setting out which planning-related functions of a local authority are not to be the responsibility of an executive of that authority.

Regulation 4 amends Schedule 2 to the 2000 Regulations to the effect that a local authority may decide whether their functions as local planning authority relating to—

1. the making or revocation of a local development order; and

2. powers of entry in section 324 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 and section 88 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990,

should be the responsibility of their executive.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private sector, voluntary sector or public sector is foreseen. An explanatory memorandum is published alongside this instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
