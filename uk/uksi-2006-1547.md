# The Natural History Museum (Authorised Repositories) Order 2006

Made: 15th June 2006

Laid before Parliament: 16th June 2006

Coming into force: 10th July 2006

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the British Museum Act 1963[^f00001] and now vested in her [^f00002], with the agreement of the Trustees of the Natural History Museum, makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Natural History Museum (Authorised Repositories) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 10th July 2006.

###### 2

At the end of paragraph 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 3 to the British Museum Act 1963[^f00003] insert “, but excluding the part of that site known as the “Central Boiler House” and located on the north side of the Natural History Museum“.

## Signed

David Lammy — Minister for Culture — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 15th June 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order alters the description of the Natural History Museum’s authorised repository in South Kensington.
