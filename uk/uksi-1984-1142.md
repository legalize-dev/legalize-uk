# The Administration of Justice Act 1982 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1984

Made: 19th July 1984

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 76(1) of the Administration of Justice Act 1982, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Administration of Justice Act 1982 (Commencement No. 2) Order 1984.

###### 2

The following sections of theAdministration of Justice Act 1982 shall come into force on 1st September 1984:

- (a) sections 34 and 35; and

- (b) section 75(1) and schedule 9 so far as they relate to section 148 of the County Courts Act 1959.

## Signed

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, C — Dated 19th July 1984

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation the following provisions of the Administration of Justice Act 1982;

(a) Section 34: amending the County Courts Act 1959 and the Charging Orders Act 1979 (c.53) (transfer of High Court judgments and orders to the county court for enforcement);

(b) Section 35: amending the County Courts Act 1959 (transfer of county court judgments exceeding a specified amount to the High Court for enforcement); and

(c) Section 75 (repeals and revocations) so far as it relates to section 148 of the County Courts Act 1959 (administration orders).
