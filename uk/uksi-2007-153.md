# The Emergency Workers (Obstruction) Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2007

Made: 25th January 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(2) of the Emergency Workers (Obstruction) Act 2006[^f00001],:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Emergency Workers (Obstruction) Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2007.

###### 2

The appointed day for the coming into force of the Emergency Workers (Obstruction) Act 2006 (so far as not already in force) is 20th February 2007.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 25th January 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 20th February 2007 sections 1 to 6 of the Emergency Workers (Obstruction) Act 2006. Section 7 came into force on Royal Assent.
