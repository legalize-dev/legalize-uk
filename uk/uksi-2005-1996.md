# The Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) Order 2005

Made: 19th July 2005

Laid before Parliament: 20th July 2005

Coming into force: 5th December 2005

The Chancellor of the Exchequer, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 34(1) and 36(4) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 5th December 2005.

#### Fees payable

###### 2

In respect of the matters set out in the first column of the Schedule to this Order, the fee specified in the second column shall be payable to the person specified in the third column.

## SCHEDULE

###### 1

Attestation by an authorised person[^f00002] of the necessary declaration[^f00003]

###### 2

Attendance of an authorised person at a place other than one provided by the registration authority, for the purpose of attesting the necessary declaration in accordance with the procedures for house-bound and detained persons[^f00004]

###### 3

Attestation by an authorised person of the necessary declaration under the special procedure[^f00005]

###### 4

Application to shorten the waiting period[^f00006]

###### 5

Issue of Registrar General’s licence

###### 6

Signing by the civil partnership registrar[^f00007] of the civil partnership schedule[^f00008]

###### 7

Attendance of the civil partnership registrar for the purpose of signing the civil partnership schedule in accordance with the procedures for house-bound and detained persons

###### 8

Attendance of the civil partnership registrar in whose presence the Registrar General’s licence is signed

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Chancellor of the Exchequer

John Healey — Financial Secretary to the Treasury HM Treasury — 19th July 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

In relation to the registration of civil partnerships, this Order specifies what fees are to be paid, the amount of them and who they are to be paid to.

This Order does not impose a charge on business.
