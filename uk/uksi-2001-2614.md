# The Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001 (Commencement No. 1) (Amendment) Order 2001

Made: 18th July 2001

Coming into force: 31st August 2001

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the power conferred on her by sections 43(3), 43(7) and 43(8) of the Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001 (Commencement No. 1) (Amendment) Order 2001;

- (b) This Order shall come into force on 31st August 2001

###### 2

This Order applies only in relation to England.

###### 3

In this Order “the Principal Order” means the Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2001[^f00002].

#### Amendment to the Principal Order

###### 4

Paragraph 5 of the Principal Order shall be amended by the substitution of “1st January 2002” for “1st September 2001”.

## Signed

Estell Morris — Secretary of State for Education and Skills. — 18th July 2001

## Explanatory note

(this note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2001. It changes the commencement date of the provisions of the Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001 set out in Part II of the Schedule to that Order from 1st September 2001 to 1st January 2002.
