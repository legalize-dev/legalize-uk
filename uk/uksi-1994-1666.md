# The Football Spectators (Seating) Order 1994

Made: 22nd June 1994

Laid before Parliament: 24th June 1994

Coming into force: 15th July 1994

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 11(1) to (3) of the Football Spectators Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consulting the Football Licensing Authority, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Football Spectators (Seating) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 15th July 1994.

###### 2

- (1) The Secretary of State hereby directs the Football Licensing Authority as set out in paragraph (2) below.

- (2) When granting any licence to admit spectators to any of the premises specified in column (1) of Schedule 1 hereto and further identified, where appropriate, in column (2) of that Schedule by the name of the football club of which they are the home ground, it shall include a condition imposing the requirements, as respects the seating of spectators at designated football matches[^f00002] at the said premises, specified in Schedule 2 hereto.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Requirements to be imposed

###### 1

Only seated accommodation shall be provided for spectators at a designated football match.

###### 2

Spectators shall only be admitted to watch a designated football match from seated accommodation.

## Signed

Peter Brooke — Secretary of State for National Heritage — 22nd June 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order directs the Football Licensing Authority to include in any licence to admit spectators (defined in section 1(7) of the Football Spectators Act 1989 (“the Act”)) to Wembley Stadium, the National Stadium in Cardiff (“Cardiff Arms Park”) or the football grounds, all in England, also listed in Schedule 1, a condition imposing requirements specified in Schedule 2 as respects the seating of spectators at designated football matches at those premises. “Designated football matches” are those association football matches designated by the Secretary of State, under powers conferred by section 1(2) of the Act, in the Football Spectators (Designation of Football Matches in England and Wales) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/1691](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1691)) and they include all association football matches played at the premises listed in Schedule 1 to this Order.
