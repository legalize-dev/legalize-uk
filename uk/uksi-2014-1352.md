# The Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Amendment) Order 2014

Made: 27th May 2014

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of May 2014Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 2(2) and (3) of that Act, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Amendment) Order2014 and shall come into force on the twenty-eighth day after the day on which it is made.

#### Amendments to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971

###### 2

The following amendments are made to Schedule 2 to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971.

###### 3

In Part 3 (which specifies the drugs which are subject to control under that Act as Class C drugs) in paragraph 1(a), after “Ketazolam”, insert—

> > Khat.

###### 4

In Part 4 (which sets out the meaning of certain expressions used in that Schedule), after the entry for “concentrate of poppy-straw” insert—

> > - “khat” means the leaves, stems or shoots of the plant of the species *Catha edulis*;

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 3 of this Order adds khat to paragraph 1(a) of Part 3 of Schedule 2 to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 which specifies drugs which are subject to control as Class C drugs under that Act. Article 4 defines khat as the leaves, stems or shoots of the plant of the species *Catha edulis*.
