# The Money Laundering (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 8th January 2015

Laid before Parliament: 9th January 2015

Coming into force

The Treasury are a government department designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the prevention of money laundering and terrorist financing.

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, make the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Money Laundering (Amendment) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 6th February 2015.

#### Amendment of the Money Laundering Regulations 2007

###### 2

The Money Laundering Regulations 2007 are amended as follows.

###### 3

In Part 1 of Schedule 3—

- (a) after paragraph 4 insert—

> > (4A) Chartered Institute of Legal Executives

, and

- (b) omit paragraph 6.

## Signed

Gavin Barwell — David Evennett — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 8th January 2015
