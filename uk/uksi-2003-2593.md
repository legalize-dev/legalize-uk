# The Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003

Made: 14th October 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 108(2) of the Police Reform Act 2002[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Police Reform Act 2002 shall come into force on 15th November 2003—

- (a) section 24 (consultation on regulations) to the extent not already in force;

- (b) section 87 (police members of NCS) to the extent not already in force;

- (c) section 91 (supplementary provisions about police membership of NCS) to the extent not already in force; and

- (d) paragraph 1(2)(a) of Schedule 4.

## Signed

Caroline Flint — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 14th October 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 15th November 2003 the provisions set out in article 2.
