# The Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Miscellaneous Amendments) (England) Regulations 2018

Made: 8th May 2018

Laid before Parliament: 10th May 2018

Coming into force

There has been consultation, as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00012], during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations so far as they relate to food.

## PART 1 — Introduction

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (Miscellaneous Amendments) (England) Regulations 2018.

- (2) They come into force on 1st June 2018, except for the following provisions which come into force on 5th July 2018—

- (a) regulation [4(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/4/2/2019-12-14);

- (b) regulations [6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/6/2019-12-14) and 7;

- (c) regulation [11(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/11/3/2019-12-14);

- (d) regulation [12(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/12/2/2019-12-14);

- (e) regulation [13](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/13/2019-12-14).

- (3) They extend to England and Wales.

- (4) The following regulations apply in relation to England only—

- (a) regulation [2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/2/2019-12-14);

- (b) regulations [6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/6/2019-12-14) and 7;

- (c) regulation [9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/9/2019-12-14);

- (d) regulations [14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/14/2019-12-14) and [15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/15/2019-12-14);

- (e) regulations [25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/25/2019-12-14) and [26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/26/2019-12-14);

- (f) regulations [30](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/30/2019-12-14) and [31](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/31/2019-12-14).

- (5) Regulation [12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/12/2019-12-14) applies to England and the sea adjacent to England out as far as the seaward boundary of the territorial sea.

- (6) In paragraph [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/1/5/2019-12-14), the sea adjacent to England has the same meaning as in regulation 1(4) and (5) of the Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016[^f00013].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments in relation to England to a number of pieces of secondary legislation within the remit of the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs.

Several provisions (listed in regulation [1(2)(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/1/2/a/2019-12-14) to [(e)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/1/2/e/2019-12-14)) reflect an amendment to Annex 3 to [Directive 2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste (OJ No L 312, 22.11.2008, p 3).

Regulation [10(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/10/3/2019-12-14) amends the Nitrate Pollution Prevention Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/668](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/668)) to correct a reference relating to the methodology used for calculating nitrate concentration in water bodies.

Regulation [14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/14/2019-12-14) amends the Water Abstraction (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1047](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1047)) to correct an error in the description of what circumstances qualify as granting a more limited abstraction licence than that applied for.

Regulation [24(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/575/regulation/24/4/2019-12-14) amends the Fruit Juices and Fruit Nectars (England) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/2775](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2775)) to implement Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1040/2014 amending Council [Directive 2001/112/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0112) relating to fruit juices and certain similar products intended for human consumption to adapt its Annex I to technical progress (OJ No L 288, 2.10.2014, p 1). This allows plant proteins from wheat, peas and potatoes to be used for the clarification of products to which the Fruit Juices and Fruit Nectars (England) Regulations 2013 apply.

The Regulations make other miscellaneous minor amendments to legislation in the fields of agriculture, animal health and welfare, environmental protection, food, horticulture, plant health, sea fisheries, seeds, waste and water, in particular amending out of date references to domestic legislation and to EU instruments.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
