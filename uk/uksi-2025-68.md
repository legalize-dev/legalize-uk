# The Fixed Penalty Offences Order 2025

Made: 22nd January 2025

Laid before Parliament: 27th January 2025

Coming into force: 24th February 2025

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(3) of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988[^f00001].

The Secretary of State has consulted with such representative organisations as appear appropriate before making this Order as required by section 88(2) of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fixed Penalty Offences Order 2025 and shall come into force on 24th February 2025.

###### 2

This Order extends to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988

###### 3

The following offences are fixed penalty offences for the purposes of Part 3 of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988—

- (a) an offence under section 2(1) of the Parks Regulation (Amendment) Act 1926[^f00002] of contravening any of the following provisions of the Royal Parks and Other Open Spaces Regulations 1997[^f00003]—

- (i) regulation 3(9)(a) (failure to comply with a direction by a constable or notice exhibited by order of the Secretary of State), insofar as the contravention relates to the regulation or control of a pedal cycle;

- (ii) regulation 3(10)(c) (use of certain vehicles in a manner that endangers or is likely to endanger any person), insofar as the contravention relates to use of a pedal cycle;

- (iii) regulation 3(11) (use of a pedal cycle or driving or riding a vehicle between sunset and sunrise or in seriously reduced visibility unless adequately lit)[^f00004];

- (b) an offence under section 28(3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (stopping of vehicles at school crossings)[^f00005];

- (c) an offence under section 26(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (holding onto vehicle in order to be towed) committed while using a pedal cycle[^f00006];

- (d) an offence under section 28(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (dangerous cycling)[^f00007];

- (e) an offence under section 29 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (careless and inconsiderate cycling)[^f00008];

- (f) an offence under section 91 of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988[^f00009] of contravening either of the following provisions of the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations 1989[^f00010]—

- (i) regulation 24(1)(a)(i) (use of lamps between sunset and sunrise), insofar as the contravention relates to use of a pedal cycle[^f00011];

- (ii) regulation 27 (use of lamps so as to cause undue dazzle or discomfort to other persons using the road), insofar as the contravention relates to entry 1 or 2 in the table in that regulation[^f00012].

###### 4

In consequence of the provisions of article 3 of this Order, Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 is amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order provides for the offences listed in article 3 to be fixed penalty offences for the purposes of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 and makes consequential modifications to Schedule 3 to that Act, which specifies offences which are fixed penalty offences for the purposes of that Act.

The offences relate to the use of pedal cycles, the stopping of vehicles at school crossings, and the use of lamps on non-motor vehicles.
