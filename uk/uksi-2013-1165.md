# The Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) (Amendment) Order 2013

Made: 20th May 2013

Laid before Parliament: 20th May 2013

Coming into force: 1st July 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(1) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) (Amendment) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st July 2013.

#### Amendments to the Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) Order 2002

###### 2

- (1) The Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) Order 2002[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2 (amount of penalty payable in respect of a penalty offence)[^f00003]—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “£80” substitute “£90”; and

- (b) in paragraph (b), for “£50” substitute “£60”.

- (3) In the heading of Part 1 of the Schedule[^f00004], for “£80” substitute “£90”.

- (4) In the heading of Part 2 of the Schedule[^f00005], for “£50” substitute “£60”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Helen Grant — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 20th May 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 3(1) of the Criminal Justice and Police Act [2001 (c.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16) and amends the Penalties for Disorderly Behaviour (Amount of Penalty) Order 2002 ([S.I. 2002/1837](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1837)) (“the 2002 Order”).

Article 2 of this Order amends the 2002 Order with the effect that the penalties payable in respect of penalty offences are increased. The term “penalty offence” is defined in section 1 of the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001. This Order raises the penalties by £10; from £80 to £90 in the case of a penalty offence falling within Part 1 of the Schedule to the 2002 Order; and from £50 to £60 in the case of a penalty offence falling within Part 2 of the Schedule to the 2002 Order.
