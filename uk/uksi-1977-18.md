# The Control of Atmospheric Pollution (Exempted Premises) Regulations 1977

Made: 5th January 1977

Laid before Parliament: 17th January 1977

Coming into Operation: 7th February 1977

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in respect of England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, in respect of Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 80(6) and 104(1) of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following regulations:—

###### 1

These regulations may be cited as the Control of Atmospheric Pollution (Exempted Premises) Regulations 1977 and shall come into operation on 7th February 1977.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these regulations as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 3

Section 80 of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 shall not apply to any premises (being premises used for the public service of the Crown) which bear a name or description specified in column (1) of the Schedule to these regulations and are situated at an address or location specified in respect of that name or description in column (2) of that Schedule.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 80 of the Control of Pollution Act 1974 provides that, subject to certain exceptions expressly set out in that section, and except so far as regulations may provide otherwise in relation to premises used for the public service of the Crown and in relation to persons in that service, a local authority may require occupiers of premises in their area to provide information concerning the emission of pollutants and other substances into the air from the premises.

These Regulations, which apply to England and Wales, specify certain premises used for the public service of the Crown to which the provisions of section 80 are not to apply.
