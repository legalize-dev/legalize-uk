# The County Council of Suffolk (Mutford Bridges) Scheme 1989 Confirmation Instrument 1989

Made: 20th November 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

The County Council of Suffolk (Mutford Bridges) Scheme 1989 is hereby confirmed modified by the replacement of “cycle track with footpath” by “cycle track including a right of way on foot”.

###### 2

The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plans are deposited at the offices of the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY; and at the offices of the County Council of Suffolk, at St Helen Court, County Hall, Ipswich IP4 2JS.

###### 3

The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 4

This Instrument may be cited as the County Council of Suffolk (Mutford Bridges) Scheme 1989 Confirmation Instrument 1989.

## THE SCHEDULE — THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF SUFFOLK (MUTFORD BRIDGES) SCHEME 1989

The County Council of Suffolk (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980 and of all other enabling powers:–

###### 1

The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of (1) Lake Lothing and (2) Mutford Lock the bridges specified respectively in Schedules 1 and 2 to this Scheme. The bridge specified in Schedule 1 is part of the highway which they are proposing to construct from the A1117 Bridge Road near its southern junction with Commodore Road in a generally southerly direction to join the A146 Victoria Road and A1117 Bridge Road (the latter south of its existing junction with Victoria Road). The bridge specified in Schedule 2 is part of the cycle track including a right of way on foot which they are proposing to construct between the southern junction of Commodore Road and A1117 Bridge Road, south, to join Bridge Road at a point 45 metres north north east of the junction between Bridge Road and the Boulevard.

###### 2

This Scheme may be cited as the County Council of Suffolk (Mutford Bridges) Scheme 1989.
