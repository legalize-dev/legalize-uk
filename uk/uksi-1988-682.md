# Act of Sederunt (Exchequer Causes) 1988

Made: 7th April 1988

Coming into force: 2nd May 1988

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 3 of the Administration of Justice (Scotland) Act 1948([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Exchequer Causes) 1988 and shall come into force on 2nd May 1988.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Lord Ordinary in Exchequer Causes

###### 2

From the date this Act of Sederunt comes into force, the Lord Ordinary in Exchequer Causes under section 2 of the Exchequer Court (Scotland) Act 1856([^f00002]) shall be the Honourable Lord Coulsfield.

## Signed

Emslie — Lord President, IPD — 7th April 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt appoints Lord Coulsfield to be the Lord Ordinary in Exchequer Causes under section 2 of the Exchequer Court (Scotland) Act 1856.
