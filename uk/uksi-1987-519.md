# The Petty Sessional Divisions (Northamptonshire) (Amendment) Order 1987

Made: 24th March 1987

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by subsections (3) and (5) of the said section 23, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Petty Sessional Divisions (Northamptonshire) (Amendment) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st April 1987.

###### 2

In this Order, “the principal Order” means the Petty Sessional Divisions (Northamptonshire) Order 1987[^f00002].

###### 3

In column (2) of Schedule 2 to the principal Order, the following substitutions shall be made in the entry specifying the area comprised in the petty sessional division of Daventry—

- (a) “East Farndon” shall be substituted for “Eash Farndon”;

- (b) “Maidwell” shall be substituted for “Maindwell”;

- (c) “Stowe Nine Churches” shall be substituted for “Stone Nine Churches”.

###### 4

In paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 to the principal Order “1988” shall be substituted for “1987” in the last place where it occurs.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 24th March 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Petty Sessional Divisions (Northamptonshire) Order 1987, which gave effect to a draft Order for the amalgamation of petty sessional divisions in the county submitted by the magistrates' courts committee, so as to correct certain errors in that Order. This Order substitutes the names of three of the parishes comprised in the petty sessional division of Daventry constituted by that Order and provides that the licensing hours in force in each of the petty sessional divisions abolished by that Order continue in force until the coming into force of an order fixing permitted hours made at the 1988 general annual licensing meeting of the justices for the respective new division created by that Order for each abolished division.
