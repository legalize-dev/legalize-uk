# The Technical and Further Education Act 2017 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2019

Made: 17th January 2019

The Secretary of State for Education makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 47(2) of the Technical and Further Education Act 2017[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Technical and Further Education Act 2017 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2019.

#### Provisions coming into force on 31st January 2019

###### 2

The following provisions of the Technical and Further Education Act 2017 come into force on 31st January 2019—

- (a) section 1, in so far as not already in force; and

- (b) Schedule 1, in so far as not already in force.

## Signed

Anne Milton — Minister of State for Apprenticeships and Skills — Department for Education — 17th January 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force the specified provisions of the Technical and Further Education Act [2017 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/19) (“the 2017 Act”) on 31st January 2019. These are the fifth commencement regulations made under that Act. Part 4 of, and paragraph 35 of Schedule 1 to, the 2017 Act came into force on the day that Act was passed.

Regulation 2 commences section 1 and Schedule 1 in so far as they are not already in force. In particular, the provisions commenced by these Regulations rename the Institute for Apprenticeships as the Institute for Apprenticeships and Technical Education and make provision regarding technical education.
