# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Business in the Regulated Sector and Supervisory Authorities) Order 2003

Made: 28th November 2003

Laid before Parliament: 28th November 2003

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 459(2) of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 9 to, the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Business in the Regulated Sector and Supervisory Authorities) Order 2003 and comes into force on 1st March 2004.

- (2) Despite paragraph (1) above—

- (a) paragraph 2(g) of Schedule 9 (as inserted by this Order) and the reference to it in paragraph 3(2) have no effect until 31st October 2004;

- (b) paragraph 2(h) of that Schedule and the reference to it in paragraph 3(2) have no effect until 14th January 2005.

- (3) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002; and

- “Schedule 9” means Schedule 9 to the Act.

#### Amendment of Part 1 of Schedule 9

###### 2

For Part 1 of Schedule 9 (regulated sector), substitute the text set out in the Schedule to this Order.

#### Amendment of Part 2 of Schedule 9

###### 3

In Part 2 of Schedule 9 (supervisory authorities), in paragraph 4 insert after sub-paragraph (1)(e)—

> > - ; the Occupational Pensions Regulatory Authority; the Gaming Board for Great Britain

#### Transitional provision

###### 4

Sections 330 and 331 of the Act shall not have effect in relation to a person who engages in any of the activities mentioned in paragraph 1(1)(f) to (n) of Schedule 9 (as substituted by article 2 above) where the information or other matter on which knowledge or suspicion that another person is engaged in money laundering is based, or which gives reasonable grounds for such knowledge or suspicion, came to that person before 1st March 2004.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

John Heppell — Nick Ainger — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 28 November 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the meaning of a business in the regulated sector in Part 1 of Schedule 9 to the Proceeds of Crime Act [2002 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/29) to take into account Directive [2001/97/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0097) of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Council Directive [91/308/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0308) on prevention of the use of the financial system for the purpose of money laundering. This Order also adds two bodies to the list of supervisory authorities in Part 2 of that Schedule.
