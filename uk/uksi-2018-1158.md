# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2018

Made: 7th November 2018

Coming into force: 8th November 2018

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 7th day of November 2018Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2018 and comes into force on 8th November 2018.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — Persons Appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 8th November 2018

- Maire Shevaun Atherton

- Stephen Graham Lowe

- Matthew John Middlemore

- Lesley Colleen Yates

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
