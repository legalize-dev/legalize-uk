# The Local Elections (Principal Areas) (Amendment) Rules 1990

Made: 5th February 1990

Laid before Parliament: 9th February 1990

Coming into force in accordance with rule 1(3)

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 36(2) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001], I hereby make the following Rules:–

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the Local Elections (Principal Areas) (Amendment) Rules 1990.

- (2) These Rules do not extend to Scotland or Northern Ireland.

- (3) These Rules shall come into force on 3rd March 1990 except that—

- (a) rule 4(8)(b) shall only apply in respect of an election with respect to which section 1 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00002] has effect;

- (b) rules 4(10) and (11)(b) and (c), 5(16) and (17) and 6(8) and (9) shall not apply in respect of an election if the date by which notice of election for that election is required to be published preceded that day; and

- (c) rules 2, 3, 4(3) to (7), (8)(a), (9), (10)(a) and (12), 5(2) to (5), (6)(a), (7), (8), (9)(a) and (c), (10) to (15) and (18) and 6(2), (3), (5)(b), (6) and (10) shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

For rule 4 of the Local Elections (Principal Areas) Rules 1986[^f00003] there shall be substituted the following rule–

> > (4) In these Rules, “principal area” means a county, district or London borough.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Rules)

These Rules amend the Local Elections (Principal Areas) Rules 1986 which after 1st April 1990 will apply in respect of the election of county, district or London borough councillors in England and Wales. Rules 2, 3, 4(3), (5) to (7), (8)(a), (9), (10)(a) and (12), 5(2) to (5), (6)(a), (7), (8), (9)(a) and (c), (10)~ to (15) and (18) and 6(2), (3), (5)(b), (6) and (10) of these Rules are consequential on the repeal of the provisions in primary legislation relating to elections to the Inner London Education Authority (see Part I of Schedule 13 to the Education Reform Act [1988 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/40).

Rule 4(8)(b) of these Rules amends the form of a candidate’s consent to nomination in consequence of the disqualification introduced by Part I of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989.

Rules 4(2), (8) and (11)(b) and (c), 5(6)(b), (9)(b), (16) and (17) and 6(4), (5)(a) and (7) to (9) either seek to clarify the drafting of the Local Elections (Principal Areas) Rules 1986 including the forms of the official poll card and declarations of identity or correct errors or omissions in those Rules.

Rule 4(4) omits a provision about rating which is equivalent to a provision in the parliamentary elections rules in Schedule 1 to the Representation of the People Act 1983 which will be repealed as from 1st April 1990 by Part I of Schedule 13 to the Local Government Finance Act [1988 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/41).

Rule 7 omits a provision which is superseded by the amendment made by rule 4(11)(c).
