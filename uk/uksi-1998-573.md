# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Tunbridge Wells Borough Council) Order 1998

Made: 4th March 1998

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 1998

Coming into force: 3rd April 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (Tunbridge Wells Borough Council) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 3rd April 1998.

#### Exemption of other catering

###### 2

Other catering[^f00002] by the council of the borough of Tunbridge Wells shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is carried out at the Town Hall, Royal Tunbridge Wells.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 4th March 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (competition), work falling within certain defined activities may be carried out by local authorities only if particular conditions are fulfilled.

Article 2 of this Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the other catering work of Tunbridge Wells Borough Council so long as that work is carried out at the Town Hall, Royal Tunbridge Wells.
