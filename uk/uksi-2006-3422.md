# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.15) Order 2006

Made: 19th December 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 336(3) and (4) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001]:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No.15) Order 2006.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The following provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 come into force on 8th January 2007—

- (a) section 50 (application of Part 7 to Northern Ireland), in so far as it applies to applications under section 44 (jury tampering) and trials ordered under section 44 or 46 (discharge of jury because of jury tampering);

- (b) section 331 (further minor and consequential amendments) in so far as it relates to the provisions referred to in paragraph (c); and

- (c) in Schedule 36 (minor and consequential amendments)

- (i) Part 4, in so far as not already in force; and

- (ii) paragraphs 91 ( in so far as not already in force), 92(1) to (5) and (7) and 93.

## Signed

Scotland of Asthal, Q.C. — Minister of State — 19th December 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 8th January 2007 the provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 referred to in article 2.
