# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (BIMM University Limited) Order 2025

Made: 6th February 2025

Coming into force: 13th March 2025

The Office for Students has requested advice from the relevant body regarding the quality of, and standards applied to, higher education provided by the provider and has had regard to that advice in accordance with section 46(1)(a) and (7) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (BIMM University Limited) Order 2025 and shall come into force on 13th March 2025.

#### Authorisation to grant awards

###### 2

BIMM University Limited (registered company number 06347465) is authorised to grant taught awards of the kind mentioned in section 42(2)(a) of the said Act.

###### 3

The authorisation in article 2 is for a fixed term beginning on 13th March 2025 and expiring on 12th March 2027 at 11.59 p.m.

###### 4

The authorisation in article 2 includes the power for BIMM University Limited to authorise other institutions to grant such awards specified in article 2 of this Order on its behalf.

## Signed

Jean Arnold — Deputy Director of Quality — Office for Students — 6th February 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order authorises BIMM University Limited to grant taught awards, including the power to authorise other institutions to grant such awards on its behalf. This authorisation is for a fixed term beginning on 13th March 2025 and expiring on 12th March 2027 at 11.59 p.m.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
