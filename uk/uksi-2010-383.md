# The Animal Welfare (Codes of Practice) (Appointed Day) (England) Order 2010

Made: 18th February 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 15(5) of the Animal Welfare Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order.

#### Citation and application

###### 1

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Animal Welfare (Codes of Practice) (Appointed Day) (England) Order 2010; and

- (b) applies in England.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The appointed day for the following codes of practice coming into force is 6th April 2010—

- (a) Code of Practice for the Welfare of Privately Kept Non-Human Primates[^f00002];

- (b) Code of Practice for the Welfare of Cats[^f00003];

- (c) Code of Practice for the Welfare of Dogs[^f00004];

- (d) Code of Practice for the Welfare of Horses, Ponies, Donkeys and their Hybrids[^f00005].

## Signed

Jim Fitzpatrick — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 18th February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order sets 6th April 2010 as the appointed day when the codes of practice specified in article 2 come into force.
