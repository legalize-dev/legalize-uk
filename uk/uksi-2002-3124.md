# The National Lottery (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made: 16th December 2002

Laid before Parliament: 17th December 2002

Coming into force: 13th January 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 7(5) and 60(4) of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Lottery (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 13th January 2003.

#### Fees for licences under section 6

###### 2

- (1) The National Lottery (Licence Fees) Order 2001[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) Article 3 (fees for licences under section 6) shall be renumbered as paragraph (1) of that article.

- (3) At the beginning of paragraph (1) of article 3 (as so renumbered) there shall be inserted “Subject to paragraph (2)”.

- (4) After paragraph (1) of article 3 (as so renumbered), there shall be inserted—

> > (2) Where a licence granted under section 6 of that Act authorises the promotion by the licensee of an unrestricted number of lotteries which fall within a particular description specified in the licence, the fee shall be £100,000

## Signed

Richard Caborn — Minister of State, — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 16th December 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the National Lottery (Licence Fees) Order 2001 (S.I. [2001/2506](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/2506)) which prescribes the fees payable on all licences granted under section 5 or 6 of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993.

The Order prescribes the sums payable by the licensee to the National Lottery Commission by way of fees on the grant of a licence under section 6 of the Act, which authorises the promotion by the licensee of an unrestricted number of lotteries which fall within a description specified in the licence. It does not amend the fees payable on any other licences granted under section 5 or 6.
