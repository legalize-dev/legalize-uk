## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1991. The amendments—

- (a) increase the basic hourly rate from £37 to £45, an increase of 21.6%;

- (b) provide for charges in connection with registration of ships following the changes to the system of registration introduced by the Merchant Shipping (Registration, etc.) Act 1993;

- (c) introduce a fee of £48.75 for examination for medical fitness certificates;

- (d) increase by about 9% certain additional fees in respect of radio surveys;

- (e) make minor corrections and updating.

- (f) bring the charges for fishing vessel surveys in line with those surveys charged at the basic hourly rate.
