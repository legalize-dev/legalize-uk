# The Organic Control (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: at 11.54 a.m. on 28th January 2021

Laid before Parliament: at 4.00 p.m. on 28th January 2021

Coming into force: 1st February 2021

In accordance with Article 38b(9) of that Regulation, the Secretary of State has consulted such bodies or persons as appear to the Secretary of State to be representative of the interests likely to be substantially affected by these Regulations and such other bodies or persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Organic Control (Amendment) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 1st February 2021.

- (2) These Regulations extend to Great Britain.

#### Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/977

###### 2

- (1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/977 derogating from Regulations [(EC) No 889/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0889) and [(EC) No 1235/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1235) as regards controls on the production of organic products due to the COVID-19 pandemic is amended as follows.

- (2) In Article 1—

- (a) in paragraph 2 omit “in the Member States and in the Third Country concerned”;

- (b) omit paragraph 4;

- (c) in paragraph 7—

- (i) omit “, and planned until 1 February 2021”;

- (ii) omit “in the Member State concerned”.

- (3) Omit Article 2.

- (4) In Article 3—

- (a) in the second paragraph omit “until 1 February 2021”;

- (b) omit the third paragraph;

- (c) in the fourth paragraph for “31 December 2020” substitute “31 December 2021”;

- (d) omit the fifth paragraph.

- (5) After Article 3, omit the words from “This Regulation” to “Member States”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/977 derogating from Regulations [(EC) No 889/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0889) and [(EC) No 1235/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1235) as regards controls on the production of organic products due to the COVID-19 pandemic (EUR 2020/977).

That Regulation provides for temporary derogations from certain provisions of [Commission Regulation (EC) No 889/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0889) laying down detailed rules for the implementation of Council Regulation [(EC) No 834/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0834) on organic production and labelling of organic products with regard to organic production, labelling and control (EUR 2008/889) and [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1235/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1235) laying down detailed rules for implementation of Council Regulation [(EC) No 834/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0834) as regards the arrangements for imports of organic products from third countries (EUR 2008/1235) relating to official controls on organic products.

Regulation 2 provides for the derogations in respect of Regulation [(EC) No 889/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0889) to be extended and revokes the derogations in respect of Regulation [(EC) No 1235/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1235) which apply until 1st February 2021.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
