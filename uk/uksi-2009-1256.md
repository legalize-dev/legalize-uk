# The Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009

Made: 19th May 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(5) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2009.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 shall come into force on 18th June 2009—

- (a) section 28 (jurisdiction to try offences committed in the UK);

- (b) sections 30 to 33 (sentencing), together with Schedule 2 (offences where terrorist connection to be considered);

- (c) sections 34 to 39 (forfeiture), together with Schedule 3 (forfeiture: consequential amendments);

- (d) section 99 in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 9 mentioned in sub-paragraph (e); and

- (e) the entries in Part 3 (forfeiture) of Schedule 9 (repeals and revocations).

## Signed

Vernon Coaker — Minister of State — 19th May 2009
