# The Birmingham Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2006

Made: 15th February 2006

Coming into force: 25th February 2006

In accordance with section 5(2) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00003], the prescribed consultation has been completed[^f00004].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Birmingham Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2006 and shall come into force on 25th February 2006.

- (2) In this Order, “the Establishment Order” means the Birmingham Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00005].

#### Amendment of nature and functions of the trust

###### 2

Article 3(2) of the Establishment Order (nature and functions of the trust) shall be replaced by the following—

> > (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, and community health services, for the purposes of the health service.

#### Increase in the number of non-executive directors of the trust

###### 3

In article 4 of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust) “5 non-executive directors” shall be replaced by “6 non-executive directors”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Warner — Minister of State for Health, — Department of Health — 15th February 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Birmingham Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994, which established the Birmingham Children’s Hospital National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order removes the references to the establishments from which the trust carries out its functions. Article 3 of the Order increases the number of non-executive directors of the trust from 5 to 6.
