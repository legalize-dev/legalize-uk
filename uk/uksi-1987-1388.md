# The Local Authorities (Recognised Bodies for Heritable Securities Indemnities) (Scotland) Order 1987

Made: 30th July 1987

Coming into force: 13th August 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 31(5A) of the Tenants' Rights, Etc. (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Recognised Bodies for Heritable Securities Indemnities) (Scotland) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 13th August 1987.

###### 2

The bodies set out in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby designated for the purposes of section 31 of the Tenants' Rights, Etc. (Scotland) Act 1980.

###### 3

The classes or descriptions of bodies set out in Schedule 2 to this Order are hereby designated for the purposes of section 31 of the Tenants' Rights, Etc. (Scotland) Act 1980.

## SCHEDULE 1 — BODIES DESIGNATED FOR THE PURPOSES OF SECTION 31 OF THE TENANTS' RIGHTS, ETC. (SCOTLAND) ACT 1980

###### 1

The Bank of England.

###### 2

The Post Office.

## SCHEDULE 2 — CLASSES OR DESCRIPTIONS OF BODIES DESIGNATED FOR THE PURPOSES OF SECTION 31 OF THE TENANTS' RIGHTS, ETC. (SCOTLAND) ACT 1980

###### 1

Insurance companies to which Part II of the Insurance Companies Act 1982[^f00002] applies.

###### 2

Recognised banks and licensed institutions within the meaning of the Banking Act 1979[^f00003] or authorised institutions under the Banking Act 1987[^f00004].

###### 3

Friendly societies and branches thereof which are registered within the meaning of the Friendly Societies Act 1974[^f00005].

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,Scottish Office — 23rd July 1987

We consent,

Michael Neubert — Nigel Lawson — Two of The Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 30th July 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates 2 bodies and 3 classes or descriptions of bodies for the purposes of section 31 of the Tenants' Rights, Etc. (Scotland) Act 1980. The bodies designated and any bodies belonging to the classes or descriptions designated become recognised bodies for the purposes of that section. Section 31, as amended by section 9 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1986 to include recognised bodies, provides that an islands or district council may, with the approval of the Secretary of State, enter into agreements with building societies and recognised bodies providing for indemnities where such societies and bodies grant loans secured by way of heritable securities.
