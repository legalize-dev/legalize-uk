# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham) (Amendment) Order 1994

Made: 10th March 1994

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1994

Coming into force: 1st April 1994

NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham) (Amendment) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 1984 Act” means the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002];

- “the 1991 Act” means the Road Traffic Act 1991;

- “the 1993 Order” means the Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham) (London Borough of Wandsworth) (Amendment) Order 1993[^f00003]; and

- a reference to a Schedule followed by a number is a reference to the Schedule to this Order bearing that number.

#### Exclusions from special parking areas

###### 3

- (1) Those areas in the London borough of Bromley referred to in Schedule 1 shall cease to form part of the special parking area designated by article 4(1)(a) of the 1993 Order.

- (2) Those areas in the London borough of Hammersmith and Fulham referred to in Schedule 2 shall cease to form part of the special parking area designated by article 4(1)(b) of the 1993 Order.

- (3) Those areas in the London borough of Lewisham referred to in Schedule 3 shall cease to form part of the special parking area designated by article 4(1)(c) of the 1993 Order.

- (4) The modifications of the 1984 and 1991 Acts contained in articles 5 and 6 of the 1993 Order shall cease to have effect in relation to the areas referred to in Schedules 1, 2 and 3.

###### 2

###### 5

The areas to be transferred from the London borough of Bromley to the London borough of Greenwich and referred to in the table to article 3 of the Bromley and Greenwich (London Borough Boundaries) Order 1993[^f00007].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham) (London Borough of Wandsworth) (Amendment) Order 1993 designated the whole of the London boroughs of Bromley, Hammersmith and Fulham and Lewisham (excluding the roads referred to in Schedules 2, 3 and 4 respectively) as special parking areas for the purposes of Part II of the Road Traffic Act 1991. It also made consequential modifications to the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 and to Part II of the 1991 Act. This Order excludes from the special parking areas so designated certain areas which, in consequence of local government boundary changes, will cease to form part of the London boroughs concerned and will be transferred to local government areas where a special parking area has not been designated. The areas concerned are described in the Schedules to this Order by reference to the relevant boundary change Orders. This Order also provides that the consequential modifications of the 1984 and 1991 Acts are to cease to apply to the transferred areas.
