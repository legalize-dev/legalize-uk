# The Criminal Justice Act 1972 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1972

Made: 17th November 1972

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 66(6) of the Criminal Justice Act 1972, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1972 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1972.

###### 2

The provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1972 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st January 1973.
