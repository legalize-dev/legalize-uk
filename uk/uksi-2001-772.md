# The Wireless Telegraphy (Television Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made: 6th March 2001

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 2 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1949[^f00001], as it has effect in the United Kingdom and as extended by the Wireless Telegraphy (Channel Islands) Order 1952[^f00002] and the Wireless Telegraphy (Isle of Man) Order 1952[^f00003], and now vested in him[^f00004], hereby, with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Television Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

#### Amendment of the 1997 Regulations

###### 2

The Wireless Telegraphy (Television Licence Fees) Regulations 1997[^f00005] shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 6.

###### 3

At the end of regulation 5 there shall be inserted the words, “; but no such sum shall be payable on the issue of a duplicate of a television licence that was issued or, as the case may be, renewed, free of charge.”.

###### 4

- (1) Schedule 2 shall be amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In Part I of that Schedule—

- (a) for “£34.50” in each place where it occurs there shall be substituted “£36.50”;

- (b) for “£104.00” in each place where it occurs there shall be substituted “£109.00”; and

- (c) in item 5 (Accommodation for Residential Care Composite Form television licence) in column 3, at the end there shall be added—

|  |  | ; but (except in the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man) for the purposes of calculating the fee, no account shall be taken of any such unit of accommodation or, as the case may be, dwelling, that is the sole or main residence of a resident aged 75 years or more. |
| --- | --- | --- |

- (3) In Part III of that Schedule—

- (a) for “£33.50” there shall be substituted “£36.50”; and

- (b) for “£101.00” there shall be substituted “£109.00”.

###### 5

- (1) Schedule 3 shall be amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In item 1 in that Schedule (premium instalment television licence), for the words “(not in Jersey or Guernsey)” there shall be substituted the words “(not in the Channel Islands)”; and for “£27.25”, in each place where it occurs, there shall be substituted “£28.50”.

- (3) For items 2 and 3 in that Schedule (budget instalment and easy entry licences) there shall be substituted the items set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

###### 6

For Schedule 4 (interim etc. television licences)[^f00006] there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the amount of the basic fee for television licences from £34.50 to £36.50 in the case of black and white and from £104.00 to £109.00 in the case of colour. In the case of licences for hotels and hospitality areas and mobile units, where the fee is calculated by reference to “the relevant amount”, that amount is increased from £33.50 to £36.50 in the case of black and white and from £101.00 to £109.00 in the case of colour. In the case of instalment licences—

- (a) the issue fee and subsequent instalments for the premium instalment licence (which is not available in the Channel Islands) are increased from £27.25 to £28.50 with the total amount payable being £114.00;

- (b) amendments are made in relation to the budget instalment licence to provide for 16 specified issue fees and a number of instalments thereafter, but in each case the total amount payable is £109.00;

- (c) amendments are made in relation to the “Easy entry” licence (which is not available in the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man) to provide for a wider range of issue fees and instalments, but in each case the total amount payable is £109.00.

These Regulations also make provision to abolish the fee payable for the replacement of a lost or destroyed licence in a case where the lost or destroyed licence was issued or renewed free of charge. They also amend (except in the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man) the provision made for calculating the fee for an Accommodation for Residential Care Composite Form television licence, such that no account is taken of any unit of accommodation or dwelling that is the sole or main residence of a resident aged 75 years or more.
