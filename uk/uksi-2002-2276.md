# The South East London Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2002

Made: 4th September 2002

Laid before Parliament: 5th September 2002

Coming into force: 30th September 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 92(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to changes in the area and functions of the South East London Health Authority and having consulted that Health Authority and the Greenwich Primary Care Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the South East London Health Authority (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 30th September 2002.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the old Trust” means the South East London Health Authority[^f00002];

- “the new Trust” means the Greenwich Primary Care Trust[^f00003]; and

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the old Trust and the new Trust and signed on behalf of the old Trust on 3rd July 2002 and on behalf of the new Trust on 18th July 2002.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On 30th September 2002 the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00004], shall be transferred from the old Trust to the new Trust.

- (2) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new Trust and any reference to the old Trust in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 4th September 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Greenwich Primary Care Trust, trust property held by the South East London Health Authority and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
