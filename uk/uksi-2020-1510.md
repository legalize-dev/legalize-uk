# The Export Control (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020

Made: at 10.54 a.m. on 10th December 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 3.30 p.m. on 10th December 2020

Coming into force: at 10.59 p.m. on 31st December 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8C(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Export Control (Amendment) (EU Exit) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 and come into force at 10.59 p.m. on 31st December 2020.

#### Amendment of the Export Control (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020

###### 2

In the Export Control (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020[^f00002], in regulation 7(9), in the text of paragraph (28B) to be inserted in regulation 4 of the Export Control (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00003], after the text of article 42N(2)(a) contained in that paragraph, for “and”, substitute “or”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Ranil Jayawardena — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for International Trade — At 10.54 a.m. on 10th December 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which come into force at 10.59 p.m. on 31st December 2020, correct a drafting error in regulation 7(9) of the Export Control (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1502](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1502)) before that regulation comes into force.

These Regulations are being issued free of charge to all known recipients of the Export Control (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary, or public sector is foreseen.
