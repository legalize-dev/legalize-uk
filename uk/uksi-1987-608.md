# The Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1987

Made: 30th March 1987

Laid before Parliament: 9th April 1987

Coming into force: 4th May 1987

In pursuance of sections 20(3) and (8) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 4th May 1987.

- (2) This Order shall not extend to Scotland.

###### 2

Section 20(3) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00002] (which specifies the maximum permitted aggregate amount of winnings in respect of games of bingo played in one week simultaneously on different club premises) shall have effect as if for the reference to the sum of £3,000 there were substituted a reference to the sum of £3,500.

###### 3

Section 20(8) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00003] (which specifies the maximum amount by which weekly winnings on any particular bingo club premises may exceed the aggregate amount of the stakes hazarded) shall have effect as if for the reference to the sum of £1,250 there were substituted a reference to the sum of £1,500.

###### 4

Article 2 of the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1984[^f00004] and Article 2 of the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1985[^f00005] shall be omitted.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Home Office — 30th March 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases in England and Wales the monetary limits specified in section 20(3) and (8) of the Gaming Act 1968 and described in Articles 2 and 3.
