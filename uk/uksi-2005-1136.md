# The Gas (Standards of Performance) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 21st March 2005

The Gas and Electricity Markets Authority[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 33A, 33AA, 33AB, 33D and 47 of the Gas Act 1986[^f00002], after consultation with the Gas and Electricity Consumer Council[^f00003], gas suppliers and gas transporters and with persons and bodies appearing to be representatives of persons likely to be affected by the Regulations, after considering the results of research to discover the views of a representative sample of persons likely to be affected, and with the consent of the Secretary of State in accordance with section 33A(2) of the Gas Act 1986[^f00004], makes the following Regulations–

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gas (Standards of Performance) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the Principal Regulations” means the Gas (Standards of Performance) Regulations 2005[^f00005].

#### Amendment of the Principal Regulations

###### 2

Regulation 1 of the Principal Regulations shall be amended by substituting for the word “April”, in paragraph (1), the word “May”.

## Signed

The seal of the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority here affixed is authenticated by the signature of

David Gray — A member of the Authority — 17th March 2005

I Consent

Mike O'Brien — Minister of State for Industry and Energy — Department of Trade and Industry — 10th March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Gas (Standards of Performance) Regulations 2005 ([S.I.2005/1135](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1135)) (the Principal Regulations).

These Regulations change the date that the Principal Regulations come into force from 1st April 2005 to 1st May 2005 so as to ensure that the coming into force of the Principal Regulations coincides with the introduction of a new standard special licence condition governing standards of performance.
