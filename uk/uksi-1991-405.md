# The Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1991

Made: 27th February 1991

Laid before the House of Commons: 11th March 1991

Coming into force: 1st April 1991

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st April 1991.

#### Amendment

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1990[^f00002] (which specifies fees to be paid in respect of specified transactions) there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 20th February 1991.

John Selwyn Gummer — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Strathclyde — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 26th February 1991

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 27th February 1991

Thomas Sackville — Sydney Chapman — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 25th February 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Poultry Flocks, Hatcheries and Processed Animal Protein (Fees) Order 1990 by substituting a new Schedule for the Schedule to that Order (article 2). The new Schedule sets out increased fees from those specified in the Schedule for some of the transactions specified in it in respect of laying and breeding flock and hatchery premises and for the purposes of certain Orders relating to poultry and protein processing.
