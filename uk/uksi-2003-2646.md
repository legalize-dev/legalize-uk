# The Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003

Made: 10th October 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 12(2) of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003.

###### 2

Section 1 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act 2003 shall come into force on 13th October 2003.

## Signed

Paul Murphy — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 10th October 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 1 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission etc.) Act [2003(c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/25) on 13th October 2003.
