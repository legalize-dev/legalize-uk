# The Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made: 25th February 2003

Laid before Parliament: 26th February 2003

Coming into force: 27th February 2003

Therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 3 and 6 of the 1998 Act[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 27th February 2003.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In Part 1 of the Schedule to the 2002 Regulations the sector “GU24” shall be omitted from the second column in the row entitled “Southern”.

## Signed

Stephen Timms, — Minister of State for E-Commerce and Competitiveness, — Department of Trade and Industry — 25th February 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations remove a defect in the Schedule to the Wireless Telegraphy (Public Fixed Wireless Access Licences) Regulations 2002, namely the inclusion of “GU24” in the row entitled “Southern”.
