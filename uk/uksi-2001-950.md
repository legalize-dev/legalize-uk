# The Community Legal Service (Financial) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made: 9th March 2001

Laid before Parliament: 14th March 2001

Coming into force: 9th April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 7 and 10 of the Access to Justice Act 1999[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Legal Service (Financial) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 9th April 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations, a regulation referred to by number alone means the regulation so numbered in the Community Legal Service (Financial) Regulations 2000[^f00002].

#### Transitional provisions

###### 3

These Regulations shall apply to applications for funded services, and further assessments under regulation 15, made on or after 9th April 2001 and applications and further assessments made before that date shall be treated as if these Regulations had not been made.

#### Amendments to the Community Legal Service (Financial) Regulations 2000

###### 4

- (1) In regulation 5(2), “£87” shall be substituted for “£84”.

- (2) In regulation 5(3), “£186” shall be substituted for “£180”.

- (3) In regulation 5(5), “£186” shall be substituted for “£180”.

- (4) In regulation 5(6), “£8,196” shall be substituted for “£8,067”.

###### 5

- (1) In regulation 38(1), “£79” shall be substituted for “£76”.

- (2) In regulation 38(2)(a), “£2,767” shall be substituted for “£2,723”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Lock — Parliamentary Secretary, — Lord Chancellor’s Department — Dated 9th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Legal Services Commission (Financial) Regulations 2000 so as to increase the income limits for the purposes of determining eligibility for services provided by the Legal Services Commission as part of the Community Legal Service. This is in line with the relevant uprating of social security benefits.
