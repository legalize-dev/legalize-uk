# The M48 Motorway (Severn Bridge Half Marathon) (Temporary Prohibition of Traffic) Order 2023

Made: 18th August 2023

Coming into force: 25th August 2023

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16A(2)(a) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the M48 Motorway (Severn Bridge Half Marathon) (Temporary Prohibition of Traffic) Order 2023 and shall come into force on 25th August 2023.

###### 2

In this Order —

- “*the relevant lengths of the M48*” means (i) the westbound carriageway of the M48 over the Severn Bridge from the tip of the nosing of the westbound exit slip road at Junction 1 to the tip of the nosing of the westbound entry slip road at Junction 2; and (ii) the eastbound carriageway of the M48 over the Severn Bridge from the tip of the nosing of the eastbound exit slip road at Junction 2 to the tip of the nosing of the eastbound entry slip road at Junction 1;

- “the slip roads” mean (i) the eastbound entry slip road at M48 Junction 2; and (ii) the westbound entry slip road at M48 Junction 1;

- “*the footpath/cycle track*” means the footpath/cycle track adjacent to the M48 eastbound carriageway between Junctions 2 and 1;

- “*the tip of the nosing*” means the first point where an entry slip road joins the carriageway of a motorway or the last point where an exit slip road leaves the carriageway of a motorway;

- “*authorised traffic*” means the following traffic classes, as specified in Schedule 4 to the Highways Act 1980[^f00002]: (a) Class VII – pedal cycles; (b) Class IX – pedestrians, perambulators, push-chairs and other forms of baby carriages and dogs on leads; (c) Class X – motor cycles where the engine cylinder size is less than 50 cubic centimetres; and (d) Class XI – invalid carriages;

- “*event*” means the Severn Bridge Half Marathon; and

- “*the event period*” means a period of 8 hours starting at 06.00 hours on any Sunday between Sunday 27th August 2023 and Monday 25th September 2023.

###### 3

Subject to article 5, no person shall, during the event period, cause or permit any vehicle to enter or proceed on the relevant lengths of the M48 and the slip roads.

###### 4

Subject to article 5, no authorised traffic, as defined in article 2 above, shall enter or proceed in the footpath/cycle track during the event period.

###### 5

Articles 3 and 4 shall apply only during such times and to such extent as shall from time to time be indicated by traffic signs and shall not apply to—

- (a) a vehicle being used for police, traffic officer, fire and rescue authority or ambulance purposes;

- (b) anything done at the direction of, or with the permission of, a constable or traffic officer in uniform.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Rupesh Mehta — A Senior Civil Servant in the Department for Transport — 18th August 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order authorises the temporary prohibition of traffic on the M48 Motorway (Severn Bridge) on 27th August 2023 to facilitate the holding of the Severn Bridge Half Marathon.
