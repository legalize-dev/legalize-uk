# The Sevenoaks (Electoral Changes) Order 2016

Made: 24th August 2016

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Local Government Boundary Commission for England has decided to give effect to the recommendations and makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 92(3) of that Act:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Sevenoaks (Electoral Changes) Order 2016.

- (2) This Order comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on the day after the day on which it is made;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors[^f00003] in 2019.

#### Related alterations of district ward boundaries

###### 2

- (1) The area that ceases to be part of the parish of Chevening and becomes part of the parish of Sevenoaks Weald in consequence of the 2014 Order ceases to be part of the district ward of Brasted, Chevening & Sundridge and becomes part of the district ward of Seal & Weald.

- (2) In this article—

- “district ward” means a district ward established by article 2 of the Sevenoaks (Electoral Changes) Order 2001[^f00004]; and

- “the 2014 Order” means the Sevenoaks District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order [2014 No.2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/2)[^f00005].

## Signed

Sealed with the seal of the Local Government Boundary Commission for England

Jolyon Jackson — Chief Executive — Local Government Boundary Commission for England — 24th August 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to recommendations made by Sevenoaks District Council to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England for a related alterations of a district ward boundary. The alteration is related to parish boundary changes made by the Sevenoaks District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order [2014 No.2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/2).

The new district ward boundary applies in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2019.

Article 2 alters the district wards of (i) Brasted, Chevening and Sundridge and (ii) Seal and Weald.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
