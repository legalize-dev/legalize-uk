# The Housing (Right to Buy) (Limit on Discount) (England) (Amendment) Order 2014

Made: 15th July 2014

Laid before Parliament: 18th July 2014

Coming into force: 20th July 2014

The Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government makes the following Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 131 of the Housing Act 1985[^f00001]:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Housing (Right to Buy) (Limit on Discount) (England) (Amendment) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 20th July 2014.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to England only[^f00002].

#### Amendment

###### 2

For article 4 of the Housing (Right to Buy) (Limit on Discount) (England) Order 2014[^f00003], substitute—

> > (4) Subject to article 3(2)—
>   (a) article 3 of the Housing (Right to Buy) (Limit on Discount) (England) Order 2012; and
>   (b) the Housing (Right to Buy) (Limit on Discount) (England) Order 2013
> are revoked.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Brandon Lewis — Minister of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 15th July 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 amends the Housing (Right to Buy) (Limit on Discount) (England) Order 2014 ([S.I. 2014/1378](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1378)) (“the 2014 Order”) which revoked the Housing (Right to Buy) (Limit on Discount) (England) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/734](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/734)) (“the 2012 Order”) in error. Article 2 rectifies this error by amending the 2014 Order to provide for revocation of only article 3 of the 2012 Order.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
