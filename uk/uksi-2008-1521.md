# The Finance Act 2007, Section 17(2) (Corporation Tax Deduction for Expenditure on Energy-Saving Items) (Appointed Day) Order 2008

Made: 11th June 2008

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2) of the Finance Act 2007[^f00001], make the following Order.

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2007, Section 17(2) (Corporation Tax Deduction for Expenditure on Energy-Saving Items) (Appointed Day) Order 2008.

###### 2

The day appointed for the purposes of section 17(2) of the Finance Act 2007 is 8th July 2008.

## Signed

Claire Ward — Frank Roy — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 11th June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 8th July 2008 as the day on or after which the amendment made by section 17(1) of the Finance Act [2007 (c.11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/11) shall have effect in relation to expenditure incurred.

Section 17(1) inserts new sections into the Income and Corporation Taxes Act [1988 (c.1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/1) in relation to a corporation tax deduction for expenditure on energy-saving items.
