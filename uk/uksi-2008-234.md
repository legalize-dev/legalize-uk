# The A556 Trunk Road (Turnpike Wood, Over Tabley-A56 Bowdon Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1996 (Revocation) Order 2008

Made: 30th January 2008

Coming into force: 7th March 2008

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and all other enabling powers:-

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the A556 Trunk Road (Turnpike Wood, Over Tabley-A56 Bowdon Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1996 (Revocation) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 7th March 2008.

###### 2

The A556 Trunk Road (Turnpike Wood, Over Tabley-A56 Bowdon Roundabout) (Detrunking) Order 1996[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of theSecretary of State for Transport

John Bagley — Divisional Director Highways Agency — 30th January 2008
