# The Church of England Pensions (Sodor and Man) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made: 9th July 2011

Laid before Parliament: 19th July 2011

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2)

The General Synod, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of that Measure, approves the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These regulations may be cited as the Church of England Pensions (Sodor and Man) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

- (2) They come into force on the day immediately following the day on which they are approved by the General Synod.

#### Dean of cathedral of diocese of Sodor and Man

###### 2

In Schedule 1 to the Church of England Pensions Regulations 1988[^f00002], at the end of paragraph 2, insert “and in the table “*dean*” does not include the office of dean of the cathedral church of the diocese of Sodor and Man”.

## Signed

Approved by the General Synod on 9th July 2011

Colin Podmore — Clerk to the Synod

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Church of England Pensions Regulations 1988 (“the 1988 Regulations”) which contain provisions relating to the Church of England Pensions Scheme (“the Past Service Scheme”).

Regulation 2 amends the 1988 Regulations so that a scheme member who has held office as dean of the cathedral church of the diocese of Sodor and Man will not thereby be entitled to have a dean’s multiplier applied to the full basic pension for the purpose of calculating the rate of pension to which he or she is entitled under the Past Service Scheme.
