# The Waveney, Lower Yare, and Lothingland Internal Drainage Board Order 2006

Made: 9th June 2006

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

the abolition of the Blundeston Flixton and Oulton Drainage Board, the Burgh Castle Bradwell Gorleston Southtown and Cobholm Island Drainage Board, the Langley Chedgrave and Toft Monks Drainage Board, the Oulton Carlton Colville and Barnby Drainage Board, the Limpenhoe & Reedham Internal Drainage Board, the Lothingland Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Second Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Third Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Yare Second Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Yare Third Internal Drainage Board and the Waveney Valley Internal Drainage Board;

the amalgamation of their respective drainage districts; and

the constitution of a new internal drainage board.

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(5) and (7) of the 1991 Act and now vested in him[^f00002]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Waveney, Lower Yare, and Lothingland Internal Drainage Board Order 2006 and shall, if confirmed by the Secretary of State in accordance with paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 3 to the Land Drainage Act 1991, come into force in accordance with that paragraph.

## SCHEDULE 1 — SCHEME AS SUBMITTED BY THE ENVIRONMENT AGENCY

###### 1

This Scheme shall come into force one month after the date of confirmation of the Order confirming this Scheme.

###### 2

In this Scheme:–

- “the Abolished Boards” means the Boards abolished by virtue of paragraph 3(1) of this Scheme;

- “the Commencement Date” means the date on which the Scheme comes into force;

- “the New Board” means the board constituted by virtue of paragraph 3(3) of this Scheme;

- “the New District” means the district constituted by virtue of paragraph 3(2) of this Scheme;

- “Property” means any property vested in any of the Abolished Boards immediately before the Commencement Date and includes books of account, other books, deeds, maps, papers and other documents;

- “Rights and Obligations of the Abolished Boards” means all such rights, powers (including Statutory Powers) duties, obligations, and liabilities as immediately before the Commencement Date were vested in and fell to be discharged by any of the Abolished Boards;

- “Statutory Powers” includes any power arising under or by virtue of any local Act or Order made under Statutory Authority;

- “The Blundeston Flixton & Oulton Drainage Board” means the drainage board constituted by An Act to confirm a Provisional Order under the Land Drainage Act 1861 in the matter of a proposed drainage district in the Parishes of Blundeston Flixton and Oulton in the County of Suffolk (8 Edward 7 ch. 121) and “The Blundeston Flixton & Oulton Drainage District” means the drainage district constituted by that Act;

- “The Burgh Castle Bradwell Gorleston Southtown & Cobholm Island Drainage Board” means the drainage board constituted by An Act to confirm certain Provisional Orders under the Land Drainage Act 1861 (47+48 Victoria ch. 41) and “The Burgh Castle Bradwell Gorleston Southtown & Cobholm Island Drainage District” means the drainage district constituted by that Act;

- “The Langley Chedgrave & Toft Monks Drainage Board” means the drainage board constituted by An Act to confirm a Provisional Order under the Land Drainage Act 1861 relating to the Langley Chedgrave and Toft Monks Marshes in the Parishes of Langley Chedgrave Toft Monks Stockton Haddiscoe Loddon Raveningham and Reedham in the County of Norfolk (59+60 Victoria ch. 93) and “The Langley Chedgrave & Toft Monks Drainage District” means the drainage district constituted by that Act;

- “The Oulton Carlton Colville & Barnby Drainage Board” means the drainage board constituted by An Act to confirm a Provisional Order under the Land Drainage Act 1861 in the matter of the Oulton Carlton &c. Marshes in the Parishes of Oulton Carlton Colville and Barnby in the County of Suffolk (7 Edward 7 ch.52) and “The Oulton Carlton Colville & Barnby Drainage District” means the drainage district constituted by the scheme confirmed by that Act;

- “The Limpenhoe & Reedham Internal Drainage Board” means the internal drainage board constituted by the scheme confirmed by The Limpenhoe and Reedham Drainage Order 1926 (SR&O 1926 No. 1537) and “*The Limpenhoe and Reedham Internal Drainage District*” means the internal drainage district constituted by that Order;

- (1) The Blundeston Flixton & Oulton Drainage Board, the Burgh Castle Southtown & Cobholm Island Drainage Board, the Langley Chedgrave & Toft Monks Drainage Board, the Oulton Carlton Colville & Barnby Drainage Board, the Limpenhoe & Reedham Internal Drainage Board, the Lothingland Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Second Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Third Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Yare Second Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Yare Third Internal Drainage Board, and the Waveney Valley Internal Drainage Board shall be abolished;

- (2) The Blundeston Flixton & Oulton Drainage District, the Burgh Castle Southtown & Cobholm Island Drainage District, the Langley Chedgrave & Toft Monks Drainage District, the Oulton Carlton Colville & Barnby Drainage District, the Limpenhoe & Reedham Internal Drainage District, the Lothingland Internal Drainage District, the Lower Waveney Internal Drainage District, the Lower Waveney Second Internal Drainage District, the Lower Waveney Third Internal Drainage District, the Lower Yare Second Internal Drainage District, the Lower Yare Third Internal Drainage District, and the Waveney Valley Internal Drainage District shall be amalgamated into, and shall constitute, one internal drainage district which shall be known as “*the Waveney Lower Yare and Lothingland Internal Drainage District*”;

- (3) There shall be constituted for the New District an internal drainage board to be known as “*the Waveney Lower Yare and Lothingland Internal Drainage Board*”.

###### 4

The New Board shall consist of 18 elected members.

###### 5

From the Commencement Date there shall be transferred to and be vested in or fall to be discharged by the New Board all Rights and Obligations of the Abolished Boards, and Property.

###### 6

All arrears of rates levied by the Abolished Boards before the Commencement Date in respect of any period ending before the Commencement Date may be recovered by the New Board, in the same manner as if they had been rates levied by the New Board.

###### 7

As respects any thing transferred under this Scheme the Scheme shall operate as conclusive evidence of the transfer without the necessity of any further assignments, conveyance or deed of transfer whatsoever.

###### 8

The accounts of each of the Abolished Boards shall be made up to the Commencement Date by the New Board, and such accounts shall be audited in like manner and be subject to the like incidents and consequences as if this Scheme had not come into force.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order confirms (with modifications) a Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency for the abolition, on a date one month after the confirmation of this Order, of the Blundeston Flixton & Oulton Drainage Board, the Burgh Castle Bradwell Gorleston Southtown & Cobholm Island Drainage Board, the Langley Chedgrave & Toft Monks Drainage Board, the Oulton Carlton Colville & Barnby Drainage Board, the Limpenhoe & Reedham Internal Drainage Board, the Lothingland Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Second Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Waveney Third Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Yare Second Internal Drainage Board, the Lower Yare Third Internal Drainage Board and the Waveney Valley Internal Drainage Board.

A new board, known as “the Waveney, Lower Yare, and Lothingland Internal Drainage Board” replaces these boards. The twelve former drainage districts are amalgamated to form the corresponding new internal drainage district.

A map of the twelve internal drainage districts amalgamated by virtue of the Scheme is available for inspection during office hours at the offices of the Environment Agency at Kingfisher House, Goldhay Way, Orton Goldhay, Peterborough PE2 5ZR and Cobham Road, Ipswich, Suffolk IP3 9JE.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
