# The Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) Revocation Regulations 1993

Made: 3rd December 1993

Laid before Parliament: 6th December 1993

Coming into force: 10th January 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 36(1) and 37(1) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) Revocation Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 10th January 1994.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The following Regulations are hereby revoked:–

- (a) The Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) (No. 2) Regulations 1988[^f00002];

- (b) The Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) Amendment Regulations 1991[^f00003]; and

- (c) The Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) Amendment Regulations 1992[^f00004].

## Signed

Fraser of Carmyllie — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 3rd December 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations relate to the financial limits upon the cost of providing advice and assistance including assistance by way of representation (ABWOR), without the prior approval of the Scottish Legal Aid Board.

The Regulations revoke the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) (No. 2) Regulations 1988 (S.I. [1988/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/2014)), the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) Amendment Regulations 1991 (S.I. [1991/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/746)) and the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) (Prospective Cost) Amendment Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/528](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/528)).
