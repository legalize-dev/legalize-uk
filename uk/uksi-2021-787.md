# The Public Procurement (International Trade Agreements) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: 1st July 2021

Coming into force: 2nd July 2021

In accordance with paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 2 to that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — GENERAL

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Procurement (International Trade Agreements) (Amendment) Regulations 2021 and come into force the day after the day they are made.

- (2) The amendments made by these Regulations have the same extent as the provisions to which they relate.

## PART 2 — AMENDMENT OF UNITED KINGDOM SECONDARY LEGISLATION

#### Amendments to the Public Contracts Regulations 2015

###### 2

- (1) The Public Contracts Regulations 2015[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) After regulation 25A (conditions relating to the GPA), insert—

> > (25B)
>   (1) Where a relevant international trade agreement applies to a procurement, the contracting authority shall accord to the works, supplies, services and economic operators of the signatories to that agreement treatment no less favourable than the treatment accorded to the works, supplies, services and economic operators of the United Kingdom.
>   (2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), a relevant international trade agreement applies if the procurement is covered by an agreement listed in Schedule 4A.

- (3) After regulation 90A (duty owed to economic operators from GPA countries), insert—

> > (90B)
>   (1) The duty owed in accordance with regulation 89 is a duty owed also to an economic operator from a country in respect of which a relevant international trade agreement applies, but only where the agreement applies to the procurement concerned.
>   (2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), a relevant international trade agreement applies if the procurement is covered by an agreement listed in Schedule 4A.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to various United Kingdom and Scottish public procurement regulations for the purpose of implementing various international trade agreements to which the United Kingdom is a signatory.

The amendments made by these Regulations are “steady state amendments”, as that term is defined in the Public Procurement (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1319](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1319)) and the Public Procurement etc. (EU Exit) (Scotland) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 ([S.S.I. 2020/468](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2020/468)) (“the EU Exit SIs”). As such, in accordance with the transitional provisions in the EU Exit SIs, these amendments do not affect any procedure (as that term is defined in the Schedules to the EU Exit SIs) launched before but not yet finalised by IP completion day.
