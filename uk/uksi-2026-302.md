# The Employment Rights Act 2025 (Enforcement) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026

Made: 16th March 2026

Laid before Parliament: 17th March 2026

Coming into force: 7th April 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 154(1) and (2) and 156(3) of the Employment Rights Act 2025[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Rights Act 2025 (Enforcement) (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 7th April 2026.

- (2) Any amendment made by these Regulations has the same extent as the provision amended.

#### Amendments to the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (3) In regulation 29(4) (records), for “section 9 of the Act” substitute “sections 96 or 97 of the Employment Rights Act 2025”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations make amendments to secondary legislation which are consequential to changes made by Part 5 (Enforcement of Labour Market Legislation) of the Employment Rights Act [2025 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/36).

An impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen. The impact assessments for the Employment Rights Bill, introduced to Parliament on 10th October 2024, contain an assessment of the effect that the reforms to employment law and industrial relations law will have on the costs of business, the voluntary sector and the public sector. They are available at: https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/3737/publications. Printed copies can be obtained from the Department for Business and Trade, Old Admiralty Building, Admiralty Place, London SW1A 2DY.
