# The Borough of Blackpool (Electoral Changes) Order 1997

Made: 21st February 1997

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(1)

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17[^f00003] and 26 of the Local Government Act 1992, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Borough of Blackpool (Electoral Changes) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st May 1997 except that, for the purpose of all proceedings preliminary or relating to an election to be held on or after that date, it shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the borough” means the borough of Blackpool;

- “existing”, in relation to a ward, means the ward as it exists on the date this Order is made;

- any reference to the map is a reference to the map prepared by the Department of the Environment, marked “Map of the Borough of Blackpool (Electoral Changes) Order 1997”, and deposited in accordance with regulation 27 of the Local Government Changes for England Regulations 1994[^f00004].

#### Wards of Blackpool

###### 2

- (1) The existing wards of the borough[^f00005] shall be abolished.

- (2) The borough shall be divided into twenty-two wards bearing the names set out in the Schedule and each ward shall comprise the area designated on the map by reference to the name of the ward and demarcated by red lines.

- (3) Where a boundary is shown on the map as running along a road, railway line, footway, waterway or other similar geographical feature, it shall be treated as running along the centre line of the feature.

- (4) The number of councillors to be elected for each of the wards of the borough shall be two.

#### Elections of the council of Blackpool

###### 3

- (1) Elections of all councillors for wards of the borough shall be held simultaneously on the ordinary day of election of councillors in 1997, 2000 and 2003 and every fourth year after 2003[^f00006].

- (2) The term of office of any councillor so elected shall be, in the case of councillors elected in 1997 and 2000, three years and, in any other case, four years.

- (3) On the fourth day after any such elections—

- (a) the persons who were councillors immediately before those elections shall retire; and

- (b) the newly elected councillors shall come into office.

- (4) Paragraphs (2) to (5) of article 7 of the Lancashire (Boroughs of Blackburn and Blackpool) (Structural Change) Order 1996[^f00007] shall be deleted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to recommendations by the Local Government Commission for England for electoral changes in the borough of Blackpool. All of the existing wards of the borough are abolished and 22 new wards are created, having the names set out in the Schedule. Each new ward has two councillors. The areas of the new wards are demarcated on the map referred to in article 1(2). Prints of the map may be inspected at all reasonable times at the offices of the Blackpool Borough Council and at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment which, until May 1997, are located at 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3EB and, thereafter, at 2nd Floor, Ashdown House, 123 Victoria Street, London SW1E 6DE.

Article 3 makes provision for elections to the council of the borough in 1997, 2000, 2003 and every fourth year after 2003.
