# The Export of Goods (Control) (Bosnia-Herzegovina) (ECSC) Order 1993

Made: 9th March 1993

Coming into force: 10th March 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Bosnia-Herzegovina) (ECSC) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 10th March 1993.

- (2) Unless the context otherwise requires, any expression used in this Order shall have the meaning it bears in the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1992[^f00003].

#### Exportation of goods to Bosnia-Herzegovina

###### 2

All goods specified in Article 1 of Decision [92/470/ECSC](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/1992/470/oj) of the Representatives of the Governments of the Member States meeting within the Council[^f00004] other than those falling within Article 4 thereof are prohibited to be exported from the United Kingdom to any destination in Bosnia-Herzegovina.

#### Exceptions

###### 3

Nothing in Article 2 of this Order shall prohibit—

- (i) the exportation of any goods under the authority of a licence granted by the Secretary of State, provided that all conditions attaching to the said licence are complied with, or

- (ii) any exportation which is in accordance with an export authorisation issued by the competent authority of a Member State in conformity with the provisions of Decision [92/470/ECSC](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/1992/470/oj).

#### Modifications and revocation of licences

###### 4

Any licence granted by the Secretary of State in pursuance of Article 3 of this Order may be modified or revoked by him at any time.

## Signed

M. V. Coolican — An Assistant Secretary, — Department of Trade and Industry — 9th March 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prohibits the export to Bosnia-Herzegovina of goods covered by the ECSC Treaty, except under the authority of a licence granted by the Secretary of State or which is in accordance with an export authorisation issued by the competent authority of a Member State in conformity with Decision [92/470/ECSC](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/1992/470/oj), as amended. By virtue of that Decision a dual licensing system applies to exports to Bosnia-Herzegovina of ECSC goods which now require an export licence, the grant of which is conditional on a prior import licence in respect of the goods being obtained from the competent authorities in Bosnia-Herzegovina. The Order does not apply to the export of goods intended for essential humanitarian need, for activities related to the United Nations Protection Force, the Conference on Yugoslavia or the European Community Monitor Mission, or whose individual value is less than 1000 ECU.
