# The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2008

Made: 24th September 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53(1) of the Police and Justice Act 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation and Extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2008.

- (2) Article 2 of this Order extends to England, Wales and Northern Ireland.

- (3) Article 3 of this Order extends to Northern Ireland.

#### Commencement: England, Wales and Northern Ireland

###### 2

The following provisions of the Police and Justice Act 2006 shall come into force on 1st October 2008—

- (a) sections 35 to 38 (computer misuse)[^f00002];

- (b) section 52 (amendments and repeals) in so far as it relates to the provisions in sub-paragraph (c) and the entries in Schedule 15 in sub-paragraph (d);

- (c) paragraphs 2, 6, 17 to 24, 26, 27 and 29 of Schedule 14 (minor and consequential amendments)[^f00003]; and

- (d) in Part 4 of Schedule 15 (repeals and revocations) the entries relating to—

- (i) sections 11, 12, 14, 16 and 17 of the Computer Misuse Act 1990[^f00004];

- (ii) Schedule 4, paragraph 7 and Schedule 8, paragraph 347 to the Courts Act 2003[^f00005]; and

- (iii) Schedule 16, paragraph 7 to the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00006].

#### Commencement: Northern Ireland

###### 3

The following provisions of the Police and Justice Act 2006 shall come into force on 1st October 2008—

- (a) section 52 in so far as it relates to the provision in sub-paragraph (b); and

- (b) paragraph 28 of Schedule 14.

## Signed

Vernon Coaker — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 24th September 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force those provisions of the Police and Justice Act 2006 set out in articles 2 and 3 on 1st October 2008.
