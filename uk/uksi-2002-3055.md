# The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002

Made: 11th December 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 458(1) of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002.

###### 2

Part 1 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (sections 1 to 5, together with Schedule 1) shall come into force on 13th January 2003.

## Signed

Bob Ainsworth — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 11th December 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, on 13th January 2003, Part 1 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, including all of Schedule 1 to the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002.
