# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (South Yorkshire Cablevision Limited) (the Boroughs of Doncaster and Rotherham) Order 1990

Made: 6th December 1990

Laid before Parliament: 12th December 1990

Coming into force: 9th January 1991

Now therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (South Yorkshire Cablevision Limited) (the Boroughs of Doncaster and Rotherham) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 9th January 1991.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 6th December 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to South Yorkshire Cablevision Limited on 1st November 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the whole of the Boroughs of Doncaster and Rotherham. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 12th December 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, South Yorkshire Cablevision Limited will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its Doncaster and Rotherham public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4N 7JJ.
