# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (Very High) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: at 2.15 p.m. on 16th October 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 2.55 p.m. on 16th October 2020

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

In accordance with section 45R of that Act, the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (Very High) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

- (2) Regulation 3(7) comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of inserting Schedule 2A, and paragraph 1 of that Schedule, on 17th October 2020;

- (b) for all other purposes, on 19th October 2020.

- (3) All other provisions of these Regulations come into force on 17th October 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) Regulations 2020

###### 2

In the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) Regulations 2020[^f00003], in Schedule 2, omit “Lancashire” and the entries from “Blackpool Council” to “Wyre Borough Council” under it.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations subject a number of areas to the restrictions set out in the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (Very High) (England) Regulations 2020 (the “Very High Regulations”) and make corresponding amendments to remove those areas from the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) Regulations 2020. They also impose closures on additional businesses in specified areas, and make a number of other minor amendments to the Very High Regulations.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
