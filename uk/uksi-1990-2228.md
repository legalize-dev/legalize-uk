# The Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1990

Made: 8th November 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 17(2) of the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1990.

###### 2

The provisions of the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st December 1990.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st December 1990 the provisions of the Road Traffic (Driver Licensing and Information Systems) Act 1989 specified in the Schedule to the Order. The bringing into force of section 6 of the Act has the effect of requiring motor cyclists who have yet to pass a test of competence to drive a motor cycle to undergo compulsory training unless exempted by Regulations from having to do so. The Regulations implementing section 6 are the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (No 5) Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/2334](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2334)). Many of the other provisions brought into force by this Order concern compulsory training for motor cyclists.
