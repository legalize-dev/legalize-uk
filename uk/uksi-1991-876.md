# The Property Services Agency Supplies Trading Fund Order 1976 (Revocation) Order 1991

Made: 31st March 1991

Coming into force: 31st March 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on himby section 1(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf,with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order,a draft of which has been laid before the House of Commons and has beenapproved by a resolution of that House in accordance with section 6(2)of that Act:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Property Services Agency SuppliesTrading Fund Order 1976 (Revocation) Order 1991 and shall come intoforce on 31st March 1991.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Property Services Agency Supplies Trading Fund Order 1976[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Michael Heseltine — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 26th March 1991

We concur

Thomas Sackville — Sydney Chapman — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 31st March 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Property Services Agency Supplies Trading Fund Order 1976established a trading fund in respect of the Crown Suppliers (then knownas the Supplies Division of the Property Services Agency). This Orderrevokes the 1976 Order.
