# The Advanced Research and Invention Agency Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2023

Made: 20th January 2023

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13 of the Advanced Research and Invention Agency Act 2022[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Advanced Research and Invention Agency Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2023.

- (2) In these Regulations, “*the 2022 Act*” means the Advanced Research and Invention Agency Act 2022.

#### Provisions of the Act coming into force on 25th January 2023

###### 2

So far as not already in force, the provisions of the Advanced Research and Invention Agency Act 2022 come into force on 25th January 2023.

## Signed

Grant Shapps — Secretary of State — Department for Business Energy and Industrial Strategy — 20th January 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force the remaining provisions of the Advanced Research and Invention Agency Act 2022 (“*the Act*”) which did not come into force on the date the Act was passed.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
