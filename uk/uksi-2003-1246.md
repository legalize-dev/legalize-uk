## SCHEDULE — **Matters in relation to which the National Assembly for Wales is designated**

Measures in the veterinary field for the protection of public health.

The designation and review of sensitive and less sensitive areas in respect of the collection, treatment and discharge of urban waste water and the treatment and discharge of waste water from certain industrial sectors.

## Signed

A.K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council
