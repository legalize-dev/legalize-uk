# The Phoenix College (Dissolution) Order 2000

Made: 14th February 2000

Laid before Parliament: 16th February 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27 of the Act, the Secretary of State, after consulting the corporation and with the consent of the further education corporation known as Merton College, Morden[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Phoenix College (Dissolution) Order 2000, and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

###### 2

On 1st April 2000 the corporation shall be dissolved and all of its property, rights and liabilities shall be transferred to Merton College, Morden, being a body corporate established for purposes which include the provision of educational facilities or services.

###### 3

Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Act shall apply to any person employed by the corporation immediately before 1st April 2000 as if the references in that section—

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;

- (b) to the operative date were to 1st April 2000;

- (c) to the transferor were to the corporation; and

- (d) to the corporation were to Merton College, Morden.

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 14th February 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves with effect from 1st April 2000 the further education corporation established to conduct Phoenix College. It provides for the transfer of its property, rights and liabilities to Merton College, Morden, and secures the rights of its employees by applying section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
