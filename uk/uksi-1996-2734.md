# The St. James’s and Seacroft University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made: 28th October 1996

Laid before Parliament: 4th November 1996

Coming into force: 25th November 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities previously managed by the former Leeds Health Authority[^f00002] resulting from the establishment of the St. James’s and Seacroft University Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00003], having consulted the Leeds Health Authority[^f00004] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the St. James’s and Seacroft University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 25th November 1996.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means the Leeds Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the St. James’s and Seacroft University Hospitals National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 17th July 1996, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 25th November 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Horam — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — 28th October 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the St. James’s and Seacroft University Hospitals National Health Service Trust on 25th November 1996 trust property held by the Leeds Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, RM IN33F, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
