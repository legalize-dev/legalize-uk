# The Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2021

Made: 21st July 2021

Coming into force: 1st August 2021

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 21st day of July 2021

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 19(1) of the Education Act 2005 is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2021 and comes into force on 1st August 2021.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty's Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru

###### 2

Claire Morgan is appointed as Her Majesty's Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru in accordance with articles 3 and 4.

###### 3

The first day of the appointment is 1st August 2021.

###### 4

The last day of the appointment is 31st December 2021.

#### Revocation

###### 5

The Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2020 is revoked .

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
