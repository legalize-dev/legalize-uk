# The London-Holyhead Trunk Road (Gwalchmai By-Pass) (Revocation) Order 1994

Made: 18th January 1994

Coming into force: 8th February 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers:—

###### 1

The London-Holyhead Trunk Road (Gwalchmai By-Pass) Order 1978[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

###### 2

This Order shall come into force on 8th February and may be cited as the London-Holyhead Trunk Road (Gwalchmai By-Pass) (Revocation) Order 1994.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

P. R. Marsden — A Grade 6 Officer Welsh Office — Dated 18th January 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes an Order made under section 7 of the Highways Act 1959, which established the line for the route of the proposed Gwalchmai By-pass.
