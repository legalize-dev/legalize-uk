# The A1400 Trunk Road (Redbridge) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1998

Made: 12th January 1998

Coming into force: 26th January 1998

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A1400 Trunk Road (Redbridge) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1998, and shall come into force on 26th January 1998.

#### Amendments

###### 2

The A1400 Trunk Road (Redbridge) Red Route Traffic Order 1996[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In the table in Schedule 3A item 2 is deleted.

###### 4

In the table in Schedule 2B a new item is added as follows—

| (1) | (2) | (3) | (4) | (5) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Item | Length of Road | Restricted Hours | Maximum Period of Parking | Minimum Interval |
| 1. | Woodford Avenue -south-west side, between a point 32.0 metres south-east of the common boundary of Nos. 41 and 43 Woodford Avenue and a point opposite the north-western boundary of No. 35 Woodford Avenue. | 7am-7pm Monday-Saturday Inclusive | 1 hour | 2 hours |

###### 5

In the table in Schedule 4 item 2 is amended as follows—

> > Between its junction with the Eastern Avenue (A12) (Gants Hill Roundabout) and a point 2.0 metres south-east of the common boundary of Nos. 25 and 27 Woodford Avenue.

###### 6

In the table in Schedule 4 item 3 is amended as follows—

> > Between a point 1.0 metres north-west of the commmon boundary of Nos. 33 and 35 Woodford Avenue and a point opposite the north-western boundary of No. 35 Woodford Avenue.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 12th January 1998
