# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2000

Made: 23rd March 2000

Laid before Parliament: 27th March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act[^f00003] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

#### Application

###### 2

This Order applies to school teachers (as defined in section 5(1) and (2) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^f00004]) in England and Wales.

#### School Teachers' Pay and Conditions

###### 3

The School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 1999[^f00005] shall be amended with effect from 1st April 2000 as follows.

###### 4

There shall be inserted in the appropriate position in paragraph 1.2 the following definition—

> > - “experience point” means a point awarded under paragraph 18.2 to a classroom teacher in respect of experience;

###### 5

> > (4.1) From 1st April 2000 a head teacher shall be paid such salary based on pay spine A set out in paragraph 4.4 as the relevant body shall determine in accordance with paragraph 5.4 or 6.
> (4.2) From 1st April 2000 a deputy head teacher shall be paid such salary based on pay spine B set out in paragraph 4.4 as the relevant body shall determine in accordance with paragraph 12.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 1999 by uprating the amounts of salary and certain allowances. The following are increased—

- (a) the head and deputy head teachers' pay spine and ranges set out in paragraphs 4, 7 and 12 of the Document;

- (b) the classroom teachers' pay spine set out in paragraph 17 of the Document;

- (c) the advanced skills teachers' pay spine set out in paragraph 23 of the Document;

- (d) the unqualified teachers' pay scale set out in paragraph 24 of the Document; and

- (e) the London Area allowances set out in paragraph 29 of the Document.

The increases are from 1st April 2000.

The Order revises the provisions concerning the award of experience points to classroom teachers in paragraph 18 of the Document with effect from 1st April 2000. The revision does not affect classroom teachers appointed to their first posts on or after 1st September 1999 or classroom teachers who were not entitled to any experience points on 1st September 1999. Paragraph 17 of the Document provides for the experience points of affected teachers to be re-calculated in accordance with the revisions on or after 1st April 2000. It makes consequential provision for those teachers whose experience point total changes following the re-calculation. The Order also makes consequential revisions to paragraphs 4, 21, 23 and 32 of the Document.

The Document is published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office and is obtainable from book shops of The Stationery Office Limited and through booksellers.
