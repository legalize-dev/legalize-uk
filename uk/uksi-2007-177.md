# The M62 Motorway (Junction 6 Improvements) (Connecting Roads) Scheme 2007

Made: 25th January 2007

Coming into force: 9th February 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by sections 16, 17 and 19 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

This scheme may be cited as the M62 Motorway (Junction 6 Improvements) (Connecting Roads) Scheme 2007 and shall come into force on 9th February 2007.

###### 2

In this Scheme:

###### 3

The Secretary of State is authorised to provide the special roads for the exclusive use of traffic of Classes I and II of the classes of traffic set out in Schedule 4 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 4

The centre line of each of the special roads is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan.

###### 5

Each of the special roads shall become a trunk road on the date when this Scheme comes into force.

## THE SCHEDULE — ROUTES OF THE CONNECTING ROADS

The routes of the connecting roads are as follows:

###### 1

A route to connect the westbound carriageway of the M62 Motorway to the northbound carriageway of the M57 Motorway. The connecting road along this route being given the reference letter A on the deposited plan.

###### 2

A route to connect the southbound carriageway of the M57 to the eastbound carriageway of the M62 Motorway. The connecting road along this route being given the reference letter B on the deposited plan.

###### 3

A route to connect the Tarbock roundabout with the M62 westbound carriageway. The connecting road along this route being given the reference letter C on the deposited plan.
