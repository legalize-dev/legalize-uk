# The M11 Motorway (Thremhall Avenue Connecting Roads) Scheme 2004

Made: 20th February 2004

Coming into force: 15th March 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by sections 16, 17 and 19 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

This scheme may be cited as the M11 Motorway (Thremhall Avenue Connecting Roads) Scheme 2004 and shall come into force on 15th March 2004.

###### 2

In this Scheme:

- (1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway:

- (2)

- (i) “connecting road” means a special road along a route described in the Schedule to this Scheme;

- (ii) “the northbound slip road” and “the southbound slip road” means respectively the special roads numbered C and D described in the Schedule to The M11 Motorway (Junction 8) Connecting Roads Scheme 2001[^f00003];

- (iii) “the plan” means the plan numbered HA16/MP/006, marked “The M11 Motorway (Thremhall Avenue Connecting Roads) Scheme 2004”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at ODPM-DfT Records Management Branch, Floor 13 (IMD), Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA; and

- (iv) “the special roads” means each connecting road.

###### 3

The Secretary of State is authorised to provide the special roads for the exclusive use of traffic of Classes I and II of the classes of traffic set out in Schedule 4 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 4

The centre line of each of the special roads is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan.

###### 5

Each of the special roads shall become a trunk road on the date when this Scheme comes into force.

## SCHEDULE

The routes of the connecting roads are:

- A route to connect the eastbound carriageway of Thremhall Avenue to the northbound slip road, from a point 240 metres east of Priory Wood Roundabout for 1,025 metres in a north-westerly direction. The centre line of this connecting road is shown on the plan and is given the reference letter A.

- A route to connect the westbound carriageway of Thremhall Avenue to the southbound slip road, from a point on Thremhall Avenue 240 metres east of Priory Wood Roundabout for 945 metres in a north-westerly direction. The centre line of this connecting road is shown on the plan and is given the reference letter B.

- A route to connect road A described above to Priory Wood Roundabout, from Priory Wood Roundabout for 575 metres in a north-westerly direction. The centre line of this connecting road is shown on the plan and is given the reference letter C.

- A route to connect Priory Wood Roundabout to road B described above, from Priory Wood Roundabout for 525 metres in a north-westerly direction. The centre line of this connecting road is shown on the plan and is given the reference letter D.
