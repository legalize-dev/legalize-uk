# The National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) Amendment Regulations 1990

Made: 7th March 1990

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 1990

Coming into force: 1st April 1990

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 126(1) and 128(1) of, and by paragraph 2A of Schedule 12 to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) Amendment Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) Regulations 1989[^f00002].

#### Amendment of regulation 20 of the principal Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 20 of the principal Regulations (redemption value of voucher for replacement or repair) —

- (a) in paragraph (1)(b) for “£26.50” there is substituted “£28.85”; and

- (b) in paragraph (3) for “£7.40” there is substituted “£7.70”.

###### 3

- (4) For Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations (voucher values — repair) there is substituted the Schedule 3 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Application of Regulations

###### 4

The amendments made by regulations 2 and 3(2), (3) and (4) of, and the Schedule to, these Regulations apply only in relation to a voucher—

- (a) accepted or used on or after 1st April 1990 pursuant to regulation 12 or regulation 18 of the principal Regulations; or

- (b) sent to a Health Authority pursuant to regulation 14(2)(c) of the principal Regulations in respect of cost incurred on or after that date.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) Regulations 1989 (“the principal Regulations”) which provide for payments to be made, by means of a voucher system, in respect of costs incurred by certain categories of persons in connection with the supply and replacement and repair of optical appliances.

Regulation 3(1)(a) and (c) alters the specification of appliances in respect of which vouchers may be used by redefining the upper limits of the spherical powers and cylindrical powers of appliances to which vouchers with letter codes A and D apply. Regulation 3(1)(b) and (d) clarifies the specification of appliances to which vouchers with letter codes C and F apply. These Regulations also amend Schedules 1 and 3 to the principal Regulations to increase by an average of 4.7 per cent. the value of vouchers issued in respect of the cost of the supply and replacement of optical appliances (regulation 3(2)) and of the repair and replacement of appliances (regulation 3(4) and the Schedule). Regulation 2 makes consequential amendments to regulation 20 of the principal Regulations (redemption value of voucher for replacement or repair). Regulation 3(3) increases the additional values for vouchers in respect of prisms, tints, photochromic lenses and special categories of appliances.
