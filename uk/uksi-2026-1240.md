# The Civil and Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) and Prohibition of Cross-Examination in Person (Fees of Court-Appointed Qualified Legal Representatives) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 24th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 26th November 2026

Coming into force: 17th December 2026

The Lord Chancellor makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(3) and 41(1)(a) and (c) and (3)(c) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^f00001], section 31X of the Matrimonial and Family Proceedings Act 1984[^f00002] and section 85L of the Courts Act 2003[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Civil and Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) and Prohibition of Cross-Examination in Person (Fees of Court-Appointed Qualified Legal Representatives) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 17th December 2026.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendments to the Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013

###### 2

- (1) The Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) in Table 1, for “injunctions under Part 1” substitute “orders and injunctions under Parts A1 and 1” in both places it occurs, and

- (b) for the heading of Table 10(a), for “injunctions under Part 1” substitute “orders and injunctions under Parts A1 and 1”.

- (3) In Schedule 5—

- (a) omit paragraph 5, and

- (b) renumber paragraph 6 as paragraph 5.

#### Amendments to the Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013

###### 3

- (1) Schedule 6 to the Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013[^f00005] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Part 2, in Table 4—

- (a) for the heading, substitute “Travelling and waiting”, and

- (b) omit the final row.

- (3) In Part 3, in Table 4—

- (a) for the heading, substitute “Travelling and waiting”, and

- (b) omit the final row.

- (4) In Part 4, in Table 4—

- (a) for the heading, substitute “Travelling and waiting”, and

- (b) omit the final row.

#### Amendments to the Prohibition of Cross-Examination in Person (Fees of Court-Appointed Qualified Legal Representatives) Regulations 2022

###### 4

In Table 6 (applicable maximum rates) in the Schedule to the Prohibition of Cross-Examination in Person (Fees of Court-Appointed Qualified Legal Representatives) Regulations 2022[^f00006] in the second column, for “£0.45” substitute “£0.55”.

#### Application

###### 5

- (1) In this regulation—

- “*relevant date*” means the date on which these Regulations come into force, and

- “*respect order*” means an order under section A1 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014[^f00007].

- (2) The amendments made by these Regulations apply to remuneration for civil legal services in relation to a respect order where a claim for payment is made on or after the relevant date.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/422](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/422)), the Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/435](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/435)) and the Prohibition of Cross-Examination in Person (Fees of Court-Appointed Qualified Legal Representatives) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/567](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/567)) to amend provisions relating to the claimable standard mileage rate when using a private vehicle for business purposes. It also amends the Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013 to ensure that services in relation to respect orders introduced by the Crime and Policing Act [2026 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/20) are paid the same fees and at the same rates as those payable in relation to anti-social behaviour injunctions.

Regulation 2 updates the Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013 by amending Schedule 1 to insert reference to Part A1 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 to ensure that for remuneration purposes, respect orders are treated the same way as anti-social behaviour injunctions. It further amends Schedule 5 to omit the maximum amount that the Lord Chancellor may pay as a disbursement in respect of an expert's vehicle mileage.

Regulation 3 amends Schedule 6 to the Criminal Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013 to omit reference to the claimable mileage rate in the regulations.

Regulation 4 amends the Prohibition of Cross-Examination in Person (Fees of Court-Appointed Qualified Legal Representatives) Regulations 2022 to provide for the increased applicable maximum mileage rate of £0.55.

Regulation 5 provides that, where a claim for payment is made on or after the date these Regulations come into force, payment is made in accordance with the relevant fees as amended by these Regulations.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
