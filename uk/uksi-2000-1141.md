# The A316 Trunk Road (Richmond) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 2000

Made: 19th April 2000

Coming into force: 10th May 2000

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A316 Trunk Road (Richmond) Red Route Traffic Order 1997 Variation Order 2000 and shall come into force on 10th May 2000.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A316 Trunk Road (Richmond) Red Route Traffic Order 1997[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In schedule 2A items 9 and 10 are deleted.

###### 4

In schedule 2B items 9 and 10 are substituted as follows:

| 9. | Between a point 14 metres south-west of the north-eastern wall of 57 Bicester Road and a point 23 metres south-west of that point. | At Any time | None | None |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10. | Between a point opposite the party wall of 263 and 265 Lower Richmond Road and a point 22 metres north-east of that point. |  |  |  |

###### 5

In schedule 3B item 7 is substituted as follows:

| 7. | Between a point 4 metres south-west of the north-eastern wall of 57 Bicester Road and a point 10 metres south-west of that point. | 7am-7pm Monday-Saturday Inclusive |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 19th April 2000
