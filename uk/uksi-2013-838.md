# The Mid Staffordshire NHS Foundation Trust (Appointment of Trust Special Administrators) Order 2013

Made: 11th April 2013

Laid before Parliament: 15th April 2013

Coming into force: 16th April 2013

Monitor, being satisfied that Mid Staffordshire NHS Foundation Trust is, or is likely to become, unable to pay its debts, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 65D(2) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], and following completion of the consultation required under section 65D(4) of the National Health Service Act 2006, makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Mid Staffordshire NHS Foundation Trust (Appointment of Trust Special Administrators) Order 2013 and comes into force on 16th April 2013.

- (2) In this Order, “the trust” means the Mid Staffordshire NHS Foundation Trust[^f00002].

#### Appointment of trust special administrators

###### 2

- (1) Three trust special administrators are authorised to be appointed to exercise the functions of the governors, chairman and directors of the trust.

- (2) The appointment of the trust special administrators appointed to the trust pursuant to paragraph (1) takes effect on 16th April 2013.

## Signed

Signed by authority of Monitor

David Bennett — Chief Executive — Monitor — 11th April 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order authorises the appointment of three trust special administrators to exercise the functions of the chairman, directors and governors of Mid Staffordshire NHS Foundation Trust. It also makes provision for the date on which the appointment of the trust special administrators is to take effect. Monitor will appoint the trust special administrators with effect from 16th April 2013 and will publish the name of the persons so appointed.
