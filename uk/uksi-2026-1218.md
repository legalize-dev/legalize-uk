# The Individual Savings Account (Amendment and Revocation) Regulations 2026

Made: 18th November 2026

Laid before the House of Commons: 19th November 2026

Coming into force: 6th April 2027

The Treasury make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 151(1) and (2) of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992[^f00001] and sections 694, 695, 695A, 696, 699(2) and 701 of the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and effect

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Individual Savings Account (Amendment and Revocation) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 6th April 2027.

- (2) Regulations 2 to 10 have effect in relation to the tax year 2028-29 and subsequent tax years.

- (3) Regulation 11 has effect in relation to the tax year 2027-28 and subsequent tax years.

#### Amendment of the Individual Savings Account Regulations 1998

###### 2

The Individual Savings Account Regulations 1998[^f00003] are amended as follows.

#### Amendment of regulation 2

###### 3

In paragraph (1)(a) of regulation 2 (interpretation)[^f00004], at the appropriate places insert—

> > - “*reporting period*” means any period beginning with the 6th day of a month and ending with the 5th day of the month immediately following;
> - “*subscription event*” has the meaning given in regulation 31A(5);

#### Amendment of regulation 4A

###### 4

- (1) In paragraph (5) of regulation 4A (repair of certain incompatible account and excess subscriptions - accounts other than junior ISA accounts)[^f00005] omit “within 30 days”.

- (2) After paragraph (5) insert—

> > (5A) A notice of discovery given by an officer of the Board[^f00006] under this regulation must be given to the person who was the account manager of the account on the date of the last subscription event which was reported to the Board.
> (5B) The account manager must comply with any directions in the notice of discovery given under paragraph (5) no later than the end of the reporting period immediately following the reporting period in which the notice of discovery was received.

#### Amendment of regulation 14

###### 5

In paragraph (2)(a) of regulation 14 (account manager - qualifications and Board’s approval) for “a form prescribed by the Board” substitute “such manner and form as the Board may direct”.

#### Amendment of regulation 21

###### 6

In regulation 21 (transfers relating to accounts other than junior ISA accounts)[^f00007]—

- (a) in paragraph (6), in sub-paragraph (b), in paragraph (iv) for “first” substitute “last”, and

- (b) omit paragraph (6ZA).

#### Amendment of regulation 21A

###### 7

In regulation 21A (further requirements relating to transfers between cash accounts)[^f00008]—

- (a) in paragraph (3), in sub-paragraph (b), for paragraph (iii) substitute—

> > (iii) in relation to the transfer of an account that is not a junior ISA account, the date on which the last subscription was made to the account in the year in which the transfer takes place,

- (b) omit paragraph (3ZA).

#### Substitution of regulation 26

###### 8

For regulation 26 (repayments in respect of tax to account manager - annual returns and annual claims)[^f00009] substitute—

> > (26)
>   (1) An account manager may make a claim for repayment in respect of a tax year (an “annual claim”) in accordance with this regulation.
>   (2) An annual claim may not be made more than 6 years after the end of the tax year to which the annual claim relates.
>   (3) An account manager must make a return (an “annual return”) specifying the amount of all income and any gains treated as arising in accordance with regulation 36(3)(a) or (b).
>   (4) No annual claim may be made unless the account manager has made an annual return in relation to that year in accordance with this regulation.
>   (5) An annual return must be provided, in such manner and form as the Board may direct, before the expiry of the period of 60 days beginning with 25th April in the year following the year to which the annual return relates.
>   (6) Where no income or gains are treated as arising in a year, the account manager must provide a return stating “nil” for that year.
>   (7) Where the aggregate of the repayments in respect of interim claims for the year shown by an annual claim exceeds the amount repayable for the year shown on the claim, the account manager must repay the amount of the excess to the Board with the claim.
>   (8) If an account manager fails to make the annual return required under this regulation within the period specified in paragraph (5), the Board may issue a notice to the account manager showing the aggregate of payments in respect of interim claims for the year and stating that the Board is not satisfied that the amount due to the account manager for that year exceeds the lower amount stated in the notice.
>   (9) If an annual return is not delivered to the Board within the period of 14 days beginning with the date on which the notice mentioned in paragraph (8) was issued, the amount of the difference between the aggregate and the lower amount stated in the notice will be immediately recoverable by the Board in the same manner as tax charged by an assessment on the account manager which has become final and conclusive.
>   (10) Where an annual return or an annual claim has been made and the account manager subsequently discovers that an error or mistake has been made, the account manager may make a supplementary return or claim within the period specified in paragraph (2).

#### Amendment of regulation 31

###### 9

- (1) In paragraph (1) of regulation 31 (returns of information by account manager)[^f00010], omit “, signed by the account manager or on his behalf”.

- (2) After paragraph (1B) of regulation 31 insert—

> > (1BA) An account manager of a Lifetime ISA must include in a return to the Board the date on which the last subscription to the Lifetime ISA was made.

- (3) In paragraph (3) of regulation 31—

- (a) in the words before sub-paragraph (a) omit “other than accounts transferred to another account manager under regulation 21(2) in that year or part of a year,”;

- (b) in sub-paragraph (a)—

- (i) after paragraph (iii) omit “and”;

- (ii) after paragraph (iv) insert—

> > (v) if the account investor is not eligible for a national insurance number, confirmation of the account investor’s non-eligibility, and
> (vi) confirmation of whether, to the best of the account manager’s knowledge, the account investor is deceased;

- (c) in sub-paragraph (b)—

- (i) omit the “and” after paragraph (i);

- (ii) after paragraph (ii) insert—

> > (iii) the date on which the last subscription was made to the account, in the year or the part of the year for which the return is made, and

- (d) in paragraph (iia) of sub-paragraph (c) omit “(except for a replacement subscription)”.

#### Insertion of regulations 31A, 31B and 31C

###### 10

After regulation 31, insert—

> > (31A)
>   (1) An account manager must provide the Board with a report (“*the report*”), in such a manner as the Board may direct, of all subscription events which occur in a reporting period in respect of accounts of which the account manager acts as an account manager.
>   (2) The particulars which must be reported are specified in regulation 31B.
>   (3) But the following do not need to be reported—
>     (a) a subscription event which does not result in a change to the current year’s subscription to an account;
>     (b) where the account investor is not eligible for a national insurance number, the amount of current year’s subscriptions to the account.
>   (4) The account manager must provide the report within the period of 14 days beginning with the day on which the reporting period ended.
>   (5) In these Regulations, a “subscription event” is—
>     (a) a subscription to an account other than a junior ISA account;
>     (b) a withdrawal of the current year’s subscription from a flexible account (unless that subscription is a subscription to which regulation 4ZA(1) or 4ZB[^f00011] applies);
>     (c) a repair under regulation 4A or 4AA[^f00012] which decreases the amount of the current year’s subscription to the account;
>     (d) any action under regulation 4A, 4AA or 21C[^f00013] which changes the amount specified in regulation 31(3)(c)(ii)[^f00014] in relation to an account;
>     (e) any action taken by the account manager to amend, in relation to an account, a previously-reported amount specified in regulation 31(3)(c)(ii).
> (31B)
>   (1) A report provided under regulation 31A must—
>     (a) be accompanied by a certificate in a form directed by the Board declaring that all the information given in the report is a complete record of subscription events in the relevant period in relation to each account, to the best of the account manager’s knowledge and belief, and
>     (b) in relation to each account in respect of which the account manager acted as account manager in the period for which the report is made, the information specified in paragraphs (2) - (8).
>   (2) The information specified in this paragraph is the account investor’s—
>     (a) full name,
>     (b) date of birth, and
>     (c) national insurance number (if the account investor is eligible for one).
>   (3) The information specified in this paragraph is—
>     (a) the number allocated to the account by the account manager;
>     (b) whether the account is a cash account, a stocks and shares account, an innovative finance account or a Lifetime ISA;
>     (c) whether the account is a flexible account;
>     (d) the cumulative total of amounts subscribed to the account in each reporting period since the date of the first report provided to the Board in respect of the account under regulation 31A.
>   (4) The information specified in this paragraph is, in relation to an innovative finance account comprising investments of the kind specified in regulation 8A(2)(a), (ca) or (cb) (qualifying investments for an innovative finance component)[^f00015], the aggregate of the amounts set out at sub-paragraphs (a) to (c)—
>     (a) for investments of the kind specified in regulation 8A(2)(a) or (ca), the outstanding principal balance as at the day on which the reporting period ended, or the date on which the account manager ceased to act or qualify as an account manager, under those qualifying investments for an innovative finance component in respect of which payments are due to be made;
>     (b) for investments of the kind specified in regulation 8A(2)(cb), their market value determined either as at the day on which the reporting period ended, or any other valuation date in that year, not falling earlier than 5th April; and
>     (c) the amount of cash held in the account as at the day on which the reporting period ended, or the date on which an account manager ceased to act or qualify as an account manager.
>   (5) The information specified in this paragraph is the date on which the last subscription event occurred in relation to the account in the period for which the report is made.
>   (6) The information specified in this paragraph is the reason for the account’s closure, where the account has been closed.
>   (7) In the case of a flexible account, if (disregarding a subscription made in accordance with regulation 5D (subscriptions disregarded for the purposes of the subscription limits in regulations 4ZA(1) and 4ZB) [^f00016] the amount of any cash withdrawn from the account exceeds the total amount subscribed to it, the amount to be reported is nil.
>   (8) In the case of a subscription event specified in regulation 31A(5)(b), the amount to be reported is nil.
> (31C)
>   (1) An account manager who is the transferor for the purposes of regulation 21 (transfers relating to accounts other than junior ISA accounts) (“*the transferor*”)[^f00017] must, no later than the end of the reporting period in which the account is transferred, provide a return to the Board containing the particulars specified in paragraph (2).
>   (2) The specified particulars are—
>     (a) the date of the last subscription event relating to the account transferred,
>     (b) in relation to each account transferred, the amount of subscriptions to the account in the year in which the account was transferred, and
>     (c) the total amount of current year’s subscriptions transferred.
>   (3) The transferee must, no later than the end of the reporting period following the reporting period in which the account is transferred, provide a return to the Board containing the particulars specified in paragraph (4).
>   (4) The specified particulars are—
>     (a) the date of the last subscription event relating to the transferred account;
>     (b) in relation to each account transferred, the amount of current year subscriptions in the transferred account; and
>     (c) the total amount of current year subscriptions transferred.
>   (5) The returns mentioned in this regulation must be provided in such form and manner as the Board may direct.

#### Amendment of the Individual Savings Account (Amendment) Regulations 2025

- (2) In paragraph (2) of regulation 1 (citation, commencement and effect), for “6th April 2027” substitute “6th April 2028” and for “tax year 2027-28” substitute “tax year 2028-29”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Individual Savings Accounts Regulations 1998 (“*the principal Regulations*”) and the Individual Savings Account (Amendment) Regulations 2025, and revoke the Individual Savings Account (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026.

Regulation 3 amends regulation 2 of the principal Regulations to provide definitions of “reporting period” and “subscription event”.

Regulation 4 amends regulation 4A of the principal Regulations to require repair of accounts which are not junior ISA accounts to take place by the end of the reporting period immediately following the reporting period in which the notice of discovery relating to such repairs is received.

Regulation 5 amends regulation 14 of the principal Regulations to allow the Commissioners for His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs (“*the Commissioners*”) to direct the manner and form of an application to be an account manager.

Regulation 6 amends regulation 21 of the principal Regulations as to the information to be provided by the transferor of an account that is not a junior ISA account.

Regulation 7 amends regulation 21A of the principal Regulations as to the information to be provided by the transferor where there is a transfer between cash accounts.

Regulation 8 substitutes regulation 26 of the principal Regulations which makes provision about annual returns and annual claims.

Regulation 9 amends regulation 31 of the principal Regulations to make changes to the requirements relating to the returns of information provided to the Commissioners.

Regulation 10 inserts new regulations 31A, 31B and 31C into the principal Regulations. Regulations 31A and 31B make provision as to subscription events and impose a requirement on account managers to report such events to the Commissioners. Regulation 31C imposes requirements on account managers to make returns to the Commissioners as to accounts other than junior ISA accounts which are transferred in accordance with regulation 21 of the principal Regulations.

Regulation 11 amends the coming into force date of regulation 4 of the Individual Savings Account (Amendment) Regulations 2025, from 6th April 2027 to 6th April 2028.

Regulation 12 revokes the Individual Savings Account (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026.

A Tax Information and Impact Note covering the instrument will be published on the HM Government website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.
