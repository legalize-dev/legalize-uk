# The Newham Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made: 21st June 1999

Coming into force: 1st July 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Newham Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st July 1999.

#### Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 2

For article 3 of the Newham Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1995[^f00005] there shall be substituted the following:–

> > (3)
>   (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1)(b) of the Act.
>   (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide and manage Trust Headquarters, Sydenham Buildings, Plaistow Hospital, Samson Street, London E13 9EH and the Mental Health Unit, Newham General Hospital site, Glen Road, Plaistow, London E13 8SL, and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services, and there and from there to provide and manage community health services.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

John Denham — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 21st June 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Newham Community Health Services National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purposes specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 to provide and manage Trust Headquarters, Sydenham Buildings, Plaistow Hospital, Samson Street, London E13 9EH and the Mental Health Unit, Newham General Hospital site, Glen Road, Plaistow, London E13 8SL.
