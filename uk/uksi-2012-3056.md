## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [1979 c. 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/5); section 5 was amended to provide a definition of “natural road fuel gas” by section 6(1) of the Finance Act [2004 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12). Section 20AA was inserted by section 2(1) of the Finance Act [1989 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/26). Relevant amendments were made to section 20AA by Part 1(4) of Schedule 23 to the Finance Act [1993 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/34) and section 10(3) of the Finance Act [2000 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/17). Section 27(3) applies the definition of “the Commissioners” in section 1(1) of the Customs and Excise Management Act [1979 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/2), namely “the Commissioners” means “the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs” (see the amendment made by paragraph 22(b) of Schedule 4 to the Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act [2005 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/11)).

[^f00002]: Amendments were made to section 8(2) by paragraph 6(a) of Schedule 5 to the Finance Act [2008 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/9). The rate of excise duty charged is specified in section 8(3). Relevant amendments were made to section 8(3) by section 6(2) of the Finance Act [1995 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/4) and section 6(2) of the Finance Act [2004 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/12). The rates in section 8(3) were most recently amended by sections 19(3) and 20(3) of the Finance Act [2011 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/11). A partial relief from excise duty charged on road fuel gas on or after 1st January and before 1st August 2012 was provided by [S.I. 2011/3064](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/3064). Section 188 of the Finance Act [2012 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/14) provides that on or after 1st August 2012 but before 1st January 2013 the Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act [1979 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/5) has effect as if the amendments made by section 20 of Finance Act 2011 had never been made.
