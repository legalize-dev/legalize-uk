# The Immigration Act 2016 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016

Made: 15th June 2016

Laid before Parliament: 20th June 2016

Coming into force: 12th July 2016

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(2) to (4) of the Immigration Act 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration Act 2016 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 12th July 2016.

#### Consequential amendments

###### 2

The Schedule to these Regulations has effect.

## SCHEDULE

## PART 1 — AMENDMENT TO SECONDARY LEGISLATION TO INSERT REFERENCE TO THE “DIRECTOR OF LABOUR MARKET ENFORCEMENT”

#### Amendment of the Official Secrets Act 1989 (Prescription) Order 1990

###### 1

In the Official Secrets Act 1989 (Prescription) Order 1990[^f00002], at the end of Schedule 2 (prescriptions: section 12(1)(g)), insert—

| Director of Labour Market Enforcement | — |
| --- | --- |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to secondary legislation which are consequential to changes made by Part 1 of the Immigration Act [2016 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/19). Regulation 2 introduces a Schedule containing those consequential amendments.

Part 1 of the Schedule inserts a reference to the “Director of Labour Market Enforcement” into Schedule 2 to the Official Secrets Act 1989 (Prescription) Order 1990. This prescribes certain persons for the purposes of the Official Secrets Act 1989, the effect of which is that those persons who are prescribed become Crown servants for all purposes of the Act.

Part 2 of the Schedule amends secondary legislation to replace references to the “Gangmasters Licensing Authority” with references to the “Gangmasters and Labour Abuse Authority”.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as these Regulations do not themselves have any impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors.
