# The Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No.5) Order 2012

Made: 19th April 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(5) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2012.

###### 2

Section 26 of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (issue and revision of code of practice) shall come into force on 30th April 2012.

## Signed

James Brokenshire — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 19th April 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 30th April 2012, section 26 of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008.
