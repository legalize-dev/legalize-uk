# The Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

Made: 31st October 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 9(2) of the Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991.

###### 2

The Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991 shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 31st October 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st January 1992 the Criminal Procedure (Insanity and Unfitness to Plead) Act 1991.
