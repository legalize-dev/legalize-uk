# The Exempt Charities Order 1987

Made: 21st October 1987

At the Court of Saint James, the 21st day of October 1987 Present The Counsellors of State in Council

Now, therefore, His Royal Highness The Prince Andrew Duke of York and Her Royal Highness The Princess Royal, being authorised thereto by the said Letters Patent, and in pursuance of paragraph (c) of Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 1960[^f00001], and by and with the advice of Her Majesty’s Privy Council, do on Her Majesty’s behalf order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities Order 1987.

###### 2

The Institute of Education, University of London, a college of the University of London, is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 1960.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Order declares the Institute of Education, University of London, to be an “exempt charity” within the meaning of the Charities Act 1960.

Exempt charities are not required to be registered with the Charity Commissioners and the Commissioners cannot exercise any of their powers under the Act of 1960 in relation to an exempt charity except at that charity’s request.
