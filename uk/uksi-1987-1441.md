# The Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council (M57 Motorway Associated Special Roads) (Huyton Spur) Revocation Scheme 1987 Confirmation Instrument 1987

Made: 22nd July 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 16 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

The Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council (M57 Motorway Associated Special Roads) (Huyton Spur) Revocation Scheme 1987 is hereby confirmed.

###### 2

The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter referred to as “The Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of The Scheme, and accompanying plan, are deposited at the Department of Transport, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY, at the offices of the Director (Transport), Departments of the Environment and Transport, North West Region, Sunley Tower, Manchester M1 4BE and at the reception, Knowsley Borough Council, Municipal Buildings, Huyton, and at the reception, Knowsley Borough Council, Municipal Buildings, Kirkby.

###### 3

The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 4

This Instrument may be cited as the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council (M57 Motorway Associated Special Roads) (Huyton Spur) Revocation Scheme 1987 Confirmation Instrument 1987.

## THE SCHEDULE TO THE INSTRUMENT — THE KNOWSLEY METROPOLITAN BOROUGH COUNCIL (M57 MOTORWAY ASSOCIATED SPECIAL ROADS) (HUYTON SPUR) REVOCATION SCHEME 1987

The Council of the Borough of Knowsley make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under Sections 16 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980 and of other enabling powers:—

###### 1

The Scheme specified in the Schedule hereto is hereby revoked to the extent specified in the second column of that Schedule.

###### 2

This Scheme may be cited as the Knowsley Metropolitan Borough Council (M57 Motorway Associated Special Roads) (Huyton Spur) Revocation Scheme 1987.

## THE SCHEDULE

## Scheme to be revoked

###### 1

| *Scheme to be revoked* | *Extent of revocation* |
| --- | --- |
| Lancashire County Council Liverpool Outer Ring Road (Kirkby to Tarbock) Special Connecting Roads Scheme 1970 | The whole scheme in so far as it relates to the routes of the special roads described in paragraph 2 of this Schedule. |

## Routes of Special Roads

###### 2

The two routes connecting the southbound and northbound carriageways of the Motorway with a roundabout at Stockbridge Lane, Knowsley (the special roads on these routes being respectively given numbers 1 and 2 on the deposited plan)
