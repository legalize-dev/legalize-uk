# The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

Made: 11th September 2008

Coming into force: 1st January 2009

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 70(1) of the Air Navigation Order 2005[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 1st January 2009.

#### Amendment of Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations 2002

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are amended by substituting for the definition of “Technical Instructions” in Regulation 3(1)—

> > - ‘Technical Instructions’ means the 2009–2010 English language edition of the Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air, approved and published by decision of the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organisation;

## Signed

Signed by authority of the

Secretary of State for Transport

Jim Fitzpatrick — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 11th September 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/2786](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2786)). The definition of “Technical Instructions” is further amended to refer to the 2009-2010 edition of the Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air, approved and published by decision of the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organisation.

The Technical Instructions can be purchased from Labeline of Midas House Chivenor Business Park Barnstaple Devon EX31 4AY; TSO Limited of PO Box 29 Norwich NR3 1GN, or from Freight Merchandising Services Limited of Unit 19 Ashford Industrial Estate Shield Road Ashford Middlesex TW15 1AU.

An impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations
