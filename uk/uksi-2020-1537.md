# The Prisoners (Disclosure of Information About Victims) Act 2020 (Commencement) Regulations 2020

Made: 9th December 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2) of the Prisoners (Disclosure of Information About Victims) Act 2020[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Prisoners (Disclosure of Information About Victims) Act 2020 (Commencement) Regulations 2020.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Sections 1 and 2 of the Prisoners (Disclosure of Information About Victims) Act 2020 come into force on 4th January 2021.

## Signed

Robert Buckland — Secretary of State for Justice — Ministry of Justice — 9th December 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring all the remaining uncommenced provisions of the Prisoners (Disclosure of Information About Victims) Act 2020 into force on 4 January 2021 (section 3 having come into force the day the Act was passed).
