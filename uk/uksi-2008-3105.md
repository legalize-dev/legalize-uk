# The Gambling (Operating Licence and Single-Machine Permit Fees) (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2008

Made: 1st December 2008

Laid before Parliament: 4th December 2008

Coming into force: 31st December 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 69(2)(g) and (5), 100(2) and (3) and 355(1) of the Gambling Act 2005[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Gambling (Operating Licence and Single-Machine Permit Fees) (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 31st December 2008.

#### Amendment of the 2006 Regulations

###### 2

The Gambling (Operating Licence and Single-Machine Permit Fees) Regulations 2006[^f00002] (“the 2006 Regulations”) are amended as set out in Regulations 3 and 4 of these Regulations.

#### Amendment of Schedule 1 (Categories of non-remote operating licences)

###### 3

For the table in Schedule 1 to the 2006 Regulations substitute the table in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

#### Amendment of Schedule 4 (Categories of remote operating licences)

###### 4

For the table in Schedule 4 to the 2006 Regulations substitute the table in Schedule 4 to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Gambling (Operating Licence and Single-Machine Permit Fees) Regulations 2006 [S.I. 2006/3284](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3284) (“the 2006 Regulations”).

The 2006 Regulations prescribe fees relating to operating licences (application, annual and other fees) and single-machine supply and maintenance permits (application fees only) issued under Parts 5 and 10 of the Gambling Act 2005 respectively.

Regulations 3 and 4 substitute tables in Schedules 1 and 4 for the tables in Schedules 1 and 4 of the 2006 Regulations. The purpose of these amendments is to ensure that there is no overlap between certain fee categories.
