# The United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made: 14th February 1990

Laid before Parliament: 22nd February 1990

Coming into force: 14th March 1990

At the Court of Saint James, the 14th day of February 1990 Present, The Counsellors of State in Council

Now, therefore, Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth The Queen Mother and His Royal Highness The Prince Charles, Prince of Wales, being authorised thereto by the said Letters Patent, and in pursuance of the powers conferred by section 5 of the West Indies Act 1962 [^f00001], and by and with the advice f Her Majesty’s Privy Council, do on Her Majesty’s behalf order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 14th March 1990.

###### 2

The United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) Order 1965 [^f00002]is amended by inserting in the Schedule in the appropriate alphabetical order the words “Virgin Islands”.

## Signed

G I de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the Virgin Islands to the list of overseas territories to which the United Kingdom Forces (Jurisdiction of Colonial Courts) Order 1965 (S.I. [1965/1203](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1965/1203)) (which provides for withdrawing the jurisdiction of the civil courts of certain overseas territories to try members of Her Majesty’s forces or of a civilian component of those forces for certain offences) extends.
