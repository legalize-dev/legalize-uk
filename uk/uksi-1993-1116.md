# The Local Fisheries Committees (Fees for Copy Byelaws) Order 1993

Made: 19th April 1993

Laid before Parliament: 30th April 1993

Coming into force: 21st May 1993

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 9 of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Fisheries Committees (Fees for Copy Byelaws) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 21st May 1993.

#### Amendment of Sea Fisheries Regulation Act 1966

###### 2

In section 9(1) of the Sea Fisheries Regulation Act 1966[^f00002] the following words shall be omitted—

> > on payment of a sum not exceeding one penny for each copy

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 19th April 1993.

John Selwyn Gummer — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 9 of the Sea Fisheries Regulation Act 1966 so as to dispense with the provision for payment (not exceeding one penny) to local fisheries committees for supplying a copy of their byelaws.
