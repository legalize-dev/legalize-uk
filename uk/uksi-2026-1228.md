# The Air Traffic Management and Unmanned Aircraft (Specified Information etc.) Regulations 2026

Made: 19th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 24th November 2026

Coming into force: 15th December 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 5(2) and 5(3) of the Air Traffic Management and Unmanned Aircraft Act 2021[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Traffic Management and Unmanned Aircraft (Specified Information etc.) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 15th December 2026.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Specified information, documentation and evidence

###### 2

The following information, documentation and evidence is specified for the purposes of paragraph 5 of Schedule 9 to the Air Traffic Management and Unmanned Aircraft Act 2021—

- (a) P’s name, date of birth and address;

- (b) evidence of insurance for P’s activities as a remote pilot or UAS operator where required under Regulation [(EC) No 785/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0785) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 April 2004 on insurance requirements for air carriers and aircraft operators[^f00002];

- (c) evidence of any wireless telegraphy licence granted under section 8 of the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006[^f00003] in respect of P’s activities as a remote pilot or UAS operator.

## Signed

Dan Jarvis — Minister of State — Home Office — 19th November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations specify, for the purposes of paragraph 5 of Schedule 9 to the Air Traffic Management and Unmanned Aircraft Act 2021, the information that a police officer may require from a person suspected of flying an unmanned aircraft.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
