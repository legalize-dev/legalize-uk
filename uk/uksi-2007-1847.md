# The Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provision) Order 2007

Made: 26th June 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 77(2) and (4) of the Electoral Administration Act 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Electoral Administration Act 2006 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provision) Order 2007.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Electoral Administration Act 2006.

#### Commencement of section 58 of the Act

###### 2

Section 58 of the Act shall come into force on 30th June 2007.

#### Transitional provision

###### 3

The requirement in section 69(2)(aa) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00002] (as inserted by section 58 of the Act) does not apply to donations reported to the Electoral Commission before 1st April 2007.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Bridget Prentice — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 26th June 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order brings section 58 of the Electoral Administration Act [2006 (c.22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/22) into force on 30th June 2007. That section amends section 69(2) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act [2000 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41) so as to require the Electoral Commission to record in the register of donations whether a donation was made in the form of sponsorship. Article 3 of this Order provides that the amendment does not apply to donations reported to the Commission before 1st April 2007.
