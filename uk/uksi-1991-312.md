# The Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (England) Order 1991

Made: 22nd February 1991

Laid before Parliament: 1st March 1991

Coming into force: 22nd March 1991

The Secretary of State for the Enviroment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2(9) and 15(5) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemption) (England) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 22nd March 1991.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Local Government Act 1988.

#### Application

###### 2

This Order applies to the activity mentioned in section 2(2)(c) of the Act (other cleaning) if carried out by a defined authority mentioned in section 1(1) of the Act which has functions in England or if carried out in England by a joint committee which falls to be treated as a defined authority by section 1(4) of the Act and of which at least one of the members is such an authority.

#### Exemption

###### 3

Where—

- (a) by virtue of article 3 of the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (England) Order 1988[^f00002] the activity to which this Order applies was not, in the case of the authority in question, treated as a defined activity in the financial year ending on 31 March 1991; and

- (b) that activity would, but for this Order, be treated as a defined activity in the case of that authority on 1st April 1991,

that activity shall not be treated as a defined activity so long as it is carried out before 1st August 1991.

## Signed

Michael Heseltine — Secretary of State for the Enviroment — 22nd February 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Under Part 1 of the Local Government Act 1988 work falling within certain defined activities may only be carried out by local authorities if specified conditions are fulfilled.

Article 3 of the Local Government Act 1988 (Defined Activities) (Exemptions) (England) Order 1988 (“the 1988 Order”) exempts an activity from the requirements of Part I where the estimated gross cost to a particular authority of carrying it out in the previous financial year does not exceed £100,000.

This Order exempts from the requirements of Part I the activity of “other cleaning” (as defined in paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 to the 1988 Act) where, in respect of the authority concerned, that activity was exempt by virtue of article 3 of the 1988 Order on 31st March 1991 but not on 1st April 1991, so long as the activity is carried out before 1st August 1991.
