# The School Boards (Scotland) Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made: 25th February 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 24(2) of the School Boards (Scotland) Act 1988( [^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the School Boards (Scotland) Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1989.

#### Appointed Dates

###### 2

- (1) The whole of the School Boards (Scotland) Act 1988 except paragraphs 1-5 and paragraph 7 of Schedule 4 shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

- (2) Paragraphs 1-5 and paragraph 7 of Schedule 4 shall come into force on 1st November 1989.

## Signed

Michael B Forsyth — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 25th February 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1989 the School Boards (Scotland) Act 1988 except paragraphs 1-5 and paragraph 7 of Schedule 4 which is brought into force on 1st November 1989.
