# The Competition Act 1998 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013

Made: 12th February 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(3) of the Competition Act 1998[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Competition Act 1998 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013.

#### Provisions coming into force on 10th March 2013

###### 2

The following provisions of the Competition Act 1998 come into force on 10th March 2013 so far as they are not already in force—

- (a) section 1 (enactments replaced);

- (b) section 74(3) (repeals); and

- (c) Part 1 of Schedule 14 (repeals).

## Signed

Jo Swinson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relation and Consumer Affairs — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 12th February 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 10th March 2013 the provisions of the Competition Act [1998 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/41) which are not already in force. The provisions in question are section 1(a), which provides for the Restrictive Practices Court Act [1976 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/33) to cease to have effect, and the provisions of section 74(3) and Part 1 of Schedule 14 which repeal that Act.
