## SCHEDULE

| *Column 1* | *Column 2* |
| --- | --- |
| *Ministers or departments* | *Matters in relation to which the Ministers or departments are designated* |
| The Secretary of State | Regulation and control of classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous substances and preparations. |
| The Secretary of State | Information on weight to be given concerning cocoa and chocolate products on packages, containers and labels, or in documents accompanying such products. |
| The Secretary of State | Regulation and control of pre-packaging of products by weight or volume. |
| The Secretary of State | Disclosure by companies of particulars concerning persons authorised to represent the company. |
| The Secretary of State and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food. | Measures relating to the recovery or payment of amounts, not being customs duties or refunds of such duties, payable on the import or export of agricultural products or goods resulting from the processing of agricultural products, including conditions attached to and relief allowed in respect of such payments. |
| Any Northern Ireland department or the Head of any such department | All the preceding items in this column. |
| The Secretary of State | Units of measurement to be used for economic, health, safety or administrative purposes and the prefixes to be used with such units. |
