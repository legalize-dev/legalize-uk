# The Superannuation (Designation of Public Offices) Order 1965

Made: 1st July 1965

Laid before Parliament: 8th July 1965

Coming into Operation: 9th July 1965

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 7(2) of the Superannuation (Amendment) Act 1965, hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

Employment which is remunerated out of the revenues of the Science Research Council or the Natural Environment Research Council shall be employment in a public office for the purposes of section 7 of the Superannuation (Amendment) Act 1965.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of this Order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 3

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Public Offices) Order 1965, and shall come into operation on 9th July 1965.

## Signed

G. H. R. Rogers — Ifor Davies — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 1st July 1965

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 6 of the Superannuation (Amendment) Act 1965 provides for the superannuation benefits of persons employed in more than one public office. “*Public office*” is defined in section 7, and this Order designates employment in the Science Research Council and the Natural Environment Research Council as employment in a public office for the purposes of section 7.
