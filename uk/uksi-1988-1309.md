# The Black Country Development Corporation (Vesting of Land) (Borough of Walsall) Order 1988

Made: 27th June 1988

Laid before Parliament: 1st July 1988

Coming into force: 26th July 1988

The Secretary of State for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 141(1) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980[^f00001], and of all the other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Black Country Development Corporation (Vesting of Land) (Borough of Walsall) Order 1988 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In this Order—

- “the Corporation” means the Black Country Development Corporation;

- “statutory undertakers” does not include any statutory undertakers in relation to whom the Secretary of State for the Environment is not the appropriate Minister;

- “urban development area” means the area designated as an urban development area by the Black Country Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1987[^f00002].

- (2) Any reference in this Order to a map followed immediately by a number is a reference to the map so numbered in the set of maps numbered 1 and 2 entitled “Maps referred to in the Black Country Development Corporation (Vesting of Land) (Borough of Walsall) Order 1988”, of which prints, signed by an Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment are deposited and available for inspection at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment, the council of the borough of Walsall, and the Corporation.

- (3) Any reference in this Order to a numbered plot is a reference to the plot so numbered and shown bounded externally with a black line and stippled or edged internally with a stippled band on a numbered map.

#### Vesting of Land

###### 3

The land described in the Schedule hereto, and now vested in the council of the borough of Walsall or any other local authority or statutory undertakers or other public body or in a subsidiary of any of them, being land within the urban development area, is hereby vested in the Corporation.

#### Easements and rights in or over land

###### 4

All existing easements and rights in or over other land within the urban development area which are vested in an authority or body mentioned in article 3 above, and which are annexed or appurtenant to land vested by that article or any part thereof, are hereby vested in the Corporation.

## Signed

Nicholas Ridley — Secretary of State for the Environment — 27th June 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order vests in the Black Country Development Corporation certain land which is at present vested in the council of the borough of Walsall or any other local authority or other body mentioned in section 141(1) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980, and related easements and similar rights. The land is shown coloured black on the map forming part of this note.

The land and rights are in the area designated by the Black Country Development Corporation (Area and Constitution) Order 1987, which establishes the Black Country Development Corporation.
