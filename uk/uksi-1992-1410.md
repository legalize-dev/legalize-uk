# The Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made: 15th June 1992

Coming into force: 30th June 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 30th June 1992.

###### 2

In article 6(5) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1992[^f00002], after the words “where a person is,”there shall be inserted the words “on or”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kenneth Carlisle — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 15th June 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 4 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1992 so as to correct a textual error.
