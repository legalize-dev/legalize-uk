# The National Insurance (Modification of Transport Undertaking Superannuation Funds) (Amendment) Regulations 1966

Made: 9th September 1966

Laid before Parliament: 19th September 1966

Coming into Operation: 6th October 1966

The Minister of Transport being the appropriate Minister by virtue of a determination of the Treasury under section 69(4) of the National Insurance Act 1946 which by virtue of section 117(1) of the National Insurance Act 1965 (hereinafter called “*the 1965 Act*”) is deemed to have been made under section 110 of the 1965 Act, in relation to the Funds specified in the National Insurance (Modification of Transport Undertaking Superannuation Funds) Regulations 1961[^f00001] in pursuance of the powers conferred upon her by section 110 of the 1965 Act and of all other powers her enabling, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations shall come into operation on 6th October 1966 and may be cited as the National Insurance (Modification of Transport Undertaking Superannuation Funds) (Amendment) Regulations 1966.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these Regulations as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 3

The National Insurance (Modification of Transport Undertaking Superannuation Funds) Regulations 1961 shall be amended by inserting, at the end of Regulation 2(1) thereof, the following definition:—

> > - `“*graduated contribution*” means such part of the amount payable under sub-paragraph (i) of section 4(1)(c) of the National Insurance Act 1965 as substituted by section 1(2) of the National Insurance Act 1966 as is equivalent to 4¼ per cent of the amount, up to nine pounds, by which the member's remuneration in any week exceeds nine pounds (or the equivalent amount for remuneration not paid weekly).'

## Signed

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister of Transport the 9th September 1966.

Barbara Castle — The Minister of Transport

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the National Insurance (Modification of Transport Undertaking Superannuation Funds) Regulations 1961 under which the contributions and benefits of members of the superannuation funds specified in those Regulations who are not contracted out of the graduated National Insurance scheme are modified to take account of the contributions and benefits payable under the scheme. These Regulations exclude from such modification the additional contributions and benefits payable in consequence of the National Insurance Act 1966.
