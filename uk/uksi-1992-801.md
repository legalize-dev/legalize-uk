# The South Ayrshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1992

Made: 12th March 1992

Coming into force: 25th March 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 12A(1) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(c) of Schedule 7A to, the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the South Ayrshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1992 and shall come into force on 25th March 1992.

- (2) In this Order “article 4” means article 4 of the South Ayrshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00002].

#### Amendment of Article 4

###### 2

For article 4 there shall be substituted the following article:—

> > (4) The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 5 non-executive directors and 5 executive directors.

## Signed

Ian Lang — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 12th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 4 of the South Ayrshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991 to make clear that the number of executive directors of the Trust shall be five.
