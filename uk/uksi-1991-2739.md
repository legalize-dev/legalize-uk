# The Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made: 4th December 1991

Laid before Parliament: 11th December 1991

Coming into force: 1st January 1992

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of his powers under section 73(1)(a), (1A)(a) and (b), and (6)(b)(i) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 1st January 1992.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Civil Aviation (Route Charges for Navigation Services) Regulations 1989 as follows:

New unit rates and transatlantic charges are introduced, reflecting forecasts of costs and traffic for 1992, and taking into account the balance of over and under recoveries of revenue as compared with costs experienced by the countries participating in the Eurocontrol charging system in 1990 (regulation 2).

The unit rates in ECUs set out in regulation 2(1) and the amount of the charges in ECUs set out in regulation 2(2) (for transatlantic flights) are calculated by reference to the costs of provision of en-route navigation services in the participating countries in the Eurocontrol charges system, the amount of traffic using each country’s airspace and the relationship of each country’s currency to the ECU over a period agreed by Ministers of the participating countries. The interaction of these elements varies in each country. In calculating the revised charges the average of the exchange rates between the ECU and the currencies of the participating countries obtaining in the month of July 1991 have been used.

The unit rates for Cyprus, Ireland and Spain (Mainland) have decreased on average by 12.8%. The unit rates for Austria, Belgium, France, Germany, Greece, Malta, Netherlands, Portugal (Mainland), Switzerland and Turkey have increased on average by 22%, for Portugal (Santa Maria) by 2.7% and for Spain (Canaries) by 3.1%. The unit rate for the United Kingdom has increased by 15%.

Banjul, Cologne-Bonn, Helsinki, Manston, Munich, Prestwick, Rome and Stockholm airports are added to certain zones for the purposes of regulation 7 of the principal regulation while Hamburg, Lamezia-Teme, Pisa and Venice airports are omitted from certain such zones (regulation 2).
