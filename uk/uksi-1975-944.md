# The Local Authorities etc. (Miscellaneous Provision) (No. 2) Order 1975

Made: 4th June 1975

Laid before Parliament: 9th June 1975

Coming into Operation: 30th June 1975

The Secretary of State for the Environment and the Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 254(1)(a) and (2)(a), (b) and (c) and 266(2) of the Local Government Act 1972 and section 34(1) of the Water Act 1973 and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following order:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Local Authorities etc. (Miscellaneous Provision) (No. 2) Order 1975 and shall come into operation on 30th June 1975.

#### Territorial extent of exercise of powers

###### 2

- (1) Article 13, in so far as it relates to the Lancashire and Western Sea Fisheries Committee, is made by the Secretary of State for the Environment.

- (2) Subject to paragraph (1), this order is made by the Secretary of State for the Environment in relation to England and by the Secretary of State for Wales in relation to Wales.

#### Interpretation

###### 3

- (1) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of this order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

- (2) In this order “*the Act*” means the Local Government Act 1972.

- (3) In this order—

- (a) references to any enactment shall be construed as references to that enactment as amended, extended or applied by or under any other enactment;

- (b) references to any instrument shall be construed as references to that instrument as amended, extended or applied by any other instrument.

- (4) Any reference in this order to a numbered article shall, unless the reference is to an article of a specified order, be construed as a reference to the article bearing that number in this order.

- (5) Any reference in any article of this order to a numbered paragraph shall, unless the reference is to a paragraph of a specified article, be construed as a reference to the paragraph bearing that number in the first-mentioned article.

#### Miscellaneous amendment of public general and other Acts

###### 4

- (1) The following provisions, namely—

section 178 of the Leeds Corporation (Consolidation) Act 1905 (corporation to kerb and sewer private streets);

section 109 of the Leeds Corporation Act 1956 (separate sewers for foul water and surface water); and

section 7 of the Leeds Corporation Act 1966 (amendment of the said section 178)

are hereby repealed.

- (2) In the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971, in Schedule 3, in paragraph 6(2)(c) (passenger transport executives) there shall be added “or under section 202(4) of the Local Government Act 1972”.

- (3) In the Essex County Council Act 1971—

in section 3(1) (interpretation) the definition of “police authority” shall cease to have effect; and

in section 49 (authorisation of officers of the county council or the police authority to appear on behalf of the police authority or officers or constables)—

in subsection (1), for “Essex and Southend-on-Sea joint constabulary” there shall be substituted “Essex police force”;

and

in subsection (3), “committee or” shall be omitted.

#### Provisions of public general Acts modified or excluded by local statutory provision

###### 5

- (1) Where—

- (a) any provision of a public general Act is modified or excluded in its application to any area by any local statutory provision to which subsection (9) of section 262 of the Act applies;

- (b) such local statutory provision ceases to have effect in its application to such area under the said subsection or is repealed before it so ceases to have effect by any local Act enacted to repeal provisions falling within the said subsection; and

- (c) no corresponding or different provision is made in relation to the first-mentioned provision in its application to such area in any local Act,

the first-mentioned provision shall have effect in relation to the area as if it had not been modified or excluded by the local statutory provision.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall have effect whether a provision of a public general Act is directly referred to in the local statutory provision or falls to be substituted for a provision so referred to by virtue of section 38(1) of the Interpretation Act 1889 or any other provision of a public general Act.

#### Byelaws made by certain London authorities

###### 9

Sections 236 to 238 of the Act shall apply in relation to byelaws made under any enactment—

#### Accounts of passenger transport executives

###### 10

Any resolution required by paragraph 7(2) or (3) of Schedule 29 to the Act in relation to the accounts of the passenger transport executive for any metropolitan county shall be a resolution of the passenger transport authority for that county.

#### Subscriptions to Association of Charter Trustees

###### 11

The following bodies namely—

#### Crediton cemetery

###### 12

- (1) In relation to the Crediton Cemetery the functions conferred by section 214 of, and Schedule 26 to, the Act shall be exercised by the District Council of Tiverton.

- (2) All property and liabilities vested in the Town Council of Crediton and the Parish Council of Crediton Hamlets for the purposes of such functions shall by virtue of this order be transferred to and vest in or attach to the said District Council, and—

- (a) all contracts, deeds, bonds, agreements and other instruments subsisting in favour of, or against, and all notices in force which were given (or have effect as if they had been given) by, or to, the said Town Council and Parish Council in respect of such property or liabilities shall be of full force and effect in favour of, or against, the said District Council; and

- (b) any action or proceeding or any cause of action or proceeding, pending or existing immediately before the coming into operation of this order, by, or against, the said Town Council and Parish Council in respect of such property or liabilities shall not be prejudically affected by reason of this article, and may be continued, prosecuted and enforced by, or against, the said District Council.

#### Property of local fisheries committees

###### 13

Any property held immediately before 1st April 1974 by a local fisheries committee for a sea fisheries district on behalf of their constituent councils may be held by the committee as reconstituted for the benefit of their constituent councils, and article 16(3) of the Local Government (England) (Property etc.) Order 1973[^f00005] and article 16(2) of the Local Government (Wales) (Property etc.) Order 1973[^f00006] shall have effect accordingly.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes further provision incidental, consequential, transitional and supplementary to the Local Government Act 1972, relating to

(*a*) the operation, amendment or repeal of certain Acts and instruments;

(*b*) agreements by certain authorities with other local authorities which will engage the direct labour forces of the first-mentioned authorities;

(*c*) byelaws made by the authorities for the City of London and the Temples;

(*d*) the accounts of passenger transport executives;

(*e*) subscriptions to the Association of Charter Trustees;

(*f*) the Crediton Cemetery;

(*g*) the property of local fisheries committees.
