# The Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) (Welsh Form and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 27th June 1999

Coming into force: 10th August 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 2(2) and (4), 101[^f00001] and 146 of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00002] as extended by section 26(3) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00003] hereby makes the following Regulations–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) (Welsh Form and Particulars) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 10th August 1999.

#### Amendment of the Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) (Welsh Form and Particulars) Regulations 1998

###### 2

The form set out in the Schedule to the Housing Renewal Grants (Prescribed Form and Particulars) (Welsh Form and Particulars) Regulations 1998[^f00004] is amended as specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Application

###### 3

These Regulations shall not have effect in relation to applications for a grant made before 10th August 1999.
