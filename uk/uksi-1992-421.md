# The Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992

Made: 2nd March 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992.

#### Commencement provisions

###### 2

The provisions of the Road Traffic Act 1991 specified in the first column of the Schedule to this Order which relate to the matters specified in the second column of that Schedule shall come into force on 1st April 1992 subject to the limitations (if any) specified in the third column of that Schedule.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS BROUGHT INTO FORCE ON 1ST APRIL 1992

| --- | --- | --- |
| Provisions of the Act | Subject matter of provisions | Limitations on commencement |
| Section 47 | Applications for licences to drive hackney carriages etc. |  |
| Section 48 | Minor and consequential amendments | To the extent necessary for the bringing into force of the provisions of Schedule 4 brought into force by this Order |
| Section 83 | Repeals | To the extent that it relates to the provisions of Schedule 8 brought into force by this Order |
| Schedule 4, paragraphs 36, 50, 73(1), (2) and (3), and 75 | Minor and consequential amendments | In relation to paragraph 73(1), to the extent that it relates to paragraph 73(2) and (3) only |
| Schedule 8 | Repeals | To the extent that it relates to the repeal of section 41(3)(b) and (c) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00002] |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

Christopher Chope — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 2nd March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 1992 provisions of the Road Traffic Act 1991. The provisions relate to:

- (a) applications for licences to drive hackney carriages and private hire vehicles;

- (b) disabled persons' vehicles; and

- (c) sealing of speed limiters.
