# The South Gloucestershire and Stroud College (Incorporation) Order 2011

Made: 10th December 2011

Laid before Parliament: 14th December 2011

Coming into force: 4th January 2012

In accordance with section 51(3)(a) of the Act, this Order is made for the purpose of giving effect to a proposal for the establishment of a body corporate under section 16(1)(a) of the Act by the Chief Executive of Skills Funding.

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the South Gloucestershire and Stroud College (Incorporation) Order 2011 and comes into force on 4th January 2012.

###### 2

There is established a body corporate called “the South Gloucestershire and Stroud College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name.

###### 3

The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order is 5th January 2012.

## Signed

John Hayes — Minister of State for Further Education, Skills and Lifelong Learning — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 10th December 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order establishes a further education corporation called “the South Gloucestershire and Stroud College” under section 16(1)(a) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 5th January 2012 as the operative date for the purposes of Part I of that Act, being the date from which the further education corporation shall operate the college.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no wider impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
