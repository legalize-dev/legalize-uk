# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (All Tiers) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020

Made: at 3.06 p.m. on 17th December 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 4.15 p.m. on 17th December 2020

Coming into force: 19th December 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act, the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (All Tiers) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 19th December 2020.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make changes to the areas which are subject to the restrictions for Tier 2 and Tier 3, as defined in the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (All Tiers) (England) Regulations 2020.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
