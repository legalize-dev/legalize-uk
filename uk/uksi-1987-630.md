# The Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) (Scotland) Order 1987

Made: 31st March 1987

Laid before Parliament: 10th April 1987

Coming into force: 4th May 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 20(3) and (8) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) (Scotland) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 4th May 1987.

- (2) This Order shall extend to Scotland only.

###### 2

Section 20(3) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00002] (which specifies the maximum permitted aggregate winnings in respect of bingo played in one week simultaneously on different club premises) shall have effect as if for the reference to the sum of £3,000 there were substituted a reference to the sum of £3,500.

###### 3

Section 20(8) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00003] (which specifies the maximum amount by which weekly winnings on any particular bingo club premises may exceed the aggregate amount of the stakes hazarded) shall have effect as if for the reference to the sum of £1,250 there were substituted a reference to the sum of £1,500.

###### 4

Article 2 of the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) (Scotland) Order 1984[^f00004] and article 2 of the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) (Scotland) Order 1985[^f00005] are revoked.

## Signed

Ian Lang — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,Scottish Office — 31st March 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only–

- (a) increases from £3,000 to £3,500 the maximum aggregate sum permitted to be paid to players as winnings in respect of all “linked” games of bingo (article 2); and

- (b) increases from £1,250 to £1,500 the maximum amount by which weekly winnings in bingo clubs may exceed the stakes hazarded (article 3).
