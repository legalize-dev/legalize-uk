# The Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1988

Made: 11th May 1988

Laid before Parliament: 20th May 1988

Coming into force: 13th June 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1, 3, 16(10), 34, 35, 50(3) and 126[^f00001] of, and paragraphs 2, 5 and 10(c) of Schedule 1 to, the Building Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 13th June 1988.

#### Transitional Provisions

###### 3

These Regulations shall not affect the application of the Building (Prescribed Fees etc) Regulations 1985 in relation to work in respect of which—

- (1) a building notice was given or full plans were deposited (under regulation 11 of the Building Regulations 1985[^f00004]); or

- (2) an initial notice or a public body’s notice was given (under section 47(1) or 54(1) of the Building Act 1984),

before 13th June 1988, notwithstanding that on or after that date a notice is or has been given, or plans are or have been deposited, in respect of substantially the same work.

#### Revocations

###### 4

Regulations 2(3)(a) and (c) and (4) of the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1986[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Nicholas Ridley — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 11th May 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Building (Prescribed Fees etc) Regulations 1985 so as to increase the flat rate fees payable to a local authority—

- (1) by approximately 7% in respect of the erection of small domestic building and connected work (Schedule 1 to the 1985 Regulations), and

- (2) by approximately 10% in respect of the erection of certain garages, carports and the making of small extensions and alterations to domestic buildings (Schedule 2 to the 1985 Regulations).

Transitional provisions are contained in regulation 3. Regulation 4 revokes the provisions of the Building (Amendment of Prescribed Fees) Regulations 1986 which are superseded by these Regulations.
