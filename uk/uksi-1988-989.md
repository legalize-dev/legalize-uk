# The Motor Vehicles (Tests) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1988

Made: 6th June 1988

Laid before Parliament: 13th June 1988

Coming into force: 4th July 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43(2) and (6) of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00001], and now vested in him [^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 199(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Tests) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 4th July 1988.

###### 2

The Motor Vehicles (Tests) Regulations 1981[^f00003] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

###### 3

In the regulations specified in column 1 of the Table below for the amounts specified in column 2 of that Table there are substituted the amounts specified in column 3 of the Table.

###### 4

In regulation 23 (duplicate test certificates), in paragraph (2) for the words “a fee of £7.50” to “same effect as the original test certificate:” there shall be substituted—

> > a fee of—
> (a) £7.50 in the case of a Class VI vehicle;
> (b) £3.96 in the case of a motor bicycle not having a side car attached to it; and
> (c) £6.61 in any other case;
> shall issue a duplicate marked “Duplicate” and the duplicate so issued shall have the same effect as the original test certificate:

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Tests) Regulations 1981 so that with effect from 4th July 1988:

- (a) the fee payable for the examination of a motor cycle not having a side car attached to it is increased from £7.48 to £7.92;

- (b) the fee payable for the examination of any other vehicle (not being a vehicle in Class VI) is increased from £12.52 to £13.22;

- (c) the fee for the supply of 100 forms of test certificate is increased from £32 to £37; and

- (d) the fee payable for the issue of a duplicate test certificate for vehicles (other than Class VI vehicles) is increased from £1 to—

- (i) £3.96 in the case of a motor bicycle not having a side car attached to it, and

- (ii) £6.61 in any other case.
