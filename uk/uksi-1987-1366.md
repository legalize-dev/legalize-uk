# The Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987

Made: 30th July 1987

Laid Before Parliament: 4th August 1987

Coming into force: 18th August 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 49 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 1987 and shall come into force on 18th August 1987.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations, the “principal Regulations” means the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Regulations 1986[^f00002] and “bursary” and “payment period” shall have the meanings assigned to them in the principal Regulations.

#### Amendment of principal Regulations

###### 3

The principal Regulations shall be amended as follows:—

- (a) in regulation 2(1) (interpretation) after sub-paragraph (a) there shall be inserted the following sub-paragraph:—

> > (aa) “British Islands” means the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man;

- (b) in regulation 4(2) at the end there shall be added the words “except in the circumstances specified in paragraph (2A)”;

- (c) after regulation 4(2) there shall be inserted the following paragraph:—

> > (2A) A relevant power may be exercised in relation to a person who is a national or a child of a national of a member state of the European Community (the Kingdom of Spain and the Portuguese Republic being deemed for the purpose of this paragraph and of the application of Schedule 1 in relation to this paragraph to have been member states of the European Community from 1st September 1983) and who does not fulfil any of the requirements specified in paragraph (3) where that person fulfils the requirements specified in paragraph 2(1)(e) of Schedule 1, but in such a case the bursary payable may only include sums in respect of tuition and other fees.

- (d) in regulation 8(1)(c) (assessment of full-time further education bursaries) there shall be substituted for the sum of “£1,380” the sum of “£2,000” and there shall be substituted for the sum of “£460” the sum of “£491” in each place where they occur;

- (e) in Schedule 1 (further requirements regarding eligibility for bursary)—

- (i) in paragraph 1(3), for the words “paragraph 2(1)(a) or (b)” there shall be substituted the words “paragraph 2(1)(a), (b) or (e)” ;

- (ii) in paragraph 1(4), for the words “paragraph 2(1)(a) or (b)” there shall be substituted the words “paragraph 2(1)(a), (b) or (e)”;

- (iii) in paragraph 2(1)(a), for the words “paragraphs (b), (c) and (d)” there shall be substituted the words “sub-paragraph (1)(b), (c), (d) and (e) below”;

- (iv) in paragraph 2(1), at the end there shall be added the following:—

> > (e) in the case of a person mentioned in regulation 4(2A), that—
>   (i) he is seeking a bursary in respect of a course of study in the area of the education authority proposing to exercise the relevant power;
>   (ii) he has been ordinarily resident in the European Community in accordance with sub-paragraph (2) throughout the period of 3 years preceding the relevant date; and
>   (iii) he does not fulfil any of the requirements specified in regulation 4(3)(a) to (d) in relation to the area of any other education authority in Scotland and would not fulfil any of them, if they applied, in relation to the area of any local education authority in England and Wales or any comparable award-making authority in any other part of the British Islands.

- (v) in paragraph 2(2), for the words “sub-paragraphs (1)(a) and (b)” there shall be substituted the words “sub-paragraph (1)(a), (b) and (e)”; and

- (vi) in paragraph 2(3), for the words “sub-paragraph (1)(a) and (b)” there shall be substituted the words “sub-paragraph (1)(a), (b) and (e)” and for the words “sub-paragraph (1)(a) or (b)” there shall be substituted the words “sub-paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (e)”.

#### Transitional Provision

###### 4

Notwithstanding these Regulations, the provisions of the principal Regulations amended in regulation 3 above shall apply, as they were in force prior to being so amended, for the purposes of regulation 9 of the principal Regulations, in relation to any payment period expiring prior to or current at the date of coming into force of these Regulations.

## Signed

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the bursary rates and the parental and spouse contribution scales which are prescribed by the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Regulations 1986, and allow authorities to pay students who are nationals, or children of nationals, of a member state of the European Community bursaries in respect of tuition and other fees in certain circumstances.
