# The Purity Criteria for Colours, Sweeteners and Miscellaneous Food Additives (England) Regulations 2009

Made: 2nd April 2009

Laid before Parliament: 9th April 2009

Coming into force: 25th May 2009

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Purity Criteria for Colours, Sweeteners and Miscellaneous Food Additives (England) Regulations 2009. They apply in relation to England only and come into force on 25th May 2009.

#### Amendment of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Colours in Food Regulations 1995[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) —

- (a) omit the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” and substitute in its place the following definition —

> > - “*Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128)*” means Commission Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128) laying down specific purity criteria concerning colours for use in foodstuffs[^f00005];

- (b) in the definition of “permitted colour”, for the expression “the annex to Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” substitute the expression “Annex I to Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128)”.

#### Amendment of the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995

###### 3

- (1) The Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995[^f00006] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) —

- (a) omit the definition of “Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” and substitute in its place the following definition —

> > - ”Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060)” means Commission Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060) laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs[^f00007];

- (b) in the definition of “permitted sweetener”, for the expression “the annex to Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” substitute the expression “Annex I to Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060)”.

#### Amendment of the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995

###### 4

- (1) The Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995[^f00008] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) —

- (a) omit the definition of “Directive [96/77/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0077)” and substitute in its place the following definition —

> > - ”Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084)” means Commission Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084) laying down specific purity criteria on food additives other than colours and sweeteners[^f00009] as that Directive was before the amendments made by Commission Directive [2009/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0010)[^f00010];

- (b) in the definition of “purity criteria”, in sub-paragraph (a) for the expression “the Annex to Directive [96/77/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0077)” substitute “Annex I to Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084)”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1 These Regulations provide for the implementation of three new Commission Directives (“*the new Directives*”) which consolidate existing European Community rules governing the purity criteria for colours, sweeteners and other miscellaneous additives used in foods.

2 These Regulations further amend in relation to England the Colours in Food Regulations 1995 ([S.I.1995/3124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3124) as amended), the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995 ([S.I.1995/3123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3123) as amended) and the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995 ([S.I.1995/3187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/3187) as amended).

3 Implementation of the Directives which the new Directives replace was by reference to them in domestic provisions, and these Regulations therefore update those references.

4 The new Directives are — a Commission Directive [2008/128/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0128) laying down specific purity criteria concerning colours for use in foodstuffs (OJ No. L6, 10.1.2009, p.20); b Commission Directive [2008/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0060) laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs (OJ No. L158, 18.6.2008, p.17); and c Commission Directive [2008/84/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0084) laying down specific purity criteria on food additives other than colours and sweeteners (OJ No. L253, 20.9.2008, p.1).

5 A full impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations as no impact on the voluntary or private sectors is foreseen.
