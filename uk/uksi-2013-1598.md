# The Evidence Through Television Links (England and Wales) Order 2013

Made: 1st July 2013

Laid before Parliament: 1st July 2013

Coming into force: 22nd July 2013

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 29(1) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Evidence Through Television Links (England and Wales) Order 2013 and comes into force on 22nd July 2013.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

#### Hearing witnesses abroad through television links in extradition proceedings

###### 2

Section 32(1A) of the Criminal Justice Act 1988[^f00002] (proceedings in which evidence may be given through television link) applies to proceedings under the Extradition Act 2003[^f00003].

## Signed

Mark Harper — Minister of State — Home Office — 1st July 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order allows for evidence to be given through television link by witnesses who are outside the United Kingdom in extradition proceedings in England and Wales.

Section 32(1) of the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (“the 1988 Act”) states that, in proceedings to which subsection (1A) applies, a witness may, with the leave of the court, give evidence through a television link, if the witness is outside the United Kingdom. Subsection (1A) applies to various criminal proceedings, but these do not include extradition proceedings.

Section 29(1) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 allows the Secretary of State by Order to provide for section 32(1A) of the 1988 Act to apply to any further description of criminal proceedings, or to all criminal proceedings. By this Order, the Secretary of State provides that section 32(1A) of the 1988 Act applies to extradition proceedings.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as no impact on business, charities, voluntary bodies or the public sector is foreseen.
