# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1) (Northern Ireland) Order 2018

Made: 7th February 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(2) of the Crime and Courts Act 2013[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1) (Northern Ireland) Order 2018.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st March 2018

###### 2

So far as extending to Northern Ireland, section 56 of, and Schedule 22[^f00002] to, the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (drugs and driving), come into force on 1st March 2018.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jesse Norman — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 7th February 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences, so far as extending to Northern Ireland, section 56 of, and Schedule 22 to, the Crime and Courts Act [2013 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/22). Section 56 creates a new drug driving offence in Great Britain, and Schedule 22 makes consequential amendments to primary legislation, including some primary legislation which extends to the United Kingdom. Section 56 and Schedule 22 came into force in England and Wales on 2nd March 2015, and will be come into force in Scotland on 1st March 2018.
