# The Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981 (Commencement) Order 1981

Made: 14th September 1981

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 13(3) of the Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981 (Commencement) Order 1981.

###### 2

The Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981 shall come into operation on 1st October 1981.

## Signed

W.S.I. Whitelaw — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 14th September 1981

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1st October 1981 the whole of the Licensing (Alcohol Education and Research) Act 1981.
