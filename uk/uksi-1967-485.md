# The Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1967

Made: 23rd March 1967

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 23rd day of March 1967

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas by section 14(2) of the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967, it is provided that that Act shall come into force on such date as Her Majesty may by Order in Council appoint:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of the said section 14(2), is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

The Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 shall come into force on 1st April 1967.

###### 2

This Order may be cited as the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (Commencement) Order 1967.

## Signed

W. G. Agnew
