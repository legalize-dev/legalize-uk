# The Mutual Assistance Provisions Order 2004

Made: 7th December 2004

Laid before the House of Commons: 7th December 2004

Coming into force: 1st January 2005

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 197(5) of the Finance Act 2003[^f00001], make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Mutual Assistance Provisions Order 2004 and comes into force on 1st January 2005.

###### 2

In section 197(4) of the Finance Act 2003[^f00002], in the definition of the “Mutual Assistance Directive”, for “and [2003/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0093)” substitute “, [2003/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0093) and [2004/56/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0056)”.

## Signed

Derek Twigg — Joan Ryan — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 7th December 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the legislative references in section 197 of the Finance Act 2003 following the adoption of a new Council Directive which amends EC Mutual Assistance legislation by updating and modernising Directive [77/799/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0799) (Mutual Assistance).

Article 1 provides for citation and commencement.

Article 2 amends the definition of the “Mutual Assistance Directive” in section 197(4) of the Finance Act 2003 to insert a reference to the new amending Council Directive [2004/56/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0056).
