## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [2006 c. 41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41). Subsection (1D) of section 3 is part of an insertion made by section 13(3) of the Health and Social Care Act [2012 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7) (“the 2012 Act”). By virtue of section 271(1) of the National Health Service Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”), the powers exercised in making these Regulations are exercisable by the Secretary of State only in relation to England. *See* section 275(1) of the 2006 Act for the definitions of “prescribed” and “regulations”.

[^f00002]: Paragraph (zza) of section 272(6) of the 2006 Act was inserted by section 13(8) of the 2012 Health Act.

[^f00003]: A clinical commissioning group is a body established under section 14D of the 2006 Act. Section 14D is part of an insertion made by section 25(1) of the 2012 Act. *See also*section 1I of the 2006 Act, inserted by section 10 of the 2012 Act.

[^f00004]: [1999 c. 33](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/33). Relevant amendments were made by section 66(1) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act [2002 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/41).

[^f00005]: [S.I. 2012/2996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/2996)

[^f00007]: The National Health Service Commissioning Board is established by section 1H of the 2006 Act. Section 1H is inserted by section 9(1) of the 2012 Act.

[^f00008]: Section 3 is amended by section 13 of the 2012 Act.

[^f00009]: Subsection (1A) is part of the insertion made by section 13(3) of the 2012 Act.

[^f00010]: Section 14A is part of the insertion into the 2006 Act of a new Chapter A2. The insertion is made by section 25(1) of the 2012 Act. By virtue of section 14A(4), a “provider of primary medical services” for the purposes of Chapter A2 is a party to an arrangement to provide primary medical services of a prescribed description. That power has been exercised in making the [National Health Service (Clinical Commissioning Groups) Regulations 2012 S.I 2012/1631](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1631)- *see*regulation 2.
