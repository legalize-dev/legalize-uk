# The Value Added Tax Act 1994 (Interest on Tax)(Prescribed Rate) Order 1996

Made: 31st January 1996

Laid before the House of Commons: 31st January 1996

Coming into force: 6th February 1996

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 74(6) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (Interest on Tax) (Prescribed Rate) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 6th February 1996.

###### 2

For the purposes of section 74 of the Value Added Tax Act 1994 the prescribed rate shall be 6.25 per cent. per annum.

## Signed

Simon Burns — Derek Conway — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 31st January 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order lowers from 7 per cent. per annum to 6.25 per cent. per annum the prescribed rate of interest for the purposes of section 74 of the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (interest on VAT recovered or recoverable by assessment).
