# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2009

Made: 8th July 2009

Coming into force: 9th July 2009

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of July 2009Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2009 and comes into force on 9th July 2009.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE

### Persons appointed on 9th July 2009

- Paul Lowery

- Michael Patrick Sheridan

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council
