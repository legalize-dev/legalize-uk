# The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987

Made: 10th July 1987

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 2(2) of the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 (Commencement) Order 1987.

###### 2

The Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 shall come into force on 1st August 1987.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 10th July 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Gaming (Amendment) Act 1987 into force on 1st August 1987.
