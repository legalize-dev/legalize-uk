# The Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1999

Made: 14th January 1999

Laid before Parliament: 21st January 1999

Coming into force: 15th February 1999

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with the sea fishing industry in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 5(1), 15(3), 20(1) and 22(2)(a) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 15th February 1999.

#### Variation of the Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1990

###### 2

- (1) The Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1990[^f00002] shall be varied in accordance with the following paragraphs of this article.

- (2) In article 2 (prohibition) after the words “(*Dicentrarchus labrax*)” there shall be inserted the following words–

> > - , or fishing for any species of sea-fish using sand-eels (*Ammodytidae*) as bait,

- (3) For the Schedule there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1992[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/1156](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1156)) (“the principal Order”).

The Order varies the principal Order to prohibit fishing for sea fish using sand eels for bait in the areas, and during the periods, specified in the Schedule to the Order (article 2(2)).

The Order substitutes a new Schedule for the Schedule set out in the principal Order (article 2(3)).

The Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1992 is revoked (article 3).

A single regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to both this Order and the Sea Fish (Specified Sea Area) (Regulation of Nets and Prohibition of Fishing Methods) (Variation) Order 1999 (S.I.
