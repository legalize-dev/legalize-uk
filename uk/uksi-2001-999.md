# The Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order (No. 2) 2001

Made: 23rd April 2001

Laid before Parliament: 30th April 2001

Coming into force: 26th May 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by paragraph 12(6) of Schedule 1 to the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order (No. 2) 2001 and shall come into force on 26th May 2001.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order 2001[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

John Reid — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 23rd April 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the order)

This order revokes the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order 2001.

The revoked order amended the financial year of the Parades Commission for Northern Ireland in paragraph 12(5) of Schedule 1 to the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998.

The relevant provisions determining the financial year of the Parades Commission for Northern Ireland are found in the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Accounts and Audit) Order 1998 ([S.I. 2001/851](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/851)).
