# The Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2007

Made: 29th March 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 148(3) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2007.

###### 2

Section 4 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (Guarantee of continued judicial independence: Northern Ireland) shall come into force on 8th May 2007.

## Signed

David Hanson — Minister of State — 29th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 4 of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 on 8th May 2007.
