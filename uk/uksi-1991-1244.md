# The Representation of the People Act 1990 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1991

Made: 22nd May 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 2(2) of theRepresentation of the People Act 1990[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People Act 1990(Commencement No. 1) Order 1991.

###### 2

The Representation of the People Act 1990 shall come into force inGreat Britain on 10th June 1991.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 22nd May 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions of the Representation of the PeopleAct 1990 into force in Great Britain on 10th June 1991.
