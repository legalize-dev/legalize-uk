# The A127 Trunk Road (M25 To Rayleigh Section and Slip Roads) (Detrunking) Order 1997

Made: 5th March 1997

Coming into force: 27th March 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A127 Trunk Road (M25 to Rayleigh Section and Slip Roads) (Detrunking) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 27th March 1997.

###### 2

In this Order—

- (i) “principal road” as a classification for a highway, means that the highway is a principal road for the purposes of enactments or instruments which refer to highways classified as principal roads and is also classified for the purpose of every other enactment and instrument which refers to highways classified by the Secretary of State;

- (ii) “the trunk road” means the A127 Trunk Road; and

- (iii) “the slip roads” means the slip roads described in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3

The length of the trunk road and the lengths of the slip roads shall cease to be trunk roads and shall be classified as principal roads from the date when this Order comes into force.
