# The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

Made: 18th January 2017

Coming into force: 31st January 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 97(1) of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 31st January 2017.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are amended by substituting for the definition of “Technical Instructions” in regulation 3(1)—

> > - “Technical Instructions” means the 2017–2018 English language edition of the Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air[^f00003], approved and published by decision of the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organisation;

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) Regulations 2015[^f00004] are revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Ahmad of Wimbledon — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 18th January 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations 2002.

The definition of “Technical Instructions” is replaced by a definition referring to the 2017-2018 English language edition of the Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods, approved and published by decision of the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organisation.

The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 are revoked.

An Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as there is no material effect on business, charities or the voluntary sector.
