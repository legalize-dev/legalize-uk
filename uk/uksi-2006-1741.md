# The Nottingham University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2006

Made: 1st July 2006

Coming into force: 20th July 2006

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 11(1) and (2) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001]:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Nottingham University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 20th July 2006.

- (2) In this Order —

- “the Act” means the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990; and

- “the Trust” means the Nottingham University Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00002].

#### Appointment of trustees

###### 2

- (1) The Secretary of State shall appoint trustees for the Trust for the purpose of exercising the functions described in section 11(1) of the Act (trust funds and trustees of NHS trusts).

- (2) The appointment of any such trustee shall be for such a period, not exceeding 4 years, as the Secretary of State may specify on making the appointment.

#### Termination and suspension of appointment

###### 3

Where the Secretary of State is of the opinion that it is not in the interests of the Trust or the health service that a trustee for the Trust should continue to hold that office, she may forthwith terminate the appointment and, pending her decision, may suspend any such trustee from such office.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Andrew Burnham — Minister of State — Department of Health — 1st July 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the Secretary of State for Health to appoint trustees for Nottingham University Hospitals National Health Service Trust to hold trust property for the purposes of the Trust or for any other purpose relating to the NHS, and for their removal or suspension from office.
