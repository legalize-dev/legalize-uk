# Licensing Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004

Made: 9th September 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 201(2) of the Licensing Act 2003[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Licensing Act 2003 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2004.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Licensing Act 2003;

- “the first appointed day” means 7th February 2005[^f00002].

#### Commencement

###### 2

- (1) The provisions of the Licensing Act 2003 specified in the Schedule to this Order, to the extent specified in the Schedule, shall come into force on the first appointed day.

- (2) Sections 6 and 9(1) and (3) shall come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the fifth Commencement Order made by the Secretary of State under the Licensing Act 2003 (“the Act”). The Order brings into force those provisions of the Act specified in article 2(2) and listed in the Schedule to the Order, to the extent specified. Those provisions are concerned with enabling licensing authorities to make the necessary arrangements for the application for and conversion of existing licences and club certificates and the variation of new licences and certificates as provided for in Schedule 8 to the Act, during the transitional period, and for applicants to make such applications, from the first appointed day (SI [2004/1739](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1739) appointed 7th February 2005 as this day) within a period of six months.

Further, the provisions of the Act which provide for the application for premises licences, club premises certificates and provisional statements and their determination are brought into force with effect from the first appointed day to enable applications for new authorisations to be made in the transitional period.
