# The Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) (Amendment) Order 2006

Made: 23rd February 2006

Laid before Parliament: 3rd March 2006

Coming into force: 31st March 2006

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 28 and 29(1)(b) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) (Amendment) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 31st March 2006.

###### 2

The Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) Order 1993[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) Omit Article 3.

- (b) In Schedule 1, omit “Workers' Educational Association, London E2”.

## Signed

Bill Rammell — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 23rd February 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Education (Designated Institutions in Further Education) Order 1993, by removing the Workers' Educational Association from the scope of that Order. It should be read in conjunction with the Workers' Educational Association (Designated Institution in Further Education) Order 2006 (S.I. [2006/409](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/409)), which redesignates the reconstituted institution for the purposes of section 28 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
