# The Financial Services Act 1986 (Extension of Scope of Act) Order 1991

Made: 30th April 1991

Laid before Parliament: 30th April 1991

Coming into force: 1st June 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 2 of the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Financial Services Act 1986 (Extension of Scope of Act) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st June 1991.

- (2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Financial Services Act 1986.

#### Extension of Scope of Act

###### 2

The Note to paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act shall be amended by the substitution of the following for the words from “but does not include” onwards—

> > - but does not, except in relation to any shares of a class defined as deferred shares for the purposes of section 119 of the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00002], include a building society incorporated under the law of, or of any part of, the United Kingdom, nor does it include an open-ended investment company or any body incorporated under the law of, or of any part of, the United Kingdom relating to industrial and provident societies or credit unions.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 30th April 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Financial Services Act 1986 so that shares of a class defined as deferred shares for the purposes of section 119 of the Building Societies Act 1986 are regarded as investments for the purposes of the Financial Services Act 1986.
