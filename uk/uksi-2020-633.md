# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2020

Made: 23rd June 2020

Coming into force: 24th June 2020

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 23rd day of June 2020Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2020 and comes into force on 24th June 2020.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — Persons Appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 24th June 2020

- Thomas Julian Dominic Anthony

- Jennifer Gibbs

- Timothy Jenner

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
