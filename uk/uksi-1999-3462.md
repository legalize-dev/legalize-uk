# The Land Registration (No. 3) Rules 1999

Made: 11th December 1999

Laid before Parliament: 30th December 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

The Lord Chancellor, with the advice and assistance of the Rule Committee appointed in pursuance of section 144 of the Land Registration Act 1925[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section, hereby makes the following rules:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These rules may be cited as the Land Registration (No. 3) Rules 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

#### Amendments to the Land Registration Rules 1925

###### 2

The Land Registration Rules 1925[^f00002] have effect subject to the substitution of the following for rule 247–

> > (247) On the first registration of land, and on a subsequent change of proprietorship of the land, the registrar shall whenever practicable enter in the register the price paid or value declared but that entry shall only remain until there is a change of proprietorship of the land.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, C — Dated 11th December 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These rules amend the Land Registration Rules 1925 so as to restore the requirement to enter on the register the price paid or value declared in respect of a registered title.
