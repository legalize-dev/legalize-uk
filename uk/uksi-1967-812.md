# The Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order 1967

Made: 24th May 1967

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of May 1967

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 15(1) of the Visiting Forces Act 1952, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

#### Citation and construction

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order 1967 and shall be construed as one with the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Orders 1954 to 1962[^f00001]

- (2) The Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Orders 1954 to 1962 and this Order may be cited together as the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Orders 1954 to 1967.

- (3) This Order shall be published by the Administrator of the Turks and Caicos Islands in the Turks and Caicos Islands Gazette.

#### Amendment of Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Order 1954

###### 2

The Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Order 1954[^f00002], as amended by the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order 1954[^f00003], by the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order 1956[^f00004], by the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order 1958[^f00005], by the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order 1959[^f00006] and by the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia) Order 1962[^f00007], is further amended—

- (a) by the deletion from paragraph (c) of section 1 of the words “Visiting Forces Act (Application to Colonies) Orders 1954 to 1959” and the substitution therefor of the words “Visiting Forces Act (Application to Colonies) Orders 1954 to 1967”; and

- (b) by the addition in the appropriate alphabetical order of the words “Turks and Caicos Islands” to the list of territories mentioned in the Schedule.

## Signed

W. G. Agnew

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 1(1) of the Visiting Forces Act 1952 provides that references in the Act to a country to which a provision of the Act applies are references to Commonwealth countries and to any country designated for the purposes of that provision by Order in Council. The Visiting Forces (Designation) Order 1954 designates certain countries including the United States of America and the provisions of that Order are, subject to certain modifications, extended by the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Order 1954 to certain colonial territories in so far as those provisions relate to the United States of America. This Order extends the provisions of the last mentioned Order, as amended, to the Turks and Caicos Islands.
