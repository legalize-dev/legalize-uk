# The A406 Trunk Road (Hanger Lane, Ealing) (Prohibition of Left Turn) Order 1987

Made: 26th March 1987

Coming into force: 1st April 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of the Road and Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Secretary of State for the Home Department, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A406 Trunk Road (Hanger Lane, Ealing) (Prohibition of Left Turn) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st April 1987.

###### 2

In this Order “Hanger Lane” means that length of the London North Circular Trunk Road (A406), in the London Borough of Ealing, known as Hanger Lane.

###### 3

Save as provided in article 4 of this Order, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in–

- (a) Hanger Lane, or

- (b) Abbey Parade

to make a left turn into Twyford Abbey Road during the period between 0700 hours and 0930 hours on Monday to Friday inclusive in any week.

###### 4

Nothing in this Order shall apply to any vehicle being used on any day which is a bank holiday under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971[^f00002] or for fire brigade, police or ambulance purposes, or to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State.

John Dawson — An Assistant Chief Engineer in the — Department of Transport — 26th March 1987
