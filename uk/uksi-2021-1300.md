# The UK Statistics (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021

Made: 22nd November 2021

Laid before Parliament: 23rd November 2021

Coming into force: 15th December 2021

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to the 2018 Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

## PART 1 — Introduction

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the UK Statistics (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021 and come into force on the 22nd day after the day on which they are laid.

## PART 2 — Amendment to primary legislation

#### Amendment of the Data Protection Act 2018

###### 2

In paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 1 to the Data Protection Act 2018[^f00003], in the definition of “social protection”, for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

## PART 3 — Amendments to subordinate legislation

#### Amendment of the Public Contracts Regulations 2015

###### 3

In Schedule 2 to the Public Contracts Regulations 2015[^f00004] (activities constituting works), for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

#### Amendment of the Electricity Supplier Obligations (Amendment & Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2015

###### 4

In regulation 2(1) of the Electricity Supplier Obligations (Amendment & Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2015[^f00005] (interpretation), in the definition of “NACE Rev 2”, for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

#### Amendment of the Concession Contracts Regulations 2016

###### 5

In Schedule 1 to the Concession Contracts Regulations 2016[^f00006] (activities constituting works), for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

#### Amendment of the Utilities Contracts Regulations 2016

###### 6

In Schedule 1 to the Utilities Contracts Regulations 2016[^f00007] (activities constituting works), for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

## PART 4 — Revocation of retained direct EU legislation and saving

#### Revocation of retained direct EU legislation and saving

###### 7

- (1) The legislation set out in Schedule 1, so far as it forms part of domestic law by virtue of section 3(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00008], is revoked.

- (2) The points in Annex XXI to the EEA agreement, as it forms part of domestic law, set out in Schedule 2 are omitted.

- (3) Notwithstanding the revocation of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2223/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/2223), the reference to paragraph 2.48 of Annex A to that Regulation in section 17(3B)(a) (power to obtain information) of the Bank of England Act 1998[^f00009] is to be read as reference to that paragraph as it had effect immediately before the Regulation was revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) (“the 2018 Act”) to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies arising from the withdrawal of the UK from the European Union. In particular, these Regulations address deficiencies under paragraphs (a), (b), (c), (d) and (g) of section 8(2) of the 2018 Act.

These Regulations also make consequential provision in exercise of the power conferred by section 41(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act [2020 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/1) in order to update references to “exit day” to “IP completion day” as a result of the creation of the implementation period by that Act.

These Regulations make amendments to legislation in the field of statistics and are made subsequent to the UK Statistics (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/489](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/489)). Part 2 amends primary legislation and Part 3 amends subordinate legislation. Part 4 revokes the EU regulations and EU decisions set out in Schedule 1. Part 4 also includes a saving provision, and amends Annex XXI to the EEA agreement as set out in Schedule 2.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
