# The Extradition (Taking of Hostages) (Amendment) Order 1987

Made: 26th November 1987

Laid before Parliament: 4th December 1987

Coming into force: 1st January 1988

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 26th day of November 1987 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 2, 17 and 21 of the Extradition Act 1870[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the Taking of Hostages Act 1982[^f00002], or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Extradition (Taking of Hostages) (Amendment) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 1st January 1988.

###### 2

The Extradition (Taking of Hostages) Order 1985[^f00003] shall be amended by adding to Schedule 2 (which names the foreign States with which the United Kingdom has extradition treaties in force and which are parties to the International Convention against the Taking of Hostages opened for signature at New York on 18th December 1979[^f00004]) the following entry—

| *State* | *Date of Extradition Treaty* | *Date of Entry into Force of Convention* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Austria | 9th January 1963 | 21st September 1986 |

###### 3

The Extradition (Taking of Hostages) Order 1985 shall be amended by adding to Part I of Schedule 3 (which names the foreign States with which the United Kingdom has no extradition treaties in force and which are parties to the International Convention against the Taking of Hostages opened for signature at New York on 18th December 1979) the following entries—

| *State* | *Date of Entry into Force of Convention* |
| --- | --- |
| Japan | 8th July 1987 |
| Senegal | 9th April 1987 |
| Togo | 24th August 1986 |

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Extradition (Taking of Hostages) Order 1985 by adding to Schedule 2 (which names the foreign states with which the United Kingdom has extradition treaties in force and which are parties to the International Convention against the Taking of Hostages opened for signature at New York on 18th December 1979) an entry for Austria, and by adding to Part I of Schedule 3 (which names the foreign states with which the United Kingdom has no extradition treaties in force and which are parties to the Convention) entries for Japan, Senegal and Togo.
