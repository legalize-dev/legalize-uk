# The Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made: 27th April 1993

Laid before Parliament: 6th May 1993

Coming into force: 1st June 1993

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of his powers under sections 73(1)(a), (3), (4) and (6)(a) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001] and of all others powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) (Third Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 1st June 1993.

###### 2

The Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) Regulations 1991[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Caithness — Minister of State, — Department of Transport — 23rd April 1993

We consent to the making of these Regulations

Tim Wood — Nicholas Baker — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 27th April 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Civil Aviation (Navigation Services Charges) Regulations 1991. The following change is made. The charge payable to the CAA by an operator of a helicopter which flies from any place in the United Kingdom to a vessel or an off-shore installation within the area of the Southern North Sea described in regulation 7(4) of the principal Regulations is increased from £46 to £68.
