# The Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No.13) Order 2003

Made: 17th March 2003

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 170(4) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2003.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 5 (charges) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

## Signed

Beverley Hughes — Minister of State — 17th March 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2003 section 5 (charges) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.
