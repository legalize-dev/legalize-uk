# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (General Levy) (Revocation) Regulations 2020

Made: 25th March 2020

Laid before Parliament: 27th March 2020

Coming into force: 31st March 2020

The Secretary of State considers that consultation in respect of these Regulations is inexpedient because of urgency[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (General Levy) (Revocation) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 31st March 2020.

#### Revocation of the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (General Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

###### 2

The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (General Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2020[^f00003] are revoked.

## Signed

Thérèse Coffey — Secretary of State for Work and Pensions — 25th March 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (General Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/195](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/195)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
