# The Education (The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2001

Made: 5th February 2001

Laid before Parliament: 9th February 2001

Coming into force: 1st August 2001

Now therefore the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 122A of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation) (Transfer to the Higher Education Sector) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st August 2001.

###### 2

On the date on which this Order comes into force The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation shall cease to be a further education corporation and shall become a higher education corporation.

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State — Department for Education and Employment — 5th February 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the transfer of The Arts Institute at Bournemouth Further Education Corporation to the higher education sector. On 1st August 2001 that corporation becomes a higher education corporation.
