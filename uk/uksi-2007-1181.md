# The Postal Services Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2007

Made: 5th April 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 130(1) of the Postal Services Act 2000[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Postal Services Act 2000 (Commencement No.5) Order 2007.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The provisions of section 127(6) of and Schedule 9 to the Postal Services Act 2000 shall come into force, insofar as they relate to the repeals specified in the Schedule to this Order, on 1st May 2007.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS COMING INTO FORCE ON 1st MAY 2007

| Reference | Short title | Extent of the repeal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1969 c.48](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/48) | Post Office Act 1969 | Sections 6, 74(2), 75(1), 87 and Schedule 1 |
| [1975 c.24](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/24) | House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975 | In Schedule 1, in Part 2, the entry relating to the Post Office |

## Signed

Jim Fitzpatrick — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Postal Services — Department of Trade and Industry — 5th April 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force, on 1st May 2007, certain repeals of enactments made by the Postal Services Act 2000 as specified in the Schedule to the Order.
