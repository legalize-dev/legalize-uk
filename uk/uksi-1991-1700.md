# The Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991

Made: 24th July 1991

Laid before Parliament: 1st August 1991

Coming into force: 22nd August 1991

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of July 1991 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 5(1) of the Extradition Act 1989[^f00001] or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Extradition (Designated Commonwealth Countries) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 22nd August 1991.

###### 2

The countries listed in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby designated for the purposes of section 1(2) of the Extradition Act 1989.

###### 3

The Orders listed in Schedule 2 to this Order are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates certain Commonwealth countries for the time being mentioned in Schedule 3 to the British Nationality Act [1981 (c. 61)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/61) for the purposes of the Extradition Act 1989.
