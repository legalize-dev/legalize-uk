# The Income Tax (Tax Treatment of Scottish Carer Supplement and Exemption of Carer’s Additional Person Payment) Regulations 2026

Made: 3rd February 2026

Laid before the House of Commons: 5th February 2026

Coming into force: 15th March 2026

The Treasury make these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) and (2)(d) of the Finance Act 2020[^f00001] and section 27(1) and (4)(b) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2023[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Tax Treatment of Scottish Carer Supplement and Exemption of Carer’s Additional Person Payment) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 15th March 2026.

#### Amendments to the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003

###### 2

- (1) ITEPA 2003[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In section 658(4)[^f00004] for “incapacity benefit income support,” substitute “incapacity benefit, income support, Scottish Carer Supplement,”.

- (3) In section 660(1)[^f00005], in Table A (taxable benefits: UK benefits— Table A), at the appropriate place insert—

| Scottish Carer Supplement | CA(CSP)(S)R 2023 [^f00006] | Regulation 14A [^f00007] |
| --- | --- | --- |

- (4) In section 661(1)[^f00008], at the appropriate place insert—

> > - Scottish Carer Supplement,

- (5) In section 677(1)[^f00009], in Table B (UK social security benefits wholly exempt from tax: Table B) in Part 2 (benefits payable under regulations), at the appropriate place insert—

| Carer's Additional Person Payment | CA(CSP)(S)R 2023 | Regulation 14B[^f00010] |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Gen Kitchen — Christian Wakeford — Two of the Lords Commissioners of His Majesty’s Treasury — 3rd February 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations make amendments to Part 10 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act [2003 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/1) in consequence of two new social security benefits introduced by the Carer’s Assistance (Miscellaneous and Consequential Amendments, Revocation, Transitional and Saving Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2025 ([S.S.I. 2025/340](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2025/340)) from 15th March 2026. The amendments ensure the Scottish Carer Supplement is chargeable to income tax and exempt the Carer’s Additional Person Payment from income tax.
