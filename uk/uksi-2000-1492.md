# The Housing Grants (Additional Purposes) (England) Order 2000

Made: 6th June 2000

Laid before Parliament: 13th June 2000

Coming into force: 4th July 2000

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 12(1)(i), 17(1)(j), 27(1)(j) and 146(2) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Housing Grants (Additional Purposes) (England) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 4th July 2000.

- (2) This Order extends to England only.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “the Act” means the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996.

#### Renovation grants

###### 3

For the purposes of section 12 of the Act (renovation grants: purposes for which grant may be given), an additional purpose specified is the following—

- to improve energy efficiency.

#### Common parts grants

###### 4

For the purposes of section 17 of the Act (common parts grant: purposes for which grant may be given), an additional purpose specified is the following—

- to improve energy efficiency.

#### HMO grants

###### 5

For the purposes of section 27 of the Act (HMO grants: purposes for which grant may be given), an additional purpose specified is the following—

- to improve energy efficiency.

#### Application

###### 6

This Order shall not have effect in relation to an application for a grant made before the date on which this Order comes into force.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Chris Mullin — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 6th June 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies an additional purpose for which a grant under sections 12 (renovation grants), 17 (common parts grants) and 27 (HMO grants) of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 may be given. That additional purpose is, in each case, to improve energy efficiency.

Works specified under section 12 or 27 become relevant works for the purposes of section 169 of the Local Government and Housing Act [1989 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/42) (powers of local authorities and Secretary of State as respects professional &c. services in relation to works).

This Order extends to England only.
