# The Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004 (Commencement) Order 2004

Made: 8th December 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 6(3) of the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004 (Commencement) Order 2004.

###### 2

9th December 2004 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe, — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Postal Services and Consumers — Department of Trade and Industry — 8th December 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 9th December 2004 the Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004.
