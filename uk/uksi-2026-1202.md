# The Traffic Orders and Notices (Provision of Digital Information) (England) Regulations 2026

Made: 16th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 19th November 2026

Coming into force

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93) and [(7) to (9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93) and [97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/97)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/97) of the [Automated Vehicles Act 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents) (“*the 2024 Act*”)[^f00001].

In accordance with [section 93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93) of [the 2024 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents), the Secretary of State considers that the provision of information in accordance with these Regulations will help to enable information about the effects of traffic regulation measures[^f00002] to be communicated to or acted upon by authorised automated vehicles[^f00003] or electronic equipment designed to undertake or facilitate the driving of other vehicles on roads.

The Secretary of State has consulted with representative organisations in accordance with [section 97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/97)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/97) of [the 2024 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents).

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Traffic Orders and Notices (Provision of Digital Information) (England) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 11th February 2027, subject as follows.

- (3) Regulations 7 (additional information to be provided for certain section 14 orders and notices from 2nd October 2028), 9(4)(b) and (c) and 9(5)(b) and (c) (times at which the prescribed information is to be submitted), come into force on 2nd October 2028.

- (4) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations—

- “*the 1984 Act*” means the [Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents)[^f00004];

- “*the 1991 Act*” means the [New Roads and Street Works Act 1991](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/contents)[^f00005];

- “*the 2024 Act*” means the [Automated Vehicles Act 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents)[^f00006];

- “*the D-TRO data specification*” means the set of specifications as published from time to time by the Secretary of State as to the manner and form in which information relating to traffic regulation measures must be provided[^f00007];

- “*provision*” in relation to a traffic regulation measure means a provision which is included in a traffic regulation measure and which prohibits, restricts or regulates the use of a road[^f00008] in any way;

- “*the traffic regulation measures digital service*” means the digital service designated as such by the Secretary of State to receive information provided in accordance with these Regulations about relevant traffic regulation measures[^f00009];

- “*working day*” means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday or any day which, under the [Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/80/contents)[^f00010], is a bank holiday in England and Wales.

#### Application

###### 3

- (1) Subject to [paragraphs (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/3/2/made) to (4), these Regulations apply to any traffic regulation measure made by a traffic regulation authority[^f00011] after the commencement date.

- (2) Where a traffic regulation measure to which [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/3/1/made) applies amends a traffic regulation measure which was made before the commencement date, the requirements in these Regulations also apply to the traffic regulation measure being amended.

- (3) Where, by virtue of paragraph (2), these Regulations apply to a traffic regulation measure which was made before the commencement date, the requirements in these Regulations do not apply to any provision within that traffic regulation measure which is not being amended.

- (4) These Regulations do not apply to—

- (a) an order made under section 1(1)(a), 1(1)(b), 6 or 14(1)(b) of the 1984 Act by virtue of [section 22C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/22C) (terrorism) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents)[^f00012], or

- (b) a notice issued under [section 14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/36/section/14)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/36/section/14)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/36/section/14) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/36/contents) by virtue of [section 22C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/36/section/22C) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/36/contents).

- (5) In [this regulation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/3/made), “the commencement date” means 11th February 2027.

#### Information to be provided in respect of a traffic regulation measure and its provisions

###### 4

- (1) In respect of a relevant traffic regulation measure, the prescribed information for the purposes of [section 93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93) of [the 2024 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents) is—

- (a) its title;

- (b) the date on which it is made;

- (c) the date on which it comes into force;

- (d) its duration or anticipated duration;

- (e) a statement of the general nature of the prohibition, regulation or restriction it imposes;

- (f) where relevant, the dates, or anticipated dates, on which and the times, or anticipated times, at which it is to have effect;

- (g) if it varies, revokes, applies or suspends another traffic regulation measure, the unique D-TRO identifier, if there is one, of the measure being varied, revoked, applied or suspended.

- (2) In respect of each provision included in a relevant traffic regulation measure, the prescribed information for the purposes of [section 93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93) of [the 2024 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents) is, so far as it is applicable—

- (a) the date on which it comes into force;

- (b) identification of the prohibition, regulation or restriction to which it gives effect;

- (c) the dates, or anticipated dates, on which it is to have effect;

- (d) the times, or anticipated times, at which it is to have effect;

- (e) details of the road or, if more than one, each road, to which it applies;

- (f) details of any alternative route or, if more than one, each alternative route, available for diverted traffic, including—

- (i) the names of the roads included in the alternative route, where a road has a name;

- (ii) the numbers of the roads included in the alternative route, where a road has a number;

- (iii) the unique street reference numbers comprising the alternative route, where such numbers are available,

provided in the sequence in which the roads form the alternative route;

- (g) whether it relates to a road for which the traffic regulation authority is not the highway authority or the traffic authority;

- (h) whether it relates to a road—

- (i) which is subject to a concession within the meaning given by [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/section/1)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/section/1) of [the 1991 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/contents), or

- (ii) in respect of which an assignation has been granted under [section 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/section/28)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/section/28) of [the 1991 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/contents);

- (i) whether it relates to a road on which a tramcar or trolley vehicle service is provided;

- (j) whether it relates to—

- (i) a road outside Greater London which is included in the route of a local service, or

- (ii) a road in Greater London which is included in the route of a London bus service;

- (k) whether it relates to a footpath, bridleway, cycle track, restricted byway or byway open to all traffic;

- (l) any conditions or exceptions to which it is subject;

- (m) details of any speed limits which are to apply;

- (n) details of any charges which are to apply;

- (o) if it varies, revokes, applies or suspends another provision, the unique D-TRO identifier, if there is one, of the provision being varied, revoked, applied or suspended.

- (3) The information provided under [paragraph (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/made)[(e)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/e/made) must include, in respect of each road affected by the provision—

- (a) the Ordnance Survey grid references;

- (b) the unique street reference number, if it has one;

- (c) any other information relating to the location as is specified in the D-TRO data specification.

- (4) In [this regulation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made)—

- “*bridleway*” has the same meaning as in [section 142](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/142)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/142) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents);

- “*byway open to all traffic*” and “*cycle track*” have the same meaning as in [section 15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/15) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents);

- “*footpath*” has the same meaning as in sections 142(1) and 15[^f00013] of the 1984 Act;

- “*highway authority*” has the same meaning as in the [Highways Act 1980](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/66/contents)[^f00014];

- “*local service*” has the meaning given by [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/67/section/2) of the [Transport Act 1985](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/67/contents)[^f00015];

- “*London bus service*” means a local service with one or more stopping places in Greater London which is not an excursion or tour as defined by [section 137](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/67/section/137) of the [Transport Act 1985](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/67/contents)[^f00016];

- “*restricted byway*” has the same meaning as in [section 142](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/142)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/142) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents)[^f00017];

- “*traffic authority*” has the meaning given by [section 121A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/121A) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents)[^f00018];

- “*tramcar*” has the same meaning as in [section 141A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/141A) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents)[^f00019];

- “*trolley vehicle*” means a mechanically propelled vehicle adapted for use on roads without rails under power transmitted to it from some external source (whether or not there is in addition a source of power on board the vehicle) and does not include a duobus as defined in the [Tramcars and Trolley Vehicles (Modification of Enactments) Regulations 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1217/contents)[^f00020];

- “*unique D-TRO identifier*” means an identification number generated by the traffic regulation measures digital service and attributed to a traffic regulation measure or provision;

- “*unique street reference number*” means the reference number allocated to a street in compliance with the system of street referencing specified in Part 1 of the publication issued by the British Standards Institution on 31st December 2019 entitled “Spatial data sets for geographical referencing – Specification for a street gazetteer” under reference No. BS 7666-1 2019[^f00021] as revised or reissued from time to time, and for the purposes of this definition, “*street*” has the same meaning as in [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/part/3) of [the 1991 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/contents)[^f00022].

#### Information to be provided about restrictions etc.

###### 5

- (1) A traffic regulation authority must provide details of each restriction, prohibition, requirement or exception to which a provision of a traffic regulation measure is subject, including, so far as relevant—

- (a) the specified vehicle classes affected or excepted;

- (b) the dimension limits of any affected or excepted vehicles, including any specified maximum vehicle height, width, length or mass;

- (c) where the carriage of specified goods is affected or excepted, the goods so affected or excepted;

- (d) where vehicles of a specified fuel type are affected or excepted, the fuel type affected or excepted;

- (e) where vehicles which exceed, or do not exceed, specified emission limits are affected or excepted, details of those limits;

- (f) where any speed limits are specified, details of any conditions or exceptions to which they are to be subject;

- (g) where vehicles are affected or excepted on the basis of specified driver or passenger characteristics, details of those specified characteristics;

- (h) where traffic is required to proceed in a specified direction, or is prohibited from so doing, details of the requirement or prohibition;

- (i) the dates on which, and times at which, the restriction, prohibition, requirement or exception is to have effect, if different to the information provided in relation to the provision under [regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/made)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/c/made) or [(d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/d/made).

- (2) In [this regulation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/5/made)—

- “*specified*” means specified by the traffic regulation authority in a traffic regulation measure, or in any provision;

- “*vehicle class*” is construed as a reference to a class of vehicle defined or described by reference to any characteristics of the vehicle, its use, or to any other circumstances.

#### Additional information to be provided for a section 9 order in certain circumstances

###### 6

- (1) This regulation applies to an order made under section 9 (experimental traffic orders)[^f00023] of the 1984 Act where—

- (a) the operation of the order, or any provision of the order, is modified or suspended in accordance with [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/7/section/10)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/7/section/10)[^f00024] of that Act, or

- (b) any prohibition, regulation or restriction effected by the order, or by any provision of the order, ceases to have effect earlier than the latest date provided under [regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/1/made)[(f)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/1/f/made) or [4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/made)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/c/made).

- (2) The traffic regulation authority must provide the following information—

- (a) where the circumstances in [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/6/1/made)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/6/1/a/made) apply—

- (i) the effect of the modification or suspension, and

- (ii) the planned duration of the modification or suspension;

- (b) where the circumstances in [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/6/1/made)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/6/1/b/made) apply—

- (i) the prohibition, regulation or restriction that has ceased to have effect, and

- (ii) the actual date on which the prohibition, regulation or restriction ceased to have effect.

#### Additional information to be provided for certain section 14 orders and notices from 2nd October 2028

###### 7

- (1) [This regulation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made) applies to an order made, or a notice issued, under [section 14](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/14) (temporary prohibition or restriction on roads) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents)[^f00025] where the traffic regulation authority is making that order or issuing that notice for the purpose of undertaking works itself.

- (2) A traffic regulation authority must, in addition to providing the information prescribed in [regulations 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made) and [5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/5/made), provide—

- (a) each instance of the actual start time of each provision in the order or notice;

- (b) each instance of the actual stop time of each provision in the order or notice.

- (3) In [this regulation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made)—

- “*start time*” means the time at which the restriction or prohibition in the provision starts to have effect;

- “*stop time*” means the time at which the restriction or prohibition in the provision ceases to have effect;

- “*works*” means works for road purposes within the meaning of [section 86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/section/86)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/section/86) of [the 1991 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22/contents).

#### Manner and form of provision of information

###### 8

The traffic regulation authority must provide the information in accordance with [regulations 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made) and [5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/5/made) and, where applicable, [regulations 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/6/made), [7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made) and [10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/10/made), to the Secretary of State—

- (a) electronically to the traffic regulation measures digital service, and

- (b) in accordance with the D-TRO data specification.

#### Times at which the prescribed information is to be submitted

###### 9

- (1) The information prescribed in [regulations 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made) and [5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/5/made) and, where applicable, [regulations 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/6/made) or [7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made) must be provided in accordance with [paragraphs (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/9/2/made) to [(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/9/6/made).

- (2) In respect of an order under section 1 (traffic regulation orders outside Greater London)[^f00026], section 6 (orders similar to traffic regulation orders)[^f00027] or section 84 (speed limits on roads other than restricted roads)[^f00028] of the 1984 Act the information must be provided as soon as practicable after the order has been made, and in any event within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which it is made.

- (3) In respect of an order under [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/9) (experimental traffic orders) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents) the information must be provided—

- (a) as soon as practicable after the order has been made, and in any event within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which it is made;

- (b) where the order is extended under section 9(4)[^f00029] of the 1984 Act, within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which the authority directs that the order is extended;

- (c) where the order is extended under [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/9)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/9)[^f00030] of the 1984 Act, within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which the Secretary of State directs that the order is extended;

- (d) where the operation of the order, or any provision of the order, is modified or suspended in accordance with section 10(2) of the 1984 Act, within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which the order, or provision, is modified or suspended;

- (e) where the prohibition, regulation or restriction effected by the order, or by any provision of the order, ceases to have effect earlier than the date provided under [regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/1/made)[(f)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/1/f/made) or [4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/made)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/2/c/made), within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which the order or the provision of the order ceases to have effect.

- (4) In respect of an order under section 14(1) of the 1984 Act, the information must be provided—

- (a) subject to sub-paragraphs (b) and (c), on the day on which the traffic regulation authority makes the order;

- (b) in relation to the information prescribed in [regulation 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/made)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/a/made), no later than—

- (i) in the case of a start time occurring within the period beginning with 12am and ending with 7.59am, 10am on the same day;

- (ii) in the case of a start time occurring within the period beginning with 8am and ending with 4.30pm, two hours after the start time;

- (iii) in the case of a start time occurring within the period beginning with 4.31pm and ending with 11.59pm, 10am on the next day;

- (c) in relation to the information prescribed in [regulation 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/made)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/b/made), no later than—

- (i) in the case of a stop time occurring within the period beginning with 12am and ending with 7.59am, 10am on the same day;

- (ii) in the case of a stop time occurring within the period beginning with 8am and ending with 4.30pm, two hours after the stop time;

- (iii) in the case of a stop time occurring within the period beginning with 4.31pm and ending with 11.59pm, 10am on the next day;

- (d) where the order is continued by direction of the Secretary of State under section 15(3) or (5) of the 1984 Act, within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which the direction was received.

- (5) In respect of a notice under section 14(2) of the 1984 Act, the information must be provided—

- (a) subject to sub-paragraphs (b) and (c), on the day on which the authority issues the notice in accordance with [regulation 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1215/regulation/10)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1215/regulation/10) of the [Road Traffic (Temporary Restrictions) Procedure Regulations 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1215/contents)[^f00031];

- (b) in relation to the information prescribed in [regulation 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/made)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/a/made), no later than—

- (i) in the case of a start time occurring within the period beginning with 12am and ending with 7.59am, 10am on the same day;

- (ii) in the case of a start time occurring within the period beginning with 8am and ending with 4.30pm, two hours after the start time;

- (iii) in the case of a start time occurring within the period beginning with 4.31pm and ending with 11.59pm, 10am on the next day;

- (c) in relation to the information prescribed in [regulation 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/made)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/2/b/made), no later than—

- (i) in the case of a stop time occurring within the period beginning with 12am and ending with 7.59am, 10am on the same day;

- (ii) in the case of a stop time occurring within the period beginning with 8am and ending with 4.30pm, two hours after the stop time;

- (iii) in the case of a stop time occurring within the period beginning with 4.31pm and ending with 11.59pm, 10am on the next day.

- (6) In respect of an order under section 16A (prohibition or restriction on roads in connection with certain events)[^f00032] of the 1984 Act the information must be provided on the day on which the traffic regulation authority makes the order.

#### Duty to keep submitted information accurate and up to date

###### 10

- (1) Where a traffic regulation authority becomes aware that information it submitted under these Regulations is or has become inaccurate, it must submit the correct information to the Secretary of State within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which it became aware of the inaccuracy.

- (2) A traffic regulation authority must, where it becomes aware of information which it is required to submit under these Regulations which it has not previously submitted, submit that information to the Secretary of State within the period of two working days beginning with the date on which it becomes aware of the information.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations make provision to require traffic regulation authorities in England to provide information to the Secretary of State about the traffic regulation measures that they make. The Secretary of State will, in turn, publish that data in open data format (available via https://www.gov.uk/guidance/digital-traffic-regulation-orders-d-tro-service) so that it can be used for the purposes set out in [section 93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/section/93) of the [Automated Vehicles Act 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/10/contents) (c. 10) (“*the 2024 Act*”).

Traffic regulation measures are orders or notices under any of the following provisions of the [Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents) (c. 27) (“*the 1984 Act*”)—

[section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/1) (traffic regulation orders);

section 6 (orders regulating traffic in Greater London);

section 9 (experimental traffic orders);

section 14 (temporary orders and notices);

section 16A (special event orders);

section 84 (speed limit orders).

[Regulation 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/3/made) provides that, subject to paragraphs (2) to (4), the Regulations apply to any traffic regulation measure made by a traffic regulation authority after the commencement date. Paragraph (2) ensures that where a measure made after commencement amends a measure which was made before commencement, the requirements to provide information under the Regulations also apply to the measure being amended. Paragraph (3) clarifies that the requirements to provide information under the Regulations do not apply to any provision of a traffic regulation measure made before commencement unless that provision is amended. Paragraph (4) excludes anti-terrorism traffic regulation measures from the scope of these Regulations.

[Regulation 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/4/made) sets out the information that a traffic regulation authority must provide in relation to each traffic regulation measure (see paragraph (2)), and each provision of such a measure (see paragraph (3)), that it makes.

[Regulation 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/5/made) sets out the information that a traffic regulation authority must provide in relation to each condition or exception to which a provision of a traffic regulation measure is subject.

[Regulation 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/6/made) specifies additional information that a traffic regulation authority must submit if a prohibition, regulation or restriction effected by an experimental traffic order under [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/9) of [the 1984 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents) ceases to have effect earlier than was anticipated, or if the operation of a section 9 order is modified or suspended under [section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/10)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/section/10) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27/contents).

[Regulation 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/7/made) specifies the additional information that a traffic regulation authority must provide in relation to the actual start and stop times of each provision of a section 14 order or notice in circumstances where the authority is executing the related works itself.

[Regulation 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/8/made) sets out the manner and form in which a traffic regulation authority must submit the prescribed information to the Secretary of State. All information must be submitted electronically and in accordance with the D-TRO data specification which can be accessed at: https://d-tro.dft.gov.uk/dtro.

[Regulation 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/9/made) specifies when a traffic regulation authority must submit information for each type of traffic regulation measure.

[Regulation 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1202/regulation/10/made) requires a traffic regulation authority to submit the correct information where previously submitted information is or has become inaccurate (paragraph (1)), and to submit any new prescribed information of which it becomes aware (paragraph (2)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant impact is foreseen on the private, voluntary or public sectors in the United Kingdom.

An Explanatory Memorandum for this instrument has been published alongside these Regulations at www.legislation.gov.uk.
