## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which apply in England, revoke the Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (England) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2043](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2043)) and remake them with additional fees for conducting tests under [Commission Regulation (EC) No 584/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0584) (OJ No L 162, 21.6.2008, p 3). The fee for taking or supervising the taking of official samples, which will affect all poultry farmers covered by this Regulation is increased from £66 to £76. Otherwise the fees are unchanged.

The Regulations make provision for the Secretary of State to charge fees for activities required under [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1003/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1003) (OJ No L 170, 1.7.2005, p 12), [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1168/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1168) (OJ No L 211, 1.8.2006, p 4), Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) (OJ No L 325, 12.12.2003, p 1), [Commission Regulation (EC) No 646/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0646), [Commission Regulation (EC) No 584/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0584) and the Animal By-Products Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2347](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2347)). The activities and the fees are listed in the table of fees in the Schedule.
