# The Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) Regulations 1990

Made: 21st May 1990

Laid before Parliament: 24th May 1990

Coming into force: 18th June 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of his powers under section 123(2), 125(3), 127(2), and 141 of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002] and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 18th June 1990.

###### 2

The Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) Regulations 1989[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

###### 3

In the Table in regulation 13, for the entry in column (3) in items 4, 6, and 7, there shall be substituted in each case “£146”.

###### 4

For regulation 16, there shall be substituted the following regulation—

> > (16)
>   (1) The prescribed manner of fixing to and exhibiting on a motor car a certificate or a licence for the purposes of section 123(2) of the Act is the manner specified in paragraph (2).
>   (2) The certificate or licence shall be—
>     (a) fixed to and immediately behind the front windscreen of the motor car on its nearside edge, and
>     (b) exhibited so that the particulars on the back of the certificate or licence are clearly visible in daylight from outside the motor car and the particulars on the front of the certificate or licence are clearly visible from the front nearside seat of the vehicle, where fitted.
>   (3) The prescribed form of certificate for the purposes of section 123(2) is a certificate in the form set out in Schedule 3 to these Regulations.

###### 5

In Schedule 3, in the heading, for “Regulation 16” there shall be substituted “Regulations 14 and 16”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Atkins — Parliamentary Under Seary of State, — Department of Transport — 21st May 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amendthe Motor Cars (Driving Instruction) Regulations 1989 by increasing from £110 to £146 the fee payable on an application for:

- (a) the retention of the applicant’s name in the register of approved driving instructors,

- (b) the entry of the applicant’s name to the register following the passing of the prescribed examination, and

- (c) the entry of the applicant’s name to the register without passing the examination by virtue of sections 125(7) or 126(3) of the Road Traffic Act 1988.

The opportunity has been taken to re-draft regulation 16 (exhibition of certificate of registration or licence).
