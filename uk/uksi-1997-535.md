# The Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1997

Made: 27th February 1997

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 1997

Coming into force: 31st March 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(7) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 31st March 1997.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the principal order” means the Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) Order 1987[^f00002]; and

- “the Schedule” means the Schedule to the principal order.

#### Amendment of the principal order

###### 3

The principal order shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

#### Bodies ceasing to be designated

###### 4

Items 30A, 51, 72 and 73 of the Schedule shall be omitted.

#### New designated bodies

###### 5

In the Schedule, after item 1 there shall be inserted the following item—

| 1A Every council constituted under section 21 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00003]. | a Bodies to whom the council may make grants under section 65 of the Health Services and Public Health Act 1968[^f00004]. b Schools or bodies which fulfil the duty of the council under the Education Act 1996[^f00005] with respect to the provision of education, and bodies connected with such schools or bodies. c Bodies which assist and coordinate activities of community bodies within the area of the council. |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Section 19 Minibus (Designated Bodies) Order 1987.

Four bodies cease to be designated under section 19 of the Transport Act 1985, the current names of five designated bodies are substituted for those given in the 1987 Order and the current addresses of nine designated bodies are so substituted (articles 4, 6 and 7).

County and county borough councils in Wales (established under section 21 of the Local Government Act 1972, as substituted by section 2 of the Local Government (Wales) Act 1994) are newly designated as bodies which can grant permits in relation to the use of small buses under section 19 of the 1985 Act (article 5).

Joint Scout and Guide units become a class of body to whom the Scout Association may grant such permits (article 8).

The entries relating to Scottish local authorities have been revised to take account of local government reorganisation in Scotland pursuant to the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (article 9).
