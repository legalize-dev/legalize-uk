# The East Yorkshire Community Healthcare and the Hull and Holderness Community Health National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made: 29th September 1999

Coming into force: 1st October 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the East Yorkshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust and the Hull and Holderness Community Health National Health Service Trust[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the East Yorkshire Community Healthcare and the Hull and Holderness Community Health National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st October 1999.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts

###### 2

The East Yorkshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust, established by the East Yorkshire National Health Service Trust Establishment Order[^f00005] and the Hull and Holderness Community Health National Health Service Trust established by the Hull and Holderness Community Health National Health Service Trust Establishment Order[^f00006] are hereby dissolved, and accordingly those Orders are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Hutton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 29th September 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st October 1999 of the East Yorkshire Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust and the Hull and Holderness Community Health National Health Service Trust.

This Order should be read in conjunction with the Hull and East Riding Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00007].
