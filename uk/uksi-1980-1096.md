# The International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) Miscellaneous Provisions Order 1980

Made: 28th July 1980

Coming into Operation: 18th August 1980

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 28th day of July 1980

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Whereas a draft of this Order has been laid before Parliament in accordance with section 10(1) of the International Organisations Act 1968 (hereinafter referred to as the Act) and has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament:

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by sections 1 and 10(3) of the Act or otherwise in Her Majesty vested, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) Miscellaneous Provisions Order 1980 and shall come into operation on 18th August 1980.

###### 2

The Orders mentioned in the Schedule to this Order shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes amendments to the Orders mentioned in the Schedule thereto consequential upon the changes in terminology for duties of customs and excise introduced by the Finance (No. 2) Act 1975 (c. 2) and the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 (c. 45).
