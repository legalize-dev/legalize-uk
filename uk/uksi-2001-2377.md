# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2001

Made: 2nd July 2001

Laid before Parliament: 3rd July 2001

Coming into force: 4th July 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) ( Amendment) Order 2001 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is laid before Parliament.

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Immigration (Destination of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3

The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2000[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE — DESIGNATED INSTRUMENTS

- Resolution 1343 (2001) of 7th March 2001 (Liberia).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 in three ways. Firstly, Resolution 1343 (2001) of 7th March 2001 is added to the list of resolutions made by the Security Council of the United Nations which are designated for the purposes of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971 (as inserted by section 8 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999). Secondly, Common Position 2001/155/CFSP of 26th February 2001 is added to the list of instruments made by the Council of the European Union which are designated for the purpose of section 8B of the Immigration Act 1971. Thirdly, Common Position 1998/725/CFSP of 14th December 1998 is removed from that list because it has been repealed by Common Position 2001/155/CFSP. Any person named by or under a designated instrument, or of a description specified in it, will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000). This Order also revokes the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2000, which amended that list of designated instruments.
