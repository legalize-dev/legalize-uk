# The Welsh Water Authority (Pendine Water Supply) Order 1989

Made: 31st August 1989

Coming into force: 31st August 1989

The Secretary of State for Wales, on the application of the Welsh Water Authority, being statutory water undertakers[^f00001], and in exercise of powers conferred by sections 23 and 59(1)[^f00002] of the Water Act 1945[^f00003] and now vested in him[^f00004], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This order may be cited as the Welsh Water Authority (Pendine Water Supply) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 31st August 1989.

- (2) This order shall be included among the enactments which may be cited together as the Welsh Water Authority Orders 1976 to 1989.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this order:–

- “the Authority” means the Welsh Water Authority;

- “the deposited plans” means the two plans prepared in duplicate signed by the authority of the Secretary of State, numbered 1 and 2 respectively and marked“Plan referred to in the Welsh Water Authority (Pendine Water Supply) Order 1989” one duplicate of each of which is deposited and available for inspection at the offices of the Authority at Cambrian Way, Brecon and the other at the Welsh Office, Cathays Park, Cardiff;

- “the undertaking” means the water undertaking of the Authority as from time to time authorised by any enactment.

#### Power to acquire waterworks

###### 3

- (1) The Authority may acquire by agreement and thereafter continue and maintain–

- (a) the service reservoir shown coloured green on number 1 of the deposited plans; and

- (b) the mains, pipes and apparatus the approximate lines and positions of which are shown by blue lines on numbers 1 and 2 of the deposited plans.

- (2) The said service reservoir, mains, pipes and apparatus when acquired by the Authority shall for all purposes form part of the undertaking.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Alwyn H. H. Jones — An Assistant Secretary, Welsh Office — 31st August 1989
