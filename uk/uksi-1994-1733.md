# The Civil Aviation (Personnel Licences) Order 1992 (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made: 30th June 1994

Laid before Parliament: 1st July 1994

Coming into force: 1st July 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001]for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002]in relation to matters relating to air transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Personnel Licences) Order 1992 (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

###### 2

- (1) The Civil Aviation (Personnel Licences) Order 1992[^f00003]shall be amended as follows.

- (2) After article 1 there shall be inserted the following article

> > (1A) In this Order “an EEA State” means a State which is a Contracting Party to the Agreement on the European Economic Area signed at Oporto on 2nd May 1992[^f00004]as adjusted by the Protocol signed at Brussels on 17th March 1993[^f00005]but until that Agreement comes into force in relation to Liechtenstein does not include the State of Liechtenstein.

- (3) In article 2 for the words “a member State” there shall be substituted the words “an EEA State”.

###### 3

This Order extends to Northern Ireland only.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Mackay of Ardbrecknish — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — 30th June 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend the Civil Aviation (Personnel Licences) Order 1992 by amending the reference, in article 2, o a national of a member State so that it refersto a national of an EEA State so as to take account of the amendment of the Annexes to the EEA Agreement done by the Decision of the EEA Joint Committee No. 7/94(a).

This Order extends to Northern Ireland only.
