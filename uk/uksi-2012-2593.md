## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes amendments consequential upon the enactment in Jersey of the Civil Partnership (Jersey) Law 2012 (L.4/2012).

The Order amends:

- the Immigration (Jersey) Order 1993 ([SI 1993/1797](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1797)) which extended to Jersey, subject to modifications, provisions of the Immigration Act [1971 (c.77)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/77);

- the Asylum and Immigration (Jersey) Order 1998 ([SI 1998/1070](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1070)), which extended to Jersey, subject to modifications, provisions of the Asylum and Immigration Act [1996 (c.49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/49); and

- the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Jersey) Order 2003 ([SI 2003/1252](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1252)), which extended to Jersey, subject to modifications, provisions of the Immigration and Asylum Act [1999 (c.43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/43).
