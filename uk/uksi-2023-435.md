# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment and Saving Provision) Regulations 2023

Made: 17th April 2023

Laid before Parliament: 19th April 2023

Coming into force: 11th May 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 113A(1)(b), 113B(1)(b), 125(1) and (5) of the Police Act 1997[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Amendment and Saving Provision) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 11th May 2023.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendments to the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 4 (fees for criminal record certificates and enhanced criminal record certificates) of the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) For paragraph (2) substitute—

> > (2) No fee is payable in relation to an application made by a volunteer.

- (3) Omit paragraph (3).

#### Saving provision

###### 3

The amendments made by regulation 2(2) and (3) are of no effect in relation to an application for a criminal record certificate or enhanced criminal record certificate made before the date on which these Regulations come into force.

## Signed

Sarah Dines — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Home Office — 17th April 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/233](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/233)) to change the categories of people who are eligible for a free criminal record certificate or enhanced criminal record certificate.

Regulation 2(2) limits the categories of people who are not required to pay a fee when applying for a criminal record certificate or an enhanced criminal record certificate to volunteers. Regulation 2(3) omits definitions that are no longer required following the amendment made by regulation 2(2). Regulation 3 clarifies that the amendments do not apply to any application for a criminal record certificate or an enhanced criminal record certificate made before these Regulations come into force.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector or community bodies is foreseen.
