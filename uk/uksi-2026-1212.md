# The Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (Commencement No. 10) Regulations 2026

Made: 18th November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/90)[(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/90) and [(7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/90) of the [Domestic Abuse Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents)[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (Commencement No. 10) Regulations 2026.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- (a) “*the Act*” means the Domestic Abuse Act 2021;

- (b) a reference to a section, Part or Schedule by number alone means the section, Part or Schedule so numbered in the Act;

- (c) “*commencement date*” means 25th November 2026;

- (d) “*civil proceedings*” means the cases referred to under “civil proceedings” in section 31(7);

- (e) “*DAPO*” means a domestic abuse protection order within the meaning of section 27;

- (f) “*DAPO proceedings*” means proceedings under Part 3 in the family court;

- (g) “*family proceedings*” means the cases referred to under “family proceedings” in section 31(2).

#### Commencement of Part 3

###### 2

- (1) In so far as not already in force, and subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), Part 3 (powers for dealing with domestic abuse) comes into force on the commencement date.

- (2) The power to impose requirements when making a DAPO in section 35 (provision that may be made by orders) is commenced in relation to DAPO proceedings, civil proceedings and family proceedings only for the purposes of requirements that do not require an assessment or participation in a programme.

- (3) Section 55 (repeal of provisions about domestic violence protection notices and orders) does not come into force by virtue of these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations are the tenth commencement regulations made under the [Domestic Abuse Act 2021](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents) (c. 17) (“*the Act*”).

[Regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/made)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/1/made) brings [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/part/3) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents) (powers for dealing with domestic abuse) into force on 25th November 2026, in so far as it is not already in force and subject to the limitations in [regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/2/made) and [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/3/made). Part 3 establishes domestic abuse protection notices (“DAPNs”) and domestic abuse protection orders (“DAPOs”). Its commencement supports the roll-out of DAPNs and DAPOs to all relevant police forces (as defined in [section 22](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/22) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents)) and courts following the end of the pilot period in specified areas on 24th November 2026.

[Regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/made)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/2/made) provides that in relation to DAPO, civil and family proceedings, the power under [section 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/35) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents) to impose requirements when making a DAPO is commenced only for the purposes of imposing requirements that do not require an assessment or participation in a programme. Programme-based requirements are initially not available in DAPO, civil and family proceedings because appropriate referral mechanisms for those requirements are not yet in place in the civil and family jurisdictions. Civil and family courts may impose other requirements under section 35 where appropriate.

[Regulation 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/made)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1212/regulation/2/3/made) excludes [section 55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/section/55) of [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents) from the provisions brought into force by these Regulations. Section 55 repeals provisions relating to domestic violence protection notices and domestic violence protection orders in the [Crime and Security Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/17/contents) (c.17). Those provisions therefore remain in force alongside the DAPN and DAPO framework.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen. An impact assessment was published in relation to [the Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/17/contents) and is available from the Home Office, 2 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DF, and on the GOV.UK website.
