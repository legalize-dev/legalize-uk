# The Birmingham Heartlands Hospital National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1996

Made: 19th March 1996

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Birmingham Heartlands Hospital National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Dissolution of NHS trust

###### 2

The Birmingham Heartlands Hospital National Health Service Trust established by the East Birmingham Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order, the East Birmingham Hospital National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 1993[^f00006] and the East Birmingham Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1994[^f00007] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gerald Malone — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 19th March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 1996 of the Birmingham Heartlands Hospital National Health Service Trust.
