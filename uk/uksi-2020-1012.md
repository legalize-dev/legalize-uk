# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North East of England) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: on 17th September 2020

Laid before Parliament at 10.30 a.m. on 18th September 2020

Coming into force: at 12.01 a.m. on 18th September 2020

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 45C(1), (3)(c), (4)(d), 45F(2) and 45P of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984 .

These Regulations are made in response to the serious and imminent threat to public health which is posed by the incidence and spread of severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) in England.

The Secretary of State considers that the restrictions and requirements imposed by these Regulations are proportionate to what they seek to achieve, which is a public health response to that threat.

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North East of England) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force at 12.01 a.m. on 18th September 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North East of England) Regulations 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North East of England) Regulations 2020 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(7)(a)(ii) for “the” the first time it occurs, substitute “be”.

- (3) In regulation 8(1) for “regulation 3, 4, 6 or 6”, substitute “regulation 3, 4, 5 or 6”.

- (4) In regulation 9(1)(a) for “64”, substitute “6”.

- (5) In regulation 13 for “111”, substitute “11”.

- (6) In the Schedule, for “PART 1” the second time it occurs, substitute “PART 2”.
