# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Directives) (England and Wales) Order 2007

Made: 26th July 2007

Coming into force: 18th August 2007

This Order makes provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and it appears to the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers that it is expedient for the references to the Community instruments in article 3 to be construed as references to those provisions as amended from time to time.

The Secretary of State, in relation to England, and the Welsh Ministers, in relation to Wales, make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972 .

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Directives)(England and Wales) Order 2007 and comes into force on 18th August 2007.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

- (3) For the purposes of this article—

- (a) England and Wales includes the sea adjacent to England and Wales out as far as the seaward boundary of the territorial sea; and

- (b) the sea adjacent to Wales has the same meaning as in section 158 of the Government of Wales Act 2006 .

#### Revocation

###### 2

- (1) The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Landfill Directive) Order 2001 ;

- (b) the Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Council Directives on Large Combustion Plants, Incineration of Waste and National Emission Ceilings) Order 2002 ; and

- (c) the Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Council Directive on Solvent Emissions) Order 2003 .

#### Designation

###### 3

- (1) The following Directives are designated as relevant directives for the purposes of paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution and Prevention Control Act 1999—

- (a) Council Directive [87/217/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0217) on the prevention and reduction of environmental pollution by asbestos ;

- (b) Council Directive [92/112/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0112) on procedures for harmonizing the programmes for the reduction and eventual elimination of pollution caused by waste from the titanium dioxide industry ;

- (c) European Parliament and Council Directive [94/63/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0063) on the control of volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions resulting from the storage of petrol and its distribution from terminals to service stations ;

- (d) Council Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0013) on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain activities and installations ;

- (e) Council Directive [1999/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0031) on the landfill of waste ;

- (f) Directive [2000/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0053) of the European Parliament and of the Council on end-of life vehicles ;

- (g) Directive [2000/76/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0076) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the incineration of waste ;

- (h) Directive [2001/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0080) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the limitation of emissions of certain pollutants into the air from large combustion plants ;

- (i) Directive [2001/81/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0081) of the European Parliament and of the Council on national emission ceilings for certain atmospheric pollutants ;

- (j) Directive [2002/96/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0096) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE) ;

- (k) Directive [2006/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0012) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste ;

- (l) Directive [2006/66/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0066) of the European Parliament and of the Council on batteries and accumulators and waste batteries and accumulators and repealing Directive [91/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0157) .

- (2) For the purposes of this Order, the references to the Directives in paragraph (1) are references to those Directives as amended from time to time.

Jane Davidson — Minister for Environment, Sustainability and Housing, one of the Welsh Ministers — 26th July 2007

Joan Ruddock — Parliamentary Under Secretary — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 23rd July 2007
