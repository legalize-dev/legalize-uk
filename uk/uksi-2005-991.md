# The Non-Domestic Rating (Chargeable Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 30th March 2005

Coming into force: 31st March 2005

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 57A, 140(4) and 143(1) and (2) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations, a draft of which has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, each House of Parliament:

#### Citation, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations, which apply to England only, may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating (Chargeable Amounts) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendments to the 2004 Regulations

###### 2

The Non-Domestic Rating (Chargeable Amounts) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00002] are amended in accordance with the following provisions.

#### Appropriate fraction

###### 3

In regulation 9(3)(b)(i) and (ii), for “1st April 2000” substitute “1st April 2005”.

#### Splits and mergers

###### 4

In Schedule 2, in paragraphs 2(1), 3(1), 4(1) and 5(1), for the words “43(4) to (6A), 44 and 45(4) to (6)” substitute “43(4) to (6A), 44, 45(4) to (6) and 54(4) to (7)”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the First Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford — Minister of State — Office of the Deputy Prime Minister — 30th March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct errors in the Non-Domestic Rating (Chargeable Amounts) (England) Regulations 2004 ([SI 2004/3387](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3387)) by way of—

- (a) the correction of references in regulation 9(3) to 1st April 2000, which should have been references to 1st April 2005; and

- (b) the insertion, in Schedule 2, of references to section 54(4) to (7) (liability for rates for hereditaments shown on the central rating list).

In view of the nature of these amendments no further Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared.
