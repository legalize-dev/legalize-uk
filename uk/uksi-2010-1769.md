# The Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 7th July 2010

Laid before Parliament: 8th July 2010

Coming into force: 16th August 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 130 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and shall come into force on 16th August 2010.

#### Amendment of the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) Regulations 2010

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 1 of the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) Regulations 2010[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (2) for “1st April 2011” substitute “1st October 2011”.

- (3) In paragraph (3) for “1st October 2010” substitute “1st April 2011”.

- (4) In paragraph (3)(a) for “1st April 2011” substitute “1st October 2011”.

- (5) In paragraph (4) for “1st October 2010” substitute “1st April 2011”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

M. E. Penning — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 7th July 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the commencement provisions of the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) Regulations 2010 to delay the commencement of those Regulations by six months.

An impact assessment of the effect of these Regulations has not been prepared as they do not themselves have any impact on the cost of business.
