# The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Art) (England) Order 1995

Made: 10th January 1995

Laid before Parliament: 16th January 1995

Coming into force: 1st August 1995

Having made, pursuant to section 242(1) of the Education Act 1993[^f00001], such arrangements for consultation about her proposals as she considered appropriate, the Secretary of State for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 4(2)(a) and (b) and (4) and 232(5) and (6) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Art) (England) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st August 1995.

- (2) In this Order “the Document” means the Document published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office in January 1995 entitled “Art in the National Curriculum (England)”[^f00003].

- (3) This Order applies to pupils at maintained schools and grant-maintained special schools (other than those established in a hospital) in England only.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Art) (England) Order 1992[^f00004] is revoked.

#### Specification of attainment targets and programmes of study

###### 3

It is hereby directed that the provisions relating to attainment targets and programmes of study set out in the Document shall have effect for the purposes of specifying attainment targets and programmes of study in relation to art.

###### 4

The examples printed in italics in the Document (which serve to illustrate the programmes of study described therein) do not form part of the provision made by this Order.

## Signed

Gillian Shephard — Secretary of State for Education — 10th January 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the attainment targets and programmes of study specified in relation to key stages 1 to 3 of a pupil’s compulsory schooling in the document published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office in January 1995 entitled “Art in the National Curriculum (England)” (ISBN 0 11 270890 0) to have effect for the purpose of specifying those elements of the National Curriculum for art. These provisions apply to pupils at maintained schools and grant-maintained special schools (but not if established in hospitals) in England only, with effect from 1st August 1995.

This Order supersedes the provisions of the Education (National Curriculum) (Attainment Targets and Programmes of Study in Art) (England) Order 1992.
