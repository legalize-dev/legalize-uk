# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) Order 2015

Made: 5th January 2015

Laid before Parliament: 8th January 2015

Coming into force: 3rd February 2015

Accordingly the Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variation) Order 2015.

#### Commencement of variation

###### 2

The variation of the Public Lending Right Scheme made by the Secretary of State on 5th January 2015, whereby paragraph (1)(a) of article 46 was varied by substituting “6.66p” for “6.20p”, comes into force on 3rd February 2015.

## Signed

Ed Vaizey — Minister of State, Department for Culture, Media and Sport — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 5th January 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 3rd February 2015 a variation of the Public Lending Right Scheme (“the Scheme”) made by the Secretary of State on 5th January 2015.

The Scheme provides for payments to be made to authors based on the number of times their books are lent out by public libraries. The effect of the variation commenced by article 2 of this Order is to increase the rate per loan as set out in article 46(1)(a) of the Scheme from 6.20p to 6.66p. The rate per loan is the sum attributable to each qualifying loan for the purpose of calculating the amount payable in respect of loans of a particular book by public libraries.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as no impact on business and the voluntary sector is foreseen.
