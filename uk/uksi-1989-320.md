# The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 1989

Made: 2nd March 1989

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1989

Coming into force: 3rd April 1989

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 45(6) of the Road Traffic Act 1972 (“the 1972 Act”)[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and by the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00003], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 199(2) of the 1972 Act, hereby makes the following Regulations –

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 3rd April 1989.

###### 2

The Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988[^f00004] are hereby amended so that in the regulations specified in column 1 of the Table below for the amounts specified in column 2 of the Table there are substituted the amounts specified in column 3 of the Table.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988 so that –

- (a) the fee payable on an application for a first examination or periodical test is increased from £25.25 to £26.50 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £14 to £14.50 in the case of a trailer;

- (b) the fee for a re-test or further re-test within 14 days after the first examination or earlier re-test and at the same station or at another auxiliary station by arrangement is increased from £13 to £13.50 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £7.50 to £7.75 in the case of a trailer. The fee for re-tests in other cirumstances is increased from £25.25 to £26.50 in the case of a motor vehicle and from £14 to £14.50 in the case of a trailer;

- (c) the fee payable on a request for an amendment to a plating certificate following a notifiable alteration or for a retest where a particular in the certificate is or may be no longer applicable is increased from £10.50 to £11; and

- (d) the fee payable on an application for a replacement for a lost or defaced plate or certificate is increased from £7.50 to £7.75.
