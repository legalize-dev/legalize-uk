# The Road Traffic (NHS Charges) Act 1999(Commencement No. 1) Order 1999

Made: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of power conferred by section 21 of the Road Traffic (NHS Charges) Act 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (NHS Charges) Act 1999 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1999.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The Road Traffic (NHS Charges) Act 1999 shall, except in relation to military hospitals as defined in section 15(2) of that Act, come into force on 5th April 1999.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hayman — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 1st April 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions of the Road Traffic (NHS Charges) Act 1999 into force on 5th April 1999, except in relation to military hospitals.
