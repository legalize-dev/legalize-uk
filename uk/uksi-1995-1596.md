# Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1995

Made: 22nd June 1995

Coming into force: 17th July 1995

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 57 of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1995 and shall come into force on 17th July 1995.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted into the Books of Sederunt.

#### Registration Appeal Court

###### 2

- (1) The Court of three judges for hearing registration appeals under section 57 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 shall be the Honourable Lord Morison, the Honourable Lord Sutherland and the Honourable Lord Marnoch.

- (2) The Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1994[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Hope of Craighead — Lord President, I.P.D. — 22nd June 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt appoints the three judges for hearing registration appeals under section 57 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 and revokes the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1994.
