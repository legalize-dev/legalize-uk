# The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2003

Made: 2nd December 2003

Laid before Parliament: 8th December 2003

Coming into force: 1st January 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(3) of the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994[^f00001] and by virtue of the Department for Transport (Driver Licensing and Vehicle Registration Fees) Order 2003[^f00002] hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st January 2004.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In Regulation 10(1) of the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 2002[^f00003] for “£25” there shall be substituted “£38”.

## Signed

David Jamieson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 2nd December 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations raise the fee payable on applications for the registration of a mechanically propelled vehicle from £25 to £38 with effect from 1st January 2004.
