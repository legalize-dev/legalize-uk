# The Patent Office (Address) Rules 1991

Made: 14th March 1991

Laid before Parliament: 18th March 1991

Coming into force: 15th April 1991

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 40(1)(e) of the Trade Marks Act 1938 and now vested in him[^f00002], and in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 36(1) of the Registered Designs Act 1949[^f00003] and section 123(1) of and paragraph 14 of Schedule 4 to the Patents Act 1977[^f00004], hereby makes the following Rules:—

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Patent Office (Address) Rules 1991 and shall come into force on 15th April 1991.

###### 2

The addresses of the Patent Office shall be—

- (a) Cardiff Road, Newport, Gwent NP9 1RH; and

- (b) for the period ending with 28th April 1991, State House, 66-71 High Holborn, London WCI 4TP, and thereafter, 25 Southampton Buildings, London WC2A 1AY.

###### 3

The address of the Manchester Branch of the Trade Marks Registry of the Patent Office shall be c/o Companies House, 75 Mosley Street, Manchester M2 3HR.

###### 4

The Patent Office (Address) Rules 1985[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Hesketh — Department of Trade and Industry — 14th March 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules revoke and replace the Patent Office (Address) Rules 1985 ([S.I. 1985/1099](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/1099)).

They give effect to the transfer of the Patent Office to two new addresses as follows:—

- (a) on 15th April 1991 to premises in Newport, Gwent; and

- (b) on 29th April 1991 to Southampton Buildings in London, from the existing address in State House.

They also specify the new address of the Manchester Branch of the Trade Marks Registry.
