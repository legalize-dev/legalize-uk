# The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (Wales) Order 2018

Made: 27th June 2018

Coming into force: 1st July 2018

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 27th day of June 2018

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 , is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (Wales) Order 2018 and shall come into force on 1st July 2018.

#### Appointment of Inspector

###### 2

Andrew John Fry is appointed an inspector for the purposes of section 28(1) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 in its application to Wales.

#### Revocation of appointment

###### 3

The Fire and Rescue Services (Appointment of Inspector) (Wales) Order 2015 is revoked.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
