# The Highways Noise Payments and Movable Homes (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001:

Made: 9th May 2001

Laid before Parliament: 10th May 2001

Coming into force: 1st June 2001

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 20A of the Land Compensation Act 1973[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Highways Noise Payments and Movable Homes (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st June 2001.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England only.

#### Definition of “specified level”

###### 2

Regulation 2 of the Highways Noise Payments and Movable Homes (England) Regulations 2000[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition in the appropriate place of the following words—

- “specified level” means a noise level of L10 (18-hour) of 68dB(A).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Larry Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 9th May 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations amend the Highways Noise Payments and Movable Homes (England) Regulations 2000 by adding a definition of the “specified level” of noise which will trigger a noise payment for an eligible movable home pursuant to regulation 3(1). “L10”, “L10 (18-hour)” and “dB(A)” are all defined in regulation 2 of those Regulations.
