# The Employment Relations Act 1999 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2003

Made: 22nd December 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on her by section 45 of the Employment Relations Act 1999[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Employment Relations Act 1999 (Commencement No. 9) Order 2003.

###### 2

In this Order, “the Act” means the Employment Relations Act 1999.

#### Commencement

###### 3

Paragraph 3, sub-paragraphs (1) to (5) of paragraph 4 and paragraph 5 (to the extent that it is not already in force) of Schedule 7 to the Act shall come into force on 6th April 2004.

###### 4

Paragraph 7 of Schedule 7 to the Act shall come into force on 6th July 2004.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe, — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Competition and Consumers, — Department of Trade and Industry — 22nd December 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6th April 2004 paragraphs 3, 4 (partially), and 5 (to the extent that it is not already in force) of Schedule 7 to the Employment Relations Act 1999 (“the 1999 Act”). These paragraphs make amendments to section 6 (charges) of the [Employment Agencies Act 1973 (C. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/35) (“the 1973 Act”) and section 9 (inspection) of that Act; and insert a new section 11B (offences: cost of investigation) into the 1973 Act. The Order brings into force on 6th July 2004 paragraph 7 of Schedule 7 to the 1999 Act which amends section 13(2) (definition of employment agency) of the 1973 Act.
