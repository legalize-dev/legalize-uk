# The Political Parties and Elections Act 2009 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2010

Made: 30th September 2010

The Lord President of the Council, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43(1) of the Political Parties and Elections Act 2009 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Political Parties and Elections Act 2009 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2010.

#### Appointed date

###### 2

Section 6 of the Political Parties and Elections Act 2009 (number of Electoral Commissioners) comes into force on 1st October 2010.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord President of the Council

Mark Harper — Parliamentary Secretary (Minister for Political and Constitutional Reform) — Cabinet Office — 30th September 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st October 2010 section 6 of the Political Parties and Elections Act [2009 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/12). Section 6 amends section 1(3) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act [2000 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41) by increasing the number of Electoral Commissioners to nine or ten from a minimum number of five and a maximum number of nine.
