# The District Electoral Areas (Northern Ireland) Order 2014

Made: 11th February 2014

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2) and (3)

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of February 2014Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 84(1) and (3) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the District Electoral Areas (Northern Ireland) Order 2014.

- (2) This Order comes into force for the purpose of all proceedings preliminary to, or relating to, a local election to be held on 22nd May 2014, on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (3) This Order comes into force for all other purposes on 22nd May 2014.

- (4) This Order extends to Northern Ireland only.

- (5) In this Order, “district” means a local government district set out in the Local Government (Boundaries) (Northern Ireland) Order 2012[^f00003], and “ward” means a ward established by that Order.

#### District electoral areas

###### 2

- (1) For the purposes of elections to district councils, each district specified in column 1 of the Schedule is to be divided into district electoral areas which—

- (a) bear the names specified in column 2 of the Schedule; and

- (b) comprise the wards specified in column 3 of the Schedule.

- (2) The number of councillors to be elected for each district electoral area is to be the number specified in column 4 of the Schedule.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The District Electoral Areas (Northern Ireland) Order 1993[^f00004] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Local Government (Boundaries) Act (Northern Ireland) [2008 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/7) provides that Northern Ireland will be divided into 11 local government districts, rather than the existing 26 local government districts. The Local Government (Boundaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 ([S.R. 2012/421](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2012/421)) sets out the names and areas of each of those 11 new local government districts and establishes new wards for each district.

Consequent on the making of [S.R. 2012/421](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2012/421), the Secretary of State appointed a District Electoral Areas Commissioner under article 2(4) of the District Electoral Areas Commissioner (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 ([S.I. 1984/360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/360)). That Commissioner was required to submit a report to the Secretary of State setting out his recommendations for the grouping together of wards into electoral areas for the purpose of local government elections in each local government district. On 11 December 2013, the Commissioner’s report was laid before Parliament under article 5 of [S.I. 1984/360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/360). A copy of the Commissioner’s report can be obtained from the Northern Ireland Office, Constitutional Policy Team, 1 Horse Guards Road, London, SW1A 2HQ.

This Order gives effect, without modification, to the recommendations of the Commissioner. Article 2 and the Schedule set out the name of, the wards comprised in, and the number of councillors to be elected for, each district electoral area. Article 3 revokes the District Electoral Areas (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 ([S.I. 1993/226](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/226)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
