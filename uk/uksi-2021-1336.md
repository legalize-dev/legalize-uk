# The Upwell Internal Drainage Board Order 2021

Made: 29th November 2021

Coming into force: 30th November 2021

Accordingly, the Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(5) and (7) of the 1991 Act and now vested in the Secretary of State.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Upwell Internal Drainage Board Order 2021 and comes into force on 30th November 2021.

#### Confirmation of the Scheme

###### 2

- (1) The Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency is confirmed.

- (2) The Scheme is set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency

###### 1

The Scheme comes into force on the day after the day on which the Order confirming this Scheme is made.

###### 2

In this Scheme:-

- “*the Act*” means the Land Drainage Act 1991[^f00003];

- “*the Board*” means the Upwell internal drainage board constituted by the River Great Ouse Catchment Board (Upwell and Nordelph Internal Drainage Districts) Order 1949[^f00004] and the River Great Ouse Catchment Board (Upwell and Nordelph Internal Drainage Districts) (Appointed Day) Order 1950[^f00005];

- “*the commencement date*” means the date on which this Scheme comes into force;

- “*elected members*” means those members of the Board who are appointed by the Secretary of State pursuant to this Scheme or elected in accordance with Schedule 1 to the Act and any applicable regulations made under the Act;

- “*property*” includes any books of account, other books, deeds, maps, papers and other documents;

- “*rights and obligations*” means all rights, powers, duties (including statutory powers and duties), obligations and liabilities.

###### 3

- (1) On the commencement date the Board is reconstituted to comprise 14 elected members[^f00006].

- (2) The first elected members of the reconstituted Board are to be appointed by the Secretary of State and are to hold office until the expiry of one year from the first occurrence of 1st November following the day on which they are appointed.

###### 4

The property, rights and obligations of the Board immediately before its reconstitution in accordance with paragraph 3 will be the property, rights and obligations of the Board after its reconstitution.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

William Harrington — Head of Waterways and Water Management Team, Flood Risk Management — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 29th November 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order confirms a Scheme submitted by the Environment Agency making provision for the reconstitution of the Upwell Internal Drainage Board so that the number of elected members is reduced from 20 to 14.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
