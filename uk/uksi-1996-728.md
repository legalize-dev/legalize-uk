# The Naval Discipline Act 1957 (Jersey) Order 1996

Made: 13th March 1996

Coming into force: 1st June 1996

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of March 1996 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 125(1) of the Naval Discipline Act 1957[^f00001], including that section as applied by section 24(5) of the Armed Forces Act 1991[^f00002], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Naval Discipline Act 1957 (Jersey) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st June 1996.

###### 2

- (1) The Naval Discipline Act 1957 (“the 1957 Act”) shall have effect in the Bailiwick of Jersey subject to the modifications specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) The provisions[^f00003] of—

- (a) the Naval Forces (Enforcement of Maintenance Liabilities) Act 1947[^f00004], and

- (b) section 10 and Part III of the Armed Forces Act 1991 (“the 1991 Act”),

shall have effect in the Bailiwick of Jersey subject to the modifications specified in Parts II and III respectively of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3

In relation to any reference to a law for the time being in force in the Bailiwick of Jersey, the modifications made by this Order have effect for all purposes of the 1957 Act (and not only in the application of that Act to the Bailiwick of Jersey).

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — modifications subject to which the naval discipline act 1957 has effect in the bailiwick of jersey

###### 1

In section 43AA(6)[^f00005], at the end insert—

> > (d) where the offender is in or removed to the Bailiwick of Jersey, any place where he could have been detained if he had been ordered (in whatever terms) to be detained in custody by a court in the Bailiwick of Jersey.

###### 2

In section 101(5)[^f00006], after “1920” insert “or the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) (Jersey) Law 1953”.

###### 3

In section 128F(10)[^f00007], in the definition of “the relevant court”, after paragraph (c) insert—

> > or
> (d) the Police Court in the Bailiwick of Jersey

###### 4

In section 128G[^f00008], in subsection (3)(b) after “creditors” insert “or any payment in the course of bankruptcy proceedings as defined in Article 13 of the Interpretation (Jersey) Law 1954”.

###### 5

- (1) Schedule 4A[^f00009] shall be modified as follows.

- (2) In paragraph 10(6)—

- (a) after paragraph (c) insert—

> > (d) where the offender is in or removed to the Bailiwick of Jersey, any place where he could have been detained if he had been ordered (in whatever terms) to be detained in custody by a court in the Bailiwick of Jersey;

- (b) at the end insert “and in relation to an offender who is removed to the Bailiwick of Jersey, includes an enactment in force in the Bailiwick of Jersey”.

- (3) In paragraph 11(4B), sub-paragraph (b) shall be omitted.

## PART II — modifications subject to which provisions of the naval forces (enforcement of maintenance liabilities) act 1947 have effect in the bailiwick of jersey

In section 1(2A)[^f00010], in paragraph (b), after “extends” insert “, and also includes a reference to an order registered in a court in the Bailiwick of Jersey under the Maintenance Orders (Facilities for Enforcement) (Jersey) Law 1953”.

## PART III — modifications subject to which provisions of the armed forces act 1991 have effect in the bailiwick of jersey

###### 1

In section 21(4), sub-paragraph (b) shall be omitted.

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1[^f00011] shall be modified as follows.

- (2) In paragraph 1, after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

> > (ba) an advocate or solicitor of the Royal Court of Jersey of at least 7 years' standing;

###### 3

In paragraph 6, at the end insert

> > and
> (e) in the case of a person who qualifies for appointment under paragraph 1(d) above by virtue of holding or having held judicial office in the Bailiwick of Jersey, with the consent of the Bailiff.

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order modifies certain provisions of the Naval Discipline Act 1957 and the Naval Forces (Enforcement of Maintenance Liabilities) Act 1947, as amended, and the Armed Forces Act 1991 in their application to the Bailiwick of Jersey.
