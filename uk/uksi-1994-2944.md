# The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Amendment (No. 3) Regulations 1994

Made: 21st November 1994

Laid before Parliament: 28th November 1994

Coming into force: 1st April 1995

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 15A(2)(b) and 189(1) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation as appropriate with organisations representing qualifying lenders likely to be affected by the Regulations and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals to make these Regulations should not be referred to it, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Amendment (No.3) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

#### Amendment of paragraph 7 of Schedule 9A to the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987

###### 2

In paragraph 7 of Schedule 9A to the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987 , for the amount “£1.00” there shall be substituted the amount “£0.80”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Roger Evans — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — 21st November 1994
