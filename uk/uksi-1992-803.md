# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1992

Made: 16th March 1992

Coming into force: 1st April 1992

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 16th day of March 1992 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 2 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

- (2) The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1992[^f00002] is revoked.

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986. It adds Ecuador to the list of the Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction signed at The Hague on 25th October 1980 (Cm 33).
