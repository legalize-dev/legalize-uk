# The A523 Trunk Road (Hazel Grove Diversion Order) 1990 (Revocation) Order 2014

Made: 8th April 2014

Coming into force: 30th April 2014

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and[^f00003]:-

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the A523 Trunk Road (Hazel Grove Diversion) Order 1990 (Revocation) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 30th April 2014.

###### 2

The A523 Trunk Road (Hazel Grove Diversion Order) 1990 is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Alan Shepherd — NDD NW Divisional Director — Highways Agency — 8th April 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the formerly made Order 1990.
