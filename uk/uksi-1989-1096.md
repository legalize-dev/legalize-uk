# The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1989

Made: 28th June 1989

Coming into force: 28th June 1989

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1989 and shall come into force forthwith.

###### 2

The interim period specified by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 shall continue until 16th July 1990.

## Signed

Northern Ireland Office

Tom King — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 28th June 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends until 16th July 1990 the period specified in section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (as extended by the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/1181](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1181))) for the operation of the temporary provisions for the government of Northern Ireland contained in Schedule 1 to that Act.
