# The Longley Park Sixth Form College (Incorporation) Order 2002

Made: 7th October 2002

Laid before Parliament: 8th October 2002

Coming into force: 1st November 2002

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Longley Park Sixth Form College (Incorporation) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st November 2002 (“the commencement date”).

###### 2

There shall be established as from the commencement date a body corporate called “Longley Park Sixth Form College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date an educational institution of that name.

###### 3

The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 6th September 2004, and the corporation shall conduct Longley Park Sixth Form College from that date.

## Signed

Margaret Hodge — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 7th October 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called Longley Park Sixth Form College under section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 6th September 2004 as the operative date for the purposes of Part 1 of that Act.
