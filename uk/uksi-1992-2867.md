# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange) Order 1992

Made: 16th November 1992

Laid before Parliament: 24th November 1992

Coming into force: 1st January 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

###### 2

The prison known as H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange and situate in the county of Nottinghamshire shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 16th November 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Young Offender Institution Lowdham Grange on 1st January 1993.
