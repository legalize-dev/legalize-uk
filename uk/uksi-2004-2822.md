# The Employment Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2004

Made: 28th October2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 55(2) and (3) of the Employment Act 2002[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Employment Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2004.

- (2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Employment Act 2002.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of –

- (a) paragraph 47(3) of Schedule 7 and section 53 of the Act so far as it relates to that paragraph; and

- (b) in Schedule 8 of the Act (repeals), the entries relating to the Employment Rights Act 1996[^f00002], the Employment Rights (Dispute Resolution) Act 1998[^f00003] and the Employment Relations Act 1999[^f00004] and section 54 so far as it relates to that Schedule,

shall be 1st November 2004.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe, — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Competition and Consumers — Department of Trade and Industry — 28th October 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on the 1st November 2004 the provisions of the Employment Act 2002 ([2002 c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/22) set out in Article 2.

The provisions are a consequential amendment and some repeals of existing legislation.
