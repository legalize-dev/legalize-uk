# The Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 12) Order 2008

Made: 21st May 2008

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 199(1) of the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (Commencement No.12) Order 2008.

#### Appointed day for provision relating to arrangements between the Commission for Healthcare Audit and Inspection and Ministers

###### 2

2nd June 2008 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 124 of the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (arrangements with Ministers etc: CHAI) in so far as it is not already in force[^f00002].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Ben Bradshaw — Minister of State — Department of Health — 21st May 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order is the twelfth commencement order under the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (“the Act”). It provides for the coming into force of section 124 of the Act in so far as it is not already in force. This has the effect of bringing section 124 into force in relation to Scotland and Northern Ireland. Section 124 provides for arrangements to be made between the Commission for Healthcare Audit and Inspection (CHAI) and Ministers for CHAI to perform functions in relation to a health and medical scheme paid for out of public funds or which correspond to functions of CHAI and relate to the Northern Irish health service.
