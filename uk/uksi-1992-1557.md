# The Combined Probation Areas (Derbyshire) Order 1992

Made: 29th June 1992

Coming into force: 1st July 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Derbyshire) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st July 1992.

###### 2

That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Derbyshire Probation Committee shall be amended by—

- (a) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Alfreton and Belper and Ilkeston; and

- (b) inserting in alphabetical order in column 1 “East Derbyshire” and corresponding to this in column 3 “4”.

###### 3

The five members of the Derbyshire Probation Committee appointed before 1st July 1992 by the justices for the petty sessions areas of Alfreton and Belper and Ilkeston shall decide by agreement, or in default of agreement by lot, which four of them shall continue in office as if appointed by the justices for the new petty sessions area of East Derbyshire[^f00003].

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Home Office — 29th June 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the amalgamation of the petty sessions areas of Alfreton and Belper and Ilkeston into the new petty sessions area of East Derbyshire effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Derbyshire) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/1511](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1511)) which comes into force on 1st July 1992.
