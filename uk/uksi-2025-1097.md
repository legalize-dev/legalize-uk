# The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) (Correction) Order 2025

Made: 16th October 2025

Coming into force: 17th October 2025

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and (8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) (Correction) Order 2025 and comes into force on 17th October 2025.

#### Corrections to the Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022

###### 2

The Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule of this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

## SCHEDULE — CORRECTABLE ERRORS

| Where the correction is to be made | How the correction is to be made | Text to be substituted, inserted or omitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article 2(1), definition of “Network Rail” | After “company” omit | “registration” |
| Article 2(1), definition of “relevant lead flood authority” | For “and any successor flood authority to these authorities” substitute | “or any successor flood authority to those authorities” |
| Article 30(5) | For “development consent)of” substitute | “development consent) of” |
| Article 51(1)(a), (b) and (c) | For “Her Majesty” substitute | “His Majesty” |
| Schedule 16, Part 5, (protection for First Corporate Shipping Limited), paragraph 50(1) and (2) | For “highways access land” substitute | “highway access land” |
| Schedule 16, Part 5, paragraph 50(3)(e) | For “use f land” substitute | “use of land” |
| Schedule 16, Part 8 (for the protection of National Grid as electricity undertaker), paragraph 90, in paragraph (a) of the definition of “specified works” | For “existing apparatus” substitute | “apparatus” |
| Schedule 16, Part 8, paragraph 98(2), (6) and (9) | For “existing apparatus” substitute | “apparatus” |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Marco Picardi — Head of the Transport and Works Act Orders Unit — Department for Transport — 16th October 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Order corrects errors and omissions in the Portishead Branch Line (MetroWest Phase 1) Order 2022, a development consent order under the Planning Act 2008, following a request under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
