# The Superannuation (National Assistance Board) Transfer Rules 1965

Made: 16th December 1965

Laid before Parliament: 23rd December 1965

Coming into Operation: 24th December 1965

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 33 of the Superannuation Act 1965, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Rules:—

###### 1

Where a person was, after the 30th September 1960 and before the 2nd February 1965, taken into the civil service of the State as an officer employed by the National Assistance Board in a Reception Centre established in accordance with subsection (1) of section 17 of the National Assistance Act 1948 after having been employed by a local authority for the purposes of functions exercised by that authority on behalf of the National Assistance Board in accordance with subsection (2) of section 17 of that Act, and his employment by the authority was, in the opinion of the Treasury, of the same nature and for the same purpose as his employment in the service of the State, then, subject to the provisions of Rule 2 of these Rules, his service in such employment by the authority before the date on which he was taken into the service of the State, but not before the 1st January 1919, may be reckoned for the purposes of the Superannuation Act 1965 as employment in an unestablished capacity within the meaning of that Act.

###### 2

Rule 1 of these Rules shall not apply to any person to whom Part II of the Superannuation (Transfers between the Civil Service and Local Government) Rules 1950 and 1955 applies or would apply if he fulfilled the conditions contained in paragraphs (c) and (d) of Rule 3(1) of those Rules.

###### 3

The Interpretation Act 1889shall apply for the interpretation of these Rules as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 4

These Rules may be cited as the Superannuation (National Assistance Board) Transfer Rules 1965, and shall come into operation on 24th December 1965.

## Signed

Ifor Davies — George Lawson — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 16th December 1965

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules provide for service (not being service before 1st January 1919 or before age 18) of certain staff with local authorities to be reckoned, subject to certain conditions, as unestablished civil service for the purposes of the Superannuation Act 1965. The staff covered by the Rules are persons taken into the civil service after 30th September 1960 and before 2nd February 1965 as officers employed by the National Assistance Board in Reception Centres established under section 17 of the National Assistance Act 1948, having previously been employed by local authorities in connection with the provision and maintenance of such Reception Centres.

The Rules do not apply to any person whose local authority service has become, or could have become, reckonable as service in the capacity of a civil servant upon payment of a transfer value by the former local authority employer under existing statutory powers.
