# The Gwent Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1995

Made: 19th October 1995

Coming into force: 1st November 1995

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and Section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gwent Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st November 1995.

#### Amendment of NHS Trust Establishment Order

###### 2

In Article 4 of the Gwent Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003] for “5 non-executive” there shall be substituted “4 non-executive” and for “5 executive” there shall be substituted “4 executive”.

## Signed

William Hague — Secretary of State for Wales — 19th October 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of an NHS Trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by decreasing the number of executive and non-executive directors from 5 to 4 in each case.
