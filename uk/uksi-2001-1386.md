# The Enforcement of Road Traffic Debts (Amendment) Order 2001

Made: 30th March 2001

Laid before Parliament: 6th April 2001

Coming into force: 1st June 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 78 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] makes the following Order:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Enforcement of Road Traffic Debts (Amendment) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st June 2001.

- (2) In this Order a reference to an article by number alone is a reference to the article so numbered in the Enforcement of Road Traffic Debts Order 1992[^f00002].

#### Amendments to the Enforcement of Road Traffic Debts Order 1993

###### 2

The Enforcement of Road Traffic Debts Order 1993 shall be amended as follows.

###### 3

For “the Act”, wherever it occurs, substitute “the 1991 Act”.

###### 4

In article 1(2)—

- (a) in the definition of “specified debts”, omit “Part II”;

- (b) after the definition of “the 1991 Act”, insert:—

> > ;
> - “the 1996 Act” means the London Local Authorities Act 1996[^f00003].

###### 5

In article 2—

- (a) in paragraph (1), omit “as Part II”;

- (b) for sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (1), substitute—

> > (a) an increased charge provided for in a charge certificate issued under—
>   (i) paragraph 6 of Schedule 6 to the 1991 Act[^f00004];
>   (ii) paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the 1996 Act[^f00005].

###### 6

For article 3(2), substitute—

> > (2) In paragraph (1) “the time for serving a statutory declaration” means, as the case may be—
>   (a) the period of 21 days allowed by—
>     (i) paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 6 to the 1991 Act; or
>     (ii) paragraph 10(1)(c) of Schedule 1 to the 1996 Act; or
>   (b) where a longer period has been allowed pursuant to—
>     (i) paragraph 8(4) of Schedule 6 to the 1991 Act; or
>     (ii) paragraph 10(4) of Schedule 1 to the 1996 Act,
>   that period.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, *C.* — Dated 30th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Enforcement of Road Traffic Debts Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/2073](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2073)) to provide for the enforcement in the county court of increased penalty charges provided for in charge certificates issued under paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the [London Local Authorities Act 1996 (c. ix)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukla/1996/9) (which deals with increased penalty charges for breach of provisions about bus lanes).
