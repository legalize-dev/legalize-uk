# The Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1982

Made: 4th August 1982

The Secretary of State for the Environment as respects England, The Secretary of State for Scotland as respects Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 74(3) of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, hereby make the following order:

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1982.

###### 2

Sections 29, 30 and 31 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 shall come into force on 6th September 1982.

## Signed

Michael R. D. Heseltine — Secretary of State for the Environment — 28th July 1982

George Younger — Secretary of State for Scotland — 29th July 1982

Nicholas Edwards — Secretary of State for Wales — 4th August 1982

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order provides for sections 29, 30 and 31 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 to come into force on 6th September 1982. The provisions in question are concerned with giving special protection to certain areas of special scientific interest.
