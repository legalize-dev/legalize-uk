# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2012

Made: 5th December 2012

Laid before Parliament: 6th December 2012

Coming into force: 7th December 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 3) Order 2012 and shall come into force on 7th December 2012.

###### 2

- (1) Part 2 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) For the paragraph beginning “Council Decision 2010/639/CFSP of 25 October 2010 (Belarus)” substitute “Council Decision 2012/642/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Belarus)[^f00003].”.

- (3) After “Council Decision 2012/285/CFSP of 31 May 2012 (Republic of Guinea-Bissau)” insert “as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2012/516/CFSP of 24 September 2012 (Republic of Guinea-Bissau)[^f00004]”.

- (4) After “as amended by Council Decision 2012/457/CFSP of 2 August 2012 (Iran)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2012/635/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Iran)[^f00005]”.

- (5) After “as amended by Council Decision 2012/170/CFSP of 23 March 2012 (Republic of Moldova)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2012/527/CFSP of 27 September 2012 (Republic of Moldova)[^f00006]”.

- (6) After “as amended by Council Decision 2012/388/CFSP of 16 July 2012 (Somalia)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2012/633/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Somalia)[^f00007]”.

- (7) For the paragraph beginning “Council Decision 2011/782/CFSP of 1 December 2011 (Syria)” substitute “Council Decision 2012/739/CFSP of 29 November 2012 (Syria)[^f00008].”.

## Signed

Mark Harper — Minister of State — 5th December 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000.

It amends Part 2 of that Schedule to list: Council Decision 2012/642/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Belarus) (which replaced Council Decision 2010/639/CFSP of 25 October 2010 (Belarus) and all the subsequent amending and implementing Council Decisions); Council Implementing Decision 2012/516/CFSP of 24 September 2012 (Republic of Guinea-Bissau); Council Decision 2012/635/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Iran); Council Decision 2012/527/CFSP of 27 September 2012 (Republic of Moldova); Council Decision 2012/633/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Somalia); and Council Decision 2012/739/CFSP of 29 November 2012 (Syria) (which replaced Council Decision 2011/782/CFSP of 1 December 2011 (Syria) and all the subsequent amending and implementing Council Decisions).

Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the Schedule (as amended) or falling within a description specified in such an instrument will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the 2000 Order).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
