# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2015

Made: 8th October 2015

Coming into force: 9th October 2015

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 8th day of October 2015Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2015 and comes into force on 9th October 2015.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — Persons appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 9th October 2015

- Robin Bosher

- Julie Anne Knight

- Stephen Michael Lee

- Catherine Ruth McEvoy

- Seamus Murphy

- Katherine Anne Powell

- Stephen James Ralph Smith

- Vanessa Jane Ward

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
