# The Ecclesiastical Property Measure 2015 (Commencement) Order 2015

Made: 18th May 2015

Coming into force: 1st July 2015

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2) of the Ecclesiastical Property Measure 2015[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Ecclesiastical Property Measure 2015 (Commencement) Order 2015.

#### Commencement

###### 2

All the provisions of the Ecclesiastical Property Measure 2015 which are not already in force come into force on 1st July 2015.

## Signed

+Justin Cantuar: — Archbishop of Canterbury

+Sentamu Ebor: — Archbishop of York — 18th May 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes provision for the commencement of the Ecclesiastical Property Measure 2015, to the extent that its provisions are not already in force.
