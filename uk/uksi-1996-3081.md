# The Consumer Credit (Exempt Agreements) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1996

Made: 9th December 1996

Laid before Parliament: 10th December 1996

Coming into force: 6th January 1997

The Secretary of State, after consulting in accordance with section 16(3) of the Consumer Credit Act 1974[^f00001] the persons referred to therein, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 16(1) and (4) and 182(2) and (4) of that Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Consumer Credit (Exempt Agreements) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 6th January 1997.

###### 2

The Consumer Credit (Exempt Agreements) Order 1989[^f00002] is hereby amended in Schedule 1.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for further miscellaneous amendments to be made to the Consumer Credit (Exempt Agreements) Order 1989.

It removes the name of one insurance company from the list of insurance companies in Part I of Schedule 1 to the Order and inserts the name of one other in that list. It also adds the names of seven bodies corporate named in Orders made under the Housing Act 1985 to the list of such bodies in Part III of that Schedule by substituting a new Part III.
