#### Citation, commencement, effect and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Communications (Television Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2026.

- (3) These Regulations have effect in respect of TV licences issued on or after 1st April 2026.

- (4) These Regulations extend to—

- (a) England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland,

- (b) the Channel Islands, except regulation 4(43) and (44) and Schedule 2, and

- (c) the Isle of Man.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Communications (Television Licensing) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/692](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/692)) by providing for increases in—

1. the amount payable for most categories of TV licence;

2. the amount of each instalment for certain categories of TV licence which are payable in instalments.

In summary—

1. the amount usually payable for a colour TV licence is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 and that for a black and white TV licence is increased from £58.50 to £60.50 (see regulations 3 and 5);

2. the instalments for a premium instalment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £179.50 to £185.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (2));

3. the instalments for a budget instalment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (3) to (9));

4. the instalments for an easy entry TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 (see regulation 4(1) and (10) to (42));

5. the instalments for a simple payment TV licence are increased so that the total amount payable is increased from £174.50 to £180.00 (see regulation 4(1), (43) and (44)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
