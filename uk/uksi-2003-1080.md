# The General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2003

Made: 19th March 2003

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 19th day of March 2003 By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken these rules into consideration, are hereby pleased to approve them with effect from 31st March 2003.

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2003.

###### 2

The General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules) Order of Council 2002[^f00002] is hereby revoked with effect from 31st March 2003.

## GENERAL OPTICAL COUNCIL

In pursuance of their powers under section 10 of the Opticians Act 1989, the General Optical Council hereby make the following rules:

###### 1

These rules may be cited as the Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules 2003.

###### 2

In the Registration and Enrolment Rules 1976 (which in their original form are scheduled to the General Optical Council (Registration and Enrolment Rules) Order of Council 1977[^f00003])the tables shown in the Appendix shall be substituted by the following:

| a For applications in respect of a registration period ending before 1st April 2003 | £62 |
| b For applications in respect of a registration period commencing on or after 1st April 2003 except where paragraph (c) applies | £115 |
| c For applications in respect of a registration period commencing on or after 1st January 2004 | £68 |
| Retention fee: | £115 |
| Restoration fee: |  |

###### 2

The Registration and Enrolment (Amendment) Rules 2002[^f00004] (which will be spent on the coming into force of these Rules) are hereby revoked.

###### 3

These Rules shall come into force on 31st March 2003.

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The rules approved by this Order increase with effect from 31st March 2003 the fees payable to the General Optical Council by ophthalmic and dispensing opticians and bodies corporate carrying on business as opticians for registration, enrolment or retention in, restoration to and transfer within the register of opticians. The fees were last fixed with effect from 1st April 2002 and have been increased by 9.5 per cent.

This Order also revokes a previous Order of Council which becomes spent on the revocation of rules which are scheduled to it.
