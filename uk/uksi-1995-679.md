# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Borough of Lambeth) (Amendment) Order 1995

Made: 9th March 1995

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1995

Coming into force: 3rd April 1995

NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powersconferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 andof all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the followingOrder:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas)(London Borough of Lambeth) (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into forceon 3rd April 1995.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the principal order” means the Road Traffic (SpecialParking Areas) (London Borough of Lambeth) Order 1994[^f00002]

#### Amendment of principal order

###### 3

- (1) The principal order shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2—

- (a) in paragraph (1) for the third unnumbered sub-paragraph there shall besubstituted—

- “a reference to the Schedule is a reference to the Schedule to thisOrder;”

- (b) in paragraph (1) for the words “Schedule 3”, in the two places wherethose words occur, there shall be substituted “the Schedule”;

- (c) in paragraph (1) for the words “that Schedule” there shall besubstituted “the Schedule”; and

- (d) paragraphs (2) and (3) shall be omitted.

- (3) For article 3 there shall be substituted—

> > (3)
>   (1) The Secretary of State hereby designates the London borough of Lambethexcept the roads named or described in the Schedule as a special parking area.
>   (2) A reference in the following provisions of this Order to the specialparking area is a reference to the area designated by this article.

- (4) For the words “an area” in article 4(1)(b), there shall besubstituted “the area”.

- (5) For the words “a special parking area” in article 4(2), article 5(1),article 5(2) (in the first place where those words occur), article 5(4) andarticle 5(5) there shall in each case be substituted “the special parkingarea”.

- (6) For Schedule 1 there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in theSchedule to this order.

- (7) Schedules 2 and 3 shall be omitted.

## THE SCHEDULE — SCHEDULE SUBSTITUTED FOR SCHEDULE 1 TO THE PRINCIPAL ORDER

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

Steven Norris — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 9th March 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Special Parking Areas) (London Boroughof Lambeth) Order 1994 (“the principal order”) so as to designate thewhole of the London borough of Lambeth (excluding the roads specified in theSchedule) as a special parking area for the purposes of Part II of the RoadTraffic Act 1991 with effect from 3rd April 1995. The new area subsumes thespecial parking areas at Brixton and North Lambeth designated by the principalorder.
