# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2008

Made: 12th March 2008

Coming into force: 31st March 2008

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 5 to that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 31st March 2008.

#### Originating capital of NHS trusts

###### 2

The originating capital of an NHS trust specified in column 1 of the Schedule shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, provided for in paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of National Health Service trusts established by the Secretary of State from 1st April 2007 to 1st October 2007.
