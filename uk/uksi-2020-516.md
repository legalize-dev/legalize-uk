# The Sea Fishing (Enforcement) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: 15th May 2020

Laid before Parliament: 19th May 2020

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the 1972 Act and it appears to the Secretary of State that it is expedient for the references to Article 10 of Council Regulation (EU) 2020/123 fixing for 2020 the fishing opportunities for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Union waters and, for Union fishing vessels, in certain non-Union waters[^f00003] to be construed as a reference to that Article as amended from time to time.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Fishing (Enforcement) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and come into force 21 days after the day on which the Regulations are laid.

#### Amendments to the Sea Fishing (Enforcement) Regulations 2018

###### 2

In the table in the Schedule to the Sea Fishing (Enforcement) Regulations 2018[^f00004], in the first column—

- (a) for “Council Regulation (EU) 2019/124 fixing for 2019 the fishing opportunities for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Union waters and, for Union fishing vessels, in certain non-Union waters” substitute “Council Regulation (EU) 2020/123 fixing for 2020 the fishing opportunities for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Union waters and, for Union fishing vessels, in certain non-Union waters”; and

- (b) in the entries specifying the Articles of that Regulation—

- (i) for “Articles 10(1), (2) and (3)” substitute “Article 10(1), (2) and (3)”; and

- (ii) for “Article 10(4)” substitute “Article 10(5)”.

## Signed

Victoria Prentis — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 15th May 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend references to certain fisheries conservation measures in Council Regulation (EU) 2019/124 (OJ No. L 29, 31.1.2019, p.1) in the table in the Schedule to the Sea Fishing (Enforcement) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/849](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/849)) to reflect the coming into force of Council Regulation (EU) 2020/123 (OJ No. L 25, 30.1.2020, p.1) which replaces Council Regulation (EU) 2019/124.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
