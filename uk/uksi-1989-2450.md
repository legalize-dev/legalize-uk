# The Brunei (Appeals) Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made: 19th December 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 2(2) of the Brunei (Appeals) Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Brunei (Appeals) Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1989.

###### 2

The Brunei (Appeals) Act 1989 shall come into force on 1st February 1990.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 19th December 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Brunei (Appeals) Act 1989 into force on 1st February 1990.
