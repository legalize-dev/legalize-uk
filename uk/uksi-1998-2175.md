# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1998

Made: 30th August 1998

Laid before Parliament: 11th September 1998

Coming into force: 16th October 1998

Now therefore, the Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 16th October 1998.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “the principal Order” means the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation Order 1993[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 3

The principal Order shall have effect as if, in article 2 of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation (Amendment) Order 1998[^f00004], for the words “the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation (Amendment) Order 1993” there were substituted “the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation Order 1993”.

## Signed

Elliot Morley — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 30th August 1998

We consent,

Bob Ainsworth — Jim Dowd — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 25th August 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/86)) (“the principal Order”), which designated an area in the South Wessex Downs as an environmentally sensitive area, in implementation of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2078/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/2078) (OJ No. L215, 30.7.92, p. 85) on agricultural production methods compatible with the requirements of protection of the environment and the maintenance of the countryside, as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2772/95](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1995/2772) (OJ No. L288, 1.12.95, p. 35) as in turn rectified by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1962/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/1962) (OJ No. L259, 12.10.96, p. 7).

This Order corrects an error made in the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (South Wessex Downs) Designation (Amendment) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/1309](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1309)) in citing the title to the principal Order incorrectly.

No regulatory appraisal has been made in respect of this Order.
