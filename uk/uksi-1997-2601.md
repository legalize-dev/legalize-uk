# The Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments (Administration of Justice Act 1920, Part II) (Amendment) Order 1997

Made: 30th October 1997

Coming into force: 1st February 1998

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 30th day of October 1997 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred on Her by section 14(2) of the Administration of Justice Act 1920[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments (Administration of Justice Act 1920, Part II) (Amendment) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st February 1998.

###### 2

The Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments (Administration of Justice Act 1920, Part II) (Consolidation) Order 1984[^f00002] shall be amended by deleting from Schedule 1 thereto the entry relating to Gibraltar.

## Signed

N.H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments (Administration of Justice Act 1920, Part II) (Consolidation) Order 1984 by deleting the entry relating to Gibraltar.

Relevant judgments given after the coming into force of this Order will be enforced in the United Kingdom in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 (Gibraltar) Order 1997 (S.I. [1997/2602](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2602)).
