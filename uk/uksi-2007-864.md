# The Diseases of Fish (England and Wales) Order 2007

Made: 15th March 2007

Laid before Parliament: 16th March 2007

Coming into force: 6th April 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 13(1) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1937[^f00001], makes the following Order.

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Fish (England and Wales) Order 2007; it extends to England and Wales and comes into force on 6th April 2007.

#### Amendment of definition of “infected” in Diseases of Fish Act 1937

###### 2

In the definition of “infected” in section 10(1) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1937[^f00002], after “infectious salmon anaemia (ISA),” insert “Koi Herpesvirus Disease,”.

## Signed

Ben Bradshaw — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 15th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order amends the list of diseases in section 10(1) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1937 by adding Koi Herpesvirus Disease.

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector has been placed in the library of both Houses of Parliament, and copies may be obtained from the Aquatic Animal Health Team, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, Area 108, 1A page Street, London SW1P 4PQ.
