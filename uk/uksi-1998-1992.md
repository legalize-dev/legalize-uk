# The Harrow and Hillingdon Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

Made: 10th August 1998

Coming into force: 26th August 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003] and considering it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Harrow and Hillingdon Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 26th August 1998.

#### Amendment of Establishment Order

###### 2

For article 3 of the Harrow and Hillingdon Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00005] (nature and functions of the trust) there shall be substituted the following article:—

> > (3)
>   (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>   (2) The trust’s functions (which include functions which the Secretary of State considers appropriate in relation to the provision of services by the trust for one or more Health Authorities) shall be:—
>     (a) to own and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act Harrow Hospital, Roxeth Hill, Harrow, Middlesex HA2 0JX and associated premises, and there to provide and manage hospital and community health accommodation and services;
>     (b) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act Rowanweald, 243–249 High Road, Harrow Weald, Harrow, Middlesex HA3 5EG, and there to provide and manage hospital accommodation and services.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tessa Jowell — Minister of State for Public Health — Department of Health — 10th August 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Harrow and Hillingdon Healthcare National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 to provide and manage Rowanweald, 243–249 High Road, Harrow Weald, Harrow, Middlesex HA3 5EG.
