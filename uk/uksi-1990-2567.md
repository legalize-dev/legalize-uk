# The Folkestone, Mid-Sussex and West Kent (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1990

Made: 5th December 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers under section 162(7) of the Water Act 1989, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consulting all such persons as he considers it appropriate to consult, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Folkestone, Mid-Sussex and West Kent (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Codes of Practice) Order 1990.

#### Codes of Practice

###### 2

The Codes of Practice prepared by the Companies and certified as such by the Secretary of State are hereby approved.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment

Dinah Nichols — An Under Secretary in the — Department of the Environment — 5th December 1990
