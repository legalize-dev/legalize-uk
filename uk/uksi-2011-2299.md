# The Landfill (Maximum Landfill Amount) Regulations 2011

Made: 15th September 2011

Coming into force: 1st October 2011

A draft of this instrument has been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament pursuant to section 28(2) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Landfill (Maximum Landfill Amount) Regulations 2011; and

- (b) come into force on 1st October 2011.

#### Landfill targets

###### 2

The maximum amount of biodegradable municipal waste by weight in tonnes that may be sent to landfill from each area specified in the table in each scheme year that is a target year must be in accordance with the table.

| Area | Target Year ending in 2013 | Target Year ending in 2020 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | 17,844,000 | 12,491,000 |
| England | 14,515,000 | 10,161,000 |
| Scotland | 1,798,000 | 1,258,000 |
| Wales | 919,000 | 643,000 |
| Northern Ireland | 612,000 | 429,000 |

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Landfill (Scheme Year and Maximum Landfill Amount) Regulations 2004[^f00003] are revoked, except for regulations 1 and 2.

## Signed

Henley — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 15th September 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations specify the maximum amount by weight of biodegradable municipal waste that is allowed to be sent each target year to landfills, for each of the United Kingdom, England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, as required by section 1(1) of the Waste and Emissions Trading Act [2003 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/33). The amounts set under regulation 2 are consistent with the obligations of the United Kingdom under Article 5(2) of Council Directive [1999/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0031) on the landfill of waste (OJ No L 182, 16.7.1999, p 1).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
