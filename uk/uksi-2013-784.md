# The Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2013

Made: 2nd April 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 32(3) of the Armed Forces Act 2011[^f00001]:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No.3) Order 2013.

###### 2

In this Order “the Act” means the Armed Forces Act 2011.

#### Commencement

###### 3

Section 25 of the Act shall come into force on 6th April 2013.

## Signed

Mark Francois — Minister of State — Ministry of Defence — 2nd April 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 25 of the Armed Forces Act [2011 (c.18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/18) on 6th April 2013.
