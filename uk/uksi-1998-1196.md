# The Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1998

Made: 5th May 1998

Coming into force: 26th May 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 26(2)(f) and 275(8) of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Scotland) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 26th May 1998.

#### Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Scotland) Order 1997

###### 2

In Class 9 (Houses) in the Schedule to the Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Scotland) Order 1997[^f00002], after the word “guesthouse” in paragraph (b) there shall be added the words “(not in either case being carried out in a flat)”.

## Signed

Calum MacDonald — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 5th May 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends Class 9 in the Schedule to the Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) (Scotland) Order 1997.

Article 2 amends paragraph (b) of this Class to make clear that it does not allow a flat to be used as a bed and breakfast establishment or guesthouse.
