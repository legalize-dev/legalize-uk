## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [1989 c. 37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/37); section 1(6A) defines “the licensing authority” as the Sports Grounds Safety Authority. Section 1(6A) was inserted by paragraph 7(1) of Schedule 2 to the Sports Grounds Safety Authority Act [2011 (c. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/6).

[^f00002]: The Football Licensing Authority was established by section 8 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Football Spectators Act [1989 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/37) and renamed the Sports Grounds Safety Authority under section 1(1) of the Sports Grounds Safety Authority Act 2011.

[^f00003]: “Licence to admit spectators” is defined in section 1(7) of the Football Spectators Act 1989.
