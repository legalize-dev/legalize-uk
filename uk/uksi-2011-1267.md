# The Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2011

Made: 12th May 2011

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(2)(b) of the Pensions Act 2007 .

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2007 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2011.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

The following provisions of the Pensions Act 2007 shall come into force on 6th April 2012—

- (a) section 15(1) (abolition of contracting-out for defined contribution pension schemes),

- (b) in Part 2 of Schedule 4 (abolition of contracting-out for defined contribution pension schemes: further amendments)—

- (i) paragraph 46, in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in sub-paragraphs (ii), (iii) and (iv) of this paragraph,

- (ii) paragraph 59(1), in so far as it relates to paragraph 59(2) and (4),

- (iii) paragraph 59(2) and (4), and

- (iv) paragraph 60, and

- (c) Part 7 of Schedule 7 (abolition of contracting-out for defined contribution pension schemes: further repeals) in so far as it relates to the repeal of the specified provisions contained within paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993, and section 137(7)(a) of the Pensions Act 1995 .

###### 3

The following provisions of the Pensions Act 2007 shall come into force on 6th April 2015—

- (a) in Part 2 of Schedule 4 (abolition of contracting-out for defined contribution pension schemes: further amendments)—

- (i) paragraph 42(1), in so far as it relates to paragraph 42(2) and (3)(b),

- (ii) paragraph 42(2) and (3)(b),

- (iii) paragraph 46, in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in sub-paragraphs (iv), (v) and (vi) of this paragraph,

- (iv) paragraphs 47, 49 and 54,

- (v) paragraph 59(1), in so far as it relates to paragraph 59(3), and

- (vi) paragraph 59(3), and

- (b) Part 7 of Schedule 7 (abolition of contracting-out for defined contribution pension schemes: further repeals) in so far as it relates to the repeal of the following provisions—

- (i) section 4C(2)(d) and (5)(g) and (h), of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 ,

- (ii) sections 8(3), 31 and 45B of the Pension Schemes Act 1993,

- (iii) section 139 of, and paragraph 36 of Schedule 5 to the Pensions Act 1995 , and

- (iv) paragraphs 34(b), 43 and 50 of Schedule 1 to the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) Act 1999 .

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Steve Webb — Minister of State, — 12th May 2011
