# The Traffic Management (London Borough of Barking and Dagenham) Permit Scheme Order 2011

Made: 26th October 2011

Coming into force: 28th November 2011

The Secretary of State for Transport having approved the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme under section 34(2) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(4) and (5) and 39(2) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management (London Borough of Barking and Dagenham) Permit Scheme Order 2011 and comes into force on 28th November 2011.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme” means the permit scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order in terms commonly known as the “London Permit Scheme” which was prepared and submitted to the Secretary of State by the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham and has been approved by the Secretary of State; and

- “specified streets” has the meaning given by regulation 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007[^f00002].

#### Commencement of Permit Scheme

###### 3

The London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme comes into effect on 28th November 2011.

#### Application of Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007

###### 4

Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 shall apply to the specified streets within the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme.

The London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme is designed to control the carrying out of works in specified streets in Barking and Dagenham by imposing an obligation to obtain a permit from the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham before undertaking the works.

*Article 3* gives effect to the permit scheme, which will come into effect on 28th November 2011. The permit scheme referred to in this article is set out in the Schedule to the Order.

*Article 4* applies Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 (which makes provision for the disapplication and modification of enactments in relation to specified works in specified streets) to the specified works and specified streets within the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme.

A copy of the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham Permit Scheme is deposited and can be inspected at the offices of the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham, Network Management, Street Scene, Environment and Enforcement, London Borough of Barking and Dagenham, Frizlands Administration Offices, Frizlands Lane, Dagenham RM10 7HX and is available to inspect on the Borough’s website www.lbbd.gov.uk.
