# The Criminal Justice Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made: 4th May 1999

Coming into force: 1st June 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 102(2) of the Criminal Justice Act 1991[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) (Amendment) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

###### 2

Article 2(5) of, and Schedule 3 to, the Criminal Justice Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

###### 3

The provisions of the Criminal Justice Act 1991 referred to in the left-hand column of the Schedule to this Order (which relate to the matters described in the right-hand column of that Schedule) shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Criminal Justice Act 1991 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/333](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/333)) to bring into force on 1st June 1999 minor supplementary provisions relating to the committal for sentence of children and young persons to local authority secure accommodation.
