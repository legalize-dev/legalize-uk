# The Local Elections (Communities) (Welsh Forms) (Amendment) Order 2014

Made: 3rd April 2014

Laid before Parliament: 7th April 2014

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Lord President of the Council, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(2) of the Welsh Language Act 1993[^f00001], as applied by section 22 of the Representation of the People Act 1985[^f00002], makes the following Order.

#### Citation, commencement, transition and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Local Elections (Communities) (Welsh Forms) (Amendment) Order 2014.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 9th April 2014 but has no effect in relation to any election in respect of which the date of the poll specified in the notice of election, as defined by rule 3 in each of Schedules 2 and 3 to the Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (England and Wales) Rules 2006[^f00003], is on or before 21st May 2014.

- (3) In this Order—

- (a) “the 2007 Order” means the Local Elections (Communities) (Welsh Forms) Order 2007 [^f00004]; and

- (b) unless otherwise stated, any reference to a numbered article or Schedule is a reference to the article or Schedule bearing that number in the 2007 Order.

#### Amendment of the 2007 Order

###### 2

- (1) The 2007 Order is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 3 (interpretation)—

- (a) after the definition of “mayoral election” insert—

> > - “*police and crime commissioner election*” means an election of a police and crime commissioner in accordance with Chapter 6 of Part 1 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011[^f00005];

- (b) in paragraph (2), in the definition of “relevant election or referendum”, after paragraph (e) insert—

> > (f) a police and crime commissioner election,

- (3) In article 4 (Welsh versions of forms of words)—

- (a) for paragraph (1) substitute—

> > (1) In the form of the front of the ballot paper in the Appendices to Schedules 2 and 3 to the 2006 Rules—
>   (a) “Pleidleisiwch ar gyfer un ymgeisydd yn unig trwy roi croes [x] yn y blwch nesaf at eich dewis” is prescribed as the form of words in Welsh for the words “Vote for only one candidate by putting a cross [x] in the box next to your choice”;
>   (b) “Pleidleisiwch ar gyfer dim mwy na ____ ymgeisydd trwy roi croes [x] yn y blwch nesaf at bob un o’ch dewisiadau” is prescribed as the form of words in Welsh for the words “Vote for no more than ____ candidates by putting a cross [x] in the box next to each of your choices.”.

- (b) in paragraph (2) for “immediately beneath” substitute “together, above or below”.

- (4) In article 9 (Welsh and English versions of forms and a form of words for use at combined polls)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(d) for “local government” substitute “community”;

- (b) in paragraph (2) for “rule 26(8)” substitute “rule 26(10)”.

- (5) In Schedule 2 (Welsh versions of forms and a form of words for use at polls taken alone), for the following forms substitute the corresponding forms in Schedule 1 to this Order—

- (a) Form 3, the form of candidate’s consent to nomination;

- (b) Form 4, the form of directions for the guidance of the voters in voting;

- (c) Form 5, the form of declaration to be made by the companion of a voter with disabilities.

- (6) In Schedule 3 (Welsh and English versions of forms and a form of words for use at polls taken alone), for the following forms substitute the corresponding forms in Schedule 2 to this Order—

- (a) Form 3, the form of postal voting statement;

- (b) Form 4, the official poll card;

- (c) Form 5, the official postal poll card;

- (d) Form 6, the official proxy poll card;

- (e) Form 7, the official proxy postal poll card;

- (f) Form 8, the form of certificate of employment;

- (g) Form 9, the notice prescribed by rule 26(8).

- (7) In Schedule 4 (Welsh versions of forms and a form of words for use at combined polls), for the following forms substitute the corresponding forms in Schedule 3 to this Order—

- (a) Form 3, the form of candidate’s consent to nomination;

- (b) Form 4, the form of directions for the guidance of the voters in voting;

- (c) Form 5, the form of declaration to be made by the companion of a voter with disabilities.

- (8) In Schedule 5 (Welsh and English versions of forms and a form of words for use at combined polls), for the following forms substitute the corresponding forms in Schedule 4 to this Order—

- (a) Form 3, the form of postal voting statement (for use where there is a joint issue and receipt of postal ballot papers);

- (b) Form 4, the form of postal voting statement (for use when a community poll is combined with another poll but the postal ballot papers are not combined);

- (c) Form 5, the official poll card;

- (d) Form 6, the official postal poll card;

- (e) Form 7, the official proxy poll card;

- (f) Form 8, the official proxy postal poll card;

- (g) Form 9, the form of certificate of employment;

- (h) Form 10, the notice prescribed by rule 26(10).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Local Elections (Communities) (Welsh Forms) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1013), the “2007 Order”) which principally prescribes Welsh versions of certain forms or forms of words to be used at community elections held in Wales. The forms prescribed by article 2(5) and (7) of and Schedules 1 and 3 to this Order are Welsh versions of certain forms which may be used at such elections in Wales. The forms prescribed by article 2(6) and (8) and Schedules 2 and 4 to this Order are the versions partly in Welsh and partly in English of certain forms which must be used at these elections in Wales.

Article 2(3)(a) and (b) provides the forms of words in Welsh to be used in the form of the front of the ballot paper and article 2(2) updates references to legislation. Article 2(3)(c) and (4) corrects errors in the 2007 Order.

The forms included in this Order reflect changes made to the English versions of the forms and forms of words made by the Local Elections (Parishes and Communities) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2014 ([S.I. 2014/492](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/492)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument because no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen other than that set out in the impact assessments which relate to Part 2 of the Electoral Registration and Administration Act 2013.
