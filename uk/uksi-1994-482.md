# The Churchill John Radcliffe National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 1994

Made: 1st March 1994

Coming into force: 11th March 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Churchill John Radcliffe National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 11th March 1994.

- (2) In this Order unless the context otherwise requires—

- “the Order” means the Churchill John Radcliffe National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00003];

- “the Trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

#### Change of Name of NHS trust

###### 2

The Trust shall be called the Oxford Radcliffe Hospital National Health Service Trust instead of the Churchill John Radcliffe National Health Service Trust and accordingly, in the definition of “the Trust” in article 1(2) and in article 2 of the Order, for the words “Churchill John Radcliffe” there shall be substituted “Oxford Radcliffe Hospital”.

#### Transitional provisions

###### 3

The change of name under this Order shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person;

- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before or after this Order comes into force) which refers to the Trust by its previous name,

and all documents or instruments which refer to the Trust by its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 1st March 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of an NHS Trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 from the Churchill John Radcliffe National Health Service Trust to the Oxford Radcliffe Hospital National Health Service Trust and makes consequential amendments to the establishment Order and transitional provisions.
