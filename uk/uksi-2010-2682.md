# The Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 2010

Made: 10th November 2010

Coming into force: 11th November 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of November 2010Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 247 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Armorial Bearings) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 11th November 2010.

###### 2

The armorial bearings which, immediately before 1st April 1974, were lawfully borne and used by the local authority named in column (1) of the following Table may be borne and used by the corresponding local authority named in column (2) of the Table, provided they have first been exemplified according to the laws of arms and recorded in the College of Arms—

| (1) | (2) |
| --- | --- |
| The corporation of the Borough of Morpeth | Morpeth Town Council |
| The corporation of the Borough of Newark | Newark Town Council |
| Barnard Castle Urban District Council | Barnard Castle Town Council |

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order authorises Morpeth Town Council, Newark Town Council and Barnard Castle Town Council to bear and use the armorial bearings formerly borne and used by the corporation of the Borough of Morpeth, the corporation of the Borough of Newark and Barnard Castle Urban District Council respectively.
