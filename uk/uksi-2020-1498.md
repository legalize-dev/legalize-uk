# The Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) (No. 3) Regulations 2020

Made: 9th December 2020

Laid before Parliament: 10th December 2020

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The requirements of paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 7 to that Act (relating to the appropriate Parliamentary procedure for these Regulations) have been satisfied.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) (No. 3) Regulations 2020.

- (2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on IP completion day.

- (3) This regulation and regulation 9 come into force immediately before IP completion day.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in section 8(1) of, and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular under section 8(2)(a)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

Regulations 3 to 7 amend Commission Implementing Regulation [(EU) 2018/1048](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2018/1048) laying down airspace usage requirements and operating procedures concerning performance-based navigation to enable them to remain operable in UK law after IP completion day.

Regulation 8 revokes Commission Implementing Regulation [(EU) 2020/1627](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2020/1627) of 3 November 2020 on exceptional measures for the third reference period (2020-2024) of the single European sky performance and charging scheme due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

Regulation 9 amends the Air Traffic Management (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 in respect of declarations of conformity or suitability for use of constituents produced by manufacturers of air traffic management systems.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as no significant impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum has been published alongside this instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
