# The University College London Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2003

Made: 10th March 2003

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 11(3) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and section 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the University College London Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the NHS Trust” means the University College London Hospitals National Health Service Trust[^f00003];

- “the new trustees” means the Trustees for University College London Hospitals National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the NHS Trust and the new trustees and signed on behalf of the NHS Trust on 4th December 2002 and on behalf of the new trustees on 15th December 2002.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On 1st April 2003 the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property[^f00004], shall be transferred from the NHS Trust to the new trustees.

- (2) In any trust instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the name by which the trustees of the trust property are to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new trustees and any reference to the NHS Trust in the name by which the charitable trust governing the trust property is to be known shall be construed as a reference to the new trustees.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hazel Blears — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 10th March 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers trust property held by the University College London Hospitals National Health Service Trust to the trustees established for that Trust under section 11(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
