# The Combined Probation Areas (Humberside) Order 1999

Made: 27th May 1999

Coming into force: 1st June 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2 of the Probation Services Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that section, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Humberside) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st June 1999.

###### 2

For that part of the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Humberside area[^f00003], there shall be substituted the following–

| *(1)* | *(2)* | *(3)* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Petty Sessions Area | Probation Area | Number of Justices on Probation Committee |
| Bainton, Wilton and Holme Beacon | Humberside | 2 |
| Beverley |  | 1 |

| Grimsby and Cleethorpes |  | 4 |
| Kingston Upon Hull |  | 6 |
| North Lincolnshire |  | 3 |
| South Hunsley Beacon |  | 2 |
| South and Middle Holderness |  | 1 |

## Signed

Gareth Williams — Minister of State — 27th May 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/1713](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1713)) to take account of the creation of the new petty sessional divisions of North Lincolnshire, Goole and Howdenshire and South Hunsley Beacon. These petty sessional divisions are created by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Humberside) Order 1999 (S.I. [1999/493](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/493)) which comes into force, subject to certain exceptions, on 1st June 1999.
