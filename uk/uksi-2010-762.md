# The House of Commons Disqualification Order 2010

Made: 17th March 2010

Coming into force: 17th March 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 17th day of March 2010Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly, Her Majesty, in exercise of the power conferred by section 5(1) of that Act and in accordance with the resolution of the House of Commons, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the House of Commons Disqualification Order 2010.

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975 (offices the holders of which are disqualified for membership of the House of Commons) is amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — AMENDMENTS OF SCHEDULE 1 TO THE HOUSE OF COMMONS DISQUALIFICATION ACT 1975
