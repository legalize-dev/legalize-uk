# The Food (Emergency Control) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 2006

Made: 22nd August 2006

Laid before Parliament: 7th September 2006

Coming into force: 1st October 2006

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of the Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Food (Emergency Control) (Revocation) (England) Regulations 2006;

- (b) shall come into force on 1 October 2006; and

- (c) shall apply in relation to England only.

#### Revocations

###### 2

The instruments specified in column 1 of the Schedule are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which apply in relation to England only, provide for the revocation of specified instruments which implemented Commission Decisions imposing conditions on the import of specific products that could be contaminated with excessive levels of aflatoxins.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
