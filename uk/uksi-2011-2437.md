EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, made under the United Nations Act 1946, amends the Iraq (United Nations Sanctions) Order 2003 ([S.I.2003/1519](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1519)) to update the definition of “restrictive goods”.
