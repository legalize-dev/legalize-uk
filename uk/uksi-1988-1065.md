# The Customs Duties (ECSC) (Amendment No. 1) Order 1988

Made: 16th June 1988

Laid before the House of Commons: 17th June 1988

Coming into force: 20th June 1988

The Treasury, by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5(1) and (3) of, and paragraph 4 of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, on the recommendation of the Secretary of State, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Customs Duties (ECSC) (Amendment No. 1) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 20th June 1988.

###### 2

Up to and including 31 December 1988, article 6(1) of the Customs Duties (ECSC) Order 1987[^f00002] (which exempts from duty goods to which that Order applies originating in certain countries) shall not apply to good which fall within headings 7207 19 31, 7207 20 71, 7216 10 00, 7216 21 00, 7216 22 00, 7216 31 00, 7216 32 00, 7216 33 00, 7216 40 10, 7216 40 90, 7216 50 10, 7216 50 90, 7216 90 10, and 7301 10 00 which originate in Yugoslavia.

## Signed

Peter Lloyd — Tony Durant — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th June 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 20th June 1988 reimposes duties on certain iron or steel products originating in Yugoslavia.

This Order implements a reintroduction of duty up to the end of 1988 made pursuant to Article 1, paragraph 3 of a decision [87/612/ECSC](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/1987/612/oj) of the representatives of governments of Member States of the European Coal and Steel Community meeting within the Council on 21 December 1987 (OJ No. L400, 31.12.1987, p.45). Decision [87/612/ECSC](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/1987/612/oj) of 21 December 1987 established ceilings for imports of certain ECSC goods originating in Yugoslavia. These ceilings have now been reached on goods falling within the above heading.

The Commission Communication to Member States giving notice of the reintroduction of custom duties is published in OJ No. C153 of 11.6.1988.
