# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2017

Made: 13th December 2017

Coming into force: 14th December 2017

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of December 2017Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2017 and comes into force on 14th December 2017.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — Person appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 14th December 2017

Paul Joseph Tomkow

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council
