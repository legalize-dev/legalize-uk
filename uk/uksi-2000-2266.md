# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made: 25th August 2000

Laid before Parliament: 25th August 2000

Coming into force: 26th August 2000

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, being designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 26th August 2000.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations" means the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000 .

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

In Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations—

- (a) after the entry relating to Bristol Port (Royal Portbury Dock) there shall be inserted the following entry—

| Gatwick Airport | All animals other than ungulates (ii) |
| --- | --- |

; and

- (b) the entry relating to Tilbury Port shall be deleted.

###### 3

In Schedule 3 Part I, paragraph 1, to the principal Regulations—

- (a) before the words “Council Directive [97/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0012)" where they appear for the second time there shall be inserted the words “ The requirements of ”;

- (b) after the entry relating to Council Directive [98/99/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0099) there shall be added the following entry—

> > Directive [2000/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0020) of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ No. L163, 4.7.00, p. 35);

and

- (c) after the entry relating to Commission Decision [99/579/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0579) there shall be added the following entry—

> > Commission Decision [2000/528/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0528) (OJ No. L214, 25.8.00, p. 42);

## Signed

Nick Brown — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 25th August 2000
