# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (New Method of Instituting Proceedings) (Public Prosecutor Specification) Order 2011

Made: 7th September 2011

Laid before Parliament: 9th September 2011

Coming into force: 3rd October 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 29(5)(h) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (New Method of Instituting Proceedings) (Public Prosecutor Specification) Order 2011 and comes into force on 3rd October 2011.

###### 2

Transport for London[^f00002] is specified as a public prosecutor for the purposes of section 29 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

J Djanogly — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 7th September 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order specifies Transport for London as a “public prosecutor” for the purposes of section 29 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003. Section 29 provides public prosecutors with the power to institute criminal proceedings on the issue of a written charge and requisition.
