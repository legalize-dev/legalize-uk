# The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 29th September 2015

Laid before Parliament: 2nd October 2015

Coming into force: 26th October 2015

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to energy and energy sources, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 26th October 2015.

#### Amendments to the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014

###### 2

- (1) The Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph 1(a)(ii) of Schedule 1, after “annual balance sheet total” insert “in excess”.

- (3) In paragraph 12 of Schedule 2, omit sub-paragraph (b).

- (4) In Schedule 4—

- (a) in paragraph 6, after “28” insert “days”;

- (b) in paragraph 12, omit “calendar”; and

- (c) in paragraph 13, for “appeal body” substitute “Planning Appeals Commission”.

## Signed

Bourne — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Energy and Climate Change — 29th September 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, issued free of charge, amend Schedules 1, 2 and 4 to the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014 to correct drafting errors. Those Regulations implement Article 8(4) to (6) of Directive 2012/27/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on energy efficiency[^f00004].

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside these Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk.
