# The Income-Related Benefits (Subsidy to Authorities) Amendment Order 2002

Made: 17th July 2002

Laid before Parliament: 23rd July 2002

Coming into force: 17th August 2002

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, with the consent of the Treasury[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 140B(1), (3) and (4), 140F(2) and 189(1) and (4) to (7) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with organisations appearing to him to be representative of the authorities concerned[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Income-Related Benefits (Subsidy to Authorities) Amendment Order 2002 and shall come into force on 17th August 2002.

- (2) In this Order, “the principal Order” means the Income-Related Benefits (Subsidy to Authorities) Order 1998[^f00004].

#### Scottish Homes

###### 2

For the year commencing on 1st April 2001, the amount referred to as the maximum in article 13(2)(a) of the principal Order[^f00005] shall be “£682,145”.

#### Sums to be used in the calculation of subsidy

###### 3

For Schedule 1 to the principal Order[^f00006], there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to this Order for the year commencing on 1st April 2001.

#### Thresholds for subsidy on rent allowances

###### 4

For the table in Part V of Schedule 4 to the principal Order[^f00007], there shall be substituted the table in Schedule 2 to this Order for the year commencing on 1st April 2001.

#### Amendment of Schedule 5

###### 5

In paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 5 to the principal Order (benefit savings threshold)[^f00008], for the year commencing on 1st April 2001—

- (a) for the definition of “F” there shall be substituted:

> > - F is— where an authority identified in column (1) of Schedule 1 is operating the weekly benefit savings scheme and that authority— has not been certified by the Secretary of State as having complied with the verification framework in the relevant year, the sum specified in column (6a) of Schedule 1; or has been certified by the Secretary of State as having complied with the verification framework in the relevant year, the sum specified in column (6b) of Schedule 1; or where an authority identified in column (1) of Schedule 1 is operating the new incentive scheme, the sum specified in column (6c) of Schedule 1;

- (b) after the definition of “intervention”, there shall be inserted:

> > - “new incentive scheme” means the scheme set out in the following circulars issued by the Secretary of State[^f00009]— HB/CTB F12/2000; and HB/CTB F19/2001;

- (c) after the definition of “verification framework”, there shall be inserted:

> > - “weekly benefit savings scheme” means the scheme set out in the following circulars issued by the Secretary of State[^f00010]— HB/CTB F17/1998; and HB/CTB F13/2000.

#### Amendment of Schedule 1

###### 6

- (1) Column (5) of Schedule 1 to the principal Order (additional subsidy) shall be amended by substituting “£134,827” for “£132,827” in respect of Sefton.

- (2) This article shall have effect in respect of the year commencing on 1st April 2000.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Income-Related Benefits (Subsidy to Authorities) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/562](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/562)) (“the principal Order”). Section 140F(2) of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5) authorises the varying of an Order before, during or after the year to which it relates. Apart from Article 6, the amendments made by this Order relate to the year commencing on 1st April 2001.

Article 2 provides that the additional subsidy payable to Scottish Homes is not to exceed £682,145.

Article 3 substitutes for Schedule 1 to the principal Order a new schedule of figures to be used in calculating subsidy payable in respect of rent allowances.

Article 4 substitutes for the table in Part V of Schedule 4 to the principal Order a new table of figures to be used in calculating thresholds for reduced subsidy in respect of rent allowances.

Article 5 amends the method by which the benefit savings threshold is calculated under Schedule 1 to the principal Order.

Article 6 makes a small remedial amendment to the subsidy due to Sefton Council for the year commencing on 1st April 2000.

This Order does not impose a charge on business.
