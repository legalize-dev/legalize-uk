# The Children Act 1989 (Contact Activity Directions and Conditions: Financial Assistance) (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (England) Regulations 2010

Made: 9th March 2010

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 2010

Coming into force: 1st April 2010

The Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 11F(1), (6) and (7) and 104(4) of the Children Act 1989[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Children Act 1989 (Contact Activity Directions and Conditions: Financial Assistance) (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (England) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 1st April 2010.

#### Revocation and transitional provision

###### 2

- (1) The Children Act 1989 (Contact Activity Directions and Conditions: Financial Assistance) (England) Regulations 2008[^f00002] (“the Contact Activity Regulations”) are revoked.

- (2) Notwithstanding the revocation of the Contact Activity Regulations, the Secretary of State may continue to make payments in accordance with those Regulations in respect of a contact activity in which a qualifying individual takes part before 1st April 2010.

## Signed

Delyth Morgan — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Children, Schools and Families — 9th March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Children Act 1989 (Contact Activity Directions and Conditions: Financial Assistance) (England) Regulations 2008, which made provision enabling financial assistance to be paid by the Secretary of State in respect of an individual ordered or directed by a court in family proceedings to take part in an activity that promotes contact with a child (regulation 2(1)).

By way of transitional provision, financial assistance may be paid after 1st April 2010 in respect of individuals who took part in contact activities prior to 1st April 2010 (regulation 2(2)).
