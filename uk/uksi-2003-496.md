# The Dartford-Thurrock Crossing (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made: 4th March 2003

Laid before Parliament: 5th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 25(1) and (2), 26, 44(3) and 46(1) [^f00001] of the Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Act 1988[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Dartford-Thurrock Crossing (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

#### Amendments to The Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Regulations 1998

###### 2

The Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Regulations 1998[^f00003] are hereby amended as follows:

- (1) For regulations 1(2) there shall be substituted—

> > - In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—
> - “axle”, “axle weight” and “wheel” have the same meaning as they have in the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986 for the purpose of those Regulations other than regulations 26 and 27;
> - “charge” means a charge imposed by regulation 4 of these Regulations, as distinct from and in addition to any road user charge;
> - “designated parking area” means an area laid out by the crossing operator for the parking, repair or refuelling of vehicles;
> - “registered keeper” in relation to a vehicle means the person in whose name the vehicle was at the relevant time registered under or by virtue of the Vehicle (Excise and Registration) Act 1994;
> - “road user charge” means a charge levied under a trunk road charging scheme made under section 168 of the Transport Act 2000;
> - “trailer” has the same meaning as in section 185 of the Road Traffic Act 1988;
> - “wheel weight” means the weight transmitted to the road surface by a wheel not transversely in line with any other wheel.

- (2) In regulation 3(3) for “tunnel offices” there shall be substituted “Crossing Offices”;

- (3) In regulation 4(1) for “toll” there shall be substituted “road user charge”;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Regulations 1998, which provide a scale of charges to be paid for abnormal loads using the Dartford-Thurrock Crossing, and make further provision regarding the carrying of dangerous goods and the removal of stationary vehicles. These Regulations do not increase the charges for abnormal loads.

The amendments are made necessary by the fact that, as from 1st April 2003, vehicles using the Crossing will no longer be subject to tolls levied under the Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Act 1988, but will instead be liable to pay charges under a trunk road charging scheme made under the Transport Act 2000[^f00008].

These Regulations also consolidate changes made to the 1998 Regulations by subsequent Regulations. Those subsequent Regulations are accordingly revoked.

As from 1st April 2003, Dartford River Crossing Limited will no longer be the crossing operator as defined in section 12(4) of the Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Act 1988. Accordingly, after that date the booklet entitled “Dangerous Traffic” referred to in regulation 2(5) of these Regulations will no longer be obtainable from Dartford River Crossing Limited, but may be obtained in person or by post from either the Le Crossing Company Ltd, Dartford Crossing Offices, South Orbital Way, Dartford, Kent DA1 5PR, or the Mersey Tunnels, Merseyside Passenger Transport Authority, George’s Dock Building, George’s Dock Way, Pier Head, Liverpool L3 1DD or the Tyne Tunnel, Wallsend, Tyne and Wear NE28 0PD.
