# The Buckinghamshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Revocation Order 2010

Made: 22nd February 2010

Coming into force: 8th March 2010

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 273(1) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Buckinghamshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Revocation Order 2010 and shall come into force on 8th March 2010.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Buckinghamshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2010[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Mike O’Brien — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 22nd February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Buckinghamshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Trust Funds: Appointment of Trustees) Order 2010 provides for the Secretary of State to appoint trustees for the Buckinghamshire Hospitals National Health Service Trust to hold trust property for the purposes of the Trust or for any other purpose relating to the National Health Service, and for their removal or suspension from office. This Order revokes that Order before it comes into force.
