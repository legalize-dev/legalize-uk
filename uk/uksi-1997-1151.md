# The Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997

Made: 3rd April 1997

In exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 6(3) of the Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 (Commencement) Order 1997.

###### 2

The Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 shall come into force on 8th June 1997.

## Signed

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 3rd April 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Dangerous Dogs (Amendment) Act 1997 into force on 8th June 1997.
