# The North Western Inshore Fisheries and Conservation (Amendment) Order 2019

Made: 4th September 2019

Laid before Parliament: 6th September 2019

Coming into force: 27th September 2019

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 152(1) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^f00001], after consulting in accordance with section 152(2) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the North Western Inshore Fisheries and Conservation (Amendment) Order 2019 and comes into force on 27th September 2019.

#### Amendment to the North Western Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010

###### 2

In Part 1 of the Schedule to the North Western Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010[^f00002], in the table, in the entry for point 15, in the second column (*Latitude*), for “53°” substitute “54°”.

## Signed

George Eustice — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 4th September 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Schedule to the North Western Inshore Fisheries and Conservation Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2200](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2200)) to correct a co-ordinate of latitude of a point on the northern boundary of the North Western Inshore Fisheries and Conservation district.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
