# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2023

Made: 15th November 2023

Coming into force: 16th November 2023

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of November 2023Present,The King’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 4) Order 2023 and comes into force on 16th November 2023.

#### Appointment of His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE — The persons appointed as His Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 15th November 2023

- Rodica Cobarzan

- Gareth David Dakin

- Rebecca Dubbins

- Jennifer-Ellen Scotland

- Sophie Alexandra Leigh Wales

- Sally Anne Wride

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
