# The Air Traffic Controller Licensing (National Supervisory Authority) Regulations 2011

Made: 9th September 2011

Laid before Parliament: 14th September 2011

Coming into force: 10th October 2011

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 .

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) in relation to measures relating to air transport .

#### Citation, commencement and expiry

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Traffic Controller Licensing (National Supervisory Authority) Regulations 2011 and come into force on 10th October 2011.

- (2) They cease to have effect at the end of 31st December 2014.

#### National Supervisory Authority

###### 2

The Civil Aviation Authority is the national supervisory authority and the competent authority for the United Kingdom for the purposes of Article 4 and Article 27 of Commission Regulation (EU) No 805/2011 of 10th August 2011 laying down detailed rules for air traffic controllers' licences and certain certificates pursuant to Regulation [(EC) No 216/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0216) of the European Parliament and of the Council.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Theresa Villiers — Minister of State — 9th September 2011
