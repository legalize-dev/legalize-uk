# The Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2008

Made: 10th December 2008

Coming into force: 11th December 2008

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of December 2008Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty in pursuance of section 19(2) of the Education Act 2005 and in accordance with that recommendation is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 11th December 2008.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education and Training in Wales or Arolygwyr Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru with effect from 11th December 2008.

## SCHEDULE

- James Barry Rees

- Rachael Bubalo

- Jassa Jane Scott

- Angharad Reed

- Claire Lilian Morgan

- Stephen Huw Davies

- Mererid Gwenllian Stone

- Rhona Edwards

- Gillian Downer

- Denise Susan Elizabeth Wade

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council
