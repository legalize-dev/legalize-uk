# The Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006

Made: 20th July 2006

Laid before Parliament: 28th July 2006

Coming into force: 1st September 2006

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Central Leeds Learning Federation (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st September 2006.

#### Exemption from the School Session Regulations

###### 2

The Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall not apply to the governing body in relation to the City of Leeds School[^f00003] of Bedford Field, Woodhouse Cliff, Leeds, West Yorkshire LS6 2LG and the Primrose High School[^f00004] of Hill Street, Leeds, West Yorkshire LS9 7NL.

#### Time period

###### 3

This Order shall have effect until 31st December 2006.

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 20th July 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 do not apply to the governing body of the Central Leeds Learning Federation, which comprises the City of Leeds School and the Primrose High School. Article 3 specifies that the Order shall have effect until 31st December 2006.
