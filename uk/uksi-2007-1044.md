# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) (Imports of Captive Birds) Regulations 2007

Made: 27th March 2007

Laid before Parliament: 29th March 2007

Coming into force: 31st March 2007

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community.

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that section:

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) (Imports of Captive Birds) Regulations 2007;

- (b) apply in relation to England only; and

- (c) come into force on 31st March 2007.

###### 2

In regulation 16(4) of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) Regulations 2006 , for “1st April 2007” substitute “ 1st July 2007 ” .

###### 3

The following are revoked—

- (a) regulation 2(3) of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 ;

- (b) the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 .

## Signed

Ben Bradshaw — Minister of State — 27th March 2007
