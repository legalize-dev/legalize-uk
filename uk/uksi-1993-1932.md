# The Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993

Made: 29th July 1993

Coming into force: 5th August 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977([^f00001]) and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990([^f00002]) and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1993 and shall come into force on 5th August 1993.

#### Amendment of Establishment Order

###### 2

In the Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993([^f00003])–

- (a) in article 1(2) the definition of “establishment date” shall be omitted;

- (b) in article 4 for the words “4 non-executive” there shall be substituted “3 non-executive” and for the words “4 executive” there shall be substituted “3 executive”;

- (c) in article 6 for the words “its establishment date” there shall be substituted “5th August 1993”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 29th July 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Teddington Memorial Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 firstly by substituting a new date in article 6 in order to enable the trust to perform limited functions before its operational date (article 2(a) and (c)) and secondly to reduce the number of non-executive and executive directors of the trust from 4 to 3 in each case (article 2(b)).
