# The Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Modification) Order 2001

Made: 11th December 2001

Coming into force: 1st February 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of December 2001 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 2(2) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (Modification) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st February 2002.

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 2 to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00002] (which specifies the drugs which are subject to control under that Act) shall be amended as follows.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds thirty-six substances to Schedule 2 to the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 which specifies drugs which are subject to control under the Act. The thirty-six substances are phenethylamine derivatives which are not covered by the definition in paragraph 1(c) of Part I of Schedule 2 to the 1971 Act. Thirty-five of the substances are added as Class A drugs; and *α*-Methylphenethylhydroxylamine is added as a Class B drug.
