# The National Health Service Pension Schemes (Member Contributions etc.) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2022

Made: 7th October 2022

Laid before Parliament: 10th October 2022

Coming into force: 31st October 2022

In accordance with section 10(4) of the 1972 Act and section 21(1) of the 2013 Act[^f00004], the Secretary of State has consulted the representatives of such persons as appear to the Secretary of State likely to be affected by these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Pension Schemes (Member Contributions etc.) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 31st October 2022.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the National Health Service Pension Schemes (Member Contributions etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

###### 2

In the National Health Service Pension Schemes (Member Contributions etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 2022[^f00005], in regulation 9 (expiry of modifications of restrictions on return to work), for “31st October 2022” substitute “31st March 2023, except for the modification in regulation 10(b) which applies until the end of 31st March 2025”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the National Health Service Pension Schemes (Member Contributions etc.) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/273](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/273)).

Regulation 2 amends those Regulations to extend the expiry date for temporary modifications to the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/300](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/300)), the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/653](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/653)) and the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/94)) to suspend the operation of various provisions applying to members who partially retire or return to NHS employment after their pension becomes payable.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
