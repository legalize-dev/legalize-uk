# The Football (Offences) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991

Made: 9th July 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 6(2) of the Football (Offences) Act 1991[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Football (Offences) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1991.

###### 2

The Football (Offences) Act 1991 shall come into force on 10th August 1991.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 9th July 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Football (Offences) Act 1991 into force on 10th August 1991.
