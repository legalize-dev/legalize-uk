# The Official Controls (Temporary Measures) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2021

Made: at 11.19 p.m. on 30th December 2021

Laid before Parliament: 4th January 2022

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(b)

In accordance with the preconditions in Article 3(2B) of that Regulation, the Welsh Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Wales, and the Scottish Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Scotland, have consented to the Secretary of State making these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Official Controls (Temporary Measures) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2021;

- (b) come into force immediately before 31st December 2021;

- (c) extend to England and Wales, and Scotland.

#### Amendment to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/466 on temporary measures to contain risks to human, animal and plant health and animal welfare during certain serious disruptions of Member States’ control systems due to coronavirus disease (COVID-19)

###### 2

- (1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/466 on temporary measures to contain risks to human, animal and plant health and animal welfare during certain serious disruptions of Member States’ control systems due to coronavirus disease (COVID-19)[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Article 6, for “31st December 2021” substitute “1st July 2022”.

## Signed

Goldsmith of Richmond Park — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — At 11.19 p.m. on 30th December 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 141 of Regulation (EU) 2017/625, which is amended by [S.I. 2020/1481](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1481). They amend Commission Implementing (EU) Regulation 2020/466 (which is amended by [S.I. 2020/1481](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1481), [S.I. 2021/78](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/78) and [S.I. 2021/681](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/681)) to extend the period of application of availability of existing easements in the system of official controls in operation in Great Britain, in order to contain risks of serious disruption to the system caused by the coronavirus pandemic, by postponing its expiry date to 1st July 2022.

The Welsh Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Wales, and the Scottish Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Scotland, have consented to the Secretary of State making these Regulations.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
