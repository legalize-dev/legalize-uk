# The Education (School Performance Information) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made: 10th July 1994

Laid before Parliament: 22nd July 1994

Coming into force: 15th August 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 16 and 19(3) of the Education (Schools) Act 1992[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Wales hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Performance Information) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 15th August 1994.

###### 2

The Education (School Performance Information) (Wales) Regulations 1994[^f00002] are amended as follows:

- (a) in the definition of “maintained school” in regulation 2(1), “secondary” is omitted in paragraphs (a) and (c); and

- (b) in regulation 4(3), “for distribution” is omitted.

## Signed

John Redwood — Secretary of State for Wales — 10th July 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct errors in the Education (School Performance Information) (Wales) Regulations 1994. The definition of “maintained school” in regulation 2(1) was incorrect as paragraph (a) and (c) referred to a “secondary school”, when they should have referred to a “school”. Regulation 4(3) has also been amended by the omission of the words “for distribution” which are superfluous and potentially misleading.
