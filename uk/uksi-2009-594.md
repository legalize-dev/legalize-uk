# The Plant Health (Forestry) (Amendment) Order 2009

This Order makes provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] and it appears to the Commissioners that it is expedient for the references in the following Order to Commission Decision [2008/840/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0840) on emergency measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of *Anoplophora chinensis* (Forster)[^f00004] to be construed as references to that instrument as amended from time to time.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Forestry) (Amendment) Order 2009 and comes into force on 1st April 2009.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Plant Health (Forestry) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2517](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2517)) (“the principal Order”) so as to implement

—Commission Directive [2009/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0007) amending Annexes I, II, IV and V to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community (OJ No L 40, 11.2.2009, p 12);

—Commission Directive [2008/109/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0109) amending Annex IV to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (OJ No L 319, 29.11.2008, p 68);

—Commission Decision [2008/840/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0840) on emergency measures to prevent the introduction into and the spread within the Community of *Anoplophora chinensis* (Forster) (OJ No L 300, 11.11.2008, p 36); and

—amendments to emergency measures to prevent the introduction and spread of *Thaumetopoea processionea*(L.), introduced by [S.I. 2008/644](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/644).

The Order amends Schedules 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 to the principal Order so as to apply new restrictions on the introduction into Great Britain of certain forest products (trees, wood, wood packaging material and bark) and tree pests so as to prevent the introduction into and spread within Great Britain of, in particular, the following tree pests: *Anoplophora chinensis* (Forster) (Citrus Longhorn Beetle), *Dendrolimus sibiricus* Tschetverikov (the Siberian Coniferous Silk Moth),*Agrilus planipennis* Fairmaire (the Emerald Ash Borer) and*Stegophora ulmea*(Schweinitz: Fries) Sydow & Sydow (Elm Black Spot) and *Thaumetopoea processionea*(L.) (the Oak Processionary Moth).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
