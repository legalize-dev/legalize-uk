# The Postgraduate Medical Education and Training (Amendment) Order of Council 2012

Made: 7th February 2012

Laid before Parliament: 16th February 2012

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall the 7th day of February 2012By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Postgraduate Medical Education and Training (Amendment) Order of Council 2012 and comes into force on 16th March 2012.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order substitutes the Schedule to the Postgraduate Medical Education and Training Order of Council 2010 ([S.I.2010/473](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/473)). The Schedule lists the type=start slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687433426specialties recognisedtype=end slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687433426 by the United Kingdom.

The substituted Schedule—

- (a) adds diagnostic neuropathology, forensic histopathology, paediatric and perinatal pathology, and vascular surgery as type=start slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687455410recognised specialties;type=end slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687455410

- (b) in accordance with Council Directive 2005/36 EC[^f00003] it includes an entry for histopathology (which requires a minimum training period of four years);

- (c) specifies a minimum training period of five years for medical oncology;

- (d) specifies a minimum training period of fours years for clinical genetics;

- (e) changes the name of type=start slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687478926the specialty oftype=end slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687478926 audiological medicine to audio vestibular medicine;

- (f) changes the name of type=start slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687505582the specialty oftype=end slip=2013-12-01 time=1386687505582 psychotherapy to medical psychotherapy.
