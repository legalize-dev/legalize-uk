# The Insolvency (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Rules 2009

Made: 1st September 2009

Laid before Parliament: 3rd September 2009

Coming into force: 1st October 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by section411 of the Insolvency Act 1986[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the Insolvency (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Rules 2009 and come into force on 1st October 2009.

- (2) In these Rules “the principal Rules” means the Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986[^f00002].

#### Amendments to the principal Rules

###### 2

- (1) The principal Rules shall be amended in the manner provided for in Schedule 1.

- (2) For Form 2.30B (Scot)[^f00003] in Schedule 5 to the principal Rules substitute the form set out in Schedule 2 to these Rules.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Insolvency (Scotland) Rules 1986 ([S.I. 1986/1915](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1915)) (“the principal Rules”).

Schedule 1 amends a number of the forms in Schedule 5 to the principal Rules to update the addresses for Companies House and the Accountant in Bankruptcy.

Schedule 2 substitutes a new Form 2.30B (Scot). When the original Form 2.30B (Scot) was inserted by [S.I.2003/2111](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2111) the form number was not included. In order to correct this error the form is now being represcribed. The only other change to the content of Form 2.30B (Scot) is to update the Companies House address.

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in relation to these Rules as they will not impose any significant burdens on business.
