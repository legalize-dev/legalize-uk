# The Farriers and Animal Health (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 5th March 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

In accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 7 to that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — Introduction

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Farriers and Animal Health (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the Act” means the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975[^f00002].

## PART 2 — Amendment and transitional provision regarding the Farriers (Registration) Act 1975

#### Amendment of the Act

###### 2

- (1) The Act is amended as follows.

- (2) Omit sections 3(2A) and 4(5)[^f00003].

- (3) In section 7[^f00004]—

- (2) The provisions of the Act continue to apply to that application as if the Act were not amended by regulation [2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/457/regulation/2/enacted) of these Regulations, but as if in section 7—

- (aa) for “the European Union (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2015” there were substituted “the 2015 Regulations by virtue of the operation of paragraph 44 of Schedule 1 to the Recognition of Professional Qualifications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00011]”;

- (bb) for “those Regulations” there were substituted “the 2015 Regulations”;

- (ii) in paragraph (g), for “those Regulations” there were substituted “the 2015 Regulations, as they applied on the day before exit day”;

- (b) after subsection (1) there were inserted—

#### Temporary and occasional provision of services: transitional provision

###### 4

- (1) This Regulation applies to a declaration—

- (a) which has been submitted or renewed in accordance with regulation 15 of the European Union (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2015, and

- (b) in respect of which paragraph 42 of Schedule 1 to the Recognition of Professional Qualifications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 applies.

- (2) The provisions of the Act continue to apply to that declaration as if the Act were not amended by regulation 2 of these Regulations, but as if in section 7(6) after “if” it were inserted “, by virtue of paragraph 42 of Schedule 1 to the Recognition of Professional Qualifications (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019,”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of domestic legislation to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular under section 8(2)(c) and (g)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

These Regulations make amendments to domestic legislation in the fields of mutual recognition of professional qualifications, in particular farriers, and animal health. Part 2 amends the Farriers (Registration) Act [1975 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/35) and makes transitional provision; Part 3 amends the Animal Health Act [1981 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/22); Part 4 amends subordinate legislation.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
