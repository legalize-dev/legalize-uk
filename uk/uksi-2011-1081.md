# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2011

Made: 7th April 2011

Coming into force: 1st June 2011

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 7th day of April 2011Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 2 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is ordered, as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 1st June 2011.

#### Revocation of 2011 Order

###### 2

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 2011[^f00002] is revoked.

#### Amendment of 1986 Order

###### 3

In the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986[^f00003], as amended by the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) Order 2009[^f00004], for Schedule 1 there is substituted Schedule 1 as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — Schedule 1 substituted in the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 2011 ([SI 2011/746](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/746)), and amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1986 ([SI 1986/1159](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/1159)) as amended by the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions (Amendment) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/702](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/702)), to substitute a new Schedule 1 for the existing Schedule 1. The new Schedule 1 includes San Marino and Ukraine in the list of Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, done at the Hague on 25th October 1980 (Cm. 33). It also includes the correct date of the coming into force of the said Convention between the United Kingdom and San Marino and between the United Kingdom and Ukraine.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument because no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
