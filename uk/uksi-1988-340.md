# The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1988

Made: 29th February 1988

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 1988

Coming into force: 1st April 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 52(1)(a) and 60(1) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st April 1988.

###### 2

The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations 1981[^f00002] are hereby amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations 1981 so that:—

- (a) the fee payable for a certificate of initial fitness is increased from £100 to £110, both on the first application, and on any subsequent application when the test includes a test of stability under regulation 6. The fee payable for the certificate on any other subsequent application is increased from £11.50 to £13;

- (b) the fees payable for type approvals are increased from £580, £55, £290 and £1240 to—respectively—£640, £60, £320 and £1320; and

- (c) the fee payable for a certificate of conformity is increased from £13 to £14.50.

- (d) a new fee of £7.50 is introduced for the issue of duplicate certificates.
