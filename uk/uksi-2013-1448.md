# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2013

Made: 13th June 2013

Coming into force: 14th June 2013

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of June 2013Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2013 and comes into force on 14th June 2013.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

- (1) The person named in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order is appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

- (2) The persons named in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Order are appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills with effect from 1st September 2013.
