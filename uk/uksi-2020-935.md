# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2020

Made: 2nd September 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 10.00 a.m. on 3rd September 2020

Coming into force: at 12.01 a.m. on 3rd September 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into force at 12.01 a.m. on 3rd September 2020.

#### Amendment of the Blackburn and Bradford Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) Regulations 2020 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 1(4), in the definition of “protected area”, in sub-paragraph (b) omit “(xxi) Windhill and Wrose,”.

## Signed

Helen Whately — Minister of State — Department of Health and Social Care — 2nd September 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/822](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/822)) to omit the ward of Windhill and Wrose, within the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council, from “the protected area” for the purposes of those Regulations.

No impact assessment has been prepared for this instrument.
