# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Prescribed Bodies) Regulations 1990

Made: 2nd March 1990

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 1990

Coming into force: 1st April 1990

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 39(3) and (4) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Prescribed Bodies) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st April 1990.

###### 2

The following levying bodies, within the meaning of section 74 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00002], are hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 39(1)(k) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989—

- (a) the London Pensions Fund Authority[^f00003], and

- (b) the South Yorkshire Pensions Authority[^f00004].

###### 3

Part IV of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 shall have effect in relation to the bodies prescribed by regulation 2 subject to the modification that the word “may” shall be substituted for the word “shall” in section 53(1).

## Signed

Chris Patten — Secretary of State for the Environment — 2nd March 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the London Pensions Fund Authority and the South Yorkshire Pensions Authority as bodies to which the new capital finance system for local authorities under Part IV of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 is to apply, subject to the modification specified in regulation 3.
