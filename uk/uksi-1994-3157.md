# The Parental Responsibility Agreement (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made: 8th December 1994

Laid before Parliament: 12th December 1994

Coming into force: 3rd January 1995

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4(2) and 104 of the Children Act 1989[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Parental Responsibility Agreement (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 3rd January 1995.

###### 2

The form set out in the Schedule to the Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991[^f00002] shall be replaced by the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern — Dated 8th December 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Parental Responsibility Agreement Regulations 1991 by prescribing a new form for the recording of an agreement between the parents of a child that the father is to have parental responsibility for the child within the meaning of section 3 of the Children Act 1989. The layout and content of the form have been improved to make it easier to understand and complete.
