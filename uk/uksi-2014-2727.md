# The Pensions Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2014

Made: 11th October 2014

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 56(1) of the Pensions Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2014.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of paragraphs 11 and 20 of Schedule 15 to the Pensions Act 2014 is 13th October 2014.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Steve Webb — Minister of State — Department for Work and Pensions — 11th October 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force certain provisions of the Pensions Act [2014 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/19) (“the Act”) on 13th October 2014.

Article 2 commences paragraphs 11 and 20 of Schedule 15 to the Act. Paragraph 11 amends section 176 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c.4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) so that regulations made by virtue of sections 14A and 45(2A) of that Act are subject to the affirmative resolution procedure. Paragraph 20 amends section 172 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7) so that regulations made by virtue of section 14A of that Act are subject to the affirmative resolution procedure.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as it has no impact on business or civil society organisations. An impact assessment for the changes made by the Act is available from the Better Regulation Unit, Department for Work and Pensions, Caxton House, 6 – 12 Tothill Street, London SW1H 9NA or

https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/pensions-act-2014-impact-assessments-may-2014
