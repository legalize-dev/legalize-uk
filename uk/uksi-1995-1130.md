# The London Priority Route (Amendment) Order 1995

Made: 21st April 1995

Laid before Parliament: 1st May 1995

Coming into force: 1st June 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 50(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consulting local authorities, the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis, the Commissioner of Police for the City of London and London Regional Transport in accordance with section 50(3) and (4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the London Priority Route (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st June 1995.

#### Amendment of the London Priority Route Order 1992

###### 2

The Schedule to the London Priority Route Order 1992[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

This Order makes changes to the priority route network in London designated, for the purposes of Part II of the Road Traffic Act 1991, by the London Priority Route Order 1992.
