# The M62 Motorway (City of Liverpool) (Speed Limit) Regulations 2003

Made: 8th October 2003

Laid before Parliament: 16th October 2003

Coming into force: 10th November 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 134(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the M62 Motorway (City of Liverpool) (Speed Limit) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 10th November 2003.

#### Imposition of speed limit

###### 2

No person shall drive any motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 50 miles per hour on a length of the westbound carriageway of the M62 Motorway from the boundary of the City of Liverpool to a point 985 metres west of that boundary.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David Jamieson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Transport — 8th October 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations impose a 50 miles per hour speed limit on the westbound carriageway of the M62 Motorway from the boundary of the City of Liverpool to a point 985 metres west of that boundary.
