# The Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Restoration of Devolved Government) Order 2000

Made: 27th May 2000

Laid before Parliament: 5th June 2000

Coming into force: 30th May 2000

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2(2) of the Northern Ireland Act 2000, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 2000 (Restoration of Devolved Government) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 30th May 2000.

###### 2

Section 1 of the Northern Ireland Act 2000 shall cease to have effect on 30th May 2000.

## Signed

Peter Mandelson — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 27th May 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that section 1 of the Northern Ireland Act 2000 is to cease to have effect on 30th May 2000. Section 1, which came into force on 12th February 2000, provides for the suspension of devolved government in Northern Ireland. The effect of the Order is to restore devolved government in Northern Ireland on 30th May 2000.
