# The Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1973

Made: 8th August 1973

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 43(6) of the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1973.

###### 2

Part III of the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 shall come into operation on 1st September 1973.

## Signed

W.S.I. Whitelaw — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretary of State — 8th August 1973

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings Part III of the Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (Prevention of Religious and Political Discrimination) into force on 1st September 1973.
