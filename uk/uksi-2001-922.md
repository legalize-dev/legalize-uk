# The Buying Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) (Change of Name) Order 2001

Made: 13th March 2001

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 2001

Coming into force: 3rd April 2001

The Chancellor of the Exchequer, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 1(1) and 6(1) of the Government Trading Funds Act 1973[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the concurrence of the Treasury, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Buying Agency Trading Fund (Amendment) (Change of Name) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 3rd April 2001.

#### Amendment of the Buying Agency Trading Fund Order 1991

###### 2

In article 3 of the Buying Agency Trading Fund Order 1991[^f00002] there shall be substituted for the words “the Buying Agency Trading Fund” the following:—

- “OGCbuying.solutions Trading Fund” .

## Signed

Gordon Brown — Chancellor of the Exchequer

We concur

David Clelland — Jim Dowd — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Buying Agency Trading Fund Order 1991 (S.I. [1991/875](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/875)) which established the Buying Agency Trading Fund.

Article 2 changes the name of the Buying Agency Trading Fund to OGCbuying.solutions Trading Fund.
