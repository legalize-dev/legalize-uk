# The Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Establishment) and the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006

Made: 14th March 2006

Coming into force: 1st April 2006

The Secretary of State for Health considers it appropriate in the interests of the health service to dissolve the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust[^f00003] and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust[^f00004], having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of, and paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00005].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Establishment) and the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust (Dissolution) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990;

- “community health services” means any services which the Secretary of State may provide under section 3(1)(d) or (e) of, or Schedule 1 to, the National Health Service Act 1977 and any service which she has a duty to provide under section 5(1) of that Act[^f00006];

- “dissolution date” means the date this Order comes into force;

- “operational date” has the meaning assigned to it in paragraph 3(1)(e) of Schedule 2 to the Act; and

- “the trust” means the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust established by article 2 of this Order.

#### Establishment and name of the trust

###### 2

There is established a National Health Service trust which shall be called the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust.

#### Nature and functions of the trust

###### 3

- (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.

- (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, and community health services, for the purposes of the health service.

#### Directors of the trust

###### 4

The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 5 executive directors and 7 non-executive directors.

#### Operational date and accounting date of the trust

###### 5

- (1) The operational date of the trust shall be 1st April 2006.

- (2) The accounting date of the trust shall be 31st March.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts and revocation of orders

###### 6

On the dissolution date—

- (a) the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust, which was established by the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust (Establishment) and the Thames Gateway National Health Service Trust and Invicta Community Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2002[^f00007]; and

- (b) the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust, which was established by the Canterbury and Thanet Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00008],

are dissolved, and accordingly their Establishment Orders, the Canterbury and Thanet Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998[^f00009] and the Canterbury and Thanet Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Amendment Order 2003[^f00010] are revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Caroline Flint — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 14th March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes the Kent and Medway National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust as a National Health Service trust under the terms set out in section 5 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990.

The Order sets out the functions of the trust (article 3), the number of executive and non-executive directors of the trust (article 4), the operational date (the date on which the trust assumes all its functions) and the accounting date of the trust (article 5).

This Order also dissolves the West Kent National Health Service and Social Care Trust and the East Kent National Health Service and Social Care Partnership Trust and revokes their Establishment Orders and the Establishment Amendment Order and Change of Name Order relating to the latter NHS trust.
