# The Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made: 4th March 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(2) of the Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 (Commencement) Order 1992.

###### 2

The Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

## Signed

Strathclyde — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 4th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the Crofter Forestry (Scotland) Act 1991 on 1st April 1992.
