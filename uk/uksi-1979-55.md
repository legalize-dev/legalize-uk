# The Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979

Made: 19th January 1979

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 7(4) of the Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 (Commencement) Order 1979.

###### 2

The Stock Exchange (Completion of Bargains) Act 1976 shall come into force on 12th February 1979.

## Signed

Stanley Clinton Davis — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Department of Trade — 19th January 1979
