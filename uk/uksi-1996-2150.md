# The Pensions Act 1995 (Commencement No. 6 : S.I. 1996/1853 : C.38) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 16th August 1996

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 180(1) of the Pensions Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 1995 (Commencement No. 6: [S.I. 1996/1853](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1853) : C.38) (Amendment) Order 1996.

#### Amendment of S.I. 1996/1853

###### 2

In article 1 of the Pensions Act 1995 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996 made 15th July 1996 and registered as [S.I. 1996/1853 (C.38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1853), for “No. 6”substitute “No. 7”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Mackay of Ardbrecknish — Minister of State, — Department of Social Security — 16th August 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Pensions Act 1995 (Commencement No. 6) Order 1996 made 15th July 1996 and registered as [S.I. 1996/1853 (C.38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1853) by re-numbering it the Commencement No. 7 Order.
