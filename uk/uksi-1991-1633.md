# The Broads Authority (Pilotage Powers) Order 1991

Made: 17th July 1991

Laid Before Parliament: 24th July 1991

Coming into force: 14th August 1991

Now therefore the Secretary of State for Transport, on the application of the Broads Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(4) of the Act of 1987, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Broads Authority (Pilotage Powers) Order 1991.

#### Application of Pilotage Act 1987

###### 2

The Broads Authority shall be a competent harbour authority in respect of the harbour for the purposes of the Act of 1987.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Patrick McLoughlin — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 17th July 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the Broads Authority is to be a competent harbour authority for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987 and the Order defines the area of its harbour for these purposes by reference to the Norfolk and Suffolk Broads Act 1988.

The application for the Order is the Broads Authority, Thomas Harvey House, 18 Colegate, Norwich, Norfolk NR3 1BQ.
