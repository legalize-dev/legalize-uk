# The Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries (Shipbuilding Industry Vesting Date) Order 1977

Made: 22nd March 1977

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 56(1) of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Act 1977 (hereinafter referred to as “*the Act*”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries (Shipbuilding Industry Vesting Date) Order 1977.

###### 2

The shipbuilding industry vesting date for the purposes of section 19(2) of the Act shall be 1st July 1977.

## Signed

Eric Varley — Secretary of State for Industry — 22nd March 1977

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies the shipbuilding industry vesting date for the purpose of section 19(2) of the Aircraft and Shipbuilding Industries Act 1977.
