# The Drugs Act 2005 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2005

Made: 22nd June 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 24(3) of the Drugs Act 2005[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Drugs Act 2005 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2005.

###### 2

Section 21 (inclusion of mushrooms containing psilocin etc. as Class A drugs) of the Drugs Act 2005 shall come into force on 18th July 2005.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 22nd June 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 21 of the Drugs Act 2005 into force on 18th July 2005.
