# The Saving Gateway Accounts Act 2009 (Revocation of Commencement) Order 2010

Made: 22nd June 2010

The Treasury make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 28(1) and 31 of the Saving Gateway Accounts Act 2009[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Saving Gateway Accounts Act 2009 (Revocation of Commencement) Order 2010.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Saving Gateway Accounts Act 2009 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2010[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Michael Fabricant — Angela Watkinson — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 22nd June 2010

## Explanatory note

type=start time=1313741669260(This note is not part of the Order)type=end time=1313741669260

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Saving Gateway Accounts Act 2009 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/921](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/921)). This latter Order would otherwise have brought the substantive provisions of the Saving Gateway Accounts Act 2009 into force from 1st July 2010.
