# The Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) (Revocation) Regulations 1992

Made: 19th October 1992

Laid before Parliament: 22nd October 1992

Coming into force: 23rd October 1992

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 2 of the said Act of 1972 hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) (Revocation) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on the day after they are laid.

###### 2

The Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) Regulations 1992[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Denton of Wakefield — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 19th October 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Child Resistant Packaging and Tactile Danger Warnings (Safety) Regulations 1992 before those Regulations come into force.
