# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Oxfordshire) (District of West Oxfordshire) Designation Order 2009

Made: 10th December 2009

Laid before Parliament: 17th December 2009

Coming into force: 25th January 2010

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Transport[^f00002] makes this Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Oxfordshire) (District of West Oxfordshire) Designation Order 2009 and shall come into force on 25th January 2010.

#### Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area

###### 2

- (1) The Secretary of State designates the area described in paragraph (2) as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and

- (b) a special enforcement area.

- (2) This Order applies to the area of the district of West Oxfordshire with the exception of the dual carriageway section of the A40, including its on and off slip roads, from its eastern end within the parish of South Leigh (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP388101) to the roundabout at Asthall Barrow where it intersects with the B4047 and becomes a single carriageway (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP288101).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Sadiq Khan — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 10th December 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the area described in article 2 as a civil enforcement area in respect of parking contraventions, and as a special enforcement area, for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
