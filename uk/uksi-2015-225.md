# The Education (Prescribed Courses of Higher Education) (Information Requirements) (England) Regulations 2015

Made: 12th February 2015

Laid before Parliament: 13th February 2015

Coming into force: 1st April 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 79(c) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Prescribed Courses of Higher Education) (Information Requirements) (England) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 1st April 2015.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to England.

#### Prescribed courses for purposes of section 79(c) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992

###### 2

The courses listed in the Schedule are prescribed for the purpose of section 79(c) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 in respect of the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Education (Prescribed Courses of Higher Education) (Information Requirements) (England) Regulations 2014[^f00002] are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe courses of higher education for the purpose of section 79(c) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 in relation to the Higher Education Funding Council for England. Section 79(c) imposes a duty on the governing body of any institution at which prescribed courses of higher education are or have been provided to give the Higher Education Funding Councils such information as required by the Councils.

Regulation 3 of these Regulations revokes the Education (Prescribed Courses of Higher Education) (Information Requirements) (England) Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/2179](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2179)).

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations as they have no impact on the costs of business, charities or voluntary bodies. The Explanatory Memorandum is published alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
