# The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1997

Made: 12th February 1997

Laid before Parliament: 24th February 1997

Coming into force: 1st June 1997

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 1997 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of sections 4(1) and (4) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st June 1997.

###### 2

Miss Susan Lewis is hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales for a period of five years commencing on 1st June 1997.

###### 3

The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1996[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

N.H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints Miss Susan Lewis to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales for a period of five years from 1st June 1997. She succeeds Roy Lewis James whose term of appointment under the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1996 ends on 31st May 1997. That Order is revoked by the present Order.
