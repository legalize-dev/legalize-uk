# The General Medical Council (Registration (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations) Order of Council 2003

Made: 13th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st April 2003

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 13th day of March 2003 By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Now, therefore, Their Lordships, having taken those regulations into consideration, are pleased to, and do hereby, approve them.

This Order may be cited as the General Medical Council (Registration (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations) Order of Council 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

## SCHEDULE — THE MEDICAL PRACTITIONERS REGISTRATION (FEES) AMENDMENT (NO. 2) REGULATIONS 2003

The General Medical Council in exercise of their powers under section 32 of the Medical Act 1983 hereby make the following regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These regulations may be cited as the Medical Practitioners Registration (Fees) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

- (2) In these regulations “the Principal Regulations” means the Medical Practitioners Registration (Fees) Regulations 1985[^f00002].

#### Amendments of the Principal Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 3(1) of the Principal Regulations, after the words “section 19, 21,” there shall be inserted “21A,”.

###### 3

In regulation 3(2) of the Principal Regulations after sub-paragraph (b) the following sub-paragraph shall be inserted—

> > (bb) For an application under section 21A£100

###### 4

In regulation 4(2) of the Principal Regulations after sub-paragraph (d) the following sub-paragraph shall be inserted—

> > (dd) On full registration under section 21A of the Act£290

#### Revocation

###### 5

The Medical Practitioners Registration (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 are revoked.

## Signed

A. K. Galloway — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The regulations approved by this Order make provision for fees to be charged to doctors from outside the UK applying for and being granted registration who are eligible specialists or qualified general practitioners as defined in section 21A of the Medical Act 1983 as inserted by the Medical Act 1983 (Amendment) Order 2002.
