# The Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1992

Made: 6th July 1992

Laid before Parliament: 14th July 1992

Coming into force: 4th August 1992

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by paragraph 2(3)(b) of Schedule 6 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 4th August 1992.

#### Valuation Date

###### 2

1st April 1993 is specified under paragraph 2(3)(b) of Schedule 6 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988 as the day by reference to which a determination of rateable value is to be made for the purposes of compiling a local or central non-domestic rating list to be compiled on 1st April 1995.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1988[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Michael Howard — Secretary of State for the Environment — 6th July 1992

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 6th July 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Non-domestic rating lists under Part III of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 are, by virtue of sections 41(2) and 52(2) of the Act, to be compiled on 1st April 1990 and on1st April in every fifth year afterwards. Article 2 of this Order provides that 1st April 1993 is to be the day by reference to which determinations of rateable value are to be made in relation to those lists compiled on 1st April 1995.

Article 3 revokes the Rating Lists (Valuation Date) Order 1988.
