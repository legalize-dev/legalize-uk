# The Prisons (Property) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

Made: 5th March 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 2(2) of the Prisons (Property) Act 2013[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Prisons (Property) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 1 (power to destroy or otherwise dispose of property) of the Prisons (Property) Act 2013 comes into force on 26th March 2015.

## Signed

Andrew Selous — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 5th March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings section 1 of the Prisons (Property) Act [2013 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/11) (“the Act”) into force on 26thMarch2015. Section 1 of the Act inserts into the Prison Act [1952 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1952/52) a new section 42A which introduces a power for prison governors and directors to destroy or otherwise dispose of unauthorised property confiscated or found in specific circumstances.
