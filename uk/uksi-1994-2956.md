# The Exempt Charities (No. 2) Order 1994

Made: 24th November 1994

Coming into force: 1st January 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 24th day of November 1994 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph (c) of Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 1993[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities (No. 2) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st January 1995.

###### 2

The University of Greenwich is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 1993.

## Signed

R. P. Bulling — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order declares the University of Greenwich to be an exempt charity within the meaning of the Charities Act 1993, with effect from 1st January 1995. An exempt charity is not required to be registered with the Charity Commissioners and the Commissioners cannot exercise any powers under the Act in relation to an exempt charity except at the charity’s request.
