# The Homes and Communities Agency (Transfer of Property etc.) Regulations 2018

Made: 8th January 2018

Laid before Parliament: 10th January 2018

Coming into force: 31st January 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53A(2) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Homes and Communities Agency (Transfer of Property etc.) Regulations 2018.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 31st January 2018.

#### Specified public bodies

###### 2

The public bodies listed in the Schedule to these Regulations are specified under section 53A(2) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 53A(1) of the Housing and Regeneration Act [2008 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/17) allows the Secretary of State to make one or more schemes for the transfer to the Homes and Communities Agency of designated property, rights or liabilities of a specified public body. These Regulations specify various NHS Trusts for that purpose.

The Homes and Communities Agency (Transfer of Property etc.) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1471](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1471)), the Homes and Communities Agency (Transfer of Property etc.) (No. 2) Regulations 2016 ([S.I. 2016/515](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/515)) and the Homes and Communities Agency (Transfer of Property etc) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/199](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/199)) specify other public bodies for the same purpose.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the private or voluntary sectors. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside this instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk.
