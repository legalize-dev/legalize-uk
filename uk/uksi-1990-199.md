# The Official Secrets Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990

Made: 9th February 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 16(6) of the Official Secrets Act 1989 [^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Official Secrets Act 1989 (Commencement) Order 1990.

###### 2

The Official Secrets Act 1989 shall come into force on 1st March 1990.

## Signed

David Waddington — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State Home Office — 9th February 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st March 1990 the Official Secrets Act 1989.
