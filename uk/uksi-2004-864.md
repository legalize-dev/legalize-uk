# The Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 2004

Made: 22nd March 2004

Coming into force: 1st April 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 3(1)(a) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Change of Name) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st April 2004.

- (2) In this Order—

- (a) “the Order” means the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993”[^f00003];

- (b) “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

#### Change of name of NHS trust and transitional provision

###### 2

- (1) The trust shall be called the Coventry and Warwickshire Ambulance National Health Service Trust instead of the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust, and accordingly in article 1 of the Order in the definition of “the trust”, and in article 2 of the Order, for “Warwickshire Ambulance Service” there shall be substituted “Coventry and Warwickshire Ambulance”.

- (2) The change of name effected by paragraph (1) shall not—

- (a) affect any right or obligation of any person; or

- (b) be taken as invalidating any instrument (whether made before or on or after the day on which this Order comes into force) which refers to the trust by its previous name,

and all instruments and other documents which refer to the trust under its previous name shall be construed as referring to it under its new name.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Stephen Ladyman — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 22nd March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993.

Article 2 of the Order changes the name of the trust from the Warwickshire Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust to the Coventry and Warwickshire Ambulance National Health Service Trust.
