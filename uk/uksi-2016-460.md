# The A11 Trunk Road (Fiveways to Thetford Improvement) (Detrunking) 2011 (Amendment) Order 2015 (Revocation) Order 2016

Made: 16th March 2016

Coming into force: 1st April 2016

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10, 12 and 325(1) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A11 Trunk Road (Fiveways to Thetford Improvement) (Detrunking) 2011 (Amendment) Order 2015 (Revocation) Order 2016 and shall come into force on 1st April 2016.

###### 2

The A11 Trunk Road (Fiveways to Thetford Improvement) (Detrunking) 2011 (Amendment) Order 2015[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

CJ Miller — A Senior Civil Servant in the Department for Transport

16th March 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the A11 Trunk Road (Fiveways to Thetford Improvement) (Detrunking) 2011 (Amendment) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1581](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1581)). That Order amended the A11 Trunk Road (Fiveways to Thetford Improvement) (Detrunking) Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1070](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1070)).
