## Footnotes

[^f00001]: [1982 c.10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/10). Sections 11 and 12 were amended by paragraphs 10 and 11 of Schedule 4 to the Employment Act [1989 (c.38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/38) and by sections 24, 25 and 29 of and paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 to the Further Education and Training Act [2007 (c.25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/25). Section 12(4) was amended by the Employment Rights (Dispute Resolution) Act [1998 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/8), section 1(2).

[^f00002]: The Board was established under the Industrial Training (Engineering Board) Order 1964 ([S.I. 1964/1086](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1964/1086)) as the Engineering Industry Training Board. The Industrial Training (Engineering Construction Board) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/1305](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1305)) re-named the Board as the Engineering Construction Industry Training Board and replaced the existing Schedule 1 with a new Schedule 1.

[^f00003]: [1998 c.46](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/46). The Engineering Construction Industry Training Board has been specified as a cross-border public authority for the purposes of section 88 of the Scotland Act 1998 by the Scotland Act 1998 (Cross-Border Public Authorities) (Specification) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/1319](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1319)).

[^f00004]: [S.I. 1976/766](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/766), as amended by [S.I. 1977/588](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1977/588) and [S.I. 1981/208](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1981/208).

[^f00005]: [S.I. 1964/1086](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1964/1086), as amended by [S.I. 1991/1305](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/1305).

[^f00006]: Regulation 16 of and Schedule 3 to the Employment Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1861](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1861)) set out the rules of procedure that apply in relation to appeals against an assessment to levy. Rule 3 of Schedule 3 makes provision about notices of appeal. Relevant amendments were made by Schedule 4 to the Constitutional Reform Act [2005 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/4), [S.I. 2004/2351](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2351), [S.I. 2005/1865](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1865) and [S.I. 2006/680](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/680).
