# The Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2008

Made: 20th November 2008

Laid before Parliament: 25th November 2008

Coming into force: 17th December 2008

The Secretary of State has consulted with such representative organisations as were thought fit in accordance with section 134(2) of the 1984 Act.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 17th December 2008.

- (2) These Regulations apply as respects England only.

#### Amendment of the 2008 Regulations

###### 2

The Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges) Regulations 2008[^f00002] (“the 2008 Regulations”) shall be amended in accordance with these Regulations.

#### Amendments to the 2008 Regulations to charges in relation to the storage of vehicles

###### 3

Regulation 5 of the 2008 Regulations is amended as follows—

- (a) for “for the purposes of sections 101A(3), 101A(4) and 102(2)(b)” substitute “for the purposes of sections 101A(3), 101A(4), 102(2)(b) and 102(2ZA)(b)”; and

- (b) after the words “or the local authority” insert the words “or the Secretary of State”.

#### Amendments to the 2008 Regulations to charges in relation to the disposal of vehicles

###### 4

Regulation 6(2) of the 2008 Regulations is amended so that after “for the purposes of sections 102(2)(c)” there is inserted “ and 102(2ZA)(c)”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Andrew Adonis — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 20th November 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Sums and Charges) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/2095](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/2095)) so that the Secretary of State can recover charges for the storage and disposal of vehicles removed by traffic officers.
