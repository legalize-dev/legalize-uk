# The Tuberculosis in Animals (England) (Amendment) Order 2025

Made: 24th June 2025

Coming into force: 15th July 2025

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 8, 15(4), and 32(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Tuberculosis in Animals (England) (Amendment) Order 2025 and comes into force on 15th July 2025.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

#### Amendment to the Tuberculosis (Non-bovine animals) Slaughter and Compensation (England) Order 2017

###### 2

- (1) The Tuberculosis (Non-bovine animals) Slaughter and Compensation (England) Order 2017[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) Omit article 3 (power to slaughter animals affected with tuberculosis).

#### Amendment to the Tuberculosis in Animals (England) Order 2021

###### 3

- (1) The Tuberculosis in Animals (England) Order 2021[^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), in the definition of “specified area” after the words “civil parishes” insert “and non-civil parish areas”.

- (3) In article 4 (extension of definition of “disease”)—

- (a) in the heading, at the end insert “and power to slaughter animals affected with tuberculosis”;

- (b) the text of the article becomes paragraph (1);

- (c) after paragraph (1) insert—

> > (2) Section 32 of the Act (power to slaughter animals) applies to that disease.

- (4) In article 8 (tuberculosis testing), in the words before paragraph (5)(a), omit the words from “or any administrative” to “common agricultural policy,”.

- (5) In article 23 (publication of information)—

- (a) for paragraph (1) substitute—

> > (1) For the purpose of helping other persons protect against the spread of tuberculosis, the Secretary of State may publish information regarding a bovine herd or bovine animal.

- (b) omit paragraph (2);

- (c) in paragraph (3) omit “or (2)”.

- (6) In Schedule 3—

- (a) in the unnumbered paragraph before Part 1, for the words “and civil parishes” substitute “civil parishes and non-civil parish areas”;

- (b) in Part 2—

- (i) in the heading “Civil parishes in the county of Berkshire”, after “Civil parishes” insert “and non-civil parish areas”;

- (ii) in the heading “Civil parishes in the county of Derbyshire”, after “Civil parishes” insert “and non-civil parish areas”;

- (iii) after the heading “*Civil parishes and non-civil parish areas in the county of Derbyshire*” (as amended by paragraph (ii)), insert—

> > Hodthorpe and Belph
> Langwith

- (iv) in the heading “Civil parishes in the county of Hampshire”, after “Civil parishes” insert “and non-civil parish areas”;

- (v) after the heading “*Civil parishes and non-civil parish areas in the county of Hampshire*” (as amended by paragraph (iv)), insert—

> > Allbrook

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Tuberculosis in Animals (England) Order 2021 ([S.I. 2021/1001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/1001)) (“*the Principal Order*”) to—

- (a) amend the Tuberculosis (Non-bovine animals) Slaughter and Compensation Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1254](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1254)) to remove the application of the power in section 32 of the Animal Health Act 1981 (for the Secretary of State to slaughter any animal affected, or suspected of being affected by tuberculosis) and reapply the power in the Principal Order (*article 3(3)*);

- (b) remove a reference to direct payment entitlements payable under the former European Union Common Agricultural Policy (*article 3(4)*);

- (c) widen the scope of the power of the Secretary of State to publish information provided it is for the purpose of helping other persons protect against the spread of tuberculosis (*article 3(5)*);

- (d) add certain parishes to Schedule 3 (*article 3(6)*).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact, or no significant impact, on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
