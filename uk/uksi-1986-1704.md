# The Poisons (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1986

Made: 1st October 1986

Laid before Parliament: 9th October 1986

Coming into Operation: 1st December 1986

In pursuance of section 7 of the Poisons Act 1972 and on the recommendation of the Poisons Board, I hereby make the following Rules:—

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Poisons (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1986 and shall come into operation on 1st December 1986.

###### 2

Schedule 4 to the Poisons Rules 1982[^f00001] shall be amended by the insertion, in Group I, after the words “fluorescent lamps”, of the words “flux in any form for use in soldering”.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 1st October 1986

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Poisons Rules 1982 by including in the general exemptions contained in Schedule 4 any form of flux for use in soldering.
