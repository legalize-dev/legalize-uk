# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions)(Designated Ports) Order 1990

Made: 31st July 1990

Coming into force: 3rd September 1990

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 16(3) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) (Designated Ports) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 3rd September 1990.

###### 2

In Part I of Schedule 6 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, there shall be omitted, under the heading “Seaports”, the word “Southampton”.

## Signed

David Waddington — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 31st July 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes Southampton from the list of seaports designated, for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 5 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, in Part I of Schedule 6 to that Act.
