# The Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2023

Made: 18th April 2023

Coming into force: 20th April 2023

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 119 of, and paragraph 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) (Correction) Order 2023 and comes into force on 20th April 2023.

#### Corrections

###### 2

The Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) Order 2022 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, where—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text to be substituted, inserted or omitted.

## SCHEDULE — Correctable Errors

| Provision | How correction is to be made | Text to be substituted, inserted or omitted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article 2 (interpretation) “carbon capture and compression plant” | After “No 7” add | “as set out” |
| Article 2 (interpretation) “carbon capture and compression plant” | After “Schedule 1” add | “and as” |
| Article 21(3) | Substitute “4A and 4B” for | “4A or 4B” |
| Article 21(3) | After “(1) are” add | “respectively” |
| Schedule 9 (procedure for discharge of requirements) paragraph 4(2)(2) sub paragraph (ii) | Substitute “pursuan” for | “pursuant” |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy, Security and Net Zero

David Wagstaff — Deputy Director Energy Infrastructure Planning — Department for Energy, Security and Net Zero — 18th April 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order corrects errors in the Keadby 3 (Carbon Capture Equipped Gas Fired Generating Station) Order 2022, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
