# The Cluster Munitions (Prohibitions) Act 2010 (Isle of Man) Order 2011

Made: 12th October 2011

Coming into force: 2nd November 2011

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of October 2011Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by section 33(3) of the Cluster Munitions (Prohibitions) Act 2010[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cluster Munitions (Prohibitions) Act 2010 (Isle of Man) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 2nd November 2011.

#### Extension of the Cluster Munitions (Prohibitions) Act 2010 to the Isle of Man

###### 2

The Cluster Munitions (Prohibitions) Act 2010 shall extend to the Isle of Man, subject to the exceptions and modifications specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE — Exceptions and modifications to be made in the extension of the Cluster Munitions (Prohibitions) Act 2010 to the Isle of Man

Any reference to an Act of Tynwald, or to a provision of an Act of Tynwald, shall be construed as including a reference to that Act or provision as amended or replaced by or under any other such Act or provision.
