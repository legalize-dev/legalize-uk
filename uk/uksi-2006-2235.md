# The Capital Allowances (Environmentally Beneficial Plant and Machinery) (Amendment) Order 2006

Made: 16th August 2006

Laid before the House of Commons: 17th August 2006

Coming into force: 7th September 2006

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 45H(3) and (4) and 45(J)(3)(b) of the Capital Allowances Act 2001[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Capital Allowances (Environmentally Beneficial Plant and Machinery) (Amendment) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 7th September 2006.

#### Amendment of the Capital Allowances (Environmentally Beneficial Plant and Machinery) Order 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Capital Allowances (Environmentally Beneficial Plant and Machinery) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2 (interpretation)[^f00003] for the definitions of “the Water Technology Criteria List” and “the Water Technology Product List” substitute—

> > “the Water Technology Criteria List” means the list dated 17th July 2006 and issued by the Secretary of State for the Environment, Food and Rural Affairs on 19th July 2006;
> “the Water Technology Product List” means the list dated 17th July 2006 and issued by the Secretary of State for the Environment, Food and Rural Affairs on 19th July 2006.

###### 3

At the end of article 3(2) add—

> > (j) efficient washing machines;
> (k) small scale slurry and sludge dewatering equipment.

## Signed

Claire Ward — Kevin Brennan — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th August 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Capital Allowances (Environmentally Beneficial Plant and Machinery) Order 2003 ([S.I. 2003/2076](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2076), as amended). That Order implemented legislation, inserted into the Capital Allowances Act [2991 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2991/2) by paragraph 3 of Schedule 30 to the Finance Act [2003 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/14) so as to introduce a scheme for 100% first-year allowances to encourage businesses to invest in environmentally beneficial plant and machinery.

Article 1 provides for citation and commencement.

Article 2 substitutes new definitions of “the Water Technology Criteria List” and “the Water Technology Product list”. These Lists can be found on the Internet at www.eca-water.gov.uk.

Article 3 adds two technology classes to the Water Technology Product list, namely efficient washing machines and small scale slurry and sludge dewatering equipment.
