# The Cirencester Tertiary College Sixth Form College Corporation Designation (England) Order 2011

Made: 18th October 2011

Laid before Parliament: 27th October 2011

Coming into force: 1st December 2011

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Education makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33B(1) and (7) of the Act:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Cirencester Tertiary College Sixth Form College Corporation Designation (England) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1st December 2011.

#### Designation

###### 2

The body corporate of the Cirencester Tertiary College, being a body corporate of the kind specified in section 33B(2) of the Act, is designated as a sixth form college corporation.

#### Continuity of the body corporate

###### 3

Designation as a sixth form college corporation under this Order does not affect the continuity of the body corporate, and its instrument and articles of government.

## Signed

Jonathan Hill — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Education — 18th October 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 33B of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (“the Act”) makes provision for the body corporate of a further education corporation, or those bodies corporate established by an order under section 143(4) of the Learning and Skills Act 2000[^f00004] in respect of an institution in England, to apply to be designated as a sixth form college corporation.

This Order designates the Cirencester Tertiary College, a former further education college, as a sixth form college corporation, the Secretary of State for Education being satisfied that the College falls within section 33B(4) of the Act.

The Order provides for the continuity of the body corporate and its instrument and articles of government.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
