# The Devon Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1992

Made: 4th June 1992

Coming into force: 11th June 1992

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of and paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Devon Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1992 and shall come into force on 11th June 1992.

#### Amendment of Establishment Order

###### 2

In article 4 of the Devon Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00003] for the words “3 non-executive” there shall be substituted “, 5 non-executive” and for the words “3 executive” there shall be substituted “5 executive”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 4th June 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Devon Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991 to alter the number of non-executive and executive directors to be appointed to the Board of that Trust from 3 to 5.
