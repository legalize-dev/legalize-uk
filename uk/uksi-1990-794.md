# The Sunderland (Castletown and Doxford Park) Enterprise Zones (Designation) Order 1990

Made: 27th March 1990

Laid before Parliament: 5th April 1990

Coming into force: 3rd August 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 5 of Schedule 32 to the Local Govemment, Planning and Land Act 1980 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Sunderland (Castletown and Doxford Park) Enterprise Zones (Designation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 27th April 1990.

###### 2

In this Order, “the deposited map” means the set of maps, numbered 1 and 2, marked “Map referred to in the Sunderland (Castletown and Doxford Park) Enterprise Zones (Designation) Order 1990” prepared in duplicate and signed by an Under Secretary in the Department of the Environment, of which one duplicate has been deposited and is available for inspection at the principal offices of Sunderland Borough Council and the other duplicate has been deposited and is available for inspection at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment.

###### 3

- (1) The areas to which the Sunderland No. 2 – Castletown and the Sunderland No. 3 – Doxford Park Enterprise Zone Schemes relate and which are shown and marked as such on the deposited map are hereby designated as enterprise zones.

- (2) The boundaries of each of the said enterprise zones are represented on the deposited map by the outside edge of the black line edged internally with a single stippled band.

###### 4

Designation under article 3 of this Order shall take effect on 27th April 1990 and the designated areas shall remain enterprise zones for a period of 10 years from that date.

###### 5

The Sunderland Borough Council is hereby designated as the enterprise zone authority for the Sunderland No. 2 – Castletown and the Sunderland No. 3 – Doxford Park Enterprise Zones.

## Signed

Chris Patten — Secretary of State for the Environment — 26th March 1990

We consent,

Kenneth Carlisle — John Taylor — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 27th March 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

[image omitted]

This Order designates as enterprise zones the areas of the Sunderland Zone No. 2 – Castletown and the Sunderland No. 3 – Doxford Park Schemes which were adopted by the Sunderland Borough Council on 26th July 1989.

The designation is effective for a period of 10 years.

The location and areas of the enterprise zones are shown on the plan forming part of this note.
