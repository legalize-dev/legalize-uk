# The Local Authorities (Categorisation) (England) Order 2005

Made: 14th March 2005

Laid before Parliament: 21st March 2005

Coming into force: 11th April 2005

The First Secretary of State, having received a report from the Audit Commission[^f00001] produced under section 99(1) of the Local Government Act 2003[^f00002], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99(4) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Categorisation) (England) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 11th April 2005.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to English local authorities[^f00003].

#### Categorisation report

###### 2

The English local authorities, to which the report of the Audit Commission dated 7th March 2005 relates, are, by this Order, categorised in accordance with their categorisation in that report.

#### Excellent authorities

###### 3

The local authorities listed in Schedule 1 to this Order are categorised as excellent.

#### Good authorities

###### 4

The local authorities listed in Schedule 2 to this Order are categorised as good.

#### Fair authorities

###### 5

The local authorities listed in Schedule 3 to this Order are categorised as fair.

#### Weak authorities

###### 6

The local authorities listed in Schedule 4 to this Order are categorised as weak.

#### Poor authorities

###### 7

The local authorities listed in Schedule 5 to this Order are categorised as poor.

#### Revocation

###### 8

The Local Authorities (Categorisation) (England) (No.2) Order 2004[^f00004] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order categorises local authorities in England into five categories according to performance. The categorisation follows a report of the Audit Commission on 7th March 2005. A copy of the report may be obtained free of charge from Susie Turner at the Office of the Deputy Prime Minister, Eland House, Bressenden Place, London SW1E 5DU; telephone: 020 7944 4357 or email Susie.Turner@odpm.gsi.gov.uk.

Article 8 of this order revokes the Local Authorities (Categorisation) (England) (No.2) Order 2004.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies.
