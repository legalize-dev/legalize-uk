# The Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments) (Uncertificated Securities) Order 2009

Made: 14th July 2009

Coming into force: 1st October 2009

In accordance with sections 784(3), 1290 and 1294(6) of that Act, a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments) (Uncertificated Securities) Order 2009.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 1st October 2009.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The provisions of the Companies Act 2006 have been brought into force in stages. TheCompanies Act 2006 (Commencement No. 8, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/2860 (C. 126)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/2860)) brings into force almost all the remaining provisions of the Companies Act 2006 on 1st October 2009. This Order makes consequential amendments to the Uncertificated Securities Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/3755](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3755)), legislation for which Her Majesty’s Treasury is responsible.
