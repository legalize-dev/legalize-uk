# The M4 Motorway (Severn Bridge) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1995

Made: 17th August 1995

Laid before Parliament: 30th August 1995

Coming into force: 1st October 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 134(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the M4 Motorway (Severn Bridge) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st October 1995.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations “motorway” has the meaning given in regulation 3(1) of the Motorways Traffic (England and Wales) Regulations 1982[^f00002].

#### Speed limit

###### 3

No person shall drive a motor vehicle at a speed exceeding 40 miles per hour on a carriageway of the length of motorway specified in the Schedule to these Regulations at any time when—

- (a) there is placed at the beginning of that carriageway a traffic sign of the size, colour and type shown in the diagram numbered 670 in Schedule 2 to the Traffic Signs Regulations 1994[^f00003]; and

- (b) that sign is directed at traffic intending to proceed along that carriageway.

## SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF MOTORWAY SUBJECT TO A SPEED LIMIT

The M4 Motorway at the Severn Bridge in the counties of Gwent and Avon between a point 13 metres west of the Wye Bridge abutment and a point 58 metres east of the Aust Viaduct abutment (a distance measured along the length of the motorway of approximately 3064 metres).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Steven Norris — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 17th August 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations impose a 40 miles per hour speed limit on traffic using the motorway carried by the Severn Bridge, whenever traffic signs indicating such a limit are displayed.
