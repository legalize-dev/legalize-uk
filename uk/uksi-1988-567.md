# The Social Security Act 1986 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1988

Made: 21st March 1988

The Secretary of State for Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 88(1) of the Social Security Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security Act 1986 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1988.

- (2) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires, references to sections and Schedules are references to sections of and Schedules to the Social Security Act 1986.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Social Security Act 1986 which are specified in the Schedule to this Order is 11th April 1988.

#### Revocations

###### 3

In the Social Security Act 1986 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1987[^f00002], the commencement in the Schedule to that Order of the following provisions of the Social Security Act 1986 are revoked, that is to say—

- (a) Schedule 3, paragraph 11, and section 39 in so far as it relates to that paragraph;

- (b) the repeals in Schedule 11, and section 86(2) so far as it relates to those repeals, of the following provisions—

- (i) in the Social Security Act 1975[^f00003], Schedule 4, Part V, paragraphs 13 and 15, and in Schedule 20, the definitions of“The deceased” and“Industrial death benefit”;

- (ii) in the Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1975[^f00004], Schedule 3, paragraph 18;

- (iii) in the Pneumoconiosis etc. (Workers' Compensation) Act 1979[^f00005], section 2(3), the words“industrial death benefit under section 76 of the Social Security Act 1975, or”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 brings into force on 11th April 1988 the provisions of paragraphs 8 and 10 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security Act 1986 and consequential repeals.

Article 3 revokes the commencement in the Social Security Act 1986 (Commencement No. 7) Order 1987 of paragraph 11 of Schedule 3 to the Social Security Act 1986 (which relates to widower’s death benefit) and of specified repeals in Schedule 11 to that Act which relate or refer to industrial death benefit.
