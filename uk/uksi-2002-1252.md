# The British Overseas Territories Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

Made: 1st May 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 8(2) of the British Overseas Territories Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the British Overseas Territories Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002.

###### 2

The following provisions of the British Overseas Territories Act 2002 shall come into force on 21st May 2002:

- (a) sections 3 to 5 and Schedule 1,

- (b) section 6, and

- (c) section 7 and Schedule 2, so far as relating to the British Nationality (Falkland Islands) Act 1983[^f00002].

## Signed

Jack Straw — Secretary of State, Foreign and Commonwealth Office — 1st May 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force those provisions of the British Overseas Territories Act 2002 which are not already in force.
