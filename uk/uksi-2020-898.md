# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: at 1.00 p.m. on 25th August 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 3.00 p.m. on 25th August 2020

Coming into force: 26th August 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 26th August 2020.

#### Amendment of the Blackburn and Bradford Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) Regulations 2020 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 1(4), for “protected area” substitute—

> > - “protected area” means the area falling within— in the area of Blackburn with Darwen Borough Council, the following wards— Audley and Queen’s Park, Bastwell and Daisyfield, Billinge and Beardwood, Blackburn Central, Little Harwood and Whitebirk, Roe Lee, Shear Brow and Corporation Park, and Wensley Fold, or the area of the City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council;

## Signed

James Bethell — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — At 1.00 p.m. on 25th August 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Blackburn with Darwen and Bradford) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/822](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/822)) to change the area within Blackburn with Darwen Borough Council which forms part of “the protected area” for the purposes of those Regulations.

No impact assessment has been prepared for this instrument.
