# The European Parliamentary Elections (Common Electoral Principles) Regulations 2004

Made: 17th May 2004

Laid before Parliament: 18th May 2004

Coming into force: 8th June 2004

The Secretary of State, being the Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 , in exercise of the powers conferred on him by that provision, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These regulations may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Common Electoral Principles) Regulations 2004.

- (2) They shall come into force on 8th June 2004.

- (3) Regulations 2 and 4 extend to each part of the United Kingdom.

- (4) Regulation 3 extends to Northern Ireland only.

#### Amendments of the European Parliamentary Elections Act 2002

###### 2

- (1) The European Parliamentary Elections Act 2002 is amended as follows.

- (2) In section 9(1) (double voting) for “9” substitute “ 10 ”.

- (3) In section 10(8) (disqualification) for “6(1)” substitute “ 7(1) and (2) ”.

- (4) In Schedule 1A (periodic reviews of distribution of MEPs), in paragraph 6(1) for “10(2)” substitute “ 11(2) ”.

#### Amendments of the European Parliamentary Elections (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2004

###### 3

- (1) The European Parliamentary Elections (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2004 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 76 (by-elections)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—

- (i) in sub-paragraph (a) for “12(2)” substitute “ 13(4) ”; and

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (b) for “that Article” substitute “ Article 13(3) ”; and

- (b) in paragraph (4) for “10(2)” substitute “ 11(2) ”.

#### Amendment of the European Parliamentary Elections (Changes to the Franchise and Qualification of Representatives) Regulations 1994

###### 4

In regulation 4 (offence of standing as a candidate in more than one Member State) of the European Parliamentary Elections (Changes to the Franchise and Qualification of Representatives) Regulations 1994 , in paragraph (1) for “9” substitute “ 10 ”.

## Signed

Signed on behalf of the Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs

Christopher Leslie — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Constitutional Affairs
