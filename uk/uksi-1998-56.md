# The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation (No. 2) Order 1998

Made: 15th January 1998

Coming into force: 30th January 1998

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation (No. 2) Order 1998, and shall come into force on 30th January 1998.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route Traffic Order 1993[^f00003] is varied as provided by the provisions of this Order.
