# The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 4) Order 2001

Made: 10th August 2001

Laid before Parliament: 10th August 2001

Coming into force: 1st September 2001

Now, therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1), (3) and (4) and 5(4) of the Act[^f00003] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and revocation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 4) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

- (2) The Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) (No. 3) Order 2000[^f00004], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions (No. 4) Order 2000[^f00005], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions) Order 2001[^f00006], the Education (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions (No. 2) Order 2001[^f00007] and the Educational (School Teachers' Pay and Conditions (No. 3) Order 2001[^f00008] are hereby revoked.

#### Application

###### 2

This Order applies to school teachers (as defined in section 5(1) and (2) of the Act[^f00009] in England and Wales.

#### School Teachers' Pay and Conditions

###### 3

It is hereby directed that the provisions set out in the document published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office and entitled “School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2001”[^f00010] (“the Document”) shall have effect from 1st September 2001.

## Signed

Stephen Timms — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 10th August 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order which is made under section 2(1) of the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (“the Act”) refers to a Document entitled “School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document 2001” (“the Document”) published by Her Majesty’s Stationery Office, which is obtainable from Government book shops and through booksellers.

The order directs that the provisions set out in the Document shall have effect from 1st September 2001.

This Order applies to all school teachers (as defined in section 5(1) and (2) of the Act) in England and Wales. By virtue of section 2(6)(a) of the Act, the remuneration of school teachers to whom the Order applies is to be determined and paid to them by local education authorities in accordance with the pay scales and other provisions of the Document. By virtue of section 2(6)(b) of the Act, the provisions set out in the Document relating to the statutory conditions of employment have effect as terms of the contracts of employment of school teachers to whom the Order applies.
