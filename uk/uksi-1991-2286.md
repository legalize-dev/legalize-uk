# The New Roads and Street Works Act 1991 (Commencement No.1) (Scotland) Order 1991

Made: 14th October 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 170(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 1991.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991.

#### Commencement of certain provisions of the Act

###### 2

- (1) The provisions of the Act specified in the first column of Schedule 1 to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in the second column thereof) shall come into force on 21st October 1991.

- (2) The provisions of the Act specified in the first column of Schedule 2 to this Order (which relate to the matters specified in the second column thereof) shall come into force on 1st November 1991.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force—

- (a) on 21st October 1991, with the exception of sections 43 and 44, the provisions of Part II (New Roads in Scotland) and various provisions of Part V (General) of, and Schedule 8 and one consequential repeal in Schedule 9 to, the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991 (“the Act”); and

- (b) on 1st November 1991, sections 43 and 44 and various provisions of Part V of, and Schedules 8 and 9 to, the Act.
