# The Offensive Weapons Act 1996 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1996

Made: 4th August 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 4(4) of the Offensive Weapons Act 1996[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Offensive Weapons Act 1996 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1996.

- (2) This Order extends to Northern Ireland.

###### 2

Subsections (1) to (3) of section 4 of the Offensive Weapons Act 1996 shall come into force on 1st September 1996.

## Signed

David Maclean — Minister of State — 4th August 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st September 1996 subsections (1) to (3) of section 4 of the Offensive Weapons Act 1996 which make it an offence to have an article with a blade or point (or offensive weapon) on school premises.
