# The Climate Change and Sustainable Energy Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2007

Made: 22nd February 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 28(3) of the Climate Change and Sustainable Energy Act 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Climate Change and Sustainable Energy Act 2006 (Commencement) Order 2007.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

Sections 15 to 17 of the Climate Change and Sustainable Energy Act 2006 Act shall come into force on 28th February 2007.

## Signed

Ian Pearson — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 22nd February 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force sections 15 to 17 of the Climate Change and Sustainable Energy Act [2006 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/19) on 28th February 2007. The rest of the Act, other than the sections coming into force on Royal Assent, came into force in accordance with section 28.
