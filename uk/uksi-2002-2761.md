# The Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made: 6th November 2002

Laid before Parliament: 7th November 2002

Coming into force: 30th November 2002

The Secretary of State, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting in exercise of the powers conferred on her by virtue of the said section 2(2), makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 30th November 2002.

#### Amendment of the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1987

###### 2

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1987[^f00003]—

- (a) in the definition of “the Commission Regulation”, for the words “Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 282/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/0282)” there shall be substituted the words “Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 796/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0796)”[^f00004]; and

- (b) in the definition of “the Council Regulation”, for the words “Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1638/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/1638)” there shall be substituted the words “Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1513/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1513)”[^f00005].

## Signed

Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of StateDepartment for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 6th November 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 30th November 2002, amend the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1987 (S.I. [1987/1783](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/1783)). They apply to Great Britain.

These Regulations update the reference in the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1987 to Commission Regulation [(EEC) No. 2568/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2568) on the characteristics of olive oil and olive-residue oil and on relevant methods of analysis, so as to cover amendments of that Commission Regulation up to and including those effected by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 796/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0796). They also update the reference to Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 136/66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1966/0136) on the establishment of a common organisation of the market in oils and fats, so as to cover amendments of that Council Regulation up to and including those effected by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1513/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1513).

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of these Regulations.
