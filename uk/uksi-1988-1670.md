# The Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988

Made: 26th September 1988

In exercise of the power conferred upon me by section 4(2) of the Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988.

###### 2

The Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 shall come into force on 1st November 1988.

## Signed

Home Office

Douglas Hurd — 26th September 1988

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Licensing (Retail Sales) Act 1988 into force on 1st November 1988.
