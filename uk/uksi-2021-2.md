# The FLEGT Licensing Scheme (Council Regulation (EC) No 2173/2005) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: at 12.20 p.m. on 4th January 2021

Laid before Parliament: at 2.45 p.m. on 4th January 2021

Coming into force: 5th January 2021

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 10(1) and 11a(3) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 2173/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2173) on the establishment of a FLEGT licensing scheme for imports of timber into the European Community[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the FLEGT Licensing Scheme (Council Regulation [(EC) No 2173/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2173)) (Amendment) Regulations 2021 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are laid.

###### 2

These Regulations extend to Great Britain only.

#### Amendment of Council Regulation (EC) No 2173/2005

###### 3

- (1) Council Regulation [(EC) No 2173/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2173) on the establishment of a FLEGT licensing scheme for imports of timber into the European Community[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Article 1(3), after “partner countries”, insert “listed in Annex 1”.

- (3) In Article 2—

- (a) in paragraph 2, after “Agreement” insert “, as listed in Annex 1”;

- (b) in paragraph 4, after “scheme” insert “, as listed in Annex 1”.

- (4) Before Annex 2 insert—

> > **Annex1**

| Partner country | Designated licensing authority |
| --- | --- |
| The Republic of Indonesia | Licence Information Unit Ministry of Environment and Forestry Indonesia |

## Signed

Rebecca Pow — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — At 12.20 p.m. on 4th January 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations insert an Annex 1 into Council Regulation [(EC) No 2173/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2173) on the establishment of a FLEGT licensing scheme for imports of timber into the European Community.

The new Annex lists the Republic of Indonesia as a partner country and lists Indonesia’s designated licensing authority.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
