# The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1990

Made: 15th October 1990

Laid before Parliament: 22nd October 1990

Coming into force: 12th November 1990

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 2(1) and (5), 3 and 20 of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1976[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations shall come into force on 12th November 1990 and may be cited as the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1990.

###### 2

For the definition of “ATP” in regulation 2 (Interpretation and transitional provision) of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following definition—

> > - “ATP” means the Agreement on the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs and on the Special Equipment used for such Carriage (ATP) concluded at Geneva on 1st September 1970 as amended pursuant to article 18 of the agreement[^f00003] and as further amended pursuant to that article by amendments to Annex 1, appendices 1 and 2 which came into operation on 6th July 1989[^f00004] and 30th September 1990[^f00005]

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Christopher Chope — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 15th October 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the definition of the Agreement on the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs and the Special Equipment Used for such Carriage (ATP) of 1st September 1970 in the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Regulations 1985 to take account of a further amendment to the agreement.
