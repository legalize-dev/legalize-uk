# The Products Containing Meat etc. (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made: 15th November 2021

Laid before Parliament: 16th November 2021

Coming into force: 12th December 2021

sections 6(4), 16(1)(a) and (e), 26(1)(a) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001]; and

sections 114A and 138(7) and (8) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00002].

There has been consultation, as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Products Containing Meat etc. (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2021, come into force on 12th December 2021 and extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the Products Containing Meat etc. (England) Regulations 2014

###### 2

- (1) The Products Containing Meat etc. (England) Regulations 2014[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 1—

- (a) in the heading, for “, commencement and expiry” substitute “and commencement”;

- (b) omit paragraphs (3) and (4).

- (3) Omit regulation 10.

- (4) In Schedule 3, omit paragraphs 1 to 6.

#### Amendment of the Food (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2021

###### 3

In regulation 6(4)(b) of the Food (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2021[^f00005], for “12th December 2021” substitute “1st October 2022”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to the Products Containing Meat etc. (England) Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/3001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3001)) and the Food (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2021 ([S.I. 2021/616](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/616)).

Regulation 2 amends the Products Containing Meat etc. (England) Regulations 2014 to remove their expiry date, review clause and provisions that are no longer in force.

Regulation 3 amends the Food (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2021 to extend the transitional period during which the requirements of the Products Containing Meat etc. (England) Regulations 2014 do not apply to certain imported meat products, so that the period ends with effect from 1st October 2022 rather than 12th December 2021. This brings the transitional period for these meat products in line with the transitional period applicable to other imported food products to which regulation 6 of the Food (Amendment and Transitional Provisions) (England) Regulations 2021 applies.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
