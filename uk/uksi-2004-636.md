# The Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2004

Made: 8th March 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 108(2) of the Police Reform Act 2002[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police Reform Act 2002 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2004.

###### 2

Section 79 of the Police Reform Act 2002 shall come into force on 9th March 2004.

## Signed

Ivor Caplin — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State Ministry of Defence — 8th March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 79 of the Police Reform Act 2002 on 9th March 2004.
