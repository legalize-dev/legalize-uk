# The Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (Commencement No. 14) Order 1992

Made: 9th March 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 195(2) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (Commencement No. 14) Order 1992.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 shall come into force on 8th May 1992—

- section 19 in so far as it is not already in force,

- section 194(1) in so far as it relates to paragraph 30 of Schedule 11.

## Signed

Michael Heseltine — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 9th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 8th May 1992 the following provisions of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989—

- section 19, in so far as it is not already in force (members of local authorities to comply with requirements in regulations for giving notices of their pecuniary interests);

- section 194(1) in so far as it amends paragraph 41 of Schedule 12 to the Local Government Act [1972 (c. 70)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70) (recording the minutes of local authorities).
