# The Value Added Tax (Drugs and Medicines) (Health and Welfare) Order 2026

Made: 18th November 2026

Laid before the House of Commons: 19th November 2026

Coming into force: 10th December 2026

The Treasury make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 30(4), 31(2) and 96(9) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and effect

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Drugs and Medicines) (Health and Welfare) Order 2026 and comes into force on 10th December 2026.

- (2) Article 2 has effect in relation to supplies made on or after 10th December 2026.

#### Amendment of Group 12 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994

###### 2

In Part 2 of Schedule 8 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (zero-rating), in Group 12 (drugs, medicines, aids for the disabled, etc), in Note (2D)[^f00002], after paragraph (j) insert—

> > (k) regulations 220A and 220B of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012[^f00003].

#### Amendment of Group 7 of Schedule 9 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994

###### 3

In Part 2 of Schedule 9 to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (exemptions), in Group 7 (health and welfare), in Note (8)[^f00004],in the definition of “Act” after paragraph (b) insert—

> > (ba) a Measure or Act of Senedd Cymru;

## Signed

Christian Wakeford — Shaun Davies — Two of the Lord Commissioners of His Majesty’s Treasury — 18th November 2026

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order, which comes into force on 10th December 2026, makes amendments to Group 12 of Schedule 8 (“Group 12”) and Group 7 of Schedule 9 (“Group 7”) to the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (“*VATA*”). Group 12 zero-rates supplies of drugs, medicines and aids for the disabled. Group 7 exempts specified supplies of health and welfare from value added tax (“*VAT*”).

Article 2 provides that regulations 220A and 220B of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012 are relevant provisions for the purpose of Item 1 and Item 1A of Group 12 of Schedule 8 to VATA. The effect of article 2 is that where a registered pharmacy technician who has the authorisation of a pharmacist, or an individual under the pharmacy technician’s supervision who has authorisation of a pharmacist to supervise, dispenses a prescription to an individual for their personal use, the supply made on or after 10th December 2026 will be zero-rated.

Article 3 relates to Group 7 of Schedule 9 where supplies of care made by a state-regulated institution are exempt from VAT. State-regulated means approved, licensed, registered or exempted by a Minister or other authority pursuant to a provision of a public general Act. The effect of article 3 is to include a Measure or Act of Senedd Cymru in the definition of “Act” which was previously omitted.

A Tax Information and Impact Note covering this instrument will be published on the website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.
