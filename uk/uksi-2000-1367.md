# The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds and Prohibition on Investment) (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made: 19th May 2000

Laid before Parliament: 19th May 2000

Coming into force: 20th May 2000

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds and Prohibition on Investment) (Amendment) Regulations 2000, and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

#### Amendment of the 1999 Regulations

###### 2

The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds and Prohibition on Investment) Regulations 1999[^f00003] are amended as follows:

- (a) for regulation 1(2)(b) there is substituted—

> > (b) “the EC Regulation” means Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1294/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1294) of 15 June 1999 concerning a freeze of funds and a ban on investment in relation to the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (FRY)[^f00004], as amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 723/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/0723) of 6 April 2000[^f00005].

- (b) in regulation 2, the existing provision shall constitute paragraph (1) and the following paragraph is added—

> > (2) Any person who, without reasonable excuse, commits a breach of Article 6(1) of the EC Regulation shall be guilty of an offence.

- (c) in regulation 3, for “Article 8(2)” there is substituted “Article 8(4)”;

- (d) in regulation 5—

- (i) in paragraph (1), for “regulation 2” there is substituted “regulation 2(1)”;

- (ii) in paragraph (2), after “under” there is inserted “regulation 2(2) above or”;

- (e) in the Schedule, in paragraph 1(2), for “Article 7(4)” there is substituted “ Article 7(5)”.

## Signed

Clive Betts — Bob Ainsworth — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 19th May 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds and Prohibition on Investment) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/1786](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1786)), which give practical effect to Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1294/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1294) concerning a freeze of funds and a ban on investment in relation to the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (“the EC Regulation”). The EC Regulation has been amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No. 723/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/0723), and these Regulations make consequential changes to S.I. [1999/1786](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1786), by amending the numbering of cross-references, and by providing for breach of the new Article 6 (requirement to provide information to facilitate compliance with the EC Regulation) to be a criminal offence.
