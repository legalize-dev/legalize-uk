# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Multiverse Group Limited) Order 2025

Made: 5th September 2025

Coming into force: 2nd December 2025

The Office for Students has requested advice from the relevant body regarding the quality of, and standards applied to, higher education provided by the provider, and has had regard to that advice, in accordance with section 46(1)(a) and (7) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Multiverse Group Limited) Order 2025 and shall come into force on 2nd December 2025.

#### Authorisation to grant awards

###### 2

Multiverse Group Limited (registered company number 10027474) is authorised to grant taught awards of the kind mentioned in section 42(2)(c) of the said Act for subjects relating to—

- (a) computing, and

- (b) business and management.

###### 3

The authorisation in article 2 is for a fixed term beginning on 2nd December 2025 and expiring at the end of the day on 1st December 2027.

###### 4

Multiverse Group Limited may only grant the awards specified in article 2 to persons enrolled with it at the time they complete the course of study for which the award is granted.

## Signed

Jean Arnold — Deputy Director of Quality — Office for Students — 5th September 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order authorises Multiverse Group Limited to grant the taught awards mentioned in article 2 of this Order for a fixed term beginning on 2nd December 2025 and expiring at the end of the day on 1st December 2027.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations. The instrument has no impact on the public sector.
