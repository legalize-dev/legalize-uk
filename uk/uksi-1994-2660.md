# The Cardiff—Glan Conwy Trunk Road (A470) (Nant Crew Improvement) Order 1994

Made: 10th October 1994

Coming into force: 7th November 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers:—

###### 1

The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in the Schedule to this Order shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

###### 2

The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

###### 3

In this Order—

###### 4

This order shall come into force on 7th November 1994 and may be cited as the Cardiff—Glan Conwy Trunk Road (A470) (Nant Crew Improvement) Order 1994.

## SCHEDULE

Route of the New Trunk Road.

The route of the new trunk road is a route in the County of Powys about 1.97 kilometres in length starting at a point about 522 metres south of the junction of the trunk road and the classified road A4059 to Hirwaun and extending in a southerly direction to a point on the trunk road about 100 metres north of Nant Crew Bridge.

## Signed
