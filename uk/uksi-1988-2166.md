# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) (No. 2) 1988'

Made: 9th December 1988

Coming into force: 11th January 1989

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001]and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Lord Lyon King of Arms, enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) (No. 2) 1988 and shall come into force on 11th January 1989.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1978

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1978[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph 7 (poinding) after sub-paragraph (f), insert the following sub-paragraphs:—

> > (g) To serving a notice under section 18(1) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00003]£4.20
> (h) To making a report under section 21(4) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£7.05
> (i) To granting a receipt under section 21(5) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£3.55
> (j) To making a report under section 22(1) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£3.55
> (k) To granting a receipt under section 41(4) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£3.55
> (l) To making a report under section 41(5)(b) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£7.05

- (b) in paragraph 8 (sales), after sub-paragraph (i) insert the following sub-paragraphs:—

> > (j) To applying for a warrant of sale under section 30(1) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£3.55
> (k) To serving a copy application for a warrant of sale under section 30(3) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 — each debtor£4.20
> (l) To granting a receipt under section 33(3) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£3.55
> (m) To making a report under section 33(5)(b) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£7.05
> (n) To arranging warrant sale under a warrant of sale obtained under section 31(2)(a) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£4.60
> (o) To intimating the date arranged for a warrant sale under section 34(1)(a) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 — each debtor or possessor£4.20
> (p) To serving a copy warrant of sale under section 34(1)(b) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 -each debtor or possessor £5.60
> (q) To giving public notice of warrant sale under section 34(4) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£2.25
> (r) To arranging locksmith and tradesman to be in attendance by virtue of the power under section 31(2)(c) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£2.25
> (s)
>   - To removing articles to place of warrant sale£11.55
>   - To reserving time therefor£4.60
>   - To arranging tradesmen therefor£2.25
>   - Porterage and transport fees in addition
> (t) To attending and conducting a warrant sale under the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 when appraised value of poinded effects does not exceed £50£8.50
>   Each additional £25 or part thereof£2.75
> (u) To attending and supervising a warrant sale£11.55
> (v)
>   - To returning articles where sale cancelled under section 36(1) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£11.55
>   - To reserving time therefor£4.60
>   - To arranging tradesmen to be in attendance therefor£2.25
>   - Porterage and transport fees in addition
> (w) To making a report under section 36(2) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£7.05
> (x) To making a report of sale under section 39(1) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£7.05

- (c) in paragraph 11 (miscellaneous), after the last item, insert the following item —

> > To making a report under section 39(3) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987£7.05

## Signed

Emslie — Lord President, I.P.D. Edinburgh — 9th December 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1978 to make provision for fees in respect of work under the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987.
