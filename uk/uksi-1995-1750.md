# The Gaming (Small Charges) (Scotland) Variation Order 1995

Made: 5th July 1995

Laid before Parliament: 11th July 1995

Coming into force: 1st August 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 40(2) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Small Charges) (Scotland) Variation Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st August 1995.

- (2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

###### 2

Article 2(a) of the Gaming (Small Charges) (Scotland) Order 1992[^f00002] shall be varied by substituting “£15” for “£6”.

## Signed

Fraser of Carmyllie — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 5th July 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases from £6 to £15 the maximum daily charge which may be made in clubs and miners' welfare institutes in Scotland in respect of a person taking part in gaming when the only games played are bridge and whist.
