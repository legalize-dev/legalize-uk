# The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Amendment to Schedule 2) Order 1998

Made: 3rd July 1998

Laid before Parliament: 17th July 1998

Coming into force: 1st January 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 7(9) of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Amendment to Schedule 2) Order 1998.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on 1st January 1999.

- (3) This Order does not extend to Northern Ireland.

###### 2

At the end of Schedule 2 to the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 (animals to be obtained only from designated breeding or supplying establishments)[^f00002], there shall be added—

> > Ferret
> Gerbil
> Pig, if genetically modified
> Sheep, if genetically modified

## Signed

Gareth Williams — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 3rd July 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order adds references to ferrets and gerbils and to pigs and sheep, if genetically modified, to the list of animals in Schedule 2 to the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986. That Schedule lists animals which must be obtained from designated breeding or supplying establishments.
