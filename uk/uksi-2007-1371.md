# The Rules of the Air (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 30th April 2007

Coming into force: 31st May 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of his powers under article 95(1) of the Air Navigation Order 2005[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Rules of the Air (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 31st May 2007.

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the Rules of the Air Regulations 2007 shall be amended as follows.

###### 3

For rule 28(5) there shall be substituted—

> > (5) Paragraph (2) shall not apply to a helicopter which—
>   (a) flies at or below 3,000 feet above mean sea level;
>   (b) flies at a speed which, having regard to the visibility, is reasonable;
>   (c) remains clear of cloud with the surface in sight; and
>   (d) is in a flight visibility of at least 1,500 metres.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

G. Merron — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 30th April 2007

Department for Transport

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Rule of the Air Regulations 2007 by adding sub-paragraph (d) to rule 28(5) and making appropriate consequential changes to sub-paragraphs (b) and (c). A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced in respect of these Regulations.
