# The A406 Trunk Road (Enfield) Red Route (Clearway) Traffic Order 1995 Variation Order 1996

Made: 4th November 1996

Coming into force: 15th November 1996

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A406 Trunk Road (Enfield) Red Route (Clearway) Traffic Order 1995 Variation Order 1996, and shall come into force on 15th November 1996.

###### 2

The A406 Trunk Road (Enfield) Red Route (Clearway) Traffic Order 1995[^f00003] is varied so that in Schedule 3A, for item 4 there is substituted the following item:

> > (4) Sterling Way, south side, between a point 146.0 metres west of the western kerbline of Bull Lane and a point 195.0 metres west of the western kerbline of Bull Lane.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 4th November 1996
