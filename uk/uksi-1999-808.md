# The Velindre National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1999

Made: 15th March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 18th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by South Glamorgan District Health Authority[^f00002] resulting from the establishment of Velindre National Health Service Trust[^f00003], having consulted Bro Taf Health Authority[^f00004] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Velindre National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

- (2) In this Order–

- “the Authority” means Bro Taf Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means Velindre National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 1st March 1999, which schedule has been signed on behalf of the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 1st April 1999 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Jon Owen Jones — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 15th March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to Velindre National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1999 trust property formerly held by Bro Taf Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Welsh Office, HFM3, New Crown Building, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF1 3NQ.
