# The Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2007

Made: 22nd October 2007

Laid before Parliament: 25th October 2007

Coming into force: 15th November 2007

The Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) of the Finance Act 1973 :

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (England) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2007 and come into force—

- (a) for the purposes of regulation 2(3), on 1st January 2008; and

- (b) for all other purposes, on 15th November 2007.

#### Amendments to the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (England) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (England) Regulations 2006 are amended as follows.

- (2) For regulation 3, substitute—

> > (3) These Regulations apply in relation to consignments of—
>   (a) plants, plant products and other objects of a description specified in the first column of Schedule 1 that are listed in Annex V, Part B of the Directive; and
>   (b) seeds of *Solanaceae* whether or not listed in Annex V, Part B of the Directive,
>   which are landed in England from a third country or are the subject of an agreement made under article 12(6) of the Plant Health (England) Order 2005 .
