# The Cleveland College of Further Education andSir William Turners Sixth Form College, Redcar (Dissolution) Order 1994

Made: 5th July 1994

Laid before Parliament: 5th July 1994

Coming into force: 1st August 1994

Now therefore in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 27 of the Act the Secretary of State for Education after consulting the Old Corporations and with the consent of the further education corporation known as Cleveland Tertiary College (“the New Corporation”) hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cleveland College of Further Education and Sir William Turners Sixth Form College, Redcar (Dissolution) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st August 1994.

###### 2

On 1st August 1994 the Old Corporations shall be dissolved and all of their respective property, rights and liabilities shall be transferred to the New Corporation, being a body corporate established[^f00003]for purposes which include the provision of educational facilities or services.

###### 3

Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Act shall apply to any person employed by either of the Old Corporations immediately before 1st August 1994 as if the references in that section—

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;

- (b) to the operative date were to 1st August 1994;

- (c) to the transferor were to either of the Old Corporations (as the case may be); and

- (d) to the corporation were to the New Corporation.

## Signed

Tim Boswell — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 5th July 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves the furthe education corporations established to conduct Cleveland College of Further Education and Sir William Turners Sixth Form College. It provides that the property, rights and liabilities of the two colleges transfer to Cleveland Tertiary College and secures the rights of employees by applying section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
