# The Local Law (Further Provisions) Order 1966

Made: 30th September 1966

Laid before Parliament: 7th October 1966

Coming into Operation: 8th October 1966

The Minister of Housing and Local Government, in exercise of his powers under section 84, as extended by section 87(2) and section 90 of the London Government Act 1963 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf (and after consultation with the Common Council in accordance with section 84(4) of the said Act), hereby makes the following order:—

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Local Law (Further Provisions) Order 1966 and shall come into operation on 8th October 1966.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act 1889 applies to the interpretation of this order as it applies to the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 3

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes further modifications to local enactments which apply to the Greater London Council, the London borough councils and the Common Council and repeals and revokes certain further enactments which are spent, obsolete or substantially superseded.
