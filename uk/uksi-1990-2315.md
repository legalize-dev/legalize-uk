# The Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1990

Made: 20th November 1990

Laid before Parliament: 28th November 1990

Coming into force: 1st January 1991

The Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 166(1) of the Social Security Act 1975[^f00001] and section 52A(1) of the Social Security Pensions Act 1975[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Occupational Pensions (Revaluation) Order 1990.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 1st January 1991.

#### The revaluation percentage for each revaluation period

###### 2

For the purposes of section 52A of the Social Security Pensions Act 1975, the revaluation percentage for each revaluation period specified in column 1 below is the appropriate percentage specified in column 2.

| *Column 1* | *Column 2* |
| --- | --- |
| *Revaluation Period* | *Revaluation Percentage* |
| 1st January 1986 – 31st December 1990 | 27.6% |
| 1st January 1987 – 31st December 1990 | 21.6% |
| 1st January 1988 – 31st December 1990 | 15.8% |
| 1st January 1989 – 31st December 1990 | 10.3% |
| 1st January 1990 – 31st December 1990 | 5.0% |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Henley — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Social Security — 20th November 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made, as required by section 52A of the Social Security Pensions Act 1975, in the revaluation year beginning on 1st January 1990. The revaluation percentages specified are relevant to the revaluation of benefits under ocupational pension schemes, as required by section 52B of, and Schedule 1A to, that Act.
