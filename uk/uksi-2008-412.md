# The Northumberland, Tyne and Wear National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2008

Made: 19th February 2008

Laid before Parliament: 26th February 2008

Coming into force: 31st March 2008

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 213, 217(2) and 272(8) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00001], having regard to changes in the functions of the Northumberland, Tyne and Wear National Health Service Trust[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Northumberland, Tyne and Wear National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 31st March2008.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the NHS Trust” means the Northumberland, Tyne and Wear National Health Service Trust;

- “the Trustees” means the trustees for the Newcastle upon Tyne Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust[^f00003];

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the NHS Trust and the Trustees and signed on behalf of the NHS Trust on 1st February 2008 and on behalf of the Trustees on 31st January 2008.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the NHS Trust to the Trustees.

- (2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference in relation to the trust property to the trustees of the trust property or to the NHS Trust shall be construed as a reference to the Trustees.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Ben Bradshaw — Minister of Health, — Department of Health — 19th February 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order transfers to the trustees for the Newcastle upon Tyne Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust, trust property held by the Northumberland, Tyne and Wear National Health Service Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
