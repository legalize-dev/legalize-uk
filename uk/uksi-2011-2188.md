# The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 26) Order 2011

Made: 5th September 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 336(3) and (4) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00001]:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 26) Order 2011.

- (2) In this Order “the 2003 Act” means the Criminal Justice Act 2003.

###### 2

Section 29(1) to (3), (5) and (6) (new method of instituting proceedings) and section 30 (further provision about the new method) of the 2003 Act come into force on the day following the day on which this Order is made but only for the purposes of criminal proceedings instituted by a public prosecutor specified in an Order made by the Secretary of State under section 29(5)(h) of the 2003 Act or a person authorised by such a person (specification of a person as a public prosecutor or a person authorised by such a person for the purposes of issuing a written charge and requisition).

###### 3

Section 29(1) to (3), (5) and (6) (new method of instituting proceedings) and section 30 (further provision about the new method) of the 2003 Act come into force on 3rd October 2011, but only for the purposes of criminal proceedings instituted by a public prosecutor within the meaning of —

- (a) section 29(5)(a) of the 2003 Act (a police force or a person authorised by a police force to institute criminal proceedings), so far as not already in force[^f00002]; and

- (b) section 29(5)(e) of the 2003 Act (a Secretary of State or a person authorised by a Secretary of State to institute criminal proceedings) where criminal proceedings are instituted by the Secretary of State for Business and Trade or a person authorised by that Secretary of State.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Secretary of State

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 29(1) to (3), (5) and (6) and section 30 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 so as to allow the following public prosecutors to institute criminal proceedings by issuing a written charge and requisition:

a person specified by the Secretary of State in an Order under section 29(5)(h) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 or a person authorised by such a person;

a police force or a person authorised by a police force to institute criminal proceedings in England and Wales, so far as not currently allowed; and

the Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills or a person authorised by the Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills to institute criminal proceedings.

The Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1999)), the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 21) Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1424](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1424)), Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 23) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2879](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2879)) and the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (Commencement No. 25) Order 2010 ([S.I. 2010/3005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/3005)) commenced the same provisions in the 2003 Act as they apply to public prosecutors in specified locations.
