# The Lewisham Primary Care Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2007

Made: 26th February 2007

Laid before Parliament: 2nd March 2007

Coming into force: 1st April 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 92(1), 96(1) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], having regard to proposed changes in the functions of the Lewisham Primary Care Trust[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Lewisham Primary Care Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st April 2007.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Trust” means the Lewisham Primary Care Trust;

- “the new trustees” means the trustees for the Guy’s and St. Thomas’ National Health Service Foundation Trust[^f00003]; and

- “the trust property” means the property specified in the schedule of trust property prepared and agreed by the Trust and the new trustees and signed on behalf of the Trust on 28th July 2006 and on behalf of the new trustees on 6th August 2006.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

- (1) On the date that this Order comes into force the trust property, and any rights and liabilities arising from that property, shall be transferred from the Trust to the new trustees.

- (2) In any instrument relating to the trust property, any reference to the trustees of the trust property or the Trust shall be construed as a reference to the new trustees.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Andy Burnham — Minister of Health — Department of Health — 26th February 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order transfers to the trustees for the Guy’s and St. Thomas’ National Health Service Foundation Trust, trust property held by the Lewisham Primary Care Trust and makes related provision. A copy of the schedule of trust property transferred under this Order can be obtained from the Department of Health, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
