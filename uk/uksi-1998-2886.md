# The Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 (Commencement No.2) Order 1998

Made: 20th November 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 63(2) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 (Commencement No.2) Order 1998.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 is 21st November 1998–

- (a) sections 7 to 10;

- (b) Schedule 1; and

- (c) Schedule 9, paragraphs 4(3) and 5(3) so far as not already in force.

## Signed

Helen Liddell — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 20th November 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 21st November 1998 those provisions of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 which are not already in force. The provisions relate to the Scottish Further Education Funding Council.
