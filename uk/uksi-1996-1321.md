# The Taxes (Interest Rate) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1996

Made: 16th May 1996

Laid before the House of Commons: 16th May 1996

Coming into force: 6th June 1996

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 178 of the Finance Act 1989[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Taxes (Interest Rate) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 6th June 1996—

###### 2

In regulation 5(1) of the Taxes (Interest Rate) Regulations 1989[^f00002].

- (a) for “6th February 1996” there shall be substituted “6th June 1996”;

- (b) for “7.25 per cent per annum” there shall be substituted “7 per cent per annum”.

## Signed

Derek Conway — Simon Burns — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th May 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 5 of the Taxes (Interest Rate) Regulations 1989 (S.I. [1989/1297](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1297) as substituted by S.I. [1994/1307](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1307)). That regulation provides for the official rate of interest for the purposes of section 160 of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (taxation of beneficial loans made to employees) by first specifying an official rate of interest generally and then specifying, by way of exception, different official rates of interest for certain beneficial loans in the currencies of countries specified in the Table to the regulation.

The amendment made by these Regulations specifies, with effect from 6th June 1996, a new general official rate of interest of 7 per cent. per annum, in substitution for the rate of 7.25 per cent. per annum specified by S.I. [1996/54](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/54).
