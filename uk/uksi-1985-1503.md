# The County Court (Forms) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1985

Made: 30th July 1985

Coming into Operation: 1st December 1985

###### 1

- (1) These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Forms) (Amendment No. 2) Rules 1985.

- (2) In these Rules a form referred to by number means the form so numbered in the Schedule to the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982[^f00001] and “*the main Schedule*” means that Schedule.

###### 2

There shall be inserted in the main Schedule after Form N.112, the new Forms N.113 to N.116 contained in the Schedule to these Rules.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the County Court (Forms) Rules 1982 so as to provide for a form of application for the registration of a maintenance order in a magistrates' court and for three forms to be used in proceedings under Part II of the Children Act 1975[^f00002], namely—

(a) a form of application for a custodianship order,

(b) a form of consent to such an order, and

(c) a medical certificate.
