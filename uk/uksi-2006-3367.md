# The Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made: 13th December 2006

Laid before Parliament: 19th December 2006

Coming into force: 10th January 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 10th January 2007.

#### Amendment of the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) Regulations 2003

###### 2

The Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) Regulations 2003[^f00004] are amended as follows—

- (a) in the definition of “Regulation 1019/2002”, for “[Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1750/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1750)” substitute “[Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1044/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1044)[^f00005]”;

- (b) In regulation 6(2), for “1st July 2006” substitute “1st July 2008”[^f00006].

## Signed

Jeff Rooker — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 13th December 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations which amend the Olive Oil (Marketing Standards) Regulations 2003 (“the 2003 Regulations”), apply to Great Britain and come into force on 10th January 2007.

The 2003 Regulations provide for the application of Article 35 of Regulation No. 136/66/EEC of the Council on the establishment of a common organisation of the market in oils and fats (OJ No.172, 30.9.66, p. 3025) and [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1019/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1019) on marketing standards for olive oil (“*Regulation 1019/2002*”) (OJ No.155, 14.6.2002 p. 27).

These Regulations update the reference in the 2003 Regulations to Regulation 1019/2002 to include amendment to that Regulation effected by [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1044/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1044) amending Regulation [(EC) No 1019/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/1019) on marketing standards for olive oil (OJ No. L187, 8.7.2006, p. 20).

The effect of that amendment is to postpone the date of application of article 5(c) of Regulation 1019/2002, (which limits the indication of organoleptic properties of virgin olive oil on the labelling), to 1st July 2008.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
