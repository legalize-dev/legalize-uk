# The Road Vehicles (Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made: 5th September 2018

Laid before Parliament: 13th September 2018

Coming into force: 5th October 2018

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 .

The Secretary of State for Transport is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of that Act for the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme and in relation to measures relating to the environment.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 5th October 2018.

#### Amendment of the Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2009

###### 2

- (1) The Road Vehicles (Approval) Regulations 2009 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 33A (defeat systems) , after paragraph (5), insert—

> > (5A) A person who breaches a prohibition in paragraphs 8(1), (2) or 9(10) of Schedule 7 is guilty of an offence.

- (3) In Schedule 7 (penalties, enforcement and other matters) , in paragraph 3(1)—

- (a) omit “who is a manufacturer”;

- (b) for “the offence”, substitute “ an offence ”;

- (c) after “33A(1)”, insert “ or (5A) ”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jesse Norman — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 5th September 2018
