# The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006 (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 11th March 2009

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 2009

Coming into force: 6th April 2009

In accordance with section 24(4)[^f00002] of that Act the Secretary of State has consulted with the Scottish Ministers and the Security Industry Authority.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006 (Amendment) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 6th April 2009.

#### Amendment to the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) Omit article 1(2).

## Signed

Alan Campbell — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 11th March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Duration of Licence)(No.2) Order 2006 so as to extend that Order to Northern Ireland.
