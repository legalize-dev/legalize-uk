# The Family Procedure (Adoption) (Amendment) Rules 2008

Made: 16th September 2008

Laid before Parliament: 18th September 2008

Coming into force: 3rd November 2008

In accordance with section 79 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00002] the Committee has consulted with persons it considered appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Family Procedure (Adoption) (Amendment) Rules 2008 and shall come into force on 3rd November 2008.

#### Amendment to the Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with this rule.

- (2) In rule 55(1)(a), at the end insert “(if he consents)”.

- (3) In rule 95, after paragraph (2) add—

> > (3) This rule does not apply to magistrates’ courts.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005 by amending—

- (a) rule 55(1)(a) (power of the court to make an order appointing the Official Solicitor as litigation friend) so as to clarify that such an order may be made only if the Official Solicitor consents; and

- (b) rule 95 (Power of the court to proceed in the absence of a party) by adding new paragraph (3) providing that rule 95 is not to apply to magistrates’ courts.

The amendments come into force on 3rd November 2008.
