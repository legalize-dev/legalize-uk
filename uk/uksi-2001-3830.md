# The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001

Made: 30th November 2001

Laid before Parliament: 3rd December 2001

Coming into force: 31st December 2001

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by the said section 2(2), hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations extend to England only, may be cited as the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 31st December 2001.

#### Amendment of the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995

###### 2

Schedule 7, Part III, to the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995[^f00003], shall be amended as follows—

- (a) for paragraph 7(2) there shall be substituted the following:

> > (2) In this Part “gas mixture” means either—
>   (a) argon, nitrogen or other inert gases, or any mixture of these gases, in atmospheric air with a maximum of 2% oxygen by volume; or
>   (b) any mixture of argon, nitrogen, or other inert gases with atmospheric air and carbon dioxide provided that the carbon dioxide concentration does not exceed 30% by volume and the oxygen concentration does not exceed 2% by volume.

- (b) in paragraph 8(c)(iii)(bb) (which provides for a warning signal if the concentration of carbon dioxide rises above or falls below certain levels) for the words “falls below 25% or rises above 30%” there shall be substituted the words “rises above 30%”; and

- (c) in paragraph 10(b)(ii) (which provides that no bird shall enter the chamber if the displayed concentration of carbon dioxide is above or below a certain volume) for the words “below 25% by volume or above 30% by volume” there shall be substituted the words “above 30% by volume”.

## Signed

Elliot Morley — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 30th November 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations amend the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995 (the 1995 Regulations).

2 They substitute a new paragraph 7(2) of Part III of Schedule 7 to the 1995 Regulations, so as to permit a new gas mixture for killing birds at a slaughterhouse. They also make consequential amendments to paragraphs 8 and 10 of that Schedule.

3 A regulatory impact assessment has not been made.
