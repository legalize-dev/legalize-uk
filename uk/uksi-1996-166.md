# The Insurance Premium Tax (Prescribed Rates of Interest) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 31st January 1996

Laid before the House of Commons: 31st January 1996

Coming into force: 6th February 1996

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 74(1) of, and paragraph 21(5) of Schedule 7 to, the Finance Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Insurance Premium Tax (Prescribed Rates of Interest) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 6th February 1996.

###### 2

In article 2 of the Insurance Premium Tax (Prescribed Rates of Interest) Order 1994[^f00002] for “5.5” there shall be substituted “6.25”.

## Signed

Simon Burns — Derek Conway — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 31st January 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the prescribed rate of interest for the purposes of paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to the Finance Act [1994 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/9) (interest on insurance premium tax recovered or recoverable by assessment) from 5.5 per cent. to 6.25 per cent. with effect from 6th February 1996.
