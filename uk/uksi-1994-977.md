# The Three Valleys, Rickmansworth and Colne Valley Water (Amendment of Local Enactments etc.) Order 1994

Made: 29th March 1994

Coming into force: 31st March 1994

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Three Valleys, Rickmansworth and Colne Valley Water (Amendment of Local Enactments etc.) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 31st March 1994.

#### Amendment of references in local statutory provisions etc.

###### 2

- (1) As from 31st March 1994 any reference to, or to the undertaking of, Rickmansworth and Colne Valley in any local statutory provision and in any charges scheme made by Rickmansworth and Colne Valley under section 76 of the Water Act 1989 or section 143 of the Water Industry Act 1991 shall have effect, subject to paragraph (2) below, as a reference to Three Valleys or, as the case may be, to the undertaking of Three Valleys.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall only apply to any local statutory provision or charges scheme in relation to the area, things or persons in relation to which or to whom that provision or scheme has effect before 31st March 1994.

#### Repeal of local statutory provisions

###### 3

The local statutory provisions referred to in the Schedule to this Order are repealed to the extent indicated in that Schedule with effect from 31st March 1994.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends references in local statutory provisions and charges schemes for the purposes of the replacement of Rickmansworth and Colne Valley Water Companies as water undertakers by Three Valleys Water plc.
