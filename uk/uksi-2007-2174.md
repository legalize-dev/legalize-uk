# The Non-Contentious Probate Fees (Amendment) Order 2007

Made: 24th July 2007

Laid before Parliament: 26th July 2007

Coming into force: 1st October 2007

In accordance with section 92(5) and (6) of that Act the Lord Chancellor has consulted the Lord Chief Justice, the Master of the Rolls, the President of the Queen’s Bench Division, the President of the Family Division, the Chancellor of the High Court, the Head of Civil Justice, the Deputy Head of Civil Justice and the Civil Justice Council.

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Contentious Probate Fees (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st October 2007.

###### 2

In this Order, “the 2004 Order” means the Non-Contentious Probate Fees Order 2004[^f00002].

###### 3

For articles 4 and 5 of the 2004 Order substitute—

> > (4) Schedule 1A applies for the purpose of ascertaining whether a party is entitled to a remission or part remission of a fee prescribed by this Order.
> (5) The Lord Chancellor may, on the ground of financial hardship or for other reasonable cause, remit in whole or in part any fee prescribed by this Order.

###### 4

After Schedule 1 of the 2004 Order, insert the Schedule in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Jack Straw — Lord Chancellor — 24th July 2007

We consent,

Frank Roy — Alan Campbell — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 24th July 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Non-Contentious Probate Fees Order 2004 ([S.I. 2005/3120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/3120)) by inserting Schedule 1A which sets out when a party is entitled to remission or part remission of a fee.
