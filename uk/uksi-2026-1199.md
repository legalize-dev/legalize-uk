# The Sussex and Brighton Combined County Authority (Transfer of Fire and Rescue Functions) Order 2026

Made: 16th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 19th November 2026

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1A(1), (8) and (9) and 60(2) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001] and sections 56(1)(a) and 105(2) of the English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026[^f00002].

## Part 1 — Introduction

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Sussex and Brighton Combined County Authority (Transfer of Fire and Rescue Functions) Order 2026 and comes into force in accordance with paragraphs 2 and 3.

- (2) Save as provided in paragraph (3) this Order comes into force on 15th December 2026.

- (3) Article 3 (transfer of functions of the ESFA to the SBCCA), article 4 (transfer of functions of the WSFRA to the SBCCA), article 8 (consequential modification) and Schedule 2 come into force on 1st April 2027.

- (4) This Order extends to England and Wales.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “*the 2004 Act*” means the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004;

- “*the ESFA*” means the East Sussex Fire Authority[^f00003];

- “*the ESFA Area*” means the area of the ESFA;

- “*the SBCCA*” means the Sussex and Brighton Combined County Authority[^f00004];

- “*the SBCCA Area*” means the area of the SBCCA;

- “*transfer date*” means 1st April 2027;

- “*the WSFRA*” means the West Sussex County Council as a fire and rescue authority[^f00005];

- “*the WSFRA Area*” means the area of the WSFRA.

## Part 2 — Transfer of functions

#### Transfer of functions of the ESFA to the SBCCA

###### 3

- (1) The functions of the ESFA that were exercisable in relation to the ESFA Area immediately before this article came into force are functions of the SBCCA.

- (2) The SBCCA is the fire and rescue authority for the ESFA Area for the purposes of the 2004 Act.

- (3) The ESFA is abolished.

- (4) The East Sussex Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1996[^f00006] is revoked.

#### Transfer of functions of the WSFRA to the SBCCA

###### 4

- (1) The functions of the WSFRA that were exercisable in relation to the WSFRA Area immediately before this article came into force are functions of the SBCCA.

- (2) The SBCCA is the fire and rescue authority for the WSFRA Area for the purposes of the 2004 Act.

## Part 3 — Transitional arrangements

#### SBCCA fire and rescue authority functions prior to 1st April 2027

###### 5

- (1) During the period beginning with the day on which this article comes into force and ending with 31st March 2027—

- (a) the relevant functions are exercisable in relation to the SBCCA Area by the SBCCA, and not by the ESFA or the WSFRA;

- (b) Schedule 1 (scrutiny of proposed budget for fire and rescue functions) applies in respect of the SBCCA’s exercise of the relevant functions.

- (2) In paragraph (1), “*the relevant functions*” means the functions of a fire and rescue authority designated by an order under section 1A of the 2004 Act, solely for the purposes of Chapter IV (precepts) and Chapter IVZA (referendums relating to council tax increases) of Part I of the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00007].

#### Preparation of ESFA statement of accounts

###### 6

- (1) The following requirements are modified in the case of the ESFA for the financial year which began on 1st April 2026.

- (2) The requirement in section 3(3) (general requirements for accounts) of the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014[^f00008] for a relevant authority to prepare a statement of accounts for each financial year[^f00009] ending with 31st March is modified to require, after 31st March 2027, the SBCCA to prepare a statement of accounts in respect of the ESFA's accounts.

- (3) For the purposes of regulations 9 (signing and approval of statement of accounts for Category 1 authorities) and 15 (commencement of the period for the exercise of public rights) of the Accounts and Audit Regulations 2015[^f00010], references to the responsible financial officer for a relevant authority are to be read as references to the responsible financial officer for the SBCCA.

#### Continuity

###### 7

- (1) The abolition of the ESFA and the transfer of its functions to the SBCCA by this Order do not affect the validity of anything done before the abolition or transfer.

- (2) Paragraphs (3) to (5) apply in respect of all functions transferred by this Order from the ESFA to the SBCCA.

- (3) There may be continued by or in relation to the SBCCA anything (including legal proceedings) which—

- (a) relates to any of the functions transferred; and

- (b) is in the process of being done by or in relation to the ESFA immediately before the transfer date.

- (4) Anything which—

- (a) was made or done by or in relation to the ESFA for the purposes of, or otherwise in connection with, the functions transferred; and

- (b) is in effect immediately before the transfer date,

has effect as if made or done by or in relation to the SBCCA.

- (5) The SBCCA is to be substituted for the ESFA in any instruments, contracts or legal proceedings which—

- (a) relate to any of the functions transferred; and

- (b) are made or commenced before the transfer date.

- (6) The transfer of the WSFRA’s functions to the SBCCA by this Order does not affect the validity of anything done before the transfer.

- (7) Paragraphs (8) to (10) apply where any functions are transferred by this Order from the WSFRA to the SBCCA.

- (8) There may be continued by or in relation to the SBCCA anything (including legal proceedings) which—

- (a) relates to any of the functions transferred; and

- (b) is in the process of being done by or in relation to the WSFRA immediately before the transfer date.

- (9) Anything which—

- (a) was made or done by or in relation to the WSFRA for the purposes of, or otherwise in connection with, the functions transferred; and

- (b) is in effect immediately before the transfer date,

has effect as if made or done by or in relation to the SBCCA.

- (10) The SBCCA is to be substituted for the WSFRA in any instruments, contracts or legal proceedings which—

- (a) relate to any of the functions transferred; and

- (b) are made or commenced before the transfer date.

## Part 4 — Consequential modification

#### Consequential modification

###### 8

Schedule 2 makes a consequential modification.

## Schedule 1 — Scrutiny of proposed budget for fire and rescue functions

#### Interpretation

###### 1

In this Schedule—

- “*overview and scrutiny committee*” means the committee referred to in Schedule 1 to the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023[^f00011];

- “*relevant scrutiny body*” means the overview and scrutiny committee of the SBCCA.

#### Notification of proposed budget for fire and rescue functions

###### 2

- (1) The SBCCA must notify the relevant scrutiny body of its proposed budget for fire and rescue functions in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2027.

- (2) The notification must be given—

- (a) before the date on which the SBCCA determines whether to approve the proposed budget; and

- (b) at a time which allows the relevant scrutiny body a reasonable amount of time to meet its obligations under paragraph 3(1) and (2).

#### Review of proposed budget

###### 3

- (1) The relevant scrutiny body must review any proposed budget notified to it under paragraph 2.

- (2) The relevant scrutiny body must make a report to the SBCCA in relation to the proposed budget.

- (3) The SBCCA must—

- (a) have regard to any report made under sub-paragraph (2) and to any recommendations made in it;

- (b) provide the relevant scrutiny body with a response to the report and any recommendations made in it;

- (c) notify the relevant scrutiny body of any material changes to the budget for fire and rescue functions after it has been finalised; and

- (d) publish the response given under paragraph (b), and any notification given under paragraph (c)—

- (i) in such manner as is required by the relevant scrutiny body; and

- (ii) in any event, in a prominent place on the website of the SBCCA.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order provides for the conferral of the functions of the East Sussex Fire Authority (“the ESFA”) and the West Sussex County Council as a fire and rescue Authority (“the WSFRA”) (together “the existing FRAs”) on the Sussex and Brighton Combined County Authority (“the SBCCA”).

A separate statutory instrument is planned to provide for the transfer of the property, rights and liabilities of the existing FRAs to the SBCCA.

Section 1A of the Fire and Rescue Services Act [2004 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/21) provides a power for the Secretary of State to designate a mayoral combined county authority as the fire and rescue authority (“the FRA”) for the whole of its area.

Article 3 provides that the SBCCA is the FRA for the area of the ESFA and transfers the functions of the ESFA to the SBCCA. It also abolishes the ESFA and revokes the East Sussex Fire Services (Combination Scheme) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2922](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2922)).

Article 4 provides that the SBCCA is the FRA for the area of the WSFRA and transfers the functions of the WSFRA to the SBCCA.

Articles 5 to 7 make transitional arrangements in relation to the transfer of functions.

Schedule 1 to this Order provides for scrutiny of the SBCCA’s proposed budget for fire and rescue functions in relation to the financial year beginning on 1st April 2027.

Schedule 2 to this Order makes a consequential modification to ensure that the requirements of the Local Government (Best Value Authorities) (Power to Trade) (England) Order 2009 ([S.I. 2009/2393](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2393)) that apply to the existing FRAs will continue to apply to the SBCCA following the transfer of functions.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
