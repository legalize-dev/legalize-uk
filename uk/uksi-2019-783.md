# The European Structural and Investment Funds Common Provisions Rules etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

Made: 28th March 2019

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

In accordance with paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 7 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, a draft of this instrument has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

## PART 1 — Introductory

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the European Structural and Investment Funds Common Provisions Rules etc. (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 and come into force on exit day.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations—

- “[Regulation 184/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0184)” means [Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 184/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0184) laying down pursuant to [Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1303) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund, the terms and conditions applicable to the electronic data exchange system between the Member States and the Commission and adopting pursuant to [Regulation (EU) No 1299/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1299) of the European Parliament and of the Council on specific provisions for the support from the European Regional Development Fund to the European territorial cooperation goal, the nomenclature of the categories of intervention for support from the European Regional Development Fund under the European territorial cooperation goal;

- “[Regulation 215/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0215)” means [Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 215/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0215) laying down rules for implementing [Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1303) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund and laying down general provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund, the Cohesion Fund and the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund with regard to methodologies for climate change support, the determination of milestones and targets in the performance framework and the nomenclature of categories of intervention for the European Structural and Investment Funds;

- “[Regulation 240/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0240)” means [Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 240/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0240) on the European code of conduct on partnership in the framework of the European Structural and Investment Funds;

- “[Regulation 821/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0821)” means [Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 821/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0821) laying down rules for the application of [Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1303) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards detailed arrangements for the transfer and management of programme contributions, the reporting on financial instruments, technical characteristics of information and communication measures for operations and the system to record and store data;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in section 8(1) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) in order to address failures of retained EU law to operate effectively and other deficiencies (in particular under section 8(2)(a), (b), (c), (d) and (g)) arising from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union.

These Regulations make amendments to retained direct EU legislation as they relate to financial support for rural development and financial support granted under [Regulation (EU) No 508/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0508). Part 2 makes amendments and Part 3 makes revocations.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
