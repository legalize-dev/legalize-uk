# The Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No. 9) (England) Order 2020

Made: 25th June 2020

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99(1) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management Act 2004 (Commencement No. 9) (England) Order 2020.

#### Commencement of provision

###### 2

Section 45 (street works register) of the Traffic Management Act 2004, except subsection (2), comes into force as respects England on 29th June 2020.

## Signed

Vere — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 25th June 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force, as respects England, section 45 of the Traffic Management Act 2004, except subsection (2), on 29th June 2020. Section 45 amends section 53 of the New Roads and Street Works Act [1991 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/22), which relates to the street works register.
