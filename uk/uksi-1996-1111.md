# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made: 15th April 1996

Laid before Parliament: 18th April 1996

Coming into force: 9th May 1996

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the said section 2(2), hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 9th May 1996.

#### Amendment of the Animal and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations 1995

###### 2

In regulation 10, paragraph (2) of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations 1995[^f00003], for the words “any additional legislation specified in Schedule 3” there shall be substituted the words “any additional requirements specified in Schedule 3”.

###### 3

For Schedules 2, 3, 5 and 6 to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations 1995, there shall be substituted the provisions of Schedules 1, 2, 3 and 4 to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Animals and Animals Products (Import and Export) Regulations 1995 (“the principal Regulations”) by replacing Schedules 2, 3, 5 and 6 of those Regulations to take into account changes in Community legislation since those Regulations were passed (regulation 3). The Community instruments containing the changes are:

Commission Decision [96/187/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0187) of 19th February 1996 amending Decision [95/357/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0357) drawing up a list of border inspection posts agreed for veterinary checks on products and animals from third countries detailed rules concerning the checks to be carried out by the veterinary experts of the Commission and repealing Decision [95/24/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0024) (OJ No. L59, 8.3.96, p.59);

Commission Decision [95/493/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0493) of 15th November 1995 repealing Decision [94/887/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0887) derogating from prohibitions relating to African swine fever for certain areas in Spain and repealing Council Decision [89/21/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1989/0021) (OJ No. L282, 24.11.95, p.28);

Council Directive [95/25/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0025) of 22nd June 1995 amending Directive [64/432/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1964/0432) on health problems affecting intra-Community trade in bovine animals and swine (OJ No. L243, 11.10.95, p.16);

Commission Decision [96/190/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0190) of 22nd February 1996 amending Decision [93/24/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0024) and Decision [93/244/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0244) and concerning additional guarantees relating to Aujeszky’s disease for pigs destined to regions free of the disease in Germany (OJ No. L60, 9.3.96, p.29);

Council Decision [95/410/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0410) of 22nd June 1995 laying down the rules for the microbiological testing by sampling in the establishment of origin of poultry for slaughter intended for Finland and Sweden (OJ No. L243, 11.10.95, p.25);

Commission Decision [95/481/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0481) of 8th November 1995 amending Decision [95/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0125) on the status of France as regards infectious haematopoietic necrosis and viral haemorrhagic septicaemia (OJ No. L275, 18.11.95, p.26);

Commission Decision [95/470/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0470) of 25th October 1995 establishing the list of approved fish farms in Belgium (OJ No. L269, 11.11.95, p.28);

Commission Decision [95/473/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0473) of 27th October 1995 establishing the list of approved fish farms in France (OJ No. L269, 11.11.95, p.31);

Commission Decision [95/479/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0479) of 7th November 1995 approving the programme on infectious haemotopoietic necrosis and viral haemorrhagic septicaemia submitted by Finland (OJ No. L275, 18.11.95, p.23);

Commission Decision [95/388/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0388) of 19th September 1995 determining the specimen certificate for intra-Community trade in semen, ova and embryos of the ovine and caprine species (OJ No. L234, 3.10.95, p.30);

Commission Decision [95/483/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0483) of 9th November 1995 determining the specimen certificate for intra-Community trade in ova and embryos of swine (OJ No. L275, 18.11.95, p.30);

Commission Decision [95/461/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0461) of 27th October 1995 on protection measures in relation to Venezuelan equine encephalomyelitis in Venezuela and in Colombia (OJ No. L265, 8.11.95, p.40);

Commission Decision [95/536/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0536) of 6th December 1995 amending Commission Decisions [92/160/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1992/0160) and [93/197/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0197) in relation to the regionalization of Egypt for the imports of registered horses (OJ No. L304, 13.12.95, p.49);

Commission Decision [96/81/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0081) of 12th January 1996 amending Decisions [92/260/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1992/0260), [93/196/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0196), [93/197/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0197) and [94/467/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0467) with regard to the categories of male equidae to which apply the requirements of equine viral arteritis (OJ No. L19, 25.1.96, p.53);

Commission Decision 96/82 of 12th January 1996 amending Decisions [93/196/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0196) and [93/197/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0197) concerning animal health certificates for imports of equidae for slaughter, registered equidae and equidae for breeding and production, with regard to piroplasmosis (OJ No. L19, 25.1.96, p.56);

Commission Decision [95/352/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0352) of 25th July 1995 on animal health conditions and the certification requirements for the importation from third countries of *Crassostrea gigas* for relaying in Community waters (OJ No. L204, 30.8.95, p.13);

Commission Decision [96/185/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0185) of 16th February 1996 concerning animal health conditions and veterinary certificates for the importation of domestic animals of the bovine and porcine species from the Slovak Republic (OJ No. L59, 8.3.96, p.23);

Commission Decision [96/186/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0186) of 16th February 1996 concerning animal health conditions and veterinary certificates for the importation of domestic animals of the bovine and porcine species from the Czech Republic and revoking decision [92/324/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1992/0324) (OJ No. L59, 8.3.96, p.41).

A minor error in the principal Regulations is corrected (regulation 2), as well as several minor errors in the Schedules to the principal Regulations.

A Compliance Cost Assessment in relation to this Order has been placed in the libraries of both Houses of Parliament and copies can be obtained from Animal Health (International Trade) Division of the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, Government Buildings, Hook Rise South, Tolworth, Surbiton, Surrey KT6 7NF.
