# The Daventry and South Northamptonshire Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made: 24th March 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(3) and (4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Daventry and South Northamptonshire Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

- (2) In this Order

- “the Order” means the Daventry and South Northamptonshire Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00003]; and

- “the trust” means the trust established by the Order.

#### Amendment of the Primary Care Trust Establishment Order

###### 2

- (1) The Schedule to the Order shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

- (2) In the entry for the District of Daventry there shall be inserted after “Barby”, “and Kilsby”, and after “Weedon”, “West Haddon and Guilsborough”.

- (3) In the entry for the District of Daventry the following entries shall be deleted;

- “Boughton and Pitsford,” “Byfield,” “and West Haddon,” “Guilsborough,” “Kilsby,” “Moulton,” and “Overstone and Walgrave”.

- (4) In the entry for the District of South Northamptonshire there shall be inserted;

- after “Cosgrove,” “Cote” and “Courteenhall”,

- after “Deanshanger”, “Downs”,

- after “Grafton,” “Grange”,

- after “Salcey”, “Silverstone”,

- after “Tove”, “Towcester Brook, Towcester Mill, Whittlewood”.

- (5) In the entry for the District of South Northamptonshire the following entries shall be deleted;

- “Bugbrooke,” “Forest,” “Gayton,” “Kislingbury,” “Milton,” “Pottespury,” “Roade”, “Slapton”, and “Towcester”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Gisela Stuart — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 24th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the establishment order of the Daventry and South Northamptonshire Primary Care Trust (“the trust”), a Primary Care Trust established by Order under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977 by making changes to the area in which the trust is to undertake its functions.
