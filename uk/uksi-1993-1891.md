# The Colleges of Further Education (Changes of Names) (Scotland) Order 1993

Made: 25th July 1993

Laid before Parliament: 27th July 1993

Coming into force: 17th August 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 3(4) and 60 of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having undertaken such consultation as appeared to him to be appropriate, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Colleges of Further Education (Changes of Names) (Scotland) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 17th August 1993.

#### Changes of names of colleges of further education

###### 2

The name of each college of further education specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order is changed to the name specified opposite that name in column 2 of that Schedule, and a corresponding change is made to the name of the board of management of each college of further education so specified.

## SCHEDULE

| *Column 1* | *Column 2* |
| --- | --- |
| Aberdeen College of Further Education | Aberdeen College |
| Angus College of Further Education | Angus College |
| Dundee College of Further Education | Dundee College |
| Fife College of Technology | Fife College of Further and Higher Education |
| James Watt College | James Watt College of Further and Higher Education |
| Moray College of Further Education | Moray College |
| Perth College of Further Education | Perth College |

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State Scottish Office — 25th July 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the names of the 7 specified Colleges of Further Education. Each of the Colleges is under the control of a separate Board of Management in terms of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 and the Order therefore also changes the name of each Board of Management to reflect the new name of the College.
