# The Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 13th June 2008

Laid before Parliament: 17th June 2008

Coming into force: 1st August 2008

The Secretary of State for Transport makes the following Regulations with the consent of the Treasury in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973 and by virtue of the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988 .

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 1st August 2008.

#### Amendments

###### 2

Regulation 3 of the Goods Vehicles (Authorisation of International Journeys) (Fees) Regulations 2001 is amended as follows —

- (1) In paragraph (1)(a), for “£121” substitute “ £127. ”

- (2) In paragraph (1)(b), for “£30” substitute “ £32. ”

- (3) In paragraph (2), for “£3” substitute “ £4. ”

- (4) In paragraph (3)(a), for “£7” substitute “ £8. ”

- (5) In paragraph (3)(b), for “£12.50” substitute “ £13. ”

- (6) In paragraph (3)(c), for “£46” substitute “ £48. ”

- (7) In paragraph (4), for “£16” substitute “ £17. ”

- (8) In paragraph (5), for “£7” substitute “ £8. ”

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jim Fitzpatrick — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 5th June 2008

We consent to the making of these Regulations.

Steve McCabe — Dave Watts — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 13th June 2008
