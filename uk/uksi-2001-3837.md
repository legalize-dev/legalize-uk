# The Birmingham (Kitts Green and Shard End) Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001

Made: 28th November 2001

Laid before Parliament: 3rd December 2001

Coming into force: 1st January 2002

Whereas an application for the purpose of extending and varying an Education Action Zone has been made to the Secretary of State with a consent of the governing bodies of every school participating in the zone. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(2), 10(3) and 138(7) of the Schools Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order shall be cited as the Birmingham (Kitts Green and Shard End) Education Action Zone (Extension and Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st January 2002.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “Birmingham (Kitts Green and Shard End) Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Birmingham (Kitts Green and Shard End) Education Action Zone Order 1998[^f00002].

#### Extension of the Birmingham (Kitts Green and Shard End) Education Action Zone Order 1998

###### 3

The Birmingham (Kitts Green and Shard End) Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st January 2002.

Stephen Timms — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 28th November 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Birmingham (Kitts Green and Shard End) Education Action Zone for a further period of two years. The Order varies the partners in the Zone.
