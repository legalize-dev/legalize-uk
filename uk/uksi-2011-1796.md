# The Veterinary Surgeons (Recognition of University Degree) (Nottingham) Order of Council 2011

Made: 20th July 2011

Laid before Parliament: 21st July 2011

Coming into force: 1st September 2011

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 20th day of July 2011By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(1)(b) of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966[^f00002], their Lordships direct as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons (Recognition of University Degree) (Nottingham) Order of Council 2011 and comes into force on 1st September 2011.

#### Recognition of university degree

###### 2

Anyone on whom the University of Nottingham confers the degree of Bachelor of Veterinary Medicine and Bachelor of Veterinary Surgery (B.V.M., B.V.S.) is qualified to be a member of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons (and shall, on application in the manner and on payment of the fee prescribed by Regulations of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons made under section 11 of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966[^f00003], be registered in the Register of Veterinary Surgeons and become a member of that College).

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the recognition of the degree of Bachelor of Veterinary Medicine and Bachelor of Veterinary Surgery (B.V.M., B.V.S.) conferred by Nottingham University. Such recognition enables the holder of such a degree to be qualified to be registered as a member of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons. Membership will occur on registration, after satisfying the relevant requirements of the College as to procedure and payment of a fee.
