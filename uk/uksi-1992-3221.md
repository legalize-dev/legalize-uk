# The Value Added Tax (Flat-rate Scheme for Farmers) (Percentage Addition) Order 1992

Made: 16th December 1992

Laid before the House of Commons: 17th December 1992

Coming into force: 1st January 1993

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 37B(4) of the Value Added Tax Act 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Flat-rate Scheme for Farmers) (Percentage Addition) Order 1992, and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

###### 2

The percentage referred to in section 37B(4) of the Value Added Tax Act 1983 shall be 4 per cent.

## Signed

Irvine Patnick — Gregory Knight — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th December 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which comes into force on 1st January 1993, specifies the flat-rate percentage for the purposes of the flat-rate scheme for farmers.
