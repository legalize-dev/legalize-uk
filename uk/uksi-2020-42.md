# The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2020

Made: 20th January 2020

Coming into force: 1st April 2020

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 20th day of January 2020By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Their Lordships make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 25(1) and (2) of the Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations Order of Council 2020 and comes into force on 1st April 2020.

#### Approval

###### 2

The Regulations set out in Schedule 1 are approved.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The instruments listed in Schedule 2 are revoked.

## SCHEDULE 1 — The Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

The Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, in exercise of its powers under section 11 of the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966, makes the following Regulations.

For Part 4 (Fees payable) of the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) Regulations 2014[^f00002], substitute—

| Type of registrant | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| New graduates registered by 31 December following graduation | 182 |
| Other persons registered between 1 April and 30 September | 364 |
| Other persons registered between 1 October and 31 March | 182 |

| Type of fee | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| Normal registration fee | 364 |
| Registration fee for practice restricted to period of six months or less | 182 |
| Retention fee | 364 |

| Circumstances | Column A – Fee payable if payment received on or before 30 April (£) | Column B – Fee payable if payment received after 30 April (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard registrant | 364 | 399 |
| Overseas practising registrant | 182 | 217 |
| Non-practising registrant | 60 | 95 |

| Fee paid (£) | New circumstance | *Extra sum payable if change takes place before 1 October* *(£)* | *Extra sum payable if change takes place on or after 1 October* *(£)* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60 or 95 | Practising, but not in the British Islands | 122 | 60 |
| 60 or 95 | Practising in the British Islands | 304 | 152 |
| 182 or 217 | Practising in the British Islands | 182 | 91 |

| Fee paid (£) | New circumstance | Sum to be credited (£) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 364 or 399 | Non-practising registrant | 152 |
| 364 or 399 | Overseas practising registrant | 91 |
| 182 or 217 | Non-practising registrant | 60 |

| Type of restoration | Fee payable (£) |
| --- | --- |
| Restoration under section 11(3) of the Act following removal for non-receipt of retention fee | 364 |
| Restoration in other circumstances | 91 |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order approves the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 (“the Regulations”) made by the Council of the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons (“RCVS”) under section 11 of the Veterinary Surgeons Act [1966 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1966/36) on 10th July 2019. Those Regulations substitute Part 4 of the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) Regulations 2014 (“the 2014 Regulations”) (as set out in the Schedule to the Veterinary Surgeons and Veterinary Practitioners (Registration) Regulations Order of Council 2014 ([S.I. 2014/3493](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/3493))).

The new Part 4 of the 2014 Regulations, which takes effect from 1st April 2020, provides for an increase of approximately 7% in the fees for registration and retention of names in the register of the RCVS.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
