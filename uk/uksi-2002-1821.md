# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2002

Made: 16th July 2002

Coming into force: 17th July 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 16th July 2002 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 1(2) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in England) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 17th July 2002.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in England with effect from 17th July 2002.
