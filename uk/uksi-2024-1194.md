# The Teignbridge (Electoral Changes) Order 2024

Made: 19th November 2024

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2), (3) and (4)

Under section 92(2) of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007[^f00001], Teignbridge District Council (“*the Council*”) made recommendations in a review to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England[^f00002] for the related alteration of the boundaries between two county electoral divisions and between two district wards within the Council’s area.

The Local Government Boundary Commission for England has decided to give effect to the recommendations and makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 92(3) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Teignbridge (Electoral Changes) Order 2024.

- (2) This article comes into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

- (3) Article 2 comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on the day after the day on which this Order is made;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England[^f00003] in 2025.

- (4) Article 3 comes into force—

- (a) for the purpose of proceedings preliminary or relating to the election of councillors, on 15th October 2026;

- (b) for all other purposes, on the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2027.

- (5) This Order extends to England and Wales but applies in relation to England only.

#### Alteration of county electoral division boundaries

###### 2

The area that ceases to be part of the parish of Newton Abbot and becomes part of the parish of Ogwell by virtue of the Teignbridge District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2022[^f00004] ceases to be part of the Newton Abbot South county electoral division and becomes part of the Ashburton & Buckfastleigh county electoral division.

#### Alteration of district ward boundaries

###### 3

The area that ceases to be part of the parish of Newton Abbot and becomes part of the parish of Ogwell by virtue of the Teignbridge District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2022 ceases to be part of the College district ward and becomes part of the Ambrook district ward.

## Signed

Sealed with the seal of the Local Government Boundary Commission for England

Ailsa Irvine — Chief Executive — Local Government Boundary Commission for England — 19th November 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order gives effect to recommendations made by Teignbridge District Council (“*the Council*”) to the Local Government Boundary Commission for England for related alterations to the boundaries between two county electoral divisions and between two district wards within the Council’s area.

The alterations are a consequence of changes to parish boundaries and warding arrangements under the Teignbridge District Council (Reorganisation of Community Governance) Order 2022, which was made by the Council under powers including section 86 of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act [2007 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/28). A hard copy of that Order is available for inspection at reasonable times at the offices of Teignbridge District Council at Forde House, Brunel Road, Newton Abbot TQ12 4XX. It is also available online at: https://www.lgbce.org.uk/sites/default/files/2023-03/the_teignbridge_district_council_reorganisation_of_community_governance_order_2022.pdf.

The changes described in article 2 (in relation to county electoral divisions) apply in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2025. The changes described in article 3 (in relation to district wards) apply in respect of elections to be held on and after the ordinary day of election of councillors in England in 2027.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
