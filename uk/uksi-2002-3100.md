# The Downham and Bellingham Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

Made: 16th December 2002

Laid before Parliament: 20th December 2002

Coming into force: 10th January 2003

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Downham and Bellingham Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 10th January 2003.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In this Order—

- “Downham and Bellingham Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Downham and Bellingham Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00002].

#### The extension of the Downham and Bellingham Education Action Zone

###### 3

The Downham and Bellingham Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 10th January 2003.

## Signed

David Miliband — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 16th December 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Downham and Bellingham Education Action Zone for a further period of two years.
