# The Tayside Regional Council (Backwater and Lintrathen Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1993

Made: 21st May 1993

Coming into force: 30th June 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Tayside Regional Council (Backwater and Lintrathen Reservoirs) Byelaws Extension Order 1993 and shall come into force on 30th June 1993.

###### 2

The period during which the Tayside Regional Council (Backwater and Lintrathen Reservoirs) Byelaws 1982 are to remain in force([^f00002]) is hereby extended to 30th June 1994.

## Signed

T.D. Macdonald — Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office — 21st May 1993
