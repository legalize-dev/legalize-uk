# The Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000

Made: 3rd September 2000

The Attorney General, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3(2) of the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 (Commencement) Order 2000.

###### 2

Sections 1 and 2 of the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 shall come into force on 1 October 2000.

## Signed

Ross Cranston — Solicitor General — 3rd September 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1 October 2000 the provisions of the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000.
