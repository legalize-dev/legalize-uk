# The Child Trust Funds (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made: 12th March 2018

Laid before Parliament: 13th March 2018

Coming into force: 6th April 2018

The Treasury make these Regulations in exercise of the powers in sections 12(2) and 28(1) of the Child Trust Funds Act 2004[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Child Trust Funds (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 6th April 2018.

#### Amendment of the Child Trust Funds Regulations 2004

###### 2

The Child Trust Funds Regulations 2004[^f00002] are amended as follows.

###### 3

In regulation 9(2) and (3) (annual limit on subscriptions)[^f00003] for “£4,128”, substitute “£4,260”.

## Signed

Paul Maynard — David Rutley — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 12th March 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Child Trust Funds Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/1450](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1450)). They provide for the annual limit on subscriptions to be increased from £4,128 to £4,260 (regulation 3).

A Tax Information and Impact Note will be published on the HM Government website at https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/tax-information-and-impact-notes-tiins.
