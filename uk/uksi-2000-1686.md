# The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) and Medicated Feedingstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2000

Made: 26th June 2000

Laid before Parliament: 28th June 2000

Coming into force: 1st August 2000

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community and medicinal products, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by the said section 2(2), hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) and Medicated Feedingstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 1st August 2000.

#### Amendment of the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999

###### 2

The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999[^f00003] shall be amended by the insertion, in Schedule 5 (Methods of Analysis), Part I (Community Methods of Analysis), in Column (1) between the entries “Halofuginone ” and “Menadione (vitamin K3)”, of the entry “Lasalocid Sodium” and, in Column (2) opposite it, of the entry “The Annex to Directive [1999/76/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0076)[^f00004]”.

#### Amendment of the Medicated Feedingstuffs Regulations 1998

###### 3

In regulation 35 (recovery of fees) of the Medicated Feedingstuffs Regulations 1998[^f00005], in each of paragraphs (5), (6), (7) and (8), for the words “the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1998” wherever they appear there shall be substituted the words “the ZT Regulations”, and at the end of that regulation there shall be added the following paragraph:

> > (12) In this regulation “the ZT Regulations” means the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999 as amended by the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) and Medicated Feedingstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2000.

## Signed

Hayman — Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 26th June 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999 by introducing a Community method of analysis for the determination of lasalocid sodium in feedingstuffs, in implementation, in relation to zootechnical additives and products with zootechnical additives in them, of Commission Directive [1999/76/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0076) (OJ No. L207, 6.8.1999, p. 13).

The Feedingstuffs (Zootechnical Products) Regulations 1999 implemented, in relation to such additives and products, a number of Community instruments including in particular Council Directive [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524) (OJ No. L270, 14.12.70, p. 1, OJ/SE Vol. 18, p. 4), with amendments up to and including those effected by Council Directive [96/51/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0051) (OJ No. L235, 17.9.96, p. 39), on additives in feedingstuffs, and Council Directive [95/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0069) (OJ No. L332, 30.12.95, p. 15) which lay down the conditions and arrangements for approving and registering certain establishments and intermediaries operating in the animal feed sector.

These Regulations also bring cross-references in the Medicated Feedingstuffs Regulations 1998 up to date.
