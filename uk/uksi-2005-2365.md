# The Specified Body (Consumer Claims) Order 2005

Made: 21st August 2005

Laid before Parliament: 26th August 2005

Coming into force: 1st October 2005

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 47B(9) of the Competition Act 1998 hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Specified Body (Consumer Claims) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st October 2005.

#### Specified body

###### 2

The Consumers' Association is specified for the purposes of section 47B of the Competition Act 1998.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Consumer Affairs — Department of Trade and Industry — 21st August 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the Consumers' Association as a body able to bring a claim to which section 47A of the Competition Act 1998 applies (monetary claims before the Competition Appeal Tribunal).

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as there are no direct costs for business.
