# The Sports Grounds Safety Authority Act 2011 (Commencement) Order 2011

Made: 27th October 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(1) of the Sports Grounds Safety Authority Act 2011[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Sports Grounds Safety Authority Act 2011 (Commencement) Order 2011.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The Sports Grounds Safety Authority Act 2011 comes into force on 1st November 2011 to the extent that it is not already in force.

## Signed

Hugh Robertson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 27th October 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 1st November 2011 Part 1 of and the Schedules to the Sports Grounds Safety Authority Act [2011 (c.6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/6). The remainder of the Act was brought into force on Royal Assent by section 8(3) of that Act.
