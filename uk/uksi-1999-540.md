# The Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 3rd March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 5th March 1999

Coming into force: 31st March 1999

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Health and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in relation to England and Wales, and the Secretary of State for Scotland in relation to Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 6(4), 16(1), 17(2), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consultation in accordance with section 48(4) of that Act with such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by the Regulations, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 31st March 1999.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1995[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the principal Regulations in the definition of “the Commission Regulation”, there shall be substituted for the words “and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1298/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/1298)[^f00003]” the words “, Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1298/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/1298) and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2521/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/2521)[^f00004]”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply to Great Britain, amend the Spreadable Fats (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1995, as amended. The Regulations make provision for the enforcement and execution of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2521/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/2521) amending Regulation [(EC) No. 577/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0577) laying down certain detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2991/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1994/2991) laying down standards for spreadable fats and of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 1898/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1987/1898) on the protection of designations used in the marketing of milk and milk products.

Regulation 2521/98 adjusts the rules concerning the indication of the fat content of spreadable fats and verification of the declared fat content.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
