# The Pensions Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) and the Pension Schemes Act 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2017

Made: 11th September 2017

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (7) of the Pensions Act 2014[^f00001] and section 89(4)(a) and (5) of the Pension Schemes Act 2015[^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) and the Pension Schemes Act 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2017.

#### Commencement of section 44 (disclosure of information about transaction costs to members etc.) of the Pensions Act 2014

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2) the day appointed for the coming into force of section 44 of the Pensions Act 2014 is 18th September 2017.

- (2) In relation to section 44(1) of that Act, this regulation does not apply to a scheme which falls within paragraphs (a) to (e) of the definition of “relevant scheme” in regulation 1(2) (citation, commencement and interpretation) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Scheme Administration) Regulations 1996[^f00003].

#### Commencement of section 38 (disclosure of information about schemes) of the Pension Schemes Act 2015

###### 3

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 38(1) and (4) of the Pension Schemes Act 2015 is 18th September 2017.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Guy Opperman — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 11th September 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the eleventh commencement instrument made under the Pensions Act [2014 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/19) (“the 2014 Act”) and the second set of commencement regulations made under the Pension Schemes Act [2015 (c.8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/8) (“the 2015 Act”).

Regulation 2(1) brings into force section 44 (disclosure of information about transaction costs to members etc.) of the 2014 Act which among other things amends section 113 (disclosure of information about schemes to members etc.) of the Pension Schemes Act [1993 (c.48)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/48) (“the 1993 Act”).

This is except in relation to certain types of occupational pension scheme which are excluded by paragraphs (a) to (e) of the definition of “relevant scheme” in regulation 1 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Scheme Administration) Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/1715](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1715)) (*see* regulation 2(2)). These include executive pension schemes, certain small schemes, public service pension schemes and schemes which provide no money purchase benefits other than those attributable to additional voluntary contributions (AVCs).

The provisions inserted by Section 44 of the 2014 Act into the 1993 Act place a duty on the Secretary of State in relation to a money purchase scheme that is an occupational pension scheme to make regulations requiring the disclosure of information about transaction costs and requiring information on transaction costs and administrative charges to be published.

Section 44 also imposes a duty on the Financial Conduct Authority to make rules to similar effect in relation to certain personal pension schemes. It does so by inserting a new section 137FA into the Financial Services and Markets Act [2000 (c.8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8).

Regulation 3 brings into force section 38(1) and (4) of the 2015 Act which further amends section 113 of the 1993 Act. Section 38(4) introduces a requirement for persons to have regard to any guidance prepared from time to time by the Secretary of State when complying with disclosure requirements under that section.
