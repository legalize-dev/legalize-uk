# The Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2017

Made: 13th March 2017

Coming into force: 1st April 2017

In accordance with section 25(3) of that Act, the consultation prescribed in regulations made under that section has been completed[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2017 and comes into force on 1st April 2017.

#### Amendment of Article 4 of the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2013

###### 2

In article 4(1) of the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2013 (directors of the trust)[^f00003] for “4 executive directors” substitute “5 executive directors”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Philip Dunne — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 13th March 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2013, which established the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 increases the number of executive directors of the trust from 4 to 5.

No impact assessment has been produced for this instrument as it has no effect on private sector and civil society organisations, and no significant effect on the public sector.
