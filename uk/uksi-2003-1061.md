# The A14/U3124 Trimley Entry Slip Road (Levington Link Road) Trunking Order 2003

Made: 2nd April 2003

Coming into force: 1st May 2003

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by Section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf.

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the A14/U3124 Trimley Entry Slip Road (Levington Link Road) Trunking Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st May 2003.

- (2) In this Order, all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway.

###### 2

The length of highway described in the Schedule to this Order shall become trunk road from the date when this Order comes into force.

###### 3

The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan numbered HA 65/02/85 marked “The A14/U3124 Trimley Entry Slip Road Trunk Road Trunking Order”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at the Records Management Branch, Ashdown House, St Leonards-on-Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA.

## THE SCHEDULE — **Length of Highway Becoming Trunk Road**

The length of highway becoming trunk road is “The A14/U3124 Trimley Entry Slip Road”, 20 metres west of the centre line of the A14/U3124 Junction at Trimley St Martin in the County of Suffolk to a point where it joins the A14 westbound main carriageway, in a north-westerly direction, a distance of 527 metres.

## Signed

Signed by Authority of the Secretary of State for Transport.

W. S. C. Wadrup — A Divisional Director, Highways Agency — 2nd April 2003
