# The Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station (Correction) Order 2016

Made: 12th July 2016

Coming into force: 13th July 2016

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to the Act, makes the following Order—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station (Correction) Order 2016.

- (2) This Order comes into force on 13th July 2016.

#### Corrections

###### 2

The Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station Order 2015 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be substituted or inserted.

## SCHEDULE — CORRECTIONS

| Column (1) | Column (2) | Column (3) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where the correction is made | How the correction is made | Text substituted or inserted |
| Article 2(1), definition of “Order land” | Replace “proposed” with | “authorised” |
| Article 5(4)(c) | Replace “width or length” with | “height” |
| Schedule 2, paragraph 17(2)(a) | Replace “12” with | “14” |
| Schedule 2, paragraph 19(2) | After “construction” insert | “staff” |
| Schedule 2, paragraph 19(2) | Replace “7C” with | “7B” |
| Schedule 2, paragraph 33(2) | After “operational” insert | “staff” |
| Schedule 2, paragraph 43(6) | Replace “**Error! Reference source not found.** or (a) (transfer of part of the benefit of the Order to a street authority or to the operator of the connection to the electricity grid network)” with | “8 (transfer of the benefit of this Order)” |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Energy and Climate Change

Giles Scott — Head of Energy Infrastructure Planning and Coal Liabilities — Department of Energy and Climate Change — 12th July 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order corrects errors identified in the Ferrybridge Multifuel 2 Power Station Order 2015, which granted development consent under the Planning Act 2008, following a written request from Multifuel Energy Limited under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
