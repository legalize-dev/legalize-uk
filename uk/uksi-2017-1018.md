# The Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2017

Made: 19th October 2017

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 185(1) of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2017.

#### Provision coming into force on 25th October 2017

###### 2

Section 154 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (which relates to littering from vehicles in England) comes into force on 25th October 2017.

## Signed

Thérèse Coffey — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th October 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 154 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (which relates to littering from vehicles in England) on 25th October 2017.
