# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Comment Cablevision Worcester Limited) Order 1994

Made: 24th March 1994

Laid before Parliament: 30th March 1994

Coming into force: 28th April 1994

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Comment Cablevision Worcester Limited) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 28th April 1994.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Neil Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 24th March 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Comment Cablevision Worcester Limited a licence on 25th September 1993 under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence in the Worcester and Redditch area. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 30th March 1994. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Comment Cablevision Worcester Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 28th April 1994.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, 50 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7JJ.
