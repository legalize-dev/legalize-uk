# The Channel Tunnel (Amendment of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Import Legislation) Order 1990

Made: 29th November 1990

Laid before Parliament: 30th November 1990

Coming into force: 1st December 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with agriculture in Scotland and Wales and the Forestry Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 11(1)(d) and (2)(b) and 13(1) and (2) of the Channel Tunnel Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Channel Tunnel (Amendment of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Import Legislation) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 1st December 1990.

#### Amendment of legislation in its application to the tunnel system

###### 2

- (1) The Acts mentioned in Schedule 1 to this Order shall be subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule.

- (2) The Statutory Instruments mentioned in Schedule 2 to this Order shall be subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule.

## SCHEDULE 1 — ACTS AMENDED IN THEIR APPLICATION TO THE TUNNEL SYSTEM

## PLANT HEALTH ACT 1967 1967 c. 8

###### 1

After subsection (2) of section 2 (control of introduction of pests into Great Britain) there shall be inserted the following subsection–

> > (3) In subsection (2) above references to the landing of any article include references to its importation through the tunnel system as defined in the Channel Tunnel Act 1987. [1987 c. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/53)

###### 2

In section 6(1) (publication of orders)[^f00002] after the words “articles” there shall be inserted “or the importation of any articles into Great Britain through the tunnel system as defined in the Channel Tunnel Act 1987 [1987 c. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/53)”.

## SEA FISHERIES (SHELLFISH) ACT 1967 1967 c. 83

After subsection (4) of section 13 (power to prohibit importation of shellfish in certain cases) there shall be inserted the following subsection–

> > (5) References in this section to importation shall include references to importation through the tunnel system as defined in the Channel Tunnel Act 1987. [1987 c. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/53)

## ANIMAL HEALTH ACT 1981 1981 c. 22

###### 3

In section 61(2)(a) (control of rabies and powers of arrest) after the words “attempted landing of any animal” there shall be inserted “or importation or attempted importation through the tunnel system as defined in the Channel Tunnel Act 1987 of any animal [1987 c. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/53)”.

###### 4

In section 74(a) (liability under the customs and excise Acts) after the words “land or ship” there shall be inserted “or brings or attempts to bring through the tunnel system as defined in the Channel Tunnel Act 1987 [1987 c. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/53)”.

## ANIMAL HEALTH AND WELFARE ACT 1984 1984 c. 40

In section 10(8) (artificial breeding of livestock) after the words “landed here” there shall be inserted “or brought here through the tunnel system as defined in the Channel Tunnel Act 1987 [1987 c. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1987/53)”.
