# The Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (Commencement No. 7, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Order 2014

Made: 24th September 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 185(1) and (7) of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014[^f00001], makes the following Order.

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (Commencement No. 7, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Order 2014.

- (2) In this Order “the 2014 Act” means the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014.

#### Provisions coming into force on 20th October 2014 in relation to England

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2014 Act come into force on 20th October 2014 in relation to England—

- (a) section 94 (new ground for serious offences or breach of prohibitions etc);

- (b) section 95 (notice requirements for new ground);

- (c) section 96 (review requirements for new ground), insofar as it is not already in force;

- (d) section 97 (corresponding new ground and notice requirements for assured tenancies);

- (e) section 100(2) and (3) (restrictions where new possession proceedings in progress etc);

- (f) section 181(1) (amendments), insofar as it relates to the provisions of Schedule 11 specified in sub-paragraph (h);

- (g) Schedule 3 (Schedule to be inserted as Schedule 2A to the Housing Act 1985);

- (h) in Schedule 11 (minor and consequential amendments), paragraphs 2, 7 to 10, 13, 14, 15(4), 16, 18, 19, 47(4) and 48.

#### Provisions coming into force on 20th October 2014

###### 3

The following provisions of the 2014 Act come into force on 20th October 2014—

- (a) Part 2 (criminal behaviour orders);

- (b) Part 3 (dispersal powers);

- (c) Part 4 (community protection);

- (d) Part 6 (local involvement and accountability) to the extent not already in force;

- (e) section 181(1) (amendments), insofar as it relates to the provisions of Schedule 11 specified in sub-paragraph (g);

- (f) Schedule 4 (ASB case reviews: supplementary provision), to the extent not already in force;

- (g) in Schedule 11 (minor and consequential amendments), the following paragraphs—

- (i) paragraphs 3 to 5;

- (ii) paragraph 21;

- (iii) paragraph 24(a), insofar as it relates to the repeal of the following provisions of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00002]—

- (aa) in section 1(1A), the reference to “1C, 1CA”;

- (bb) in section 1A(2)(a), the reference to “1CA”;

- (cc) section 1C;

- (dd) section 1CA;

- (ee) section 1D(1)(c) and (d) and (6);

- (ff) section 1D(2) insofar as it relates to a case falling within subsection (1)(c) or (d);

- (gg) section 1D(4)(c) insofar as it relates to a case falling within subsection (1)(c) or (d);

- (hh) section 1I(1)(b);

- (ii) section 1J(1)(c);

- (jj) section 1K(2);

- (iv) paragraphs 25 and 26;

- (v) paragraph 30;

- (vi) paragraphs 32 to 45;

- (vii) in paragraph 50, the repeals relating to—

- (aa) the Licensing Act 2003[^f00003];

- (bb) sections 14(3)(a) and 56(1) of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003[^f00004];

- (cc) paragraph 59 of Schedule 26 to the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00005];

- (dd) paragraph 36 of Schedule 7 to the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00006];

- (ee) the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005[^f00007];

- (ff) paragraph 7 of Schedule 1 to the Drugs Act 2005[^f00008];

- (gg) sections 8(7) and 26 of the Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006[^f00009];

- (hh) section 118 of, and Schedule 20 to, the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008[^f00010];

- (ii) the Policing and Crime Act 2009[^f00011];

- (jj) the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011[^f00012];

- (kk) the Localism Act 2011[^f00013].

#### Saving provision in relation to the coming into force of Part 2

###### 4

Notwithstanding the commencement of paragraphs 25 and 26 of Schedule 11 to the 2014 Act, section 8 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 as in force immediately before the commencement of those provisions continues to apply to an anti-social behaviour order within the meaning of section 1 of that Act.

#### Transitional provision in relation to the coming into force of sections 94 and 97 in England

###### 5

In relation to the coming into force of sections 94 and 97 of the 2014 Act (absolute grounds for possession) in England, condition 5 in section 84A(7) of the Housing Act 1985[^f00014] and in Ground 7A of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Housing Act 1988[^f00015] is only met if the offence under—

- (a) section 80(4) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00016] (breach of abatement notice in relation to statutory nuisance), or

- (b) section 82(8) of that Act (breach of court order to abate statutory nuisance etc.),

## Signed

Norman Baker — Minister of State — Home Office — 24th September 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force various provisions of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (“the 2014 Act”).

Article 2 brings into force provisions relating to the recovering of possession of dwelling houses on anti-social behaviour grounds, in relation to England, as well as minor and consequential amendments relating to provisions mentioned in articles 2 and 3.

Article 3 brings into force provisions relating to criminal behaviour orders, dispersal powers, community protection and local involvement and accountability.

Article 4 makes saving provision in relation to the coming into force of Part 2 of the Act to allow for parenting orders to be made where a criminal behaviour order has been made.

Article 5 makes transitional provision in relation to the coming into force of sections 94 and 97 of the 2014 Act in England to ensure that condition 5 in the new absolute grounds for possession is only met where the offence under the Environmental Protection Act 1990 is committed on or after 20th October 2014.
