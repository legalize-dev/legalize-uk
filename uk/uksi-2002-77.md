# The Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Haslar) Order 2002

Made: 17th January 2002

Laid before Parliament: 18th January 2002

Coming into force: 8th February 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 37 of the Prison Act 1952[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Closure of Prisons (H.M. Prison Haslar) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 8th February 2002.

###### 2

The prison known as H.M. Prison Haslar and situate in the county of Hampshire shall close on the coming into force of this Order.

## Signed

Beverley Hughes — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 17th January 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the closure of H.M. Prison Haslar on 8th February 2002.
