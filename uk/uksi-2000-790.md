# The Meat Products (Hygiene) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2000

Made: 18th March 2000

Laid before Parliament: 20th March 2000

Coming into force

Make the following Regulations:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Meat Products (Hygiene) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2000; they extend to England only and come into force–

- (a) in case of all provisions other than regulation 3, on 30th March 2000; and

- (b) in the case of regulation 3, on 31st March 2000.

#### Amendment to the Meat Products (Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

###### 2

In so far it extends to England, regulation 2(10)(k) of the Meat Products (Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00004] is revoked.

#### Amendment to the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994

###### 3

- (1) The Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994[^f00005] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2) below in so far as they extend to England.

- (2) The following paragraph is substituted for paragraph 2(a) of Part IX of Schedule 2–

> > (2)
>   (a) The meat product to be used in the prepared meal shall as soon as it has been cooked–
>     (i) be mixed with the other ingredients as soon as practically possible; in that event the time during which the temperature of the meat product is between 10°C and 60°C shall not exceed two hours,
>     (ii) be refrigerated to 10°C or less before being mixed with the other ingredients, or
>     (iii) be cooled and mixed with the other ingredients in such a way (to be specified in the approval document for the establishment concerned) that the time during which the temperature of the meat product is between 10°C and 60°C is kept to a minimum;

#### Consequential amendment

###### 4

In so far as it extends to England, Schedule 2 to the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996[^f00006] (Regulations relevant to intra-Community trade) is amended by the insertion of the following reference at the end of its paragraph 5–

> > The Meat Products (Hygiene) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2000.

## Signed

Hayman — Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 15th March 2000

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Health, — Department of Health — 18th March 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations extend to England only. They amend– a the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994 (S.I. [1994/3082](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3082), as already amended); b the Meat Products (Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/683](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/683)); and c the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996 (S.I. [1996/3124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3124), as already amended), in each case in so far as they extend to England (S.I. [1994/3082](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3082), S.I. [1999/683](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/683) and S.I. [1996/3124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3124) all extend to the whole of Great Britain).

2 The effect of the amendments to S.I. [1994/3082](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3082) and S.I. [1999/683](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/683) is that in accordance with point 2(a) of Chapter IX of Annex B to Council Directive [77/99/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0099) (inserted by Council Directive [95/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0068), OJ No. L332, 30.12.95, p.10) a third method of preparation for meat-based prepared meals is permitted with effect from 31st March 2000. The amendment to S.I. [1996/3124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3124) is consequential on the above amendments and is effected in reliance on section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 ([1972 c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68).

3 No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
