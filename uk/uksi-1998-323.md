# The Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

Made: 18th February 1998

Coming into force: 2nd March 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having completed the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of that latter Act[^f00003] and consider it appropriate that the functions of the trust should include functions in relation to the provision of services for one or more Health Authorities[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 2nd March 1998.

#### Amendment of Establishment Order

###### 2

For Article 3 of the Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] there shall be substituted the following Article:—

> > (3)
>   (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>   (2) The trust’s functions shall be—
>     (a) to own and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act Friarage Hospital, Northallerton, North Yorkshire DL6 1JG and associated hospitals, and there to provide hospital accommodation and services;
>     (b) to manage pursuant to section 5(1)(a) of the Act community health services provided from Friarage Hospital, Northallerton, North Yorkshire DL6 1JG and to own the associated premises;
>     (c) to provide and manage pursuant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act The Friary, Victoria Road, Richmond, North Yorkshire DL10 4DW and The Friary Lodge, 4 Victoria Road, Richmond, North Yorkshire DL10 4DW and there to provide hospital accommodation and services; and
>     (d) to manage pursant to section 5(1)(b) of the Act community health services provided from The Friary, Victoria Road, Richmond, North Yorkshire DL10 4DW and The Friary Lodge, 4 Victoria Road, Richmond, North Yorkshire DL10 4DW.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Alan Milburn — Minister of State — Department of Health — 18th February 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Order (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Northallerton Health Services National Health Service Trust.

The Establishment Order is amended to confer on the trust the purpose specified in section 5(1)(b) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 and, in pursuance of that purpose, extends the functions of the trust to include the function of providing and managing The Friary, Victoria Road, Richmond, North Yorkshire DL10 4DW and The Friary Lodge, 4 Victoria Road, Richmond, North Yorkshire DL10 4DW.
