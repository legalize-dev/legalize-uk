# The Gaming (Small Charges) (Amendment) Order 1995

Made: 30th June 1995

Laid before Parliament: 7th July 1995

Coming into force: 1st August 1995

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 40(2) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Small Charges) (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st August 1995.

- (2) This Order does not extend to Scotland.

###### 2

Article 2(a) of the Gaming (Small Charges) Order 1992[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the words “the sum of £6” the words “the sum of £15”.

## Signed

Home Office

Michael Forsyth — Minister of State. — 30th June 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases from £6 to £15 the maximum daily charge which amy be made in clubs and miners' welfare institutes in respect of a person taking part in gaming when the only games played are bridge and whist.
