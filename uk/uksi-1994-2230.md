# The Brancaster Staithe Fishery (Variation) Order 1994

Made: 12th August 1994

Laid before Parliament: 30th August 1994

Coming into force: 20th September 1994

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by the said section 1 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Title, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Brancaster Staithe Fishery (Variation) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 20th September 1994.

- (2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Brancaster Staithe Fishery Order 1979.

#### Variation of the principal Order

###### 2

The principal Order shall be varied in accordance with article 3 of this Order.

###### 3

In article 4 of the principal Order for the words “for 15 years from the date of its coming into operation” there shall be substituted the words “for 30 years from the date of its coming into operation”.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on

William Waldegrave — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 12th August 1994.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the duration of the Brancaster Staithe Fishery Order 1979 for a period of 15 years.
