# The Cadishead Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006

Made: 27th August 2006

Laid before Parliament: 8th September 2006

Coming into force: 30th September 2006

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cadishead Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 30th September 2006.

#### Exemption from the School Session Regulations

###### 2

Regulation 3(a) of the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002] shall not apply to the governing body in relation to the Cadishead Primary School[^f00003] of Allotment Road, Cadishead, Manchester M44 5JD.

#### Time period

###### 3

This Order shall have effect until 31st January 2007.

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 27th August 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that regulation 3(a) of the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 does not apply to the governing body of Cadishead Primary School. This will allow the governing body to make alterations to the school day at the start of the school term in January, rather than at the beginning of the school year. Article 3 specifies that the Order shall have effect until 31st January 2007.
