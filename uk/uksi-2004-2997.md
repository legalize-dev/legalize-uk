# The Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2004

Made: 16th November 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 170(4) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (Commencement No. 16) Order 2004.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Section 164 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 shall come into force on 1st December 2004.

## Signed

Des Browne — Minister of State — 16th November 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st December 2004 section 164 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.
