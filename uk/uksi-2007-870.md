# The Lottery Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 15th March 2007

Laid before the House of Commons: 16th March 2007

Coming into force: 6th April 2007

The Commissioners for Revenue and Customs[^f00001] make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 27(2) of the Finance Act 1993[^f00002]:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Lottery Duty (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 6th April 2007.

###### 2

In regulation 8 of the Lottery Duty Regulations 1993[^f00003] (payment of lottery duty in respect of the National Lottery), for “body corporate” substitute “person”.

## Signed

Paul Gray — Mike Eland — Two of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs — 15th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Regulation 2 amends regulation 8 of the Lottery Duty Regulations 1993 ([S.I. 1993/3212](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3212)) such that lottery duty payable in respect of the National Lottery[^f00004] shall be paid by the person licensed[^f00005] to run that lottery instead of by the promoter.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.

Sections 1 and 6 were amended as of 1st October 2006 by sections 3(a) and 3(c) respectively of the National Lottery Act [2006 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/23) and article 2(a) of [S.I. 2006/2630 (C. 89)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2630).
