# The Retained Organs Commission (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made: 10th January 2002

Laid before Parliament: 11th January 2002

Coming into force: 1st February 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 11(4) and 126(4) of, and paragraph 12 of Schedule 5 to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Retained Organs Commission (Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 1st February 2002.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England only.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 3 of the Retained Organs Commission Regulations 2001[^f00002] (disqualification for appointment) sub-paragraph (g) of paragraph (1) is deleted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hazel Blears — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 10th January 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 3 of the Retained Organs Commission Regulations 2000 to remove the disqualification of the chairmen and members of primary care trusts from serving as the chairman or a non-officer member of the Retained Organs Commission.
