# The Local Government and Rating Act 1997 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1998

Made: 12th March 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 34(1) of the Local Government and Rating Act 1997[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government and Rating Act 1997 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1998.

#### Commencement of minor and consequential amendments and repeals

###### 2

The following provisions of the Local Government and Rating Act 1997 shall come into force on 18th March 1998—

- (a) section 33, in so far as it relates to the provisions mentioned in paragraphs (b) and (c) below;

- (b) in Schedule 3, paragraph 17; and

- (c) in Schedule 4, the repeals in the Local Government Act 1972[^f00002].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Nick Raynsford — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 12th March 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 18th March 1998 paragraph 17 of Schedule 3 to the Local Government and Rating Act 1997 (amendment of section 36 of the Representation of the People Act 1983) and minor repeals in the Local Government Act 1972.
