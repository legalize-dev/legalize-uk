# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) (Partial Revocation) Order 1998

Made: 19th January 1998

Laid before Parliament: 20th January 1998

Coming into force: 21st January 1998

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 1(1) and (2) and section 24(1) and (3) of the said Act[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) (Partial Revocation) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 21st January 1998.

#### Partial Revocation

###### 2

- (1) The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) Order 1991 is hereby revoked—

- (a) with respect to the designated area numbered 1A in Schedule 1 to that Order;

- (b) with respect to that part of the designated area numbered 2 in Schedule 1 to that Order which is set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order;

- (c) with respect to that part of the designated area numbered 11 in Schedule 1 to that Order which is set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) In consequence of that revocation that Order shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in article 6(1)(a)(i) and 6(1)(b)(i) the word “1A,” shall be deleted;

- (b) for paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 there shall be substituted the paragraph set out in Part III of the Schedule to this Order; and

- (c) for paragraph 11 of Schedule 1 there shall be substituted the paragraph set out in Part IV of the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further partially revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Radioactivity in Sheep) (Wales) Order 1991 (“the principal Order”) which contains emergency prohibitions restricting various activities in order to prevent human consumption of food which has been or which may have been rendered unsuitable for that purpose in consequence of the escape of radioactive substances from a nuclear reactor situated at Chernobyl in the Ukraine.

This partial revocation reduces two designated areas in the County of Gwynedd subject to the restrictions set out in the principal Order and also provides that a designated area in the County of Powys is no longer subject to the said restrictions.

Maps showing the areas which are designated by the principal Order and the area derestricted by this Order are available for inspection during normal office hours at the offices of the Welsh Office Agriculture Department at Penrallt, Caernarfon, LL55 1EP; Government Buildings, Spa Road East, Llandrindod Wells, Powys LD1 5HA and Cathays Park, Cardiff CF1 3NQ.
