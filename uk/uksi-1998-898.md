# The Local Government Staff Commission (England) (Winding Up) Order 1998

Made: 24th March 1998

Laid before Parliament: 6th April 1998

Coming into force: 12th May 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 23(1) and (4) of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Staff Commission (England) (Winding Up) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 12th May 1998.

#### Winding Up of Staff Commission

###### 2

The Local Government Staff Commission (England) is wound up and its rights and liabilities (if any) shall transfer to and vest in the Secretary of State.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Local Government Staff Commission (England) Order 1993[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hilary Armstrong — Minister of State — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 24th March 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order winds up the Local Government Staff Commission (England) (“the Staff Commission”) which was established in connection with the review of the structure of English local government. Any rights and liabilities of the Staff Commission are transferred to the Secretary of State. The order which established the Staff Commission is revoked.
