# The Watford and South of St Albans – Redbourn – Kidney Wood, Luton (Special Road Scheme 1957) (Park Street to Beechtrees Partial Revocation) Scheme 2004 Revocation Scheme 2004

Made: 12th November 2004

Coming into force: 16th December 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Scheme in exercise of powers conferred by section 16 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

This scheme may be cited as the Watford and South of St Albans − Redbourn − Kidney Wood, Luton (Special Road Scheme 1957) (Park Street to Beechtrees Partial Revocation) Scheme 2004 Revocation Scheme 2004 and shall come into force on 16th December 2004.

###### 2

The Watford and South of St Albans−Redbourn−Kidney Wood, Luton (Special Road Scheme 1957) (Park Street to Beechtrees Partial Revocation) Scheme 2004[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of theSecretary of State for Transport

I. Scholey — A Divisional Director Highways Agency — 12th November 2004
