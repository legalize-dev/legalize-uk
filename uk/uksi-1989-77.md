# The Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1989

Made: 20th January 1989

Laid before Parliament: 27th January 1989

Coming into force: 1st April 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5(1) and 43(1) of the Fire Precautions Act 1971[^f00001], I hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

###### 2

An application for a fire certificate in respect of any premises shall be in the form specified in the Schedule to these Regulations or a form to the like effect.

###### 3

The Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1976[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE — FIRE PRECAUTIONS ACT 1971, s.5

#### APPLICATION FOR A FIRE CERTIFICATE

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 20th January 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations supersede the Fire Precautions (Application for Certificate) Regulations 1976 and prescribe a new form of application for a fire certificate under the Fire Precautions Act 1971. The new form in the Schedule to these Regulations makes changes in the information required by paragraph 3 of the form as to the number of persons at work in the premises to which the application relates and requires details to be given in paragraph 8 of the fire-fighting equipment available for use in the premises.
