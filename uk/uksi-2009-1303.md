# The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2009

Made: 20th May 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 241(3) and (8) of the Planning Act 2008[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2009.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the Planning Act 2008.

#### Provisions coming into force on 23rd June 2009 in relation to England

###### 2

The following provisions of the Act (so far as they are not yet in force) shall come into force, in relation to England on 23rd June 2009—.

- (a) sections 188 and

- (b) section 238 and Schedule 13, so far as they give effect to the repeals specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, on 23rd June 2009, the following provisions of the Planning Act 2008, in relation to England, so far as they are not already in force —

section 188 (local development orders: removal of requirement to implement policies); and

section 238 and Schedule 13 so far as they give effect to the repeals specified in the Schedule to this Order, which relate to local development orders.
