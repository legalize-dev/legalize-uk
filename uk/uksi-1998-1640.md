# The South West Regional Flood Defence Committee Order 1998

Made: 7th July 1998

Coming into force: 8th July 1998

Now, therefore, the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him[^f00002] by section 16(5) and (8) of the Environment Act 1995 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the South West Regional Flood Defence Committee Order 1998 and shall come into force on 8th July 1998.

- (2) In this Order “the committee” means the South West Regional Flood Defence Committee, being the regional flood defence committee established by section 14 of the Environment Act 1995 for which the councils of the counties specified in the Schedule to this Order are the constituent councils.

#### Number of members of the committee to be appointed by each constituent council

###### 2

The number of members to be appointed to the committee by the council of any county[^f00003] specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order shall be the number specified in the corresponding entry in column (2) of that Schedule.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The South West Regional Flood Defence Committee Order 1993[^f00004] is revoked.

| Cornwall | 3 |

## Signed

Elliot Morley — Parliamentary Secretary Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 7th July 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the number of members of the regional flood defence committee known as the South West Regional Flood Defence Committee who are to be appointed by each constituent council. The order takes account of changes in local government responsibilities which took effect on 1st April 1998. Regional flood defence committees of the Environment Agency were established by section 14 of the Environment Act 1995, replacing committees of the National Rivers Authority. The corresponding order for the former committee is revoked.
