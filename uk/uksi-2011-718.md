# The Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made: 10th March 2011

Coming into force: 6th April 2011

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 113B(1)(b) of the Police Act 1997[^f00001] as extended by the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records) (Guernsey) Order 2009[^f00002] makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement, interpretation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and shall come into force on 6th April 2011.

- (2) In these Regulations “the 2009 Regulations” mean the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) Regulations 2009[^f00003].

- (3) These Regulations extend to Guernsey.

#### Amendment of regulation 4 of the 2009 Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 4(b) of the 2009 Regulations (fees for criminal record certificates and enhanced criminal record certificates) for “£36” substitute “£44”.

## Signed

Lynne Featherstone — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 10th March 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police Act 1997 (Criminal Records and Registration) (Guernsey) Regulations 2009 by increasing the fee for an application made under section 113B of the Police Act 1997 for an enhanced criminal records certificate from £36 to £44 with effect from 6th April 2011. No fee is payable in relation to an application made by a volunteer under this section.
