# The National Endowment for Science, Technology and the Arts (Increase of Endowment) Order 2006

Made: 16th February 2006

Coming into force: 17th February 2006

In accordance with section 19(8) of the National Lottery Act 1998, a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Endowment for Science, Technology and the Arts (Increase of Endowment) Order 2006 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Increase of endowment

###### 2

For the purpose of increasing the amount of the endowment of the National Endowment for Science, Technology and the Arts (“NESTA”), the Secretary of State is to be permitted to pay to NESTA out of the money held in the National Lottery Distribution Fund and allocated, in accordance with section 22(3)(f) of the National Lottery etc. Act 1993[^f00003], for expenditure on or connected with health, education or the environment five sums of £15,000,000 each.

## Signed

James Purnell — Minister of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 16th February 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order permits the Secretary of State to increase the amount of the endowment of the National Endowment for Science, Technology and the Arts. For this purpose, the Order enables the Secretary of State to pay to NESTA £75,000,000 out of the money held in the National Lottery Distribution Fund and allocated for expenditure on or connected with health, education or the environment.
