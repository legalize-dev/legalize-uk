# The Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) (Amendment) Order 1995

Made: 8th March 1995

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 1995

Coming into force: 31st March 1995

NOW, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) and section 77(6) of the Road Traffic Act 1991 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) (Amendment) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 31st March 1995.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 1984 Act” means the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002];

- “the 1991 Act” means the Road Traffic Act 1991;

- “the excluded areas” means the areas in the royal borough of Kingston upon Thames referred to in the Schedule to this Order; and

- “the principal order” means the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) Order 1994[^f00003].

#### Exclusions from special parking area

###### 3

- (1) The excluded areas shall cease to form part of the special parking area designated by article 3(1) of the principal order.

- (2) The modifications of the 1984 and 1991 Acts contained in articles 4 and 5 of the principal order shall cease to have effect in relation to the excluded areas.

#### Amendment of Schedule to the principal order

###### 4

- (1) The Schedule to the principal order shall be amended as follows.

- (2) For paragraph 10 there shall be substituted—

> > (10) Haycroft Road between its junction with Hook Road and a point 6.5m west of that junction.

- (3) For paragraph 13 there shall be substituted—

> > (13) Hook Road—
>   (a) (west side) between the southern kerbline of Gladstone Road and a point 8.5m south of the common boundary of 3 and 4 King Edward Drive; and
>   (b) (east side) between a point 2.5m south of the northern flank wall of 177 Hook Road and a point 8.5m south of the common boundary of 3 and 4 King Edward Drive, including the circulatory carriageway of Hook roundabout.

- (4) For paragraph (iv) of paragraph 21(b) there shall be substituted—

> > (iv) (service road fronting 1 to 67 Robin Hood Way) between its junction with Robin Hood Way (A3) and a point 2m north of the southern flank wall of 23 Robin Hood Way.

## SCHEDULE — AREAS IN THE ROYAL BOROUGH OF KINGSTON UPON THAMES CEASING TO FORM PART OF THE SPECIAL PARKING AREA

###### 1

The areas to be transferred from the royal borough of Kingston upon Thames to the county of Surrey and the district of Elmbridge and referred to in article 3(1) and (2) of the Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) Order 1994[^f00004].

###### 2

The areas to be transferred from the royal borough of Kingston upon Thames to the county of Surrey and the district of Epsom and referred to in article 4(1) of the Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) Order 1994.

###### 3

The areas to be transferred from the royal borough of Kingston upon Thames to the county of Surrey and the district of Mole Valley and referred to in article 5(1) of the Greater London and Surrey (County and London Borough Boundaries) Order 1994.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Steven Norris — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 8th March 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Special Parking Area) (Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames) Order 1994 (“the principal order”) by excluding from the special parking area designated by that order certain areas which are to be transferred to the county of Surrey in accordance with local government boundary changes. The consequential modifications made by the principal order to the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 and the Road Traffic Act 1991 cease to apply to the excluded areas. Minor amendments are made to the list of roads excluded from the special parking area as set out in the Schedule to the principal order.
