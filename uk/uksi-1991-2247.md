# The Dorset, Hampshire, West Sussex and Wiltshire (County Boundaries) Order 1991

Made: 7th October 1991

Laid before Parliament: 15th October 1991

Coming into force—

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 51(2) and 67(4) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order which gives effect with modifications to proposals submitted to him under sections 47, 48(1) and 51(1) of that Act, more than six weeks before the making of this Order by the Local Government Boundary Commission for England:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Dorset, Hampshire, West Sussex and Wiltshire (County Boundaries) Order 1991.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on 5th November 1991 for any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976[^f00002].

- (3) For all other purposes, this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1992.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “district” means borough or district, as the case may be;

- “map” means one of the 14 maps prepared by the Department of the Environment, marked “Maps of the Dorset, Hampshire, West Sussex and Wiltshire (County Boundaries) Order 1991”, duly numbered for reference, and deposited in accor dance with regulation 5 of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976;

- any reference to an electoral division is a reference to an electoral division of the county to which an area is transferred;

- any reference to a ward is a reference to a ward of the district to which an area is transferred; and any reference to Dorset, Hampshire, West Sussex or Wiltshire is a reference to the county of that name.

#### Town and country planning

###### 9

- (1) In this article, “provision” means a provision of a development plan (as defined in section 336 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00003]

- (2) Any provision applying to an area transferred by this Order shall continue to apply to that area, notwithstanding the transfer, until replaced by a provision which so applies, and which is expressed to supersede the preserved provision.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers areas between the counties of Dorset, Hampshire, West Sussex and Wiltshire. Consequential changes are made to districts, parishes, local government electoral areas, petty sessional divisions and coroner’s districts.

The map forming part ot this note (page 7) gives a general guide to the areas affected. Prints of the detailed boundary maps (described in article 2) may be inspected at the offices of the council for each of the boroughs and districts whose area is altered (those boroughs and districts being Chichester, Christchurch, East Dorset, East Hampshire, Havant, Kennet, New Forest, Salisbury and Test Valley), and at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment located in 2, Marsham Street, London SW1P 3EB.

The areas transferred have approximately the following areas and populations—

| *Areas transferred—* | *Area (hectares)* | *Population* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| From Dorset to Hampshire | 26,548 | 31 |
| From Hampshire to Dorset | 18.6 | 260 |
| From Hampshire to Wiltshire | 940.46 | 2,336 |
| From Wiltshire to Hampshire | 1.27 | 6 |
| From Hampshire to West Sussex | 39.1 | 31 |
| From West Sussex to Hampshire | 10.00 | 148 |

The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976 contain general provisions regarding the effect of orders such as the present one.
