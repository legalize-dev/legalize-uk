# The Companies (Forms) Regulations 1991

Made: 25th March 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on himby sections 190(5), 353(2) and 744[^f00001]of the Companies Act 1985[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Regulations1991.

###### 2

With effect from 1st May 1991 form 363s in Schedule 2 to theCompanies (Forms Amendment No. 2 and Company’s Type and PrincipalBusiness Activities) Regulations 1990[^f00003] is a form prescribed for the purposes of sections 190(5)and 353(2) of the Companies Act 1985, but only where such a form hasbeen delivered to a company by the registrar of companies for thepurposes of an annual return by that company.

## Signed

David Durham — A Grade 4 Officer in the — Department of Trade and Industry — 25th March 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations enable companies to use annual return form 363s,which was prescribed by the Companies (Forms Amendment No. 2 andCompany’s Type and Principal Business Activities) Regulations 1990, tonotify:

- (a) the place where any register of debenture holders or duplicate ofsuch a register is kept, and any change in that place, and

- (b) the place where the register of members is kept and any change inthat place.
