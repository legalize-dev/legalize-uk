# The Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

Made: 28th January 1997

Laid before Parliament: 6th February 1997

Coming into force: 28th February 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 2 of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 28th February 1997.

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1 to the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1992[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Schedule 1 to the Motor Vehicles (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1992 and are made in consequence of Commission Directives [89/491/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0491), [96/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0020), [96/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0044), [95/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0054), [95/56/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0056), [96/37/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0037), [96/38/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0038), [96/64/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0064), [96/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0036), [95/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0048), [95/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0028) and Directives [96/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0069), [96/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0001) and 96/27 which were made by the European Parliament and Council.

Directive [89/491/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0491) amends Council Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157) in relation to permitted sound levels and exhaust systems of motor vehicles, Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) in relation to emissions from motor vehicles, Council Directive [72/245/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0245) relating to the suppression of radio interference on motor vehicles, [72/306/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0306) in relation to emissions from diesel engines, [80/1268/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/1268) in relation to fuel consumption of motor vehicles and [80/1269/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/1269) in relation to the engine power of motor vehicles.

Directive [96/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0020) further amends Council Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157) in relation to permitted sound levels and exhaust systems of motor vehicles.

Directive [96/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0044) further amends Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) in relation to emissions from motor vehicles.

Directive [95/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0054) amends Council Directive [72/245/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0245) the suppression of radio interference on motor vehicles.

Directive [95/56/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0056) amends Council Directive [74/61/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0061) in relation to anti-theft devices.

Directive [96/37/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0037) further amends Council Directive [74/408/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1974/0408) in relation to the strength of seats and their anchorages in motor vehicles.

Directive [96/38/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0038) further amends Council Directive [76/115/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0115) in relation to seat belt anchorages for motor vehicles.

Directive [96/64/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0064) amends Council Directive [77/389/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0389) in relation to motor vehicle towing devices.

Directive [96/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0036) further amends Council Directive [77/541/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0541) in relation to seat belts and restraint systems of motor vehicles.

Directive [95/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0048) amends Council Directive [92/21/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0021) in relation to masses and dimensions of category M1 motor vehicles.

Directive [95/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0028) sets out requirements in relation to the burning behaviour of materials used in the interior construction of certain categories of motor vehicles. The directive provides that Member States may not refuse EEC type approval or national type approval for vehicles which have complied with the requirements.

Directive [96/69/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0069) further amends Council Directive [70/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0220) in relation to emissions from motor vehicles.

Directive [96/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0001) amends Council Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077) in relation to emissions from diesel engines for use in vehicles.

Directive [96/27/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0027) provides that from 1st October 1998 Member States may no longer grant EC-type approval for motor vehicles which do not comply with requirements of the directive, in relation to the protection of occupants in the event of side impact resistance. From 1st October 2003 Member States must consider certificates of conformity which accompany new vehicles to be no longer valid if they do not certify that there has been compliance with the requirements.

Copies of EEC and EC Directives which are relevant to these Regulations can be obtained from Her Majesty’s Stationery Office. The details of the Directives are set out below.
