# The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Supply, Sale and Export of Petroleum and Petroleum Products) (Revocation) Regulations 2001

Made: 8th January 2001

Laid before Parliament: 8th January 2001

Coming into force: 31st January 2001

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to the interruption or reduction in part or completely, of economic relations with one or more countries which are not Member States, in exercise of powers conferred on him by that section, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Supply, Sale and Export of Petroleum and Petroleum Products) (Revocation) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 31st January 2001.

###### 2

The following Regulations shall be revoked:

- (a) The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Supply, Sale and Export of Petroleum and Petroleum Products) (Penalties and Licences) Regulations 1999[^f00003].

- (b) The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Supply, Sale and Export of Petroleum and Petroleum Products) (Penalties and Licences) (No. 3) Regulations 1999[^f00004].

## Signed

Kim Howells, — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, for Competition and Consumer Affairs, — Department of Trade and Industry — 8th January 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2228/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2228) (OJ No. L255, 9.10.2000, p. 3) repealed Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2111/99](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/2111) (OJ No. L 258, 5.10.1999, p. 12) which prohibited the supply, sale and export of specified petroleum and petroleum products to certain parts of the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia.

The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Supply, Sale and Export of Petroleum Products) (Penalties and Licences) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/1516](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1516)) and The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Supply, Sale and Export of Petroleum and Petroleum Products) (Penalties and Licences) (No. 3) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/2821](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/2821)) applied Council Regulation 900/99 (OJ No. L114, 1.5.99, p. 1) and 2111/99 respectively in the United Kingdom, to the extent required by those Council Regulations. As a consequence of the repeal of Council Regulations 900/99 and 2111/99 these Regulations revoke the implementing Regulations.
