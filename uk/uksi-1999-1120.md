# The National Health Service Trusts (Wales) (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made: 11th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1), and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Wales) (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Dissolution of NHS Trusts

###### 2

Each of the National Health Service Trusts specified in Schedule 1 is hereby dissolved and the Orders specified in column (1) of Schedule 2 are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the National Health Service Trusts set out in Schedule 1 and for the revocation of the relevant establishment orders and related amendment orders set out in Schedule 2.
