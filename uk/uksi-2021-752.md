# The Pension Schemes Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2021

Made: 23rd June 2021

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 131(1) and (4) of the Pension Schemes Act 2021[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pension Schemes Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

#### Provisions coming into force on 25th June 2021

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Pension Schemes Act 2021 is 25th June 2021—

- (a) section 116 (minor and consequential amendments) and paragraphs 2 and 12(1) of Schedule 7 (amendments to the Pensions Act 2004), only insofar as they relate to paragraph 12(2) of that Schedule (amendment to section 316 of the Pensions Act 2004); and

- (b) paragraph 12(2) of that Schedule.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Mims Davies — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Work and Pensions — 23rd June 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These are the second commencement regulations made under the Pension Schemes Act [2021 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/1) (“the Act”).

Regulation 2 corrects by bringing into force on 25th June 2021 paragraph 12(2) of Schedule 7 to the Act (amendment to section 316 of the Pensions Act 2004) and section 116 (minor and consequential amendments) and paragraphs 2 and 12(1) of Schedule 7 (amendments to the Pensions Act 2004), insofar as they relate to paragraph 12(2).

Paragraph 12(2) of Schedule 7 to the Act amends section 316 of the Pensions Act [2004 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/35) (Parliamentary control of subordinate legislation) so as to include regulations under section 38E(2)(a) (section 38 contribution notice: constitution of resources of employer) and 38E(2)(b) (section 38 contribution notice: valuation of resources of employer) in the list of regulations and orders under the Pensions Act 2004 which are subject to the affirmative procedure.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
