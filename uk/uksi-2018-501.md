# The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Amendment) Order 2018

Made: 16th April 2018

Coming into force: 1st June 2018

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Amendment) Order 2018; it applies in England and comes into force on 1st June 2018.

#### Amendments to the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 2011

###### 2

- (1) The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 2011[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) omit "“BPEX” means the division of the Agriculture and Horticulture Development Board with responsibility for the pig industry in England”;

- (b) after the definition of "CPH number" insert “"the database operator” means the operator nominated by the Secretary of State under article 12(A1);”.

- (3) In article 12 (movement of pigs other than movements to markets or collection centres)—

- (a) before paragraph 1 insert—

> > (A1) The Secretary of State shall nominate a person to operate a computerised central database to record movements of pigs.

- (b) in paragraph (1) for “BPEX” substitute “the database operator”;

- (c) in paragraph (2)—

- (i) for “BPEX”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “the database operator”;

- (ii) for “BPEX movement recording system” substitute “central database”;

- (d) in paragraph (3)—

- (i) for “BPEX” substitute “the database operator”;

- (ii) for “MLCSL”, in both places it occurs, substitute “the database operator”;

- (e) in paragraph (4) for “MLCSL” substitute “the database operator”;

- (f) in paragraph 5 for “BPEX” substitute “the database operator”;

- (g) in paragraph 6 omit "“BPEX movement recording system” means the electronic movement recording system maintained for the purpose of notifications under this order by BPEX and”.

- (4) In article 14 (Movement of pigs to markets or collection centres) for “BPEX” substitute “the database operator”.

- (5) In article 16(2) (Movement of pigs from shows) for “BPEX or MLCSL” substitute “the database operator”.

- (6) In article 17(2) (Transport of pigs) for “BPEX” substitute “the database operator”.

- (7) In article 18 (Arrival of pigs at a holding)—

- (a) in paragraph 2—

- (i) in sub-paragraph (a) for “record on the BPEX pig movement system” substitute “notify the database operator electronically of”;

- (ii) in sub-paragraph (b) for “MLCSL” substitute “the database operator”.

- (8) In article 19 (Export of Pigs) for “MLCSL” substitute “the database operator”.

## Signed

Gardiner of Kimble — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 16th April 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) Order 2011 ([S.I. 2011/2154](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/2154)). It removes the requirement for movements to be reported to BPEX or MLCSL and replaces it with a requirement to notify movements to a person nominated by the Secretary of State.
