# The Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2018

Made: 12th September 2018

The Treasury make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(5) and (9)(b)(i) of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2018.

#### Appointed Days

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act 2018 is—

- (a) 1st October 2018 for section 14 (levy under FSMA 2000 for debt advice expenses of devolved authorities); and

- (b) 1st January 2019 for section 20 (FCA general rules: information about the availability of guidance).

## Signed

Craig Whittaker — Paul Maynard — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 12th September 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Regulation 2 of these Regulations commences section 14 of the Financial Guidance and Claims Act [2018 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/10) on 1st October 2018, and section 20 of that Act on 1st January 2019.
