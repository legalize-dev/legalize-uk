# The Social Security (Incapacity for Work) (General) Amendment Regulations 1998

Made: 25th February 1998

Coming into force: 6th April 1998

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Social Security, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 171D, 171G(2) and 175(2) to (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002]and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals to make these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00003], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Incapacity for Work) (General) Amendment Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 6th April 1998.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Incapacity for Work) (General) Regulations 1995

###### 2

In regulation 17 of the Social Security (Incapacity for Work) (General) Regulations 1995[^f00004] (exempt work), in sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph (2), for “£46.50” there shall be substituted “£48.00”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Frank Field — Minister of State, — Department of Social Security — 25th February 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Social Security (Incapacity for Work) (General) Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/311](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/311)) so that the weekly earnings limit which must not be exceeded if work is to be treated as exempt work is increased from £46.50 to £48.00.

These Regulations do not impose a charge on business.
