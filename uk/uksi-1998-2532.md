# The Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (Commencement No.8) Order 1998

Made: 6th October 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 184(2) of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (Commencement No.8) Order 1998.

#### Day appointed

###### 2

30th June 1999 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 103 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994.

## Signed

Sewel — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 6th October 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for commencement on 30th June 1999 of section 103 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994. This provision inserts sections 37A and 37B into the Sewerage (Scotland) Act 1968 which require sewerage authorities to maintain a register for the purposes of Part II of that Act and allow information to be excluded from the register in certain circumstances.
