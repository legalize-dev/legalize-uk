# The Plant Health (Great Britain) (Amendment) Order 1995

Made: 23rd May 1995

Laid before Parliament: 26th May 1995

Coming into force: 27th May 1995

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food in relation to England, the Secretary of State for Scotland in relation to Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales in relation to Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 2, 3 and 4(1) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001], as read with section 20 of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972[^f00002], and now vested in them[^f00003] and of all other powers in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Great Britain) (Amendment) Order 1995, shall apply to Great Britain and shall come into force on 27th May 1995.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993

###### 2

The Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with articles 3 to 18 below.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993 so as to implement—

- (a) the requirements of Commission Directive [93/106/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0106) (OJ No. L298, 3.12.93, p.34), which makes changes in Commission Directive [92/76/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0076) (OJ No. L305, 21.10.92, p.12), and Commission Directive [93/110/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0110) (OJ No. L303, 10.12.93, p.19), which makes changes in Council Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093) (OJ No. L26, 31.1.77, p.20) relating to certain protected zones, and

- (b) Commission Directive [95/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0004) (OJ No. L44, 28.2.95, p.56), which makes changes in Council Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093) relating to the disease Pseudomonas solanacearum and the pest Thrips palmi,

and to make provisions consequential upon the Accession of Austria, Finland and Sweden to the plant health single market on 1 January 1995.

The definition of “plant” in article 2(1) is amended and certain deletions are made of items in the Schedules relating to forest trees to eliminate any overlap with the Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/1283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1283)).

An inspector may now require items (whether or not suspected of contamination) to be removed after importation to premises specified by him. This is to facilitate inspection (Article 9).

Minor corrections and improvements are also made.
