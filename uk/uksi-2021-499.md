# The British Museum (Authorised Repositories) Order 2021

Made: 20th April 2021

Laid before Parliament: 4th May 2021

Coming into force: 1st June 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section10(2) of the British Museum Act 1963[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002], with the agreement of the Trustees of the British Museum.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the British Museum (Authorised Repositories) Order 2021 and comes into force on 1st June 2021.

#### Amendments to Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the British Museum Act 1963

###### 2

In Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the British Museum Act 1963 (sites of authorised repositories – British Museum)[^f00003]—

- (a) omit paragraph 3; and

- (b) at the end insert—

> > (9) The site in Shinfield lying to the east of Cutbush Lane East, known as the British Museum Archaeological Research Collection, Cutbush Lane East, Shinfield, Reading, RG2 9AA.

## Signed

Caroline Dinenage — Minister of State for Digital and Culture — Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport — 20th April 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order creates a new authorised repository for the collections of the British Museum, by adding a further site to those already listed in Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the British Museum Act 1963, namely the site known as the British Museum Archaeological Research Collection in Shinfield, Reading. It also removes from Part 1 of Schedule 3 the site of an authorised repository (namely 6 Burlington Gardens, in London) which is no longer used by the Museum.
