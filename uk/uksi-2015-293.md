# The Traffic Management (Council of the City of Wakefield MDC) Permit Scheme Order 2015

Made: 9th February 2015

Coming into force: 31st March 2015

The Secretary of State for Transport having approved the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme under section 34(2) of the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(4) and (5) and 39(2) of that Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Traffic Management (Council of the City of Wakefield MDC) Permit Scheme Order 2015 and comes into force on 31st March 2015.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “*the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme*” means the permit scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order in terms commonly known as the “Yorkshire Common Permit Scheme” which was prepared and submitted to the Secretary of State by the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC and has been approved by the Secretary of State; and

- “*specified streets*” has the meaning given by regulation 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007[^f00002].

#### Commencement of Permit Scheme

###### 3

The Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme comes into effect on 31st March 2015.

#### Application of Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007

###### 4

Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 shall apply to the specified streets within the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order gives effect to the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme.

The Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme is designed to control the carrying out of works in specified streets in Wakefield by imposing an obligation to obtain a permit from the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC before undertaking the works.

*Article 3* gives effect to the permit scheme, which will come into effect on 31st March 2015. The permit scheme referred to in this article is set out in the Schedule to the Order.

*Article 4* applies Part 8 of the Traffic Management Permit Scheme (England) Regulations 2007 (which makes provision for the disapplication and modification of enactments in relation to specified works in specified streets) to the specified works and specified streets within the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme.

A copy of the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC Permit Scheme is deposited and can be inspected at the offices of the Council of the City of Wakefield MDC at The Town Hall, Wood Street, Wakefield, WF1 2HQ and is available to inspect on the Council’s website www.wakefield.gov.uk.
