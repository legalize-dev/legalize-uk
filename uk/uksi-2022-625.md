# The Armed Forces Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2022

Made: 7th June 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 24(1) of the Armed Forces Act 2021[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, the Isle of Man and the British Overseas Territories except Gibraltar.

#### Provisions coming into force on 15th June 2022

###### 2

Section 10 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Armed Forces Act 2021 (service complaints appeals) come into force, in so far as they are not already in force, on 15th June 2022.

## Signed

Leo Docherty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Defence — 7th June 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force on 15th June 2022 section 10 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Armed Forces Act [2021 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/35) in so far as they are not already in force.

[S.I. 2022/471](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/471) brought section 10 and Schedule 3 into force for the purpose only of conferring power to make subordinate legislation. Section 10 amends sections 340D and 340H of the Armed Forces Act [2006 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/52) as part of wider reforms to the statutory service complaints system. Schedule 3 makes consequential amendments to legislation governing when a service complaint is deemed to be withdrawn for the purposes of accessing the employment tribunal.
