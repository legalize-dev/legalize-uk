# The Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2005

Made: 3rd March 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 29(1) of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004[^f00001] makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

The following provisions of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 are in force—

- (a) section 7 (grant of licence);

- (b) section 8 (general power of Gangmasters Licensing Authority to make rules);

- (c) section 9 (modification, revocation or transfer of licence);

- (d) section 15 (enforcement and compliance officers);

- (e) section 16 (powers of officers);

- (f) section 17 (entry by warrant);

- (g) section 18 (obstruction of officers);

- (h) section 19 (information relating to gangmasters);

- (i) section 20 (application of the Act to bodies corporate);

- (j) section 21 (application of the Act to unincorporated associations);

- (k) section 22 (application of the Act to partnerships);

- (l) section 23 (annual report);

- (m) section 24 (financial provision);

- (n) section 28 (application of the Act to Northern Ireland) in so far as it relates to paragraphs 9, 10, 15, 16, 17 and 18 of Schedule 2; and

- (o) paragraphs 9, 10, 15, 16, 17 and 18 of Schedule 2.

## Signed

Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 3rd March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Gangmasters (Licensing) Act 2004 specified in article 2 of the Order.
