# The Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2007

Made: 6th December 2007

Laid before Parliament: 7th December 2007

Coming into force: 31st December 2007

The Secretary of State has been designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to persistent organic pollutants, dangerous substances, preparations and chemicals. The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations under the powers conferred by that section:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 31st December 2007.

#### Amendment

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations Regulations 2006[^f00003] as amended by the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations (Amendment) Regulations 2007[^f00004] is replaced by Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Phil Woolas — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 6th December 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations substitute a new Schedule 1 to the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/3311](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/3311)) as amended by the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations (Amendment) Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007/1596](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1596)).

In that Schedule the following are inserted—

point 19a which gives effect to Directive [2007/51/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0051) of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ L257, 03.10.2007, p.13) with regard to measuring devices containing mercury; and

point 52 which gives effect to Directive [2006/122/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0122) of the European Parliament and of the Council (OJ L372, 27.12.2006, p.32) with regard to perfluorooctane sulfonates (PFOS).

Both these Directives amend the restrictions set out in Annex I of Council Directive [76/769/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1976/0769) on the approximation of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States relating to restrictions on the marketing and use of certain dangerous substances and preparations (OJ L262, 27.9.1976, p.201).

A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector, and a transposition note, are available from the Chemicals and Nanotechnology Division, Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, Nobel House, 17 Smith Square, London SW1P 3JR and at www.defra.gov.uk. Copies have been placed in the library of each House of Parliament.
