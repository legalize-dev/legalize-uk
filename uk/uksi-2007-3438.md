# The Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2007

Made: 6th December 2007

Laid before Parliament: 7th December 2007

Coming into force: 31st December 2007

The Secretary of State has been designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to measures relating to persistent organic pollutants, dangerous substances, preparations and chemicals. The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations under the powers conferred by that section:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 31st December 2007.

#### Amendment

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations Regulations 2006 as amended by the Controls on Dangerous Substances and Preparations (Amendment) Regulations 2007 is replaced by Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.
