# The Cultural Property (Armed Conflicts) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017

Made: 7th November 2017

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 32(2) of the Cultural Property (Armed Conflicts) Act 2017[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Cultural Property (Armed Conflicts) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of those provisions of the Cultural Property (Armed Conflicts) Act 2017 that are not already in force is 12th December 2017.

## Signed

John Glen — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport — 7th November 2017

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Part 6 of the Cultural Property (Armed Conflicts) Act 2017 came into force on Royal Assent (see section 32(1) of that Act). These Regulations bring the remaining provisions of the Act into force on 12th December 2017.
