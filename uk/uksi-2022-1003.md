# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2022

Made: 22nd September 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 186(6) and (7) of the Health and Care Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation, extent and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Care Act 2022 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

- (3) In these Regulations—

- “*the 2012 Act*” means the Health and Social Care Act 2012[^f00002];

- “*the 2022 Act*” means the Health and Care Act 2022.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st October 2022

###### 2

The following provisions of the 2022 Act come into force on 1st October 2022—

- (a) section 97;

- (b) section 99, only in so far as it inserts section 277A(6) into the 2012 Act;

- (c) section 100;

- (d) section 177.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st November 2022

###### 3

The following provisions of the 2022 Act come into force on 1st November 2022 so far as they relate to water supplied to areas in England—

- (a) section 175;

- (b) section 176.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care

Neil O’Brien — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — 22nd September 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force specified provisions of the Health and Care Act [2022 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/31) (“*the Act*”) on 1st October 2022 (regulation 2) and on 1st November 2022 (regulation 3). They are the third set of commencement regulations to be made under the Act.

Regulation 2(a) brings into force on 1st October 2022 section 97 (general duties of Health and Social Care Information Centre etc) of the Act.

Regulation 2(b) brings into force on 1st October 2022 section 99 (collection of information about adult social care) of the Act in so far as it inserts section 277A(6) into the Health and Social Care Act [2012 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/7) (“*the 2012 Act*”).

Regulation 2(c) brings into force on 1st October 2022 section 100 (enforcement of duties against private providers) of the Act.

Regulation 2(d) brings into force on 1st October 2022 section 177 (review into disputes relating to treatment of critically ill children) of the Act.

The provisions being commenced by regulation 2(b) and (c) of these Regulations relate to the enforcement of requests for information from the Secretary of State under section 277A(1) to (5) of the 2012 Act, which were inserted by section 99 of the Act when that section was commenced by the Health and Care Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional and Saving Provision) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/734](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/734)) (C. 45) on 31st July 2022.

Regulation 3 brings into force on 1st November 2022 sections 175 (fluoridation of water supplies) and 176 (fluoridation of water supplies: transitional provision) of the Act in so far as they relate to water supplied to areas in England.

A full impact assessment has been prepared in relation to the Act, and a copy is available at https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/health-and-care-bill-combined-impact-assessments. A hard copy can be obtained by writing to the Department of Health and Social Care, 39 Victoria Street, London, SW1H 0EU.
