# The Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Revocation) Order 2006

Made: 9th January 2006

Coming into force: 9th January 2006

Accordingly, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 143 of the Act, makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Revocation) Order 2006.

###### 2

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) Order 1990;

- (b) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1992[^f00003];

- (c) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1993[^f00004];

- (d) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1994[^f00005];

- (e) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1996[^f00006];

- (f) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1998[^f00007];

- (g) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 1999[^f00008];

- (h) the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) (Amendment) Order 2003[^f00009].

## Signed

Sainsbury of Turville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Science and Innovation — Department of Trade and Industry — 9th January 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The licensing scheme certified by the Secretary of State, which was operated by the Educational Recording Agency Limited, and was set out in the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) Order 1990 ([SI 1990/879](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/879)) (the 1990 Order) has ceased to operate.

This Order revokes the 1990 Order and a number of other Orders which amended the 1990 Order following variations in the licensing scheme.

The scheme certified in the 1990 Order has been superseded by a scheme certified and set out in the Copyright (Certification of Licensing Scheme for Educational Recording of Broadcasts) (Educational Recording Agency Limited) Order 2005 ([SI 2005/222](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/222)).
