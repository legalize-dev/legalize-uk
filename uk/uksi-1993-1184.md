# The British Wool (Guaranteed Prices) (Revocation) Order 1993

Made: 29th April 1993

Laid before Parliament: 30th April 1993

Coming into force: 1st May 1993

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales, and the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of powers conferred by sections 1, 11, 35(3) and 36(3) of the Agriculture Act 1957[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury and after consultation in accordance with section 9(1) of that Act with such bodies of persons as appear to them to represent the interests of producers of wool, hereby make the following Order:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the British Wool (Guaranteed Prices) (Revocation) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st May 1993.

#### Revocation of Wool Guarantee

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2) below, The British Wool (Guaranteed Prices) Order 1955[^f00003] and the British Wool (Guaranteed Prices) (Amendment) Order 1976[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

- (2) Notwithstanding their revocation by paragraph (1) above, the Orders mentioned in that paragraph shall continue to have effect, on and after the date on which this Order comes into force, in relation to any period before that date.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 29th April 1993.

John Selwyn Gummer — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Hector Monro — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 21st April 1993

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 22nd April 1993

Patrick Mayhew — Secretary of State for Northern Ireland — 26th April 1993

We consent

Gregory Knight — Tim Kirkhope — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 21st April 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the British Wool (Guaranteed Prices) Order 1955, S.I. [1955/487](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1955/487), as amended by S.I. [1976/602](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/602), which provided for the payment to wool producers of guaranteed prices for wool under the Agriculture Act 1957. Article 2(2) of the Order provides that S.I. [1955/487](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1955/487) and [1976/602](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/602) remain in force in relation to any period before the date on which they were revoked.
