# The Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 24th June 1992

Laid before Parliament: 29th June 1992

Coming into force: 20th July 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 4(3) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], I hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration (Registration with Police) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 20th July 1992.

###### 2

In Regulation 10(3) of the Immigration (Registration with Police) Regulations 1972[^f00002] (which relates to the fee paid for the issue of registration certificates), for the figure “ £27.50” there shall be substituted the figure “£30”.

## Signed

Home Office

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 24th June 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fee payable when aliens are required to register with the police from £27.50 to £30.
