# The Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2016

Made: 13th July 2016

Coming into force: 14th July 2016

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 13th day of July 2016Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 113(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2016 and comes into force on 14th July 2016.

#### Appointment to the office of Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

Amanda Mary Victoria Spielman is appointed to the office of Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills for a period of 5 years from 1st January 2017.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2011 is revoked[^f00002].

## Signed

Ceri King — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints Amanda Mary Victoria Spielman to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills for a period of 5 years from 1st January 2017.
