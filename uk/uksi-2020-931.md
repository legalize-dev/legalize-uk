# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020

Made: at 11.00 a.m. on 2nd September 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 2.00 p.m. on 2nd September 2020

Coming into force: at 12.00 p.m. on 2nd September 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020 and come into force at 12.00 p.m. on 2nd September 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) Regulations 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) Regulations 2020 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1)—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (g);

- (b) omit sub-paragraph (k);

- (c) omit sub-paragraph (l);

- (d) for sub-paragraphs (o) to the end, substitute—

> > (o) in the area of Calderdale Metropolitan Borough Council, the following wards—
>   (i) Illingworth and Mixenden,
>   (ii) Northowram and Shelf,
>   (iii) Ovenden,
>   (iv) Park,
>   (v) Skircoat,
>   (vi) Sowerby Bridge,
>   (vii) Town,
>   (viii) Warley, or
> (p) in the area of Kirklees Metropolitan Council, the following wards—
>   (i) Batley East,
>   (ii) Batley West,
>   (iii) Dewsbury East,
>   (iv) Dewsbury South,
>   (v) Dewsbury West.

## Signed

Matt Hancock — Secretary of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — At 11.00 a.m. on 2nd September 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (North of England) Regulations 2020 to remove the following areas from “the protected area” for the purposes of those Regulations:

- (a) in Greater Manchester; Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council, and

- (b) in Lancashire; Burnley Borough Council, and Hyndburn Borough Council.

In the areas of Calderdale Metropolitan Borough Council and Kirklees Metropolitan Council, the “protected area” for the purposes of those Regulations is amended to cover only the wards listed.

No impact assessment has been carried out for these Regulations.
