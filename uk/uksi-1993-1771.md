# The Commons Registration (Disposal of Disputed Registrations) (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made: 14th July 1993

Laid before Parliament: 23rd July 1993

Coming into force: 13th August 1993

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(1), 6(2), 19(1)(k) and (4)[^f00001] of the Commons Registration Act 1965[^f00002], and now vested in them[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Commons Registration (Disposal of Disputed Registrations) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 13th August 1993.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Commons Registration (Disposal of Disputed Registrations) Regulations 1972[^f00004].

###### 2

- (1) In regulation 3 of the principal Regulations, after “30,”, there shall be inserted “30A,”.

- (2) In Part 1 of the Schedule to the principal Regulations, after the specimen entry numbered 30, there shall be inserted the specimen entry set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

###### No. 30A

For rights section of register: an example of a disputed registration becoming final with modifications.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Tim Yeo — Minister of State, — Department of the Environment — 9th July 1993

John Redwood — Secretary of State for Wales — 14th July 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Commons Registration (Disposal of Disputed Registrations) Regulations 1972, which provided for the indication of the disposal of disputed registrations in the registers maintained under the Commons Registration Act 1965. They prescribe a new model entry on the register so that when a registration has become final with modifications that fact may be indicated by an entry setting out the registration in its modified form.
