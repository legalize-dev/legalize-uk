# The European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act 2020 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2020

Made: 18th March 2020

The Minister for the Cabinet Office, in exercise of the power conferred by section 42(7) of the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act 2020[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act 2020 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2020.

###### 2

In these Regulations, “the Act” means the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act 2020.

#### Commencement

###### 3

The following provisions of the Act come into force on the day after the day on which these Regulations are made—

- (a) section 15(3) (Independent Monitoring Authority for the Citizens’ Rights Agreements) so far as it relates to the provisions mentioned in paragraph (b);

- (b) the following provisions of Schedule 2 (Independent Monitoring Authority for the Citizens’ Rights Agreements)—

- (i) paragraphs 14 to 17;

- (ii) paragraphs 20 and 21;

- (iii) the definition of “Part 2” in paragraph 22(3); and

- (iv) paragraph 32.

###### 4

Paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 (Protection for certain rights, safeguards etc. in Belfast Agreement) of the Act comes into force on 23rd March 2020.

## Signed

Penny Mordaunt — Paymaster General — Cabinet Office — 18th March 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force provisions of the European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act [2020 (c. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/1) (“the Act”). These are the second commencement regulations made under the Act.

Regulation 3 brings into force the specified paragraphs of Schedule 2 to the Act relating to the Independent Monitoring Authority for the Citizens’ Rights Agreements (“the IMA”). These provisions place duties on the IMA relating to accounts and audit, public records, investigation by the Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration, the Freedom of Information Act and public sector equality duty and require the IMA to produce an annual plan and guidance.

Regulation 4 brings into force paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 to the Act, which amends section 71 of the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (restrictions on proceedings) concerning the Northern Ireland Human Rights Commission’s ‘own motion’ standing powers to challenge the compatibility of legislation with the European Convention on Human Rights through the Human Rights Act 1998.
