# The Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax) Order 1989

Made: 14th July 1989

Laid before the House of Commons: 27th July 1989

Coming into force: 1st September 1989

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 20(3) of the Value Added Tax Act 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Refund of Tax) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

###### 2

The National Rivers Authority (a body corporate established by section 1 of the Water Act 1989[^f00002]) is hereby specified for the purposes of section 20 of the Value Added Tax Act 1983.

## Signed

David Maclean — David Lightbown — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 14th July 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

By this Order the National Rivers Authority, a body corporate established by section 1 of the Water Act 1989, will be entitled to claim refunds of value added tax under section 20 of the Value Added Tax Act 1983 on supplies to, or importations by it if those supplies or importations are not for the purposes of any business carried on by it.
