# The National Treatment Agency (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made: 19th December 2001

Laid before Parliament: 21st December 2001

Coming into force: 30th January 2002

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 11(4) and 126(4) of, and paragraph 12 of Schedule 5 to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Treatment Agency (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 30th January 2002.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England only.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 3 of the National Treatment Agency Regulations 2001[^f00002] (disqualification for appointment) sub-paragraph (d) of paragraph (1) is deleted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Hazel Blears — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 19th December 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 3 of the National Treatment Agency Regulations 2001 to remove the disqualification of the chairmen and vice-chairmen of health authorities, special health authorities or NHS trusts and the disqualification of chairmen of primary care trusts from serving as the chairman or a non-officer member of the National Treatment Agency.
