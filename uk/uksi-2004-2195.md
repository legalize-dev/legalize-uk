# The Courts Act 2003 (Commencement No.7) Order 2004

Made: 24th August 2004

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 110 of the Courts Act 2003 [^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Courts Act 2003 (Commencement No.7) Order 2004.

###### 2

Section 97 (collection of fines and discharge of fines by unpaid work) of, and Schedule 6 (discharge of fines by unpaid work) to, the Courts Act 2003 shall come into force on 21st September 2004, in so far as they are not already in force.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Filkin — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Constitutional Affairs — 24th August 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions mentioned in article 2 into force on 21st September 2004, in so far as they have not already been commenced.
