# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The County Council of Durham) (Durham District) Designation Order 2008

Made: 1st October 2008

Laid before Parliament: 6th October 2008

Coming into force: 3rd November 2008

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Transport[^f00002] makes this Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The County Council of Durham) (Durham District) Designation Order 2008 and shall come into force on 3rd November 2008.

#### Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area

###### 2

- (1) The Secretary of State designates the area described in paragraph (2) as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and

- (b) a special enforcement area.

- (2) This Order applies to the area of Durham District with the exception of—

- (a) the section of the A1M that lies within the area; and

- (b) the slip roads and circulatory carriageways at junctions 61 and 62 of that section.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Rosie Winterton — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 1st October 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the area described in paragraph (2) of article 2 as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
