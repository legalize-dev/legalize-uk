# The Police Cadets (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made: 16th October 1993

Laid before Parliament: 28th October 1993

Coming into force: 22nd November 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 35 of the Police Act 1964[^f00001], after taking into consideration the recommendations made by the Police Negotiating Board for the United Kingdom and furnishing the said Board with a draft of the Regulations in accordance with section 2(1) of the Police Negotiating Board Act 1980[^f00002], I hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Cadets (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 22nd November 1993, but regulation 2 shall have effect as from 1st September 1992.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Police Cadets Regulations 1979[^f00003].

#### Pay

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 13(1)(a) of the principal Regulations is amended by substituting —

- (a) for the words “1st July 1991” the words “1st September 1992”; and

- (b) for the words “31st August 1991” the words “31st August 1993”.

- (2) Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the Table—

| Age | London | Provinces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part I—pay from 1st September 1992 to 31st August 1993 | Part I—pay from 1st September 1992 to 31st August 1993 | Part I—pay from 1st September 1992 to 31st August 1993 |
| Under 17 years | £5,220 a year | £4,413 a year |
| 17 years | £5,490 a year | £4,683 a year |
| 18 years or over | £6,018 a year | £5,211 a year |
| Part II—pay from 1st September 1993 | Part II—pay from 1st September 1993 | Part II—pay from 1st September 1993 |
| Under 17 years | £5,286 a year | £4,479 a year |
| 17 years | £5,559 a year | £4,752 a year |
| 18 years or over | £6,096 a year | £5,289 a year |

#### Charge for board and lodging

###### 3

Paragraph 2 of Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the figure “£639” the figure “£675” and for the figure “£591” the figure “£624”.

## Signed

Michael Howard — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 16th October 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulation)

These Regulations further amend the Police Cadets Regulations 1979. Regulation 2 increases pay from 1st September 1992 and again from 1st September 1993 (retrospection is authorised by section 33(4), as applied by section 35(2), of the Police Act 1964). Regulation 3 increases charges payable by cadets for board and lodging provided by police authorities.
