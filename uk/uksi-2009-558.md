# The Monkseaton Community High School (Governing Body Procedures) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 9th March 2009

Laid before Parliament: 16th March 2009

Coming into force: 8th May 2009

On the application of the governing body, the Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1) and 3(1) of the Act.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Monkseaton Community High School (Governing Body Procedures) (Amendment) Order 2009 and comes into force on 8th May 2009.

#### Variation of The Monkseaton Community High School (Governing Body Procedures) Order 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Monkseaton Community High School (Governing Body Procedures) Order 2006 is amended as follows.

- (2) For Article 3, substitute the following Article—

> > (3) This Order shall have effect until 7th May 2012.

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Children, Schools and Families — 9th March 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Monkseaton Community High School (Procedures) Order 2006 enables the governing body of the Monkseaton Community High School to hold meetings otherwise than in accordance with regulation 12(2) of the School Governance (Procedures) (England) Regulations 2003, as amended, by relaxing the requirements for all governors to be physically present at such meetings. The Monkseaton Community High School (Procedures) Order 2006 has effect until 11th May 2009. Article 2 of this Order extends the Monkseaton Community High School (Procedures) Order 2006 so that it has effect until 7th May 2012.
