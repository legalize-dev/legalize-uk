# The Companies Act 1989 (Commencement No. 11) Order 1991

Made: 24th June 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 215 of the Companies Act 1989[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Companies Act 1989 (Commencement No. 11) Order 1991.

#### Provisions brought into force by the Order

###### 2

Sections 35 and 36 of the Companies Act 1989 shall come into force on 26th June 1991.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 24th June 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order brings sections 35 and 36 of the Companies Act 1989 into force on 26th June 1991. Section 35 obliges the Secretary of State to make regulations requiring the keeping of a register of auditors. Section 36 obliges the Secretary of State to make regulations requiring recognised supervisory bodies under Part II of the Companies Act 1989 to keep and make available to the public certain information relating to bodies corporate and partnerships eligible for appointment as company auditor.
