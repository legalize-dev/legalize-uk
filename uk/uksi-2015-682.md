# The Scotland Act 2012 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2015

Made: 17th March 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 44(5) of the Scotland Act 2012[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Scotland Act 2012 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2015.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Scotland Act 2012 is 1st July 2015—

- (a) section 1 (administration of elections);

- (b) section 3 (supplementary and transitional provision about elections).

## Signed

David Mundell — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Scotland Office — 17th March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order commences sections 1 and 3 of the Scotland Act [2012 (c.11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/11) on 1st July 2015. Section 1 transfers certain of the executive functions in section 12 of the Scotland Act [1998 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/46) relating to the conduct and administration of Scottish Parliament elections, which are currently exercisable by the Secretary of State, to the Scottish Ministers. It also introduces a new section 12A which sets out the powers to make provision about elections that continue to be exercisable by the Secretary of State. Section 3 makes supplementary and transitional provision in connection with the transfer of responsibility to the Scottish Ministers.
