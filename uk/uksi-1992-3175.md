# The Farm Business Non-Capital Grant (Amendment) Scheme 1992

Made: 2nd December 1992

Laid before Parliament: 21st December 1992

Coming into force: 18th January 1993

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 1 of the Farm Land and Rural Development Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury, hereby make the following Scheme:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the Farm Business Non-Capital Grant (Amendment) Scheme 1992 and shall come into force on 18th January 1993.

#### Amendment of the principal Scheme

###### 2

The Farm Business Non-Capital Grant Scheme 1988[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs 3 and 4 below.

###### 3

In paragraph 5, after sub-paragraph (1) there shall be inserted the following subparagraph:—

> > (1A) The appropriate Minister shall not approve any feasibility study plan or marketing plan that is received by him after 17th January 1993.

###### 4

In paragraph 7—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (2), the words from “and the eligible person” to the end of that sub-paragraph shall be omitted;

- (b) sub-paragraph (5)(b) shall be omitted.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 1st December 1992.

John Selwyn Gummer — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Hector Monro — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 1st December 1992

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 2nd December 1992

We approve,

Greg Knight — Tim Boswell — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 2nd December 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

This Scheme varies the Farm Business Non-Capital Grant Scheme 1988 by introducing a closing date of 18th January 1993 for the submission of feasibility study plans and marketing plans for approval. This closing date applies to both new applications (paragraph 3) and also to the submission of feasibility study plans or marketing plans after withdrawing an approved plan (paragraph 4).
