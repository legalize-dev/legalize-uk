# The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2013

Made: 28th November 2013

Laid before Parliament: 4th December 2013

Coming into force: 25th December 2013

The Secretary of State is satisfied that the fireplace exempted by this Order can be used for burning fuel other than authorised fuels[^f00002] without producing any smoke or a substantial quantity of smoke.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2013;

- (b) comes into force on 25th December 2013.

#### Amendment to the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) Order 2013

###### 2

- (1) The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) (No. 2) Order 2013[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In the Schedule in the entry beginning “Sirius 490 Sigma MF”, after “Sirius 490 Scene MF” insert, “Sirius 790 Sigma MF”.

## Signed

Dan Rogerson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for the Environment — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 28th November 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (England) Order (No. 2) 2013 ([S.I. 2013/2112](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/2112)) which exempts various fireplaces from the provisions of section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993 (prohibition of smoke emissions in smoke control areas), subject to the conditions set out in the Schedule.

One entry has been inserted into the Schedule in order to exempt an additional fireplace (the Sirius 790 Sigma MF).

A full impact assessment has not been produced as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
