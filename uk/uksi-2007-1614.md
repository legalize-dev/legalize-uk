# The Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007

Made: 6th June 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 53(1) of the Police and Justice Act 2006[^f00001].

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Police and Justice Act 2006 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales, except that article 3 extends to England only.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Police and Justice Act 2006 shall come into force on 29th June 2007—

- (a) section 2 (amendments to the 1996 Act) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 2 (amendments to the 1996 Act) referred to in sub-paragraph (b);

- (b) paragraphs 7(1) and (2) (functions of police authorities) of Schedule 2;

- (c) section 3 (delegation of police authority functions);

- (d) section 13 (supply of information to police etc by Registrar General);

- (e) section 18 (arrest for failing to comply with conditional caution);

- (f) section 41 (immigration and asylum enforcement function: complaints and misconduct);

- (g) section 52 (amendments and repeals) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedules 14 (minor and consequential amendments) and 15 (repeals and revocations) in sub-paragraphs (h) and (i);

- (h) paragraphs 53 to 58 of Schedule 14; and

- (i) in Part 2 of Schedule 15 (repeals: powers of the police etc) the entry relating to the Criminal Justice Act 2003[^f00002].

###### 3

The following provisions of the Police and Justice Act 2006 shall come into force on 1st August 2007—

- (a) sections 23 to 25 (parenting contracts and parenting orders);

- (b) section 22 and Schedule 9 (amendments to the Crime and Disorder Act 1998);

- (c) section 52 (amendments and repeals) in so far as it relates to the entries in Schedule 15 in sub-paragraph (c);

- (d) paragraphs 53 to 57 of Schedule 14;

- (e) in Part 3 of Schedule 15 (repeals: crime and anti-social behaviour) the entries relating to—

- (i) the Crime and Disorder Act 1998[^f00003];

- (ii) the Police Reform Act 2002[^f00004]; and

- (iii) the Clean Neighbourhoods and Environment Act 2005[^f00005].

## Signed

Tony McNulty — Minister of State — 6th June 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force those provisions of the Police and Justice Act 2006 set out in articles 2 and 3 on 29th June 2007 and 1st August 2007 respectively.
