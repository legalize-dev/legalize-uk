# The Walkergate Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2007

Made: 6th May 2007

Laid before Parliament: 14th May 2007

Coming into force: 4th June 2007

In exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by section 2(1) of the Act, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills, on the application of the governing body, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Walkergate Primary School (Change to School Session Times) Order 2007 and comes into force on 4th June 2007.

#### Exemption from the School Session Regulations

###### 2

The Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999[^f00002] are not to apply to the governing body of Walkergate Primary School[^f00003] of Sutton Street, Newcastle upon Tyne NE6 4SD.

#### Time period

###### 3

This Order has effect until 27th July 2007.

## Signed

Jim Knight — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 6th May 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made under section 2 of the Education Act 2002. Article 2 provides that the Changing of School Session Times (England) Regulations 1999 do not apply to the governing body of Walkergate Primary School. Article 3 specifies that the Order shall have effect until 27th July 2007.
