# The Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1988

Made: 8th March 1988

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1988

Coming into force: 1st April 1988

The Industrial Assurance Commissioner, with the approval of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 43 of the Industrial Assurance Act 1923[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 1st April 1988.

###### 2

In the Regulations “the Act” means the Industrial Assurance Act 1923.

###### 3

The fees set out in the Schedule hereto shall be payable in respect of the matters set out therein.

###### 4

The Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1987[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations supersede the Industrial Assurance (Fees) Regulations 1987. They generally increase by about 6% the fees payable in connection with the exercise by the Industrial Assurance Commissioner of his functions under the Industrial Assurance Act 1923.
