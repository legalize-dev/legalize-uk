# The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 27th April 1999

Laid before Parliament: 6th May 1999

Coming into force: 28th May 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 14(2) and 51 of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], and after consultation with the Gaming Board for Great Britain, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 28th May 1999.

###### 2

Regulation 5(1) of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) Regulations 1984[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting for the figure “£8.50”, in both places where it occurs, the figure “£10.00”.

###### 3

Regulation 5(1) of the Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Scotland) Regulations 1984[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for the figure “£8.50”, in both places where it occurs, the figure “£10.00”.

###### 4

The Gaming Clubs (Hours and Charges) (Amendment) Regulations 1998[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

George Howarth — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 27th April 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the maximum charges which may be made for admission to gaming on bingo club premises from £8.50 to £10.00.
