# The Stockport Acute Services and the Stockport Healthcare National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2000

Made: 20th March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the Stockport Acute Services National Health Service Trust and the Stockport Healthcare National Health Service Trust[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Stockport Acute Services and the Stockport Healthcare National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts

###### 2

The Stockport Acute Services National Health Service Trust established by the Stockport Acute Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00005] and the Stockport Healthcare National Health Service Trust established by the Stockport Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00006] are hereby dissolved, and accordingly those Orders are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Hunt — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 20th March 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 2000 of the Stockport Acute Services and the Stockport Healthcare National Health Service Trusts.

This Order should be read in conjunction with the Stockport National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00007].
