# The Consular Fees Order 1994

Made: 2nd November 1994

Coming into force: 24th November 1994

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 2nd day of November 1994 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 1(1) of the Consular Fees Act 1980[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Consular Fees Order 1994 and shall come into force on 24th November 1994.

###### 2

In this Order—

- (a) “consular officer” means any person authorised by the Secretary of State to exercise consular functions, or functions in the United Kingdom which correspond with consular functions, (including persons who are not, as well as persons who are, consular officers);

- (b) “consular employee” means any person employed in the administrative or technical service of the consular post or diplomatic mission;

- (c) “consular premises” means the buildings or parts of buildings used for the purposes of the consular post or diplomatic mission;

- (d) “entry clearance” means a visa, entry certificate, entry permit or other document which, in accordance with the applicable immigration laws or rules, is to be taken as evidence of a person’s eligibility for entry into the United Kingdom, a dependency of the United Kingdom, or any other country or territory, as the case may be (but does not include a work permit).

- (e) “replacement passport” means a passport issued in substitution for a valid passport in order to incorporate an addition or amendment thereto.

###### 3

The fees set forth in the table of fees in the Schedule to this Order are prescribed to be levied by consular officers and by marriage officers under the Foreign Marriage Act 1892[^f00002] and the Marriage with Foreigners Act 1906[^f00003] in the execution of their duties, and the said table of fees shall be construed as part of this Order.

###### 4

The following Orders are hereby revoked—

- The Consular Fees Order 1989[^f00004]

- The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1989[^f00005]

- The Consular Fees (Amendment) (No 2) Order 1991[^f00006]

- The Consular Fees (Amendment) (No 3) Order 1991[^f00007]

- The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1992[^f00008]

- The Consular Fees (Amendment) Order 1993[^f00009].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes and replaces the Consular Fees Order 1989 and subsequent amending Orders. It increases some consular fees by amounts that are intended to meet, to some extent, the increase in the cost of providing the services. The increase is a step towards eventual full cost recovery. The average increase is approximately 50 per cent.
