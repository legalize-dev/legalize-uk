# The Loughborough College of Art and Design Higher Education Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1998

Made: 23rd June 1998

Laid before Parliament: 25th June 1998

Coming into force: 1st August 1998

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 128(1), (2) and (6) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Employment, after consultation with Loughborough College of Art and Design higher education corporation[^f00002] and the Higher Education Funding Council for England in accordance with subsection (4) of the said section 128 and with the consent of Loughborough University given in accordance with subsection (2) of that section, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Loughborough College of Art and Design Higher Education Corporation (Dissolution) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st August 1998.

- (2) In this Order “the corporation” means Loughborough College of Art and Design higher education corporation.

###### 2

On 1st August 1998 the corporation shall be dissolved and all of its property, rights and liabilities shall be transferred to and vest in Loughborough University.

###### 3

Section 127 of the Education Reform Act 1988 shall apply to any person employed by the corporation immediately before 1st August 1998 as if the reference in that section—

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;

- (b) to the transfer date were to 1st August 1998;

- (c) to the transferor authority were to the corporation; and

- (d) to the corporation were to Loughborough University.

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 23rd June 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves Loughborough College of Art and Design higher education corporation. It provides that the property, rights and liabilities of the dissolved corporation transfer to Loughborough University and secures the rights of employees by applying section 127 of the Education Reform Act 1988.
