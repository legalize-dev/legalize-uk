# The Government Trading Act 1990 (Appointed Day) Order 1991

Made: 28th January 1991

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 4(3) of the Government Trading Act 1990[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Government Trading Act 1990 (Appointed Day) Order 1991.

###### 2

Section 4 of the Government Trading Act 1990 (which relates to the repeal of the Borrowing (Control and Guarantees) Act 1946[^f00002]) shall come into force on 11th February 1991.

## Signed

Gregory Knight — Nicholas Baker — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 28th January 1991
