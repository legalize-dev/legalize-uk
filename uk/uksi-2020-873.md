# The Power to Award Degrees etc. (Arden University Limited) Order 2020

Made: 17th August 2020

Coming into force: 1st September 2020

The Office for Students, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 42(1) of the Higher Education and Research Act 2017 , makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Power to Award Degrees etc. (Arden University Limited) Order 2020 and shall come into force on 1st September 2020.

#### Competence to grant awards

###### 2

Arden University Limited shall be competent to grant taught awards of the kind mentioned in section 42(2)(a) and defined under section 42(3) of the said Act for a fixed one- year term beginning on 1st September 2020 and expiring on 31st August 2021.

###### 3

Arden University Limited may authorise other institutions to grant such awards as specified in paragraph 2 of this Order on its behalf.

## Signed

Susan Lapworth — Director of Competition and Registration — 2020-08-17
