# The Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1994

Made: 1st March 1994

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1994

Coming into force: 31st March 1994

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 74A and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consultation in accordance with section 84(1) of the said Act with such persons or organisations as appear to them to represent the interests concerned, and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, being Ministers designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00003] in relation to the common agriculture policy of the European Community, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1994 and shall come into force on 31st March 1994.

#### Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1991

###### 2

The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1991[^f00004] shall be further amended in accordance with regulation 3 below.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1991 (“the principal Regulations”) and implement the following Community legislation:

- (1) Commission Directive [93/26/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0026) (OJ No. L179, 22.7.93, p.2) amending the Annex to Council Directive [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471) concerning certain products used in animal nutrition (regulation 3(2)(b), (c) and (d));

- (2) Commission Directive [93/27/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0027) (OJ No. L179, 22.7.93, p.5) amending the Annexes to Council Directive [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524) concerning additives in feeding stuffs (regulation 3(1)(a)(i) and (b));

- (3) Commission Directive [93/55/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0055) (OJ No. L206, 18.8.93, p.11) amending Annex I to Council Directive [70/524/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0524) (regulation 3(1)(a)(ii));

- (4) Commission Directive [93/56/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0056) (OJ No. L206, 18.8.93, p.13) amending the Annex to Council Directive [82/471/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1982/0471) (regulation 3(2)(a) . . . and (d)).

Regulation 3(1) amends Schedule 4 to the principal Regulations, which relates to permitted additives in feeding stuffs. Regulation 3(2) amends Schedule 7 to those Regulations, which relates to certain products used in animal nutrition.
