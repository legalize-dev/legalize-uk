# The Reserve Forces (Provision of Information by Persons Liable to be Recalled) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 9th November 2005

Laid before Parliament: 11th November 2005

Coming into force: 5th December 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 75 of the Reserve Forces Act 1996 [^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement, interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Reserve Forces (Provision of Information by Persons Liable to be Recalled) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 5th December 2005.

- (2) In these Regulations “the 1997 Regulations” means the Reserve Forces (Provision of Information by Persons Liable to be Recalled) Regulations 1997[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the 1997 Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 4(2)(b)of the 1997 Regulations for the words “his marital status” there shall be substituted “his marital or civil partnership status”.

## Signed

Don Touhig — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State Ministry of Defence — 9th November 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations amend the 1997 Regulations in consequence of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 which came into force on 5th December 2005 by adding civil partnership status to the information which the reservist is required to provide.
