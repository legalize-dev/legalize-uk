# The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Approved Investments) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 5th June 1992

Laid before Parliament: 15th June 1992

Coming into force: 6th July 1992

The Secretary of State for the Environment, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 66(1)(a) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Approved Investments) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 6th July 1992.

###### 2

The Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Approved Investments) Regulations 1990[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition of the following paragraph at the end of Part II of the Schedule:

> > (28) A district council established under the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972[^f00003].

## Signed

Michael Howard — Secretary of State for the Environment — 5th June 1992

David Hunt — Secretary of State for Wales — 5th June 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Local Authorities (Capital Finance) (Approved Investments) Regulations 1990 which contain a list of investments approved by the Secretary of State for the purposes of Part IV of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (revenue accounts and capital finance of local authorities).

The amendment adds district councils in Northern Ireland to the list of persons or bodies in Part II of the Schedule to the 1990 Regulations with the effect that any advance made to, or a loan instrument issued by or to, any such council is an approved investment for the purposes of Part IV.
