# The European Parliamentary Elections (Day of By-election) (North East Scotland Constituency) Order 1998

Made: 13th October 1998

Laid before Parliament: 20th October 1998

Coming into force: 21st October 1998

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1978[^f00002], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Day of By-election) (North East Scotland Constituency) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 21st October 1998.

###### 2

The by-election to fill the vacancy in the European Parliamentary constituency of North East Scotland shall be held on 26th November 1998.

## Signed

George Howarth — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 13th October 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 26th November 1998 as the day on which the poll for the by-election to fill the vacancy in the North East Scotland constituency shall be held.
