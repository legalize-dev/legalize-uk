# The Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No.8) Order 1992

Made: 22nd May 1992

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 124(3)of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (Commencement No.8) Order 1992.

###### 2

The provisions of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 specified in the Schedule hereto shall come into force on 1st June 1992.

## SCHEDULE

| *Provision of the Act* | *Subject matter of provision* |
| --- | --- |
| Paragraph 13 of Schedule 18 and section 125(3) so far as it relates to that paragraph. | Consequential amendment |
| Section 125(7) and Schedule 20 so far as they provide for the repeal of, or of words in, the following provisions— sections 7 and 33(4)(b) (and the word “or” immediately preceding it) of the Solicitors Act 1974[^f00002] section 45 of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00003] section 9(8) of, and paragraph 4(2)(b) (and the word “or”immediately preceding it) of Schedule 2 to, the Administration of Justice Act 1985[^f00004]. | Repeals |

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — 22nd May 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st June 1992 provisions in the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 relating to the administration of the solicitors' profession, including the scope of solicitors' accounts rules. It also repeals provisions in section 45 of the County Courts Act 1984 which relates to costs in civil proceedings.
