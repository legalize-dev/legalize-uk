# The Companies (Cross-Border Mergers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 9th February 2015

Laid before Parliament: 12th February 2015

Coming into force: 6th April 2015

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the creation, operation, regulation or dissolution of companies and other forms of business organisation .

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of that Act the Secretary of State makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Cross-Border Mergers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 6th April 2015.

###### 2

In regulation 16 of the Companies (Cross-Border Mergers) Regulations 2007 , for paragraph (3) substitute—

> > (3) After the consequences of the cross-border merger have taken effect (see regulation 17)—
>   (a) an order made under this regulation is conclusive evidence that—
>     (i) the conditions set out in paragraph (1) have been satisfied; and
>     (ii) the requirements of regulations 7 to 10 and 12 to 15 (pre-merger requirements) have been complied with; and
>   (b) the cross-border merger may not be declared null and void.

## Signed

Jo Swinson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations and Consumer Affairs — 9th February 2015
