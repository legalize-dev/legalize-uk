# The Cornwall Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1993

Made: 19th March 1993

Coming into force: 1st April 1993

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of powers conferred on her by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, on the application of the NHS trust concerned[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to that latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cornwall Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust Dissolution Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

#### Dissolution of NHS Trust

###### 2

The Cornwall Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust is hereby dissolved, and accordingly the Cornwall Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00005] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Virginia Bottomley — Secretary of State for Health — 19th March 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the Cornwall Community Healthcare National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1993.
