# The Private Hire Vehicles (London) (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 6th December 2007

Laid before Parliament: 10th December 2007

Coming into force: 31st December 2007

In accordance with section 37(3) of that Act she has consulted with Transport for London.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Private Hire Vehicles (London)(Transitional Provisions)(Amendment) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 31st December 2007.

#### Amendments to regulation 6

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 6 of the Private Hire Vehicles (London)(Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2004 is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (1)(d), for “paragraph (4)” substitute “paragraph (3)”.

- (3) Paragraph (1)(e) shall be deleted.

- (4) In paragraph (4), for “1988” substitute “1998”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Rosie Winterton — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 6th December 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend, with effect from 31st December 2007, the Private Hire Vehicles (London)(Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2004 so as to correct drafting errors which occurred at the time that the latter were made.

Regulation 6(1)(d) is amended so as to insert a corrected reference to paragraph (3) in place of an incorrect reference to paragraph (4). The effect of the corrected provision is that if a temporary permit ceases to have effect with respect to an existing private hire vehicle in the circumstances specified in paragraph (3), then it shall cease to have effect on the date specified or agreed as mentioned in paragraph (3).

Regulation 6(1)(e) is deleted as it was redundant, in that it erroneously related to a paragraph (5).

Regulation (6)(4) is amended so as to refer to the Private Hire Vehicles (London) Act 1998 as “the 1998” Act rather than the “1988” Act.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
