# The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011

Made: 4th October 2011

Coming into force: 1st November 2011

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust following the completion of the consultation prescribed under section 25(3) of, and paragraph 28(3) of type=start time=1329746396491Schedule 4 to, thetype=end time=1329746396491 National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 1st November 2011.

#### Dissolution of NHS trust

###### 2

- (1) The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust is dissolved.

- (2) The Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990[^f00003] is revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Paul Burstow — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 4th October 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st November 2011 of the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust established on 21st December 1990 and revokes the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1990 that established the trust.
