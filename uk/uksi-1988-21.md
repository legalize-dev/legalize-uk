# The Highland Regional Council (Loch an t-Seana Bhaile) (Amendment) Water Order 1988

Made: 11th January 1988

Coming into force: 18th January 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17(2) and 107(1)(b) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the Highland Regional Council, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Highland Regional Council (Loch an t-Seana Bhaile) (Amendment) Water Order 1988 and shall come into force on 18th January 1988.

- (2) This Order and the County of Ross and Cromarty (Loch an t-Seana Bhaile) Water Order 1962[^f00002] (hereinafter referred to as Amendment of the principal Order

###### 2

In article 5(2)(a) of the principal Order for the expression “10,000 gallons” there shall be substituted the expression “103 cubic metres (22,600 gallons)” .

#### Miscellaneous

###### 3

The Highland Regional Council shall at all times after the expiration of six months from the date on which this Order comes into force keep a copy thereof at the office of the Chief Executive of the Council.

## Signed

Alasdair C Paton — Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office — 11th January 1988
