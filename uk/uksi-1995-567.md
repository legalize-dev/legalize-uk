# The North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995

Made: 1st March 1995

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 1995

Coming into force: 1st April 1995

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92 1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Gwynedd Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Health Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92 3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

- (2) In this Order:

- “the Authority” means the Gwynedd Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in aschedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 31st March 1994, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 1st April 1995 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

John Redwood — Secretary of State for Wales — 1st March 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the North Wales Ambulance National Health Service Trust on 1st April 1995 trust property formerly held by the Gwynedd Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Orders refers can be obtained from HFM3 Welsh Office, New Crown Building, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF1 3NQ.
