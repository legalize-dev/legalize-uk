# The Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust and the St Mary’s National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2007

Made: 18th September 2007

Coming into force: 1st October 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust and the St Mary’s National Health Service Trust, following the completion of the consultation prescribed under section 25(3) of, and paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4 to, the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) and the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust and the St Mary’s National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st October 2007.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the National Health Service Act 2006;

- “community health services” means any services which the Secretary of State may provide under section 3(1)(d) or (e) of, or paragraph 2 or 3 of Schedule 1 to, the Act and any service which he has a duty to provide under paragraph 1 or 8 of that Schedule;

- “dissolution date” means the 1st October 2007;

- “operational date” has the meaning assigned to it in paragraph 5(5) of Schedule 4 to the Act; and

- “the trust” means the Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust established by article 2.

#### Establishment and name of the trust

###### 2

There is established a National Health Service trust which shall be called the Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust.

#### Nature and functions of the trust

###### 3

- (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 25(1) of the Act.

- (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, and community health services, for the purposes of the health service.

#### Directors of the trust

###### 4

- (1) The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 5 executive directors and 7 non-executive directors.

- (2) Since the trust is to be regarded as having a significant teaching commitment within the meaning of paragraph 5(3) of Schedule 4 to the Act, one of the non-executive directors shall be appointed from the Imperial College of Science, Technology and Medicine.

#### Operational date and accounting date of the trust

###### 5

- (1) The operational date of the trust shall be 1st October 2007.

- (2) The accounting date of the trust shall be 31st March.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts and revocation of orders

###### 6

On the dissolution date—

- (a) the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust, which was established by the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1994[^f00003]; and

- (b) the St Mary’s National Health Service Trust, which was established by the St Mary’s National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00004],

- are dissolved, and accordingly those Orders, and the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2005[^f00005], are revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Ann Keen — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Health — 18th September 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order establishes the Imperial College Healthcare National Health Service Trust as a National Health Service trust. The Order sets out the functions of the trust (article 3), the number of executive and non-executive directors of the trust (article 4), the operational date (the date on which the trust begins to undertake all of the functions conferred on it) and the accounting date of the trust (article 5).

The Order also dissolves the Hammersmith Hospitals National Health Service Trust and the St Mary’s National Health Service Trust and revokes their Establishment Orders.
