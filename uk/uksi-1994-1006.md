# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Insight Communications Guildford Limited) Order 1994

Made: 28th March 1994

Laid before Parliament: 12th April 1994

Coming into force: 11th May 1994

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Insight Communications Guildford Limited) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 11th May 1994.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

###### 3

The Public Telecommunication System Designation (British Cable Services Limited) Order 1987[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Patrick McLoughlin — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Trade and Industry — 28th March 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Insight Communications Guildford Limited on 13th October 1993 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence in the Guildford, Aldershot and Woking area.

A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 12th April 1994. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Insight Communications Guildford Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force on 11th May 1994.

The licence replaces the licence granted to British Cable Services Limited on 8th May 1987 which has been revoked at the request of the licensee. The Public Telecommunication System Designation (British Cable Services Limited) Order 1987 (S.I. [1987/827](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1987/827)) falls to be revoked as spent.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, 50 Ludgate Hill, London, EC4M 7JJ.
