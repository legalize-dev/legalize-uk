# The Income Tax (Building Societies) (Dividends and Interest) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made: 2nd May 1995

Laid before the House of Commons: 2nd May 1995

Coming into force: 3rd May 1995

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 477A(1) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Building Societies) (Dividends and Interest) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 3rd May 1995.

###### 2

In the Income Tax (Building Societies) (Dividends and Interest) Regulations 1990[^f00002], in regulation 4(1), after sub-paragraph (s) there shall be added—

> > (t) an amount which is deemed under section 730A(2) of the Taxes Act[^f00003] (treatment of price differential on sale and repurchase of securities) to be a payment of interest.

## Signed

S. C. T. Matheson — C. W. Corlett — Two of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue — 2nd May 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Income Tax (Building Societies) (Dividends and Interest) Regulations 1990 (S.I. [1990/2231](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/2231)) so as to provide that deemed payments of interest under section 730A of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (treatment of price differential on sale and repurchase of securities) are payments from which income tax is not required to be deducted under the 1990 Regulations.
