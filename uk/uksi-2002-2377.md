# The Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002

Made: 16th September 2002

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 44 of the Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The following provisions of the Vehicles (Crime) Act 2001 shall come into force on 17th September 2002—

- (a) section 32;

- (b) section 33;

- (c) section 43 for the purposes of paragraphs 3, 4, 5 and 6 of the Schedule to that Act.

## Signed

Signed by Authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

David Jamieson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Transport — 16th September 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences sections 32 of the Vehicle (Crime) Act 2001 on 17th September 2002 and 33 of the same Act on the same date together with consequential provisions. Section 32 amends and clarifies provisions regarding applications for registration of motor vehicles. Section 33 confers a power to make regulations providing that the Secretary of State may refuse a new registration document where he is not satisfied that the vehicle for which it is sought is the registered vehicle.
