# The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made: 19th September 1995

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 19(2) of the Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995.

###### 2

The Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 shall come into force on 20th September 1995.

## Signed

Virginia Bottomley — Secretary of State for National Heritage — 19th September 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Olympic Symbol etc. (Protection) Act 1995 into force.
