# Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1990

Made: 28th September 1990

Coming into force: 22nd October 1990

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 57 of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1990 and shall come into force on 22nd October 1990.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Registration Appeal Court

###### 2

- (1) From the date this Act of Sederunt comes into force, the court of three judges for hearing registration appeals under section 57 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 shall be the Honourable Lord Cowie, the Honourable Lord Morison and the Right Honourable the Lord Morton of Shuna.

- (2) From the date this Act of Sederunt comes into force, the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1989[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

J.A.D. Hope — Lord President, I.P.D. — 28th September 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt appoints the three judges for hearing registration appeals under section 57 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 and revokes the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 1989.
