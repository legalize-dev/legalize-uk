# The Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996

Made: 14th November 1996

Laid before Parliament: 21st November 1996

Coming into force: 12th December 1996

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by the Wakefield and Pontefract Community Health National Health Service Trust and now managed by the Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust, having consulted both of those Trusts in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 12th December 1996.

- (2) In this Order —

- “the first Trust” means the Wakefield and Pontefract Community Health National Health Service Trust;

- “the second Trust” means the Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the first Trust and the second Trust on 8th July 1996, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the first Trust and the second Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 12th December 1996 the trust property shall be transferred from the first Trust to the second Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Horam — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — 14th November 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Leeds Community and Mental Health Services Teaching National Health Service Trust on 12th December 1996 trust property formerly held by the Wakefield and Pontefract Community Health National Health Service Trust. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
