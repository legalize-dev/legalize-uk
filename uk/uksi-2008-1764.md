# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Dudley) Designation Order 2008

Made: 4th July 2008

Laid before Parliament: 4th July 2008

Coming into force: 7th July 2008

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Transport[^f00002] makes this Order—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Dudley) Designation Order 2008 and shall come into force on 7th July 2008.

#### Revocation of the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The Borough Council of Dudley) Designation Order 2008

###### 2

The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The Borough Council of Dudley) Designation Order 2008[^f00003] is revoked.

#### Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area

###### 3

The Secretary of State designates the whole of the area of the metropolitan district of The Borough Council of Dudley as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and

- (b) a special enforcement area.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Jim Fitzpatrick — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 4th July 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes and replaces the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (The Borough Council of Dudley) Designation Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1518](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1518)) (“the revoked Order”) solely for the purpose of correcting a drafting error.

The revoked Order incorrectly designated The Borough Council of Dudley as a civil enforcement area and a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004 when it should have designated the whole of the area of the Council for those purposes. This Order corrects that error.

No Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no significant effect on the cost to business and the voluntary sector.
