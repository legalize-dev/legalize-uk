# The Insolvency (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made: 27th February 1991

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 1991

Coming into force: 2nd April 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by rule 12.1 of the Insolvency Rules 1986[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Insolvency (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd April 1991.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations references to “the principal Regulations” are to the Insolvency Regulations 1986[^f00002].

## Explanatory note

(This note does not form part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Insolvency Regulations 1986. They increase broadly in line with inflation the rates of remuneration payable to the official receiver where he is an interim receiver or provisional liquidator or where he performs any duty as liquidator or trustee for which remuneration or a fee has not otherwise been set by statutory instrument.
