# The Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (Consequential Amendments) (England) Order 2010

Made: 1st March 2010

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 2010

Coming into force: 1st April 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 85(2) and (3) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (Consequential Amendments) (England) Order 2010.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on 1st April 2010.

#### Amendments to the Town and Country Planning (General Development Procedure) Order 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Town and Country Planning (General Development Procedure) Order 1995[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 1(2) (citation, commencement and interpretation) after the definition of “reserved matters” insert—

> > “responsible regional authority” has the same meaning as in section 72 (responsible regional authorities) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;

- (3) In article 2B(3)(a) (local development orders) for “the body recognised” to “regional planning body” substitute “the responsible regional authority”.

- (4) In article 10(2)(n) (consultations before the grant of permission)—

- (a) omit “section 7 and”; and

- (b) after the words “that Act” insert “in relation to London or a regional strategy within the meaning of section 70 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (regional strategy) in relation to the rest of England”.

- (5) In the heading to article 11 (consultation with RPB or county planning authority) in relation to England for “RPB” substitute “responsible regional authority”.

#### Amendments to the Town and Country Planning (Development Plan) (England) Regulations 1999

###### 3

- (1) The Town and Country Planning (Development Plan) (England) Regulations 1999[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) for the definition of “RSS” substitute ““regional strategy” has the same meaning as in Part 5 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;”.

- (3) In regulation 24A(11)(b) (further deposit of proposals) for “an RSS” substitute “a regional strategy”.

#### Amendments to the Town and Country Planning (Local Development) (England) Regulations 2004

###### 4

- (1) The Town and Country Planning (Local Development) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) for the definition of “regional planning body” substitute—

> > “regional strategy” has the same meaning as in section 70 (regional strategy) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;

- (b) after the definition of “relevant authority” insert—

> > “responsible regional authority” has the same meaning as in section 72 (responsible regional authorities) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;

; and

- (c) in the definition of “specific consultation bodies”—

- (i) in paragraph (a) for “regional planning body” substitute “responsible regional authority”; and

- (ii) in paragraph (a)(viii) omit the words “is in or”.

- (3) In regulation 5(2)(b) (survey of areas: county councils) for “regional planning body” substitute “responsible regional authority”.

- (4) Omit regulation 15(1)(a) (local development documents: additional matters to which regard to be had).

- (5) In regulation 17(2)(d) (public participation) omit “(2)(b) or”.

- (6) In regulation 26(2)(d) (public participation in the preparation of a statement of community involvement) for “regional planning body” substitute “responsible regional authority”.

- (7) In regulation 28(4) (representations relating to a development plan document) omit “(6) or” and “RPBs and”.

- (8) In regulation 29(1) (conformity with regional strategy) omit “(2)(a) or”.

- (9) Omit regulation 29(2).

- (10) In regulation 40(2)(d) (section 21(4) directions (call-in): supplementary) omit “(2)(a) or”.

- (11) Omit regulation 40(2)(d)(i).

- (12) In regulation 45(1)(f) (Secretary of State’s default power)—

- (a) for “RPB (if any)” substitute “responsible regional authority”; and

- (b) for “RSS” substitute “regional strategy”.

- (13) In regulation 45A(2) (provision supplementary to regulation 45) for “RPB” substitute “responsible regional authority”.

#### Amendment to the Town and Country Planning (Isles of Scilly) Order 2005

###### 5

Article 4(2) (exceptions, adaptations and modifications) of the Town and Country Planning (Isles of Scilly) Order 2005[^f00005] is omitted.

#### Amendments to the Town and Country Planning (Mayor of London) Order 2008

###### 6

- (1) The Town and Country Planning (Mayor of London) Order 2008[^f00006] is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 6(2)(e) (Mayor’s power to direct refusal of a PSI application) for “regional spatial strategy” substitute “regional strategy”.

- (3) For article 6(11) substitute—

> > (11) For the purposes of paragraph (2), “regional strategy” has the same meaning as in section 70 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (regional strategy).

#### Amendments to the Local Government (Structural Changes) (Transitional Arrangements) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

###### 7

- (1) The Local Government (Structural Changes) (Transitional Arrangements) (No. 2) Regulations 2008[^f00007] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 20 (modifications relevant to regional functions)—

- (a) in paragraph (1) for “Part 1 of the 2004 Act (regional functions)” substitute “Part 5 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (regional strategy)”;

- (b) in paragraph (1)(a) for “section 4(4) (assistance from certain local authorities)” substitute “section 71(3) (Leaders’ Boards)”; and

- (c) in paragraph (1)(b) for “section 4(4)” substitute “section 71(3)”.

#### Amendments to the Infrastructure Planning (National Policy Statement Consultation) Regulations 2009

###### 8

- (1) The Infrastructure Planning (National Policy Statement Consultation) Regulations 2009[^f00008] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “regional planning body”; and

- (b) after the definition of “relevant Northern Ireland department” insert—

> > “responsible regional authority” has the same meaning as in section 72 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (responsible regional authorities);

- (3) In regulation 3(3)(d) (consultation) for “Regional planning bodies” substitute “responsible regional authorities”.

#### Amendments to the Infrastructure Planning (Applications: Prescribed Forms and Procedure) Regulations 2009

###### 9

- (1) The Infrastructure Planning (Applications: Prescribed Forms and Procedure) Regulations 2009[^f00009] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “Regional Planning Body”; and

- (b) after the definition of “replacement land” insert—

> > “responsible regional authority” has the same meaning as in section 72 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (responsible regional authorities);

- (3) In Schedule 1 for “Regional Planning Body” substitute “responsible regional authority”.

#### Revocations

###### 10

The following statutory instruments are revoked—

- (a) the Town and Country Planning (Regional Planning) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00010];

- (b) the Town and Country Planning (Initial Regional Spatial Strategy) (England) Regulations 2004[^f00011];

- (c) the Town and Country Planning (Regions) (National Parks) (England) Order 2004[^f00012];

- (d) the Town and Country Planning (Regional Planning Guidance as Revision of Regional Spatial Strategy) Order 2004[^f00013]; and

- (e) the Town and Country Planning (Regions) (New Forest National Park) (England) Order 2007[^f00014].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Rosie Winterton — Minister of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 1st March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes consequential amendments in connection with the commencement of Part 5 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act [2009 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/20) (regional strategy). No impact assessment has been produced for this Order as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
