# The Direct Payments to Farmers (Crop Diversification Derogation) (England) Regulations 2020

Made: at 12.46 p.m. on 30th April 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 2.45 p.m. on 30th April 2020

Coming into force: 1st May 2020

The Secretary of State is of the opinion that, in order to resolve problems arising from flooding in England, it is necessary and justifiable in England to derogate from the provisions of Regulation (EU) 1307/2013 for claim year 2020 as respects the crop diversification requirements, the extent and period of that derogation being, in the Secretary of State’s opinion, strictly necessary.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Direct Payments to Farmers (Crop Diversification Derogation) (England) Regulations 2020 and come into force on 1st May 2020.

- (2) These Regulations apply in England.

#### Derogation from the crop diversification requirements

###### 2

Articles 43(2)(a) and 44 of Regulation (EU) 1307/2013[^f00003] do not apply in respect of claim year 2020.

## Signed

Victoria Prentis — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — At 12.46 p.m. on 30th April 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make derogations from Regulation (EU) 1307/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing rules for direct payments to farmers under support schemes within the framework of the common agricultural policy. Under these Regulations, the crop diversification requirements are disapplied in England for claim year 2020.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
