# The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 4) Order 1994

Made: 19th July 1994

Coming into force: 1st August 1994

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of July 1994 Present, The Queens Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 2 of the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 4) Order 1994, and shall come into force on 1st August 1994.

###### 2

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 1994[^f00002]is revoked.

###### 3

The Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1993[^f00003]is amended by deleting the Schedule and substituting therefor the following—

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which is made under the Child Abduction and Custody Act 1985, amends the Child Abduction and Custody (Parties to Conventions) Order 1993[^f00004]in order to specify that, for the purposes of that Act, Finland and St. Kitts and Nevis are Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, The Hague, 25th October 1980, with effect from 1st August 1994.
