# The Glan-y-Môr National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1998

Made: 6th March 1998

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1998

Coming into force: 31st March 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and other establishments and facilities formerly managed by West Glamorgan District Health Authority[^f00002] resulting from the establishment of Glan-y-Mor National Health Service Trust[^f00003], having consulted Morgannwg Health Authority[^f00004] and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Glan-y-Môr National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 31st March 1998.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Authority” means Morgannwg Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means Glan-y-Môr National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 31st March 1997, which schedule has been signed on behalf of the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 31st March 1998 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

Win Griffiths — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 6th March 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to Glan-y-Môr National Health Service Trust on 31st March 1998 trust property formerly held by Morgannwg Health Authority. A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained from the Welsh Office, HFM3, New Crown Building, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF1 3NQ.
