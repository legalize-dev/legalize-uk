# The Social Security (Non-Benefit Payments) Regulations 2026

Made: 16th November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 18th November 2026

Coming into force: 18th December 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 121DA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/121DA)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/121DA) and [189](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/189)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/189) and [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/189) of the [Social Security Administration Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents)[^f00001].

In accordance with [section 173](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/173)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/173)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/173) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents), the Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that the proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Non-Benefit Payments) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 18th December 2026.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland.

#### Prescribed non-benefit payments

###### 2

- (1) A payment made under the Jobs Guarantee or Youth Jobs Grant is a prescribed non-benefit payment for the purposes of [section 121DA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/121DA)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/121DA) of the [Social Security Administration Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents).

- (2) For the purposes of [paragraph (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/1207/regulation/2/1/made)—

- (a) “*Jobs Guarantee*” means an arrangement made by the Secretary of State under [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50/section/2)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50/section/2) of the [Employment and Training Act 1973](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50/contents)[^f00002] and known by that name, and

- (b) “*Youth Jobs Grant*” means an arrangement made by the Secretary of State under [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50/section/2)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50/section/2) of the [Employment and Training Act 1973](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50/contents) and known by that name.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations specify which payments are a “non-benefit payment” under [section 121DA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/121DA)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/121DA) of the [Social Security Administration Act 1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents) (“*the Administration Act*”).

Where a Jobs Guarantee or Youth Jobs Grant payment is made in consequence of a misrepresentation or failure to disclose a material fact, it will be a recoverable overpayment in the circumstances set out in [section 71ZI](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/71ZI) of [the Administration Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents) (overpayment of non-benefit payments: overview and recovery), and may be recovered as if it were recoverable under [section 71](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/71) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents) (overpayments—general). It also means that an administrative penalty can be offered as an alternative to prosecution under [section 115A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/115A) of [the Administration Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents), in relation to that overpayment.

Defining a payment as a non-benefit payment also expands the offences in [sections 111A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/111A) (dishonest representation for obtaining benefit etc) and 112 (false representations for obtaining benefit etc) of [the Administration Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents) to include conduct relating to prescribed non-benefit payments, meaning that the Secretary of State can investigate a person for fraud under those offences in relation to Jobs Guarantee or Youth Jobs Grant payments. Once [section 89](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/28/section/89) (DWP offence) of the [Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025 (c. 28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/28/contents) comes into force, the fraud offences under [sections 111A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/111A) and [112](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/section/112) of [the Administration Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents) can be investigated using the expanded enforcement powers in [the Administration Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5/contents).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary sector or community bodies is foreseen.
