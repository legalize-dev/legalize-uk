# The Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2023

Made: 19th May 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 6(2) of the Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022 (Commencement) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Provisions coming into force on 22nd May 2023

###### 2

Sections 1 to 5 of the Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022 come into force on 22nd May 2023.

## Signed

Benyon — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th May 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force the remaining provisions of the Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act [2022 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/22) (“*the Act*”) that did not come into force on the day on which the Act was passed. Those provisions (sections 1 to 5 of the Act) come into force on 22nd May 2023.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
