# The Importation of Live Fish of the Salmon Family Order 1986

Made: 17th February 1986

Coming into Operation: 18th February 1986

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food in relation to England and the marine waters adjacent to England, the Secretary of State for Wales in relation to Wales and the marine waters adjacent to Wales and the Secretary of State for Scotland in relation to Scotland (including the marine waters thereof), in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 1(6) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1937[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following order, a draft of which has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament in accordance with section 1(9) of the said Act:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Importation of Live Fish of the Salmon Family Order 1986 and shall come into operation on the day after the day on which it was made.

#### Disapplication of section 1(1) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1937

###### 2

Live fish of the salmon family which have been taken from Northern Ireland and have not at any time been in any country or territory outside Northern Ireland are hereby specified for the purposes of section 1(6) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1937.

## Signed

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 11th February 1986.

Michael Jopling — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Nicholas Edwards — Secretary of State of Wales — 17th February 1986

Malcolm Rifkind — Secretary of State for Scotland — 13th February 1986

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order specifies a description of live fish of the salmon family, namely, fish which have been taken from Northern Ireland and have never been in any country or territory outside Northern Ireland, for the purpose of excluding fish of that description from section 1(1) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1937 (“the Act”), which would otherwise make it unlawful to import or bring into Great Britain any live fish of the salmon family.

By virtue of the order and section 1(8) of the Act, it is lawful to import or bring into Great Britain fish of the specified description provided that the licensing provisions contained in section 1(2) to (5) of the Act are satisfied.
