# The Gas Act 1995 (Consequential Modifications of Local Acts and Orders) Order 1996

Made: 18th February 1996

Laid before Parliament: 21st February 1996

Coming into force: 1st March 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 16(3) of the Gas Act 1995[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gas Act 1995 (Consequential Modifications of Local Acts and Orders) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st March 1996.

###### 2

The local Acts specified in columns 1 and 2 of Schedule 1 hereto are hereby modified to the extent specified in column 3 of the Schedule.

###### 3

References, however expressed, to public gas suppliers or any particular public gas supplier in the local Acts specified in columns 1 and 2 of Schedule 2 hereto shall have effect as references to a public gas transporter within the meaning of Part I of the Gas Act 1986[^f00002].

###### 4

References in the local Acts specified in columns 1 and 2 of Schedule 3 hereto to any person authorised to carry on, in a particular area, an undertaking for the supply of gas shall have effect as references to any person carrying on the undertaking of a public gas transporter within the meaning of Part I of the Gas Act 1986 whose authorised area, within the meaning of that Part of that Act, includes that area.

###### 5

References, however expressed, to public gas suppliers or any particular public gas supplier in the local Orders specified in columns 1 and 2 of Schedule 4 hereto shall have effect as references to a public gas transporter within the meaning of Part I of the Gas Act 1986.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes modifications to local Acts and Orders in consequence of the enactment of the Gas Act 1995. Article 2 modifies the local Acts in the manner set out in Schedule 1. Article 3 amends the local Acts in Schedule 2 so as to substitute references to a public gas transporter within the meaning of Part I of the Gas Act 1986 for references to a public gas supplier. Article 4 amends references in the local Acts in Schedule 3 to any person authorised to carry on, in a particular area, an undertaking for the supply of gas so as to substitute references to a public gas transporter within the meaning of Part I of the Gas Act 1986 whose authorised area includes that area. Article 5 amends the local Orders in Schedule 4 so as to substitute references to a public gas transporter within the meaning of Part I of the Gas Act 1986 for references to a public gas supplier.
