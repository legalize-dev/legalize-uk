# The Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2014

Made: 11th May 2014

The Attorney General, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 185(5) of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014[^f00001], makes the following Order.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2014.

#### Provision coming into force on 2nd June 2014

###### 2

Section 149 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (inspection of Serious Fraud Office) comes into force on 2nd June 2014.

## Signed

Dominic Grieve — Attorney General — 11th May 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 149 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014. Section 149 amends the Crown Prosecution Service Inspectorate Act 2000 to allow for the inspection of the Serious Fraud Office by the Chief Inspector of the Crown Prosecution Service. It also provides the Serious Fraud Office with powers to share information with the Chief Inspector of the Crown Prosecution Service for the purposes of an inspection.
