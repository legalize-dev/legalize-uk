# The National Minimum Wage Act 1998 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999

Made: 9th March 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 51(1)(b) and 56(2) of the National Minimum Wage Act 1998[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the National Minimum Wage Act 1998 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order 1999.

- (2) In this Order–

- “the 1998 Act” means the National Minimum Wage Act 1998;

- “the 1948 Act” means the Agricultural Wages Act 1948[^f00002];

- “the 1949 Act” means the Agricultural Wages (Scotland) Act 1949[^f00003].

#### Commencement

###### 2

The provisions of the 1998 Act specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Transitional provisions

###### 3

- (1) Neither the amendments made to the 1948 Act by Schedule 2 to the 1998 Act, nor the repeals relating to the 1948 Act contained in Schedule 3 to the 1998 Act, shall have effect in respect of work done before 1st April 1999.

- (2) Neither the amendments made to the 1949 Act by Schedule 2 to the 1998 Act, nor the repeals relating to the 1949 Act contained in Schedule 3 to the 1998 Act, shall have effect in respect of work done before 1st April 1999.

- (3) The amendments made to the Agricultural Wages (Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00004] by Schedule 2 to the 1998 Act shall not have effect in respect of work done before 1st April 1999.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)
