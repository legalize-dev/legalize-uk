# The Employment Protection (National Health Service) Order 1996

Made: 7th March 1996

Laid before Parliament: 7th March 1996

Coming into force: 1st April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 18B(2)(b) of Schedule 13 to the Employment Protection (Consolidation) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Employment Protection (National Health Service) Order 1996, and shall come into force on 1st April 1996.

#### Specified descriptions of employment

###### 2

The following descriptions of employment are hereby specified for the purpose of paragraph 18B of Schedule 13 to the Employment Protection Consolidation Act 1978 (computation of period of employment—professional training in the health service)—

- (a) employment as a registered medical practitioner or registered dental practitioner in the grade of Registrar, Senior Registrar, Specialist Registrar, Registrar (Public Health), Senior Registrar (Public Health) and Specialist Registrar (Public Health)

- (b) employment in the grade of clinical scientist trainee or clinical psychology trainee; and

- (c) employment in the grade of general management training scheme trainee or financial management training scheme trainee.

## Signed

Stephen Dorrell — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 7th March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies for the purposes of paragraph 18B of schedule 13 to the Employment Protection (Consolidation) Act 1978 the descriptions of employment (being employment in which persons are engaged while undergoing professional training which involves their being employed successively by a number of different National Health Service bodies) where the continuity of the employee’s period of employment is, by virtue of that paragraph, maintained on his changing his employer for that purpose.
