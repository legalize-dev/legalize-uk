## Footnotes

[^f00001]: Section 48(1) of the Finance Act [2001 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/9) defines “the Commissioners” as those of Customs and Excise for the purposes of Part 2 of that Act and “prescribed” as meaning prescribed by regulations made by the Commissioners under that Part. The functions of the Commissioners of Customs and Excise were transferred to the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs by section 5(2) of the Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act [2005 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/11). Section 50(1) of the latter Act provides that a reference to the Commissioners of Customs and Excise shall be taken as a reference in any enactment to the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs.

[^f00002]: [2001 c. 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/9). Section 24(4) came into force on 11th January 2002 (article 3 of [S. I. 2001/4033 (C. 129)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/4033)); paragraph (da) of section 17(3) was inserted by section 22(3) of the Finance Act [2007 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/11).
