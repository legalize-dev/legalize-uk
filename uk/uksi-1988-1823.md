# The European Parliamentary Elections (Day of By-election) (Hampshire Central Constituency) Order 1988

Made: 24th October 1988

Laid before Parliament: 28th October 1988

Coming into force: 1st November 1988

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the European Parliamentary Elections Act 1978[^f00002], I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Day of By-election) (Hampshire Central Constituency) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st November 1988.

###### 2

The by-election to fill the vacancy in the European Parliamentary constituency of Hampshire Central shall be held on 15th December 1988.

## Signed

Douglas Hurd — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 24th October 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 15th December 1988 as the day on which the by-election to fill the vacancy in the European Parliamentary constituency of Hampshire Central shall be held.
