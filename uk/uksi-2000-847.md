# The Andover District Community Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2000

Made: 19th March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Andover District Community Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

#### Dissolution of NHS trust

###### 2

The Andover District Community Health Care National Health Service Trust established by the Andover District Community Health Care National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order[^f00005] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 19th March 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution on 1st April 2000 of the Andover District Community Health Care National Health Service Trust.
