# The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made: 5th December 2012

Coming into force: 1st January 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 132(1) of the Air Navigation Order 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 1st January 2013.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are amended by substituting for the definition of “Technical Instructions” in regulation 3(1)—

> > “Technical Instructions” means the 2013–2014 English language edition of the Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air, approved and published by decision of the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organisation[^f00003];

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2011[^f00004] are revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Simon Burns — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 5th December 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Air Navigation (Dangerous Goods) Regulations 2002 by substituting the definition of “Technical Instructions” in those Regulations. The new definition refers to the 2013-2014 English language edition of the Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air, approved and published by decision of the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organisation.
