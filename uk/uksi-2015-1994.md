# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2015

Made: 7th December 2015

Laid before Parliament: 10th December 2015

Coming into force: 21st December 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2015 and comes into force on 21st December 2015.

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3

The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2015[^f00003] is revoked.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

James Brokenshire — Minister of State — Home Office — 7th December 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 (“the 2000 Order”) by substituting a new Schedule into the 2000 Order. Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the new Schedule, or falling within a description in such an instrument, will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the 2000 Order).

The effect of substituting the Schedule is to add to and delete from the list of designated instruments.

The additions to Part 1 of the Schedule are UN Resolution 2219 (2015) of 28 April 2015 (Côte d’Ivoire), UN Resolution 2213 (2015) of 27 March 2015 (Libya), UN Resolution 2206 (2015) of 3 March 2015 (South Sudan) and UN Resolution 2216 (2015) of 14 April 2015 (Yemen).
