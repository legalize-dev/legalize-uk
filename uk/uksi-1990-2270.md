# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable Hackney and Islington Limited) Order 1990

Made: 15th November 1990

Laid before Parliament: 20th November 1990

Coming into force: 18th December 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable Hackney and Islington Limited) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 18th December 1990.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 15th November 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Cable Hackney and Islington Limited on 29th October 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the Hackney and Islington area. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 20th November 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Cable Hackney and Islington Limited will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
