# The Air Navigation (Amendment) Order 2008

Made: 9th July 2008

Laid before Parliament: 16th July 2008

Coming into force: 6th August 2008

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 9th day of July 2008Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 60 (other than sub-section (3)(r)) and 102 of and Schedule 13 to the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Amendment) Order 2008 and comes into force on 6th August 2008.

#### Amendment of the Air Navigation Order 2005

###### 2

The Air Navigation Order 2005[^f00002] is amended by inserting after article 167(1)(b)—

> > (c) the competent authority of the United Kingdom for the purposes of Annex III of Council Regulation [(EEC) No 3922/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3922) of 16th December 1991 on the harmonisation of technical requirements and administrative procedures in the field of civil aviation[^f00003].

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Civil Aviation Authority is the competent authority for the purposes of Annex III of Council Regulation [(EEC) No 3922/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/3922) (OJ No. L 373, 31.12.91, p.4. Annex III was inserted by Council Regulation [(EC) No 8/08](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1908/0008) of 11th December 2007, OJ No. L 10, 12.1.08, p.1).

Annex III contains operating rules for commercial air transport aeroplanes including provision for the issue or air operator’s certificates to commercial air transport operators.

An Impact Assessment has not been provided for this Instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
