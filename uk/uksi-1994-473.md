# The Combined Probation Areas (Buckinghamshire) Order 1994

Made: 27th February 1994

Coming into force: 1st April 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 2 of the Probation Service Act 1993[^f00001], and after the consultation required by that section, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (Buckinghamshire) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

###### 2

That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the Buckinghamshire Probation Area shall be amended by–

- (a) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Aylesbury, Buckingham, Burnham, Chiltern and Wycombe;

- (b) inserting, in alphabetical order–

- (i) in column 1 “Central Buckinghamshire” and corresponding to this in column 3 “3”;

- (ii) in column 1 “Wycombe and Beaconsfield” and corresponding to this in column 3 “3”.

###### 3

The six members of the Buckinghamshire Probation Committee appointed before 1st April 1994 by the justices for the petty sessions areas of Aylesbury, Buckingham and Chiltern shall decide by agreement, or in default of agreement by lot, which three of them shall continue in office as if appointed by the justices for the new petty sessions area of Central Buckinghamshire[^f00003].

###### 4

The five members of the Buckinghamshire Probation Committee appointed before 1st April 1994 by the justices for the petty sessions areas of Burnham and Wycombe shall decide by agreement, or in default of agreement by lot, which three of them shall continue in office as if appointed by the justices for the new petty sessions area of Wycombe and Beaconsfield[^f00003].

## Signed

Michael Howard — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 27th February 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the amalgamation of the petty sessions areas of Aylesbury, Buckingham and Chiltern into the new petty sessions area of Central Buckinghamshire and the amalgamation of the petty sessions areas of Burnham and Wycombe into the new petty sessions area of Wycombe and Beaconsfield both effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (Buckingham– shire) Order 1993 (S.I. [1993/2517](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/2517)) which comes into force on 1st April 1994.
