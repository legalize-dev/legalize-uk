# The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1990

Made: 9th January 1990

Laid before Parliament: 17th January 1990

Coming into force: 6th February 1990

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in the case of land in England, and the Secretary of State, in the case of land in Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 to the Agriculture Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Application, title and commencement

###### 1

This Order, which applies to land in England and Wales, may be cited as the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 6th February 1990.

#### Amendment of principal Order

###### 2

The Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby amended by substituting for the Schedule thereto the provisions contained in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on

John Gummer — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 8th January 1990.

Peter Walker — Secretary of State for Wales — 9th January 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) Order 1986, by substituting for the Schedule to that Order a new Schedule, in which the quota per hectare prescribed by article 3 of that Order is revised (article 2). Quota per hectare is prescribed for the purposes of calclating the payment which a tenant in England and Wales is entitled, in certain circumstances, to obtain from his landlord in respect of milk quota on the termination of the tenancy of the land, by Schedule 1 to the Agriculture Act 1986.
