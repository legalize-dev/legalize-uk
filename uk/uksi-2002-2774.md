# The Hastings and St Leonards Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

Made: 7th November 2002

Laid before Parliament: 8th November 2002

Coming into force: 1st December 2002

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Hastings and St Leonards Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st December 2002.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “Hastings and St Leonards Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Hastings and St Leonards Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00002].

#### The extension of the Hastings and St Leonards Education Action Zone

###### 3

The Hastings and St Leonards Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st December 2002.

## Signed

David Miliband — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 7th November 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Hastings and St Leonards Education Action Zone for a further period of two years.
