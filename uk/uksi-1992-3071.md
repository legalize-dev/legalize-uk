# The Civil Courts (Amendment No. 4) Order 1992

Made: 7th December 1992

Laid before Parliament: 10th December 1992

Coming into force

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 99(1) of the Supreme Court Act 1981[^f00001] and by section 2(1) of the County Courts Act 1984[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Courts (Amendment No. 4) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993 except for articles 3 and 4 which shall come into force on 28th January 1993.

###### 2

- (1) The holding of the Beverley, Gainsborough and Great Malvern County Courts shall be discontinued and Schedule 3 to the Civil Courts Order 1983[^f00003] shall accordingly be amended by deleting the entries relating to those courts.

- (2) The Bridlington and Kingston-upon-Hull County Courts[^f00004] shall have jurisdiction in proceedings commenced in the Beverley County Court before this Order comes into force.

- (3) The Lincoln County Court shall have jurisdiction in proceedings commenced in the Gainsborough County Court before this Order comes into force.

- (4) The Worcester County Court shall have jurisdiction in proceedings commenced in the Great Malvern County Court before this Order comes into force.

- (5) No process shall be invalid only because the holding of the court described therein by name has been discontinued by this Order.

###### 3

A district registry of the High Court shall be established at Salford, which district shall be defined by reference to the district of the Salford County Court.

###### 4

The Civil Courts Order 1983 shall be further amended as follows.

- (1) In Schedule 1—

- (a) in the second column opposite the entry relating to the Manchester District Registry the word “Salford” shall be omitted;

- (b) after the entry relating to the St. Helens District Registry there shall be inserted in each column the word “Salford”.

- (2) In Schedule 3 the letters “D.R.” shall be inserted in the first column beneath the entry relating to Salford.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 7th December 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Civil Courts Order 1983 so as to—

- (a) close the Beverley, Gainsborough and Great Malvern County Courts; the Bridlington, Kingston-upon-Hull, Lincoln and Worcester County Courts are given jurisdiction where proceedings have been commenced before the closures take effect (*article 2*);

- (b) to establish a district registry of the High Court at Salford (*articles 3 and 4*).
