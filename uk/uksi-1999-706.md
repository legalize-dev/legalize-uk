# The East Durham and Houghall Community College (Incorporation) Order 1999

Made: 10th March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the East Durham and Houghall Community College (Incorporation) Order 1999, and shall come into force on 1st April 1999 (“the Commencement Date”).

###### 2

On the Commencement Date there shall be established a body corporate called “East Durham and Houghall Community College” for the purpose of establishing and conducting from the operative date a new educational institution of that name (to be formed by the merger of Durham College of Agriculture and Horticulture and East Durham Community College).

###### 3

The “operative date” in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 1st June 1999, and the corporation shall conduct East Durham and Houghall Community College from that date.

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 10th March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation under section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of establishing and conducting East Durham and Houghall Community College and appoints 1st June 1999 as the “operative date” for the purposes of Part I of that Act.
