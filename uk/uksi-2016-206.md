# The Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2016

Made: 23rd February 2016

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 115(7) of the Deregulation Act 2015[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Deregulation Act 2015 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2016.

#### Provision relating to appeals coming into force on 2nd March 2016

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 91 (London street trading appeals: removal of role of Secretary of State in appeals) of the Deregulation Act 2015 is 2nd March 2016.

## Signed

Nick Boles — Minister of State for Skills — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 23rd February 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force a provision of the Deregulation Act 2015 (“the Act”) that transfers the function of determining certain London street trading appeals under the London Local Authorities Act 1990[^f00002] and the City of Westminster Act 1999[^f00003] from the Secretary of State to the Magistrates’ Courts, which hears all other appeals under those Acts.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on businesses and civil society organisations.
