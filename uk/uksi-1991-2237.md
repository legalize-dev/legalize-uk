# The Design Right (Semiconductor Topographies) (Amendment) Regulations 1991

Made: 3rd October 1991

Laid before Parliament: 9th October 1991

Coming into force: 30th October 1991

The Secretary of State, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the conferment and protection of exclusive rights in the topographies of semiconductor products, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by the said section 2(2) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Design Right (Semiconductor Topographies) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 30th October 1991.

###### 2

The Schedule to the Design Right (Semiconductor Topographies) Regulations 1989[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for Parts II and III thereof the Parts II and III set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## PART II — SPECIFIED COUNTRIES: CITIZENS, SUBJECTS, HABITUAL RESIDENTS, BODIES CORPORATE AND OTHER BODIES HAVING LEGAL PERSONALITY

- Australia

- Austria

- French overseas territories (French Polynesia; French Southern and Antarctic Territories; Mayotte; New Caledonia and dependencies; Saint-Pierre and Miquelon; Wallis and Futuna Islands)

- Japan

- Sweden

- Switzerland

- United States of America

## PART III — SPECIFIED COUNTRIES: CITIZENS, SUBJECTS AND HABITUAL RESIDENTS ONLY

- Finland

- Iceland

- Norway

## Signed

Reay — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Industry and Technology, — Department of Trade and Industry — 3rd October 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations replace Parts II and III of the Schedule to the Design Right (Semiconductor Topographies) Regulations 1989 with two new Parts to show the current position on the additional classes of qualifying persons enjoying legal protection of topographies of semiconductor products. The addition of Australia to the Part II countries and the transfer of the French overseas territories from Part III to Part II give effect to Council Decision [90/510/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1990/0510) (O.J. No. L285, 17.10.90, page 29). The United States of America and Switzerland are included by virtue of Commission Decision [90/541/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1990/0541) (O.J. No. L307, 7.11.90, page 21) and Council Decision [90/511/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1990/0511) (O.J. No. L285, 17.10.90, page 31).
