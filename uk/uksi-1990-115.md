# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable North (Dumbarton) Ltd) Order 1990

Made: 29th January 1990

Laid before Parliament: 30th January 1990

Coming into force: 28th February 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Cable North (Dumbarton) Ltd) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 28th February 1990.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Eric Forth — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 29th January 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Cable North (Dumbarton) Ltd on 18th January 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in Dumbarton Town and the Vale of Leven. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 30th January 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunica-tion systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Cable North (Dumbarton) Ltd will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Atlantic House, Holborn Viaduct, London EC1N 2HQ.
