# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2011

Made

Coming into force

Laid before Parliament-

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2011 and shall come into force on 28th February 2011.

###### 2

At the end of Part 1 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] add “Resolution 1970(2011) of 26th February 2011 (Libya).”.

## Signed

Alistair Burt — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 27th February 2011

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 (“the 2000 Order”) by adding to Part 1 of the Schedule to the 2000 Order United Nations Security Council Resolution 1970 (2011) of 26th February 2011 made in response to the situation in Libya.
