# The Environmental Noise (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 11th February 2010

Laid before Parliament: 22nd February 2010

Coming into force: 18th March 2010

These Regulations and the Regulations they amend, the Environmental Noise (England) Regulations 2006[^f00003], make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the Act. They make references to Directive [2002/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0049) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 June 2002 relating to the assessment and management of environmental noise[^f00004]. It appears to the Secretary of State that it is expedient for references in those Regulations to certain provisions in the Directive to be construed as references to those provisions as amended from time to time.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Environmental Noise (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2010; and

- (b) come into force on 18 March 2010.

#### Amendment of the Environmental Noise (England) Regulations 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Environmental Noise (England) Regulations 2006 are amended as follows.

- (2) Regulation 2 (interpretation) is amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (2), for the definition of “Directive” substitute—

> > - “the Directive” means Directive [2002/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0049) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 June 2002 relating to the assessment and management of environmental noise

; and

- (b) after paragraph (2), insert—

> > (3) In these Regulations, any reference to Annex I or VI or paragraph 7 of Annex IV of the Directive is to be construed as a reference to that Annex or paragraph of the Directive as amended from time to time.

- (3) For paragraph (2)(b) of regulation 4 (strategic noise maps: general requirements), substitute—

> > (b) the supplementary noise indicators,

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Environmental Noise (England) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/2238](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2238) as amended by [S.I. 2008/375](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/375) and [S.I. 2009/1610](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1610)) (“the 2006 Regulations”). The 2006 Regulations implement Directive [2002/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0049) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 June 2002 relating to the assessment and management of environmental noise (OJ No L 189, 18.07.2002, p 12) (“the Directive”).

The Environmental Noise (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/1610](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/1610)) amended the definition of “Directive” in the 2006 Regulations by adding the words “as amended from time to time”. The amendment was made in reliance on the power paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972. The effect of the amendment meant that all of the references to the Directive in the 2006 Regulations were to be construed as references to the Directive as amended from time to time in the future. This is referred to as an ambulatory reference.

These Regulations ensure that the scope of the ambulatory reference is limited to references in the 2006 Regulations to Annexes I and VI and paragraph 7 of Annex IV of the Directive (*regulation 2(2)(b)).* The Regulations also make an amendment to regulation 4(2)(b) of the 2006 Regulations to clarify the intended application of the supplementary noise indicators defined in Schedule 3 *(regulation 2(3*)).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the public, private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
