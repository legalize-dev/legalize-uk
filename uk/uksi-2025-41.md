# The Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2025

Made: 16th January 2025

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(3) of the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Provisions coming into force on 17th January 2025

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 2 (Power to make consequential provision) and in so far as not already in force the Schedule to the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act 2023 is 17th January 2025.

## Signed

Justin Madders — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Business and Trade — 16th January 2025

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations are the second commencement regulations made under the Neonatal Care (Leave and Pay) Act [2023 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/20) (“*the 2023 Act*”).

These Regulations bring into force, on 17th January 2025, the provisions of the 2023 Act not already in force.

Section 3 of the 2023 Act (Extent, commencement and short title) came into force on 24th May 2023 on Royal Assent.
