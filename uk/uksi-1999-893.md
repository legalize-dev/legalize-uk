# The Chase Farm Hospitals and the Wellhouse National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999

Made: 19th March 1999

Coming into force: 1st April 1999

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00003], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Chase Farm Hospitals and the Wellhouse National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 1999.

#### Dissolution of NHS trusts

###### 2

- (1) The Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust established by the Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00005] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

- (2) The Wellhouse National Health Service Trust established by the Wellhouse National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1991[^f00006] shall be dissolved on 1st April 1999, and accordingly that Order revoked on that date.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

John Denham — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 19th March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves on 1st April 1999:

- the Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust, and

- the Wellhouse National Health Service Trust.

It should be read in conjunction with the Barnet and Chase Farm Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00007], which establishes a new NHS trust.
