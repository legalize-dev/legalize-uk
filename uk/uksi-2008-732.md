# The Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 12th March 2008

Laid before Parliament: 14th March 2008

Coming into force: 6th April 2008

The Secretary of State for Innovation, Universities and Skills, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973 , makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 6th April 2008.

#### Amendment of the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Fees) Regulations 2004

###### 2

In Schedule 1 to the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Fees) Regulations 2004 , the following is substituted for paragraph (a)—

> > (a) an amount per officer for the time spent in providing the service, calculated in accordance with the following table—

| *Regulations relating to the relevant service* | *Grade of Officer* | *Amount per hour* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regulations 4, 5, 7, 8 and 8B | Test EngineerApprovals EngineerManager | £80.00£100.00£125.00 |
| Regulation 6 | Support OfficerAssessor/ Manager | £80.00£95.00 |
| Regulation 8A and 8B | Auditor | £85.00 |

#### Consequential revocations

###### 3

The following regulations are revoked—

- (a) regulation 2(b) of the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Fees) (Amendment) 2006 ; and

- (b) regulation 2(5) of the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Fees) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2006 .

## Signed

Ian Pearson — Minister of State for Science and Innovation, — 12th March 2008

We consent

Alan Campbell — Dave Watts — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 12th March 2008
