# The Exempt Charities Order 1995

Made: 23rd November 1995

Coming into force: 30th November 1995

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 23rd day of November 1995 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of paragraph (c) of Schedule 2 to the Charities Act 1993[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Exempt Charities Order 1995 and shall come into force on 30th November 1995.

###### 2

The University of Derby is hereby declared to be an exempt charity for the purposes of the Charities Act 1993.

## Signed

N. H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order declares the University of Derby to be an exempt charity within the meaning of the Charities Act 1993, with effect from 30th November 1995. An exempt charity is not required to be registered with the Charity Commissioners and the Commissioners cannot exercise any powers under the Act in relation to an exempt charity except at the charity’s request.
