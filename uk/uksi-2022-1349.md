# The Skills and Post-16 Education Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Regulations 2022

Made: 14th December 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(3) of the Skills and Post-16 Education Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Skills and Post-16 Education Act 2022 (Commencement No. 2) (England) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Provision coming into force on 1st January 2023

###### 2

Section 14 of the Skills and Post-16 Education Act 2022 (information about technical education and training: access to English schools) comes into force on 1st January 2023.

## Signed

Robert Halfon — Minister of State — Department for Education — 14th December 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force section 14 of the Skills and Post-16 Education Act [2022 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/21) (“*the Act*”), which increases the requirements on schools in England to provide access to providers of technical education, on 1st January 2023. They are the second commencement regulations to be made under the Act.
