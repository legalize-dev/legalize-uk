# The Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2004

Made: 2nd February 2004

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(1) and (2) of the Health Act 1999[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 14) Order 2004.

- (2) This Order applies to England only.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1st April 2004 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 6(2) of the Health Act 1999 (delegation of Health Authority functions relating to pilot schemes and section 28C arrangements)[^f00002].

## Signed

John Hutton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 2nd February 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2004 section 6(2) of the Health Act 1999 which inserts new section 28EE into the National Health Service Act 1977 (delegation of Health Authority functions relating to pilot schemes and section 28C arrangements).
