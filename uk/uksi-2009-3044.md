# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 15th November 2009

Laid before Parliament: 20th November 2009

Coming into force: 11th December 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 11 December 2009.

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] substitute the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3

The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2008[^f00003] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 (“the 2000 Order”) by substituting the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order for the Schedule to the 2000 Order (which was last substituted by the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment) Order 2008 (“the 2008 Order”)). Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the new Schedule or falling within a description in such an instrument will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the 2000 Order).

The effect of substituting the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order for the Schedule to the 2000 Order is to add to and delete from, the list of designated instruments, and to make some amendments to the list.

The additions to Part 1 of the Schedule are UN Resolution 1893 (2009) of 29 October 2009 (Côte d’Ivoire), UN Resolution 1857 (2008) of 22 December 2008 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), UN Resolution 1874 (2009) of 12 June 2009 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea), UN Resolution 1854 (2008) of 19 December 2008 (Liberia) and UN Resolution 1844 (2008) of 20 November 2008 (Somalia).

The additions to Part 2 of the Schedule are Council Common Position 2009/314/CFSP of 6 April 2009 (Belarus), Common Position 2009/351/CFSP of 27 April 2009 (Burma/Myanmar), Common Position 2009/615/CFSP of 13 August 2009 (Burma/Myanmar), Common Position 2008/873/CFSP of 18 November 2008 (Côte d’Ivoire), Common Position 2009/573/CFSP of 27 July 2009 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea), Common Position 2009/66/CFSP of 26 January 2009 (Democratic Republic of the Congo), Council Decision 2009/349/CFSP of 27 April 2009 (Democratic Republic of Congo), Common Position 2009/788/CFSP of 27 October 2009 (Republic of Guinea), Common Position 2009/164/CFSP of 26 February 2009 (International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia), Common Position 2009/139/CFSP of 16 February 2009 (Moldovan Republic), Common Position 2009/138/CFSP of 16 February 2009 (Somalia), Common Position 2009/68/CFSP of 26 January 2009 (Zimbabwe).

The deletions from Part 1 of the Schedule are UN Resolution 1782 (2007) of 29 October 2007 (Côte d’Ivoire) and UN Resolution 1792 (2007) of 19 December 2007 (Liberia).

The deletions from Part 2 of the Schedule are Common Position 2008/844/CFSP of 10 November 2008 (Belarus), Common Position 2008/349/CFSP of 29 April 2008 (Burma/Myanmar), Common Position 2007/761/CFSP of 22 November 2007 (Côte d’Ivoire), Common Position 2004/133/CFSP of 10 February 2004 (Former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia), Common Position 2008/104/CFSP of 8 February 2008 (Former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia), Common Position 2008/223/CFSP of 11 March 2008 (International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia), Council Decision 2007/455/CFSP of 25 June 2007 (Zimbabwe) and Common Position 2008/135/CFSP of 18 February 2008 (Zimbabwe).

Article 3 of this Order revokes the 2008 Order.
