# The Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1997

Made: 24th September 1997

Coming into force: 1st January 1998

The Registrar General, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 13(1), 16(1) and (2), 18(1) and (2), 18A(1), 19(2), 21(2), 21(4) and (5), 22(1), 24(1), 25(1) and (2), 27(1) and (3), 28A(3), 32(1), 40(2), 43(3), (4) and (5), 48, 54(1)(b) and 56 of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf and with the approval of the Secretary of State under section 54 of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 1st January 1998.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires–

- “the Act” means the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965;

- “birth” does not include a still-birth;

- “name” means Christian name or forename;

- “district registrar” has the same meaning as in the Act;

- “still-born child” means a child which has issued forth from its mother after the twenty-fourth week of pregnancy and which did not at any time after being completely expelled from its mother breathe or show any other signs of life; and the expression “still-birth” shall be construed accordingly.

- (2) Any reference in these Regulations–

- (a) to a numbered section is to a section bearing that number in the Act; and

- (b) to a numbered Schedule is to a Schedule bearing that number in these Regulations.

###### 27

#### Form for use in connection with the correction of errors, etc

###### 28

The Register of Corrections Etc. shall comprise insertions in the form set out in Schedule 25.

#### Forms for use in connection with alteration of status

###### 29

The notice prescribed for the purposes of section 48 shall–

- (a) in respect of a decree of divorce or nullity of marriage be in the form of an extract of the decree;

- (b) in the respect of a decree of declarator altering the status of a person be in the form set out in Schedule 26.

#### Revocations

###### 30

The Regulations specified in Schedule 27 are hereby revoked so far as still in force.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations consolidate with only minor drafting amendments and revoke the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1965 and the Regulations amending those Regulations. Minor amendments include changes to the style and layout of the forms.
