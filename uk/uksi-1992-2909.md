# The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 6) Regulations 1992

Made: 19th November 1992

Laid before Parliament: 26th November 1992

Coming into force: 31st December 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1), (2) and (5) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) (No. 6) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 31st December 1992 immediately after the Road Vehicles (Construction Use) (Amendment) (No. 5) Regulations 1992[^f00002] come into force.

#### Preliminary

###### 2

The Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986[^f00003] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

#### Amendments related to exhaust emissions

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 61 shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (4)(b)(ii), after the words “31st December 1992” there shall be inserted the words “, or in a case where the vehicle is a vehicle to which Part III of Schedule 1B of the Type Approval (Great Britain) Regulations[^f00004] applies,”.

- (3) In paragraph (7), before “(8)” there shall be inserted “(7A),”.

- (4) After paragraph (7), there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> > (7A) In relation to a vehicle to which Part III of Schedule 1B of the Type Approval (Great Britain) Regulations applies, item 8 of Table II shall have effect as if for the entry in column (3) there were substituted “31st December 1993”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Kenneth Carlisle — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 19th November 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations further amend the Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986.

2 S.I. [1992/2137](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2137) amended the 1986 Regulations by requiring certain classes of vehicle first used on or after 31st December 1992 to meet the exhaust emission requirements set by the Council Directive [91/441/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0441).

3 S.I. [1992/1341](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1341) amended the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984 (S.I. [1984/981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1984/981)) by imposing similar requirements as compulsory type approval requirements applicable to vehicles of the relevant classes as from 31st December 1992.

4 The 1984 Regulations were further amended by S.I. [1992/2908](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2908) so as to exempt a limited class of vehicles from compulsory compliance with the new type approval requirements until 30th December 1993.

5 The effect of the amendments made by these Regulations is that vehicles which are exempt from the new compulsory type approval requirements until 30th December 1993 are also exempt from the equivalent requirements in the 1986 Regulations provided that they are first used on or before that date.
