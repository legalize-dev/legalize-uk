# The Court of Protection (Amendment) Rules 2003

Made: 10th July 2003

Laid before Parliament: 11th July 2003

Coming into force: 1st August 2003

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 106 and 108 of the Mental Health Act 1983[^f00001], makes the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Court of Protection (Amendment) Rules 2003 and shall come into force on 1st August 2003.

###### 2

Column 2 of the Appendix to the Court of Protection Rules 2001[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph 1, for “£65” substitute “£70”;

- (b) in paragraph 1A, for “£500” substitute “£515”;

- (c) in paragraph 2, for “£205” substitute “£220”;

- (d) in each of paragraphs 3(1)(iv), (v) and (vi), for “£115” substitute “£120”;

- (e) in paragraph 3(2), for “£115” substitute “£120”;

- (f) in paragraph 3(3), for “£490” substitute “£505”;

- (g) in paragraph 3(4), for “£180” substitute “£185”; and

- (h) in paragraph 3(5), for “£150” substitute “£155”.

## Signed

Falconer of Thoroton, *C* — Dated 8th July 2003

We consent

Jim Murphy — Joan Ryan — Two of the Lord’s Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 10th July 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules increase various fees payable under the Court of Protection Rules 2001 (S.I. [2001/824](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/824)) as follows—

| Fees | Old Fee | New Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commencement fee (rule 77) | £65 | £70 |
| Receivership appointment fee (rule 77A) | £500 | £515 |
| Administration fee (rule 78) | £205 | £220 |
| Transaction fees (rule 79): applications for orders etc under— |  |  |
| Mental Health Act 1983, s.96(1)(k) (exercise of powers) | £115 | £120 |
| Trustee Act 1925, s.54 (trusts) | £115 | £120 |
| Trusts of Land and Appointment of Trustees Act 1996, s.20 (authorisation of person to act as trustee) | £115 | £120 |
| Trustee Act 1925, s.36(9) (appointment of trustees) | £115 | £120 |
| Mental Health Act 1983, s.96(1)(e) (execution of will) | £490 | £505 |
| Application for the appointment of a new receiver | £180 | £185 |
| Application for an order or direction under s.96(1)(b) or (c) of the Act ordering or authorising the sale or purchase of any land | £150 | £155 |
