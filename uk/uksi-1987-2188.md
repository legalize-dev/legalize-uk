# The Kirkcaldy and North East Fife Districts (Montrave Estate) Boundaries Amendment Order 1987

Made: 10th December 1987

Laid before Parliament: 31st December 1987

Coming into force: 15th February 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 17(2) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973([^f00001]) to give effect to proposals made by the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland under section 14(2) of that Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Kirkcaldy and North East Fife Districts (Montrave Estate) Boundaries Amendment Order 1987, and shall come into force on 15th February 1988.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 1977 Regulations” means the Local Government Area Changes (Scotland) Regulations 1977([^f00002]); and

- “boundary map” means the map prepared by the Scottish Office, marked “The Kirkcaldy and North East Fife Districts (Montrave Estate) Boundaries Amendment Order 1987 Map” and deposited in accordance with regulation 3 of the 1977 Regulations;

and, in the Schedule to this Order, any reference to a boundary of a district is a reference to a boundary existing immediately before the commencement of this Order.

#### Application of Regulations

###### 3

The 1977 Regulations shall apply for the purposes of this Order to such an extent as may be required to implement the area changes effected by article 4 of this Order.

#### Area changes

###### 4

Those parts of Montrave Estate in Kirkcaldy District, being Area A, Area B and Area C described in the Schedule to this Order and shown delineated, hatched and marked “Area A”, “Area B” and “Area C” on the boundary map, shall be transferred to North East Fife District.

#### Consequential changes of electoral arrangements

###### 5

- (1) Area A described in the Schedule to this Order (being the Area A transferred by article 4 of this Order)—

- (a) shall form part of electoral division 26 (Pittenweem/St Monance/Elie/Largo) of Fife Region([^f00003]) and of ward 14 (Largo) of North East Fife District([^f00004]); and

- (b) shall cease to form part of electoral division 13 (Kennoway and Windygates) of Fife Region and of ward 26 (Kennoway) of Kirkcaldy District ([^f00005]).

- (2) Area B described in the Schedule to this Order (being the Area B transferred by article 4 of this Order)—

- (a) shall form part of electoral division 26 (Pittenweem/St Monance/Elie/Largo) of Fife Region and of ward 14 (Largo) of North East Fife District; and

- (b) shall cease to form part of electoral division 13 (Kennoway and Windygates) of Fife Region and of ward 25 (Windygates) of Kirkcaldy District.

- (3) Area C described in the Schedule to this Order (being the Area C transferred by article 4 of this Order)—

- (a) shall form part of electoral division 26 (Pittenweem/St Monance/Elie/Largo) of Fife Region and of ward 14 (Largo) of North East Fife District; and

- (b) shall cease to form part of electoral division 12 (Leven) of Fife Region and of ward 24 (Leven East) of Kirkcaldy District.

## SCHEDULE

#### Area A

The area bounded by a line commencing at a point Grid Reference NO 36810594 where the unnamed burn flowing from the north side of Kilmux Wood crosses the district boundary, thence northeastward along the unnamed burn to a point Grid Reference NO 36900595, thence generally southeastward by field boundaries to a point Grid Reference NO 37460516 on the south side of the A916 road, thence northeastward along the south side of the A916 road to join the district boundary, thence northeastwards and thereafter following that boundary generally southwestwards to the point of commencement.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers an area of approximately 1005 acres at Montrave Estate from Kirkcaldy District to North East Fife District and makes consequential changes to electoral arrangements.

The map annexed to and forming part of this note gives a general guide to the areas affected by this Order as from 15th February 1988.

Copies of the detailed boundary map defined in article 2 of this Order may be inspected at the offices of any of the local authorities referred to in this Order, and may be inspected at or purchased from the Scottish Development Department’s Map Library, New St Andrew’s House, St James Centre, Edinburgh, EH1 3SZ.
