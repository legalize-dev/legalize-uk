# The Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2012

Made: 16th November 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 32(3) of the Armed Forces Act 2011[^f00001]:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Armed Forces Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2012.

###### 2

In this Order “the Act” means the Armed Forces Act 2011.

#### Commencement

###### 3

The following provisions of the Act shall come into force on 14th December 2012—

- (a) sections 7 and 22;

- (b) section 30(1) so far as is necessary to bring into force paragraphs 5, 6 and 7 of Schedule 4; and

- (c) section 30(2) so far as is necessary to bring into force the entry in Schedule 5 relating to section 336(3) of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^f00002].

## Signed

Mark Francois — Minister of State — Ministry of Defence — 16th November 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force certain provisions of the Armed Forces Act [2011 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/18) on 14th December 2012.
