# The Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995

Made: 10th November 1995

In exercise of the power conferred on me by section 2 of the Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995 (Commencement) Order 1995.

###### 2

The Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995 shall come into force on 17th November 1995.

## Signed

P. B. B. Mayhew — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 10th November 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 17th November 1995 the Northern Ireland (Remission of Sentences) Act 1995.
