# The Central Manchester National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994

Made: 14th February 1994

Laid before Parliament: 22nd February 1994

Coming into force: 15th March 1994

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 92(1) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, having regard to the change in the arrangements for the administration of hospitals and facilities formerly managed by Central Manchester Health Authority resulting from the establishment of the Manchester Central Hospitals and Community Care National Health Service Trust[^f00002], having consulted that Authority and that Trust in accordance with section 92(3) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Central Manchester National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Trust Property) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 15th March 1994.

- (2) In this Order: “the Authority” means Central Manchester Health Authority;

- “the Trust” means the Central Manchester National Health Service Trust;

- “the trust property” means the items specified in a schedule of trust property prepared by and agreed upon by the Authority and the Trust on 11th March 1993, which schedule has been signed on behalf of both the Authority and the Trust.

#### Transfer of trust property

###### 2

On 15th March 1994 the trust property shall be transferred from the Authority to the Trust.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health 14th February 1994

Tom Sackville — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers to the Central Manchester National Health Service Trust on 15th March 1994 trust property formerly held by the Central Manchester Health Authority.

A copy of the schedule of trust property to which the Order refers can be obtained at the Department of Health, National Health Service Trust Unit, Quarry House, Quarry Hill, Leeds LS2 7UE.
