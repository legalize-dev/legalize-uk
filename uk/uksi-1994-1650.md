# The Bournemouth and West Hampshire Water (Amendment of Local Enactments etc.) Order 1994

Made: 20th June 1994

Coming into force: 1st July 1994

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Bournemouth and West Hampshire (Amendment of Local Enactments etc.) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st July 1994.

#### Amendment of references in local statutory provisions etc.

###### 2

- (1) As from 1st July 1994 any reference to, or to the undertaking of, Bournemouth and West Hampshire in any local statutory provision and in any charges scheme made by Bournemouth and West Hampshire under section 76 of the Water Act 1989 or section 143 of the Water Industry Act 1991 shall have effect, subject to the following provisions of this article, as a reference to Bournemouth and West Hampshire Water Plc or, as the case may be, to the undertaking of Bournemouth and West Hampshire Plc.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall only apply to any local statutory provision or charges scheme in relation to the area, things or persons in relation to which or to whom that provision or scheme has effect before 1st July 1994.

- (3) In section 11 of the Bournemouth Gas and Water Act 1940[^f00003] (restrictions on abstraction of water from river Avon), in subsection (2)(a), for the words from “the Avon and Stour Catchment Board” to “or any of them” there shall be substituted “and the Avon and Stour Catchment Board (in this section referred to as”“the catchment board”) or either of them

#### Repeal of local statutory provisions

###### 3

The local statutory provisions referred to in the Schedule to this Order are repealed to the extent indicated in that Schedule with effect from 1st July 1994.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends references in local statutory provisions and charges schemes for the purposes of the replacement of Bournemouth Water Plc and West Hampshire Water Plc as water undertakers by Bournemouth and West Hampshire Water Plc.
