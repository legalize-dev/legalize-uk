# The Service Voters’ Registration Period (Northern Ireland) Order 2008

Made: 30th June 2008

Coming into force: 1st July 2008

Accordingly, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(9) of the 1983 Act makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Service Voters’ Registration Period (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 1st July 2008.

- (2) This Order extends to Northern Ireland only.

#### Persons registered in a register of electors pursuant to a service declaration

###### 2

Section 15(2)(a) of the Representation of the People Act 1983 shall have effect in relation to the persons mentioned in section 14(1)(a) and (d) of that Act (members of the forces and their spouses or civil partners who have made a service declaration and are registered or entitled to be registered as electors in pursuance of it)[^f00002], as if for “the period of 12 months” there were substituted “the period of 3 years”.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 30th June 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order extends the duration of a service voter’s registration as an elector from 12 months to 3 years. It applies only to persons whose service qualification comes from being a member of the forces or the spouse or civil partner of such a member who is registered pursuant to a service declaration.
