# The Pig Carcase (Grading) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2003

Made: 17th November 2003

Laid before Parliament: 18th November 2003

Coming into force: 12th December 2003

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by that section, makes the following Regulations:

#### Title, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pig Carcase (Grading) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 12th December 2003.

- (2) These Regulations apply to England only.

#### Amendment to the Pig Carcase (Grading) Regulations 1994

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) of the Pig Carcase (Grading) Regulations 1994 there shall be substituted for the definition of “the Commission Decision” the following definition—

> > “*the Commission Decision*” means Commission Decision [88/234/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1988/0234) authorising methods for grading pig carcases in the United Kingdom as amended by Commission Decision [88/478/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1988/0478) , Commission Decision [92/557/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1992/0557) , Commission Decision [93/445/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0445) , Commission Decision [94/336/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0336) and Commission Decision [2003/750/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0750) .

Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 17th November 2003
