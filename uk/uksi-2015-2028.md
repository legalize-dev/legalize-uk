# The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Local Authorities) Order 2015

Made: 14th December 2015

Coming into force: 5th May 2016

The Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster, in exercise of the power conferred by section 75(1) of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement, and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Local Authorities) Order 2015.

- (2) It comes into force on 5th May 2016.

- (3) In this Order “police area” means a police area listed in Schedule 1 to the Police Act 1996 (police areas outside London)[^f00002].

#### Designation of local authorities

###### 2

- (1) This article designates for the purposes of section 75(1) of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011 (appropriate officers) a local authority for each police area in England listed in the table in Schedule 1 and a local authority for each police area in Wales listed in the table in Schedule 2.

- (2) In respect of a police area in England listed in the left-hand column of the table in Schedule 1, the designated local authority is the corresponding local authority listed in the right-hand column.

- (3) In respect of a police area in Wales listed in the left-hand column of the table in Schedule 2, the designated local authority is the corresponding local authority listed in the right-hand column.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Local Authorities) (No. 2) Order 2012[^f00003] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates for the purposes of section 75(1) of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act [2011 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13) (“the Act”) a local authority for the police areas in England listed in Schedule 1 and the police areas in Wales listed in Schedule. The head of paid service of a designated local authority is the “appropriate officer” for the purposes of Chapter 6 of Part 1 of the Act (elections and vacancies of police and crime commissioners).

Article 3 revokes the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designations of Local Authorities) (No. 2) Order 2012 ([S.I. 2012/2084](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/2084)).

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
