# The Gender Recognition Act 2004 (Commencement) Order 2005

Made: 11th January 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 26 of the Gender Recognition Act 2004[^f00001], and having consulted the Scottish Ministers and the Department of Finance and Personnel in Northern Ireland, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Gender Recognition Act 2004 (Commencement) Order 2005.

###### 2

The Gender Recognition Act 2004 shall come into force on the 4th April 2005 in so far as it is not already in force.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Cathy Ashton — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Department for Constitutional Affairs — Date 11th January 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on the 4th April 2005 those provisions of the Gender Recognition Act [2004 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/7) which are not already in force. Sections 23 to 26, 28 and 29 came into force on Royal Assent.
