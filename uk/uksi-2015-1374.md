# The Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No. 2) Order 2015

Made: 8th June 2015

Laid before Parliament: 11th June 2015

Coming into force: 3rd August 2015

In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act, he has consulted with such persons or bodies of persons as appear to him to be requisite.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

The title of this Order is the Safety of Sports Grounds (Designation) (No. 2) Order 2015 and it comes into force on 3rd August 2015.

#### Designation

###### 2

The Hive Stadium, Camrose Avenue, Edgware, Middlesex, HA8 6AG, being a sports ground—

- (a) in England and Wales,

- (b) at which association football matches are played,

- (c) which is occupied by Barnet Football Club, a member of the Football League Limited, and

- (d) which in the opinion of the Secretary of State has accommodation for more than 5,000 spectators[^f00002],

is designated as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

## Signed

Tracey Crouch — Minister for Sport and Tourism — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 8th June 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the Hive Stadium in Edgware, Middlesex, which is occupied by Barnet Football Club and others, as a sports ground requiring a safety certificate under the Safety of Sports Grounds Act 1975.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument.

## Footnotes

The functions of the Secretary of State were, so far as exercisable in relation to Scotland, transferred to the Scottish Ministers by virtue of section 53 of the Scotland Act [1998 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/46).
