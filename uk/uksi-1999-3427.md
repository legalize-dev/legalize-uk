# The Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1999

Made: 15th December 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 68(3) of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999[^f00001], the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1999.

###### 2

Section 67(2) of, and Schedule 5 to, the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 (pre-consolidation amendments) shall come into force on 1st January 2000.

###### 3

The following provisions of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 shall come into force on 1st April 2000:

- (a) section 67(3); and

- (b) the entries relating to that Act in Schedule 6 (repeals).

## Signed

Jack Straw — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 15th December 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 67(2) of, and Schedule 5 to, the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 on 1st January 2000. Schedule 5 contains pre-consolidation amendments relating to youth justice. The Order also brings into force a number of repeals of provisions in the 1999 Act which are consequential upon the introduction of detention and training orders under the Crime and Disorder Act [1998 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37) on 1st April 2000.
