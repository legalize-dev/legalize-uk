# The Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1991

Made: 20th November 1991

Coming into force: 25th November 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws Extension Order 1991 and shall come into force on 25th November 1991.

###### 2

The period during which the Central Regional Council (Gartmorn Reservoir) Byelaws 1981 are to remain in force is hereby extended to 29th February 1992.

## Signed

T.D. Macdonald — Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office — 20th November 1991
