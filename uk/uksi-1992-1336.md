# The Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992

Made: 3rd June 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 8(3) of the Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992[^f00001] I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992 (Commencement) Order 1992.

###### 2

The Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992 shall come into force on 1st August 1992.

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 3rd June 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st August 1992 all the provisions of the Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992.
