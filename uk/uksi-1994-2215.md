# The Fishguard—Bangor Trunk Road (A487) (Pont Seiont Improvement, Caernarfon) Order 1994

Made: 25th August 1994

Coming into force: 8th September 1994

The Secretary of State for Wales makes this Order in exercise of his powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers:—

###### 1

The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in Schedule 1 to this Order shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

###### 2

The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

###### 3

The length of trunk road described in Schedule 2 to this Order and shown by broad striped hatching on the deposited plan shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a classified road as from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies the County Council of Gwynedd that the new trunk road is open for through traffic.

###### 4

In this Order—

###### 5

This order shall come into force on 8th September 1994 and may be cited as the Fishguard—Bangor Trunk Road (A487) (Pont Seiont Improvement, Caernarfon) Order 1994.

## SCHEDULE 1 — ROUTE OF THE NEW TRUNK ROAD

The route of the new trunk road is a route at Pont Seiont in Caernarfon in the County of Gwynedd as follows:—

- A route about 270 metres in length starting at a point on the trunk road 25 metres southeast of its junction with Pant Road and terminating at a point on the trunk road 145 metres northeast of its junction with St Helen’s Road.

## SCHEDULE 2 — LENGTH OF TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is that length of the Trunk Road (South Road) for a distance of approximately 180 metres between a point about 55 metres north east of its junction with Pant Road and a point 100 metres north of its junction with Saint Helen’s Road.

## Signed
