# The Local Review Committee (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1989

Made: 20th September 1989

Laid before Parliament: 4th October 1989

Coming into force: 25th October 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 59(6) of the Criminal Justice Act 1967([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Rules:

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1989 and shall come into force on 25th October 1989.

###### 2

There is added to the Schedule to the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Rules 1967([^f00002]), the following:—

> > Polmont young offenders institution

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 20th September 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Local Review Committee (Scotland) Rules 1967 by providing for the establishment of a local review committee at Polmont young offenders institution.
