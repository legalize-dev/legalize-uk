# The A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995 Amendment Order 1995

Made: 19th October 1995

Coming into force: 7th November 1995

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995, and shall come into force on 7th November 1995.

###### 2

The A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1995 is hereby amended so that—

- (a) in schedule 2B, the words in column (2) in item 9 are omitted, and

- (b) in schedule 4, after item 7, the following item is inserted—

> > (7A) From a point 6 metres north of the northern wall of 70 West Park Avenue northwards for a distance of 24 metres

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 19th October 1995

## Explanatory note

(This Note is not part of the Order)

This order amends the A205 Trunk Road (Richmond and Wandsworth) Red Route Experimental Order 1995 so that—

- the exemptions for parking and disabled persons' vehicles on the east side of Mortlake Road from a point of 6 metres north of the northern wall of 70 West Park Avenue northwards for a distance of 24 metres are deleted, and

- that area of road becomes subject to the prohibition of stopping at all times.

- A8.1.4. TRO 071
