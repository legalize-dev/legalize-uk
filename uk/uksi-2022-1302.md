# The Water Abstraction (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2022

Made: 6th December 2022

Laid before Parliament: 8th December 2022

Coming into force: 31st December 2022

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(2) and 219(2)(f) of the Water Resources Act 1991[^f00001] and sections 103(1) and 104(4) and (6) of the Water Act 2003.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Water Abstraction (Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2022 and come into force on 31st December 2022.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and apply to England only.

#### Amendment of the Schedule to the Water Abstraction (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2017

###### 2

- (1) The Water Abstraction (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2017[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the Schedule, in Part 2, in paragraph 10—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1) for “31st December 2022” substitute “the determination end date”;

- (b) after sub-paragraph (2) insert—

> > (3) In this paragraph, “*determination end date*”, in relation to England, means 30th June 2023.

## Signed

Rebecca Pow — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 6th December 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Water Abstraction (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1047](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1047)) (the “*Transitional Regulations*”) in England. The Transitional Regulations were made pursuant to amendments made by the Water Act[2003 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/37)to the Water Resources Act[1991 (c. 57)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/57)(“*the 1991 Act*”). Those amendments limited or removed certain exemptions from the restriction on abstraction in the 1991 Act (i.e. to limit or remove certain categories of abstraction for which a licence was not required). The Transitional Regulations provide a regime whereby certain abstractors who are not currently required to hold a licence to abstract water can obtain a licence. These Regulations extend the deadline by which the Environment Agency (as appropriate agency) must notify the applicant in writing of its decision on an application for a licence, from 31stDecember 2022 to 30th June 2023.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
