# The Police Cadets (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1990

Made: 20th December 1990

Laid before Parliament: 8th January 1991

Coming into force: 1st February 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 35 of the Police Act 1964[^f00001], and after taking into consideration the recommendations made by the Police Negotiating Board for the United Kingdom and furnishing the said Board with a draft of the Regulations in accordance with section 2(1) of the Police Negotiating Board Act 1980[^f00002], I hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Cadets (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1990.

###### 2

These Regulations shall come into force on 1st February 1991 and shall have effect for the purposes of regulation 3(a) and (b) as from 1st September 1990.

###### 3

The Police Cadets Regulations 1979[^f00003] shall be amended as follows:

- (a) in regulation 13(1), there shall be substituted for paragraphs (a) and (b) thereof the words “Schedule 2 hereto”;

- (b) for the Table in Schedule 2 (scales of pay) there shall be substituted the following Table—

| *Age* | *London* | *Provinces* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay from 1st September 1990 | Pay from 1st September 1990 | Pay from 1st September 1990 |
| Under 17 years | £4,704 a year | £4,002 a year |
| 17 years | £4,947 a year | £4,245 a year |
| 18 years or over | £5,427 a year | £4,725 a year. |

- (c) for paragraph 2 of Schedule 3 (charge for board and lodging) there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

> > (2) The annual rate of charge shall be—
>   (a) in the case of a police cadet attached to the Metropolitan Police force, £603;
>   (b) in any other case, £558.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 20th December 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Police Cadets Regulations 1979. Regulation 3 and (b) increase pay with effect from 1st September 1990 (retrospection is authorized by section 33(4), as applied by section 35(2), of the Police Act 1964). Regulation 3(c) increases charges payable by cadets for board and lodging provided by police authorities, with effect from 1st February 1991.
