# The Matrimonial Homes and Property Act 1981 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1981

Made: 2nd September 1981

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 9 of the Matrimonial Homes and Property Act 1981 (“the Act”), hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Matrimonial Homes and Property Act 1981 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1981.

###### 2

The provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into operation on 1st October 1981.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS COMING INTO OPERATION ON 1ST OCTOBER 1981

| Provisions of the Act | Subject matter of provisions |
| --- | --- |
| Section 7 | Powers of court to order sale of property in matrimonial proceedings. |
| Section 8 | Amendments consequent on powers of court to order sale of property. |
| Section 9 | Commencement. |
| Section 10(1) and (3) | Short title and extent. |

## Signed

Hailsham of St. Marylebone, C — Dated 2nd September 1981

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1st October the provisions of the Matrimonial Homes and Property Act 1981 specified in the Schedule to the Order.
