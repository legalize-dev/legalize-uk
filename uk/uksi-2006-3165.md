# The National Park Authorities (England) Order 2006

Made: 27th November 2006

Laid before Parliament: 1st December 2006

Coming into force: 8th May 2007

He has consulted the council for every principal area the whole or any part of which is comprised in a National Park in England other than the New Forest National Park as required by paragraph 2(3) of that Schedule.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Park Authorities (England) Order 2006 and comes into force on 8th May 2007.

#### Amendment of the National Park Authorities (England) Order 1996

###### 2

The National Park Authorities (England) Order 1996[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) for Schedule 1 (membership of National Park authorities) substitute the text set out in the Schedule to this Order; and

- (b) in paragraph 10 (disposal of abandoned vehicles) of Schedule 5 (application of enactments and instruments), for “regulation 12(1)” substitute “regulation 12(1)(e)”.

#### Amendment of the New Forest National Park Authority (Establishment) Order 2005

###### 3

In paragraph 9 (disposal of abandoned vehicles) of Schedule 3 (application of enactments and instruments) of the New Forest National Park Authority (Establishment) Order 2005[^f00003], for “regulation 12(1)(aa)” substitute “paragraph (aa) of regulation 12(1)(e)”.

## Signed

Barry Gardiner — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 27th November 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the National Park Authorities (England) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/1243](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/1243)) so as to alter the number of members appointed to the National Park authorities for each of the National Parks in England other than Northumberland and the New Forest.

The total membership of the Peak District National Park Authority is reduced from 38 to 30, and that of each of the other authorities is reduced from 26 to 22. Changes are made, in the case of each authority, to the numbers of members appointed by the Secretary of State and by local authorities.

The opportunity is also taken to correct a reference in that Order, and a similar one in the New Forest National Park Authority (Establishment) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/421](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/421)), to a provision in the Removal and Disposal of Vehicles Regulations 1986 ([S.I. 1986/183](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/183)).

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
