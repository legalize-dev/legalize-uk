# The A63 Trunk Road (Welton Interchange Slip Roads) (Trunking) Order 1995

Made: 14th February 1995

Coming into force: 1st April 1995

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf—

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the A63 Trunk Road (Welton Interchange Slip Roads) (Trunking) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

- (2) In this Order all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway.

###### 2

The lengths of highway described in the Schedule to this Order shall become a trunk road from the date when this Order comes into force.

###### 3

The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan numbered HA10/YHNMD 25, marked “The A63 Trunk Road (Welton Interchange Slip Roads) (Trunking) Order 1995”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at the Department of Transport, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY.

## SCHEDULE — LENGTHS OF HIGHWAY BECOMING TRUNK ROAD

- The slip road marked 1 on the plan north-east from its junction with the A63 trunk road for a distance of 40 metres.

- The slip road marked 2 on the plan south-west from its junction with the A63 trunk road for a distance of 80 metres.

- The overall length of the works described above shall be for a distance of approximately 0.12 kilometres, in the East Yorkshire Borough of Beverley in the County of Humberside.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

K. A. Lasbury — Director Network Management Division — 14th February 1995
