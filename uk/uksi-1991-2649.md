# The Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) (Designated Ports) Order 1991

Made: 21st November 1991

Coming into force: 2nd December 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 16(3) of the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) (Designated Ports) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

###### 2

In Part I of Schedule 6 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisons) Act 1989, there shall be omitted, under the heading “Seaports”, the word “Torquay”.

## Signed

Kenneth Baker — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secrataries of State — 21st November 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes Torquay from the list of seaports designated, for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 5 to the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisons) Act 1989, in Part I of Schedule 6 to that Act.
