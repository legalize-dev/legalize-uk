# The A27 Trunk Road (Southerham to Beddingham Improvements) Order 2006

Made: 10th August 2006

Coming into force: 21st August 2006

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the A27 Trunk Road (Southerham to Beddingham Improvements) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 21st August 2006.

- (2) In this Order, all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway.

###### 2

The length of highway described in the Schedule to this Order shall become a trunk road from the date when this Order comes into force.

###### 3

The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan numbered HA10/MP/064, marked “The A27 Trunk Road (Southerham to Beddingham Improvements) Order 2006”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at the ODPM-DfT Records Management Branch, Floor 13 (IMD), Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, East Sussex TN37 7GA.

## THE SCHEDULE — LENGTH OF HIGHWAY BECOMING TRUNK ROAD

The length of highway becoming trunk road is a route from a point starting on the A27 trunk road 310 metres north west of its level crossing of the Lewes to Eastbourne Railway and passing in a generally eastwards direction over the Lewes to Eastbourne Railway to end at the A27/A26 Beddingham roundabout as proposed to be improved, a distance of 0.81 kilometres.
