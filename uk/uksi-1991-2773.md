# The Waltham Forest Housing Action Trust (Area and Constitution) Order 1991

Made: 8th December 1991

Coming into force: 9th December 1991

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 60 and 62(1)(a) of the Housing Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consultation with the council of the London Borough of Waltham Forest in accordance with section 61(1) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order, a draft of which has been laid before Parliament and has been approved by resolution of each House of Parliament:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Waltham Forest Housing Action Trust (Area and Constitution) Order 1991 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, “the map” means the four maps each entitled “Map referred to in the Waltham Forest Housing Action Trust (Area and Constitution) Order 1991”, of which prints, signed by an Under Secretary of State in the Department of the Environment, are deposited and available for inspection at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment and the offices of the council of the London Borough of Waltham Forest.

#### Designation of housing action trust area

###### 3

The area comprising the six parcels of land shown on the map bounded externally by a broken black line edged internally with a stippled band is hereby designated as a housing action trust area.

#### Establishment of the Waltham Forest Housing Action Trust

###### 4

For the area designated by article 3 above there is hereby established a housing action trust, named the Waltham Forest Housing Action Trust.

## Signed

Michael Heseltine — Secretary of State for the Environment — 8th December 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates six parcels of land totalling approximately 26 hectares in the London Borough of Waltham Forest as a housing action trust area, and establishes a housing action trust for that area to be called the Waltham Forest Housing Action Trust. The six parcels of land are shown hatched black on the map forming part of this note.
