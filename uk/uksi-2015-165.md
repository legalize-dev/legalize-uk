# The Intellectual Property Act 2014 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2015

Made: 5th February 2015

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of powers conferred by section 24(1) and (2) of the Intellectual Property Act 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Intellectual Property Act 2014 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2015.

###### 2

In this Order “the Act” means the Intellectual Property Act 2014.

#### Commencement

###### 3

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 10(2) for all remaining purposes and sections 10(3) to (11) of the Act is 6th April 2015.

## Signed

Neville-Rolfe — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Business, Innovation and Skills — Department for Business, Innovation and Skills — 5th February 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the fourth commencement order made under the Intellectual Property Act [2014 (c.18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/18) (“the Act”). It brings into force on 6th April 2015 sections 10(2) to (11) of the Act. Section 10(2) of the Act is already in force only to the extent necessary to appoint the appointed person ([S.I. 2014/1715](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1715)). This Order gives full effect to new sections 27A and 27B of the Registered Designs Act [1949 (c.88)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1949/88), which introduces a new route of appeal against decisions made by the registrar relating to designs. Users now have a choice of using either a person appointed by the Lord Chancellor (an “appointed person”) or the High Court.
