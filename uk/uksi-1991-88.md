# The Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) Amendment Order 1991

Made: 21st January 1991

Laid before Parliament: 22nd January 1991

Coming into force: 19th February 1991

The Secretary of State for Trade and Industry, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 100(2)(b) of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001] and after consultation with the Director General of Electricity Supply and the Director General of Fair Trading,[^f00002] hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and comm

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) Amendment Order 1991 and shall come into force on 19th February 1991.

#### Amendment of the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) (No. 2) Order 1990

###### 2

In paragraph 49(2) in Part II of the Schedule to the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) (No. 2) Order 1990[^f00003] (descriptions of agreements exempt from the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976),[^f00004] the word “either” is omitted and there is inserted after paragraph (a)—

> > (aa) it is in, or is substantially in, the form of agreement specified in Schedule 3 to the Master Agreement; or
> (ab) it is in, or is substantially in, a standard form of agreement other than that referred to in sub-paragraph (2)(aa) above—
>   (i) of which a copy is provided to the Department not less than 21 days before the date on which the agreement is made, and
>   (ii) in respect of which the Department does not, within 21 days of the provision of the copy, give notice of objection to the party providing it; or

#### Amendment of the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) (No. 3) Order 1990

###### 3

The following amendments are made to Part II of the Schedule to the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) (No. 3) Order 1990[^f00005] (descriptions of agreements exempt from the Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976)—

- (a) in paragraph 69(2), the word “either”is omitted and there is inserted after paragraph (a)—

> > (aa) it is in, or is substantially in, the form of a greement specified in Schedule 3 to the agreement specified in paragraph 1 in Part I of this Schedule; or
> (ab) it is in, or is substantially in, a standard form of agreement other than that referred to in sub-paragraph(2)(aa) above—
>   (i) of which a copy is provided to the Department not less than 21 days before the date on which the agreement is made, and
>   (ii) in respect of which the Department does not, within 21 days of the provision of the copy, give notice of objection to the party providing it; or

- (b) in paragraph 71(2), the word “either”is omitted and there is inserted after paragraph (a)—

> > (aa) it is in, or is substantially in, the form of a greement specified in Schedule 3 to the agreement specified in paragraph 2 in Part I of this Schedule; or
> (ab) it is in, or is substantially in, a standard form of agreement other than that referred to in sub-paragraph (2)(aa) above—
>   (i) of which a copy is provided to the Department not less than 21 days before the date on which the agreement is made, and
>   (ii) in respect of which the Department does not, within 21 days of the provision of the copy, give notice of objection to the party providing it; or

; and

- (c) in paragraph 72(2), the word “either”is omitted and there is inserted after paragraph (a)—

> > (aa) it is in, or is substantially in, the form of a greement specified in Schedule 2 to the agreement specified in paragraph 3 in Part I of this Schedule; or
> (ab) it is in, or is substantially in, a standard form of agreement other than that referred to in sub-paragraph (2)(aa) above—
>   (i) of which a copy is provided to the Department not less than 21 days before the date on which the agreement is made, and
>   (ii) in respect of which the Department does not, within 21 days of the provision of the copy, give notice of objection to the party providing it; or

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 21st January 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Order amends the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) (No. 2) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/1319](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1319)) and the Electricity (Restrictive Trade Practices Act 1976) (Exemptions) (No. 3) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/1490](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1490)) by modifying the conditions applicable in relation to certain descriptions of agreements specified in Part II of the Schedule to each of those Orders.
