# The Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2007

Made: 27th August 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 107 of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 2007.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to England only.

#### Commencement of section 72 of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006

###### 2

Section 72 of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006 comes into force on 1st October 2007.

## Signed

Phil Woolas — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 27th August 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, which applies in relation to England only, brings section 72 of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act [2006 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/16) into force on 1st October 2007. This section amends the Road Traffic Regulation Act [1984 (c. 27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/27) by inserting sections 22BB and 22BC which make provision for traffic regulation on byways etc and other unsurfaced roads in National Parks.

Detailed explanation of section 72 can be found in the Explanatory Notes for the 2006 Act, which are available from The Stationery Office, PO Box 29, Norwich NR3 1GN (or online at www.opsi.gov.uk).
