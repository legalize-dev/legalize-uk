# The Tendring Hundred Waterworks Company (Constitution and Regulation) Order 1991

Made: 4th September 1991

Laid before Parliament: 13th September 1991

Coming into force: 1st December 1991

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred on him by section 101 (2), (6) and (7) of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Tendring Hundred Waterworks Company (Constitution and Regulation) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 1st December 1991.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 1845 Act” means the Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845[^f00004];

- “the 1863 Act” means the Companies Clauses Act 1863[^f00005]; and

- “the Third Schedule” means the Third Schedule to the Water Act 1945[^f00006].

#### Approval of proposal contained in the special resolution

###### 2

The proposal contained in the special resolution is hereby approved.

#### Date on which memorandum and articles come into force etc.

###### 3

The date specified for the purposes of section 101(2) of the Act as the date on which the memorandum and articles shall come into force and the relevant provisions shall cease to have effect is 1st December 1991.

#### Consequential provision

###### 4

The local statutory provisions referred to in Schedule 2 hereto are repealed to the extent indicated in that Schedule with effect from 1st December 1991.

## SCHEDULE 1 — DOCUMENTS HAVING EFFECT AS MENTIONED IN SECTION 101(2)(b) OF THE WATER ACT 1989

The documents comprising the Memorandum of Association of Tendring Hundred Water Services Limited and the Articles of Association of Tendring Hundred Water Services Limited[^f00007].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 101(2) of the Water Act 1989 provides for the replacement of provisons relating to the constitution and regulation of a statutory water company contained in its local Acts and Orders by provision contained in a memorandum and articles which have been approved by special resolution of the members of the company. The replacement provisions have effect only if they are approved by the Secretary of State and come into force on such date as he determines.

This Order approves a proposal by the Tendring Hundred Waterworks Company that a memorandum and articles of association should have effect in substitution for provisions contained in the Company’s local Acts and Orders (article 2). It also provides that the date on which those provisions cease to have effect is 1st December 1991 (article 3) and for the repeal of the superseded provisions and for consequential repeals (article 4).

This Order is associated with the company’s proposal to convert to the status of a registered company under the Companies Act 1985
