# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2010

Made: 10th November 2010

Coming into force: 11th November 2010

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of November 2010Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 5) Order 2010 and comes into force on 11th November 2010.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE 1

### Person appointed on 11th November 2010

James Eugene Kilner

## Signed

Judith Simpson — Clerk of the Privy Council
