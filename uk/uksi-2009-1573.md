# The Olympic Route Network Designation Order 2009

Made: 23rd June 2009

Laid before Parliament: 30th June 2009

Coming into force: 22nd July 2009

In accordance with section 11(4)(a) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted the relevant authorities referred to in that section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Olympic Route Network Designation Order 2009 and comes into force on 22nd July 2009.

#### Designation of Olympic Route Network

###### 2

The roads specified in the Schedule to this Order are designated as roads in the Olympic Route Network.

#### Interpretation of the Schedule

###### 3

In the Schedule—

- (a) the description of a designated road includes—

- (i) slip roads leading to or from it;

- (ii) roundabouts at either end of it including slip roads to and from such roundabouts;

- (iii) roundabouts that it crosses including slip roads to and from such roundabouts;

- (iv) gyratories at either end of it including slip roads to and from such gyratories;

- (v) gyratories that it crosses including slip roads to and from such gyratories;

- (vi) junctions at either end of it; and

- (vii) junctions that it crosses.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 11 of the London Olympic and Paralympic Games Act 2006 allows the Secretary of State for Transport to designate roads for the purpose of facilitating travel to and from London Olympic events or for other purposes connected with the London Olympics. Such roads are listed in the Schedule and form part of what will be known as the Olympic Route Network with effect from the coming into force of the Order.

In accordance with Section 40(6) of that Act the designation shall cease to have effect at the end of the fifth day after the day of the closing ceremony of the Paralympic Games 2012.

An impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from the Department for Transport, Zone 2/19, Great Minster House, 76 Marsham Street, London SW1P 4DR (Tel: 020 7944 2074) and is annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside the instrument on the OPSI website (www.opsi.gov.uk). Copies of each document have been placed in the library of each House of Parliament and may also be found by visiting the Department’s website at www.dft.gov.uk.
