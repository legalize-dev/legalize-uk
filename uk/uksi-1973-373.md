# The Local Government Act 1972 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 1973

Made: 2nd March 1973

The Secretary of State for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 273(2) of the Local Government Act 1972 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:—

### Title, application and interpretation

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1972 (Commencement No. 1) (England) Order 1973.

###### 2

This order shall not apply to the area consisting of the counties established by section 20 (new local government areas in Wales).

###### 3

- (1) The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of this order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

- (2) In this order—

- “*the Act*” means the Local Government Act 1972; and

- “*the Common Council*” means the Common Council of the City of London.

- (3) Any reference in this order to a numbered section, Part or Schedule shall be construed as a reference to the section, Part or Schedule bearing that number in the Act.

### Other provisions

###### 7

###### 8

Section 105 shall come into force—

- (i) a committee of an authority to whom the section applies other than a new county or district council or a parish council; and

- (ii) a joint committee other than one appointed by authorities all of whom are parish councils,

on 1st April 1973;

- (b) as respects members of a committee of a new county council, on 16th April 1973;

- (c) as respects members of a committee of a metropolitan district council, on 14th May 1973, and

- (d) as respects members of a committee of a non-metropolitan district council, on 11th June 1973.

- (b) as respects the accounts of district councils and the committees and officers thereof—

- in the case of metropolitan district councils, on 14th May 1973;

- in the case of non-metropolitan district councils, on 11th June 1973;

and

- (c) as respects the accounts of any authority to which article 6 above applies, at the date specified in that article in respect of that authority.

- (2) Sections 154(2) and 164 shall come into force—

- (a) as respects the accounts of the rate fund and superannuation fund of the City of London, on 1st April 1973.

- (b) as respects the accounts of authorities to be covered by resolutions of district councils under section 154(2)(d)—

- in the case of non-metropolitan district councils, on 11th June 1973;

- (c) as respects the accounts of any such joint committee as is described in section 154(2)(e), on the date on which the joint committee is established.

- (3) Section 251(1) in so far as it extends to paragraph 7(2) to (5) of Schedule 29 shall come into force on 1st April 1973.

### Interim action

###### 11

For the purposes of the preparation or service of any precept, or the preparation or approval of any rate, to be made in respect of a period commencing on 1st April 1974, any provision of the Act which is, under section 273(1) thereof, not in force on 1st January 1974 shall come into force on that date.

###### 12

For the purposes of taking any action authorised by any enactment (whether or not involving the expenditure of money) by any authority to ensure effective and convenient local government as from 1st April 1974, the provisions of the Act to which section 273(1) applies, so far as they have, on 1st April 1973, not been brought into force, shall be deemed to have come into force on that date.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 273(1) of the Local Government Act 1972 provides that the provisions of the Act specified in section 273(3) shall, except in so far as brought into force earlier by an Order under section 273(2), not come into force until 1st April 1974. This Order made under section 273(2), brings a number of the provisions specified in section 273(3) into force at earlier dates, which differ according to the purposes and areas in question. The Order applies to England.
