# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variations) Order 1998

Made: 13th May 1998

Laid before Parliament: 14th May 1998

Coming into force: 6th June 1998

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003] and now vested in him[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variations) Order 1998.

###### 2

The variation in the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 which was made on 13th May 1998 by the Secretary of State and is set out in the Appendix to this Order shall come into force on 6th June 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force a variation to the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 made by the Secretary of State on 13th May 1998.

The variation is set out in the Appendix. The effect of the variation is that Schedule 2 to the Scheme which groups service points, is amended in consequence of local government reorganisation, and set out in consolidated form.
