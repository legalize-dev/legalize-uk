# The Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made: 6th March 2008

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 2008

Coming into force: 30th March 2008

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 97(5) of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001]:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) (Amendment) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 30th March 2008.

###### 2

The Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) Order 2004[^f00002] is amended as follows—

- (a) in article 2 for the words “31st March 2008”, substitute “31st March 2009”; and

- (b) in the Schedule, after the entries relating to the local justice areas[^f00003] in Cheshire: Halton, Warrington insert—

> > - Hartlepool
> - Langbaurgh East
> - Teesside

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Hanson — Minister of State — Ministry of Justice — 6th March 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work (Pilot Schemes) Order 2004 ([S.I. 2004/2198](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2198)) (“the 2004 Order”) which specifies the local justice areas in which the provisions of Schedule 6 (Discharge of Fines by Unpaid Work) to the Courts Act [2003 (c.39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/39) are to be piloted and the period of the pilot. This Order extends the period of the pilot schemes provided for in the 2004 Order for a further year ending 31st March 2009. It also adds the following local justice areas in Cleveland to the pilot: Hartlepool, Langbaurgh East and Teesside.
