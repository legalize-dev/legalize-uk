# The Trinidad and Tobago Republic Appointed Day Order 1976

Made: 15th November 1976

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of November 1976

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, by virtue and in exercise of the powers vested in Her by section 1(1) of the Trinidad and Tobago Republic Act 1976, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Trinidad and Tobago Republic Appointed Day Order 1976.

###### 2

The appointed day for the purposes of section 1(1) of the Trinidad and Tobago Republic Act 1976 shall be 1st August 1976.

## Signed

N.E. Leigh

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies 1st August 1976 as the appointed day for the purposes of section 1(1) of the Trinidad and Tobago Republic Act 1976.
