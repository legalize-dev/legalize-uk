# The Hillingdon Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made: 23rd April 2001

Coming into force: 1st May 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in the exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 16A(1), (2) and (3) and 126(4) of, and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 5A to, the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, following compliance with the consultation requirements contained in regulations made under section 16A(5) of the Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Hillingdon Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st May 2001.

#### Amendment of Establishment Order

###### 2

In the Hillingdon Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2000[^f00003]—

- (a) in article 1(2), the definition of “community health services” shall be omitted; and

- (b) article 8 (exercise of functions) shall be omitted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

John Denham — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 23rd April 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the establishment order (“the order”) of the Hillingdon Primary Care Trust (“the trust”), a Primary Care Trust established by Order under section 16A of the National Health Service Act 1977 (“the Act”) by removing the restriction in article 8 of the order whereby, in the exercise of functions, the trust shall not provide services directly to patients, other than community health services.
