# The Football Spectators Act 1989 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1993

Made: 8th July 1993

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27(2) and (3) of the Football Spectators Act 1989[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Football Spectators Act 1989 (Commencement No.4) Order 1993.

###### 2

Section 9 of the Football Spectators Act 1989 shall come into force on 1st August 1993.

## Signed

Peter Brooke — Secretary of State for National Heritage — 8th July 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st August 1993 section 9 of the Football Spectators Act 1989 which makes it an offence to admit spectators to watch a football match designated under that Act unless the premises where the match is played are licensed under that Act by the Football Licensing Authority.
