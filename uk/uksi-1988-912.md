# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 9) Order 1988

Made: 18th May 1988

Coming into force: 20th June 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 9) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 20th June 1988.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of each pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — designated owners

- Blackfriars Oil Company Ltd., whose registered office is at Dutch House, 307/308 High Holborn, London WC1V 7LL.

- Hamilton Brothers UK Petroleum Corporation, whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at Devonshire House, Piccadilly, London W1X 6AQ.

- Hamilton Oil GB p.l.c., whose registered office is at Devonshire House, Piccadilly, London W1X 6AQ.

- Renown Petroleum Ltd., whose registered office is at 30 St. James’s Street, London SW1A 1HB.

- RTZ Oil and Gas Ltd., whose registered office is at 10 Babmaes Street, London SW1Y 6HE.

- Texaco North Sea UK Company, whose principal place in the United Kingdom is at 1 Knightsbridge Green, London SW1X 7QJ.
