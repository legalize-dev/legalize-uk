# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020

Made: at 11.45 a.m. on 30th October 2020

Laid before Parliament: at 3.00 p.m. on 30th October 2020

Coming into force: 31st October 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act, the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 31st October 2020.

#### Amendments to the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) Regulations 2020

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 2 to the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) Regulations 2020 is amended as follows.

- (2) After “The areas of the following are within the Tier 2 area—”, insert—

> > Luton Borough Council

- (3) After “*Derbyshire”,*insert—

> > Amber Valley Borough Council
> Bolsover District Council

- (4) After “Chesterfield Borough Council”, insert—

> > Derby City Council
> Derbyshire Dales District Council

- (5) For the words from “In the area of” to “Whitfield” substitute—

> > High Peak Borough Council

- (6) After “North East Derbyshire District Council”, insert—

> > South Derbyshire District Council

- (7) After “Durham County Council”, insert—

> > East Riding of Yorkshire Council
> Hull City Council

- (8) After “Westminster City Council”, insert—

> > Telford and Wrekin Council

- (9) After “*Leicestershire”*, insert—

> > Charnwood Borough Council

- (10) After “Oadby and Wigston Borough Council”, insert—

> > North East Lincolnshire Council
> North Lincolnshire Council

- (11) After “City of York Council”, insert—

> > Oxford City Council

- (12) After “*Staffordshire”*, insert—

> > Cannock Chase District Council
> East Staffordshire Borough Council
> Lichfield District Council
> Newcastle-under-Lyme Borough Council
> South Staffordshire District Council
> Stafford Borough Council
> Staffordshire Moorlands District Council

- (13) After “Stoke-on-Trent City Council”, insert—

> > Tamworth Borough Council

- (14) After “Coventry City Council”, insert—

> > Dudley Metropolitan Borough Council

## Signed

Nadine Dorries — Minister of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — At 11.45 a.m. on 30th October 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make changes to the areas which are subject to the restrictions set out in the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Local COVID-19 Alert Level) (High) (England) Regulations 2020.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
