# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Kingdom Cablevision Limited) (Glenrothes and Kirkcaldy) Order 1991

Made: 10th May 1991

Laid before Parliament: 15th May 1991

Coming into force: 12th June 1991

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powersconferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the followingOrder:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication SystemDesignation (Kingdom Cablevision Limited) (Glenrothes and Kirkcaldy)Order 1991 and shall come into force on 12th June 1991.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as apublic telecommunication system.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State — Department of Trade and Industry — 10th May 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Kingdom Cablevision Limited alicence on 18th April 1991 under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to thatlicence, in the Glenrothes and Kirkcaldy area. A copy of the licence waslaid before Parliament on 15th May 1991. This Order designates thosetelecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems.Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Kingdom CablevisionLimited will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of itsGlenrothes and Kirkcaldy public telecommunication systems when the Ordercomes into force on12th June 1991.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office ofTelecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
