# The Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (London Underground Works) (Amendment) Order 1999

Made: 28th July 1999

Coming into force: 1st August 1999

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(1) of the Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in this behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (London Underground Works) (Amendment) Order 1999, and shall come into force on 1st August 1999.

#### Amendment of the definition of the London Underground Works in the Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (London Underground Works) Order 1999

###### 2

In article 2 of the Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (London Underground Works) Order 1999[^f00002], in paragraph (c) of the definition of the London Underground works, for the words “dated 8th July 1999” there shall be substituted the words “dated 9th July 1999”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions

Glenda Jackson — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 28th July 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends part of the definition of the London Underground Works in the Channel Tunnel Rail Link (Nomination) (London Underground Works) Order 1999.
