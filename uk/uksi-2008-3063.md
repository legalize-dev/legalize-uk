# The Export Control (Iran) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made: 25th November 2008

Laid before Parliament: 26th November 2008

Coming into force: 27th November 2008

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and by sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7 of the Export Control Act 2002[^f00003], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export Control (Iran) (Amendment) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 27th November 2008.

###### 2

Article 1(2) of the Export Control (Iran) Order 2007[^f00004] is amended as follows—

- (a) in the definition of “the Regulation” after the words “Council Regulation [(EC) No 618/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0618)” insert “, [Commission Regulation (EC) No 116/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0116)[^f00005] and Council Regulation [(EC) No 1110/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1110)[^f00006]”.

- (b) in the definition of “Annex I items” after the words “listed in Annex I” insert “or Annex IA”.

## Signed

Ian Pearson — Economics and Business Minister — Department for Business, Enterprise and Regulatory Reform — 25th November 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order implements in part Council Regulation [(EC) 1110/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1110) which amends Council Regulation [(EC) 423/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0423)[^f00007] (“*the original Regulation*”).

Council Regulation [(EC) 1110/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1110) was adopted pursuant to Council Common Position 2008/652/CFSP[^f00008] which provides, amongst other issues, for the prohibition of the supply, sale or transfer of certain goods and technology that could contribute to the proliferation of sensitive nuclear activities or the development of nuclear weapons delivery systems.

This Order makes amendments to the Export Control (Iran) Order 2007 that correspond to the amendments to the original Regulation.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of business.
