# The A34 Trunk Road (Chieveley Interchange)(40 Miles Per Hour Speed Limit) Order 2007

Made: 24th January 2007

Coming into force: 12th February 2007

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 82(2), 84(1)(a) and (2) and 122(A) of, and paragraph 27(1) of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A34 Trunk Road (Chieveley Interchange) (40 Miles Per Hour Speed Limit) Order 200 and shall come into force on 12th February 2007.

###### 2

In this Order:

- “the A34” means the A34 Trunk Road near Chieveley in the District of West Berkshire;

- “the M4” means the M4 Motorway near Chieveley in the District of West Berkshire;

- “Junction 13” means the junction of the M4 with the A34;

- “carriageway” means any way (other than a cycle track) comprised in the trunk road over which the public have a right of way for the passage of vehicles and which has a surface suitable for the exercise of that right;

- “on-slip” means any road leading to the A34; and

- “off-slip” means any road leading from the A34.

###### 3

No person shall cause or permit any vehicle to be driven at a speed exceeding 40 miles per hour on the lengths of the A34 specified in Schedule 1 to this Order.

## SCHEDULE 1 — LENGTHS OF THE TRUNK ROAD SUBJECT TO A SPEED LIMIT OF 40 MILES PER HOUR

The following lengths of the A34 in the District of West Berkshire:

- (a) the Junction 13 Roundabout, point to point, a total distance of 554 metres from the eastern point where the centre of the M4 crosses the centreline of the Junction 13 Roundabout;

- (b) the A34 northbound off-slip to Junction 13 from a point 370 metres southwest of the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout to a point at its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout, a total distance of 370 metres;

- (c) the A34 southbound on-slip from Junction 13 from a point at the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout to a point 419 metres southwest from the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout, a total distance of 419 metres;

- (d) the southbound link between the Chalky Lane Roundabout and the A34 southbound on-slip from Junction 13 from a point 10 metres southwest from the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Chalky Lane Roundabout to a point 60 metres southwest from the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Chalky Lane Roundabout, a total distance of 50 metres;

- (e) the westbound link between the A34 northbound off-slip to Junction 13 and Oxford Road Roundabout from a point at its commencement from its diverge taper from the southbound off-slip to the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Oxford Road Roundabout, a total distance of 155 metres;

- (f) the eastbound link between Oxford Road roundabout and the A34 northbound off-slip to Junction 13 from the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Oxford Road Roundabout to a point at the termination of the merging taper with the A34 northbound off-slip, a total distance of 140 metres;

- (g) the A34 northbound on-slip from a point at the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout to a point 134 metres north of the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout, a total distance of 134 metres;

- (h) the A34 southbound off-slip from a point 129 metres north of the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout to a point at the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Junction 13 Roundabout, a total distance of 129 metres; and

- (i) the southbound link road between the A34 southbound on-slip from Junction 13 of the M4 and the Chieveley Services Roundabout, from a point 33 metres north of the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Chieveley Service Roundabout to a point at the centreline of its junction with the external perimeter of Chieveley Services Roundabout, a total distance of 33 metres.
