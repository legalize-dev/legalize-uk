# The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 9th March 1992

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 1992

Coming into force: 30th March 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10(1), 52(1)(a) and 60 of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001] and by the Department of Transport (Fees) Order 1988[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 30th March 1992.

###### 2

The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations 1981[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment, Use and Certification) Regulations 1981 as follows.

- (a) The fee payable for a certificate of initial fitness is increased from £130.50 to £136.50, both on the first application, and on any subsequent application when the test which that application will require includes a test of stability under Regulation 6. The fee payable for the certificate on any other subsequent application is increased from £15.60 to £16.30.

- (b) The fees payable for type approvals are increased from £753.00, £71.00, £373.00 and £1611.00 to respectively £788.00, £74.00, £390.00 and £1685.00.

- (c) The fee payable for the issue of a duplicate certificate is increased from £8.80 to £9.30.
