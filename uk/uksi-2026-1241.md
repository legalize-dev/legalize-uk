# The Pension Schemes Act 2026 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2026

Made: 24th November 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 133(9), (13)(a) and (14)(a) of the Pension Schemes Act 2026[^f00001].

## Part 1 — Preliminary

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pension Schemes Act 2026 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2026.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “*the 2004 Act*” means the Pensions Act 2004[^f00002];

- “*the 2005 Order*” means the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00003];

- “*the 2026 Act*” means the Pension Schemes Act 2026;

- “*the Board*” means the Board of the Pension Protection Fund;

- “*pension scheme*” means an occupational pension scheme within the meaning given in section 1 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00004] or section 1 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00005], as the case may be, and in regulations 2 and 3 it is the pension scheme in relation to which the Board has assumed responsibility giving rise to an entitlement to compensation.

- (3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00006], except section 39 (time), applies to Part 4 as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## Part 2 — Commencement

#### Commencement of section 109 of the 2026 Act

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into force of section 109 of the 2026 Act (indexation of periodic compensation for pre-1997 service: Great Britain), so far as it is not in force, is—

- (a) 31st December 2026, except in relation to a person entitled to—

- (i) periodic compensation in accordance with paragraphs 3, 5, 8, 11, 15 or 22 of Schedule 7 to the 2004 Act (pension compensation provisions)[^f00007] where paragraph (2) applies in relation to the person, or

- (ii) compensation on the discharge of a pension compensation credit in accordance with Schedule 5 to the Pensions Act 2008 (pension compensation payable on discharge of pension compensation credit)[^f00008] where paragraph (2) applies in relation to the transferor[^f00009];

- (b) 31st December 2027, for all purposes.

- (2) This paragraph applies where, in relation to the person or, where paragraph (1)(a)(ii) applies, the transferor—

- (a) the pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period,

- (b) that accrual was in relation to GMP indexed service in respect of which the compensation is payable, and

- (c) the admissible rules of the pension scheme—

- (i) did not include a requirement for all or any part of so much of the annual rate of a pension in payment under the scheme as is attributable to the person’s or, where paragraph (1)(a)(ii) applies, the transferor’s pre-1997 service to be increased annually,

- (ii) included such a requirement but it applied only in relation to a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the scheme or for the purposes of complying with a GMP equalisation obligation, or

- (iii) included such a requirement but it did not apply in relation to the pre-1997 service in respect of which the compensation is payable.

- (3) In any case where it is unclear whether the pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period, paragraph (2) has effect as if the scheme so provided.

- (4) In any case where it is unclear whether the accrual of a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the pension scheme (including by virtue of paragraph (3)) was in relation to particular GMP indexed service, paragraph (2) has effect as if the accrual was in relation to such service.

- (5) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the assessment date, the admissible rules of the pension scheme included a requirement of the kind mentioned in paragraph (2)(c)(i), paragraph (2) has effect as if the scheme included such a requirement.

- (6) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the assessment date, a requirement of the pension scheme of a kind mentioned in paragraph (2)(c)(i) (including such a requirement included by virtue of paragraph (5)) applied in relation to particular pre-1997 service, paragraph (2) has effect as if the requirement applied in relation to such service.

- (7) In this regulation—

- “*admissible rules*” in relation to the pension scheme, has the meaning given in paragraph 35 of Schedule 7 to the 2004 Act[^f00010];

- “*assessment date*” has the meaning given in paragraph 2 of Schedule 7 to the 2004 Act;

- “*GMP equalisation obligation*”, “*GMP indexation period*”, “*GMP indexed service*”, “*guaranteed minimum pension*” and “*pre-1997 service*” have the meanings given in paragraph 28(6) of Schedule 7 to the 2004 Act[^f00011].

#### Commencement of section 110 of the 2026 Act

###### 3

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into force of section 110 of the 2026 Act (indexation of periodic compensation for pre-1997 service: Northern Ireland), so far as it is not in force, is—

- (a) 31st December 2026, except in relation to a person entitled to—

- (i) periodic compensation in accordance with paragraphs 3, 5, 8, 11, 15 or 22 of Schedule 6 to the 2005 Order (pension compensation provisions)[^f00012] where paragraph (2) applies in relation to the person, or

- (ii) compensation on the discharge of a pension compensation credit in accordance with Schedule 4 to the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (pension compensation payable on discharge of pension compensation credit)[^f00013] where paragraph (2) applies in relation to the transferor[^f00014];

- (b) 31st December 2027, for all purposes.

- (2) This paragraph applies where, in relation to the person, or where paragraph (1)(a)(ii) applies, the transferor—

- (a) the pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period,

- (b) that accrual was in relation to GMP indexed service in respect of which the compensation is payable, and

- (c) the admissible rules of the pension scheme—

- (i) did not include a requirement for all or any part of so much of the annual rate of a pension in payment under the scheme as is attributable to the person’s or, where paragraph (1)(a)(ii) applies, the transferor’s pre-1997 service to be increased annually,

- (ii) included such a requirement but it applied only in relation to a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the scheme or for the purposes of complying with a GMP equalisation obligation, or

- (iii) included such a requirement but it did not apply in relation to the pre-1997 service in respect of which the compensation is payable.

- (3) In any case where it is unclear whether the pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period, paragraph (2) has effect as if the scheme so provided.

- (4) In any case where it is unclear whether the accrual of a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the pension scheme (including by virtue of paragraph (3)) was in relation to particular GMP indexed service, paragraph (2) has effect as if the accrual was in relation to such service.

- (5) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the assessment date, the admissible rules of the pension scheme included a requirement of the kind mentioned in paragraph (2)(c)(i), paragraph (2) has effect as if the scheme included such a requirement.

- (6) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the assessment date, a requirement of the pension scheme of a kind mentioned in paragraph (2)(c)(i) (including such a requirement included by virtue of paragraph (5)) applied in relation to particular pre-1997 service, paragraph (2) has effect as if the requirement applied in relation to such service.

- (7) In this regulation—

- “*admissible rules*” in relation to the pension scheme, has the meaning given in paragraph 35 of Schedule 6 to the 2005 Order[^f00015];

- “*assessment date*” has the meaning given in paragraph 2 of Schedule 6 to the 2005 Order;

- “*GMP equalisation obligation*”, “*GMP indexation period*”, “*GMP indexed service*”, “*guaranteed minimum pension*” and “*pre-1997 service*” have the meanings given in paragraph 28(6) of Schedule 6 to the 2005 Order[^f00016].

#### Commencement of section 111 of the 2026 Act

###### 4

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into force of section 111 of the 2026 Act (Financial Assistance Scheme: indexation of payments for pre-1997 service), so far as it is not in force, is—

- (a) 31st December 2026, except in relation to a person entitled to an annual payment, initial payment or ill health payment, where paragraph (2) applies in relation to the person;

- (b) 31st December 2027, for all purposes.

- (2) This paragraph applies where, in relation to the person—

- (a) the qualifying pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period,

- (b) that accrual was in relation to GMP indexed service in respect of which the annual payment, initial payment or ill health payment, as the case may be, is payable, and

- (c) the rules of the qualifying pension scheme—

- (i) did not include a requirement for all or any part of so much of the annual rate of a pension in payment under the qualifying pension scheme as is attributable to the person’s pre-1997 service to be increased annually,

- (ii) included such a requirement but it applied only in relation to a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the qualifying pension scheme or for the purposes of complying with a GMP equalisation obligation, or

- (iii) included such a requirement but it did not apply in relation to the pre-1997 service in respect of which the annual payment, initial payment or ill health payment is payable.

- (3) In any case where it is unclear whether the qualifying pension scheme provided a guaranteed minimum pension that accrued during the GMP indexation period, paragraph (2) has effect as if the qualifying pension scheme so provided.

- (4) In any case where it is unclear whether the accrual of a guaranteed minimum pension provided by the qualifying pension scheme (including by virtue of paragraph (3)) was in relation to particular GMP indexed service, paragraph (2) has effect as if the accrual was in relation to such service.

- (5) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the qualifying pension scheme began to wind up, the rules of the qualifying pension scheme included a requirement of the kind mentioned in paragraph (2)(c)(i), paragraph (2) has effect as if the qualifying pension scheme included such a requirement.

- (6) In any case where it is unclear whether, immediately before the qualifying pension scheme began to wind up, a requirement of the qualifying pension scheme of a kind mentioned in paragraph (2)(c)(i) (including such a requirement included by virtue of paragraph (5)) applied in relation to particular pre-1997 service, paragraph (2) has effect as if the requirement applied in relation to such service.

- (7) In this regulation—

- “*the 2005 Regulations*” means the Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005[^f00017];

- “*annual payment*” means the amount payable to a person in respect of each year determined in accordance with regulation 17 or 17C of, and Schedule 2 to, the 2005 Regulations;

- “*GMP equalisation obligation*”, “*GMP indexation period*”, “*GMP indexed service*” and “*pre-1997 service*” have the meanings given in paragraph 9 of Schedule 2 to the 2005 Regulations;

- “*guaranteed minimum pension*” has the meaning given in paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 2 to the 2005 Regulations;

- “*ill health payment*” means the amount payable to a person in respect of each year determined in accordance with regulation 17A of, and Schedule 2A to, the 2005 Regulations;

- “*initial payment*” means a payment made to a person in accordance with regulation 18 of, and Schedule 2 to, the 2005 Regulations;

- “*qualifying pension scheme*” has the meaning given in regulation 9 of the 2005 Regulations.

## Part 3 — Transitional provisions: Great Britain

#### Transitional provisions: value of “corresponding PPF liability”

###### 5

- (1) The amendments to Schedule 7 to the 2004 Act brought into force by regulation 2, so far as they affect the value of the “corresponding PPF liability” in section 73(4)(b) of the Pensions Act 1995 (preferential liabilities on winding up)[^f00018], do not apply to a pension scheme that is being wound up if the winding up began (or is treated as having begun) before 31st December 2026.

- (2) The amendments to Schedule 7 to the 2004 Act brought into force by regulation 2(1)(b), so far as they affect the value of the “corresponding PPF liability” in section 73(4)(b) of the Pensions Act 1995, do not apply to a pension scheme that is being wound up if the winding up began (or is treated as having begun) on or after 31st December 2026 but before 31st December 2027.

#### Transitional provisions: calculation of “protected liabilities”

###### 6

- (1) Where, in relation to a pension scheme, an assessment period has begun but not ended before 31st December 2026, and on or after 31st December 2026 the Board determines that the condition in section 127(2)(a) or 128(2)(a) of the 2004 Act would be satisfied had the amendments to Schedule 7 to that Act that have been brought into force by regulation 2, as at the date of the determination, been taken into account in the calculation of “*protected liabilities*” for the purposes of that section—

- (a) section 127(2)(a) or 128(2)(a), as the case may be, is treated as satisfied, and

- (b) if section 127(2)(b) and (c), or 128(2)(b) and (c), as the case may be, are also satisfied, the Board must assume responsibility for the pension scheme and give a copy of the determination to the trustees or managers of the scheme.

- (2) Where, in relation to a pension scheme, an assessment period begins on or after 31st December 2026 but before 31st December 2027 and has not ended before 31st December 2027, and on or after 31st December 2027 the Board determines that section 127(2)(a) or 128(2)(a) of the 2004 Act would be satisfied had the amendments to Schedule 7 to that Act that have been brought into force by regulation 2(1)(b) been taken into account in the calculation of “*protected liabilities*” for the purposes of that section—

- (a) section 127(2)(a) or 128(2)(a), as the case may be, is treated as satisfied, and

- (b) if section 127(2)(b) and (c), or 128(2)(b) and (c), as the case may be, are also satisfied, the Board must assume responsibility for the pension scheme and give a copy of the determination to the trustees or managers of the scheme.

- (3) In this regulation, “*assessment period*” has the meaning given in section 132 of the 2004 Act.

#### Transitional provision: reviewable matters

###### 7

Where a determination under regulation 6 is made—

- (a) Schedule 9 to the 2004 Act applies as if a determination by the Board under regulation 6(1) or (2) (whether the condition in section 127(2)(a) or 128(2)(a) would have been satisfied) were a reviewable matter,

- (b) the following Regulations apply to a review of a determination by the Board under regulation 6(1) or (2) (whether the condition in section 127(2)(a) or 128(2)(a) would have been satisfied) as they apply to a review of an approval of a valuation under section 144(2) of the 2004 Act[^f00019]—

- (i) the Pension Protection Fund (Review and Reconsideration of Reviewable Matters) Regulations 2005[^f00020], and

- (ii) the Pension Protection Fund (Reference of Reviewable Matters to the PPF Ombudsman) Regulations 2005[^f00021], and

- (c) the Pension Protection Fund (Provision of Information) Regulations 2005[^f00022] apply as if they required trustees or managers of the scheme to which a determination under regulation 6(1) or (2) relates to provide to members of that scheme a copy of a summary of the determination containing—

- (i) the matters in paragraphs (a) to (d) of the definition of a determination summary in Schedule 2 to the Pension Protection Fund (Provision of Information) Regulations 2005, and

- (ii) a statement describing how an application may be made to the Board for the review of the determination, how it will be considered and the Board’s powers on making a review decision[^f00023],

within the period of 28 days beginning on the day on which the trustees or managers receive a copy of a determination under regulation 6(1)(b) or (2)(b), as the case may be, of these Regulations.

## Part 4 — Transitional provisions: Northern Ireland

#### Transitional provisions: value of “corresponding PPF liability”

- (2) The amendments to Schedule 6 to the 2005 Order brought into force by regulation 3(1)(b), so far as they affect the value of the “corresponding PPF liability” in article 73(4)(b) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, do not apply to a pension scheme that is being wound up if the winding up began (or is treated as having begun) on or after 31st December 2026 but before 31st December 2027.

- (1) Where, in relation to a pension scheme, an assessment period has begun but not ended before 31st December 2026, and on or after 31st December 2026 the Board determines that the condition in article 111(2)(a) or 112(2)(a) of the 2005 Order would be satisfied had the amendments to Schedule 6 to that Order that have been brought into force by regulation 3, as at the date of the determination, been taken into account in the calculation of “*protected liabilities*” for the purposes of that article—

- (a) article 111(2)(a) or 112(2)(a), as the case may be, is treated as satisfied, and

- (b) if article 111(2)(b) and (c), or 112(2)(b) and (c), as the case may be, are also satisfied, the Board must assume responsibility for the pension scheme and give a copy of the determination to the trustees or managers of the scheme.

- (2) Where, in relation to a pension scheme, an assessment period begins on or after 31st December 2026 but before 31st December 2027 and has not ended before 31st December 2027, and on or after 31st December 2027 the Board determines that article 111(2)(a) or 112(2)(a) of the 2005 Order would be satisfied had the amendments to Schedule 6 to that Order that have been brought into force by regulation 3(1)(b) been taken into account in the calculation of “*protected liabilities*” for the purposes of that article—

- (a) article 111(2)(a) or 112(2)(a), as the case may be, is treated as satisfied, and

- (b) if article 111(2)(b) and (c), or 112(2)(b) and (c), as the case may be, are also satisfied, the Board must assume responsibility for the pension scheme and give a copy of the determination to the trustees or managers of the scheme.

- (3) In this regulation, “*assessment period*” has the meaning given in article 116 of the 2005 Order.

#### Transitional provision: reviewable matters

###### 10

Where a determination under regulation 9 is made—

- (a) Schedule 8 to the 2005 Order applies as if a determination by the Board under regulation 9(1) or (2) (whether the condition in article 111(2)(a) or 112(2)(a) would have been satisfied) were a reviewable matter,

- (b) the following Regulations apply to a review of a determination by the Board under regulation 9(1) or (2) (whether the condition in article 111(2)(a) or 112(2)(a) would have been satisfied) as they apply to a review of an approval of a valuation under article 128(2) of the 2005 Order[^f00025]—

- (i) the Pension Protection Fund (Review and Reconsideration of Reviewable Matters) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00026], and

- (ii) the Pension Protection Fund (Reference of Reviewable Matters to the PPF Ombudsman) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00027], and

- (c) the Pension Protection Fund (Provision of Information) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00028] apply as if they required trustees or managers of the scheme to which a determination under regulation 9(1) or (2) relates to provide to members of that scheme a copy of a summary of the determination containing—

- (i) the matters in paragraphs (a) to (d) of the definition of a determination summary in Schedule 2 to the Pension Protection Fund (Provision of Information) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, and

- (ii) a statement describing how an application may be made to the Board for the review of the determination, how it will be considered and the Board’s powers on making a review decision[^f00029],

within the period of 28 days beginning on the day on which the trustees or managers receive a copy of a determination under regulation 9(1)(b) or (2)(b), as the case may be, of these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

Part 2 of these Regulations brings sections 109 to 111 of the Pension Schemes Act [2026 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/22) (“*the 2026 Act*”) into force on 31st December 2026, except in the circumstances set out, and for all purposes on 31st December 2027.

Part 3 of these Regulations makes transitional provisions that apply to Great Britain. Regulation 5 makes transitional provisions relating to winding up and the impact of the amendments made by the 2026 Act on the value of “corresponding PPF liability” in section 73(4)(b) of the Pensions Act [1995 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/26). Regulation 6 makes transitional provisions relating to the value of “*protected liabilities*” for the purposes of a valuation under section 143 of the Pensions Act [2004 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/35) (“*the 2004 Act*”) where a pension scheme is in an assessment period (within the meaning given in section 132 of the 2004 Act). Where a determination is made under regulation 6, regulation 7 makes further transitional provision so that a determination by the Board of the Pension Protection Fund as to whether the condition in section 127(2)(a) or 128(2)(a) of the 2004 Act would have been satisfied is treated as a reviewable matter for the purposes of Schedule 9 to the 2004 Act and regulations made under that Act.

Part 4 of these Regulations makes equivalent transitional provisions that apply to Northern Ireland. Regulation 8 makes transitional provisions relating to winding up and the impact of the amendments made by the 2026 Act on the value of “corresponding PPF liability” in article 73(4)(b) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 ([S.I. 1995/3213 (N.I. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1995/3213)). Regulation 9 makes transitional provisions relating to the value of “*protected liabilities*” for the purposes of a valuation under article 127 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/255 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2005/255)) (“*the 2005 Order*”) where a pension scheme is in an assessment period (within the meaning given in article 116 of the 2005 Order). Where a determination is made under regulation 9, regulation 10 makes further transitional provision so that a determination by the Board of the Pension Protection Fund as to whether the condition in article 111(2)(a) or 112(2)(a) of the 2005 Order would have been satisfied is treated as a reviewable matter for the purposes of Schedule 8 to the 2005 Order and regulations made under that Order.

The amendments made by section 109 or 110 of the 2026 Act do not affect the amount of a lump sum payable under paragraph 24 of Schedule 7 to the 2004 Act or, as the case may be, paragraph 24 of Schedule 6 to the 2005 Order, where a person becomes entitled to periodic compensation before the commencement date that applies to them by virtue of these Regulations. The amendments made by section 111 of the 2026 Act do not affect the amount of a lump sum payable under regulation 17D of the Financial Assistance Scheme Regulations 2005 ([S.I. 2005/1986](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1986), amended by [S.I. 2010/1149](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/1149)), where a person becomes entitled to a payment under those Regulations before the commencement date that applies to the person by virtue of these Regulations.
