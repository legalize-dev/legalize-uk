# The Police (Retirement of Senior Officers) (Amendment) Regulations 1973

Made: 29th November 1973

Laid before Parliament: 11th December 1973

Coming into Operation: 24th December 1973

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 260 of the Local Government Act 1972, I hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police (Retirement of Senior Officers) (Amendment) Regulations 1973 and shall come into operation on 24th December 1973.

###### 2

In Regulation 3(1)(a) of the Police (Retirement of Senior Officers) Regulations 1973[^f00001] (application of section 260 of the Local Government Act 1972) for the date “1st January 1974” there shall be substituted the date “24th December 1973”.

## Signed

Robert Carr — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — 29th November 1973

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police (Retirement of Senior Officers) Regulations 1973 (which come into operation on 24th December 1973) under Regulation 3(1)(a) of which only senior police officers serving as such on 1st January 1974 can qualify for the benefit of the Regulations. The present Regulations change this qualifying date to 24th December 1973.
