# The Public Order Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986

Made: 27th November 1986

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 41 of the Public Order Act 1986, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Public Order Act 1986 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1986.

- (2) In this Order “*the 1986 Act*” means the Public Order Act 1986.

###### 2

The provisions of the 1986 Act specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st January 1987.
