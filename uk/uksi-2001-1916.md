# The Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made: 22nd March 2001

Coming into force: 1st April 2001

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Order” means the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00004];

- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by the Order.

#### Amendment of the NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 3

- (1) For article 3 of the Order (nature and functions of the trust) there shall be substituted the following article—

> > (3)
>   (1) The trust is established for the purposes specified in section 5(1) of the Act.
>   (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services, and community health services, for the purposes of the health service, at or from Lymington Hospital, Southampton Road, Lymington, SO41 9ZH;
>     - and at or from any associated hospitals, establishments and facilities.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Gisela Stuart — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 22nd March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992 (“the Establishment Order”) which established the Southampton Community Health Services National Health Service Trust.

This Order should be read in conjunction with the West Hampshire National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00005], the Winchester and Eastleigh Healthcare National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001[^f00006], the Mid Hampshire Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00007], the New Forest Primary Care Trust (Establishment) Order 2001[^f00008], as well as the Southampton East Healthcare Primary Care Trust Change of Name and (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001[^f00009].
