# The Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1991

Made: 31st October 1991

Laid before Parliament: 11th November 1991

Coming into force: 2nd December 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 18(1) and 24(2) of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

–

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 2nd December 1991.

- (2) This Order shall extend to Scotland only.

###### 2

The fee payable under paragraph 18 of Schedule 3 to the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976[^f00002] (which relates to permits for the commercial provision of amusements with prizes) shall, instead of £25, be £32.

###### 3

The Amusements with Prizes (Variation of Fees) (Scotland) Order 1988[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 31st October 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, increases from £25 to £32 the fee payable to a local authority or other appropriate authority on the grant or renewal of a permit for amusements with prizes provided under section 16 of the Lotteries and Amusements Act 1976.
