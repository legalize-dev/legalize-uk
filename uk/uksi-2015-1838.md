# The Tuberculosis (Miscellaneous Amendments) (England) Order 2015

Made: 19th October 2015

Coming into force: 10th November 2015

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and now vested in the Secretary of State[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Tuberculosis (Miscellaneous Amendments) (England) Order 2015 and comes into force on 10th November 2015.

#### Amendment to the Cattle Compensation (England) Order 2012

###### 2

In paragraph 4(1)(a) of the Schedule to the Cattle Compensation (England) Order 2012 (compensation payable following delays in testing for tuberculosis)[^f00003], for “the Tuberculosis (England) Order 2007” substitute “the Tuberculosis (England) Order 2014[^f00004]”.

#### Amendment to the Tuberculosis (England) Order 2014

###### 3

In article 9(3)(a) of the Tuberculosis (England) Order 2014 (pre-movement testing), omit the words “in an area that is”.

## Signed

George Eustice — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th October 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order amends the Cattle Compensation (England) Order ([S.I. 2012/1379](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1379)) to replace a reference to the Tuberculosis (England) Order 2007 ([S.I. 2007/740](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/740)) with a reference to the Tuberculosis (England) Order 2014 ([S.I. 2014/2383](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/2383)) (“the 2014 Order”).

Article 3 amends article 9 of the 2014 Order to narrow the scope for exemption from a pre-movement test so that bovine animals in a herd subject to routine tuberculosis testing more frequently than biennially are subject to skin tests before they are moved off the premises.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
