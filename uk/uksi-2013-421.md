# The Allocation and Transfer of Proceedings (Amendment) Order 2013

Made: 25th February 2013

Laid before Parliament: 4th March 2013

Coming into force: 25th March 2013

The Lord Chancellor, after consulting the Lord Chief Justice, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 92(6) of and Part 1 of Schedule 11 to the Children Act 1989[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Allocation and Transfer of Proceedings (Amendment) Order 2013 and shall come into force on 25th March 2013.

#### Revocation of jurisdiction

###### 2

Trowbridge County Court shall cease to be a family hearing centre.

#### Family hearing centre

###### 3

Chippenham and Trowbridge County Court shall be a family hearing centre[^f00002].

#### Amendments to the Allocation and Transfer of Proceedings Order 2008

###### 4

- (1) The Allocation and Transfer of Proceedings Order 2008[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 1 omit the entry relating to Trowbridge County Court.

- (3) In Schedule 1 after the entry for Chichester County Court insert—

- (a) in the first column “Chippenham and Trowbridge County Court”;

- (b) in the second column “Yes”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Helen Grant — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 25th February 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Allocation and Transfer of Proceedings Order 2008. On 25th March Trowbridge County Court will cease to be family hearing centre and Chippenham and Trowbridge County Court will become a family hearing centre.
