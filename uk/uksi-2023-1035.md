# The Health and Care Act 2022 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2023

Made: 20th September 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 186(6) and (7) of the Health and Care Act 2022[^f00001].

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Care Act 2022 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st October 2023

###### 2

The following provisions of the Health and Care Act 2022[^f00002] come into force on 1st October 2023—

- (a) sections 109 to 135 (the HSSIB);

- (b) section 169 (medical examiners), except subsection (5);

- (c) Schedules 13 to 15 (the HSSIB; exceptions to the prohibition on disclosure of HSSIB material; consequential amendments relating to the HSSIB).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health and Social Care

Markham — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Health and Social Care — 20th September 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations bring into force specified provisions of the Health and Care Act [2022 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/31) (“*the Act*”) on 1st October 2023. They are the seventh set of commencement regulations to be made under the Act.

Regulation 2 commences provisions which establish the Health Services Safety Investigations Body and certain provisions in relation to medical examiners. Section 169(5) of the Act has not been commenced because it amends a definition which is yet to be inserted into section 41(1) of the Births and Deaths Registration Act [1953 (c. 20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1953/20); that insertion will be made once the relevant part of paragraph 21(1) of Schedule 21 to the Coroners and Justice Act [2009 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/25) is commenced.

A full impact assessment has been prepared in relation to the Act, and a copy is available at https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/health-and-care-act-2022-combined-impact-assessments. A hard copy can be obtained by writing to the Department of Health and Social Care, 39 Victoria Street, London, SW1H 0EU.
