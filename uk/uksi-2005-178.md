# The Police (Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 29th January 2005

Laid before Parliament: 7th February 2005

Coming into force: 1st March 2005

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 50 of the Police Act 1996[^f00001], and after furnishing a draft of the Regulations to the Police Advisory Board for England and Wales and taking into consideration any representations made by that Board in accordance with section 63(3)[^f00002] of the Police Act 1996, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police (Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 2005, and shall come into force on 1st March 2005.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment of the Police (Promotion) Regulations 1996

###### 2

- (1) The Police (Promotion) Regulations 1996[^f00003] are amended in accordance with these Regulations.

- (2) For “qualifying examination”, in each place where the words occur, substitute “qualifying assessment” (and for “qualifying examinations”, substitute “qualifying assessments”).

###### 3

In regulation 5 (period of probation for constable promoted to sergeant), after paragraph (2) insert the following paragraph–

> > (3) This regulation shall not apply to a person who is completing the work-based assessment under Part IIB of the qualifying assessment.

###### 4

- (1) Regulation 6 (temporary promotion) shall be renumbered regulation 6(1).

- (2) In regulation 6, after paragraph (1) insert the following paragraph–

> > (2) A member of a police force who is successful in the written paper under Part I of the qualifying assessment, and who has commenced the period of work-based assessment under Part IIB of the qualifying assessment, shall, at the commencement of the work-based assessment, be temporarily promoted to the rank of sergeant or inspector as the case may be.

###### 5

- (1) For paragraph 1(2)(b) of Schedule 1, substitute the following paragraph–

> > (b) either Part IIA, consisting of practical tests, or Part IIB, consisting of a work-based assessment

- (2) After paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 1, insert the following paragraph–

> > (2A) The Part IIB work-based assessment shall consist of a period of a minimum of 12 months.

- (3) In paragraph 1(3) of Schedule 1for “each Part I” substitute “Parts I and IIA”.

- (4) In paragraph 2(2)(b) of Schedule 1 for “Part II” substitute “Part IIA”.

- (5) After sub-paragraph 2(2)(b) of Schedule 1 insert the following sub-paragraph–

> > (c) whether a police force promotes its members using the assessment set out in Part IIA or Part IIB.

- (6) In paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 1, after “a constable” insert “in a police force using the Part IIA assessment process”.

- (7) After paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 1 insert–

> > (1A) Subject to sub-paragraphs (4) and (5), a constable in a force using the Part IIB assessment process who has completed 2 years'service and his probationary service is eligible to take Part I of the qualifying assessment for promotion to the rank of sergeant in that year.

- (8) In paragraph 5(3) of Schedule 1, after “a police force” insert “using the Part IIA assessment process”.

- (9) After paragraph 5(3) of Schedule 1 insert–

> > (3A) Subject to sub-paragraphs (4) and (5), a member of a police force using the Part IIB assessment process who holds the rank of sergeant, otherwise than on temporary promotion under regulation 6, is eligible to take Part I of the qualifying assessment for promotion to the rank of inspector in that year.

- (10) In paragraph 5(5) of Schedule 1 after “Part I of that” for “examination” substitute “assessment”.

- (11) In paragraph 5(6) of Schedule 1 for “Part II”, substitute “Part IIA”.

## Signed

Hazel Blears — Minister of State — 29th January 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Police (Promotion) Regulations 1996, and introduce an alternative to Part II of the qualifying examination (Regulations 5 and 6(1)). Police forces will be able to promote their members either by using the current system of practical assessments, or by using work-based assessments. These Regulations will come into force on 1st March 2005.
