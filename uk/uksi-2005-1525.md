# The Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 (Amendment) Regulations 2005

Made: 7th June 2005

Laid before Parliament: 8th June 2005

Coming into force: 29th June 2005

The Treasury are designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to (i) measures relating to the interruption or reduction, in part or completely, of economic relations with one or more countries which are not Member States, and (ii) measures relating to the movement of capital and to payments between Member States and between Member States and countries which are not Member States;

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by that section, make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 (Amendment) Regulations 2005 and come into force on 29th June 2005.

#### Amendment to the Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001

###### 2

For the definition of “Council Decision” in article 2(1) of the Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 substitute—

> > “*Council Decision*” means Council Decision 2005/221/CFSP of 14th March 2005 implementing Article 2(3) of Regulation [(EC) No 2580/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/2580) and repealing Council Decision [2004/306/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0306) of 2nd April 2004 ;“.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Terrorism (United Nations Measures) Order 2001 (Amendment) Regulations 2004 are revoked.

## Signed

Gillian Merron — Joan Ryan — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 7th June 2005
