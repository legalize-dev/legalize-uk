# The Friendly Societies Act 1974 (Seal of the Financial Services Authority) Regulations 2001

Made: 20th November 2001

Laid before Parliament: 20th November 2001

Coming into force: 17th December 2001

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 109(1)(b) of the Friendly Societies Act[^f00001] hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Friendly Societies Act 1974 (Seal of the Financial Services Authority) Regulations 2001 and come into force on 17th December 2001.

#### The Seal of the Financial Services Authority

###### 2

The seal of the Financial Services Authority to be used for the registration of any society or document (including any amendment to any document) under the Friendly Societies Act 1974, the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965[^f00002], the Building Societies Act 1986[^f00003], or the Friendly Societies Act 1992[^f00004], or otherwise for sealing any document for the purposes of any of those Acts must bear the words “Financial Services Authority” and “Mutual Societies”.

## Signed

Nick Ainger — Anne McGuire — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 20th November 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify the form of the seal of the Financial Services Authority to be used for certain purposes under enactments relating to mutual societies.
