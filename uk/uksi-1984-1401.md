# The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1984

Made: 30th August 1984

Laid before Parliament: 10th September 1984

Coming into Operation: 1st October 1984

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 47(1) and 50(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00001], now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 199(2) of the said Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations shall come into operation on 1st October 1984 and may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) (Amendment) Regulations 1984.

###### 2

The Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984 are hereby amended so that in Schedule 1 (Type Approval Requirements) for items 14B and 14C there are substituted items 14B and 14C in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1 These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Type Approval) (Great Britain) Regulations 1984 by revising the type approval requirements relating to noise and silencers with the effect that:— (*a*) from 1st October 1985, vehicles for which, or for a model of which, there is no type approval or Minister's approval certificate in force on that date must comply with the provisions of either Council Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157) as amended by Commission Directive [73/350/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0350), Council Directive [77/212/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0212) and Commission Directive [81/334/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0334) or with the provisions of those Directives as most recently amended by Commission Directive [84/372/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0372); (*b*) vehicles for which, or for a model of which, there is a type approval or Minister's approval certificate in force on 1st October 1985 may comply with the provisions of Council Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157) as amended by Commission Directive [73/350/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1973/0350) and Council Directive [77/212/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0212), or with the provisions of those Directives as further amended by Commission Directive [81/334/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0334), or with the provisions of those Directives as most recently amended by Commission Directive [84/372/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0372); and (*c*) in item 14C, in column (3)(*c*), in relation to Council Directive [70/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1970/0157) as amended by Commission Directive [81/334/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1981/0334), a derogation is added in respect of certain vehicles having 5 or more forward gears.

2 Copies of the EEC Directives referred to in these Regulations may be obtained from Her Majesty's Stationery Office.
