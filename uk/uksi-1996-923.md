# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Pennine Dales) Designation (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 25th March 1996

Laid before Parliament: 27th March 1996

Coming into force: 17th April 1996

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Pennine Dales) Designation (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 17th April 1996.

#### Amendment

###### 2

- (1) The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Pennine Dales) Designation Order 1992[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the following paragraphs of this article.

- (2) In paragraph (1) of article 2 (interpretation) the definition of “managed woodland” shall be revoked.

- (3) In article 6 (rates of payment under agreement)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—

- (i) for “£210” there shall be substituted “£240”,

- (ii) the words “or Schedule 3” shall be revoked, and

- (iii) the phrase beginning after the second comma shall be revoked; and

- (b) in paragraph (3), for “£15,000” there shall be substituted “£20,000”.

- (4) Schedule 3 is revoked.

#### Saving

###### 3

- (1) Paragraphs (2), (3)(a)(ii) and (iii) and (4) of article 2 of this Order shall not apply in relation to any agreement made before the date of coming into force of this Order.

- (2) Paragraph (3)(a)(i) and (b) of article 2 of this Order shall not apply in respect of any annual payment to a farmer which relates wholly or in part to any period before 1st April 1996.

## Signed

Tim Boswell — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 25th March 1996

We consent,

Simon Burns — Liam Fox — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 21st March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Pennine Dales) Designation Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/55), amended by S.I. [1992/301](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/301), S.I. [1993/460](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/460), S.I. [1994/253](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/253) and S.I. [1994/930](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/930)) which designated an area in the Pennine Dales as an environmentally sensitive area, in compliance with Title VII of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2328/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2328) (OJ No. L218, 6.8.91, p.1) on improving the efficiency of agricultural structures. This Order complies with Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2078/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/2078) (OJ No. L215, 30.7.92, p.85) on agricultural production methods compatible with the requirements of protection of the environment and the maintenance of the countryside which superseded Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2328/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2328).

Subject to a saving provision, this Order discontinues the option of making payments of grant to farmers pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 for excluding livestock from woodland (*articles 2(2), (3)(a)(ii) and (iii) and (4) and 3(1)*). Subject to a saving provision, the Order also amends the rates of payments to be made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food pursuant to that kind of agreement (*articles 2(3)(a)(i) and (b) and 3(2)*).

No Compliance Cost Assessment in relation to this Order has been prepared.
