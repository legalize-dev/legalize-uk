# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) (Revocation) Regulations 1989

Made: 12th July 1989

Coming into force: Forthwith

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by article 69 of the Air Navigation Order 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) (Revocation) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force forthwith.

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Molesworth Aerodrome) Regulations 1987[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

R E Clarke — An Under Secretary in the — Department of Transport — 12th July 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the restrictions on flying in specified airspace around Molesworth Aerodrome.
