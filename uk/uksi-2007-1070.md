# The Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2007

Made: 29th March 2007

Coming into force: 8th April 2007

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order on the application of Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust following the completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 4 to the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 8th April 2007.

#### Dissolution of NHS trust

###### 2

The Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust established by the Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003] is dissolved, and accordingly that Order is revoked.

## Signed

Signed by the Secretary of State for Health

Patricia Hewitt — Secretary of State for Health — Department of Health — 29th March 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the dissolution on 8th April 2007 of the Good Hope Hospital National Health Service Trust established on 4th November 1992.
