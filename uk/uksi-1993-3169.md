# The Local Government Act 1992 (Commencement No. 3) Order 1993

Made: 16th December 1993

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 30(3) of the Local Government Act 1992[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1992 (Commencement No.3) Order 1993.

- (2) In this Order “the 1992 Act” means the Local Government Act 1992.

#### Provisions of the 1992 Act coming into force on

###### 2

Section 11 of the 1992 Act so far as it relates to paragraph 12 of Schedule 1, and that paragraph, shall come into force on 6th January 1994.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

David Curry — Minister of State, — Department of the Environment — 16th December 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 11 of the Local Government Act 1992, so far as it relates to paragraph 12 of Schedule 1 to the Act, and that paragraph, on 6th January 1994.Paragraph 12 of that Schedule amends section 6 of the Local Government Act 1988 (functional work: restrictions).
