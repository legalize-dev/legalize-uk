# The Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made: 9th January 2003

Coming into force: 31st January 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 1, 7, 8 and 83 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] makes the following Order:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) (Amendment) Order 2003; it extends to England only and comes into force on 31st January 2003.

#### Amendment to the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) Order 2002

###### 2

Article 1(2) of the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) Order 2002[^f00002] shall be amended by substituting “1st April 2003” for “1st February 2003”.

## Signed

Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of StateDepartment for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 9th January 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) Order 2002 (S.I. [2002/202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/202) as amended) so that that Order continues in effect until 1st April 2003.
