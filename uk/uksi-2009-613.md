# The Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Designation of Participating Countries) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2009

Made: 10th March 2009

Laid before Parliament: 12th March 2009

Coming into force: 3rd April 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 51(2)(b) of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (‘the 2003 Act’), makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Designation of Participating Countries) (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) Order 2009 and shall come into force on the 3rd April 2009.

###### 2

This Order extends to England and Wales and Northern Ireland.

###### 3

Bulgaria and Romania are designated as participating countries under section 51(2)(b) of the 2003 Act for the purposes of sections 4, 4B , 31, 32, 35, 43, 44, 45, 47 and 48 of, and paragraph 15 of Schedule 2 to, that Act.

###### 4

Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Estonia, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Poland, Slovakia and Slovenia are designated as participating countries under section 51(2)(b) of the 2003 Act for the purposes of sections 4, 4B, 31, 47 and 48 of, and paragraph 15 of Schedule 2 to, that Act.

## Signed

Lord West of Spithead — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 10th March 2009
