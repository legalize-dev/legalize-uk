# The Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2004

Made: 30th September 2004

Laid before Parliament: 1st October 2004

Coming into force: 2nd October 2004

The Treasury are a government department designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to measures relating to (i) the interruption or reduction, in part or completely, of economic relations with one or more countries which are not Member States, and (ii) the movement of capital and to payments, between Member States and between Member States and countries which are not Member States;

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) (Amendment No.2) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 2nd October 2004.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 shall be revoked.

#### Amendment of the Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) Regulations 2004

###### 3

For the definition of “the EC Regulation” in regulation 1(2) of the Liberia (Freezing of Funds and Economic Resources) Regulations 2004 , substitute—

> > “*the EC Regulation*” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 872/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0872) of 29th April 2004 concerning the freezing of funds of former Liberian President Charles Taylor and certain persons and entities associated with him ,as amended by—
> (a) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1149/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1149) of 22 June 2004 amending Council Regulation [(EC) No 872/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0872) concerning further restrictive measures in relation to Liberia ,
> (b) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1478/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1478) of 18 August 2004 amending Council Regulation [(EC) No 872/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0872) concerning further restrictive measures in relation to Liberia , and
> (c) Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1580/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/1580) of 8 September 2004 amending Council Regulation [(EC) No 872/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0872) concerning further restrictive measures in relation to Liberia .

## Signed

Joan Ryan — John Heppell — Two Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 30th September 2004
