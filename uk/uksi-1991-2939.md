# The Tayside Regional Council (Allt Girnaig, Moulin) (Amendment) Water Order 1991

Made: 30th December 1991

Coming into force: 6th January 1992

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17(2) and 107(1)(b) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the Tayside Regional Council, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Tayside Regional Council (Allt Girnaig, Moulin) (Amendment) Water Order 1991 and shall come into force on 6th January 1992.

- (2) This Order and the Tayside Regional Council (Allt Girnaig, Moulin) Water Order 1981[^f00002] (hereinafter referred to as “the principal Order”) may be cited together as the Tayside Regional Council (Allt Girnaig, Moulin) Water Orders 1981 and 1991.

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 2

In article 5 of the principal Order—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(a) for the expression “4,600 cubic metres” there shall be substituted the expression “3,000 cubic metres” ; and

- (b) in paragraph (2)(b) for the expression “4,500 cubic metres” there shall be substituted the expression “6,100 cubic metres”.

#### Miscellaneous

###### 3

The Tayside Regional Council shall at all times after the expiration of six months from the date on which this Order comes into force keep a copy thereof at the office of the Chief Executive of the Council.

## Signed

T.D. Macdonald — Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office — 30th December 1991
