# The Competition Act 1998 (Consequential Provisions) Order 2013

Made: 12th February 2013

Laid before Parliament: 15th February 2013

Coming into force: 10th March 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 75 of the Competition Act 1998[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Competition Act 1998 (Consequential Provisions) Order 2013 and comes into force on 10th March 2013.

#### Consequential repeals and revocations

###### 2

The provisions specified in the Schedule are repealed or revoked to the extent specified.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Competition Act 1998 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2013 ([S.I.2013/284 (C. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/284)) brings into force on 10th March 2013 provisions of the Competition Act 1998 repealing the Restrictive Practices Court Act [1976 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/33). This Order makes consequential repeals and revocations.
