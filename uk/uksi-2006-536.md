# The Social Security (Incapacity Benefit Work-focused Interviews) Amendment Regulations 2006

Made: 1st March 2006

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 2006

Coming into force: 3rd April 2006

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2A, 189(1), (4) to (6) and (7A) and 191 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 .

The Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit Work-focused Interviews) Amendment Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 3rd April 2006.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit Work-focused Interviews) Regulations 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Social Security (Incapacity Benefit Work-focused Interviews) Regulations 2003 are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation) in the definition of “relevant person”—

- (a) in paragraph (a)(i)(bb), after “Part 2” there is inserted “ or Part 6 ”;

- (b) in paragraph (a)(ii)—

- (i) in head (aa), for “2001” there is substituted “ 1997 ”,

- (ii) in head (bb), for “2002” there is substituted “ 1998 ”, and

- (iii) at the end of head (bb), there is inserted—

> > or
> (cc) Part 6 of that Schedule and who made such a claim before 5th April 2004,

- (3) In Part 2 of the Schedule —

- (a) in paragraph 2(a)—

- (i) there is omitted “ Mendip District Council; ”, “ Sedgemoor District Council; ”, “ South Somerset District Council; ”, “ Taunton Deane Borough Council; ”, and “ West Somerset District Council; ”, and

- (ii) there is inserted at the appropriate place “ Brentwood District Council; ”; and

- (b) in paragraph 2(b)—

- (i) for “DH2 1AH” to “DH2 1XG”, there is substituted “ DH2 ”, and

- (ii) for “DH3 1A” to “DH3 2T”, there is substituted “ DH3 ”.

- (4) In Part 4 of the Schedule , in paragraph 4(b) there is inserted at the appropriate place—

> > G13 0, G13 4;
> G14 0;
> G60;
> G63 0;
> G81 1 to G81 6;
> G82 1 to G82 5;
> G83 0, G83 8, G83 9;

- (5) After Part 5 of the Schedule , there is inserted as Part 6 the Part set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.
