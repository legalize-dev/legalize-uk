# The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Russian Aircraft) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

Made: at 7.57 p.m. on 25th February 2022

Coming into force: with immediate effect

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by article 239 of the Air Navigation Order 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Russian Aircraft) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 and they come into force with immediate effect.

#### Amendment of the Regulations

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Russian Aircraft) Regulations 2022[^f00002] are amended by deleting in regulation 2(1) “on a scheduled service”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Robert Courts — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — At 7.57 p.m. on 25th February 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Air Navigation (Restriction of Flying) (Russian Aircraft) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/183](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/183)). The amendment extends the restriction of flying to include all aircraft which are Russian owned, chartered or operated by a person connected to Russia, or which is registered in Russia.

Full details of this Statutory Instrument will be promulgated by Notice to Aviation (NOTAM).
