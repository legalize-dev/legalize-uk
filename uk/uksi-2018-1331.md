# The Single European Sky (National Supervisory Authority) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

Made: 5th December 2018

Laid before Parliament: 12th December 2018

Coming into force: 10th January 2019

The Secretary of State is designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to measures relating to air transport.

In exercise of the powers conferred by that section the Secretary of State makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Single European Sky (National Supervisory Authority) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 10th January 2019.

#### Amendments to the Single European Sky (National Supervisory Authority) Regulations 2013

###### 2

For Schedule 2 to the Single European Sky (National Supervisory Authority) Regulations 2013 substitute the Schedule which is set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — Schedule to be substituted for Schedule 2 to the Single European Sky (National Supervisory Authority) Regulations 2013
