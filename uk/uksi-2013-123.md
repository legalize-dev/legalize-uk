# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Directives) (England and Wales) Order 2013

Made: 17th January 2013

Coming into force: 10th February 2013

The Secretary of State, in relation to England, and the Welsh Ministers, in relation to Wales, make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Directives) (England and Wales) Order 2013 and comes into force on 10th February 2013.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

- (3) This Order applies to the sea adjacent to England and Wales out as far as the seaward boundary of the territorial sea; and for that purpose—

- (a) the sea adjacent to England is so much of the sea adjacent to Great Britain as is not the sea adjacent to Wales or the sea adjacent to Scotland;

- (b) the sea adjacent to Wales has the same meaning as it has by virtue of section 158(3) or (4) of the Government of Wales Act 2006[^f00002]; and

- (c) the sea adjacent to Scotland has the same meaning as the internal waters and territorial sea of the United Kingdom adjacent to Scotland has by virtue of section 126(2) of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00003].

#### Designation

###### 2

The following Directives are designated as relevant directives for the purposes of paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999—

- (a) Council Directive [80/68/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1980/0068) on the protection of groundwater against pollution caused by certain dangerous substances[^f00004];

- (b) Council Directive [87/217/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0217) on the prevention and reduction of environmental pollution by asbestos[^f00005];

- (c) Council Directive [92/112/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0112) on procedures for harmonizing the programmes for the reduction and eventual elimination of pollution caused by waste from the titanium dioxide industry[^f00006];

- (d) European Parliament and Council Directive [94/63/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0063) on the control of volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions resulting from the storage of petrol and its distribution from terminals to service stations[^f00007];

- (e) Council Directive 96/29/EURATOM[^f00008] laying down basic safety standards for the protection of the health of workers and the general public against the dangers arising from ionising radiation;

- (f) Council Directive [1999/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0013) on the limitation of emissions of volatile organic compounds due to the use of organic solvents in certain activities and installations[^f00009];

- (g) Council Directive [1999/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0031) on the landfill of waste[^f00010];

- (h) Directive [2000/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0053) of the European Parliament and of the Council on end-of-life vehicles[^f00011];

- (i) Directive [2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy[^f00012];

- (j) Directive [2000/76/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0076) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the incineration of waste[^f00013];

- (k) Directive [2001/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0080) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the limitation of emissions of certain pollutants into the air from large combustion plants[^f00014];

- (l) Directive [2001/81/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0081) of the European Parliament and of the Council on national emission ceilings for certain atmospheric pollutants[^f00015];

- (m) Directive [2002/96/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0096) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste electrical and electronic equipment[^f00016];

- (n) Council Directive 2003/122/EURATOM[^f00017] on the control of high-activity sealed radioactive sources and orphan sources;

- (o) Directive [2006/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0021) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the management of waste from extractive industries and amending Directive [2004/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0035)[^f00018];

- (p) Directive [2006/66/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0066) of the European Parliament and of the Council on batteries and accumulators and waste batteries and accumulators and repealing Directive [91/157/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0157)[^f00019];

- (q) Directive [2006/118/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0118) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of groundwater against pollution and deterioration[^f00020];

- (r) Directive [2008/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0001) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning integrated pollution prevention and control[^f00021];

- (s) Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste and repealing certain Directives[^f00022];

- (t) Directive [2009/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0031) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the geological storage of carbon dioxide and amending Council Directive 85/337 EEC, European Parliament and Council Directives [2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060), [2001/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0080), [2004/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0035), [2006/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0012), [2008/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0001) and Regulation [(EC) No 1013/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1013)[^f00023];

- (u) Directive [2009/126/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0126) of the European Parliament and of the Council on Stage II petrol vapour recovery during refuelling of motor vehicles at service stations[^f00024];

- (v) Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on industrial emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control) (Recast)[^f00025].

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Directives) (England and Wales) Order 2011[^f00026] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates 22 Directives as relevant directives for the purposes of paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act [1999 (c. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/24).

The effect of this Order is that regulations may be made under that Act making provision which corresponds or is similar to any provision made, or capable of being made, under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act [1972 (c. 68)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68) in connection with any of the relevant directives.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the public, private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
