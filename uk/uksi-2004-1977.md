# The Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Amendment)Regulations 2004

Made: 21st July 2004

Laid before Parliament: 10th August 2004

Coming into force: 6th September 2004

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and by section 302(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00003] and by section 56 of the Finance Act 1973[^f00004], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 6th September 2004.

#### Amendment of the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1996

###### 2

Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1996[^f00005] shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation and revocation), in the definition of “Maritime and Coastguard Agency”, the words after “Transport” shall be omitted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1996, [S.I. 1996/3243](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/3243) (“the 1996 Regulations”) to prescribe changes in some of the fees charged and to introduce some new fees. In particular—

- (1) the hourly rate for fees relating to certain services listed in Parts I, XII and XIII has been increased from £76 to £78. Examination and certification fees, which are based on the hourly survey rate, have also been increased;

- (2) certain other seafarers' examination and certification fees have been revised;

- (3) new fees which have been introduced include fees relating to the granting of exemptions by the Secretary of State:

- (a) under the Merchant Shipping and Fishing Vessels (Port Waste Reception Facilities) Regulations 2003; and

- (b) under section 48 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 in relation to strategic ships.

The Regulations also amend the statutory references in Part I of the Schedule by adding new Parts O (Port Waste Exemptions) ([S.I. 2003/1809](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1809)) and Part P (Strategic Ships) ([S.I. 1995/1427](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1427)).
