# The Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Amendment)Regulations 2004

Made: 21st July 2004

Laid before Parliament: 10th August 2004

Coming into force: 6th September 2004

Whereas the Secretary of State is a Minister designated for purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 , in relation to measures relating to maritime transport and to water resources ,

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and by section 302(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 and by section 56 of the Finance Act 1973 , and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 6th September 2004.

#### Amendment of the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1996

###### 2

Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1996 shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) (interpretation and revocation), in the definition of “Maritime and Coastguard Agency”, the words after “Transport” shall be omitted.
