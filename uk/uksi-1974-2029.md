# The Pensions Increase (Judicial Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1974

Made: 3rd December 1974

Laid before Parliament: 12th December 1974

Coming into Operation: 2nd January 1975

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(3) and (4) of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions Increase (Judicial Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1974, and shall come into operation on 2nd January 1975.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act 1889 shall apply for the interpretation of these Regulations as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

###### 3

- (1) Paragraph 16 of the Schedule to the Pensions Increase (Judicial Pensions) Regulations 1972[^f00001] (as substituted by Regulation 5(1)(c) of and Schedule 2 to the Pensions Increase (Judicial Pensions) (Amendment) Regulations 1974[^f00002] shall be amended by inserting before the last entry in the table the following entries:—

| 1.8.70 | 1.1.71 | 1.3694. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1.71 | 1.1.72 | 1.2733. |

- (2) Any increase of pension payable by virtue of this Regulations shall take effect from 1st December 1973.

## Signed

Given under the official seal of the Minister for the Civil Service on 3rd December 1974.

C. R. Morris — Minister of State — Civil Service Department

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Pensions Increase (Judicial Pensions) Regulations 1972, which modify the provisions of the Pensions (Increase) Act 1971 in their application to certain pensions payable in respect of service in judicial and other similar offices.

Paragraph 16 of the Schedule to the 1972 Regulations (as substituted by Schedule 2 to the Pensions Increase (Judicial Pensions) (Amendment) Regulations 1974) deals with pensions payable in respect of service as a county court registrar. The table in paragraph 16, which sets out the multiplier to be applied to the basic rate of the pension, contains no entry for pensions beginning after 1st August 1970 but not later than 1st January 1971, nor for pensions beginning after 1st January 1971 but not later than 1st January 1972. These Regulations insert the appropriate entries in the table.

Under the power conferred by section 5(4) of the 1971 Act, the Regulations provide for increases by virtue of the Regulations to take effect from 1st December 1973.
