# The Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Isle of Man) (No. 2) Order 1990

Made: 20th November 1990

Coming into force: 7th January 1991

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 20th day of November 1990 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 304(4) and (6) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Isle of Man) (No. 2) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 7th January 1991.

###### 2

- (1) Paragraphs 1 to 11, 17 to 23, 25, 26, 28 and 30 of Schedule 5 to the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 shall extend to the Isle of Man subject to the modification specified in paragraph (3) below.

- (2) Section 303(2) of and Schedule 8 to the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 shall extend to the Isle of Man so far as they relate to the repeal of the following provisions of the Patents Act 1977[^f00002]—

- (a) sections 14(4) and (8);

- (b) in section 28(3), paragraph (b) and the word “and” preceding it;

- (c) section 28(5) to (9);

- (d) sections 72(3) and 88;

- (e) in section 130(7), the words “88(6) and (7)”, and

- (f) n Schedule 5, paragraphs 2, 7 and 8.

- (3) The modification referred to in paragraph (1) above is that any reference to an Act of Parliament or to a provision of such an Act shall be construed, unless the contrary intention appears, as a reference to that Act or provision as it has effect in the Isle of Man.

## Signed

G I de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends to the Isle of Man, subject to the modification in article 2(3), certain amendments in the Patents Act [1949 (c. 87)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1949/87) and the Patents Act 1977 which are effected by Schedule 5 to the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 and extends there the repeal of certain provisions in the Patents Act 1977 effected by section 303(2) of and Schedule 8 to that Act of 1988. Other provisions of that Act of 1988 have already been extended to the Isle of Man by the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Isle of Man) (No. 2) Order 1989 (S.I. [1989/1292](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1292)) and the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (Isle of Man) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/1505](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1505)).
