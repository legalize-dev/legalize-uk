# The Criminal Legal Aid (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

Made: 16th January 2024

Laid before Parliament: 18th January 2024

Coming into force: 10th February 2024

The Lord Chancellor makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14(h), 41(1)(a) and (b) and 41(2)(a) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Criminal Legal Aid (General) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 10th February 2024.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

#### Amendment to the Criminal Legal Aid (General) Regulations 2013

###### 2

- (1) The Criminal Legal Aid (General) Regulations 2013[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 9 (criminal proceedings)—

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (uf)[^f00003] omit “and”;

- (b) after sub-paragraph (uf) insert—

> > (ug) proceedings under sections 21, 28 or 29 of the Public Order Act 2023[^f00004] in relation to a serious disruption prevention order; and

## Signed

Mike Freer — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 16th January 2024

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Criminal Legal Aid (General) Regulations 2013 ([S.I. 2013/9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/9)) in order to specify proceedings relating to a Serious Disruption Prevention Order under sections 21, 28 or 29 of the Public Order Act [2023 (c. 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/15) as criminal proceedings for the purposes of section 14(h) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act [2012 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/10).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
