# The A501 Trunk Road (Euston Road, Camden) (Temporary Prohibition of Traffic) Order 1996

Made: 21st June 1996

Coming into force: 24th June 1996

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 14 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A501 Trunk Road (Euston Road, Camden) (Temporary Prohibition of Traffic) Order 1996 and shall come into force on the 24th June 1996.

###### 2

This Order is made because the Traffic Director for London is satisfied that traffic in Midland Road, Camden should be prohibited from turning left into Euston Road because works are being or are proposed to be executed on or near the junction of those roads.

###### 3

During the period starting at 08.00 hours on 24th June 1996 and ending at 23:59 hours on 23rd September 1996 or when those works have been completed, whichever is the sooner, subject to the provisions of article 4 below, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in Midland Road, Camden to turn left eastbound into Euston Road.

###### 4

- (1) The provisions of article 3 above shall apply only during such times and to such extent as may from time to time be indicated by traffic signs.

- (2) The provisions specified in article 3 above shall not apply in respect of—

- (i) any vehicle being used for the purpose of those works, or for fire brigade, ambulance or police purpose, or

- (ii) anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London, TRO 153 — 21st June 1996
