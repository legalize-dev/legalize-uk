# The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey)(Bus Lanes) Red Route Experimental 1996

Made: 28th February 1996

Coming into force: 15th March 1996

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) (Bus Lanes) Red Route Experimental Order 1996, and shall come into force on 15th March 1996.

###### 2

The A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route (Bus Lanes) Traffic1993[^f00003] as amended by the A1 Trunk Road (Haringey) (Bus Lanes) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation Order 1994 shall have effect as if:—

- (a) in article 2, after definition of “Schedule”, there were inserted the following definition—

> > - “vehicle” includes part of a vehicle

- (b) in article 5, for the words “enter or to proceed” there were substituted the word “be”; and,

- (c) in article 8, after paragraph (3) there were inserted the following paragraph—

> > (4) Nothing in article 5 shall apply so as to prevent a vehicle being in the bus lane so long as may be necessary—
>   (i) to enable a person to board or alight from the vehicle; or
>   (ii) to enable goods to be loaded onto or be unloaded from the vehicle;
> if and in so far as the activity is not prohibited by provisions other than the provision of this order.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State for Transport.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 28th February 1996
