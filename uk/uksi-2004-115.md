# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stages I–IV) Designation (Amendment) Order 2004

Made: 21st January 2004

Laid before Parliament: 22nd January 2004

Coming into force: 16th February 2004

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 18(1) and (4)(d)[^f00002] of the said Act, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stages I–IV) Designation (Amendment) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 16th February 2004.

#### Amendment of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage I) Designation Order 2000

###### 2

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage I) Designation Order 2000[^f00003] shall be amended by the substitution for the number “170” of the number “350” in each of the following places—

- (a) Schedule 1, Part 3, item 7, column 2;

- (b) Schedule 2, Part 3, item 7, column 2;

- (c) Schedule 3, Part 3, item 8, column 2;

- (d) Schedule 4, Part 3, item 8, column 2; and

- (e) Schedule 5, Part 3, item 4, column 2.

#### Amendment of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation Order 2000

###### 3

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation Order 2000[^f00004] shall be amended by the substitution for the number “170” of the number “350” in each of the following places—

- (a) Schedule 1, Part 3, item 5, column 2;

- (b) Schedule 2, Part 3, item 5, column 2;

- (c) Schedule 3, Part 3, item 9, column 2;

- (d) Schedule 4, Part 3, item 10, column 2; and

- (e) Schedule 5, Part 3, item 8, column 2.

#### Amendment of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage III) Designation Order 2000

###### 4

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage III) Designation Order 2000[^f00005] shall be amended by the substitution for the number “170” of the number “350” in each of the following places—

- (a) Schedule 1, Part 3, item 8, column 2;

- (b) Schedule 2, Part 3, item 13, column 2;

- (c) Schedule 3, Part 3, item 10, column 2;

- (d) Schedule 4, Part 3, item 5, column 2;

- (e) Schedule 5, Part 3, item 8, column 2; and

- (f) Schedule 6, Part 3, item 11, column 2.

#### Amendment of the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage IV) Designation Order 2000

###### 5

The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage IV) Designation Order 2000[^f00006] shall be amended by the substitution for the number “170” of the number “350” in each of the following places—

- (a) Schedule 1, Part 3, item 11, column 2;

- (b) Schedule 2, Part 3, item 6, column 2;

- (c) Schedule 3, Part 3, item 11, column 2;

- (d) Schedule 4, Part 3, item 7, column 2;

- (e) Schedule 5, Part 3, item 11, column 2; and

- (f) Schedule 6, Part 3, item 9, column 2.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends—

- the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage I) Designation Order 2000 (S.I. [2000/3049](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3049)) (which designated areas in The Broads, the Pennine Dales, the Somerset Levels and Moors, the South Downs and West Penwith as environmentally sensitive areas);

- the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage II) Designation Order 2000 (S.I. [2000/3050](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3050)) (which designated areas in Breckland, Clun, North Peak, the Suffolk River Valleys and the Test Valley);

- the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage III) Designation Order 2000 (S.I. [2000/3051](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3051)) (which designated areas in the Avon Valley, Exmoor, the Lake District, the North Kent Marshes, the South Wessex Downs and South West Peak); and

- the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (Stage IV) Designation Order 2000 (S.I. [2000/3052](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/3052)) (which designated areas in the Blackdown Hills, the Cotswold Hills, Dartmoor, the Essex Coast, the Shropshire Hills and the Upper Thames Tributaries).

The Order increases, with effect from 16th February 2004, the maximum rates of certain payments by the Secretary of State pursuant to agreements made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 relating to land in an environmentally sensitive area (*Articles 2–5)*. The payments concerned are those relating to the provision of public access.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
