# The Local Government Act 1988 (Competition) (Wales) Regulations 1998

Made: 1st September 1998

Laid before Parliament: 9th September 1998

Coming into force: 30th September 1998

The Secretary of State for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 6(3) and 15(6) of the Local Government Act 1988[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Act 1988 (Competition) (Wales) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 30th September 1998.

#### Amendments

###### 2

The Regulations specified in column (1) of the Schedule to these Regulations are amended to the extent indicated in column (2) of that Schedule.

#### Revocations

###### 3

In the Local Government Act 1988 (Competition) (Wales) Regulations 1997[^f00002], regulations 2(2), 3(2), 4(1) and 5(2) are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Regulation 2 of and the Schedule to these Regulations amend the Regulations specified in that Schedule (“the respective Regulations”) which were made under Part I of the Local Government Act 1988 (Competition) in respect of the defined activities of construction and property services (S.I. [1997/127](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/127)), financial services (S.I. [1997/130](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/130)), housing management services (S.I. [1997/935](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/935)), legal services (S.I. [1997/126](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/126)), information technology services (S.I. [1997/125](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/125)), personnel services (S.I. [1997/129](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/129)) and supervision of parking, management of vehicles and security work (S.I. [1997/128](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/128)). Those respective Regulations (and in consequence the present Regulations) apply only to certain defined authorities in Wales.

In the case of all services, in regulation 3 (but in the case of supervision of parking, management of vehicles and security work in regulation 2) of the respective Regulations a new date is specified for the application of section 6 (functional work: restrictions) of that Act of 1988. The respective new dates are:

- (a) 1st April 2000 (instead of 1st April 1999) in relation to management of vehicles;

- (b) 1st June 2000 (instead of 1st June 1999) in relation to security work and supervision of parking;

- (c) 1st October 2000 (instead of 1st October 1999) in relation to all the other services.

Regulation 3 makes consequential revocations in S.I. [1997/1699](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/1699).
