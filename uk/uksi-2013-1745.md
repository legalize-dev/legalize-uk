# The Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2013

Made: 11th July 2013

Laid before Parliament: 12th July 2013

Coming into force: 17th July 2013

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section8B(5) of the Immigration Act 1971[^f00001].

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) (AmendmentNo.2) Order 2013 and shall come into force on 17th July 2013.

###### 2

In Part 1 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000[^f00002] after “Resolution 1985 (2011) of 10 June 2011 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea).” insert “Resolution 2094 (2013) of 7 March 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea).”.

###### 3

- (1) Part 2 of the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000 is amended as follows.

- (2) After “Council Implementing Decision 2013/73/CFSP of 31 January 2013 (Afghanistan)” insert “and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2013/145/CFSP of 21 March 2013 (Afghanistan)[^f00003] and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2013/219/CFSP of 16May 2013 (Afghanistan)[^f00004]”.

- (3) After “Council Decision 2012/642/CFSP of 15 October 2012 (Belarus)” insert “as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2013/248/CFSP of 29 May 2013 (Belarus)[^f00005] and as amended by Council Decision 2013/308/CFSP of 24 June 2013 (Belarus)[^f00006]”.

- (4) After “Council Decision 2012/158/CFSP of 19 March 2012 (Bosnia and Herzegovina)” insert “as amended by Council Decision 2013/134/CFSP of 18 March 2013 (Bosnia and Herzegovina)[^f00007]”.

- (5) For “Council Decision 2010/232/CFSP of 26 April 2010 (Burma/Myanmar) as amended by Council Decision 2011/239/CFSP of 12 April 2011 (Burma/Myanmar) and as amended by Council Decision 2011/504/CFSP of 16 August 2011 (Burma/Myanmar) and as amended by Council Decision 2012/98/CFSP of 17 February 2012 (Burma/Myanmar) and as amended by Council Decision 2012/225/CFSP of 26 April 2012 (Burma/Myanmar)” substitute “Council Decision 2013/184/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Burma/Myanmar)[^f00008]”.

- (6) After “Council Decision 2013/124/CFSP of 11 March 2013 (Iran)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2013/270/CFSP of 6 June 2013 (Iran)[^f00009]”.

- (7) For “Council Decision 2010/800/CFSP of 22 December 2010 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) as amended by Council Decision 2011/860/CFSP of 19 December 2011 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) and as amended by Council Decision 2013/88/CFSP of 18 February 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea)” substitute “Council Decision 2013/183/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea)[^f00010]”.

- (8) After “Council Implementing Decision 2011/300/CFSP of 23 May 2011 (Libya)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2013/182/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Libya)[^f00011]”.

- (9) For “Council Decision 2012/739/CFSP of 29 November 2012 (Syria) as amended by Council Decision 2013/109/CFSP of 28 February 2013 (Syria).” substitute—

> > - Council Decision 2012/739/CFSP of 29 November 2012 (Syria) as amended by Council Decision 2013/109/CFSP of 28 February 2013 (Syria) and as implemented by Council Implementing Decision 2013/185/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Syria)[^f00012].
> - Council Decision 2013/255/CFSP of 31 May 2013 (Syria)[^f00013].

- (10) After “Council Decision 2013/89/CFSP of 18 February 2013 (Zimbabwe)” insert “and as amended by Council Decision 2013/160/CFSP of 27 March 2013 (Zimbabwe)[^f00014]”.

## Signed

Mark Harper — Minister of State — Home Office — 11th July 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Schedule to the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order 2000.

It amends Part 1 of that Schedule to list Resolution 2094 (2013) of 7 March 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea). It amends Part 2 of that Schedule to list: Council Implementing Decision 2013/145/CFSP of 21 March 2013 (Afghanistan) and Council Implementing Decision 2013/219/CFSP of 16May 2013 (Afghanistan); Council Implementing Decision 2013/248/CFSP of 29 May 2013 (Belarus) and Council Decision 2013/308/CFSP of 24 June 2013 (Belarus); Council Decision 2013/134/CFSP of 18 March 2013 (Bosnia and Herzegovina); Council Decision 2013/184/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Burma/Myanmar) (which replaced Council Decision 2010/232/CFSP of 26 April 2010 (Burma/Myanmar) and all subsequent amending Council Decisions); Council Decision 2013/270/CFSP of 6 June 2013 (Iran); Council Decision 2013/183/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) (which replaced Council Decision 2010/800/CFSP of 22 December 2010 (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) and both subsequent amending Council Decisions); Council Decision 2013/182/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Libya); Council Implementing Decision 2013/185/CFSP of 22 April 2013 (Syria) and Council Decision 2013/255/CFSP of 31 May 2013 (Syria); and Council Decision 2013/160/CFSP of 27 March 2013 (Zimbabwe).

Any person named by or under an instrument listed in the Schedule (as amended) or falling within a description specified in such an instrument will be excluded from the United Kingdom (subject to the exceptions specified in article 3 of the Immigration (Designation of Travel Bans) Order2000).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
