# The Digital Economy Act 2017 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2018

Made: 24th May 2018

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 118(6) of the Digital Economy Act 2017[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Digital Economy Act 2017 (Commencement No. 5) Regulations 2018.

#### Provisions coming into force on 25th May 2018

###### 2

Subsections (8) and (9) of section 111 of the Digital Economy Act 2017 (amendments relating to section 108 of that Act) come into force on 25th May 2018.

## Signed

Margot James — Minister of State — Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport — 24th May 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force subsections (8) and (9) of section 111 of the Digital Economy Act [2017 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/30) which amends a number of enactments in light of the repeal of Part 3 of the Data Protection Act 1998. Subsections (1) to (7) of section 111, if commenced, would repeal Part 3 of the Data Protection Act 1998 and make a number of consequential amendments to that Act. Subsections (1) to (7) of section 111 have now been superseded by paragraph 44 of Schedule 19 to the Data Protection Act [2018 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12) which provides for the repeal of the 1998 Act. It is therefore only necessary to commence subsections (8) and (9) of section 111 of the 2017 Act.

These Regulations are the fifth commencement regulations for the Digital Economy Act 2017. The Welsh Ministers have, in addition, made commencement regulations in relation to the disclosure of information to or by a water or sewerage undertaker for an area which is wholly or mainly in Wales, and in relation to the disclosure of information by the Welsh Revenue Authority ([S.I. 2018/342 (W. 62) (C.29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2018/342)).

The Explanatory Notes for the Digital Economy Act 2017 provide an explanation of the provisions in the Act and are available online (http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/30/pdfs/ukpgaen_20170030_en.pdf) and in hard copy from TSO, PO Box 29, Norwich, NR3 1GN (telephone orders/general enquiries: 0333 202 5070).
