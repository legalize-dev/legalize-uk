# The Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Order 1994

Made: 12th January 1994

Laid before Parliament: 24th January 1994

Coming into force: 14th February 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 7A(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975([^f00001]) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 14th February 1994.

#### Non-domestic rates for 1994-95

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7A of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Secretary of State prescribes in respect of the financial year 1994-95 for each local authority set out in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order the non-domestic rate, expressed as an amount of pence per pound, set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.
