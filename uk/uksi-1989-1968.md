# The Water (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 1989

Made: 25th October 1989

Laid before Parliament: 3rd November 1989

Coming into force: 27th November 1989

The Secretary of State for the Environment, and the Secretary of State for Wales, being satisfied that it is expedient by reason of the coming into force of provisions of the Water Act 1989[^f00001] to make additional consequential amendments of the public general enactments and subordinate legislation specified in these Regulations, and acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 185(2)(e) and 190 of that Act, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Water (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 1989 and shall come into force on 27th November 1989.

#### Amendments of public general enactments

###### 2

The enactments listed in column (1) of Schedule 1 to these Regulations are amended to the extent indicated in column (2) of that Schedule.

#### Amendments of subordinate legislation

###### 3

The instruments listed in column (1) of Schedule 2 are amended to the extent indicated in column (2) of that Schedule.
