# The Income Tax (Employments) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 1995

Made: 4th May 1995

Laid before the House of Commons: 4th May 1995

Coming into force: 25th May 1995

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 203(2) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988[^f00001], hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Income Tax (Employments) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 25th May 1995.

###### 2

In regulation 54(4) of the Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993[^f00002] for the words from “for the purposes of” to the end there shall be substituted the words “for the purposes of proceedings under sections 65, 66 and 67 of the Management Act[^f00003].”.

## Signed

S. C. T. Matheson — C. W. Corlett — Two of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue — 4th May 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 54(4) of the Income Tax (Employments) Regulations 1993 (S.I. [1993/744](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/744)) (recovery of PAYE tax) so as to omit the reference to summary proceedings. The change reflects an amendment made by section 156 of the Finance Act [1995 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/4) to section 67 of the Taxes Management Act [1970 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/9) (proceedings for tax in sheriff court).
