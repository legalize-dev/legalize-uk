# The Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (Scotland) Regulations 1991

Made: 26th April 1991

Coming into force: 30th April 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 11(2), 36(2)(b) and 37(1) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations, a draft of which has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament:

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) (Scotland) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 30th April 1991.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the Act” means the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986.

###### 2

For the weekly sum of £64 specified in section 11(2)(a)[^f00002] of the Act, there is hereby substituted the weekly sum of £70.

###### 3

The maximum amount of fees or outlays which a client shall be liable to pay under section 11(2) of the Act[^f00003], where his disposable income falls within a range specified in the first column of the following table, shall be the amount specified in relation to that range in the second column:—

| *Disposable income range* | *Maximum contribution* |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Exceeding £70 but not exceeding £74 a week | £5 |
| 2 Exceeding £74 but not exceeding £78 a week | £12 |
| 3 Exceeding £78 but not exceeding £84 a week | £19 |
| 4 Exceeding £84 but not exceeding £90 a week | £25 |
| 5 Exceeding £90 but not exceeding £96 a week | £32 |
| 6 Exceeding £96 but not exceeding £102 a week | £38 |
| 7 Exceeding £102 but not exceeding £108 a week | £45 |
| 8 Exceeding £108 but not exceeding £114 a week | £51 |
| 9 Exceeding £114 but not exceeding £120 a week | £58 |
| 10 Exceeding £120 but not exceeding £125 a week | £64 |
| 11 Exceeding £125 but not exceeding £130 a week | £70 |
| 12 Exceeding £130 but not exceeding £135 a week | £75 |

###### 4

Regulations 4 and 5 of the SAdvice and assistance (Financial Conditions) (Scotland) Regulations 1990[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

James Douglas-Hamilton — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 26th April 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

These Regulations increase the weekly disposable income above which a person is required to pay a contribution in respect of advice and assistance under the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986 from £64 to £70. They also prescribe the scale of contributions to be paid where the weekly disposable income exceeds £70 but does not exceed £135.
