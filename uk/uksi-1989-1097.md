# The Outer Space Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1989

Made: 29th June 1989

Coming into force: 31st July 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 15(2) of the Outer Space Act 1986[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Outer Space Act 1986 (Commencement) Order 1989.

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Outer Space Act 1986 shall be 31st July 1989.

## Signed

Robert Atkins — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 29th June 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Outer Space Act 1986 into force on the 31st July 1989.
