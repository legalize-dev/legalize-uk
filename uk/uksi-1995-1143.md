# The Welfare Food (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made: 24th April 1995

Laid before Parliament: 1st May 1995

Coming into force: 22nd May 1995

The Secretary of State for Health in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(3) and (4) of the Social Security Act 1988[^f00001] and section 175(2) to (5) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002] (as applied by section 15A of the Social Security Act 1988) and all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) —These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare Food (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 22nd May 1995.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Welfare Food Regulations 1988[^f00003].

#### Amendment of regulation 5 of the principal Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 5(1) of the principal Regulations (purchase of welfare food) for “£3.50” there shall be substituted “£3.55”.

#### Amendment of Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations

###### 3

In Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations (dried milk specified for the purpose of the Regulations)—

- (a) after the entry relating to Aptamil there shall be inserted the following entries—

| Boots Formula 1 | The Boots Company plc |
| --- | --- |
| Boots Formula 2 | The Boots Company plc; and |

- (b) for the entry in column (2) “Farley Health Products Ltd” in both places where it occurs there shall be substituted the entry “H J Heinz Company Ltd”.

#### Revocation

###### 4

Regulation 4 of the Welfare Food Amendment Regulations 1994[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Virginia Bottomley — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State, — Department of Health — 24th April 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Welfare Food Regulations 1988 (“the principal Regulations”).

Regulation 2 amends regulation 5 of the principal Regulations (purchase of welfare food) to increase the price paid for dried milk by a person entitled to purchase it from £3.50 to £3.55 for 900 grammes per week.

Regulation 3 amends Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations to specify two additional brands of dried milk and to take account of a change of manufacturer of two dried milk products.

Regulation 4 makes a consequential revocation.
