# The Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Falkland Islands) Order 1989

Made: 16th May 1989

Laid before Parliament: 24th May 1989

Coming into force: 14th June 1989

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 16th day of May 1989 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Confirmation of Legislation) (Falkland Islands) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 14th June 1989.

###### 2

The Merchant Shipping (Transfer of Functions) Ordinance 1988[^f00002], enacted by the Legislature of the Falkland Islands, is hereby confirmed.

## Signed

G. I. de Deney — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, made under section 735(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894, confirms an Ordinance of 1988 enacted by the Legislature of the Falkland Islands, which provides for the transfer of the functions of the Collector of Customs as the Registrar of British Ships under section 4(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1894 to the Registrar General.
