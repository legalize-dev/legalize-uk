# The Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2001

Made: 21st June 2001

Laid before Parliament: 22nd June 2001

Coming into force: 1st August 2001

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Designated Institutions) (No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st August 2001.

###### 2

The institution which is specified in the Schedule to this Order, being an institution which appears to the Secretary of State to fall within subsection (2) of section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988, is hereby designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Higher Education Funding Council for England.

## SCHEDULE

The Conservatoire for Dance and Drama

## Signed

Margaret Hodge — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 21st June 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates as from 1st August 2001 the Conservatoire for Dance and Drama as an institution eligible to receive funds from the Higher Education Funding Council for England.
