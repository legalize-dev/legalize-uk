# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2014

Made: 5th March 2014

Coming into force: 6th March 2014

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 5th day of March 2014Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills (No. 2) Order 2014 and comes into force on 6th March 2014.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The person named in the Schedule to this Order is appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.

## SCHEDULE

### Person appointed as Her Majesty’s Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills on 6th March 2014

Geraint Huw Evans

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council
