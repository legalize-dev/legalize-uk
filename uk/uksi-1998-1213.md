# The Cumbria Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998

Made: 12th May 1998

Coming into force: 26th May 1998

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cumbria Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1998 and shall come into force on 26th May 1998.

#### Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 2

In Article 4 of the Cumbria Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00004] for “4 non-executive” there shall be substituted “3 non-executive” and for “4 executive” there shall be substituted “3 executive”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Alan Milburn — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 12th May 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by reducing the numbers of non-executive directors and executive directors from 4 to 3 in each case.
