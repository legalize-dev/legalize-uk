# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment) Order 2013

Made: 25th June 2013

Laid before Parliament: 27th June 2013

Coming into force

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 128(2) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation, interpretation and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) (Amendment) Order 2013.

- (2) In this Order “the 2007 Order” means the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007[^f00002].

- (3) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

#### Commencement

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2), this Order shall come into force on 21st July 2013.

- (2) Article 4 of this Order shall come into force on 3rd October 2013.

#### Designated sites

###### 3

- (1) The 2007 Order is amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2(1) omit sub-paragraph (e).

- (3) In article 2(1)(g) for “(12)” substitute “(13)”.

- (4) After article 2(12) insert—

> > (13) So much of the site of St James’s Palace, Cleveland Row, London, SW1A 1BA, as is within the dotted line that is marked on the map in Schedule 12 (the St James’s Palace site).

- (5) After Schedule 11 insert—

> > **SCHEDULE12**

###### 4

- (1) The 2007 Order is further amended as follows.

- (2) In article 2(1)(g) for “(13)” substitute “(14)”.

- (3) After article 2(13) insert—

> > (14) So much of the site of Anmer Hall, Anmer, Norfolk, PE31 6RW, as is within the dotted line that is marked on the map in Schedule 13 (Anmer Hall site).

- (4) After Schedule 12 insert—

> > **SCHEDULE13**

## Signed

Theresa May — Secretary of State — 25th June 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Designated Sites under Section 128) Order 2007 (“the 2007 Order”). Article 3 substitutes the existing description of St James’s Palace in article 2(1)(e) in the 2007 Order with a map. The map sets out the part of the St James’s Palace site that is to be designated for the purposes of section 128 of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005. Article 4 further designates a new site, Anmer Hall, which is added to the list of existing sites designated in the 2007 Order. If a person enters on the designated site as a trespasser, that person will commit an offence under section 128(1) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005.
