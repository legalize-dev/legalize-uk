# The Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) (Revocation) Order 2015

Made: 19th March 2015

Coming into force: 1st July 2015

The Secretary of State makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 157(5) of the Water Industry Act 1991[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) (Revocation) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st July 2015.

#### Revocations

###### 2

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2003[^f00002], and

- (b) the Anglian Water Parks Byelaws (Extension) Order 2004[^f00003].

## Signed

Tom Surrey — A Senior Civil Servant — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes two Orders which extended by twelve months the life of the Anglian Water Services Ltd Water Parks Byelaws 1993.

No impact statement has been prepared as there is no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument at www.legislation.co.uk.
