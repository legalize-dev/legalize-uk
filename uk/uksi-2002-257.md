# The Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) Order 2002

Made: 12th February 2002

Laid before Parliament: 22nd February 2002

Coming into force: 18th March 2002

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 12th day of February 2002 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 39 of the Drug Trafficking Act 1994[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) (Amendment) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 18th March 2002.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales only.

###### 2

There shall be inserted in Schedule 1 to the Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) Order 1996[^f00002] (designation of countries and territories for the purposes of sections 39 and 40 of the Drug Trafficking Act 1994), at the appropriate places in alphabetical order, the countries specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the countries listed in the Schedule to this Order to the list of designated countries and territories in Schedule 1 to the Drug Trafficking Act 1994 (Designated Countries and Territories) Order 1996.
