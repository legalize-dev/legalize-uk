# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 12th March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 16th March 1999

Coming into force: 12th April 1999

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 9, 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988[^f00001], and with the consent of the Treasury, makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement, interpretation and transitional provisions

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 12th April 1999.

- (2) In these Regulations, unless the context requires otherwise, a reference to a regulation by number alone means the regulation so numbered in the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989[^f00002].

- (3) These Regulations shall apply to applications for advice and assistance made on or after 12th April 1999 and applications made before that date shall be treated as if these Regulations had not come into force.

#### Amendment to Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 11 shall be amended by substituting:

- (a) “£83” for “£80” in paragraph (1); and

- (b) “£178” for “£172” in paragraph (2).

- (2) Regulation 12 shall be amended by substituting:

- (a) “£75” for “£72” in each of the places where that amount is stated in paragraphs (1), (2) and (5);

- (b) “£178” for “£172” in paragraph (1).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

G.W. Hoon — Minister of State, — Lord Chancellor’s Department — Dated 7th March 1999

We consent

Jane Kennedy — Jim Dowd — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 12th March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations 1989, to increase the weekly income limits for legal advice and assistance (other than assistance by way of representation (“ABWOR”)) from £80 to £83; for non-contributory ABWOR from £72 to £75; and for contributory ABWOR from £172 to £178.
