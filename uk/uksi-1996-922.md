# The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 25th March 1996

Laid before Parliament: 27th March 1996

Coming into force: 17th April 1996

Now, therefore, the said Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 18(1) and (4)[^f00002] of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 17th April 1996.

#### Amendment

###### 2

- (1) The Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation Order 1986[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2) of this article.

- (2) In paragraph (1) of article 6 (rates of payment under agreement), for “£70” there shall be substituted “£75”.

#### Saving

###### 3

Article 2 of this Order shall not apply in respect of any annual payment to a farmer which relates wholly or in part to any period before 1st April 1996.

## Signed

Tim Boswell — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 25th March 1996

We consent,

Simon Burns — Liam Fox — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 21st March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Environmentally Sensitive Areas (West Penwith) Designation Order 1986 (S.I. [1986/2251](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1986/2251), amended by S.I. [1992/51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/51), S.I. [1994/250](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/250) and S.I. [1994/933](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/933)) which designated an area in West Penwith as an environmentally sensitive area, in compliance with Title VII of Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2328/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2328) (OJ No. L218, 6.8.91, p.1) on improving the efficiency of agricultural structures. This Order complies with Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2078/92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1992/2078) (OJ No. L215, 30.7.92, p.85) on agricultural production methods compatible with the requirements of protection of the environment and the maintenance of the countryside which superseded Council Regulation [(EEC) No. 2328/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2328).

Subject to a saving provision, the Order amends the rates of payments to be made by the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food pursuant to a management agreement made under section 18(3) of the Agriculture Act 1986 (articles 2 and 3).

No Compliance Cost Assessment in relation to this Order has been prepared.
