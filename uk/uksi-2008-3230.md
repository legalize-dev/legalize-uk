# The Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made: 15th December 2008

Laid before Parliament: 19th December 2008

Coming into force: 15th January 2009

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Title, application and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2008;

- (b) apply in England; and

- (c) come into force on 15th January 2009.

#### Amendment to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) Regulations

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2006[^f00003] is replaced with the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations replace Schedule 1 to the Products of Animal Origin (Third Country Imports) (England) Regulations 2006. The new Schedule incorporates a number of new and amended pieces of Community legislation that have come into force since those Regulations came into force.

The new Schedule provides that the provisions in Schedule 1 are now ambulatory.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.
