# The Local Government Act 1985 (Severn-Trent Water Authority Regional Land Drainage Committee) (Revocation) Order 1988

Made: 22nd March 1988

Laid before Parliament: 23rd March 1988

Coming into force: 1st April 1988

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 101 of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001] as read with section 103(6) of that Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 1985 (Severn-Trent Water Authority Regional Land Drainage Committee) (Revocation) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st April 1988.

#### Revocation of previous Order

###### 2

The Local Government Act 1985 (Severn-Trent Water Authority Regional Land Drainage Committee) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

In Witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 22nd March 1988.

John MacGregor — Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order made under the Local Government Act 1985, revokes the Local Government Act 1985 (Severn-Trent Water Authority Regional Land Drainage Committee) Order 1986 (the provisions of which are now replaced by provisions in the Severn-Trent Water Authority (Regional Land Drainage Committee) Order 1988 (S.I. [1988/566](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/566)).
