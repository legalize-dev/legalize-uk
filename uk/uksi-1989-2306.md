# The Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Commencement No. 2) Order 1989

Made: 6th December 1989

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 30(3) of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act (Commencement No. 2) Order 1989.

- (2) This Order does not extend to Northern Ireland.

###### 2

The provisions of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 mentioned in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 1st January 1990.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS OF THE ANIMALS (SCIENTIFIC PROCEDURES) ACT 1986 COMING INTO FORCE ON 1ST JANUARY 1990

| *Provision of Act* | *Subject matter of provision* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 7 | Breeding and supplying establishments |
| Section 10(3) | Conditions relating to breeding and supplying establishments |
| Schedule 2 | Animals to be obtained only from designated breeding or supplying establishments |

## Signed

David Waddington — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 6th December 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which does not extend to Northern Ireland, brings into force on 1st January 1990, those provisions of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 which are not already in force.
