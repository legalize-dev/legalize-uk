# The Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2002

Made: 10th July 2002

Coming into force: 31st July 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her under sections 1, 7 and 8 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, makes the following Order:

#### Title, commencement and extent

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2002; it extends to England only and shall come into force on 31st July 2002.

#### Amendment of the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) Order 2002

###### 2

- (1) The Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) Order 2002[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this article.

- (2) In article 2—

- (a) before the definition of “animal gathering”, the following definition shall be inserted—

> > - “animals” means cattle (excluding bison and yak), deer, goats, sheep and swine;

; and

- (b) after the definition of “approved disinfectant”, the following definition shall be added—

> > - “equipment” shall include pens and hurdles.

- (3) In the Schedule–

- (a) in paragraphs 1 and 2, in each place where the words “28 days” occur, there shall be substituted the words “27 days”; and

- (b) in paragraphs 1 and 4, the words “pens, hurdles and any other” shall be deleted.

## Signed

Whitty — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 10th July 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note does not form part of the Order)

This Order amends the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (England) Order 2002 (S.I. [2002/202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/202) as amended by S.I. [2002/1328](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/1328)).

The Order—

- (a) Restricts the categories of animals to which the Order applies so as to include only cattle (excluding bison and yak),deer, goats, sheep and swine.

- (b) Defines the terms “equipment” for the purposes of the Order and amends the references to “pens, hurdles and other equipment” accordingly.

- (c) Reduces the 28 day restricted periods to 27 days.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order.
