# The Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2022

Made

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 17(3) of the Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act 2018[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2022.

#### Provision coming into force on 18th August 2022

###### 2

Section 12 of the Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act 2018 (police body cameras) comes into force on 18th August 2022.

## Signed

Gillian Keegan — Minister of State — Department of Health and Social Care — 13th August 2022

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are the third Commencement Regulations made under the Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act [2018 (c. 27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/27).

These Regulations bring into force, on 18th August 2022, section 12 of the Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act 2018. This provision requires a police officer to wear a body camera, if reasonably practicable, when entering and assisting staff in a mental health unit.
