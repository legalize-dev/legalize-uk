# The National Lottery Charities Board (Increase in Membership) (Revocation) Order 1999

Made: 1st July 1999

Laid before Parliament: 2nd July 1999

Coming into force: 26th July 1999

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 1 of Schedule 5 to, and section 60[^f00001] of, the National Lottery etc. Act 1993[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Lottery Charities Board (Increase in Membership) (Revocation) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 26th July 1999.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The National Lottery Charities Board (Increase in Membership) Order 1995[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Chris Smith — Secretary of State for Culture, Media and Sport — 1st July 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the [National Lottery Charities Board (Increase in Membership) Order 1995 (S.I. 1995 No. 1645](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1645)), which increased the number of members of the National Lottery Charities Board to a chairman and twenty-one other members. The effect of the revocation of that Order is to reduce the membership back to a chairman and sixteen other members as originally provided for in the National Lottery etc. Act 1993.
