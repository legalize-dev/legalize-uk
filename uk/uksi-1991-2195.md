# The Finance Act 1985 (Interest on Tax) (Prescribed Rate) (No. 4) Order 1991

Made: 1st October 1991

Laid before the House of Commons: 4th October

Coming into force: 6th October 1991

The Treasury, in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 18(8) of the Finance Act 1985[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 1985 (Interest on Tax) (Prescribed Rate) (No.4) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 6th October 1991.

###### 2

For the purposes of subsections (1), (3) and (6) of section 18 of the Finance Act 1985 the prescribed rate shall be 9.25 per cent.

## Signed

Sydney Chapman — Nicholas Baker — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 1st October 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reduces from 10 per cent to 9.25 per cent the prescribed rate of interest for the purposes of section 18 of the Finance Act 1985 (interest on tax etc recovered or recoverable by assessment).
