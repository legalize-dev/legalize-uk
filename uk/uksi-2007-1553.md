# The Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2007

Made: 27th May 2007

Laid before Parliament: 30th May 2007

Coming into force: 31st May 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(1) of the Commons Registration Act 1965[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 31st May 2007.

- (2) These Regulations apply in relation to England only.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007[^f00003] are revoked.

## Signed

Ian Pearson — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 27th May 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Commons Registration (General) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2007. The latter Regulations would have revoked on 1st June 2007, in relation to England, provisions of the Commons Registration (General) Regulations 1966 relating to official searches of the commons registers and certificates of search. Those provisions will instead now remain in force.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
