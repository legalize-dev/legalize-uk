# The Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards (Amendment) Order 2004

Made: 4th March 2004

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 2004

Coming into force: 1st April 2004

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 4(1) and(2) and 28(1)(b) of the Local Government Act 1999[^f00001], and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and having consulted, in accordance with section 4(3) of the Act, persons appearing to him to represent best value authorities concerned and such other persons as he thought fit, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards (Amendment) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 1st April 2004.

#### Application

###### 2

This Order applies—

- (a) in relation to England, to all best value authorities; and

- (b) in relation to Wales, only to authorities falling within section 1(1)(d) or (e) of the Local Government Act 1999 (police and fire authorities).

#### Amendment of the Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards Order 2003

###### 3

The Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Government (Best Value) Performance Indicators and Performance Standards Order 2003 sets statutory performance indicators and standards for best value authorities in England and for fire and police authorities in Wales. These indicators relate to all of the functions of these authorities.

This amending order makes alterations to those indicators and standards. The order removes all of those indicators that required authorities to measure performance using user satisfaction surveys. It also updates the various indicators that applied to last financial year (2003 to 2004) so that these indicators apply in relation to the current financial year.

Article 4 makes amendments to corporate health performance indicators.

Article 5 makes amendments to social services performance indicators.

Article 6 makes amendments to housing and related services performance indicators.

Article 7 makes amendments to performance indicators for housing benefit and council tax benefit.

Article 8 makes amendments to performance indicators for environmental services.

Article 9 makes amendment to performance indicators for transport.

Article 10 makes amendments to performance indicators for planning.

Article 11 makes amendments to performance standards for planning.

Article 12 makes amendments to performance indicators for cultural and related services.
