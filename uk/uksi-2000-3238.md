## SCHEDULE 1 — DESIGNATIONS

| (1) | Ministers or Departments |
| --- | --- |
| (2) | Matters in relation to which Ministers or Departments are designated |
| The Secretary of State | Matters in respect of noise emission in the environment by equipment for use outdoors. |
| The Lord Chancellor | Matters relating to the jurisdiction over, and the recognition and enforcement of judgements in, matrimonial matters and in matters of parental responsibility for the children of both spouses. |
| Any Northern Ireland department | Matters relating to the promotion of rural development. |
