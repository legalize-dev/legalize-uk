# The Maritime Security (Jersey) Order 2014

Made: 11th February 2014

Coming into force in accordance with Article 1

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of February 2014Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 51(1) of the Aviation and Maritime Security Act 1990[^f00001] and section 30(3) of the Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997[^f00002], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Maritime Security (Jersey) Order 2014 and shall come into force on the seventh day after the day on which it is registered by the Royal Court of Jersey.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 1990 Act” means the Aviation and Maritime Security Act 1990 as amended by the Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997 and by the British Overseas Territories Act 2002[^f00003];

- “Jersey” means the Bailiwick of Jersey.

- (3) The Interpretation (Jersey) Law 1954[^f00004] shall apply to this Order as though it were an enactment within the meaning of that Law.

#### Extension and modification

###### 2

- (1) Sections 9 to 43, 45 to 46 and 50 of, and Schedule 2 to, the 1990 Act shall extend to Jersey subject to the exceptions, adaptations and modifications specified in the right hand column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) For the purpose of construing the provisions of the 1990 Act, as extended by this Order as part of the law of Jersey, any reference to an enactment which extends to Jersey shall, except where a contrary intention appears, be construed as a reference to that enactment as it has effect in Jersey.

#### Revocation of the Maritime Security (Jersey) Order 1996

###### 3

The Maritime Security (Jersey) Order 1996[^f00005] is revoked.

## SCHEDULE — Exceptions, adaptations and modifications of sections 9 to 32, 34 to 42, 45 to 46 and 50 of, and Schedule 2 to, the 1990 Act as they extend to Jersey

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Various provisions of the Aviation and Maritime Security Act 1990 (“the 1990 Act”) were extended to Jersey by the Maritime Security (Jersey) Order 1996 ([S.I. 1996/2881](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2881)) subject to modifications. This Order revokes and replaces that Order. In doing so, it provides for certain of the provisions of the 1990 Act to extend to Jersey with fresh modifications, specified in the Part II of the Schedule to the Order, that reflect –

- (a) amendments to the 1990 Act made by the Merchant Shipping and Maritime Security Act 1997 in the United Kingdom; and

- (b) legislative provisions made in Jersey by the Shipping (Jersey) Law 2002.
