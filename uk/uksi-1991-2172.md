# The A12 Trunk Road (Eastern Avenue, Redbridge) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1991

Made: 24th September 1991

Coming into force: 28th September 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1), (2) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Secretary of State for the Home Department pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A12 Trunk Road (Eastern Avenue, Redbridge) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 28th September 1991.

###### 2

In this Order—

- “Eastern Avenue” means that length of the London to Great Yarmouth Trunk Road (A12) in the London Borough of Redbridge known as Eastern Avenue;

- “central reservation” means that part of Eastern Avenue which separates the two carriageways of Eastern Avenue for the safety and guidance of vehicles using that road;

- “the eastbound right-turn lane” means that traffic lane of the eastbound carriageway of Eastern Avenue which is bounded on its southern side by the central reservation, has a width of 3.5 metres, and extends from the junction westwards for a distance of 95 metres; and

- “the westbound right-turn lane” means that traffic lane of the westbound carriageway of Eastern Avenue which is bounded on its northern side by the central reservation, has a width of 3.5 metres and extends from the junction eastwards for a distance of 92 metres.

###### 3

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in the eastbound right-turn lane shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Beehive Lane.

###### 4

Every person causing any vehicle to proceed in the westbound right-turn lane shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Beehive Lane.

###### 5

No person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in either carriageway of Eastern Avenue, other than in the eastbound right-turn lane or the westbound right-turn lane, to turn right at the Junction of Eastern Avenue with Beehive Lane.

###### 6

No person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in either carriageway of Eastern Avenue to make a U-turn at the junction of Eastern Avenue with Beehive Lane so as to proceed in the opposite direction to that in which it was proceeding.

###### 7

Nothing in this Order shall apply to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.
