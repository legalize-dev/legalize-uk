# The Political Parties and Elections Act 2009 (Commencement No.6) Order 2013

Made: 21st January 2013

The Lord President of the Council makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43(1) of the Political Parties and Elections Act 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This order may be cited as the Political Parties and Elections Act 2009 (Commencement No.6) Order 2013.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Political Parties and Elections Act 2009.

#### Provisions coming into force on 22nd January 2013

###### 2

Subsections (1), (2) and (4) of section 27 (returning officers for elections to the European Parliament) of the Act come into force on 22nd January 2013.

#### Provision coming into force on 1st January 2014

###### 3

Section 27(3) of the Act comes into force on 1st January 2014.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord President of the Council

Chloë Smith — Minister for Political and Constitutional Reform — Cabinet Office — 21st January 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings section 27(1), (2) and (4) of the Political Parties and Elections Act [2009 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/12) into force on 22nd January 2013, and brings section 27(3) into force on 1st January 2014. Section 27 amends section 6 of the European Parliamentary Elections Act [2002 (c.24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/24). Subsections (2) and (4) provide that the Greater London Authority returning officer is eligible to be designated by the Secretary of State as a returning officer for European Parliamentary elections. Subsection (3) provides that the local returning officers for European Parliamentary elections will be local authority returning officers, rather than returning officers for United Kingdom parliamentary elections.
