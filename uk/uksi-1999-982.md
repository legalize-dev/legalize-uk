# The Sweeteners in Food (Amendment) Regulations 1999

Made: 24th March 1999

Laid before Parliament: 30th March 1999

Coming into force: 22nd April 1999

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Health and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in relation to England and Wales, and the Secretary of State for Scotland in relation to Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6(4), 16(1)(a) and (e), 17(1), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to, the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations, after consultation in accordance with section 48(4) of the said Act with such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by the Regulations.

#### Title, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sweeteners in Food (Amendment) Regulations 1999 and shall come into force on 22nd April 1999.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

The principal Regulations shall be amended by the addition, at the end of the definition of “Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031)” in regulation 2(1), of the words “as amended by Directive [98/66/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0066) [^f00003]”.

#### Consequential provision

###### 3

- (1) References to the principal Regulations in the provisions listed in paragraph (2) below shall be taken to be references to the principal Regulations as amended up to and including the amendment effected by these Regulations.

- (2) The provisions to which paragraph (1) above refers are–

- (a) the definition of “permitted sweetener” in regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of each of–

- (i) the Jam and Similar Products Regulations 1981[^f00004], and

- (ii) the Jam and Similar Products (Scotland) Regulations 1981[^f00005];

- (b) the definition of “additive” in regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of each of–

- (i) the Meat Products and Spreadable Fish Products Regulations 1984[^f00006], and

- (ii) the Meat Products and Spreadable Fish Products (Scotland) Regulations 1984[^f00007];

- (c) the definition of “sweetener” in Part II of Schedule 1 (categories of food additives) to the Food Additives Labelling Regulations 1992[^f00008];

- (d) the definition of “sweetener” in regulation 2(1) (interpretation) of the Miscellaneous Food Additives Regulations 1995[^f00009]; and

- (e) in the Food Labelling Regulations 1996[^f00010]–

- (i) paragraph (1) of regulation 34 (foods containing sweeteners, added sugar and sweeteners, aspartame or polyols); and

- (ii) in Schedule 8 (misleading descriptions) Part 1 (general), the condition in column 2 opposite the description “ice cream” in column 1.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply to Great Britain, further amend the Sweeteners in Food Regulations 1995, by bringing up to date a reference to Directive [95/31/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0031) on specific purity criteria so as to cover its amendment by Directive [98/66/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0066), which changed the specification for isomalt, and by bringing up to date references to the 1995 Regulations in other Regulations.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
