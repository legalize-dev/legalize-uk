# The Environment and Rural Affairs (Miscellaneous Revocations) Order 2018

Made: 19th June 2018

Laid before Parliament: 20th June 2018

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales: 20th June 2018

Coming into force: 11th July 2018

The Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers make this Order after consulting in accordance with section 61E(4) of the Land Drainage Act 1991.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Environment and Rural Affairs (Miscellaneous Revocations) Order 2018 and comes into force on 11th July 2018.

- (2) Article 3 applies to England only.

#### Revocation of orders relating to milk quotas

###### 2

The following orders are revoked—

- (a) the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) Order 1986[^f00006];

- (b) the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1987[^f00007];

- (c) the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1988[^f00008];

- (d) the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1990[^f00009];

- (e) the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1991[^f00010];

- (f) the Milk Quota (Calculation of Standard Quota) (Amendment) Order 1992[^f00011].

#### Revocation of the Agricultural Wages Committees (Transitional Provisions) Order 1974

###### 3

The Agricultural Wages Committees (Transitional Provisions) Order 1974[^f00012] is revoked.

#### Revocation of the Importation of Hay and Straw Order 1979

###### 4

The Importation of Hay and Straw Order 1979[^f00013] is revoked.

#### Revocation of the Code of Practice on Environmental Procedures for Flood Defence Operating Authorities (Internal Drainage Boards and Local Authorities) Approval Order 1996

###### 5

The Code of Practice on Environmental Procedures for Flood Defence Operating Authorities (Internal Drainage Boards and Local Authorities) Approval Order 1996[^f00014] is revoked.

## Signed

George Eustice — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — 19th June 2018

Lesley Griffiths — Cabinet Secretary for Energy, Planning and Rural Affairs one of the Welsh Ministers — 19th June 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes six instruments relating to milk quotas, one instrument relating to Agricultural Wages Committees and one instrument relating to the importation of hay and straw. All of them are spent or otherwise superseded. The Order also revokes one instrument relating to flood defence procedures, which is no longer required.

All of the orders are revoked in relation to England and Wales, except the Agricultural Wages Committees (Transitional Provision) Order 1974 ([S.I. 1974/514](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1974/514)) which is revoked only in relation to England.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.

An Explanatory Memorandum is available, in relation to England, alongside the instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk, and in relation to Wales, from the Economy, Skills and Natural Resources Department of the Welsh Government.
