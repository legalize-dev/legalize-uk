# The Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2012

Made: 16th April 2012

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 121(1) and 122(3) of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004[^f00001] and after consultation with the Welsh Ministers[^f00002] makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2012.

#### Provisions coming into force on 30 April 2012 in relation to Wales

###### 2

Sections 40 and 41 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 so far as not already in force, shall come into force in relation to Wales on 30 April 2012.

## Signed

Signed by Authority of the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Bob Neill — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 16th April 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order brings into force on 30 April 2012, the following provisions of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, in relation to Wales, so far as they are not already in force—

section 40 and Schedule 1 (local development orders); and

section 41 (effect of revision or revocation of development order on incomplete development).
