# The A12 Trunk Road (Redbridge) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1997

Made: 2nd July 1997

Coming into force: 14th July 1997

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A12 Trunk Road (Redbridge) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1997, and shall come into force on 14th July 1997.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A12 Trunk Road (Redbridge) Red Route Traffic Order 1996[^f00003] is varied in accordance with the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In the table in Schedule 2B for the words “No items.” substitute the following item—

| 1. | Eastern Avenue (A12)—north side, between a point 2.5 metres east of the party wall of Nos. 743 and 745 Eastern Avenue and a point 0.5 metres west of the party wall of Nos. 747 and 749 Eastern Avenue. | 7am–7pm Monday–Saturday Inclusive | 20 minutes | 40 minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

###### 4

In the table in Schedule 3A a new item 3 is added as follows—

| Eastern Avenue (A12)—north side, between a point 0.5 metres west of the party wall of Nos. 747 and 749 and a point 6.5 metres west of the flank wall of No. 755 Eastern Avenue. |
| --- |

###### 5

In the table in Schedule 4 for item 1 substitute the following item—

| Between a point 2.0 metres east of the party wall of Nos. 369 and 371 Eastern Avenue and a point 2.5 metres east of the party wall of Nos. 743 and 745 Eastern Avenue, including Gants Hill roundabout. |
| --- |

###### 6

In the table in Schedule 4 after item 1 insert the following new item—

| Between a point 6.5 metres west of the flank wall of No. 755 Eastern Avenue and a point 1.0 metre east of the western wall of Nos. 153 and 169 Bawdsey Avenue. |
| --- |

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 2nd July 1997
