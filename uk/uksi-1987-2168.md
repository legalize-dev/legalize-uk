# The Islington (Prescribed Routes) (No. 4) Traffic Order 1985 (Variation) Order 1987

Made: 8th December 1987

Coming into force: 14th December 1987

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of, and paragraph 27 of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984([^f00001]), and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with the Secretary of State for the Home Department, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Islington (Prescribed Routes) (No. 4) Traffic Order 1985 (Variation) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 14th December 1987.

###### 2

The Islington (Prescribed Routes) (No. 4) Traffic Order 1985([^f00002]) shall be varied and have effect as though for article 9 of that Order there were substituted the article similarly numbered in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

###### 9

No person shall cause any vehicle to enter the carriageway on the south-west side of the central island site in City Road situated at or adjacent to its junction with Wakley Street:

- Provided that nothing in this Article shall apply in relation to— a public service vehicle being used in the provision of a London local service; a public service vehicle being used in accordance with a time-table to carry passengers for hire or reward at separate fares other than one being used in the provision of a local service; a school bus; or a works bus.

- In this Article— “London local service” and “local service” have the same meanings as in the Transport Act 1985([^f00003]) “public service vehicle” has the same meaning as in the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981([^f00004]).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

R. M. C. Edridge — Director (Transport) London Regional Office of the — Department of Transport — 8th December 1987
