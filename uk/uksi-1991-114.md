# The Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) (No.2) Regulations 1991

Made: 24th January 1991

Laid before Parliament: 28th January 1991

Coming into force: 7th February 1991

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 3A(1) and 26(1) of the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) (No.2) Regulations 1991 and shall come into force on 7th February 1991.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations,“theAct” means the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987.

#### Non-domestic rates for 1991-92

###### 3

For the purposes of section 3A of the Act, the Secretary of State prescribes in respect of the financial year. 1991-92 for each local authority set out in column 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations the non-domestic rate, expressed as an amount of pence per pound, set out in column 2 of that Schedule opposite the name of that local authority.

#### Revocation

###### 4

The Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Regulations 1991[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the non-domestic rate for the financial year 1991-92 for each local authority listed in the Schedule (regulation 3 and the Schedule).

These Regulations also supersede and revoke the Non-Domestic Rates (Scotland) Regulations 1991 (S.I. [1991/42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/42)) which were to have come into force on 8th February 1991.
