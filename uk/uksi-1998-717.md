# The Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Commencement) Order 1998

Made: 25th February 1998

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 19(2) of the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 (Commencement) Order 1998.

###### 2

The provisions of the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 in so far as they are not already in force shall come into force on 2nd March 1998.

## Signed

Marjorie Mowlam — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 25th February 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force those parts of the Public Processions (Northern Ireland) Act 1998 not yet in force and appoint 2nd March 1998 as the day for their commencement.
