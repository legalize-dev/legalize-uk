# The North of Scotland Milk Marketing Board Dissolution Order 1996

Made: 10th April 1996

Coming into force: 29th April 1996

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 14(5) of the Agriculture Act 1993[^f00001] and all other powers enabling him in that behalf, having been given notice by the North of Scotland Milk Marketing Board[^f00002] that it has wound up its affairs, after consultation with the Board and being satisfied that the Board has carried out its remaining functions, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the North of Scotland Milk Marketing Board Dissolution Order 1996 and shall come into force on 29th April 1996.

###### 2

The North of Scotland Milk Marketing Board shall be dissolved on 29th April 1996.

## Signed

Mike Foulis — Assistant Secretary, Scottish Office — 10th April 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the North of Scotland Milk Marketing Board shall be dissolved on 29th April 1996.
