# The Building Societies (Funding) and Mutual Societies (Transfers) Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014

Made: 16th October 2014

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(2) of the Building Societies (Funding) and Mutual Societies (Transfers) Act 2007[^f00001], make the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Building Societies (Funding) and Mutual Societies (Transfers) Act 2007 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2014.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Building Societies (Funding) and Mutual Societies (Transfers) Act 2007.

#### Provisions coming into force

###### 2

20th November 2014 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 2 of the Act.

## Signed

Gavin Barwell — John Penrose — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th October 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the second Commencement Order made under the Building Societies (Funding) and Mutual Societies (Transfers) Act [2007 (c.26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/26) (“the Act”). This Order brings into force section 2 of the Act on 20th November 2014, which inserts section 90B into the Building Societies Act [1986 (c.53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/53). This enables the Treasury by order to ensure that when a building society is being wound up, its assets are applied in satisfying its liabilities to shareholders and to creditors *pari passu.*
