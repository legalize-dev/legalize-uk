# The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Regulations 2024

Made: 15th March 2024

Laid before Parliament: 18th March 2024

Coming into force: 8th April 2024

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 70(8), 113(1)(a) and 175(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001], sections 5(1)(p), 155(3) and 189(1), (4) and (5) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00002], section 42(2) and (6) of, and paragraphs 1(1) and 4(2)(d) of Schedule 6 to, the Welfare Reform Act 2012[^f00003] and sections 53 and 54(5) of the Pensions Act 2014[^f00004].

These Regulations contain provisions in consequence of an order under sections 150 and 150A[^f00005] of the Social Security Administration Act 1992.

The Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00006].

#### Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Regulations 2024 and come into force on 8th April 2024.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and Scotland, subject as follows.

- (3) Regulation 2 (insofar as it applies to a benefit which is devolved under Part 3 of the Scotland Act 2016[^f00007]) and regulation 4 extend to England and Wales only.

- (4) The revocation under regulation 6 has the same extent as the provisions that are revoked.

- (5) In these Regulations, “*the Up-rating Order*” means the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2024[^f00008].

#### Exceptions relating to payment of additional benefit by virtue of the Up-rating Order

###### 2

Section 155(3) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (effect of alteration of rates of benefit under Parts 2 to 5 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992) shall not apply if a question arises as to either—

- (a) the weekly rate at which the benefit is payable by virtue of the Up-rating Order, or

- (b) whether the conditions for receipt of the benefit at the altered rate are satisfied,

until that question has been determined in accordance with the provisions of the Social Security Act 1998[^f00009].

#### Persons not ordinarily resident in Great Britain

###### 3

Regulation 5 of the Social Security Benefit (Persons Abroad) Regulations 1975[^f00010] (application of disqualification in respect of up-rating of benefit) and regulation 21 of the State Pension Regulations 2015[^f00011] (entitlement to state pension for overseas residents) shall apply to any additional benefit payable by virtue of the Up-rating Order and to any up-rating increase as defined in section 22(1) of the Pensions Act 2014[^f00012] respectively.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976

###### 4

In regulation 8(1) of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976[^f00013] (circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as gainfully employed) for “£139”, in both places where it occurs, substitute “£151”.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987

###### 5

In paragraph 4(2A) of Schedule 9 to the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations 1987[^f00014] (deductions from benefit and direct payment to third parties) for “£29.75”, in each place where it occurs, substitute “£31.75”.

#### Revocation

###### 6

The Social Security Benefits Up-rating Regulations 2023[^f00015] are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

This instrument contains provisions necessary to give full effect to the 2024 benefits and pensions up-rating exercise.

Regulation 2 provides that where a question has arisen about the effect of the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2024 ([S.I. 2024/242](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/242)) on a benefit already in payment, the altered rates will not apply until that question is determined by the Secretary of State, the First-tier Tribunal or the Upper Tribunal.

Regulation 3 applies the provisions of regulation 5 of the Social Security Benefit (Persons Abroad) Regulations 1975 ([S.I. 1975/563](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1975/563)) and regulation 21 of the State Pension Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/173](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/173)) so as to restrict the application of the increases specified in the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Order 2024 in cases where the beneficiary is not ordinarily resident in Great Britain.

Regulation 4 increases from £139 to £151 the amount which a person eligible for payment of carer’s allowance can earn in the immediately preceding week without being deemed to be gainfully employed and, therefore, losing their entitlement to carer’s allowance. The Social Security Amendment (Carer’s Allowance) Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/2497](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2497)) replace references to “invalid care allowance” with references to “carer’s allowance” in certain legislative provisions, but “Invalid Care Allowance” remains part of the title of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976 ([S.I. 1976/409](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/409)) for statutory purposes.

Regulation 5 increases from £29.75 to £31.75 the amount allowed for personal expenses for a person in certain accommodation, where that person’s benefit is paid to the accommodation provider.

Regulation 6 revokes the Social Security Benefits Up-rating Regulations 2023 ([S.I. 2023/340](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/340)).

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sector is foreseen.
