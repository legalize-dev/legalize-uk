# The Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 10) Order 1987

Made: 24th July 1987

Coming into force: 17th August 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of powers conferred by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Amendment No. 10) Order 1987 and shall come into force on 17th August 1987.

###### 2

The Export of Goods (Control) Order 1985[^f00003] shall be further amended as follows:

- (a) After Article 2(xi) there shall be added the following:

> > (xii) scheduled goods indicated by the letter “L” are prohibited to be exported to any destination in Libya;
> (xiii) scheduled goods indicated by the letter “Z” are prohibited to be exported to any destination in South Africa or Namibia

- (b) in Group 3E of Part II of Schedule 1, in entry IL1460 (Aircraft and helicopters, aircraft engines and aircraft and helicopter equipment), after head (1A), there shall be added the following:

> > (1B) Aircraft and helicopters having a maximum all up weight of 680 kg or more L, Z

- (c) in Group 3H of Part II of Schedule 1, for the entry relating to Aluminium alloys, there shall be substituted the following entry:

> > - Aluminium alloys, in which zinc predominates by weight over any other single alloying element, in the following forms: tubes, rods, forgings, bars and billets A

## Signed

E. W. Beston — An Assistant Secretary, — Department of Trade and Industry — 24th July 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Export of Goods (Control) Order 1985.

Export Control is:

- (a) *introduced* on aircraft and helicopters to any destination in Libya, South Africa or Namibia;

- (b) *relaxed* on high strength aluminium alloys.
