# The Sunday Working (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004

Made: 3rd March 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 3 of the Sunday Working (Scotland) Act 2003 [^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Sunday Working (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

6th April 2004 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 1 (Sunday working: shop and betting workers in Scotland) of the Sunday Working (Scotland) Act 2003.

## Signed

ANNE McGUIRE — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State Scotland Office — Department for Constitutional Affairs — 3rd March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6th April 2004 section 1 of the Sunday Working (Scotland) Act 2003 which extends to shop and betting workers in Scotland certain provisions of the Employment Rights Act 1996 relating to Sunday working.

There are no earlier commencement orders.
