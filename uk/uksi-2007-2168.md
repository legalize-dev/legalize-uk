# The Aggregates Levy (Registration and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 25th July 2007

Laid before the House of Commons: 25th July 2007

Coming into force: 16th August 2007

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs[^f00001] make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 24(4) and 45(5) of the Finance Act 2001[^f00002]:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Aggregates Levy (Registration and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 16th August 2007.

###### 2

- (1) In regulation 3(2) of the Aggregates Levy (Registration and Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations 2001[^f00003], after “(d),” insert “(da),”.

- (2) Paragraph (1) only has effect in relation to the commercial exploitation on or after 16th August 2007 of aggregate which is exempt under section 17(3)(da) of the Finance Act 2001[^f00004].

## Signed

Steve Lamey — Dave Hartnett — Two of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs — 25th July 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 17(3)(da) of the Finance Act 2001 renders exempt for aggregates levy purposes aggregate removed from the ground[^f00005] along the line and in the course of improving, maintaining or constructing railways, tramways or monorails (railways aggregate).

A person commercially exploiting only exempt aggregate is exempt from the requirement of registration by HM Revenue and Customs for aggregates levy[^f00006]. These Regulations incorporate railways aggregate into the latter exemption[^f00007].

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
