# The M11 London-Cambridge Motorway (Redbridge-Stump Cross Section) Scheme 1970 (Revocation) Scheme 2001 Revocation Scheme 2004

Made: 20th February 2004

Coming into force: 15th March 2004

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this scheme in exercise of powers conferred by sections 16, 17, 19 and 326 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf:

###### 1

This scheme may be cited as the M11 London-Cambridge Motorway (Redbridge-Stump Cross Section) Scheme 1970 (Revocation) Scheme 2001 Revocation Scheme 2004 and shall come into force on 15th March 2004.

###### 2

The M11 London-Cambridge Motorway (Redbridge-Stump Cross Section) Scheme 1970 (Revocation) Scheme 2001[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of theSecretary of State for Transport

P Wallwork — A Divisional Director Highways Agency — 20th February 2004
