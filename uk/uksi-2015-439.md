# The Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Order 2015

Made: 2nd March 2015

Coming into force: 6th April 2015

The Treasury make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 150(9) and (10)(a)(i) and 189(4) of the 1992 Act and now vested in them[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Order 2015 and comes into force on 6th April 2015.

#### Amendment of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992

###### 2

In paragraph 5 of Part 3 of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00003] (weekly rate of guardian’s allowance) for “£16.35” substitute “£16.55”.

## Signed

Alun Cairns — David Evennett — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 2nd March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is made by the Treasury following a review of the general level of prices conducted under section 150 of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5) in the tax year ending 5th April 2015.

The weekly rate of guardian’s allowance was last amended by the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Order 2014 ([S.I. 2014/828](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/828)).

Article 2 increases the weekly rate of guardian’s allowance prescribed by paragraph 5 of Part 3 of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) from £16.35 to £16.55 with effect from 6th April 2015.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
