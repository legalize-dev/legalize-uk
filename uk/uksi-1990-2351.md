# The Education (School Premises) (Amendment)Regulations 1990

Made: 21st November 1990

Laid before Parliament: 28th November 1990

Coming into force: 1st January 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 10(1) of the Education Act 1944[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Science, as respects England, and the Secretary of State for Wales, as respects Wales, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School Premises) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 and shall come into force on 1st January 1991.

###### 2

Part II of Schedule 1 to the Education (School Premises) Regulations 1981[^f00002] shall be amended by the substitution in the heading and in paragraph 3 of “1996” for “1991”.

## Signed

K. Clarke — Secretary of State for Education and Science — 21st November 1990

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Wyn Roberts — Minister of State, Welsh Office — 21stNovember 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (School Premises) Regulations 1981 so as to extend the effect of the transitory provisions in Schedule 1 thereto until 1st September 1996.
