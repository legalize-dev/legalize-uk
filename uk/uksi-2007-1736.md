# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking area) (City of Manchester) (Amendment) Order 2007

Made: 14th June 2007

Laid before Parliament: 19th June 2007

Coming into force: 13th July 2007

Accordingly the Secretary of State for Transport makes this Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) (Amendment) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 13th July 2007.

#### Amendment of the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999

###### 2

Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999[^f00003] is amended as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (City of Manchester) Order 1999 (“the principal order”) which lists the roads excluded from the permitted parking and special parking areas designated by the principal order. Schedule 1 is amended by substituting a new Part 1 that omits a number of the roads previously excluded from the permitted parking and special parking areas designated by the principal order.
