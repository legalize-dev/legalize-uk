# The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

Made: 11th September 2001

Laid before Parliament: 11th September 2001

Coming into force: 2nd October 2001

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 2nd October 2001.

###### 2

At the end of Regulation 1(2)(b) of the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds) Regulations 2001[^f00003], there is added “, as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1205/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1205) of 19th June 2001 amending, for the first time, Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2488/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2488) of 10th November 2000 maintaining a freeze of funds in relation to Mr Milosevic and those persons associated with him”[^f00004].

## Signed

Tony McNulty — Graham Stringer — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 11th September 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2488/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2488) of 10th November 2000 maintains a freeze on the funds of Mr Milosevic and of the persons associated with him who are listed in Annex I to the Regulation. Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1205/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/1205) of 19th June 2001 amends Regulation 2488/2000 by substituting a new, and much shorter, version of Annex I.

The Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (Freezing of Funds) Regulations 2001 provide that breaches of certain provisions of Regulation 2488/2000 are to be criminal offences. The present Regulations amend those Regulations so that they refer to Council Regulation 2488/2000 as amended by Commission Regulation 1205/2001.
