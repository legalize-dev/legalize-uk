# The Finance Act 2002 Section 140 (Appointed Day) Order 2004

Made: 10th March 2004

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 140 of the Finance Act 2002 hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2002[^f00001] Section 140 (Appointed Day) Order 2004.

###### 2

The date appointed as the day upon which section 140 of the Finance Act 2002 (administration of UK gilts) is to come into force is 11th March 2004.

## Signed

John Heppell — Nick Ainger — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 10th March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 140 of the Finance Act [2002 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/23) into force on 11th March 2004. That section amends section 47 of the Finance Act [1942 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1942/21) so as to permit the Treasury to make regulations for the administration of UK gilts by such person or persons as they appoint, and to make consequential incidental, supplementary and transitional provisions.
