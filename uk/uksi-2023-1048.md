# The Export Control (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2023

Made: 25th September 2023

Laid before Parliament: 2nd October 2023

Coming into force: 23rd October 2023

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 15(1) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 428/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0428) of 5 May 2009 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items (Recast)[^f00001] and by sections 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 of the Export Control Act 2002[^f00002] makes these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Export Control (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 23rd October 2023.

- (2) Regulations 1 and 2 extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

- (3) Regulation 3 extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

#### Amendment of the Export Control 2008 Order in relation to Syria

###### 2

In Part 2 of Schedule 4 to the Export Control Order 2008[^f00003], omit “Syria”.

#### Amendment of Council Regulation (EC) No 428/2009 of 5 May 2009 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items (Recast)

###### 3

- (1) Council Regulation [(EC) No 428/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0428) of 5 May 2009 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items (Recast)[^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Annex I (list of dual-use items)—

- (a) in the section headed “Category 3 — Electronics”, in paragraph 3 of the Technical Notes in entry 3A001.b.12., after *“phase”* omit *“control for transmission and reception of signals.”* in the first place those words appear;

- (b) in the section headed “Category 7 — Navigation and Avionics”, in paragraph 2 of the Technical Notes for entry 7A003 omit—

| *“c.* | *Designed for “aircraft”, land vehicles or vessels, providing position without the use of ‘positional aiding references’, and having any of the following “accuracies” subsequent to normal alignment:* | *Designed for “aircraft”, land vehicles or vessels, providing position without the use of ‘positional aiding references’, and having any of the following “accuracies” subsequent to normal alignment:* |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | *1.* | *0,8 nautical miles per hour (nm/hr) “*Circular Error Probable*” (“*CEP*”) rate or less (better);* |
|  | *2.* | *0,5 % distanced travelled “CEP” or less (better); or* |
|  | *3.* | *Total drift of 1 nautical mile “CEP” or less (better) in a 24 hr period;”.* |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend Schedule 4 to the Export Control Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/3231](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/3231)) (“*the 2008 Order*”) and Council Regulation [(EC) No 428/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0428) of 5 May 2009 setting up a Community regime for the control of exports, transfer, brokering and transit of dual-use items (Recast) (EUR 2009/428) (“*the retained Dual-Use Regulation*”).

Regulation 2 omits Syria from the list in Part 2 of Schedule 4 to the 2008 Order. This change is to remedy an ambiguity in the 2008 Order as to the position of Syria in Schedule 4.

Regulation 3 amends Annex I to the retained Dual-Use Regulation, which lists dual-use goods, software and technology subject to export controls. These changes are to correct two drafting errors introduced by the Export Control (Amendment) Regulations 2023 ([S.I. 2023/695](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/695)) in which words already contained in the retained Dual-Use Regulation were inserted a second time.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is available from the Export Control Joint Unit, Old Admiralty Building, London SW1A 2DY. An Explanatory Memorandum has also been published alongside the instrument at www.legislation.gov.uk and a copy has been placed in the Libraries of both Houses of Parliament.
