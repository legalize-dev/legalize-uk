# The Greater Manchester Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997

Made: 21st October 1997

Coming into force: 1st November 1997

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Greater Manchester Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1997 and shall come into force on 1st November 1997.

#### Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 2

In Article 4 of the Greater Manchester Ambulance Service National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00003] for “5 non-executive” there shall be substituted “4 non-executive” and for “5 executive” there shall be substituted “4 executive”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health,

Alan Milburn — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 21st October 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by reducing the number of non-executive directors and executive directors from 5 to 4 in each case.
