# The Copyright and Performances (Application to Other Countries) (Amendment) Order 2015

Made: 11th February 2015

Laid before Parliament: 18th February 2015

Coming into force: 6th April 2015

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of February 2015Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Accordingly Her Majesty, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, in exercise of the powers conferred upon Her by sections 159 and 208 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988[^f00003], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Copyright and Performances (Application to Other Countries) (Amendment) Order 2015 and comes into force on 6th April 2015.

#### Amendment of the Copyright and Performances (Application to Other Countries) Order 2013

###### 2

- (1) The Copyright and Performances (Application to Other Countries) Order 2013[^f00004] is amended as follows.

- (2) In the Schedule to the 2013 Order after the entry relating to Guatemala insert—

| Guernsey, the Bailiwick of | Applies (+) | Applies (X) | Applies | Designated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Copyright and Performances (Application to Other Countries) Order 2013.

An entry is inserted into the Schedule to the 2013 Order relating to Guernsey. This ensures protection for Guernsey copyright works and performances in the United Kingdom.

No impact assessment has been prepared in relation to this instrument as no impact on the private and voluntary sectors is foreseen. An explanatory memorandum is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
