# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Fees) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2026

Made: 6th July 2026

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2)

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 302(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001], section 82OA(1) and (2) of the Energy Act 2008 [^f00002], section 110A(1) and (2) of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009[^f00003], and paragraph 7 of Schedule 4 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00004].

The Secretary of State has obtained the consent of the Treasury in accordance with section 302(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 and paragraph 10 of Schedule 4 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.

In accordance with paragraphs 12(1) and 38 of Schedule 7 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, a draft of this instrument was laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pollution Prevention and Control (Fees) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Amendments to the Offshore Petroleum Activities (Conservation of Habitats) Regulations 2001

###### 2

In the Offshore Petroleum Activities (Conservation of Habitats) Regulations 2001[^f00005], in regulation 20A(2)—

- (a) for “£210” substitute “£256”; and

- (b) for “£114” substitute “£130”.

In the Offshore Petroleum Licensing (Offshore Safety Directive) Regulations 2015[^f00006], in regulation 13A(2)—

- (a) for “£210” substitute “£256”; and

- (b) for “£114” substitute “£130”.

#### Amendments to the Pollution Prevention and Control (Fees) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Other Provisions) Regulations 2015

###### 4

In the Pollution Prevention and Control (Fees) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Other Provisions) Regulations 2015[^f00007], in regulation 7(1)—

- (a) for “£210” substitute “£256”; and

- (b) for “£114” substitute “£130”.

#### Amendments to the Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration, Production, Unloading and Storage (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2020

###### 5

In the Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration, Production, Unloading and Storage (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2020[^f00008], in regulation 22(2)—

- (a) for “£210” substitute “£256”; and

- (b) for “£114” substitute “£130”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations make provision to amend the fees charged in relation to activities carried out by the Secretary of State relating to the environmental regulation of the offshore oil and gas industry.

Regulations 2 to 5 amend the hourly rates used to calculate fees charged under:

1. the Offshore Petroleum Activities (Conservation of Habitats) Regulations 2001 ([S.I. 2001/1754](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/1754));

2. the Offshore Petroleum Licensing (Offshore Safety Directive) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/385](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/385));

3. the Pollution Prevention and Control (Fees) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Other Provisions) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1431](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1431)) which make provision for charging fees: relating to certain activities under the Merchant Shipping (Oil Pollution Preparedness, Response and Co-operation Convention) Regulations 1998 ([S.I. 1998/1056](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1056)), the Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/398](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/398)) and the Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/310](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/310)); relating to certain licences under regulation 55 of the Conservation of Offshore Marine Habitats and Species Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1013)); in connection with consents under section 82A of the Energy Act [2008 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/32) and emergency safety notices under sections 82F and 82G(8) of that Act; in respect of monitoring compliance with the requirements of the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme Regulations 2014 ([S.I. 2014/1643](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2014/1643)); and for applications for certain licences under section 71, and for stop notices and emergency safety notices under sections 102 to 105, of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 ([2009 c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/23);

4. the Offshore Oil and Gas Exploration, Production, Unloading and Storage (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1497](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1497)).

The fees affected by regulations 2 to 5 are calculated by first determining the number of hours of work carried out by specialist and non-specialist officers and then multiplying those numbers by the relevant hourly rate. In each case these Regulations amend the hourly rate for specialist officers from £210 to £256 and for non-specialist officers from £114 to £130.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
