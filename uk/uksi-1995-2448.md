# The Glanusk Park (Crickhowell)-Llyswen Trunk Road (A479) (Dderw Improvement) Order 1995

Made: 8th September 1995

Coming into force: 5th October 1995

The Secretary of State for Wales makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in Schedule 1 to this Order shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into force.

###### 2

The central line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

###### 3

The length of trunk road described in Schedule 2 to this Order and shown by broad striped hatching on the deposited plan shall cease to be a trunk road and shall be classified as a principal road as from the date on which the Secretary of State notifies the County Council of Powys that the new trunk road is open for through traffic.

###### 4

In this Order—

###### 5

This Order shall come into force on 5th October 1995 and may be cited as the Glanusk Park (Crickhowell)-Llyswen Trunk Road (A479) (Dderw Improvement) Order 1995.

## SCHEDULE 1 — ROUTE OF THE NEW TRUNK ROAD

The route of the new trunk road is a route at Llyswen in the County of Powys about 0.54 kilometres in length, starting at a point on the trunk road about 200 metres west of its junction with the A4079 and extending in a south easterly direction to a point about 279 metres south east of its junction with the A4079.

## SCHEDULE 2 — LENGTH OF TRUNK ROAD CEASING TO BE A TRUNK ROAD

The length of trunk road ceasing to be a trunk road is that length of the trunk road which is situated at the junction with the A4079 extending in a south westerly direction about 0.01 kilometres in length.
