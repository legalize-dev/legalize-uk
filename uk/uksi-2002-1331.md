# The Education (QCA Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

Made: 10th May 2002

Laid before Parliament: 14th May 2002

Coming into force: 1st July 2002

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 36 and 54(3) of the Education Act 1997[^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (QCA Levy)(Amendment) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 1st July 2002.

#### Amendment of regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Education (QCA Levy) Regulations 2002[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 5(3) before “awards” there shall be inserted “leviable”.

## Signed

Ivan Lewis — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 10th May 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations make an amendment to regulation 5 of the Education (QCA Levy) Regulations 2002, which provide for the imposition of a levy on persons awarding National Vocational Qualifications which are accredited by the Qualifications and Curriculum Authority. The amendment corrects a drafting error.
