# The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route (Prohibited Turn) Traffic Order 1998

Made: 22nd July 1998

Coming into force: 5th August 1998

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A10 Trunk Road (Haringey) Red Route (Prohibited Turn) Traffic Order 1998, and shall come into force on 5th August 1998.

#### Intepretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- (a) a reference to an article or a schedule followed by a number is a reference to the article of, or the schedule to, this Order so numbered;

- (b) causing includes permitting;

- (c) “West Green Road” means the road of that name in the London Borough of Haringey.

#### Prohibited Turn

###### 3

Subject to the provisions of article 4 no person shall cause any vehicle to turn left from A10 High Road into West Green Road.

#### Exemptions

###### 4

The provisions specified in article 3 shall not apply in respect of—

- (i) any vehicle being used for fire brigade, ambulance or police purpose, or

- (ii) anything done with the permission or at the direction of a police constable in uniform or a traffic warden.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 22nd July 1998
