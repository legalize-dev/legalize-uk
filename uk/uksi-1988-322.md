# The Assisted Areas (Amendment) Order 1988

Made: 25th February 1988

Laid before Parliament: 26th February 1988

Coming into force: 21st March 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 1(1), (3) and (4) of the Industrial Development Act 1982[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Assisted Areas (Amendment) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 21st March 1988.

#### Amendment to the Assisted Areas Order 1984

###### 2

The Assisted Areas Order 1984[^f00002] shall be amended by inserting at the end of article 7(1) the following:

## Signed

R. Atkins — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 25th February 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 7(1) of the Assisted Areas Order 1984 so that as from 21st March 1988 the reference to the travel to work area of Workington in Schedule 1 to that Order shall be a reference to that area as it existed on 8th October 1987.
