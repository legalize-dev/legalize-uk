# The Co-operatives and Community Benefit Societies Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003

Made: 16th October 2003

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 7 of the Co-operatives and Community Benefit Societies Act 2003[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Co-operatives and Community Benefit Societies Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003.

- (2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Co-operatives and Community Benefit Societies Act 2003.

#### Appointed days and application

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 2 and 3 of the Act is 1st April 2004.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 4 to 6 of the Act is 20th October 2003.

- (3) Section 4 of the Act (purported contracts, deeds and obligations) shall only apply to contracts or deeds made, and obligations undertaken, on or after 20th October 2003.

## Signed

Nick Ainger — Jim Murphy — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th October 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings sections 4 to 6 of the Co-operatives and Community Benefit Societies Act 2003 into force on 20th October 2003, and sections 2 and 3 of that Act into force on 1st April 2004. Section 4 of that Act (purported contracts, deeds and obligations) will only apply to contracts and deeds made, and obligations undertaken, on or after 20th October 2003.
