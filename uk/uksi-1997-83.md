# The Road Vehicles (Prohibition) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

Made: 20th January 1997

Laid before Parliament: 28th January 1997

Coming into force: 3rd March 1997

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 72(8) and (9) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Prohibition) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 3rd March 1997.

#### Preliminary

###### 2

The Road Vehicles (Prohibition) Regulations 1992[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the provisions of these Regulations.

#### Amendments relating to fees

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 7 shall be amended as follows.

- (2) For paragraph (4) there shall be substituted the following paragraph–

> > (4) Regulations 12(4) and (4A), and 16(4), (4A), (5) and (5A) of the 1988 Regulations[^f00003] shall apply with appropriate modification to an inspection or further inspection after a vehicle has failed an inspection.

###### 4

- (1) Regulation 8 shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (3), for the words “Regulation 20(2A), (3), (3A) and (4) of the 1981 Regulations”[^f00004] there shall be substituted the words “Regulation 20(2A), (2B), (3), (3A) and (4) of the 1981 Regulations”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

John Bowis — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 20th January 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Road Vehicles (Prohibition) Regulations 1992 (“the 1992 Regulations”).

Regulation 7 of the 1992 Regulations, which prescribes the fee for the inspection of a goods vehicle with a view to the removal of a prohibition, is amended by regulation 3 of these Regulations as a consequence of amendments made to the Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) Regulations 1988 ([S.I. 1988/1478](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1988/1478)) by the Goods Vehicles (Plating and Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 ([S.I. 1997/82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/82)).

Regulation 8 of the 1992 Regulations, which prescribes the fee for the inspection of a vehicle, other than a goods vehicle, with a view to the removal of a prohibition, is amended by regulation 4 of these Regulations as a consequence of amendments made to the Motor Vehicles (Tests) Regulations 1981 ([S.I. 1981/1694](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1981/1694)) by the Motor Vehicles (Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 ([S.I. 1997/81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/81)).
