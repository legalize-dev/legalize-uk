# The School Support Staff Negotiating Body (Prescribed Organisations) Regulations 2009

Made: 15th December 2009

Laid before Parliament: 22nd December 2009

Coming into force: 12th January 2010

The Secretary of State for Children, Schools and Families makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 15 to the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00001]:

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the School Support Staff Negotiating Body (Prescribed Organisations) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 12th January 2010.

- (2) These Regulations apply only in relation to England.

#### Prescribed organisations

###### 2

For the purposes of paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 15 to the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009, the organisations listed in the Schedule are the prescribed organisations.

## SCHEDULE — Prescribed Organisations

| Category of organisation | Organisation |
| --- | --- |
| School support staff organisations | GMB UNISON Unite the Union |
| School support staff employer organisations | Catholic Education Service for England and Wales Church of England Education Division Foundation and Aided Schools National Association Local Government Association |

## Signed

Vernon Coaker — Minister of State — Department for Children, Schools and Families — 15th December 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The organisations listed in the Schedule to these Regulations are those organisations which the Secretary of State must consult before making or revising the constitutional arrangements for the School Support Staff Negotiating Body (“SSSNB”), and the SSSNB’s constitutional arrangements must provide for the members of the SSSNB to include persons representing the interests of these organisations.
