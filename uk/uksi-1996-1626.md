# The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Amendment) Regulations 1996

Made: 24th June 1996

Coming into force: 1st August 1996

The Registrar General in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 20(a) of the Registration Service Act 1953[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the approval of the Chancellor of the Exchequer[^f00002] hereby makes the following Regulations —

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Amendment) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 1st August 1996.

#### Amendment of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages Regulations 1968

###### 2

Paragraph (1) of regulation 10 of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages Regulations 1968[^f00003] (transaction of private business by registration officers) is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Given under my hand on

Dr. D. Holt — Registrar General — 21st June 1996

Signed by authority of the Chancellor of the Exchequer

Angela Knight — Economic Secretary to the Treasury — 24th June 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke regulation 10(1) of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages Regulations 1968, which prohibits registration officers from transacting, or furthering the transaction of commercial activities.
