# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 6) Order 1988

Made: 9th May 1988

Coming into force: 1st June 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 6) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st June 1988.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated the owner of each pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — designated owners

- Britoil p.l.c., whose registered office is at 301 St Vincent Street, Glasgow G2 5DD.

- Deminex U.K. Oil and Gas Ltd., whose registered office is at Bowater House, 68 Knightsbridge, London SW1X 7LD.

- Kerr-McGee Oil (U.K.) p.l.c., whose registered office is at 75 Davies Street, Mayfair, London W1Y 1FA.

- Lasmo North Sea p.l.c. whose registered office is at Bastion House, 140 London Wall, London EC2Y 5DN.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the companies listed in Part I of the Schedule as owners of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule, which lie between offshore installations above the Beatrice oilfield, which is situated east of the Moray Firth.
