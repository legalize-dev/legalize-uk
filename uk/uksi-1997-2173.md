# The Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 1997

Made: 9th September 1997

Laid before Parliament: 10th September 1997

Coming into force: 1st October 1997

The Secretary of State for Education and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 50 and 89(4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 and shall come into force on 1st October 1997.

###### 2

The Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) Regulations 1995[^f00002] shall be amended in regulation 6(2)(b) by—

- (a) deleting the words “which immediately precedes the academic year”, and

- (b) adding to the end of the sub-paragraph the words “or the academic year which immediately precedes that academic year, as the governing body may determine.”

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Employment — 9th September 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 1st October 1997, amend the Education (Further Education Institutions Information) (England) Regulations 1995. The 1995 Regulations concern the publication of information by institutions within the further education sector in England only.

The 1995 Regulations require institutions to publish information about the occupation of students who have obtained qualifications in the academic year preceding the academic year in respect of which other information is published in the same document. The amendments allow institutions to choose whether to publish this information in relation to the preceding academic year as at present, or to publish the information in relation to the same academic year in respect of which the other information is published.
