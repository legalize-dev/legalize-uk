# The Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1988

Made: 13th October 1988

Laid before Parliament: 14th October 1988

Coming into force: 17th October 1988

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretaries of State respectively concerned with the sea fishing industry in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 5(1), (5), 15(3) and 20(1) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 17th October 1988.

#### Variation

###### 2

The Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1988[^f00002] is hereby varied by inserting in article 2 thereof (prohibition), after the words “is prohibited” the words “except on and between 17 October and 31 October 1988”.

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food is hereunto affixed on 13th October 1988

Trumpington — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Sanderson of Bowden — Minister of State, Scottish Office — 13th October 1988

Peter Walker — Secretary of State for Wales — 13th October 1988

Tom King — Secretary of State for Northern Ireland — 13th October 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Cod (Specified Sea Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1988 “the principal Order”, which prohibits fishing for cod (*Gadus morhua* —

- (a) by any British fishing boat registered in the United Kingdom or the Isle of Man within any part of a sea area specified in the Schedule to that Order, or

- (b) by any British fishing boat registered in any of the Channel Islands or by any British-owned fishing boat within any part of such a sea area which lies inside British fishery limits.

This Order varies article 2 of the principal Order to provide that the above-mentioned prohibition shall not apply on and between 17th October and 31st October 1988.

The principal Order ceases to have effect on 31st December 1988.
