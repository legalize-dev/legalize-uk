# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Member Contributions) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

Made: at 9.30 a.m. on 25th May 2023

Laid before Parliament: at 2.30 p.m. on 25th May 2023

Coming into force: 31st May 2023

In accordance with section 22(2)(b) of the 2013 Act, the Secretary of State has laid a report before Parliament[^f00002] in relation to these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement, effect and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Pension Scheme (Member Contributions) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 31st May 2023.

- (3) These Regulations have effect retrospectively from 1st April 2023.

- (4) These Regulations extend to England and Wales.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the National Health Service Pension Scheme Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/94)) (“*the 2015 Regulations*”).

Regulation 2 amends the 2015 Regulations to update the pensionable earnings bands that determine a member’s contribution rate for the 2023/24 scheme year.

These Regulations come into force on 31st May 2023 and have retrospective effect from 1st April 2023. Section 3(3)(b) of the Public Service Pensions Act [2013 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/25) provides that scheme regulations may make retrospective provision.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary of public sector is foreseen.
