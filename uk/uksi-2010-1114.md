# The Motor Cycles Etc. and Tractors Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 30th March 2010

Laid before Parliament: 1st April 2010

Coming into force: 1st June 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 .

He has been designated for the purposes of section 2(2) in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Cycles Etc. and Tractors Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 1st June 2010.

#### Amendment of the Motor Cycles Etc. Regulations

###### 2

###### 3

- (1) The Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) Regulations 2005 are amended as follows.
