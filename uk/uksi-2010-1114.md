# The Motor Cycles Etc. and Tractors Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 30th March 2010

Laid before Parliament: 1st April 2010

Coming into force: 1st June 2010

He has been designated[^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) in relation to the regulation of the type, description, construction or equipment of vehicles, and of components of vehicles, and in particular any vehicle type approval scheme.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Cycles Etc. and Tractors Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into force on 1st June 2010.

#### Amendment of the Motor Cycles Etc. Regulations

###### 2

In the Table in Schedule 1 (separate directives) to the Motor Cycles Etc. (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1999[^f00003], omit item 7 and at the end add the item set out in the Table below—

| Item | Principal Directives | Official Journal Reference | Subject Matter and (where applicable) the Chapter of the Annex |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19 | 2009/139/EC† | L322, 9.12.2009, p. 3 | Statutory markings for two- or three-wheel motor vehicles |

#### Amendment of the Tractor etc Regulations

###### 3

- (1) The Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) Regulations 2005[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1), in the definition of “separate directive”, for “the Motor Cycles Etc. and Tractors Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2009”, substitute “the Motor Cycles Etc. and Tractors Etc. (EC Type Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2010”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*Regulation*2 of these Regulations amends the Motor Cycles Etc. (EC Type Approval) Regulations 1999 in order to transpose Directive [2009/139/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0139) of the European Parliament and of the Council.

The Directive codifies and repeals Council Directive [93/34/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0034) on statutory markings for two- or three-wheel motor vehicles (O.J. L 188, 29.7.1993, p. 38) which has been amended but makes no changes of substance to the requirements for the type approval of the vehicles to which it relates. Copies of the codified Directive may be obtained from the Stationary Office (www.tso.co.uk) or the EUROPA website (http://eur-lex.europa.eu/en/index.htm).

*Regulation 3* of these Regulations amends the Tractor etc (EC Type-Approval) Regulations 2005 in order to transpose Directive [2009/144/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0144) of the European Parliament and the Council on certain components and characteristics of wheeled agricultural or forestry tractors (O.J. L27, 30.1.2010, p. 33).

A full impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business or the voluntary sector.

Commission Directive [2009/108/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0108) amending, for the purposes of adapting it to technical progress, Directive [97/24/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0024) of the European Parliament and of the Council on certain components and characteristics of two- or three-wheel motor vehicles (O.J. L 213,18.8.2009, p.10) has been transposed by virtue of the ambulatory provision in the definition of “separate directive” in regulation 3(1) of the 1999 Regulations.

This Explanatory Note incorporates the Transposition Note in respect of Directives [2009/139/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0139), [2009/144/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0144) and [2009/108/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0108) which would otherwise have been provided separately.

An Explanatory Memorandum for these Regulations is available alongside the instrument on the OPSI website, www.opsi.gov.uk.
