# The Public Trustee (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1993

Made: 10th March 1993

Coming into force: 1st April 1993

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 9 of the Public Trustee Act 1906[^f00001], and with the sanction of the Lord Chancellor, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Trustee (Fees) (Amendment) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 1st April 1993.

###### 2

In article 20 of the Public Trustee (Fees) Order 1985[^f00002]—

- (a) the figure “6½” shall be substituted for the figure “5½” in paragraph (2); and

- (b) the figure “1 2/13” shall be substituted for the figure “1” in paragraph (3).

## Signed

Tim Wood — Tim Kirkhope — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 9th March 1993

I hereby signify my sanction

Mackay of Clashfern *,C* — Dated 10th March 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order further amends the Public Trustee (Fees) Order 1985 by adjusting the withdrawal fee rate fixed by that Order.
