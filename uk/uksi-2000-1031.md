# The Yorkshire Water and York Waterworks (Amendment of Local Enactments Etc.) Order 2000

Made: 28th March 2000

Coming into force: 1st April 2000

Now therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the Water Industry Act 1991, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Yorkshire Water and York Waterworks (Amendment of Local Enactments Etc.) Order 2000 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

- (2) In this Order—

- “Yorkshire” means Yorkshire Water Services Limited; and

- “York” means The York Waterworks Limited.

#### Amendment of references in local statutory provisions etc.

###### 2

- (1) Any reference to York, or to the undertaking of York, in any local statutory provision and in any charges scheme made by York under section 76 of the Water Act 1989 or section 143 of the Water Industry Act 1991 shall have effect, subject to paragraph (2), as a reference to Yorkshire or, as the case may be, to the undertaking of Yorkshire.

- (2) Paragraph (1) only applies to any local statutory provision or charges scheme in relation to the area, things or persons in relation to which or whom that provision or scheme has effect before 1st April 2000.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Bob Dinwiddy — A Senior Civil Servant in the — Department of the Environment, Transport and the Regions — 28th March 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends references in local statutory provisions and charges schemes for the purposes of the replacement of The York Waterworks Limited as water undertaker by Yorkshire Water Services Limited.
