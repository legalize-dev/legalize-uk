# The Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1987

Made: 22nd December 1987

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 38(2) of the Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982 (Commencement No. 4) Order 1987.

###### 2

The provisions of the Act specified in the Schedule hereto shall come into force on 1st February 1988.

## SCHEDULE

| *Provisions of the Act* | *Subject matter of provision* |
| --- | --- |
| Section 22. | Application of criminal law etc. |
| Section 23, so far as not already in force[^f00002]. | Application of civil law. |
| Section 27(1)(a). | Prosecutions. |
| Section 37, so far as it relates to the provisions of | Amendments and repeals. |
| Schedules 3 and 4 specified in this Schedule. |  |
| Schedule 3, paragraphs 2, 3, 34, 42 and 43. | Minor and consequential amendments. |
| Schedule 4, so far as it relates to repeals of sections 3 and 11(3) of the Continental Shelf Act 1964[^f00003], and sections 8, 9(5) and 10 of the Mineral Workings (Offshore Installations) Act 1971[^f00004] (in the case of section 10, so far as not already repealed[^f00005]. | Repeals. |

## Signed

Peter Morrison — Minister of State, — Department of Energy — 22nd December 1987

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings sections 22 and 23 of the Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982, which make provision for the application of the criminal and civil law of the United Kingdom in relation to certain offshore activities, into force on 1st February 1988. The Order also brings into force on the same day section 27(1)(a) of the Act (prosecution of offences to which section 22 applies) together with further provisions of Schedules 3 and 4.
