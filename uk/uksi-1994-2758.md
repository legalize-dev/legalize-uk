# The Shetland Islands Council (Laxa Burn, Mid Yell) (Amendment) Water Order 1994

Made: 25th October 1994

Coming into force: 1st November 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 107(1)(b) and (3) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the Shetland Islands Council, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Shetland Islands Council (Laxa Burn, Mid Yell) (Amendment) Water Order 1994 and shall come into force on 1st November 1994.

- (2) This Order and the Zetland County Council (Laxa Burn, Mid Yell, Yell) Water Order 1965[^f00002] (hereinafter referred to as “the principal Order”) may be cited together as the Shetland Islands Council (Laxa Burn, Mid Yell) Water Orders 1965 and 1994.

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 2

In articles 4(b) and 5(2)(b) of the principal Order for the expression “60,000 gallons” there shall be substituted the expression “350 cubic metres (77,000 gallons)”.

#### Miscellaneous

###### 3

The Shetland Islands Council shall at all times after the expiration of six months from the date on which this Order comes into force keep a copy thereof at the office of the Chief Executive of the Council.

## Signed

T.D. Macdonald — Deputy Chief Engineer, The Scottish Office — 25th October 1994
