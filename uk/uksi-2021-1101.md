# The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2021

Made: 29th September 2021

Laid before Parliament: 1st October 2021

Coming into force: 22nd October 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 122(1), 123 and 124 of the Act.

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2021 and comes into force on 22nd October 2021.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales.

- (3) This Order applies to school teachers[^f00003] in England.

#### The Document

###### 2

- (1) The provisions set out in Section 2 of the Document have effect on and after 1st September 2021 for the purposes of determining—

- (a) the remuneration of school teachers; and

- (b) other conditions of employment of school teachers which relate to their professional duties or working time.

- (2) In paragraph (1) “the Document” means the document entitled “School teachers’ pay and conditions document 2021 and guidance on school teachers’ pay and conditions” published on the gov.uk website[^f00004].

#### Revocation

###### 3

The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2020[^f00005] and the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Order 2021[^f00006] are revoked.

## Signed

Robin Walker — Minister of State — Department for Education — 29th September 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes provision for the determination of the remuneration of school teachers (within the meaning of section 122 of the Education Act 2002) in England and other conditions of employment of school teachers which relate to their professional duties and working time.

The Order makes this provision by reference to Section 2 of a document entitled “School teachers’ pay and conditions document 2021 and guidance on school teachers’ pay and conditions” (“the Document”). It can be accessed on the Government website, at www.gov.uk. Copies are available to purchase from TSO Customer Services, PO Box 29, Norwich, NR3 1GN, telephone 0333 202 5070 or for inspection at reasonable times at the offices of the Department for Education, Sanctuary Buildings, Great Smith Street, London, SW1P 3BT.

The Order makes retrospective provision, under section 123(3) of the Education Act 2002, to provide that the provisions in Section 2 of the Document have effect on and after 1st September 2021 notwithstanding that the Order comes into force after that date.

The Order revokes the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) Order 2020 ([S.I. 2020/1020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1020)) and the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (England) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Order 2021 ([S.I. 2021/806](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/806)).

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order as no impact on businesses or civil society organisations is foreseen. The impact on the public sector is minimal.
