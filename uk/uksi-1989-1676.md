# The Orkney Islands Council (Loch of Boardhouse) (Amendment) Water Order 1989

Made: 11th September 1989

Coming into force: 18th September 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 17(2) and 107(1)(b) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, on the application of the Orkney Islands Council, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Orkney Islands Council (Loch of Boardhouse) (Amendment) Water Order 1989 and shall come into force on 18th September 1989.

- (2) This Order and the Orkney County Council (Loch of Boardhouse) Water Order 1958[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the principal Order”) may be cited together as the Orkney Islands Council (Loch of Boardhouse) Water Orders 1958 and 1989.

#### Amendment of the principal Order

###### 2

In article 5 of the principal Order for the expression “300,000 gallons” there shall be substituted the expression “2730 cubic metres (600,000 gallons)”.

#### Miscellaneous

###### 3

The Orkney Islands Council shall at all times after the expiration of six months from the date on which this Order comes into force keep a copy thereof at the office of the Chief Executive of the Council.

## Signed

Alasdair Paton — Deputy Chief Engineer, Scottish Office — 11th September 1989
