# The Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 (Commencement No. 6) (England) Order 2006

Made: 16th February 2006

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 93(2)(a) of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 (Commencement No. 6) (England) Order 2006.

#### Provisions coming into force in England on 6th April 2006

###### 2

Sections 48 to 52[^f00002] of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 shall, to the extent not already in force, come into force in England on 6th April 2006.

## Signed

Hazel Blears — Minister of State — 16th February 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings sections 48 to 52 of the Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003 into force, to the extent they are not already in force, in England on 6th April 2006.
