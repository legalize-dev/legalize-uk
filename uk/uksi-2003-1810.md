# The Burma (Freezing of Funds) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

Made: 16th July 2003

Laid before Parliament: 16th July 2003

Coming into force: 6th August 2003

Now, therefore, the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by that section, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Burma (Freezing of Funds) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 6th August 2003.

#### Amendment of the Burma (Freezing of Funds) Regulations 2000

###### 2

At the end of regulation 1(2)(b) of the Burma (Freezing of Funds) Regulations 2000[^f00003], there is added “, as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 744/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0744) of 28th April 2003[^f00004] and Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1070/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1070) of 20th June 2003[^f00005]”.

## Signed

John Heppell — Jim Murphy — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 16th July 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1081/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/1081) of 22nd May 2000 provided, among other matters, for a freeze on the funds of certain persons related to important governmental functions in Burma/Myanmar, and their families. These persons were listed in Annex II to that Regulation, and an amended version of that Annex was substituted in October 2002. Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1070/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1070) of 20th June 2003 substituted a further revised version of Annex II in place of the earlier replacement.

Meanwhile, Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 744/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0744) of 28th April 2003 had amended Annex III to the Council Regulation, which lists relevant address details for the European Commission and the competent authorities of the Member States.

The Burma (Freezing of Funds) Regulations 2000 provide that breaches of certain provisions of Council Regulation 1081/2000, and certain other acts and omissions, are to be criminal offences. The present Regulations amend those Regulations so that they refer to Council Regulation 1081/2000 as amended by Commission Regulations 744/2003 and 1070/2003.
