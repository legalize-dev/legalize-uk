# The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2009

Made: 2nd April 2009

Laid before Parliament: 8th April 2009

Coming into force: 3rd May 2009

Representative organisations have been consulted in accordance with section 61(2) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00004].

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Amendment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2009 and shall come into force on 3rd May 2009.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales[^f00005].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) Regulations 1986[^f00006] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulations 12(1)(a) and 12(2)(a), for “£12” substitute “£13”; and

- (b) in regulations 12(1)(b) and 12(2)(b), for “£57” substitute “£60”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Jim Fitzpatrick — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 2nd April 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations increase the fees for the registration of, or the variation of the registration of, a local bus service.

The fees for a community bus service are increased from £12 to £13. This represents an increase of 8.3%. The fees for community bus services were last increased on 1st April 2007.

The fees for a local bus service other than a community bus service are increased from £57 to £60. This represents an increase of 5.3%. These fees were last increased on 1st August 2008.

A full Impact Assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from: VOSA Corporate Office, Berkeley House, Croydon Street, Bristol, BS5 0DA. The Impact Assessment is also annexed to the Explanatory Memorandum which is available alongside the instrument on the OPSI website at www.opsi.gov.uk.
