# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Court Ordinary Cause Rules Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 1996

Made: 17th September 1996

Coming into force: 1st November 1996

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 13 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Extracts Act 1892[^f00001], section 32 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971[^f00002], section 1(3) of the Administration of Justice (Scotland) Act 1972[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, having approved, with modifications, draft rules submitted to them by the Sheriff Court Rules Council in accordance with section 34 of the said Act of 1971, do hereby enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Court Ordinary Cause Rules Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 1996 and shall come into force on 1st November 1996.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Savings

###### 2

Nothing in this Act of Sederunt shall affect any cause commenced before1st November 1996, and any such cause shall proceed according to the law and practice in force immediately before that date.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Ordinary Cause Rules in the Sheriff Court by:–

- (a) amending Chapter 9 (standard procedure in defended causes) to provide that where a second or subsequent Options Hearing requires to be fixed, the sheriff clerk will intimate to all parties the new dates in relation to defences, adjustment and the hearing (paragraph 3(13)); further amending Chapter 9 to provide that where an Options Hearing is continued, and further adjustment or amendment is made to the pleadings, the pursuer is required to lodge a certified copy of the pleadings in process not later than 2 days before the continued Options Hearing (paragraph 3(14)); and further providing for the lodging of the note of the basis for preliminary pleas where an Options Hearing is so continued (paragraph 3(29));

- (b) amending rule 12.2 (provisions in relation to interlocutors) to make provision for the content of interlocutors in undefended causes, other than family actions, in which evidence has been led, and to require the sheriff, when requested to do so by a party, to provide a note setting out the reasons for his decision in any other interlocutor (paragraph 3(17));

- (c) amending Chapter 14 (applications by minute) to introduce a new procedure for minutes, and providing new forms (paragraph 3(20), (21) and (65)(b) and (c));

- (d) amending Chapter 15 (motions) to introduce a new procedure for motions, and providing new forms (paragraph 3(22) and (65)(b));

- (e) amending Chapter 19 (counterclaims) to prescribe the form of record where a counterclaim is lodged (paragraph 3(27));

- (f) extending rule 29.6 (hearing parts of proof separately) to apply to all types of action (paragraph 3(39));

- (g) amending Chapter 31 (appeals) to introduce new rules to regulate the procedure in appeals and providing a new form (paragraph 3(43) and (65)(i));

- (h) amending Chapter 33 (family actions) to make provision for applications for financial provision on divorce where the order is in respect of a pension lump sum under section 12A of the Family Law (Scotland) Act 1985, including the provision of a new form (paragraph 3(45), (50), (51) and (65)(k)); and

- (i) making minor consequential and typographical amendments (all other sub-paragraphs of paragraph 3).
