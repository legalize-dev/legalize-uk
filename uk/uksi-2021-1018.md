# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No. 19) Order 2021

Made: 6th September 2021

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(2) of the Crime and Courts Act 2013[^f00001]

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No. 19) Order 2021.

###### 2

This Order extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Provisions coming into force on 17th September 2021

###### 3

Section 50 of and Schedule 20 (extradition) to the Crime and Courts Act 2013 come into force for all remaining purposes on 17th September 2021.

## Signed

Damian Hinds — Minister of State — Home Office — 6th September 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences section 50 of and Schedule 20 to the Crime and Courts Act [2013 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/22) for all remaining purposes. Section 50 introduces Schedule 20 which amends the Extradition Act 2003. Part 1 of that Schedule introduces the forum bar. Part 2 amends the requirements on the Secretary of State to consider human rights issues when making an extradition order. Part 3 deals with devolution issues in Scotland and is already in force.
