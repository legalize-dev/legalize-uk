# The Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) (Amendment) (Coronavirus) Regulations 2020

Made: 15th May 2020

Laid before Parliament: 18th May 2020

Coming into force: 8th June 2020

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(3) and 41(1) and (2) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012 :

#### Citation, Commencement and Expiry

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) (Amendment) (Coronavirus) Regulations 2020.

- (2) They come into force on 8th June 2020.

- (3) They cease to have effect on 8th June 2021.

#### Amendment to the Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013

###### 2

- (1) The Civil Legal Aid (Remuneration) Regulations 2013 are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 1, in Part 1 (Civil Standard and Graduated Fees), in Table 4(a) (Immigration and Asylum Standard Fees) insert as a new final column—

| *Stage 2c–online procedure (controlled legal representation)* |
| --- |
| £627 |
| £527 |

## Signed

Alex Chalk — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — 2020-05-15
