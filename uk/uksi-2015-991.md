# The Dentists Act 1984 (Medical Authorities) Order 2015

Made: 24th March 2015

Coming into force: 30th March 2015

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 24th day of March 2015By the Lords of Her Majesty’s Most Honourable Privy Council

Their Lordships make the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by article 7(4)(b) of the Health Care and Associated Professions (Miscellaneous Amendments and Practitioner Psychologists) Order 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Dentists Act 1984 (Medical Authorities) Order 2015 and comes into force on 30th March 2015.

#### Designation of University College London

###### 2

University College London is designated for the purposes of article 7(4)(b) of the Health Care and Associated Professions (Miscellaneous Amendments and Practitioner Psychologists) Order 2009 and accordingly is a “medical authority” for the purposes of section 3 of the Dentists Act 1984[^f00002] (power of medical authorities to hold examinations and grant licences in dentistry).

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates University College London as a medical authority which is authorised to hold examinations in dentistry and grant licences certifying the fitness of the holders to practise dentistry.
