# The Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2010

Made: 25th March 2010

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 116(1) of the Policing and Crime Act 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2010.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Policing and Crime Act 2009.

#### Provisions coming into force on 19th April 2010

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act is 19th April 2010:

- (a) section 3 (regulations about senior officers);

- (b) section 4 (metropolitan police force appointments);

- (c) section 112(2) (minor and consequential amendments and repeals and revocations) insofar as it relates to the provisions of Part 1 of Schedule 8 (repeals and revocations);

- (d) Part 1 of Schedule 8.

#### Provision coming into force on 8th May 2010

###### 3

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 112(1) of the Act in so far as it relates to paragraph 23 of Schedule 7 (amendments to the Licensing Act 2003[^f00002]) to the Act and in relation to Wales only is 8th May 2010.

#### Provision coming into force on 1st September 2010

###### 4

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 2 of the Act (Police Senior Appointments Panel) is 1st September 2010.

## Signed

David Hanson — Minister of State — 25th March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the fifth Commencement Order made under the Policing and Crime Act 2009 (“the Act”).

Article 2 lists provisions of the Act relating to the appointment of senior police officers which come into force on 19th April 2010.

Article 3 will allow the Welsh Ministers to bring provisions in the Act relating to sexual entertainment venues into force on 8th May 2010.

Article 4 brings section 2 of the Act (police senior appointments panel) into force on 1st September 2010.
