# The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 2003

Made: 18th November 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 162(1) and (6) of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 2003.

###### 2

This Order shall come into force on the day after it is made.

###### 3

In Schedule 1 to the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2003[^f00002] in column 2, in relation to section 123 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (advice about work permit, & c.), for “1st December 2003” substitute “1st April 2004”.

## Signed

Beverley Hughes — Minister of State — 18th November 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2003. Article 3 amends the date on which section 123 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 comes into force from 1st December 2003 to 1st April 2004.
