# The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1995

Made: 14th July 1995

Coming into force: 14th July 1995

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1995, and shall come into force forthwith.

###### 2

The interim period specified by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 shall continue until 16th July 1996.

## Signed

Northern Ireland Office

P. B. B. Mayhew — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 14th July 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends until 16th July 1996 the period specified in section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (as extended by the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1994 (S.I. [1994/1772](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1772))) for the operation of the temporary provisions for the government of Northern Ireland contained in Schedule 1 to that Act.
