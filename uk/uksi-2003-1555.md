# The Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) (Revocation) Order 2003

Made: 12th June 2003

Coming into force: 14th June 2003

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 1 of the Import, Export and Customs Powers (Defence) Act 1939[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order.

#### Name and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) (Revocation) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 14th June 2003.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Export of Goods (Control) (Iraq and Kuwait Sanctions) Order 1990[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Nigel Griffiths, — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State for Small Business, — Department of Trade and Industry — 12th June 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

In accordance with the United Nations Security Council Resolution 1483 lifting the sanctions against Iraq, this Order revokes the prohibition on exports to Iraq and Kuwait.
