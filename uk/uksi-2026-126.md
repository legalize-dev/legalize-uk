# The Digital Economy Act 2017 (Commencement No. 2) (Northern Ireland) and Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2026

Made: 11th February 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 118](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/30/section/118) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/30/section/118) of the [Digital Economy Act 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/30/contents)[^f00001] and [section 142](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/section/142) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/section/142) of the [Data (Use and Access) Act 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/contents)[^f00002].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Digital Economy Act 2017 (Commencement No. 2) (Northern Ireland) and Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 7) Regulations 2026.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “*the 2017 Act*” means the [Digital Economy Act 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/30/contents);

- “*the 2025 Act*” means the [Data (Use and Access) Act 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/contents).

###### 3

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations bring into force specified provisions in the Digital Economy Act 2017 (“*the 2017 Act*”) and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (“*the 2025 Act*”).

Regulation 2 brings into force, in relation to Northern Ireland only, specified provisions in Part 5 (Digital Government) of the 2017 Act that relate to information-sharing powers for public authorities and other persons.

Regulation 3 brings into force section 123 of the 2025 Act, which amends the power in section 35 of the 2017 Act (disclosure of information to improve public service delivery) so that it may additionally be exercised to support businesses and bodies established for charitable purposes.
