# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2020

Made: 31st July 2020

Coming into force: 1st August 2020

Laid before Parliament: 3rd August 2020

In accordance with section 45R of that Act the Secretary of State is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make this instrument without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 1st August 2020.

#### The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) Regulations 2020

###### 2

In regulation 1(3) of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) Regulations 2020, for the definition of “the protected area”, substitute—

> > - “the protected area” means the area of Leicester City Council;

## Signed

Matt Hancock — Secretary of State, — Department of Health and Social Care — 31st July 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (Leicester) Regulations 2020 to remove the area of the Borough of Oadby and Wigston from the protected area.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
