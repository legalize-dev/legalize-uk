# The Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No.1) (England and Wales) Order 2014

Made: 8th December 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 61(2) of the Crime and Courts Act 2013[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (Commencement No.1) (England and Wales) Order 2014.

#### Provisions coming into force on 2nd March 2015

###### 2

So far as extending to England and Wales, section 56 of, and Schedule 22 to, the Crime and Courts Act 2013 (drugs and driving) come into force on 2nd March 2015.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Robert Goodwill — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 8th December 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the first commencement order relating only to England and Wales made under the Crime and Courts Act [2013 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/22). It brings into force in England and Wales, section 56 of that Act together with Schedule 22 to that Act, which deal with drugs and driving.
