# The Hackney Education Action Zone (Variation) Order 2001

Made: 3rd August 2001

Laid before Parliament: 9th August 2001

Coming into force: 1st September 2001

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(3) and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencment

###### 1

This Order shall be cited as the Hackney Education Action Zone (Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “Hackney Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Hackney Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00002].

#### Variation of the Hackney Education Action Zone Order 1999

###### 3

- (1) The Hackney Education Action Zone Order 1999 is varied as follows:

- (2) In Schedule 2 of that Order the words “St John and St James CE Primary School, DfEE No. 3000, Isabella Road, London, E9 6DU” are added.

- (3) In Schedule 2 of that Order the words “Woodbury Down Community Primary School” are substituted for “Woodbury Down Infants and Junior School”.

## Signed

Stephen Timms — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 3rd August 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Hackney Education Action Zone by adding one school and changing the name of another.
