# The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order 2005

Made: 7th March 2005

Laid before Parliament: 7th March 2005

Coming into force: 28th March 2005

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 10(1) of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and having consulted the National Assembly of Wales where they have thought it appropriate, hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 28th March 2005.

#### Designation

###### 2

The bodies listed in Schedule 1 to this Order are designated in respect of the financial year ending on 31st March 2005 for the purposes of section 10 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the bodies listed in Schedule 1 in relation to the financial year ending on 31st March 2005 for the purposes of section 10 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000. The effect of this designation is that these bodies are required to prepare and present to the Treasury such financial information in relation to that financial year as the Treasury require to enable them to prepare Whole of Government Accounts.
