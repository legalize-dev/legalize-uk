# The Montrose (Pilotage) Harbour Revision Order 1990

Made: 4th April 1990

Laid before Parliament: 7th March 1991

Coming into force: 1st May 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport in exercise of the powersconferred on him by section 14 of the Harbours Act 1964[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002]and of all powers enabling him in that behalf and on theapplication of the Trustees of the harbour of Montrose in accordancewith section 7(5) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Montrose (Pilotage) Harbour RevisionOrder 1990 and shall come into force on the date fixed in accordancewith the provisions of the Statutory Orders (Special Procedure) Acts1945 and 1965[^f00004].

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “harbour” means the harbour of Montrose as described in Article 19 of theMontrose Harbour Revision Order 1974[^f00005];

- “level of high water” means the level of mean high water springs;

- “signed plan” means the plan signed in triplicate by an Under Secretary in theDepartment of Transport copies of which have been deposited as follows,that is to say, one copy at the Department of Transport, one copy withthe Sheriff Clerk of the Sheriff Court district of Arbroath and one copyat the principal office of the Trustees at South Quay, Ferryden,Montrose DD10 9SL;

- “Trustees” means the Trustees of the harbour incorporated by the MontroseHarbour Act 1837[^f00006].

#### Extension of limits of jurisdiction for purposes of pilotage

###### 3

The limits within which the Trustees shall have jurisdiction for thepurposes of pilotage under Part 1 of the Pilotage Act 1987 shall include(in addition to the harbour) the area to the east of the existingharbour, shaded pink on the signed plan, and bounded by a straight linefrom the eastern extremity of the level of high water from Scurdie NessLighthouse for a distance of 1450 metres or thereabouts to a pointlatitude 56°42"09'N longitude 02°24"58'Wintersecting an arc of 1210 metres radius from Scurdie Ness Lighthouseand then in an arc of 1210 metres radius from Scurdie Ness Lighthouse toa point latitude 56°42"28'N longitude 02°25"12'Wthen in a straight line in a west-south-westerly direction for adistance of 1230 metres or thereabouts to a position latitude56°42"19.2'N longitude 02°26"20.9'W.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Patrick McLoughlin — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 4th April 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

By virtue of section 7(5) of the Pilotage Act 1987 a directiongiven by a harbour authority for the purpose of making pilotagecompulsory for ships navigating in a specified area shall not apply toany area which is outside the authority’s harbour unless the limits ofjurisdiction of the authority for pilotage purposes have been extendedto cover that area.

This Order accordingly extends the limits of jurisdiction of theTrustees of the harbour of Montrose for pilotage purposes under Part Iof the Pilotage Act 1987 so as to include an area outside their harbourin respect of which the Trustees consider that pilotage should becompulsory.

The applicants for this Order are the Trustees of the harbour ofMontrose.
