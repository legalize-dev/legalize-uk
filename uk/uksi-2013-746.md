# The Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Regulations 2013

Made: 27th March 2013

Laid before Parliament: 27th March 2013

Coming into force: 8th April 2013

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 113(1) and 175(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001], sections 155(3), 189(1), (4) and (5), 190 and 191 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^f00002], sections 113(1) and 171(1), (3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00003] and sections 135(3), 165(1), (4) and (5) and 167(1) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00004] and now vested in them[^f00005].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Regulations 2013 and come into force on 8th April 2013.

- (2) In these Regulations “the Up-rating Orders” means the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Order 2013[^f00006] and the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating (Northern Ireland) Order 2013[^f00007].

#### Exceptions relating to payments of allowance by virtue of the Up-rating Orders

###### 2

Neither section 155(3) of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 nor section 135(3) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 shall apply if a question arises as to either—

- (a) the weekly rate at which guardian’s allowance is payable by virtue of either of the Up-rating Orders, or

- (b) whether the conditions for receipt of that allowance at the altered rate are satisfied,

until that question has been determined in accordance with the provisions of section 8 of the Social Security Act 1998[^f00008] or article 9 of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00009].

#### Persons not ordinarily resident in either Great Britain or Northern Ireland

###### 3

Regulation 5 of the Social Security Benefit (Persons Abroad) Regulations 1975[^f00010] and regulation 5 of the Social Security Benefit (Persons Abroad) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1978[^f00011] (application of disqualification in respect of up-rating of benefit) shall apply to any additional benefit payable by virtue of either of the Up-rating Orders.

## Signed

Simon Bowles — Jim Harra — Two of the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs — 27th March 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations supersede the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Regulations 2012 ([S.I. 2012/845](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/845)).

Regulation 2 provides that where a question has arisen about the effect of the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Order 2013 or the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating (Northern Ireland) Order 2013 (“the Up-rating Orders”) on a guardian’s allowance already in payment, the altered rates will not apply until that question is determined by Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs, an appeal tribunal or a Commissioner.

Regulation 3 applies the provisions of regulation 5 of the Social Security Benefit (Persons Abroad) Regulations 1975 and regulation 5 of the Social Security Benefit (Persons Abroad) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1978 so as to restrict the application of the increases specified in the Up-rating Orders in cases where the beneficiary lives abroad.

A full Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
