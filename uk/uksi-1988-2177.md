# The Iron Casting (Scientific Research Levy)(Abolition) Order 1988

Made: 14th December 1988

Coming into force: 15th December 1988

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Industrial Organisation and Development Act 1947[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Iron Casting (Scientific Research Levy) (Abolition) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 15th December 1988.

###### 2

The Iron Casting Industry (Scientific Research Levy) Order 1971[^f00002] shall have effect as if in article 5 after the words “in each year” there were inserted the words “up to and including the levy period ending on 30th June 1990”.

## Signed

Francis Maude — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,Department of Trade and Industry — 14th December 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the Iron Casting (Scientific Research Levy) Order 1971, as amended, shall cease to have effect as respects the imposition of the levy for any period after 30th June 1990.

The 1971 Order imposed an obligation to pay levies on the iron casting industry to finance scientific research to be carried out in connection with the industry by the British Cast Iron Research Association.
