# The Virgin Islands Constitution (Interim Amendment) (Revocation) Order 2026

Made: 3rd February 2026

Laid before Parliament: 10th February 2026

Coming into force: 13th March 2026

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 3rd day of February 2026Present,The King's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

His Majesty, in the exercise of the powers conferred upon Him by sections 5 and 7 of the West Indies Act 1962[^f00001] and all other powers enabling Him to do so, is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Virgin Islands Constitution (Interim Amendment) (Revocation) Order 2026.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on 13th March 2026.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The [Virgin Islands Constitution (Interim Amendment) Order 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/627/contents)[^f00002] is revoked.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order in Council)

Explanatory Note

This Order revokes the [Virgin Islands Constitution (Interim Amendment) Order 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/627/contents) ([S.I. 2022/627](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/627)) (“*the 2022 Order*”), which allowed for certain provisions of the Constitution of the Virgin Islands to be suspended, in particular those relating to ministerial government and the House of Assembly. It provided for certain offices to be vacated, including that of the Premier, and for the dissolution of the House of Assembly. It also made temporary provision for the government of the Virgin Islands by the Governor. The 2022 Order was made but never brought into force.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the United Kingdom private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
