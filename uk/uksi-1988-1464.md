# The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1988

Made: 15th August 1988

Coming into force: 1st January 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 8(1) and 135 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976 [^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st January 1989.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976.

###### 2

The fee payable by any applicant to a licensing board in respect of any matter described in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order and arising under the provision of the Act specified in column 2 of that Schedule shall be the amount specified in the corresponding entry in column 3, instead of the amount specified opposite thereto in column 4 [^f00002].

###### 3

The Liquor Licensing (Fees) (Scotland) Order 1987 [^f00003] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the fees payable from 1st January 1989 under the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976 by an applicant to a licensing board in respect of the matters listed in the Schedule to the Order. The fees are increased by 33 1/3 per cent.
