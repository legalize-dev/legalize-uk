# The Gambling Act 2005 (Exempt Gaming in Alcohol-Licensed Premises) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made: 27th July 2007

Laid before Parliament: 30th July 2007

Coming into force: 1st September 2007

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 279(2) and 280(1) of the Gambling Act 2005[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Gambling Act 2005 (Exempt Gaming in Alcohol-Licensed Premises) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st September 2007.

#### Amendment of the Gambling Act 2005 (Exempt Gaming in Alcohol-Licensed Premises) Regulations 2007

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 2 of the Gambling Act 2005 (Exempt Gaming in Alcohol-Licensed Premises) Regulations 2007[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (2), for “paragraphs (3) and (4)” substitute “paragraph (3)”.

- (3) In paragraph (3), leave out “Subject to paragraph (4)”.

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 27th July 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the [Gambling Act 2005 (Exempt Gaming in Alcohol-Licensed Premises) Regulations 2007 (S. I. 2007/1940)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/1940). Regulation 2 removes references to ‘paragraph (4)’ in regulation 2 of those Regulations, given that no such paragraph exists.
