# The Central Sussex College (Incorporation) Order 2005

Made: 28th February 2005

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 2005

Coming into force: 1st April 2005

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 16(1) and 17 of the Act and for the purpose of giving effect to that proposal, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Central Sussex College (Incorporation) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005 (“the commencement date”).

###### 2

There shall be established as from the commencement date a body corporate called “Central Sussex College”, for the purpose of establishing and conducting as from the operative date a new educational institution of that name.

###### 3

The operative date in relation to the further education corporation established by this Order shall be 1st August 2005, and the corporation shall conduct Central Sussex College from that date.

## Signed

Kim Howells — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 28th February 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes a further education corporation called “Central Sussex College” under section 16(1) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 for the purpose of conducting the educational institution called by the same name. It appoints 1st August 2005 as the operative date for the purposes of Part 1 of that Act, being the date from which the FE Corporation shall operate the College.
