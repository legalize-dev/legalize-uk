# The Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made: 26th February 2008

Laid before Parliament: 29th February 2008

Coming into force: 6th April 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43F of the Employment Rights Act 1996[^f00001]:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order 2008 and shall come into force on 6th April 2008.

#### Amendment

###### 2

The Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order 1999[^f00002] is amended in accordance with article 3.

###### 3

After the entry relating to Scottish Social Services Council, there shall be inserted the entries set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

| First Column | Second Column |
| --- | --- |
| Persons and descriptions of people | Descriptions of Matters |
| Children’s Commissioner | Matters relating to the views and interests of children. |
| Commissioner for Children and Young People in Scotland | Matters relating to promoting and safeguarding the rights of children and young people. |

## Signed

Pat McFadden — Minister of State for Employment Relations and Postal Affairs — Department for Business, Enterprise and Regulatory Reform — 26th February 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order 1999 by inserting in the Schedule to that Order entries relating to the Children’s Commissioner and the Commissioner for Children and Young People in Scotland.

Section 43F of the Employment Rights Act 1996 provides a worker with the right not to suffer detriment, as a result of making a qualifying disclosure to a prescribed person in accordance with the requirements of that Act. The 1999 Order prescribes those persons and specifies the matters for which those persons are prescribed.
