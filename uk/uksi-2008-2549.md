# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Directives) (England and Wales) Order 2008

Made: 28th September 2008

Coming into force: 6th October 2008

This Order makes provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and it appears to the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers that it is expedient for the references to the Community instruments in article 2(1) to be construed as references to those provisions as amended from time to time.

The Secretary of State, in relation to England, and the Welsh Ministers, in relation to Wales, make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972 .

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order—

- (a) may be cited as the Pollution Prevention and Control (Designation of Directives) (England and Wales) Order 2008;

- (b) comes into force on 6th October 2008; and

- (c) extends to England and Wales.

- (2) For the purposes of this article—

- (a) England and Wales includes the sea adjacent to England and Wales out as far as the seaward boundary of the territorial sea; and

- (b) the sea adjacent to Wales has the same meaning as in section 158 of the Government of Wales Act 2006 .

#### Designation

###### 2

- (1) The following Directives are designated as relevant directives for the purposes of paragraph 20(2)(c) of Schedule 1 to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999—

- (a) Directive [2006/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0021) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the management of waste from extractive industries and amending Directive [2004/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0035) ; and

- (b) Directive [2008/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0001) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning integrated pollution prevention and control .

- (2) For the purposes of this Order, the references to the Directives in paragraph (1) are references to those Directives as amended from time to time.

## Signed
