# The Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Commencement No. 16) Order 1994

Made: 6th November 1994

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 164(3) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (Commencement No. 16) Order 1994.

#### Provision coming into force on or after 1st December 1994

###### 2

- (1) Section 162(2) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990, insofar as it relates to the repeal of the Alkali, &c. Works Regulation Act 1906[^f00002] (“the 1906 Act”) in Part I of Schedule 16, shall come into force, for the purposes of the application of the 1906 Act to activities which fall within a description of a process which has been but has ceased to be a prescribed process—

- (a) on 1st December 1994; or, if later,

- (b) on the date on which that description of process ceases to be a prescribed process.

- (2) In this article “prescribed process” has the meaning given in section 1 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Paul Beresford — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment — 6th November 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the repeal of the Alkali, &c. Works Regulation Act 1906, for the purposes of the application of that Act to activities which fall within a description of process which has been but has ceased to be a prescribed process for the purposes of Part I of the Environmental Protection Act 1990, on 1st December 1994 or, if later, on the date on which that description of process ceases to be so prescribed.
