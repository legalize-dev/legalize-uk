# The HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline (Correction) Order 2024

Made: 15th October 2024

Coming into force: 16th October 2024

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 119 of, and paragraphs 1(4) and 1(8) of Schedule 4 to, the Act, makes the following Order—

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline (Correction) Order 2024.

###### 2

This Order comes into force on 16th October 2024.

#### Correction of original order

###### 3

- (1) The HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline Order 2024 is corrected as set out in the table in the Schedule to this Order, in which—

- (a) column 1 sets out where the correction is to be made;

- (b) column 2 sets out how the correction is to be made; and

- (c) column 3 sets out the text which is to be submitted, inserted or omitted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order corrects errors in the HyNet Carbon Dioxide Pipeline Order 2024 ([S.I. 2024/436](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/436)), a development consent order made under the Planning Act 2008, following a request made under paragraph 1(5)(a) of Schedule 4 to that Act.
