# The Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire)Order 1992

Made: 2nd December 1992

Coming into force: 1st January 1993

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 54(4) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to, the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973[^f00001] , and after the consultation required by that paragraph, I hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Combined Probation Areas (West Yorkshire) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 1st January 1993.

###### 2

That part of Schedule 2 to the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986[^f00002] which relates to the West Yorkshire Probation Area[^f00003] shall be amended by—

- (a) omitting the entries in respect of the petty sessions areas of Brighouse, Calder and Todmorden; and

- (b) inserting in column 1, after “Brighouse”, “Calderdale” and, corresponding to this, in column 3 “5”.

###### 3

The 5 members of the West Yorkshire Probation Committee appointed before1st January 1993 by the justices for the petty sessions areas of Brighouse, Calder and Todmorden shall continue in office as if appointed by the justices for the new petty sesssions area of Calderdale[^f00004].

## Signed

Kenneth Clarke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 2nd December 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Combined Probation Areas Order 1986 to take account of the amalgamation of the petty sessions areas of Brighouse, Calder and Todmorden into the new petty sessions area of Calderdale effected by the Petty Sessional Divisions (West Yorkshire) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/2376](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/2376)) which comes into force on 1st January 1993.
