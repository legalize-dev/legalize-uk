# The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) Amendment (England) Regulations 2012

Made: 29th February 2012

Laid before Parliament: 8th March 2012

Coming into force: 9th April 2012

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) Amendment (England) Regulations 2012 and shall come into force on 9th April 2012.

#### Increase in sum needed for personal requirements

###### 2

In regulation 2 of the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (England) Regulations 2003[^f00002], for “£22.60” substitute “£23.50”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Paul Burstow — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 29th February 2012

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (England) Regulations 2003 so that the weekly sum which local authorities in England are to assume, in the absence of special circumstances, that residents in accommodation arranged under Part 3 of the National Assistance Act 1948 will need for their personal requirements is increased to £23.50.

The Regulations come into force on 9th April 2012.
