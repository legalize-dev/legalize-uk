# The Diseases of Animals (Fees for the Testing of Disinfectants) Order 1991

Made: 7th May 1991

Laid before Parliament: 10th May 1991

Coming into force: 1st June 1991

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 84(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation, extent and commencement

###### 1

This Order, which may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Fees for the Testing of Disinfectants) Order 1991, shall apply to Great Britain and shall come into force on 1st June 1991.

#### Fees for the testing of disinfectants

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2) below, a person who submits a disinfectant for testing in accordance with article 7 of the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00002] shall pay to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food the fees prescribed in the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Until 1st August 1991 the fee payable in respect of each testing of a disinfectant for efficacy against Foot and Mouth Disease shall be £168.00 and the fee payable in respect of each testing of a disinfectant for efficacy against Swine Vesicular Disease shall be £219.00.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Diseases of Animals (fees for the Testing of Disinfectants) Order 1981[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes revised fees payable for the testing of disinfectants for the purpose of determining their suitability for listing as approved disinfectants in Schedule 1 to the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978.
