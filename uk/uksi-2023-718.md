# The Direct Payments to Farmers (Eligible Hectares and Afforested Areas) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2023

Made: at 10.45 a.m. on 28th June 2023

Laid before Parliament: at 3.00 p.m. on 28th June 2023

Coming into force: 19th July 2023

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 9(1)(a) and (c) and 50(3) of the Agriculture Act 2020[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement, extent and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Direct Payments to Farmers (Eligible Hectares and Afforested Areas) (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 19th July 2023.

- (2) These Regulations extend to England and Wales but apply in relation to England only.

#### Amendment of Article 32 of Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013

###### 2

- (1) Article 32 of Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing rules for direct payments to farmers under support schemes within the framework of the common agricultural policy[^f00002] (activation of payment entitlements) is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph 2—

- (a) in subparagraph (b)(ii), omit the words from “or under” to “Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013”;

- (b) after subparagraph (b)(ii), insert—

> > (iia) for the duration of the relevant commitment by the individual farmer, is afforested under a national scheme the conditions of which comply with—
>   (aa) Article 43(1), (2) and (3) of Regulation [(EC) No 1698/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1698)[^f00003]; or
>   (bb) Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013[^f00004], except that the duration of the relevant commitment may exceed the maximum period of twelve years specified in paragraph 1 of that Article; or

## Signed

Mark Spencer — Minister of State — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — At 10.45 a.m. on 28th June 2023

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made under the Agriculture Act [2020 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/21). They apply in relation to England only.

Regulation 2 amends Article 32 of Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing rules for direct payments to farmers under support schemes within the framework of the common agricultural policy (EUR 2013/1307). Regulation 2 amends the meaning of “*eligible hectare*” for the purposes of a claim for direct payments under the Basic Payment Scheme to include land which is afforested under a national scheme for a duration longer than the maximum period of twelve years specified in Article 22 of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council on support for rural development by the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD) (EUR 2013/1305).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant impact, on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
