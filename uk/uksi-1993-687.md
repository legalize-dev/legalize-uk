# The Nuclear Installations (Application of Security Provisions) Order 1993

Made: 12th March 1993

Laid before Parliament: 12th March 1993

Coming into force: 24th March 1993

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under section 2(1B) of the Nuclear Installations Act 1965[^f00002] and all other powers in that behalf enabling him, hereby orders as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Nuclear Installations (Application of Security Provisions) Order 1993 and shall come into force on 24th March 1993.

###### 2

The provisions set out in Schedule 1 to the Nuclear Installations Act 1965[^f00003] shall have effect in relation to Urenco (Capenhurst) Limited.

## Signed

Tim Eggar — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — Dated 12th March 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order applies to Urenco (Capenhurst) Limited the security provisions set out in Schedule 1 to the Nuclear Installations Act 1965, as amended.
