# The Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1991

Made: 4th July 1991

Coming into force: 4th July 1991

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1991 and shall come into force forthwith.

###### 2

The interim period specified by section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 shall continue until 16th July 1992.

## Signed

P. L. Brooke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 4th July 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends until 16th July 1992 the period specified in section 1(4) of the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (as extended by the Northern Ireland Act 1974 (Interim Period Extension) Order 1990 (S.I. [1990/1442](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1442))) for the operation of the temporary provisions for the government of Northern Ireland contained in Schedule 1 to that Act.
