# The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) (Environmental Standards) (Amendment) Order 2015

Made: 11th February 2015

Laid before Parliament: 18th February 2015

Coming into force: 11th March 2015

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of February 2015Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by section 8(1) and (2)(p) of the Civil Aviation Act 1949[^f00001], as extended to certain territories by the Civil Aviation Act 1949 (Overseas Territories) Order 1969[^f00002] and section 108(1) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00003] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) (Environmental Standards) (Amendment) Order 2015 and comes into force on 11th March 2015.

#### Amendment of the Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) (Environmental Standards) Order 2014

###### 2

The Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) (Environmental Standards) Order 2014[^f00004] is amended as follows.

#### Noise Certification

###### 3

In article 3(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “noise certificate”, after the first “issued” insert “or validated”.

## Signed

Richard Tilbrook — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order corrects an error in the definition of “noise certificate” in article 3(1) of the Air Navigation (Overseas Territories) (Environmental Standards) Order 2014.
