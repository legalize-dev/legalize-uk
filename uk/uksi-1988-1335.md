# The London Government Reorganisation (Property) Order 1988

Made: 27th July 1988

Laid before Parliament: 4th August 1988

Coming into force: 25th August 1988

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 67(3) of that Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the London Government Reorganisation (Property) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 25th August 1988.

###### 2

The property described in Schedule 1 to this Order shall vest in the Inner London Education Authority.

###### 3

The property described in Schedule 2 to this Order shall vest in the council of the London borough in which it is situated.

###### 4

All rights and liabilities of the London Residuary Body acquired or incurred exclusively in connection with any property transferred by this Order shall vest in the body to which such property is transferred.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order gives effect to proposals of the London Residuary Body, as modified by the Secretary of State, for the transfer of certain of the properties which it holds as successor in title to the Greater London Council. The effect of the modifications is that the transfers made by articles 2 and 3 are unconditional.

Article 2 of the Order transfers the properties listed in Schedule 1 from the Residuary Body to the Inner London Education Authority. Article 3 transfers the properties listed in Schedule 2 from that Body to the councils of the London boroughs in which they are situated.

Article 4 transfers relevant rights and liabilities.

Plans showing the land transferred may be inspected at the offices of the transferee authorities and at the Department of the Environment, Romney House, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1 3PY.
