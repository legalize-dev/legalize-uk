# The Kent County Council (Dartford Creek Bridge) Scheme 1990 Confirmation Instrument 1991

Made: 7th February 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport makes this Instrument in exercise of powers conferred by section 106(3) of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers:

###### 1

The Kent County Council (Dartford Creek Bridge) Scheme 1990 is hereby confirmed without modifications.

###### 2

The said Scheme as confirmed (hereinafter referred to as “the Scheme”) is set out in the Schedule hereto and copies of the Scheme with its accompanying plans are deposited al the offices of the Department of Transport, 43 Marsham Street, London SW1P 3PY and at the offices of the Kent County Council, Room 1:102, County Hall, Maidstone, Kent ME14 1XQ.

###### 3

The Scheme will become operative on the date on which notice that it has been confirmed is first published in accordance with paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Highways Act 1980.

###### 4

This Instrument may be cited as the Kent County Council (Dartford Creek Bridge) Scheme 1990 Confirmation Instrument 1991.

## THE SCHEDULE — THE KENT COUNTY COUNCIL (DARTFORD CREEK BRIDGE) SCHEME 1990

The Kent County Council (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) make this Scheme in exercise of their powers under Section 106 of the Highways Act 1980 and all other enabling powers:—

1 The Council are authorised to construct over the navigable waters of Dartford Creek (River Darent) the Bridge specified in the Schedule to this Scheme as part of the highway which they are proposing to construct from the A206 Thames Road, Bexley/Burnham Road, Dartford in a north easterly and then generally easterly and then south easterly direction to the West roundabout of Stone Marshes Interchange (Junction la) on the A282 South Orbital Road in the London Borough of Bexley and in the Borough of Dartford in the County of Kent.

2 This Scheme may be cited as “The Kent County Council (Dartford Creek Bridge) Scheme 1990”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport
