# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2001

Made: 2nd May 2001

Laid before Parliament: 3rd May 2001

Coming into force: 4th June 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Part I of Schedule 11 to the Children Act 1989[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 4th June 2001.

#### Amendment to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991

###### 2

In Schedule 1 to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991[^f00002], under the heading “**Western Circuit**”, after “Weymouth County Court” there shall be inserted “Yeovil County Court”.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, *C.* — Dated 2nd May 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order 1991 so as to include Yeovil County Court in the list of Family Hearing Centres.
