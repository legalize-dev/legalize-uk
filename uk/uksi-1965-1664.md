# The Import Duty Reliefs (No. 1) Order 1965

Made: 1st September 1965

Laid before the House of Commons: 7th September 1965

Coming into Operation: 8th September 1965

Whereas it appears to the Treasury expedient that the relief from import duties provided for by this Order should be allowed with a view to conforming with an international agreement made on 29th June 1964 relating to the immunities and privileges to be accorded to the European Organisation for the Development and Construction of Space Vehicle Launchers:

Now, therefore, the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury, by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 5 of the Import Duties Act 1958, and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, on the recommendation of the Board of Trade hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

Import duty shall not be chargeable on any goods which appear to the Commissioners of Customs and Excise to be imported for the purposes of the exercise of the said Organisation's official activities.

###### 2

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Import Duty Reliefs (No. 1) Order 1965.

- (2) The Interpretation Act 1889, shall apply for the interpretation of this Order as it applies for the interpretation of an Act of Parliament.

- (3) This Order shall come into operation on 8th September 1965.

## Signed

Harriet Slater — Ifor Davies — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty's Treasury — 1st September 1965

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for relief from import duty on goods imported for the development and construction of European space vehicle launchers.
