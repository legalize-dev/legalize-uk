# The Export Control (Uzbekistan) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made: 6th May 2009

Laid before Parliament: 7th May 2009

Coming into force: 8th May 2009

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7 of the Export Control Act 2002[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Export Control (Uzbekistan) (Amendment) Order 2009 and shall come into force on 8th May 2009.

###### 2

- (1) The Export Control (Uzbekistan) Order 2005[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Article 1(2), in the definition of “the Regulation” after the words “14 November 2005” insert “as amended by Council Regulation [(EC) No 154/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0154) of 23 February 2009[^f00003]”.

## Signed

Ian Pearson — Economic and Business Minister — Department for Business, Enterprise and Regulatory Reform — 6th May 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order implements in part Council Regulation [(EC) No 154/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0154) (“*the Regulation*”) which amends Council Regulation [(EC) No 1859/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1859) (“*the Original Regulation*”)[^f00004].

The Regulation replaces Annex I of the Original Regulation. Annex I lists equipment which might be used for internal repression.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has minimal impact on the costs of business.
