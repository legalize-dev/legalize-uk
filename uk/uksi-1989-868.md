# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 18) Order 1989

Made: 11th May 1989

Coming into force: 12th June 1989

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001] (hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:–

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 18) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 12th June 1989.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the companies listed in Part I of the Schedule as owners of the pipe-lines described in Part II of the Schedule. The pipe-lines lie between the Audrey gasfield and a Lincolnshire Offshore Gas Gathering System (LOGGS) platform, east of the coast of Lincolnshire.
