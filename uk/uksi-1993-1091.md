# The Bexley and Greenwich (London Borough Boundaries) Order 1993

Made: 16th April 1993

Laid before Parliament: 27th April 1993

Coming into force

The Secretary of State, in exercise of his powers under sections 51(2) and 67(4) of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order which gives effect, with modifications, to proposals submitted to him under sections 47, 48(1) and 51(1) of that Act, more than six weeks before the making of this Order, by the Local Government Boundary Commission for England:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Bexley and Greenwich (London Borough Boundaries) Order 1993.

- (2) This Order shall come into force on 1st December 1993 for any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976[^f00002].

- (3) For all other purposes, this Order shall come into force on 1st April 1994.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Orderany reference to Bexley or Greenwich is a reference to the London borough of that name;

- “the map” means one of the 5 maps prepared by the Department of the Environment, marked “Maps of the Bexley and Greenwich (London Borough Boundaries) Order 1993”, duly numbered for reference, and deposited in accordance with regulation 5 of the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976;

and any reference to a ward is a reference to a ward of the London borough from or to which an area is transferred.

#### Town and country planning

###### 5

- (1) In this article, “provision” means a provision of a development plan (as defined in section 336(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^f00003]).

- (2) Any provision applying to an area transferred by this Order shall continue to apply to that area, notwithstanding the transfer, until replaced by a provision which so applies, and which is expressed to supersede the preserved provision.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes changes to the central and southern sections of the boundary between the London boroughs of Bexley and Greenwich.

The map and table at the end of this Note give a general guide to the areas affected and the changes made.

The table also gives an approximate figure for the total area of land involved (in hectares), and for the population affected.

For full details of the transfers made, the order maps (described in article 2) should be inspected.

Any person may inspect them at any reasonable time at the offices of each of the boroughs whose area is affected, and also at the offices of the Secretary of State for the Environment located in 2, Marsham Street, London SW1P 3EB.

Consequential changes are also made to petty sessional divisions and coroners' districts.

The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976 ([S. I. 1976/246](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1976/246), amended by [S. I. 1978/247](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/247)) contain general provisions regarding the effect of orders such as this one.
