# The Treatment of Spruce Bark (Amendment) Order 2002

Made: 12th February 2002

Laid before Parliament: 12th February 2002

Coming into force: 5th March 2002

The Forestry Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 3(1) to (4) of the Plant Health Act 1967[^f00001] as read with section 20 of the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1972[^f00002], hereby make the following Order:

#### Title, extent and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Treatment of Spruce Bark (Amendment) Order 2002, shall apply to England and Wales and shall come into force on 5th March 2002.

#### Amendment of the Treatment of Spruce Bark Order 1993

###### 2

The Treatment of Spruce Bark Order 1993[^f00003] shall be amended by substituting for the Schedule to that Order the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3

The Treatment of Spruce Bark (Amendment) Order 1994[^f00004] is revoked.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

In witness whereof the Official Seal of the Forestry Commissioners is hereunto affixed on 12th February 2002.

Frank Strang — Secretary to the Forestry Commissioners

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Treatment of Spruce Bark Order 1993 by substituting a new Schedule describing the areas in which the movement of spruce bark is prohibited unless treated against the tree pest *Dendroctonus micans* Kugelann (the Great Spruce Bark Beetle) (article 2).

The spread of *Dendroctonus micans* is also controlled by the Plant Health (Forestry) (Great Britain) Order 1993 ([S.I.1993/1283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/1283), amended by [S.I.1994/3094](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3094), [1995/1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/1989), [1996/751](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/751), [1998/2206](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2206) and [3109](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/3109), [2001/295](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/295) and [2002/295](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/295)).

This Order also revokes the Order ([S.I.1994/3093](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/3093)) which previously amended the Schedule to the Treatment of Spruce Bark Order 1993 (article 3).

No Regulatory Appraisal has been prepared in respect of this Order.
