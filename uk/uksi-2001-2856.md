# Breaking the Cycle Bridgwater Education Action Zone (Variation) Order 2001

Made: 3rd August 2001

Laid before Parliament: 9th August 2001

Coming into force: 1st September 2001

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 10(3) and 138(7) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencment

###### 1

This Order shall be cited as the Breaking the Cycle Bridgwater Education Action Zone (Variation) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st September 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “Bridgwater Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Breaking the Cycle Bridgwater Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00002].

#### Variation of the Bridgwater Education Action Zone Order 1999

###### 3

- (1) The Bridgwater Education Action Zone Order 1999 is varied as follows.

- (2) The following words are deleted from Schedule 1, “Somerset Training and Enterprise Council/Somerset Lifelong Learning Partnership, East Reach House, East Reach, Taunton, Somerset, TA1 3EN (Registration No. 2396977)”, and “the University of the West of England, Redland Campus, Redland Hill, Bristol, BS6 6UZ”.

## Signed

Stephen Timms — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 3rd August 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies the Bridgwater Education Action Zone by removing two named partners.
