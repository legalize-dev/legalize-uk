# The Newcastle Upon Tyne, North Tyneside and Northumberland Combined Authority (Adult Education Functions) Order 2019

Made: 4th November 2019

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

A draft of this statutory instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament pursuant to section 117(2) of the Local Democracy Act and section 112(3) of the Deregulation Act 2015.

## PART 1 — General

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Newcastle Upon Tyne, North Tyneside and Northumberland Combined Authority (Adult Education Functions) Order 2019 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) Part 2 of this Order applies only in relation to the provision of education or training in an academic year beginning on or after 1st August 2020.

- (3) In paragraph (2), “academic year” means a period beginning with 1st August and ending with the next 31st July.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 2009 Act” means the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009[^f00005];

- “a person subject to adult detention” has the meaning given by section 121(4)[^f00006] of the 2009 Act;

- “apprenticeship training” has the meaning given by section 83(5)[^f00007] of the 2009 Act;

- “the Area” means the area of the Combined Authority; and

- “the Combined Authority” means the Newcastle Upon Tyne, North Tyneside and Northumberland Combined Authority, a body corporate established under the Newcastle Upon Tyne, North Tyneside and Northumberland Combined Authority (Establishment and Functions) Order 2018[^f00008].

## PART 2 — Adult education functions of the Secretary of State transferred to the Combined Authority or to be exercisable concurrently with the Combined Authority

#### Transfer of functions from the Secretary of State to the Combined Authority in relation to the Area

###### 3

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the functions of the Secretary of State set out in the following provisions of the 2009 Act are exercisable by the Combined Authority in relation to the Area—

- (a) section 86 (education and training for persons aged 19 or over and others subject to adult detention)[^f00009];

- (b) section 87 (learning aims for persons aged 19 or over: provision of facilities)[^f00010]; and

- (c) section 88 (learning aims for persons aged 19 or over: payment of tuition fees)[^f00011].

- (2) The functions mentioned in paragraph (1) do not include —

- (a) any functions relating to apprenticeship training;

- (b) any functions relating to a person subject to adult detention; or

- (c) any power to make regulations or orders.

- (3) The functions mentioned in paragraph (1) are exercisable by the Combined Authority instead of by the Secretary of State.

#### Functions of the Secretary of State to be exercisable concurrently with the Combined Authority in relation to the Area

###### 4

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the functions of the Secretary of State set out in the following provisions of the 2009 Act are exercisable by the Combined Authority in relation to the Area—

- (a) section 90 (encouragement of education and training for persons aged 19 or over and others subject to adult detention)[^f00012]; and

- (b) section 100(1)(provision of financial resources)[^f00013].

- (2) The functions mentioned in paragraph (1) do not include—

- (a) any function relating to apprenticeship training; or

- (b) any function relating to a person subject to adult detention.

- (3) The functions mentioned in paragraph (1) are exercisable concurrently with the Secretary of State in relation to the Area.

#### Conditions on the exercise of functions mentioned in articles 3 and 4

###### 5

- (1) The Combined Authority must adopt rules of eligibility for awards by an institution to which it makes grants, loans or other payments under section 100(1) of the 2009 Act in accordance with any direction given by the Secretary of State.

- (2) In exercising the functions mentioned in articles 3 and 4, the Combined Authority must have regard to guidance issued by the Secretary of State for the purpose of this article (as amended from time to time or as replaced by a subsequent document)[^f00014].

- (3) In subsection (1), “award” has the same meaning as in regulation 2 of the Fees and Awards (England) Regulations 2007[^f00015].

#### Modification of provisions in the 2009 Act

###### 6

For the purpose of the exercise by the Combined Authority of the functions mentioned in articles 3 and 4, sections 86 to 88, 90, 100, 101, 103, 115 and 121 of the 2009 Act apply in relation to the Combined Authority with the modifications set out in the Schedule.

## PART 3 — Amendments to the Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015

#### Amendments to the Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015

###### 7

- (1) Part 1 of Schedule 6 (Specified authorities) to the Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015[^f00016] is amended as follows.

- (2) In the entry under the heading “Education, child care etc” that begins with “The provider of education or training”—

- (a) after “Secretary of State or” insert “a combined authority established under section 103 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009”; and

- (b) omit “the Chief Executive of Skills Funding”[^f00017].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the conferral of certain adult education functions of the Secretary of State under the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (“the 2009 Act”) on the Newcastle Upon Tyne, North Tyneside and Northumberland Combined Authority (‘the Combined Authority’).

Article 1 of the Order provides for coming into force on the day after the day the Order is made; and for Part 2 to apply only in relation to the provision of education or training in an academic year beginning on or after 1st August 2020. This enables the Combined Authority to carry out the functions in relation to the 2020/21 academic year from the day the Order comes into force.

Article 3 of the Order provides for the transfer to the Combined Authority of adult education functions under section 86 to 88 of the 2009 Act, with the exception of such functions relating to apprenticeship training, a person subject to adult detention or any power to make regulations or orders. The transferred functions will be exercisable by the Combined Authority instead of by the Secretary of State in relation to the area of the Combined Authority.

Article 4 also provides for the functions of the Secretary of State under section 90 of the 2009 Act, which relate to the encouragement of education and training for persons aged 19 or over, and under section 100(1) of that Act, which relate to the provision of financial resources, to be exercisable by the Combined Authority in relation to the area. The functions will be exercisable by the Combined Authority concurrently with the Secretary of State.

Article 5 sets conditions on the exercise of the functions mentioned in Articles 3 and 4. The Combined Authority must adopt rules of eligibility for awards by an institution to which it makes grants, loans or other payments under section 100(1) of the 2009 Act in accordance with any direction given by the Secretary of State. In addition, in exercising the transferred functions, the Combined Authority must have regard to guidance issued by the Secretary of State (as amended from time to time or replaced by a subsequent document).

Article 6 and the Schedule to the Order apply certain provisions of the 2009 Act with modifications to the Combined Authority for the purpose of the Combined Authority exercising the functions conferred on it by articles 3 and 4.

Article 7 amends paragraph 1 of Schedule 6 to the Counter-Terrorism and Security Act 2015 to insert a reference to a combined authority and omit the reference to the Chief Executive of Skills Funding.
