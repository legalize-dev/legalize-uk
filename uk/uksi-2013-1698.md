# The Wirral Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2013

Made: 8th July 2013

Coming into force: 24th July 2013

In accordance with section 25(3) of that Act, the prescribed consultation has been completed[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Wirral Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2013 and comes into force on 24th July 2013.

- (2) In this Order, “the Establishment Order” means the Wirral Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2011[^f00003].

#### Amendment of article 4 of the Establishment Order

###### 2

In article 4(1) of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust)—

- (a) for “2 executive directors” substitute “4 executive directors”; and

- (b) for “2 non-executive directors” substitute “4 non-executive directors”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 8th July 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Wirral Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2011, which established the Wirral Community National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 increases the number of executive directors of the trust from 2 to 4 and the number of non-executive directors of the trust from 2 to 4. This is in addition to the chairman.
