# The Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Order 2006

Made: 29th March 2006

Coming into force: 10th April 2006

Accordingly the Treasury in exercise of the powers conferred by section 150(2) of that Act and now exercisable by them, make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Guardian’s Allowance Up-rating Order 2006 and shall come into force on 10th April 2006.

#### Amendment of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992

###### 2

In paragraph 5 of Part III of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00002] 1992 (amount of guardian’s allowance) for “£12.20” substitute “£12.50”[^f00003].

## Signed

Gillian Merron — Joan Ryan — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 29th March 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made by the Treasury as the consequence of a review, conducted by them under section 150 of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5), of the general level of prices. It details the amount mentioned in section 150(1) which it falls to the Treasury to determine in consequence of the transfer of functions in respect of guardian’s allowance under section 49(3) of the Tax Credits Act [2002 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/21).

Article 1 provides for the citation and commencement of the Order.

Article 2 increases the weekly rate of guardian’s allowance prescribed in paragraph 5 of Part III of Schedule 4 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) from £12.20 to £12.50.

This Order does not impose any new costs on business.
