# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 1992

Made: 9th March 1992

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 1992

Coming into force: 31st March 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 105, 108(1)[^f00001], and 192(1)[^f00002] of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00003] and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 195(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 and shall come into force on 31stMarch 1992.

###### 2

The Motor Vehicles (Driving Liences) Regulations 1987[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

###### 3

In paragraph (4) of regulation 33 (Effect of change in classification from groups to categories) for “1st April 1992” there shall be substituted “1st October 1992”.

###### 4

In paragraph (6) of regulation 34 (Effect of change in classification on entitlement to drive large buses) for “1st April 1992” there shall be substituted “1st October 1992”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Christopher Chope — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 9th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulations 33 and 34 of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations 1987 by extending a person’s entitlement to drive large buses by virtue of either of those regulations from 31st March 1992 to 30th September 1992.
