# The Education (Grant) (Bishop Perowne High School) Regulations 1995

Made: 2nd July 1995

Laid before Parliament: 13th July 1995

Coming into force: 7th August 1995

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(1)(b) and (3) of the Education Act 1944[^f00001], the Secretary of State for Education[^f00002] hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Grant) (Bishop Perowne High School) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 7th August 1995.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “grant” means a grant in pursuance of these Regulations;

- “the governing body” means the governing body of the Bishop Perowne High School, Merrimans Hill, Worcester; and

- “the School” means the Bishop Perowne High School, Merrimans Hill, Worcester.

#### Payment of Grant

###### 2

The Secretary of State may pay grant of such amount as she may determine, not exceeding £6,610, to the governing body in respect of expenditure incurred by them in respect of legal and other expert advice relating to a defective roof of a gymnasium used for the provision of educational services at the School.

#### Requirements to be observed

###### 3

Where grant is paid under these Regulations the governing body shall comply with such requirements (including requirements as to the repayment of grant and the payment of interest on sums due) as may be specified by the Secretary of State.

## Signed

Robin Squire — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Education — 2nd July 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations authorise the Secretary of State to pay grant to the governing body of the Bishop Perowne High School in respect of legal and other expert advice relating to a defective roof of a gymnasium used for the provision of educational services at the School.
