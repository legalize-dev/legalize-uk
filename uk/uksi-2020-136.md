# The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2020

Made: 11th February 2020

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

In accordance with section 7(1) and (2)(ha) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00002], the Minister for the Cabinet Office has consulted the Electoral Commission before making this Order.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2020.

- (2) It comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (3) In this Order “police area” means a police area listed in Schedule 1 to the Police Act 1996 (police areas outside London)[^f00003], other than Greater Manchester[^f00004].

#### Designation of police area returning officers in England

###### 2

The police area returning officer for a police area in England listed in column 1 of the table in Schedule 1 is the person who is the acting returning officer[^f00005] for the parliamentary constituency in England listed in the corresponding entry in column 2 of that table.

#### Designation of police area returning officers in Wales

###### 3

The police area returning officer for a police area in Wales listed in column 1 of the table in Schedule 2 is the person who is the acting returning officer[^f00006] for the parliamentary constituency in Wales listed in the corresponding entry in column 2 of that table.

#### Revocation and saving

###### 4

The Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2015[^f00007] is revoked, but continues to have effect in respect of any election which takes place before 7th May 2020.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes provision in relation to elections in England and Wales of police and crime commissioners under Part 1 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act [2011 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/13).

Article 2 and Schedule 1 designate the persons who, in an election of police and crime commissioners, are to be the returning officer for each police area in England, other than the metropolitan police district, Greater Manchester, and City of London police areas. The functions of a police and crime commissioner in respect of Greater Manchester were transferred to the mayor of the Greater Manchester Combined Authority by virtue of [S.I. 2017/470](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/470).

Article 3 and Schedule 2 designate the persons who are to be the returning officer for each police area in Wales.

Article 4 revokes the Police and Crime Commissioner Elections (Designation of Police Area Returning Officers) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/2031](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/2031)), but provides that it is to continue to have effect in respect of any election which took place before 7th May 2020.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
