# The Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2007

Made: 4th April 2007

Coming into force: 5th April 2007

At the Court at Windsor Castle, the 4th day of April 2007Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 114(1) of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills Order 2007 and comes into force on 5th April 2007.

#### Appointment of Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Education, Children’s Services and Skills.
