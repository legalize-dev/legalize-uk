# The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1996

Made: 19th December 1996

Laid before Parliament: 7th January 1997

Coming into force: 1st March 1997

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 19th day of December 1996 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(1), (4) and (5) of the School Inspections Act 1996[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 1st March 1997.

###### 2

Roy Lewis James, Esquire, is hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales for a period commencing on 1st March 1997 and expiring on 31st May 1997.

###### 3

The Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1994[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

N.H. Nicholls — Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order reappoints Roy Lewis James to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales for the period from 1st March 1997 to 31st May 1997. He was appointed under the Education (Chief Inspector of Schools in Wales) Order 1994 for a period of two years and two months commencing on 31st December 1994.
