# The Birmingham Specialist Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made: 27th July 2000

Coming into force: 7th August 2000

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after completion of the consultation prescribed under section 5(2) of the latter Act[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Birmingham Specialist Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 7th August 2000.

#### Amendment of NHS trust Establishment Order

###### 2

In article 4 of the Birmingham Specialist Community Health National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999[^f00004], for “5 non-executive” substitute “6 non-executive”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Yvette Cooper — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Public Health, — Department of Health — 27th July 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the constitution of the Birmingham Specialist Community Health National Health Service Trust, an NHS trust established by Order under Part I of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, by increasing the number of non-executive directors from 5 to 6.
