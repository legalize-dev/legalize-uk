# The Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1992

Made: 15th July 1992

Laid before Parliament: 23rd July 1992

Coming into force: 14th August 1992

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 15th day of July 1992 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 5(2) of the Education (Schools) Act 1992[^f00001] is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Inspectors of Schools in Wales) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 14th August 1992.

###### 2

The persons named in the Schedule to this Order are hereby appointed Her Majesty’s Inspectors of Schools in Wales with effect from 1st September 1992.
