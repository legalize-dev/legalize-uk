# The Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 4) Order 2003

Made: 27th September 2003

Laid before Parliament: 10th October 2003

Coming into force: 31st October 2003

The First Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1(5) and (6) of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) (Public Bodies) (England) (No. 4) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 31st October 2003.

#### Designation as a public body

###### 2

Yorkshire and Humberside Grid for Learning Foundation[^f00002], being a person appearing to the Secretary of State to be exercising a function of a public nature, shall be a public body for the purposes of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970, in its application to England[^f00003].

#### Restrictions on agreements

###### 3

Agreements which may be entered into by Yorkshire and Humberside Grid for Learning Foundation, by virtue of this Order, shall be subject to the following restrictions—

- (a) that any such agreements are for legal or secretarial services to be provided to Yorkshire and Humberside Grid for Learning Foundation; and

- (b) that any such agreements are for services provided by North Lincolnshire District Council.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the First Secretary of State

Phil Hope — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Office of the Deputy Prime Minister — 27th September 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 designates Yorkshire and Humberside Grid for Learning Foundation (YHGfL) as a public body for the purposes of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970 (“the 1970 Act”).

The effect of the designation is to allow the local authority concerned to provide legal or secretarial services to YHGfL by way of an agreement for the purpose prescribed by section 1(1) of the Act.

Article 3 imposes restrictions on the agreement that can be made by virtue of this Order.
