# The Water Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) (Designated Persons) (Amendment) Order 1989

Made: 3rd August 1989

Laid before Parliament: 11th August 1989

Coming into force: 1st September 1989

The Secretary of State for the Environment and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 173 of the Water Act 1989[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:–

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Water Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) (Designated Persons) (Amendment) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 1st September 1989.

#### Amendment of Order

###### 2

The Water Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) (Designated Persons) Order 1989[^f00002] is amended by the substitution for article 2(2)(a) of the following sub-paragraph–

> > (a) Water International Limited

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Colin Moyniham — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of the Environment — 2nd August 1989

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Ian Grist — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Welsh Office — 3rd August 1989

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Water Reorganisation (Pensions etc.) (Designated Persons) Order 1989 by substituting for the reference in article 2(2)(a) to British Water International Limited a reference to Water International Limited. British Water International Limited changed its name to Water International Limited on 22nd April 1988.
