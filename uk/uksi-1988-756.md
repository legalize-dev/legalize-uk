# The Income Tax (Interest on Unpaid Tax and Repayment Supplement) Order 1988

Made: 21st April 1988

Laid before the House of Commons: 22nd April 1988

Coming into force: 6th May 1988

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 40(2) of the Finance Act 1967 [^f00001], by section 89(2) of the Taxes Management Act 1970 [^f00002] and by sections 824(6) and 825(5) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 [^f00003], hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Income Tax (Interest on Unpaid Tax and Repayment Supplement) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 6th May 1988.

###### 2

The prescribed rate of interest for the purposes of each of the provisions set out in the Table below shall be 7.75 per cent. per annum.

| Section 8 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1947[^f00004] (unpaid profits tax, excess profits tax and excess profits levy) |
| --- |
| Paragraph 10(1) of Schedule 7 to the Finance Act 1960[^f00005] (unpaid profits tax recovered to make good loss due to taxpayer’s fault) |
| Sections 86[^f00006], 86A[^f00007], 87[^f00008], and 88[^f00009], of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (unpaid income tax, surtax, capital gains tax, corporation tax, including advance corporation tax, development land tax, and overpaid development land tax) |
| Paragraph 15(1) of Schedule 2 to the Oil Taxation Act 1975[^f00010] (unpaid petroleum revenue tax) |

###### 3

The prescribed rate of interest for the purposes of section 47(1) of the Finance (No. 2) Act 1975[^f00011] and sections 824(1) and 825(2) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (supplement in respect of repayments of certain taxes) shall be 7.75 per cent. per annum.

## Signed

Mark Lennox Boyd — Michael Neubert — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 21st April 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

On and after 6th May 1988 this Order reduces from 8.25 per cent. per annum to 7.75 per cent. per annum the rate of interest chargeable on unpaid income tax, surtax, capital gains tax, corporation tax (including advance corporation tax), development land tax, petroleum revenue tax, profits tax, excess profits tax, excess profits levy and on overpaid development land tax. The Order also provides that on and after 6th May 1988 supplement in respect of repayments of certain taxes will be at the rate of 7.75 per cent. per annum instead of 8.25 per cent. per annum.
