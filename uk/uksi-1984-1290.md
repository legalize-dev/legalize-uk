# The Trade Union (Nominations) (Amendment) Regulations 1984

Made: 14th August 1984

Laid before Parliament: 24th August 1984

Coming into Operation: 14th September 1984

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by paragraph 31 of Schedule 1 to the Trade Union and Labour Relations Act 1974 (“the 1974 Act”) as amended by paragraphs 31 and 32 of Part III of Schedule 16 to the Employment Protection Act 1975[^f00001] (“the 1975 Act”) and article 2 of the Administration of Estates (Small Payments) (Increase of Limit) Order 1984[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Trade Union (Nominations) (Amendment) Regulations 1984 and shall come into operation on 14th September 1984.

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) (power to make nominations) and regulation 6(1) (payments on death) of the Trade Union (Nominations) Regulations 1977 for the references to £1,500 there shall be substituted references to £5,000.

## Signed

Signed by order of the Secretary of State.

Alan Clark — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Employment — 14th August 1984

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations increase from £1,500 to £5,000 the limits on the amount which a trade union member may nominate another person to receive on his death from trade union funds, and on the amount which a trade union may distribute on the death of a member to persons appearing to be entitled.
