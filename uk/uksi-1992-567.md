# The National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1992

Made: 9th March 1992

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 67(2) of the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and extent

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 (Commencement No. 9) Order 1992 and shall extend only to England and Wales.

#### Day appointed

###### 2

6th April 1992 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 66(2) of and Schedule 10 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990 so far as they relate to the repeal of section 22(7) of the National Assistance Act 1948[^f00002].

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health

Virginia Bottomley — Minister of State — 9th March 1992

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force in relation to England and Wales the repeal of section 22(7) of the National Assistance Act 1948 (local authorities' power to charge for a child accompanying an adult in accommodation provided for the adult under Part III of the National Assistance Act 1948).
