# The A12 Trunk Road (Eastern Avenue East and Eastern Avenue West, Havering) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1992

Made: 16th March 1992

Coming into force: 23rd March 1992

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of his powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers, and after consulting the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A12 Trunk Road (Eastern Avenue East and Eastern Avenue West, Havering) (Prescribed Routes) Order 1992 and shall come into force on23rd March 1992.

###### 2

In this Order—

- “Eastern Avenue East” and “Eastern Avenue West” mean those lengths of the A12 trunk road in the London Borough of Havering known by those names;

- “northeastern traffic island” means the island site in the westbound carriageway of Eastern Avenue East which extends to the junction from a point 17 metres northeast of the junction;

- “southwestern traffic island” means the island site in the eastbound carriageway of Eastern Avenue West which extends to the junction from a point 17 metres southwest of the junction; and

- “the junction” means the junction of Eastern Avenue East and Eastern Avenue West with the highways known as Havering Road (B175) and North Street (A125).

###### 3

Every person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the right of the southwestern traffic island—

- (a) shall cause that vehicle to turn right into North Street; or

- (b) is permitted to cause that vehicle to make a U-turn at the junction in order to proceed in Eastern Avenue West in the opposite direction.

###### 4

Every person causing or permitting a vehicle to pass to the right of the northeastern traffic island—

- (a) shall cause that vehicle to turn right into Havering Road; or

- (b) is permitted to cause that vehicle to make a U-turn at the junction in order to proceed in Eastern Avenue East in the opposite direction.

###### 5

Save as provided in articles 3 and 4 above, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in Eastern Avenue East or Eastern Avenue West to make a right turn or a U-turn at the junction.

###### 6

Nothing in this Order shall apply to—

- (a) any vehicle being used for fire brigade, police or ambulance purposes; or

- (b) anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or of a traffic warden. Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport J C Miles Director (Network Management) London Regional Office of the 16th March 1992 Department of Transport
