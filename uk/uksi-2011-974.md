# The Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made: 28th March 2011

Laid before Parliament: 30th March 2011

Coming into force: 31st March 2011

The Secretary of State makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 130 of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

- (2) They come into force on 31st March 2011.

#### Amendment of the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) Regulations 2010

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 1 of the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) Regulations 2010[^f00002] is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (2)[^f00003] for “1st October 2011” substitute “1st April 2012”.

- (3) In paragraph (3)[^f00004] for “1st April 2011” substitute “1st October 2011”.

- (4) In paragraph (3)(a)[^f00005] for “1st October 2011” substitute “1st April 2012”.

- (5) In paragraph (4)[^f00006] for “1st April 2011” substitute “1st October 2011”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Mike Penning — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Transport — 2011-03-23

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend for a second time the commencement provisions of the Merchant Shipping (Ship-to-Ship Transfers) Regulations 2010 to delay the commencement of those Regulations by a further six months.

An impact assessment of the effect of these Regulations has not been prepared as they do not themselves have any impact on the cost of business.
