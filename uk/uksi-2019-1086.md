# The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

Made: 4th July 2019

Laid before Parliament: 8th July 2019

Coming into force: 1st October 2019

In accordance with section 24(4) of that Act the Secretary of State has consulted the Scottish Ministers, the Department of Justice in Northern Ireland and the Security Industry Authority.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 1st October 2019.

#### Amendments to the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Licences) Regulations 2007

###### 2

- (1) The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Licences) Regulations 2007[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 8(1) (fee to be paid on application for licence)[^f00003] for “£220” substitute “£210”.

- (3) In regulation 9 (transitional provision)[^f00004] for “1st January 2012”, substitute “1st October 2019”.

## Signed

Nick Hurd — Minister of State — Home Office — 4th July 2019

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Licences) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 amend regulations 8(1) and 9 of the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (Licences) Regulations 2007.

The first amendment substitutes a fee of £210 for the previous fee of £220 for an application for a licence. The second amendment provides that the fee in regulation 8(1) applies to any application for a licence received by the Security Industry Authority on or after 1st October 2019.

A full impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business, the voluntary sector and the public sector is available from www.legislation.gov.uk. Hard copies can be obtained by writing to the Home Office Sponsorship Unit, Home Office, 2 Marsham Street, SW1P 4DF.
