# The Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004

Made: 16th March 2004

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 94 of that Act, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) The Order may be cited as the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2004.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003.

###### 2

Sections 49 to 51 of the Act shall come into force on 26th March 2004.

###### 3

- (1) The following provisions of the Act shall come into force on 26th April 2004.

- (2) Those provisions are—

- sections 1 to 9;

- sections 13 to 19, together with Schedule 1;

- sections 26 to 31, together with Schedule 2;

- sections 47 and 48;

- sections 52 and 53;

- sections 80 to 85;

- section 88;

- section 91;

- paragraphs 1 to 4, 7 to 16, 38 to 44, 61 to 71, 75 to 83 of Schedule 5; and

- the entries in Schedule 6 which are listed in paragraph (3).

- (3) The entries in Schedule 6 (repeals) are those relating to—

- the Criminal Justice Act 1987[^f00002];

- the Criminal Justice (International Co-operation) Act 1990[^f00003];

- the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994[^f00004];

- the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00005];

- the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^f00006]; and

- the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00007].

## Signed

Caroline Flint — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — 16th March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003 which are listed in articles 2 and 3 on 26th March 2004 and 26th April 2004, respectively.
