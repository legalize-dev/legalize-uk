# The Workmen’s Compensation (Supplementation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme 1997

Made: 13th March 1997

Laid before Parliament: 17th March 1997

Coming into force: 7th April 1997

The Secretary of State for Social Security, with the consent of the Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2 of Schedule 8 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Scheme:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the Workmen’s Compensation (Supplementation) (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme 1997 and shall come into force on 7th April 1997.

#### Amendment of the Workmen’s Compensation (Supplementation) Scheme 1982

###### 2

For Article 18 of the Workmen’s Compensation (Supplementation) Scheme 1982[^f00002] (sums not payable when delay in claiming) there shall be substituted the following Article—

> > (18) An allowance shall not be payable to any person in respect of any period more than three months before the claim for the allowance is made.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Social Security.

Roger Evans — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Social Security — 13th March 1997

We consent,

Patrick McLoughlin — Bowen Wells — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 13th March 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

This Scheme amends the Workmen’s Compensation (Supplementation) Scheme 1982 (S.I. [1982/1489](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1982/1489)) by limiting to three months the period of backdating for an allowance.

This Scheme does not impose any costs on business.
