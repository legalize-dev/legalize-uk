# The Westminster Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2003

Made: 3 March 2003

Laid before Parliament: 13 March 2003

Coming into force: 1 May 2003

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 [^f00001] the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Westminster Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st May 2003.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In this Order-

- “Westminster Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Westminster Education Action Zone Order 2000 [^f00002].

#### The extension of the Westminster Education Action Zone

###### 3

The Westminster Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 1st May 2003.

## Signed

David Miliband — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 3

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Westminster Education Action Zone for a further period of two years.
