# The City of Glasgow and Eastwood Districts (Muirend Road/Netherlee Road) Boundaries Amendment Order 1989

Made: 21st February 1989

Laid before Parliament: 6th March 1989

Coming into force: 1st April 1989

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 17(2) of the said Act, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, the Secretary of State hereby makes the following Order to give effect to the proposals of the Local Government Boundary Commission for Scotland as submitted to him:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the City of Glasgow and Eastwood Districts (Muirend Road/Netherlee Road) Boundaries Amendment Order 1989, and shall come into force on 1st April 1989.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the 1977 Regulations” means the Local Government Area Changes (Scotland) Regulations 1977([^f00002]); and

- “boundary map” means the map prepared by the Scottish Office marked “The City of Glasgow and Eastwood Districts (Muirend Road/Netherlee Road) Boundaries Amendment Order 1989 Map” and deposited in accordance with regulation 3 of the 1977 Regulations;

and, in the Schedule to this Order, any reference to a boundary of a district is a reference to a boundary existing immediately before the commencement of this Order.

#### Application of Regulations

###### 3

Save insofar as they can have no application in relation to the area changes effected by this Order, the provisions of the 1977 Regulations shall apply in relation thereto for the purposes of, or in consequence of, this Order.

#### Area changes

###### 4

- (1) Those areas of the City of Glasgow District, being the Area A and Area B described in Part I of the Schedule to this Order and shown delineated, hatched and marked “A” and “B” on the boundary map, shall be transferred to Eastwood District.

- (2) Part of Eastwood District, being the Area C described in Part II of the Schedule to this Order and shown delineated, hatched and marked “C” on the boundary map, shall be transferred to the City of Glasgow District.

#### Consequential changes of electoral arrangements

###### 5

- (1) Area A described in Part I of the Schedule to this Order (being the Area A transferred by article 4(1) of this Order)—

- (a) shall cease to form part of electoral division 36 (Pollokshaws/Newlands) of Strathclyde Region([^f00003]) and of ward 56 (Newlands) of the City of Glasgow District([^f00004]); and

- (b) shall form part of electoral division 70 (Eastwood North) of Strathclyde Region and of ward 3 (Giffnock East) of Eastwood District([^f00005]).

- (2) Area B described in Part I of the Schedule to this Order (being the Area B transferred by article 4(1) of this Order)—

- (a) shall cease to form part of electoral division 36 (Pollokshaws/Newlands) of Strathclyde Region and of ward 56 (Newlands) of the City of Glasgow District; and

- (b) shall form part of electoral division 70 (Eastwood North) of Strathclyde Region and of ward 4 (Netherlee) of Eastwood District.

- (3) Area C described in Part II of the Schedule to this Order (being the Area C transferred by article 4(2) of this Order)—

- (a) shall cease to form part of electoral division 70 (Eastwood North) of Strathclyde Region and of ward 4 (Netherlee) of Eastwood District; and

- (b) shall form part of electoral division 36 (Pollokshaws/Newlands) of Strathclyde Region and of ward 56 (Newlands) of the City of Glasgow District.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order transfers 2 areas extending in total to approximately 20 acres from the City of Glasgow District to Eastwood District and an area of approximately 20 acres from Eastwood District to the City of Glasgow District and makes consequential changes to electoral arrangements.

The map annexed to and forming part of this note gives a general guide to the area affected by this Order as from 1st April 1989.

Copies of the detailed boundary map defined in article 2 of this Order may be inspected at the offices of the local authorities referred to in this Order, and may be inspected at or purchased from the Scottish Development Department’s Map Library, New St Andrew’s House, St James Centre, Edinburgh, EH1 3SZ.
