# The North West Water Authority (Wilmslow and Alderley Edge) Order 1988

Made: 20th January 1988

Coming into force: 21st January 1988

The Secretary of State for the Environment, on the application of the North West Water Authority, being statutory water undertakers[^f00001], and in exercise of powers conferred by sections 33 and 59(1)[^f00002] of the Water Act 1945[^f00003] and now vested in him[^f00004], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order :

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the North West Water Authority (Wilmslow and Alderley Edge) Order 1988 and shall come into force on the 21st January 1988.

#### Repeal of local enactment

###### 2

Section 61 of the Stockport Corporation Water Act 1899[^f00005] (which provides for the Corporation to soften and clarify the water supplied by them) shall cease to have effect.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Dinah Nichols — An Under Secretary in the — Department of the Environment — 20th January 1988
