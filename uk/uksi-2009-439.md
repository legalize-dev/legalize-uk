# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Walsall) Designation Order 2009

Made: 27th February 2009

Laid before Parliament: 5th March 2009

Coming into force: 1st April 2009

Accordingly, the Secretary of State for Transport[^f00002] makes this Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (Walsall) Designation Order 2009 and shall come into force on 1st April 2009.

#### Designation of civil enforcement area and special enforcement area

###### 2

The Secretary of State designates the area described in the Schedule as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and

- (b) a special enforcement area.

## SCHEDULE — Area designated as a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions and a special enforcement area

This Order applies to the area of the Walsall Metropolitan Borough Council with the exception of—

- (a) the M6 southbound carriageway from the boundary of the district of South Staffordshire (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SJ979023) to the boundary of the Metropolitan Borough of Sandwell (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP017954);

- (b) the M6 northbound carriageway from the boundary of the Metropolitan Borough of Sandwell (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP017954) to the boundary of the district of South Staffordshire (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SJ979023);

- (c) the section of the southbound M6/M5 link road from Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP017956 to the boundary of the Metropolitan Borough of Sandwell (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP017955);

- (d) the section of the northbound M5/M6 link road from the boundary of the Metropolitan Borough of Sandwell (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP017953) to Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SP017955;

- (e) the M6 Toll from the boundary of the district of Cannock Chase (at Ordnance Survey National Grid Reference point SK030072) to the boundary of the district of Lichfield (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SK033071);

- (f) the A5 from the boundary of the district of Cannock Chase (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SK031065) to the boundary of the district of Lichfield (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SK051065); and

- (g) the A5 from the boundary of the district of Lichfield (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SK054065) to the boundary of the district of Lichfield (at Ordnance Survey National Grid reference point SK061065).

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Andrew Adonis — Minister of State — Department for Transport — 27th February 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the area of the Walsall Metropolitan Borough Council as a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions, and as a special enforcement area, for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004. Part of the Council’s area (being motorways and trunk roads) has been excluded from the designation.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
