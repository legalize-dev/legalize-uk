# The Courts Act 2003 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2007

Made: 5th September 2007

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 110 of the Courts Act 2003[^f00001]:

###### 1

Subsections (2) and (3) of section 50 of the Courts Act 2003 (youth courts) shall come into force.

## Signed

Bridget Prentice — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State — Ministry of Justice — 5th September 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 50(2) and (3) of the Courts Act 2003. Section 50(2) omits Schedule 2 of the Children and Young Persons Act [1933 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1933/12) (constitution of youth courts) and section 50(3) omits section 146 of the Magistrates’ Courts Act [1980 (c.43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1980/43) (rules relating to youth court panels and the composition of youth courts).
