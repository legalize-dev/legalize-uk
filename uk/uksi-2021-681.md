# The Official Controls (Temporary Measures) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2021

Made: at 12.30 p.m. on 8th June 2021

Laid before Parliament: at 4.30 p.m. on 8th June 2021

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(b)

In accordance with the preconditions in Article 3(2B) of that Regulation, the Welsh Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Wales, and the Scottish Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Scotland, have consented to the Secretary of State making these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

These Regulations—

- (a) may be cited as the Official Controls (Temporary Measures) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2021;

- (b) come into force on 30th June 2021, regulation 3 coming into force immediately after regulation 2;

- (c) extend to England and Wales, and Scotland.

#### Amendment to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/466 on temporary measures to contain risks to human, animal and plant health and animal welfare during certain serious disruptions of member States’ control systems due to coronavirus disease (COVID-19)

###### 2

- (1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/466 on temporary measures to contain risks to human, animal and plant health and animal welfare during certain serious disruptions of member States’ control systems due to coronavirus disease (COVID-19)[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Article 6, for “1st July” substitute “31st December”.

#### Amendment to the Official Controls (Temporary Measures) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

###### 3

- (1) The Official Controls (Temporary Measures) (Coronavirus) (Amendment) Regulations 2021[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) Omit regulation 2(4).

## Signed

Benyon of Englefield — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs — At 12.30 p.m. on 8th June 2021

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 141 of Regulation (EU) 2017/625, which is amended by [S.I. 2020/1481](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1481). They amend Commission Implementing (EU) Regulation 2020/466 (which is amended by [S.I. 2020/1481](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1481) and [S.I. 2021/78](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2021/78)) to extend the period of application of availability of existing easements in the system of official controls in operation in Great Britain, in order to contain risks of serious disruption to the system caused by the coronavirus pandemic, by postponing its expiry date to 31st December 2021.

The Welsh Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Wales, and the Scottish Ministers, in relation to the application of these Regulations in relation to Scotland, have consented to the Secretary of State making these Regulations.
