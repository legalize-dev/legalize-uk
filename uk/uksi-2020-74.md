# The European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: 29th January 2020

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 23(6) and 25(4) of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional Provisions and Amendment) Regulations 2020.

#### Provisions coming into force on exit day

###### 2

The following provisions of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 come into force on exit day[^f00002]—

- (a) section 7(1) and (6) (status of retained EU law);

- (b) section 23(5) (consequential provision) so far as relating to any provision in paragraph (c)(i) and (ii); and

- (c) the following paragraphs of Schedule 8—

- (i) 7 and 8 (existing powers to make subordinate legislation etc.); and

- (ii) 10 to 12 (future powers to make subordinate legislation).

#### Amendment of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (Commencement and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2018

###### 3

- (1) The European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (Commencement and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2018[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In the heading to regulation 4, for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

- (3) In regulation 4, for “exit day” substitute “IP completion day”.

- (4) In regulation 5, for the words from “exit day” to the end of that regulation substitute “IP completion day, as a reference to an “international obligation to the extent it is not an EU obligation.”.”.

## Signed

Steve Barclay — Secretary of State for Exiting the European Union — Department for Exiting the European Union — 29th January 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into force provisions of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act [2018 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) (“the Act”) on “exit day”. “Exit day” means 31 January 2020 at 11 p.m. in accordance with section 20(1) of the Act.

Regulation 2 brings into force section 7(1) and (6), which relates to the status of retained EU law, and paragraphs 7, 8 and 10 to 12 of Schedule 8, which provide glosses to existing and future powers to make subordinate legislation respectively.

These Regulations also amend the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 (Commencement and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/808](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/808)) (“the First Commencement Regulations”).

Regulation 3 amends the day appointed for the coming into force of provisions in the Act relating to the repeal of the European Union Act [2011 (c. 12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2011/12) (to the extent not already been commenced) from exit day to IP completion day (31 December 2020 at 11 p.m.). It also amends the transitional provision in the First Commencement Regulations to make it clear that following the repeal of the reference to EU obligations in section 30 of the Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act [2015 (c. 26)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/26) the remaining reference to international obligations does not include EU obligations during the period from 4 July 2018 to IP completion day.
