# The Heavy Goods Vehicles (Drivers' Licences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1988

Made: 24th June 1988

Laid before Parliament: 1st July 1988

Coming into force: 26th September 1988

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 119(1), 120(1) and 124 of the Road Traffic Act 1972[^f00001], now vested in him[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 199(2) of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Heavy Goods Vehicles (Drivers' Licences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1988 and shall come into force on 26th September 1988.

###### 2

The Heavy Goods Vehicles (Drivers' Licences) Regulations 1977[^f00003] shall be further amended in accordance with the following provisions of these Regulations.

###### 3

In the regulations specified in column 1 of the Table below for the words specified in column 2 of that Table there shall be substituted the words specified in column 3 of that Table.

| *1* | *2* | *3* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Regulation* | *Existing Words* | *Substituted Words* |
| 8 | £3 | £5 |
| 8 | £7.50 | £10 |
| 14(1) | £1.50 | £5 |
| 14(3) | £1.50 | £5 |

###### 4

The amendments effected by these Regulations shall not apply where an application for a licence (full, provisional, exchange or duplicate) was received before 26th September 1988.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Peter Bottomley — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, — Department of Transport — 24th June 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Heavy Goods Vehicles (Drivers' Licences) Regulations 1977 by providing for increases in fees as follows—

- (a) in regulation 8, for a full hgv driver’s licence, from £7.50 to £10;

- (b) in regulation 8, for a provisional hgv driver’s licence, from £3 to £5; and

- (c) in regulation 14(1) and (3), for a duplicate and exchange hgv driver’s licence respectively, from £1.50 to £5.

The increases take effect in relation to applications for licences received on or after 26th September 1988.
