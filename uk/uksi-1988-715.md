# The Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988

Made: 5th April 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9(2) of the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988 (Commencement) Order 1988.

###### 2

The Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988 shall come into force on 12th April 1988.

## Signed

Geoffrey Howe — Her Majesty’s Principal Secretary of State for Foreign and Commonwealth Affairs — 5th April 1988

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 12th April 1988 the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988.
