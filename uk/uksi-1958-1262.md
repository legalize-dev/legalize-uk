# The Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order 1958

Made: 30th July 1958

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 30th day of July, 1958

Present,

The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in exercise of the powers conferred on Her by subsection (1) of section 15 of the Visiting Forces Act, 1952, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

#### Citation and construction

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order, 1958, and shall be read and construed as one with the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Order, 1954[^f00001], hereinafter called “the Principal Order”.

#### Amendment of section 1 of Principal Order

###### 2

In the Principal Order, as amended by the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order, 1954[^f00002], and the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) (Amendment) Order, 1956[^f00003], the words “for the purposes of its extension to Cyprus” in section 1 are hereby replaced by the words “for the purposes of its extension to Cyprus, Gibraltar and Malta”.

## Signed

W. G. Agnew

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The purpose of this Order is to amend the Visiting Forces (Designation) (Colonies) Order, 1954, by extending to Gibraltar and Malta the provisions of the Visiting Forces (Designation) Order, 1954, in so far as these provisions relate to France.
