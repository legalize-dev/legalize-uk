# The Public Telecommunication System Designation (Clyde Cablevision) (Greater Glasgow) Order 1991

Made: 5th June 1991

Laid before Parliament: 13th June 1991

Coming into force: 11th July 1991

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Clyde Cablevision) (Greater Glasgow) Order 1991 and shall come into force on 11th July 1991.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

John Redwood — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 5th June 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Clyde Cablevision on 29th January 1991 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in all but the north-western part of the City of Glasgow and small adjoining parts of the Districts of Strathkelvin and Eastwood. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 13th June 1991. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Clyde Cablevision will be a public telecommunications operator in respect of its Greater Glasgow public telecommunication systems when the Order comes into force on 11th July 1991.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
