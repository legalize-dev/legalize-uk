# The European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2013

Made: 19th August 2013

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(1)

In accordance with section 7(2)(b) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00002], the Lord President of the Council has consulted the Electoral Commission prior to making this Order.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2013 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

- (2) In this Order a reference to an electoral region or constituency is a reference to that region or constituency as it is for the time being.

#### Revocation and savings

###### 2

The European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2008[^f00003] is revoked.

#### Returning Officers in England

###### 3

The designated returning officer at a European Parliamentary election for the electoral region in England (including the combined region[^f00004]) specified in the left-hand column of the Schedule to this Order shall be the person who is the acting returning officer[^f00005] at a parliamentary election for the constituency specified in the right-hand column of the Schedule.

#### Returning Officer for Wales

###### 4

The designated returning officer at a European Parliamentary election for the electoral region of Wales shall be the person who is the acting returning officer at a parliamentary election for the Preseli Pembrokeshire constituency.

#### Returning Officer for Scotland

###### 5

The designated returning officer at a European Parliamentary election for the electoral region of Scotland shall be the person who is the returning officer[^f00006] at a parliamentary election for the Falkirk County constituency.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes and replaces the European Parliamentary Elections (Returning Officers) Order 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1914](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1914)). The 2008 Order appointed returning officers for the electoral regions in England, Scotland and Wales for the European Parliamentary Elections in 2009. Article 3 of, and the Schedule to, this Order designate returning officers at European Parliamentary elections for the electoral regions in England. Articles 4 and 5 designate the returning officer for such elections for the electoral regions of Wales and Scotland respectively.
