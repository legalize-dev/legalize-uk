# The A12 Trunk Road (Redbridge) (No. 1) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1998

Made: 12th January 1998

Coming into force: 19th January 1998

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 6 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A12 Trunk Road (Redbridge) (No. 1) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Variation Order 1998, and shall come into force on 19th January 1998.

#### Variation

###### 2

The A12 Trunk Road (Redbridge) (No. 1) Red Route Traffic Order 1996[^f00003] is varied so that in Schedule 2A, a new item number 7 is added as follows:

> > (7) Area of footway 0.3 metres from and parallel to the southern kerbline between a point 4.5 metres west of the party wall of Nos. 140 and 142 Eastern Avenue and a point 5.0 metres east of the party wall of Nos. 146 and 148 Eastern Avenue and having a width of 2.5 metres throughout.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The A12 Trunk Road (Redbridge) (No. 1) Red Route Traffic Order 1996 Experimental Variation Order 1997[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 12th January 1998
