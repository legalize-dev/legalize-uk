# The Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) (No. 2) Order 2001

Made: 18th December 2001

Laid before Parliament: 19th December 2001

Coming into force: 14th January 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 21(8) and 51(4) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) (No. 2) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 14th January 2002.

- (2) This Order extends to England and Wales and Scotland.

###### 2

It is hereby directed that paragraph (c) of section 21(2) of the Gaming Act 1968[^f00002] (conditions on which gaming for prizes is permitted at licensed club premises) shall have effect as if for the sum specified in that paragraph there were substituted the sum of £25.

###### 3

Article 4(c) of the Gaming Act (Variation of Monetary Limits) Order 1999[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Richard Caborn — Minister of State — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 18th December 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the maximum cash prize that may be offered in a prize bingo game played in a bingo club from £15 to £25.
