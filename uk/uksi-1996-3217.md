# The Civil Evidence Act 1995 (Commencement No. 1)Order 1996

Made: 19th December 1996

Coming into force: 31st January 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 16(2) of the Civil Evidence Act 1995[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Evidence Act 1995 (Commencement No. 1) Order 1996.

###### 2

Except for sections 10 and 16(5), the Civil Evidence Act 1995 shall come into force on 31st January 1997.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern,C. — Dated 19th December 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that, except for sections 10 and 16(5), the Civil Evidence Act 1995 shall come into force on 31st January 1997.
