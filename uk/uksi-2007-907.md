# The Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2007

Made: 21st March 2007

Coming into force: 1st June 2007

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 21st day of March 2007Present,The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 19(1) of the Education Act 2005 and in accordance with that recommendation, is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st June 2007.

###### 2

Miss Susan Lewis is hereby re-appointed Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru for a period of six months commencing on 1st June 2007.

###### 3

The Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2002[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Christine Cook — Deputy Clerk of the Privy Council

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order re-appoints Susan Lewis to be Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru for a further period of six months from 1st June 2007. Her present appointment under the Education (Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales) Order 2002 ends on 31st May 2007. That Order is revoked by the present Order.
