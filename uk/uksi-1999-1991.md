# The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation Order 1999

Made: 13th July 1999

Coming into force: 2nd August 1999

The Traffic Director for London, in accordance with a direction given to him by the Secretary of State under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:–

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998 Experimental Variation Order 1999, and shall come into force on 2nd August 1999.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A205 Trunk Road (Lewisham) Red Route Experimental Traffic Order 1998[^f00003] is varied by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In schedule 1 item 3 is substituted as follows:–

> > Stanstead Road link road between the north arm and south arm,

| 3 | both sides of the carriageway west of the eastern island (including the island situated between the eastern island and western island); and both sides of the carriageway east of that island. |
| --- | --- |

###### 4

In schedule 1 item 5 is substituted as follows:–

> > Catford Road,

| 5A | north side, from its junction with Catford Hill, to a point 65.0 metres west of its junction with Rushey Green. |
| --- | --- |
| 5B | south side, from its junction with Catford Hill, to a point 40.0 metres west of its junction with Rushey Green. |
| 5C | both sides of the northern limb, between its junction with Rushey Green and a point 65.0 metres west of that junction. |
| 5D | both sides of the southern limb, between its junction with Rushey Green and a point 40.0 metres west of that junction. |
| 5E | both sides of the road between the northern limb of Catford Road and the southern limb of Catford Road adjoining the island site on the west of Rushey Green. |

###### 5

In schedule 4 item 6 is substituted as follows:–

> > Stanstead Road link road between the north arm and south arm,

| 6 | both sides of the carriageway west of the eastern island (including the island situated between the eastern island and western island); and both sides of the carriageway east of that island. |
| --- | --- |

###### 6

In schedule 4 item 7 is substituted as follows:–

> > Stanstead Road link road between the north arm and south arm,

| 7 | west side of the carriageway east of the eastern island from the projected southern kerbline of Stanstead Road (north arm), south for a distance of 3.0 metres. |
| --- | --- |

###### 7

In schedule 4 item 8 is deleted.

###### 8

In schedule 4 item 15 is substituted as follows:–

> > Catford Road,

| 15 | south side, from its junction with Catford Hill, to a point 40.0 metres west of its junction with Rushey Green. |
| --- | --- |

###### 9

In schedule 4 item 17 is substituted as follows:–

> > Catford Road,

| 17A | north side, from a point 34.0 metres east of the eastern boundary of Thomas Lane, to a point 65.0 metres west of its junction with Rushey Green. |
| --- | --- |
| 17B | both sides of the northern limb, between its junction with Rushey Green and a point 65.0 metres west of that junction. |
| 17C | both sides of the southern limb, between its junction with Rushey Green and a point 40.0 metres west of that junction. |
| 17D | both sides of the road between the northern limb of Catford Road and the southern limb of Catford Road adjoining the island site on the west of Rushey Green. |

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State.

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 13th July 1999
