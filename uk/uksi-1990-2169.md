# The Public Telecommunication System Designation(Coastal Cablevision of Dorset Ltd) (West Dorset)Order 1990

Made: 1st November 1990

Laid before Parliament: 7th November 1990

Coming into force: 5th December 1990

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 9 of the Act, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Telecommunication System Designation (Coastal Cablevision of Dorset Ltd) (West Dorset) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 5th December 1990.

###### 2

Each of the Applicable Cabled Systems is hereby designated as a public telecommunication system.

## Signed

Douglas Hogg — Minister of State, — Department of Trade and Industry — 1st November 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Secretary of State granted to Coastal Cablevision of Dorset Ltd on 28th September 1990 a licence under section 7 of the Telecommunications Act 1984 to run the telecommunication systems specified in Annex A to that licence, in the greater part of West Dorset District and the whole of the Borough of Weymouth and Portland. A copy of the licence was laid before Parliament on 7th November 1990. This Order designates those telecommunication systems as public telecommunication systems. Consequently, by virtue of section 9(3) of that Act, Coastal Cablevision of Dorset Ltd will be a public telecommunications operator when the Order comes into force.

A copy of the licence may be obtained from the Office of Telecommunications, Export House, 50 Ludgate Hill, London EC4M 7JJ.
