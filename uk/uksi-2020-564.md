# The A2 Trunk Road (Bean and Ebbsfleet Junction Improvements) (Slip Roads and Roundabouts) Order 2020

Made: 27th May 2020

Coming into force: 11th June 2020

This Order is made in compliance with the provisions of Parts I and III of Schedule 1 to that Act.

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A2 Trunk Road (Bean and Ebbsfleet Junction Improvements) (Slip Roads and Roundabouts) Order 2020 and shall come into force on 11th June 2020.

###### 2

In this Order –

- (1) all measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway;

- (2) –

| (a) | “Highways England” | means Highways England Company Limited, which is a strategic highways company (Company No. 9346363), which is a company registered in England and Wales and whose Registered Office is Bridge House, Walnut Tree Close, Guildford, Surrey, GU1 4LZ; |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (b) | “the plan” | means one of the plans marked “Key Plan”, “Site Plan 1” and “Site Plan 2” contained in the plan folio numbered HE/10/MP/001 and marked “The A2 Trunk Road (Bean and Ebbsfleet Junction Improvements) (Slip Roads and Roundabouts) Order 2020”, signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport and deposited at Department for Transport, Room F13, Ashdown House, St Leonards on Sea, Hastings, TN37 7GA and at the office of Highways England Company Limited, Lateral, 8 City Walk, Leeds, LA11 9AT, a duplicate copy has been deposited at the office of the Secretary of State for Transport; and |
| (c) | “the proposed new trunk roads” | means the new slip roads and roundabout extension which are proposed to be constructed by Highways England along the routes described in the Schedule to this Order. |

###### 3

The centre line of each of the proposed new trunk roads is indicated by a heavy black line on the plan.

###### 4

The Secretary of State for Transport hereby directs that the proposed new trunk roads shall become trunk roads from the date when this Order comes into force and that Highways England, as a strategic highways company, shall become the highway authority for those trunk roads from that date.

## SCHEDULE — ROUTES OF THE SLIP ROADS AND ROUNDABOUT EXTENSION

The lengths of new highways becoming trunk roads are:

- (1) Bean Junction:

1 A A2 Trunk Road eastbound entry slip road from the B255 Bean North Roundabout carriageway as proposed to be modified by Highways England to the existing A2 Trunk Road eastbound carriageway. The entry slip road (A-B) along this route being given the number 1 on site plan 1.

2 A A2 Trunk Road westbound entry slip road from the B255 Bean South Roundabout carriageway as proposed to be modified by Highways England to the existing A2 Trunk Road westbound carriageway. The entry slip road (C-D) along this route being given the number 2 on site plan 1.

3 A A2 Trunk Road westbound exit slip road from the existing A2 Trunk Road westbound exit slip carriageway to the B255 Bean South Roundabout carriageway as proposed to be modified by Highways England. The exit slip road (E-F) along this route being given the number 3 on site plan 1. 2 Ebbsfleet Junction:

1 A A2 Trunk Road eastbound exit slip road from the existing A2 Trunk Road eastbound exit slip carriageway to the Ebbsfleet West Roundabout to Ebbsfleet East Roundabout connector road carriageway as proposed to be modified by Highways England. The exit slip road (G-H) along this route being given the number 4 on site plan 2.

2 A A2 Trunk Road roundabout extension to the Ebbsfleet East Roundabout carriageway as proposed to be modified by Highways England. The partial roundabout extension (J-K) along this route being given the number 5 on site plan 2.

3 A A2 Trunk Road eastbound entry slip road from the Ebbsfleet East Roundabout carriageway as proposed to be modified by Highways England (as (2)2. above) to the existing A2 Trunk Road eastbound carriageway. The entry slip road (L-M) along this route being given the number 6 on site plan 2. [image omitted] [image omitted] [image omitted] [image omitted]

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides that 5 new slip roads and 1 modified roundabout to connect the A2 Trunk Road to the B255 Bean North and B255 Bean South Roundabouts and Ebbsfleet East Roundabout shall become trunk roads from the date on which this Order comes into force and that Highways England, as a strategic highways company, shall become the highway authority for those trunk roads from that date.
