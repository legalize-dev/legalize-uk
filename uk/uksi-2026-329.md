# The Higher Education (Fee Limits and Fee Limit Condition) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 20th March 2026

Coming into force: 21st March 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 119](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/section/119) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/section/119) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/section/119) and [(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/section/119) of, and [paragraphs 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/2) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/2), [2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/2) [(11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/2), [3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/3) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/3), [3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/3) [(10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/3) and [4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/4) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/4) of [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2) to the [Higher Education and Research Act 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/contents)[^f00001].

In accordance with [paragraph 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/5) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/5) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/5) of [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2) to [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/contents) the Secretary of State is satisfied that the increases to the higher amounts are no greater than are required to maintain the value of those amounts in real terms.

In accordance with [paragraph 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/5) [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/5) of [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2) to [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/contents) the Secretary of State has had regard to the index of prices specified in regulations treated as made by the Secretary of State under [that sub-paragraph](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/schedule/2/paragraph/5).[^f00002]

In accordance with [section 119](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/section/119) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/section/119) [(i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/section/119) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29/contents), a draft of this instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Higher Education (Fee Limits and Fee Limit Condition) (England) Regulations 2018 ([S.I. 2018/903](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/903)) (“*the 2018 Regulations*”) and the Higher Education (Fee Limits for Accelerated Courses) (England) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/214](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/214)) (“*the 2019 Regulations*”). The amendments introduce new, increased course fee limits for qualifying courses provided by certain English higher education providers. The new fee limits apply to academic years beginning on or after 1st August 2026 but before 1st August 2027 and academic years beginning on or after 1st August 2027. The fee limits apply only to courses which begin before 1 January 2027.

The fee limits that currently apply to academic years beginning on or after 1st August 2019 but before 1st August 2026 are preserved. The fee limits that currently apply to lower-fee foundation years that begin on or after 1st August 2025 are also preserved.

The fee changes apply to English higher education providers which are required to limit their tuition fees by virtue of section 10 of the Higher Education and Research Act [2017 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/29) (“*the 2017 Act*”) as a condition of registration with the regulator of higher education providers, the Office for Students.

Regulations 3 and 11 make amendments such that the fee limits apply only to courses which begin before 1 January 2027.

Regulations 4 to 6 make amendments such that the 2018 Regulations introduce new, increased higher amounts of fees and floor amounts in respect of those higher amounts. These expressions are defined by paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to the 2017 Act.

Regulations 7 to 9 make amendments such that the 2018 Regulations introduce new, increased basic amounts of fees and floor amounts in respect of those basic amounts. These expressions are defined by paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 to the 2017 Act.

Regulations 10 to 15 make similar amendments to the 2019 Regulations such that the higher, floor and basic amounts for accelerated courses which begin before 1 January 2027 are increased.

The fee increases to the higher amounts are required to maintain the value of those amounts in real terms, having regard to the index of prices specified in the Student Fees (Inflation Index) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/507)). Fees for academic years beginning on or after 1st August 2026 but before 1st August 2027 are increased by 2.71%. Fees for academic years beginning on or after 1st August 2027 are increased by 2.68%.

A full impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business, the voluntary sector and the public sector has been produced and is published alongside these Regulations on www.legislation.gov.uk. Hard copies are available from the Department for Education, Sanctuary Buildings, Great Smith Street, London SW1P 3BT.
