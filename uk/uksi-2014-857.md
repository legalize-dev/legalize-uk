# The West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Dissolution) Order 2014

Made: 28th March 2014

Coming into force: 31st March 2014

In accordance with section 166(2) of that Act the Secretary of State has consulted Northamptonshire County Council, South Northamptonshire Council, Daventry District Council and Northampton Borough Council (being the local authorities in whose area the relevant urban development area is situated).

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the West Northamptonshire Development Corporation (Dissolution) Order 2014 and shall come into force on 31st March 2014.

#### Dissolution of the Corporation

###### 2

The West Northamptonshire Development Corporation shall be dissolved on 31st July 2014.

## Signed

Signed by the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Eric Pickles — Secretary of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 28th March 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

By virtue of section 166(3) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980, on the coming into force of this Order the West Northamptonshire Development Corporation will cease to act except for the purpose of preparing its final accounts and report and winding up its affairs. The corporation will be dissolved on 31st July 2014.

No Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
