# The Emulsifiers and Stabilisers in Food (Amendment) Regulations 1993

Made: 26th April 1993

Laid before Parliament: 10th May 1993

Coming into force: 31st May 1993

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Health and the Secretary of State for Wales, acting jointly, in relation to England and Wales, and the Secretary of State for Scotland in relation to Scotland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 6(4), 16(1)(a) and (e) and (3), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to, the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations, after consultation in accordance with section 48(4) of the said Act with such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by the Regulations:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Emulsifiers and Stabilisers in Food (Amendment) Regulations 1993 and shall come into force on 31st May 1993.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations “the English and Welsh Regulations” means the Emulsifiers and Stabilisers in Food Regulations 1989[^f00002] and “the Scottish Regulations” means the Emulsifiers and Stabilisers in Food (Scotland) Regulations 1989[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the English and Welsh and the Scottish Regulations

###### 3

In Part II of Schedule 1 to, respectively, the English and Welsh Regulations and the Scottish Regulations there shall be added, after the words “Commission Directive [90/612/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0612)” in the entry relating to E473 Sucrose esters of fatty acids, the words “and Article 1 of Commission Directive [92/4/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0004)”[^f00004].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply to Great Britain, come into force on 31st May 1993.

The Regulations amend the Emulsifiers and Stabilisers in Food Regulations 1989 and the Emulsifiers and Stabilisers in Food (Scotland) Regulations 1989 by specifying that the purity criteria for sucrose esters of fatty acids given in Part II of Schedule 1 to both sets of Regulations are those contained in Council Directive [78/663/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0663) as further amended by Commission Directive [92/4/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0004) (OJ No.L55, 29.2.92, p. 96) (regulation 3).
