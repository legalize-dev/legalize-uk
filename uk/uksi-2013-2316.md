# The Pensions Act 2004 (Codes of Practice) (Reporting Late Payment of Contributions) Appointed Day Order 2013

Made: 11th September 2013

The Secretary of State for Work and Pensions makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 91(9) of the Pensions Act 2004[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions Act 2004 (Codes of Practice) (Reporting Late Payment of Contributions) Appointed Day Order 2013.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into effect of—

- (a) the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 5: Reporting late payment of contributions to occupational pension schemes: Second issue; and

- (b) the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 6: Reporting late payment of contributions to personal pension schemes: Second issue,

is 20th September 2013.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions.

Steve Webb — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 11th September 2013

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 20th September 2013 as the day for the coming into effect of—

- the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 5: Reporting late payment of contributions to occupational pension schemes: Second issue; and

- the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 6: Reporting late payment of contributions to personal pension schemes: Second issue.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no direct impact on businesses, the public sector or civil society organisations.
