# The A23 Trunk Road (Streatham High Road, Lambeth) (Prohibition of Right Turn) Order 1994

Made: 26th August 1994

Coming into force: 3rd September 1994

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) and (3) of, and Part IV of Schedule 9 to, the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after consultation with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis pursuant to section 7(6) of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A23 Trunk Road (Streatham High Road, Lambeth) (Prohibition of Right Turn) Order 1994 and shall come into force on 3rd September 1994.

###### 2

In this Order:

- “the trunk road” means that length of the A23 Trunk Road in the London Borough of Lambeth known as Streatham High Road; and

- “London local service” has the meaning given in section 34(1) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00003].

###### 3

- (1) The Lambeth (Prescribed Routes) (No. 4) Traffic Order 1966[^f00004], shall be varied as follows.

- (2) Article 4 shall be omitted.

###### 4

Save as provided in article 5 below, no person shall cause or permit any vehicle proceeding in the trunk road to make a right turn into Gleneldon Road.

###### 5

Nothing in article 4 above shall apply—

- (a) to a public service vehicle being used in the provision of a London local service;

- (b) to a vehicle being used for police, ambulance or fire brigade purposes; or

- (c) to anything done with the permission or at the direction of a constable in uniform or of a traffic warden.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

P. J. Bevis — A Grade 6 Officer Highways Agency London Office — 26th August 1994
