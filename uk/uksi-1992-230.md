# The Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Guernsey) Order 1992

Made: 11th February 1992

Coming into force: 11th March 1992

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of February 1992 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 108(1) of the Civil Aviation Act 1982[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Guernsey) Order 1992 and shall come into force on 11th March 1992.

###### 2

The provisions of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 which are listed in Part I of Schedule 1 to this Order shall extend to the Bailiwick of Guernsey with the modifications specified in Part II of that Schedule.

###### 3

The Orders specified in Schedule 2 to this Order are hereby revoked to the extent that they relate to the extension to the Bailiwick of Guernsey of any enactment which is repealed by and re-enacted in the Civil Aviation Act 1982.

###### 4

The Civil Aviation Act 1982 (Guernsey) Order 1986[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends those provisions of the Civil Aviation Act 1982 which are set out in Part I of Schedule 1 to this Order to the Bailiwick of Guernsey; the provisions are subject to the modifications specified in Part II of that Schedule. The Order also revokes the Orders specified in Schedule 2 to this Order to the extent that they relate to the extension to the Bailiwick of Guernsey of enactments replaced by that Act.
