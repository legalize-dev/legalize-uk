# The Return of Cultural Objects (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2001

Made: 10th December 2001

Laid before Parliament: 11th December 2001

Coming into force: 1st January 2002

The Secretary of State, being a Minister designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the return of cultural objects unlawfully removed from the territory of a member State, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 2(2) of that Act and of all other powers enabling her in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Return of Cultural Objects (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st January 2002.

#### Amendment

###### 2

- (1) The Return of Cultural Objects Regulations 1994 shall be amended in accordance with the provisions of this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) at the end there shall be inserted “ and Directive [2001/38/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0038) of the European Parliament and of the Council ”.

- (3) In regulation 2(3) the words “in United Kingdom currency” shall be omitted and at the end there shall be inserted “ and for the purposes of converting the financial thresholds into United Kingdom currency the values expressed in euro in the Schedule shall be converted at the rate of exchange on 31 December 2001 published in the Official Journal of the European Communities ”.

- (4) In the Schedule to the Regulations—

- (a) for the figure and word “0 (Zero)” there shall be substituted the words “ Whatever the value ”; and

- (b) for the figure “£11,900.00” there shall be substituted the figure “ [euro]15,000.00 ”; and

- (c) for the figure “£23,800.00” there shall be substituted the figure “ [euro]30,000.00 ”; and

- (d) for the figure “£39,600.00” there shall be substituted the figure “ [euro]50,000.00 ”; and

- (e) for the figure “£119,000.00” there shall be substituted the figure “ [euro]150,000.00 ”.

## Signed

Tessa Blackstone — Minister of State, — Department for Culture, Media and Sport — 10th December 2001
