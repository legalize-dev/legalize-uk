# The Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1985

Made: 25th March 1985

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 9(2) of the Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984, I hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984 (Commencement) Order 1985.

###### 2

The Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984 shall come into force on 15th April 1985.

## Signed

Leon Brittan — One of Her Majesty's Principal Secretaries of State — Home Office — 25th March 1985

## Explanatory note

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force on 15th April 1985 the Repatriation of Prisoners Act 1984.
