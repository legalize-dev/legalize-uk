# The Greenwich-Time to Succeed Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002

Made: 8th August 2002

Laid before Parliament: 14th August 2002

Coming into force: 6th September 2002

Now therefore, in exercise of the power conferred on the Secretary of State by section 10(2) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, the Secretary of State for Education and Skills hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order shall be cited as the Greenwich-Time to Succeed Education Action Zone (Extension) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 6th September 2002.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “Greenwich-Time to Succeed Education Action Zone” means the Education Action Zone established by the Greenwich-Time to Succeed Education Action Zone Order 1999[^f00001].

#### Extension of the Greenwich-Time to Succeed Education Action Zone

###### 3

The Greenwich-Time to Succeed Education Action Zone is extended for a further period of two years beginning with 6th September 2002.

## Signed

David Miliband — Minister of State, — Department for Education and Skills — 8th August 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the Greenwich-Time to Succeed Education Action Zone for a further period of two years.
