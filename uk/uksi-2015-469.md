# The Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2015

Made: 2nd March 2015

Laid before Parliament: 4th March 2015

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Secretary of State is a Minister designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the environmental aspects of product design and makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations come into force on 1st July 2015.

- (3) Regulations 2(3) and 3 come into force on 1st January 2016.

#### Amendment of the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010

###### 2

- (1) The table at paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (declaration of conformity) to the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010[^f00003] is amended as follows.

- (2) After item 22 insert—

| 23 | A small, medium or large power transformer | Commission Regulation (EU) No 548/2014 implementing [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for small, medium and large power transformers[^f00004] |
| --- | --- | --- |

- (3) After item 23 (as inserted by paragraph (2)) insert—

| 24 | A ventilation unit | Commission Regulation (EU) No 1253/2014 implementing [Directive 2009/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0125) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to ecodesign requirements for ventilation units[^f00005] |
| --- | --- | --- |

#### Amendment of the Energy Information Regulations 2011

###### 3

- (1) The table at Schedule 1 (EU measures) to the Energy Information Regulations 2011[^f00006] is amended as follows.

- (2) After the entry relating to domestic ovens and range hoods insert—

| Residential ventilation units | Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1254/2014 supplementing Directive 2010/30/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to energy labelling of residential ventilation units[^f00007] | 1st January 2016 |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Amber Rudd — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department of Energy and Climate Change — 2nd March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products Regulations 2010 ([S.I. 2010/2617](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2617)) (“the 2010 Regulations”) and the Energy Information Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1524](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1524)) (“the 2011 Regulations”) and partially implement Commission Regulations (EU) Nos 548/2014 and 1253/2014, and Commission Delegated Regulations (EU) No 1254/2014 as follows—

- (a) regulation 2(2) and (3) adds two new products to Schedule 1 (declaration of conformity) to the 2010 Regulations—

- (i) small, medium and large power transformers to apply from 1st July 2015;

- (ii) ventilation units to apply from 1st January 2016; and

- (b) regulation 3 adds residential ventilation units to Schedule 1 (EU measures) to the 2011 Regulations to apply from 1st January 2016.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
