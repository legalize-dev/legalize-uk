# The Finance Act 2001, section 16, (Appointed Day) Order 2002

Made: 25th March 2002

The Treasury, in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 16(6) of the Finance Act 2001[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2001, section 16, (Appointed Day) Order 2002.

###### 2

The date appointed as the commencement date for the purposes of section 16 of the Finance Act 2001 is 1st April 2002.

## Signed

Nick Ainger — Anne McGuire — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 25th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st April 2002 as the commencement date for the purpose of section 16 of the Finance Act [2001 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/9), which contains the charging provisions for aggregates levy.
