# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Experimental Variation No.3 Order 1996

Made: 16th January 1996

Coming into force: 5th February 1996

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 9 and 10 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Experimental Variation No.3 Order 1996, and shall come into force on 5th February 1996.

###### 2

The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In article 4—

- (a) paragraph (2)(b) is renumbered (2)(c);

- (b) after paragraph (2)(a) add the following paragraph—

> > (b) in any length of road specified in Part VII and VIII of schedule 2 for a period of up to 20 minutes not being a period separated by an interval of less than 40 minutes from a previous period when that vehicle was at rest in that length of road during the prescribed hours.

- (c) in paragraph (3)(a) for the words “Parts I to V” substitute the words “Parts I to VIII”;

- (d) in the table in paragraph 5 in item 5 add to column (3) “Schedule 2, Parts VI and VII”;

- (e) in the table in paragraph 5 in item 9 add to column (3) “Schedule 2, Part VIII”.

###### 7

If it appears to the Traffic Director for London, or some person authorised by him, essential in the interests of the expeditious, convenient and safe movement of traffic, or of the provision of suitable and adequate parking facilities on the highway, or for preserving or improving the amenities of the area through which any road affected by this Order runs, and after consulting with the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis, he may modify or suspend any provision of this Order, save that no modification shall make an addition.
