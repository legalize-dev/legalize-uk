# The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2007

Made: 22nd October 2007

Laid before Parliament: 26th October 2007

Coming into force: 30th November 2007

There has been open and transparent consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00003] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (England) (Revocation) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 30th November 2007.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (England) Regulations 2005[^f00004] are revoked.

#### Consequential amendments to the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (England) Regulations 2004

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 8 (inspection, seizure and detention of suspected animal feed) of the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (England) Regulations 2004[^f00005] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In paragraph (5) the expression “Subject to paragraph (6)” is substituted for the expression “Subject to paragraphs (5A), (5B) and (6)”.

- (3) Paragraphs (5A) and (5B) are omitted.

#### Consequential amendment of the Official Feed and Food Controls (England) Regulations 2006

###### 4

In Schedule 2 (definition of relevant feed law) of the Official Feed and Food Controls (England) Regulations 2006[^f00006], paragraph (f) is omitted.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Dawn Primarolo — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 22nd October 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulation)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1 These Regulations revoke the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (England) Regulations 2005 ([S.I.2005/1265](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1265)), and make consequential amendments to the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (England) Regulations 2004 ([S.I. 2004/2334](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2334)) and the Official Feed and Food Controls (England) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/15)).

2 The Regulations implement Commission Decision [2007/157/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0157) repealing Decision [2005/317/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0317) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism Bt 10 in maize products (OJ No. L68, 8.3.2007, p.8).

3 A full regulatory assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
