# The Agricultural Wages Committees (Areas) (England) Order 1995

Made: 6th December 1995

Coming into force: 1st January 1996

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(1) of the said Act and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Agricultural Wages Committees (Areas) (England) Order 1995 and shall come into force on 1st January 1996.

#### Establishment of new agricultural wages committees

###### 2

Instead of the agricultural wages committees for each of the counties set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order and for the combination of counties set out in Part II of that Schedule, there shall be established an agricultural wages committee for each of the combinations set out in Part III of that Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I

### COUNTIES FOR WHICH THERE ARE SEPARATE AGRICULTURAL WAGES COMMITTEES

- Cumbria

- North Yorkshire

## PART II

### COMBINATIONS OF COUNTIES FOR WHICH THERE ARE AGRICULTURAL WAGES COMMITTEES

- Cleveland, Durham, Northumberland and Tyne and Wear.

## PART III

### NEW COMBINATIONS OF COUNTIES FOR WHICH THERE ARE AGRICULTURAL WAGES COMMITTEES

###### 1

Northumberland and Tyne and Wear and Cumbria.

###### 2

Cleveland and Durham and North Yorkshire.

## Signed

Tim Boswell — Parliamentary Secretary, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food — 6th December 1995

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order establishes new agricultural wages committees for the counties of Northern England. In place of the committees for Cumbria and North Yorkshire and the committee for Cleveland, Durham, Northumberland and Tyne and Wear, the following two committees are established for the following areas:—

- (a) Northumberland and Tyne and Wear and Cumbria; and

- (b) Cleveland and Durham and North Yorkshire.
