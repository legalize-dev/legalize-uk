# The Crime and Disorder (Overview and Scrutiny) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

Made: 4th March 2010

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 2010

Coming into force: 1st April 2010

In accordance with section 20(4) of that Act, the Secretary of State has consulted with the Welsh Ministers[^f00002] regarding the provisions in relation to local authorities in Wales.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Crime and Disorder (Overview and Scrutiny) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into force on 1st April 2010.

#### Amendment of the Crime and Disorder (Overview and Scrutiny) Regulations 2009

###### 2

In regulation 3 (co-opting of additional members) of the Crime and Disorder (Overview and Scrutiny) Regulations 2009[^f00003] for paragraph (4) substitute—

> > (4) The crime and disorder committee of a local authority (or authorities) shall not co-opt a person to serve on the committee who is a member of the executive of that local authority (or authorities).

## Signed

David Hanson — Minister of State — 4th March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made under section 20(3) (in respect of local authorities in England) and section 20(4) (in respect of local authorities in Wales) of the Police and Justice Act 2006. The Regulations amend the Crime and Disorder (Overview and Scrutiny) Regulations 2009 ([S.I. 2009/942](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/942)) which make provision for the exercise of powers by crime and disorder committees of local authorities.

The amendment in regulation 2 provides for co-option of additional members onto crime and disorder committees. It amends regulation 3(4) of the 2009 Regulations to provide that the only persons who are excluded from co-option are executive members of the local authority (or authorities) of the committee.
