# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) (Amendment and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2026

Made: 23rd November 2026

Laid before Parliament: 25th November 2026

Coming into force: 16th December 2026

The Secretary of State makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 22C(11), 31A(3), 34(8) and 104(4) of the Children Act 1989[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement, extent, application and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) (Amendment and Transitional Provision) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 16th December 2026.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales and apply in relation to England only.

- (4) In these Regulations “the 2010 Regulations” means the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the 2010 Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The 2010 Regulations are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 8ZA (contact with a child in care) for “(d)” substitute “(e)”.

- (3) In regulation 8(2) (persons to be notified of refusal or variation of contact otherwise required by section 34 of the Children Act 1989) after paragraph (b) insert—

> > (ba) subject to paragraph (bb), any person mentioned in section 34(1)(e)[^f00003] (“*S*”), unless S is a child and it would not be appropriate to do so having regard to S’s age and understanding,
> (bb) where S is a child and due to their age and understanding it is not appropriate to give the specified information to S, the person with parental responsibility for S or any adult with whom S is living,

- (4) In regulation 13(2) (notification of placement to specified persons)—

- (a) in paragraph (c) after “person” in the first place it occurs insert “mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 34(1)”,

- (b) after paragraph (c) insert—

> > (ca) subject to paragraph (cb), if C[^f00004] is in the care of the responsible authority[^f00005], any person mentioned in section 34(1)(e) (“*S*”), unless S is a child and it would not be appropriate to do so having regard to S’s age and understanding,
> (cb) where S is a child and due to their age and understanding it is not appropriate to give the written notification to S, the person with parental responsibility for S or any adult with whom S is living,

- (5) In Schedule 1 (care plans)[^f00006], after paragraph 3(2) (family and social relationships) insert—

> > (2A) Subject to sub-paragraph (2B), if C is in the care of the responsible authority, details of the arrangements made in accordance with section 34(1) for C’s contact with any person mentioned in section 34(1)(e), including the following—
>   (a) the nature of the contact, and
>   (b) the intended frequency of the contact.
> (2B) Where contact has not been arranged by the responsible authority, the reasons for this and what steps will be taken by the responsible authority to make arrangements as appropriate.

#### Transitional provision

###### 3

The responsible authority must amend the care plan prepared in accordance with Part 2 (arrangements for looking after a child) of the 2010 Regulations at the next review carried out under Part 6 (reviews of the child’s case) of the 2010 Regulations that takes place after the coming into force of these Regulations, so that the care plan complies with paragraph 3(2A) and (2B) of Schedule 1 to the 2010 Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations amend the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010 (“*the 2010 Regulations*”), which make provision about care planning for children who are looked after by local authorities. The amendments are made as a result of the commencement of the amendment to section 34 of the Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41) (“the 1989 Act”) made by section 10 of the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act [2026 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/21).

Regulation 2(2) amends regulation 8ZA of the 2010 Regulations to require local authorities to have regard to the child’s care plan when considering whether contact between the child and any siblings, half or step-siblings mentioned in section 34(1)(e) of the 1989 Act (“siblings”) is consistent with safeguarding and promoting the welfare of the child.

Regulation 2(3) amends regulation 8 of the 2010 Regulations to require local authorities to provide the information specified in regulation 8(3) to any siblings, unless it would not be appropriate due to the age and understanding of the sibling, where contact otherwise required by section 34 of the 1989 Act is refused as a matter of urgency. Where the specified information is not provided to the sibling because it is not appropriate due to their age and understanding, the specified information must be provided to the person with parental responsibility for the sibling or to any adult with whom the sibling is living.

Regulation 2(4) amends regulation 13 of the 2010 Regulations to require local authorities to provide written notification of the arrangements for the child’s placement before it is made, or within 5 working days of the start of the placement if made in an emergency, to any siblings. This requirement does not apply if it would not be appropriate due to the age and understanding of the sibling. Where the information is not provided to the sibling because it is not appropriate due to their age and understanding, the written notification must be provided to the person with parental responsibility for the sibling or to any adult with whom the sibling is living.

Regulation 2(5) requires a child’s care plan to include details of contact arrangements made (including reasons and steps to be taken where no arrangements are made) by the local authority in relation to the child and any siblings in accordance with the duty under section 34(1) of the 1989 Act.

Regulation 3 makes transitional provision for the care plan to be amended to comply with the new requirements provided for by regulation 2(5) at the next statutory review that takes place after these Regulations come into force.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
