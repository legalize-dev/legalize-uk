# The Fireworks Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

Made: 14th July 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 18(1) of the Fireworks Act 2003[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Fireworks Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Fireworks Act 2003.

#### Provisions of the Act commenced by this Order

###### 2

15th July 2004 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act listed in the first column of the Schedule to this Order for the purposes set out in the second column.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS OF THE FIREWORKS ACT 2003 COMING INTO FORCE ON 15TH JULY 2004

| Provisions of the Fireworks Act 2003 | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Section 4 (prohibition of supply etc in certain circumstances) | For all purposes |
| Section 6 (public fireworks displays | For all purposes |
| Section 7 (licensing of suppliers) | For all purposes |
| Section 8 (information about fireworks) | For all purposes |
| Section 9 (prohibition of importation etc of fireworks) | For all purposes |
| Section 11 (offences) | For the purposes of fireworks regulations made in exercise of powers conferred by the provisions of the Act brought into force by this Order |
| Section 12 (enforcement) | For the purposes of fireworks regulations made in exercise of powers conferred by the provisions of the Act brought into force by this Order |
| Section 13 (savings for certain privileges) | For the purposes of fireworks regulations made in exercise of powers conferred by the provisions of the Act brought into force by this Order |
| Section 16 (parliamentary procedure for regulation | For the purposes of fireworks regulations made in exercise of powers conferred by the provisions of the Act brought into force by this Order |

## Signed

Gerry Sutcliffe, — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Employment Relations, Competition and Consumers, — Department of Trade and Industry — 14th July 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 15th July sections 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 of the Fireworks Act 2003 and (for the purposes of fireworks regulations made in exercise of powers conferred by those provisions) sections 11, 12, 13 and 16 of that Act.
