# The Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2007

Made: 17th January 2007

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(3)(a) of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 [^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 (Commencement No.1) Order 2007.

###### 2

Section 4 (remuneration of members of the Northern Ireland Assembly) of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 shall come into force on 26th January 2007.

## Signed

Peter Hain — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 17th January 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force section 4 of the Northern Ireland (St Andrews Agreement) Act 2006 on 26th January 2007.
