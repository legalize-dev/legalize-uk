# The Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2014

Made: 20th March 2014

Coming into force: 31st March 2014

The Treasury has consented to the making of this Order as required by paragraph 1(7) of that Schedule.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) Order 2014 and comes into force on 31st March 2014.

#### Originating capital of NHS trust

###### 2

The originating capital of the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust[^f00002] is £72,544,000.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Earl Howe — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department of Health — 17th March 2014

We consent,

Sam Gyimah — David Evennett — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 20th March 2014

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order determines the amount of the originating capital, in accordance with paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 5 to the National Health Service Act 2006, of the Gloucestershire Care Services National Health Service Trust.
