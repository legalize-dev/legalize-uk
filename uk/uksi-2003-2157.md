# The International Fund for Agricultural Development (Fifth Replenishment) Order 2003

Made: 3rd July 2003

Coming into force: 3rd July 2003

Now therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by section 11 of the Act, and with the approval of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the International Fund for Agricultural Development (Fifth Replenishment) Order 2003 and shall come into force forthwith.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Agreement means the Agreement establishing the International Fund for Agricultural Development dated 13th June 1976, as ratified by the Government of the United Kingdom on 9th September 1977[^f00002];

- “the Fund” means the International Fund for Agricultural Development established by the Agreement;

- “the Government” means the Government of the United Kingdom;

- “the Resolution” means the Resolution 119/XXIV of the Governing Council of the Fund on the Fifth Replenishment of the Fund’s resources, adopted on 22nd February 2001.

###### 2

The Secretary of State may, on behalf of the Government, out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) make payment of a further contribution to the Fund not exceeding £18,531,000 in accordance with arrangements made between the Government and the Fund in accordance with the Resolution; and

- (b) make payment of sums required to redeem any non-interest-bearing and non-negotiable notes or other obligations which may be issued or created by her and accepted by the Fund pursuant to the arrangements mentioned in paragraph (a) above.

## Signed

Valerie Amos — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 30th June 2003

We approve,

John Heppell — Nick Ainger — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 3rd July 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order approves the making of a payment on behalf of the Government of the United Kingdom of a sum not exceeding £18,531,000 as a contribution to the resources of the International Fund for Agricultural Development in accordance with the arrangements made pursuant to the Resolution entitled “the Resolution 119/XXIV of the Governing Council of the Fund on the Fifth Replenishment of the Fund’s resources”, adopted on 22nd February 2001.

The Order further approves the redemption of non-interest-bearing and non-negotiable notes issued by the Secretary of State in payment of the further contribution.

The provisions of the Resolution may be obtained by application to the Department for International Development, 1 Palace Street, London SW1E 5HE.
