# The Pilotage Act 1987 (Abolition of Pilotage Commission: Appointed Day) Order 1991

Made: 18th April 1991

Laid before Parliament: 23rd April 1991

Coming into force: 29th April 1991

The Secretary of State for Transport, in exercise of the powersconferred on him by section 26(1) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf,hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pilotage Act 1987 (Abolition ofPilotage Commission: Appointed Day) Order 1991 and shall come into forceon 29th April 1991.

###### 2

The day appointed for the Pilotage Commission ceasing to exist is30th April 1991.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Transport

Roger Freeman — Minister of State, — Department of Transport — 18th April 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 30th April 1991 as the day on which the Pilotage Commission ceases to exist.
