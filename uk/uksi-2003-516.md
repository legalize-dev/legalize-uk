# The Rugby College of Further Education (Dissolution) Order 2003

Made: 3rd March 2003

Laid before Parliament: 10th March 2003

Coming into force: 1st August 2003

Now therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27 of the Act, the Secretary of State, after consulting the corporation and with the consent of the further education corporation known as Warwickshire College, Royal Leamington Spa and Moreton Morrell[^f00003], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rugby College of Further Education (Dissolution) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st August 2003.

###### 2

On 1st August 2003 the corporation shall be dissolved and all of its property, rights and liabilities shall be transferred to Warwickshire College, Royal Leamington Spa and Moreton Morrell being a body corporate established for purposes which include the provision of educational facilities or services.

###### 3

Section 26(2), (3) and (4) of the Act shall apply to any person employed by the corporation immediately before 1 August 2003 as if the references in that section –

- (a) to a person to whom that section applies were to a person so employed;

- (b) to the operative date were to 1st August 2003;

- (c) to the transferor were to the corporation; and

- (d) to the corporation were to Warwickshire College, Royal Leamington Spa and Moreton Morrell.

## Signed

Margaret Hodge — Minister of State — Department for Education and Skills — 3rd March 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves with effect from 1st August 2003 the further education corporation established to conduct Rugby College of Further Education. It provides for the transfer of its property, rights and liabilities to Warwickshire College, Royal Leamington Spa and Moreton Morrell and secures the rights of its employees by applying section 26(2) to (4) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992.
