# The Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) (No. 2) Order 1990

Made: 18th October 1990

Laid before Parliament: 19th October 1990

Coming into force: 16th November 1990

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 64 of the Banking Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with the Deposit Protection Board, hereby order as follows:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) (No. 2) Order 1990 and shall come into force on 16th November 1990.

#### Increase of borrowing limit

###### 2

The sum of £50 million is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 64(1) of the Banking Act 1987 (borrowing powers of the Deposit Protection Board).

#### Revocation of earlier instrument

###### 3

The Deposit Protection Board (Increase of Borrowing Limit) Order 1990[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Gregory Knight — Irvine Patnick — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 18th October 1990

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a limit of £50 million for the borrowing power of the Deposit Protection Board in place of the limit of £10 million stipulated in section 64(1) of the Banking Act 1987. It also revokes an earlier Order which was defective.
