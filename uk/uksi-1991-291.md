# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1991

Made: 19th February 1991

Coming into force: 25th March 1991

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00001], section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby, with the consent of the Lord Lyon King of Arms, enact and declare:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1991 and shall come into force on 25th March 1991.

- (2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

#### Amendment of fees

###### 2

- (1) Subject to the following paragraphs of this rule, for the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1990[^f00003], substitute the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt.

- (2) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1990[^f00003] as substituted by paragraph (1) above shall apply to work done on or after the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

- (3) The Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1990[^f00003] having effect before the substitution under paragraph (1) above shall continue to have effect in respect of work done before the date on which this Act of Sederunt comes into force.

- (a) Service

- (i) each person at a different address

*Inhibitions*

- (a) Inhibition only

- (a) Interdict only

- (a) Serving notice of entry

- (a) Arranging and executing poinding where appraised value is-

###### 7

*Sales*

- (a)

- (i) Application for warrant of sale or variation of a warrant of sale

###### 8

*Ejections*

- (a) Arranging ejection

###### 9

*Taking possession of effects*

- (a) Arranging possession

###### 10

*Apprehensions*

- (a) Arranging apprehension

###### 11

*Taking possession of children*

- (a) Arranging to take possession

###### 12

*Arresting vessels, aircraft and cargo*

- (a) Arranging to arrest

###### 13

*Miscellaneous*

## Signed

J.A.D. Hope — Lord President, IPD — 19th February 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1990 by increasing the fees payable to Messengers-at-Arms by about 14%. The last increase was in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 1989 (S.I. [1989/1019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/1019)).
