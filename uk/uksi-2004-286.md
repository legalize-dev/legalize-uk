# The Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2004

Made: 6th February 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred upon him by section 8(2) of the Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003 [^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003 (Commencement) Order 2004.

###### 2

The Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003 shall come into force on 3rd March 2004.

## Signed

David Blunkett — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 6th February 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Female Genital Mutilation Act 2003 into force on 3rd March 2004.
