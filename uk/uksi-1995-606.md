# The Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1995

Made: 2nd March 1995

Laid before Parliament: 9th March 1995

Coming into force: 1st April 1995

The Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland, the Secretary of State for Wales and the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland, acting jointly, as respects the United Kingdom, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 9(1) and 36 of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] as extended to Northern Ireland[^f00002], and the said Minister and the Secretary of State, acting jointly, as respects the Isle of Man, in exercise of the said powers as extended to the Isle of Man[^f00003], and in exercise of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the approval of the Treasury in accordance with section 9(1) of that Act, hereby make the following Regulations:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 and shall come into force on 1st April 1995.

#### Amendment of the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) Regulations 1990

###### 2

In item 4 in the first column of Part II of the Schedule to the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) Regulations 1990[^f00004] there shall be inserted after the word “fenugreek,” the word “leek,”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Plant Breeders' Rights (Fees) Regulations 1990 (as amended) by adding leeks to the list of plant varieties for which fees are payable in respect of tests or examination in connection with an application for a grant of plant breeders' rights.
