# The Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 7) Order 1988

Made: 10th May 1988

Coming into force: 1stJune 1988

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 33(3) of the Petroleum and Submarine Pipe-lines Act 1975[^f00001](hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, and after giving those persons affected an opportunity of being heard with respect to the matter, hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Submarine Pipe-lines (Designated Owners) (No. 7) Order 1988 and shall come into force on 1st June 1988.

###### 2

For the purposes of Part III of the Act, each person specified in Part I of the Schedule hereto is hereby designated as the owner of each pipe-line described in Part II of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — DESIGNATED OWNERS

- ARCO British Ltd., whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at ARCO House, 48 Grosvenor House, London W1X 0AN.

- Canadian Superior Oil U.K. Ltd., whose registered office is at Mobil Court, 3 Clements Inn, London WC2A 2BB.

- Deminex U.K. Oil and Gas Ltd., whose registered office is at Bowater House, 68 Knightsbridge, London SW1X 7LD.

- North Sea Sun Oil Company Ltd., whose principal place of business in the United Kingdom is at 90 Long Acre, London WC2E 9RG.

- Superior Oil U.K. Ltd., whose registered office is at Mobil Court, 3 Clements Inn, London WC2A 2EB.
