# The Critical Third Parties (Designation) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made: 16th November 2026

Coming into force: 23rd November 2026

The Treasury make these Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 312L(1) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^f00001] (“*the Act*”).

Having had regard to the matters in section 312L(3) of the Act, in the Treasury’s opinion, a failure in, or disruption to, the provision of services by the persons designated by these Regulations could threaten the stability of, or confidence in, the UK financial system.

Pursuant to section 312L(4) of the Act, the Treasury has— consulted the Financial Conduct Authority, the Prudential Regulation Authority and the Bank of England; given notice in writing to the persons to be designated by these Regulations specifying a reasonable period within which those persons may make representations in writing about the proposal; and had regard to any representations made.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Critical Third Parties (Designation) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 23rd November 2026.

- (3) These Regulations extend to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

#### Amendment of the Critical Third Parties (Designation) Regulations 2026

###### 2

In the Critical Third Parties (Designation) Regulations 2026, in regulation 2 (designation of critical third parties)[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph (d), for “2026.” substitute “2026;”;

- (b) after paragraph (d), insert—

> > (e) BlackRock Financial Management, Inc., a company incorporated in the state of Delaware, USA (file number 2446204), from 23rd November 2026;
> (f) Bloomberg L.P., a limited partnership formed in the state of Delaware, USA (file number 2110234) and registered in the UK as an overseas entity (OE018811), from 23rd November 2026;
> (g) Bottomline Technologies Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 08098450), from 23rd November 2026;
> (h) Broadridge Financial Solutions Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 01870679), from 23rd November 2026;
> (i) Form3 UK Limited, a company incorporated in England and Wales (company number 09954425), from 23rd November 2026.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations designate BlackRock Financial Management, Inc., Bloomberg L.P., Bottomline Technologies Limited, Broadridge Financial Solutions Limited and Form3 UK Limited as critical third parties. The Regulations amend the Critical Third Parties (Designation) Regulations 2026 ([S.I. 2026/777](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/777)) to add the entities to the list of designated critical third parties.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
