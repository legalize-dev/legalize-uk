# The Parliamentary Commissioner Order 1999

Made: 10th February 1999

Laid before Parliament: 22nd February 1999

Coming into force: 15th March 1999

Court at Buckingham Palace, the 10th day of February 1999 Present, The Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty in Council

Her Majesty, in pursuance of section 4(2) of the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967[^f00001], is pleased, by and with the advice of Her Privy Council, to order, and it is hereby ordered, as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Parliamentary Commissioner Order 1999 and shall come into force on 15th March 1999.

(This note is not part of the Order)
