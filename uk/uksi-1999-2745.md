# The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation (No. 3) Order 1999

Made: 6th October 1999

Coming into force: 18th October 1999

The Traffic Director for London, following a direction given to him by the Secretary of State for Transport under section 58(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 6 and 124(1)(d) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002], and of all other enabling powers, hereby makes the following order:—

#### Commencement and citation

###### 1

This order may be cited as the A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993 Variation (No. 3) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 18th October 1999.

#### Variations

###### 2

The A1 Trunk Road (Islington) Red Route Traffic Order 1993[^f00003] is varied as provided by the following provisions of this Order.

###### 3

In Article 4—

- (a) for paragraph (4) substitute the following paragraph—

> > (4) In this article “restricted hours” means the period from 0700 hours to 1900 hours on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive.

- (b) for the table in paragraph (5) substitute the following Table—

| *(1)* | *(2)* | *(3)* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item | Prescribed hours | Provision |
| 1 | 0700 hours to 1900 hours on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive | Schedule 2, Parts I and III Schedule 3, Parts I and II |
| 2 | 0700 hours to 1600 hours on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive | Schedule 2, Part V |
| 3 | 0700 hours to 1300 hours on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive | Schedule 3, Parts III and IV |
| 4 | 1000 hours to 1600 hours on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive | Schedule 2, Parts II and IV Schedule 3, Parts V and VI |
| 5 | 1300 hours to 1900 hours on Mondays to Saturdays inclusive | Schedule 3, Parts VII and VIII |

###### 4

In Schedule 1 Part I for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

###### 5

In Schedule 1 after item 7. delete—“PART II LENGTHS OF ROAD WHERE WAITING (IF NOT PROHIBITED BY ARTICLE 5) IS RESTRICTED BETWEEN 8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

###### 6

In Schedule 2 Part I for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

###### 7

In Schedule 2 Part II for “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

###### 8

In Schedule 2 Part III for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

###### 9

In Schedule 2 Part IV for “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)” substitute “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)”

###### 10

In Schedule 2 Part V for “8 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)”

###### 11

In Schedule 2 Part VI for “8 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (DISABLED PERSONS VEHICLES)”

###### 12

In Schedule 2 Part VII for “8 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)” substitute “7 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE (ALL VEHICLES)”

###### 13

In Schedule 3 Part I for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

###### 14

In Schedule 3 Part II for “8 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “7 am to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

###### 15

In Schedule 3 Part III for “8 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “7 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

###### 16

In Schedule 3 Part IV for “8 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “7 am to 1 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

###### 17

In Schedule 3 Part V for “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “10 am to 4 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

###### 18

In Schedule 3 Part VII for “1 pm to 7 pm MONDAYS TO FRIDAYS INCLUSIVE” substitute “1 pm to 7 pm MONDAYS TO SATURDAYS INCLUSIVE”

## Signed

Signed in accordance with a direction from the Secretary of State

Derek Turner — Traffic Director for London — 6th October 1999
