# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 1998

Made: 23rd January 1998

Laid before Parliament: 28th January 1998

Coming into force: 1st April 1998

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by sections 7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 1998 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

#### Non-domestic rate for 1998-99

###### 2

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Secretary of State prescribes a rate of 47.4 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 1998-99.

## Signed

Calum MacDonald — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State, Scottish Office — 23rd January 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a rate of 47.4 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 1998-99.
