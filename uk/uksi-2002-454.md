# The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order 2002

Made: 1st March 2002

Laid before Parliament: 4th March 2002

Coming into force: 1st April 2002

The Treasury, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 10(1) of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:—

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

#### Amendment of the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order 2000

###### 2

The bodies listed in Schedule 1 to this Order are removed from the Schedule to the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order 2000[^f00002].

#### Designation

###### 3

The bodies listed in Schedule 2 to this Order are designated in respect of the financial year ending with 31st March 2003 for the purposes of section 10 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates bodies, for the financial year ending with 31st March 2003, for the purposes of section 10 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000. The Treasury intend that the “whole of government” accounts to be prepared for that year under section 9 of the Act should relate in part to each of the bodies designated by this Order, and the designation enables the Treasury to require those bodies to provide financial information in relation to that year.

This Order also amends the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order 2000 by removing certain bodies from the Schedule thereto, which lists the bodies designated for the financial year ending with 31st March 2002 for the purposes of section 10 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act 2000.
