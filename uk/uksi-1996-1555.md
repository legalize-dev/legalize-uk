# The Legal Aid in Family Proceedings (Remuneration) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1996

Made: 14th June 1996

Laid before Parliament: 14th June1996

Coming into force: 8th July 1996

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 34 and 43 of the Legal Aid Act 1988,[^f00001] having had regard to the matters specified in section 34(9) and having consulted the General Council of the Bar and the Law Society, and with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations: —

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid in Family Proceedings (Remuneration) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1996 and shall come into force on 8th July 1996.

###### 2

The Legal Aid in Family Proceedings (Remuneration) Regulations l991[^f00002] shall be amended, in respect of work done on or after 8th July 1996, by substituting

- (a) “£2.10” and “£1.85” for “£2.05” and “£1.80” respectively in ITEM 2 (Receiving routine letters), Columns 2 (*High Court*) and 3 (*County Court or Magistrates' Court*) of Part I (PREPARATION) of Schedule 1 (CARE PROCEEDINGS);

- (b) “£4.25” for “£4.20” in ITEM 3 (Routine telephone calls), Column 2 (*High Court*) of Part I (PREPARATION) of Schedule 1A (CARE PROCEEDINGS);

- (c) “£2.10” and “£1.85” for “£2.05” and “£1.80” respectively in ITEM 2 (Receiving routine letters), Columns 2 (*High Court*) and 3 (*County Court*) of Part I (PREPARATION) of Schedule 2(a) (PRESCRIBED FAMILY PROCEEDINGS);

- (d) “£32.00 – £51.00” for “£32.00 – £51.75” in ITEM 18 (Preparing the bill etc), Column 3 (*County Court*) of Part V (TAXATION AND REVIEW OF TAXATION) of Schedule 2(a);

- (e) “£4.25” for “£4.20” in ITEM 3 (Routine telephone calls), Column 2 (*High Court*) of Part I (PREPARATION) of Schedule 2A(a) (PRESCRIBED FAMILY PROCEEDINGS); and

- (f) “£32.50 – £51.75” for “£32.50 – £51.00” in ITEM 18 (Preparing the bill etc), Column 3 (*County Court*) of Part V (TAXATION AND REVIEW OF TAXATION) of Schedule 2A(a) (PRESCRIBED FAMILY PROCEEDINGS).

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 11th June 1996

We Consent,

Michael Bates — Simon Burns — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 14th June 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revise some of the rates of remuneration for work done under a legal aid certificate in family proceedings provided by the Legal Aid in Family Proceedings (Remuneration) (Amendment) Regulations 1996.
