# The Hertfordshire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010

Made: 7th October 2010

Coming into force: 1st November 2010

The Secretary of State for Health makes this Order following the completion of the consultation prescribed under section 25(3) of the National Health Service Act 2006[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Hertfordshire Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 2010 and shall come into force on 1st November 2010.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Act” means the National Health Service Act 2006;

- “community health services” means any services—whose provision must be arranged by an integrated care board under section 3(1)(g) or (i) of the Act;which are of a kind mentioned in section 3(1)(g) or (i) of the Act, but—whose provision must be arranged by NHS England under regulations made under section 3B of the Act[^f00003]; orwhich may be provided by the Secretary of State or a local authority under section 2B of the Act;which a local authority must provide under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Act;which a local authority may provide under paragraph 2 or 7A of Schedule 1 to the Act; orwhich the Secretary of State must provide under paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to the Act;

- “the trust” means the National Health Service trust established by article 2.

#### Establishment and name of the trust

###### 2

There is established a National Health Service trust which shall be called the Hertfordshire Community National Health Service Trust.

#### Nature and functions of the trust

###### 3

The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services, namely hospital accommodation and services and community health services, for the purposes of the health service.

#### Directors of the trust

###### 4

- (1) The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 4 executive directors and 4 non-executive directors.

- (2) If the Secretary of State suspends the chairman, or a non-executive director of the trust, the number of non-executive directors of the trust shall increase during the period of suspension by the number of persons so suspended.

#### Operational date and accounting date of the trust

###### 5

- (1) The operational date of the trust shall be 1st November 2010.

- (2) The accounting date of the trust shall be 31st March.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Health.

Simon Burns — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 7th October 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order establishes a National Health Service trust called the Hertfordshire Community National Health Service Trust.

The Order sets out the functions of the trust (article 3), the number of executive and non-executive directors of the trust (article 4), the operational date (the date on which the trust assumes all its functions) and the accounting date of the trust (article 5).
