# The Superannuation (Admission to Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972) (No. 3) Order 1997

Made: 17th March 1997

Laid before Parliament: 17th March 1997

Coming into force: 7th April 1997

The Minister for the Civil Service, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(5) and (8)(a) of the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Admission to Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972) (No. 3) Order 1997 and shall come into force on 7th April 1997.

###### 2

The office of the social fund Commissioner for Great Britain is hereby added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 with effect from 1 June 1995.

###### 3

The office of the social fund Commissioner for Northern Ireland is hereby added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 with effect from 1 June 1995.

## Signed

Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster for the Minister for the Civil Service

Roger Freeman — 17th March 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds—

- (a) the office of the social fund Commissioner for Great Britain, and

- (b) the office of the social fund Commissioner for Northern Ireland

to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 so that section 1 of that Act (under which the Principal Civil Service Pension Scheme, the Civil Service Additional Voluntary Contribution Scheme and the Civil Service Compensation Scheme have been made) may apply to them.

Provision for the appointment of the social fund Commissioner for Great Britain was made by section 65 of the Social Security Administration Act [1992 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/5).

Provision for the appointment of the social fund Commissioner for Northern Ireland was made by section 63 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8).

Under the power conferred by section 1(8)(a) of the Superannuation Act 1972 this Order takes effect from 1 June 1995.
