# The Education (Free School Lunches) (State Pension Credit) Order 2005

Made: 23rd March 2005

Laid before Parliament: 6th April 2005

Coming into force: 30th April 2005

The Secretary of State for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred upon her by sections 512ZB(4)(a)(iv) and 568 of the Education Act 1996 [^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Education (Free School Lunches) (State Pension Credit) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 30th April 2005.

- (2) This Order applies only in relation to England.

#### Prescribed benefit

###### 2

State Pension Credit payable under section 1 of the State Pension Credit Act 2002[^f00002] is prescribed for the purposes of section 512ZB(4)(a)(iv) of the 1996 Act in circumstances where the parent is receiving guarantee credit under section 1.

## Signed

Derek Twigg — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Education and Skills — 23rd March 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes, for the purposes of section 512ZB of the Education Act 1996, that where a parent is in receipt of the guarantee credit part of State Pension Credit their child is entitled to free school lunches.

A previous Order (The Education (Free School Lunches) ({Prescribed Tax Credits) Order 2003 ([SI 2003/383](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/383))) prescribed Child Tax Credit for the purposes of section 512ZB.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
