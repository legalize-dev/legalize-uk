# The A483 Swansea-Manchester Trunk Road (Improvement at Bryn Sadwrn) Order 1989

Made: 18th January 1989

Coming into force: 1st March 1989

The Secretary of State for Wales makes this Order in exercise of powers conferred bysection 10 of the Highways Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other enabling powers:—

###### 1

The new highway which the Secretary of State proposes to construct along the route described in the Schedule to this Order shall become a trunk road as from the date when this Order comes into operation.

###### 2

The centre line of the new trunk road is indicated by a heavy black line on the deposited plan.

###### 3

In this Order—

- All measurements of distance are measured along the route of the relevant highway: “the deposited plan” means the plan numbered HA10/2 WO35, marked “The A483 Swansea-Manchester Trunk Road (Improvement at Bryn Sadwrn) Order 1989” signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales and deposited at the Welsh Office Repository, Curran Embankment, Cardiff; “the new trunk road” means the highway mentioned in article 1 of this Order; “the trunk road” means the A483 Swansea-Manchester Trunk Road.

###### 4

This Order shall come into force on 1st March 1989 and may be cited asthe A483 Swansea-Manchester Trunk Road (Improvement at Bryn Sadwrn) Order 1989.

## THE SCHEDULE — ROUTE OF THE NEW TRUNK ROAD

The route of the new trunk road is a route at Bryn Sadwrn in the County of Powys as follows:—

A route about 1,275 metres in length, starting at a point on the trunk road about 150 metres north of its junction with the Classified Road C212 and C357 and running in a northerly direction to a point on the trunk road about 20 metres south of its junction with the unclassified road to Neuadd Farm.

## Signed
