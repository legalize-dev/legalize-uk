# Payments to Redundant Churches Fund Order 1988

Made (Approved by the General Synod): 9th November 1988

Laid before Parliament: 16th November 1988

Coming into operation: 1st April 1989

We, the Church Commissioners for England, in exercise of the powers conferred on us by section 53(1) of the Pastoral Measure 1983[^f00001] do hereby order as follows:—

###### 1

The amount to be paid to the Redundant Churches Fund by the Church Commissioners during the five year period 1st April 1989 to 31st March 1994 shall not exceed £3,700,000.

###### 2

The amount of the payments which may be made to the said Fund under section 52(1)(a) of the said Measure out of the one-third of the net proceeds of sale and premiums shall not exceed £1,200,000 and such payments shall be applied towards the £3,700,000 referred to in paragraph 1.

###### 3

Subject to paragraph 4 hereof, the balance of the said amount of £3,700,000 shall be paid by way of grants made to the said Fund by the Church Commissioners under section 44(10) of the said Measure out of their General Fund.

###### 4

A payment on account of the said £3,700,00 shall be made to the said Fund only when the Church Commissioners are satisfied that (1) the payment is an appropriate proportion of a specific sum of money needed by the Fund and (2) the balance of that sum will be paid to the Fund out of moneys provided by Parliament.

###### 5

This Order may be cited as the Payments to Redundant Churches Fund Order 1988 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1989.

## Signed

THE COMMON SEAL of the Church Commissioners was hereunto affixed this 13th day of October 1988

J. E. Shelley — Secretary

APPROVED by the General Synod of The Church of England the 9th day of November 1988

W. D. Pattinson — Secretary General
