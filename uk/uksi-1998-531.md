# The Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1998

Made: 3rd March 1998

Laid before Parliament: 11th March 1998

Coming into force: 1st April 1998

The Secretary of State for the Environment, Transport and the Regions in exercise of powers conferred by section 302(1) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, with the consent of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 and shall come into force on 1st April 1998.

#### Amendment of Regulations

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1996 (the “Fees Regulations”) and the Merchant Shipping (Port Waste Reception Facilities) Regulations 1997 (the “Waste Regulations”).

In addition to amending the table of statutory instruments in Part I of the Schedule of the Fees Regulations and correcting a reference to Part III of the Register of Ships, these Regulations amend the fees to be charged for certain services as follows:

| service | old fee | new fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| services in relation to the Radio Rules (as defined in the Fees Regulations) (paragraph 2(2)(a) of Part I of the Schedule)— |  |  |
| for fishing vessels | £23 | £23.66 |
| for other ships | £26 | £26.75 |
| registration of a ship following provisional registration by the Registrar (Part VII of the Schedule) | £150 | £50 |
| simple registration of four transfers of shares in relation to a fishing vessel (Part VIII of the Schedule) | £70 | £88 |
| waiting time prior to commencement of and during the course of a radio survey or inspection for periods in excess of one hour— |  |  |
| for surveys in the United Kingdom |  |  |
| of fishing vessels | £23 | £23.66 |
| of other ships | £26 | £26.75 |
| for surveys abroad |  |  |
| of fishing vessels | £46 | £47.32 |
| of other ships | £52 | £53.50 |
| for abortive visits for radio surveys in the United Kingdom, wasted time in excess of one hour and travelling time |  |  |
| in relation to fishing vessels | £23 | £23.66 |
| in relation to other ships | £26 | £26.75 |
| for radio surveys at unusual hours, including travelling time |  |  |
| on Mondays to Fridays inclusive between 6pm and 8am and all day Saturday |  |  |
| in relation to fishing vessels | £11.50 | £11.83 |
| in relation to other ships | £13 | £13.38 |
| on Sundays and Public Holidays |  |  |
| in relation to fishing vessels | £23 | £23.66 |
| in relation to other ships | £26 | £26.75 |
| (Part XII of the Schedule) |  |  |

The amendment to the Waste Regulations corrects the reference to the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 1996.
