# The Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) Order 2014

Made: 7th July 2014

Laid before Parliament: 10th July 2014

Coming into force: 1st September 2014

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(1) and 258(2) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (“the Act”)[^f00001], and now vested in her[^f00002], makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st September 2014.

#### Fees payable

###### 2

In respect of the matters set out in column 1 of Schedule 1, the fee specified in column 3 is payable to the person specified in column 4.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) Order 2005[^f00003];

- (b) the Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) (No. 2) Order 2005[^f00004];

- (c) the Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2010[^f00005];

- (d) the Registration of Civil Partnerships (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2012[^f00006].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order consolidates all previous statutory instruments made under section 34(1) of the Act.

In addition, it increases some of the fees payable from 1st September 2014 in relation to the registration of civil partnerships. The Order specifies the amount of the fee, both before and after the 1st September 2014, and to whom it is payable.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
