# The Dŵr Cymru Cyfyngedig (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1995

Made: 19th May 1995

Now, therefore the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers under section 182(2) of the Water Industry Act 1991, and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, after consulting all such persons as he considers it appropriate to consult, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Dŵr Cymru Cyfyngedig (Pipelaying and Other Works) (Code of Practice) Order 1995.

#### Code of Practice

###### 2

The Code of Practice prepared by the Company and certified as such by the Secretary of State is hereby approved.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Wales

G C G Craig — An Under Secretary in the Welsh Office — 19th May 1995
