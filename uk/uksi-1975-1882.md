# The Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975

Made: 19th November 1975

The Secretary of State in exercise of the power conferred by section 2(2) of the Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 (Commencement) Order 1975.

###### 2

The Hearing Aid Council (Extension) Act 1975 shall come into operation on 29th December 1975.

## Signed

Alan Williams — Minister of State — Department of Prices and Consumer Protection — 19th November 1975
