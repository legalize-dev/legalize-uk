# The Unitary Development Plans (Greater London and Dudley) (Appointed Day) Order 1989

Made: 29th June 1989

Laid before Parliament: 6th July 1989

Coming into force: 11th August 1989

The Secretary of State for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 4(1) of the Local Government Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Unitary Development Plans (Greater London and Dudley) (Appointed Day) Order 1989 and shall come into force on 11th August 1989.

###### 2

11th August 1989 is appointed as the day on which Part I of Schedule 1 to the Local Government Act 1985 shall come into force in the areas of the local planning authorities listed in the Schedule hereto.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Local Government Act 1985 abolished the Greater London Council and metropolitan county councils. The Act consequently provides for the replacement in their areas of existing structure and local development plans under town and country planning legislation by “unitary development plans”. This Order brings the provisions of the 1985 Act relating to unitary development plans into force in the county of Greater London other than the London Boroughs of Hackney, Hammersmith and Fulham, Hillingdon, Hounslow and Lewisham, and in the Metropolitan Borough of Dudley in the West Midlands on 11th August 1989.
