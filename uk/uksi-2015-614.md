# The Energy Act 2013 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015

Made: 10th March 2015

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the power conferred by section 156(1) of the Energy Act 2013[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Energy Act 2013 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015.

#### Provision coming into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made

###### 2

Section 150 of the Energy Act 2013 (smoke and carbon monoxide alarms) comes into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

## Signed

Signed by the Secretary of State for Communities and Local Government

Eric Pickles — Secretary of State — Department for Communities and Local Government — 10th March 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order is the second commencement order made under the Energy Act [2013 (c.32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/32) (“the Act”). It brings section 150 of the Act into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.
