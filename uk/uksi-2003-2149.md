# The Robert Jones and Agnes Hunt Orthopaedic and District Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2003

Made: 13th August 2003

Coming into force: 3rd September 2003

The Secretary of State for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001], and by section 5(1) of, and paragraph 3 of Schedule 2 to, the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Robert Jones and Agnes Hunt Orthopaedic and District Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2003 and shall come into force on 3rd September 2003.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the Establishment Order” means the Robert Jones and Agnes Hunt Orthopaedic and District Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00003]; and

- “the trust” means the National Health Service Trust established by the Establishment Order.

#### Increase in the number of non-executive and executive directors of the trust

###### 2

In article 4 of the Establishment Order (directors of the trust) “4 non-executive directors and 4 executive directors” shall be replaced by “5 non-executive directors and 5 executive directors”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State

Rosie Winterton — Minister of State, — Department of Health — 13th August 2003

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Robert Jones and Agnes Hunt Orthopaedic and District Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993, which established the Robert Jones and Agnes Hunt Orthopaedic and District Hospital National Health Service Trust.

Article 2 of the Order increases the number of both the non-executive and the executive directors of the trust from 4 to 5.
