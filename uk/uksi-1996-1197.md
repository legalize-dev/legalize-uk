# The Financial Services Act 1986 (Gas Industry Exemption) (Amendment) Order 1996

Made: 1st May 1996

Laid before Parliament: 1st May 1996

Coming into force: 22nd May 1996

The Treasury in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 46[^f00001] and section 205A[^f00002] of the Financial Services Act 1986[^f00003], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Financial Services Act 1986 (Gas Industry Exemption) (Amendment) Order 1996 and shall come into force on 22nd May 1996.

#### Amendments

###### 2

In article 1 of the Financial Services (Gas Industry Exemption) Order[^f00004] the words“Financial Services Act 1986 (Gas Industry Exemption) Order 1996” shall be substituted for the words “Financial Services (Gas Industry Exemption) Order”.

## Signed

Michael Bates — Simon Burns — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — 1st May 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 1 of the Financial Services (Gas Industry Exemption) Order by substituting for the words “Financial Services (Gas Industry Exemption) Order” in that article the words “Financial Services Act 1986 (Gas Industry Exemption) Order 1996”.
