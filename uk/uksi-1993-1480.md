# The Council Tax Limitation (England) (Maximum Amounts) Order 1993

Made: 10th June 1993

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

Now, therefore, the Secretary of State, after considering all such information as he thinks is relevant, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 57(2) and (6) of the Local Government Finance Act 1992 and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Title and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Council Tax Limitation (England) (Maximum Amounts) Order 1993 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

#### Maximum Amounts

###### 2

The amount which the amount calculated by an authority listed in the Schedule to this Order as its budget requirement for the financial year beginning in 1993 is not to exceed is the amount shown against the name of the authority in the Schedule, which amount does not in the case of any such authority exceed the amount already calculated by the authority as its budget requirement for that year.

## SCHEDULE — MAXIMUM AMOUNTS

| The Council of the District of Castle Point | £7,970,000 |
| --- | --- |
| The Council of the County of Gloucestershire | £306,610,000 |
| The Council of the District of Harlow | £11,800,000 |

## Signed

John Selwyn Gummer — Secretary of State for the Environment — 10th June 1993

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order states the amount which the amount calculated by each of the Councils of the Districts of Castle Point and Harlow and the County of Gloucestershire as its budget requirement for the financial year beginning in 1993 is not to exceed.
