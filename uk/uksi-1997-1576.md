# The Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variations) Order 1997

Made: 21st June 1997

Laid before Parliament: 24th June 1997

Coming into force: 15th July 1997

Now therefore, the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(7) of the Public Lending Right Act 1979[^f00003] and now vested in him[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:—

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 (Commencement of Variations) Order 1997.

###### 2

The variations in the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 which were made on 21st June 1997 by the Secretary of State and are set out in the Appendix to this Order shall come into force on 15th July 1997.

## APPENDIX — VARIATIONS IN THE PUBLIC LENDING RIGHT SCHEME 1982 MADE BY THE SECRETARY OF STATE ON 21st JUNE 1997

###### 1

Article 4(1)(b) of the Scheme shall be varied by deleting “(within the meaning of section 48 of the Copyright Act 1956)”.

###### 2

Article 9(A) of the Scheme shall be varied by substituting for paragraph (4) the following—

> > (4) Each eligible person’s share of Public Lending Right in respect of a book with two or more authors (including any who are not eligible persons but disregarding a translator, editor or compiler) shall not exceed fifty per cent. of that Right unless the Registrar is satisfied that any share exceeding fifty per cent. which is specified in accordance with article 17(1)(c) in the application for first registration of the Right or in accordance with article 17(2) in the application for first registration of an eligible author’s share of the Right is reasonable in relation to that author’s contribution.

###### 3

Article 20 of the Scheme shall be varied by substituting “seventy” for “fifty”.

###### 4

Article 36 of the Scheme shall be varied by substituting “38(5)” for “38(6)”.

###### 5

Articles 39, 40(1) and 44(1) of the Scheme shall be varied by substituting “38(5)(a)” for “38(6)(a)”.

###### 6

Article 46(3) of the Scheme shall be varied by substituting “£5” for “£1”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force variations to the Public Lending Right Scheme 1982 made by the Secretary of State on 21st June 1997.

The Appendix sets out the variations. The principal variations are as follows.

Provisions relating to the calculation of an illustrator’s or co-author’s share of Public Lending Right in respect of a book are modified. Where an illustrator’s or co-author’s share in Public Lending Right in respect of which there are more than one authors (disregarding a translator, editor or compiler) exceeds 50 per cent. the Registrar will need to be satisfied that it is reasonable in relation to that author’s contribution to the book.

The duration of the Public Lending Right in respect of any book and the period during which the right may be transferred is extended to seventy years.

The minimum eligibility for payment due in respect of Public Lending Right for each financial year is increased to £5.

Schedule 2, which groups service points, is amended in consequence of local government re-organisation and set out in consolidated form.
