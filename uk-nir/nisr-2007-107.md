# Milk (Cessation of Production) (Revocation) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 22nd February 2007

Coming into operation-: 6th April 2007

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Scheme in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 3 of the Milk (Cessation of Production) (Northern Ireland) Order 1985[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the Milk (Cessation of Production) (Revocation) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2007.

#### Revocation and saving

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2) the Milk (Cessation of Production) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00002] is revoked.

- (2) Article 9 of the Scheme referred to in paragraph (1) shall continue to have effect in relation to any sum paid under that Scheme as if that paragraph had not been revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 22nd February 2007.

Dr. John Speers — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme revokes the Milk (Cessation of Production) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 1987 as it is no longer in operation.
