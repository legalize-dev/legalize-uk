# The Road Traffic (Amendment) (1991 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 27th January 2011

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Road Traffic (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Amendment) (1991 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 7 of the Road Traffic (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002] (compulsory training courses for riders of motor cycles) is 21st February 2011.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 27th January 2011

Deirdre Kenny — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 21st February 2011 Article 7 of the Road Traffic (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 which has the effect of requiring motor cyclists, who have yet to pass a test of competence to drive a motor bicycle, to undergo compulsory training unless exempted by Regulations from having to do so.
