# The Supreme Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 4th August 2004

Coming into operation: 1st September 2004

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 116(1) and (4) of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001], after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court Fees (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2004.

#### Fees to be taken in proceedings in the Supreme Court

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Supreme Court Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] there shall be substituted the new Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Lord Chancellor

I concur

Christopher Leslie — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Constitutional Affairs — Dated 1st August 2004

We concur

John Heppell — Joan Ryan — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 4th August 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Supreme Court Fees Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 so as to increase a number of fees to be taken in proceedings in the Supreme Court.
