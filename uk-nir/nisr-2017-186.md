# The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made: 13th September 2017

Coming into operation: 25th September 2017

The Department for Communities makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(3) of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 .

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 25th September 2017.

- (2) In regulation 2—

- (a) paragraph (a) applies only in relation to a person—

- (i) first diagnosed with diffuse mesothelioma on or after 1st April 2017; or

- (ii) where the date of the first diagnosis with diffuse mesothelioma is unknown, if a claim under section 1(1) of the Mesothelioma etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 is made by that person on or after 1st April 2017; and

- (b) paragraph (b) applies only in relation to the dependant of a person who dies on or after 1st April 2017 and who, immediately before death, had diffuse mesothelioma.

#### Amendment of the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 —

- (a) for Table 1 substitute the Table set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations; and

- (b) for Table 2 substitute the Table set out in Part 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 are revoked .
