# The Welfare of Animals (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 24th October 2012

Coming into operation: 1st January 2013

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 59 of the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare of Animals (2011 Act) (Commencement No.3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) Section 6 (docking of dogs’ tails) other than subsections (16) and (17); and

- (b) Schedule 1 (dogs specified for the purposes of section 6(7)),

of the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is the 1st day of January 2013.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 24th October 2012

C. McMaster — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences certain provisions of the Welfare of Animals (Northern Ireland) Act 2011.
