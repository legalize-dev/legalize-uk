# Food (Emergency Control) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 5th May 2004

Coming into operation: 5th May 2004

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Food (Emergency Control) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 5th May 2004.

#### Amendment to the Food (Peanuts from China) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

###### 2

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Peanuts from China) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00003] for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there shall be substituted the following definition –

> > - “the Commission Decision” means Commission Decision [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) imposing special conditions on the import of peanuts and certain products derived from peanuts originating in or consigned from China[^f00004] as amended by Commission Decision [2002/233/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0233)[^f00005], Commission Decision [2002/678/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0678)[^f00006], Commission Decision [2003/550/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0550)[^f00007] and the Commission Decision of 29th April 2004 amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community[^f00008];

#### Amendment to the Food (Figs, Hazelnuts and Pistachios from Turkey) (Emergency Control No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

###### 3

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Figs, Hazelnuts and Pistachios from Turkey) (Emergency Control No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00009] for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there shall be substituted the following definition –

> > - “the Commission Decision” means Commission Decision [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) imposing special conditions on the import of figs, hazelnuts and pistachios and certain products derived thereof originating in or consigned from Turkey[^f00010] as amended by Commission Decision [2002/233/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0233)[^f00011], Commission Decision [2002/679/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0679)[^f00012], Commission Decision [2003/552/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0552)[^f00013] and the Commission Decision of 29th April 2004 amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community[^f00014];

#### Amendment to the Food (Brazil Nuts) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 4

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Brazil Nuts) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00015] for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there shall be substituted the following definition –

> > - “the Commission Decision” means Commission Decision [2003/493/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0493) imposing special conditions on the import of Brazil nuts in shell originating in or consigned from Brazil[^f00016] as amended by the Commission Decision of 29th April 2004 amending Decision [2003/493/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0493) as regards the points of entry through which Brazil nuts in shell originating in or consigned from Brazil may only be imported into the Community[^f00017];

#### Amendment to the Food (Pistachios from Iran) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 5

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Pistachios from Iran) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00018] for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there shall be substituted the following definition –

> > - “the Commission Decision” means Commission Decision [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830) repealing Commission Decision [97/613/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0613) and imposing special conditions on the import of pistachios and certain products derived from pistachios originating in or consigned from Iran[^f00019] as amended by Commission Decision [1998/400/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1998/0400)[^f00020], Commission Decision [2000/238/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0238)[^f00021], Commission Decision [2003/551/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0551)[^f00022] and the Commission Decision of 29th April 2004 amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community[^f00023];

#### Amendment to the Food (Peanuts from Egypt) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 6

In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Food (Peanuts from Egypt) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00024] for the definition of “the Commission Decision” there shall be substituted the following definition –

> > - “the Commission Decision” means Commission Decision [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049) repealing Decision [1999/356/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0356) and imposing special conditions on the import of peanuts and certain products derived from peanuts originating in or consigned from Egypt[^f00025] as amended by Commission Decision [2003/580/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0580)[^f00026] and the Commission Decision of 29th April 2004 amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community[^f00027];

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 5th May 2004.

Denis McMahon — A senior officer of the — Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

1 These Regulations amend – a the Food (Peanuts from China) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No. 293](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/293) as already amended), the Food (Figs, Hazelnuts and Pistachios from Turkey) (Emergency Control No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No. 307](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/307) as already amended), the Food (Pistachios from Iran) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No. 360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/360)) and the Food (Peanuts from Egypt) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No. 377](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/377)) to implement the Commission Decision of 29th April 2004 amending Decisions [97/830/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0830), [2000/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0049), [2002/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0079) and [2002/80/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0080) as regards the points of entry through which the products concerned may only be imported into the Community (regulations 2, 3, 5 and 6); and b the Food (Brazil Nuts) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No. 353](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/353) as already amended) to implement the Commission Decision of 29th April 2004 amending Decision [2003/493/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0493) as regards the points of entry through which Brazil nuts in shell originating in or consigned from Brazil may only be imported into the Community (regulation 4).

2 Each of the instruments that is amended by these Regulations implements a Commission Decision imposing special conditions on the import of a certain type of food.

3 Each of those instruments provides that the food concerned has to be imported through certain points of entry listed in the relevant Decision.

4 Those points of entry are revised by the Decisions that are implemented by these Regulations.
