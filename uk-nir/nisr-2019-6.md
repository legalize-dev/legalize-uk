# The Road Traffic and Vehicles and Waterways (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 21st January 2019

Coming into operation: 18th February 2019

The Department for Infrastructure makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic and Vehicles and Waterways (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 18th February 2019.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00010] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of the European Communities (Motor Vehicles: Compulsory Insurance) Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The European Communities (Motor Vehicles: Compulsory Insurance) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1973[^f00011] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) for the definition of “the Ministry” substitute ““the Department” means the Department for Infrastructure;”.

- (3) In regulation 6(1) for “the Ministry” substitute “the Department”.

- (4) In regulation 7(4) for “the Ministry, the Ministry” substitute “the Department, the Department”.

#### Amendment of the Carriage of Goods (Prohibition of Discrimination) Regulations

###### 3

In regulation 2(1) of the Carriage of Goods (Prohibition of Discrimination) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00012] in the definition of “the Department” for “Department of the Environment” substitute “Department for Infrastructure”.

#### Amendment of the Road Transport (Qualifications of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations

###### 4

For regulation 2 (interpretation) of the Road Transport (Qualifications of Operators) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00013] substitute—

> > (2) 2. In these Regulations—
>   - “the Act of 1967” means the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00014];
>   - “the Department” means the Department for Infrastructure.

#### Amendment of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) Regulations

#### Amendment of the Road Vehicles (Testing) (Disclosure of Information) Regulations

###### 8

- (2) In regulation 1 (citation, commencement and interpretation) after paragraph (2) insert—

> > (3) In these Regulations “the Department” means the Department for Infrastructure.

- (3) In regulation 2 (disclosure of testing information to member State competent authorities) for “Department of the Environment” substitute “Department”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 8(1) of the Departments (Transfer of Functions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 (“the 2016 Order”), transferred departmental functions between Departments, including from the Department of the Environment (DoE) to the Department for Infrastructure (DfI), the Department for Communities and the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs. DoE was subsequently dissolved by the Departments Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.

However the 2016 Order did not specifically address subordinate legislation made under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972. Accordingly, and for transparency, these Regulations are intended to clarify that statutory responsibility for the Statutory Rules hereby amended rests with DfI.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available online alongside this Statutory Rule at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
