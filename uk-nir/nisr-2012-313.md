# The Taxis (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 7th August 2012

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59 of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Taxis (2008 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 1 of the Schedule for the purpose only of making regulations, is 8th August 2012.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 2 of the Schedule is, 1st September 2012.
