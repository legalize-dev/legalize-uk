# The Pesticides, Genetically Modified Organisms and Fertilisers (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 13th December 2018

Coming into operation: 7th January 2019

The Department is designated for the purposes of Section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to— materials providing or intended to provide nutrients to plants[^f00003]; measures in the veterinary and phytosanitary fields for the protection of public health[^f00004]; the common agricultural policy of the European Union[^f00005]; measures relating to the control and regulation of genetically modified organisms[^f00006]; prevention, reduction and elimination of pollution of water[^f00007].

materials providing or intended to provide nutrients to plants[^f00003];

measures in the veterinary and phytosanitary fields for the protection of public health[^f00004];

the common agricultural policy of the European Union[^f00005];

measures relating to the control and regulation of genetically modified organisms[^f00006];

prevention, reduction and elimination of pollution of water[^f00007].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pesticides, Genetically Modified Organisms and Fertilisers (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and come into operation on 7 January 2019.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00008] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made by the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs using powers under section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972 ([1972 c.68](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/68)).

The Regulations make miscellaneous minor amendments to legislation relating to pesticides, fertilisers, seed marketing, nitrates and genetically modified organisms, updating out of date references. The purpose of these Regulations is to ensure that references are correct and, in particular, that the amended provisions will operate effectively on exit day.

An impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as they provide for technical correction and updating which does not involve any policy changes or impact on the private or voluntary sector.
