# The Mental Health Review Tribunal (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 18th February 2016

Coming into operation: 10th March 2016

The Department of Justice makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 83(1) and 83(4) of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00001] and now vested in the Department of Justice[^f00002] after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Mental Health Review Tribunal (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 10th March 2016.

#### Amendments to the Mental Health Review Tribunal (Northern Ireland) Rules 1986

###### 2

Rule 26(2) of the Mental Health Review Tribunal (Northern Ireland) Rules 1986[^f00003] shall be amended as follows—

- (1) after “circumstances of the case” insert “where in the interests of justice”;

- (2) omit “,with the exception of the periods of notice specified in rule 16(4), rule 20 and rule 25(2),”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 18th February 2016

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Mental Health Review Tribunal (Northern Ireland) Rules 1986 ([S.R. 1986 No. 193](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1986/193)) so as to allow the Tribunal to extend or abridge the time appointed by the Rules for doing any act where in the interests of justice to do so.
