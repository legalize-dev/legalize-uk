# The Criminal Justice (2013 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 6th June 2013

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the power conferred by section 13(2) of the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00001] and, in accordance with section 13(5) of that Act with the approval of the First Minister and deputy First Minister acting jointly, and following a request from the Chief Constable, and after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (2013 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

- (2) In this Order, “the 2013 Act” means the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2013.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day for the coming into operation of section 13(1) of the 2013 Act is 8 June 2013.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 6 June 2013

David Ford — Minister of Justice

Approved by the First Minister and deputy First Minister acting jointly on 6 June 2013

Peter Robinson — Martin McGuinness — First Minister — deputy First Minister

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation section 13(1) of the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) [2013 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2013/7) (“the 2013 Act”).

Section 13(1) of the 2013 Act disapplies section 7 of the Sunday Observance Act (Ireland) 1695 to allow for a magistrates’ court, in the exercise of its criminal jurisdiction, to be held on a Sunday.

In accordance with section 13(2)(b) of the 2013 Act, this Order will cease to be in operation one month from the day appointed in Article 2 of this Order for the coming into operation of section 13(1) of the 2013 Act.
