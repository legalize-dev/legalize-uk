# The Financial Assistance for Young Farmers Scheme (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 2nd December 2005

Coming into operation: 23rd December 2005

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3 (1) to (4) and (7) of the Financial Assistance for Young Farmers (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Financial Assistance for Young Farmers Scheme (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 23rd December 2005.

#### Amendment of the Financial Assistance for Young Farmers Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

In paragraph 5 of the Schedule to the Financial Assistance for Young Farmers Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00002] (closing date for applications) for the words “3rd April 2008” there shall be substituted the words “5th June 2008”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 2nd December 2005

Norman Fulton — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the closing date for applications for a grant under the Financial Assistance for Young Farmers Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2005 from 3rd April 2008 to 5th June 2008.
