## SCHEDULE 1 — **Matters arising under the Cereal Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994**

#### Initial fees

###### A

Initial fee per hectare or part thereof for crops intended to produce:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed

#### Crop inspection fees

###### B

Fee for official examination or re-examination of crops intended to produce seed as set out in A(i), (ii) or (iii) per hectare or part thereof examined

#### Seed lot fee

###### C

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot entered as:—

- (i) breeder's, pre-basic or basic seed, certified seed of the first generation of wheat, durum wheat, spelt wheat, barley, triticale, oats or certified seed of rye and maize

#### Seed testing fees

###### D

Fee for an official examination of seed for loose smut infection of wheat, durum wheat, spelt wheat and barley (per thousand embryos examined)

#### Seed lot re-entry fee

###### E

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot which had been entered for one category and been withdrawn and is being re-entered as:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed, certified seed of the first generation of wheat, durum wheat, spelt wheat, barley, triticale and oats or certified seed of rye and maize

## SCHEDULE 2 — **Matters arising under the Fodder Plant Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994**

#### Initial fees

###### A

Initial fee per hectare or part thereof for crops intended to produce:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed of—

- (a) grasses, legumes (except field beans and field peas) and crucifers

#### Crop inspection fees

###### B

Fee for official examination or re-examination of crops intended to produce seed as set out in A(i), (ii) or (iii) per hectare or part thereof examined

#### Seed lot fee

###### C

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot entered as:—

- (i) breeder’s seed of—

- (a) grasses, legumes (except field beans and field peas) and crucifers

#### Seed lot re-entry fee

###### D

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot which had been entered for one category and been withdrawn and is being re-entered as:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed of—

- (a) grasses, legumes (except field beans and field peas) and crucifers

## SCHEDULE 3 — **Matters arising under the Oil and Fibre Plant Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994**

#### Initial fees

###### A

Initial fee per hectare or part thereof for crops intended to produce:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed

#### Crop inspection fees

###### B

Fee for official examination or re-examination of crops intended to produce seed as set out in A(i) or (ii) per hectare or part thereof examined

#### Seed lot fee

###### C

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot entered as:—

- (i) breeder's, pre-basic or basic seed

#### Seed testing fees

###### D

Fees for an official examination of seed for:—

- (i) sunflower for *Botrytis* spp

#### Seed lot re-entry fee

###### E

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot which had been entered for one category and been withdrawn and is being re-entered as:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed

## SCHEDULE 4 — **Matters arising under the Beet Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994**

#### Initial fees

###### A

Initial fee per hectare or part thereoffor crops intended to produce:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed

#### Crop inspection fees

###### B

Fee for official examination or re-examination of crops intended to produce seed as set out in A(i) or (ii) per hectare or part thereof examined

#### Seed lot fee

###### C

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot entered as:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed

#### Seed lot re-entry fee

###### D

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot which had been entered for one category and been withdrawn and is being re-entered as:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed

## SCHEDULE 5 — **Matters arising under the Vegetable Seeds Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994**

#### Initial fees

###### A

Initial fee for crops intended to produce:—

- (i) pre-basic or basic seed of—

- (a) all kinds, except peas, broad beans, cucumber, gherkin and tomato, per hectare or part thereof entered

#### Crop inspection fees

###### B

Fee for official examination or re-examination of crops intended to produce:—

- (i) A(i)(a) or (b) and A(ii)(a) or (b) above, per hectare or part thereof examined

#### Seed lot fee

###### C

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot entered

#### Seed lot re-entry fee

###### D

Fee for the issue of an official certificate in respect of a seed lot which had been entered for one category and been withdrawn and is being re-entered

## SCHEDULE 6 — **Matters arising under the Seeds (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994**

#### Representations by, and hearings of, a seed merchant, processor or packer

###### A

- (i) Fee for making written representations to the Department

#### Licensed Seed Testing Establishments

###### B

- (i) Annual fee payable during the currency of the licence (or a proportion thereof in respect of a part of a year)
