# The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (Test Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 25th August 2005

Coming into operation: 1st October 2005

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 5(3), (4) and (5) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, and in accordance with Article 19C(3)[^f00002] of that Order with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00003] as regards regulation 2, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) (Test Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2005.

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

For Schedule 4 of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] there shall be substituted –

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 by substituting a new Schedule 4. In the new Schedule 4 the following fees have been increased –

- (a) the fee for a test, other than an extended test, for vehicles in categories A1, B1 (other than B1 invalid carriages) P and A is increased from £47·00 to £53·50 for a test commencing during normal hours and from £59·00 to £66·00 for a test commencing out of hours;

- (b) the fee for a test, other than an extended test, for a vehicle in category B is increased from £39·00 to £42·00 for a test commencing during normal hours and from £50·50 to £58·00 for a test commencing out of hours;

- (c) the fee for a test for a vehicle in category F, G, H and K is increased from £39·00 to £42·00 for a test commencing during normal hours;

- (d) the fee for a test for a vehicle in category B+E, C1, C1+E, D1, D1+E, C, C+E, D and D+E is increased from £55·00 to £61·00 for a test commencing during normal hours and from £83·00 to £92·00 for a test commencing out of hours;

- (e) the fee for an extended test during normal hours for a vehicle in category B is increased from £78·00 to £84·00;

- (f) the fee for an extended driving test during normal hours for a vehicle in category A is increased from £94·00 to £107·00.
