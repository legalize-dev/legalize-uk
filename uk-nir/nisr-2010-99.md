# The Organic Farming (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 11th March 2010

Coming into operation: 1st May 2010

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as The Organic Farming (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1st May 2010.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Organic Farming Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

###### 2

- (1) The Organic Farming Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00005] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation (2)(2)(b) remove the definition ““the Compendium of Organic Standards” means the Compendium of Organic Standards published by the Department of Environment, Food and Rural Affairs, in September 2006;”.

- (3) In regulation (3)(1)(c) remove the words “the Compendium of Organic Standards”.

- (4) In regulation (4)(2)(b) remove the words “the Compendium of Organic Standards and with”.

- (5) In regulation (5)(1)(b) remove the words “with all relevant provisions of the Compendium of Organic Standards,”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 11th March 2010.

John Speers — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development
