# The Justice (2015 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 3rd August 2015

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 106(2) of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Justice (2015 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015 is 1st September 2015 —

- (a) section 28 (the Victim Charter);

- (b) section 29 (meaning of victim);

- (c) section 31 (procedure for issuing Charters);

- (d) section 32 (effect of non-compliance);

- (e) section 33 (persons to be afforded opportunity to make victim statement);

- (f) section 34 (supplementary statement); and

- (g) section 35 (use of victim statement).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 3rd August 2015.

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings sections 28, 29 and 31 – 35 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) [2015 (c.9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/9) into operation on 1 September 2015.

Sections 28 and 29 relate to a Victim Charter and the meaning of a victim. Section 31 sets out the procedure for issuing a Charter, while section 32 deals with non-compliance with the Charter by a criminal justice agency. Sections 33 sets out who is to be afforded the opportunity to make a victim statement, section 34 provides for making a supplementary statement and section 35 provides that the court must have regard to a victim statement in determining a sentence.
