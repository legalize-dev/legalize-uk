# The Civil Legal Services (Scope) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 30th November 2016

Coming into operation: 1st December 2016

In accordance with Article 46(5) of that Order, a draft of this Rule has been laid before and approved by a resolution of the Assembly.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Legal Services (Scope) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st December 2016.

#### Amendment to Schedule 2 to the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003

###### 2

In paragraph 2(d) of Schedule 2 to the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, after paragraph (xxiv) insert—

> > (xxv) under paragraph 2, 6 or 7 of Schedule 3 to the Human Trafficking and Exploitation (Criminal Justice and Support for Victims) Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00003];
> (xxvi) under section 57, 60 or 61 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004];

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 30th November 2016.

Claire Sugden — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 to the Access to Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 regarding the proceedings which may be funded as civil legal services.

The effect of these amendments is to provide that representation in a court of summary jurisdiction relating to applications for Slavery and Trafficking Prevention Orders and applications for Violent Offences Prevention Orders may be funded as civil legal services.

The Regulations come into operation on 1st December 2016.
