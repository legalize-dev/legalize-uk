# The Criminal Evidence (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made: 13th June 2000

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Evidence (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The following provision of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 shall come into operation on 14th June 2000.

## Signed

Peter Mandelson — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 13th June 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order commences as from 14th June 2000 Article 39(1) of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 which provides for the making of court rules to bring the provisions of the Order into effect. The first part of the rule making powers was commenced on 1st June 2000 by the Criminal Evidence (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000.
