# The Hemp (Third Country Imports) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made: 17th November 2026

Coming into operation: 01st January 2027

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of these Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00003], laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Citation, Commencement and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as The Hemp (Third Country Imports) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 01st January 2027.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004], shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendments to The Hemp (Third Country Imports) Regulations 2002

###### 2

- (1) The Hemp (Third Country Imports) Regulations 2002[^f00005] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—

- (i) in the definition of “authorisation”, for “pursuant to the third indent of the first sub-paragraph of Article 5(2) of Council Regulation 1673/2000” substitute “in accordance with Article 9 of Commission Regulation 2016/1237”;

- (ii) omit the definition of “Commission Regulation 245/2001”;

- (iii) omit the definition of “Commission Regulation 1093/2001”;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made under section 11 of, paragraph 11M of Part 1C of Schedule 2 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018. The EU provisions relating to the import of hemp from third countries are set out in [Regulation (EU) 1308/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1308) which is directly applicable in Northern Ireland under Article 5(4) of, and Annex 2 to, the Windsor Framework. These Regulations amend the Hemp (Third Country Import) Regulations 2002 ([S.I. 2002/787](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/787)) as they apply in Northern Ireland in relation to third country imports.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on businesses or the voluntary/community sector is foreseen.
