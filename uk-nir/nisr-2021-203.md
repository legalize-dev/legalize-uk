# The Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (Relevant Benefits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

The Department of Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 14(7) of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (Relevant Benefits) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation 21 days after this Order is made.

#### Amendment of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016: relevant benefits

###### 2

In section 14 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (application for deduction from benefits), in subsection (3), before paragraph (a), insert—

> > (za) universal credit;

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 5th July 2021

Naomi Long — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends section 14 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) [2016 (2016 c.21 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2016/21)) to add universal credit to the list of benefits from which deductions may be made under a collection order for the payment of a fine or other penalty.
