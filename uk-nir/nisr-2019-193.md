# The Mental Capacity (Research) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 27th September 2019

Coming into operation: 1st October 2019

The Department of Health[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 132 of the Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mental Capacity (Research) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2019.

- (2) In these Regulations “HSC Trust” has the same meaning as in the Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.

#### Appropriate body for the purpose of research

###### 2

An appropriate body is a person or body designated as an appropriate body by an HSC trust or a university.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 27th September 2019.

Mark Lee — A senior officer of the Department of Health.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provides who the appropriate body for approving research conducted under Part 8 of the Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 is.
