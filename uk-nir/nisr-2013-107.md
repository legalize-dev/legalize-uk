# Agriculture (Student fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 18th April 2013

Coming into operation: 1st September 2013

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5A(1) and (2) of the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (Student fees)(Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on the 1st September 2013.

#### Amendment to the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

The Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] shall be amended as provided by regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

In regulation 4 (1)[^f00004] for “£1,380” substitute “£1,425”:

###### 4

In regulation 5(2)[^f00005] for “£680” substitute “£700”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 18th April 2013.

Norman Fulton — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007.

These Regulations increase the level of fee to be paid by persons undertaking a course of higher education provided by the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development as set out in Regulations 3 and 4.
