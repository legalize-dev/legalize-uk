# The Superannuation (District Councils) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 10th March 2015

Coming into operation: 1st April 2015

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(4) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (District Councils) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2015.

#### Addition to list of employments

###### 2

There shall be added to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 with effect from 1st April 2015, employment in the Northern Ireland district councils[^f00003].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 10th March 2015.

Grace Nesbitt — A senior officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order adds employment in the Northern Ireland district councils to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes.
