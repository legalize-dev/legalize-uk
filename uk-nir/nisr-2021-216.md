# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: at 5.00 p.m. on 22nd July 2021

Laid before the Assembly: at 6.00 p.m. on 22nd July 2021

Coming into operation: 23rd July 2021

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 23rd July 2021.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) For regulation 15(1), substitute—

> > (15)
>   (1) These Regulations expire on 24th March 2022.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 5.00 pm on 22nd July 2021

Robin Swann — Minister of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 in order to change the expiry date.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
