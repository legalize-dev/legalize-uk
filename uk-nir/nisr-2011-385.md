# Control of Pollution (Oil Storage) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 2nd November 2011

Coming into operation: 30th November 2011

The Department of the Environment, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 14 and 61(2) of the Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Control of Pollution (Oil Storage) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 30 November 2011.

#### Amendment to the Control of Pollution (Oil Storage) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

###### 2

In regulation 8(1)(a) of the Control of Pollution (Oil Storage) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002] (notices to minimise pollution risks in transitional cases) delete the words “or (2)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 2 November 2011

Liz Loughran — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend regulation 8(1)(a) of the Control of Pollution (Oil Storage) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 to correct a drafting error.
