# The Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 30th April 2009

Coming into operation: 1st June 2009

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2[^f00002] and 3[^f00003] of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00004], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2009.

#### Amendments to the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00005] is amended as follows.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No.82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/82)) (“the principal Order”) so as to implement Commission Directive [2009/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0007) amending Annexes I, II, IV and V to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community (OJ No L 40, 11.2.2009, p 12). The Order makes various amendments to Schedules 1, 2, 4 and 6 to the principal Order so as to apply new restrictions on the introduction into Northern Ireland of certain plants and plant pests.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen. A Transposition Note is available and can be obtained from the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development.
