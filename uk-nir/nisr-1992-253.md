# The Pollution Control and Local Government (1978 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992

Made: 4th June 1992

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Pollution Control and Local Government (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 (hereinafter referred to as “the Order”) and paragraphs 2(1) and 2(2) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974 and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pollution Control and Local Government (1978 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Order which are specified in column 1 of the Schedule and which relate to the subject matter specified in column thereof shall be 1st July 1992.

## SCHEDULE

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Provisions of the Order | Subject matter of Provisions |
| Part II |  |
| Article 14 | Collection of waste |
| Article 15(1), (1A), (2), (3), (4) and (5) | Dustbins, etc. |
| Part VI |  |
| Article 87 so far as it relates to the provisions in Schedule 7 of the Order specified below | Repeals |
| Schedule 7 |  |
| In the Public Health (Ireland) Act 1878 , section 42 (insofar as it remains unrepealed), section 53, and in section 54 the words from “where the district council” to “any such premises”. |  |
| Section 26(2) of the Public Health Acts Amendment Act 1890. |  |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 4th June 1992.

R. W. Rogers — Assistant Secretary
