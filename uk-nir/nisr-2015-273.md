# The Planning (Development Management) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 15th June 2015

Coming into operation: 6th July 2015

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 25(2), 26(1) and 247(1) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Development Management) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 6th July 2015.

#### Amendment of the Planning (Development Management) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

###### 2

In the Schedule (major development thresholds) to the Planning (Development Management) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00002] in paragraph 3(c) of column 3 in the table, after “connected to land” insert “and”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 15th June 2015.

*Angus Kerr* — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Regulation 2 amends the Schedule to the Planning (Development Management) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 to correct a drafting error in those Regulations.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared in relation to these Regulations.

The Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the Regulations on the government’s website www.legislation.gov.uk.
