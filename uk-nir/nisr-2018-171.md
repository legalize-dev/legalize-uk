# The Ship Recycling Facilities (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 4th October 2018

Coming into operation: 29th October 2018

The Department has been designated[^f00003] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in respect of measures relating to the prevention, reduction and elimination of pollution caused by waste and in relation to the environment.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Ship Recycling Facilities (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 29th October 2018.

#### Amendment of the Ship Recycling Facilities Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

###### 2

In the Ship Recycling Facilities Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004], in regulation 2 (interpretation), in the definition of “the European List”, after “established” insert “and as amended from time to time”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 4th October 2018.

John Mills — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs — 4th October 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in Section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, in order to allow the amended provision to operate effectively.
