# Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made: 19th September 2004

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 12th November 2004

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of section 25 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], read with section 18 of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962[^f00002] and after consulting, in accordance with section 25(8) of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998, the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association and, after taking into account the recommendations made by the Police Negotiating Board for the United Kingdom and supplying that Board with a draft of the regulations in accordance with section 62(1) of the Police Act 1996[^f00003], hereby makes the following regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

- (2) These regulations shall come into operation on 12th November 2004 , but –

- (a) Regulations 2, 3 and 4 shall have effect from the dates mentioned therein;

- (b) Regulation 6 shall have effect from 19th October 2000;

- (c) Regulations 7 and 8 shall have effect from 1st April 2003;

- (d) Regulation 11 shall have effect from 1st April 2002;

- (e) Regulation 16 shall have effect from 4th April 2001;

- (f) Regulation 17(2) shall have effect from 1st September 2001;

- (g) Regulation 17(3) shall have effect from 1st September 2002;

- (h) Regulation 17(4) shall have effect from 1st September 2003.

- (3) In these Regulations “the principal regulations” means the Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996[^f00004].

- (4) In these regulations “the amending regulations” means the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2002[^f00005].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Royal Ulster Constabulary Regulations 1996 (“the 1996 Regulations”).

Regulations 2, 3 and 4 make retrospective changes to the previous amending regulation [2002 No. 95](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/95) which also amended the 1996 regulations.

Regulation 5, 10 and 18 amend the 1996 Regulations inserting the definition of British Transport Police (BTP) allowing a member who joined or rejoined the force on or after the 1st September 1994 from the BTP Force to reckon his service with the BTP as service for the purposes of regulation 36 (rate of pay). If the member of the force is of the rank of chief inspector or higher then a contrary agreement can be reached.

Regulation 6 amends regulation 23 of the 1996 Regulations increasing the amount of hours between the ending of a member’s daily period of duty and the next one from 8 hours to 11 hours.

Regulation 7 amends regulation 24 of the 1996 Regulations extending the disregard of casual overtime to time off as well as paid overtime compensation.

Regulation 8 amends regulation 25 of the 1996 Regulations decreasing the eight day threshold for triggering the higher rate of compensation to five.

Regulation 9 makes a minor correction to regulation 37 of the 1996 Regulations.

Regulation 11 amends regulation 49 of the 1996 Regulations increasing the Removal allowance.

Regulations, 12, 13 and 14 remove regulations 49, 50 and 52 and Schedule 8 of the 1996 Regulations.

Regulation 15 removes regulation 57 of the 1996 Regulations.

Regulation 16 amends Schedule 4 of the 1996 Regulations changing the compensation arrangement for officers recalled from annual leave.

Regulation 17 amends Schedule 10 of the 1996 Regulations increasing the Dog handler’s allowance.
