# The Quality of Bathing Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 25th May 2012

Coming into operation: 1st June 2012

The Department of the Environment being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972([^f00002] in relation to the environment, acting in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by that section, makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Quality of Bathing Water (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2012.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954([^f00003] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendments to the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

###### 3

For Schedule 1 to the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00004] substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — Schedule substituted for Schedule 1 to the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 25th May 2012

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend Schedule 1 of the Quality of Bathing Water Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 to remove Cranfield (Nicholson’s Strand) from the list of bathing waters set out in the Schedule to those Regulations.
