# The Planning (General Development) (Amendment No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 8th October 2007

Coming into operation: 17th December 2007

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning (General Development) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 17th December 2007.

#### Amendment of the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

###### 2

- (1) Part 6 (agricultural buildings and operations) of Schedule 1 to the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In Class A (permitted development) for the date “1st January 2008” substitute “1st January 2009”.

- (3) In Class A2 (permitted development) for the date “31st December 2007” substitute “31st December 2008”.

- (4) In paragraph A.3 (interpretation of Classes A and A2)—

- (a) in sub–paragraph (1)(b)(ii) omit “(being a period following the operative date of this Order)”;

- (b) in sub–paragraph (1)(c)—

- (i) at the beginning insert the words “subject to sub-paragraph (1)(d)”; and

- (ii) omit “(being a period commencing after 31 December 2007)”;

- (c) after sub–paragraph (1)(c) add the following—

> > ;
> (d) for the purposes of sub–paragraph (1)(c), buildings, works, structures, plant or machinery provided prior to 1st January 2009 shall be disregarded

; and

- (d) in sub–paragraph (3) for the dates “1 January 2008” and “31 December 2007” substitute “1st January 2009” and “31st December 2008” respectively.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 8th October 2007

Pat McBride — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Part 6 (agricultural buildings and operations) of Schedule 1 to the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 by extending until 31st December 2008 the period during which the maximum permitted size of:

- (i) a building or excavation used or to be used for the storage of slurry or manure; or

- (ii) a building or structure used for both the accommodation of livestock and the storage of slurry or manure,

is increased from 300 square metres to 600 square metres.
