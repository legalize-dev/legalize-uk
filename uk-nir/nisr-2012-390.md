# The Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 26th October 2012

Coming into operation: 1st November 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(3)(c) and 113(2) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2012.

#### Amendment of the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 36 of the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 (certain schemes providing average salary benefits excluded from being qualifying schemes)—

- (a) in paragraph (2) for sub-paragraph (c) substitute—

> > (c) a discretionary power may be exercised in the revaluation of such benefits,

- (b) in paragraph (4)—

- (i) in sub-paragraph (a) omit “in relation to a scheme which has members on 1st July 2012,”;

- (ii) in sub-paragraphs (b) and (c) omit “in relation to any scheme,”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 26th October 2012

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the — Department for Social Development
