# The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 6th May 2005

Coming into operation: 18th June 2005

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following By-Laws:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 18th June 2005.

#### By-Laws amended

###### 2

In the Schedule to the By-Laws made in respect of Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951[^f00002], after the entry for Malone Road there shall be added –

| MAYS MEADOW | North Side: from a point approximately 36 metres west of the south kerbline of East Bridge Street for a distance of 95 metres in a westerly direction. Carriages shall stand in a single line by the kerbline and parallel therewith. | 16 | At any time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 6th May 2005.

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the By-Laws)

These By-Laws further amend the Schedule to the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-Laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951 by making provision for a new taxi stand at Mays Meadow.
