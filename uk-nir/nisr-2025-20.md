# The Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025

Made: 6th February 2025

Coming into operation-: 28th February 2025

The Department for the Economy[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3(1), 3(2) and 8(4) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00002] makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025 and come into operation on 28th February 2025.

- (2) Regulation 2 (amendments relating to the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme) applies to the provision of support to a student in relation to designated courses beginning or continuing after 28th February 2025.

- (3) In these Regulations, “*designated course*” has the meaning given by regulation 2 of the Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

###### 2

- (1) The Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In Part 1 of the Schedule (eligible students: interpretation), in paragraph (1)—

- (a) in the definition of “person granted leave under one of the Ukraine Schemes”[^f00004], after “Ukraine Extension Scheme” insert “, a person granted leave under the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme”;

- (b) after the definition of “person granted leave under the Ukraine Family Scheme” insert—

> > - “*person granted leave under the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme*” means a person— who has leave to remain in the United Kingdom under the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme; and who has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and Islands throughout the period since the person was granted such leave;

#### Amendment of the Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024

###### 3

- (1) The Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024[^f00005] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) Before the heading ‘CHAPTER 1’, insert—

> > (1A)

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for the Economy on 6th February 2025.

Ms Louise Watson — A senior officer of the Department for the Economy

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend legislation which makes provision about student support in the form of grants in respect of fees payable in connection with attendance on courses at an institution of further education.

These Regulations come into force on 28th February 2025.

These Regulations include the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme in the definition of “person granted leave under one of the Ukraine Schemes”. A definition of “person granted leave under the Ukraine Permission Extension Scheme” is also inserted.

In addition, these Regulations amend the Further Education (Student Support) (Eligibility) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024 to insert wording confirming the Regulations to be amended.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this rule as no, or no significant impact on the private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
