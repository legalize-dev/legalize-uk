# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 26th October 2011

Coming into operation: 5th December 2011

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 5th December 2011.

#### Amendment of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

###### 2

Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00002] (fixed penalty offences) is amended as follows—

- (a) in items A1 and A2[^f00003] for “Article” substitute “Section”;

- (b) before item 1 and before the cross heading which precedes that item insert—

| ***Offences under the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons (Northern Ireland) Act 1978***[^f00004] | ***Offences under the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons (Northern Ireland) Act 1978***[^f00004] |  |

| A3 | Section 14(4BA) and (4BC)[^f00005] | Failure to produce disabled person’s badge. |
| A4 | Section 14B(1) and (3)[^f00006] | Wrongful use of disabled person’s badge. |

- (c) after item 7 insert—

| 7A | Article 79A(7)[^f00007] | Driving a taxi when it is standing or plying for hire or carrying passengers for hire with no taxi driver’s licence. |

- (d) omit item 12;

- (e) omit items 48 and 49;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 (“the 1997 Order”) by specifying additional offences committed in respect of a vehicle under the various statutory provisions detailed in the Order which are fixed penalty offences for the purposes of Part IV of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

Any liability to conviction for these offences may be discharged by the payment of a fixed penalty which is—

- (a) £30.00 for the offences set out in items to be inserted in the 1997 Order specified in Article 2(b) and (f); and

- (b) £60.00 for the offence set out in the item specified in Article 2(c),

by virtue of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.R. 2007 No. 319](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/319)).

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment has been produced and is available from Road Safety Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
