# The Meat (Official Controls Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 10th January 2007

Coming into operation: 10th January 2007

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety has been designated[^f00003] for the purposes of that section in relation to measures relating to food (including drink) including the primary production of food.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Meat (Official Controls Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 10th January 2007.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Meat (Official Controls Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 3

- (1) Schedule 2 of the Meat (Official Controls Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 [^f00005] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In paragraph 1, for “paragraphs”, substitute with “paragraph”.

- (3) In paragraph 2(3)(a), for “25th”, substitute with “31st”.

- (4) In paragraph 4, in the third entry of the first column of the table, for “aged 6 weeks or more at slaughter”, there shall be substituted “aged less than 6 weeks at slaughter”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 10th January 2007.

Don Hill — A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations correct typographical errors which appear in Schedule 2, paragraphs 1, 2 and 4 of the Meat (Official Controls Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([SR 2006 No. 454](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/454)).
