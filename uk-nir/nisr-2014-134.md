# The Police and Criminal Evidence (1989 Order) (Codes of Practice) (Revision of Codes C and H) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 8th May 2014

Coming into operation: 2nd June 2014

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 66(4) of that order, makes the following order—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police and Criminal Evidence (1989 Order) (Codes of Practice) (Revision of Codes C and H) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014, and shall come into operation on 2nd June 2014.

#### Revision of Codes of Practice

###### 2

The revised codes of practice C and H laid before the Assembly on 12th May 2014 shall come into operation on 2nd June 2014.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 8th May 2014.

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 2nd June 2014 revised codes of practice C and H issued under Article 65 of the Police and Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1989.

The amendments to Code C and Code H facilitate the transposition of EU Directive 2012/13 on the right to information in criminal proceedings.

The codes of practice are published by the Stationery Order and printed copies may be obtained from www.tsoshop.co.uk or through customer services, email: customer.service@tso.co.uk or phone 0870 600 5522. The revised codes of practice will also be available on the Department of Justice website.
