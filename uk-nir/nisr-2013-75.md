# Policing (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2013

Made: 13th March 2013

The Department of Justice makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 1(2)(b) of the Policing (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00001], and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Policing (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2013.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of article 12 of the Policing (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 is 25th March 2013.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 13th March on 2013

David Ford — Minister of Justice — Department

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation the powers of a constable under article 12 of the Policing (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 (Closure of roads, etc). These powers relate to roads, rights of way and waterways.
