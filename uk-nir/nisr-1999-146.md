# The Protection from Harassment (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 22nd March 1999

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Protection from Harassment (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 1 and 5(3) to (9) of the Protection from Harassment (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 shall be 12th April 1999.

## Signed

Adam Ingram — Minister of State — 22nd March 1999

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order appoints 12th April 1999 for the coming into operation of Articles 1 and 5(3) to (9) of the Protection from Harassment (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.
