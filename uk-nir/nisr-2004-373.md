# The Anti-social Behaviour (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 25th August 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by Article 1(2) of the Anti-social Behaviour (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Anti-social Behaviour (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Articles 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 of the Anti-social Behaviour (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall come into operation on 25th August 2004.

## Signed

Paul Murphy — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 25th August 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Commencement Order brings into operation, other than Article 6, the Anti-social Behaviour (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 on 25th August 2004.
