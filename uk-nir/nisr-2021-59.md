## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 to increase amounts of lump sums payable to people with diffuse mesothelioma or to their dependants under the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008. The increase in the amounts paid from 1st April 2021 in each case is 0.5 per cent., rounded up or down to the nearest £1 as appropriate.
