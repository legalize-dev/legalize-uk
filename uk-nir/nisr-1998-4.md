# Race Relations (Prescribed Public Bodies) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 12th January 1998

Coming into operation: 21st February 1998

The Department of Finance and Personnel, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 71(5) of the Race Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Race Relations (Prescribed Public Bodies) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 21st February 1998.

#### Prescribed Public Bodies

###### 2

The public bodies specified in the Schedule to these Regulations shall be public bodies prescribed for the purposes of Article 71(5) of the Race Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.

## SCHEDULE — **Prescribed Public Bodies**

- Northern Ireland Assembly

- The Comptroller and Auditor General

- Police Authority for Northern Ireland

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on

L. O'Reilly — Assistant Secretary — 12th January 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

Article 71(5) of the Race Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 (“the 1997 Order”) provides that nothing in that Order invalidates any rules restricting employment in the service of the Crown or by public bodies prescribed under that Article to persons of particular birth, nationality, descent or residence. The same paragraph provides that the 1997 Order does not render unlawful the publication, display or implementation of such rules or the publication of advertisements stating the gist of such rules. These Regulations prescribe certain public bodies under and for the purposes of Article 71(5) of the 1997 Order.
