# Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made: 17th July 2001

Coming into operation: 18th July 2001

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(5) of the Lands Tribunal and Compensation Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^f00001] and section 18 of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962[^f00002] and now vested in me[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 18th July 2001.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004] is hereby revoked.

#### Increases in salaries

###### 3

- (1) The annual salary payable to the President of the Lands Tribunal shall be increased as follows—

- (a) from £92,810 to £95,873 from 1st April 2000;

- (b) from £95,873 to £99,420 from 1st April 2001.

- (2) The annual salary payable to any other member of the Lands Tribunal shall be increased as follows—

- (a) from £89,306 to £92,253 from 1st April 2000;

- (b) from £92,253 to £95,666 from 1st April 2001.

## Signed

John Reid — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 17th July 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for changes in the annual salaries payable to members of the Lands Tribunal for Northern Ireland following recommendations made in the Report of the Review Body on Senior Salaries in 2000 (HMSO CM 4567) and the Report of the Review Body on Senior Salaries in 2001 (HMSO CM 4995).

It increases, with retrospective effect from 1st April 2000 under Section 18 of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, the annual salary of the President of the Lands Tribunal and all other members of the Tribunal.

It also increases with retrospective effect from 1st April 2001 the annual salary of the President of the Lands Tribunal and all other members of the Tribunal.

Article 2 revokes the Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999.
