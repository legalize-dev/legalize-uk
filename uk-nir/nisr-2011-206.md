# Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 18th May 2011

Coming into operation: 30th June 2011

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(7A)(*b*) of the Road Races (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00002] and now exercisable by it[^f00003], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These regulations may be cited as The Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 30th June 2011.

#### Charges

###### 2

The maximum charge for a spectator badge in respect of each person shall be £25.00.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 18th May 2011

D J Millar — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke and replace the Dundrod Circuit (Admission Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003.

The Regulations increase the maximum admission charge from £15.00 to £25.00.
