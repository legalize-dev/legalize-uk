# Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 29th November 2002

Coming into operation: 1st January 2003

Now, therefore, the Department in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 50(1) and (2) and Article 51(2) of the 1998 Order and now vested in it[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2003.

#### Amendment of the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00004] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 29th November 2002.

Patricia McAuley — Assistant Secretary

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000 (“the 2000 Order”) which specifies a number of persons or bodies as public authorities for certain purposes under Part VII of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 (“the 1998 Order”) and provides for the persons who are to be treated for such purposes as the employees of some of those authorities.

The Schedule to the 2000 Order lists in Part I the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 52 to 65 of the 1998 Order and in Part II the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 62 to 65 of the 1998 Order.

Article 2 of this Order substitutes a new Schedule to the 2000 Order.

The following bodies are added:

| Part I | Northern Ireland Practice and Education Council for Nursing and Midwifery Northern Ireland Social Care Council Invest Northern Ireland Royal Mail Group plc |
| --- | --- |
| Part II | Secretary of State for Work and Pensions. |

The following bodies are deleted having merged with, or been succeeded by, other bodies listed in the Order:

| Part I | Consignia plc Northern Ireland Hotel and Catering College Local Enterprise Development Unit National Board for Nursing, Midwifery and Health Visiting |
| --- | --- |
| Part II | Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food Secretary of State for Social Security. |
