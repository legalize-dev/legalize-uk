# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 24th February 1999

Coming into operation: 1st April 1999

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 5(3), 13(1), 19C(1) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and in accordance with Article 19C(3) of that Order with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00002] as regards regulation 2(3), hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1999.

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

- (1) The Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 29[^f00004] (nature of tests other than extended tests), for paragraph (6) there shall be substituted—

> > (6) In all cases the theory test shall—
>   (a) be conducted as a written examination consisting of 35 questions, the questions being in either a multiple choice or multiple response form and testing a candidate on the specified matters in accordance with Schedule 6;
>   (b) have a duration of 40 minutes or, in the circumstances specified in paragraph (7), 80 minutes.

- (3) For Schedule 2[^f00005] (licence fees) there shall be substituted—

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996.

Regulation 29 is amended so as to increase the number of questions in the theory test for categories C and D from 25 questions to 35 questions, in line with other categories.

Fees payable for licences are amended as follows—

- (a) a new fee of £12 is introduced for the grant of a provisional licence following the revocation of a licence under Article 5(1) of the Road Traffic (New Drivers) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998;

- (b) a new fee of £13.50 is introduced for the grant of a full licence in exchange for a full Community licence or an exchangeable licence;

- (c) a new fee of £8.50 for a full Group 1 licence where the applicant has previously been granted a provisional licence (previously no fee was charged);

- (d) a new fee of £8.50 for the grant of a first full licence authorising the driving of vehicles of a class included in category C where the applicant has previously been granted a provisional licence for that category (previously no fee was charged);

- (e) a new fee of £8.50 for the grant of a first full licence authorising the driving of vehicles of a class included in category D where the applicant has previously been granted a provisional licence for that category (previously no fee was charged); and

- (f) the existing fee of £6 is increased to £13.50 for a full licence granted in exchange for a full Great Britain licence which was granted under section 88 of the Road Traffic Act 1972 or section 97 of the Road Traffic Act 1988.
