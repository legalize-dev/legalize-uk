# The Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2024

Made: 19th December 2024

Coming into operation: 15th January 2025

The Department of Health, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(4) and (5) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00001] as adapted by section 7(9) of that Act and now vested in it[^f00002] and after consultation with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs in accordance with section 7(7) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2024 and shall come into operation on 15th January 2025.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

###### 2

- (1) The following amendments are made to Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00004] (which specifies Controlled Drugs to which section 7(4) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 applies).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 ([S.R. 2001 No. 431](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2001/431)) (“*the Order*”).

Regulation 2(2) adds twenty-one substances (four acyl piperazine opioids, two chemically bridged acyl piperazine derivatives, and fifteen novel benzodiazepines and related compounds) to Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Order.

Regulation 2(3) adds an additional common name and the full International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry name to the entry for methoxyphenidine, which is already included in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Order.

Regulation 2(4) adds a range of 2-benzyl benzimidazole variants to the Order.

Section 7(3) of the Misuse of Drugs Act [1971 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38) requires the Department of Health to make regulations to allow drugs controlled under that Act to be used for medicinal purposes. Section 7(3), however, does not apply to any drug which is designated by order under section 7(4) of that Act. Controlled drugs are designated where the Department of Health is of the opinion that it is in the public interest for production, supply and possession of that drug to be either wholly unlawful or unlawful except for research or other special purposes, or for medicinal use of the drug to be unlawful except under licence. Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Order specifies the list of controlled drugs to which section 7(4) applies.
