# Planning (General Development) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 3rd November 2004

Coming into operation: 29th November 2004

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 13 of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning (General Development) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 29th November 2004.

#### Amendment of the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

###### 2

- (1) The Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In Article 2(1) for the definition of the “Use Classes Order” there is substituted –

> > - “the Use Classes Order” means the Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00003].

- (3) For Part 3 of Schedule 1 there is substituted Part 3 as set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE — SUBSTITUTION FOR PART 3 OF SCHEDULE 1 TO THE PLANNING (GENERAL DEVELOPMENT) ORDER (NORTHERN IRELAND) 1993

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 3rd November 2004.

Marianne Fleming — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 (the “General Development Order”) to take account of the Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 (the “2004 Order”).

Article 2 of the General Development Order provides a definition of the Use Classes Order and describes the Planning (Use Classes) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989 (the “1989 Order”). This Order substitutes a new definition of the Use Classes Order to take account of the replacement of the 1989 Order by the 2004 Order.

Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the General Development Order describes development, for which planning permission is granted, for certain changes of use by reference to classes of use specified in the 1989 Order. This amendment substitutes a new Part 3 taking account of the new classes of use set out in the 2004 Order (Article 3).
