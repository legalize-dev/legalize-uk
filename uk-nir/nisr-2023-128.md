# The Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made: 5th September 2023

Coming into operation: 1st October 2023

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 13(5), 30(1) and 56(1) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Taxi Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2023.

#### Amendment of the Taxi Licensing Regulations

###### 2

In the Taxi Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004] for Schedule 1 substitute—

> > **SCHEDULE1**

| Column (1) Matter | Column (2) Fees |
| --- | --- |
| Application for a taxi licence | £147.00 |
| Further application for a taxi licence within 21 days | £29.50 |
| New taxi licence, plate or sign to replace one lost, accidentally defaced or destroyed | £10.00 |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 5th September 2023

Chris Hughes — A senior officer of the — Department for Infrastructure

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend Schedule 1 to the Taxi Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 to increase fees payable as follows—

- (1) Application for a taxi licence fee increased from £138.50 to £147.00; and

- (2) Further application for a taxi licence within 21 days increased from £19.50 to £29.50.

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment has been produced and are available from Safe and Accessible Travel Division, Department for Infrastructure, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online alongside this Regulation at http//www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
