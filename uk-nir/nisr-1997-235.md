# Public Order (Prescribed Form) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 30th April 1997

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 9th May 1997

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of Article 3(2) of the Public Order (Northern Ireland) Order 1987[^f00001] hereby makes the following regulations:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Order (Prescribed Form) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 9th May 1997.

#### Notice of Public Processions

###### 2

Advance notice of a public procession shall be given in the Form in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

### **Form 11/1**

## Signed

P. B. B. Mayhew — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 30th April 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulation.)

This Regulation prescribes the form for notice of a public procession as required by Article 3(2) of the Public Order (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.
