# The Charities Act 2008 (Designated Religious Charities) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 24th February 2016

Coming into operation: 1st March 2016

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 165(2)(a) of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Charities Act 2008 (Designated Religious Charities) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st March 2016.

#### Disapplication of Act in relation to designated religious charities

###### 2

Section 86 of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (persons disqualified for being trustees of a charity) does not apply to a designated religious charity.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 24th February 2016

Chris Hughes — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies section 86 of the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 as a provision which does not apply to designated religious charities.
