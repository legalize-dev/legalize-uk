# The Crown Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 29th January 2018

Laid before Parliament: 1st February 2018

Coming into operation in accordance with Rule 1

The Crown Court Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 52(1) and 53A of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001] and regulation 61 of the Criminal Justice (European Investigation Order) Regulations 2017[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Rules shall be cited as the Crown Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on the twenty-first day after the date upon which this instrument is laid before Parliament.

###### 2

In these Rules “the principal Rules” means the Crown Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00003], and a reference to a rule is a reference to that rule so numbered in the principal Rules.

#### Amendment to the Principal Rules

###### 3

In the principal Rules, after Rule 62B, insert the new Part VIIIA set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Crown Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1979 ([S.R. 1979 No. 90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1979/90)) to take account of the Criminal Justice (European Investigation Order) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/730](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/730)) which transpose [Directive 2014/41/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0041) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 April 2014 regarding the European Investigation Order in criminal matters. (OJ NO L 130, 1.5.2014, p.1).
