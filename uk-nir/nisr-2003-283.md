# Planning Applications (Exemptions from Publication) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 3rd June 2003

Coming into operation: 30th June 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 and 21(2) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning Applications (Exemptions from Publication) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 30th June 2003.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Planning Applications (Exemptions from Publication) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 3rd June 2003.

John McConnell — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Planning Applications (Exemptions from Publication) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999.
