# The Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made: 27th October 2022

Coming into force: 2nd January 2023

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12(1) of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00002], makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2022.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The appointed day for the coming into operation of –

- (a) Section 4 (powers to require removal of waste unlawfully deposited); and

- (b) Section 5 (councils to enforce Articles 4 and 5 of 1997 Order), of the Waste and Contaminated land (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 2nd January 2023.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 27th October 2022.

John Mills — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order brings into operation sections 4 and 5 of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 on 2nd January 2023.
