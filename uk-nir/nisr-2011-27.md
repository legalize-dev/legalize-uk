# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 3rd February 2011

Coming into operation: 28th February 2011

In accordance with section 56(1), (2) and (5) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00004] the Department of Finance and Personnel consents to the making of these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 28th February 2011.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 3

The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 are amended in accordance with regulations 4 and 5.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations replace Part I of Schedule 2 and Schedule 6 to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 401](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/401)). The new Schedules incorporate a number of new and amended pieces of EU legislation that have come into force since those Regulations came into operation.

An Impact Assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on private, voluntary or public sectors is foreseen.
