# The Pensions (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 6) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 3rd February 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 6) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 55 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (Article 51: end of annual increase in guaranteed minimum pensions) is 4th February 1997.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

John O'Neill — Assistant Secretary — 3rd February 1997.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of Article 55 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 on 4th February 1997.
