# Fisheries (Amendment No. 2) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 27th November 2008

Coming into operation: 1st January 2009

The Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland, with the approval of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure [^f00001], makes the following Byelaws in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 26(1), 37 and 115(1)(b) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 [^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Byelaws may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment No. 2) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2009.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

The Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003 [^f00003] are amended in accordance with byelaws 3 and 4.

#### List of waters where there is no close season for rainbow trout

###### 4

In Schedule 6 in the list of rainbow trout waters where there is no annual close season for angling for rainbow trout —

- (a) under the heading County Antrim insert before the reference to “Cairn Farm Fly Fishery, Carrickfergus” a reference to “Beechgrove Fisheries, Cullybackey”;

- (b) under the heading County Antrim insert before the reference to “North Woodburn Reservoir” a reference to “Murloc Angling Centre, Aghalee”.

#### Revocation

###### 5

Byelaw 3 of the Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004] is revoked.

(This note is not part of the Byelaws)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Byelaws amend the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003.

The principal changes are as follows:–

1 The Byelaws increase the licence duties payable from 1st January 2009 for some fishing rod licences and some commercial fishing engines used for the taking of salmon and freshwater fish other than eels, together with the fee payable for a licence which authorises the holder to buy and sell salmon, trout and eels (Byelaw 3). 2 The Byelaws designate two waters as rainbow trout waters for the purposes of Byelaw 73(2)(b) of the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003 which provides that there shall be no close season for angling for rainbow trout in waters so designated (Byelaw 4).

3 The Byelaws revoke Byelaw 3 of the Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2007 which amended licence fees for 2008 (Byelaw 5).
