# Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stages 1 and 2) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 2nd September 1997

Coming into operation: 30th September 1997

The Department of Education in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 7(1)(*b*), (2)(*a*)(ii) and (6) of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stages 1 and 2) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997, and shall come into operation on 30th September 1997.

#### Amendment of the Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stages 1 and 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

The Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stages 1 and 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] shall be amended as provided in regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

In Article 2 (Application) for paragraph (2) there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

> > (2) This Order shall not have effect in relation to a pupil in respect of whom a board is maintaining a statement of special educational needs under Article 16 of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00003] and who, on the making of an assessment of his special educational needs, has been assessed by that board under Article 15 of that Order as having severe learning difficulties.

###### 4

In Article 3 (Interpretation) for the definition of “Assessment Document” there shall be substituted—

> > - “Assessment Document” means the document published by the Stationery Office entitled “Assessment Arrangements in Key Stages 1 and 2”[^f00004];

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on

C. Jendoubi — Assistant Secretary — 2nd September 1997.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Education (Assessment Arrangements for Key Stages 1 and 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 (“the principal Order”).

The Order makes the following changes—

Article 3 amends Article 2(2) of the principal Order to take account of the making of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

Article 4 amends the definition of “the Assessment Document” to take account of the publication of a revised document.
