# The Civil Legal Services (Remuneration) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 9th November 2016

Coming into operation: 1st December 2016

In accordance with Article 47(1) of the 2003 Order, the Department has had regard to the matters specified in that provision.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Civil Legal Services (Remuneration) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st December 2016.

#### Amendments to the Civil Legal Services (Remuneration) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

###### 2

Schedule 3 to the Civil Legal Services (Remuneration) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00003] is amended as follows.

###### 3

In Part 3, in the heading above Table 10, after “Proceedings under Part 13A of the Prison and Young Offenders Centre Rules (Northern Ireland) 1995”, insert —

> > **Proceedings under paragraph 2, 6 or 7 of Schedule 3 to the Human Trafficking and Exploitation (Criminal Justice and Support for Victims) Act (Northern Ireland) 2015**[^f00004]
> **Proceedings under section 57, 60 or 61 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015**[^f00005]

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 9th November 2016.

Claire Sugden — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Civil Legal Services (Remuneration) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 by inserting new provisions to provide that remuneration is available under civil legal services for representation in those proceedings referred to in Articles 3.

The Order comes into operation on 1st December 2016.
