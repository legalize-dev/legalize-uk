# The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004

Made: 7th October 2004

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 19 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2004.

###### 2

Section 10 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 shall come into force on 29th October 2004.

## Signed

Paul Murphy — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 7th October 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into force section 10 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act [2004 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/4) on 29th October 2004.
