# The General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 20th April 2015

Coming into operation: 20th May 2015

The Department of Education makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 34 of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 20th May 2015.

#### Amendment of the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

###### 2

In regulation 7 (appointed members) of the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00002] in paragraph (b), for sub-paragraph (i) substitute—

> > (i) the Education Authority;

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 20th April 2015.

Faustina Graham — A senior officer of the — Department of Education

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 to replace a reference to the Association of Education and Library Boards as an appointing body with a reference to the Education Authority.
