# The Deposits in the Sea (Exemptions) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 18th January 2008

Coming into operation: 8th February 2008

The Depart of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7(1) and (2) and 25(3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Deposits in the Sea (Exemptions) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 8th February 2008.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] shall apply to the Order as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment to Deposits in the Sea (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

###### 2

- (1) Article 4 of the Deposits in the Sea (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00003] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph 1(a)(ii), the word “and” shall be deleted.

- (3) In paragraph (1)(b), for “interest.” there shall be substituted “interest; and”.

- (4) After paragraph 1(b), there shall be inserted the following sub-paragraph—

> > (c) that operation does not involve the recovery or disposal of hazardous waste as defined by regulations 2(1) and 6 of the Hazardous Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004].

#### Revocations

###### 3

The Deposits in the Sea (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18th January 2008.

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Deposits in the Sea (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995 exempts specified operations from the licensing requirements of Part II of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00006]. In order to comply with the Hazardous Waste Directive it is necessary to exempt hazardous waste operations covered by Article 3 of the Hazardous Waste Directive from the Deposits in the Sea (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995.

The Statutory Order will amend the Deposits in the Sea (Exemptions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995 by restricting the prescribed operations to ensure that they do not involve the recovery or disposal of hazardous waste as defined by regulations 2(1) and 6 of the Hazardous Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005.
