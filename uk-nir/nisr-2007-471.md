# Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 9th November 2007

Coming into operation: 1st January 2008

The Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland, makes the following Byelaws, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 26(1), 37, 51(2), 52(2), 70(1) and (2), 71(2)(g), 72(1), 89, 95, 97, 114(1)(b) and 115(1)(b) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00001], and with the approval of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure[^f00002]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Byelaws may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2008.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

The Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00003] shall be amended as provided in Byelaws 3 to 7.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Byelaws)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Byelaws amend the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003.

The principal changes are as follows:–

1 The Byelaws increase the licence duties payable from 1st January 2008 for some fishing rod licences and some commercial fishing engines used for the taking of salmon and freshwater fish other than eels, together with the fee payable for a licence which authorises the holder to buy and sell salmon, trout and eels (Byelaw 3). The new licence fee rates for 2008 and the size of the increases are set out below:– *Previous* *Rate* *New* *Rate* Increase£££ Season game fishing rod licence16.0016.500.50Fourteen day game fishing rod licence8.008.500.50Three day game fishing rod licence3.503.500.00Concessionary season fishing rod licence5.005.000.00Juvenile season fishing rod licence2.002.000.00Amount payable by the holder of a current Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights game fishing rod and line licence for a game fishing rod licence 1.001.000.00Season coarse fishing rod licence16.0016.500.50Fourteen day coarse fishing rod licence8.008.000.00Three day coarse fishing rod licence for use by the holder of a three day coarse fishing permit issued by the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure to fish in waters in which the Department has the fishing rights. 3.503.500.00Licence for a draft net for the capture of salmon and freshwater fish on Lough Neagh119.50125.005.50Licence for a single wall set net or trammel net for the capture of trout or rainbow trout in Lough Neagh per 91 metres (100 yards)16.0016.500.50Licence for a single wall set net or trammel net for the taking of freshwater fish other than trout or rainbow trout per 91 metres (100 yards)16.0016.500.50Licence for a bait net for the taking of freshwater fish other than trout or rainbow trout for use as bait – per net5.005.000.00Licence for a fixed tidal draft net378.50395.0016.50Licence for a tidal draft net314.00328.0014.00Drift net licence for the taking of salmon in the sea314.00328.0014.00Licence for a bag net used in tidal waters709.00740.0031.00Dealer’s licence46.5048.502.00

2 The Byelaws designate a water as a rainbow trout water for the purposes of Byelaw 73(2)(b) of the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003 which provides that there shall be no close season for angling for rainbow trout in waters so designated (Byelaw 4).

3 The Byelaws provide that the conditions under which an angling licence is issued and the format of an angling licence are amended to include an entry for the holder’s date of birth (Byelaws 5 and 6).

4 The Byelaws revoke Byelaw 3 of the Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland)
