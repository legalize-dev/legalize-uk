# The Food (Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 15th January 2019

Coming into operation: 11th February 2019

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00005] as last amended by Regulation (EU) 2017/745 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00006].

## Part 1 — Introductory

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food (Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and come into operation on 11th February 2019.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00007] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to a number of pieces of secondary legislation relating to food and feed.

Regulation 13 amends the Food Hygiene Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/3)) to amend the definition of Regulation [(EC) 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0852) on the hygiene of foodstuffs (OJ No L 139, 30.4.2004, p. 1) so as to require that Regulation to be read with new [Commission Regulation (EU) No 2017/2158](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2158/2017) establishing mitigation measures and benchmark levels for the reduction of the presence of acrylamide in food (OJ No L 304, 21.11.2017, p. 24).

Regulation 27 amends the Fruit Juices and Fruit Nectars Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 ([S.R. 2013 No. 253](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2013/253)) to implement Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1040/2014 amending Council [Directive 2001/112/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0112) relating to fruit juices and certain similar products intended for human consumption to adapt its Annex I to technical progress (OJ No L 288, 2.10.2014, p. 1). This allows plant proteins from wheat, peas and potatoes to be used for the clarification of products to which the Fruit Juices and Fruit Nectars Regulations (Northern Ireland 2013 apply.

These Regulations make other miscellaneous minor amendments to legislation relating to food and feed, in particular amending out of date references to domestic legislation and to EU instruments.
