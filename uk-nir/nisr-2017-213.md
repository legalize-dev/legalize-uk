# The Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment) 2017

Made: 4th October 2017

Coming into operation: 8th January 2018

The Northern Ireland Court of Judicature Rules Committee[^f00001] makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 55 and 55A of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Rules shall be cited as the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment) 2017.

- (2) These Rules shall come into operation on 8th January 2018 and shall apply to proceedings lodged on or after that date.

- (3) In these Rules “the principal Rules” means the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980[^f00003]; and an Order or rule referred to by number means the Order or rule so numbered in the principal Rules.

#### Amendment to the principal Rules

###### 2

In Order 53, rule 4(1) of the principal Rules, omit “promptly and in any event”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980 ([S.R. 1980 No. 346](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1980/346)) to remove the requirement of promptitude from the time limit for making an application for leave to apply for judicial review so that the time limit is three months from the date grounds for the application first arose.
