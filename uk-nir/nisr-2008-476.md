## SCHEDULE

| *Fishing Engine* *(1)* | *Licence Duty* *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| Fyke net | £8.50 |
| The existing eel weir at Toome | £1770.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Upper Row, Portna | £1770.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Lower Row, Portna | £1770.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Belleek | £57.00 |
| Long line used in the Erne Catchment Area or Lough Neagh, excluding its tributaries and their branches, containing not more than 1,200 hooks | £93.00 |
| Long line used in the Erne Catchment Area or Lough Neagh, excluding its tributaries and their branches, containing more than 1,200 hooks | £129.00 |
| Eel draft net not exceeding 118 metres in length used in Lough Neagh | £129.00 |
