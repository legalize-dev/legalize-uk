# Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 29th October 2012

Coming into operation: 1st December 2012

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure , makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(1) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 and now vested in it .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and come into operation on 1st December 2012.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

The Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 are amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

#### Annual close season for commercial fishing

###### 3

In regulation 82(1) for “the period beginning on 17th March in any year and ending on 17th December in that year, both dates inclusive” substitute “ the period beginning on 1st March in any year and ending on 30th November in that year, both dates inclusive ”.

#### Rainbow trout waters where there is no close season for angling for rainbow trout

###### 4

In Schedule 6, under the heading County Down and immediately before the entry relating to Lough Creevy, insert “ Cleland's Lake, Crossgar ”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure on 29th October 2012.

M. Cory — A senior officer of the — Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure
