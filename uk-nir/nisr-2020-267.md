# The Food Hygiene Rating Act (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 17th November 2020

Coming into operation: 23rd December 2020

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00004], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Food Hygiene Rating Act (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 23rd December 2020.

#### Amendment to the Food Hygiene Rating Act (Northern Ireland) 2016

###### 2

- (1) The Food Hygiene Rating Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00005] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In section 1(5) (food hygiene rating) for “Regulation [(EC) 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882)” substitute “Regulation (EU) 2017/625”.

- (3) In section 2(2) (notification and publication) for “Regulation [(EC) 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882)” substitute “Regulation [(EC) 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178)”.

- (4) In section 17(1) (interpretation etc.)—

- (a) in the definition of “food”—

for “Regulation [(EC) 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882)” substitute “Regulation [(EC) 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178)”;

- (b) after the definition of “operator” insert—

> > - “Regulation [(EC) 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178)” means Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 28 January 2002 laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety (as amended from time to time),

- (c) for the definition of “Regulation [(EC) 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882)” substitute—

> > - “Regulation (EU) 2017/625” means Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 March 2017 on official controls and other official activities performed to ensure ## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Food Hygiene Rating Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 (“the Act”) by updating references to the repealed Regulation [(EC) 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) in the Act with references to Regulation (EU) 2017/625 (rules that replaced Regulation [(EC) 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882)) or Regulation [(EC) 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) (for definitions of “operator” and “food”).

An impact assessment has not been produced for this Statutory Rule as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
