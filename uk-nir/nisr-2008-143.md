# Donaghadee (Harbour Area) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 27th March 2008

Affirmed by resolution of the Assembly: 17th June 2008

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

In accordance with section 1(6) of that Act the Department has consulted with the Donaghadee Harbour Commissioners and other harbour authority or local authority which the Department considered likely to be affected by the Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Donaghadee (Harbour Area) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on the day after that on which it is affirmed by resolution of the Assembly.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order—

- “the Commissioners” means the Commissioners appointed under the Donaghadee Harbour Act 1820[^f00004];

- “the level of high water” means the level of mean high water springs.

#### Harbour Area

###### 3

- (1) The Commissioners shall exercise their functions within the harbour of Donaghadee, being the area shown on the map bounded by—

- (a) a straight line drawn from reference point A (Latitude 54° 38.68 North, Longitude 05° 32.28 West) (Shore Street Baths) to reference point B (Latitude 54° 39.00 North, Longitude 05° 31.75 West);

- (b) a straight line drawn from reference point B (Latitude 54° 39.00 North, Longitude 05° 31.75 West) to reference point C (Latitude 54° 38.78 North, Longitude 05° 31.30 West);

- (c) a straight line drawn from reference point C (Latitude 54° 38.78 North, Longitude 05° 31.30 West) to reference point D (Latitude 54° 38.52 North, Longitude 05° 31.70 West) (Dormans Point);

- (d) and on its landward sides by the level of high water from reference point D (Latitude 54° 38.52 North, Longitude 05° 31.70 West) (Dormans Point), extending in a westerly direction to reference point A (Latitude 54° 38.68 North, Longitude 05° 32.28 West) (Shore Street Baths);

together with all docks, landing places and other works and land of the Commissioners for the time being held by them for the purposes of or in connection with the harbour of Donaghadee.

- (2) In this Article “the map” means the map marked “Donaghadee (Harbour Area) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008” which has been signed and sealed by the Department with reference to this Order and deposited at the office of the Department for Regional Development at Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 27th March 2008.

B. R. D. White — A senior officer of the — Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order fixes the area at Donaghadee Harbour within which the powers of the Harbour Commissioners may be exercised.

A map showing the harbour area is available for inspection at any reasonable hour at the office of the Department for Regional Development, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB.
