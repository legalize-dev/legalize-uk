# The Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 12th March 2015

Coming into operation: 14th April 2015

The Department intends the accounts under section 14 of the Act for the financial year ending with 31st March 2015 to relate in part to those bodies.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Whole of Government Accounts (Designation of Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 14th April 2015.

#### Designation of Bodies

###### 2

For the purposes of section 15 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001, the Department designates the bodies listed in the Schedule in respect of the financial year ending with 31st March 2015.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates bodies, for the financial year ending with 31st March 2015, for the purposes of section 15 of the Government Resources and Accounts Act (Northern Ireland) 2001. The Department of Finance and Personnel (“the Department”) intends to pass the information provided by bodies so designated in the Schedule to this Order to HM Treasury for use in the preparation of the Whole of Government Accounts (WGA). The designation enables the Department to require those bodies to provide financial information in relation to the year in question. The bodies designated include the District Councils for the 11 Local Government Districts established pursuant to the Local Government (Boundaries) Act (NorthernIreland) 2008.

WGA are financial statements prepared on the basis of Generally Accepted Accounting Practice (GAAP) which will cover the whole of the public sector as far as practically possible.
