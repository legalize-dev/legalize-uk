# The Food Hygiene (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 5th November 2014

Coming into operation: 13th December 2014

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00004] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of the following Regulations.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Food Hygiene (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and come into operation on 13th December 2014.

#### Amendments to the Food Hygiene Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Food Hygiene Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00005] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In Regulation 17 (offences and penalties)—

- (a) for paragraph 3 substitute—

> > (3) A person guilty of an offence under—
>   (a) regulation 15 shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both.
>   (b) Schedule 6 paragraph 1(2) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale

- (3) In Schedule 1 (definitions of EU legislation) for the definition of “Regulation 2075/2005” substitute-

> > - “Regulation 2075/2005” means [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 2075/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2075) laying down specific rules on official controls for *Trichinella* in meat as last amended in respect of its articles by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 216/2014.

[^f00006]

- (4) In Schedule 6 (restrictions on the sale of raw milk intended for direct human consumption)—

- (a) for paragraph 1 substitute,—

> > (1) Any person who sells raw milk intended for direct human consumption in contravention of—
>   (1) paragraph 5, or
>   (2) paragraph 7A (1) or (2), shall be guilty of an offence

- (b) after paragraph 7, insert the following type=start slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915017521paragraphtype=end slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915017521—

> > (7A)
>   (1) Subject to type=start slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915033053sub-paragraph (3)type=end slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915033053 and except in cases to which type=start slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915045412sub-paragraph (2)type=end slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915045412 applies the container in which any raw milk is sold shall be marked or labelled “This milk has not been heat-treated and may therefore contain organisms harmful to health”.
>   (2) Subject to type=start slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915060975sub-paragraph (3)type=end slip=2014-11-01 time=1416915060975, in the case of any raw milk which is not prepacked and is sold at a catering establishment, the words “Milk supplied in this establishment has not been heat-treated and may therefore contain organisms harmful to health” must appear—
>     (a) on a label attached to the container in which that milk is sold, or
>     (b) on a ticket or notice that is readily discernible by an intending purchaser at the place where the purchaser chooses that milk.

- (ii) before the definition of “occupier”, insert—

> > - “labelling”, in relation to a food, includes any words, particulars, trade mark, brand name, pictorial matter or symbol relating to the food and appearing on the packaging of the food or on any document, notice, label, ring or collar accompanying the food;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations implement Commission Regulation (EU) No. 216/2014 amending Regulation [(EC) No. 2075/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2075) laying down specific rules on *Trichinella* in meat (regulation 2(3)).

These Regulations make amendments to the Food Hygiene Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 to make continuing provision for the labelling of raw milk intended for direct human consumption with prescribed information relating to the absence of heat treatment (regulation 2(2) and (4)).
