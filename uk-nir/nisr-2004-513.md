# Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme (Confirmation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 16th December 2004

Coming into operation: 31st January 2005

NOW THEREFORE the Department in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(1) and (2) of the Act and now vested in it[^f00004], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme (Confirmation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

#### Confirmation of Scheme

###### 2

The Department hereby confirms without modification the 2004 Scheme set out in the Schedule.

#### Date of Operation of Scheme

###### 3

The 2004 Scheme set out in the Schedule shall be put into operation on and from 31st January 2005.

## SCHEDULE — LOUGH NEAGH (LEVELS) SCHEME 2004

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 2004.

###### 2

The Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1955[^f00005] as supplemented and modified by the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1995[^f00006] shall have effect with the substitution of the following for Article 5, that is to say:

> > (5) In this Scheme references to the level of the Lough shall be construed as references to the mean level of the Lough as recorded on the system maintained by the Department at Toome on the northern shore of the Lough and at Oxford Island on the southern shore.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 16th December 2004.

J. R. Hagan — Senior Officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order confirms and brings into operation on 31st January 2005 the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 2004 (“the 2004 Scheme”) which supplements and modifies the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1955 (“the 1955 Scheme”) (scheduled to the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1955 (Operative Date) Order (Northern Ireland) 1958) as supplemented and modified by the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1995, (scheduled to the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme (Confirmation) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999). The 2004 Scheme substitutes a new Article for Article 5 of the 1955 Scheme so that the mean level of the Lough shall now be the mean level as recorded on the systems maintained by the Department at Toome and Oxford Island on the northern and southern shores respectively of the Lough. The reference to Derryadd on the southern shore shall no longer apply.
