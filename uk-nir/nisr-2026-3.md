# The Further Education (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made: 8th January 2026

Coming into operation: 27th February 2026

The Department for the Economy makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 19(7) and 24(6) of the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Further Education (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026 and comes into operation on 27th February 2026.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order, the references to the 1997 Order are to the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.

#### Financial year for institutions of further education

###### 3

In Article 19(6) of the 1997 Order for “31st July” substitute “31st March”.

#### Transitional provisions

###### 4

- (1) The period starting with 1st August 2025 for a statement of accounts required under Article 19 of the 1997 Order ends on 31st March 2026 (and any annual amounts in the statement of accounts are to be pro rated accordingly).

- (2) A development plan submitted under Article 20(1) of the 1997 Order before the coming into operation of Article 3 of this Order, in respect of a period ending in a year after 2025, need not be revised in consequence of the amendment in Article 3.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for the Economy on 8th January 2026.

Kathryn McCamley — A senior officer of the Department for the Economy

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This is the first time that the enabling power under 19(7) has been used to amend the Further Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 to change the financial year of the Further Education Colleges.

Article 3 amends Article 19(6) to change the ending of the financial year from the period of 12 months ending on 31st July to the period of 12 months ending on 31st March, the same as the financial year of the Department for the Economy.

Article 4 provides the transitional provisions for the requirements relating to the statement of accounts and the development plan.
