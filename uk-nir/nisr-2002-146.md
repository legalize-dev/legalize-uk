# The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002

Made: 28th March 2002

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 79 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2002.

###### 2

The provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 15th April 2002.

| Section 14 | Establishment of district policing partnerships |
| Section 15 | Default of council |
| Section 16 | General functions of district policing partnerships |
| Section 17 | Annual report by district policing partnership to council |
| Section 18 | Reports by district policing partnership to Board |
| Section 19 | Code of practice for district policing partnerships |
| Section 21 | District policing partnership sub-groups for Belfast |

| Section 23 | Other community policing arrangements |
| Section 57(2)(i) and (j) | Annual and other reports by the Board |
| Section 72 | Video recording of interviews |
| Section 78 for the purpose of the provisions of Schedules 6 and 7 mentioned below | Amendment, transitional and transitory provisions and repeals |
| Paragraph 14 of Schedule 6 | Amendements |
| Paragraph 3 of Schedule 7 | Transitional and Transitory Provisions |

## Signed

John Reid — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 28th March 2002

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Commencement Order, which is the fourth to be made under the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000, brings into force on 15th April 2002 those provisions of the Act set out in the Schedule to the Order.
