# Motor Vehicles (Payments in Respect of Applicants for Exemption from Wearing Seat Belts) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 2nd April 1996

Coming into operation: 20th May 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 25(3) and 110(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Payments in Respect of Applicants for Exemption from Wearing Seat Belts) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 20th May 1996.

#### Amendment of Article 25 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995

###### 2

In Article 25 (payments in respect of applicants for exemption from wearing seat belts) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995, in paragraph (2)(b), after the words “income support,” there shall be inserted the words “an income-based jobseeker’s allowance within the meaning of the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995,”[^f00002].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on .

Trevor Pearson — Assistant Secretary — 2nd April 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends Article 25 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 so as to enable the Department to make payments in respect of an applicant for exemption from wearing seat belts who is either in receipt of an income-based jobseeker’s allowance paid under the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 or is the dependant of such a person.
