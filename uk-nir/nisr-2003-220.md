# Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (Application of Article 112B to Adoptions from Overseas) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 7th April 2003

Coming into operation: 8th April 2003

The Department for Employment and Learning[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 112B(8) of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations –

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (Application of Article 112B to Adoptions from Overseas) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 8th April 2003.

- (2) In these Regulations –

- “adoption from overseas” means the adoption of a child who enters Northern Ireland from outside the United Kingdom in connection with or for the purposes of adoption which does not involve the placement of the child for adoption under the law of any part of the United Kingdom.”;

- “the Order” means the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

#### Application of Article 112B of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 to adoptions from overseas

###### 2

Article 112B of the Order shall have effect in relation to adoptions from overseas with the modifications of the provisions specified in the first column of the Schedule that are set out opposite those provisions in the second column of that Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

Article 112B of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 contains the powers under which the Department may make regulations entitling an employee to paternity leave. These Regulations, made under Article 112B(8) of that Order, provide for Article 112B to have effect, with modifications, in relation to an adoption from overseas. This means the adoption of a child who enters Northern Ireland from outside the United Kingdom in connection with or for the purposes of an adoption which does not involve the placement of the child for adoption under the law of any part of the United Kingdom.

Where Article 112B refers to the placement of a child, it is modified by these Regulations to refer to the entry of the child into Northern Ireland. Where Article 112B refers to the person with whom a child is placed, it is modified to refer to the child’s adopter.
