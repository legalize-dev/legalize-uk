# The Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 11th June 2014

Coming into operation: 2nd July 2014

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2 and 3(1) of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002]

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014 and comes into operation on 2nd July 2014.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 2

The Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00003] is amended in accordance with article 3.

#### Diabrotica virgifera Le Conte

###### 3

- (1) Part 7A[^f00004] (management zones for plant health purposes) is omitted.

- (2) In Part B (plant pests known to occur in the European Community) of Schedule 1, item 1a[^f00005] under the heading “Insects, mites and nematodes” is omitted.

- (3) In article 44(1)(a) (offences), head (xiiiA)[^f00006] is omitted.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 11th June 2014

Malcolm Beatty — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No.82](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/82)) (“the principal Order”).

Article 3 removes the provisions in the principal Order in relation to the organism *Diabrotica virgifera*Le Conte so as to implement—

(a) Commission implementing Directive 2014/19/EU amending Annex I to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community (OJ No L 38, 7.2.2014, p.30); and

(b) Commission Implementing Decision 2014/62/EU repealing Decision [2003/766/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0766) on emergency measures to prevent the spread within the Community of *Diabrotica virgifera* Le Conte (OJ No L 38, 7.2.2014, p.45).
