# The Rates (Social Sector Value) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 6th January 2009

Affirmed by resolution of the Assembly on: 2nd February 2009

Coming into operation: 1st April 2009

The Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 23A of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Rates (Social Sector Value) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2009.

#### Amendment of the Rates (Social Sector Value) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

For Schedule 1 to the Rates (Social Sector Value) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — Schedule substituted for Schedule 1 to the Rates (Social Sector Value) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 6th January 2009

Brian McClure — A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Regulations amend the Rates (Social Sector Value) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 (“the 2007 Regulations”) by replacing Schedule 1 in those Regulations with the Schedule to these Regulations. This substitution provides for a change in the number and composition of prescribed registered housing associations to which the 2007 Regulations apply (regulation 2).
