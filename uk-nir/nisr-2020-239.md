# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 12) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: at 5.30 p.m. on 3rd November 2020

Laid before the Assembly: at 9.00 a.m. on 4th November 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1(2)

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 12) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at the time and on the date they are made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

###### 3

- (1) In schedule 2, at the end of paragraph 5(2)(c) omit “or” and in paragraph 5(2)(d) after “no more than 15 persons” insert—

> > ; or
> (e) Physical education delivered by schools, pre-schools and other education providers.

- (2) In paragraph 8(7) of schedule 2, for “is made available” substitute “, tables or other facilities are made available”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 5.30 pm on 3rd November 2020

Dr. Naresh Chada — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

Regulation 3(1) provides for physical education to take place in schools and other educational settings. Regulation 3(2) provides that tables or other facilities in an area adjacent to hospitality premises are to be treated as part of the premises.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
