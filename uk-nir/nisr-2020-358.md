# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 25) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: at 7.00 p.m. on 29th December 2020

Laid before the Assembly: at 8.00 p.m. on 29th December 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1(2)

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 25) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at the time and on the date they are made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) After paragraph (2)(c) of Regulation 4C insert—

> > (d) Taxi or vehicle hire businesses.

- (3) In paragraph (1)(c) of Regulation 4DA, omit “in response to orders placed”.

- (4) In the heading to Regulation 4DB, for “licenced” substitute “unlicenced”.

- (5) In paragraph (1)(c) of Regulation 4DB, omit “in response to orders placed”.

- (6) In Regulation 4DD, omit “in response to orders placed”.

- (7) In Regulation 5B (Restrictions on sporting events), in paragraph (2) omit sub-paragraph (c), and in paragraph 3 omit sub-paragraphs (c) and (d).

- (8) In paragraph (1) of Regulation 6C, after “apply to a person P” insert “during the period ending at 23:59 on 1 January 2021”.

- (9) For Schedule 2 substitute–

> > **SCHEDULE 2**
>   - “Close contact service” means— hairdressing or barbering; provision of beauty or aesthetics treatments including treatments in relation to nails or makeup; tattooing; tanning; services in spas; sports and massage therapy; well-being and holistic therapies; dress fitting, tailoring and fashion design (unless social distance is maintained); body piercing; electrolysis; training in any of the services listed at (a) to (j) (unless social distancing is maintained); driving instruction for the test of competence to drive a vehicle other than a motorcycle established by Article 5 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.”

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 24) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 3

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 24) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020( ) are amended in accordance with this Regulation.

- (2) Regulation 26 is revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 7.00 pm on 29th December 2020

Dr Lourda Geoghegan — A Senior Officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 by permitting taxi businesses to operate after 8.00 pm in the period up to 2 January 2021. They limit the operating hours of businesses selling food and drink. They provide that the power to require people to return home operates only up to 2 January 2021. The remaining Regulations correct errors in previous regulations.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
