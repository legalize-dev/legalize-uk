# Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 27th October 2010

Coming into operation: 29th November 2010

The Department for Employment and Learning[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 67F of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 29th November 2010.

#### Amendment

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00005] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No. 401](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/401)) by replacing the Schedule to that Order.

It adds to the list of prescribed persons: the Charity Commission for Northern Ireland; the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure; and the Financial Reporting Council Limited and its operating bodies (the Professional Oversight Board, the Financial Reporting Review Panel and the Accountancy and Actuarial Discipline Board).

It makes amendments to the description of matters for the Criminal Cases Review Commission, the Department for Agriculture and Rural Development, the Department for Social Development, the Director of the Serious Fraud Office, the Northern Ireland Authority for Utility Regulation and the Pensions Regulator.

The reference to the Chairman of the Criminal Cases Review Commission has been amended to read Criminal Cases Review Commission. The reference to the Northern Ireland Authority for Energy Regulation has been amended to read Northern Ireland Authority for Utility Regulation. The reference to the Registrar of Credit Unions for Northern Ireland has been amended to read the Registrar of Credit Unions and Industrial and Provident Societies.

The Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland has been removed from the list of prescribed persons as it was abolished in 2009.
