# Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 15th June 2006

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 7(3) of theSchedule to the Northern Ireland Act 2000

Coming into operation: 24th July 2006

The Department of Finance and Personnel, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(5) of the Lands Tribunal and Compensation Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^f00001] and section 18 of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962[^f00002] and now vested in it [^f00003] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 24th July 2006.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004] is revoked.

#### Increases in salaries

###### 3

- (1) The annual salary payable to the President of the Lands Tribunal shall be increased as follows –

- (a) from £116,515 to £117,680 from 1st April 2006;

- (b) from £117,680 to £120,300 from 1st November 2006.

- (2) The annual salary payable to any other member of the Lands Tribunal shall be increased as follows —

- (a) from £112,116 to £113,237 from 1st April 2006;

- (b) from £113,237 to £114,400 from 1st November 2006.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 15th June 2006.

Christopher Thompson — A senior officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for changes in the annual salaries payable to members of the Lands Tribunal for Northern Ireland following recommendations made in the Report of the Review Body on Senior Salaries in 2006 (HMSO CM 6727).

Its increases, with effect from 1st April 2006, under section 18 of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, the annual salary of the President of the Lands Tribunal and all other members of the Tribunal.

The increases take effect in two stages. The first increases apply in relation to the period from 1st April 2006 to 31st October 2006. The salaries are then further increased with effect from 1st November 2006.

Article 2 revokes the Lands Tribunal (Salaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.
