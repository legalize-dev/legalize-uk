# The Green Park Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 27th August 1998

Coming into operation: 1st September 1998

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 10(1) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, having consulted the persons or bodies referred to in Article 10(2) of that Order, hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Green Park Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 1st September 1998.

#### Amendment to the Green Park Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992

###### 2

In Article 3(2) of the Green Park Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00002] the words “Belvoir Park Hospital, Hospital Road, Belfast BT8 8JR;” shall be omitted.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

John McGrath — Assistant Secretary — 27th August 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Green Park Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992. It removes from the Trust’s functions the ownership and management of Belvoir Park Hospital with effect from 1st September 1998.
