# The Health and Safety (Miscellaneous Repeals, Revocations and Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made: 18th November 2026

Coming into operation: 21st December 2026

It appears to the Department that: the revocation of the first four Instruments listed in Table 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations is expedient for the purposes of Article 54(1) of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978; and it is not appropriate to consult bodies in respect of such revocation in accordance with Article 54(5) of that Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Safety (Miscellaneous Repeals, Revocations and Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 21st December 2026.

#### Repeals and revocations

###### 2

- (1) The Acts specified in column 1 of Table 1 of the Schedule are repealed to the extent specified in the corresponding entry in column 3 of that Table.

- (2) The Instruments specified in column 1 of Table 2 of the Schedule are revoked to the extent specified in the corresponding entry in column 3 of that Table.

#### Amendments to the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

###### 3

- (1) The Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00008] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) in the definition of “dangerous goods”, for the words “means any goods which fall within the definition of “dangerous goods” in regulation 2(1) of CDG 2010”, substitute “has the meaning as assigned by regulation 2(3) of CDG 2010”.

- (3) In Schedule 2, Part IV, paragraph 48K(2) for the words “has the same meaning as in regulation 2(1) of CDG 2010”, substitute “has the meaning as assigned by regulation 2(3) of CDG 2010”.

#### Amendment to the Biocidal Products and Chemicals (Appointment of Authorities and Enforcement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

###### 4

- (1) In paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Biocidal Products and Chemicals (Appointment of Authorities and Enforcement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00009] in the definition of “Plant protection product” for the reference “94/414/EEC” substitute “91/414/EEC”.

#### Amendments to the Control of Major Accident Hazards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

###### 5

- (1) The Control of Major Accident Hazards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00010] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1) omit the definition of “pipelines”.

- (3) In regulation 3 (application and exceptions), in paragraph (2) after sub-paragraph (c) insert—

> > (ca) the transport of dangerous substances in pipelines, including associated apparatus, except where that activity takes place at a site which is an establishment despite that activity;
> (cb) the transport of dangerous substances and directly related intermediate temporary storage by road, rail, internal waterways, sea or air, including loading and unloading and transport to and from another means of transport at docks, wharves or marshalling yards, except where that activity takes place at a site which is an establishment despite that activity;

- (4) In regulation 24(3)(c) (domino effects and domino groups) for the words “the Planning (Hazardous Substances) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015”, substitute “the Planning (Hazardous Substances) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015”[^f00011].

#### Amendment to the Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

###### 6

- (1) In regulation 2(4)(a) (construction of the expression “combined operation”) of the Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00012] omit the word “such”.

#### Amendment to the Radiation (Emergency Preparedness and Public Information) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

###### 7

- (1) In regulation 6(4) (review of hazard evaluation and consequences assessment) of the Radiation (Emergency Preparedness and Public Information) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019[^f00013] after the words “regulation 4(2) to (4)” insert “, and regulation 5, where applicable”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations are made under the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 (“*the 1978 Order*”) and the Offshore, and Pipelines, Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1992. They repeal certain provisions of the Factories Act (Northern Ireland) 1965 and the Office and Shop Premises Act (Northern Ireland) 1966. The Regulations also revoke six Instruments. The legislation being removed is either redundant or has been replaced by more recent provisions. The Regulations make minor amendments to five Statutory Rules. These repeals, revocations and amendments were the subject of a public Consultation in accordance with Article 46(3) of the 1978 Order.

Regulation 2 repeals or revokes the legislation set out in Tables 1 and 2 in the Schedule.

Regulation 3 amends the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 to correct the definition of “dangerous goods” in regulation 2(1) and Schedule 2, Part IV, paragraph 48K(2).

Regulation 4 makes one minor correction to the Biocidal Products and Chemicals (Appointment of Authorities and Enforcement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013.

Regulation 5 makes amendments to the Control of Major Accident Hazards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015. Paragraph (2) removes an unnecessary definition of “pipelines”. Paragraph (3) amends regulation 3 of those Regulations (concerning application and exceptions) by including exceptions in relation to the transport of dangerous substances in pipelines where that activity takes place outside of an establishment, and also the transport of dangerous substances and directly related intermediate temporary storage where that activity takes place outside of an establishment. Paragraph (4) amends regulation 24(3)(c) to refer to the correct Regulations.

Regulation 6 amends the Offshore Installations (Offshore Safety Directive) (Safety Case etc.) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 to remove a superfluous “such” from regulation 2(4)(a).

Regulation 7 makes one minor correction to the Radiation (Emergency Preparedness and Public Information) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for these Regulations, as they have no impact on costs for businesses, charities or voluntary bodies.
