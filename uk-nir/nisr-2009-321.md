# Agriculture (Student fees)(Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 16th September 2009

Coming into operation: 16th October 2009

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5A(1) and (2) of the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (Student fees)(Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on the 16th October 2009.

#### Amendment to the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

The Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] shall be amended as provided by regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

In Regulation 4(1)[^f00004] for “£1255” substitute “£1285”.

###### 4

In Regulation 5(2)[^f00005] for “£625” substitute “£640”.

#### Revocations

###### 5

In the Agriculture (Student fees)(Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00006] the following regulations are revoked—

- (1) Regulation 4(2);

- (2) Regulation 5.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 16th September 2009

Norman Fulton — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007. They also revoke certain provisions of the Agriculture (Student fees)(Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.

These Regulations increase the level of fee to be paid by persons undertaking a course of higher education provided by the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development as set out in Regulations 3 and 4.
