# The County Court (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 27th April 2009

Coming into operation: 18th May 2009

The County Court Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 47 of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 18th May 2009.

#### Amendments

###### 2

In the Arrangement of Orders in the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002] (“the principal Rules”), at the entry for Order 27, insert “European Small Claims Procedure”.

###### 3

In Order 1, rule 1(1) of the principal Rules, after “Order 26 Rule 29,” insert “Order 27, Rule 8,”.

###### 4

In Order 26, rule 46 of the principal Rules, for “Table 3” substitute “Table 2”.

###### 5

After Order 26 of the principal Rules, insert the new Order 27 set out in Schedule 1.

###### 6

In Appendix 1 to the principal Rules, after Form 125, insert the new Form 126 set out in Schedule 2.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981 [[S.R. 1981 No. 225](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1981/225)] to make provision for procedures under the European small claims procedure under Regulation [(EC) No 861/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0861) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 July 2007 establishing a European small claims procedure.
