# Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 30th July 1998

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 11th September 1998

In exercise of the powers conferred by Article 4(1) and (7) of the Audit (Northern Ireland) Order 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling me in that behalf, I hereby make the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 11th September 1998.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

#### Salary

###### 3

The annual salary payable to the holder of the office of Comptroller and Auditor General shall be increased as follows—

- (a) from £80,930 to £85,767 from 1st April 1998,

- (b) from £85,767 to £87,028 from 1st December 1998.

## Signed

Marjorie Mowlam — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 30th July 1998

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for increases in the annual salary payable to the Comptroller and Auditor General following recommendations made in the Report of the Review Body on Senior Salaries in 1998 (HMSO CM 3837).

These increases include an increase made with retrospective effect from 1st April 1998 under Article 4(7) of the Audit (Northern Ireland) Order 1987.

Article 2 revokes the Salaries (Comptroller and Auditor General) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997.
