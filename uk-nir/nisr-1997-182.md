# The Pharmaceutical Services (1992 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 25th March 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1 of the Pharmaceutical Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00001], paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Services (1992 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

25th March 1997 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of The Pharmaceutical Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1992.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

Joan Dixon — Assistant Secretary — 25th March 1997.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This order provides for the coming into operation on 25th March1997 of the Pharmaceutical Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1992.
