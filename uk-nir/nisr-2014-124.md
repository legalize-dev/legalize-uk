# The Westlink (Busways) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 30th April 2014

Coming into operation: 11th June 2014

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 20(3) of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Westlink (Busways) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 11th June 2014.

#### Amendments

###### 2

- (1) The Westlink (Busways) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00004] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In Article 3(a) for the word “northerly” substitute the words “north-easterly”.

- (3) For the Schedule substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 30th April 2014

D J Millar — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend The Westlink (Busways) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 (“the Regulations”) by extending the length of the busways.

A person who acts in contravention of the Regulations shall be guilty of an offence under Article 20(4) of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine exceeding level 4 on the standard scale (£2,500).
