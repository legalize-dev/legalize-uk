# Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 2nd June 2010

Coming into operation: 19th July 2010

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure [^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section’s 26(1) and 37(1) (e) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 [^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 19th July 2010.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

The Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 [^f00003] are amended as provided by regulations 3 and 4.

#### List of waters where there is no close season for rainbow trout

###### 3

In Schedule 6 in the list of rainbow trout waters where there is no annual close season for angling for rainbow trout —

- (a) under the heading County Antrim insert before the reference to “Murloc Angling Centre, Aghalee” a reference to “McCutcheon’s Lake, Moorfields, Ballymena”;

- (b) under the heading County Antrim insert before the reference to “Raloo Fishery” a reference to “Quoilie Reservoirs, Broughshane”;

- (c) under the heading County Fermanagh insert before the reference to “Lough Coolyermer” a reference to “Castle Hume ‘Back’ Lough”;

- (d) under the heading County Londonderry insert before the reference to “Gauger’s Inn Fishery Lake” a reference to “Dunalis Reservoir”.

#### Appeals against the refusal to issue or restore a fishing licence

###### 4

In the heading for Part III and in regulations 27, 28 and 29 for “the Department” substitute “a magistrates’ court” [^f00004].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure on 2nd June 2010.

M. Cory — A senior officer of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003.

The principal changes are as follows:–

1. The Regulations designate four waters as rainbow trout waters for the purposes of regulation 73(2)(b) of the Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 which provides that there shall be no close season for angling for rainbow trout in waters so designated (regulation 3).

2. Appeals against the revocation or refusal to issue or renew a fishing licence are now to a magistrates’ court instead of the Department (regulation 4).
