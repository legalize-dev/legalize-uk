# The Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 6) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 9th November 2010

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00001] and in accordance with Article 1(5) of that Order.

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 6) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010.

- (2) In this Order “the Order” means the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Order specified in Part 1 of the Schedule shall be 13th December 2010.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Order specified in Part II of the Schedule shall be 14th December 2010.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation the provisions of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 specified in the Schedule.

Article 16 and Schedule 2 amend Part IV of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (“the Offenders Order”) to enable vehicle examiners to issue fixed penalty notices for fixed penalty offences.

Articles 38, 39 and Schedule 4 provide for a new system of endorsement of driving licences. Article 38 inserts the definition of “driving record” into the Offenders Order. Article 39, together with Schedule 4, provides for endorsable fixed penalties to be issued to drivers who do not hold a driving licence issued in Northern Ireland by enabling their driving record to be checked and subsequently endorsed with penalty points.

Article 86(2) and Parts III and IV of Schedule 8 contain a number of consequential repeals.
