# The General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 5th August 2014

Coming into operation: 8th September 2014

The Department of Education makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 34 of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 8th September 2014.

#### Amendment of the General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

###### 2

- (1) The General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland (Constitution) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 8 (term of office), in paragraph (1) at the beginning insert “ Subject to regulation 8A, ”.
