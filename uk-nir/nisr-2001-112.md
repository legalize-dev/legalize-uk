# Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made: 14th March 2001

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 9th April 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3(2), 7(3), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 9th April 2001.

- (2) In these Regulations, “the Order” means the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Financial Conditions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00003] are hereby revoked*.*

#### Persons eligible for advice and assistance

###### 3

For the weekly sum of “£180” specified in Article 3(1) of the Order[^f00004] there shall be substituted the weekly sum of “£186”.

#### Contributions from persons receiving advice and assistance

###### 4

For the weekly sum of “£76” specified in Article 7(2) of the Order[^f00005] there shall be substituted the weekly sum of “£79”.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, *C.* — Dated 14th March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 so as to:

- (a) increase the upper income limit to make legal advice and assistance available to those with disposable income of not more than £186 a week (instead of £180) (Regulation 3);

- (b) increase the lower income limit below which legal advice and assistance is available without payment of a contribution to £79 a week (instead of £76) (Regulation 4).

No changes are made to the capital limits.
