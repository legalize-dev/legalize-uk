# The Benefit Cap (Annual Limit) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made: 26th September 2023

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1

The powers are exercisable by the Department for Communities by virtue of Article 2 of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Cessation of Transitory Provision) Order 2020[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Benefit Cap (Annual Limit) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 27th September 2023.

- (3) The amendments made by regulations 2 and 3 apply in relation to an award of universal credit whenever made but only in relation to each assessment period[^f00004] in respect of that award which begins on or after 10th April 2023.

- (4) The amendments made by regulations 2 and 4 apply only on and after 3rd April 2023 in relation to housing benefit in respect of rent payable at intervals of a week or any multiple of a week.

- (5) In this regulation “*housing benefit*” means housing benefit under section 129 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00005].

#### Amendment of the Welfare Reform Order

###### 2

In Article 101(5A) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (benefit cap)[^f00006] for “£20,000 or £13,400” substitute “£22,020 or £14,753”.

#### Amendment of the Universal Credit Regulations

###### 3

In regulation 80A(2) of the Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 (relevant amount)[^f00007]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£13,400” substitute “£14,753”;

- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£20,000” substitute “£22,020”.

#### Amendment of the Housing Benefit Regulations

###### 4

In regulation 73CA(2) of the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 (determination of the relevant amount)[^f00008]—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£13,400” substitute “£14,753”;

- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£20,000” substitute “£22,020”.

#### Revocation

###### 5

The Benefit Cap (Annual Limit) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023[^f00009] are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 26th September 2023

David Tarr — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This is not part of the Regulations)

Explanatory Note

These Regulations revoke and re-enact the provisions of the Benefit Cap (Annual Limit) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 which would otherwise cease to have effect by virtue of Article 102(1A) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015.

These Regulations are made following a review by the Secretary of State under section 96A of the Welfare Reform Act [2012 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/5), and amend the sums specified as the annual limit for the purposes of the benefit cap.

Regulation 1 brings these Regulations into operation for all purposes on 27th September 2023 subject to the provisions in regulation 1(3) and (4). The amended annual limits will only apply to an award of universal credit in relation to each assessment period commencing on or after 10th April 2023. For an award of housing benefit in respect of which rent is paid at intervals of a week or multiples thereof, the amended annual limits will only apply from 3rd April 2023.

Regulation 2 amends the annual limit as set out in Article 101(5A) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 ([S.I. 2015/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/2006)).

Regulation 3 makes a consequential amendment to the Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 ([S.R. 2016 No. 216](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2016/216)) as, in order to calculate and apply the benefit cap, the annual limit is also specified in the secondary legislation.

Regulation 4 makes a consequential amendment to the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 405](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/405)) as, in order to calculate and apply the benefit cap, the annual limit is also specified in the secondary legislation.

Regulation 5 revokes the original Benefit Cap (Annual Limit) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 ([S.R. 2023 No. 42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2023/42)) as they were subject to the confirmatory procedure and due to the absence of a functioning Assembly cease to have effect from 30th September 2023.

These Regulations make provision corresponding to provision contained in Regulations made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraphs 3A and 10 of schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8) are not subject to the requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
