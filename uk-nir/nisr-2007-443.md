# The Contaminants in Food (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 23rd October 2007

Coming into operation: 23rd November 2007

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003]**,** there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Contaminants in Food (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and come into operation on 23rd November 2007.

#### Amendment of the Contaminants in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

- (1) The Contaminants in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “the Commission Regulation” substitute—

> > - ”the Commission Regulation” means [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1881/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1881) setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs[^f00005] as amended by [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1126/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1126) amending Regulation (EC) No.1881/2006 setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs as regards *Fusarium*-toxins in maize and maize products[^f00006]

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 23rd October 2007.

Don Hill — A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Contaminants in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.R. 2007 No.66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/66)) (“the 2007 Regulations”). They make provision for the execution and enforcement of [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1126/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1126) amending Regulation [(EC) No. 1881/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1881) setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs as regards *Fusarium* toxins in maize and maize products (OJ No.L255, 29.9.2007, p.14).

The Regulations amend the definition of the “the Commission Regulation” in the 2007 Regulations so as to include the amendments effected by [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1126/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1126) (*regulation 2*).
