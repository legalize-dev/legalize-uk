# Seed Potatoes (Crop Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 23rd March 2005

Coming into operation: 14th April 2005

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 1 of the Seeds Act (Northern Ireland) 1965[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, after consultation with representatives of such interests as appear to it to be concerned, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Crop Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 14th April 2005.

#### Amendment to the Seed Potatoes (Crop Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

###### 2

The Seed Potatoes (Crop Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with Articles 3 to 6.

#### Interpretation

###### 3

In regulation 1 there shall be inserted “(3) “Pre-basic tissue culture” has the meaning assigned to it in Schedule 2 to the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00004]”.

#### Fees

###### 4

- (1) Regulation 2 shall be amended in accordance with the following provisions of this regulation.

- (2) After the words “seed potato crops” there shall be inserted “except for Pre-basic Tissue Culture”.

- (3) After the words “in accordance with” there shall be inserted “Part A to”.

###### 5

After regulation 2 there shall be inserted the following new regulation –

> > (2A) Subject to regulation 8 of the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001, in respect of certification and classification of Pre-basic Tissue Culture, a fee shall be payable for the services specified and shall be determined by the work involved, charged at an hourly rate in accordance with Part B to the Schedule. Where a charge is made by reference to an hour, the full hourly charge shall be payable in respect of part of an hour.

###### 6

For the Schedule to the Seed Potatoes (Crop Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 there shall be substituted the Schedule as set out in the Schedule to these regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 23rd March 2005.

David Small — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Seed Potatoes (Crop Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 which charge a fee per hectare or half hectare for the certification and classification of all seed potato crops. These Regulations facilitate the reinstatement of charges for the certification and classification of Pre-basic Tissue Culture potatoes by introducing hourly rates, thus taking into account different methods of growing that do not lend themselves to charging per hectare or half hectare.

Regulation 3 exempts the certification and classification of Pre-basic Tissue Culture from the previous charging rate of per hectare or per half hectare.

Regulation 4 inserts new regulations introducing fees for the certification of Pre-basic Tissue Culture according to an hourly rate.

Regulation 5 substitutes the Schedule to the Seed Potatoes (Crop Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 with a new Schedule. Part B to the Schedule sets the fee for the certification and classification of Pre-basic Tissue Culture at £38·00 per hour or part of an hour.
