# Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 12th March 1996

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 8th April 1996

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 7(2), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 8th April 1996.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

#### Amendment to Schedule 2

###### 3

For the table set out in Schedule 2 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00004], there shall be substituted the following table—

| *Disposable Income Range* | *Maximum Contribution* |
| --- | --- |
| Exceeding £67 but not exceeding £74 a week | £7 |
| Exceeding £74 but not exceeding £81 a week | £14 |
| Exceeding £81 but not exceeding £88 a week | £21 |
| Exceeding £88 but not exceeding £95 a week | £28 |
| Exceeding £95 but not exceeding £102 a week | £35 |
| Exceeding £102 but not exceeding £109 a week | £42 |
| Exceeding £109 but not exceeding £116 a week | £49 |
| Exceeding £116 but not exceeding £123 a week | £56 |
| Exceeding £123 but not exceeding £130 a week | £63 |
| Exceeding £130 but not exceeding £137 a week | £70 |
| Exceeding £137 but not exceeding £144 a week | £77 |
| Exceeding £144 but not exceeding £151 a week | £84 |
| Exceeding £151 but not exceeding £162 a week | £95 |

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, C. — Dated 12th March 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 so as to substitute a new scale of contributions payable for legal advice and assistance under Article 7(2) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
