# The Sunbeds (Fixed Penalty) (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

Coming into operation: 1st May 2012

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12(3) and 16(1) of, and paragraph 14 of Schedule 2 to, the Sunbeds Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Sunbeds (Fixed Penalty) (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1st May 2012.

#### Form of fixed penalty notice

###### 2

A fixed penalty notice shall be in the form set out in the Schedule.
