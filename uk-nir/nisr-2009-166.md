# The Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 15th April 2009

Coming into operation-: 1st June 2009

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 5 of the Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00001]**,**makes the following Regulations with the approval of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00002]

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2009.

###### 2

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003]**.**

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 3

In regulation 2 of the principal Regulations (registration fees) for “£345” there shall be substituted “£372”.

###### 4

In regulation 3 of the principal Regulations (restoration fees) for “£112” there shall be substituted “£121”.

###### 5

In regulation 4 of the principal Regulations (retention fees)–

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£345” there shall be substituted “£372”;

- (b) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£158” there shall be substituted “£170”;

- (c) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£126” there shall be substituted “£372”

## Signed

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the [Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 (S.R.1994 No.202)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/202) by increasing fees payable in respect of—

- (d) registration as a pharmaceutical chemist (regulation 2);

- (e) restoration of a person’s name to the register (regulation 3);

- (f) retention fees payable in respect of members of the society (regulation 4).
