# The Sunbeds (Fixed Penalty) (Amount) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made

Coming into operation: 1st May 2012

type=end slip=2012-04-01 time=1334825039835The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12(3) and 16(1) of, and paragraph 4 of Schedule 2 to, the Sunbeds Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].type=start slip=2012-04-01 time=1334825051460

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sunbeds (Fixed Penalty) (Amount) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1st May 2012.

- (2) In these Regulations “the Act” means the Sunbeds Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Amount of fixed penalty

###### 2

The fixed penalty in respect of an alleged offence shown in column (1) of the Schedule shall be the amount shown opposite in column (2).

## SCHEDULE — Amount of Fixed Penalty

| *(1)* *Offence* | *(2)* *Fixed Penalty* |
| --- | --- |
| An operator of sunbed premises who allows a person who is under 18 to use a sunbed on those premises (section 1(1)(a) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| An operator of sunbed premises who allows a person who is under 18 to be present (except in the course of providing services to the operator for the purposes of the business of the sunbed premises) in a restricted zone on those premises (section 1(1)(b) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| A person who sells a sunbed to a person who is under 18 (section 2(1) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| A person who hires a sunbed to a person who is under 18 (section 2(2) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| An operator of sunbed premises who fails to provide a person who proposes to use a sunbed on those premises with such information as is prescribed under section 4(11) of the Act on every occasion (section 4(1) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| A person who sells a sunbed to a person and who fails to provide a person who is buying the sunbed with such information as prescribed under section 4(11) of the Act (section 4(4) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| A person who hires a sunbed to a person and who fails to provide a person who is hiring the sunbed with such information as prescribed under section 4(11) of the Act (section 4(6) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| An operator of sunbed premises who fails to display a notice containing such information as prescribed under section 5(4) of the Act in a position where it is readily visible to persons proposing to use a sunbed on the premises (section 5(1) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| An operator of sunbed premises who provides or displays any material that contains statements relating to the health effects of sunbed use other than that prescribed under sections 4(11), 5(4) or 6(1)(b) of the Act (section 6(1) of the Act). | £50.00 |
| An operator of sunbed premises who fails to make available protective eyewear for a person who proposes to use a sunbed on those premises and/or fails to secure as far as reasonably practicable that a person who uses a sunbed on those premises wears protective eyewear (section 7(1) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| A person who sells a sunbed to a person and who fails to provide a person who is buying the sunbed with protective eyewear (section 7(4) of the Act). | £250.00 |
| A person who hires a sunbed to a person and who fails to provide a person who is hiring the sunbed with protective eyewear (section 7(6) of the Act). | £250.00 |
