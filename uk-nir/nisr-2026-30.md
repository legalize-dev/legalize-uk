# The Deaths and Still-Births (Signing of Certificates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made: 26th February 2026

Coming into operation: 23rd March 2026

The Department of Finance[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 15(4)(za) and 25(2A) of the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Deaths and Still-Births (Signing of Certificates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2026 and shall come into operation on 23rd March 2026.

###### 2

In these Regulations “*the Order*” means the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976.

#### Signing of Certificate of Still-Birth

###### 3

A certificate of still-birth, under Article 15(3) of the Order, may be signed by a registered medical practitioner or a registered midwife in the following manner:

- (a) approved electronic means; or

- (b) signed in ink.

#### Signing of Medical Certificate of Cause of Death

###### 4

A medical certificate of cause of death, under Article 25(2) of the Order, may be signed by a registered medical practitioner in the following manner:

- (a) approved electronic means; or

- (b) signed in ink.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 26th February 2026.

P. D. Wales — A senior officer of the Department of Finance

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe the manner for the signing of a certificate of still-birth by a registered medical practitioner or a registered midwife.

They also provide the manner for the signing of a medical certificate of cause of death by a registered medical practitioner.
