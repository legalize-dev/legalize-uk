# The Planning (General Development) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 29th November 2011

Coming into operation: 30th December 2011

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning (General Development) (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 30th December 2011.

#### Amendment of the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

###### 2

In Part 5 (caravan sites) of Schedule 1 to the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002], in paragraph A.2 (interpretation of Part 5) for “paragraphs 2, 3 and 6 to 10” substitute “paragraphs 2 to 10”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 29th November 2011.

Maggie Smith — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Part 5 of Schedule 1 to the Planning (General Development) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993. The amendment has the effect of adding paragraphs 4 and 5 of the Schedule to the Caravans Act (Northern Ireland) 1963 to the circumstances in which use of land as a caravan site is permitted under Part 5.

Part 5 of Schedule 1 describes permitted development rights in respect of the use of land as a caravan site. Where such rights apply, no specific application for planning permission is needed as permission is granted by Article 3 of that Order.

The Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument on the government’s legislation website www.legislation.gov.uk .
