# The Rural Needs (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 10th May 2018

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs [^f00001]**,** in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 of the Rural Needs Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 [^f00002] makes the following Order.

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rural Needs (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

Insofar as not already in operation, the day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 1 to 4 of the Rural Needs Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, as they apply to Public Authorities listed in the Schedule to that Act, is 1st June 2018.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 10th May 2018

Catherine McCallum — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Regulations brings into operation sections 1 to 4 of the Rural Needs Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 for the Public Authorities (other than Northern Ireland departments and district councils) listed in the Schedule to that Act. Sections 1 to 4 of that Act have already been brought into operation for Northern Ireland departments and district councils.
