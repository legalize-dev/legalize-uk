# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 18th March 2016

Coming into operation: 31st May 2016

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 31st May 2016.

#### Amendment of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 by specifying additional offences committed under sections 5, 12, 14 to 20, 22, 24, 37, 39, 43 and 44 of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 to be fixed penalty offences for the purposes of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
