# The Motor Vehicles (Compulsory Insurance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 29th October 2007

Coming into operation: 11th December 2007

The Department therefore makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Compulsory Insurance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 11th December 2007.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 in respect of motor insurance requirements

###### 3

- (1) The Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00004] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In Article 92(2)(b)[^f00005] (requirements in respect of policies) for “£250,000” substitute “£1,000,000”.

- (3) In Article 98(6)[^f00006] (duty of insurers or persons giving security to satisfy judgment against persons insured or secured against third-party risks) for “£250,000”, on each of the occasions when it occurs, substitute “£1,000,000”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 29th October 2007.

Stanley Duncan — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations implement Directive [2005/14/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0014)[^f00007] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11th May 2005 relating to insurance against civil liability in respect of the use of motor vehicles, specifically that part of Article 2 which sets down the minimum level of insurance cover required in relation to property damage.

Directive [2005/14/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0014) amends Council Directives [72/166/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1972/0166)[^f00008], [84/5/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0005)[^f00009], [88/357/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0357)[^f00010], [90/232/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0232)[^f00011] and [2000/26/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0026)[^f00012] of the European Parliament and of the Council and increases the minimum level of compulsory insurance in respect of property damage under Article 1.2 (b) of Directive [84/5/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1984/0005) to 1 million Euros per claim. In order to ensure compliance with this requirement, in the light of possible currency fluctuations, the sum of £1 million has been substituted in the relevant Articles of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

A full Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared and is available from Road Safety Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB or the Department of the Environment website at www.roadsafetyni.gov.uk.

A transposition note has been prepared for these Regulations and is available from the above address.

Copies of the relevant Directives can be obtained from TSO, 16 Arthur Street, Belfast BT1 4GD.
