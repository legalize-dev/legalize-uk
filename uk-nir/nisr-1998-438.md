# Carriage of Dangerous Goods (Classification, Packaging and Labelling) and Use of Transportable Pressure Receptacles (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 9th December 1998

The Department of Economic Development, being the Department concerned[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 17(1) and (3) of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, after consultation in accordance with Article 46(1) of that Order with the Health and Safety Agency for Northern Ireland and such other bodies as appeared to the Department to be appropriate, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Carriage of Dangerous Goods (Classification, Packaging and Labelling) and Use of Transportable Pressure Receptacles (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998.

#### Amendment of the Carriage of Dangerous Goody (Classification, Packaging and Labelling) and Use of Transportable Pressure Receptacles Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

###### 2

The Carriage of Dangerous Goods (Classification, Packaging and Labelling) and Use of Transportable Pressure Receptacles Regulations(Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00003] shall be amended as follows—

- (a) for regulation 1(2) (commencement), there shall be substituted—

> > (2) Regulation 22(1) shall come into operation on 1st January 1999.

; and

- (b) regulation 23(3) and Schedule 7 are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Economic Development on

R. G. Fry — Assistant Secretary — 9th December 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke regulation 23(3) of, and Schedule 7 to, the Carriage of Dangerous Goods (Classification, Packaging and Labelling) and Use of Transportable Pressure Receptacles Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 ([S.R. 1997 No. 247](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/247) as amended by [S.R. 1997 No. 360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/360)). Accordingly the statutory provisions referred to in Schedule 7 to those Regulations are no longer revoked on 1st January 1999.
