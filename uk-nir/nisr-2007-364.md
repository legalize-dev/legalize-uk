# The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 28th August 2007

Coming into operation: 28th August 2007

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and come into operation on 28th August 2007.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment

###### 3

- (1) The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (Interpretation) for the definition of “the Decision” substitute—

> > - “the Decision” means Commission Decision [2007/554/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0554)[^f00005] concerning certain protection measures against foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom as amended by Commission Decision [2007/588/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0588)[^f00006];

- (3) In paragraph (2)(a) of regulation 5 (dispatch of meat products) and paragraph (2)(d) of regulation 7 (dispatch of dairy products) for “Great Britain” substitute—

> > the protection zones and the surrounding consolidated surveillance zone established in accordance with Article 21 of Directive [2003/85/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0085) in County Surrey in Great Britain[^f00007]

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 28th August 2007.

Elizabeth Redmond — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.R. 2007 No. 357](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/357)). The Regulations give effect to Commission Decision [2007/588/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0588) of 24th August 2007 which amends Commission Decision [2007/554/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0554) concerning certain protection measures against foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom.
