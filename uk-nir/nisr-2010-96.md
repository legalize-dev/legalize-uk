# The Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 11th March 2010

Coming into operation: 6th April 2010

Accordingly the Department for Social Development makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Social Security Pensions (Low Earnings Threshold) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2010.

#### Low earnings threshold

###### 2

For the purposes of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^f00005], it is directed that the low earnings threshold[^f00006] for the tax years following the tax year 2009 – 2010 shall be £14,100.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 11th March 2010

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, which corresponds to an Order ([S.I. 2010/468](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/468)) made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions under section 148A of the Social Security Administration Act 1992, directs that the low earnings threshold for the purposes of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 for the tax years following 2009-2010 shall be £14,100. The low earnings threshold is used for the purposes of calculating entitlement to the additional pension (the state second pension) in a state retirement pension.
