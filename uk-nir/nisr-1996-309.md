# Parking Places on Roads (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 22nd July 1996

Coming into operation: 2nd September 1996

NOW THEREFORE the Department, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 104(1)(*c*) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Parking Places on Roads (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 2nd September 1996.

#### Amendment

###### 2

The Parking Places on Roads Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] shall be amended by—

- (a) the addition to Schedule 1 to that Order of the items in Schedule 1;

- (b) the deletion from Schedule 1 to that Order of the items in Schedule 2.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order further amends the Parking Places on Roads Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 (“the 1993 Order”).

The amendments provide for the introduction of parking places on the parts of the roads specified in Schedule 1 and for the deletion from Schedule 1 to the 1993 Order of the parking places specified in Schedule 2.
