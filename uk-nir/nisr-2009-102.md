# Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 11th March 2009

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 6th April 2009

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 7(2), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2009.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

#### Amendment to Schedule 2

###### 3

For the table set out in Schedule 2 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00004] there shall be substituted the following table—

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 so as to substitute a new scale of contributions payable for legal advice and assistance under Article 7(2) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
