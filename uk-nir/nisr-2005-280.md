# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 27th May 2005

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.

- (2) In this Order, any reference to a numbered Article is a reference to the Article of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 bearing that number.

#### Appointed Days

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 159 (supplementary provisions about pension protection levies) –

- (a) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 31st May 2005, and

- (b) for all other purposes, is 20th June 2005.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 2(6) (general interpretation) is 1st June 2005.

- (3) The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 158 (pension protection levies) in so far as it relates to a consultation by the Board under Article 159, is 20th June 2005.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 27th May 2005.

John O'Neill — A senior officer of the — Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes further provision for the coming into operation of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (“the Order”) on the following dates –

- 31st May 2005 for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations relating to supplementary provisions about pension protection levies. The provision is brought fully into operation on 20th June 2005;

- 1st June 2005 for provisions relating to persons to be regarded as members or prospective members of occupational pension schemes and the times and circumstances at which they are treated as becoming, or ceasing to be, such a member;

- 20th June 2005 for other provisions relating to Pension Protection Levies.
