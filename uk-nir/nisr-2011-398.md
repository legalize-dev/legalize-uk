# The Planning (Fees) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 15th November 2011

Coming into operation: 12th December 2011

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 127 of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Fees) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 12th December 2011.

#### Amendment of the Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (7).

- (2) In regulations 10(1) (amount of reduced fees and refunds) and 12(3) (exemption, etc. – deemed applications) for “£60” substitute “£62”.

- (3) In regulation 14(1) (fees for applications for consent to display advertisements) for “£178” substitute “£183”.

- (4) In regulation 16A (fee for an application for planning permission for EIA development) for “£10,000” substitute “£10,290”.

- (5) In regulation 18 (fees for applications for certificates of lawful use or development)—

- (a) in paragraph (5)(b) for “£237” substitute “£244”; and

- (b) in paragraph (6) for “£237” substitute “£244” and for “£11,834” substitute “£12,177”.

- (6) In Schedule 1 (fees in respect of applications and deemed applications for planning permission or for approval of reserved matters)—

- (a) in Part 1, in paragraph 4(2) and (3) for “£521” substitute “£536”; and

- (b) for Part 2, substitute Part 2 as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (7) In Schedule 2 (fees for hazardous substances consent)—

- (a) for “£320” where it twice occurs substitute “£329”;

- (b) for “£402” substitute “£414”; and

- (c) for “£639” substitute “£658”.

## SCHEDULE 1 — SUBSTITUTION OF PART 2 OF SCHEDULE 1 TO THE PLANNING (FEES) REGULATIONS (NORTHERN IRELAND) 2005

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 15th November 2011.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 which prescribe fees payable to the Department of the Environment in respect of applications made under the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991. The Regulations increase planning fees by approximately 2.9% overall.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in connection with these Regulations. A copy may be obtained from the Department of Environment, Planning Headquarters, Millennium House, 17-25 Great Victoria Street, Belfast BT2 7BN (Tel: 028 9041 6967) or accessed at http://www.planningni.gov.uk/

The Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument on the Government’s legislation website: www.legislation.gov.uk
