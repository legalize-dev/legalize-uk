# The Ulster, North Down and Ards Hospitals Health and Social Services Trust (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 25th March 1998

Coming into operation: 1st April 1998

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by paragraph 23(1) of Schedule 3 to, the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, considering it appropriate in the interests of the health and personal social services to make this order, and after completion of the consultation prescribed by paragraph 23(3) of Schedule 3 to the said Order[^f00002], hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Ulster, North Down and Ards Hospitals Health and Social Services Trust (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 1st April 1998.

#### Dissolution of HSS trust

###### 2

The Ulster, North Down and Ards Hospitals Health and Social Services Trust is hereby dissolved.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Ulster, North Down and Ards Hospitals Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

John McGrath — Assistant Secretary — 25th March 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This order provides for the dissolution of the Ulster, North Down and Ards Hospitals Health and Social Services Trust on 1st April 1998.

It revokes the Ulster, North Down and Ards Hospitals Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992, as amended, from that date.
