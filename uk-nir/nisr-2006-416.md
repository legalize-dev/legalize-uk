# The Dangerous Wild Animals (2004 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 8th November 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by Article 1(3) of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Dangerous Wild Animals (2004 Order) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 3 to 11 of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 is 4th December 2006.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 8th November 2006.

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 4th December 2006 as the day for the coming into operation of Articles 3 to 11 of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004.
