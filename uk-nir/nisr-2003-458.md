# Plant Health (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 21st October 2003

Coming into operation: 12th November 2003

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 2[^f00002], 3(1)[^f00003], 3A[^f00004], 3B[^f00005] and 4(1)[^f00006] of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00007] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 12th November 2003.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

###### 2

The Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00008] shall be amended in accordance with Articles 3 to 22.

###### 3

In Article 3(1) –

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)” there shall be substituted –

> > - “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)” means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)[^f00009] of 8th May 2000 on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community as amended by Commission Directives [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033)[^f00010] of 8th May 2001, [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028)[^f00011] of 19th March 2002, [2002/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0036)[^f00012] of 29th April 2002, [2003/22/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0022)[^f00013] of 24th March 2003, and by [2003/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0047)[^f00014] of 4th June 2003 amending certain annexes to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029);”

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

1 This Order amends the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 so as to implement: Commission Directives [2002/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0036) (O.J. No. L116, 3.5.02, p. 16), and [2003/22/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0022) (O.J. No. L78, 25.3.03, p. 10) which amend various Annexes to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (O.J. No. L169, 10.7.00, p. 1) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community; and Commission Directive [2003/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0021) (O.J. No. L78, 25.3.03, p. 8) which amends some protective zones exposed to particular plant health risks in the Community in Commission Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) (O.J. No. L127, 9.5.01, p. 38).

2 The Order also takes into account amendments required by Directives [2003/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0046) and [2003/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0047) (O.J. No. L138, 5.6.03, p. 45 and 47), which correct clerical errors in Directives [2002/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0036) and [2003/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0021).

3 The principal changes introduced by this Order are: a addition of *Naupactus leucoloma* (Article 5) and *Anisogramma anomala* (Article 9) to the list of quarantine plant pests. It introduces specific measures for the import of *Corylus* from Canada and the United States (Article 13(a)); b restriction of the quarantine provisions on *Liriomyza bryoniae* to the Protected Zones of the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland (Articles 4, 6, and 20(b)); c restriction of the quarantine provisions on *Liriomyza huidbobrensis* and *Liriomyza trifolii* to a list of host plants (Articles 4 and 8); d extension of plant passport and phytosanitary certificate requirements for certain plants or plant products in respect of *Bemisia tabaci*, *Liriomyza sativae*, *Amauromyza maculosa*, *Liriomyza huidobrensis*, *Liriomyza trifolii* and *Thrips palmi* (Articles 13(b) to (g), 14, 15(f), 16, 17(c), 18(b), (c) and (d)); e amendment of the provisions against Beet necrotic yellow vein virus (Articles 15(c), (e), and (g) to (j), 17(a) and (b), and 19(a) and (b)); f addition of South Africa to the list of countries where *Tilletia indica* is known to occur (Articles 13(h) and 18(a)); g removal of areas in Sweden from the Protected Zones for *Beet necrotic yellow vein virus* (Articles 7, 15(c), (e), and (g) to (j), and 22) and removal of areas in Italy from the Protected Zone for *Erwinia amylovora* (Articles 11, 12 and 15(d) and 21); h restriction of the Protected Zone for *Gonipterus scutellatus* to Greece and Portugal (Azores) (Articles 10, 15(a) and 20(a)); and i inclusion of the definition of relevant international Standards for Phytosanitary Measures and details of their availability (Article 3(b)).
