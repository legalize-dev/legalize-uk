# Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 3rd December 2007

Coming into operation-: 1st January 2008

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2008.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 2 of the Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00006] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) For the definition of “the Commission Regulation” there shall be substituted—

> > - “*the Commission Regulation*” means [Commission Regulation (EC) No 2707/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2707) laying down rules for applying Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255) as regards Community aid for supplying milk and certain milk products to pupils in educational establishments, as amended from time to time;

- (3) For the definition of “the Council Regulation”, there shall be substituted—

> > - “*the Council Regulation*” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255)[^f00007] on the common organisation of the market in milk and milk products;

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 3rd December 2007.

Dr. John Speers — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which come into operation on 1st January 2008, amend regulation 2 of the Milk and Milk Products (Pupils in Educational Establishments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 ([S.R. 2001 No. 129](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2001/129)) by amending the definitions of “the Commission Regulation” and “the Council Regulation” to take account of—

- (a) amendments to [Commission Regulation (EC) No 2707/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2707) laying down rules for applying Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255) as regards Community aid for supplying milk and certain milk products to pupils in educational establishments (and references to [Commission Regulation (EC) No 2707/2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2000/2707) are references to that Regulation as amended from time to time); and

- (b) amendments to Council Regulation [(EC) No 1255/1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1999/1255) on the common organisation of the market in milk and milk products.
