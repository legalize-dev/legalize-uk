# Seed Potatoes (Tuber and Label Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made: 12th October 2000

Coming into operation: 13th November 2000

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 1 of the Seeds Act (Northern Ireland) 1965[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, after consultation with representatives of such interests as appear to it to be concerned, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Tuber and Label Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 13th November 2000.

#### Amendment of the Seed Potatoes (Tuber and Label Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1982

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Seed Potatoes (Tuber and Label Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1982[^f00003] there shall be substituted:—

> > **SCHEDULE**

|  | Matter | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  |  | £ |
| 1. | Fee for the inspection of seed potato tubers prior to their being sealed under regulation 9(2) of the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00004] | 3·40 per tonne or part of a tonne |
| 2. | Fee for the provision of official labels whether or not details have been printed thereon by the Department | 0·46 per 20 |

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Seed Potatoes (Tuber and Label Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 12th October 2000.

R. S. Johnston — Senior Officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke and replace the Seed Potatoes (Tuber and Label Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995.

The Regulations increase the fee payable for the inspection of seed potatoes from £3·35 per tonne or part of a tonne to £3·40 per tonne or part of a tonne.

The fee payable for the provision of official labels, whether or not the details have been printed thereon by the Department, is unchanged.
