# The Regulation and Improvement Authority (Consultation on Transfer of Staff) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 17th February 2005

Coming into operation: 17th February 2005

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 3(2) and 48 of, and paragraph 16(3) of Schedule 1 to, the Health and Personal Social Services (Quality, Improvement and Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These regulations may be cited as the Regulation and Improvement Authority (Consultation on Transfer of Staff) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 17th February 2005.

- (2) In this Order –

- “the Order” means the Health and Personal Social Services (Quality, Improvement and Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003;

- “scheme” means a scheme made by order under paragraph 16(1) of Schedule 1 to the Order.

#### Consultation on Transfer of Staff

###### 2

The requirements prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 16(3) of Schedule 1 to the Order (requirements about consultation to be complied with before a scheme is made by order by the Department) are that before a scheme is made by the Department, each Health and Social Services Board shall consult –

- (a) the Regulation and Improvement Authority;

- (b) every professional body or trade union known to the Health and Social Services Board as representing the interests of eligible employees; and

- (c) each eligible employee.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 17th February 2005.

Noel McCann — Senior Officer of the — Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order prescribes, for the purposes of paragraph 16(3) of Schedule 1 to the Health and Personal Social Services (Quality, Improvement and Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, the requirements about consultation to be complied with by Health and Social Services Boards before the Department, by order, makes a scheme for the transfer of staff from Health and Social Services Boards to the Northern Ireland Health and Personal Social Services Regulation and Improvement Authority.
