# The Scrapie (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 2nd March 2010

Coming into operation: 1st April 2010

makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Scrapie (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2010.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these regulations—

- “the Principal Regulations” means the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00004].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 3

- (1) The principal Regulations shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 4(1) (fees), after “opposite the activity” insert “(in place of the old fee in column (4))”.

- (3) For the Schedule substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 by substituting a new schedule. In the new Schedule the following fees are increased—

- (a) the fee for the initial flock inspection is increased from £ 183.65 to £ 189.07.

- (b) the fee for the subsequent annual inspection is increased from £ 87.78 to £ 90.31.
