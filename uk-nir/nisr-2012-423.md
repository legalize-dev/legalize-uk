# The Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 30th November 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 41(1) of the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Child Maintenance (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

- (2) In this Order any reference to a numbered section or Schedule is a reference to the section of, or Schedule to, the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) section 28 (additional special cases);

- (b) section 32 (pilot schemes); and

- (c) in paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 (minor and consequential amendments) sub-paragraphs (20)(b), (21), (22), (26) and (27), and section 38(1) and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 4 so far as relating to those sub-paragraphs,

is 3rd December 2012.

- (2) The day appointed for coming into operation of—

- (a) section 9 (power to regulate supersession);

- (b) sections 23 and 24 (power to accept part payment of arrears in full and final satisfaction and to write off arrears);

- (c) section 26 (meaning of “child”);

- (d) in Schedule 1 (changes to the calculation of maintenance) paragraphs 2, 5(2) and 7 to 9, and section 8 and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 so far as relating to those paragraphs; and

- (e) in Schedule 5 (repeals) the repeal relating to Schedule 1 to the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00002], and section 39 so far as it relates to that repeal,

for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 3rd December 2012.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 30th November 2012

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation the following provisions of the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008—

- sections 28 and 32 and minor amendments in Schedule 4, and section 38(1) and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 4 so far as relating to those amendments; and

- for the purposes only of authorising the making of regulations— sections 9, 23, 24 and 26, paragraphs 2, 5(2) and 7 to 9 of Schedule 1, and section 8 and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 so far relating to those paragraphs; and a consequential repeal in Schedule 5, and section 39 so far as relating to that repeal,

on 3rd December 2012.
