# The Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 11th October 2019

Coming into operation: 1st November 2019

The Department of Finance makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 37(4) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00001]

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2019.

#### Amendment of the Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

###### 2

For Part 2 of the Schedule to the Valuation (Telecommunication, Natural Gas and Water) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002] there shall be substituted the Part 2 set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## PART 2 — Gas transportation hereditaments

| GNI (UK) Ltd. |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 11th October 2019

Alan Brontë — A senior officer of the Department of Finance

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Valuation (Telecommunications, Natural Gas and Water) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010, (“the 2010 Regulations”), provide that certain hereditaments of any company listed in the Schedule shall, for the purposes of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977, be treated as a single hereditament occupied by that company.

These Regulations substitute the list in Part 2 of the Schedule to the 2010 Regulations to take account of changes to the gas market in Northern Ireland.
