# Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 17th February 2006

Coming into operation: 6th March 2006

The Department has accepted the recommendation of the Executive made under Article 33(2) of that Order that the rents referred to in Article 33(1) of that Order should be increased.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006, and shall come into operation on 6th March 2006.

#### Increase of registered rents

###### 2

The rents registered under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 for dwelling houses which are let under regulated tenancies shall be increased by 2.7 percent.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 19 February 2006

D M Crothers — A senior officer of the — Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the rents registered with the Northern Ireland Housing Executive under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 (“the Rent Order”) for the dwelling houses, which are let under regulated tenancies, by 2.7 percent from 6th March 2006. Under Article 22(4) of the Rent Order a notice of increase of rent which gives effect to an increase made by virtue of this Order shall not take effect earlier than 4 weeks after the commencement of this Order, i.e. not earlier than 3rd April 2006.

A landlord must give a tenant at least 4 weeks' notice of the increase in accordance with Article 22(3)(b) of the Rent Order.
