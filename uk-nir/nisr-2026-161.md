# The Coronavirus Act 2020 (Extension of Provisions Relating to Live Links for Courts and Tribunals) (No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made: 17th September 2026

The Department of Justice[^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/section/90) [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/section/90) of the [Coronavirus Act 2020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/contents)[^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Coronavirus Act 2020 (Extension of Provisions Relating to Live Links for Courts and Tribunals) (No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026.

#### Extension of expiry date

###### 2

[Parts 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/schedule/27/part/1) and [2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/schedule/27/part/2) of [Schedule 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/schedule/27) to the [Coronavirus Act 2020](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/contents), and [section 57](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/section/57) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7/contents) so far as it relates to those Parts, do not expire at the time when they would otherwise expire and expire instead on 24th March 2027.

Naomi Long — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order extends the operation of the provisions in the Coronavirus Act [2020 (c. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/7) which allow the wider and extended use of video or audio live links by all or any courts or a statutory tribunal (Part 1 of Schedule 27). It extends the provisions, which facilitate public participation in legal proceedings if, and when live links are used while protecting those proceedings from unauthorised recordings or transmission (Part 2 of Schedule 27).

The Coronavirus Act 2020 (Extension of Provisions Relating to Live Links for Courts and Tribunals) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026 ([SR.2026 No.55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2026/55)) extended the operation of these provisions until 24th September 2026.

Article 2 extends the operation of the provisions until 24th March 2027.
