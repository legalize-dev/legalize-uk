# Motor Vehicles (Approval) (Amendment No. 2) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 21st May 2003

Coming into operation: 16th June 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 31A(1), 31D(1) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Approval) (Amendment No. 2) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 16th June 2003.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Motor Vehicles (Approval) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 21st May 2003.

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke the Motor Vehicles (Approval) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003.
