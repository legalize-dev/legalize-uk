# The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 (Commencement No.4) Order 2010

Made: 15th March 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 (Commencement No.4) Order 2010.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 shall come into force on 12th April 2010—

- (a) section 8(9) (Guidance for criminal justice organisations on human rights standards);

- (b) section 18 (Repeals), to the extent necessary to bring into force the provisions of Schedule 4 listed in paragraph (c);

- (c) in Schedule 4 (Repeals), the entry in respect of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002[^f00002] in so far as it is not already in force.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 15th March 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004 specified in article 2 on 12th April 2010.
