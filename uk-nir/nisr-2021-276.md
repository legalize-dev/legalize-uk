# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (Amendment No. 16) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: at 3.00 p.m. on 30th September 2021

Laid before the Assembly: at 4.00 p.m. on 30th September 2021

Coming into operation: at 6.00 p.m. on 30th September 2021

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (Amendment No. 16) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at 6 p.m. on 30th September 2021.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00003] are amended in accordance with Regulations 3 to 8.

###### 3

In Regulation 4(2)(j) after “(Requirement in relation to social distancing)” insert “except in relation to persons seated at a performance, recording or rehearsal”.

###### 4

In Regulation 17(1) after “operation of” omit “a relevant place or”.

###### 5

Omit Regulation 17(2).

###### 6

Omit Regulation 17(2A)(b).

###### 7

At the end of Regulation 17(2B) omit “and in a relevant place”.

###### 8

- (1) In Regulation 17(3)(a) after “visitors to” omit “a relevant place or”.

- (2) In Regulation 17(3)(b) omit “relevant place or” on the two occasions where this occurs.

- (3) Omit Regulation 17(3)(d).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 3.00 pm on 30th September 2021

Robin Swann — Minister of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 to remove social distancing requirement for persons seated at a performance, recording or rehearsal.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
