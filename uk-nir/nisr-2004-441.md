# Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 14th October 2004

Coming into operation: 1st December 2004

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following By-Laws:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 1st December 2004.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the By-Laws.)

These By-Laws amend the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-Laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951 by revising the rates of fare to be charged.

The fare structure is now made up of 4 different rates which apply at different times of the day and at Christmas and New Year holidays.

The fare charged shall be the sum total of the charges for the individual stages of the journey, which are calculated on distance or time, and such other additional charges as may apply.

The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2001 are revoked as a consequence of these By-Laws.
