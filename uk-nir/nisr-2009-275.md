# The Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2009

Made: 12th July 2009

The Secretary of State makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2009.

#### Commencement

###### 2

Article 15 of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999, in so far as it relates to adult witnesses in summary proceedings before the magistrates’ court, shall come into operation on 1st August 2009.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 12 July 2009

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 1st August 2009 Article 15 of the Criminal Evidence (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 as it applies to adult witnesses in the magistrates’ court.
