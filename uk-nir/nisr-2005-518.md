# The Taxis (Strabane) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 24th November 2005

Coming into operation: 6th January 2006

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Bye-Laws:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Bye-Laws may be cited as the Taxis (Strabane) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 6th January 2006.

#### Bye-Laws amended

###### 2

The Taxis (Strabane) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1988[^f00002] shall be amended as follows—

- (1) In bye-law 2 (interpretation) for the definition of “taxi” there shall be substituted—

> > - “taxi” means a public service vehicle used in standing or plying for hire and used to carry passengers for hire and being a vehicle seating not more than eight passengers in addition to the driver

- (2) After bye-law 3 there shall be inserted—

> > (4) No vehicle other than a taxi shall use the stands or starting places specified in Column 2 of the Schedule at any time.

- (3) In the Schedule for the entry relating to Main Street there shall be substituted—

| Location | Position of stands and starting places | Maximum number of Taxis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main Street | South-west side, from its junction with Castle Place for 65m in a north-westerly direction (in front of Nos. 66 – 80 Main Street). | 11 |

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Taxis (Strabane) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 24th November 2005

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Bye-Laws)

These Bye-laws further amend the Taxi (Strabane) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1988 by—

- (1) increasing, in the definition of “taxi”, the number of seated passengers who can be carried from 6 to 8 in addition to the driver;

- (2) prohibiting vehicles other than taxis from using the stands and starting places; and

- (3) prescribing the new location of the taxi stand in Main Street, Strabane and increasing from 6 to 11 the number of taxis that may stand at that location.

The Taxis (Strabane) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1996 are revoked as a consequence.
