# Goods Vehicles (Testing) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 18th June 1998

Coming into operation: 1st August 1998

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 65(1) and (2) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Testing) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 1st August 1998.

#### Amendment of the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 3 to the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In the Table in paragraph 1, in column (2) of item 1, for “6 to 11” there shall be substituted “6, 7, 10 and 11”.

- (3) In sub-paragraph 5(*a*), the reference to item 8 shall be omitted.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

J. Ritchie — Assistant Secretary — 18th June 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

The Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 require the examination of goods vehicles to ensure that they comply with specified construction and use requirements. These Regulations amend the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 by removing the requirement for the examination of goods vehicles to ensure compliance with regulations 8 and 9 of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1989. In those Regulations, regulation 8 concerns the height of the vehicle and regulation 9 concerns the indication of overall travelling height.
