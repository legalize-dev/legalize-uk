# The Care Tribunal (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 7th July 2016

Coming into operation: 1st August 2016

The Department of Justice makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 44(2) to (4) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Quality, Improvement and Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Care Tribunal (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st August 2016.

#### Amendment of the Care Tribunal Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 6 to the Care Tribunal Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) For paragraph 1(1) substitute—

> > (1)
>   (1) A person who wishes to appeal to the Care Tribunal under section 15 of the 2001 Act against a relevant decision of the Council under Part 1 of that Act in respect of registration must do so by application in writing to the Care Tribunal.
>     - “Relevant decision” means— a decision under Part 1 of the 2001 Act in respect of registration, and a decision under Part 3 of the European Communities (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2007[^f00004] in respect of an aptitude test, or period of adaptation, in connection with a person becoming permitted (by virtue of that Part of those Regulations) to have access to, and pursue the profession of social worker in the United Kingdom.

- (3) At the end of paragraph 1(4)(e)(iv) omit “or”.

- (4) After paragraph 1(4)(e)(v) insert—

> > (vi) the making of the applicant’s registration in a part of the register subject to conditions (whether or not for a specified period);
> (vii) the variation or revocation of the conditions to which the applicant’s registration in a part of the register is subject;
> (viii) the variation of the duration for which the applicant’s registration in a part of the register is subject to conditions;
> (ix) a warning given to the applicant; or
> (x) a requirement to complete an adaptation period or take and pass an aptitude test.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 7th July 2016

Claire Sugden — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend Schedule 6 to the Care Tribunal Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 ([S.R. 2005 No. 178](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/178)) to extend the grounds on which an applicant may make an appeal to the Care Tribunal under section 15 of the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 ([2001 c. 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/3)) against a decision of the Northern Ireland Social Care Council.
