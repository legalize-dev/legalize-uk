# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 18th September 2014

Coming into operation: 31st October 2014

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 31st October 2014.

#### Amendment of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order

###### 2

In Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00002] (fixed penalties) before item 1 insert—

| A1. | A fixed penalty offence under section 11 of the HGV Road User Levy Act 2013[^f00003] | £300 |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18th September 2014

Iain Greenway — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 by inserting an amount of £300 for the fixed penalty prescribed for the offence under section 11 of the HGV Road User Levy Act 2013.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
