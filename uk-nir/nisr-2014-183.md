# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 15) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 24th June 2014

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 15) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 105 (insolvency event, insolvency date and insolvency practitioner), 122 (payment of scheme benefits) and 145 (effect of Board assuming responsibility for a scheme) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 in so far as they are not already in operation is 25th June 2014.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on June 2014

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the remaining provisions of Articles 105 (paragraph (9)(b)), 122 (paragraph (10)(a)) and 145 (paragraph (3)(b)) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 on 25th June 2014.

The provisions brought into operation concern the operation of the Board of the Pension Protection Fund which is established by section 107 of the Pensions Act [2004 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/35).
