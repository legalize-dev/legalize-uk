# The Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 15th May 2008

Coming into operation: 1st June 2008

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 5 of the Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00001], makes the following Regulations with the approval of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2008.

###### 2

In these Regulations “the principal regulations” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal regulations

###### 3

In regulation 2 of the principal regulations (registration fees) for “£217” there shall be substituted “£345”.

###### 4

In regulation 3 of the principal regulations (restoration fees) for “£104” there shall be substituted “£112”.

###### 5

In regulation 4 of the principal regulations (retention fees)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£319” there shall be substituted “£345”;

- (b) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£146” there shall be substituted “£158”;

- (c) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£117” there shall be substituted “£126”;

- (d) in paragraph (2)(c) for “£53” there shall be substituted “£126”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 ([S.R. 1994 No. 202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/202)) by increasing fees payable in respect of—

- (a) registration as a pharmaceutical chemist (regulation 2);

- (b) restoration of a person’s name to the register (regulation 3);

- (c) retention fees payable in respect of members of the society (regulation 4).
