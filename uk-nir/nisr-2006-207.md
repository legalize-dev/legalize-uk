# The Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 8th May 2006

Coming into operation: 1st June 2006

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 5 of the Pharmacy (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00001]makes the following Regulations with the approval of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

–

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2006.

- (2) In these Regulations “the principal regulations”means the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal regulations

###### 2

In regulation 3 of the principal regulations (restoration fees) for “£87”there shall be substituted “£104”.

###### 3

In regulation 4 of the principal regulations (retention fees)–

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a) for “£245”there shall be substituted “£295”;

- (b) in paragraph (2)(a) for “£113”there shall be substituted “£135”;

- (c) in paragraph (2)(b) for “£90”there shall be substituted “£108”;

- (d) in paragraph (2)(c) for “£41”there shall be substituted “£49”.

## Signed

Terence T. Hannawin — Secretary & Registrar

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 ([S.R. 1994 No. 202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/202)) by increasing fees payable in respect of–

- (a) restoration of a person’s name to the register (regulation 3);

- (b) retention fees payable in respect of members of the Society (regulation 4);
