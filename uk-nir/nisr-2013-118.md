# The Scrapie (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 26th April 2013

Coming into operation: 1st June 2013

makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Scrapie (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2013.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00005].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 3

For the Schedule substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, as amended, by substituting a new schedule. In the new Schedule the following fees are increased—

- (a) the fee for the initial flock inspection is increased from £189.07 to £207.61, and

- (b) the fee for the subsequent annual inspection is increased from £90.31 to £93.40.
