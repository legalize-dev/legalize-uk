# The Licensing (Designation of Outdoor Stadia) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 26th October 2020

Coming into force: 30th November 2020

The Department for Communities[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 2AA(c) of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing (Designation of Outdoor Stadia) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation on 30th November 2020.

#### Designation of outdoor stadium

###### 2

Down Royal Racecourse at Maze, Lisburn, BT27 5RW is designated as a stadium which is of importance to the whole of Northern Ireland.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on the 26th October 2020.

Anthony Carleton — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations designate Down Royal Racecourse, Maze, Lisburn, BT27 5RW as a stadium which is of importance to the whole of Northern Ireland. This designation allows Down Royal Racecourse to apply to the Courts for a liquor licence at the stadium.
