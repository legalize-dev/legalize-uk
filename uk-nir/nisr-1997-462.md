# Dogs (Licensing and Identification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 21st October 1997

Coming into operation: 1st January 1998

The Department of Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 31(1) of the Dogs (Northern Ireland) Order 1983[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and revocation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Dogs (Licensing and Identification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 1st January 1998.

- (2) The Dogs (Licensing and Identification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

#### Amendment of the Dogs (Licensing and Identification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1983

###### 2

For Schedule 8 to the Dogs (Licensing and Identification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1983[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture on

R. S. Johnston — Assistant Secretary — 21st October 1997.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Dogs (Licensing and Identification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1983 by setting out, for the period 1st January 1998 up to and including 31st December 2001, the shape, dimensions and colour of licence identification discs issued by district councils.

While the shape and dimensions of the disc remain unchanged throughout the period, its colour varies each year so that it must be blue where a dog licence or block licence is issued in 1998, orange where such a licence is issued in 1999, green where such a licence is issued in 2000 and yellow where such a licence is issued in 2001.

The Regulations also revoke the Dogs (Licensing and Identification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1993.
