# The Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

Coming into operation

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 13th May 2019.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

###### 3

The Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004] shall be amended as provided by regulation 4.

###### 4

In regulation 4(4)(b) for “£100” substitute “£200”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 11th April 2019.

Jason Foy — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs — 11

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Common Agricultural Policy (Review of Decisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. 2015 No. 318](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/318)).

These Regulations increase the level of fee to be paid by applicants for the review of a relevant determination made by the Department by an independent Panel.
