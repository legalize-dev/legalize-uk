# The Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Amendment No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 11th October 2018

Coming into operation: 1st November 2018

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(4) and (5) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971[^f00002], as adapted by section 7(9) of that Act and now vested in it[^f00003] and after consultation with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs in accordance with section 7(7) of that Act.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) (Amendment No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2018.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001

###### 2

- (1) Schedule 1 to the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00005] (which specifies the controlled drugs to which section 7(4) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 applies) is amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph 1(a) of Part 1[^f00006] —

- (a) after “Cannabinol” insert “(not being the product specified in paragraph 5 (1) or (2) of Part 2 of this Schedule)”;

- (b) after “Cannabinol derivatives not being dronabinol or its stereoisomers” insert “(and not being the product specified in paragraph 5(1) or (2) of Part 2 of this Schedule)”;

- (c) for “Cannabis (not being the substance specified in paragraph 4 of Part 2 of this Schedule)”, substitute “Cannabis (not being the substance specified in paragraph 4 of Part 2 of this Schedule or product specified in paragraph 5(1) or (2) of Part 2 of this Schedule)”; and

- (d) after “Cannabis resin” insert “(not being the product specified in paragraph 5(1) or (2) of Part 2 of this Schedule)”.

- (3) In Part 2 (which specifies controlled drugs excepted from Part 1), after paragraph 4 insert—

> > (5)
>   (1) A cannabis based product for medicinal use in humans.
>   (2) A product which is—
>     (a) specified in Part 1 as a consequence of the application of paragraphs 2 to 5 of Part 1 to a preparation or other product (not being the substance specified in paragraph 4 of Part 2) which is or contains cannabis, cannabis resin, cannabinol or a cannabinol derivative (not being dronabinol or its stereoisomers ); and
>     (b) produced for medicinal use in humans.
>   (3) In this paragraph—
>     - “cannabis based product for medicinal use in humans” means a preparation or other product (not being the substance specified in paragraph 4 of Part 2), which— is or contains cannabis, cannabis resin, cannabinol or a cannabinol derivative (not being dronabinol or its stereoisomers); is produced for medicinal use in humans; and is— a medicinal product, or a substance or preparation for use as an ingredient of, or in the production of an ingredient of, a medicinal product; and
>     - “medicinal product” has the same meaning as in the Human Medicines Regulations 2012[^f00007].
>   (4) In this Schedule “dronabinol” does not include any substance which—
>     (a) has the international non-proprietary name dronabinol (recommended by the World Health Organisation); and
>     (b) is derived from cannabis, cannabis resin or their constituents,
>   and stereoisomers of dronabinol are to be construed accordingly.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 7(3) of the Misuse of Drugs Act [1971 (c.38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/38) (the 1971 Act) requires regulations to be made to allow the use for certain purposes, including medical use, of the drugs which are subject to control under that Act. Section 7(4)(b) of the 1971 Act provides, however, that designated controlled drugs will be exempt from this easement and so cannot lawfully be prescribed, administered, produced, compounded or supplied except under licence or other authority issued by the Department of Health. The designations in question are in the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 (the 2001 Order) and include cannabis, cannabis resin, cannabinol and cannabinol derivatives not being dronabinol or its stereoisomers. Article 2 varies the 2001 Order to exclude cannabis based products for medicinal use in humans, and some related products, from the relevant designations.
