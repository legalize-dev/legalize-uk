# The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008

Made: 20th November 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 [^f00001].

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2008.

- (2) In this Order

- “the 2004 Order” means the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004

#### Commencement

###### 2

The provisions of the 2004 Order specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into operation on 2nd February 2009.

## SCHEDULE

###### 1

Article 10 (live links in criminal proceedings)

###### 2

Article 11 (effect of, and rescission of, direction)

###### 3

Article 12 (Magistrates’ Court permitted to sit at other locations)

###### 4

Article 13 (warning to jury)

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 20th November 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation on 2nd February 2009 provisions of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 dealing with the provisions of live links in criminal proceedings.
