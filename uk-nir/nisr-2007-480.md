# The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 20th November 2007

Coming into operation: 17th December 2007

The Department of the Environment makes the following By-Laws in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 17th December 2007.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00003] and the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004] are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 20th November 2007.

Stanley Duncan — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the By-Laws)

These By-Laws revoke and replace with amendments the Schedule to the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-Laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951.

They make provision for new taxi stands at Bedford Street, Brunswick Street, Donegall Street, Dublin Road, University Road and Waring Street.

In the taxi stand on the east side of Great Victoria Street “the hours available for use” are amended from “7.00 p.m. to 7.00 p.m.” to “7.00 p.m. to 7.00 a.m.” to correct an earlier drafting error.
