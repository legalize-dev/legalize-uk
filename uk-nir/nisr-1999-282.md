# Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 23rd June 1999

Coming into operation: 28th July 1999

The Department of Agriculture, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, and shall come into operation on 28th July 1999.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

###### 2

Regulation 2(1) of the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00004] shall be amended by substituting for the definition of “the Directive” the following definition—

> > - “the Directive” means Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market[^f00005], as amended by— Commission Directive [93/71/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0071)[^f00006]; Commission Directive [94/37/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0037)[^f00007]; Commission Directive [94/79/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0079)[^f00008]; Commission Directive [95/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0035)[^f00009]; Commission Directive [95/36/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0036)[^f00010]; Commission Directive [96/12/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0012)[^f00011]; Commission Directive [96/46/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0046)[^f00012]; Commission Directive [96/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0068)[^f00013]; Council Directive [97/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0057)[^f00014]; Commission Directive [97/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0073)[^f00015]; Commission Directive [98/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0047)[^f00016]; Commission Directive [1999/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0001)[^f00017];

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00018] and the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00019] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland on

Liam McKibben — Assistant Secretary — 23rd June 1999.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No. 371](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/371)), as amended by [S.R. 1996 No. 456](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/456), [S.R. 1997 No. 471](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/471), [S.R. 1997 No. 507](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/507) and [S.R. 1999 No. 57](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/57) (“the principal Regulations”) which implement in Northern Ireland Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) (O.J. No. L230, 19.8.91, p. 1 to be read with the Corrigenda published in O.J. No. L170, 25.6.92, p. 40) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market (“the Directive”), as amended.

The Regulations amend the definition of the Directive provided in the principal Regulations in consequence of Commission Directive [97/73/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0073) (O.J. No. 353, 24.12.97, p. 26) and Commission Directive [1999/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0001) (O.J. No. L21, 28.1.99 p. 21) which include two active substances (respectively imazalil and kresoxim methyl) into Annex I to the Directive.

The Regulations revoke the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 456](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/456)) and the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No. 57](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/57)), both of which make amendments that are consolidated in the amendments made by the present Regulations.
