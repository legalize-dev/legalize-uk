# The Water and Sewerage Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 16th June 2010

The Department for Regional Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Water and Sewerage Services (2006 Order) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010.

- (2) In this Order—

- “the 2006 Order” means the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2006 Order specified in the Schedule is 16th June 2010.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS COMING INTO OPERATION ON 16th June 2010

| *Provision* | *Subject matter* |
| --- | --- |
| Article 70 | Water resources management plans: preparation and review |
| Article 71 | Water resource management plans: publication and representations |
| Article 72 | Water resource management plans: supplementary |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 16th June 2010.

John Mills — A senior officer of the — Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences Articles 70, 71, and 72 of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 on 16th June 2010.
