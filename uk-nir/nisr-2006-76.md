# The Credit Unions (Limit on Membership) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 2nd March 2006

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 7(3) of the Schedule to the Northern Ireland Act 2000

Coming into operation: 1st April 2006

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13(3) of the Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985[^f00002], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Credit Unions (Limit on Membership) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2006.

#### Limit on membership

###### 2

Article 13(2) of the Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985 (which provides that the maximum number of members of a credit union shall be 5,000) is amended, so as to substitute “10,000” for “5,000”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 2nd March 2006.

Mike Bohill — A senior officer of the — Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Credit Unions (Limit on Membership) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 substitutes for the maximum membership limit of 5,000 for a credit union a new maximum membership limit of 10,000.
