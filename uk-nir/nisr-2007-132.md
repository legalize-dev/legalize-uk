# The Health and Social Services Trusts (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 5th March 2007

Coming into operation: 1st April 2007

In accordance with paragraph 23(3) of Schedule 3 to that Order the prescribed consultation has taken place with the relevant Health and Social Services Councils and staff interests [^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Services Trusts (Dissolution) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2007.

#### Dissolution of Health and Social Services Trust

###### 2

The Health and Social Services trusts listed in Schedule 1 are dissolved.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The statutory rules listed in Schedule 2 are revoked

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the dissolution of the health and social services trusts listed in Schedule 1 on 1st April 2007.

It revokes the health and social services trust establishment orders, as amended, listed in Schedule 2 from that date.
