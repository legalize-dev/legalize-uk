# The Rates (Exemption for Automatic Telling Machines in Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 23rd June 2020

Affirmed by resolution of the Assembly on: 7th July 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

The Department of Finance[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 42(1G) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rates (Exemption for Automatic Telling Machines in Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020 and comes into operation on the day after the day on which it is affirmed by resolution of the Assembly.

#### Definition of “relevant year”

###### 2

For the purposes of the definition of “relevant year” in Article 42(1G) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977 the Department of Finance specifies the later date of 1st April 2021.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Rates (Exemption for Automatic Telling Machines in Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00003] is revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 23rd June 2020

Alan Brontë — A senior officer of the Department of Finance

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 42(1F) and (1G) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977 (“the 1977 Order”) provide that there shall be distinguished in the NAV list as wholly exempt from rates any automatic telling machine which is situated in a rural area during a relevant year ending before such date as is specified by an Order made by the Department of Finance. 1st April 2021 is specified by this Order.

This Order also revokes the Rates (Exemption for Automatic Telling Machines in Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 which specified 1st April 2017 for the purposes of Article 42(1F) and (1G) of the 1977 Order.
