# The Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001

Made: 21st March 2001

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 79 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001.

###### 2

The provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 specified in the Schedule to this Order shall come into force on 30th March 2001.

## SCHEDULE — **Provisions of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 coming into force on 30th March 2001**

| Provision of Act | Subject matter of provision |
| --- | --- |
| Section 20 | Police districts |
| Section 39 | Appointment of police trainees |
| Section 40 | Appointment of police reserve trainees |
| Section 41 | Status etc. of trainees |
| Section 46 | Discrimination in appointments |
| Section 47 | Expiry, renewal and repeal of temporary provisions |
| Section 50 | Service by members of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve with other police services |
| Section 70 | The Royal Ulster Constabulary GC Foundation |
| Section 78 for the purposes of the provision of Schedule 6 mentioned below | Amendments, transitional and transitory provisions and repeals |
| Schedule 6, paragraph 24(3) |  |

## Signed

John Reid — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 21st March 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Commencement Order, which is the second to be made under the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 brings into force on 30th March 2001 the provisions of that Act specified in the Schedule to the Order.
