# The Houses in Multiple Occupation (Space Standard) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 7th March 2019

Coming into operation: 1st April 2019

The Department for Communities [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 43(5) and (6) of the Houses in Multiple Occupation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 [^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Houses in Multiple Occupation (Space Standard) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2019.

#### Measurement of the area of the room to determine the Space Standard

###### 2

- (1) The Department specifies that for the purposes of section 43 (Space Standard) of the Houses in Multiple Occupation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, the floor area of a room is to be determined in the following manner.

- (2) All measurements of the floor area shall be made at floor level and shall extend to the back of any projected skirting.

- (3) A determination of the floor area shall include:—

- (a) the floor area of a room has a sloping roof or ceiling so as to reduce the height of the room to less than 1.525 metres (5 feet);

- (b) the floor area is formed by a projecting chimney breast or hot press; or

- (c) the floor area immediately behind the door is no wider than the door itself;

then that floor area shall be excluded from the determination.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 7th March 2019.
