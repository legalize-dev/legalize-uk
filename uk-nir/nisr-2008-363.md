# The Magistrates’ Courts (Costs in Criminal Cases) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 3rd September 2008

Coming into operation: 1st October 2008

The Magistrates’ Courts Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and section 7 of the Costs in Criminal Cases Act (Northern Ireland) 1968[^f00002] after consultation with the Lord Chancellor and with the agreement of the Lord Chief Justice.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Costs in Criminal Cases) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2008.

#### Amendment to the Magistrates’ Courts (Costs in Criminal Cases) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1988

###### 2

In rule 5 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Costs in Criminal Cases) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1988[^f00003], for “section 2”, substitute “section 2 or 3”.

## Signed

George Conner — P Kelly — John Rea — John P B Maxwell — Sean McCann — Dated 3rd September 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Magistrates’ Courts (Costs in Criminal Cases) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1988 so as provide that rule 5 refers to an order made under section 2 or 3 of the Costs in Criminal Cases Act (Northern Ireland) 1968.
