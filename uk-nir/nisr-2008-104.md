# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 11) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 11th March 2008

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 11) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 250 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00002] (resolution of disputes)—

- (a) for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 12th March 2008, and

- (b) for all other purposes, is 6th April 2008.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 11th March 2008

John O’Neill — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of Article 250 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005—

- for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations relating to the internal dispute resolution procedure, on 12th March 2008; and

- for all other purposes, on 6th April 2008.
