# Motor Hackney Carriage (Newry) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made: 26th May 2000

Coming into operation: 7th July 2000

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Bye-Laws:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Bye-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriage (Newry) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 7th July 2000.

#### Bye-Laws amended

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Bye-Laws made in respect of Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire by the Urban District Council of Newry on 8th April 1968 and confirmed by the Ministry of Home Affairs on 25th April 1968[^f00002] there shall be substituted—

> > **SCHEDULE**

| *(1)* | *(2)* | *(3)* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Street | Site in Street | Number of Motor Hackney Carriages |
| Margaret Square | North-East and North- West sides. Motor hackney carriages to face south. | 8 |
| Merchants Quay | East Side: from a point approximately 15 metres south of Needham Bridge to a point approximately 39 metres south of that bridge. Motor hackney carriages to face north. | 4 |

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Motor Hackney Carriage (Newry) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1983[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 26th May 2000.

J. Ritchie — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Bye-Laws.)

These Bye-Laws amend the Bye-Laws relating to Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire made by Newry Urban District Council on 8th April 1968 by reducing the number of taxi stands in Merchants Quay from 8 to 4.

The Motor Hackney Carriage (Newry) (Amendment) Bye-Laws (Northern Ireland) 1983 are revoked as a consequence of these Bye-Laws.
