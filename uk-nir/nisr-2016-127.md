# The Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Register of Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 2nd March 2016

Coming into operation: 1st October 2016

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20 of the Licensing of Pavement Cafés Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing of Pavement Cafés (Register of Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and come into operation on 1st October 2016.

#### Matters to be notified

###### 2

A district council must notify the person responsible for keeping the licensing register of matters as set out in Schedule 1.

#### Documents to be sent

###### 3

A district council must send the person responsible for keeping the licensing register copies of documents as set out in Schedule 2.

#### Matters to be recorded

###### 4

The person responsible for keeping the licensing register must record particulars of matters relating to a relevant pavement café licence as set out in Schedule 1. These particulars of matters must be recorded in column 6A of the Schedule to The Licensing (Register of Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00003].

#### Documents to be kept

###### 5

The person responsible for keeping the licensing register must keep all documents as set out in Schedule 2.

6. A copy of a relevant pavement café licence suspension notice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations set out the matters a district council must notify, and the documents it must send, to the person responsible for maintaining the Register of Licences under Article 34 of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (licences permitting premises to sell intoxicating liquor by retail to the public) in relation to a relevant pavement café licence. It also sets out the matters the person responsible for maintaining the Register of Licences must record in the register and the documents that must be kept.
