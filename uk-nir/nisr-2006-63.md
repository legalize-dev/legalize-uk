# The Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2006

Made: 17th February 2006

The Secretary of State in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Article 1(3) of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 [^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order (Commencement No. 3) Order 2006.

###### 2

The provisions of Part V, in so far as it is not already commenced and Schedules 1 and 2 of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall come into operation on 3 April 2006.

## Signed

David Hanson — One of Her Majesty’s Principle Secretaries of State Northern Ireland Office — 17th February 2006

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Commencement Order brings into operation Part V, in so far as it is not already commenced and Schedules 1 and 2 of the Criminal Justice (Evidence) Order 2004 on 3 April 2006.
