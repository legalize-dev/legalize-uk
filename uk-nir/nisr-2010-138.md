# The Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 31st March 2010

Coming into operation: 1st April 2010

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(3) of the Mesothelioma, etc., Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2010.

- (2) In regulation 2—

- (a) paragraph (a) shall apply in relation to a person—

- (i) first diagnosed with mesothelioma on or after 1st April 2010; or

- (ii) where the date of their first diagnosis with mesothelioma is unknown, if a claim is made by that person on or after 1st April 2010; and

- (b) paragraph (b) shall apply in relation to the dependant of a person who dies on or after 1st April 2010.

#### Amendment of the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations

###### 2

In the Schedule to the Mesothelioma Lump Sum Payments (Conditions and Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002]—

- (a) for Table 1 substitute the Table set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations; and

- (b) for Table 2 substitute the Table set out in Part 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations.
