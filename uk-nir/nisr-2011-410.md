# Superannuation (Commissioner for Older People for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 6th December 2011

Coming into operation: 30th December 2011

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and effect

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation) (Commissioner for Older People for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011, shall come into operation on 30th December 2011 and shall have effect from 8th February 2011.

#### Addition to list of offices

###### 2

There shall be added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, the office of Commissioner for Older People for Northern Ireland[^f00003].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 6th December 2011

Grace Nesbitt — A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order adds the office of Commissioner of Older People for Northern Ireland to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes. It has retrospective effect by virtue of Article 3(7) of that Order.
