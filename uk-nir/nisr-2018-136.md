# The Agriculture (Student fees)(Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 4th July 2018

Coming into operation: 1st September 2018

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5A(1) and (2) of the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (Student fees)(Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on the 1st September 2018.

#### Amendment to the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

The Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] shall be amended as provided by regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

In regulation 4 (1)[^f00004] for “£1,595” substitute “£1,645”:

###### 4

In regulation 4 (2)[^f00005] for “£9,000” substitute “£9,250”:

###### 5

In regulation 5(2)[^f00006] for “£780” substitute “£805”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 4th July 2018

Seamus McErlean — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007.

These Regulations increase the level of fee to be paid by persons undertaking a course of higher education provided by the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs as set out in Regulations 3 and 4.
