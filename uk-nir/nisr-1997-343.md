# Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 18th July 1997

Coming into operation: 18th August 1997

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 14, 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], and with the concurrence of the Treasury, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 18th August 1997.

- (2) In these Regulations, a Schedule referred to by number means the Schedule so numbered in the Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00003].

#### Transitional provisions

###### 2

These Regulations shall apply to all determinations and re-determinations made on or after 18th August 1997.

#### Amendment to Schedule 1

###### 3

Rule 6 of Schedule 1 shall be amended by adding after sub-paragraph (*d*) the following sub-paragraph—

> > (e) a payment made under Article 15A of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00004].

#### Amendment to Schedule 2

###### 4

Rule 6B of Schedule 2 shall be amended by adding after sub-paragraph (*b*), the following sub-paragraph—

> > (c) any arrears of payments made under Article 15A of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, *C.* — Dated 18th July 1997

We concur

Jim Dowd — Jon Owen Jones — Two of the Lords Commissioners of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 25th July 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Aid (Assessment of Resources) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 to provide that a payment made under Article 15A of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 shall be disregarded in computing disposable income and capital.
