# Eel Fishing (Licence Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 12th December 2006

Coming into operation: 1st January 2007

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure[^f00001], makes the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 15(1) and 19(1) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Eel Fishing (Licence Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2007.

#### Licence Duties

###### 2

There shall be payable to the Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland by way of licence duties in respect of licences for the use of fishing engines for the taking of eels referred to in column 1 of the Schedule the amounts respectively specified in column 2 of the Schedule.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Eel Fishing (Licence Duties) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

| The existing eel weir at Upper Row, Portna | £1630.50 |

| Eel draft net not exceeding 118 metres in length used in Lough Neagh | £119.00 |

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the licence duties payable to the Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland in respect of licences for the use of fishing engines for the taking of eels. The new duties, and the size of the increases where applicable, are set out below.

|  | Previous Rates | New Rates | Increase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | £ | £ | £ |
| Fyke Net | 8.00 | 8.00 | 0.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Toome | 1595.50 | 1630.50 | 35.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Upper Row, Portna | 1595.50 | 1630.50 | 35.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Lower Row, Portna | 1595.50 | 1630.50 | 35.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Belleek | 52.00 | 53.00 | 1.00 |
| Long line used in the Erne Catchment Area or Lough Neagh, excluding its tributaries and their branches, containing not more than 1,200 hooks | 84.50 | 86.00 | 1.50 |
| Long line used in the Erne Catchment Area or Lough Neagh, excluding its tributaries and their branches, containing over 1,200 hooks | 116.50 | 119.00 | 2.50 |
| Eel draft net not exceeding 118 metres in length used in Lough Neagh | 116.50 | 119.00 | 2.50 |
