## SCHEDULE

| *Fishing Engine* *(1)* | *Licence Duty* *(2)* |
| --- | --- |
| Fyke net | £8.00 |
| The existing eel weir at Toome | £1630.50 |
| The existing eel weir at Upper Row, Portna | £1630.50 |
| The existing eel weir at Lower Row, Portna | £1630.50 |
| The existing eel weir at Belleek | £53.00 |
| Long line used in the Erne Catchment Area or Lough Neagh, excluding its tributaries and their branches, containing not more than 1,200 hooks | £86.00 |
| Long line used in the Erne Catchment Area or Lough Neagh, excluding its tributaries and their branches, containing more than 1,200 hooks | £119.00 |
