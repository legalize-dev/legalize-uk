# The Rates (Coronavirus: Making of District Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: 3rd February 2021

Coming into operation: 5th February 2021

The Department of Finance[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 8 of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Rates (Coronavirus: Making of District Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and comes into operation on 5th February 2021.

#### Amendment of the Rates Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) of the Rates Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003], for “15th February” substitute “1st March”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 3rd February 2021

Alan Brontë — A senior officer of the Department of Finance

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the date by which a district council must make their district rate for a forthcoming rating year from 15th February to 1st March.

The change is made in order to allow district councils more time to address financial planning considerations arising from the Covid-19 pandemic.

Article 1 provides for citation and commencement.

Article 2 makes an amendment to the Rates Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 to amend the date by which a district council must make the district rates from 15th February to 1st March.
