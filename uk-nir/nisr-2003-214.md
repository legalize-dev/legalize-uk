# Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 3rd April 2003

Coming into operation: 19th May 2003

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 28(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00002] and now exercisable by it[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 19th May 2003.

#### Amendments

###### 2

- (1) The Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004] shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) to (12).

- (2) In regulation 11 (proportions and form of letters, numerals, symbols and other characters) –

- (a) for paragraph (1) there shall be substituted the following paragraph –

> > (1) Subject to paragraphs (2), (3) and (5) –
>   (a) all letters, numerals and other characters incorporated in the signs or parts of the signs shown in the diagrams in Schedules 1 to 5, 7, 10 and 12 which have a red, blue, brown, black or green background (other than those incorporated in the bottom panels of diagrams 544.1A and 674, the top panels of diagrams 2919 and 2920, the petrol price display in diagram 2919, diagrams 5001.1, 5001.2, 5003, 5003.1, 5005 and 5005.1 and the top and bottom panels of diagram 7008) shall have the proportions and form shown in Part I of Schedule 13; and
>   (b) all letters, numerals and other characters incorporated in the signs or parts of the signs shown in the diagrams in Schedules 1 to 5, 7, 10 and 12 which have a white, yellow or orange background (other than those incorporated in the bottom panels of diagrams 544.1A and 674, the top panels of diagrams 2919 and 2920 and the top and bottom panels of diagram 7008) shall have the proportions and form shown in Part II of Schedule 13.

- (b) for paragraph (4) there shall be substituted the following paragraph –

> > (4) Any letters, numerals or other characters incorporated in the bottom panels of diagrams 544.1A and 674, the top panels of diagrams 2919 and 2920, the petrol price display in diagram 2919 and the top and bottom panels of diagram 7008, may have proportions and form other than the proportions and form shown in Schedule 13.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997. The principal amendments are to –

- (a) regulation 11 to permit the top and bottom panels of the sign shown in diagram 7008 to have proportions and form other than those shown in Schedule 13 (regulation 2(2));

- (b) regulation 21(2)(b) to redefine the expression “bus lane” (regulation 2(3));

- (c) transfer the sign in diagram 674.1 from Schedule 2 to Schedule 1 and re-number it as diagram 544.1A (regulation 2(4) and (5));

- (d) the sign in diagram 784 by changing the dimensions and weight of vehicles to which it applies and by retaining its original vehicular application as a permitted variant (regulation 2(6));

- (e) add the sign in diagram 881 to indicate a waterway (and bridge, if appropriate) (regulation 2(7));

- (f) add a permitted variant to the signs in diagrams 958 and 959 to permit the use of with-flow bus lanes by motorcycles (regulation 2(8)(a) and (b));

- (g) add a permitted variant to the sign in diagram 961 to permit a reference to the month of the year to be added (regulation 2(8)(c)); and

- (h) add the sign in diagram 7008 to indicate the nature of street works, giving the names of the employer and contractor and an emergency telephone number (regulation 2(9)).

These Regulations have been notified to the European Commission pursuant to Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) of 22nd June 1998 laying down a procedure for the provision of information in the field of technical standards and regulations and of rules on Information Society Services (O.J. No. L204, 21.07.98, p. 37-48) as amended by Directive [98/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0048) of 20th July 1998 (O.J. No. L217, 05.08.98, p. 18-26).
