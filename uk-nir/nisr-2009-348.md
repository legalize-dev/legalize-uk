# The Feed (Specified Undesirable Substances) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 21st October 2009

Coming into operation: 23rd November 2009

There has been consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of section 84(1) of the Agriculture Act 1970 or as appropriate of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00004] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Feed (Specified Undesirable Substances) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 23rd November 2009.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

After Chapter D of Schedule 5 (prescribed limits for undesirable substances) to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00006], as Chapter E add the entries set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1 These Regulations make further amendments to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 ([S.R. 2005 No.545](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/545) as already amended by [S.R. 2006 No. 18](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/18), [S.R. 2006 No. 427](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/427), [S.R. 2006 No. 471](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/471), [S.R. 2007 No. 450](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/450), [S.R. 2008 No.260](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/260) and [S.R. 2009 No. 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2009/27)) (“the Feeding Stuffs Regulations”).

2 These Regulations provide for the implementation of Commission Directive [2009/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0008) amending Annex I to Directive [2002/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards maximum levels of unavoidable carry-over of coccidiostats or histomonostats in non-target feed (OJ No. L40, 11.2.2009, p.19).

3 The Regulations amend Schedule 5 to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations by the addition of a Chapter E setting specified limits in relation to the carry-over into non-target feeding stuffs of certain zootechnical feed additives, (*regulation 2 and the Schedule*).
