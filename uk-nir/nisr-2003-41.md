# Planning (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 28th January 2003

Coming into operation: 3rd March 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 127 and 129(1) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These regulations may be cited as the Planning (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 3rd March 2003.

#### Amendment of the Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (8).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) after the definition of “the Commission” insert the following definition–

> > - “deemed refusal” means a refusal of planning permission deemed to have been made by virtue of regulation 9(7) or 9(8) of the Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999.

- (3) In regulation 9(1)(b) and regulation 9(2)(a)(iii) after the word “refusal” insert the words “or deemed refusal”.

- (4) In regulations 11(1) (reduced fees and refunds), 13(3) (fee for certain deemed applications), 16 (fees for applications for listed building consent) and 19(2) (fee for certain statutory authorities) for “£35” substitute “£40”.

- (5) In regulation 18(1) (fee for application for a determination) for “£31” substitute “£35”.

- (6) In Schedule 1–

- (a) in Part I, in paragraphs 4(1) (fee for application or deemed application by certain non-profit making organisations), 5(2) and (3) (fee for certain applications for approval of reserved matters) for “£310” substitute “£355”; and

- (b) for Part II, substitute Part II set out in the Schedule to these regulations.

- (7) In Schedule 2 (fees for applications for consent to display advertisements)–

- (a) for “£58” where it twice occurs substitute “£65”; and

- (b) for “£267” substitute “£305”.

- (8) In regulation 19(1)(e) for “the Police Authority for Northern Ireland” substitute “the Northern Ireland Policing Board”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Planning (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 (“the 1995 Regulations”) which prescribe fees payable to the Department of the Environment in respect of applications made under the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991–

- (a) for planning permission for development of land and for approval of matters reserved in an outline planning permission;

- (b) for consent for the display of advertisements;

- (c) for listed building consent.

The Regulations increase the following fees by approximately 14%–

- (i) reduced fees, certain fees for deemed applications, for listed building consent and fees for certain statutory authorities (regulation 2(4));

- (ii) fees for applications for determination as to the need for planning permission or listed building consent (regulation 2(5));

- (iii) fees for applications or deemed application by certain non-profit making organisations and for certain applications for approval of reserved matters (regulation 2(6));

- (iv) fees for applications for consent to display advertisements (regulation 2(7)).

The general effect of these regulations will be to increase planning fees by approximately 14% overall.
