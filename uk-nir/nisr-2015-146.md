# The Shared Parental Leave and Statutory Shared Parental Pay (Consequential Amendments to Subordinate Legislation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 12th March 2015

Coming into operation: 15th March 2015

The Department for Employment and Learning makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(1) and (2) of the Work and Families Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Shared Parental Leave and Statutory Shared Parental Pay (Consequential Amendments to Subordinate Legislation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

- (2) The provisions of this Order specified in article 26 come into operation on 15th March 2015.

- (3) The remaining provisions of this Order come into operation on 5th April 2015.

- (a) in the title, after “adoption pay period,” insert “shared parental pay period,”;

- (b) omit sub-paragraph (2)(aa);

- (c) at the end of paragraph (2)(b) for “.” substitute “; or”;

- (d) after paragraph (2)(b) insert—

> > (c) the shared parental pay period in respect of which statutory shared parental pay is paid to a person.

- (e) in paragraph (5)—

- (i) the words after “In this regulation” become sub-paragraph (a);

- (ii) omit “additional paternity pay period” and “additional statutory paternity pay”

- (iii) after sub-paragraph (a) (as renumbered) insert—

> > (b) “statutory shared parental pay” means statutory shared parental pay payable in accordance with Part 12ZC of that Act and “shared parental pay period” means the weeks in respect of which statutory shared parental pay is payable to a person under section 167ZZ2(2) of that Act.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order makes amendments to secondary legislation in consequence of Part 2 of the Work and Families Act (Northern Ireland) 2015 which makes provision for new entitlements to shared parental leave and statutory shared parental pay in place of additional paternity leave and additional statutory paternity pay.
