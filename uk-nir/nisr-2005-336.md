# The Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 14th July 2005

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by Article 1 (2) of the Special Educational Needs and Disability (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Special Educational Needs and Disability (2005 Order) (Commencement No 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1st September 2005 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Special Educational Needs and Disability (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 listed in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE — PROVISIONS OF THE SPECIAL EDUCATIONAL NEEDS AND DISABILITY (NORTHERN IRELAND) ORDER 2005 COMING INTO OPERATION ON 1ST SEPTEMBER 2005

| Provisions | Subject matter of Provision |
| --- | --- |
| Part II and Schedule 1 | Special Educational Needs |
| Chapter I of Part III and Part I of schedule 2 | Disability Discrimination in Education – Schools |
| Chapter III of Part III | Disability Discrimination in Education – General Qualifications Bodies |
| Articles 43 to 48 so far as relating to Chapters I and III of Part III | Miscellaneous |
| Article 50(1) and Schedule 5 | Minor and Consequential Amendments |
| Article 50(2) and schedule 6 except so far as relating to the [Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (c50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/50) | Unopposed appeals |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 14th July 2005

June Ingram — Senior Officer of the — Department of Education

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation on 1 September 2005 the provisions of the Special Educational Needs and Disability (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (S.I. [2005/1117 (N.I. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2005/1117)) specified in the Schedule. The provisions being brought into operation relate to special educational needs and disability discrimination in schools.
