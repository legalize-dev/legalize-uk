# Superannuation (Victims and Survivors Service Limited) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 14th December 2012

Coming into operation: 11th February 2013

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Victims and Survivors Service Limited) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, shall come into operation on 11th February 2013 and shall have effect from 1st November 2012.

#### Addition to list of employments

###### 2

There shall be added to the kinds of employment listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, employment by Victims and Survivors Service Limited[^f00003].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 14th December 2012

John McKibbin — A senior officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order adds employment by Victims and Survivors Service Limited to the kinds of employment listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes. It has retrospective effect from 1 November 2012 by virtue of Article 3(7) of that Order.
