# The Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 5th March 2002

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 61(2) of the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002.

- (2) In this Order:

- “the Act” means the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001.

#### Appointed Days

###### 2

- (1) 1st April 2002 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of section 39 of the Act.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act set out in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order (the subject matter of each provision being set out in column (2)) is 1st April 2002.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 1st April 2002 certain provisions of the Health and Personal Social Services Act (Northern Ireland) 2001, namely section 39 (repeal of law about fund-holding practices) and Schedule 5, in so far as the repeals in that Schedule are relevant to that provision.
