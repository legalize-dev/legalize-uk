# The Grants to Water and Sewerage Undertakers Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made: 24th January 2017

Coming into operation: 1st April 2017

The Department for Infrastructure [^f00001] makes this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 213(4A) of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 [^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Grants to Water and Sewerage Undertakers Order (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2017.

#### Amendment to the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006

###### 2

In paragraph (3) of Article 213 of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 substitute “the period ending on 31st March 2017” with “the period ending on 31st March 2022”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 24th January 2017

Linda MacHugh — A senior officer of the — Department for Infrastructure

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Article 213 of the Water and Sewerage Services (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 to extend the period during which the Department for Infrastructure shall make grants to relevant undertakers, in accordance with that Article, to 31st March 2022.
