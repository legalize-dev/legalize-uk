# Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2011

Made: 21 October 2011

The Department of Justice makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(4) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 [^f00001] and now vested in it [^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2011.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The following provisions of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 shall come into operation on the 1st January 2012—

- (a) Article 45 (supervised activity order for default in payment of certain fines); and

- (b) Schedule 3 (supervised activity orders: further provisions).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 21 October 2011

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation Article 45 of and Schedule 3 to the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 on 1st January 2012. These provisions provide the court with the power to make a Supervised Activity Order (SAO) as an alternative to committal to custody for fine default.
