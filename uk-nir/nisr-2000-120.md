## SCHEDULE 2 — **Prohibition of Certain Substances Hazardous to Health for Certain Purposes**

###### 1

2-naphthylamine; benzidine; 4-aminodiphenyl; 4-nitrodiphenyl; their salts and any substance containing any of those compounds, in a total con-centration equal to or greater than 0·1 per cent by mass.

###### 2

Sand or other substance containing free silica.

###### 3

A substance—

- (a) containing compounds of silicon calculated as silica to the extent of more than 3 per cent by weight of dry material, other than natural sand, zirconium silicate (zircon), calcined china clay, calcined aluminous fireclay, sillimanite, calcined or fused alumina, olivine; or

- (b) composed of or containing dust or other matter deposited from a fettling or blasting process.

###### 4

Carbon disulphide.

###### 5

Oils other than white oil, or oil of entirely animal or vegetable origin or entirely of mixed animal and vegetable origin.

###### 6

Ground or powdered flint or quartz other than natural sand.

###### 7

Ground or powdered flint or quartz other than—

- (a) natural sand; or

- (b) ground or powdered flint or quartz which forms parts of a slop or paste.

###### 8

Dust or powder of a refractory material containing not less than 80 per cent of silica other than natural sand.

###### 9

White phosphorus.

###### 10

Hydrogen cyanide.

###### 11

Benzene and any substance containing benzene in a concentration equal to or greater than 0·1 per cent by mass, other than—

- (a) motor fuels covered by Council Directive [85/210/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0210) (OJ No. L96, 3.4.85, p. 25);

- (b) waste covered by Council Directive [75/442/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0442) (OJ No. L194, 25.7.75, p. 39), as amended by Council Directives [91/156/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0156) (OJ No. L78, 26.3.91, p. 32) and [91/689/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0689) (OJ No. L377, 31.12.91, p. 20).

###### 12

The following substances—

- Chloroform CAS No. 67–66–3;

- Carbon Tetrachloride CAS No. 56–23–5;

- 1,1,2 Trichloroethane CAS No. 79–00–5;

## Part II — Containment Measures for Health and Vetrinary Care Facilities, Laboratories and Animal Rooms

###### 1

The workplace is to be separated from any other activities in the same building.

###### 2

Input air and extract air to the workplace are to be filtered using high efficiency particulate absorption filters tested in accordance with and matching the requirements of BS: 3928 or equivalent.

###### 3

Access is to be restricted to authorised persons only.

###### 4

The workplace is to be sealable to permit disinfection.

###### 5

Specified disinfection procedures.

###### 6

The workplace is to be maintained at an air pressure negative to atmosphere.

###### 7

Efficient vector control e.g. rodents and insects.

###### 8

Surfaces impervious to water and easy to clean.

###### 9

Surfaces resistant to acids, alkalis, solvents, disinfectants.

###### 10

Safe storage of biological agents.

###### 11

An observation window, or alternative, is to be present, so that occupants can be seen.

###### 12

A laboratory is to contain its own equipment.

###### 13

Infected material, including any animal, is to be handled in a safety cabinet or isolator or other suitable containment.

###### 14

Incinerator for disposal of animal carcasses.
