# The Student Fees (Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 14th April 2014

Coming into operation: 1st September 2015

The Department for Employment and Learning makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 4(8) and 14(4) of the Higher Education (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 .

In accordance with Article 4(11)(a)(i) and (b)(i) the Department is satisfied that the increases to the basic and higher amounts are no greater than are required to maintain the value of those amounts in real terms.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Student Fees (Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2015.

#### Increases to the prescribed basic and higher amounts

###### 2

The Student Fees (Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 shall be amended as provided by regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 3 shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) For “£1,465” substitute “ £1,510 ”.

- (3) For “£3,685” substitute “ £3,805 ”.

###### 4

- (1) Regulation 4 shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) For “£720” substitute “ £740 ”.

- (3) For “£1,835” substitute “ £1,895 ”.

#### Revocation

###### 5

The Student Fees (Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 14th April 2014.

Mrs Nuala Kerr — A senior officer of the — Department for Employment and Learning
