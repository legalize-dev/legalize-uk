# The Misuse of Drugs and Misuse of Drugs (Safe Custody) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 30th July 2007

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 2

In accordance with section 31(3) of that Act, it has consulted with the Advisory Council on the Misuse of Drugs.

#### Citation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs and Misuse of Drugs (Safe Custody) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007.

#### Commencement

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraphs (2), (3) and (4), these Regulations shall come into operation on 20th August 2007.

- (2) Regulation 5(12)(b) shall come into operation on 1st September 2007.

- (3) The following regulations shall come into operation on 1st January 2008—

- (a) regulation 4(3);

- (b) regulation 5(6)(e);

- (c) regulation 5(8);

- (d) regulation 5(12)(a);

- (e) regulation 5(13) to (14).

- (4) The following regulations shall come into operation on 1st February 2008—

- (a) regulation 5(10) to (11);

- (b) regulation 5(15).

#### Interpretation

###### 3

- (1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- (a) “the 1973 Regulations” means the Misuse of Drugs (Safe Custody) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1973[^f00004];

- (b) “the 2002 Regulations” means the Misuse of Drugs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00005].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Misuse of Drugs (Safe Custody) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1973 ([S.R. 1973 No. 179](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1973/179)) (“the 1973 Regulations”), the Misuse of Drugs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No. 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/1)) (“the 2002 Regulations”) the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, ([S.R. 2006 No. 264](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/264)) and the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) (No. 4) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 (S.R [2006 No. 334](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/334)).

Regulation 4(2) amends the 1973 regulations to update the references to various premises covered by these Regulations.

Regulation 5(3) updates references in the 2002 Regulations to ‘sister’ to ‘senior registered nurse’.

Regulations 5(2) and (16) update definitions of various healthcare practitioners.

Regulations 5(5) to (7) amend regulations 8, 9 and 10 of the 2002 Regulations to allow operating department practitioners to possess and supply or offer to supply any controlled drug specified in Schedules 2, 3 or 5 or any drug specified in Schedule 4 which is contained in a medicinal product, for the purposes of administration to a patient in a ward,theatre or department, in accordance with the directions of a doctor, dentist, supplementary prescriber acting under a clinical management plan or (to the extent he is authorised to prescribe) a nurse independent prescriber. Regulation 5(5)(c) and 5(6)(b) extends the authority of a senior registered nurse or acting senior registered nurse to supply the same categories of drugs.

Regulation 5(8) amends regulation 14 of the 2002 Regulations so that a person (other than a wholesale dealer or a person responsible for dispensing and supply of medicines at a hospital or nursing home) who supplies a controlled drug for human use on a requisition must add to that requisition his name and address and send it to the relevant National Health Service agency.

Regulations 5(10) and (15) amend regulation 19 and omit Schedule 6 to the 2002 Regulations so that the prescribed form of the controlled drugs register is replaced with prescribed headings to be used in that register. Regulation 5(11) also amends regulation 20 to impose a requirement that a separate page in the register be used for each strength and form of the drug and that the class of drug, its strength and form be specified at the head of each such page.

Regulation 5(12) amends regulation 23 of the 2002 Regulations so that requisitions no longer need to be preserved for a period of 2 years and that original prescriptions mentioned in regulation 23(4) are to be sent to the relevant National Health Service agency.

Regulations 5(13) and (14) reclassify Midazolam from Part 1 of Schedule 4 to Schedule 3 to the 2002 Regulations. Regulation 5(6)(e) amends regulation 9 of the 2002 Regulations to enable Midazolam to continue to be supplied under a patient group direction and regulation 4(3) amends Schedule 1 to the 1973 Regulations so that Midazolam continues to be an exempted drug under those Regulations.

Regulations 6 and 7 repeal prospective amendments to the 2002 Regulations concerning the format of the controlled drug register which are redundant in consequence of these Regulations.
