# The Salaries (Public Services Ombudsman) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 9th March 2016

Coming into operation: 1st April 2016

The Northern Ireland Assembly Commission, in exercise of the power conferred by paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 to the Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00001] makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Salaries (Public Services Ombudsman) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2016.

#### Salary

###### 2

The annual salary payable to the Public Services Ombudsman shall be £92,000 from 1st April 2016.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Assembly Commission on 9th March 2016.

Trevor Reaney — Clerk to the Assembly

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order sets the salary for the new office of Northern Ireland Public Services Ombudsman.

This provision is made with effect from 1st April 2016 under paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 to the Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland 2016.
