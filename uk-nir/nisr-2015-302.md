# The Lands Tribunal (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 8th July 2015

Coming into operation: 10th August 2015

In accordance with that subsection it has consulted with the President of the Lands Tribunal for Northern Ireland.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Lands Tribunal (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 10th August 2015.

#### Amendment to the Lands Tribunal Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976

###### 2

The Lands Tribunal Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with rule 3.

###### 3

For Schedule 2 substitute the new Schedule 2 set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 8th July 2015.

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Lands Tribunal Rules (Northern Ireland) 1976 ([S.R. 1976 No. 146](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1976/146)) to amend the fees for appeals in relation to an entry in a NAV list and for copies of documents.
