# Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made: 29th March 2000

Coming into operation: 10th May 2000

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 9(4)(*b*) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 10th May 2000.

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

For regulation 51(8B)(*b*) of the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] (circumstances in which a person suffering from diabetes requiring insulin may hold a licence) there shall be substituted—

> > (b) he has not, during the period of twelve months ending on the date of the application, required the assistance of another person to treat an episode of hypoglycaemia suffered whilst he was driving;

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 29th March 2000.

J. Ritchie — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Driving Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 542](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/542)) by altering the conditions under which a person suffering from insulin-treated diabetes may be granted a licence to drive vehicles in sub-categories C1 and C1 + E (8.25 tonnes).
