# Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made: 19th September 2004

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 12th November 2004

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of section 26 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998[^f00001], read with section 18 of the Administration and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1962[^f00002] and after consulting, in accordance with section 26(6) of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998, the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association, hereby makes the following regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

- (2) These regulations shall come into operation on 12th November 2004, but –

- (a) regulations 2 and 3 shall have effect from the dates mentioned therein;

- (b) regulations 5 and 6 shall have effect from 1st April 2003;

- (c) regulation 4 shall have effect from 19th October 2000;

- (d) regulation 7 shall have effect from 1st April 2002;

- (e) regulation 12 shall have effect from 4th April 2001;

- (f) regulation 13(2) shall have effect from 1st September 2001;

- (g) regulation 13(3) shall have effect from 1st September 2002;

- (h) regulation 13(4) shall have effect from 1st September 2003.

- (3) In these regulations “the principal regulations” means the Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-Time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Regulations 1996[^f00003].

- (4) In these regulations “the amending regulations” means the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Regulations 2002[^f00004].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve (Full-time) (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Regulations 1996 (“the 1996 Regulations”).

Regulation 2 and 3 make retrospective changes to the previous amending regulation [2002 No. 96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/96) which also amended the 1996 regulations.

Regulation 4 amends regulation 21 of the 1996 Regulations increasing the amount of hours between the ending of a member’s daily period of duty and the next one from 8 hours to 11 hours.

Regulation 5 amends regulation 22 of the 1996 Regulations extending the disregard of casual overtime to time off as well as paid overtime compensation.

Regulation 6 amends regulation 23 of the 1996 Regulations decreasing the eight day threshold for triggering the higher rate of compensation to five.

Regulation 7 amends regulation 38 of the 1996 Regulations increasing the Removal allowance.

Regulations 8, 9, 10 and 11 remove regulations 38, 39, 40 & 45 and Schedule 6 of the 1996 Regulations.

Regulation 12 amends Schedule 3 of the 1996 Regulations changing the compensation arrangement for officers recalled from annual leave.

Regulation 13 amends Schedule 8 of the 1996 Regulations increasing the Dog handler’s allowance.
