# The Penalty Charges (Prescribed Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 17th May 2012

Coming into operation -: 4th July 2012

The Department for Regional Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 4(4) of the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Penalty Charges (Prescribed Amounts) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 4th July 2012.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In regulation 2 of The Penalty Charges (Prescribed Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 [^f00002] (penalty charges) for “£60” substitute “£90.00”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 17th May 2012

D J Millar — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend The Penalty Charges (Prescribed Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 by increasing the penalty charges for road traffic offences subject to civil enforcement from £60.00 to £90.00.
