# The Remuneration of the Chairman of the Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 29th September 2016

Coming into operation: 1st November 2016

The Department of Health[^f00001], in conjunction with the Department of Finance[^f00002], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 89(1), 106(b) and 107(6) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 [^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Remuneration of the Chairman of the Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2016.

#### Remuneration

###### 2

The chairman of the Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency, appointed by the Department of Health in accordance with Article 3(1) of the Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency (Establishment and Constitution) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00004], shall be a prescribed person for the purposes of Article 89(1)(b) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for the chairman of the Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency to be a prescribed person for the purposes of Article 89(1) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 (“the 1972 Order”).

Under Article 89(1) the Department of Health may pay such remuneration and such allowances as the Department may, with the approval of the Department of Finance, determine to members of any body established or appointed under the 1972 Order; or, a special agency; or, such other persons as may be prescribed.
