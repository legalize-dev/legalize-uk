# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2016

Made: 15th March 2016

Those powers are exercisable by the Secretary of State by virtue of Article 4(1)(a) of that Order.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2016.

#### Provisions coming into force on 17th March 2016

###### 2

The following provisions in Part 6 (social security: general) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 come into force on 17th March 2016—

- (a) Article 107(1) to (6);

- (b) in Schedule 11, paragraphs 1 to 8 and 12 to 15.

#### Amendment of earlier commencement order

###### 3

In the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2016[^f00002], in Article 5, for paragraph (a) substitute—

> > (a) in Chapter 2 (employment and support allowance)—
>   (i) Article 57(4) comes into force on 17th March 2016;
>   (ii) Article 57(1) to (3) and Article 58 come into force on 28th November 2016;

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Priti Patel — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 15th March 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order:

- brings into force provisions of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 relating to requirements to consider revising a decision before appeal, and

- amends the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2016 in respect of provision relating to employment and support allowance.
