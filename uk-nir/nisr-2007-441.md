# The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 23rd October 2007

Coming into operation: 24th October 2007

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and come into operation on 24th October 2007.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment

###### 3

- (1) The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (Interpretation) for the definition of “the Decision” substitute—

> > - “the Decision” means Commission Decision [2007/554/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0554) concerning certain protection measures against foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom[^f00005] as amended by Commission Decision [2007/588/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0588)[^f00006], Commission Decision [2007/608/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0608)[^f00007], Commission Decision [2007/663/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0663)[^f00008] and Commission Decision [2007/664/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0664)[^f00009];

- (3) In regulation 8 (Dispatch of semen, ova and embryos) after (c) insert—

> > (d) the health certificate accompanying ovine or caprine semen shall bear the following words—
> “Frozen ovine/caprine semen conforming to Commission Decision [2007/554/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0554) of 9 August 2007 concerning certain protection measures against foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom.”.
> (e) The health certificate accompanying ovine or caprine embryos shall bear the following words—
> “Frozen ovine/caprine embryos conforming to Commission Decision [2007/554/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0554) of 9 August 2007 concerning certain protection measures against foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom.”

- (4) In regulation 10 (Dispatch of animal products) for paragraph 7(b) substitute—

> > (b) medicinal products as defined in Directive [2001/83/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0083) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 November 2001 on the Community Code relating to medicinal products for human use[^f00010], medical devices manufactured utilising animal tissue which is rendered non-viable as referred to in Article 1(5)(g) of Directive [93/42/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1993/0042), veterinary medicinal products as defined in Directive [2001/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0082) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 November 2001 on the Community Code relating to veterinary medicinal products[^f00011] and investigational medicinal products as defined in Directive [2001/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0020) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 April 2001 on the approximation of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States relating to the conduct of clinical trials on medicinal products for human use[^f00012],
> provided they are accompanied by a commercial document stating that the products are for use as in-vitro diagnostic, laboratory reagents, medical products or medical devices and the products are clearly labelled “for in-vitro diagnostic use only” or “for laboratory use only” as “medicinal products” or as “medical devices”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 23rd October 2007.

Elizabeth Redmond — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend The Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.R. 2007 No. 357](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/357) as amended by [S.R. 2007 No. 364](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/364) and [S.R. 2007 No. 399](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/399)). The Regulations give effect to Commission Decision [2007/663/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0663) of 12th October 2007 and Commission Decision [2007/664/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0664) of 12th October 2007 which amend Commission Decision [2007/554/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0554) concerning certain protection measures against foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom.
