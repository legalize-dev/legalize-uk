# The Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 25th August 2005

Coming into operation: 1st October 2005

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 61(1), 66(1) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2005.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1985 by increasing some of the fees payable –

- (1) For an initial application for a vehicle licence (regulation 5(3)) the following fees have increased –

- (a) Buses –

- Class I, II and III from £63·50 to £83·00;

- Class IA, from £75·00 to £95·00;

- Class IIA, from £86·50 to £107·50; and

- Class IIIA, from £97·50 to £119·50.

- (b) Taxi, from £120·00 to £122·00.

- (2) For a re-application for a vehicle licence (regulation 9(2)) the following fees have increased –

- (a) Buses –

- Class I, II and III from £63·50 to £83·00;

- Class IA, from £75·00 to £95·00;

- Class IIA, from £86·50 to £107·50; and

- Class IIIA, from £97·50 to £119·50.

- (b) Taxi, from £120·00 to £122·00.

- (3) For a re-application for a vehicle licence made within 21 days from the date of the service of the notice of refusal (regulation 9(3)) the following fees have increased –

- (a) Buses –

- All classes, from £18·50 to £19·50.

- (b) Taxi, from £18·50 to £19·50.

- (4) For a re-application for a vehicle licence made under regulation 9(4) within 21 days from the date of the service of the notice of refusal (see regulation 9(5)) the following fees have increased –

- Buses – Class IA, from £18·50 to £19·50; Class IIA, from £30·00 to £32·00; and Class IIIA, from £42·00 to £45·00.

- (5) All other fees remain unchanged.

The Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 are revoked.
