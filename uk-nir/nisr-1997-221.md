# The Newry and Mourne Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 22nd April 1997

Coming into operation: 23rd April 1997

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 10(1) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3A of Schedule 3 to, the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This order may be cited as the Newry and Mourne Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 23rd April 1997.

#### Amendment of the establishment order

###### 2

- (1) The Newry and Mourne Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) For Article 3A (operational area of the trust) there shall be substituted—

> > (3A) The operational area of the trust shall be—
>   (a) for the purposes of the statutory provisions specified in Part I of the Schedule, the local government district of Newry and Mourne and the ward of Rathfriland in the local government district of Banbridge;
>   (b) for the purposes of the statutory provisions specified in Part II of the Schedule, Northern Ireland.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

B. McGahan — Assistant Secretary — 22nd April 1997.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This order amends the Newry and Mourne Health and Social Services Trust (Establishment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993 ([S.R. 1993 No. 455](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1993/455)) with effect from 23rd April 1997 by redefining the operational area of the trust.
