# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 28th July 2011

Coming into operation: 12th September 2011

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 12th September 2011.

- (2) This Order applies in relation to fixed penalty offences alleged to have been committed on or after 12th September 2011.

#### Amendment of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order

###### 2

- (1) The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00002] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (5).

- (2) After Article 1 insert—

> > (1A) In Schedule 2—
>   - “the AETR” means the European Agreement[^f00003] concerning the Work of Crews of Vehicles engaged in International Road Transport of 1 July 1970, as amended, as applied by Article 2(3) of the EC Regulation;
>   - “the Authorised Weights Regulations” means the Motor Vehicles (Authorised Weight) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004];
>   - “the Community Recording Equipment Regulation” has the meaning given in regulation 2(1) of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00005];
>   - “the Construction and Use Regulations” means the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00006];
>   - “the Drivers’ Hours Regulations” means the Vehicles (Drivers’ Hours of Duty) Regulations 1991[^f00007];
>   - “the EC Regulation” means Regulation [(EC) No. 561/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0561) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00008] as amended from time to time.

- (a) for “the Schedule”, substitute “Schedule 1”; and

> > (2A) The amount of the fixed penalty for an offence specified in Schedule 2 shall be the amount indicated in relation to that offence by that Schedule, by reference to the circumstances of the offence, those being the nature of the contravention, and, where specified, its seriousness.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes the amount of fixed penalties for certain fixed penalty offences by amending the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 (“the Principal Order”).

It amends the Principal Order to provide for new fixed penalty offences which have been specified as such by Article 2 of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) (Order) (Northern Ireland) 2011. The new fixed penalties relate to record keeping (including obstructing an officer or failing to comply with requirements), failure to comply with a prohibition issued in respect of a bus, coach or goods vehicle except under a public service vehicle or Roads Service licence, and failing to hold the required Community licence or authorisation.

The Order also provides that, in respect of certain offences, the applicable amount of the fixed penalty is to be determined according to the nature of the contravention or failure constituting the offence and, in specified cases, its seriousness. The offences in question relate to drivers’ hours (and related recording equipment) for goods and public passenger vehicles, certain roadworthiness defects, and overloading. The applicable amount, determined in relation to the nature of the offence and, in specified cases, its seriousness, is set out in column (2) of each of the tables in the new Schedule 2 (Article 2(4) and (5)).

An Explanatory Memorandum and a Regulatory Impact Assessment has been produced and is available from Road Safety Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
