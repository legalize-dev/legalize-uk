# The Fair Employment (Monitoring) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024

Made: 27th November 2024

Coming into operation: 9th December 2024

In accordance with Article 53(1) of that Order it has consulted with the Equality Commission for Northern Ireland,

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Fair Employment (Monitoring) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2024 and shall come into operation on 9th December 2024.

#### Amendment of the Fair Employment (Monitoring) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

###### 2

In regulation 6 of the Fair Employment (Monitoring) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004] (date or period to which prescribed information relates), after paragraph (5) insert-

> > (6) Nothing in paragraphs (2), (3) or (4) shall require an employer to include in any monitoring return information in relation to employment as a teacher in a school at any time before the coming into operation of sections 1 and 2 of the Fair Employment (School Teachers) Act (Northern Ireland) 2022[^f00005].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Executive Office on 27th of November 2024.

Richard Irwin — A senior officer of the Department

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Fair Employment (Monitoring) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, to require existing registered concerns (including public authorities) to provide monitoring information from 12th May 2024 in respect of teachers in their first annual monitoring return, following the revocation of the school teachers’ exception contained in the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998.
