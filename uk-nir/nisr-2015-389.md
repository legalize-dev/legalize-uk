# The Taxis (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 1st December 2015

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59 of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Taxis (2008 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 1 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making regulations, is 2nd December 2015.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 2 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making an application for a taxi licence, is 9th May 2016.

- (3) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in Part 3 of the Schedule, for all purposes, is 31st May 2016.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation the provisions of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 specified in—

- (a) Part 1 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making regulations, on 2nd December 2015;

- (b) Part 2 of the Schedule, for the purpose only of making an application for a taxi licence, on 9th May 2016; and

- (c) Part 3 of the Schedule, for all purposes, on 31st May 2016.
