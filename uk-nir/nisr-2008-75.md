# The Waste (Amendment) (2007 Order) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 27th February 2008

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 [^f00001]:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Waste (Amendment) (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008.

- (2) In this Order “the 2007 Order” means the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 10 of the 2007 Order shall be 7th April 2008.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 27th February 2008.

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings Article 10 of the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 (“the 2007 Order”) into operation on 7th April 2008.
