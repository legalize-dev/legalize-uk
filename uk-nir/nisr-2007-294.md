# The Waste (Amendment) (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 4th June 2007

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by Article 1(3) of the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00001]:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Waste (Amendment) (2007 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

- (2) In this Order “the 2007 Order” means the Waste (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

- (3) In this Order “the 1997 Order” means the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00002].

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2007 Order specified in the Schedule shall be 25th June 2007.
