# The Debt Relief (2010 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 21st January 2011

Coming into operation: 30th June 2011

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001] makes the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(1) of the Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00002]

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Debt Relief (2010 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, in so far as not already in operation, is 30th June 2011.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 21st January 2011

Michael J Bohill — A senior officer of the — Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Debt Relief Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, in so far as not already in operation, on 30th June 2011
