# Fisheries (Tagging and Logbook) (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 3rd December 2002

Coming into operation: 1st January 2003

The Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 26(1) and 37(1) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, with the approval of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure[^f00002], hereby makes the following Byelaws:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Byelaws may be cited as the Fisheries (Tagging and Logbook) (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2003.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries (Tagging and Logbook) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2001

###### 2

The Fisheries (Tagging and Logbook) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00003] shall be amended as provided in Byelaws 3 to 5.

#### Interpretation

###### 3

In Byelaw 2(1)–

- (a) for the definition of “game fishing rod licence” there shall be substituted the following definition–

> > - “game fishing rod licence” means a game fishing rod licence, a concessionary season fishing rod licence, or a juvenile season fishing rod licence issued pursuant to the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004];

- (b) in the definition of “logbook” for the word “one” there shall be substituted the word “three”;

- (c) the definition of “one day angling logbook” shall be deleted; and

- (d) after the definition of “tail tag” there shall be inserted the following definition–

> > - “three day angling logbook” means a logbook as set out in the format prescribed in Schedule 1;

#### Issue of logbooks

###### 4

For Byelaw 7 there shall be substituted the following Byelaw–

> > (7) The Board or an authorised agent shall issue–
>   (a) a salmon and sea trout angling logbook to the holder of a game fishing rod licence other than a three day game fishing rod licence;
>   (b) a three day angling logbook to the holder of a three day game fishing rod licence.

#### Format of three day angling logbook

###### 5

For Schedule 1 (one day angling logbook) there shall be substituted the following Schedule–

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Byelaws.)

These Byelaws amend the Fisheries (Tagging and Logbook) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2001 by–

- (a) amending the definition of “game fishing rod licence” so that it also includes concessionary and juvenile season fishing rod licences, deleting the definition of “one day angling logbook”, and inserting a definition of “three day angling logbook” in Byelaw 2(1) (Byelaw 3);

- (b) replacing references to “one day angling logbook” with references to “three day angling logbook” (Byelaws 3 and 4); and

- (c) replacing the format of the one day angling logbook prescribed in Schedule 1 with the format for a three day angling logbook (Byelaw 5).
