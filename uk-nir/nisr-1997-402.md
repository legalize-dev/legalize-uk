# Seed Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 5th September 1997

Coming into operation: 13th October 1997

The Department of Agriculture, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2) and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 13th October 1997.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981

###### 2

In the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00004] in Schedule 4, Part I (Requirements for an official label)—

- (a) for paragraph 1(*c*) there shall be substituted—

> > (c) set out:
> - EC Rules and Standards
> - Certification authority
> - Member State
> - Country of production
> - Basic Seed Potatoes
> - Class
> - Community grade (as appropriate)
> - Merchant’s number
> - Grower’s number
> - Variety indicated at least in Roman characters
> - Size
> - Net weight
> - Month and year of sealing.

- (b) after paragraph 1 there shall be added the following paragraph—

> > (2) Official labels printed prior to 13th October 1997 with the abbreviation “EEC” instead of “EC” may be used until 31st December 2001.

#### Revocation

###### 3

Regulation 12(*a*) of the Seed Potatoes (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00005] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture on

R. S. Johnston — Assistant Secretary — 5th September 1997.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Seed Potatoes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981. The Regulations implement as respects Northern Ireland Council Directive [96/72/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0072) and provide for the substitution of the letters “EC” for the letters “EEC” on official labels for seed potatoes but provide that labels printed before the commencement of the Regulations bearing the abbreviation “EEC” may continue to be used until 31st December 2001.
