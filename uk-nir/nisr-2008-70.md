# Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 25th February 2008

Coming into operation-: 1st April 2008

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972[^f00005].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2008.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00006] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 3

The Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00007] shall be amended in accordance with the Schedule.

#### Consequential amendment

###### 4

In regulation 3 of the Dairy Produce Quotas (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00008], paragraph (2) shall be omitted.

>     - “*registered wholesale quota*” means wholesale quota registered pursuant to regulation 4(3) and (4);
>     - “*relevant competent authority*” has the same meaning as it has in regulation 3 of the General Provisions Regulations;
>     - “*relevant person*” means a producer, a purchaser, any employee or agent of a producer or of a purchaser, any milk haulier, any person undertaking butterfat testing for purchasers in a laboratory, a processor of milk or milk products, or any other person involved in the buying, selling or supply of milk or milk products obtained directly from a producer or purchaser, but does not include a consumer of milk or milk products;
>     - “*Scottish Islands area*” means either— the islands of Orkney except for the island of Stronsay; or the islands of Jura, Gigha, Arran, Bute, Great Cumbrae and Little Cumbrae, the Kintyre peninsula south of Tarbert and the areas of land within the Argyll and Bute District comprising those parts of the parishes of Dunoon and Kilmun and Inverchaolain shown bounded by a red line on a map marked “Map referred to in sub-paragraph (b) of the definition of Scottish Islands area in regulation 2(1) of the Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations 2005”, dated 31st January 2005, signed on behalf of the Secretary of State and deposited at the offices of the Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs at Nobel House, 17 Smith Square, London SW1P 3JR;

>     - “*transferor*” means— where quota is transferred with a holding or part of a holding, a person who is replaced by another occupier of that holding or part of a holding; and in any other case, the person from whom quota is transferred;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which come into operation on 1st April 2008, amend the Dairy Produce Quotas Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, ([S.R. 2005 No.70](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/70)) (“the 2005 Regulations”).

The 2005 Regulations implement Council Regulation [(EC) No 1788/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1788) establishing a levy in the milk and milk products sector (OJ No L270, 21.10.2003, p 123) and [Commission Regulation (EC) No 595/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0595) laying down detailed rules for applying Council Regulation [(EC) No 1788/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1788) establishing a levy in the milk and milk products sector (OJ No L94, 31.3.2004, p 22).

Council Regulation [(EC) No 1788/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1788) is repealed on 1st April 2008 and replaced by Articles 55(1)(a) and 55(2), in so far as it relates to milk and other milk products, and Section III of Chapter III of Part II (Articles 65 to 84) of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234) establishing a common organisation of agricultural markets and on specific provisions for certain agricultural products (Single CMO Regulation) (OJ No L299, 16.11.2007, p. 1).

These Regulations replace the references in the 2005 Regulations to the provisions of the Council Regulation [(EC) No 1788/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/1788) with the corresponding references to the provisions of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234). These Regulations also re-enact the definitions in regulation 2(1) of the 2005 Regulations and add a new regulation 2(1A) which provides that the references to certain provisions of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234) and references to [Commission Regulation (EC) No 595/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0595) are references to those provisions and to that Commission Regulation as amended from time to time.
