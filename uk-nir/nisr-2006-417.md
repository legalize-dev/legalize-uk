# The Dangerous Wild Animals (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 8th November 2006

Coming into operation: 4th December 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by Article 3 (2) of the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004[^f00001]

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Dangerous Wild Animals (Fees) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 4th December 2006.

#### Fee payable in connection with an application for a licence

###### 2

An application made to the Department for a licence shall be accompanied by a fee of £80.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 8th November 2006.

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides that an application to the Department of the Environment for a licence under the Dangerous Wild Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 2004 shall be accompanied by a fee of £80.
