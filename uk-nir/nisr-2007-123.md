# The Colours in Food (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 28th February 2007

Coming into operation: 6th April 2007

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Colours in Food (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2007.

#### Amendment of the Colours in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation) of the Colours in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] for the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” there is substituted—

> > - “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” means Commission Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) laying down specific criteria of purity concerning colours for use in foodstuffs[^f00005] as amended by Directive [1999/75/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0075)[^f00006], Directive [2001/50/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0050)[^f00007], Directive [2004/47/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0047)[^f00008] and Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033)[^f00009]

”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 28th February 2007

Don Hill — A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, implement Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) amending Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) as regards sunset yellow FCF (E110) and titanium dioxide (E171) (OJ No. L82, 21.3.2006, p.10).

They do so by updating the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” (Commission Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) laying down specific criteria of purity concerning colours for use in foodstuffs, (OJ No. L226, 29.9.1995, p.l) that appears in regulation 2(1) of the Colours in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No.49](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/49), as already amended) to reflect the amendments made to that Directive by Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) (*regulation 2*).

Commission Directive [2006/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0033) amends Commission Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) by substituting revised purity criteria for the colours sunset yellow FCF (E110) and titanium dioxide (E171).

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared to accompany these Regulations, as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
