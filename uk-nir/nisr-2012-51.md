# Superannuation (Commissioner of the Northern Ireland Law Commission) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 17th February 2012

Coming into operation: 14th March 2012

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Commissioner of the Northern Ireland Law Commission) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012, and shall come into operation on 14th March 2012.

#### Addition to list of offices

###### 2

There shall be added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 with effect from 1st February 2012, the office of Commissioner of the Northern Ireland Law Commission[^f00003].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 17th February 2012

John McKibbin — A senior officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order adds the office of Commissioner of the Northern Ireland Law Commission to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes.
