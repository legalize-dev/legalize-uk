# The Addition of Vitamins, Minerals and Other Substances (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 17th December 2020

Coming into operation: 30th December 2020

In accordance with Article 47(3A) of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991, the Department of Health has had regard to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Addition of Vitamins, Minerals and Other Substances (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 30th December 2020.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Addition of Vitamins, Minerals and Other Substances (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the official seal of the Department of Health on 17th December 2020.

Dr. Michael McBride — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Addition of Vitamins, Minerals and Other Substances (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 ([S.R. 2020 No.310](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2020/310)).
