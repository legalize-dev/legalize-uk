# Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 17th November 2006

Coming into operation: 10th December 2006

The Department for Employment and Learning[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 67F of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002], and now vested in it[^f00003]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 10th December 2006.

#### Amendment

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00005] is hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE

Chairman of the Criminal Cases Review Commission

Acts or omissions which have an adverse or potentially adverse effect on fish in the sea and fish health.

Income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, petroleum revenue tax, inheritance tax, stamp duties, national insurance contributions, statutory maternity pay, statutory sick pay, tax credits, child benefits, collection of student loans and the enforcement of the national minimum wage.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 17th November 2006.

D.S.S. McAuley — A senior officer of the — Department for Employment and Learning

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Public Interest Disclosure (Prescribed Persons) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No. 401](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/401)) by replacing the Schedule to that Order. The amendments to the Schedule are the inclusion of the Northern Ireland Commissioner for Children and Young People and the Secretary of State for Transport; the inclusion of the Chairman of the Criminal Cases Review Commission in substitution for the Chief Executive of the Criminal Cases Review Commission; the deletion of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety and Auditors appointed by that Department; and amendments to the description of matters for which the Comptroller and Auditor General for Northern Ireland, the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, the local government auditors appointed by the Department of the Environment from the staff of the Northern Ireland Audit Office, the Northern Ireland Authority for Energy Regulation, Northern Ireland District Councils, the Northern Ireland Social Care Council, the Office of Communications and the Office of Fair Trading are prescribed.

The references to the Commissioners of Customs and Excise and Commissioners of the Inland Revenue have been amended to read ‘Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs’, the reference to the Health and Social Services Board Registration and Inspection Units has been amended to read ‘Regulation and Quality Improvement Authority’, and the Occupational Pensions Regulatory Authority has been amended to read ‘Pensions Regulator’.
