# The Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 12) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 1st February 2018

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 118(1) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2008 No. 2 Act) (Commencement No. 12) Order (Northern Ireland) 2018.

#### Commencement of provisions

###### 2

Paragraph 15 of Schedule 6 to the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (amendments of Schedule 6 to the 2005 Order), and section 100 of that Act in so far as it relates to that paragraph, shall come into operation on 2nd February 2018.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 1st February 2018

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation paragraph 15 of Schedule 6 to the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 (“*the Act*”) (and section 100 of the Act in so far as it relates to that paragraph) on 2nd February 2018.

Paragraph 15 of Schedule 6 to the Act amends paragraph 33 of Schedule 6 to the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/255 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2005/255)). Paragraph 33 provides a power to modify Schedule 6 in its application to such schemes as may be prescribed. The amendment adds sub-paragraphs (2) to (6) to paragraph 33 to make specific provision about the exercise of the modification power in relation to “variable-rate schemes”, as defined in sub-paragraph (3).
