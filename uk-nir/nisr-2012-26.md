# The Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 30th January 2012

Coming into operation: 1st May 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by Article 6(1) of the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 1st May 2012.

#### Amendment of the Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) Regulations

###### 2

In part V of the Schedule to the Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00003] (Endorsement) for “Article 44(1)” substitute “Article 44(1) and (2)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 30th January 2012

Michael Daly — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Registration of Clubs (Certificate of Registration) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 by inserting a reference to Article 44(2) of the Registration of Clubs (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, which provides for the endorsement of penalty points, on a certification of registration issued to clubs by the courts.
