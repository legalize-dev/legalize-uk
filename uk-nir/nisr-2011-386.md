# The Planning (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 2nd November 2011

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 254 (1) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001], makes the following Order.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning (2011 Act) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 247 and 248 of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 1st December 2011.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 2nd November 2011

Maggie Smith — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order brings into operation on 1st December 2011, sections 247 (Regulations and orders) and 248 (Amendment of certain time periods in relation to enforcement) of the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

The Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the Order on the government’s legislation website www.legislation.gov.uk.
