# The Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 8th July 2011

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 60(1) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 13(1) to (6) and 15 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00002] (work-related activity and directions about work-related activity) is 11th July 2011.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 8th July 2011

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the following provisions of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007—

- section 13(1) to (6) (work-related activity); and

- section 15 (directions about work-related activity),

on 11th July 2011.
