# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 6) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: at 7.00 p.m. on 25th September 2020

Laid before the Assembly: at 9.00 a.m. on 28th September 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1(2)

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 6) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on the date and at the time they are made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020(3) are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

###### 3

- (1) In regulation 4A(1), 4A(3), 4B(1), 4B(2), 4B(3), 4B(4), 4B(5), 4C(1) and 4C(2), for “an indoor venue” at each place where it occurs, substitute "a venue".

- (2) After regulation 4A(4) insert “(5) Regulations 4A, 4B and 4C shall not apply to a place of worship.”

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 7.00 p.m. on 25th September 2020

Lourda Geoghegan — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make changes to the places where restrictions will apply. Outdoor venues where intoxicating liquor may be consumed are now subject to regulations 4A, 4B, and 4C, and places of worship are excluded.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
