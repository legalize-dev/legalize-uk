# Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Verification Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 25th February 2009

Coming into operation: 1st April 2009

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment, with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (5) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Verification Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2009.

#### Revocation and saving

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Verification Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] are revoked.

- (2) The Regulations referred to in paragraph (1) shall continue to apply in relation to any verification carried out before 1st April 2009.

#### Interpretation

###### 3

- (1) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “the 1988 Regulations” means the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) Regulations 1988[^f00005];

- “the 2000 Regulations” means the Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2000[^f00006]; and

- “the Department” means the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment.

#### Fees relating to EEC initial verification

###### 4

- (1) Schedule 1 shall have effect for prescribing the fee to be paid in respect of the EEC initial verification of measuring instruments to which regulation 3, as modified by regulation 13(9), of the 1988 Regulations applies.

- (2) The appropriate fee shall be payable whether or not the instrument passes the EEC initial verification.

#### Fees relating to EC verification

###### 5

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2), Schedule 2 shall have effect for prescribing the fee to be paid in respect of the EC verification of measuring instruments to which regulation 3(1) of the 2000 Regulations applies.

- (2) Measuring instruments, to which regulation 3(2) of the 2000 Regulations applies, submitted for EEC initial verification under the 1988 Regulations shall be subject to the fee prescribed in Schedule 2 as if they were submitted for EC verification under the 2000 Regulations.

- (3) The appropriate fee shall be payable whether or not the instrument passes the EC verification.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe the fees to be paid in respect of EC verification under the Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) Regulations 1988 (“the 1988 Regulations”) and the Non-automatic Weighing Instruments Regulations 2000 (“the 2000 Regulations”) and increase them by an average of 3.3%.

Regulation 4 and Schedule 1 prescribe the fees to be paid in respect of EEC initial verification of measuring instruments. The obligations of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment (“the Department”) relating to EEC initial verification are as required by regulation 13 of and Schedule 3 to the 1988 Regulations.

Regulation 5 and Schedule 2 prescribe the fees to be paid in respect of EC verification of non-automatic weighing instruments. Such verification takes place in accordance with regulation 11 and, where applicable, regulation 14 of the 2000 Regulations. By a designation numbered 0326, the Department was designated as an approved body under those Regulations.

The Measuring Instruments (EEC Requirements) (Verification Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 are revoked except in relation to any verification carried out before 1st April 2009.
