# Optical Charges and Payments (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 30th July 2013

Coming into operation: 1st September 2013

In accordance with Article 62(3) of that Order it has consulted with such organisations as appeared to the Department to be representative of medical practitioners practising as ophthalmic medical practitioners and ophthalmic opticians.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Optical Charges and Payments (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 1 September 2013.

- (2) In these Regulations “the Principal Regulations” means the Health and Personal Social Services (Optical Charges and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00003].

#### Amendment of regulation 19 of the principal Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 19 of the principal Regulations (redemption value of voucher for replacement or repair)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(b) for “£54.20” substitute “£54.70”; and

- (b) in paragraph (3) for “£14.00” substitute “£14.10”.

#### Amendments of the Schedules to the principal Regulations

###### 3

- (1) In Schedule 1 to the principal Regulations (voucher letter codes and face values – supply and replacement)[^f00004], in column 3 (face value of voucher), for each amount specified in column 1 of the Table below (old amount) substitute the amount specified in relation to it in column 2 of that Table (new amount).

- (2) In Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations (prisms, tints, photochromis lenses, small and special glasses and complex appliances)—

- (a) in paragraph 1(1)(a) (prism – single vision lens) for “£12.10” substitute “£12.20”;

- (b) in paragraph 1(1)(b) (prism – other lens) for “£14.60” substitute “£14.70”;

- (c) in paragraph 1(1)(e) (small glasses)—

- (i) in sub-head (i) for “£61.20” substitute “£61.80”;

- (ii) in sub-head (ii) for “£54.20” substitute “£54.75”; and

- (iii) in sub-head (iii) for “£29.30” substitute “£29.60”;

- (d) in paragraph 1(1)(g) (specially manufactured frames) for “£61.20” substitute “£61.80”;

- (e) in paragraph 2(a) (minimum complex appliance payment – single vision lenses) for “£14.00” substitute “£14.10”; and

- (f) in paragraph 2(b) (minimum complex appliance payment – other cases) for “£35.50” substitute “£35.90”.

- (3) For Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations (voucher value – repair)[^f00005], substitute Schedule 3 as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Application of Regulations 2 and 3

###### 4

The amendments made in regulations 2 and 3 apply only in relation to a voucher accepted or used pursuant to regulation 12 or regulation 17 of the principal Regulations on or after 1 September 2013.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Health and Personal Social Services (Optical Charges and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 (“the principal Regulations”) which provide for payment to be made, by means of a voucher system, in respect of costs incurred by certain categories of persons in connection with the supply, replacement and repair of optical appliances.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 19 of the principal Regulations to increase the redemption value of a voucher issued towards the cost of replacement of a single contact lens and to increase the maximum contribution by way of a voucher to the cost of repair of a frame.

Regulation 3 and the Schedule amend the Schedules to the principal Regulations. Firstly, the value of vouchers issued towards the costs of the supply and replacement of glasses and contact lenses is increased; secondly, the additional values for vouchers for prisms, tints, photochromic lenses and special categories of appliances are increased; and thirdly, the value of vouchers issued towards the cost of the repair and replacement of optical appliances is increased.

Regulation 4 provides that the increases contained in regulation 2 and 3 apply only in relation to vouchers accepted or used on or after 1 September 2013.
