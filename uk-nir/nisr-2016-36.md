# The College Street, Belfast (Stopping-Up) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 10th February 2016

Coming into operation: 16th March 2016

Two objections have been received and subsequently withdrawn.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The College Street, Belfast (Stopping-Up) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 16th March 2016.

#### Application

###### 2

- (1) The road described in the Schedule shall be stopped-up to motor vehicles at the line specified therein.

- (2) The Department shall carry out such works as it considers necessary to prevent or restrict to such extent as the Department thinks fit, access to the road described in the Schedule by motor vehicles.

###### 3

- (1) All existing cables, wires, mains, pipes or other apparatus placed along, across, over or under the road to be stopped-up shall be retained.

- (2) All existing rights as to the use or maintenance of such cables, wires, mains, pipes or other apparatus shall be preserved.

## SCHEDULE — ROAD TO BE STOPPED UP TO MOTOR VEHICLES

College Street, Belfast, at its junction with College Avenue and College Square East more particularly delineated and shown by the line marked A-B on Map No. DR1/16/58483.

A copy of the map has been deposited at the Department’s Headquarters, Room 3-01, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast, and TransportNI Eastern Division, Hydebank, 4 Hospital Road, Belfast.
