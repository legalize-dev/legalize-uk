# The Water Environment (Floods Directive) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 27th November 2018

Coming into operation: 21st December 2018

The Department for Infrastructure, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that Section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Water Environment (Floods Directive) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 21st December 2018.

#### Amendment to the Water Environment (Floods Directive) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

###### 2

- (1) The Water Environment (Floods Directive) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 12(2)(c) omit “referred to in Annex 1 to [Directive 2008/1/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0001) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning integrated pollution prevention and control”.

- (3) In regulation 12(3) substitute “regulation 8(2) of the 2003 Regulations” with “regulation 2 of the 2017 Regulations[^f00004]”.

- (4) In regulation 17(1)(a)(viii) substitute “the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991” with “the Planning Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00005]”.

- (5) In Schedule 3, Part 1 Paragraph 4—

- (a) in sub paragraph (a) substitute “Council [Directive 85/337/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0337)” with “Council [Directive 2011/92/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2011/0092)[^f00006], as amended by [Directive 2014/52/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2014/0052)[^f00007]”;

- (b) in sub paragraph (b) substitute “Council [Directive 96/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0082)” with “Council [Directive 2012/18/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2012/0018)[^f00008]”.
