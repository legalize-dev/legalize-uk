# The Service of Certain Documents (Prescribed Body) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 27th September 2004

Coming into operation: 1st November 2004

The Department for Social Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 104(2) of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992[^f00002], and now exercisable by it[^f00003], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Service of Certain Documents (Prescribed Body) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2004.

#### Prescribed Body

###### 2

The Department hereby prescribes a registered housing association as a prescribed body for the purposes of Article 104 of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 27th September 2004.

D. M. Crothers — A senior officer of the — Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

Article 104(2) of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 (Service of Certain Documents) makes provision for the Department to prescribe a body which may serve certain documents by ordinary post. These Regulations prescribe a registered housing association as a prescribed body.
