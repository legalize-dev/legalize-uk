# The Flags (2000 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made: 7th September 2000

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Article 1(2) of the Flags (Northern Ireland) Order 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Flags (2000 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Flags (Northern Ireland) Order 2000 is the day after the day on which the Order is made.

## Signed

Peter Mandelson — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — Dated 7th September 2000

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 7th September 2000 the provisions of the Flags (Northern Ireland) Order 2000 (S.I. [2000/1347 (N.I.3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2000/1347)).
