# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 21) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: at 7.00 p.m. on 17th December 2020

Laid before the Assembly: at 9.00 a.m. on 18th December 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with Regulation 1(2)

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 21) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at the time and on the date they are made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended in accordance with these regulations.

###### 3

In the heading to Regulation 5, after “Restrictions on” insert “outdoor”.

###### 4

In regulation 5(1) omit “indoor or”.

###### 5

In the heading to Regulation 6, for “other” substitute “indoor”.

###### 6

In Regulation 6(2), for “paragraph 5” substitute “paragraph 7”.

###### 7

After regulation 6(2) insert-

> > (2A) In an indoor venue
> (a) each group at a table shall be deemed to be a separate gathering, if no entertainment is provided; and
> (b) all the persons in a room shall be deemed to be a single gathering if entertainment is provided.
> (2B) For the purposes of these Regulations, “entertainment” means
> (a) any type of performance, live or otherwise; and
> (b) dancing, singing or music or any other entertainment of a like kind.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 7.00pm on 17th December 2020

Lourda Geoghegan — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 by adding provisions in relation to entertainment venues.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
