# The Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Period) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 7th May 2013

Coming into operation: 12th June 2013

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 51(2) and 52(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Disposal of Vehicles (Prescribed Period) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 12th June 2013.

#### Prescribed period

###### 2

The prescribed period for the purposes of Articles 51(2)(c)(ii) and 52(2)(c)(ii) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 shall be 7 days.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 7th May 2013

D J Millar — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe the period (7 days) for the purposes of a notice served under Article 51(2)(c)(ii) or Article 52(2)(c)(ii) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 requiring the owner of a vehicle to remove the vehicle from the custody of a police constable or, as the case may be, the Department within the prescribed period from the day on which the notice was served.
