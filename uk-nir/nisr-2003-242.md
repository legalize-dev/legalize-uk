# Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) Revocation Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 17th April 2003

Coming into operation: 18th April 2003

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, having reason to believe that the waters in the area specified in the Schedule to the Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 are no longer infected waters, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 1(1) of the Diseases of Fish Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) Revocation Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 18th April 2003.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 17th April 2003.

N. B. Cornick — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 which contained provisions for the prevention, eradication and control of the fish disease Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis in the area as described in the Schedule to that Order.
