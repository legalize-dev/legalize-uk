# The Direct Payments to Farmers Single Application Amendment Date (Amendment) (Coronavirus) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 15th May 2020

Coming into operation: 31st May 2020

type=start slip=2020-07-01 time=1594035262540The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairstype=end slip=2020-07-01 time=1594035262540 in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 78(b) of Regulation (EU) No. 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy[^f00001] makes these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Direct Payments to Farmers Single Application Amendment Date (Amendment) (Coronavirus) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on the 31st May 2020.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

- (3) In these Regulations, “claim year 2020” is to be interpreted in accordance with section 1(7) of the Direct Payments to Farmers (Legislative Continuity) Act 2020[^f00003].

#### Amendment of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 809/2014

###### 2

For the purposes of claim year 2020, the Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 809/2014 of 17 July 2014 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) No. 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to the integrated administration and control system, rural development measures and cross compliance[^f00004] applies as if in Article 15(2), for “31 May of the year concerned” there were substituted “9 June 2020”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on the 15th May 2020.

Jason Foy — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Regulation 2 modifies the application of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 809/2014 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) No. 1306/2013 with regard to the integrated administration and control system, rural development measures and cross compliance for claim year 2020.

Regulation 2 modifies Article 15(2) to extend the deadline for making amendments to a single application or payment claim from 31 May 2020 to 9 June 2020.

An impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
