# The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 2nd June 2009

Coming into operation: 30th September 2009

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 28(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 30th September 2009.

#### Amendment

###### 2

The Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004] shall be amended by inserting “(1300)” under “(1200)” in the list of numbers to the left of diagram 670 in Schedule 2.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 2nd June 2009

R Sherman — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 by adding a new dimension for the sign shown in diagram 670 (regulation 2).

These Regulations have been notified to the European Commission pursuant to Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) of 22nd June 1998 laying down a procedure for the provision of information in the field of technical standards and regulations and of rules on Information Society Services (O.J. No. L204, 21.07.98, p. 37-48) as amended by Directive [98/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0048) of 20th July 1998 (O.J. No. L217, 05.08.98, p. 18-26). Copies of the Directives can be obtained from The Stationery Office Bookshop, 16 Arthur Street, Belfast, BT1 4GD.
