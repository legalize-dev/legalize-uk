# Fish Health (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 22nd April 2008

Coming into operation: 23rd April 2008

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Fish Health (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 23rd April 2008.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Fish Health Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998

###### 2

The Fish Health Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00004] shall be amended by:

- (a) substituting for regulation 9(2A) the following paragraph—

> > (2A) Subject to paragraph (3), a person shall not relay any live molluscs, eggs or gametes from any part of an area referred to in Schedule 7 in any other place or area (including any other area referred to in Schedule 7) within Northern Ireland unless he has been authorized to do so in writing by the Department.

; and

- (b) substituting for Schedule 7 the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — Replacement Schedule 7 to the Fish Health Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 22nd April 2008.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Fish Health Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 (‘the 1998 Regulations’) which implement in respect of Northern Ireland Council Directive [91/67/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0067) (O.J. No. L46, 19.2.91, p.1) concerning the animal health conditions governing the placing on the market of aquaculture animals and products, as amended, and Council Directive [95/70/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0070) (O.J. No. L332, 30.12.95, p.33) introducing minimum community measures for the control of certain diseases affecting bivalve molluscs, to the extent that they are not implemented by other legislation.

The 1998 Regulations were amended in 2005 to prohibit the relaying within Northern Ireland of live molluscs eggs and gametes from Lough Foyle following confirmation of *Bonamia ostreae* in the Lough. Following the confirmation of this disease in Strangford Lough, these Regulations similarly prohibit the relaying within Northern Ireland of live molluscs eggs and gametes from that Lough.
