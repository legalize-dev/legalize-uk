# The Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 24th April 2014

Coming into operation: 20th May 2014

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 70(8) and 171(1) and (3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 , and now vested in it .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 20th May 2014.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 8(1) of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1976 (circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as gainfully employed) for “£100” substitute “ £102 ”.

#### Revocation

###### 3

Regulation 2 of the Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 is revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 24th April 2014

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the — Department for Social Development
