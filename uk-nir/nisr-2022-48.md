# The Marriage, Civil Partnership and Civil Registration (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made: 17th February 2022

Coming into operation: 11th March 2022

The Department of Finance [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 10(1), 10(4), 10(4A), 11(1), 21(1), 21(5), 39, 40 and 40A of the Births and Deaths Registration (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 [^f00002], Articles 3(3), 7(1), 35(3) and 36 of the Marriage (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 [^f00003], and sections 139(2), 143, 155(3), 156(1) and 159 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 [^f00004], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Marriage, Civil Partnership and Civil Registration (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and shall come into operation on 11th March 2022.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Marriage Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003, the Civil Partnership Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and the Civil Registration Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 to enable certified copies of life events to be produced in English, Irish or Bi-lingual English/Irish.

The Regulations prescribe alternative forms for the registration of births, still-births and deaths and alternative forms for the production of certified copies.

The Regulations also prescribe updated marriage and civil partnership notice forms and provide alternative forms for marriage and civil partnership schedules and alternative forms for the production of certified copies of marriage or civil partnership registration.
