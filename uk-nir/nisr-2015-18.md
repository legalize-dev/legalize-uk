# The Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 26th January 2015

Coming into operation: 17th February 2015

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 5(1) and (2) of the Ombudsman (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 and Article 4(1) and (2) of the Commissioner for Complaints (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 and now vested in it makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall have effect from 1st April 2014.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland 2014 is revoked.

#### Increase in Salaries

###### 3

The annual full time rate of salaries payable to the Assembly Ombudsman and the Commissioner for Complaints shall be increased from £129,579 to £130,875 from 1st April 2014.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 26th January 2015.

Stephen Boyd — A senior officer of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister
