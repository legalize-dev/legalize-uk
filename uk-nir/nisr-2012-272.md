# The Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment) 2012

Made: 4th July 2012

Coming into operation: 1st August 2012

The Northern Ireland Court of Judicature Rules Committee[^f00001] makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 55 and 55A of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) (Amendment) 2012 and come into operation on 1st August 2012.

#### Amendments to the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980

###### 2

In Order 79, in rule 11, after “Where a person has been released on bail” insert “, including bail under section 91 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011,”.

## Signed

Declan Morgan — John Gillen — W R B Stephens — F P Girvan

Dated 2nd July 2012

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 55A (3) of the Judicature Act 1978, I allow these Rules.

Signed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 4th July 2012

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Rules of the Court of Judicature (Northern Ireland) 1980 [[S.R. 1980 No. 346](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1980/346)] to take account of section 91 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 which provides the magistrates’ courts with the power to grant bail on compassionate grounds.
