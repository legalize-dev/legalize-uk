# The Rules of the Supreme Court (Northern Ireland) (Amendment) 2007

Made: 20th March 2007

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 16th April 2007

The Northern Ireland Supreme Court Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 55 and 55A of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978[^f00001]

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Rules of the Supreme Court (Northern Ireland) (Amendment) 2007 and shall come into operation on the 16th April 2007.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Rules of the Supreme Court (Northern Ireland) 1980 (“the principal Rules”), so as to—

- make a number of amendments consequential to the Civil Partnership Act [2004 (c.33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/33);

- transfer probate business from the Family Division to the Chancery Division and make a small number of consequential amendments;

- update a number of references to merchant shipping legislation, which has been repealed and consolidated in the Merchant Shipping Act [1995 (c.21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/21);

- provide for taxation of solicitor’s professional costs as a single item under the short form procedure;

- provide for proceedings against or concerning the International Oil Pollution Compensation Supplementary Fund 2003;

- make a minor amendment in respect of giving evidence by video link;

- make a number of amendments in relation to the procedure for applications for statutory review and appeals under the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act [2002 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/41), as amended by the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act [2004 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/19) and the Immigration, Asylum and Nationality Act [2006 (c.13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/13); and

- prescribe the procedure for applications to the High Court under the European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 ([S.R. 2004 No. 417](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2004/417)) and the European Cooperative Society (Involvement of Employees) Regulations 2006) ([S.I. 2006/2059](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2006/2059)).
