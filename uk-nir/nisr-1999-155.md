# The Northern Ireland Science Park Foundation Limited (Funding) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 26th March 1999

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Coming into operation: 7th May 1999

The Department of Economic Development in exercise of the power conferred on it by Article 30(1)(b) of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Science Park Foundation Limited (Funding) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 7th May 1999.

#### Specification of the Foundation

###### 2

The Northern Ireland Science Park Foundation Limited is hereby specified as a body to which the Department of Economic Development may make grants under paragraph (1)(b) of Article 30 of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Economic Development on

M. A. Warnock — Assistant Secretary — 26th March 1999.

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on

D. W. Thomson — Assistant Secretary — 26th March 1999.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

Article 30 of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982 enables the Department of Economic Development to make grants to a body or person which it may specify, for the purpose of enabling that body or person to stimulate or assist industrial enterprise, the development of industry or the provision of employment.

This Order specifies the Northern Ireland Science Park Foundation Limited as a body which may receive such grants.

The Northern Ireland Science Park Foundation Limited is a company limited by guarantee which was formed on 25th March 1999.
