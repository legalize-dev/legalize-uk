# The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2020

Made: 10th January 2020

Coming into operation: 1st April 2020

In accordance with section**s** 25(8) and 26(6) of that Act the Department has consulted the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association for Northern Ireland before making these Regulations**.**

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2020 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2020.

#### Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 3(1) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday”, for 2020 only, for the words “first Monday in May” substitute “Friday 8th May”.

#### Amendment of Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006

###### 3

- (1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In Schedule 1 in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday”, for 2020 only, for the words “first Monday in May” substitute “Friday 8th May”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 10th January 2020

Peter May — A senior officer of the Department of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 and Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006 to allow for the public holiday on the first Monday in May to be replaced by Friday 8th May 2020 to mark the 75th anniversary of VE Day.
