# The Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 16th April 1996

Coming into operation: 16th April 1996

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 14(1) and (4) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001], and all other powers enabling it in that behalf, and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel in accordance with Article 14(8) of that Order, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 16th April 1996.

#### Amendment

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following:

| *Column I* | *Column 2* | *Column 3* | *Column 4* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Hss trust* | *Originating Capital Debt* | *Initial Loan* | *Public Dividend Capital* |
|  | *£* | *£* | *£* |
| Causeway HSS Trust | 28,119,078 | 14,059,539 | 14,059,539 |
| Northern Ireland Ambulance Service HSS Trust | 8,373,243 | 4,186,621 | 4,186,622 |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on .

B. McGahan — Assistant Secretary — 16th April 1996

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on .

J. G. Sullivan — Assistant Secretary — 16th April 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 which determines the originating capital debt, interest bearing initial loan and public dividend capital of HSS trusts established under the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 with an operational date of 1st April 1995.

The Order varies the originating capital debt, interest bearing initial loan and public dividend capital of Causeway HSS Trust.
