# The Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 4th August 2006

Coming into operation: 5th August 2006

The Department for Employment and Learning[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(5) of and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 to the Education (Student Loans) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003] makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 5th August 2006.

###### 2

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Education (Student Loans) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00004].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 3

The principal Regulations shall be amended as provided by regulations 4 and 5.

###### 4

- (1) In regulation 4[^f00005] (Conditions of eligibility) shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) in paragraph (1D) for “30th April 2007” there shall be substituted “1st August 2007”.

- (3) for paragraph (1G) there shall be substituted—

> > (1G) Paragraph (1D) shall not apply in the case of a person who would have been eligible for a loan under paragraph (1) before 5th August 2006.

###### 5

In regulation 7[^f00006] (Terms of loans), for paragraph (1) there shall be substituted—

> > (1) Every agreement for a loan made on or after 5th August 2006 shall include the terms set out in Part 1 of Schedule 2.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 4th August 2006

June Ingram — A senior officer of the Department for Employment and Learning

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Education (Student Loans) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 (the “1998 Regulations”). The 1998 Regulations, made under the Education (Student Loans) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990, govern mortgage style repayment loans.

Regulations 4 and 5 correct drafting errors.
