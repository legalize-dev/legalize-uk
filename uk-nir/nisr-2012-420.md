# Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 26th November 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 18 of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Licensing and Registration of Clubs Amendment (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

10th December 2012 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule, for the purposes set out in column 2 of the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

| Provisions of the Act | Subject Matter |
| --- | --- |
| Section 1 | Closure of licensed premises |
| Section 7 | Closure of registered clubs |
| Schedule 1,in so far as it relates to:- Article 69A(4) | Keeping premises open after closure made under Article 69A |
| Article 69B(6) | Permitting premises to be open in contravention of closure order under Article 69B |
| Article 69F(5) of the Licensing Order | Permitting premises to open etc. in contravention of court’s decision about closure order |
| Schedule 2, in so far as it relates to:- Article 41A(4) | Keeping premises open after closure made under Article 41A |
| Article 41B(6) | Permitting premises to be kept open after closure order Article 41B |
| Article 41F(5) of the Registration of Clubs Order | Permitting premises to be kept open etc. in contravention of court’s decision about closure order |
|  |  |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 26th November 2012

Michael Daly — A senior officer of the — Department social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Licensing and Registration of Clubs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 listed in the Schedule to this Order, on 10th December 2012.
