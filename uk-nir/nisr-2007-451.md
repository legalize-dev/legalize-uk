# The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 24th October 2007

Coming into operation: 30th November 2007

There has been open and transparent consultation during the preparation of these Regulations in accordance with the requirements of Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00004] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 30th November 2007.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00006] are revoked.

#### Consequential amendments to the Genetically Modified Animal Feed Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

###### 3

- (1) Regulation 8 (inspection, seizure and detention of suspected animal feed) of the Genetically Modified Animal Feed Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00007] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In paragraph (5) for “Subject to paragraphs (5A), (5B) and (6)” substitute “Subject to paragraph (6)”.

- (3) Paragraphs (5A) and (5B) are omitted.

#### Consequential amendment to the Official Feed and Food Controls Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 4

In Schedule 2 (definition of relevant feed law) of the Official Feed and Food Controls Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00008], paragraph (f) is omitted.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 24th October 2007.

Don Hill — A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1 These Regulations revoke the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 ([S.R.2005 No. 233](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/233)), and make consequential amendments to the Genetically Modified Animal Feed Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 ([S.R. 2004 No. 386](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2004/386)) and the Official Feed and Food Controls Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/2)).

2 These Regulations implement Commission Decision [2007/157/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0157) repealing Decision [2005/317/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0317) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism Bt 10 in maize products (OJ No. L68, 8.3.2007, p.8).
