# Drainage (Environmental Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 17th December 1998

Coming into operation: 29th January 1999

The Department of Agriculture, being a department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to measures relating to the requirement for an assessment of the impact on the environment of projects likely to have significant effects on the environment, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by the said section 2(2) and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## Part I — Introductory and Application

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Drainage (Environmental Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 29th January 1999.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations, “the Drainage Order” means the Drainage (Northern Ireland) Order 1973[^f00003].

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Application

###### 3

- (1) Part II shall not apply to any drainage works–

- (a) commenced before 29th January 1999;

- (b) the proposals for which have been announced in accordance with regulation 5(1) of the Drainage (Environmental Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00005] before 29th January 1999;

- (c) carried out pursuant to any scheme under Article 30(1) of the Drainage Order which was prepared before 29th January 1999;

- (d) carried out pursuant to an agreement or arrangement with a person outside Northern Ireland which was entered into by the Department under Article 30(2) of the Drainage Order for the purposes of a scheme under paragraph (1) of that Article before 29th January 1999; or

- (e) carried out under a drainage scheme, whether prepared before or after 29th January 1999, which was confirmed under Article 13 of the Drainage Order before 29th January 1999 or treated by virtue of Article 8(1) or 9(1) of that Order as if they had been so carried out.

- (2) Part III shall not apply to any drainage scheme prepared before 29th January 1999.

## Part II — Drainage Works Generally

#### Amendment of the Drainage (Environmental Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

###### 4

The Drainage (Environmental Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 shall be amended in accordance with regulations 5 to 13.

#### Interpretation

###### 5

In regulation 2–

- (a) in paragraph (1), before the definition of “the Department” there shall be inserted–

> > - “another member State” means a member State other than the United Kingdom;

; and

- (b) after paragraph (3) there shall be inserted–

> > (3A) In these Regulations, any reference to significant effects on the environment includes a reference to such effects on the environment in another member State.

#### Preparation of an environmental statement

###### 6

For regulation 5 there shall be substituted–

> > (5)
>   (1) The Department shall consider whether any proposed drainage works are likely to have significant effects on the environment and ought, therefore, to be made subject to an environmental statement.
>   (2) Where the Department considers that any proposed drainage works ought to be made subject to an environmental statement, it shall by notice published in the Belfast Gazette and at least two local newspapers–
>     (a) announce that it is proposed that the works in question will be carried out;
>     (b) describe briefly their nature, size and location; and
>     (c) state that the Department proposes to make the works subject to an environmental statement.
>   (3) Where the Department considers that any proposed drainage works ought not to be made subject to an environmental statement, it shall by notice published in the Belfast Gazette and at least two local newspapers–
>     (a) announce that it is proposed that the works in question will be carried out;
>     (b) describe briefly their nature, size and location;
>     (c) state that the Department does not propose to make the works subject to an environmental statement; and
>     (d) state that any person may make representations to the Department in writing in relation to the likely environmental effects of the proposed works at the address specified in the notice within 28 days of the date of the publication of the notice in the Belfast Gazette.
>   (4) Where the Department publishes a notice in accordance with paragraph (2) or (3) it shall send a copy of that notice to–
>     (a) the Drainage Council;
>     (b) the district council of the district or, as the case may be, each district in which the proposed drainage works are situate; and
>     (c) any other person who appears to it to have an interest in the matter.
>   (5) Where the Department has stated pursuant to paragraph (2)(*c*) that it proposes to make any proposed drainage works subject to an environmental statement, it shall proceed to prepare such a statement.
>   (6) The Department shall consider any representations made in accordance with paragraph (3)(*d*) and if, having done so, it decides that any proposed drainage works ought to be made subject to an environmental statement, it shall so inform the persons who made those representations and proceed to prepare such a statement.
>   (7) Where the Department has stated in accordance with paragraph (3)(*c*) that it does not propose to make any proposed drainage works subject to an environmental statement and either–
>     (a) no representations made in accordance with paragraph (3)(*d*) are received in relation to those works within the time limit therein specified; or
>     (b) such representations are so received but the Department, after considering them, decides that the proposed works ought not to be made subject to an environmental statement,
>   it may proceed to undertake those works or do anything else referred to in regulation 4(1) in relation to them, without preparing such a statement.

#### Publicity for an environmental statement

###### 7

For regulation 6 there shall be substituted–

> > (6) Where the Department has prepared an environmental statement in relation to any proposed drainage works, it shall–
>   (a) by notice published in the Belfast Gazette and at least two local newspapers announce the preparation of the statement and state that any person who wishes to make representations in relation to the likely environmental effects of the works to which the statement relates should make them in writing to the Department at the address specified in the notice within 28 days of the date of the publication of the notice in the Belfast Gazette;
>   (b) make available for inspection at an office of the Department or at some other convenient place, for a period of at least 28 days following the date of the publication of the notice in the Belfast Gazette, the details of the proposed drainage works and the environmental statement relating thereto, and ensure that a reasonable number of copies of the statement are made available;

>   (d) at the same time as the notice referred to in sub-paragraph (*a*) is published supply copies of the statement to–
>     (i) the Drainage Council;
>     (ii) the district council of the district or, as the case may be, each district in which the proposed drainage works are situate; and
>     (iii) any other person who appears to it to have an interest in the matter.

#### Consultation with other persons

>   (2) Where under paragraph (1) the Department consults with any person about an environmental statement, it shall give that person not less than 28 days notice beginning with the day on which it sends a copy of the environmental statement to him, that it proposes to consider the statement and shall not do so until after the expiration of that period.

#### Charges

>   (b) any copy, in excess of one, of the whole or any part of any further information supplied to that person in accordance with regulation 9(5)(*a*) to (*c*).

#### Provision of information

###### 10

For regulation 9 there shall be substituted–

> > (9)
>   (1) Subject to paragraph (6), where the Department proposes to prepare an environmental statement in accordance with this Part, it may enter into consultations with any person to determine whether that person has in his possession any information which the Department considers relevant to the preparation of the statement, and that person shall make any such information which he has available to the Department.
>   (2) Where the Department has prepared an environmental statement in relation to any drainage works and made that statement available in accordance with regulation 6 it may determine that further information is relevant to any matter which is required to be, or may be, dealt with in the statement.

>   (4) Where, pursuant to paragraph (3), the Department obtains any further information it shall–

###### 11

After regulation 9 there shall be inserted the following regulation–

> > (9A)
>   (1) Where it appears to the Department that the proposed drainage works are likely to have a significant effect on the environment in another member State or where another member State thus potentially affected so requests, the Department shall forward to that other member State–
>     (a) a copy of the environmental statement prepared in accordance with regulation 5(5) or (6);
>     (b) a copy of any further information relevant to the environmental statement and obtained pursuant to regulation 9(3).
>     (2) Any copy of the environmental statement forwarded to another member State in accordance with paragraph (1)(*a*) shall be forwarded to that other member State at the same time as the publication in the Belfast Gazette of the notice referred to in regulation 6(*a*).
>     (3) Any copy of the further information forwarded to another member State in accordance with paragraph (1)(*b*) shall be forwarded to that other member State at the same time as the publication in the Belfast Gazette of the notice referred to in regulation 9(4).
>     (4) Where the Department pursuant to paragraph (1) forwards to another member State a copy of the environmental statement or the further information relevant to the environmental statement, it shall give that other member State not less than 28 days beginning with the day on which it forwards the copy of the environmental statement or the further information to the member State, to consider the environmental statement or further information, as the case may be, and to make representations in relation to the likely environmental effects of the works.

> > (10) Where the Department has prepared an environmental statement in relation to any drainage works it shall not proceed to undertake those works or do anything else referred to in regulation 4(1) in relation to those works unless it has first taken into consideration–

>   (e) any representations made by another member State in accordance with regulation 9A(4) in relation to the likely environmental effects of the works which are received within the time limit therein specified.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Drainage (Environmental Assessment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 which implement in respect of Northern Ireland, in relation to drainage schemes and drainage works carried out by the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland (“the Department”), the requirements of Council Directive [85/337/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1985/0337) on the assessment of the effects of certain public and private projects on the environment (O.J. No. L175, 5.7.85, p. 40). Part III of these Regulations further amends the Drainage (Northern Ireland) Order 1973.

The Regulations make the following changes of substance–

- (a) the procedures to be followed by the Department where it considers that an environmental statement should, or alternatively should not, be prepared in respect of a proposed drainage scheme or drainage works, are modified (regulations 6 and 16);

- (b) where a proposed drainage scheme or drainage works are likely to have a significant effect on the environment in a member State of the European Communities other than the United Kingdom the Department is required to forward a copy of the environmental statement or further environmental information relevant to that statement to that member State giving it an opportunity to make representations;

- (c) the Department is required to notify its decision to proceed with a proposed drainage scheme or drainage works which have been made subject to an environmental statement and to confirm in writing that it has considered the proposals, the environmental statement, any information received in respect thereof and any representations made in respect thereof including representations from another member State (regulations 13 and 16);

- (d) when notifying its decision to vary the provisions of any drainage scheme, which has been the subject of an environmental statement, the Department is also required to confirm that it has considered the proposals, the environmental statement, any information received in respect thereof and any representations made in respect thereof (regulation 17).
