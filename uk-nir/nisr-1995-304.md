# The Arts Council (1995 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995

Made: 1st August 1995

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Arts Council (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Arts Council (1995 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1995.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Arts Council (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 shall be 1st September 1995.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on

R Jordan — Assistant Secretary — 1st August 1995.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Arts Council (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 into operation on 1st September 1995.
