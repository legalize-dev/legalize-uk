# The Social Security (1989 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 23rd August 2007

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security (1989 Order) (Commencement No. 4) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

- (2) In this Order “the Order” means the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1989.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) paragraph 3(1), (3) and (4) of Schedule 5 to the Order (non-compliance: compulsory levelling up) for the purposes of—

- (i) paragraph 5A[^f00003] of that Schedule (unfair paternity leave provisions), and

- (ii) paragraph 5B of that Schedule (unfair adoption leave provisions), and

- (b) Article 25 of the Order (equal treatment for men and women) in so far as it relates to the provisions specified in paragraph (a),

is 24th August 2007.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 23rd August 2007

John O’Neill — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of paragraph 3 (in part) of Schedule 5 to the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 for the purposes of paragraphs 5A and 5B of that Schedule on 24th August 2007.
