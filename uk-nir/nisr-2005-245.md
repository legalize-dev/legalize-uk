# The Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 29th April 2005

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (1998 Order) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The following provisions of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 shall come into operation on 29th April 2005, that is to say –

- (a) Articles 55 to 60;

- (b) Articles 67 and 68; and

- (c) Articles 70 to 72.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on 29th April 2005.

Katrina Godfrey — Senior Officer of the — Department of Education

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into operation the following Articles of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998, Part VII, Financing of Schools:

- Articles 55 to 57 provide for the suspension, review, and appeal procedures relating to the withdrawal of financial delegation;

- Article 58 which makes provisions relating to the staff of controlled and maintained schools with delegated budgets;

- Articles 59 and 60 provides for the financing by boards of special schools, and Article 67 provides for the preparation of financial statements for special schools managed by the board;

- Article 68 provides for building and equipment grants for voluntary schools; and

- Articles 70 to 72 provide for miscellaneous financial provisions relating to schools.
