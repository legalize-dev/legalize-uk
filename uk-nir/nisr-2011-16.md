# Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 24th January 2011

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(1) of the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Rates (Amendment) (2009 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

- (2) In this Order “the 2009 Act” means the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

- (1) 10th February 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2009 Act listed in Schedule 1.

- (2) 1st October 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2009 Act listed in Schedule 2.

| section 7 | Capital value of hereditaments: change of state of property to be disregarded |
| Paragraphs 5 and 7 of Schedule 1 and section 17 in so far as it relates to them. | Minor and consequential amendments |
