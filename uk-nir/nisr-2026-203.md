# The Administrative and Financial Provisions (2026 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made

The Department of Finance[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(2) of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 2026[^f00002], makes the following Order.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Administrative and Financial Provisions (2026 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “*the 2026 Act*” means the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 2026.

#### Appointed Day

###### 3

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2026 Act listed in Schedule 1 is the first day after that on which this Order is made.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the 2026 Act listed in Schedule 2 is 1st April 2027.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation provisions of the Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 2026 (“*the 2026 Act*”).

Article 3(1) and Schedule 1 bring sections 6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 14, 15, 17, and 19 of the 2026 Act into operation on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

Article 3(2) and Schedule 2 bring sections 1, 13, 18 and 20 of the 2026 Act into operation on 1st April 2027.
