# The Penalty Charges (Additional Contraventions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 12th November 2014

Coming into operation: 14th January 2015

The Department for Regional Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 4(3) of the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Penalty Charges (Additional Contraventions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 14th January 2015.

#### Amendments

###### 2

In Schedule 1 to the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (contraventions subject to penalty charges)[^f00002]—

- (a) in the part entitled “Offences under the 1997 Order” after the first paragraph there shall be inserted the following paragraphs—

> > An offence under Article 4(5) of contravening a prohibition or restriction on the entering or proceeding of a vehicle in a bus lane.
> An offence under Article 4(5) of contravening a prohibition on the use of motor vehicles under The Prohibition of Traffic (Chichester Street, Belfast) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003].
> An offence under Article 4(5) of contravening a prohibition on motor vehicles entering Donegall Square West, Belfast under The Prohibition of Traffic (Belfast) Order (Northern Ireland) 1979[^f00004].
> An offence under Article 4(5) of contravening a prohibition on the entering or proceeding of a vehicle in the link road from Dublin Road to Market Square, Antrim under The Control of Traffic (Antrim) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00005].
> An offence under Article 4(5) of contravening the prohibition on the use of a length of Castle Street, Belfast under Article 7 of The Control of Traffic (Belfast) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00006].

; and

- (b) in the part entitled “Other Offences” before the first paragraph there shall be inserted the following paragraphs—

> > An offence under Article 20(4) of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993[^f00007] of contravening a prohibition on the entering, proceeding or waiting of a vehicle under The Westlink (Busways) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00008].
> An offence under Article 20(4) of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993[^f00009] of contravening a prohibition on the entering, proceeding or waiting of a vehicle in a bus lane under Article 9 of The Motorway Traffic Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00010].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 12th November 2014

G F McKenna — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend Schedule 1 to the Traffic Management (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (the “2005 Order”) by adding—

- (a) Further offences under Article 4(5) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 of—

- (i) contravening a prohibition or restriction on the entering or proceeding of a vehicle in a bus lane;

- (ii) contravening a prohibition on the use of motor vehicles under The Prohibition of Traffic (Chichester Street, Belfast) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007;

- (iii) contravening a prohibition on motor vehicles entering Donegall Square West, Belfast under The Prohibition of Traffic (Belfast) Order (Northern Ireland) 1979;

- (iv) contravening a prohibition on the entering or proceeding of a vehicle in the link road from Dublin Road to Market Square, Antrim under The Control of Traffic (Antrim) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008; and

- (v) contravening the prohibition on the use of a length of Castle Street, Belfast under Article 7 of The Control of Traffic (Belfast) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

- (b) Further offences under Article 20(4) of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 of—

- (i) contravening a prohibition on the entering, proceeding or waiting of a vehicle under The Westlink (Busways) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008; and

- (ii) contravening a prohibition on the entering, proceeding or waiting of a vehicle in a bus lane under Article 9 of The Motorway Traffic Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.

These additional contraventions are subject to a penalty charge as provided in Article 4(1) of the 2005 Order.
