# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 23) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: at 2.00 p.m. on 23rd December 2020

Laid before the Assembly: at 3.00 p.m. on 23rd December 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with Regulation 1(2)

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 23) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at the time and on the date they are made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended in accordance with these Regulations.

###### 3

In Regulation 5(9), after “shall not apply to a gathering” insert “on one day only”.

###### 4

In Regulation 6(3A), after “shall not apply to a gathering” insert “on one day only”.

###### 5

In paragraph 3 of Schedule 2, omit sub-paragraph (2A).

###### 6

In paragraph 4(4) of Schedule 2, after ““Christmas linked household”” insert “on one day only”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 2.00 pm on 23rd December 2020

Dr Naresh Chada — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 by limiting a Christmas “bubble” to one day and disallowing overnight stays connected to a Christmas “bubble”.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
