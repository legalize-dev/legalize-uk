# The Insolvency (Amendment) (2026 Act) (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

Made: 18th November 2026

The Department for the Economy[^f00001] makes the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 120(2) and (3) of the Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026[^f00002].

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Insolvency (Amendment) (2026 Act) (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 19th November 2026.

#### Amendment of the Insolvency (Amendment) (2026 Act) (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026

###### 2

- (1) The Insolvency (Amendment) (2026 Act) (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026[^f00003] is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In Article 2, in paragraph (2)(w)(xix) after “paragraph 38” insert “(1) and (2)”.

- (3) In Article 4, for paragraph (2) substitute paragraph (2) as set out in the Schedule.

- (4) In Article 5—

- (a) in paragraph (4) for “16th October 2026” in each place where it occurs substitute “24th December 2026”;

- (b) in paragraph (7) for “16th October 2026” in each place where it occurs substitute “24th December 2026” and after the words “such a case” insert “paragraphs (1) to (3) and (5) and (6) in”;

- (c) in paragraph (8), for “the modified version of Article 10A” substitute “paragraph (4) in the modified version of Article 10”.

###### 3

The amendments made by Article 2(2), (3) and (4)(c) have effect from 16th October 2026.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Insolvency (Amendment) (2026 Act) (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2026 (“the Commencement No. 1 Order”).

Article 2 of this Order amends Articles 2, 4 and 5 of the Commencement No. 1 Order.

In Article 2, paragraph (2)(w)(xix) is amended to clarify that it is only sub-paragraphs (1) and (2) of paragraph (38) of Schedule 4 to the Insolvency (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2026 which were brought into operation on 16th October 2026.

In Article 4, paragraph 2 is replaced with the substitute set out in the Schedule. The subparagraphs which had been designated by the letters (aa) to (gg) in the original paragraph are instead designated as (z1) to (z7) in the substitute.

In Article 5 the dates which had been incorrectly entered in two places in each of paragraphs (4) and (7) as 16th October 2026 are changed to 24th December 2026. A further amendment to paragraph (7) establishes that it is paragraphs (1) to (3) and (5) and (6) of the modified version of Article 10 of the 2002 Order which had effect up until 24 December 2026 which are to continue to have effect in cases where a partnership entered insolvency proceedings before that date. An amendment to paragraph (8) puts right an error by providing that it is paragraph (4) of the modified version of Article 10 of the 2002 Order which had effect up until 16 October 2026 which is to is to continue to have effect where a partnership entered insolvency proceedings before that date.

Article 3 of this Order provides for the amendments made by Article 2 to Articles 2, 4 and 5(8) of the Commencement No. 1 Order to have effect from 16 October 2026.
