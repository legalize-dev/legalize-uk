# Planning Applications (Exemption from Publication) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 26th July 1999

Coming into operation: 30th August 1999

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 13 and 21(2) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Planning Applications (Exemption from Publication) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 30th August 1999.

#### Exempted applications

###### 2

Article 21(1) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 (publication of notice of planning applications) shall not apply to any application for planning permission for development where that development—

- (a) is of a class specified in the Schedule; and

- (b) is not in a conservation area.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Planning Applications (Exemption from Publication) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## SCHEDULE

#### Class A

The enlargement, improvement or alteration of a dwelling house.

#### Class B

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 26th July 1999.

J. McConnell — Assistant Secretary

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

Article 2 of and the Schedule to this Order provide that applications for planning permission for development need not be published in the press in accordance with Article 21(1) of the Planning (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 where that development is—

- (a) of a class specified in the Schedule; and

- (b) not in a conservation area.

Article 3 of this Order revokes the Planning Applications (Exemption from Publication) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991.
