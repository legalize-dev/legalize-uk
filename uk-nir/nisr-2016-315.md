# The Hybrid Schemes Quality Requirements (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 31st August 2016

Coming into operation: 1st October 2016

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by section 24(2) to (4) of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Hybrid Schemes Quality Requirements (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2016.

#### Amendment of the Hybrid Schemes Quality Requirements Rules

###### 2

- (1) The Hybrid Schemes Quality Requirements Rules (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (6).

- (2) In rule 1(2) (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “contracted-out employment”;

- (b) omit the definition of “contracted-out scheme”;

- (c) in the definition of “the paragraph (b) quality requirements” for “sections 21 to 23” substitute “sections 21 to 23A[^f00004]”.

- (3) In rule 2(1) and (3) (application: general) for “rules 3 to 8” substitute “rules 4 to 8”.

- (4) Omit rule 3 (contracted-out hybrid schemes).

- (5) In rule 4(1) (general rule for hybrid schemes) for sub-paragraph (b) substitute—

> > (b) do not include any scheme falling within a description specified in any of rules 5 to 7.

- (6) In rule 8(1)(a) and (b) and (2)(b) (schemes where more than one rule applies) for “rules 3 to 7” substitute “rules 4 to 7”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 31st August 2016

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Hybrid Schemes Quality Requirements Rules (Northern Ireland) 2012, in consequence of the [Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2015 (c. 5 (N.I.)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2015/5)), to reflect the introduction of the alternative tests of scheme quality for defined benefit and hybrid schemes and the abolition of contracting-out for salary related pension schemes.
