# The Personal and Occupational Pension Schemes (Pensions Ombudsman) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 20th May 1996

Coming into operation: 1st July 1996

The Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 142(4)(*b*) of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Personal and Occupational Pension Schemes (Pensions Ombudsman) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 1st July 1996.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to a Measure of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Personal and Occupational Pension Schemes (Pensions Ombudsman) Regulations

###### 2

After regulation 2 of the Personal and Occupational Pension Schemes (Pensions Ombudsman) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00003], there shall be inserted the following regulation—

> > (2A)
>   (1) The Pensions Ombudsman may investigate and determine any complaint in relation to the administration of a scheme, involving an authorised complainant and the administrator of the scheme.
>   (2) Where the Pensions Ombudsman commences an investigation under paragraph (1), the provisions of Part X of the Act (investigations: the Pensions Ombudsman) shall apply in relation to the administrator as they apply in relation to the trustees or managers of the scheme.
>   (3) In this regulation—
>     - “administrator” means any person, other than the trustees or managers of a scheme or the employer to which the scheme relates or has related, concerned with the administration of the scheme;
>     - “scheme” means a personal or an occupational pension scheme.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland on

W. G. Purdy — Assistant Secretary — 20th May 1996.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Personal and Occupational Pension Schemes (Pensions Ombudsman) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 by inserting a new regulation (regulation 2A) which extends the jurisdiction of the Pensions Ombudsman to include complaints against scheme administrators.
