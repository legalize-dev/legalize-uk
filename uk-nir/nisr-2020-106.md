# The Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 19th June 2020

Coming into operation: 10th July 2020

The Department of Health[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 14(4) and 297(3), paragraph 5(1)(a) of Schedule 1, paragraph 1(2) and 3(1)(a) of Schedule 2 and paragraph 3(1)(a) of Schedule 3 to the Mental Capacity Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00002], makes the following Regulation:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation on 10th July 2020.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 19th June 2020.

Mark Lee — A senior officer of the Department of Health.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Mental Capacity (Deprivation of Liberty) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.
