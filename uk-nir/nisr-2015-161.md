# The Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 (Judicial Offices) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 10th March 2015

Coming into operation: 1st April 2015

The Department of Justice makes this Order in exercise of the power conferred by paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 (Judicial Offices) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and comes into operation on 1st April 2015.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

For the purposes of this Order, an office is “held on a salaried basis” if service in that office is remunerated by payment of a salary.

#### Judicial Offices

###### 3

A person who holds one of the offices specified in the Schedule is a holder of judicial office for the purposes of section 1(2)(b) of the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014.

## SCHEDULE — Specified Judicial Offices

- President of appeals tribunals (within the meaning of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00002]) appointed under Article 6 of that Order.

- Member of a panel constituted under Article 7(1) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 who is appointed to serve as a member of that panel and is a barrister or solicitor.

- President or Vice-President of the Industrial Tribunals and the Fair Employment Tribunal, appointed under Article 82 of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00003].

- Member of a panel of chairmen appointed under Article 82 of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998.

- Member of a panel of chairmen appointed under regulation 4(1)(a) of the Industrial Tribunals (Constitution and Rules of Procedure) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004].

- President or other member of the Lands Tribunal[^f00005], where that office is held on a salaried basis.

## Signed

*David Ford* — Minister of Justice
