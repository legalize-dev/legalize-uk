# The Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (No. 2) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 20th June 2007

Coming into operation-: 21st June 2007

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety has been designated for the purposes of that section in relation to measures in the veterinary and phytosanitary fields for the protection of public health[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (No. 2) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 21st June 2007.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendments to the Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00005] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) —

- (a) the following definitions of certain Community instruments are substituted for the definitions of the Community instruments appearing immediately after the definition of “cutting up”—

> > - “Decision 2007/411”, “Directive 2004/41”, “Regulation 999/2001”, “Regulation 178/2002”, “Regulation 852/2004”, “Regulation 853/2004”, “Regulation 854/2004”, “Regulation 882/2004”, “Regulation 1688/2005”, “Regulation 2073/2005”, “Regulation 2074/2005”, Regulation 2075/2005”, “Regulation 2076/2005”, “Regulation 575/2006”, “Regulation 1664/2006”, “Regulation 1665/2006”, Regulation 1666/2006”, “Regulation 1791/2006” and “Regulation 1923/2006” have the meanings respectively given to them in the Schedule;

; and

- (b) “Decision 2007/411” is substituted for “Decision 2005/598” appearing in the combined definition of “material” and “product”.

- (3) For the Schedule (definitions of Community legislation) there is substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1 These Regulations amend the Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 ([S.R. 2005 No. 515](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/515)) by— a updating the definitions of certain Community instruments that are referred to in those Regulations *(regulation 2(2)(a) and (3))*; and b substituting a reference to the Commission Decision that has repealed and replaced an earlier Commission Decision for the reference to that earlier Decision that appears in those Regulations (*regulation 2(2(b))*.

2 The Bovine Products (Restriction on Placing on the Market) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 gave effect in relation to Northern Ireland to Article 1.1 of Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) prohibiting the placing on the market of products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1st August 1996 for any purpose and exempting such animals from certain control and eradication measures laid down in Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) (OJ No. L204, 5.8.2005, p.22). Article 1.1 of Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) provided that certain products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1st August 1996 could not be placed on the market.

3 Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) has now been repealed and replaced by Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0411) prohibiting the placing on the market of products derived from bovine animals born or reared within the United Kingdom before 1st August 1996 for any purpose and exempting such animals from certain control and eradication measures laid down in Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) and repealing Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) (OJ No. L155, 15.6.2007, p.74).

4 Article 1.1 of Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0411) is in identical terms to Article1.1 of Commission Decision [2005/598/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0598) and the Bovine Products (Placing on the Market) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 as amended by these Regulations give effect in relation to Northern Ireland to Article1.1 of Commission Decision [2007/411/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0411).
