# Seed Potatoes Marketing Board Assets (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 27th February 2004

Coming into operation: 29th March 2004

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by paragraph 10 of Schedule 2 to the Agricultural Marketing Act (Northern Ireland) 1964[^f00002], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Seed Potatoes Marketing Board Assets (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 29th March 2004.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Seed Potato Marketing Board Assets Order (Northern Ireland) 1985[^f00003] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 27th February 2004.

David Small — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order revokes the Seed Potato Marketing Board Assets Order (Northern Ireland) 1985.
