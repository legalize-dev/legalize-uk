# The Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 5th December 2019

Coming into operation: 14th December 2019

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00004] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and come into operation on 14th December 2019.

#### Amendment of the Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

The Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00005] are amended as follows.

###### 3

In regulation 2 (Interpretation)—

- (a) at paragraph (1)—

- (i) for the definitions paragraph—

> > - “Directive 2004/41”, “Regulation 2406/96”, “Regulation 178/2002” “Regulation 852/2004”, “Regulation 853/2004”, “Regulation 854/2004”, “Regulation 882/2004”, “Regulation 1688/2005”, “Regulation 2073/2005”, “Regulation 2074/2005” and “Regulation 2015/1375” have the meanings respectively given to them in the Schedule;

substitute—

> > - “Directive 2004/41”, “Regulation 2406/96”, “Regulation 852/2004”, “Regulation 853/2004”, “Regulation 1688/2005”, “Regulation 2073/2005”, “Regulation 2074/2005”, “Regulation 2015/1375”, “Regulation 2017/185”, “Regulation 2017/625”, “Regulation 2019/624” and “Regulation 2019/627” have the meanings respectively given to them in the Schedule;

- (ii) in the definition “first placing on the market”, for “Regulation 882/2004” substitute “Regulation 2017/625”;

- (iii) in the definition “first sale in a fish market”, for “Regulation 882/2004” substitute “Regulation 2017/625”;

- (iv) in the definition “official controls”, for “paragraph 1 of Article 2 of Regulation 882/2004” substitute “Article 2(1) of Regulation 2017/625”;

- (v) in the definition “processing”, for “Chapter V of Section B of Annex IV to Regulation 882/2004” substitute “Regulation 2017/625”;

- (vi) in the definition “third country import” for “is payable under Part 3 of the Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011” substitute “set out in Annex 4 to Regulation 2017/625 is payable”.

- (b) after paragraph (2) insert—

> > (3) In these Regulations, any reference to an EU instrument defined in the Schedule is a reference to that instrument as amended from time to time.

###### 4

In regulation 3 (Actual costs)—

- (a) for “listed in Annex VI to Regulation 882/2004” substitute “referred to in Articles 81 and 82 of Regulation 2017/625”;

- (b) for “Annex III to Regulation 854/2004” substitute “Title 6 of and Annex 6 to Regulation 2019/627”.

###### 5

In regulation 4 (Sterling equivalents of Euro), in paragraph (3) —

- (a) after “in each subsequent year” insert “until the coming into operation of the Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019”;

- (b) after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

> > (c) after the coming into operation of the Fishery Products (Official Controls Charges) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019, the average of the rates published in the C Series of the Official Journal of the European Union for each of the days during the period of charge when the rate is published.

###### 6

In regulation 9 (Sums remitted from one district council to another), for “Annex III to Regulation 854/2004” substitute “Title 6 of and Annex 6 to Regulation 2019/627”.

###### 7

In Part 2 (OFFICIAL CONTROLS CHARGES OTHER THAN CHARGES FOR THIRD COUNTRY IMPORTS), for “Annex III to Regulation 854/2004” substitute “Title 6 of and Annex 6 to Regulation 2019/627” in each place it occurs.

###### 8

For the Schedule (DEFINITIONS OF COMMUNITY LEGISLATION), substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

the application of food and feed law, rules on animal health and welfare, plant health and plant protection products, amending Regulations [(EC) No. (EU) 2016/429 and (EU) 2016/2031 of the European Parliament and of the Council, Council Regulations [(EC) No. 1/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0001) and [(EC) No. 1099/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1099) and Council Directives [98/58/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0058), [1999/74/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0074), [2007/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0043), 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) and [(EC) No. 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council, Council Directives [89/608/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0608), [89/662/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0662), [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425), [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496),
