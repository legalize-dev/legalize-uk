# The Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018

Made: 21st May 2018

Coming into operation: 25th June 2018

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2018 and shall come into operation on 25th June 2018.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Revocation

###### 3

Regulation 11 of the Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004] (Active Farmers) is revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 21st May 2018.

Brian Doherty — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs — 21st May 2018

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. 2015 No. 191](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/191)) (“the 2015 Regulations”). The 2015 Regulations make provision for the implementation of the Common Agricultural Policy schemes in Northern Ireland from 2015 by providing legal definition to a number of administration arrangements established by the Department.

Article 3(3)(d) of Regulation (EU) 2017/2393 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13December 2017 amending Regulations (EU) No 1305/2013 on support for rural development by the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD), (EU) No 1306/2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy, (EU) No 1307/2013 establishing rules for direct payments to farmers under support schemes within the framework of the common agricultural policy, (EU) No 1308/2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and (EU) No 652/2014 laying down provisions for the management of expenditure relating to the food chain, animal health and animal welfare, and relating to plant health and plant reproductive material, amends Article 9 of (EU) 1307/2013 to enable the Department to discontinue the application of Article 9(2) of (EU) 1307/2013 (negative list).

Regulation 2 of these Regulations revokes regulation 11 (Active Farmers) of the 2015 Regulations which provides further clarification on the application of the common agriculture policy ‘negative list’.

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as they have no impact on the costs of business.
