# Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 2nd March 2004

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 12th April 2004

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 7(2), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 12th April 2004.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

#### Amendment to Schedule 2

###### 3

For the table set out in Schedule 2 to the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00004] there shall be substituted the following table –

| Disposable Income Range | Maximum Contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Exceeding £83 but not exceeding £90 a week | £7 |
| Exceeding £90 but not exceeding £97 a week | £14 |
| Exceeding £97 but not exceeding £104 a week | £21 |
| Exceeding £104 but not exceeding £111 a week | £28 |
| Exceeding £111 but not exceeding £118 a week | £35 |
| Exceeding £118 but not exceeding £125 a week | £42 |
| Exceeding £125 but not exceeding £132 a week | £49 |
| Exceeding £132 but not exceeding £139 a week | £56 |
| Exceeding £139 but not exceeding £146 a week | £63 |
| Exceeding £146 but not exceeding £153 a week | £70 |
| Exceeding £153 but not exceeding £160 a week | £77 |

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

Lord Filkin — Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State, — Department for Constitutional Affairs — Dated 2nd March 2004

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981 so as to substitute a new scale of contributions payable for legal advice and assistance under Article 7(2) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
