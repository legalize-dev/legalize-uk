# The Social Security (Income-related Benefits) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 27th February 2019

Coming into operation: 31st March 2019

Regulations 5 and 6 are made with the consent of the Department of Finance[^f00008].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Income-related Benefits) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 and come into operation on 31 March 2019.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00009] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Income Support (General) Regulations

###### 2

In Schedule 9 of the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00010] (sums to be disregarded in the calculation of income other than earnings), after paragraph 72B, insert—

> > (72C) Any payment of carer’s allowance supplement made under section 81 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018.

#### Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations

###### 3

In Schedule 6 of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00011] (sums to be disregarded in the calculation of income other than earnings), after paragraph 68B, insert—

> > (68C) Any payment of carer’s allowance supplement made under section 81 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018.

#### Amendment of the State Pension Credit Regulations

###### 4

In regulation 15(1) of the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00012] (Income for purposes of the Act), after sub-paragraph (qa), insert—

> > (qb) carer’s allowance supplement payable under section 81 of the social Security (Scotland) Act 2018;

#### Amendment of the Housing Benefit Regulations

###### 5

In Schedule 6 of the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00013] (sums to be disregarded in the calculation of income other than earnings), after paragraph 66, insert—

> > (67) Any payment of carer’s allowance supplement made under section 81 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018.

#### Amendment of the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations

###### 6

In regulation 27(1)(h) of the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00014] (meaning of income), after paragraph (xvb), insert—

> > (xvc) carer’s allowance supplement payable under section 81 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018;

#### Amendment of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations

###### 7

In Schedule 8 of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00015] (sums to be disregarded in the calculation of income other than earnings), after paragraph 64, insert—

> > (65) Any payment of carer’s allowance supplement made under section 81 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make amendments to social security legislation as a consequence of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Consequential Modifications) Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018/872](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/872)).

Scottish Ministers have agreed to pay a “carer’s allowance supplement” to qualifying individuals who, on dates to be determined by the Scottish Ministers, are both in receipt of “carer’s allowance” under section 70 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act [1992 (c. 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4) and resident in Scotland.

These Regulations provides for carer’s allowance supplement to be disregarded as income when determining a claimant’s entitlement to benefits under: the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987; the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996; the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006; and the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008. In both the State Pension Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 and the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, the supplement is excluded from being a social security benefit which would otherwise be taken into account as income in determining entitlement to those benefits.
