# Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 16th June 2008

Coming into operation-: 1st August 2008

The Department for Employment and Learning[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 50(1) and (2) and 134(1) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and come into operation on 1st August 2008.

###### 2

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Students Awards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00004].

#### Revocation

###### 3

The following provisions of the Student Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00005] are revoked—

- (a) regulation 13; and

- (b) the Schedule.

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 4

The principal Regulations are amended as provided by regulations 5 to 9.

###### 5

- (1) Regulation 2 (Interpretation) shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In the definition of “new academic term” for “2007” there shall be substituted “2008”.

- (3) For the definition of “new payment” there shall be substituted—

> > - “new payment” means a payment made under these Regulations as amended by the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006, the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, and the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008;

- (4) For the definition of “old payment” there shall be substituted—

> > - “old payment” means a payment which would have been made under these Regulations, as amended by the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004, the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005, the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 if they had not been amended by the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008;

###### 6

In regulation 9 (Terms and conditions) in paragraph (2)(a) and (b) for “2006” in each place where it occurs, there shall be substituted “2007”.

###### 7

In Schedule 5 (Fees) for paragraph (iv) there shall be substituted—

> > (iv) in the case of a course at the University of Buckingham, £2,975 payable in four instalments of £744, £744, £744 and £743;

###### 8

In Schedule 7 (Resources) in paragraph 4(4) for “the Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007” there shall be substituted “the Education (Student Support) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008”.

###### 9

The figures in the third column of the table in the Schedule are substituted for the figures in the second column where they appear in the provisions of the principal Regulations set out in the first column of the table.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations further amend the Students Awards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No. 459](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/459)) “the principal Regulations”. The principal Regulations ensure that students who began their courses before 1st September 1998, and certain other students, continue to receive students awards and payments under those awards until the end of their courses.

Regulation 5 amends the principal Regulations so that the new payment rates prescribed in the Schedule to these Regulations shall apply only in relation to an academic term which commences on or after 1st September 2008. Where an academic year starts before 1st September 2008, but ends after 1st September 2008, the old and new payment rates shall be applied proportionally.

Regulation 7 specifies increases in the rates of fee awards in relation to courses at the University of Buckingham. Increases in relation to other fee awards and grants are provided for in regulation 9 and set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

These Regulations also revoke in part the Students Awards (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.R. 2007 No. 336](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/336)).

A full regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for these Regulations as it has no impact on the costs of business.
