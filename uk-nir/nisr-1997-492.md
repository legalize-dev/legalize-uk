# Food Safety (Enforcement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 13th November 1997

Coming into operation: 8th December 1997

The Department of Agriculture and the Department of Health and Social Services, acting jointly as the Department concerned, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 26(1A) of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001] and of every other power enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Safety (Enforcement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 8th December 1997.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order —

#### Enforcement of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991

###### 3

- (1) For the purposes of Article 26(1A) of the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 and subject to paragraph (2) the whole of that Order is specified in the case of—

- (a) dairy farms;

- (b) liquid milk plants;

- (c) the importation of milk;

- (d) the transport of milk;

- (e) meat plants other than—

- (i) meat plants which are not, and do not share a common curtilage with, licensed premises and which are used exclusively for the production or storage of one or more of the following—

- (aa) meat products;

- (bb) minced meat which is not intended for sending out of the United Kingdom;

- (cc) meat preparations;

- (dd) other products of animal origin;

- (ii) meat plants or parts of meat plants which are, or share a common curtilage with, licensed premises and which are used exclusively for the production or storage of one or more of the following—

- (aa) meat products;

- (bb) other products of animal origin.

- (2) Paragraph (1)(*e*) shall apply only in relation to the Department of Agriculture’s functions under the Poultry Meat, Farmed Game Bird Meat and Rabbit Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995, the Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, the Wild Game Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997, the Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and the Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture on

L. McKibben — Assistant Secretary — 13th November 1997.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

W. B. Smith — Assistant Secretary — 13th November 1997.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the Food Safety (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 to be enforced and executed by the Department of Agriculture in the case of certain premises and activities.
