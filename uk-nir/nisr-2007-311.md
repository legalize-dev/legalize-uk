# The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 26th June 2007

Coming into operation: 8th October 2007

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 28(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Traffic Signs (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 8th October 2007.

#### Amendments

###### 2

- (1) The Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00004] shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (2) to (8).

- (2) In Schedule 1 (warning signs) in diagram 563 in item 3 of the table after the words “13. “Pedestrians crossing”” there shall be inserted the following—

> > (14) “Runners in road”
> (15) “Walkers in road”

- (3) In Schedule 2 (regulatory signs)—

- (a) for diagram 639.1A and the words specified in and under it there shall be substituted the diagram and the words specified in and under it specified in Part I of the Schedule;

- (b) in diagram 660 in item 3 of the table after the words “5. “Resident permit holders only”” there shall be inserted the following—

- Permit identification code letters, as shown in diagram 660.3, may be added and may be varied in the manner indicated in that diagram in item 3 of the table.”;

- (c) in diagram 660.3 in item 3 of the table after the word “appropriate” there shall be inserted “. The size of the code letter and patch may be varied and may be in any contrasting colours. The code letter may be varied to a number or a combination of letters (capitals, lower case or both) and number, or a name identifying a parking zone or parking permit identification”;

- (d) in diagram 660.4 in item 3 of the table after the words ““Loading only”” there shall be inserted the following—

- Permit identification code letters, as shown in diagram 660.3, may be added and may be varied in the manner indicated in that diagram in item 3 of the table.;

- (i) after the words “Permitted variants: Schedule 16, item 21” there shall be inserted “The legend “Meter ZONE” may be placed below the no waiting roundel.”;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the Traffic Signs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997.

The principal amendments are to—

- (a) substitute new diagrams, and the words and tables immediately below them, for the signs shown in diagrams 639.1A, 660.6 and 660.7 (regulation 2(3)(a) and (f) and (7)(b), (c) and (d));

- (b) prescribe new permitted variants for the signs shown in diagrams 563, 660, 660.3, 660.4, 660.5, 661A, 661.1, 661.2, 661.3, 661.4, 662, 663 and 663.1 (regulation 2(2) and (3)(b) to (e), (g) to (i) and (k) to (n)); and

- (c) prescribe four new signs shown in diagrams 670.3 (for use in indicating that the maximum speed limit is in miles per hour), 879.1 (for use in indicating an “average speed” enforcement area), 880 (for use in reminding drivers of the maximum speed limit and that speed cameras are in place) and 7001.3 (to advise vehicular traffic that it should proceed slowly owing to workforce in road ahead) (regulations 2(3)(o), (4)(d), (6), (7)(a) and (8)).

These Regulations have been notified to the European Commission pursuant to Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) of 22nd June 1998 laying down a procedure for the provision of information in the field of technical standards and regulations and of rules on Information Society Services (O.J. No. L204, 21.07.98, p. 37-48) as amended by Directive [98/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0048) of 20th July 1998 (O.J. No. L217, 05.08.98, p. 18-26). Copies of the Directives can be obtained from The Stationery Office Bookshop, 16 Arthur Street, Belfast, BT1 4GD.
