# The Review Tribunal (Revocation) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 27th September 2019

Coming into operation: 1st October 2019

The Department of Justice makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 83(1) and (4) of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00001] and now vested in the Department of Justice[^f00002] after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Review Tribunal (Revocation) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2019.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Review Tribunal (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2019 are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 27th September 2019.

Peter May — A senior officer of the Department of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules revoke the Review Tribunal (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2019.
