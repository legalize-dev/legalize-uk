# Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2012

Made

Coming into operation

The Department has taken into consideration the recommendations made by the Police Negotiating Board for the United Kingdom and supplied the Board with a draft of the Regulations in accordance with section62(1ZA) of the Police Act 1996[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2012 and shall come into operation on 27th May 2012.

#### Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 3(1) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday”, for 2012 only, for the words “and last Monday in May” substitute “,4th and 5th June”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 25th April 2012

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 to allow for the public holiday on the last Monday in May to be replaced by public holidays on 4th and 5th June to mark Her Majesty the Queen’s Diamond Jubilee.
