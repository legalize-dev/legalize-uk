# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 17) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made: 18th July 2023

The Powers are exercisable by the Department by virtue of Article 2 the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Cessation of Transitory Provision) Order 2020[^f00003].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 17) Order (Northern Ireland) 2023.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

- (1) The following provisions of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 come into operation on 19th July 2023.

- (2) In Part 7 (Miscellaneous), Article 127(2)(b) and (3).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 18th July 2023.

David Tarr — A senior officer of the Department

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Explanatory Note

This Order brings into operation provisions in the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015.

Article 2 brings Article 127(2)(b) and (3) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 into operation on the 19th of July to amend Article 7 of the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 with regard to collection of child support maintenance.
