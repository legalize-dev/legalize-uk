# The Financial Provisions (2014 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 18th August 2016

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(2) of the Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Financial Provisions (2014 Act) (Commencement No. 3) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 8 of the Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 is 22nd August 2016.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 18th August 2016.

Claire Sugden — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings section 8 of the Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 into operation on 22nd August 2016.
