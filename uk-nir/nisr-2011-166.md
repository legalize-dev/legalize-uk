# The Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency (Establishment and Constitution) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 24th March 2011

Coming into operation: 26th May 2011

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 3(2) and 3(4) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Special Agencies) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency (Establishment and Constitution) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 26th May 2011.

- (2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Northern Ireland Medical and Dental Training Agency (Establishment and Constitution) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00002].

#### Amendment of Article 5 of the principal Order

###### 2

In Article 5(1) (*Disqualification for appointment*) of the principal Order, delete sub-paragraph (h).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 24th March 2011

Diane Taylor — A senior officer of the — Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order removes the disqualification for appointment relating to persons holding employment of office with a trade union which represents the interests of members who are employed by a health and social services body.
