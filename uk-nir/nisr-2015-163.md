# The Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 16th March 2015

Coming into operation: 6th April 2015

The Social Security Advisory Committee has agreed that proposals in respect of these regulations should not be referred to it[^f00008].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendments No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 6 April 2015.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00009] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendments of the Income Support (General) Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 39(2)(a) of the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00010] (deduction of tax and contributions for self-employed earners)—

- (a) for “11(1)” substitute “11(2)”;

- (b) for “11(3)” substitute “11(8)”; and

- (c) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

#### Amendment of the Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations

###### 3

In paragraphs 2A(4)(a) and 3(6)(a) of Schedule 1 to the Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00011](calculation of N and M) for “section 11(1) or, as the case may be, (3)” substitute “section 11(2) or, as the case may be, (8)”.

#### Amendment of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations

###### 4

In regulation 102(2)(a) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00012](deduction of tax and contributions for self-employed earners)—

- (a) for “11(1)” substitute “11(2)”;

- (b) for “11(3)” substitute “11(8)”; and

- (c) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

#### Amendment of the Child Support (Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases) Regulations

###### 5

In paragraph 7(5)(a) of the Schedule to the Child Support (Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00013] (net weekly income) for “section 11(1) or, as the case may be, (3)” substitute “section 11(2) or, as the case may be, (8)”.

#### Amendment of the Housing Benefit Regulations

###### 6

- (1) The Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00014] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 31(c) (disregard of changes in tax, contributions etc.) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

- (3) In regulation 36(2)(a) (deduction of tax and contributions of self-employed earners)—

- (a) for “11(1)” substitute “11(2)”;

- (b) for “11(3)” substitute “11(8)”; and

- (c) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

#### Amendment of the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations

###### 7

- (1) The Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00015] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 32(c) (disregard of changes in tax, contributions etc.) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

- (3) In regulation 38(2)(a) (deduction of tax and contributions of self-employed earners)—

- (a) for “11(1)” substitute “11(2)”;

- (b) for “11(3)” substitute “11(8)”; and

- (c) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

#### Amendment of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations

###### 8

In regulation 99(3)(a) of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00016](deduction of tax and contributions for self-employed earners)—

- (a) for “11(1)” substitute “11(2)”;

- (b) for “(3)” substitute “(8)”; and

- (c) for “small earnings exception” substitute “small profits threshold”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend a number of different regulations relating to social security in consequence of certain provisions of Schedule 1 to the National Insurance Act [2015 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/5) (“the 2015 Act”) coming into operation. Paragraph 12 of Schedule 1 to the 2015 Act substitutes section 11 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c.7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7), which relates to liability for Class 2 National Insurance contributions. From 6th April 2015 self-employed earners with profits in a tax year of £5,965 (the Small Profits Threshold) or over will pay Class 2 contributions via the Self-Assessment system.

Regulation 1 provides for citation, commencement and interpretation.

Regulations 2 to 8 amend the following regulations:

- the Income Support (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987;

- the Child Support (Maintenance Assessments and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992;

- the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996;

- the Child Support (Maintenance Calculations and Special Cases) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001;

- the Housing Benefit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006;

- the Housing Benefit (Persons who have attained the qualifying age for state pension credit) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006;

- the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008,

in particular so as to align the language and legislative references with those contained in the 2015 Act.
