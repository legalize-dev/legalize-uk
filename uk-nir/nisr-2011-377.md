# The Rates (Information Requirement) (Appointed Day) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 26th October 2011

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(5) of the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rates (Information Requirement) (Appointed Day) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

1st November 2011 is the day appointed for the purposes of section 6(5) of the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 26th October 2011

Brian McClure — A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Section 6(5) of the Rates (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009 extended the power of the Department of Finance and Personnel (“the Department”) to require information in respect of hereditaments subject to unoccupied rating to all domestic property until such day as the Department by order appoints. This Order appoints 1st November 2011.
