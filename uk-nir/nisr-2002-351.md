# The Social Security (2002 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 18th November 2002

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 9(1) of the Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security (2002 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2002.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of–

- (a) the provisions of the Act specified in Part I of the Schedule, is 19th November 2002; and

- (b) the provisions of the Act specified in Part II of the Schedule for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations, is 19th November 2002.

## SCHEDULE

## PART I — provisions coming into operation on 19th november 2002

| Provisions of the Act | Subject Matter |
| --- | --- |
| Section 6 | Use of information for, or relating to, employment and training |
| The following repeals in Schedule 2 and section 8(2) in so far as it relates to them– section 116C(8) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002]; and paragraph 69 of Schedule 6 to the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00003] | Repeals |

## PART II — provisions coming into operation on 19th november 2002 for the purpose only of making regulations

| Provisions of the Act | Subject Matter |
| --- | --- |
| Section 1 | Maternity pay period |
| Section 2 | Rate of statutory maternity pay |
| Section 3 | Entitlement to statutory maternity pay |
| Section 4(1)(b) and (2) | Rate of maternity allowance |
| The following repeal in Schedule 2 and section 8(2) in so far as it relates to it– in section 160(2)(a) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00004], the words “, wholly or partly because of pregnancy or confinement” | Repeals |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 18th November 2002.

John O'Neill — Senior Officer of the — Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of provisions of the Social Security Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 as follows–

- 19th November 2002 for provisions relating to the use of information for, or relating to, employment and training, and consequential repeals; and

- 19th November 2002 for provisions relating to the maternity pay period, the rate of, and entitlement to, statutory maternity pay, the rate of maternity allowance and a consequential repeal, for the purpose only of authorising the making of regulations.
