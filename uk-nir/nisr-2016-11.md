# Change of District Name (Armagh, Banbridge and Craigavon) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 18th January 2016

Coming into operation: 24th February 2016

In accordance with that section 51(1), the Department of the Environment has received an application from Armagh, Banbridge and Craigavon Borough Council to change the name of the district of the council from Armagh, Banbridge and Craigavon to Armagh City, Banbridge and Craigavon.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Change of District Name (Armagh, Banbridge and Craigavon) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 24th February 2016.

#### Change of district name

###### 2

The name of the district of Armagh, Banbridge and Craigavon shall be changed to Armagh City, Banbridge and Craigavon.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18th January 2016.

Nichola Creagh — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order changes the name of the local government district of Armagh, Banbridge and Craigavon identified in Article 2(1) of, and Schedule 1 to, the Local Government (Boundaries) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 to Armagh City, Banbridge and Craigavon.
