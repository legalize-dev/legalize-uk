# The Scrapie (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 9th March 2016

Coming into operation: 1st April 2016

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development— being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2); and in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 56(1), (2) and (5) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00003]; and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel in respect of the powers conferred by that section 56,

being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Union in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2); and

in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 56(1), (2) and (5) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00003]; and

with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel in respect of the powers conferred by that section 56,

makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Scrapie (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and come into operation on 1st April 2016.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it shall apply to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00005].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 3

For the Schedule substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Scrapie (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008, as amended, by substituting a new schedule. In the new Schedule the following fees are increased—

- (a) the fee for the initial flock inspection is increased from £207.61 to £233.44, and

- (b) the fee for the subsequent annual inspection is increased from £93.40 to £115.04.
