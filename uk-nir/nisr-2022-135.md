## SCHEDULE — INCREASE OF LIMITS

|  | *Column 1* *Relevant statutory provision* | *Column 2* *Subject of provision* | *Column 3* *Old Limit**[^f00019]* | *Column 4* *New Limit* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | Article 40(6) of the 1995 Order | Minimum amount of compensation where individual expelled from trade union in contravention of Article 38 of the 1995 Order and not re-admitted to the union by the date of application to the tribunal. | £10,334 | £10,840 |
| 2. | Article 23(1) of the 1996 Order | Maximum amount of “a week’s pay” for the purpose of calculating a redundancy payment or various awards, including the basic or additional award of compensation for unfair dismissal. | £566 | £594 |
| 3. | Article 63(1) of the 1996 Order | Limit on amount of guarantee payment payable to an employee in respect of any day. | £30.00 | £31.00 |
| 4. | Article 77E(3) of the 1996 Order | Amount of award for unlawful inducement relating to trade union membership or activities, or for unlawful inducement relating to collective bargaining. | £4,557 | £4,780 |
| 5. | Article 154(1) of the 1996 Order | Minimum amount of basic award of compensation where dismissal is unfair by virtue of Article 132(1)(a) and (b), 132A(1)(d)[^f00020], 133(1), 134 or 136(1) of the 1996 Order. | £6,890 | £7,228 |
| 6. | Article 158(1) of the 1996 Order | Limit on amount of compensatory award for unfair dismissal. | £89,669 | £94,063 |
| 7. | Article 231(1)(a) and (b) of the 1996 Order | Limit on amount in respect of any one week payable to an employee in respect of a debt to which Part XIV of the 1996 Order applies and which is referable to a period of time. | £566 | £594 |
