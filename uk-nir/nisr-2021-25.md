# The Superannuation (Commissioner for Survivors of Institutional Childhood Abuse) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made

Coming into operation

The Department of Finance makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Commissioner for Survivors of Institutional Childhood Abuse) Order (Northern Ireland) 2021.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 25th February 2021 and shall have effect from 2nd November 2020.

#### Addition to list of offices

###### 2

There shall be added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, the Office of Commissioner for Survivors of Institutional Childhood Abuse[^f00003].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 3rd February 2021.

Grace Nesbitt — A senior officer of the — Department of Finance

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order adds the Office of Commissioner for Survivors of Institutional Childhood Abuse to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972, for which the Department of Finance may make pension schemes. It has retrospective effect from 2nd November 2020 by virtue of Article 3(7) of the 1972 Order.
