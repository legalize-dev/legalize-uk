# The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2010

Made: 25th February 2010

The Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 87 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002[^f00001], makes the following Order:

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 13) Order 2010.

###### 2

The following provisions of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 shall come into force on 1st March 2010—

- (a) section 78 (Power to abolish Court Service);

- (b) section 82 (Excepted matters: judicial office-holders);

- (c) section 83 (Reserved matters: new institutions);

- (d) section 86 (Repeals and revocations) to the extent necessary to bring into force the provisions listed in paragraph (e);

- (e) In Schedule 13 (Repeals and revocations), the entries in respect of—

- (i) the Northern Ireland Act 1998[^f00002] in so far as it extends to—

- (aa) paragraph 11 of Schedule 2 to that Act;

- (bb) paragraphs 6, 12, 14, 22, 24 and 36 of Schedule 10 to that Act;

- (ii) the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 in so far as it extends to section 9(13) of that Act.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 25th February 2010

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force the provisions of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 specified in article 2 on 1st March 2010.
