# The Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme (Confirmation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 8th February 2010

Coming into force: 1st March 2010

The Department has complied with the provisions of section 2 of the Act and has considered such observations as were sent to it in relation to the 2010 Scheme and does not consider it proper to hold an inquiry.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme (Confirmation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010.

#### Confirmation and date of operation of Scheme

###### 2

The Department confirms without modification the 2010 Scheme which shall come into operation on and from 1st March 2010.

## SCHEDULE — LOUGH NEAGH (LEVELS) SCHEME 2010

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 2010.

###### 2

The Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1955[^f00006] as supplemented and modified by the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1995[^f00007] and the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 2004[^f00008] shall have effect with the substitution of the following for Article 5, that is to say:

- “5. In this Scheme references to the level of the Lough shall be construed as references to the mean level of the Lough as recorded on the system maintained by the Department at Toomebridge on the northern shore of the Lough, at Oxford Island on the southern shore, at Loughview Road on the eastern shore and at Ardboe Point on the western shore”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 8th February 2010.

John C Clarke — A Senior Officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order confirms and brings into operation the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 2010 (“the 2010 Scheme”) which supplements and modifies the Lough Neagh (Levels) Scheme 1955 (“the 1955 Scheme”). The 2010 Scheme substitutes a new Article for Article 5 of the 1955 Scheme so that the mean level of the Lough shall now be the mean level as recorded on the systems maintained by the Department at Toomebridge and Oxford Island, on the northern and southern shores respectively, and additionally at Loughview Road and Ardboe Point on the eastern and western shores respectively of the Lough. The range of levels within which Lough Neagh is controlled will not change under the new Scheme.
