# The Water Framework Directive (Priority Substances and Classification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 7th December 2012

Coming into operation: 31st December 2012

The Department of the Environment, being a Department designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 in relation to the environment, acting in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by that section and by Article 5 of the Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 , makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Water Framework Directive (Priority Substances and Classification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 31st December 2012.

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Water Framework Directive (Priority Substances and Classification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

###### 3

In Regulation 8 (inventory of emissions, discharges and losses) of the Water Framework Directive (Priority Substances and Classification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 , for the date “31 December 2012” substitute “ 22nd December 2013 ”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 7th December 2012

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment
