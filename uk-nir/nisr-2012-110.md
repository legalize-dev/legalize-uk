# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Disclosure of Restricted Information by the Pensions Regulator) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 9th March 2012

Coming into operation: 6th April 2012

In accordance with Article 81(2) of that Order it has consulted with the Pensions Regulator.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Disclosure of Restricted Information by the Pensions Regulator) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2012.

#### Amendment of Schedule 3 to the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005

###### 2

In Schedule 3 to the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (restricted information held by the Regulator: certain permitted disclosures to facilitate exercise of functions) in the second column of the entry relating to any body carrying on activities concerned with any of the matters set out in section 16(2) of the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004[^f00002] etc.[^f00003]—

- (a) in paragraph (e) for the words from “Institute of Actuaries” to the end substitute “Institute and Faculty of Actuaries[^f00004] (“the Institute and Faculty”), or persons who are not such members but are subject to the rules of that body in performing actuarial functions (“members”),”;

- (b) in paragraph (h) for “Institute or Faculty”, in each place where it occurs, substitute “Institute and Faculty”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 9th March 2012

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends Schedule 3 to the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 to replace references to the Institute of Actuaries and to the Faculty of Actuaries with references to the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries, which is the new body formed by the merger of the Institute of Actuaries and the Faculty of Actuaries.
