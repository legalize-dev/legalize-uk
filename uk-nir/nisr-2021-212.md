# The Police Rehabilitation and Retraining Trust (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: 15th July 2021

Coming into operation: 10th August 2021

The Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 11(2) of the Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Rehabilitation and Retraining Trust (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 10th August 2021.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these Regulations, “the Board” means the Board of Trustees of the Police Rehabilitation and Retraining Trust.

#### Procedures

###### 3

- (1) In the Police Rehabilitation and Retraining Trust Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00002], existing regulation 6 (procedures) shall become regulation 6(1).

- (2) After regulation 6(1) insert—

> > (2) Those procedures may provide for the discharge, under the general direction of the Board, of any of the Board’s functions by a committee of the Board or by one or more of the members of the Board or of its staff.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 15th July 2021

Naomi Long — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police Rehabilitation and Retraining Trust Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 ([S.R. 2014 No. 163](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2014/163)) to provide that the Board of the Trust may delegate any of its functions to a Committee of the Board or to one or more of the members of the Board or to its staff.
