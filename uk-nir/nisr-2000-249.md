# The Housing Benefit (General) (Amendment No. 4) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made: 1st August 2000

Coming into operation: 25th August 2000

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1C), 165(1), (3) to (5) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00003] in so far as its consent is required, and after agreement by the Social Security Advisory Committee that proposals in respect of these Regulations should not be referred to it[^f00004], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Benefit (General) (Amendment No. 4) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 25th August 2000.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of regulation 2B of the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 2B of the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00006] (cases in which section 1(1A) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 is disapplied)—

- (a) in paragraph (*a*) “or” shall be omitted;

- (b) after paragraph (*b*) there shall be added the following—

> > ; or
> (c) to a claim for a further grant of housing benefit made before 5th September 2000 under regulation 72(11) to (13) (time and manner in which claims are to be made).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on

John O'Neill — Senior Officer of the — Department for Social Development — 1st August 2000.

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on

Brian Delaney — Senior Officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel — 2nd August 2000.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Housing Benefit (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987.

Regulation 2 provides, in cases where a claim for a further grant of housing benefit is made or invited before 5th September 2000, an exemption from the requirements in respect of national insurance numbers imposed by section 1 of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992.

These Regulations do not impose any charge on business.
