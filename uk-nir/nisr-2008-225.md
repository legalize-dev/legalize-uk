# The Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 20th May 2008

Coming into operation: 23rd June 2008

The Magistrates’ Courts Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and Article 165 of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00002] after consultation with the Lord Chancellor and with the agreement of the Lord Chief Justice.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 23rd June 2008.

#### Amendment to the Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

The Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with rule 3 below.

###### 3

In rule 2 (*Matters prescribed for the purposes of the Order*), in paragraph (5)(a) for “and 9” substitute “, 9 and 11”.

## Signed

George Conner — John Rea — John P.B. Maxwell — Sean McCann — P Kelly — Dated 20th May 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 323](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/323)) to allow a lay magistrate hearing an ex parte application under rules 2(5)(a) and rule 5 to appoint a guardian ad litem.
