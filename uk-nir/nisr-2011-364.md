# The M1/Trunk Road T3 (Motorway Service Area) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 18th October 2011

Coming into operation: 5th December 2011

The Department makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 14(1), 15(1) and 16(1) and (2) of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993 and now exercisable by it[^f00003]:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as The M1/Trunk Road T3 (Motorway Service Area) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 5th December 2011.

- (2) In this Order “the map” means the drawing numbered 1BH0024/2018 Rev E, marked “The M1/Trunk Road T3 (Motorway Service Area) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011” copies of which have been deposited at the Department’s Roads Service offices, Headquarters, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB and Eastern Division, Hydebank, 4 Hospital Road, Belfast BT8 8JL.

#### Designation and direction

###### 2

The Department designates and directs that the roads described in the Schedule shall become special and trunk roads and be part of the Belfast-Ballygawley-Londonderry Land Frontier Trunk Road T3.

#### Classification of traffic

###### 3

The roads described in the Schedule shall be used only by traffic of Classes I and II as set out in Schedule 1 to the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993.

## SCHEDULE — THE NEW ROADS TO BE CONSTRUCTED AS SPECIAL AND TRUNK ROADS AND PART OF THE M1 MOTORWAY AND T3

439 metres of new slip roads in the townlands of Tullynacross and Ballyskeagh and County of Down consisting of:

- (a) 99 metres of slip road adjacent to the Belfast bound carriageway of the M1 motorway extending in a generally north-easterly direction from a point on the M1 718 metres north-east of the centre point of Tullynacross Bridge, more particularly delineated, coloured red and marked S1 on the map;

- (b) 144 metres of slip road adjacent to the Belfast bound carriageway of the M1 motorway extending in a generally north-easterly direction from a point on the M1 966 metres north-east of the centre point of Tullynacross Bridge, more particularly delineated, coloured red and marked S2 on the map;

- (c) 161 metres of slip road adjacent to the Dungannon bound carriageway of the M1 motorway extending in a generally south-westerly direction from a point on the M1 700 metres north-east of the centre point of Tullynacross Bridge, more particularly delineated, coloured red and marked S3 on the map; and

- (d) 35 metres of slip road adjacent to the Dungannon bound carriageway of the M1 motorway extending in a generally south-westerly direction from a point on the M1 1010 metres north-east of the centre point of Tullynacross Bridge, more particularly delineated, coloured red and marked S4 on the map.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 18th October 2011

D J Millar — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides that 439 metres of new slip roads described in the Schedule and more particularly delineated and coloured red on the map shall be special roads subject to motorway traffic restrictions and be part of the Belfast-Ballygawley-Londonderry-Land Frontier Trunk Road T3.

The map referred to above means Drawing No 1BHOO24/2018 Rev E marked “The M1/Trunk Road T3 (Motorway Service Area) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011” copies of which have been deposited at the Department’s Roads Service offices, Headquarters, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Belfast BT2 8GB and Eastern Division, Hydebank, 4 Hospital Road, Belfast BT8 8JL.
