# The Feeding Stuffs and the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 25th January 2006

Coming into operation: 16th February 2006

There has been consultation in accordance with the requirements of section 84(1) of the Agriculture Act 1970 or as appropriate by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00007].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs and the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 16th February 2006.

#### Amendments to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

- (1) In Schedule 5 (prescribed limits for undesirable substances) to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00008], Chapter A is amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4).

- (2) In relation to the entries for fluorine—

- (a) for the expression “Mineral mixtures for cattle, sheep and goats” in column 2 substitute the expression “Complementary feeding stuffs containing 4% phosphorous or less” and opposite that expression, in column 3 for the figure “2000” substitute “500”;

- (b) for the expression “Other complementary feeding stuffs” in column 2 substitute the expression “Complementary feeding stuffs containing more than 4% phosphorous” and opposite that expression, in column 3 for the expression “(fluorine content per percentage point phosphorous in the feeding stuff)” substitute “per 1% phosphorous”.

- (3) In relation to the entries for lead, for the expression “grass meal, lucerne meal or clover meal” in column 2 substitute the expression “green fodder (including products such as hay, silage, fresh grass, etc)”.

- (4) In relation to the entries for mercury, after the entry “–feed materials produced by the processing of fish or other marine animals” in column 2 insert the entry “–calcium carbonate” and opposite that entry, in column 3 insert the figure “0.3”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

1 These Regulations amend the Feeding Stuffs Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 (“the 2005 Regulations”) and the Feeding Stuffs (Sampling and Analysis) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 (“the 1999 Regulations”).

2 The Regulations implement the following European Community legislation — a Commission Directive [2005/6/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0006) (OJ No. L24, 27.1.2005, p.33) amending Directive [71/250/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1971/0250) as regards reporting and interpretation of analytical results required under Directive [2002/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0032); b Commission Directive [2002/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0007) (OJ No. L27, 29.1.2005, p.41) amending Directive [2002/70/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0070) establishing requirements for the determination of levels of dioxins and dioxin-like PCBs in feedingstuffs; and c Commission Directive [2002/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0008) (OJ No. L27, 29.1.2005, p.44) amending Annex 1 to Directive [2002/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0032) of the European Parliament and of the Council on undesirable substances in animal feed.

3 The Regulations also make consequential amendments to the 1999 Regulations to reflect the fact that as from 1.1.2006 the general obligation on Member States, formerly contained in Council Directive [95/53/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0053), to ensure that sampling and analysis carried out in pursuit of official controls follows prescribed Community methods, is now given effect by Article 11 of Regulation [(EC) No 882/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0882) of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls performed to ensure the verification of compliance with feed and food law, animal health and animal welfare rules (OJ No. L191, 28.5.2004, p.1).

4 Regulation 2 of these Regulations implements Commission Directive [2005/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0008) by amending Schedule 5 to the 2005 Regulations in respect of certain entries for lead, fluorine and mercury.

5 In regulation 3 of these Regulations — a paragraphs (2) and (5) implement Commission Directive [2005/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0007) in transposing by reference the amendments made by that Directive to Commission Directive [2002/70/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0070); b paragraph (3) makes the consequential amendments referred to in paragraph 3; c paragraphs (4) and (6) implement Commission Directive [2005/6/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0006) by introducing new requirements regarding expanded measurement uncertainty and correction for recovery when analysing and reporting on the analysis of animal feed to determine the levels of undesirable substances.
