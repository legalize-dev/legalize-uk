# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: at 6.00 p.m. on 30th April 2021

Laid before the Assembly: at 6.30 p.m. on 30th April 2021

Coming into operation: at 6.00 p.m. on 30th April 2021

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at the time they are made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

###### 2

The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 [^f00003] are amended in accordance with these Regulations.

- (a) In regulation 1(2), in the definition of “educational activity”, for subparagraph (f) substitute-

> > (f) a provider in receipt of grant funding from the Education Authority to deliver youth activities for persons aged 4 to 25 years in an informal setting;

- (b) In regulation 5(1)(d), after “in this regulation” insert “and in regulation 17”.

- (c) In regulation 17(3)(b), for “household or a bubble” substitute “household, a bubble or a party seated at the same table in accordance with regulation 5(1)(d)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 6.00 p.m. on 30th April 2021

Dr Michael McBride — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 in relation to the carrying out of certain educational activities and seating at hospitality premises.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
