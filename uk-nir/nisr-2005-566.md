# The Street Works (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 14th December 2005

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Street Works (1995 Order) (Commencement No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Article 60(2) of and Schedule 4 to the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 in so far as they relate to Article 79 of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993[^f00004] shall be 9th January 2006.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 14th December 2005.

R. Sherman — A senior officer of the — Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 9th January 2006 for the coming into operation of Article 60(2) of and Schedule 4 to the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 in so far as they relate to Article 79 of the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993.
