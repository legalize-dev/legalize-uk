# The Health and Safety at Work Order (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 14th September 2007

Coming into operation: 5th November 2007

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002]. The Department has been designated for the purposes of that subsection in relation to dangerous substances or preparations or dangerous or environmentally hazardous goods[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Safety at Work Order (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 5th November 2007.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Health and Safety at Work Order (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Health and Safety at Work Order (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00005] shall be amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation), in the definition of “the Directives”—

- (a) after sub-paragraph (f) there shall be added the following sub-paragraph—

> > (fa) Directive [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the approximation of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States relating to the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous preparations[^f00006];

- (b) after sub-paragraph (h) there shall be added the following sub-paragraph—

> > (ha) Directive [2000/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0018) of the European Parliament and of the Council on minimum examination requirements for safety advisers for the transport of dangerous goods by road, rail or inland waterway[^f00007];

- (c) at the end of sub-paragraphs (k) and (l) the word “and” shall be omitted;

- (d) after sub-paragraph (l) there shall be added the following sub-paragraphs—

> > (m) Commission Directive [2001/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0060) adapting to technical progress Directive [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045) of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the approximation of the laws, regulations and administrative provisions of the Member States relating to the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous preparations[^f00008];
> (n) Commission Directive [2003/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0028) adapting for the fourth time to technical progress Council Directive [94/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0055) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by road[^f00009];
> (o) Commission Directive [2003/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0029) adapting for the fourth time to technical progress Council Directive [96/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0049) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by rail[^f00010];
> (p) Commission Directive [2004/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0089) adapting for the fifth time to technical progress Council Directive [96/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0049) on the approximation of the laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by rail[^f00011]

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The Health and Safety at Work Order (Application to Environmentally Hazardous Substances) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 (“the 2003 Regulations”) extend the reference to dangerous substances in Article 3(1)(c) of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 (“the 1978 Order”) to include environmentally hazardous substances. The purpose of the 2003 Regulations is to enable regulations to be made under Article 17 of the 1978 Order to implement any obligation imposed by the Directives referred to in regulation 2(1) of the 2003 Regulations.

These Regulations amend regulation 2(1) of the 2003 Regulations to add references to Directives [1999/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0045) and [2000/18/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0018) and Commission Directives [2001/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0060), [2003/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0028), [2003/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2003/0029), [2004/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0089), [2004/110/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0110), [2004/111/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0111), [2006/89/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0089) and [2006/90/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0090) so that Regulations can be made under Article 17 of the 1978 Order to implement any obligation imposed by these Directives.

These Regulations have no impact on business.
