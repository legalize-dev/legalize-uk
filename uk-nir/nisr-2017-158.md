# The Pension Schemes Act 2015 (Judicial Pensions) (Consequential Provision No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made: 18th August 2017

Coming into operation: 21st August 2017

The Department for Communities makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 83 of the Pension Schemes Act 2015 .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Pension Schemes Act 2015 (Judicial Pensions) (Consequential Provision No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 21st August 2017.

#### Provision consequential on section 78 of the Pension Schemes Act 2015

###### 2

In section 31(4) of the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014 (new public body pension schemes) in the definition of “public body pension scheme” after “an existing scheme” insert “ or a scheme established under section 18A of the Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993 ”.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Pension Schemes Act 2015 (Judicial Pensions) (Consequential Provision) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 18th August 2017

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the — Department for Communities
