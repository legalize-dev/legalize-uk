# Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 2nd April 1996

Coming into operation: 3rd June 1996

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 79A(2) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 3rd June 1996.

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991

###### 2

In regulation 4(1) (fee for a licence) of the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00002] for “£60” there shall be substituted “£58·50”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on .

Trevor Pearson — Assistant Secretary — 2nd April 1996

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby approves these Regulations.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on

J. G. Sullivan — Assistant Secretary — 2nd April 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations further amend the Motor Vehicles (Taxi Drivers' Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1991 by decreasing the fee for a renewal of a licence from £60 to £58·50.

The fees for a first licence and a duplicate licence remain unchanged.
