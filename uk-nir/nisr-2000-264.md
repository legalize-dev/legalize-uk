# Students Awards (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

Made: 30th August 2000

Coming into operation: 31st August 2000

The Department of Higher and Further Education, Training and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 50(1) and 134(1)[^f00001] of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003] hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Students Awards (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 and shall come into operation on 31st August 2000.

#### Amendment of the Students Awards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

###### 2

- (1) The Students Awards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00004] shall be amended as provided in this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 11(1)(*a*) for the words “Schedule 5;” there shall be substituted the words “Schedule 5 less —”.

- (3) In regulation 18(4) for the words “paragraph (4)” there shall be substituted the words “paragraph (5)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Higher and Further Education, Training and Employment on 30th August 2000.

Robson Davison — A senior officer of the — Department of Higher and Further Education, Training and Employment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations correct many drafting errors in regulations 11(1) and 18(4) of the Students Awards Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 31st August 2000.
