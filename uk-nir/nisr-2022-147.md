# The Agriculture (2020 Act)(Commencement No. 1) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made: 23rd March 2022

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 57(4)(a) of the Agriculture Act 2020[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (2020 Act)(Commencement No. 1) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022.

#### Provisions of the Act coming into operation on 28th March 2022

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of paragraph 8 of Part 2 of Schedule 6 (exceptional market conditions: powers available to DAERA) to the Agriculture Act 2020 is 28th March 2022.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 23rd March 2022.

Norman Fulton — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations bring into operation paragraph 8 of Part 2 of Schedule 6 (Exceptional market conditions: powers available to DAERA) to the Agriculture Act 2020.
