# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No 15) Order 2021

Made: 18th August 2021

The Powers are exercisable by the Department by virtue of Article 2 the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Cessation of Transitory Provision) Order 2020[^f00003].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No 15) Order 2021.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 (universal credit: amendments) to the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 is 19th August 2021

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 18th August 2021.

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department

(This note is not part of the Order)
