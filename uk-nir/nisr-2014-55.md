# The Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 27th February 2014

Coming into force

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 5(1) and (2) of the Ombudsman (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 and Article 4(1) and (2) of the Commissioner for Complaints (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 and now vested in it makes the following Order:

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall have effect from 1st April 2013.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Salaries (Assembly Ombudsman and Commissioner for Complaints) Order (Northern Ireland 2009 is revoked.

#### Increase in Salaries

###### 3

The annual salaries payable to the Assembly Ombudsman and the Commissioner for Complaints shall be increased from £128,296 to £129,579 from 1st April 2013.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 27th February 2014.

Stephen Boyd — A senior officer of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister
