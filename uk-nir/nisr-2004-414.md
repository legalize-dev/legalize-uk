# Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 29th September 2004

Coming into operation: 1st November 2004

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 66(1) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2004.

#### Revocation

###### 2

- (1) Regulation 46(14)(b) of, and Schedule 6 to, the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

- (2) The Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 29th September 2004.

Wesley Shannon — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No. 447](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/447)) (“the 1995 Regulations”) by revoking regulation 46(14)(b) of, and Schedule 6 to, the 1995 Regulations, which provide for a taxi fitted with a taximeter and licensed for public hire to display a plate.

Schedule 6 to the 1995 Regulations was substituted by the Public Service Vehicles (Conditions of Fitness, Equipment and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 ([S.R. 2000 No. 136](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2000/136)), which are also revoked by these Regulations.
