# The Rural Needs (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made: 13th April 2017

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 of the Rural Needs Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 [^f00002], makes the following Order.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rural Needs (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2017.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of sections 1 to 4 of the Rural Needs Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, as they apply to Northern Ireland departments and district councils as listed in the Schedule to that Act, is 1st June 2017.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 13th April 2017

Pauline Keegan — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation, for Northern Ireland departments and district councils only, sections 1 to 4 of the Rural Needs Act (Northern Ireland) 2016.
