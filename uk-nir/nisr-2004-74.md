# Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 2nd March 2004

Coming into operation: 29th March 2004

The Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 26(1), 37(1), 114(1)(b) and 115(1)(b) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, with the approval of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure[^f00002], hereby makes the following Byelaws:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Byelaws may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 29th March 2004.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

The Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00003] shall be amended as provided in Byelaw 3.

###### 3

For Parts II and III of Schedule 3 (licences for fishing engines other than rod and line or hand line, and dealer’s licence) there shall be substituted the following Parts –

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Byelaws.)

These Byelaws amend the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003.

The Byelaws increase the duties payable for commercial fishing licences, other than eel licences, together with the fee payable for a licence which authorises the holder to buy and sell salmon, trout and eels (Byelaw 3).

The new licence duties and fee, and the size of the increases, are set out below: –

|  | Previous Rates | New Rates | Increase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | *£* | *£* | *£* |
| Draft net for the capture of salmon and freshwater fish on Lough Neagh | 101·00 | 110·00 | 9·00 |
| Single wall set net or trammel net for the capture of trout or rainbow trout in Lough Neagh per 91 metres (100 yards) | 14·00 | 15·00 | 1·00 |
| Single wall set net or trammel net for the taking of freshwater fish other than trout or rainbow trout per 91 metres (100 yards) | 14·00 | 15·00 | 1·00 |
| Bait net for the taking of freshwater fish other than trout or rainbow trout for use as baitper net | 4.50 | 5·00 | 0.50 |
| Fixed tidal draft net | 338.50 | 348.50 | 10·00 |
| Tidal draft net | 280.50 | 289·00 | 8.50 |
| Drift net for the taking of salmon in the sea | 280.50 | 289·00 | 8.50 |
| Bag net used in tidal waters | 634·00 | 653·00 | 19·00 |
| Dealer’s licence | 42·00 | 43·00 | 1·00 |

Duties will no longer be payable in respect of licences for the following fishing engines –

- (a) draft net for the capture of freshwater fish other than trout and rainbow trout on Lough Erne;

- (b) draft net for use in freshwater other than Lough Neagh or Lough Erne; and

- (c) salmon box or crib.

In addition, the enhanced duty payable in respect of a licence for a fixed tidal draft net which operates in the estuary of a river upstream of the defined mouth will no longer be payable.
