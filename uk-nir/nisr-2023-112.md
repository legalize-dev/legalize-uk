# The Child Support Fees (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made: 18th July 2023

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1

The Department for Communities makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(1) of the Child Maintenance Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00001], and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Child Support Fees (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 and shall come into operation on 19th July 2023.

#### Amendment of the Child Support Fees Regulations

###### 2

- (1) The Child Support Fees Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3 (the collection fee), paragraphs (6)[^f00004] and (7) are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 18th July 2023

David Tarr — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Child Support Fees Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 so as to revoke regulation 3(6) and (7).

The Child Support Collection (Domestic Abuse) Act [2023 (c. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/24) makes corresponding provision for collect and pay arrangements in child support. Regulation 3(6) and (7) of the 2014 Regulations is thereby revoked by regulation 2 to these regulations.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
