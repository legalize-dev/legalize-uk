# The Criminal Justice (European Protection Order) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 8th October 2015

Coming into operation: 2nd November 2015

The Department of Justice, being designated for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 [^f00001] in relation to criminal justice [^f00002], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by that section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Criminal Justice (European Protection Order) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and come into operation on 2nd November 2015.

#### Amendments to the Criminal Justice (European Protection Order) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2014

###### 2

- (1) The Criminal Justice (European Protection Order) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2014 are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 3 (Interpretation - Part 2), in paragraph (1) -

- (a) in the definition of “European protection order” for “means an order made in accordance with” substitute “has the meaning given by”; and

- (b) in the definition of “protection measure”, in subparagraph (c), for “approaching the protected person to within a particular distance” substitute “approaching the protected person whether at all or within a particular distance”.

- (3) In regulation 10 (Reviewing, modifying and revoking a protection measure and related European protection order), in the heading, for “Reviewing” substitute “Renewing”.

- (4) In regulation 14 (Procedural requirements relating to the making of a restraining order), in paragraph (1), subparagraph (a), for “member” substitute “issuing”.

- (5) In regulation 17 (Modification of the European protection order (and giving effect to a modified order)), in paragraph (6) for “the appropriate article” substitute “that Article”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 8th October 2015

David Ford — Minister of Justice
