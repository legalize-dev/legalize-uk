# Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Licensing of Fishing Engines) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2003

Made: 9th December 2003

Coming into operation: 1st January 2004

The Foyle Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission, in exercise of its powers under section 13(1) (amended by section 34(3) of the British-Irish Agreement Act 1999[^f00001] and section 5(1) of the British-Irish Agreement (Amendment) Act 2002[^f00001]) and 13(2) of the Foyle Fisheries Act 1952[^f00001] which stand transferred to it under section 34(1) of the British-Irish Agreement Act 1999, and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) and (2) of the Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, with the approval of the Government of Ireland and the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, under the agreement made between those Governments on 19th November 2002[^f00004] and section 5(1) of the British-Irish Agreement (Amendment) Act 2002, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Foyle Area and Carlingford Area (Licensing of Fishing Engines) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2003 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2004.

#### Amendment of the Foyle and Carlingford Area (Licensing of Fishing Engines) Regulations 2001

###### 2

The Foyle and Carlingford Area (Licensing of Fishing Engines) Regulations 2001[^f00005] shall be amended as provided in regulations 3 and 4.

#### Interpretation

###### 3

In regulation 2 (interpretation) for the definition of “coarse fishing rod licence” there shall be substituted the following definition –

> > - “coarse fishing rod licence” means a fishing licence issued by the Commission for two rods and lines to fish for, take and kill eels and coarse fish;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

- (a) providing for a coarse fishing rod licence to authorise the use of two rods and lines to fish for, take and kill eels and coarse fish;
