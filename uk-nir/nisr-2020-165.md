# The Universal Credit (Managed Migration and Miscellaneous Amendments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: at 1.33 p.m. on 3rd August 2020

Laid before Parliament: 4th August 2020

Coming into operation: 4th August 2020

Those powers are exercisable by the Secretary of State by virtue of Article 4(1) of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Universal Credit (Managed Migration and Miscellaneous Amendments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and come into operation on 4th August 2020.

#### Amendment of the Universal Credit (Managed Migration and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019

###### 2

- (1) The Universal Credit (Managed Migration and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 4 (two week run-on of income-based jobseeker’s allowance and income-related employment and support allowance: day appointed for abolition), after “beginning with the day” insert “after the day”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Will Quince — Parliamentary Under Secretary of State — Department for Work and Pensions — 1.33 p.m on 3rd August 2020

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Universal Credit (Managed Migration and Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2019 ([S.R. 2019 No. 152](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2019/152)) to correct a technical error and ensure regulation 4 of those Regulations provides for a full two week run-on of income-based jobseeker’s allowance and income-related employment and support allowance.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, public or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
