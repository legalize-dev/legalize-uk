# Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 27th November 1996

Coming into operation: 27th January 1997

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 50(4)(*c*) and 211A of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Roads (Speed Limit) (No. 9) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 27th January 1997.

#### Speed restrictions on certain roads

###### 2

The Department hereby directs that each of the roads and lengths of road specified in the Schedule shall be a restricted road for the purposes of Article 50 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

## SCHEDULE

#### Restricted Roads

###### 1

Audley Roundabout, Route A26, Ballymena.

###### 2

Greenmount Roundabout, Route A26, Ballymena.

###### 3

North Road, Route A26, Ballymena, from its junction with Audley Roundabout, Route A26, to its junction with Greenmount Roundabout, Route A26.

###### 4

Park Way, Route A42, Ballymena, from its junction with Greenmount Roundabout, Route A26, to its junction with Thomas Street Roundabout, Route A42.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 27th November 1996.

J. Carlisle — Assistant Secretary

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order makes the roads and lengths of road specified in the Schedule restricted roads, thereby imposing a 30 miles per hour speed limit on those roads.
