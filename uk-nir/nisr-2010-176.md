# The Biosecurity Guidance (Specification of Brucellosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 6th May 2010

Coming into operation: 1st June 2010

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 4A(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Biosecurity Guidance (Specification of Brucellosis) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2010.

#### Biosecurity guidance

###### 2

Brucellosis is hereby specified for the purposes of Article 4A(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 6th May 2010.

Colette McMaster — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies brucellosis for the purposes of Article 4A(1) of the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, enabling the Department to introduce statutory biosecurity guidance for that disease.
