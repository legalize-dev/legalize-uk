# Education Reform (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 19th March 1998

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 1 to the Northern Ireland Act 1974

Coming into operation: 30th July 1998

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 6(6) of the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education Reform (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998 and shall come into operation on 30th July 1998.

#### Amendment to Schedule 2 to the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989

###### 2

In Schedule 2 to the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^f00002] in columns (3) and (4) after the entry “French or German or Italian or Spanish or Irish (A)” there shall be inserted the entry “Irish (in Irish speaking schools only) (A)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education on

C. Jendoubi (Mrs.) — Assistant Secretary — 19th March 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends Schedule 2 to the Education Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 to add the compulsory contributory subject “Irish (in Irish speaking schools only) (A)” to the Language Studies Area of Study at key stages 3 and 4.

This Order comes into operation on 30th July 1998.
