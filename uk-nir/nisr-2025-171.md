# The County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2025

Made: 23rd October 2025

Coming into operation: 26th November 2025

The County Court Rules Committee makes and the Department of Justice, after consultation with the Lady Chief Justice, allows the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 47 of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the County Court (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2025 and shall come into operation on 26th November 2025.

###### 2

In these Rules—

- (a) “*the principal Rules*” means the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00002]; and

- (b) a reference to an Order, Rule, paragraph, Appendix or Form is a reference to that Order, Rule, paragraph, Appendix or Form so numbered in the principal Rules.

#### Amendments to the principal Rules

###### 3

The principal Rules are amended as follows—

- (1) In Order 19, Rule 5 from “(a)” to “2018” substitute “£43.00”.

- (2) In Order 24, Rule 9, for paragraph (7A) substitute—

> > (7A) The sum to be paid or tendered under paragraph (7)(a) to—
>   (a) a police officer is £17.00; and
>   (b) any other person is £23.00.

- (3) In Order 33, Rule 10, paragraph (6) from “(a)” to “2018” substitute “£10.00”.

- (4) In Order 55, Rule 17 from “(a)” to “2018” substitute “£4.00”.

- (5) For Appendix 2 substitute the new Appendix 2 set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the County Court Rules (Northern Ireland) 1981 ([S.R. 1981 No. 225](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1981/225)) to—

- amend Orders 19, 24, 33 and 55 and insert a new Appendix 2 to increase scale costs by 23%, increase travel entitlements by 32%, and provide for a fee of £75 to be payable for any necessary affidavit not otherwise provided for.
