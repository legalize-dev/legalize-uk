# The Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 24th February 2016

Coming into operation: 1st April 2016

The Department of Finance and Personnel makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 42(1G) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2016.

#### Designation of rural areas

###### 2

Wards as set out in the Schedule are designated as rural areas for the purpose of Article 42 (1F) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00002] is revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order sets out those local government wards which are designated as rural areas for the purposes of Article 42(1F) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977 which exempts from rates any automatic telling machine which is situated in an area so designated.
