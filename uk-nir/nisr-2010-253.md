# The Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 4th August 2010

Coming into operation: 28th August 2010

In accordance with section 56(1) and (5) of the Finance Act 1973, the Department of Finance and Personnel consents to the making of these Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 28th August 2010.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00006] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

###### 2

- (1) The Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) Regulations 2008[^f00007] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (3).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “official control samples”, after (iii) substitute “.” with “; or”.

- (b) in the definition of “official control samples”, after (iii) insert—

> > (iv) point 4 of part D of Annex II to Regulation [(EC) No. 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) as regards a Community target for the reduction of salmonella and other specific food-bourne zoonotic agents on laying flocks of *Gallus gallus* as last amended by [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1237/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1237)[^f00008] and as amended from time to time; or
> (v) part 1(b)(iii) of the Annex to [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 584/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0584)[^f00009] implementing Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) as regards a Community target for the reduction of the prevalence of certain salmonella serotypes in flocks of *Meleagrisgallopavo* as amended from time to time.

- (3) For the Schedule substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.

They make provision for the Department to charge fees for activities required under [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1003/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1003) (OJ No L 170, 1.7.2005, p 12), [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1168/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1168) (OJ No L 211, 1.8.2006, p 4), Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 646/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0646) and Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) (OJ No L 325, 12.12.2003, p1). The activities and the fees are listed in the Schedule.

A regulatory impact assessment, which includes compliance cost assessment of the effect that these Regulations are likely to have on business costs, has been placed in the Library of the Assembly. Copies may be obtained from the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, Animal Disease Control and Trade Policy Branch, Room 715, Dundonald House, Stormont Estate, Upper Newtownards Road, Belfast, BT4 3SB.
