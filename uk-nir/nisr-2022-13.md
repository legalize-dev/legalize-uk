# The Rates (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made: 21st January 2022

Coming into operation: 1st February 2022

The Department of Finance[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 8 of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Rates (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and comes into operation on 1st February 2022.

#### Amendment of the Rates Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

In regulation 2(1) of the Rates Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003], for “1st March” substitute “15th February”.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Rates (Coronavirus: Making of District Rates) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00004] are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 21st January 2022

Sharon Magee — A senior officer of the Department of Finance

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations restore the date by which a district council must make their district rate for a forthcoming rating year from 1st March to 15th February.

The change is made following a temporary adjustment during the height of the Covid-19 pandemic in order to allow district councils more time to address financial planning considerations.

Article 1 provides for citation and commencement.

Article 2 makes an amendment to the Rates Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 to restore the date by which a district council must make the district rates from 1st March back to 15th February.
