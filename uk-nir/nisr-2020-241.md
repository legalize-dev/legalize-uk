# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Amendment No. 18) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 6th November 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with Regulation 1

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25B and 25F(2) of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Amendment No. 18) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) Regulations 2 and 3, insofar as they apply to Denmark, shall come into operation at the time and on the date that the Regulations are made.

- (3) The remainder of the Regulations shall come into operation at 4.00 am on 7th November 2020.

- (4) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The principal Regulations are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

- (2) In relation to any person who arrived in Northern Ireland on or after 10th July 2020 and before the coming into operation of any amendment made by regulation 3 the principal Regulations apply as if that amendment had not been made.

#### Amendments to Schedule 3

###### 3

In Schedule 3 (Exempt countries, territories and parts of countries or territories), omit “Denmark”, “Germany” and “Sweden”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 6th November 2020

Dr Michael McBride — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 (“the principal Regulations”).

Denmark, Germany and Sweden are omitted from the list of countries and territories in Schedule 3 to the principal Regulations exempt from the requirement in regulation 4 of those Regulations to self-isolate for 14 days after arrival in Northern Ireland.

The requirement to self-isolate in respect of travellers from Denmark comes into operation at the time and on the date that the Regulations are made. The requirement to self-isolate in respect of travellers from Germany and Sweden comes into operation at 4.00 am on 7th November 2020.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
