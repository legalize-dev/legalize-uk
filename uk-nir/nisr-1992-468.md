# The Criminal Justice (1991 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992

Made: 22nd October 1992

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(3) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 I hereby make the following order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (1991 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1992.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The following provisions of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 shall come into operation on 1st November 1992—

- (a) Article 6; and

- (b) Article 8 and the Schedule so far as they relate to the Criminal Jurisdiction Act 1975 and the Criminal Damage (Northern Ireland) Order 1977 .

## Signed

P. B. B. Mayhew — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 22nd October 1992
