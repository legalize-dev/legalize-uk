# The Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 4th February 2015

Coming into operation: 1st March 2015

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 50 and 51 of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 and now vested in it .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 1st March 2015.

#### Amendment of the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013 is revoked.

## SCHEDULE

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 4th of February 2015.
