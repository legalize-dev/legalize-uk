# The Regulation (EC) No 1370/2007 (Public Service Obligations in Transport) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (Northern Ireland) (Revocation) Regulations 2020

Made

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 of, and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 and paragraph 21 of Schedule 7 to, the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018[^f00002], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) (Public Service Obligations in Transport) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (Northern Ireland) (Revocation) Regulations 2020 and come into operation immediately before IP completion day.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954(**c**) applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Revocation of Regulations

###### 2

The Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) (Public Service Obligations in Transport) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2020(**d**) are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 17th December 2020

Bernie Rooney — A senior officer of the — Department for Infrastructure

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke the Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) (Public Service Obligations in Transport) (Amendment) (EU Exit) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2020 [S.R. 2020/252](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2020/252) as Regulation [(EC) No 1370/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1370) is not retained EU Law for Northern Ireland.
