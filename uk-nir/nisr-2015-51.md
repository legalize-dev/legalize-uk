# The Attorney General’s Human Rights Guidance (Probation Board for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 12th February 2015

Coming into operation: 23rd March 2015

The Attorney General, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(3)(c) of that Act, makes the following order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Attorney General’s Human Rights Guidance (Probation Board for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 23rd March 2015.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly[^f00003].

#### Coming into operation of the guidance

###### 2

The guidance on international human rights standards in respect of the Probation Board for Northern Ireland shall come into operation on the day on which this Order comes into operation.

## Signed

John F. Larkin QC — Attorney General for Northern Ireland — 12th February 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation guidance by the Attorney General for Northern Ireland, pursuant to section 8 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004, which was laid before the Assembly on 15th December 2014.
