# County Court Divisions Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 14th May 2013

Coming into operation: 19th July 2013

The Department of Justice makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(1) of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980[^f00001] after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the County Court Divisions Order (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 19th July 2013.

###### 2

In this Order—

- “petty sessions districts” means the petty sessions districts specified by the Petty Sessions Districts Order (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00002].

#### Number and names of divisions

###### 3

For the purposes of the County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980, Northern Ireland shall be divided into seven divisions the names of which shall be listed in column 1 of the Schedule.

#### Composition and boundaries of divisions

###### 4

Each division listed in column 1 of the Schedule shall comprise the petty sessions districts specified in relation to that division in column 2 of the Schedule and the boundary of each division shall be such as to include the petty sessions districts so specified.

#### Revocation

###### 5

The County Court Divisions Order (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003] is revoked.

#### Transitional provision

###### 6

Proceedings commenced on or after 10th June 2013 and before 19th July 2013 in the county court division of Belfast may continue to be dealt with in the county court divisions of Antrim, Belfast, Craigavon, Fermanagh and Tyrone or Londonderry.

## SCHEDULE

| *(1)* *Name of division* | *(2)* *Petty sessions districts comprised in division* |
| --- | --- |
| Antrim | Antrim Ballymena Larne North Antrim |
| Ards | Ards Castlereagh Down North Down |
| Armagh and South Down | Armagh Banbridge Newry and Mourne |
| Belfast | Belfast and Newtownabbey |
| Craigavon | Craigavon Lisburn |
| Fermanagh and Tyrone | East Tyrone Fermanagh Omagh Strabane |
| Londonderry | Limavady Londonderry Magherafelt |

## Signed

Sealed with the official seal of the Department of Justice on 14 May 2013.

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order specifies the county court divisions into which Northern Ireland is divided and lists the petty session districts comprised in each division; and revokes the County Court Divisions Order (Northern Ireland) 1994.
