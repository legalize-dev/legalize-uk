# The Sea Fish Industry (Coronavirus) (Fixed Costs) (Amendment) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 4th June 2020

Coming into operation: 8th June 2020

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001] with the approval of the Department of Finance[^f00002], makes the following Scheme in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(1) and (2) of the Fisheries Act 1981[^f00003], as read with paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 2 of the Sea Fisheries (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00004], and now vested in it[^f00005].

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as the Sea Fish Industry (Coronavirus) (Fixed Costs) (Amendment) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2020 and comes into operation on 8 June 2020.

#### Amendment of the Sea Fish Industry (Coronavirus) (Fixed Costs) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Sea Fish Industry (Coronavirus) (Fixed Costs) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00006] is amended as follows.

- (2) In Article 2(2), in the definition of “length overall” for “[Council Regulation (EEC) No 2930/86](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1986/2930)”[^f00007] substitute “Regulation (EU) 2017/1130”[^f00008].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 4th June 2020.

Claire Vincent — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

The Department of Finance approves this Scheme.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance on 4th June 2020.

Emer Morelli — A senior officer of the — Department of Finance

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme amends the definition of “length overall” in article 2 of the Sea Fish Industry (Coronavirus) (Fixed Costs) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2020 to refer to the most recent EU legislation (EU) 2017/1130 that provides for the characteristics of fishing vessels.
