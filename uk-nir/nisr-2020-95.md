## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Scheme)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme amends the definition of “length overall” in article 2 of the Sea Fish Industry (Coronavirus) (Fixed Costs) Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2020 to refer to the most recent EU legislation (EU) 2017/1130 that provides for the characteristics of fishing vessels.
