# The Honey (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 13th March 2008

Coming into operation: 14th April 2008

As required by Article9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Honey (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 14th April 2008.

#### Amendment of the Honey Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Honey Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In Schedule 2 (specifications for specified honey products), opposite the words “honeydew and chestnut honey and blends of these except with those listed below”, for the words “not more than 0.8 mS/cm” there is substituted “not less than 0.8 mS/cm”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 13th March 2008.

Andrew McCormick — A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make a minor amendment to the Honey (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No.383](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/383)). They implement the Corrigendum to Council Directive [2001/110/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0110) relating to honey (OJ L52, 21.2.2007) which corrected an error in Annex II to Council Directive [2001/110/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0110) (OJ L10, 12.1.2002).
