# The Attorney General’s Human Rights Guidance (Police Service of Northern Ireland – Protection of Life) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 12th October 2015

Coming into operation: 1st December 2015

The Attorney General, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8(3)(c) of that Act, makes the following order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Attorney General’s Human Rights Guidance (Police Service of Northern Ireland – Protection of Life) Order (Northern Ireland) 2015 and shall come into operation on 1st December 2015.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 shall apply to this Order as it applies to an Act of the Assembly[^f00004].

#### Coming into operation of the guidance

###### 2

The guidance on international human rights standards relevant to the protection of the right to life in respect of the Police Service of Northern Ireland shall come into operation on the day on which this Order comes into operation.

## Signed

John F. Larkin QC — Attorney General for Northern Ireland — 12th October 2015

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation guidance by the Attorney General for Northern Ireland, pursuant to section 8 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2004, which was laid before the Assembly on 18th September 2015.
