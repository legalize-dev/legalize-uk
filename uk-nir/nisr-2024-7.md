# The Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) (Amendment) Order 2024

Made: 17th January 2024

Coming into operation: 8th February 2024

In accordance with section 8A(5) of the Act the Department of Finance have consulted, where they think it appropriate, with the Treasury.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) (Amendment) Order 2024 and shall come into operation on 8th February 2024.

#### Amendments to the principal Order

###### 2

- (1) The principal Order is amended as follows:

- (2) For the Schedule (designated bodies) to the principal Order, substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order, and

- (3) In this Order, “*the principal Order*” means the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2023”[^f00003].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2023 ([S.I. 2023 No. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/53)) (“*the principal Order*”), which designates specified central government bodies in relation to named government departments for the purpose of those departments’ supply estimates and resource accounts.

A new Schedule is substituted for the Schedule to the principal Order. The effect of this amendment is that one body has been removed.

The designations have effect for the financial year that ends on 31 March 2024.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
