# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) (Amendment) (No.7) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: at 11.30 a.m. on 28th October 2021

Laid before the Assembly: at 12.30 p.m. on 28th October 2021

Coming into operation: at noon on 31st October 2021

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) (Amendment) (No.7) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation at noon on 31st October 2021.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended as follows—

###### 3

At the end of regulation 4A(2)(j) omit “or”.

###### 4

In Regulation 4A(2)(k) omit “seated” and for the full stop after “(including intoxicating liquor)” substitute “; or”.

###### 5

After Regulation 4A(2)(k) insert—

> > (l) by a person dancing in a relevant place.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 11.30 am on 28th October 2021

Sir Michael McBride — Chief Medical Officer

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Wearing of Face Coverings) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 by removing the requirement for anyone to wear a face covering when eating or drinking in an indoor setting and when dancing.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
