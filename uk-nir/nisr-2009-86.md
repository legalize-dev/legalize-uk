# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 9th March 2009

Coming into operation: 6th April 2009

The Department makes these Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 6th April 2009.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00004] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) for paragraph (5) substitute—

> > (5) Unless specifically defined in these Regulations, any expression used in these Regulations has the meaning it bears in—
>   (a) Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425) concerning veterinary and zootechnical checks applicable in intra-Community trade in certain live animals and products with a view to the completion of the internal market (O.J. No. L224, 18.8.90, p. 29 as last amended by Directive [2002/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0033) of the European Parliament and of the Council, O.J. No. L315, 19.11.2002, p. 14); and
>   (b) Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) laying down the principles governing the organisation of veterinary checks on animals entering the Community from third countries (O.J. No. L268, 24.9.1991, p. 56 as last amended by Council Directive [2006/104/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0104), O.J. No. L363, 20.12.2006, p. 352).

- (b) after paragraph (5) insert—

> > (5A) Any reference to an instrument of the European Community in these Regulations is a reference to that instrument as amended from time to time.

- (3) In paragraph (5) of regulation 7 (transport of animals and animal products) for “under Article 12 of the Welfare of Animals (Transport) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 in accordance with Schedule 9 to that Order”, substitute—

> > in accordance with Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/0001), as applied by the Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00005] or other sanctions or controls pursuant to Article 26 of that Council Regulation

- (4) In paragraph (5)(a) of regulation 15 (importation) omit—

> > and any additional requirements specified in that part

- (5) Schedule 1 is revoked.

- (6) For Part I of Schedule 2 and Schedule 6, substitute the replacements as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The following Regulations are revoked to the extent specified—

- (a) Regulations 11 and 12 of The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Circuses and Avian Quarantine) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00006];

- (b) Regulations 6 and 7 of The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00007].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations replace Part I of Schedule 2 and Schedule 6 to the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 401](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/401) as amended by [S.R. 2007 No. 224](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/224), [S.R. 2007 No. 327](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/327) and [S.R. 2008 No. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/53)). The new Schedules incorporate a number of new and amended pieces of Community legislation that have come into force since those Regulations came into operation.

The Regulations provide that the Community instruments referred to in those Regulations are now references as amended from time to time.

An Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this rule as no impact on business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.
