# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (2021 Consolidation) (Amendment No. 13) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: 15th December 2021

Coming into operation: at 11.00 a.m. on 16th December 2021

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25B and 25F of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (2021 Consolidation) (Amendment No. 13) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at 11.00 a.m. on 16th December 2021.

- (3) In these Regulations “*the principal Regulations*” means the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 2

The principal Regulations are amended as mentioned in regulation 3.

#### Amendment of Schedule 7 (Managed Isolation)

###### 3

In Schedule 7—

- (a) in paragraph 17(1)(b), after “are to be read as references to a” omit “managed isolation”;

- (b) in paragraph 18(a)—

- (i) in head (x), for “; and” substitute ”,”;

- (ii) after head (x) insert—

> > (xi) a person whom the Department considers requires exceptional arrangements to be made on compassionate grounds; and

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 15th December 2021

Dr Naresh Chada — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 (“*the principal Regulations*”).

These Regulations permit those red list passengers who were isolating in accordance with Schedule 7 immediately before 4.00 a.m. on 15th December 2021 to complete their period of self-isolation at a place other than the place specified in their managed isolation package.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
