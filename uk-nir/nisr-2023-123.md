# The Taxi Drivers’ Licences (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made: 5th September 2023

Coming into operation: 1st October 2023

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 23(5), 30(1) and 56(1) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Taxi Drivers’ Licences (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2023.

#### Amendment of the Taxi Drivers’ Licences Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 13 of the Taxi Drivers’ Licences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00004] (fee for a practical test) —

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a) for “£60.00” substitute “£70.00”; and

- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “£90.00” substitute “£109.00”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Infrastructure on 5th September 2023

Chris Hughes — A senior officer of the — Department for Infrastructure

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Taxi Drivers’ Licences Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 in order to increase the fee for a practical test of competence to drive a taxi from £60.00 to £70.00 where a test commences at any time between 8.30am and 4.30pm during the days Monday to Friday and from £90.00 to £109.00 where a test commences at any other time.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Safe and Accessible Travel Division, Department for Infrastructure, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online alongside this Regulation at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
