# The Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 23rd October 2008

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 60(1) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (2007 Act) (Commencement No. 7) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 13(7) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (work-related activity) is 27th October 2008.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 23rd October 2008

John O’Neill — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Article 2 of this Order provides for the coming into operation of section 13(7) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (“the Act”) on 27th October 2008.

Section 13(7) defines “work-related activity” for the purposes of Part 1 of the Act in relation to employment and support allowance.
