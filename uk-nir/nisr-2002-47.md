# Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 13th February 2002

Coming into operation: 1st April 2002

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 61(6), 62 and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicle Testing (Amendment) (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2002.

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicle Testing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Motor Vehicle Testing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 by increasing the fees payable for examinations and re-examinations as follows—

- (1) Examinations under regulations 7(2), 10(3) and 15(1)—

- (a) for vehicles in Classes I or II, from £12·00 to £17·50;

- (b) for vehicles in Classes III or IV, from £18·50 to £25·00;

- (c) for vehicles in Class V, from £24·00 to £35·50;

- (d) for vehicles in Class VA—

- (i) with 13 to 16 seats, exclusive of the driver, from £34·00 to £47·00;

- (ii) with 17 to 35 seats, exclusive of the driver, from £43·00 to £58·00;

- (iii) with more than 35 seats, exclusive of the driver, from £52.50 to £72·50;

- (e) for vehicles in Class VI, from £24·00 to £35·50; and

- (f) for vehicles in Class VIA, from £34·00 to £47·00.

- (2) Re-examination made within 21 days of the issue of the notice of refusal under regulation 10(4)—

- (a) for vehicles in Class I or II, from £8·50 to £12·00;

- (b) for vehicles in Class III or IV, from £10·50 to £16·00; and

- (c) for vehicles of all other Classes, from £13·50 to £18·50.

- (3) Re-examination under regulation 10(5)—

- (a) for vehicles in Class VA—

- (i) with 13 to 16 seats, exclusive of the driver, from £13·50 to £18·50;

- (ii) with 17 to 35 seats, exclusive of the driver, from £23·00 to £30·00;

- (iii) with more than 35 seats, exclusive of the driver, from £32·50 to £41·00;

- (b) for vehicles in Class VIA, from £13·50 to £18·50.
