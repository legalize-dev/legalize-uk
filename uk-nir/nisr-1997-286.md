# The Protection from Harassment (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 16th June 1997

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Protection from Harassment (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Protection from Harassment (1997 Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 2, 3, 4, 5(1) and (2), 6, 7, 8 and 9 of the Protection from Harassment (Northern Ireland) Order 1997 shall be 17th June 1997.

## Signed

Marjorie Mowlam — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 16th June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order appoints 17th June 1997 for the coming into operation of Articles 2, 3, 4, 5(1) and (2), 6, 7, 8 and 9 of the Protection from Harassment (Northern Ireland) Order 1997.
