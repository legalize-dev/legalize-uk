# Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made: 3rd September 2001

Coming into operation: 1st October 2001

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2) and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 1st October 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00003].

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 3

In Schedule 2 Part I.1 to the principal Regulations after the entry relating to Council Directive [99/579/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0579) there shall be added the following entry—

> > - Directive [2000/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0020) of the European Parliament and of the Council (O.J. No. L163, 4.7.00, p. 35);

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 3rd September 2001.

R. S. Johnston — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 ([S.R. 2000/253](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2000/253)) (the principal Regulations).

A reference to Council Directive [2000/20/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0020) of the European Parliament and Council amending Council Directive [64/432/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1964/0432) on animal health problems affecting intra-Community trade in bovine animals and swine has been inserted to Schedule 2 Part I.1.
