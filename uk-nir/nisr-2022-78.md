# The Licensing (Sample) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made: 2nd March 2022

Coming into operation: 7th April 2022

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 52B(3) of the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing (Sample) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and shall come into operation on 7th April 2022.

###### 2

In these Regulations “*the 1996 Order*” means the Licensing (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.

#### Application

###### 3

These Regulations apply to a local producer’s licence in accordance with Articles 52B and 52D of the 1996 Order.

#### Sample

###### 4

A sample of intoxicating liquor, is an amount (whether in one serving or more) which does not exceed the amount set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

| Intoxicating Liquor | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| (*) ABV = Alcohol by volume | (*) ABV = Alcohol by volume |
| Beer – up to and including 6% ABV[^f01000] | 852 mls |
| Beer – above 6% ABV | 568 mls |
| Cider – up to and including 6% ABV | 852 mls |
| Cider – above 6% ABV | 568 mls |
| Spirits | 105 mls |
| Liqueur | 210 mls |
|  |  |
|  |  |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 2nd March 2022.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe the amount of intoxicating liquor which may be provided as a sample for consumption on a tour given of production premises under Article 52B of the Licensing (NI) Order 1996 (“*the 1996 Order*”) or at an event not on licensed premises under Article 52D of the 1996 Order. The amount (whether in one serving or more) must not exceed:

- (a) 852 mls beer or cider up to and including 6% ABV;

- (b) 568 mls beer or cider above 6% ABV;

- (c) 105 mls of any spirit;

- (d) 210 mls of any liqueur.
