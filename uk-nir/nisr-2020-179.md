# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel and Public Health Advice for Persons Travelling to Northern Ireland) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 14th August 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with Regulation 1

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25B and 25F(2) of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel and Public Health Advice for Persons Travelling to Northern Ireland) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) Regulation 2(4) shall come into operation at 4.00am on 15th August 2020.

- (3) The remainder of the Regulations shall come into operation at 4.00am on 18th August 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 2 (Interpretation), for paragraph (4) substitute—

> > (4) For the purposes of these Regulations, a person (“P”) is not treated as arriving in, departing from or transiting through a country or territory if, at all times whilst in that country or territory—
>   (a) P remains on a conveyance and no other passenger is permitted to be taken on board, or
>   (b) P is kept separated from passengers who did not arrive on the same conveyance.

- (3) In regulation 4 (Requirement to self-isolate) —

- (a) for sub-paragraph (j) of paragraph (9) substitute—

> > (j) to take exercise alone or with members of P’s household, or

- (4) In regulation 11 (Review of need for restrictions), for the words “every 21 days” substitute “every 28 days” and for the words “by 29 June 2020” substitute “by 7 September 2020”.

- (5) For Schedule 3 (Exempt countries and Territories), substitute Schedule 3 as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (6) In Schedule 4 (Specified competitions)—

- (a) insert the following entries at the appropriate place in alphabetical order—

- “Dubai Duty Free Irish Open Golf Tournament (European Tour)”;

- “The Northern Ireland Golf Open Tournament (European Challenge Tour supported by the R&A”; and

- (b) Omit “European Tour Golf - The Irish Challenge”.

- (7) In relation to any person who arrived in Northern Ireland on or after 10th July 2020 and before the coming into operation of any amendment made by these Regulations the principal Regulations apply as if that amendment had not been made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Public Health Advice for Persons Travelling to Northern Ireland) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 3

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Public Health Advice for Persons Travelling to Northern Ireland) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 [^f00004] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 4 (Review), for the words “every 21 days” substitute “every 28 days” and for the words “by 29 June 2020” substitute “by 7 September 2020”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 (“the International Travel Regulations”) and the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Public Health Advice for Persons Travelling to Northern Ireland) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 (“the Public Health Advice Regulations”).

Regulation 2 is amended to ensure those travelling to a part of the Republic of Ireland from another part of the Republic of Ireland who pass through Northern Ireland on route are not required to complete a passenger locator form provided they remain in their vehicle at all times.

Regulation 3 is amended to allow those self-isolating to leave their place of self-isolation to exercise alone or with other members of their household.

Aruba, France, Monaco, Malta, the Netherlands, and the Turks and Caicos Islands are removed from the list of exempt countries in Schedule 3.

Two golf tournaments planned for September in Northern Ireland have been added to the list of specified competitions in Schedule 4.

The review period of both regulations is amended to 28 days.
