# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 18th March 2016

Coming into operation: 31st May 2016

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 31st May 2016.

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes the amount of fixed penalties for certain fixed penalty offences by substituting Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 (“the Principal Order”).

It amends the Principal Order to provide for new fixed penalty offences which have been specified as such by Article 2 of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016. The new fixed penalties relate to offences committed under the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
