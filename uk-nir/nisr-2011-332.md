# The Dogs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 28th September 2011

Coming into operation: 3rd October 2011

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Section 18 of the Dogs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Dogs (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Dogs (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act listed in the Schedule is 3rd October 2011.

## SCHEDULE

| Section 8 | Control conditions on dog licences |

C. McMaster — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development
