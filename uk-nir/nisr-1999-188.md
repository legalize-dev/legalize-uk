# Pre-School Education in Schools (Admissions Criteria) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 20th April 1999

Coming into operation: 20th April 1999

The Department of Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 32(6) and 90(3) of the Education (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Pre-School Education in Schools (Admissions Criteria) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 20th April 1999.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In the Pre-School Education in Schools (Admissions Criteria) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00002], regulation 5 (Matters not to be included in the criteria) is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Education for Northern Ireland on

Dr. M. Browne — Assistant Secretary — 20th April 1999.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Pre-School Education in Schools (Admissions Criteria) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998 by removing regulation 5 which prohibits the selection of children for admission to pre-school education in schools by reference to ability or aptitude or the performance of any child in a test or examination held by, or on behalf of, the Board of Governors.
