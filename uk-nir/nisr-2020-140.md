# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 10th July 2020

Coming into operation: 12th July 2020

The Department of Health[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25B and 25F(2) of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation on 12th July 2020.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

- (2) In relation to any person who arrived in Northern Ireland on or after 10th July 2020 and before the coming into operation of any amendment made by these Regulations the principal Regulations apply as if that amendment had not been made.

#### Amendment to Schedule 3

###### 3

In Schedule 3[^f00004], in Part 1 omit “Serbia”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 10th July 2020

Dr Michael McBride — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 (“The International Travel Regulations”) to omit Serbia from a list of exempt countries and territories from the requirement in regulation 4 of the International Travel Regulations to self-isolate for 14 days after their arrival in Northern Ireland.

No impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
