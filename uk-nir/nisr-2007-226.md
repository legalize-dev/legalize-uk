# The Community Benefit Societies (Restriction on Use of Assets) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 6th April 2007

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 7(3) of the Schedule to the Northern Ireland Act 2000

Coming into operation: 4th June 2007

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 9 of the Industrial and Provident Societies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Benefit Societies (Restriction on Use of Assets) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and come into operation on 4th June 2007.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment to the Community Benefit Societies (Restriction on Use of Assets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 3

- (1) The Community Benefit Society (Restriction on Use of Assets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (5).

- (2) In Regulation 2 (2), before the definition of “prescribed community benefit society” insert—

> > “community interest company” has the meaning given by section 26 of the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004[^f00004];

- (3) In Regulation 6 (Prescribed circumstances), after paragraph (b)(i) insert—

> > (aa) a community interest company;

- (4) In Schedule 1 (Restriction on Use), in the rule to be included in a prescribed community benefit society’s rules, after paragraph (f)(i) insert—

> > (aa) a community interest company;

- (5) In Schedule 2 (Application of the 1969 Act with Modifications), in paragraph 6, in the subsection substituted for section 61(1) of the 1969 Act, before subsection (1)(a) insert—

> > (az) a community interest company (within the meaning of section 26 of the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004);

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 6th April 2007

M Bohill — A senior officer of the of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which are made under Article 9 of the Industrial and Provident Societies (Northern Ireland) Order [2006 (N.I. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2006/314), amend the Community Benefit Societies (Restriction on Use of Assets) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No. 258](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/258)) to permit community benefit societies to transfer their assets to community interest companies under prescribed circumstances.

The Regulations also make a consequential modification to the application of the [Industrial and Provident Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969 (C. 24)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1969/24).
