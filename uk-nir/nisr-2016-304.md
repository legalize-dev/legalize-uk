# The Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 5th July 2016

Coming into operation in accordance with Rule 1

The Magistrates’ Courts Rules Committee makes the following rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

These rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Amendment No. 2) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on the same day as section 1 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00002].

###### 2

In these Rules—

- (a) “the principal Rules” means the Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984[^f00003]; and

- (b) a reference to a rule, paragraph, Schedule or Form is a reference to that rule, paragraph, Schedule or Form so numbered in the type=start slip=2016-09-01 time=1473338774723principal Rulestype=end slip=2016-09-01 time=1473338774723.

#### Amendment to the principal Rules

###### 3

> > - “administrative court division” means such division specified under section 2 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015 for any purposes of a county court;

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These rules amend the Magistrates’ Courts Rules (Northern Ireland) 1984 ([S.R. 1984 No. 225](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1984/225)) to take account of Part 1 of the Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 2015 which creates a single jurisdiction for county courts and magistrates’ courts whereby the jurisdiction and powers of those courts are exercisable throughout the whole of Northern Ireland.
