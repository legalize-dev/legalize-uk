# The Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2016

Made: 25th May 2016

Those powers are exercisable by the Secretary of State by virtue of Article 4(1)(a) of that Order.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2016.

#### Provisions coming into force on 1st June 2016

###### 2

- (1) The following provisions of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 come into force on 1st June 2016.

- (2) In Part 3 (working-age benefits)—

- (a) in Chapter 1 (jobseeker’s allowance)—

- (i) Articles 51 and 52, for the purpose of making regulations;

- (ii) in Schedule 7, paragraph 3, for the purpose of making regulations;

- (b) in Chapter 2 (employment and support allowance), Article 62.

- (3) In Part 4 (other benefit changes)—

- (a) Article 70(3) and (4);

- (b) Article 72(1) and (2), for the purpose of making regulations.

#### Provisions coming into force on 31st October 2016

###### 3

- (1) The following provisions of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 come into force on 31st October 2016.

- (2) In Part 4 (other benefit changes)—

- (a) Article 70(1) and (2);

- (b) Article 71;

- (c) Article 72(1) and (2), for remaining purposes;

- (d) Articles 73 and 74.

- (3) In Part 8 (repeals), Part 6 of Schedule 12.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions

Priti Patel — Minister of State, — Department for Work and Pensions — 25th May 2016

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force provisions of the Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015 relating to, in particular:

- jobseeker’s allowance;

- employment and support allowance;

- industrial injuries benefit.
