# The Contaminants in Food (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 12th December 2013

Coming into operation: 17th January 2014

In accordance with Article 47(3A) of that Order, it has had regard to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Contaminants in Food (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and come into operation on 17th January 2014.

#### Amendment of the Contaminants in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

###### 2

- (1) The Contaminants in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) For regulation 2(3) substitute—

> > (3) Subject to regulation 2(4), any reference to an EU instrument is a reference to it as amended at the date of making these Regulations.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 12th December 2013.

Linda Devlin — A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations make a minor amendment to regulation 2(3) of the Contaminants in Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 ([S.R. 2013 No. 229](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2013/229)) in order to correct an error therein.
