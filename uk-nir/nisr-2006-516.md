# The Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made

Coming into operation: 1st April 2007

The Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 42(1G) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f00002]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Rates (Automatic Telling Machines) (Designation of Rural Areas) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2007.

#### Designation of rural areas

###### 2

Wards as set out in the Schedule are designated as rural areas for the purposes of Article 42(1F) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order sets out local government wards designated as rural areas for the purposes of exempting automatic telling machines from rates under Article 42(1F) of the Rates (Northern Ireland) Order 1977.
