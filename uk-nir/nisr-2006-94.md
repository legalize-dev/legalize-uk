# The Companies (1986 Order) (Operating and Financial Review) (Repeal) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 8th March 2006

Coming into operation: 31st March 2006

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by Article 265 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (1986 Order) (Operating and Financial Review) (Repeal) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 31 March 2006.

- (3) In these Regulations “the 1986 Order” means the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986.

#### Repeal of requirement to prepare operating and financial review

###### 2

- (1) Article 242AA of the 1986 Order (duty of directors of quoted company to prepare operating and financial review)[^f00003] is repealed.

- (2) In consequence of that repeal the 1986 Order is amended in accordance with the Schedule to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations remove the requirement in Article 242AA of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 for directors of quoted companies (as defined in Article 270(1) of that Order) to prepare operating and financial reviews for financial years beginning on or after 1st April 2005 (regulation 2(1)). Regulation 2(2) and Schedule amend the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 consequentially on that repeal.

A separate regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business in Northern Ireland has not been carried out. The regulatory impact assessment carried out in GB is available electronically at www.dti.gov.uk/cld.
