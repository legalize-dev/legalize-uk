# The Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 11th March 2011

Coming into operation: 24th March 2011

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 55(1), (2), (4) and (6) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 24th March 2011.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In regulation 17A(1) and (2) of the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00002] as set out in regulation 4 of the Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00003] for “1st June 2010” substitute “1st June 2011”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 11th March 2011

Deirdre Kenny — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations correct an error in regulation 4 of the Road Vehicles Lighting (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011. In new regulation 17A of the Road Vehicles Lighting Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 as set out in the said regulation 4 the date 1st June 2010 is amended to 1st June 2011.
