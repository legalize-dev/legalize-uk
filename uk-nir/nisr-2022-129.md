# The Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2022

Made: 11th March 2022

Coming into operation: 18th April 2022

In accordance with sections 25(8) and 26(6) of that Act the Department has consulted the Northern Ireland Policing Board and the Police Association for Northern Ireland before making these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Police Service of Northern Ireland (Amendment) Regulations 2022 and shall come into operation on 18th April 2022.

#### Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 3(1) in paragraph (a) of the definition of “public holiday” for 2022 only, for the words “and last Monday in May” substitute “, 2nd and 3rd June”.

#### Amendment of the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006

###### 3

- (1) The Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006 shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In Schedule 1 in the definition of “public holiday”,type=end slip=2022-03-01 time=1648560644317 for 2022 only, for the words “and last Monday in May” substitute “, 2nd and 3rd June”.

## Signed

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Police Service of Northern Ireland Regulations 2005 and the Police Service of Northern Ireland and Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve (Injury Benefit) Regulations 2006 to allow, for 2022 only, the public holiday on the last Monday in May to be replaced by public holidays on 2nd and 3rd June to mark Her Majesty the Queen’s Platinum Jubilee.
