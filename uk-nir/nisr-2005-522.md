# The Education (Recognised Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 23rd November 2005

Coming into operation: 22nd December 2005

The Department for Employment and Learning [^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 5(1) of the Education (Unrecognised Degrees) (Northern Ireland) Order 1988 [^f00002] and now vested in it [^f00003] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Education (Recognised Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 22nd December 2005.

#### Recognised Bodies

###### 2

For the purposes of Article 5(1) of the Education (Unrecognised Degrees) (Northern Ireland) Order 1988, the Department for Employment and Learning hereby designates the bodies listed in the Schedule as those bodies which appear to it to be recognised bodies.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The Education (Recognised Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989[^f00004] and the Education (Recognised Bodies) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991[^f00005] are hereby revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order repeals and replaces the Education (Recognised Bodies) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989, as amended. The Order lists all those bodies which appear to the Department for Employment and Learning to be recognised within Article 3(2)(a) or (b) of the Education (Unrecognised Degrees) (Northern Ireland) Order 1988. Article 3(1) of that Order makes it an offence to grant, offer to grant, or issue any invitation relating to certain unrecognised degrees and awards. That Article does not apply to any award made by a recognised body.
