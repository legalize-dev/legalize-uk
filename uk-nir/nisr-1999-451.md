# Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 15th November 1999

Coming into operation: 1st January 2000

Now, therefore, the Department in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 50(1) and (2) of the 1998 Order and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2000.

#### Amendment of the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989[^f00003] there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Economic Development on 15th November 1999.

R. Gamble — Assistant Secretary

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order further amends the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 1989 (“the 1989 Order”) which specifies a number of persons or bodies as public authorities for certain purposes under Part VII of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 and provides for the persons who are to be treated for such purposes as the employees of some of those authorities.

The Schedule to the 1989 Order lists in Part I the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 52 to 65 of the 1998 Order and in Part II the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 62 to 65 of the 1998 Order.

Article 2 of this Order substitutes a new Schedule to the 1989 Order.

The following bodies are added:

| Part I | — Equality Commission for Northern Ireland. |
| --- | --- |
| Part I | — Northern Ireland Human Rights Commission. |
| Part II | — Health and Safety Executive for Northern Ireland. |

The following bodies are deleted:

| Part I | — Commission for Racial Equality for Northern Ireland. |
| --- | --- |
| Part I | — Equal Opportunities Commission for Northern Ireland. |
| Part I | — Fair Employment Commission for Northern Ireland. |
| Part II | — Health and Safety Agency for Northern Ireland. |

The following amendments have been made to the Schedule:

| Part I | — The governing body of the East Tyrone College of Further Education is now the governing body of the East Tyrone College of Further and Higher Education. |
| --- | --- |
| Part I | — The governing body of the Fermanagh College of Further Education is now the governing body of the Fermanagh College of Further and Higher Education. |
| Part I | — The governing body of the Limavady College of Further Education is now the governing body of the Limavady College of Further and Higher Education |
| Part I | — Ulster Sheltered Employment Limited is now Ulster Supported Employment Limited. |
