# The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 4th February 2008

Coming into operation: 1st April 2008

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 35 of the Street Works (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2008.

#### Amendment

###### 2

In regulation 3(1) of The Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004] (fees, chargeable inspections, units of inspection, phases of works and estimated numbers of units of inspection) for “£24.00” there shall be substituted “£25.00”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 4th February 2008

J Carlisle — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend The Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 by increasing the fee prescribed for each chargeable inspection from £24.00 to £25.00.
