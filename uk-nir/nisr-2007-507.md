# The Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 20th December 2007

Coming into operation: 1st January 2008

The Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 50 and 51 of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^f00002] and now vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2008.

#### Amendment of the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00004] substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

#### Revocations

###### 3

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00005],

- (b) the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00006].

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 20th December 2007.

Gerard Mulligan — A senior officer of the — Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 (“the 2004 Order”) which specifies a number of persons or bodies as public authorities for certain purposes under Part VII of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 (“the 1998 Order”) and provides for the persons who are to be treated for such purposes as the employees of some of those authorities.

The Schedule to the 2004 Order lists in Part I the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 52 to 65 of the 1998 Order and in Part II the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 62 to 65 of the 1998 Order.

Article 2 of this Order substitutes a new Schedule to the 2004 Order.

The following bodies are added to the Schedule:

- **Part I** Belfast Health and Social Services Trust The governing body of Belfast Metropolitan College The governing body of North West Regional College The governing body of Northern Regional College The governing body of South Eastern Regional College The governing body of South West College The governing body of Southern Regional College Middletown Centre for Autism Limited Northern Health and Social Services Trust Northern Ireland Water Limited South Eastern Health and Social Services Trust Southern Health and Social Services Trust Western Health and Social Services Trust

- **Part II** The Secretary of State Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs
