# The Modern Slavery Act 2015 (Power of arrest: Code of Practice) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 24th February 2016

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

Accordingly, the Department of Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 23(6)(b), makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Modern Slavery Act 2015 (Power of arrest: Code of Practice) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2016.

#### Code of Practice

###### 2

The code of practice entitled “Code of Practice to be followed by Northern Ireland Constables and Enforcement Officers when arresting a person under the power conferred by paragraph 22(2) of Schedule 2 to the Modern Slavery Act 2015” comes into operation upon commencement of Part 3 of and Schedule 2 to the 2015 Act.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Justice on 24th February 2016.

David Ford — Minister of Justice

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation a Code of Practice to be followed by Northern Ireland constables and enforcement officers when arresting a person under the power conferred by paragraph 22(2) of Schedule 2 to the Modern Slavery Act 2015. The Code takes effect upon commencement of Part 3 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Modern Slavery Act 2015.
