# The Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 14th June 1999

The Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) and (4) of the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 2 and Consequential and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999.

- (2) In this Order “the Transfer Order” means the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

Subject to Article 4 of this Order, the day appointed for the coming into force of—

- (a) the provisions of the Transfer Order which are specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order, in so far as they authorise the making of regulations, is 15th June 1999;

- (b) the provisions of the Transfer Order which are specified in Part II of that Schedule, in so far as they are not already in force, is 5th July 1999.

#### Consequential amendments

###### 3

- (1) Subject to Article 4 of this Order, the Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] shall on 5th July 1999 be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (4) of this Article.

- (2) In regulation 1 (interpretation)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—

- (i) after the definition of “appeal” there shall be inserted the following definition—

> > - “the Board” means the Commissioners of Inland Revenue;

- (ii) in the definition of “claimant” in paragraph (b) for “the Department” there shall be substituted “an officer of the Board”, and

- (iii) in the definition of “official error” after “the Department” in each place where it occurs there shall be inserted “or the Board”; and

- (b) after paragraph (2) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> > (2A) In so far as these Regulations relate to a decision made under the Pension Schemes Act by virtue of section 165(2) of that Act[^f00003], any reference to the Department shall be construed as if it were a reference to an officer of the Board.

- (3) In regulation 3 (revision of decisions)—

- (a) in paragraph (4) for “the Department” there shall be substituted “an officer of the Board”; and

- (b) in paragraph (11) after sub-paragraph (b) there shall be added—

> > or
> (c) in the case of a contributions decision which falls within Part II of Schedule 3 to the Order, a National Insurance Contributions office of the Board or an office of the Department; or
> (d) in the case of a decision made under the Pension Schemes Act by virtue of section 165(2) of that Act, a National Insurance Contributions office of the Board.

- (4) In regulation 33(1) (making of appeals and applications) for sub-paragraph (b) there shall be substituted the following sub-paragraph—

> > (b) be sent or delivered to—
>   (i) in the case of a contributions decision which falls within Part II of Schedule 3 to the Order, a National Insurance Contributions office of the Board or an office of the Department,
>   (ii) in the case of a decision made under the Pension Schemes Act by virtue of section 165(2) of that Act, a National Insurance Contributions office of the Board,
>   (iii) in any other case, an office of the Department;

#### Transitional provision

###### 4

Notwithstanding paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 7 to the Transfer Order (which provides for certain determinations made by the Department to have effect as if made by the Board), Article 15(2) of the Transfer Order shall not apply with respect to any decision made before 5th July 1999 by the Department on any such question as is mentioned in section 165(1) of the Pensions Act (which includes such a question among the questions to which section 15(1) of the Administration Act applies).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into force further provisions of the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (“the Transfer Order”).

The provisions of the Transfer Order to which this Order relates are concerned with the determination of issues under the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act [1993 (c. 49)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/49) (“the Pensions Act”) and with the procedures for decision making and appeals on social security matters where other issues arise which fall to be determined by officers of the Commissioners of Inland Revenue (“the Board”). In accordance with Article 2 of this Order, the provisions of the Transfer Order specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order come into force on 15th June 1999 in so far as they authorise the making of regulations and the provisions specified in Part II of that Schedule come into force on 5th July 1999.

Article 3 of this Order makes consequential amendments to the Social Security and Child Support (Decisions and Appeals) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999, in particular so that references in those Regulations to the Department of Health and Social Services in the context of decisions under the Pensions Act are replaced by, or construed as, references to an officer of the Board, and so that documents relating to such decisions are sent to officers of the Board.

Article 4 of this Order makes transitional provision, in respect of decisions under section 165(1) of the Pensions Act, in connection with the coming into force of Article 15(2) of the Transfer Order.

Article 23 of the Transfer Order was brought into force on 25th March 1999 by an Order (The Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (1999 Order) (Commencement of Article 23) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999) which was not made by a statutory rule and copies of which can be obtained from the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland, Social Security Policy and Legislation Division, Room D3, Castle Buildings, Stormont, Belfast BT4 3SQ.
