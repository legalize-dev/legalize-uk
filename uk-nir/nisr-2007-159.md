# The Local Government (Boundaries 2006) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 9th March 2007

The Department of the Environment makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3)(b) of the Local Government (Boundaries) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Boundaries 2006) (Commencement No.1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

For the purposes of enabling the Secretary of State and the District Electoral Areas Commissioner to perform their functions under Article 2 of the District Electoral Areas Commissioner (Northern Ireland) Order 1984[^f00002], the appointed day for the coming into operation of Article 3 of the Local Government (Boundaries) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 is 12th March 2007.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 9th March 2007.

John Ritchie — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation Article 3 of the Local Government (Boundaries) (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 to enable the Secretary of State and the District Electoral Areas Commissioner to perform their functions under Article 2 of the District Electoral Areas Commissioner (Northern Ireland) Order 1984.
