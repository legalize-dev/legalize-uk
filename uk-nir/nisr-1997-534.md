# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release and Risk Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 11th December 1997

Coming into operation: 30th January 1998

The Department of the Environment, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], in relation to measures relating to the control and regulation of genetically modified organisms, in exercise of the powers conferred by that section and by Articles 5(7), 8(1), (4) and (7) of the Genetically Modified Organisms (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release and Risk Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 30th January 1998.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Genetically Modified Organisms (Risk Assessment) (Records and Exemptions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 3

In the Genetically Modified Organisms (Risk Assessment) (Records and Exemptions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00007] in paragraph (2) of regulation 4 (exemptions from requirement to carry out risk assessments) there shall be added at the end the following sub-paragraph—

> > ;
> (e) are or are contained in a novel food or novel food ingredient which is authorised for marketing in accordance with the provisions of Regulation [(EC) No. 258/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0258) of the European Parliament and of the Council.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations implement as respects Northern Ireland Commission Directive [97/35/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0035) (O.J. No. L169, 27.6.97, p. 72) adapting to technical progress for the second time Council Directive [90/220/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0220) on the deliberate release into the environment of genetically modified organisms, by amending the Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 ([S.R. 1994 No. 144](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/144)) (the 1994 Regulations).

Regulation 2(3) substitutes the Schedule for Schedule 2 to the 1994 Regulations.

In addition these Regulations amend the 1994 Regulations to take account of Regulation [(EC) No. 258/97](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1997/0258) (O.J. No. L43, 14.2.97, p. 1) concerning novel foods and novel food ingredients (the Novel Foods Regulation), with regard to the cases and circumstances in which a marketing consent is required under the 1994 Regulations.

These Regulations also amend the Genetically Modified Organisms (Risk Assessment) (Records and Exemptions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 442](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/442)) to take account of the Novel Foods Regulation with regard to exemptions from the requirement to carry out risk assessments.

Copies of the Directive and Regulation and extracts from the Official Journal of the European Communities may be obtained from The Stationery Office, 16 Arthur Street, Belfast BT1 4GD.
