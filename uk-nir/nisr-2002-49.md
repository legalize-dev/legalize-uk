# Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 14th February 2002

Coming into operation: 1st April 2002

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 61(1), 66(1) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2002.

#### Amendment of the Public Service Vehicles Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1985

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1985 by increasing the fee payable—

- (1) For an initial or re-application for a vehicle licence for—

- (a) Buses—

- Class I, II and III, from £46·50 to £60·50;

- Class IA, from £54·00 to £72·00;

- Class IIA, from £63·50 to £83·50; and

- Class IIIA, from £73·00 to £94·50;

- (b) Taxi, from £33·50 to £38·50.

- (2) For a re-application for a vehicle licence made within 21 days from the date of the service of the notice of refusal for—

- (a) Buses, all Classes, from £13·50 to £18·50; and

- (b) Taxi, from £12·00 to £16·50.

- (3) For a re-application under regulation 9(4) within 21 days from the date of the service of the notice of refusal for—

- Buses— Class IA, from £13·50 to £18·50; Class IIA, from £23·00 to £30·00; and Class IIIA from £32·50 to £42·00. The Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and the Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 are revoked.
