# Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 28th January 2002

Coming into operation: 4th March 2002

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### Amendment of the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

###### 2

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### Transitional arrangements for provisional approvals

###### 3

The Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 shall be modified so that—

- (a) the inclusion of the active substance flupyrsulfuron-methyl in Annex I shall not make it necessary to revoke any approval granted under regulation 7(1) (provisional approvals), or extended pursuant to regulation 7(5), in respect of any plant protection products containing that active substance, and any such approval shall continue to have effect until the date a decision made on an application for approval under regulation 5(1) (standard approvals) has effect, or 30th November 2002, whichever is the earlier; and

- (b) the inclusion of the active substance fenhexamid in Annex I shall not make it necessary to revoke any approvals issued pursuant to regulation 7(1), or extended under regulation 7(5), in respect of any plant protection products containing that active substance, and any such approvals which have expired shall be deemed to have revived until the date a decision made on an application for approval of the product under regulation 5(1) has effect, or 1st August 2002, whichever is the earlier.

#### Revocation

###### 4

The Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00021] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 28th January 2002.

Liam McKibben — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development
