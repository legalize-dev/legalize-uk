# The Strangford Lough (Sea Fishing Exclusion Zones) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 15th February 2011

Coming into operation: 14th March 2011

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of its powers conferred by sections 19(1), 124(1), 124(2) and 124(2A) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00002] ).

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Strangford Lough (Sea Fishing Exclusion Zones) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011 and shall come into operation on 14th March 2011.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations—

- “co-ordinate” means a co-ordinate of latitude and longitude on the World Geodetic System 1984;

- “Strangford Lough” means the sea area to the northwest of an imaginary straight line drawn from Killard Point to Ballyquintin Point, County Down;

- “Zones1 and 2” means those parts of Strangford Lough which lie within the boundaries specified in paragraph (2).

- (2) Those boundaries are—

- (a) in relation to Zone 1, a straight line joining the co-ordinates lettered A, B, C and D in the table in Part 1 of the Schedule; and

- (b) in relation to Zone 2, a straight line joining the co-ordinates lettered A, B, C and D in the table in Part 2 of the Schedule

#### Prohibition of fishing

###### 3

All fishing for sea-fish within Zones 1 and 2 by any sea-fishing boat is prohibited.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prohibit all fishing for sea fish by any sea-fishing boat within the two zones in Strangford Lough specified in the Schedule.

A chart showing the two zones is published for illustrative purposes on www.dardni.gov.uk/fisheries/sea-fisheries.

Contravention of these Regulations is an offence under section 124(3) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966. The penalty on summary conviction is a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale (£5,000)..
