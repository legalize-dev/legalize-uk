# The General Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 14th February 2007

Coming into operation: 12th March 2007

In accordance with Article 6(7) of that Order, the Department has consulted such district councils and such other interested bodies or persons as the Department considered appropriate.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the General Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 12th March 2007.

#### Specified bodies

###### 2

The following bodies are hereby specified for the purpose of Article 6(6) of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 namely—

- (a) Employers’ Organisation for Local Government;

- (b) Northern Ireland Joint Council for Local Government Services;

- (c) Joint Negotiating Committee for Clerks/Chief Executives of Local Authorities in Northern Ireland;

- (d) Local Government Training Group;

- (e) Local Authorities’ Co-ordinators of Regulatory Services; and

- (f) Local Government International Bureau.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The General Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 14th February 2007.

John Ritchie — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke and replace the General Grant (Specified Bodies) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001. The bodies specified for the purpose of Article 6(6) of the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 are amended to take account of:—

- (a) payments previously made to the National Joint Council for Local Government Services, which will now be made to the Employers’ Organisation for Local Government; and

- (b) the renaming of the Local Authorities’ Co-ordinating Body on Trading Standards (LACOTS), which is now known as the Local Authorities Co-ordinators of Regulatory Services.
