# Motor Vehicles (Exchangeable Licences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 24th October 2002

Coming into operation: 9th December 2002

The Department of the Environment, being no longer satisfied that satisfactory provision is made by the laws of Kenya as respects the grant of driving licences to drive motor vehicles, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 19D(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Exchangeable Licences) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 9th December 2002.

#### Revocation of the designation of Kenya

###### 2

Kenya shall cease to be designated for the purposes of the definition of “exchangeable licence” in Article 19D(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00002].

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Exchangeable Licences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1994

###### 3

In the Motor Vehicles (Exchangeable Licences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00003], in Article 2, the word “Kenya” shall be omitted.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 24th October 2002.

J. Ritchie — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

The Motor Vehicles (Exchangeable Licences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1994 ([S.R. 1994 No. 364](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1994/364)) designates various countries and territories as countries and territories who have satisfactory provision for the issue of driving licences. A person who holds such a licence may exchange their licence for a corresponding Northern Ireland licence. The effect of this Order is to remove Kenya from the list of countries and territories so designated. The holder of a Kenyan driving licence will therefore no longer be able to exchange that licence for a corresponding Northern Ireland one.
