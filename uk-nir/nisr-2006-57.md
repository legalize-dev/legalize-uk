# Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 17th February 2006

Coming into operation: 7th April 2006

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 1(1) of the Diseases of Fish Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 7th April 2006.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) (No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 17th February 2006.

N. B. Cornick — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Infected Waters (Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis) (No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 which contained provisions for the prevention, eradication and control of the fish disease Infectious Pancreatic Necrosis in the area as described in the Schedule to that Order.
