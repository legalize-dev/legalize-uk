# The Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 31st May 2007

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 1st July 2007

The Lord Chancellor makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 36(3) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002]. In accordance with Article 36(3) of that Order, he has consulted with the Lord Chief Justice and the Attorney General and has obtained the approval of the Treasury:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007, and shall come into operation on 1st July 2007.

#### Alteration of date

###### 2

In rules 6(3) and 9(5)(a) of the Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] for the date “30th June 2007” there shall be substituted the date “30th June 2008”.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Hanson — Minister of State — Ministry of Justice — Dated 31st May 2007

We approve

Frank Roy — Dave Watts — Two of the Lords Commissioners — of Her Majesty’s Treasury — Dated 6th June 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Legal Aid in Criminal Proceedings (Costs) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1992 so as to alter the date after which certain work may be remunerated at discretionary instead of prescribed rates from 30th June 2007 to 30th June 2008.
