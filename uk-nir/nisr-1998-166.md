# Litter (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998

Made: 30th April 1998

Coming into operation: 1st June 1998

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 6(7) of the Litter (Northern Ireland) Order 1994[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Litter (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 1998, and shall come into operation on 1st June 1998.

#### Amount of fixed penalty

###### 2

For the amount of £10 specified as the amount of the fixed penalty in Article 6(6) of the Litter (Northern Ireland) Order 1994 there shall be substituted the amount of £25.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on

R. W. Rogers — Assistant Secretary — 30th April 1998.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

Article 6 of the Litter (Northern Ireland) Order 1994 makes provision for the payment of a fixed penalty to discharge any liability to conviction for the offence of leaving litter under Article 3 or permitting dogs to foul under Article 4 of that Order.

This Order increases the amount of the fixed penalty from £10 to £25.
