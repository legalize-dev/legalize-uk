# Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 9th December 2003

Coming into operation: 1st January 2004

Now, therefore, the Department in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 50(1) and (2) and Article 51(2) of the 1998 Order and now vested in it[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2004.

#### Amendment of the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000

###### 2

The Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with Articles 3 to 5.

###### 3

In paragraph (2) of Article 1, for the definition of “police force” there shall be substituted –

> > - “police” has the same meaning as in the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000[^f00005];

###### 4

In paragraph (d) of Article 2 –

- (a) for “Chief Constable of the Royal Ulster Constabulary” there shall be substituted “Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland”; and

- (b) the word “force” shall be deleted.

###### 5

For the Schedule there shall be substituted the Schedule to this Order.

#### Revocation

###### 6

The following Orders are hereby revoked –

- (a) the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00006];

- (b) the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00007].

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister on 9th December 2003.

P. McAuley — A Senior Officer of the Office of the First Minister and deputy First Minister

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2000 (“the 2000 Order”) which specifies a number of persons or bodies as public authorities for certain purposes under Part VII of the Fair Employment and Treatment (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 (“the 1998 Order”) and provides for the persons who are to be treated for such purposes as the employees of some of those authorities.

The Schedule to the 2000 Order lists in Part I the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 52 to 65 of the 1998 Order and in Part II the bodies who are public authorities for the purposes of Articles 62 to 65 of the 1998 Order.

Articles 3 and 4 of this Order repeat amendments included in the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2001 which, by virtue of Article 6, is now revoked as is the Fair Employment (Specification of Public Authorities) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002.

Article 5 of this Order substitutes a new Schedule to the 2000 Order.

The following bodies are added:

- Part I Commissioner for Children and Young People for Northern Ireland

- Economic Research Institute of Northern Ireland Limited

- General Teaching Council for Northern Ireland

- Ilex Urban Regeneration Company Limited

- Northern Ireland Legal Services Commission

- Office of Communications

- Strategic Investment Board Limited

- Tourism Ireland Limited

- Part II First Secretary of State

- Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs

- Secretary of State for Transport

- Northern Ireland Health and Personal Social Services Regulation and Improvement Authority.

The following bodies are deleted:

- Part I The Juvenile Justice Board

- Part II Secretary of State for Transport, Local Government and the Regions.
