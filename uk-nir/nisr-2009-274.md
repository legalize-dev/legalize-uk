# The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 23rd July 2009

Coming into operation: 17th August 2009

The Department of the Environment makes the following By-Laws in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 17th August 2009.

#### By-Laws amended

###### 2

In the Schedule to the By-Laws made in respect of Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951[^f00002], after the entry for Brunswick Street insert—

| CHICHESTER STREET | North Side: from a point 38 metres west of the west kerbline of Victoria Street for a distance of 53 metres in a westerly direction. Carriages shall stand in a single line by the kerbline and parallel therewith facing east. | 10 | At any time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 23rd July 2009.

Stanley Duncan — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the By-Laws)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These By-Laws further amend the Schedule to the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-Laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951 by making provision for a new taxi stand on the north side of Chichester Street.
