# The Contracting Out (Functions Relating to Child Support) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 28th June 2006

Coming into operation: 3rd July 2006

Accordingly, the Department for Social Development makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Contracting Out (Functions Relating to Child Support) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 3rd July 2006.

- (2) In this Order “the 1991 Order” means the Child Support (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00004].

#### Contracting out of functions relating to child support

###### 2

- (1) Subject to paragraph (2) or (3), any function of the Department relating to child support under or by virtue of the provisions of the 1991 Order, may be exercised by, or by employees of, such person (if any) as may be authorised by the Department.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall not include functions under the following provisions—

- (a) Article 17 of the 1991 Order[^f00005] (powers of inspectors);

- (b) Article 35 of the 1991 Order (enforcement of liability orders);

- (c) Article 36A of the 1991 Order[^f00006] (commitment to prison and disqualification from driving); or

- (d) Article 37A of the 1991 Order[^f00007] (disqualification from driving: further provision); ...

- (e) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

- (3) Paragraph (1) shall not include any function if—

- (a) it is a power or right of entry, search or seizure into or of any property; or

- (b) it is a power or duty to make subordinate legislation.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 28th June 2006

John O'Neill — A senior officer of the — Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision to enable the Department to authorise another person to exercise functions relating to child support other than certain functions which are excluded from contracting out by section 71 of the Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994 such as rights of entry and seizure, and the power to make subordinate legislation.
