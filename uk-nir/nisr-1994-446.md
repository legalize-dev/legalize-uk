# The Criminal Justice (1994 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1994

Made: 15th November 1994

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1994[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (1994 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1994.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1994 shall be 9th January 1995.

## Signed

P. B. B. Mayhew — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 15th November 1994

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order appoints 9th January 1995 for the coming into operation of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1994.
