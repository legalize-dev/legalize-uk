# The Salaries (Public Services Ombudsman) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made: 17th July 2019

Coming into operation: 8th August 2019

The Northern Ireland Assembly Commission, in exercise of the power conferred by paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 to the Public Services Ombudsman Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Salaries (Public Services Ombudsman) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019 and shall come into operation on 8th August 2019.

#### Retrospection

###### 2

Article 3 shall have effect from 1st August 2016.

#### Salary

###### 3

The annual salary payable to the Public Services Ombudsman is:

- (1) for the period 1st August 2016 to 31st July 2017, £92,920;

- (2) for the period 1st August 2017 to 31st July 2018, £94,778; and

- (3) from 1st August 2018, and in each subsequent year, £97,337.

#### Revocation

###### 4

The Salaries (Public Services Ombudsman) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016 is revoked.

## Signed

Signed by authority of the Assembly Commission on 17th July 2019.

Lesley Hogg — Clerk to the Assembly
