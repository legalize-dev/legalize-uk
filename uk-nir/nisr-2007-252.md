# Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Amendment No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 19th April 2007

Coming into operation: 21st May 2007

The Department is designated[^f00004] for the purposes of the said section 2(2) in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Amendment No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 21st May 2007.

- (2) In these Regulations—

- “the principal Regulations” means the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005.

- (3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

The principal Regulations shall be amended in accordance with regulations 3 and 4.

###### 3

In Schedule 1, insert “, or part thereof” after the following words each time they appear: “for each additional 100 units”, “for each additional 1,000 units”, “for each additional 10kg”, “for each additional 100kg”, “for each additional 1,000kg” and “for each additional 25,000kg”.

###### 4

For Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations there shall be substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE — New Schedule 2 to be inserted in the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend The Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 ([S.R. 2005 No. 373](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/373)) (“the principal Regulations”), which implemented Article 13(d) of Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (O.J. No. L 169, 10.7.00, p. 1). This Directive requires Member States to charge fees to cover the costs occasioned by the documentary checks, identity checks and plant health checks of certain import plants, plant products and other objects from third countries.

Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations includes provision for reduced rates of certain plants and plant products agreed under the procedure provided for in Articles 13d(2) and 18(2) of the Directive. These Regulations substitute a new Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations, to give effect to the latest agreement in respect of reduced rate fees.
