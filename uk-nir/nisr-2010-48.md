# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 23rd February 2010

Coming into operation: 24th March 2010

This Order makes provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004] and it appears to the Department that it is expedient for the references in the following Order to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community[^f00005] to be construed as references to that instrument as amended from time to time.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and shall come into operation on 24th March 2010.

#### Amendments to The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006([^f00006] is amended as follows:

- (2) In Article 2(1)

For the definition of “the Directive” substitute—

> > - “the Directive” means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community, as amended from time to time;

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 23rd February 2010.

David Small — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No.66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/66)) (‘the principle Order’) so as to implement Commission Directive [2009/118/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0118) amending Annexes II to V of Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community (OJ No L 239, 10.09.2009, p51).

Article 2(2) makes the references to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) in the principal Order ambulatory.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as there will be minimal impact on the private or voluntary sectors.
