# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Amendment No. 16) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 23rd October 2020

Coming into operation: at 4.00 a.m. on 25th October 2020

The Department of Health makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 25B and 25F(2) of the Public Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967 .

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) (Amendment No. 16) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 and shall come into operation at 4.00 am on 25th October 2020.

- (2) In these Regulations, “*the principal Regulations*” means the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 .

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The principal Regulations are amended in accordance with regulation 3.

- (2) In relation to any person who arrived in Northern Ireland on or after 10th July 2020 and before the coming into operation of any amendment made by regulation 3 the principal Regulations apply as if that amendment had not been made.

#### Amendments to Schedule 3

###### 3

In Schedule 3 (Exempt countries, territories and parts of countries or territories)—

- (a) for “Greece excluding the island of Mykonos” substitute “ Greece ”;

- (b) at the appropriate place in alphabetical order, insert “ The Canary Islands ”, “Denmark” and “The Maldives”; and

- (c) omit “Liechtenstein”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 23rd October 2020

Dr Michael McBride — A senior officer of the — Department of Health
