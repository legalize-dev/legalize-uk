# The Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2023

Made: 28th March 2023

Coming into operation: 19th April 2023

In accordance with section 8A(5) of the Act the Department of Finance have consulted, where they think it appropriate, with the Treasury.

#### Title and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001 (Estimates and Accounts) (Designation of Bodies) Order 2023 and shall come into operation on 19th April 2023.

#### Designation of Bodies

###### 2

A body which is listed in a Part of the Schedule to this Order is a designated body for the purposes of sections 8A and 8B of the Government Resources and Accounts (Northern Ireland) Act 2001—

- (a) in relation to the department named in the heading to that Part, and

- (b) for the financial year which ends on 31st March 2024.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates specified central government bodies in relation to named government departments for the purpose of those departments’ supply estimates and resource accounts.

The designations have effect for the financial year that ends on 31st March 2024.

An impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen. An Explanatory Memorandum is available alongside the instrument on www.legislation.gov.uk.
