# Change of Council Name (Derry and Strabane City Council) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 19th January 2016

Coming into operation: 24th February 2016

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2) of the Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Change of Council Name (Derry and Strabane City Council) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 and shall come into operation on 24th February 2016.

#### Change of council name

###### 2

The name of the council for the district of Derry and Strabane shall be changed to Derry City and Strabane District Council.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 19 January 2016.

Nichola Creagh — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment
