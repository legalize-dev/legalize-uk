# The Social Security (Coronavirus) (Electronic Communications) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 26 May 2020

Coming into operation: 27 May 2020

In accordance with section 1(3) of that Act it considers that the authorisation of the use of electronic communications by this Order for any purpose is such that the extent (if any) to which records of things done for that purpose will be available will be no less satisfactory in cases where use is made of electronic communications than in other cases.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Coronavirus) (Electronic Communications) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020 and comes into operation on 27th May 2020.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987

###### 2

- (1) The Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987[^f00002] are amended as follows.

- (2) In regulation 4ZC(2)[^f00003] (electronic claims for benefit) after “state pension under Part 1 of the 2015 Act” insert “, state pension credit”.

- (3) In paragraph 2 of Schedule 8D[^f00004] (conditions for the use of electronic communication)—

- (i) after “state pension under Part 1 of the 2015 Act” insert “, state pension credit”;

- (ii) at the end omit “and state pension credit”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 26 May 2020

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Social Security (Claims and Payments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1987 (“the Claims and Payments Regulations”) to allow electronic claims to be made for State Pension Credit. Due to the impact of the Covid-19 outbreak in Northern Ireland, an online digital platform has been developed to provide claimants with an additional route for claiming and to ease the operational impacts of the pandemic.

Article 1 provides for citation and commencement.

Article 2 amends regulation 4ZC of, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 8D to, the Claims and Payments Regulations to permit the use of an electronic communication in connection with a claim for State Pension Credit. The use of an electronic communication by claimants must be in accordance with a direction of the Department.
