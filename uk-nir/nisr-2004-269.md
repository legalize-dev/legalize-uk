# Goods Vehicles (Testing) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 24th June 2004

Coming into operation: 28th June 2004

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 67(1)(g) and 110(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Goods Vehicles (Testing) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 28th June 2004.

#### Amendment of the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

In regulation 11(b) of the Goods Vehicles (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] (time of application for first tests or periodical tests) for “three months” there shall be substituted “six months”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 24th June 2004.

Stanley Duncan — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Goods Vehicle (Testing) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 by increasing the period, within which a person may make an application for a first test or periodical test before the test is due, from 3 months to 6 months, (regulation 2).
