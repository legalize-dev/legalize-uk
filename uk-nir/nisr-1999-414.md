# The Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999

Made: 6th October 1999

Coming into operation: 6th October 1999

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 14(1) and (4) of the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991[^f00001], and all other powers enabling it in that behalf, and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel in accordance with Article 14(8) of that Order, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 and shall come into operation on 6th October 1999.

#### Amendment

###### 2

For the Schedule to the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] there shall be substituted the following:

| *Column 1* | *Column 2* | *Column 3* | *Column 4* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HSS Trust | Originating Capital Debt | Initial Loan | Public Dividend Capital |
|  | £ | £ | £ |
| Ulster Community and Hospitals Trust | 53,928,000 | 26,964,000 | 26,964,000 |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health and Social Services on

Leslie Frew — Assistant Secretary — 6th October 1999.

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on

J. G. Sullivan — Assistant Secretary — 6th October 1999.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order amends the Health and Social Services Trusts (Originating Capital Debt) Order (Northern Ireland) 1999 which determines the originating capital debt, interest bearing initial loan and public dividend capital of HSS trusts established under the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 with an operational date of 1st April 1998.

The Order varies the originating capital debt, interest bearing initial loan and public dividend capital of the Ulster Community and Hospitals Trust.
