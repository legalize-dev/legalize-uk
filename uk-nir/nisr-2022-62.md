# The Social Security Benefits (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made: 24th February 2022

Coming into operation: 3rd March 2022

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5(1)(j), 165(1) and (5) of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security Benefits (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022 and shall come into operation on 3rd March 2022.

#### Amendment of the Social Security Benefits (Claims and Payments) (Amendment) Regulations

###### 2

Omit paragraph (2) of regulation 1 of the Social Security Benefits (Claims and Payments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00003].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 24th February 2022

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Regulation 2 makes a technical amendment to remove an unnecessary provision from the Social Security Benefits (Claims and Payments) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021[^f00004].
