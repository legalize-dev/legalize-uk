# The Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made: 20th March 2017

Coming into operation: 10th April 2017

The Department for Communities makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 70(8) and 171(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 , and now vested in it .

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 10th April 2017.

#### Amendment of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 8(1) of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1976 (circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as gainfully employed) for “£110” substitute “ £116 ”.

#### Revocation

###### 3

The Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 20th March 2017

Thomas O'Reilly — A senior officer of the — Department for Communities
