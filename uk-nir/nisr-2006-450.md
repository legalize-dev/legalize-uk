# The Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 10th November 2006

Coming into operation-: 18th December 2006

The Department of the Environment makes the following By-Laws in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 65(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These By-Laws may be cited as the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) (Amendment No. 2) By-Laws (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 18th December 2006.

#### By-Laws amended

###### 2

In the Table in by-law 26(1)(a) of the By-Laws relating to Motor Hackney Carriages standing or plying for hire made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951[^f00002] column (4), as it relates to item 4, is amended as follows—

- (a) for the words “2 minutes 20 seconds (140 seconds)” substitute “4 minutes 30 seconds (270 seconds)”; and

- (b) for the words “15 seconds” substitute “30 seconds”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 10th November 2006.

Maggie Smith — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the By-Laws)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These By-Laws further amend the Motor Hackney Carriages (Belfast) By-Laws made by the Council of the County Borough of Belfast on 4th June 1951 by increasing the length of time for which—

- (a) the initial rate of fare is charged from 2 minutes 20 seconds (140 seconds) to 4 minutes 30 seconds (270 seconds), and

- (b) the subsequent rate of fare is charged from 15 seconds to 30 seconds

during the periods from Christmas Eve to 27th December and from New Year’s Eve to 2nd January.
