# The Limited Liability Partnerships (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 22nd July 2004

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 15(1) of the Limited Liability Partnerships Act (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Limited Liability Partnerships (2002 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Limited Liability Partnerships Act (Northern Ireland) 2002, in so far as it is not already in operation, is 13th September 2004.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 22nd July 2004.

James McKeown — A senior officer of the — Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings the Limited Liability Partnerships Act (Northern Ireland) 2002 fully into operation on 13th September 2004.
