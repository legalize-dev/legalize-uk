# The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 31st July 2013

Coming into operation: 31st August 2013

In accordance with that Article, the Department of the Environment has had regard, amongst other things, to the sulphur content of the fuels described in regulation 2.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 31st August 2013.

#### Authorised fuels

###### 2

For the purposes of Article 2(2) of the Clean Air (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, the following are authorised fuels—

- (a) anthracite;

- (b) semi-anthracite;

- (c) electricity;

- (d) gas;

- (e) low volatile steam coals; and

- (f) the fuels described in the Schedule.

#### Revocations and savings

###### 3

- (1) The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012[^f00002] are revoked.

- (2) Any fuel manufactured before the coming into operation of these Regulations which was an authorised fuel continues to be an authorised fuel, notwithstanding the revocation of the Regulations specified in paragraph (1).

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations revoke and replace with amendments the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012. The Regulations list fuels to be authorised fuels for the purposes of Article 2(2) of the Clean Air (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

Three additional fuels are authorised (Schedule, paragraphs 57 to 59).

The Regulations contain a savings provision to provide that stocks of authorised fuels manufactured before these Regulations came into operation remain as authorised fuels (regulation 3(2)).
