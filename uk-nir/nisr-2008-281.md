# Penalty Fares (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 2nd July 2008

Coming into operation: 11th August 2008

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by paragraph 4(2) of Schedule 1B to the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Penalty Fares (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 11th August 2008.

#### Increase in amount of penalty fares

###### 2

In paragraph 4(1)(a) of Schedule 1B to the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, for “£5” substitute “£20”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 2nd July 2008.

B. R. D. White — A senior officer of the — Department

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Schedule 1B to the Transport Act 1967 (as inserted by [S.I. 1990/994 (N.I. 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1990/994) makes provision for penalty fares to be paid by a person who travels on a bus or train service without paying the proper fare.

Paragraph 4(1) of that Schedule provides that the amount of a penalty fare shall be the full single fare for the journey plus £5. The penalty fare was increased in 1991 to £10.

This Order increases that amount by an additional £10 to £20.
