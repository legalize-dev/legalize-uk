# The Trustee (2001 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 23rd July 2002

The Department of Finance and Personnel in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 45 of the Trustee Act (Northern Ireland) 2001[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Trustee (2001 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The provisions of the Trustee Act (Northern Ireland) 2001 shall come into operation on 29th July 2002.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 23rd July 2002.

G. W. Johnston — A Senior Officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation on 29th July 2002 the Trustee Act (Northern Ireland) 2001.
