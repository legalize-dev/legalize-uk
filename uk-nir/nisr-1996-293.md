# County Courts (Amendment) (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 10th July 1996

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the County Courts (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the County Courts (Amendment) (1996 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the County Courts (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 shall be 1st September 1996.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.* — Dated 10th July1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the rules.)

This Order brings into operation on 1st September 1996 the provisions of the County Courts (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 1996.
