# The Local Government (General Grant) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 10th February 2006

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 7(3) of the Schedule to the Northern Ireland Act 2000

Coming into operation: 1st April 2006

In accordance with Article 4(7) of that Order the Department has consulted such district councils and other interested bodies or persons as the Department considers appropriate:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as The Local Government (General Grant) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1st April 2006.

#### The Local Government (General Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

- (1) The Local Government (General Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “employment deprivation scores” for the words “July 2001” there shall be substituted “May 2005”; and

- (b) in the definition of “income deprivation scores” for the words “July 2001” there shall be substituted “May 2005”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 10th February 2006

J Ritchie — A senior officer of the — Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Local Government (General Grant) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003. The reference to the published date of the Northern Ireland Multiple Deprivation Measure, produced by Northern Ireland Statistics and Research Agency of the Department of Finance and Personnel, is amended to May 2005.

The Northern Ireland Multiple Deprivation Measure (ISBN 0 33 708 746 6) is available from The Stationery Office (TSO), 16 Arthur Street, Belfast, BT1 4GD.
