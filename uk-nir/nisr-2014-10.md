# The Deregulation (Improvement of Enforcement Procedures) (Food Safety) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 20th January 2014

Coming into operation: 17th February 2014

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 9(1) and (4) of and paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 to the Deregulation and Contracting Out (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, after open and transparent public consultation, makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Deregulation (Improvement of Enforcement Procedures) (Food Safety) (Revocation) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014 and comes into operation on 17th February 2014.

#### Revocation of the Deregulation (Improvement of Enforcement Procedures) (Food Safety) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

The Deregulation (Improvement of Enforcement Procedures) (Food Safety) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00003] is revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 20th January 2014.

Julie Thompson — A senior officer of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order revokes the Deregulation (Improvement of Enforcement Procedures) (Food Safety) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 579](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/579)).
