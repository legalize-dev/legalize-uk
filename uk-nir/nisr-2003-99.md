# Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 25th February 2003

Coming into operation: 1st April 2003

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 61(1), 66(1) and 218(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 1st April 2003.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Public Service Vehicles Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1985 by increasing some of the fees payable –

- (1) For an initial application for a vehicle licence (regulation 5(3)) the following fees have increased –

- (a) Buses –

- Class I, II and III, from £60·50 to £63·50;

- Class IA, from £72·00 to £75·00;

- Class IIA, from £83·50 to £86·50; and

- Class IIIA, from £94·00 to £97·50;

- (b) Taxi, from £38·50 to £61·50.

- (2) For a re-application for a vehicle licence (regulation 9(2)) the following fees have increased –

- (a) Buses –

- Class I, II and III from £60·50 to £63·50;

- Class IA, from £72·00 to £75·00;

- Class IIA, from £83·50 to £86·50; and

- Class IIIA, from £94·00 to £97·50;

- (b) Taxi, from £38·50 to £61·50.

- (3) For a re-application for a vehicle licence made within 21 days from the date of the service of the notice of refusal (regulation 9(3)) the following fee has increased –

- (a) Taxi, an increase from £16·50 to £18·50.

- (4) All other fees remain unchanged.

The Public Service Vehicles (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 are revoked.
