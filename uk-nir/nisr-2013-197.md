# The Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 19th July 2013

Coming into operation: 1st September 2013

In accordance with Article 91(1) of that Order the Department has consulted with such representative organisations as it thinks fit.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Amendment No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2013.

#### Amendment of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order

###### 2

For Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00002] substitute—

> > **SCHEDULE1**

|  | *Offence* | *Fixed Penalty* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | A fixed penalty offence under section 4(4) of the Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00003] | £200 |
| 2. | A fixed penalty offence under Article 81(2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00004] | £200 |
| 3. | A fixed penalty offence under Article 86 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 | £200 |
| 4. | A fixed penalty offence under Article 90 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 | £200 |
| 5. | A fixed penalty offence under Article 80(1) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00005] | £200 |
| 6. | A fixed penalty offence under regulation 7(3) of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00006] | £200 |
| 7. | A fixed penalty offence under regulation 7D of the Passenger and Goods Vehicles (Recording Equipment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00007] | £200 |
| 8. | A fixed penalty offence under Article 91E(6) of the Road Traffic Offenders (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00008] | £200 |
| 9. | A fixed penalty offence under section 1(3) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 [^f00009] | £200 |
| 10. | A fixed penalty offence under section 3(10) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 | £200 |
| 11. | A fixed penalty offence under section 1(6) of the Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00010] | £200 |
| 12. | A fixed penalty offence under Article 177 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00011] | £120 |
| 13. | A fixed penalty offence under regulation 3 of the Goods Vehicles (Community Authorisations) Regulations 1992[^f00012] | £60 |
| 14. | A fixed penalty offence under section 33(1) of the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994[^f00013] | £60 |
| 15. | A fixed penalty offence under Article 63 of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00014] | £60 |
| 16. | A fixed penalty offence under regulation 2 of the Road Service Licensing (Community Licences) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00015] | £60 |
| 17. | A fixed penalty offence under section 3(11) of the Taxis Act (Northern Ireland) 2008 | £60 |
| 18. | Any other fixed penalty offence involving obligatory endorsement | £60 |
| 19. | Any other fixed penalty offence. | £30 |

> >   In this Schedule at items 18 and 19 a “fixed penalty offence” is an offence specified in the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00016].

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prescribes the amount of fixed penalties for certain fixed penalty offences by amending the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 (“the Principal Order”).

It amends the Principal Order to provide for new fixed penalty offences which have been specified as such by Article 2 of the Road Traffic (Fixed Penalty) (Offences) (Amendment No.2) (Order) (Northern Ireland) 2013.

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr.
