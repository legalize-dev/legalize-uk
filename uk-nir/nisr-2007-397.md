# The Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 12th September 2007

Coming into operation: 22nd October 2007

The Magistrates’ Courts Rules Committee makes the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 13 of the Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and Article 165 of the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00002] after consultation with the Lord Chancellor and with the agreement of the Lord Chief Justice.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Rules may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) (Amendment) Rules (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 22nd October 2007.

#### Amendment to the Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

After rule 18 insert—

> > (18A) The court may allow a witness to give evidence through a video link or by any other method of direct communication.

## Signed

George Conner — John P.B. Maxwell — Mandy Kilpatrick — Nigel Broderick — Robin Steer — Dated 12th September 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Rules)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Rules amend the Magistrates’ Courts (Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) Rules (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 323](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/323)) to enable the court to allow a witness to give evidence through a video link or by any other method of direct communication.
