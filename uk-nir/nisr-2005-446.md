# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 10th October 2005

Coming into operation: 7th November 2005

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the Common Agricultural Policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 7th November 2005.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 3

The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with these Regulations.

###### 4

In paragraph (1) of Regulation 2 (Interpretation)—

- (a) After “2.—(1) In these Regulations—” there shall be inserted—

> > - “approved assembly centre” means an assembly centre approved by the Department in accordance with regulation 12;

- (b) After “dealer” there shall be inserted—

> > - “the Department” means the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development;

###### 5

For paragraph 4 of Regulation 2 (Interpretation) there shall be substituted the following paragraphs—

> > (4) References in these Regulations to the European Union legislation listed in this paragraph shall be construed as follows—
>   - “Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 282/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0282)” means Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 282/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0282) introducing a document for the declaration of, and veterinary checks on, animals from third countries entering the Community[^f00005]
>   - “Council Directive [64/432/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1964/0432)” means Council Directive [64/432/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1964/0432) on animal health problems affecting intra-Community trade in bovine animals and swine[^f00006], as amended by and as read with the instruments listed in paragraph 1 of Part I of Schedule 2;
>   - “Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425)” has the meaning given in paragraph 4(a);
>   - “Council Directive [90/427/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0427)” means Council Directive [90/427/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0427) on the zootechnical and genealogical conditions governing intra-Community trade in equidae[^f00007], as amended by and as read with the instruments listed in paragraph 17 of Part I of Schedule 2;
>   - “Council Directive [90/539/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0539)” means Council Directive [90/539/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0539) on animal health conditions governing intra-Community trade in, and imports from third countries of, poultry and hatching eggs[^f00008], as amended by and as read with, in relation to intra-Community trade with the instruments listed in paragraph 6 of Part I of Schedule 2 and in relation to imports from third countries, the instruments listed in paragraph 9 of Part II of Schedule 4;
>   - “Council Directive [91/67/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0067)” means Council Directive [91/67/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0067) concerning the animal health conditions governing the placing on the market of aquaculture animals and products[^f00009], as amended by and as read with the instruments listed in paragraph 8 of Part I of Schedule 2;
>   - “Council Directive [91/68/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0068)” means Council Directive [91/68/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0068) on animal health conditions governing intra-Community trade in ovine and caprine animals[^f00010], as amended by and as read with the instruments listed in paragraph 11 of Part I of Schedule 2;
>   - “Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496)” has the meaning given in paragraph 4(b);
>   - “Council Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065)” means Council Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065) laying down animal health requirements governing trade in and imports into the Community of animals, semen, ova and embryos not subject to animal health requirements laid down in specific Community rules referred to in Annex A(1) to Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425)[^f00011], as amended by and as read with, in relation to intra-Community trade, the instruments listed in paragraph 12 of Part I of Schedule 2, and in relation to imports from third countries, the instruments listed in paragraph 10 of Part II of Schedule 4;
>   - “Regulation [(EC) No. 998/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0998)” means Regulation [(EC) No. 998/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0998) on the animal health requirements applicable to the non-commercial movement of pet animals and amending Council Directive [92/65/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0065)[^f00012], as amended by Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2054/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/2054)[^f00013];

>   (a) Council Directive [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425) concerning veterinary and zootechnical checks applicable in intra-Community trade in certain live animals and products with a view to the completion of the single market[^f00014] as amended by and as read with the instruments listed in paragraph 1 of Schedule 1; and
>   (b) Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) laying down the principles governing the organisation of veterinary checks on animals entering the Community from third countries[^f00015], as amended by and as read with the instruments listed in paragraph 2 of Schedule 1.

###### 6

In Regulation 4 (Application of Part II) the words—

> > (concerning the animal health conditions governing the placing on the market of aquaculture animals and aquaculture products.)

shall be omitted.

###### 7

In Regulation 5 (Exports)—

- (a) for sub-paragraph 1(d) there shall be substituted the following—

> > (d) if the animal is acquired through or transits an assembly centre, that centre is an approved assembly centre.

- (b) in paragraph (6) the words—

> > - (laying down animal health requirements governing trade in and imports into the Community of animals, semen, ova and embryos not subject to animal health requirements laid down in specific Community rules referred to in Annex A (1) to Council Directives [90/425/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0425))

shall be omitted.

###### 8

In Regulation 6 (Imports) in sub-paragraphs (4)(a) and (b) and in paragraph (6) there shall be inserted the word “approved” before the words “assembly centre”.

###### 9

In paragraph (1) of Regulation 11 (Duties on consignees) the words “(on the zootechnical and genealogical conditions governing intra-Community trade in equidae)” shall be omitted.

###### 10

For sub-paragraph (2)(a) of Regulation 12 (Assembly centres and slaughterhouses) there shall be substituted—

> > (a) in the case of an assembly centre used for cattle or pigs, the centre complies with the requirements of paragraphs (a) to (d) of Article 11(1) of Council Directive [64/432/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1964/0432).

###### 11

Schedule 2 shall be substituted by Schedule 2 as set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

###### 12

Schedule 4 shall be substituted by Schedule 4 as set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 [S.R. 2005 No. 78](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2005/78)).

Regulation 7 specifies the compliance required under Community law for assembly centres handling sheep and goats which are permitted to be exported to other Member States under these Regulations.

Schedules 2 and 4 have been revised and updated in order to bring the implementation of Community instruments as up-to-date as possible at the time of making. The changes made to the Schedules are in paragraphs 1, 4, 6, 8, 11, 12 and 17 of Part I of Schedule 2, paragraphs 1 and 5 of Part I of Schedule 4, (to which a new paragraph 6 has been added to provide separately for a list of third countries from which equines and their genetic material may be imported); and paragraphs 2, 9, 10 and 14 of Part II of Schedule 4 have also been revised and updated.

In particular—

- (a) at paragraph 4 of Schedule 2, Part I, Council Directive 90/426//EEC is now to be read with Council Directive [90/427/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0427) and Commission Decision [93/623/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0623) as amended by Commission Decision [2000/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0068), relating to equine identification in respect of trade, and the notes to that paragraph amend the details relating to the Tripartite Agreement to allow movements of equidae for breeding and production to take place between Ireland, France and the UK without need for official health certification (such animals must be accompanied by a passport in accordance with Commission Decision [93/62/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1993/0062) as amended by Commission Decision [2000/68/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0068)); and

- (b) at paragraph 6 of the Schedule 2, Part I, the reference to Council Decision [95/410/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1995/0410) now reflects amendments made by Council Decision 98/227, and the requirements for certificates as to testing for Finland and Sweden have been updated to refer to Commission Decisions [2003/644/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0644) and [2004/235/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2004/0235) in respect of breeding poultry, day-old chicks, laying hens and poultry for slaughter.
