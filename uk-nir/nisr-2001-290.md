# The Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001

Made: 24th July 2001

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 1st September 2001

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 5(3)(*a*) and 22(1) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following regulations:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Amendment No. 3) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 and shall come into operation on 1st September 2001.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In these regulations “the principal regulations” means the Legal Advice and Assistance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1981[^f00003].

#### Amendment of the principal Regulations

###### 3

In regulation 3 of the principal regulations after the meaning of “Legal Aid Committee” there shall be added the following meaning—

> > - “Life Sentence Review Commissioners” means the Life Sentence Review Commissioners appointed by the Secretary of State under Article 3 of the Life Sentences (Northern Ireland) Order 2001[^f00004];

###### 4

In regulation 17(3)(*b*) of the principal regulations—

- (a) the words “it is made” in sub-paragraph (iii) shall be omitted;

- (c) after sub-paragraph (iii), there shall be added the following sub-paragraph—

> > (iv) in respect of a hearing before the Life Sentence Review Commissioners.

## Signed

Irvine of Lairg, *C.* — Dated 24th July 2001

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations extend assistance by way of representation, under Article 5 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, to life sentence prisoners at hearings before the Life Sentence Review Commissioners.
