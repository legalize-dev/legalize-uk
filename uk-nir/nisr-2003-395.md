# Air Route Development (Northern Ireland) Limited (Funding) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 3rd September 2003

To be laid before Parliament under paragraph 7(3) of the Schedule to the Northern Ireland Act 2000

Coming into operation: 22nd October 2003

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 30(1)(b) of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982[^f00002], and of every other power enabling it in that behalf and with the consent of the Department of Finance and Personnel, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Air Route Development (Northern Ireland) Limited (Funding) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003 and shall come into operation on 22nd October 2003.

#### Purpose

###### 2

Air Route Development (Northern Ireland) Limited is hereby specified as a body to which the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment may make grants under paragraph (1)(b) of Article 30 of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 3rd September 2003.

Malcolm Briant — A Senior Officer of the — Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

The Department of Finance and Personnel hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 3rd September 2003.

Jack Layberry — A Senior Officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

Article 30 of the Industrial Development (Northern Ireland) Order 1982 enables the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment to make grants to a body or person which it may specify, for the purpose of enabling that body or person to stimulate or assist industrial enterprise, the development of industry or the provision of employment.

This Order specifies Air Route Development (Northern Ireland) Limited as a body which may receive such grants.

Air Route Development (Northern Ireland) Limited is a company limited by guarantee which was formed on 30th July 2003.
