# Game Preservation (Special Protection for Irish Hares) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 26th September 2007

Coming into operation: 1st November 2007

Accordingly the Department makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Game Preservation (Special Protection for Irish Hares) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007, and shall come into operation on 1st November 2007.

#### Prohibition of killing, taking, selling or trading of Irish hares

###### 2

A person shall not kill, take, sell, or purchase Irish Hares at any time during the period 1st November 2007 to 31st March 2008 inclusive.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 26th September 2007.

M Smith — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order prohibits a person from killing, taking, selling or purchasing Irish hares during the period outlined in Article 2.

Any person who acts in contravention of or fails to comply with any provision of the order shall be guilty of an offence under section 7F(6) of the Game Preservation Act (Northern Ireland) 1928.
