# The Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 20th September 2007

Coming into operation: 15th October 2007

There has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation of the Regulations as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council[^f00003] laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and come into operation on 15th October 2007.

#### Amendment to the Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

###### 2

- (1) The Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 10 (offences and enforcement)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), insert at the beginning “Subject to paragraph (2A),”; and

- (b) immediately after paragraph (2), add the following—

> > (2A) A person shall not be considered to have contravened the provisions contained in regulation 2 where the sale which would otherwise have constituted a contravention of those provisions is the sale of an infant formula falling within the derogation set out in Article 1 of [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1609/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1609) authorising the placing on the market of infant formulae based on hydrolysates of whey protein derived from cows’ milk protein for a two-year period[^f00005].

#### Amendment to the Foods Intended for Use in Energy Restricted Diets for Weight Reduction Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

###### 3

- (1) The Foods Intended for Use in Energy Restricted Diets for Weight Reduction Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00006] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 4 (labelling, advertising and presentation) omit “or to a reduction in the sense of hunger or an increase in the sense of satiety”.

#### Amendment to the Medical Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000

###### 4

- (1) The Medical Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00007] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” there is substituted the following—

> > - “*the Directive*” means Commission Directive [1999/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0021) on dietary foods for special medical purposes[^f00008] as amended by the Act concerning the conditions of accession of the Czech Republic, the Republic of Estonia, the Republic of Cyprus, the Republic of Latvia, the Republic of Lithuania, the Republic of Hungary, the Republic of Malta, the Republic of Poland, the Republic of Slovenia and the Slovak Republic and the adjustments to the Treaties on which the European Union is founded[^f00009] and Commission Directive [2006/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0082) adapting Directive 91/321 on infant formulae and follow-on formulae and Directive [1999/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0021) on dietary foods for special medical purposes, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania[^f00010];

#### Amendment to the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002

###### 5

- (1) The Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00011] are amended in accordance with paragraph(2).

- (2) In paragraph (5) of regulation 3 (restrictions on sale) for “1st January 2007” substitute “1st January 2010”.

#### Amendment to the Processed Cereal-based Foods and Baby Foods for Infants and Young Children Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 6

- (1) The Processed Cereal-based Foods and Baby Foods for Infants and Young Children Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00012] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

- (2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” there is substituted the following—

> > - “*the Directive*” means Commission Directive [2006/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0125) on processed cereal-based foods and baby foods for infants and young children[^f00013];

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Regulations identified in the following paragraphs in the manner described in those paragraphs.

These Regulations amend the Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No.85](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/85), as amended, “the 1995 Regulations”) to provide for the execution and enforcement of [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1609/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1609) authorising the placing on the market of infant formula based on hydrolysates of whey protein derived from cows’ milk protein for a two-year period (OJ No. L299, 28.10.2006, p.9). They amend the 1995 Regulations to provide that a person shall not be considered to have contravened the provisions contained in regulation 2 of the 1995 Regulations where the sale which would otherwise have constituted a contravention of those provisions and thus an offence is the sale of an infant formula falling within the derogation set out in [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1609/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1609) (*regulation 2(2)*).

These Regulations amend the Foods Intended for Use in Energy Restricted Diets for Weight Reduction Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 ([S.R. 1997 No.450](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/450), as amended, “the 1997 Regulations”) to implement Commission Directive [2007/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0029) amending Directive [96/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0008) as regards labelling, advertising or presenting foods intended for use in energy-restricted diets for weight reduction (OJ No. L139, 31.5.2007, p.22). They amend the 1997 Regulations to provide for the removal of the prohibition on selling certain foods where the labelling, advertising or presentation of that food refers to a reduction in the sense of hunger or an increase in the sense of satiety (*regulation 3(2)*).

These Regulations amend the Medical Food Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000 ([S.R. 2000 No.187](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2000/187), as amended, “the 2000 Regulations”) to implement Commission Directive [2006/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0082) adapting Directive [91/321/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0321) on infant formulae and follow-on formulae and Directive [1999/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0021) on dietary food for special medical purposes, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania (OJ No. L362, 20.12.2006, p.94). They amend the 2000 Regulations by substituting a revised definition of “*the Directive*” (to take account of Commission Directive [2006/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0082)) for the existing definition of “the Directive” in regulation 2 (interpretation) (*regulation 4(2)*).

These Regulations amend the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002 ([S.R. 2002 No.264](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2002/264), as amended, “the 2002 Regulations”) to implement Commission Directive [2007/26/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0026) amending Directive [2004/6/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0006) to extend its period of application (OJ No. L118, 8.5.2007, p.5). They amend the 2002 Regulations to provide that certain restrictions contained in those Regulations shall not apply to certain substances until 1st January 2010 (*regulation 5(2)).*

These Regulations amend the Processed Cereal-based Foods and Baby Foods for Infants and Young Children Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 ([S.R. 2003 No.530](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/530), as amended, “the 2003 Regulations”) to implement Commission Directive [2006/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0125) on processed cereal-based foods and baby foods for infants and young children (OJ No. L339, 6.12.2006, p.16). They amend the 2003 Regulations by substituting a revised definition of the “*the Directive*” (so that it now refers to Commission Directive [2006/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0125)) for the existing definition of “the Directive” in paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) (*regulation 6(2)).*
