# The Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009

Made: 23rd February 2009

Coming into operation: 27th March 2009

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 5 and 36 of the Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001], and, being a department designated [^f00002] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 [^f00003] in relation to the environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by that section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2009 and shall come into operation on 27th March 2009.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^f00004] applies to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendments to the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

###### 3

- (1) The Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 [^f00005] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (6).

- (2) In paragraph 2 of regulation 2 (Interpretation), for “ Directive [79/923/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0923) (quality required of shellfish waters)” substitute “Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the quality required of shellfish waters (“Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113)”)”[^f00006].

- (3) In regulation 3 after “brackish waters” insert “for the purposes of Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113)”.

- (4) In the following provisions, for “Directive [79/923/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0923)” (in each place) substitute “Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113)”.

- (a) regulation 5(b) (Guideline values and no deterioration principle);

- (b) regulation 9(a) (Modification of section 4C of the Water Act (Northern Ireland) 1972).

- (5) In the heading for column 1 in the table contained in Schedule 1 (Criteria for classification of waters as shellfish waters) for “79/923/EEC” substitute “Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113)”.

- (6) In the heading for column 1 in the table contained in Schedule 2 (Guideline values in relation to the quality of shellfish waters) for “79/923/EEC” substitute “Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113)”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Surface Waters (Shellfish) (Classification) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 1997 (“the 1997 Regulations”).

These Regulations update the 1997 Regulations to reflect the revocation of Council Directive [79/923/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0923) as amended, and the consolidation of its terms with minor amendments in Directive [2006/113/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0113) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the quality required of shellfish waters, (O.J. No L376, 27.12.2006, p.14).

The 1997 Regulations as amended prescribe a system for classifying the quality of shellfish waters, and apply to coastal and brackish waters requiring protection or improvement in order to support shellfish life and growth, to contribute to the high quality of shellfish products directly edible by man.
