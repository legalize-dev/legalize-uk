# The Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 10th November 2007

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 17th December 2007

The Lord Chancellor, after consultation with the Lord Chief Justice, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 164(5) of and Schedule 7 to the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 17th December 2007.

#### Amendment to the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

###### 2

type=start slip=2008-02-01 time=1202382882067In Article 4, in paragraph (2), for “6 or 8” substitute “6, 8 or 9A”.type=end slip=2008-02-01 time=1202382882067

###### 3

After Article 9 insert—

> > (9A) A family care centre (the “transferring court”) shall transfer proceedings under the 1995 Order to another family care centre where—
>   (a) having regard to the principle set out in Article 3(2) of the 1995 Order the transferring court considers that the transfer is in the interests of the child; and
>   (b) the receiving court consents to the transfer.

## Signed

Signed by the authority of the Lord Chancellor

David Hanson — Minister of State — Ministry of Justice — Dated 10th November 2007

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Children (Allocation of Proceedings) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 ([S.R. 1996 No. 300](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1996/300)) to provide, in certain circumstances, for the transfer of proceedings under the Children (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 from one family care centre to another.
