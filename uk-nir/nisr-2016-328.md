# The Food Hygiene Rating (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 13th September 2016

The Department of Health[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(3) of the Food Hygiene Rating Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00002], makes the following Order.

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Food Hygiene Rating (2016 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Food Hygiene Rating Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, in so far as it is not already in operation, is 7th October 2016.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 13th September 2016.

Anne Kilgallen — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the Food Hygiene Rating Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 on the 7th October 2016.
