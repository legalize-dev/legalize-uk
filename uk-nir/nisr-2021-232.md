# The Lotteries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: 11th August 2021

Coming into operation: 7th September 2021

In accordance with Article 138(2) of that Order, the Department has consulted with such associations of district councils as appear to the Department likely to be affected by these Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as The Lotteries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 7th September 2021.

#### Amendment of the Lotteries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994

###### 2

The Lotteries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with regulation 3.

###### 3

For regulation 5 (machines) substitute—

> > (5) type=start slip=2021-09-01 time=1632298254652Except for the sale of tickets or chances in a lottery by means of the internet, no tickets or chances in a lottery shall be sold by means of a machine.type=end slip=2021-09-01 time=1632298254652

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 11th August 2021

Anthony Carleton — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Lotteries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1994 to permit the sale of tickets or chances in societies’ lotteries by means of the internet. Except for these purposes, the sale of tickets or chances in societies’ lotteries by means of a machine remains prohibited.

An Equality Impact Assessment has been prepared and can be viewed at www.communities-ni.gov.uk.
