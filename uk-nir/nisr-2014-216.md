# The Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 24th July 2014

Coming into operation: 8th September 2014

The Department of the Environment makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 55(1) and (2) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 8th September 2014.

#### Amendment of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations

###### 2

In regulation 93 of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00002] (over-riding weight restrictions), for “exceeded” in each place where it occurs substitute “equalled or exceeded”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 24th July 2014

Iain Greenway — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend regulation 93 of the Motor Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 by changing the upper limits on weight bands for vehicles in line with Directive [1999/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0062).

An Explanatory Memorandum has been produced and is available from Road Safety and Vehicle Regulation Division, Department of the Environment, Clarence Court, 10-18 Adelaide Street, Town Parks, Belfast BT2 8GB or viewed online at http://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr
