# Tourism (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 15th March 2011

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(4) of the Tourism (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00002]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Tourism (Amendment) (2011 Act) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of section 1(1), (2) and (4), and sections 2 and 3 of the Tourism (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 11th April 2011.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 15thMarch 2011

Noel Cornick — A senior officer of the — Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of section 1(1), (2) and (4) and sections 2 and 3 of the Tourism (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

Sections 4(2), 5, 6 and the Schedule came into operation on the day after Royal Assent.
