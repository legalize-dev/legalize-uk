# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 16th September 2002

Coming into operation: 23rd September 2002

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 2[^f00002], 3(1)[^f00003], 3A[^f00004], 3B(1)[^f00005] and 4(1)[^f00006] of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00007] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and comencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002 and shall come into operation on 23rd September 2002.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00008] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (6).

- (2) In Article 2(1) –

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093)”[^f00009] there shall be substituted the following definition:

> > - “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)” means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)[^f00010] of 8th May 2000 on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community as amended by Commission Directive [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033)[^f00011] of 8th May 2001 amending certain annexes to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029);” and by Commission Directive [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028)[^f00012] of 19th March 2002 amending certain annexes to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029);

and

- (b) in the definition of “protected zone”, for “Article 16a of Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093)” there shall be substituted “Article 18 of Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)”.

- (3) In Article 26, for “Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093)” there shall be substituted “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)”.

- (4) In Schedule 2, in Part B (WOOD AND ISOLATED BARK WHICH MAY NOT BE INTRODUCED INTO CERTAIN PROTECTED ZONES AND WHOSE MOVEMENT WITHIN THOSE ZONES IS PROHIBITED IF THEY ARE INFECTED WITH CERTAIN PESTS)

- (a) in column (4) for the words opposite Item 1 there shall be substituted-

> > - Greece, Ireland, Spain, United Kingdom (Scotland, Northern Ireland, England: the following counties: Barnsley, Bath and North East Somerset, Bedfordshire, Bournemouth, Bracknell Forest, Bradford, Bristol, Brighton and Hove, Buckinghamshire, Calderdale, Cambridgeshire, Cornwall, Cumbria, Darlington, Devon, Doncaster, Dorset, Durham, East Riding of Yorkshire, East Sussex, Essex, Gateshead, Greater London, Hampshire, Hartlepool, Hertfordshire, Kent, Kingston Upon Hull, Kirklees, Leeds, Leicester City, Lincolnshire, Luton, Medway Council, Middlesbrough, Milton Keynes, Newbury, Newcastle Upon Tyne, Norfolk, Northamptonshire, Northumberland, North Lincolnshire, North East Lincolnshire, North Tyneside, North West Somerset, Nottingham City, Nottinghamshire, Oxfordshire, Peterborough, Plymouth, Poole, Portsmouth, Reading, Redcar and Cleveland, Rotherham, Rutland, Sheffield, Slough, Somerset, Southend, Southampton, South Tyneside, Stockton-on-Tees, Suffolk, Sunderland, Surrey, Swindon, Thurrock, Torbay, Wakefield, West Sussex, Windsor and Maidenhead, Wokingham, York, The Isle of Wight, The Isles of Scilly, and the following parts of counties, districts and unitary authorities in England – *Derby City:* that part of the unitary authority which lies to the north of the northern boundary of the A52(T) road together with that part of the unitary authority which lies to the north of the northern boundary of the A6(T) road; *Derbyshire:* that part of the county which lies to the north of the northern boundary of the A52(T) road, and that part of the county which lies to the north of the northern boundary of the A6(T) road; *North Yorkshire:* the whole county, except that part of the county which comprises the district of Craven; *South Gloucestershire:* that part of the unitary authority which lies to the south of the southern boundary of the M4 motorway; ## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order implements certain elements of Commission Directives [2002/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0029) (O.J. No. L77, 20.3.02, p. 26) and [2002/28/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0028) (O.J. No. L77, 20.3.02, p. 23) also measures required by Council Directives [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) and [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033) both of 8th May 2001 (O.J. No. L127, 9.5.2001, p. 38 and O.J. No. L127, 9.5.2001, p. 42 respectively) by amending the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 1993. Council Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) amends the protected zones established under the principal Plant Health Directive, Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (O.J. No. L169, 10.7.2000, p. 1) and Council Directive [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033) provides for consequential amendments to certain Annexes of that Directive.

The Order is amended to remove the protected zone in respect of the pest *Pissodes* spp. (European) which is present in Great Britain and which has now spread to Northern Ireland and Ireland (Article 2(4)), and to modify the protected zone within England in respect of the pest *Dendroctonus micans*. (Article 2(5)). Also the amendment removes the protected zone in Corsica in respect of the pest *Matsucoccus feytaudi* Duc. (Article 2(6)).

Amendments are also made to update the references to the principal Plant Health Directive, Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029), having replaced the previous Directive, [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093) (O.J. No. L26, 31.1.77, p. 20) (Articles 2(2), (3) and (6)).

No Regulatory Appraisal has been prepared in respect of this Order.
