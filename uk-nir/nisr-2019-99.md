# The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019

Made

Coming into operation

This Order makes provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00004] and it appears to the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs that it is expedient for references to the European Union instrument mentioned in article 3(1) to be construed as references to that instrument as amended from time to time.

#### Citation, commencement and application

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2019 and comes into operation on 7th May 2019.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 2

The Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00005] is amended as follows.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the Plant Health (Wood and Bark) Order (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006/66](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/66) (“the principal Order”) in relation to Northern Ireland to implement—

Article 3(1) provides for references to five EU instruments to be read as references to those instruments as amended from time to time.

These Regulations make amendments to legislation in the field of plant health and are made to address deficiencies arising from the withdrawal of the UK from the European Union.
