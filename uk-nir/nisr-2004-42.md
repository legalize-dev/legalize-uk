# Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 9th February 2004

Coming into operation: 8th March 2004

The Department for Social Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 33(2A) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^f00002] and now exercisable by it[^f00003] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Registered Rents (Increase) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004, and shall come into operation on 8th March 2004.

#### Increase of registered rents

###### 2

The rents registered under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 for dwelling houses which are let under regulated tenancies shall be increased by 3 percent.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 9th February 2004.

D. M. Crothers — A Senior Officer of the — Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order increases the rents registered with the Northern Ireland Housing Executive under Part V of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 for the dwelling houses, which are let under regulated tenancies, by 3 percent from 8th March 2004. Under Article 22(4) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978 a notice of increase of rent which gives effect to an increase made by virtue of this Order shall not take effect earlier than 4 weeks after the commencement of this Order, i.e., not earlier than 5th April 2004.

A landlord must give a tenant at least 4 weeks' notice of the increase in accordance with Article 22(3)(b) of the Rent (Northern Ireland) Order 1978.
