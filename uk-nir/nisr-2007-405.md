# The Surface Waters (Fishlife) (Classification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 18th September 2007

Coming into operation: 29th October 2007

The Department of the Environment, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to matters relating to water resources, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred on it by that section.

#### Citation, commencement and extent

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Surface Waters (Fishlife) (Classification) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 29th October 2007.

#### Amendments to the Surface Waters (Fishlife) (Classification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997

###### 2

- (1) The Surface Waters (Fishlife) (Classification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraphs(2) to(5).

- (2) In paragraph (2) of regulation 2 (interpretation) for “Directive [78/659/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0659)[^f00004] (the quality of fresh waters needing protection or improvement in order to support fishlife)” substitute “Directive [2006/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0044)[^f00005] of the European Parliament and of the Council on the quality of fresh waters needing protection or improvement in order to support fishlife (“Directive [2006/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0044)”)”.

- (3) In paragraph(b) of regulation 5 for “Directive [78/659/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0659)” substitute “Directive [2006/454/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0454)”.

- (4) In the heading of column1 to the table contained in PartI of Schedule 1 (criteria for classification of waters as salmonid and cyprinid waters) for “No.in Annex1 to 78/659/EEC” substitute “No.in Annex1 to Directive [2006/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0044)”.

- (5) In the heading of column1 to the table contained in PartI of Schedule 2 (guideline values in relation to the quality of salmonid and cyprinid waters) for “No.in Annex1 to 78/659/EEC” substitute “No.in Annex1 to Directive [2006/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0044)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 18 September 2007

Maggie Smith — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Surface Waters (Fishlife) (Classification) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997.

These amendments update the 1997 Regulations to reflect the consolidation of Council Directive [78/659/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1978/0659) and its amendments in Directive [2006/44/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0044) of the European Parliament and of the Council.
