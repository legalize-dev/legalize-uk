# The Criminal Justice (Confiscation) (1990 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991

Made: 23rd May 1991

In exercise of the powers conferred on me by Article 1(2) of the Criminal Justice (Confiscation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990[^f00001], I hereby make the following Order:—

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Confiscation) (1990 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 1991.

#### Appointed Days

###### 2

The provisions of the Criminal Justice (Confiscation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 shall, with the exception of Article 27, come into operation on 1st July 1991 and Article 27 shall come into operation forthwith.

## Signed

P. L. Brooke — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 23rd May 1991

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order appoints 1st July 1991 for the coming into operation of the Criminal Justice (Confiscation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990 with the exception of Article 27 which comes into operation forthwith.
