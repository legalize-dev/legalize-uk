## SCHEDULE 1

###### 1

Vehicles propelled by spark ignition engines.

###### 2

Vehicles with less than 4 wheels if they are constructed not to exceed a speed of 30 mph on the level under their own power or if they have an unladen weight of less than 400kg.

###### 3

Vehicles fuelled by liquid petroleum gas.

###### 1

Vehicles propelled by spark ignition engines.

###### 2

Vehicles with less than 4 wheels if they are constructed not to exceed a speed of 30 mph on the level under their own power or if they have an unladen weight of less than 400kg.

###### 3

Vehicles fuelled by liquid petroleum gas.

###### 1

Vehicles having—

- (a) an engine with a cylinder capacity of not less than 700cc; and

- (b) a rated power speed (within the meaning of Council Directive [88/77/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0077) amended as specified in column 3(a) of more than 3,000 revolutions per minute.

###### 2

Vehicles propelled by spark ignition engines.

###### 3

Vehicles with less than 4 wheels if they are constructed not to exceed a speed of 30 mph on the level under their own power or if they have an unladen weight of less than 400kg.

###### 4

Vehicles fuelled by liquid petroleum gas.
