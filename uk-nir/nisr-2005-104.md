# The Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 14th March 2005

Coming into operation: 1st April 2005

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by Article 1(3) of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (2003 Order) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The 1st April 2005 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of all the provisions of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 (except Article 46 of that Order), in so far as those provisions are not already in operation[^f00002].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety on 14th March 2005.

A. Hamilton — A senior officer of the — Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings into operation those Articles of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003 which will place the Pre-Employment Consultancy Service Register on a statutory basis by the creation of two new lists: the Disqualification from Working with Children List and the Disqualification from Working with Vulnerable Adults List. It brings with it a duty for childcare organisations to check against the lists and to refer to the Department for consideration of inclusion on the list(s) those individuals deemed unsuitable to work with children. It also brings with it a duty for care providers to refer to the Department for consideration of inclusion on the list(s) those individuals deemed unsuitable to work with vulnerable adults. It is now an offence for those individuals disqualified from working with children to seek work in a regulated position or for an employer to knowingly offer them work in a regulated position. The Order allows for those individuals who are included on the statutory lists a right of appeal against those listings.
