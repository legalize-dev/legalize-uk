# The Welfare of Animals (Transport) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 19th January 2007

Coming into operation: 12th February 2007

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on it by that section.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare of Animals (Transport) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 12th February 2007.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations “the principal Regulations” means The Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00003].

- (2) The Interpretation Act (NI) 1954[^f00004] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of The Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 3

The Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 are amended in accordance with regulations 4 to 8.

###### 4

In regulation 2(1) (Interpretation)—

- (a) before the definition of “Council Regulation 1/2005” there shall be inserted—

> > - “animal” means all vertebrate and cold-blooded invertebrate animals;

- (b) after the definition of “Council Regulation 1255/97” there shall be inserted—

> > - “inspector” means a person appointed to be an inspector for the purposes of these Regulations by the Department;

###### 5

In regulation 3 (Extension of the definition of “animals”) of the principal Regulations after the words “Part I” there shall be inserted the words “and Part II”.

###### 6

Regulation 24 (Powers of inspectors) of the principal Regulations shall be amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (3)—

- (i) the words “An inspector” shall be omitted; and

- (ii) before the word “may”, there shall be inserted the words—

> > A notice under paragraph (2)

- (b) in paragraph (7)—

- (i) the words “an inspector” shall be omitted; and

- (ii) after the words “In particular,” there shall be inserted the words—

> > a notice under paragraph (6)

- (c) after paragraph (8) there shall be inserted —

> > (9) A person who fails to comply with a notice served under paragraph (2) or (6) is guilty of an offence.

###### 7

In regulation 27 (Production of plans) of the principal Regulations, after paragraph (2) there shall be inserted—

> > (3) Subject to paragraph (2), a person who fails to comply with paragraph (1) is guilty of an offence.

###### 8

Regulation 29 (Offences) of the principal Regulations shall be omitted.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 19th January 2007

E. Redmond — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Welfare of Animals (Transport) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 (the principal Regulations).

Regulation 4 amends regulation 2(1) of the principal Regulations by inserting a definition of “animal” and “inspector”.

Regulation 5 amends regulation 3 of the principal Regulations, by inserting a reference to Part II of Schedule 1 to the Diseases of Animals (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

Regulation 6 amends paragraphs (3) and (7) of regulation 24 of the principal Regulations, it also inserts a new paragraph (9), the effect of which is to specify that a person who fails to comply with a notice served under regulation 24(2) or, (6) is guilty of an offence.

Regulation 7 amends regulation 27 of the principal Regulations by inserting a new paragraph (3), the effect of which is to specify that failure to comply with regulation 27(1) constitutes an offence.

Regulation 8 omits regulation 29 of the principal Regulations.
