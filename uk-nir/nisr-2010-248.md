# The Control of Salmonella in Turkey Flocks Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2010

Made: 26th July 2010

Coming into operation: 19th August 2010

The Department considers it expedient that references to the Community instruments implemented by this Order should have effect as references to those instruments as amended from time to time.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Control of Salmonella in Turkey Flocks Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2010 and comes into operation on 19th August 2010.

#### Control of salmonella in turkey flocks scheme

###### 2

For the purposes of keeping turkey flocks, so far as is practicable, free from Salmonella and controlling and, so far as practicable, reducing the prevalence of Salmonella, the Department makes the Scheme set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE — CONTROL OF SALMONELLA IN TURKEY FLOCKS SCHEME (NORTHERN IRELAND) 2010

#### Citation

###### 1

This Scheme may be cited as The Control of Salmonella in Turkey Flocks Scheme (Northern Ireland) 2010 and comes into operation on 19th August 2010.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In this Scheme—

- “approved laboratory” means a laboratory approved by the Department for the purposes of this Order;

- “the Department” means the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development;

- “flock” means all turkeys of the same health status kept on the same premises or in the same enclosure and constituting a single epidemiological unit and, in the case of housed turkeys, includes all birds sharing the same airspace;

- “holding” means any holding on which one or more turkey flocks are kept or are intended to be kept;

- “identity of the flock” means—the date when that flock was placed on the holding; andthe epidemiological unit or house relating to that flock;

- “keeper” means any natural or legal person responsible for or in charge of turkeys in terms of contract or by law whether on a permanent or temporary basis;

- “turkeys” means birds of the species *Meleagris gallopavo;*

- “turkey flock” means—a flock of more than 250 turkeys kept for breeding;a flock of more than 500 turkeys kept for the production of meat intended for human consumption unless—the flock has less than 10,000 individual turkeys kept in any 12 month period at the holding where the flock is found, andall the meat is supplied to the final consumer or to local retail establishments.

- (2) In this Scheme, a reference to—

- (a) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1177/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1177) implementing Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards requirements for the use of specific control methods in the framework of the national programmes for the control of salmonella in poultry; and

- (b) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1190/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1190) concerning a Union target for the reduction of *Salmonella Enteritidis* and *Salmonella Typhimurium* in flocks of turkeys, as provided for in Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160),

is, in each case, a reference to those instruments as amended from time to time.

#### Competent authority

###### 3

The Department is the competent authority for the purposes of—

- (a) [Commission Regulation (EC) No 1177/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1177); and

- (b) [Commission Regulation (EU) No 1190/2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2012/1190).

#### Notification of turkey flocks

###### 4

- (1) Subject to paragraph 3 the keeper of one or more turkey flocks must notify the Department of the information in paragraph (4)—

- (a) within three months of the coming into operation of this Order; or

- (b) in the case of a holding on which one or more turkey flocks are kept which is established after the date this Order comes into operation, within three months of the establishment of the holding.

- (2) The keeper must notify the Department of any change or addition to that information within three months of the change or addition.

- (3) This article does not apply to any keeper who has notified the Department of that information under any other statutory provision.

- (4) The information to be notified is—

- (a) the name, address and telephone number of the holding;

- (b) the name, address and telephone number of the keeper and (if different) of the person who owns each flock on the holding;

- (c) the number of flocks on the holding;

- (d) the identity of the flock;

- (e) the number of turkeys in each flock;

- (f) the expected date of arrival at the holding of every new flock intended to be moved onto the holding at least two weeks before the expected date of arrival.

#### Sampling

###### 5

- (1) The keeper must carry out sampling in accordance with point 1 and 2 of the Annex to Regulation 1190/2012 and must dispatch samples to an approved laboratory in accordance with point 2.2.4 of that Annex (and for the purposes of that point “express mail” includes first class post).

#### Records of samples

###### 6

- (1) The keeper must, as soon as is reasonably practicable after taking a sample, record—

- (a) the type of sample taken;

- (b) the date on which the sample was taken;

- (c) the identity of the flock from which the sample was taken;

- (d) the age of the flock sampled;

- (e) the approved laboratory to which the sample was sent;

- (f) the intended date of slaughter of the flock sampled.

- (2) The keeper must record the result of each test when it is received from the approved laboratory.

#### Records of movements

###### 7

- (1) When turkeys are moved on to a holding the keeper must record for each flock—

- (a) the number of turkeys moved;

- (b) the age of the turkeys moved;

- (c) the identity of the flock.

- (2) When turkeys are moved off a holding the keeper must record for each flock—

- (a) the date of the movement;

- (b) the number of turkeys moved;

- (c) the age of the turkeys moved;

- (d) the identity of the flock;

- (e) the address of the holding or the slaughterhouse to which they were sent.

#### Duties of the person in charge of an approved laboratory

###### 8

Where an approved laboratory tests samples, the person in charge of the laboratory must ensure that they are tested in accordance with point 3 of the Annex to Commission Regulation (EU) 1190/2012 .

#### Prohibition on the use of antimicrobials

###### 9

A person may not administer any antimicrobial to any turkey as a specific method to control salmonella in breach of Article 2 of Commission Regulation [(EC) 1177/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1177) (use of antimicrobials).

#### Prohibition on the use of vaccines

###### 10

A person may not administer any live salmonella vaccine to any turkey in breach of Article 3(1) of Commission Regulation [(EC) 1177/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1177) (use of vaccines).

#### Records

###### 11

Any person required to keep a record under this Scheme must keep it for two years from the date it is made and must produce it on demand to an inspector or officer of the Department and allow a copy of it to be made or an extract from it to be taken.

#### Tampering with samples

###### 12

A person must not tamper with a sample or do anything to it that is likely to affect the result of any test required to be carried out under this Scheme.

#### Powers of the Department in cases of default

###### 13

If any person fails to take any action required by this Scheme, an inspector may arrange for such action to be taken at the expense of the person in default.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order enforces Commission Regulation (EC) No.584/2008 and Commission Regulation (EC) No.1177/2006.

It makes provision for the registration of turkey flocks of birds of the species *Meleagris gallopavo*, and imposes record-keeping requirements.

It makes provision for sampling turkey flocks of birds of the species *Meleagris gallopavo*.

It prohibits the use of antimicrobials and live Salmonella vaccine for the control of Salmonella.

Breach of the Order is an offence under Article 52 of the Disease of Animals Order 1981.
