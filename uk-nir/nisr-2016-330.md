# The Pensions (2005 Order) (Code of Practice) (Governance and Administration of Occupational Trust-based Schemes Providing Money Purchase Benefits) (Appointed Day) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016

Made: 15th September 2016

The Department for Communities[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 86(7) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005[^f00002].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pensions (2005 Order) (Code of Practice) (Governance and Administration of Occupational Trust-based Schemes Providing Money Purchase Benefits) (Appointed Day) Order (Northern Ireland) 2016.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the purposes of Article 86(7) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (procedure for issue and publication of codes of practice) for the coming into operation of the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 13: Governance and administration of occupational trust-based schemes providing money purchase benefits: Second issue is 21st September 2016.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 15th September 2016

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints the day for the purposes of Article 86(7) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 for the coming into operation of the Pensions Regulator Code of practice no. 13: Governance and administration of occupational trust-based schemes providing money purchase benefits: Second issue as 21st September 2016.

The Code of Practice is available on the Pensions Regulator’s website: http://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/doc-library/codes.aspx.
