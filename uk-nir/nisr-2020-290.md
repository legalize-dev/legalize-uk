# The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 18) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: at 7.30 p.m. on 27th November 2020

Laid before the Assembly: at 9.00 a.m. on 30th November 2020

Coming into operation in accordance with regulation 1(2)

In accordance with section 25Q of that Act the Department of Health is of the opinion that, by reason of urgency, it is necessary to make these Regulations without a draft having been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Assembly.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) (Amendment No. 18) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation at the time and date they are made.

#### Amendment of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020

###### 2

- (1) The Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020[^f00003] is amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In paragraph 9A(4) for “4 persons” substitute “eight persons”.

- (3) In paragraph 1(1)(f) of schedule 2, after “sports and massage therapy” insert “(but not sports massage therapy)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health at 7.30pm on 27th November 2020

Dr Lourda Geoghegan — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health Protection (Coronavirus, Restrictions) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.

The amendment in regulation 2(2) of these Regulations increases the number of persons who can attend a place of worship for the purpose of recording or live streaming an act of worship to no more than 8 persons.

The amendment in regulation 2(3) removes sports massage therapy from the list of close contact services.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations.
