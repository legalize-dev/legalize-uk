# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

Made: 24th April 2008

Coming into operation-: 26th April 2008

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00004] there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of the following Regulations.

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 and shall come into operation on 26th April 2008.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00005] shall apply to these regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006

###### 2

- (1) The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00006] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (5).

- (2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) —

- (a) immediately before the definition of “bovine animal” there is inserted—

> > “*the amended Community TSE Regulation*” means the Community TSE Regulation as amended by [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 722/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0722) amending Annexes II, V, VI, VIII, IX and XI to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies and [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 357/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0357) amending Annex V to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies

- (b) in the definition of “cutting plant”, for “Schedule 6, paragraph 10(2)(c)” there is substituted “paragraph 9(2)(b)(iii) of Schedule 6”; and

- (c) immediately before the definition of “TSE” there is inserted—

> > “*specified risk material*” (save as specified in paragraph 20(6) of Schedule 6) has the meaning given to it in Article 3.1(g) of the amended Community TSE Regulation;

- (3) In regulation 5(e) (the schedules), for “recovered” there is substituted “separated”.

- (4) For Schedule 6 (specified risk material, mechanically separated meat and slaughtering techniques) there is substituted the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (5) In Schedule 7 (restrictions on dispatch to member States and to third countries) for paragraph 2 (exports to third countries) there is substituted—

> > (2) A person is guilty of an offence if he fails to comply with point 10.3 of Annex V to the amended Community TSE Regulation

#### Revocations

###### 3

The Beef Bones Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997[^f00007] and the Beef Bones (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999[^f00008] are revoked.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1 These Regulations amend the Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 ([S.R. 2006 No.202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202), as amended by [S.R. 2007. No 339](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/339)), which provide for the enforcement of Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies (OJ No. L147, 31.5.2001, p.1) as amended by and as read with the Community instruments set out in Schedule1 to [S.R. 2006 No.202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202).

2 The principal amendment is that a revised Schedule 6 is inserted into [S.R. 2006 No.202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202) (regulation 2(4)).

3 Schedule 6 to [S.R. 2006 No.202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202) currently provides for the enforcement of the revised text of AnnexV to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) that was inserted into that Regulation by [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 722/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0722) (OJ No. L164, 26.6.2007, p.7).

4 [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 357/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0357) (OJ No. L111, 23.4.2008, p.3) has amended that revised text by substituting for the provision designating vertebral column of bovine animals aged over 24months at slaughter as specified risk material a provision designating vertebral column of bovine animals aged over 30 months at slaughter as specified risk material.

5 The revised Schedule 6 inserted into [S.R. 2006 No.202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202) by these Regulations provides for the enforcement of Annex V to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) as now amended by [Commission Regulation (EC) No. 357/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0357). Paragraph 20 of Schedule 6 provides for transitional arrangements in relation to the consignment of meat derived from bovine animals slaughtered before the coming into operation of these Regulations and aged 30 months or less at slaughter to butcher shops and cutting plants.

6 These Regulations also— a make consequential amendment to paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) of [S.R. 2006 No.202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202) in the light of the amendment to the revised text of Annex V to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) referred to above and the revised Schedule 6 to [S.R. 2006 No. 202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202) (regulation 2(2)); b update the wording of regulation 5(e) of [S.R. 2006 No. 202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202) to reflect the title of the revised Schedule 6 (regulation 2(3)); c update the wording of paragraph 2 of Schedule 7 to [S.R. 2006 No.202](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2006/202) to provide for the enforcement of point 10.3 of Annex V to Regulation [(EC) No. 999/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2001/0999) (prohibition on export outside the Community of heads and fresh meat of bovines, ovines and caprines containing specified risk material) (regulation 2(5)); and d revoke the Beef Bones Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1997 ([S.R. 1997 No.540](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1997/540)) and the Beef Bones (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No.495](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/495)) (regulation 3).
