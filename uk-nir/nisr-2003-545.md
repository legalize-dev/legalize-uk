# The Insolvency (2002 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 22nd December 2003

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(3) of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order –

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Insolvency (2002 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

Subject to Articles 2 to 7 of the Insolvency (2002 Order) (Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00002], the day appointed for the coming into operation of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2002, in so far as it is not already in operation, is 2nd February 2004.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 22nd December 2003.

Michael J. Bohill — A senior officer of the — Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order brings the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 fully into operation on 2nd February 2004.

Transitional provisions in connection with the coming into operation of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 are contained in the Insolvency (2002 Order) (Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003, [S.R. 2003 No. 546](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2003/546).
