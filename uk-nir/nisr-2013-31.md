# The Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013

Made: 11th February 2013

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 39(1) of the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00001].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Forestry (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2013.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 2010.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

17th March 2013 is the day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Act listed in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

Section 31

the entries relating to Schedules 1 and 2 to the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 1977[^f01000]

the entries relating to the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 1953[^f01001] but only in so far as they relate to the repeal of section 14(1), (1A), (2), (3) and (4)

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 11th

February 2013.

Malcolm Beatty — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Forestry Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 listed in the Schedule to this Order, on 17th March 2013.
