# The Historical Institutional Abuse (Northern Ireland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 26th March 2020

The Executive Office, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33(2) of the Historical Institutional Abuse (Northern Ireland) Act 2019[^f00001], makes the following Order.

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Historical Institutional Abuse (Northern Ireland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means the Historical Institutional Abuse (Northern Ireland) Act 2019.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the following provision of the Act is the 27th March 2020–

- (a) Part 1 (the Historical Institutional Abuse Redress Board); and

- (b) Schedule 1 (the Historical Institutional Abuse Redress Board).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Executive Office on 26th March 2020.

Gareth Johnston — A senior officer of — The Executive Office

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into force specified provisions of the Historical Institutional Abuse (Northern Ireland) Act 2019.

Part 1 of the Act makes provision for the establishment of the Historical Institutional Abuse Redress Board; the entitlement to compensation; the determination of compensation; appeals and miscellaneous provisions.

Schedule 1 to the Act makes further provisions in relation to the administration of the Redress Board.
