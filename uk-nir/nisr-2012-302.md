# The Labour Relations Agency Arbitration Scheme (Jurisdiction) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 26th July 2012

Coming into operation: 27th September 2012

The Department for Employment and Learning , in exercise of the power conferred by Article 84A(1)(b) of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 , and now vested in it , makes the following Order.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Labour Relations Agency Arbitration Scheme (Jurisdiction) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012 and shall come into operation on 27th September 2012.

#### Statutory provisions in respect of which an Agency arbitration scheme may apply

###### 2

The Schedule specifies the statutory provisions in respect of which, under Article 84A(1)(b) of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992, the Agency may prepare a scheme providing for arbitration in the case of disputes involving proceedings, or claims which could be the subject of proceedings, before an industrial tribunal.

## SCHEDULE — Statutory provisions in respect of which an Agency arbitration scheme may apply

- Section 2(1) of the Equal Pay Act (Northern Ireland) 1970[^f00004];

- Article 63 of the Sex Discrimination (Northern Ireland) Order 1976[^f00005];

- Article 33, 36, 38 or 61 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00006];

- section 17A of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00007];

- any of the following provisions of the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00008] – Article 28 or 30 (access to employment); Article 43 (itemised pay statement); Article 55 (protection of wages); Article 66 (guarantee payments); Article 71 or 74 (protection from detriment, etc. in employment); Article 79, 82, 85, 85B, 88, 91, 91C or 95 (time off work); Article 102 (suspension from work); Article 112(1) (parental leave); Article 125 (written statement of reasons for dismissal); Article 198 or 205 (redundancy payment); Article 220 or 233 (handling of redundancies and protective awards);

- Article 6 of the Industrial Tribunals Extension of Jurisdiction Order (Northern Ireland) 1994[^f00009];

- Article 52 of the Race Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00010];

- section 11, 20(1)(b) or 24 of the National Minimum Wage Act 1998[^f00011];

- regulation 30 of the Working Time Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1998[^f00012];

- regulation 27 or 32 of the Transnational Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations 1999[^f00013];

- regulation 8 of the Part-time Workers (Prevention of Less Favourable Treatment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2000[^f00014];

- regulation 7 or 9(5) of the Fixed-term Employees (Prevention of Less Favourable Treatment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2002[^f00015];

- regulations 4(a) and 18 of the Merchant Shipping (Working Time: Inland Waterways) Regulations 2003[^f00016];

- regulation 34 of the Employment Equality (Sexual Orientation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00017];

- regulations 4(a) and 19 of the Fishing Vessels (Working Time: Sea-fishermen) Regulations 2004[^f00018];

- regulation 41 or 45 of the European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00019];

- regulation 29 or 33 of the Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00020];

- regulation 12 or 16 of and paragraphs 10(3) or (4) and 11 of Schedule 1 to the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006[^f00021];

- paragraph 4 or 8 of the Schedule to the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Consultation by Employers) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00022];

- regulation 12 or 16 of the Service Provision Change (Protection of Employment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00023];

- regulation 41 of the Employment Equality (Age) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006;[^f00024]

- regulation 30 or 34 of and paragraphs 9 and 13 of Schedule 3 to the European Cooperative Society (Involvement of Employees) Regulations 2006;[^f00025]

- regulation 45 or 51 of and paragraphs 6 and 10 of Schedule 2 to the Companies (Cross-Border Mergers) Regulations 2007[^f00026];

- regulation 17 of the Cross-border Railway Services (Working Time) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00027];

- section 56 of the Pensions (No. 2) Act (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00028];

- regulation 28 or 32 of the European Public Limited-Liability Company (Employee Involvement) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2009[^f00029];

- regulation 18 of the Agency Workers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00030].

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Employment and Learning on 26th July 2012.

Dr Stephen Farry — Minister for Employment and Learning
