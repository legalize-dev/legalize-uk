# The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) (Amendment No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023

Made

Coming into operation

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs makes these Regulations after consultation in accordance with paragraph 8 of Schedule 4 to the Act and is satisfied as to the matters specified in paragraph 9(1) of that Schedule.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) (Amendment No.2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 and come into operation on 1st February 2024.

#### Amendment of the Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

- (1) The Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] are amended as follows.

- (2) In Schedule 2 (recycling obligations)––

- (a) in paragraph 3(4), in the formula, for “72” substitute “75”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on the 18th December 2023.

Shane Doris — A senior officer of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of these Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging Waste) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.I. 2007 No. 198](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/198)) (the “*2007 Regulations*”). Those Regulations impose on producers obligations to recycle packaging waste in order to attain overall recycling and material-specific recycling targets.

These Regulations update the formula used in the calculation of the amount of glass packaging waste to be recycled by re-melt.
