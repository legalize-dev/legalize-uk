# The Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (Offensive Weapons) (Exemption) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997

Made: 2nd June 1997

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 25th July 1997

The Secretary of State, in pursuance of Article 54(3)(*c*) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:—

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (Offensive Weapons) (Exemption) Order (Northern Ireland) 1997 and shall come into operation on 25th July 1997.

#### Exemptions

###### 2

Article 54(1) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996 (sale of knives or certain other articles with blade or a point to persons under sixteen) shall not apply to—

- (a) a folding pocket-knife if the cutting edge of its blade does not exceed 7.62 centimetres (3 inches), or

- (b) razor blades permanently enclosed in a cartridge or housing where less than 2 millimetres of any blade is exposed beyond the plane which intersects the highest point of the surfaces preceding and following such blades.

## Signed

Marjorie Mowlam — One of Her Majesty’s Principal Secretaries of State — 2nd June 1997

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

By virtue of Article 54(1) of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1996, it is an offence to sell a knife or certain other articles to persons under sixteen. This Order, when it comes into operation on 25th July 1997, will exempt from the offence the sale of folding pocket-knives of less than 3 inches and disposable razor blades.
