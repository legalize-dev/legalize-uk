# The Social Security (Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance) (Waiting Days) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made: 18th September 2014

Coming into operation: 27th October 2014

The Department for Social Development makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 36(2)(a) of, and paragraphs 4 and 10(1) of Schedule 1 to, the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00001], and now vested in it[^f00002], and section 25(2)(a) of, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 to, the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Jobseeker’s Allowance and Employment and Support Allowance) (Waiting Days) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 and shall come into operation on 27th October 2014.

#### Increase in waiting days

###### 2

- (1) In regulation 46(2) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996[^f00004] (waiting days) for “3” substitute “7”.

- (2) In regulation 144(1) and (2)(d) of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00005] for “3” substitute “7”.

#### Consequential amendments

###### 3

- (1) The Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In regulation 141(2)[^f00006] (circumstances in which an income-based jobseeker’s allowance is payable to a person in hardship) for “4th” substitute “8th”.

- (3) In regulation 146C(2)(a)[^f00007] (circumstances in which a joint-claim jobseeker’s allowance is payable where a joint-claim couple is a couple in hardship) for “4th” substitute “8th”.

#### Transitional provision

###### 4

- (1) Regulations 2(1) and 3 do not apply where the jobseeking period[^f00008] for the purposes of paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (supplementary provisions: waiting days) to the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 began before 27th October 2014.

- (2) Regulation 2(2) does not apply where the period of limited capability for work for the purposes of paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 (supplementary provisions: waiting days) to the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 began before 27th October 2014.

- (3) The amendment to regulation 144(2)(d) of the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 made by regulation 2(2) does not apply where the beginning of the period of limited capability for work which relates to the former claimant’s entitlement began before 27th October 2014.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations increase from 3 to 7 the number of waiting days which apply before a person may be entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or an employment and support allowance.

Regulation 2 achieves this by amending the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and the Employment and Support Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008.

Regulation 3 makes consequential amendments to the hardship provisions of the Jobseeker’s Allowance Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996.

Regulation 4 contains transitional provisions. These ensure that the amendments made by these Regulations do not apply where the waiting period in question began before 27th October 2014.

These Regulations make in relation to Northern Ireland only provision corresponding to provision contained in Regulations made by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions in relation to Great Britain and accordingly, by virtue of section 149(3) of, and paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to, the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act [1992 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/8), are not subject to the requirement of section 149(2) of that Act for prior reference to the Social Security Advisory Committee.
