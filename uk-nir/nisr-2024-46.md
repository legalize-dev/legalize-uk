# The Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Specified Diseases and Prescribed Occupations) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2024

Made: 8th March 2024

Coming into operation in accordance with Article 1

The Department for Communities makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(1) of, and paragraph 8(1) of the Schedule to, the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1979[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Specified Diseases and Prescribed Occupations) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2024 and shall come into operation on the day after it is affirmed by resolution of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Specified Diseases) Order

###### 2

For Article 2 of the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Specified Diseases) Order (Northern Ireland) 1986[^f00003] substitute—

> > (2) The following diseases are specified for the purposes of the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1979—
>   (a) asbestos-related primary carcinoma of the lung;
>   (b) unilateral or bilateral diffuse pleural thickening.

#### Amendment of the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Prescribed Occupations) Order

###### 3

In the table in Part 2 of the Schedule to the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Prescribed Occupations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00004] (occupations prescribed in relation to the other diseases to which the Order applies)—

- (a) in the left hand column (disease), in the entry relating to primary carcinoma of the lung, for “one or both of the following” until the end, substitute “asbestosis”;

- (b) after the entry relating to primary carcinoma of the lung, insert—

| Primary carcinoma of the lung (except where there is evidence of asbestosis) | Any occupation involving exposure to asbestos in the course of— |
| --- | --- |
| Primary carcinoma of the lung (except where there is evidence of asbestosis) | (a) the manufacture of asbestos textiles; |
| Primary carcinoma of the lung (except where there is evidence of asbestosis) | (b) spraying asbestos; |
| Primary carcinoma of the lung (except where there is evidence of asbestosis) | (c) asbestos insulation work; or |
| Primary carcinoma of the lung (except where there is evidence of asbestosis) | (d) applying or removing materials containing asbestos in the course of shipbuilding. |

- (c) in the left hand column, in the entry relating to bilateral diffuse pleural thickening, insert at the beginning “Unilateral or”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Communities on 8th March 2024

David Tarr — A senior officer of the Department for Communities

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Under the Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 ([S.I. 1979/925 (N.I. 9))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1979/925) (“*the 1979 Order*”) lump sum payments may be made to certain persons disabled by a disease to which the 1979 Order applies, or to dependants of persons who were so disabled before they died. The diseases covered by the 1979 Order are pneumoconiosis, byssinosis, diffuse mesothelioma, and such other diseases as are specified by the Department. The Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Specified Diseases) Order (Northern Ireland) 1986 ([S.R. 1986 No. 168](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1986/168)) specifies additional diseases to which the 1979 Order applies.

The Pneumoconiosis, etc., (Workers’ Compensation) (Prescribed Occupations) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 ([S.R. 2007 No. 337](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/337)) lists the occupations prescribed for the purpose of each disease to which the 1979 Order applies. It is a condition of entitlement under the 1979 Order that every relevant employer has ceased business. The occupations prescribed in [S.R. 2007 No. 337](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/337) are relevant in determining whether there is a relevant employer.

Article 2 amends the diseases specified in [S.R. 1986 No. 168](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1986/168) as follows—

1. Primary carcinoma of the lung where there is accompanying evidence of asbestosis and/or bilateral diffuse pleural thickening has been replaced with asbestos-related primary carcinoma of the lung.

2. Bilateral diffuse pleural thickening has been replaced with unilateral or bilateral diffuse pleural thickening.

Article 3 makes amendments to the occupations prescribed in [S.R. 2007 No. 337](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2007/337) which are relevant to determine whether there is a relevant employer.
