# The Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011

Made: 5th December 2011

The Department of Finance and Personnel, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(1) of the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00001], makes the following Order:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) (2011 Act) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 is 14th December 2011.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 5th December 2011

O G Paulin — A senior officer of the Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the Damages (Asbestos-related Conditions) Act (Northern Ireland) 2011 to come into operation on 14th December 2011.
