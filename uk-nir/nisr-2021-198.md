# The Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: 1st July 2021

Laid before the Assembly on: 1st July 2021

Coming into operation: 23rd July 2021

In accordance with Article 23(8) of that Order it has consulted with such persons as it considers appropriate.

#### Citation and Commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) (Revocation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on 23rd July 2021.

#### Revocation

###### 2

The Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020 [^f00002] are revoked.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Health on 1st July 2021

Eilis McDaniel — A senior officer of the Department of Health

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These regulations revoke the Establishment and Agencies (Fitness of Workers) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2020.
