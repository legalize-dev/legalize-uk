# The Waste Management Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017

Made: 29th September 2017

Coming into operation: 1st November 2017

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 3(3)(a) of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001], makes the following Regulations.

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Waste Management Licensing (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2017 and shall come into operation on 1st November 2017.

#### Amendment to the Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend regulation 2 of the Waste Management Licensing Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2003 (the 2003 Regulations) to specify and update the list of offences contained in Schedule 6 to the Regulations and to remove outdated offences.
