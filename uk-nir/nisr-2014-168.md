# The New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Amendment) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014

Made

Coming into operation-

The Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 10(1), (3), (4) and (5) of the Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984 [^f00001], and now vested in it[^f00002] and with the approval of the Department of Finance and Personnel in accordance with article 10(1) of that Order makes the following Order:

#### Citation, commencement and effect

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Amendment) (No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2014.

- (2) Subject to paragraphs (3) to (5) this Order shall come into operation on 1st July 2014.

- (3) Paragraph 1 of the Schedule (which relates to article 4 (scheme membership) of Part 2) shall have effect from 1st October 2013.

- (4) Sub-paragraphs (ii), (iii) and (iv) of paragraph 2(b) of the Schedule (which relates to article 17A (additional pension benefit: long service) of Part 3 (personal awards)) shall have effect from 11th April 2011.

- (5) Paragraph 3 of the Schedule (which substitutes for article 17B (additional pension benefit: continual professional development) in Part 3, article 17B (additional pension benefit)) shall have effect in relation to paragraph (3) and the definitions of “beginning date” and “following relevant tax year” in paragraph (6) of article 17B from 11th April 2011.

#### Amendment of the New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

The New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] shall be amended in accordance with the Schedule.

#### Transitional provisions: transfer into Firefighters’ Pension Fund

###### 3

- (1) The amendment made by article 2 and paragraph 6 of the Schedule to article 86 (payments and transfers into Firefighters’ Pension Fund) of Part 13 (Firefighters’ Pension Fund) shall not have effect in relation to a person who had entered into a contract of employment with the Board before the date on which this Order came into operation.

- (2) In a case to which paragraph (1) applies, article 86 of Part 13 of the New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Northern Ireland), in the form in which it existed immediately before the coming into operation of this Order, shall continue to have effect in relation to such a person.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order amends the New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2007. Some of the amendments introduce new provisions to the New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Order (Northern Ireland) 2007 (“the Scheme”) set out in that Order. Others make minor corrections to the Scheme.

Except as mentioned below the Order has effect from 1st July 2014. Power to give the Order retrospective effect is conferred by article 10 of the Fire Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1984.

The amendments made by paragraphs 1, 2(a), 4 and 5 of the Schedule are to make minor changes to the Scheme. The amendment made by paragraph 1 is to ensure that those who took up employment as a firefighter before 6th April 2006 and were either not eligible to be a member of the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme 2007 or made an election not to pay pension contributions required by that scheme, are treated as a firefighter member of the Scheme when automatically enrolled into that Scheme. This amendment has retrospective effect from 1st October 2013.

The amendment made by paragraph 2(b)(i) is to make a correction so that it is clear which pensionable service can count for the additional pension benefit: long service increment in article 17A of Part 3.

Paragraph 3 of the Schedule inserts a new article 17B which extends additional pension benefit so that it covers—

- payments to reward additional skills and responsibilities outside the requirements of the firefighter member’s duties under the contract of employment but which are within the wider functions of the job;

- any additional pay received whilst on temporary promotion or whilst temporarily carrying out the duties of a higher role;

- any non-consolidated performance related payment.

Any payments in respect of a firefighter member’s continuing professional development continue to be covered by additional pension benefit.

The amendments made by sub-paragraphs (ii), (iii) and (iv) of paragraph 2(b) and paragraph 3 in relation to paragraphs (3) and (4) of the new article 17B amend the method of uprating additional pension benefit for the long service increment (article 17A of Part 3) and continuing professional development (article 17B) from a specific index, retail price index, to an index in accordance with the Pension (Increase) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971. There is a specific provision to provide that the consumer price index is used for the tax year 2010/2011. These amendments have effect from 11th April 2011.

Paragraph 5(a) of the Schedule amends the definition of pensionable pay to include payments which are pensionable under additional pension benefit (article 17B) and provide that payments which are not within the definition of pensionable pay in article 61(1)(a) as amended, or additional pension benefits payable for long service or in respect of a firefighter’s continual professional development, should remain pensionable whilst the firefighter continues to receive them. Paragraph 5(b) amends the payments treated as final pensionable pay to exclude additional pension benefit payments payable within article 17B of Part 3.

The amendments made by paragraph 6 of the Schedule require the Board to transfer into the Firefighters’ Pension Fund an amount equal to the amount of pension paid to a person in respect of whom the authority choose not to exercise its discretion to withdraw or abate the permitted part of that individual’s pension under article 52 (withdrawal of pension during service as a firefighter) of Part 9 (review, withdrawal and forfeiture of awards).

The amendment made to Schedule 2 (appeal to independent medical referee) by paragraph 7 of the schedule enable—

- the independent medical referee to reconsider its decision where the parties agree that it has made a material error of fact;

- the Board to recover some or all of the expenses of the independent medical referee where the appellant has withdrawn the appeal or requested the date for an interview or examination to be cancelled or postponed less than 21 working days before the date appointed.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this Order as no impact on the private or voluntary sector is foreseen.
