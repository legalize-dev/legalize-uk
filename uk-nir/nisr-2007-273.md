# Phosphorous (Use in Agriculture) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 3rd May 2007

Coming into operation: 1st June 2007

The Department of the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Articles 32, 44 and 74 of the Waste and Contaminated Land (Northern Ireland) Order 1997[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Phosphorous (Use in Agriculture) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on 1st June 2007.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

- (1) In these Regulations—

- “the Principal Regulations” means the Phosphorous (Use in Agriculture) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006[^f00002].

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Principal Regulations

###### 3

- (1) The Principal Regulations shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) to (3).

- (2) In regulation 8(1) after “regulations 2(1)” replace “3, 4 or 5” with “3 or 4”.

- (3) In regulation 9 after “A controller” delete the word “found”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of the Environment on 3rd May 2007

Maggie Smith — A senior officer of the Department of the Environment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Phosphorous (Use in Agriculture) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2006 (“the Principal Regulations”). They reflect technical amendments to the Principal Regulations.
