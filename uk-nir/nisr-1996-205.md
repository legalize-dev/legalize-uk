# Legal Advice and Assistance (Prospective Cost) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 17th May 1996

To be laid before Parliament

Coming into operation: 10th June 1996

The Lord Chancellor, in exercise of the powers conferred by Articles 6(2), 22 and 27 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00001] and now vested in him[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:—

#### Citation, commencement and revocation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Legal Advice and Assistance (Prospective Cost) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 10th June 1996.

- (2) The Legal Advice and Assistance (Prospective Cost) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

#### Financial limit on prospective cost of advice and assistance

###### 2

For the limit of £86·50 specified in Article 6(2) of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981[^f00004] there shall be substituted the limit of £88·00.

## Signed

Mackay of Clashfern, *C.* — Dated 17th May 1996

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations revoke and replace the Legal Advice and Assistance (Prospective Cost) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1992 to increase from £86·50 to £88·00 the limit on the cost of legal advice and assistance provided under Articles 3 and 4 of the Legal Aid, Advice and Assistance (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 which a solicitor may incur without obtaining the approval of the appropriate authority.
