# The Health and Social Care Pensions (Abatement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022

Made: 23rd March 2022

Coming into operation: 25th March 2022

In accordance with Article 12(1) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 and section 3(5) of the Public Service Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2014, these Regulations are made with the consent of the Department of Finance.

## PART 1 — Introductory

#### Citation, commencement and effect

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health and Social Care Pensions (Abatement) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2022.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 25 March 2022 and have effect from 25 March 2022

## PART 2 — Amendment of the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995

#### General

###### 2

The Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 are amended as provided by regulation 3.

#### Amendment to regulations 84 and 85

###### 3

These Regulations have effect as if, for the period 25 March 2022 to 31 October 2022, (both dates inclusive)–

- (a) regulation 84 (suspension of pension on return to HPSS employment) were omitted, and

- (b) in the opening words of regulation 85(3) (reduction of pension on return to HPSS employment) the words “or (c)” were omitted.

## PART 3 — Amendments to the Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008

#### General

###### 4

The Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008[^f00003] are amended as provided by regulation 5.

#### Amendment to regulations 50 and 181

###### 5

These Regulations have effect as if for the period 25 March 2022 to 31 October 2022 ( both dates inclusive) –

- (a) in regulation 50 (abatement of pension following increase in pensionable pay) paragraph (2) (a) were omitted; and

- (b) in regulation 181 (abatement of pension following increase in engagement in employment) paragraph (2) (a) were omitted.

## PART 4 — Amendments to the Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

#### General

###### 6

The Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015[^f00004] are amended as provided by regulation 7.

#### Amendment to regulation 85

###### 7

These Regulations have effect for the period 25 March 2022 to 31 October 2022 (both dates inclusive) as if in regulation 85 (abatement of pension following continuation of employment) paragraph (3) were omitted.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1995 ([S.R. 1995 No.95](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1995/95)) (“*the 1995 Regulations*”), the Health and Social Care (Pension Scheme) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2008 ([S.R. 2008 No.256](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2008/256)) (“*the 2008 Regulations*”), the Health and Social Care Pension Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. 2015 No.120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/120)) (“*the 2015 Regulations*”), the Health and Personal Social Services (Superannuation) (Additional Voluntary Contributions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1999 ([S.R. 1999 No.294](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/1999/294)) (“*the AVC Regulations*”), The Health and Personal Social Services (Injury Benefits) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2001 ([S.R. 2001 No.367](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2001/367)) (“*the Injury Benefit Regulations*”) and the Health and Social Care Service Pension Scheme (Transitional and Consequential Provisions) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 ([S.R. 2015 No.120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2015/120)) (“*the Transitional and Consequential Regulations*”) .
