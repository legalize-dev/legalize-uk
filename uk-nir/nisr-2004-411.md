# Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 28th September 2004

Coming into operation: 10th November 2004

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development, being a Department designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the said section 2(2), and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation, commencement and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Protection Products (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 10th November 2004.

- (2) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00003] shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to an Act of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Amendment of the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004[^f00004] shall be amended in accordance with this regulation.

- (2) In regulation 27 (disapplication) paragraph (4) shall be deleted.

- (3) For the contents of Schedule 1 (instruments amending Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414)) there shall be substituted the contents of the Schedule to these Regulations.

## SCHEDULE

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 28th September 2004.

D. Small — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations amend the Plant Protection Products Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 ([S.R. 2004 No. 126](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2004/126)) (“the principal Regulations”) which implement in Northern Ireland Council Directive [91/414/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0414) (O.J. No. L230, 19.8.91, p. 1 to be read with the Corrigenda published in O.J. No. L170, 25.6.92, p. 40) concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market (“the Directive”), as amended.

Schedule 1 which sets out the instruments that amend that Directive is replaced by the Schedule to these Regulations, which lists an additional eight Directives that add a further 17 active substances to Annex I. In consequence the list of active substances which can be used in products capable of being approved under regulations 5 and 6 of the 2004 Regulations for placing on the market is extended accordingly.

In addition, a drafting correction is made to regulation 27.
