# The Agriculture (Student fees) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021

Made: 1st July 2021

Coming into operation-: 2nd August 2021

The Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5A(1) and (2) of the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949[^f00002], makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (Student fees) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2021 and shall come into operation on the 2nd August 2021.

#### Amendment to the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

The Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] shall be amended as provided by regulation 3.

#### Amendment to regulation 4

###### 3

In regulation 4, for paragraph (8)[^f00004] substitute—

> > (8) Subject to regulation 5, where a person has been granted limited leave to enter or remain (“pre-settled” status) or indefinite leave to enter or remain (“settled” status) under the EU Settlement Scheme contained in Appendix EU to the immigration rules[^f00005], the fee payable to the Department is set out in paragraph (1).

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs on 1st July 2021

Seamus McErlean — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Agriculture (Student fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007.

Regulation 3 replaces regulation 4(8) with an enhanced definition of the terms ‘EU Settlement Scheme’, ‘pre-settled’ status and ‘settled’ status. The EU Settlement Scheme referred is provided under Appendix EU to the immigration rules.
