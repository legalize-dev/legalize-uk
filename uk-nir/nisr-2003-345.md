# The Company Directors Disqualification (2002 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003

Made: 22nd July 2003

The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 1(2) of the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^f00001] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order: –

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Company Directors Disqualification (2002 Order) (Commencement) Order (Northern Ireland) 2003.

#### Interpretation

###### 2

In this Order “the 2002 Order” means the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002.

#### Appointed days

###### 3

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of Articles 1, 2 and 26(3) and (4) of the 2002 Order is 22nd July 2003.

- (2) Subject to Articles 3 to 6 of the Company Directors Disqualification (2002 Order) (Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland 2003[^f00002], the day appointed for the coming into operation of the 2002 Order, other than Articles 1, 2 and 26(3) and (4) thereof, is 5th September 2003.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment on 22nd July 2003.

Michael J. Bohill — A senior officer of the — Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002. Articles 1 (title and commencement), 2 (interpretation) and 26(3) and (4) (power to make supplementary, incidental or consequential provisions and transitory, transitional or saving provisions) are brought into operation on 22nd July 2003 and the remainder of the Order is brought into operation on 5th September 2003.
