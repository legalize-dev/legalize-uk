# The Plant Health (Phytophthora ramorum) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

Made: 19th May 2005

Coming into operation: 10th June 2005

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 2[^f00002], 3(1)[^f00003], 3B[^f00004], and 4(1)[^f00005] of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00006], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (*Phytophthora ramorum*) (Amendment) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005 and shall come into operation on 10th June 2005.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health (Phytophthora ramorum) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005

###### 2

- (1) The Plant Health (*Phytophthora ramorum*) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005[^f00007] shall be amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

- (2) In Article 13(2) for the words “Subject to paragraph (3) an” there shall be substituted the word “An”.

- (3) In Article 15(1)(a) for the words “Articles 3 or 5” there shall be substituted “Articles 3, 5, 6, 7(1) or (3)”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 19th May 2005.

David Small — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order removes drafting defects from the Plant Health (*Phytophthora ramorum*) Order (Northern Ireland) 2005.
