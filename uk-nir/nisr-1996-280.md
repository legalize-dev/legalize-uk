# The Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3 and Saving and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 10th July 1996

The Department of Health and Social Services, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 67(3) and 70(3) of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (Commencement No. 3 and Saving and Transitional Provisions) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996.

- (2) In this Order references to sections and Schedules are references to sections of and Schedules to the Disability Discrimination Act 1995.

- (3) The Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954[^f00002] shall apply to this Order as it applies to a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly.

#### Appointed days

###### 2

- (1) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions specified in Part I of the Schedule to this Order is 11th July 1996.

- (2) The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions specified in Part II of the Schedule to this Order, subject to the saving and transitional provisions set out in Article 3, is 2nd December 1996.

#### Saving and transitional provisions

###### 3

Notwithstanding the repeal of sections 9 to 11, 12, 14 and 19 of the Disabled Persons (Employment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1945[^f00003] by the bringing into operation by this Order of section 61[^f00004] and Schedule 7[^f00005]:

- (a) sections 9, 12 and 19 of the Disabled Persons (Employment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1945 shall continue to have effect so as to allow prosecutions to be brought until 3rd December 1997 against persons alleged to have contravened section 9(2), section 9(5) or section 12(2) of that Act prior to but excluding 2nd December 1996;

- (b) section 14 of the Disabled Persons (Employment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1945 and regulation 9 of the Disabled Persons (General) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 1946[^f00006] shall continue to have effect so as to allow prosecutions to be brought until 3rd December 1997 against persons alleged to have contravened section 9(2), section 9(5) or section 12(2) of that Act prior to but excluding 2nd December 1996 and, where it is alleged that contravention of section 14(6) or section 14(7) of that Act hinders such prosecution, so as to allow prosecutions to be brought against persons alleged to have contravened those subsections.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation on 11th July 1996 of provisions in the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (“the Act”) authorising the making of Regulations.

(In its application to Northern Ireland the Act has effect subject to the modifications contained in Schedule 8 thereof.)

This Order also provides for the coming into operation on 11th July 1996 of sections 53 and 54, which relate to Codes of Practice issued by the Department of Economic Development, and of section 56 of the Act, which relates to help for persons suffering discrimination.

It further provides for the coming into operation on 2nd December 1996 of provisions of the Act relating to employment, including discrimination against applicants and employees; meaning of “discrimination” for this purpose; duty of employer to make adjustments; exemption for small businesses; enforcement, remedies and procedure; validity of certain agreements; charities and support for particular groups of persons; advertisements suggesting that employers will discriminate against disabled persons; discrimination against contract workers; discrimination by trade organisations; meaning of “discrimination” in relation to trade organisations; alterations to premises occupied under leases; occupational pension schemes; and insurance services.

It further provides for the coming into operation on 2nd December 1996 of provisions of the Act relating to goods, facilities and services, including discrimination in relation to goods, facilities and services; meaning of discrimination for this purpose; discrimination in relation to premises; exemption for small dwellings; meaning of “discrimination” for these purposes; enforcement, remedies and procedure; and validity and revision of certain agreements.

It further provides for the coming into operation on 2nd December 1996 of supplemental and miscellaneous provisions of the Act including victimisation; aiding unlawful acts; liability of employers and principals; appointment by Department of Economic Development of advisers; amendment of Disabled Persons (Employment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1945; application to Crown etc.; Government appointments outside Part II; interpretation; short title, commencement, extent etc.; consequential amendments and repeals.
