# The Road Traffic (Amendment) (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020

Made: 5th November 2020

The Department for Infrastructure[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26(1) of the Road Traffic (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2016[^f00002] and vested in it[^f00003].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Amendment) (2016 Act) (Commencement No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2020.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Road Traffic (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2016, specified in the Schedule, shall be 9th November 2020.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order brings into operation on the 9th November 2020 the provisions of the Road Traffic (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 2016 specified in the Schedule.

Section 6 amends Article 19 (choice of specimens of breath) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00004] by removing the option (commonly called ‘the statutory option’) whereby a driver had a right to opt for a blood or urine specimen to replace a breath specimen indicating the proportion of alcohol in their breath if their breath test was marginally over the prescribed limit.
