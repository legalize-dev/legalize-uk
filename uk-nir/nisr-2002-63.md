# The Welfare Reform and Pensions (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 11) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002

Made: 25th February 2002

The Department for Social Development, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(2) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^f00001], and now vested in it[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform and Pensions (1999 Order) (Commencement No. 11) Order (Northern Ireland) 2002.

- (2) In this Order any reference to a numbered Article or Schedule is to the Article of, or the Schedule to, the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 bearing that number.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) paragraph 12 of Schedule 2 (miscellaneous amendments), in so far as not already in operation, and Article 17 in so far as it relates to that paragraph; and

- (b) in Part I of Schedule 10 (repeals) the repeal in Article 58(6)(*a*) of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^f00003] and Article 76 in so far as it relates to it,

is 19th March 2002.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 25th February 2002.

John O'Neill — Senior Officer of the — Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order provides for the coming into operation of paragraph 12 of Schedule 2 to the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 (occupational pension schemes: certificates etc. relating to a minimum funding requirement), in so far as not already in operation, and Article 17 of that Order in so far as it relates to that paragraph and a related repeal in Schedule 10 to that Order, on 19th March 2002.
