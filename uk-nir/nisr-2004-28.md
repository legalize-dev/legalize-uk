# Superannuation (Office of the Commissioner for Children and Young People for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004

Made: 2nd February 2004

Coming into operation: 8th March 2004

The Department of Finance and Personnel in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Article 3(4) and (7) of the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002] and of every other power enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Office of the Commissioner for Children and Young People for Northern Ireland) Order (Northern Ireland) 2004 and shall come into operation on 8th March 2004.

#### Addition to list of employments and offices

###### 2

There shall be added to the employments listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 with effect from 1st October 2003 employment in the Office of the Commissioner for Children and Young People for Northern Ireland[^f00003].

###### 3

There shall be added to the offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 with effect from 1st October 2003 the office of Commissioner for Children and Young People for Northern Ireland.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Finance and Personnel on 2nd February 2004.

Michael Daly — A Senior Officer of the — Department of Finance and Personnel

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order adds employment in the Office of the Commissioner for Children and Young People for Northern Ireland and the office of Commissioner for Children and Young People for Northern Ireland to the employments and offices listed in Schedule 1 to the Superannuation (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 (“the 1972 Order”) for which the Department of Finance and Personnel may make pension schemes. It is retrospective by virtue of Article 3(7) of the 1972 Order.
