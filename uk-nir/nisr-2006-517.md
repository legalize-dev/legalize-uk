# Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2006

Made: 13th December 2006

Coming into operation: 1st January 2007

The Fisheries Conservancy Board for Northern Ireland, makes the following Byelaws, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 26(1), 37, 51(2), 52(2), 70(1) and (2), 71(2)(g), 72(1), 89, 95, 97, 114(1)(b) and 115(1)(b) of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966[^f00001], and with the approval of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure[^f00002]:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Byelaws may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2006 and shall come into operation on 1st January 2007.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003

###### 2

The Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003[^f00003] shall be amended as provided in Byelaws 3 and 4.

#### Licence duties and fees

###### 3

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### List of waters where there is no close season for rainbow trout

###### 4

In Schedule 6 in the list of rainbow trout waters where there is no annual close season for angling for rainbow trout —

- (a) under the heading County Antrim there shall be inserted before the reference to “Cornmill Fishery” a reference to “Cairn Farm Fly Fishery, Carrickfergus”;

- (b) under the heading County Armagh there shall be inserted after the reference to “Craigavon City Park North Lake” a reference to “Drummanbeg Lake, Drumnamore Road, Armagh” and

- (c) under the heading County Londonderry there shall be inserted after the reference to “Springwell Fishery” a reference to “Stonefalls Trout Fishery, Castlerock”.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Byelaws)

These Byelaws amend the Fisheries Byelaws (Northern Ireland) 2003.

The principal changes are as follows:–

1 The Byelaws increase the licence duties payable from 1st January 2007 for some fishing rod licences and some commercial fishing engines used for the taking of salmon and freshwater fish other than eels, together with the fee payable for a licence which authorises the holder to buy and sell salmon, trout and eels (Byelaw 3).
