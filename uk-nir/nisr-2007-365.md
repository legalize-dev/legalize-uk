# The Street Works (2007 Amendment Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 28th August 2007

The Department for Regional Development[^f00001] makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Street Works (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007[^f00002].

#### Citation and interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as The Street Works (2007 Amendment Order) (Commencement No. 1) Order (Northern Ireland) 2007.

- (2) In this Order ‘the Order’ means the Street Works (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of the provisions of the Order specified in the Schedule is 1st October 2007.

## SCHEDULE — Provisions of the Order for which 1st October 2007 is appointed as the day of coming into operation.

| Provision of the Order | Subject Matter |
| --- | --- |
| Article 25 | Increase of fines for certain summary offences |
| Article 26 | Inquiries |
| Article 27 | Delegation of Functions |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Regional Development on 28th August 2007.

R Sherman — A senior officer of the Department for Regional Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order appoints 1st October 2007 for the coming into operation of the provisions of The Street Works (Amendment) (Northern Ireland) Order 2007 as specified in the Schedule.
