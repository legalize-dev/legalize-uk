# Agriculture (Student fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Made: 21st September 2007

Coming into operation: 22nd October 2007

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 5A (1) and (2) of the Agriculture Act (Northern Ireland) 1949[^f00002] makes the following Regulations:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (Student Fees) (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on the 22nd October 2007.

#### Amendment to the Agriculture (Student Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

###### 2

The Agriculture (Student Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007[^f00003] shall be amended as provided by regulations 3 to 5.

###### 3

For Regulation 1(1) (Citation, commencement and interpretation) substitute:

> > (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture (Student Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007 and shall come into operation on the 29th January 2007.

###### 4

Regulation 4 (Fees) shall be amended as provided by paragraphs (1) and (2).

- (1) In regulation 4(1) for “£1200” substitute “£1225”.

- (2) For regulation 4(2) substitute:

> > (2) The Department may charge a person a fee of such amount as it may determine in respect of any course of further education or part-time education undertaken by that person.

###### 5

In Regulation 5(2) (Reduced fees for higher education courses) for “£600” substitute “£610”.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development on 21st September 2007

Norman Fulton — A senior officer of the — Department of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Agriculture (Student Fees) (Amounts) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007, so as to substitute a new level of fee to be paid by persons undertaking a course of higher education provided by the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development (Regulations 3 and 4(1)). The Regulations also further clarify provision for the Department to apply fees to courses of further education (Regulation 4(2)), and correct an error (Regulation 1).
