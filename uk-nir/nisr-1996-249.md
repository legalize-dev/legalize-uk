# Plant Health (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996

Made: 25th June 1996

Coming into operation: 29th July 1996

The Department of Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 2[^f00001] and 3(1)[^f00002], 3A[^f00003], 3B(1)[^f00004] and 4(1)[^f00005] of the Plant Health Act (Northern Ireland) 1967[^f00006] and of all powers enabling it in that behalf, hereby makes the following Order:

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Amendment No. 2) Order (Northern Ireland) 1996 and shall come into operation on 29th July 1996.

#### Amendment of the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993

###### 2

The Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993[^f00007] shall be amended in accordance with Articles 3 to 10.

###### 3

In Article 5, for paragraph (4) there shall be substituted—

> > (4) A person shall not without the authority of the Department or an inspector engage in any activity which involves genetic modification of a pest or engage in any activity which to his knowledge involves genetically modified pests.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order.)

This Order further amends the Plant Health Order (Northern Ireland) 1993.

This Order implements as respects Northern Ireland—

- (i) Commission Directive [96/14/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0014) (O.J. No. L.68, 19.3.96, p. 24) amending certain Annexes to Council Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community;

- (ii) Commission Directive [96/15/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0015) (O.J. No. L.70, 20.3.96, p. 35) amending Directive [92/76/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0076) recognising protected zones exposed to particular plant health risks in the Community.

The Order makes the following principal changes—

1 A person engaged in activities involving the genetic modification of pests or genetically modified plant pests under authority of a licence granted by the Department of Agriculture need no longer obtain additional authorisation for those activities from an inspector (regulation 3).

2 Protected zones recognised in respect of various specified plant pests are amended (regulations 4 to 9).
