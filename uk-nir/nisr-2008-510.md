# Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement) Order 2008

Made: 24th December 2008

The Secretary of State makes the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 1(3) of the Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008[^f00001]:

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 (Commencement) Order 2008.

#### Commencement

###### 2

The Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008, in so far as not already in operation, shall come into operation on 2nd February 2009.

## Signed

Paul Goggins — Minister of State — 24 December 2008

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences the Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 2008 as from the 2nd February 2009. Part 1 and Articles 80 and 82 of the 2008 Order came into operation one week after the day on which that Order was made.
