# The Welfare of Animals (2011 Act) (Commencement and Transitional Provisions No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 30th March 2012

Coming into operation: 2nd April 2012

The Department of Agriculture and Rural Development[^f00001], makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 59 of the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^f00002].

#### Citation and Interpretation

###### 1

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Welfare of Animals (2011 Act) (Commencement and Transitional Provisions No.2) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

- (2) In this Order “the Act” means The Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.

#### Appointed Day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) the provisions of the Act specified in Schedule 1; and

- (b) Section 57(2) of and Schedule 5 to the Act insofar as may relate to the statutory provisions listed in Schedule 2,

is the 2nd day of April 2012.

#### Repeals

###### 3

The Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 1972[^f00003] is hereby repealed to the extent specified in Schedule 3.

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order commences certain provisions of the Welfare of Animals (Northern Ireland) Act 2011 and repeals certain provisions of the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 1972.

This Order specifically commences certain repeals contained in Schedule 5 of the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011. These repeals are consequential upon the commencement of the earlier repeal of provisions in the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 and their replacement by provisions of the Welfare of Animals Act (Northern Ireland) 2011.
