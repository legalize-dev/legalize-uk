# The Welfare Reform (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012

Made: 30th November 2012

The Department for Social Development makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(2) of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2010[^f00001].

#### Citation

###### 1

This Order may be cited as the Welfare Reform (2010 Act) (Commencement No. 5) Order (Northern Ireland) 2012.

#### Appointed day

###### 2

The day appointed for the coming into operation of—

- (a) section 31 (payments of child support maintenance); and

- (b) section 32 (child support maintenance; offences relating to information) in so far as not already in operation,

of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2010, is 3rd December 2012.

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department for Social Development on 30th November 2012

Anne McCleary — A senior officer of the Department for Social Development

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Order)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order brings into operation sections 31 and 32, in so far as not already in operation, of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2010 on 3rd December 2012.
