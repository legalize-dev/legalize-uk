# The Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015

Made: 1st May 2015

Coming into operation: 1st June 2015

The Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure [^f00001], makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 26(1), 89 and 95(1) (of the Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 [^f00002] and now vested in it [^f00003].

#### Citation and commencement

###### 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2015 and come into operation on 1st June 2015.

#### Amendment of the Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014

###### 2

The Fisheries Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014[^f00004] shall be amended as provided in Regulation 3.

#### Exemptions to Catch and Release for Salmon Angling

###### 3

For Schedule 7 (List of Waters where Catch and Release for Salmon Angling does not apply) there shall be substituted the following Schedule:

> > **SCHEDULE 7**

| *List of Waters* *(1)* | *Dates when catch and release does not apply* *(2)* | *Restrictions on number and length of salmon which may be retained in respect of each person* *(3)* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lough Melvin (Co. Fermanagh) | 1st February to 30th September in any year (both dates inclusive) | 1st February to 30th September (both dates inclusive) – 2 salmon |
| Glenarm River (Co. Antrim) | 1st June to 31st October in any year (both dates inclusive) | 1st June to 31st October (both dates inclusive) – 2 salmon |
| Clady River (Co. Londonderry) | 1st June to 31st October in any year (both dates inclusive) | 1st June to 31st October (both dates inclusive) – 2 salmon |
|  |  |  |

## Signed

Sealed with the Official Seal of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure on 1st May 2015.

F.Devitt — A senior officer of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure

## Explanatory note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the list of waters which are now exempt from catch and release for salmon angling.
